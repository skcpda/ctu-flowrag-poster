# Accidental Death Benefit Scheme (PBAOCWWB)

**Short Title:** ADBS-PBAOCWWB

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Accident, Death, Construction, Worker, Financial Assistance

The &quot;Accidental Death Benefit Scheme&quot; is a Welfare Scheme by the Puducherry Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board, Labour Department, Union Territory of Puducherry. This scheme extends financial assistance of ₹2,25,000/- to the nominees/dependents of the deceased Construction Worker, apart from the ₹2,00,000/- extended through the Life Insurance Corporation (LIC). The amount is credited through Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) into the bank account of the nominee/dependent. The applications are accepted offline.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Crisis/Disaster/Accident

**Where it applies:** Puducherry; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - **Financial Assistance:** ₹2,25,000/-.- **Time of Disbursal:** Within 15 days of the application.- **Mode of Disbursal:** Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) into the bank account.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Labour Department, Puducherry.
