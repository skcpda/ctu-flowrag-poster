# Deendayal Upadhay Sahkarita Kisan Kalyan Yojana

**Short Title:** DDUSKKY

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Loan, Interest Free, BPL, Farmers, Self Help Groups, Agriculture

The scheme &quot;Deendayal Upadhyay Sahakarita Kisan Kalyan Yojana&quot; by the Cooperative Societies, Uttarakhand Government, aims to provide economic facilities to farmers, aiming to double their agricultural production income, and to make the loan distribution scheme beneficial under the state government&#39;s plan to double farmers&#39; income. It also seeks to check migration from rural areas, create employment opportunities, and provide self-employment opportunities to farmers to improve their livelihoods. The scheme is implemented by Cooperative Societies, District Cooperative Banks, and State Cooperative Banks.

**Who is this for:** Individual, Family, Self Help Groups (SHGS); Category: Banking,Financial Services and Insurance; Sector: Employment services and jobs

**Where it applies:** Uttarakhand; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** 1. Interest-free loan up to ₹1,00,000/- for agricultural activities for individuals. 1. Interest-free loan up to ₹3,00,000/- for non-agricultural activities for individuals. These activities include, but are not limited to, animal husbandry, medicinal plant production, aromatic plants, spice production, dairy business, fisheries, poultry, mushroom production, flower production, horticulture, agricultural processing, agricultural mechanization, organic farming, off-season vegetable production, and poly-house. 1. Interest-free loan up to ₹5,00,000/- for Self-Help Groups. 1. **Additional Information:**
Briefdescription: The scheme aims to provide economic facilities to farmers to double their agricultural production income. Through this scheme, interest-free loans for individuals and for Self-Help Groups are provided to the general, small, marginal, and Below Poverty Line farmers.
