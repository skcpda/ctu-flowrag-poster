# Mukhyamantri Kanya Vivah Yojana

**Short Title:** MKVY

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Financial Assistance, Girl Marriage, Poor Family, BPL

The scheme &amp;quot;Mukhyamantri Kanya Vivah Yojana&amp;quot; was introduced by the Social Welfare Department, Government of Bihar, and aims to provide a grant at the time of marriage to girls from BPL and other economically disadvantaged families, to offer financial assistance to their families during the marriage. Under the scheme, girls are encouraged to complete their education, and upon getting married at the appropriate age, they receive financial assistance from the government. This initiative aims to raise awareness among the public, thereby contributing to an increase in the literacy rate of Bihar. With the introduction of this scheme, the state government has also made a concerted effort to curb social evils like dowry and bring about a positive change in societal mindset. 

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Financial assistance

**Where it applies:** Bihar; State

**DBT Scheme:** Yes (Direct Benefit Transfer)

**Benefits / Assistance:** - Under the scheme, an amount of ₹5,000/- will be given at the time of marriage of the girl child. 

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Social Welfare Department.

Briefdescription: The purpose of this scheme is to provide financial assistance to poor families at the time of marriage, to encourage girls' education, and prevent child marriage.
