# Mukhyamantri Gyansetu Merit Scholarship Yojana

**Short Title:** MMGMY

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Scholarship, Education, Student, Financial Assistance

The scheme &amp;quot;Mukhyamantri Gyansetu Merit Scholarship Yojana&amp;quot; by the Education Department, Government of Gujarat aims to select 30,000 brilliant students who have completed their Class 5 education consecutively in government, aided, or RTE-approved schools and provide them with scholarships to study in selected government, aided, or private schools from Class 6th to Class 12th. The selection is done through a Common Entrance Test conducted by the State Examination Board, Gandhinagar. 

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Education & Learning; Sector: Scholarships and student finance

**Where it applies:** Gujarat; State/ UT

**DBT Scheme:** Yes (Direct Benefit Transfer)

**Benefits / Assistance:** > **Government Schools and Government -aided Schools (Approved by Education Department):** - For Class 6th to 8th: ₹5,000/- per year. - For Class 9th to 10th: ₹6,000/- per year. - For Class 11th to 12th: ₹7,000/- per year. > **Private (Selected) Schools:** - For Class 6th to 8th: ₹20,000/- per year. - For Class 9th to 10th: ₹22,000/- per year. - For Class 11th to 12th: ₹25,000/- per year. > **Frequency of Disbursement:** - Disbursed annually in two installments; the first 50% is given after confirmation of at least 80% attendance in the first semester. 

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Education Department.
