# Coaching Help Scheme

**Short Title:** CHS

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Tuition Fee Assistance, Education

The &amp;quot;Coaching Help Scheme&amp;quot; introduced by the Social Justice and Empowerment Department, Gujarat, offers an annual incentive to eligible students for tuition classes outside their school/college. The aim is to help academically bright students continue their studies without financial constraints.  

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Education & Learning; Sector: Financial assistance

**Where it applies:** Gujarat; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** **Financial Assistance:** ₹15,000/- per year for coaching expenses outside the school/college. 

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Exclusions / Not eligible:** - Education or tuition fees paid in school/college are not eligible for the scheme.

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Social Justice and Empowerment Department.

**References / Annexures:** - **Guidelines**: https://gueedc.gujarat.gov.in/Tution-Help-Scheme.html - **User Manual**: https://esamajkalyan.gujarat.gov.in/ApplicationDocuments/GUEEDC/HowtoApplyGUEEDC.pdf

**Additional Information:**
Briefdescription: The "Coaching Help Scheme" provides financial support to non-reserved category students pursuing science in Standard-11 and Standard-12 by covering additional tuition costs.
