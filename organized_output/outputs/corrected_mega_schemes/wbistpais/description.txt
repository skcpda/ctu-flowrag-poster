#  The West Bengal Incentive Scheme: Tourism Promotion Assistance in lieu of Interest Subsidy

**Short Title:** WBISTPAIS

**Scheme For:** Infra

**Tags:** Tourism, Tourism Promotion, Interest Subsidy, Entrepreneurship

The scheme “State Capital Investment Subsidy” is a sub scheme under “The West Bengal Incentive Scheme”. It was launched on 22nd February, 2021, by the Department of Tourism, Government of West Bengal, and shall remain valid upto 31st December, 2025. &gt; **Objective of the &amp;quot;West Bengal Incentive Scheme” Scheme:** The object of the Scheme is to provide financial assistance and help enthusiastic intending or prospective entrepreneurs to start their own business as a self-employed youth who will become an employer in upcoming days and generate more employment. &gt; **Objective of the “Tourism Promotion Assistance in lieu of Interest Subsidy” scheme:** Through this incentive, an eligible unit shall be provided with a Tourism Promotion Assistance for State Goods and Service Tax (SGST) irrespective of the location of the project, which will be in lieu of interest subsidy.

**Who is this for:** Business Entity; Category: Business & Entrepreneurship; Sector: Setting up / start-up / entrepreneurship

**Where it applies:** West Bengal; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** **Pattern of Assistance:** Assistance at the rate of 75% of SGST for 5 years irrespective of the location of the project, which will be in lieu of interest subsidy. &lt;br&gt;

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Exclusions / Not eligible:** - The West Bengal Incentive Scheme shall stand non-applicable in respect of those tourism units that have been registered for and have been sanctioned incentives under any previous scheme. - Any unit which may have applied for registration with the Directorate of Tourism under WBIS (West Bengal Incentive Scheme ) 2015, before the date of commencement of this Scheme and whose applications may have not been disposed off prior to the coming into effect of this 2021 Scheme or deemed to have been disposed of shall be entitled to all benefits under WBIS 2015 only.

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Tourism Department.
