# Indira Gandhi National Disability Pension Scheme (Punjab)

**Short Title:** IGNDPSP

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Pension, Indira Gandhi, Disability, PwD

The &quot;Indira Gandhi National Disability Pension Scheme (IGNDPS)&quot; is implemented by the Department of Social Security and Development of Women and Children, Government of Punjab. Under this scheme, persons with disabilities aged 18 years and above, having a disability level of 80% or more, are eligible to receive a pension of ₹300/- per month. After attaining the age of 80 years, the pension amount increases to ₹500/- per month. Dwarfs are also included as an eligible category under this scheme.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Pension

**Where it applies:** Punjab; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - Pension is Rs. 300/- per month (age 18-79 year) - Pension is Rs. 500/- per month (age 80 and above year)

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Department of Social Security and Development of Women and Children.

**References / Annexures:** - **Guidelines**: https://sswcd.punjab.gov.in/en/indira-gandhi-national-disability-pension-scheme-igndps-0
