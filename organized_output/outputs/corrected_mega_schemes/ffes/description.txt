# Feed And Fodder Entrepreneurship Scheme

**Short Title:** FFES

**Launch Date:** 2014-01-01

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Fodder Value Addition, Total Mixed Ration, Storage Facilities, Farmers Scientist Interaction, Fodder Block Making

**Objectives** 1. Establishing an effective seed production chain. 1. Capacity building of state functionaries and livestock owners in forage production, conservation and utilization. 1. Establishing convergence and synergy among ongoing Plan programmes and stakeholders for fodder resource development.  **Salient Feature** 1. Under this activity, the fodder seed chain i.e. breeder, foundation and certified quality fodder seed production will be incentivized. 1. This component targeted towards the multiplication of seeds of high yielding fodder varieties which has been developed through research and innovations, significantly showing better performance, for which incentives will be provided.

**Who is this for:** Individual, Self Help Groups (SHGS); Category: Agriculture,Rural & Environment; Sector: Animal husbandry

**Where it applies:** Mizoram; State
