# Rehabilitation Treatment For The Disabled

**Short Title:** RTFD

**Launch Date:** 2007-04-01

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Medical Treatment, Assistive Devices, Rehabilitation Services, Training, Education

- Financial assistance to disabled persons who cannot afford to undergo treatment inside and outside the state.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Medicine and health products

**Where it applies:** Meghalaya; State
