# Viranarayan Singh Shram Ann Yojana

**Short Title:** VSSAY

**Launch Date:** 2018-01-17

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Food Assistance, Unorganized Worker, Hot Meal

Labor Department of Chhattisgarh State started this scheme &amp;amp;quot;Viranarayan Singh Shram Ann Yojana&amp;amp;quot; for unorganized worker on 17 January 2018. The main objective of the scheme to provide the food assistance for unorganized worker at their work places.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Food Security / Public Distribution System

**Where it applies:** Chhattisgarh; State

**Benefits / Assistance:** 1. Hot food is being distributed to construction workers at ₹ 5/- in food centers. 1. Cooked rice, dal, vegetable or khichdi, fai dal, vegetable or cumin rice, fried dal, vegetable. 1. Pickles will also be payable along with the food.

**Benefit Type:** In Kind

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Labour Department.
