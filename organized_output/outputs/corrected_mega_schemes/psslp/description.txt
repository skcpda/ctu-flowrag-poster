# Prize Schemes to the Students (State Level Prizes)

**Short Title:** PSSLP

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Minority Welfare, Scholarship, Education, Financial Assistance

The &amp;quot;Prize Schemes to the Students (State Level Prizes)&amp;quot; launched by the Backward Classes, Most Backward Classes and Minorities Welfare Department, Tamil Nadu, aimed at supporting students from Backward Classes (BC), Most Backward Classes (MBC), and Denotified Communities (DNC). The prizes are distributed to students from both Government/Government Aided and Private Schools who have studied Tamil as their first language. Awards are given in various categories, with no income limit.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Education & Learning; Sector: Scholarships and student finance

**Where it applies:** Tamil Nadu; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - **First Prize:** ₹50,000/- (boys from BC), ₹25,000/- (girls from BC) - **Second Prize:** ₹30,000/- (boys from BC), ₹20,000/- (girls from BC) - **Third Prize:** ₹20,000/- (boys from BC), ₹15,000/- (girls from BC)

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Exclusions / Not eligible:** - State Level Topper students who are awarded prizes by the Education Department are not eligible.

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Backward Classes Most Backward Classes and Minorities Welfare Department.

**References / Annexures:** - **Guidelines**: https://www.tn.gov.in/scheme/data_view/27495 - **Guidelines**: https://cms.tn.gov.in/sites/default/files/schemes/bcmbcmw_2.pdf - **Community List**: https://bcmbcmw.tn.gov.in/community_list.pdf

**Additional Information:**
Briefdescription: The scheme awards students from Backward Classes (BC), Most Backward Classes (MBC), and Denotified Communities (DNC) for their excellent performance in academic exams studying in Government/Government Aided and Private Schools.
