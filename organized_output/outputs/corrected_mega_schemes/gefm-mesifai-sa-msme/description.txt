# "Green and Eco-Friendly Measures" under “Motivation of Entrepreneurs to Start Industries and Fiscal Assistance to Industries - State Awards to Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises”

**Short Title:** GEFM-MESIFAI-SA-MSME

**Scheme For:** Infra

**Tags:** Award, Entrepreneur, MSME, Business, Environment

&quot;Green and Eco-Friendly Measures&quot; under “Motivation of Entrepreneurs to Start Industries and Fiscal Assistance to Industries - State Awards to Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises” is a scheme by the Industries and Commerce Department, Union Territory of Puducherry. The award is conferred upon the industries that are green and eco-friendly as per the standards of the Department of Science, Technology and Environment (DSTE), Puducherry. The awardee is given a cash award of ₹2,00,000/- and a citation. The applications are accepted offline in the prescribed form available on the Official Website of the Department.

**Who is this for:** Business Entity; Category: Business & Entrepreneurship; Sector: Pollution and waste management

**Where it applies:** Puducherry; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** Cash Award of ₹2,00,000/- and a Citation.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Industries and Commerce Department, Puducherry.

**References / Annexures:** - **Guidelines**: https://industry.py.gov.in/sites/default/files/state-award-go.pdf
