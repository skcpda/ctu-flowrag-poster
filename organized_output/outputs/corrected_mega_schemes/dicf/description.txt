# District Innovation And Challenge Fund

**Short Title:** DICF

**Scheme For:** Infra

**Tags:** Infrastructure, Innovation, Development, Policy

&quot;District Innovation and Challenge Fund (DICF)&quot; is an innovation scheme by the Finance, Planning, and Investment Department (Planning &amp; Investment Division), Govt. of Arunachal Pradesh. The objectives of the scheme are: 1. Instill a sense of competitiveness amongst the Deputy Commissioners for developmental interventions. 1. To bring innovation in the design, planning, and implementation of critical infrastructure. 1. Introduce cutting-edge and latest technology in filling up infrastructural gaps in the districts. 1. To instill a sense of participation amongst the Deputy Commissioners for major infrastructural interventions in the districts. 1. Foster partnerships between State Government and the community. 1. Foster community involvement and participation. 1. Deliver effective outcomes from State’s policies and fund allocation.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Transport & Infrastructure; Sector: Public transport and private vehicles

**Where it applies:** Arunachal Pradesh; State
