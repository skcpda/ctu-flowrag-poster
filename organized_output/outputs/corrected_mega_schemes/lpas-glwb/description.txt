# Laptop Purchase Assistance Scheme- Gujarat Labour Welfare Board

**Short Title:** LPAS-GLWB

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Laptop, Labour, Industrial Worker, Student

The “Laptop Purchase Assistance Scheme” is implemented by the Gujarat Labour Welfare Board, Labour, Skill Development &amp;amp; Employment Department, Government of Gujarat. The objective of the scheme is to encourage the children of laborers who are enrolled in professional or design courses by providing assistance for purchasing laptops, thereby reducing the financial burden of education.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Financial assistance

**Where it applies:** Gujarat; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** 1. Under the scheme, 50% of the amount or ₹25,000/- whichever is less will be given to the student admitted in a professional or designing course, with consideration to the laptop price limit of ₹50,000/-.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: Gujarat Labour Welfare Board; Nodal Department: Labour and Employment Department.
