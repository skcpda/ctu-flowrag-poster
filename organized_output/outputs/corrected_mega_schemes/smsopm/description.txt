# Seed Multiplication Scheme of Paddy, Millets, Pulses, Oilseeds and Cotton

**Short Title:** SMSOPM

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Agriculture, Farmer, Seed Multiplication, Paddy, Crops

The “Seed Multiplication Scheme of Paddy, Millets, Pulses, Oilseeds, and Cotton” by the Agriculture-Farmers Welfare Department, Tamil Nadu, offers subsidies to farmers to produce seeds under contract with the Department of Agriculture. Farmers receive training, source seeds, and guidelines for maintaining seed quality. After quality checks and processing, farmers are paid based on the quality of seeds produced. A 90% Minimum Support Price payment is made during processing, with the remainder settled after quality certification.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Agriculture,Rural & Environment; Sector: Financial assistance

**Where it applies:** Tamil Nadu; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - **Premium:** On seeds ranges from ₹1/- to ₹3/- per kg. - **Foundation Class Seeds:** The premium is between 7.5% to 30% over the Local Market Rate per kg, depending on the type of oil seed. - **For Certified Class Seeds:** The premium is between 5% to 20% over the Local Market Rate per kg, based on the specific oil seed. - **Training:** The farmer so desirous of enrolling himself/herself will be given a training by the authorities concerned at the beginning of each year

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Agriculture Department.
