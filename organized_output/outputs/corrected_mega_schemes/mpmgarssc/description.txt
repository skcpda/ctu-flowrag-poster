# Mamasaheb Phadke Management of Govt Adarsh ​​Residential Schools (SC) Scheme

**Short Title:** MPMGARSSC

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Hostel, Student, Textbooks, Uniform, Scheduled Caste

The “Mamasaheb Phadke Management of Government Adarsh Residential Schools (SC)” scheme was launched by the Social Justice and Empowerment Department, Government of Gujarat. The scheme aims to expand access to quality residential education for scheduled caste (SC) students in standards 9th to 12th. Under this scheme, eligible SC students receive free boarding, lodging, textbooks, uniforms, meals, and additional support services to enhance academic participation. 

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Education & Learning; Sector: Resources for schools

**Where it applies:** Gujarat; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** **Free Services Provided:** - Board and lodging, meals, textbooks, uniforms, and water - Study materials, mid‑day meals, and other academic support - Ration card, PH identity card, and other entitlements, as applicable 

**Benefit Type:** In Kind

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Social Justice and Empowerment Department.
