# Free Cycles to SC Girl Students Studying in Standard IX (Sarasvati Sadhna Yojana)

**Short Title:** FCSCGSSSIX(SSY)

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Free Cycle, Scheduled Caste, Girl Student

The “Free Cycles to SC Girl Students Studying in Standard IX (Sarasvati Sadhna Yojana)” scheme is administered by the Directorate of Scheduled Caste Welfare, Department of Social Justice &amp;amp; Empowerment, Government of Gujarat. Under this scheme, free bicycles are given to scheduled caste girl students who are studying in Standard IX.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment

**Where it applies:** Gujarat; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - Under this scheme, free cycles are given to scheduled caste girl students who are studying in Standard IX.

**Benefit Type:** In Kind

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: DisDeputy Director (SCW) Office, Gujarat; Nodal Department: Social Justice and Empowerment Department.
