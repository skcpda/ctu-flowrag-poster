# Sindhu Darshan Pilgrimage Scheme - Rajasthan

**Short Title:** SDPSR

**Launch Date:** 2016-04-01

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Reimbursement, Pilgrimage, Pilgrim, Sindhu

The scheme &amp;quot;Sindhu Darshan Pilgrimage Scheme&amp;quot; was introduced by the Devasthan Department, Government of Rajasthan, on 1st April 2016. The objective of this scheme is to provide financial assistance to pilgrims undertaking a pilgrimage to the Sindhu River in the Ladakh region of India. Under this scheme, selected beneficiaries receive reimbursement of their travel expenses, up to a maximum of ₹10,000/- per pilgrim. > **Total Number of Beneficiaries** The scheme is limited to 200 pilgrims per year. If there are more applicants than available slots, selection will be by lottery.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Travel & Tourism; Sector: Tourism in India

**Where it applies:** Rajasthan; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - Reimbursement of 50% of actual travel expenses, up to a maximum of ₹10,000 per pilgrim. > **Note:** Payment is made via online bank transfer or through account payee cheque or demand draft, depending on departmental conditions.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Devasthan Department.
