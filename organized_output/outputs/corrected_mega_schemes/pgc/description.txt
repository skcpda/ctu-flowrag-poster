# Palna Ghar (Crèche)

**Short Title:** PGC

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Palna Ghar, Creche, Childcare, Day Care

The scheme “Palna Ghar (Crèche)” is implemented by the Women Welfare Department, Government of Uttarakhand. The scheme aims to provide day care facilities for children aged 6 months to 6 years of working couples. 

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Women and Child; Sector: Childcare

**Where it applies:** Uttarakhand; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** Under the scheme, a crèche is run for the children of working couples, providing day care facilities for children in the age group of 6 months to 6 years. The following fees are applicable under the scheme: - For BPL families: ₹20/- per child per month - For families with an income less than ₹12,000/-: ₹100/- per child per month - For families with an income above ₹12,000/-: ₹200/- per child per month 

**Benefit Type:** In Kind

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: Women Welfare Department, Government Of Uttarakhand; Nodal Department: Department Of Woman Empowerment & Child Development.

**References / Annexures:** - **Guidelines (Page No. 24)**: https://uk.gov.in/department92/library_file/file-30-01-2025-04-25-16.pdf

**Additional Information:**
Briefdescription: The scheme aims to provide day care facilities for children aged 6 months to 6 years of working couples at a nominal monthly fee in Crèches.
