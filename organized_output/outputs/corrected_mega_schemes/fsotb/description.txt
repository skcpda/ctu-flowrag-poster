# Free Supply of Text Books

**Short Title:** FSOTB

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Student, Education, School, Book, Financial Assistance

The scheme &quot;Free Supply of Text Book&quot; by the Department of Education (DoE), Government of NCT of Delhi, aims to provide textbooks or cash in lieu of textbooks and writing material to students of Government and Aided Schools, ensuring equitable access to educational resources. The scheme offers financial assistance for purchasing textbooks and geometry boxes, with varying amounts based on class levels. Eligible beneficiaries include all students from Nursery to XII in Government and Aided Schools, irrespective of gender or income. The scheme is implemented by the Department of Education, GNCT of Delhi.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Education & Learning; Sector: Resources for schools

**Where it applies:** Delhi; State/ UT

**DBT Scheme:** Yes (Direct Benefit Transfer)

**Benefits / Assistance:** > **Cash Subsidy for Textbooks and Writing Materials** - Class 1st to 5th: ₹550/- per annum. - Class 6th, 7th, 11th, and 12th (Without Math): ₹800/- per annum. - Class 8th, 11th, and 12th (With Math): ₹830/- per annum. - Class 5th: ₹630/- per annum. - Class 10th: ₹730/- per annum. > **Additional Support** Cash subsidy of ₹30/- per student for classes VIII, IX, and X, and for students of classes XI and XII who opt for Mathematics, for purchasing a Geometry Box. **Mode of Disbursement** - For Cash Subsidies: The amount is transferred directly to the student’s/parent’s bank account. - For Textbooks: The school distributes the books to eligible students.

**Benefit Type:** Composite

**Exclusions / Not eligible:** Students of private unaided schools are not eligible.

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Education Department.
