# Jhota Buggi/Camel/Mule Cart Scheme (HSFDC)

**Short Title:** JBCMCSHSFDC

**Scheme For:** Infra

**Tags:** Jhota Buggi, Camel Cart, Mule Cart, HSFDC, BPL, Scheduled Caste, Loan

The scheme &amp;quot;Jhota Buggi/Camel/Mule Cart&amp;quot; is implemented by the Haryana Scheduled Castes Finance and Development Corporation (HSFDC) in collaboration with the bank tie-up schemes. The HSFDC is a Company registered under the Companies Act, 1956 on 02.01.1971. It is a wholly Government-owned Corporation with a 51% share of the State Government and a 49% share of the Government of India. Under this scheme, the Corporation provides loans to individuals from Below Poverty Line (BPL) families, whose annual family income does not exceed ₹1,80,000/-, in both rural and urban areas for purchasing animal-driven carts such as Jhota Buggis, camel carts, and mule carts. The scheme provides a subsidy of 50% of the total cost, with a maximum subsidy amount of ₹10,000/-.
