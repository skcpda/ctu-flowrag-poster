# Distress Relief Scheme

**Short Title:** DRS

**Scheme For:** Family

**Tags:** Financial Assistance, Family, Death, BPL, Relief

The &quot;Distress Relief Scheme&quot; was launched by the Social Welfare and Nutritious Meal Programme Department, Government of Tamil Nadu. The scheme&#39;s objective is to provide financial assistance of ₹20,000/- to the BPL households on the death of their primary breadwinner (male or female).

**Who is this for:** Family; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Financial assistance

**Where it applies:** Tamil Nadu; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** Financial Assistance of ₹20,000/-.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: The Revenue Department; Nodal Department: Social Welfare and Nutritious Meal Programme Department.

**References / Annexures:** - **Citizen's Charter**: https://www.tnsocialwelfare.tn.gov.in/website-345/sites/default/files/2022-01/citizen_chart_2017_18.pdf - **Guidelines**: https://cms.tn.gov.in/sites/default/files/gos/rev/rev470-e.pdf
