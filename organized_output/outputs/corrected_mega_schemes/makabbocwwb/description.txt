# Medical Assistance (Karmika Arogya Bhagya) (K.B.O.C.W.W.B)

**Short Title:** MAKABBOCWWB

**Scheme For:** Family

**Tags:** Labour, Building Worker, Construction Worker, Medical Assistance, Hospitalisation

The &amp;quot;Medical Assistance (Karmika Arogya Bhagya)&amp;#39;&amp;#39; by the BOCW Welfare Board, Department of Labour, Karnataka, is a welfare scheme for the registered workers of the board. Under this Scheme, registered construction workers and their dependents are eligible for monetary support during hospitalisation for treatment in government, private, or hospitals recognised under any insurance scheme of the State Government. The assistance shall be towards hospitalisation for a minimum of 48 hours continuously.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Health & Wellness; Sector: Financial assistance

**Where it applies:** Karnataka; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** **Financial Coverage:** ₹300/- per day to the maximum of ₹20,000/- for a continuous period of Hospitalization subject to actuals. &gt; **Note:****** The assistance shall be towards hospitalisation for a minimum of 48 hours continuously.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Labour Department.
