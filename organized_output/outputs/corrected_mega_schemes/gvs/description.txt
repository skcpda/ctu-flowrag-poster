# Goat Valley Scheme

**Short Title:** GVS

**Scheme For:** Infra

**Tags:** Loan, Self Employment, Goat Rearing, Entrepreneurship, Livestock

The “Goat Valley” scheme was launched in 2022 by the Department of Animal Husbandry, Government of Uttarakhand, as a cluster-based model for promoting entrepreneurship in the animal husbandry sector in the state. Its objectives include establishing Goat Valleys as clusters for organized goat rearing, promoting youth self-employment, transforming traditional goat rearing into profit-oriented enterprises and organising the sector to enhance year-round livelihood opportunities for farmers. This initiative brings together various schemes, such as the District Goat Rearing Program, State Goat Rearing Program, NCDC Loan, Deen Dayal Upadhyay Loan, and REAP to develop dedicated goat-saturated zones in each district.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Agriculture,Rural & Environment; Sector: Financial assistance

**Where it applies:** Uttarakhand; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** **1. Goat Unit Establishment:** Each beneficiary establishes a goat unit of 15+1 animals. **2. Financial Support Structure:** - **Loan:** ₹30,000 loan from NCDC (National Cooperative Development Corporation).- **Subsidy:** ₹63,000 subsidy.- **Beneficiary Contribution:** Requires a ₹7,000 beneficiary contribution. > **Conditions/Aspects/Criteria associated with the benefits:** - The goat unit size is fixed at 15+1 animals. - The specific financial contributions (loan, subsidy, beneficiary) are predetermined for each unit.

**Benefit Type:** Composite

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Department Of Animal Husbandry.
