# Goa Traditional Bakers (Poders) Subsidy Scheme

**Short Title:** GTBPSS

**Scheme For:** Infra

**Tags:** Baker, Tradition, Subsidy, Financial Assistance, Bread

Launched in 2012, the scheme &quot;Goa Traditional Bakers (Poders) Subsidy Scheme&quot; by the Department of Industries, Government of Goa aims to provide financial assistance to traditional bakers (poders) in Goa to ensure the protection and growth of this traditional occupation. Through this scheme, subsidies for the purchase of maida (flour) are provided to traditional bakers who produce essential Goan breads like &quot;Pao,&quot; &quot;Undho,&quot; &quot;Poie,&quot; and &quot;Kakon.&quot; The scheme is implemented by the Goa Handicrafts, Rural &amp; Small Scale Industries Development Corporation Ltd. (GHRSSIDC). The objectives of the scheme include maintaining the price of 50gm &quot;Pao&quot; at ₹3 for the next 5 years and supporting bakers who have been in the business for at least 20 years.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Financial assistance

**Where it applies:** Goa; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** The subsidy will be provided for the purchase of maida required for the production of bread, depending on the category the bakers belong to. - **Category A:** Bakers who use less than or equal to 50 kgs. of maida in a day will get a cash subsidy of ₹4 per kg.- **Category B:** Bakers who use between 51 kgs. to 100 kgs. of maida in a day will get a cash subsidy of ₹4 per kg for the first 50 kgs. and ₹2 per kg for the remaining amount of maida.- **Category C:** Bakers who use between 101 kgs. to 150 kgs. of maida in a day will get a cash subsidy of ₹4 per kg for the first 50 kgs., ₹2 per kg for the next 50 kgs., and ₹1 per kg for the remaining amount of maida. ****The subsidy will be disbursed every quarter.*** ****No subsidy for maida beyond the consumption of 150 kgs per day.***

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Labour and Employment Department.
