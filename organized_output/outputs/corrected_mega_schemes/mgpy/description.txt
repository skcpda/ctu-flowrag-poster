# Mukhyamantri Gram Parivahan Yojana

**Short Title:** MGPY

**Launch Date:** 2021-08-07

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Vehicle Purchase Grant, E-Rickshaw Subsidy, Healthcare Transportation, E-Mobility Promotion

The &amp;quot;Mukhyamantri Gram Parivahan Yojana&amp;quot; launched by the Transport Department, Government of Bihar, aimed at improving transportation facilities in all panchayats of the state. It offers financial assistance to beneficiaries for purchasing vehicles.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Financial assistance

**Where it applies:** Bihar; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - **Standard vehicles:** 50% of the purchase price or up to ₹1,00,000/-. - **E-rickshaws:** 50% of the purchase price or up to ₹70,000/-. - **Ambulances:** 50% of the purchase price or up to ₹2,00,000/-.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Transport Department.

**References / Annexures:** - **Guidelines**: https://state.bihar.gov.in/cache/16/Smart%20City/Mukhyamantri%20Gram%20Parivahan%20Yojana/9613-7-8-24.pdf - **User Manual**: http://mmgpy.bihar.gov.in/attachments/instruction.pdf

**Additional Information:**
Briefdescription: The Scheme provides financial assistance for purchasing vehicles to enhance transportation facilities in rural areas. It includes grants for regular vehicles, e-rickshaws, and ambulances under specified conditions.
