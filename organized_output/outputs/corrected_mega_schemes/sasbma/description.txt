# State Agriculture Scholarship for B.Sc. & M.Sc. Agriculture

**Short Title:** SASBMA

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Agriculture, Scholarship, BSc, MSc, Student

The scheme “State Agriculture Scholarship for B.Sc. &amp;amp; M.Sc. Agriculture” was launched by the Department of Agriculture, Government of Nagaland to provide educational assistance to students who are undergoing undergraduate (B.Sc. Agriculture) and post-graduate programme (M.Sc. Agriculture). **Objectives:** - To strengthen human resource development by upgrading technical expertise. - To support the students/scholars undergoing graduate and post-graduate studies in Agriculture disciplines within and outside the state.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Education & Learning; Sector: Scholarships and student finance

**Where it applies:** Nagaland; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** 1. Under the scheme, the eligible students will get the scholarship at the following rates: **Sl. No.****Programme Name****Scholarship Amount**1B.Sc. Agriculture₹700/- per month2M.Sc. Agriculture₹900/- per month2. The State Department of Agriculture also provides book grants &amp;amp; reimbursements annually to the enrolled students under the programme.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: Department Of Agriculture, Government Of Nagaland; Nodal Department: Agriculture Department.
