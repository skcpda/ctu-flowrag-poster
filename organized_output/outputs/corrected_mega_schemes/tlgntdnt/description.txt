# Term Loan Scheme (GNDTDC)

**Short Title:** TLSGNTDNT

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Business, Loan, Small-scale Business, Nomadic Tribes, Denotified Tribes

The scheme &amp;quot;Term Loan Scheme&amp;quot; is implemented by the Gujarat Nomadic and Denotified Tribes Development Corporation (GNDTDC) under the Social Justice and Empowerment Department, Government of Gujarat. The scheme aims to promote self-employment among individuals from the Nomadic and Denotified Tribes communities by providing financial assistance for agriculture and allied activities, small-scale businesses, craftsmanship &amp;amp; traditional occupations as well as service-based businesses/trades. Under this scheme, beneficiaries can avail loans at concessional interest rates to purchase equipment, raw materials, or working capital. 

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Banking,Financial Services and Insurance; Sector: Loan

**Where it applies:** Gujarat; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** **Loan Amount Details:** - **Loan Amount:** Up to ₹20,00,000/-.- **Interest Rate:** 6% per annum. - **Loan Contribution:** 85% will be from the National Corporation, 10% from the State Government, 5% from the beneficiary.- **Repayment:** The total loan amount, including interest, must be repaid in 60 equal monthly instalments. 

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: Gujarat Nomadic And Denotified Tribes Development Corporation; Nodal Department: Social Justice and Empowerment Department.

**References / Annexures:** - **Guidelines**: https://sje.gujarat.gov.in/gntdnt/Term-loan - **Online Application**: https://gndcdconline.gujarat.gov.in/ - **User Manual**: https://gndcdconline.gujarat.gov.in/Home/index
