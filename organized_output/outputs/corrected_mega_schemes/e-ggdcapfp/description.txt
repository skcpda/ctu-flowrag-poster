# Ex-Gratia Grant for Disability to CAPF Personnel

**Short Title:** E-GGDCAPFP

**Scheme For:** Infra

**Tags:** Ex-Gratia, Grant, Disability, CAPF Personnel, PwD, Sainik

The scheme &amp;quot;Ex-Gratia Grant for Disability to CAPF Personnel&amp;quot; is implemented by the Sainik and Ardh Sainik Welfare Department, Government of Haryana. Under the scheme, ex-gratia grant will be given to those personnel of the CAPF hailing from Haryana who get disabled for life and are boarded out of service due to disability declared as in war/operation, in operational area, terrorist activities and natural calamities etc. The grant is given depending on the percentage of disability suffered in the first instance during life.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Crisis/Disaster/Accident

**Where it applies:** Haryana; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** **Ex-gratia grant to disabled CAPF Personnel as per their percentage of disability:** • **Disability 75% and above:** ₹35,00,000/- • **Disability 50% to 74%:** ₹25,00,000/- • **Disability 25% to 49%:** ₹15,00,000/-

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: Sainik And Ardh Sainik Welfare Department, Government Of Haryana; Nodal Department: Sainik and Ardh Sainik Welfare Department.
