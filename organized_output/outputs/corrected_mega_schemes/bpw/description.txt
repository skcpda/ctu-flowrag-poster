# Bhajan Pakhawaj Workshops

**Short Title:** BPW

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Culture, Artist, Bhajan, Workshop, Skill

The scheme &quot;Bhajan Pakhawaj Workshops&quot; is a state-level cultural scheme by the Kala Academy, Department of Art and Culture, Government of Goa. The objective of the scheme is to provide knowledge of appropriate and traditional Bhajan Singing and Pakhawaj playing to Bhajan performers through experts from the respective field. Applications are invited by announcement in local print and electronic media. Certificates are awarded to the participants. A Suitable Honorarium is paid to conductors of workshops.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Sports & Culture; Sector: Culture

**Where it applies:** Goa; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - Knowledge of appropriate and traditional Bhajan Singing and Pakhawaj playing is provided to the Bhajan performers through experts from the respective field. - Certificates are awarded to the participants. - A suitable Honorarium is paid to conductors of workshops. _*The venue of the workshop is decided by Kala Academy._

**Benefit Type:** Composite

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Art, Culture and Museums Department.

**References / Annexures:** - **Guidelines [Page No. 37]**: https://artandculture.goa.gov.in/sites/default/files/Kala%20Academy%20Goa%20Schemes.pdf
