# Dr. Syama Prasad Mookerjee Durghtana Sahayta Yojana

**Short Title:** DSPMDSY

**Launch Date:** 2017-04-01

**Tags:** Accidental Assistance, Death Compensation, Disability, Financial Assistance

Launched in 2017, the &amp;quot;Dr. Syama Prasad Mookerjee Durghtana Sahayta Yojana&amp;quot; is a state-level welfare scheme of the Government of Haryana under the Social Justice and Empowerment Department. It provides financial assistance of ₹1,00,000/- to individuals between 18 and 70 years of age who have suffered accidental death or disability and are not registered under the Pradhan Mantri Suraksha Bima Yojana (PMSBY). The scheme is applicable only to permanent residents of Haryana residing in the state. The scheme is implemented by the Social Justice and Empowerment Department.

**Who is this for:** Category: Banking,Financial Services and Insurance; Sector: Insurance

**Where it applies:** Haryana; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** > **Monetary Benefit:** ₹1,00,000/- is provided in case of: - Accidental death. - Total and permanent loss of both eyes. - Loss of both hands or both feet. - Loss of one eye and one hand or one foot.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Exclusions / Not eligible:** - The applicant should not be registered under the Pradhan Mantri Suraksha Bima Yojana (PMSBY).  

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Social Justice and Empowerment Department.

**References / Annexures:** - **Guidelines**: https://socialjusticehry.gov.in/dr-syama-prasad-mookerjee-durghatna-sahayata-yojana/
