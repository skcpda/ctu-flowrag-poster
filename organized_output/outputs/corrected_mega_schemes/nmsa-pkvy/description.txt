# National Mission For Sustainable Agriculture-Parmparagat Krishi Vikas Yojana (NMSA-PKVY)

**Short Title:** NMSA-PKVY

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Organic Farming, Sustainable Agriculture, Infrastructure Support

The scheme &quot;National Mission For Sustainable Agriculture-Parmparagat Krishi Vikas Yojana (NMSA-PKVY)&quot; is a Centrally Sponsored Scheme that aims to promote traditional and sustainable organic farming practices among farmers by providing financial support and infrastructure assistance. The scheme extends financial assistance for a maximum of 2 hectares per farmer for organic farming inputs, infrastructure development for on-farm organic input production, and related activities. 

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Agriculture,Rural & Environment; Sector: Agricultural Inputs- seeds, fertilizer etc.

**Where it applies:** Gujarat; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - **Financial Assistance:** Assistance is provided for a maximum of 2 hectares per farmer for inputs for organic farming, on-farm input production infrastructure, etc. 

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Agriculture, Farmers Welfare and Cooperation Department.

**References / Annexures:** - **Guidelines (Scheme No. 378)**: https://mariyojana.gujarat.gov.in/Schemeatoz.aspx
