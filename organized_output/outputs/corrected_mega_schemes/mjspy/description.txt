# Mukhyamantri Jankalyan (Shiksha Protsahan Yojna)

**Short Title:** MJSPY

**Launch Date:** 2018-09-01

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Financial Assistance, Students, Higher Education

The main objective of this scheme is to provide higher education to the children of unorganized workers. Through this scheme, the tuition fee for the higher education of all the students whose parents are registered as unorganized workers in the Labor Department of the Government of Madhya Pradesh will be borne by the Government of Madhya Pradesh. Now the students of the state will not be deprived of education due to financial constraints, because the Madhya Pradesh government will provide them higher education. The state&amp;#39;s unemployment rate will also decrease through the Chief Minister&amp;#39;s Jan Kalyan Shiksha Protsahan Yojana. Apart from this, this scheme will also prove effective in improving the standard of living of the state&amp;#39;s citizens.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Education & Learning; Sector: Scholarships and student finance

**Where it applies:** Madhya Pradesh; State

**Benefits / Assistance:** 1. The tuition fees for all such students on admission to graduate, polytechnic, diploma and ITI courses will be borne by the state government. 1. For all these courses, the admission fee and actual fee will also be paid by the government as an expenditure. 1. Mess charges and caution money charges are not included in this. 1. Only that fee will be included under this scheme, which has been determined by the Regulatory Committee or Madhya Pradesh Private University Regulatory Commission and the State Government of India. 1. The students of the state will be able to get education. 1. Students will not have to face any financial crunch to get an education. 1. Reducing the unemployment rate of the state. 1. The standard of living of the citizens of the state will also improve.

**Benefit Type:** In Kind

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Technical Education and Man Power Planning Department.
