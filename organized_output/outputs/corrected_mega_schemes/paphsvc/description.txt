# Planting Assistance Program for Hybrid Seeds of Vegetable Crops

**Short Title:** PAPHSVC

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Agriculture, Vegetable Crop, Plantation Assistance, Natural Farming

The scheme &amp;quot;Planting Assistance Program for Hybrid Seeds of Vegetable Crops&amp;quot; introduced by the Agriculture, Farmers Welfare and Cooperation Department, Gujarat, aims to provide financial support to farmers for the cultivation of hybrid vegetable seeds and encourage natural farming practices.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Agriculture,Rural & Environment; Sector: Financial assistance

**Where it applies:** Gujarat; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** 1. Financial support of 40% of the cost incurred for the hybrid seed plantation, ₹50,000/- per hectare unit cost. 1. Maximum assistance of ₹20,000/- per hectare under both components: - Hybrid Seed Plantation. - Promotion of Natural Farming in Vegetable Crops. 

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Agriculture, Farmers Welfare and Cooperation Department.

**References / Annexures:** - **Guidelines**: https://mariyojana.gujarat.gov.in/MoreDetails.aspx - **User Manual (Applicant Registration)**: https://ikhedut.gujarat.gov.in/site/iKhedut%202.0%20User%20Manual%20PPT_Gujarati.pdf

**Additional Information:**
Briefdescription: This scheme offers financial assistance to farmers for the plantation of hybrid seeds and promotion of natural farming in vegetable crops across Gujarat.
