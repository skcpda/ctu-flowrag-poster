# Medical Assistance (ANBOCWWB)

**Short Title:** MA(ANBOCWWB)

**Scheme For:** Family

**Tags:** Medical Assistance, Building Worker, Construction Worker, Labour, Hospital

The scheme “Medical Assistance” was started by the Andaman &amp;amp; Nicobar Islands Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board (ANBOCWWB), Department of Labour, Employment &amp;amp; Training, Andaman &amp;amp; Nicobar Administration. Under this scheme, medical assistance shall be provided to the Building &amp;amp; Other Construction Workers, who are registered under the A&amp;amp;N Islands Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board, and his/her dependents&amp;#39; family members during hospitalization because of an accident or illness.

**Who is this for:** Family, Individual; Category: Health & Wellness; Sector: Financial assistance

**Where it applies:** Andaman and Nicobar Islands; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** 1. Medical assistance will be provided to the beneficiaries @ ₹200/- per day for the first 5 days of hospitalization and ₹100 per day for the remaining days subject to a maximum of ₹5,000/-. 1. The beneficiary shall be entitled to ₹1,00,000/- in the event of medical referred cases to the Mainland of the beneficiary or his/her dependents to the Mainland by the Govt. Medical Authorities for treatment. 1. Subject to production of a referral letter from competent authority ₹1,000/- for Beneficiary or their &amp;quot;Family&amp;quot; in case of emergency treatment in a hospital operating in Andaman &amp;amp; Nicobar Islands (once in a year). 1. Subject to the production of referral remarks by Govt. Hospital/PHC in outer Islands ₹600/- per day for the period of their stay in Port Blair subject to a maximum of ₹15,000/- (Medical referral cases from outer Islands of Andaman &amp;amp; Nicobar Islands to Port Blair).

**Benefit Type:** In Kind

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: Andaman & Nicobar Islands Building And Other Construction Workers Welfare Board; Nodal Department: Labour, Employment & Training Department.
