# Sponsorship for Employment

**Short Title:** SFE

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Employment, Contractual Employment, Ex-serviceman, UPNL

The scheme &amp;quot;Sponsorship for Employment&amp;quot; is implemented by Uttarakhand Purv Sainik Kalyan Nigam Limited (UPNL) under the Sainik Welfare Department, Government of Uttarakhand. The scheme aims to provide employment opportunities to ex-servicemen and their legal dependents by sponsoring them for contractual posts. Under this scheme, eligible candidates are sponsored for various departmental positions based on demand and their qualifications. 

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Skills & Employment; Sector: Career information

**Where it applies:** Uttarakhand; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** Under this scheme, employment opportunities are provided to ex-servicemen and their legal dependents by sponsoring them for contractual posts through outsourcing. 

**Benefit Type:** In Kind

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: Uttarakhand Purv Sainik Kalyan Nigam Limited; Nodal Department: State Sainik Board.
