# Visit of Contract Farming Farmers to the Research Stations in Karnataka

**Short Title:** VCFRSK

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Agriculture, Farmer, Contract Farming, Research Stations Visit

The scheme “Visit of Contract Farming Farmers to the Research Stations in Karnataka” by the Agriculture-Farmers Welfare Department, Tamil Nadu, aims to enhance the agricultural practices of contract farming farmers by enabling visits to research stations in Karnataka. A batch of 50 farmers will be sponsored for a 3-day visit. The scheme focuses on increasing exposure to advanced techniques and research findings to improve farming outcomes.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Agriculture,Rural & Environment; Sector: Financial assistance

**Where it applies:** Tamil Nadu; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - **Financial Assistance:** ₹1,12,500/- per batch of 50 farmers for 3 days.- **Learning Program:** Exposure to the latest agricultural research and innovations.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Agriculture Department.

**References / Annexures:** - **Guidelines**: https://www.tn.gov.in/scheme/data_view/6858

**Additional Information:**
Briefdescription: The scheme “Visit of Contract Farming Farmers to the Research Stations in Karnataka” facilitates visits for contract farming farmers to research stations across Karnataka to gain agricultural knowledge. Financial support is provided for a 3-day visit.
