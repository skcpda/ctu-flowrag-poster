# INDIRAMMA Widow Pension (Urban)

**Short Title:** IWP-URBAN

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Widow, Woman, Urban, Below Poverty Line, BPL, Pension

Launched in 2006-07, the scheme &quot;INDIRAMMA Widow Pension (Urban)&quot; by the Department of Rural Development, Government of Andhra Pradesh, aims to provide financial assistance to widows irrespective of their age. To be eligible, they should be from a BPL family, be a resident of a district of Andhra Pradesh, and not be covered under any other Pension Scheme. The eligible individuals will be identified by their respective Ward Sabhas. The Bill Collector disburses the pension to the eligible individuals on the first date of every month.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Pension

**Where it applies:** Andhra Pradesh; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** **Pension Amount:** The scale of benefit under the pension will be notified by the Government of Andhra Pradesh. **Mode of Disbursement:** The Bill Collector disburses the pension to the eligible individuals in the presence of Ward members and office bearers of DWCUA. **Time of Disbursement:** The pension is disbursed on the first date of every month.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Panchayat Raj Rural Employment.
