# Transgender Utthan Kosh - College/Higher/Technical Educational Fees Refund for Transgender Students

**Short Title:** TUK-CHTERTS

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Transgender, Educational Fees, Reimbursement Of Fees, Higher Education, Technical Education, Student

The &amp;quot;Transgender Utthan Kosh - College/Higher/Technical Educational Fees Refund for Transgender Students&amp;quot; scheme was introduced by the Social Justice and Empowerment Department, Government of Rajasthan. It aims to promote higher and technical education among transgender individuals by providing financial assistance for their educational expenses. Under this scheme, transgender students receive a refund of their school, college, higher, or technical education fees.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Education & Learning; Sector: Scholarships and student finance

**Where it applies:** Rajasthan; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** ***Educational Fee Refund:*** - Full reimbursement of prescribed fees is provided for transgender students enrolled in schools, colleges, or technical institutes pursuing higher or technical education. ***Direct Bank Transfer:*** - The amount is credited directly to the beneficiary&#39;s bank account after verification.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Social Justice and Empowerment Department.
