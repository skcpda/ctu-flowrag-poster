# Scheme of Financial Assistance to Indigent Artists

**Short Title:** SOFATIA

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Indigent, Artist, Financial Assistance, Art

The &quot;Scheme of Financial Assistance to Indigent Artists&quot; was launched by the Art and Culture Department, Government of Tamil Nadu. The scheme aims to provide financial assistance of ₹3,000/- per month to artists who are in indigent circumstances and their dependents. The Indigent artists who have rendered services in the field of Art and Culture during their lifetime ar eligible for this scheme.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Sports & Culture; Sector: Culture

**Where it applies:** Tamil Nadu; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** Financial Assistance of ₹3,000/- per month.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Art and Culture Department.
