# Sakhi Niwas - Working Women's Hostel

**Short Title:** SNWWH

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Working Woman, Hostel, Accommodation Facilities

The scheme &amp;quot;Sakhi Niwas - Working Women&amp;#39;s Hostel&amp;quot; is implemented by the Department of Women Empowerment &amp;amp; Child Development, Government of Uttarakhand. The scheme aims to provide hostel facilities for women with higher education or professional training, as well as working women, to stay in urban areas. 

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Women and Child; Sector: Hostel Service

**Where it applies:** Uttarakhand; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - Under this scheme, hostels have been arranged for women to stay in urban areas by paying a nominal fee. - Facilities include: Accommodation, Mess, Library, Gym, TV Hall, Creche, and more. 

**Benefit Type:** In Kind

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Department Of Woman Empowerment & Child Development.
