# Mukhyamantri Nikah Yojana

**Short Title:** MMNY

**Scheme For:** Family

**Tags:** Mass Marriage, Group Marriage, Nikah, Muslim Families, Girl, Widow, Abandoned Woman, Financial Assistance

The scheme &amp;quot;Mukhyamantri Nikah Yojana&amp;quot; was started by the Social Justice and Empowerment of Persons with Disabilities Department, Government of Madhya Pradesh in the year 2012. The objective of this scheme is to provide financial assistance to needy girls, widows, and abandoned women from destitute or poor Muslim families in the state at the time of their marriage (In case of mass marriage).

**Who is this for:** Family; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Marriage

**Where it applies:** Madhya Pradesh; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** Under this scheme, the following assistance is provided: - Each eligible bride (including girls, widows, or abandoned women) participating in the mass marriage (Nikah) ceremony will receive a total of ₹49,000/- through an Account Payee Cheque. - The organizing body that conducts the mass marriage (Nikah) ceremony will receive ₹6,000/- per beneficiary. **Note:** A total of ₹55,000/- is provided per beneficiary under the scheme.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: Municipal Corporation Or Municipality Or City Council Or District Panchayat Of Madhya Pradesh; Nodal Department: Department of Social Justice and Empowerment of Persons with Disabilities.
