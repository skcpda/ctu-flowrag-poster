# Scheme to Provide Electric Scooter to Disabled Person

**Short Title:** SPESDP

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Disability Person, Electric Scooter Subsidy, Orthopedic Disability, Below Poverty Level, BPL

The &amp;quot;Scheme to Provide Electric Scooter to Disabled Person&amp;quot; is a welfare scheme by the Social Justice and Empowerment Department of Gujarat. The scheme aims to promote mobility and independence for orthopedically disabled individuals in Gujarat by offering a financial subsidy. The beneficiary can purchase an electric scooter through an agency approved by JEDA. After verification, the financial assistance amounting to ₹25,000 (or 50% of the scooter and kit cost, whichever is lower) will be directly deposited into the beneficiary&amp;#39;s bank account.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Financial assistance

**Where it applies:** Gujarat; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - **Financial Assistance:** Up to the limit of 50% of the basic cost of the electric scooter + 50% of the disability kit or ₹25,000/-.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Social Justice and Empowerment Department.

**References / Annexures:** - **Guidelines (Scheme No. 169)**: https://mariyojana.gujarat.gov.in/MoreDetails.aspx - **Guidelines 2**: https://sje.gujarat.gov.in/dsd/Schemes/14689

**Additional Information:**
Briefdescription: The "Scheme to Provide Electric Scooter to Disabled Person" provides financial assistance of up to ₹25,000 for the purchase of electric scooters to eligible persons with orthopedic disabilities.
