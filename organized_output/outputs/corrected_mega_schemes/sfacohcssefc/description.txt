# Scheme of Financial Assistance for Cultural Organization for Holding Conference, Seminar, Symposium, Exhibition, Festival, Competition etc.

**Short Title:** SFACOHCSSEFC

**Scheme For:** Infra

**Tags:** Financial Assistance, Organization, Culture, Institution

The &quot;Scheme of Financial Assistance for Cultural Organization for Holding Conference, Seminar, Symposium, Exhibition, Festival, Competition etc.&quot; was launched by the Arts and Culture Department, Puducherry. The scheme aims to provide a annual grant to the institution/organization organizing a seminar, symposium. The applications are accepted offline.

**Who is this for:** Business Entity, University / Institution; Category: Sports & Culture; Sector: Culture

**Where it applies:** Puducherry; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - Financial Assistance of 10,000/- annually.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Arts and Culture Department, Puducherry.

**References / Annexures:** - **Citizens Charter**: https://art.py.gov.in//sites/default/files/ecit1.pdf - **Official Website**: https://art.py.gov.in/ - **Application Form**: https://art.py.gov.in/sites/default/files/application-11.pdf
