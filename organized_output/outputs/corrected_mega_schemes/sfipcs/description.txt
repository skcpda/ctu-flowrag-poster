# PMMSY: Ice Plant/Cold Store of Minimum 30-Ton Capacity - Haryana

**Short Title:** FIPCS

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Entrepreneurs, Fish Farmers, Cold Chain Infrastructure, Fish Preservation

The Pradhan Mantri Matsya Sampada Yojana: Ice Plant/Cold Store of minimum 30-ton capacity, introduced by the Ministry Of Fisheries, Animal Husbandry &amp; Dairying, Government of India, aims to provide the subsidy to support the establishment of ice plants or cold storage facilities with a minimum capacity of 30 tons to strengthen fish preservation and cold chain infrastructure in Haryana. This centrally sponsored scheme is jointly funded 50% by the Central Government and 50% by the State Government. Beneficiaries must submit an undertaking in their DPR/SCP to keep the unit operational, bear all post-construction costs, and ensure ice is supplied to fishers and fish farmers at affordable prices. 

**Who is this for:** Business Entity, Individual; Category: Agriculture,Rural & Environment; Sector: Fishing and hunting

**Where it applies:** Haryana; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - The project cost is ₹120,00,000/- > **Subsidy Distribution :** 1. A 40% subsidy is available for the General Category. 1. A 60% subsidy is offered for SC (Scheduled Caste) and Women beneficiaries.

**Benefit Type:** Composite

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Fisheries Department.
