# C H Muhammedkoya Scholarship

**Short Title:** CHMS

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Latin, Christian, Under Graduate, Post Graduate, Hostel, Stipend

The scheme &quot;C H Muhammedkoya Scholarship&quot; by the Minority Affairs Department, Government of Kerala, aims to provide financial assistance to girl students from Muslim, Latin, and Converted Christian communities who are natives of Kerala. Through this scheme, scholarships are provided for undergraduate, postgraduate, and professional courses, along with a separate hostel stipend for eligible beneficiaries. The scheme is implemented by the Minority Affairs Department. 

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Education & Learning; Sector: Scholarships and student finance

**Where it applies:** Kerala; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** The scholarship provides monetary benefits to eligible students for their studies and hostel expenses. - **Undergraduate:** ₹4,000/- per annum (for 3,000 students).- **Post Graduates:** ₹5,000/- per annum (for 1,000 students).- **Professional Courses:** ₹6,000/- per annum (for 1,000 students).- **Hostel Stipend:** ₹12,000/- per annum (for 2,000 students). 

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Exclusions / Not eligible:** Those eligible for Hostel stipend will not be eligible for the scholarship (i.e., the course-related monetary benefit). 

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Directorate of Minority Welfare.
