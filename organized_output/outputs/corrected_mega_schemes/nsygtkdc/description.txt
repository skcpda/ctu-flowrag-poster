# New Swarnima Yojana for Women (GTKVN)

**Short Title:** NSYW(GTKVN)

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Business, Entrepreneurship, Loan, Self-Employment, Woman, BPL, Thakor Caste, Koli Caste

The &amp;quot;New Swarnima Yojana&amp;quot; is implemented by the Gujarat Thakor and Koli Vikas Nigam (GTKVN) under the Social Justice and Empowerment Department, Government of Gujarat. The scheme aims to promote economic upliftment of women from Thakor and Koli communities living below poverty line by supporting them in self-employment ventures. Under this scheme, 100% financial assistance up to ₹1,00,000/- is provided to eligible women for starting a business or trade of their choice. 

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Banking,Financial Services and Insurance; Sector: Loan

**Where it applies:** Gujarat; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - **Loan Amount:** Up to ₹1,00,000 for eligible business/trade activities.- **Interest Rate:** 5% per annum.- **Loan Coverage:** 100% of the approved project/business cost.- **Repayment Term:** 60 equal monthly installments (5 years), including interest. 

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: Gujarat Thakor And Koli Vikas Nigam; Nodal Department: Social Justice and Empowerment Department.

**References / Annexures:** - **Guidelines**: https://sje.gujarat.gov.in/gtkvn/Schemes/1448?lang=English - **Official Website**: https://sje.gujarat.gov.in/gtkvn/Home?lang=English
