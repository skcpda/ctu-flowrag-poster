# Distribution of Minikits At Free of Cost

**Short Title:** DOMAFOC

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Agriculture, Farmer, Minikits, Subsidy

The “Distribution of Minikits at Free of Cost” scheme by the Agriculture-Farmers Welfare Department, Tamil Nadu, provides subsidies for minikits to maize-growing farmers in specific districts including Coimbatore, Dindigul, Erode, Dharmapuri, Krishnagiri, Namakkal, Perambalur, Virudhunagar, Theni, Villupuram, Vellore, Salem, Pudukkottai, Madurai, Sivagangai, Trichy, Tirunelveli, and Thoothukudi.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Agriculture,Rural & Environment; Sector: Agricultural Inputs- seeds, fertilizer etc.

**Where it applies:** Tamil Nadu; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - **Quantum of Assistance:** Subsidy for Minikits. 

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Agriculture Department.

**References / Annexures:** - **Guidelines**: https://www.tn.gov.in/scheme/data_view/6855

**Additional Information:**
Briefdescription: The "Distribution of Minikits at Free of Cost" scheme provides subsidies for minikits to maize-growing farmers in some of the selected districts of Tamil Nadu.
