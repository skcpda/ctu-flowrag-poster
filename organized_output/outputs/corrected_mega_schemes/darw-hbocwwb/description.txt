# Death Assistance For Registered Worker (HBOCWWB)

**Short Title:** DARW-(HBOCWWB)

**Scheme For:** Family

**Tags:** Construction Worker, Death, Financial Assistance, Dependent Family Member

The scheme “Death Assistance for Registered Worker (HBOCWWB)” was launched by the Haryana Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board (HBOCWWB), Labour Department Haryana. Under the scheme, financial assistance of ₹2,00,000/- will be given to the nominee/legal heir/dependent family members of the registered worker on his natural death.

**Who is this for:** Family; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Crisis/Disaster/Accident

**Where it applies:** Haryana; State

**Benefits / Assistance:** 1. The financial assistance of ₹2,00,000/- will be given to the nominee/legal heir/dependent family members of the registered worker on his natural death.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: Haryana Building And Other Construction Workers Welfare Board; Nodal Department: Labour Department.
