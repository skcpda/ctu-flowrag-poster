# Scheme for the Award of Scholarship for Post Graduate Students of Various Faculties of Junagadh Agricultural University

**Short Title:** SAPGJAU

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Agricultural Scholarship, Fellowship, Higher Education, Student, Post Graduate, Ph D

The &amp;quot;Scheme for the Award of Scholarship for Post Graduate Students of Various Faculties of Junagadh Agricultural University&amp;quot; launched by the Agriculture, Farmers Welfare and Cooperation Department, Gujarat, aims to provide monthly financial assistance to eligible Under Graduate students at Junagadh Agricultural University. The objective is to attract the bright and talented students to seek admission in various faculties of P.G. level, it is necessary to provide financial assistance.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Education & Learning; Sector: Scholarships and student finance

**Where it applies:** Gujarat; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - **Master’s degree students:** ₹2,000/- per month for 2 years. - **Ph.D. degree students:** ₹3,000/- per month for 3 years.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Exclusions / Not eligible:** - An in-service candidate of the University or a deputee from any other institute/ organization shall not eligible for the scholarship.

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Agriculture, Farmers Welfare and Cooperation Department.

**References / Annexures:** - **Guidelines (Scheme No. 522)**: https://mariyojana.gujarat.gov.in/MoreDetails.aspx
