# Scheme for Giving Awards to Traditional Artists

**Short Title:** SFGATTA

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Artist, Award, Painting, Financial Assistance, Exhibition

&quot;Scheme for Giving Awards to Traditional Artists&quot; was launched by the Art and Culture Department, Government of Tamil Nadu. Through this scheme, awards are conferred to the participant artists whose works are adjudged outstanding among the entries at the State-level Traditional Art Exhibition conducted by the Kuzhu. The awarded works are put on display for public view in the exhibition. The applications are accepted offline in the prescribed form available at the Oviya Nunkalai Kuzhu.

**Who is this for:** Artists; Category: Skills & Employment; Sector: Culture

**Where it applies:** Tamil Nadu; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** Prize Money of ₹5,000/- per artist is given to 10 artists.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Art and Culture Department.

**References / Annexures:** - **Guidelines**: https://www.tn.gov.in/rti/proactive/inftour/handbook_artculture.pdf
