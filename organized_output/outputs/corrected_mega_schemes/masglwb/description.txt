# Maternity Assistance Scheme- Gujarat Labour Welfare Board

**Short Title:** MASGLWB

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Maternity, Woman, Financial Assistance, Organized Worker, Industrial Worker

The “Maternity Assistance Scheme” was launched by the Gujarat Labour Welfare Board, Labour, Skill Development &amp;amp; Employment Department, Government of Gujarat. Under the scheme, financial assistance is provided to female workers and the wives of workers to cover expenses incurred for health check-ups, medicine, nutritious diet, etc., during the maternity period.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Pregnancy Care

**Where it applies:** Gujarat; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** 1. Under this scheme, financial assistance of ₹10,000/- is provided to female workers and the wives of workers to cover expenses incurred during the maternity period.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: Gujarat Labour Welfare Board; Nodal Department: Labour and Employment Department.
