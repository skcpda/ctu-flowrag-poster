# Distance Education Scholarship–Scholarship For Disabled Students Pursuing Degree, PG Courses

**Short Title:** DES-SDSPDPGC

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Distance Education, Scholarship, Student, Person With Disability, Degree, PG Courses, Open University

The scheme “Distance Education Scholarship–Scholarship for Disabled Students Pursuing Degree, PG Courses” was launched by the Department of Social Justice, Government of Kerala. Under the scheme, differently abled students pursuing Degrees, and PG courses through Open Universities, distance education, or Private registration (Universities inside Kerala) are eligible to apply for scholarships. The scholarship is being provided for meeting the expenses of Registration fee, Course fee, Tuition fee, Exam fee, Purchase of books, study materials, etc. Further, financial assistance will be granted only for the duration of the course concerned.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Education & Learning; Sector: Scholarships and student finance

**Where it applies:** Kerala; State

**Benefits / Assistance:** 1. Under the scheme, the scholarship is given to eligible students for meeting the expenses of Registration fees, Course fees, Tuition fees, Exam fees, Purchase of books, study materials, etc. 1. The amount of assistance is provided up to a maximum of ₹10,000/-. **Note:** Financial assistance will be granted only for the duration of the course concerned.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Exclusions / Not eligible:** The students who are receiving financial assistance under any other scheme of Central and State Governments are not eligible for financial assistance under this scheme.

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: District Social Justice Office, Kerala; Nodal Department: Social Justice Department.
