# Bihar State Journalist Insurance Scheme

**Short Title:** BSJIS

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Journalist, Mediclaim, Accident, Media

The &amp;quot;Bihar State Journalist Insurance Scheme, 2014&amp;quot; was launched by the Information and Public Relations Department, Government of Bihar. The scheme aims to provide financial benefits of ₹5,00,000/- &amp;amp; facilities under a group mediclaim &amp;amp; personal accident insurance scheme to working media representatives. Under this scheme, spouse and two dependent children of the media representative will be included in this Insurance Scheme. &lt;br&gt; &gt; **Insurance Period:** This insurance is valid for one year with provisions for annual renewal. &lt;br&gt;

**Who is this for:** Journalist; Category: Skills & Employment; Sector: Insurance

**Where it applies:** Bihar; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - Personal accident insurance coverage up to ₹5,00,000/- - Medical expense coverage up to ₹5,00,000/- for the insured and their dependents. **NOTE:** Media representatives contribute 20% of the premium, while the remaining 80% is covered by the government. 

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Exclusions / Not eligible:** The maternity related cases are not covered under mediclaim policy of this insurance scheme.

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Information and Public Relations Department Department.

**References / Annexures:** - **Guidelines**: https://state.bihar.gov.in/cache/10/Old%20Office%20Document/Old%20Act%20and%20Policy/Journalist%20Insurance%20Scheme%202014.pdf
