# Mukhyamantri Anusuchit Jaati Vishesh Pariyojana Vitt Poshan Yojana

**Short Title:** MAJVPVPY

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Scheduled Caste, Finance, Assisted Enterprises, Development

The Mukhyamantri Anusuchit Jaati Vishesh Pariyojana Vitt Poshan Yojana under the Tribal Welfare and Scheduled Caste (SC) Department, Madhya Pradesh, aims to provide financial assistance for special project proposals received in the interest of the Scheduled Caste (SC) category. The scheme&#39;s primary objective is to provide financial support to projects that benefit the SC community in the state. It focuses on enhancing welfare initiatives, improving living standards, and providing opportunities for development in various sectors for the SC category.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Financial assistance

**Where it applies:** Madhya Pradesh; State/ UT

**DBT Scheme:** Yes (Direct Benefit Transfer)

**Benefits / Assistance:** The scheme offers a 100% grant based on government approval for project proposals from line departments, recommended by the evaluation committee.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Tribal Welfare and Scheduled Caste (SC) Department.
