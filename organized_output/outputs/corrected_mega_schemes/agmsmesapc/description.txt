# Aatmanirbhar Gujarat Scheme for assistance to MSMEs: Assistance for Power Connection Charges

**Short Title:** AGMSMESAPC

**Launch Date:** 2022-10-05

**Scheme For:** Infra

**Tags:** Business, Entrepreneurship, Subsidy, Power Connection

The &amp;quot;Aatmanirbhar Gujarat Scheme for MSMEs&amp;quot; is an umbrella scheme launched by the Industries and Mines Department, Gujarat. It aims to empower the state&amp;#39;s dynamic MSME sector, which significantly contributes to employment, GDP, and exports. Effective from October 5, 2022, to October 4, 2027, this initiative supports growth, investments, and the vision of &amp;quot;Aatmanirbhar Gujarat&amp;quot; and &amp;quot;Aatmanirbhar Bharat.&amp;quot; The component &amp;quot;Assistance for Power Connection Charges&amp;quot; financial assistance to eligible Micro, Small, and Medium Enterprises (MSMEs) to offset costs incurred for power connection charges. It covers charges for new connections, additional load for expansion, or shifting connections or service lines, subject to conditions.

**Who is this for:** Business Entity, Industries; Category: Business & Entrepreneurship; Sector: Setting up / start-up / entrepreneurship

**Where it applies:** Gujarat; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - **Financial Assistance:** Assistance of 35% on charges paid to the distribution licensee for LT/HT service lines maximum assistance of up to ₹5,00,000/- during the operative period of the scheme.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Industries and Mines Department.
