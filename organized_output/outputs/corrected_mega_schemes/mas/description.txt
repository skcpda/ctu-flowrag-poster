# Marketing Assistance Scheme 

**Short Title:** MAS

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Financial Assistance, Marketing, Artisans, Leather

The &quot;Marketing Assistance Scheme&quot; was implemented by Karnataka Leather Industries Development Corporation Ltd, Govt of Karnataka. The scheme aims to empower SC leather artisans by enabling them to market their products through Lidkar Leather Emporia under the Lidkar brand. It helps artisans avoid exploitation by private dealers and provides regular procurement of their products based on demand.  **Objective:** Regular procurement of products (once every 1-2 months) based on showroom demand. Branding and marketing through Lidkar Leather Emporia.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Skills & Employment; Sector: Setting up / start-up / entrepreneurship

**Where it applies:** Karnataka; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - **Financial assistance of ₹50,000/- to ₹1,00,000/- worth of procurement from each artisan.**

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: Karnataka Leather Industries Development Corporation Ltd; Nodal Department: Social Welfare Department.

**References / Annexures:** - **Guidelines**: https://schemes.lidkar.com/schemes/self-employment-programs/
