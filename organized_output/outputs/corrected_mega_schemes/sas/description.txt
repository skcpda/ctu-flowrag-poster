# Study Abroad Scholarships (Scheduled Tribe)

**Short Title:** SAS

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Abroad, Scholarships, Scheduled Tribe, Higher Education

Launched in 2003, the scheme &quot;Study Abroad Scholarships&quot; by the Tribal Affairs Department, Government of Madhya Pradesh covers tuition fees of 40,000 US Dollars per year, along with a living allowance of 9,000 US Dollars and a contingency allowance of 1,000 US Dollars. To be eligible, students must have passed the graduation final examination with at least 55% marks for postgraduate studies, and the postgraduate examination with at least 50% marks for Ph.D. The scheme provides scholarships to Scheduled Tribe students for pursuing higher education abroad.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Education & Learning; Sector: International education

**Where it applies:** Madhya Pradesh; State/ UT

**DBT Scheme:** Yes (Direct Benefit Transfer)

**Benefits / Assistance:** 1. The scheme covers tuition fees of 40,000 US Dollars per year, along with a living allowance of 9,000 US Dollars and a contingency allowance of 1,000 US Dollars. 1. Additional expenses such as visa fees, insurance, poll tax, airfare, and rail/bus fare are also provided. **NOTE:** The fees are paid into the University account and subsistence/contingency allowance and other allowances are paid directly into the applicant&#39;s bank account.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Tribal Welfare and Scheduled Caste (SC) Department.
