# Mukhyamantri Samajik Sahayata Prakalpa - Vulnerable Women 

**Short Title:** MSSP-VM

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Divorced, Women, Unmarried, Financial Assistance, Pension

The scheme “Mukhyamantri Samajik Sahayata Prakalpa - Vulnerable Women&quot; was launched by the Directorate of Social Welfare and Social Education, Government of Tripura. The scheme provides a monthly pension of ₹2,000/- to the vulnerable women, such as Widows, Deserted Women, Unmarried Women, and also to the women who are in need of special care, support or protection because of their age, disability or risk of abuse or neglect.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Pension

**Where it applies:** Tripura; State/ UT

**DBT Scheme:** Yes (Direct Benefit Transfer)

**Benefits / Assistance:** - **Pension Amount:** ₹2000/- per month.- **Mode of Disbursal:** The sanctioned grant will be transferred directly to the individual bank account of the applicant.- The list of beneficiaries will be made available on the [official website](https://socialwelfare.tripura.gov.in/) of the Social Welfare &amp; Social Education Department, Govt. of Tripura.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Exclusions / Not eligible:** - Income taxpayers are not eligible. - Government employees are not eligible. - The individuals whose family members are already getting benefits under any of the Category/Sub-Category under ‘Mukhyamantri Samajik Sahayata Prakalpa&#39; (except infirm &amp; PwDs) are not eligible. - Pensioners who are getting benefits under the “National Social Assistance Program (NSAP)” or the 32 State Social Pension Schemes run by the Social Welfare &amp; Social Education Department, Govt. of Tripura are not eligible. 

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Directorate Of Social Welfare & Social Education.
