# 100% State Government Divyangjan Term Loan Yojana

**Short Title:** SGDTLY

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Loan, Disability, Social Welfare, Differently Abled

The scheme &amp;quot;100% State Government Divyangjan Term Loan Yojana&amp;quot; was launched by the Social Justice and Empowerment Department, Government of Gujarat, to provide financial support to native residents of Gujarat who have a disability of 40% or more. The eligible individuals can avail of a term loan ranging from ₹50,000/- to ₹1,00,000/- at a concessional interest rate of 6% per annum for a repayment period of five years. This initiative empowers PwDs to become self-reliant by supporting their livelihood and business ventures.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Loan

**Where it applies:** Gujarat; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** Loans ranging from ₹50,000/- to ₹1,00,000/- are disbursed at an annual rate of 6% for a period of 5 years.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Social Justice and Empowerment Department.
