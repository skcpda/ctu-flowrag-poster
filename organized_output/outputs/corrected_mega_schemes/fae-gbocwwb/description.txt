# Financial Assistance for Education (GBOCWWB)

**Short Title:** FAE-GBOCWWB

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Student, Education, Financial Assistance, Labour, Worker

&quot;Financial Assistance for Education (GBOCWWB)&quot; is a Welfare Scheme by the Goa Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board of the Department of Labour and Employment, Government of Goa. Through this scheme, the worker registered with the Board is provided with financial assistance, as determined by the Board, for the education of their children. The applications are accepted offline.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Education & Learning; Sector: Scholarships and student finance

**Where it applies:** Goa; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** Financial Assistance, as determined by the Board, for the education of the children of the Worker.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: Goa Building And Other Construction Workers Welfare Board; Nodal Department: Labour and Employment Department.
