# Passenger Auto Rickshaw Loan Scheme (DAADC)

**Short Title:** PARLS

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Passenger Auto Rickshaw Loan, Antyodaya, Scheduled Caste, Employment

The &amp;quot;Passenger Auto Rickshaw Loan Scheme&amp;quot; introduced by the Social Justice and Empowerment Department, Gujarat, aims to provide financial assistance in the form of loans to Scheduled Caste individuals from Antyodaya families to purchase passenger auto rickshaws, enabling them to generate sustainable income through transport services. The scheme is being implemented by the Dr. Ambedkar Antyodaya Development Corporation.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Banking,Financial Services and Insurance; Sector: Loan

**Where it applies:** Gujarat; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - Financial assistance in the form of a loan.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: Dr. Ambedkar Antyodaya Development Corporation; Nodal Department: Social Justice and Empowerment Department.

**References / Annexures:** - **Guidelines (Scheme No. 417)**: https://mariyojana.gujarat.gov.in/MoreDetails.aspx

**Additional Information:**
Briefdescription: The "Passenger Auto Rickshaw Loan Scheme" introduced by the Social Justice and Empowerment Department offers financial assistance in the form of loans to Scheduled Caste individuals from Antyodaya families to purchase passenger auto rickshaws and enhance their livelihood opportunities.
