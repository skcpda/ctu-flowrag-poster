# Financial Assistance to Troupes Going to Mumbai/Poona and Other Parts of the Country & Vice Versa

**Short Title:** FATGMPOPCVV

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Culture, Artist, Stage, Performance, Tour, Financial Assistance

The scheme &quot;Financial Assistance to Troupes Going to Mumbai/Poona and Other Parts of the Country &amp; Vice Versa&quot; by the Tiatr Academy of Goa (TAG), Goa, aims to compensate Goan dramatic troupes towards their transportation costs, recognizing that staging Tiatrs in Mumbai, Poona, and other parts of the country is not always financially viable. The scheme provides financial support to cover transport expenses for Goan troupes staging a minimum of five shows of the same Tiatr in Mumbai/Poona, or for Mumbai/Poona troupes staging five shows in Goa. Benefits can also be availed for only one show staged in other locations such as Kolkata, Delhi, Gujarat, Nagpur, Bangalore, or Udaipur. Applications for this scheme are accepted one month in advance of the performance date, on a &#39;First Come First Serve&#39; basis, and are subject to the availability of funds. 

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Sports & Culture; Sector: Culture

**Where it applies:** Goa; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - The scheme provides financial support to compensate for transportation expenses incurred while staging Tiatrs, thereby promoting Goan Tiatr. - The extent of the financial support shall be fixed by the Tiatr Academy of Goa.  > ***Conditions*** _*The Tiatr Academy of Goa (TAG) may appoint a representative to witness the performance, and payments will be released based on their report._ _*A compulsory announcement stating ‘This Tiatr is organized in collaboration with Tiatr Academy of Goa (TAG)’ must be made both before the commencement of the Tiatr and again during the interval._ _*No adverse remarks must be made, either in written or verbal form, against any Governments, Individuals, Institutions, or on the basis of caste, creed, or religion within the scripts, song lyrics, performances, or speeches. If such remarks are proven, the Tiatr Academy of Goa (TAG) reserves the right to disqualify the performance and either withhold payment or recall any payment already made to the applicant._ _*For performances at Mandd Sobhann, Mangalore, the payment will only be made after a certificate of performance is issued by the Mandd Sobhann._ 

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Art, Culture and Museums Department.

**References / Annexures:** - **Guidelines**: https://www.goa.gov.in/wp-content/uploads/2021/06/Citizen-Charter-TAG.pdf

**Additional Information:**
Briefdescription: The scheme aims to compensate Goan dramatic troupes for transportation expenses when staging Tiatrs in Mumbai, Poona, and other parts of India, and vice versa. It offers financial assistance to promote Goan Tiatr, benefiting directors and writers of Goan and Mumbai/Poona troupes.
