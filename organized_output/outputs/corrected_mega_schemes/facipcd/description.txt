# Finance Assistant to PACS for Computerization and Integration of PACS through Core Banking System with District Cooperative Banks

**Short Title:** FACIPCD

**Scheme For:** Infra

**Tags:** Computerization, Management Information System, Digital Infrastructure

The scheme &amp;quot;Finance Assistant to PACS for Computerization and Integration of PACS through Core Banking System with District Cooperative Banks&amp;quot; introduced by the Agriculture, Farmers Welfare and Cooperation Department, Gujarat, aims to bring transparency, accuracy, and consistency in PACS accounts using a common accounting and management information system. By enabling PACS to be digitally connected with District and State Cooperative Banks through CBS, this scheme also boosts rural financial inclusion and ensures seamless banking facilities in remote areas.

**Who is this for:** Registered Societies; Category: Agriculture,Rural & Environment; Sector: Financial assistance

**Where it applies:** Gujarat; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:**  **Standard of Assistance****Remarks****Minimum Time Limit (in Year) for Reclaiming Benefits)**the cost is estimated at ₹ 3,91,369/- per PACS, out of which the Central Government&amp;amp;amp;#39;s contribution is 60% at ₹1,85,441/- and State Government&amp;amp;amp;#39;s contribution as per 40% per PACS ₹1,23,627/- will be incurred.The agency empaneled by NABARD on behalf of the Government of India for the performance of software training while the procurement of hardware is to be done by the state government.There is no provision regarding re-benefit.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Agriculture, Farmers Welfare and Cooperation Department.

**Additional Information:**
Briefdescription: The scheme provides financial assistance to Primary Agricultural Credit Societies (PACS) for computerization and integration with District Cooperative Banks (DCCBs) through a Core Banking System (CBS), enhancing rural banking infrastructure and digital access.
