# Rajiv Gandhi Family Insurance Scheme

**Short Title:** RGFIS

**Launch Date:** 2006-06-01

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Family, Insurance, Accidental Death, Permanent Disability, Financial Assistance

The scheme “Rajiv Gandhi Family Insurance Scheme” was launched by the Department of Social Justice and Empowerment, Government of Haryana on 1st April 2006. Under this scheme, compensation is given in case of accidental death/permanent disability in the age group of 18-60 years, who is a resident of Haryana and whose name appears in the electoral roll or holds a ration card issued by the competent authority and Income taxpayers and government employees are not. The scheme has been amended and implemented on 30th August 2011 and accordingly, the compensation amount will not be given to the person having an income of more than ₹2.50 Lakh per annum.

**Who is this for:** Individual, Family; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Financial assistance

**Where it applies:** Haryana; State

**Benefits / Assistance:** The financial assistance/compensation will be given to eligible beneficiaries as under: Accidental Death/Permanent Disability₹1.00 LakhLoss of two limbs, two eyes, one limb, and one eye₹50,000/-Loss of one eye or one limb₹25,000/-

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: Department Of Social Justice And Empowerment, Government Of Haryana; Nodal Department: Social Justice and Empowerment Department.
