# Scheme for assistance for Start Ups/ Innovation: Assistance to Enroll and Participate for Acceleration Program

**Short Title:** SFASIEPAP

**Launch Date:** 2020-08-07

**Scheme For:** Infra

**Tags:** Start Up, Innovation, Entrepreneurship, Acceleration Program

The &amp;amp;quot;Scheme for Assistance for Start Ups/ Innovation&amp;amp;quot; is an umbrella scheme launched by the Industries and Mines Department, Gujarat under the Gujarat Industrial Policy. Effective from August 7th, 2020 to August 6th, 2025, it aims to drive economic growth by generating jobs, fostering competition, and introducing next-gen solutions. The component &amp;amp;quot;Assistance to Enroll and Participate for Acceleration Program&amp;amp;quot;, provides financial assistance to startups to enroll and participate in nationally or internationally recognized acceleration programs. This initiative is designed to support innovative projects across all sectors, enabling startups to access advanced resources, mentorship, and opportunities for growth.

**Who is this for:** Business Entity, Industries; Category: Business & Entrepreneurship; Sector: Exhibitions/Trade fairs/Buyer-seller meet, market development

**Where it applies:** Gujarat; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - **Financial Support:** Startups can receive financial assistance of up to ₹3,00,000/- to participate in acceleration programs.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Exclusions / Not eligible:** - Entities formed by splitting or restructuring an existing business are not considered startups under this scheme. - Startups availing benefits under other startup schemes of the Government of Gujarat are ineligible.

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Industries and Mines Department.
