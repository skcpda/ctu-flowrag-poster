# Chief Ministergi Artistessingi Tengbang - Assistance To The Cultural Group For Training With New Production

**Short Title:** CMST-ACGTNP

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Artist, Training, Workshop

Manipur is widely known for its varied traditions and culture belonging to different communities that have been passed on from generation to generation through practice and oral traditions. The scheme &amp;quot;Chief Ministergi Artistessingi Tengbang - Assistance to the Cultural Group for Training with New Production&amp;quot; by the Department of Art and Culture, Govt. of Manipur aims at providing financial assistance to registered cultural groups that have been relentlessly working on providing training to the younger generation with new products to enrich the traditions, art forms, and culture. &lt;br&gt;

**Who is this for:** Artists, Individual; Category: Sports & Culture; Sector: Culture

**Where it applies:** Manipur; State
