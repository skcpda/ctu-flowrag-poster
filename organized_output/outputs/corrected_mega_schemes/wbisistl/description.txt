#  The West Bengal Incentive Scheme: Interest Subsidy on Term Loan

**Short Title:** WBISISTL

**Launch Date:** 2021-02-22

**Scheme For:** Infra

**Tags:** Tourism, Incentives, Interest Subsidy, Tourism Units, Entrepreneurship, Subsidy, Reimbursement, Term Loan, MSME

“Interest Subsidy on Term Loan” is a sub scheme under “The West Bengal Incentive Scheme”. It was launched on 22nd February, 2021, by the Department of Tourism, Government of West Bengal, and shall remain valid upto 31st December, 2025. &gt; ***Objective of the &amp;quot;West Bengal Incentive Scheme” Scheme:*** The object of the Scheme is to provide financial assistance and help enthusiastic intending or prospective entrepreneurs to start their own business as a self-employed youth who will become an employer in upcoming days and generate more employment. &gt; ***Objectives of the &amp;quot;Interest Subsidy on Term Loan&amp;quot; Scheme:*** Through this incentive, an approved project of an eligible unit shall receive an Interest Subsidy on annual interest liability on the Term Loan borrowed from a Commercial Bank/Financial Institution.

**Who is this for:** Business Entity, Individual; Category: Business & Entrepreneurship; Sector: Setting up / start-up / entrepreneurship

**Where it applies:** West Bengal; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** **Group-&#39;A’ and ‘B&#39; areas:** 50%, subject to a limit of ₹ 25,00,000/- per year, for 5 years. **Group-&#39;C&#39; and &#39;D&#39; areas:** 60%, subject to a limit of ₹ 25,00,000/- per year, for 7 years. **Special Areas:** 75% , subject to a limit of ₹ 30,00,000/- per year, for 7 years. **Note:** The interest subsidy will be payable annually subject to submission of statement or certificate by the lending bank/financial institution to prove that the unit has paid the due interest to the institution on the due dates. The subsidy shall, however, not be paid in respect of term loans availed for second-hand plant and machinery or hire-purchase equipment. 

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Exclusions / Not eligible:** - The West Bengal Incentive Scheme shall stand non-applicable in respect of those tourism units that have been registered for and have been sanctioned incentives under any previous scheme. - Any unit which may have applied for registration with the Directorate of Tourism under WBIS (West Bengal Incentive Scheme ) 2015, before the date of commencement of this Scheme and whose applications may have not been disposed off prior to the coming into effect of this Scheme or deemed to have been disposed of shall be entitled to all benefits under WBIS 2015 only.

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Tourism Department.
