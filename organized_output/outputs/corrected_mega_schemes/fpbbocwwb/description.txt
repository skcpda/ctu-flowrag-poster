# Family Pension (BBOCWWB)

**Short Title:** FPBBOCWWB

**Scheme For:** Family

**Tags:** Construction, Building, Pension, Family, Worker

The scheme &quot;Family Pension&quot; by the Bihar Building &amp; Other Construction Workers Welfare Board (BBOCWWB), Labour Resources Department, Government of Bihar, provides pension to the dependent of the deceased pensioner of the Board.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Pension

**Where it applies:** Bihar; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** 50% of the pension amount or ₹100/-, whichever is higher.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Building Construction Department.
