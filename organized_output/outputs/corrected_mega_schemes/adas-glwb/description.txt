# Accidental Death Assistance Scheme- Gujarat Labour Welfare Board

**Short Title:** ADAS-GLWB

**Scheme For:** Family

**Tags:** Accidental Death, Industrial Worker, Organized Worker, Labour, Financial Assistance

The “Accidental Death Assistance Scheme” is implemented by the Gujarat Labour Welfare Board, Labour, Skill Development &amp;amp; Employment Department, Government of Gujarat. Under the scheme, financial assistance is provided to the family members of workers (Shramyogis) in the event of their death due to an accident during their course of employment. This assistance aims to help the worker&amp;#39;s family cope with the sudden calamity. However, post-sickness and natural death of workers will not be admissible under the scheme.

**Who is this for:** Family; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Crisis/Disaster/Accident

**Where it applies:** Gujarat; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - Under the scheme, families of workers (Shramyogis) will receive financial assistance of ₹2,00,000/- in the event of the worker&amp;#39;s death due to an accident during their course of employment.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Exclusions / Not eligible:** 1. Post-sickness and natural death of worker will not be admissible.

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: Gujarat Labour Welfare Board; Nodal Department: Labour and Employment Department.
