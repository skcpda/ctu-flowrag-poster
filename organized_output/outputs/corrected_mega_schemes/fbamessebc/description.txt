# Food Bill Assistant for Medical & Engineering Students of SEBC

**Short Title:** FBAMESSEBC

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Financial Assistance, Student, SEBC, Medical, Engineering

The &quot;Food Bill Assistant for Medical &amp; Engineering Students of SEBC&quot; scheme was launched by the Social Justice and Empowerment Department, Government of Gujarat. The scheme aims to provide financial relief for food expenses to SEBC category students pursuing higher education in the medical and engineering fields. Under this scheme, financial assistance per month for a maximum of 10 months is provided to eligible students. 

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Education & Learning; Sector: Scholarships and student finance

**Where it applies:** Gujarat; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - **Financial Assistance:** - ₹300/- per month (₹3,000/- annually) towards mess charges for most eligible SEBC students. - ₹700/- per month (₹7,000/- annually) towards mess charges for selected students (limited number of beneficiaries). - ₹1,500/- per month (₹15,000/- annually) for SEBC students studying in self-financed medical/engineering colleges and residing in hostels (with a fixed ceiling on the number of beneficiaries). - **Duration:** Maximum of 10 months in an academic year. 

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Social Justice and Empowerment Department.

**References / Annexures:** - **Guidelines (S. No. 183)**: https://mariyojana.gujarat.gov.in/Schemeatoz.aspx

**Additional Information:**
Briefdescription: The Government of Gujarat provides financial assistance to students belonging to Socially and Educationally Backward Classes (SEBC) who are studying in self-financed medical and engineering colleges/universities.
