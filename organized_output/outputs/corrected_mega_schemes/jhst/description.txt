# Junior Hostel (Scheduled Tribes)

**Short Title:** JHST

**Launch Date:** 1966-01-01

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Education, Student, Scheduled Tribe, Hostel

Launched on 1st January 1966, the scheme &quot;Junior Hostel&quot; by the Tribal Welfare and Scheduled Caste (SC) Department, Government of Madhya Pradesh provides free accommodation and mess facilities to students studying in classes 6th to 8th.    

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Education & Learning; Sector: Scholarships and student finance

**Where it applies:** Madhya Pradesh; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** Free Accommodation and Mess Facilities.

**Benefit Type:** In Kind

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Tribal Welfare and Scheduled Caste (SC) Department.
