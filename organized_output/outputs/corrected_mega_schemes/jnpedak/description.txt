# Jan Nayak Pustakalaya evam Digital Adhyan Kendra 

**Short Title:** JNPEDAK

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Digital, Pustakalaya, Students, Learning

The &quot;Jan Nayak Pustakalaya evam Digital Adhyan Kendra&quot; scheme, under the BC &amp; EBC Welfare Department, Government of Bihar, aims to develop all Jannayak Karpoori Thakur Hostels, other backward class welfare hostels, and OBC girls’ hostels across the state into “Jan Nayak Pustakalaya evam Digital Adhyan Kendra.” **Objectives** (a) To ensure the availability of competitive and high-quality books and study material. (b) To ensure quality education and guidance through digital learning. (c) To organize inspirational guidance sessions through experts and previously successful candidates in competitive examinations. Through this initiative, students—especially those in remote areas—will have access to high-quality study material and expert guidance in a modern educational environment.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Education & Learning

**Where it applies:** Bihar; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** The scheme provides students with access to quality study materials, digital learning, and expert guidance to enhance their academic and competitive exam preparation.

**Benefit Type:** In Kind

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: BC & EBC Welfare Department.
