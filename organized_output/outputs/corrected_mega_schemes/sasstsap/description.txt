# Arunachal Pradesh State Stipend Scheme

**Short Title:** APSSS

**Launch Date:** 2012-04-01

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Financial Assistance, Scheduled Tribe, Higher Education, Technical Education, Students, Courses

The “Arunachal Pradesh State Stipend Scheme” was launched by the Department of Education, Government of Arunachal Pradesh. Under this scheme, monthly stipends and book grants are provided to ST students to help them complete recognized courses without financial hindrance. > **Objective** - The object of the scheme is to provide financial assistance to Scheduled Tribe students of Arunachal Pradesh so as to enable them to pursue various courses of higher studies approved by the Government of Arunachal Pradesh. > **Approved Courses List** - BA / B.Com / B.P.Ed - B.Sc. - MA / M.Com / LL.B. / M.P.Ed / LL.M / MPMC / M.Ed - M.Sc. - M.Phil - Ph.D - B.Sc. (Agriculture / Horticulture / Forestry / Fishery / BE / B.VSc) - MBBS - BDS / BHMS / BAMS - Diploma (Engineering) / X-Ray Technician / Ophthalmic Assistant - B.Pharma / B.Sc. (Nursing) - D.Pharma - Laboratory Technician / ECG Technician - BCA - MCA - BBA - MBA - PGDM (Communication) - IT &amp; HM (Diploma) - BFA - MFA - B.Ed - CA (Chartered Accountancy) - M.Tech  

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Education & Learning; Sector: Scholarships and student finance

**Where it applies:** Arunachal Pradesh; State

**Benefits / Assistance:** **Tangible Benefits:** - Monthly Stipend for the duration of the course (except vacation periods). - Initial Book Grant for the first year. - Subsequent Book Grant for the following years. **Mode of Disbursement:** - For students within Arunachal Pradesh: Disbursed by the Head of Institution. - For students outside Arunachal Pradesh: Disbursed via Demand Draft by the Sanctioning Authority to Heads of Institutions. **Frequency of Disbursement:** - Monthly, except for vacation periods. - Begins from the month of admission (if before the 20th of the month), otherwise from the following month. **Conditions for Benefit:** - Continued only on satisfactory academic progress and good conduct. - Stipend will be withheld or canceled for misconduct, poor attendance (&lt;75%), or unsatisfactory progress. - Not valid for dual/subsidiary courses (e.g., LLB with MA), correspondence, or distance education.

**Benefit Type:** Composite

**Exclusions / Not eligible:** - The applicant must not be a recipient of another scholarship/stipend. If receiving another, the student must choose one.

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: Directorate Of Higher And Technical Education; Nodal Department: Education Department.

**References / Annexures:** - **Guidelines**: https://scholarships.gov.in/public/schemeGuidelines/ArunachalPradeshStipendSchemeGuideline.pdf

Briefdescription: Under this scheme, provide financial support to scheduled tribe (ST) students pursuing higher and technical education through stipends and book grants.
