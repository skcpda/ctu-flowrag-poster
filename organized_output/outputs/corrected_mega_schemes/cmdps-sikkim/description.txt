# Chief Minister's State Disability Pension Scheme - Sikkim

**Short Title:** CMDPS-SIKKIM

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Disability, Pension, Below Poverty Line, Direct Benefit Transfer

The scheme “Chief Minister&amp;#39;s State Disability Pension Scheme (CMDPS)” was started by the Women &amp;amp; Child Welfare Department, Govt. of Sikkim to provide financial assistance to the differently abled person who has a disability of 40% and above. This scheme is 100% State Innovative Scheme and the beneficiary will receive a monthly pension of ₹ 1500/-.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Pension

**Where it applies:** Sikkim; State

**DBT Scheme:** Yes (Direct Benefit Transfer)
