# Old Age Pension Scheme (P.B.O.C.W.W.B)

**Short Title:** OAPSPBOCWWB

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Labour, Building Worker, Construction Worker, Old Age Pension, Senior Citizen

The &amp;quot;Old Age Pension Scheme&amp;#39;&amp;#39; by the BOCW Welfare Board, Department of Labour, Punjab, is a welfare scheme for the registered workers of the board. Under this scheme, the Board will provide financial assistance in the form of pension to the registered construction workers aged above 60 years and have completed 3 years of membership with the Board. &lt;br&gt;

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Pension

**Where it applies:** Punjab; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** **Financial Assistance:** A pension of ₹2,000/- Per Month.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Department of Labour.
