# Maternity Benefit (HPBOCWWB)

**Short Title:** MBHPBOCWWB

**Scheme For:** Infra

**Tags:** Construction, Building, Maternity, Pregnant, Labour, Worker, Birth

The &quot;Maternity Benefit&quot; scheme by the Himachal Pradesh Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board .under this scheme, registered female beneficiaries (women who are officially enrolled in the scheme) will receive ₹25,000/- annually as financial support when they give birth or during the delivery of a child. The purpose of the scheme is to provide maternity financial assistance to construction workers in Himachal Pradesh.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Childcare

**Where it applies:** Himachal Pradesh; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** ₹25,000/- to registered female worker at the time of delivery or upon the birth of a child.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Labour and Employment Department, Himachal Pradesh.
