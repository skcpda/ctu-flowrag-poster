# Teelu Rauteli Pension Scheme

**Short Title:** TRPS

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Pension, Agricultural Activities, Disability, PwD

The &amp;quot;Teelu rauteli Pension&amp;quot; scheme was launched by the Department of Social Welfare, Government of Uttarakhand. The scheme aims to provide financial assistance to male/female individuals above 18 years of age who have incurred a partial disability of 20% to 40% while performing agricultural activities. 

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Pension

**Where it applies:** Uttarakhand; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - A pension of ₹1,200/- per month is provided to each eligible beneficiary. 

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Department Of Social Welfare.

**References / Annexures:** - **Guidelines (Page No. 17)**: https://uk.gov.in/department92/library_file/file-04-12-2023-06-02-23.pdf - **Online Application Portal**: https://ssp.uk.gov.in/OnlineRegistration/FrmKisanOnlineApplicationForm.aspx - **Official Website**: https://socialwelfare.uk.gov.in/service/apply-for-birth-certificate/

**Additional Information:**
Briefdescription: The scheme aims to provide a monthly pension of ₹1,200/- to male and female individuals above 18 years of age who have incurred a partial disability of 20% to 40% while performing agricultural activities.
