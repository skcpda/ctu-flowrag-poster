# Free Travelling Facility (HBOCWWB)

**Short Title:** FTF(HBOCWWB)

**Scheme For:** Family

**Tags:** Free Travelling, Building Worker, Construction Worker, Labour, Historical Places, Religious Places

The scheme “Free Travelling Facility” is implemented by the Haryana Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board (HBOCWWB), Labour Department, Government of Haryana. Under this scheme, the amount spent by registered workers and 4 family members for visiting famous religious/historical places once in four years will be paid at the rates prescribed by Haryana Roadways (ordinary fare) or actual railway fare (Second Class).

**Who is this for:** Family, Individual; Category: Travel & Tourism; Sector: Tourism in India

**Where it applies:** Haryana; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** 1. Under this scheme, free travelling facility is provided to the registered construction workers along with 4 family members to visit religious/historical places once in four years. 1. The actual fare incurred by the beneficiary is paid at the rates prescribed by Haryana Roadways (ordinary fare) or actual railway fare (Second Class).

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: Haryana Building & Other Construction Workers Welfare Board; Nodal Department: Labour Department.
