# 15 Days Skill Up-gradation Training

**Short Title:** 15DSUGT

**Launch Date:** 2013-08-27

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Stipend, Construction Worker, Skill Upgradation, Training

The A. P. Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board launched a welfare scheme &quot;15 Days Skill Up-gradation Training&quot;. The scheme aims to provide skill training, safety, and hygiene education for building and other construction workers registered with the Andhra Pradesh Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board. Upon successful completion of the training, workers will receive stipends and tool kits. This scheme will help workers acquire new skills or upgrade existing ones, improving their productivity and employability.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Skills & Employment; Sector: Training and Skill Up-gradation

**Where it applies:** Andhra Pradesh; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - Stipend of ₹7,000/-. - Skill development training for construction workers. - Tool kits provided post-training.

**Benefit Type:** Composite

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Labour Department.
