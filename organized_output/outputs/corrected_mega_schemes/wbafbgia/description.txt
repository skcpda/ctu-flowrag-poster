# West Bengal Artisans Financial Benefit Scheme 2024: Grant to Individual Artisans

**Short Title:** WBAFBGIA

**Launch Date:** 2024-03-07

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Artisans, Financial Benefit, Purchase, Repair, Equipment, Tool Kits

“Grant to Individual Artisans” is a sub-scheme under “West Bengal Artisans Financial Benefit Scheme 2024&amp;quot;, launched by the Micro, Small &amp;amp; Medium Enterprise and Textiles Department, Government of West Bengal on 7th March, 2024, and it shall remain in force for a period of five years. The objective of the scheme is to provide the artisans of Bengal systematic financial support for procurement of modern tool kits to individual artisans.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Financial assistance

**Where it applies:** West Bengal; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** One Time Grant up to ₹15,000/- for purchase &amp; repair of tool kits/ equipment.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Exclusions / Not eligible:** - Any central/state govt employee and his/her family members shall not be eligible. - Should not have availed of any assistance /grant from Central /State Govt. for the same purpose. - Handloom and Khadi weavers, and their societies (as they are covered under separate schemes).

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Micro, Small & Medium Enterprises and Textiles Department.

**References / Annexures:** - **Guidelines**: https://wbmsme.gov.in/sites/default/files/cms/circularpdf/1709828650Notification%20for%20West%20Bengal%20Artisans%20Financial%20Benefit%20Scheme%202024.pdf
