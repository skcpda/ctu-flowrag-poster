# Uniform Assistance for Other Backward Classes (Minority) Students Studying in Standard 1st to 8th

**Short Title:** UAOBCMOE

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Uniform Assistance, Education, Other Backward Classes, Minority

The scheme &amp;quot;Uniform Assistance for Other Backward Classes (Minority) Students Studying in Standard 1st to 8th&amp;quot; by the Social Justice and Empowerment Department, Government of Gujarat aims to promote education and reduce the economic burden on families of minority students from the Other Backward Classes (OBC) by providing them with uniform assistance. The scheme ensures that eligible students from classes 1 to 8 studying in government and aided primary schools receive ₹900/- to cover the cost of three sets of school uniforms.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Education & Learning; Sector: Resources for schools

**Where it applies:** Gujarat; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - **Financial Assistance:** ₹900/- is provided to each eligible student for the purchase of three pairs of school uniforms.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Social Justice and Empowerment Department.
