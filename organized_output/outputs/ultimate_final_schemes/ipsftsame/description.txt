# Investment Promotion Scheme (IPS) for Thrust Sectors: Scheme For Medical Equipment And Accessories, Medical Diagnostics And Ayush Products Manufacturing Units

**Short Title:** IPSFTSAME

**Scheme Brief Description:** “Scheme For Medical Equipment And Accessories, Medical Diagnostics And Ayush Products Manufacturing Units'' under the “Investment Promotion Scheme (IPS)” for Thrust Sectors, by the Dept. of Industries, DNH & DD, aims to provide subsidy to Medical Equipment and AYUSH Products Manufacturing Units.

**Launch Date:** 2015-07-03

**Scheme For:** Infra

**Tags:** Thrust Sectors, Medical Equipment, Incentives, Entrepreneurship, Subsidy, Reimbursement, IPS, Medical Accessories, Medical Diagnostics, Ayush Products, Medical Manufacturing Units

The scheme “Scheme For Medical Equipment And Accessories, Medical Diagnostics And Ayush Products Manufacturing Units” is a sub scheme under the “Investment Promotion Scheme (IPS)” for Thrust Sector. It was launched on 3rd July, 2015 for a period of five years by the Dept. of Industries, U.T. Administration Of Dadra &amp;amp; Nagar Haveli And Daman &amp;amp; Diu. Now it has been extended further from 20th May, 2022 and shall be operative till 19th May, 2027. &gt; ***Objective of the &amp;quot;Investment Promotion Scheme (IPS)”:*** The scheme envisages to provide further fillip to the specialized Thrust Sectors such as Plastic Furniture, Steel Furniture, Brass Furniture, Metal Furniture, Cane Furniture, Wooden Furniture, Bamboo Furniture, Fibre Glass Furniture and Marble Furniture etc, therefore making the U.T. a Special Hub for the furniture sector: maximize Women participation in the business sector, reduce unemployment among the local residents and incentivize the industries to employ resident laborers etc. &gt; ***Objective of the &amp;quot;Scheme For Medical Equipment And Accessories, Medical Diagnostics And Ayush Products Manufacturing Units&amp;quot; Sub-scheme:*** Under this Scheme, Capital Investment Subsidy and Credit Linked Interest Subsidy will be provided to Medical Equipment and Accessories, Medical Diagnostics and AYUSH Products Manufacturing Units.

**Who is this for:** Business Entity, Industries; Category: Business & Entrepreneurship; Sector: Setting up / start-up / entrepreneurship

**Where it applies:** Dadra & Nagar Haveli and Daman & Diu; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - **Capital Investment Subsidy:** 20% of Gross Fixed Capital Investment made will be reimbursed subject to maximum of ₹50,00,000/- per unit. - **Credit Linked Interest Subsidy:** 60% per annum limited to maximum of ₹50,00,000/- lakhs per annum for Five years OR for the period of repayment of loan whichever is earlier.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Exclusions / Not eligible:** The Enterprise will not be eligible for Interest Subsidy under this Scheme if, term loan is sanctioned after one year from the date of commencement of commercial production.
