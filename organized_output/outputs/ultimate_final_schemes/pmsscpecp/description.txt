# Additional Financial Assistance for the Children of Persons Engaged in the Cleaning Profession

**Short Title:** AFACPECP

**Scheme Brief Description:** This scheme aims to provide additional financial assistance of ₹500/- per month to children of individuals working in cleaning/sanitation profession. This assistance is given to students already receiving a scholarship under Post-Matric Scholarship Scheme, ensuring their continued education.

**Launch Date:** 2021-10-06

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Scholarship, Post-Matric, Sanitation Worker, Scavenger, Student

The &amp;quot;Additional Financial Assistance for the Children of Persons Engaged in the Cleaning Profession&amp;quot; scheme was launched by the Social Justice and Empowerment Department, Government of Rajasthan. The scheme provides an additional financial assistance of ₹500/- per month to children of individuals working in the cleaning or sanitation profession. This assistance is given to students already receiving a scholarship under the Post-Matric Scholarship Scheme, ensuring their continued education.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Education & Learning; Sector: Scholarships and student finance

**Where it applies:** Rajasthan; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** **Financial Assistance:** - This scheme provides ₹500/- per month as additional financial assistance to children of individuals working in the cleaning/sanitation profession. **Note:** Assistance will be provided for a maximum of 10 months per year.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**How to apply:** > **Registration** **Step-1:** Applicant have to visit the [official portal](https://sso.rajasthan.gov.in/signin). **Step-2:** Click on the option “[Register](https://sso.rajasthan.gov.in/register)”. **Step-3:** Then you will be redirected to the SSO registration page. The registration page will appear with the following options. - Citizen **Step-4:** Choose the either one option from the Jan Aadhaar Or Google to process further. - Jan Aadhaar : Enter the Jan Aadhaar number, click on the ‘Next’ button, - Select your name, the name of the head of the family and all the other members and - Click on the ‘Send OTP’ button. Enter the ‘OTP’ and - Click on the ‘Verify OTP’ button to Complete the registration. - Google : Enter the Gmail ID, click on the ‘Next’ button, - Enter the password. - A new link appear on screen, now click on new SSO link. - SSO id will appear on screen, now create the password. - Enter Mobile number, click on registration. > **Apply** **Step-1:** Applicant have to visit the [official portal](https://sso.rajasthan.gov.in/signin). **Step-2:** After login, dashboard will open. **Step-3:** Click on “IFMS-RAJSSP&quot; option. **Step-4:** Select &quot;Continue to RajSSP&quot;. **Step-5:** Now click on &quot;Apply for Scheme&quot; on screen. **Step-6:** In this new page, will get a list of names of all the schemes, and click on the name of &quot;Yojana&quot; **Step-7:** Select the category &quot;sanitation worker&quot;**** **Step-8:** Fill out all relevant details and upload the required document. **Step-9:** Submit.
