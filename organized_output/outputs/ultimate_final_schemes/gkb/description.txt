# Gaon Ki Beti

**Short Title:** GKB

**Scheme Brief Description:** MP Gaon Ki Beti Yojna 2022-23 is a scholarship provided by the Madhya Pradesh government. The MP state government has started this scheme to encourage the higher education of girl children in remote areas in 2005.

**Launch Date:** 2005-06-01

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Financial Assistance, Higher Education, Scholarship

This scheme aims to provide financial assistance to talented girls from rural areas to encourage them to pursue higher education. It seeks to motivate young girls to continue their studies and build a bright future.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Education & Learning; Sector: Scholarships and student finance

**Where it applies:** Madhya Pradesh; State

**Benefits / Assistance:** 1. Each year, girls who pass the 12th standard with first-class marks from a village will be eligible. 1. Selected students will receive a scholarship of ₹500/- per month. 1. The scholarship will be provided for a maximum of 10 months per year.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**How to apply:** 1. SC/ST/OBC/General girl students should register themselves on the [portal.](http://www.scholarshipportal.mp.nic.in/Index.aspx) 1. Click on New Registration for Gaon Ki Beti and fill out the required details. 1. Now they can apply for the benefits of the scheme by logging in with the registration ID on the [portal](http://www.scholarshipportal.mp.nic.in/sLogin.aspx).
Mode: Online.

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Higher Education Department.
