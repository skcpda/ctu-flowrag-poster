# Uttarakhand State Shilp Ratna Award

**Short Title:** USSRA

**Scheme Brief Description:** Under this scheme, every year, 25 artisans of Uttarakhand are honored. The award includes a cash prize of ₹1,00,000, a certificate of appreciation, a memento, an angavastra (ceremonial stole), and a citation letter.

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Award, Artisans, Traditional Crafts, Cash Prize

The scheme &amp;quot;Uttarakhand State Shilp Ratna Award&amp;quot; is implemented by the Department of Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises, Government of Uttarakhand. The scheme aims to honor exceptionally skilled artisans of the state for their outstanding contributions to the traditional craft sector. This annual award celebrates heritage, promotes excellence, and supports artisans whose expertise has preserved and enriched cultural craftsmanship.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Skills & Employment

**Where it applies:** Uttarakhand; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** **Award Details:** - A cash prize of ₹1,00,000/-. - A memento (symbolic representation). - An angavastra (ceremonial stole). - A certificate of appreciation.

**Benefit Type:** Composite

**How to apply:** > **Application Process:** **Step 1:** The interested applicant should visit (during office hours) the General Manager, District Industries Centre and request a hard copy of the prescribed format of the application form from the staff exclusively entrusted to issue and collect filled-in applications. **OR** The interested applicant downloads the application form from the official website link: [https://investuttarakhand.uk.gov.in](https://investuttarakhand.uk.gov.in) [https://doi.uk.gov.in/](https://doi.uk.gov.in/) **Step 2:** In the application form, fill in all the mandatory fields and attach copies of all the mandatory documents (self-attested, if required). **Step 3:** Submit the duly filled and signed application form along with the documents to the General Manager, District Industries Centre. &gt; **Post-application Process:** **Step 1:** The application is reviewed by a District-Level Committee chaired by the District Magistrate. **Step 2:** Shortlisted applications are forwarded to the State-Level Selection Committee, chaired by the Principal Secretary, MSME Department. **Step 3:** Final awardees are selected based on merit and recommendations.
Mode: Offline.

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Department Of The Industrial Development.
