# Kayaka Spoorthi Scheme Karnataka 

**Short Title:** KSSK

**Scheme Brief Description:** The "Kayaka Spoorthi Scheme" aims to provide financial assistance to women Self-Help Groups (SHGs) engaged in the leather industry. The goal is to promote self-sufficiency and economic independence by enabling these women to start or expand business activities related to leatherwork.

**Scheme For:** Infra

**Tags:** Financial Assistance, SHG, Business Activities, Leather Industry

The &quot;Kayaka Spoorthi Scheme&quot; aims to provide financial assistance to women Self-Help Groups (SHGs) engaged in the leather industry. The goal is to promote self-sufficiency and economic independence by enabling these women to start or expand business activities related to leatherwork. A group of 10 members receives financial support under this scheme.

**Who is this for:** Self Help Groups (SHGS); Category: Women and Child; Sector: Financial Assistance

**Where it applies:** Karnataka; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - Financial Assistance of ₹2,50,000/- per SHG with 10 members.  **NOTE:** - Each member gets ₹15,000/- as a subsidy and ₹10,000/- as a loan from the corporation. - 33% representation for women artisans and 5% for physically challenged individuals.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**How to apply:** > **Registration Process:** **Step-01:** Visit the official [Seva Sindhu Portal](https://sevasindhu.karnataka.gov.in/Sevasindhu/English). **Step-02:** Choose &quot;New Users Register Here&quot; and fill in your Aadhaar Number and the Captcha code. **Step-03:** An OTP will be sent to your Aadhaar-linked mobile number. Enter the OTP to continue. **Step-04:** Once your DigiLocker account is created, click &quot;Allow&quot; to proceed. **Step-05:** Fill out your email ID, mobile number, create a password, and enter the captcha, then click &quot;Submit&quot;. **Step-06:** You will receive OTPs on both your email and mobile number. Enter both OTPs and select &quot;Validate&quot;. **Step-07:** Once validated, a confirmation message will indicate your registration is successful.  > **Applying for a Scheme::** **Step-01:** Go to [Seva Sindhu Portal](https://sevasindhu.karnataka.gov.in/Sevasindhu/English) and select &quot;Department &amp; Services&quot;. **Step-02:** Select department &quot;Social Welfare Department&quot; and click on the scheme &quot;Kayaka Spoorthi Scheme&quot; **Step-03:** Click &quot;Apply Online&quot; and enter your registered email ID/mobile number and the OTP. **Step-04:** Select the scheme and provide your personal, address, and banking details as required. **Step-05:** Once submitted, you will receive an Application ID. You can use this ID to track your application status on the portal.
Mode: Online.

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Social Welfare Department.
