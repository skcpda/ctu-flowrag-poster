# Aatmanirbhar Gujarat Scheme for Assistance to Mega Industries: Assistance of Interest Subsidy

**Short Title:** AGSMAIS

**Scheme Brief Description:** The "Aatmanirbhar Gujarat Scheme for Assistance to Mega Industries" aims to strengthen the overall industrial ecosystem in the State and also enable a larger set of manufacturing companies to emulate the adoption of clean energy and make them future-ready to compete in global markets.

**Launch Date:** 2022-10-05

**Scheme For:** Infra

**Tags:** Business, Entrepreneurship, Mega Industries, Interest Subsidy, Thrust Manufacturing Sector

The &amp;quot;Aatmanirbhar Gujarat Scheme for Assistance to Mega Industries&amp;quot; is an umbrella scheme launched by the Industries and Mines Department, Gujarat. It aims to empower the state&amp;#39;s dynamic mega-industry sector, which significantly contributes to employment, GDP, and exports. Effective from October 5, 2022, to October 4, 2032, this initiative supports growth, investments, and the vision of &amp;quot;Aatmanirbhar Gujarat&amp;quot; and &amp;quot;Aatmanirbhar Bharat.&amp;quot; The component &amp;quot;Assistance of Interest Subsidy&amp;quot; offers a fixed quantum of interest subsidy for new industrial setups or expansion projects, facilitating industrial investments and encouraging economic development.

**Who is this for:** Business Entity, Industries; Category: Business & Entrepreneurship; Sector: Setting up / start-up / entrepreneurship

**Where it applies:** Gujarat; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** &gt; **Substantial Interest Reduction:** - **Interest Subsidy:** @7% on the term loan. - **Capped Amount:** A maximum of 1.2% of eligible fixed capital investment per annum for 10 years.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**How to apply:** > **Registration Process:** **Step-1:** Visit the [Investor Facilitation Portal (IFP)](https://ifp.gujarat.gov.in/DIGIGOV/) portal. Click on &quot;New Investor Registration&quot; to access the registration form. **Step-2:** Fill in the Registration Form. Click on &quot;Submit&quot;. **Step-3:** Verify the E-mail ID using the link sent on your registered E-mail ID. **Step-4:** After verification, you will be registered successfully.  > **Post-Registration Process:** **Step-1:** Visit the [Investor Facilitation Portal (IFP)](https://ifp.gujarat.gov.in/DIGIGOV/) portal. Click on &quot;Login&quot;. **Step-2:** Enter the email address and password you used during registration. **Step-3:** Click the &quot;Login&quot; button to access your account. **Step-4:** Now fill in the required details and mandatory documents about your business to complete the application process.  > Helpdesk: [Contact Us.](https://ifp.gujarat.gov.in/DIGIGOV/IFP-pages/contact.jsp?pagedisp=static)
Mode: Online.

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Industries and Mines Department.
