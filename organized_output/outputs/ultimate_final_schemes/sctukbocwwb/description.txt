# Financial Assistance for Construction of Toilet (UKBOCWWB)

**Short Title:** FACT(UKBOCWWB)

**Scheme Brief Description:** The scheme provides financial assistance of up to ₹12,000/- to registered construction workers for building toilets. The assistance is disbursed in two installments: the first installment of ₹8,000/- and the second installment of ₹4,000/-.

**Scheme For:** Family

**Tags:** Toilet, Construction Worker, Labour, Building Worker, Financial Assistance

The “Financial Assistance for Construction of Toilet” scheme is implemented by the Uttarakhand Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board (UKBOCWWB) under the Labour Department, Government of Uttarakhand. The scheme aims to provide financial support to registered construction workers for building toilets. The financial assistance is disbursed in two installments. 

**Who is this for:** Family; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Financial assistance

**Where it applies:** Uttarakhand; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** &gt; Under the scheme, registered construction workers receive financial assistance of ₹12,000/- in two installments for the construction of toilets. - **First Installment:** ₹8,000/- - **Second Installment:** ₹4,000/- 

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**How to apply:** - The online application can be submitted through Shramik Suvidha Kendras. At the time of application, the following documents are required: Shramik Card, Aadhaar Card, Self-Declaration Certificate, Aadhaar-linked bank account details, and a photograph showing the worker along with the toilet under construction. The photograph must clearly show the toilet with concrete walls, roof with lintel, tank, water arrangement, and seat. - Land-related documents of the site where the toilet is constructed, such as Dakhal Kharij/Khatauni/Registry or land ownership certificate, along with a permanent residence certificate, must also be attached. Additionally, an affidavit is required stating that the applicant has not availed benefits from any other scheme and has not applied for assistance from any other department. - If the application is verified and found correct during the departmental investigation, the first installment is credited to the applicant’s bank account. After completion of the construction, the applicant must reapply after one month with all relevant documents to receive the second installment of ₹4,000/-. 
Mode: Online - via CSC.

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: Uttarakhand Building And Other Construction Workers’ Welfare Board; Nodal Department: Department Of Labour.
