# Incentive Scheme for Establishment of New Super Specialty Hospital in Private Sector

**Short Title:** ISENSSHPS

**Scheme Brief Description:** The scheme offers financial incentives (capital subsidy, interest subvention, and electricity duty reimbursement) to private/NGO entities establishing new super specialty hospitals (50+ beds) in Gujarat, targeting underserved areas.

**Scheme For:** Infra

**Tags:** Hospital, Health, Incentive, Medical, Subsidy

Launched on 13th December 2016, the &quot;Incentive Scheme for Establishment of New Super Specialty Hospital in Private Sector&quot; by the Health &amp; Family Welfare Department, Government of Gujarat, aims to address the shortage of high-end medical facilities in remote areas by incentivizing private and non-governmental organizations to establish super specialty hospitals. The scheme provides 10% capital subsidy on medical equipment loans (up to ₹10 crore), 4% interest subvention (max ₹40 crore loan) for 7 years, and reimbursement of electricity duty for 5 years. Eligible entities include registered doctors, firms, trusts, and corporations. 

**Who is this for:** Individual, Registered Societies, Trust; Category: Transport & Infrastructure; Sector: Infrastructure

**Where it applies:** Gujarat; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** > **Capital Subsidy** - 10% of bank loans for medical equipment (max ₹10 crore). - Disbursed post-hospital operationalization. > **Interest Subvention** - 4% interest relief on loans up to ₹40 crore for 7 years. - Paid in 6 annual installments after a 1-year grace period. > **Electricity Duty Reimbursement** 100% reimbursement for 5 years (quarterly claims). > **Employment Generation** Mandates hiring qualified medical/paramedical staff. > **Disbursement** - **Capital Subsidy:** After Equipment Installation and Hospital Launch.- **Interest Subvention:** Annual Installments Post 1-Year Grace Period.- **Electricity Reimbursement:** Quarterly Claims. 

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Exclusions / Not eligible:** Excludes Government/Semi-Government Bodies. 

**How to apply:** **Step 1:** The interested applicant should take a printout of the [prescribed format](https://gujhealth.gujarat.gov.in/images/pdf/Application-forms-for-Establishment-of-New-Super-Specialty-Hospital-new.pdf) of the application form. **Step 2:** In the application form, fill in all the mandatory fields, and attach copies of all the mandatory documents (self-attest, if required). **Step 3:** Submit the duly filled and signed application form along with the documents, within the prescribed period (if any), to - ***Commissioner, Health &amp; Medical Education, Dr. Jivraj Mehta Bhavan, Gandhinagar***. Pay ₹20,000/- scrutiny fee via Demand Draft. **Step 4:** Request a receipt or acknowledgement from the concerned authority to whom the application has been submitted. Ensure that the receipt contains essential details such as the date of submission.  **Approval &amp; Verification** - State-Level Committee scrutinizes applications. - Technical Group conducts physical inspections. **Compliance** - Submit quarterly utilization reports. - Maintain services for 7 years (penalty: repayment if violated). 
Mode: Offline.

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Health & Family Welfare Department.
