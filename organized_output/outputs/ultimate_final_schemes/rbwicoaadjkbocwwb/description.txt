# Registered BOC Workers in case of an Accidental Death (JKBOCWWB)

**Short Title:** RBWICOAADJKBOCWWB

**Scheme Brief Description:** The "Registered BOC Workers in Case of Accidental Death" scheme by the JKBOCW Welfare Board, Labour Department, Jammu & Kashmir, provides financial assistance to the nominee or dependents of registered BOC workers in the event of accidental death.

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Worker, Construction, Labour, Financial Assistance, Accident, Death

Launched on 1st January 2018, the scheme &quot;Registered BOC Workers in Case of Accidental Death&quot; is a Welfare Scheme by the Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board, Labour Department, Jammu &amp; Kashmir. Through this scheme, financial assistance of ₹4,00,000/- is provided to the nominee or dependents of registered BOC workers in the event of an accidental death. The applications are accepted online as well as offline.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Financial assistance

**Where it applies:** Jammu and Kashmir; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** ₹4,00,000/- is provided to the nominee or dependents of the deceased worker.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**How to apply:** ***Application -*** **Step 1:** Go to the [official website ](https://www.jkbocw.gov.in/Index.aspx)of the Jammu &amp; Kashmir Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board. **Step 2:** Click on [&quot;Login,&quot;](https://portal.jkbocw.gov.in/LoginGuest.aspx) enter your details, and sign in to your account. **Step 3:** After logging in, click on &quot;Scheme Registration&quot; and select &quot;Apply for Scheme.&quot; **Step 4:** Choose the scheme you wish to apply for in order to avail the benefits. **Step 5:** Enter all required information and upload the necessary documents as prompted. **Step 6:** Finally, click on &quot;Submit Application&quot; to complete the process.  ***Status Tracking -*** Applicant can track their application status online through the [official website](https://www.jkbocw.gov.in/Index.aspx) of the Jammu &amp; Kashmir Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board. Relevant updates will be sent to the registered mobile number.
Mode: Online.

**Additional Application Processes:** **Process 2:** **Step 1:** The interested applicant should visit the JKBOCWWB office during office hours and request a hard copy of the prescribed application form from the staff responsible for issuing and collecting filled-in applications. OR The interested applicant can print the Scheme [application form](https://www.jkbocw.gov.in/downloads/circ.pdf) (Page no. 19). **Step 2:** In the application form, fill in all mandatory fields, paste a passport-sized photograph (signed across, if required), and attach copies of all mandatory documents (self-attested, if required). **Step 3:** Submit the duly filled and signed application form along with the required documents to the Assistant Labour Commissioner (ALC ) of your district. **Step 4:** Request a receipt or acknowledgement from the concerned authority upon submission. Ensure the receipt contains essential details such as the date and time of submission and a unique identification number (if applicable). **NOTE:** Ensure that the application is submitted within the prescribed period, if any.

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Department of Labour and Employment.
