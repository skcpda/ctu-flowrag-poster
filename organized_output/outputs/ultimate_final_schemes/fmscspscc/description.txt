# Fodder Minikits under Schedule Castes Sub Plan (SC Category)

**Short Title:** FMSCSP(SCC)

**Scheme Brief Description:** The scheme “Fodder Minikits under Schedule Castes Sub Plan” is implemented by the Directorate of Animal Husbandry, Government of Gujarat to provide an improved variety of fodder seeds to Scheduled Castes beneficiaries for widespread publicity, demonstration, and adaptation by farmers.

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Farmer, Scheduled Caste, Fodder Minikits, Seed

The scheme “Fodder Minikits under Schedule Castes Sub Plan” is implemented by the Directorate of Animal Husbandry, Agriculture, Farmers Welfare and Co-operation Department, Government of Gujarat. Under this scheme, improved varieties of fodder seeds are provided to beneficiaries for widespread publicity, demonstration, and adaptation by farmers.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Agriculture,Rural & Environment; Sector: Animal husbandry

**Where it applies:** Gujarat; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** 1. Under this scheme, the fodder seed minikit of 3 Kg, 7 Kg, or 10 Kg is given to the eligible beneficiary.

**Benefit Type:** In Kind

**How to apply:** To avail the benefits of the scheme, the scheduled caste beneficiary may contact a nearby Intensive Cattle Development Project Sub Centre.
Mode: Offline.

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: Directorate Of Animal Husbandry, Government Of Gujarat; Nodal Department: Agriculture, Farmers Welfare and Cooperation Department.
