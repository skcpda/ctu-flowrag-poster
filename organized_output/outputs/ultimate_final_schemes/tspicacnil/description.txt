# Scheme for Providing Incentives to Coaches of Athletes who have Won Competitions at the National and International Levels

**Short Title:** SPICACNIL

**Scheme Brief Description:** The scheme aims to honour coaches who have significantly contributed to the success of athletes in the field of sports. Under this scheme, financial incentives are provided to coaches of athletes who have won competitions at national and international levels.

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Incentive, Coach, Athlete, Sports

The “Scheme for Providing Incentives to Coaches of Athletes who have Won Competitions at the National and International Levels” scheme was launched by the Sports, Youth and Cultural Activities Department, Government of Gujarat. The scheme aims to honour coaches who have significantly contributed to the success of athletes in the field of sports. Under this scheme, financial incentives are provided to coaches of athletes who have won competitions at national and international levels. 

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Sports & Culture; Sector: Sports

**Where it applies:** Gujarat; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** Under this scheme, financial incentives of up to 10% of the prize money awarded are provided to coaches of winning players in national and international level competitions, subject to a maximum limit of ₹10 lakh. 

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**How to apply:** **Step 1:** The interested applicant should visit office of the Sports, Youth and Cultural Activities Department and request a hard copy of the prescribed format of the application form from the staff exclusively entrusted to issue and collect filled-in applications. **Step 2:** In the application form, fill in all the mandatory fields, and attach copies of all the mandatory documents (self-attested, if required). **Step 3:** Submit the duly filled and signed application form, along with the required documents, to the office of the Sports, Youth, and Cultural Activities Department. 
Mode: Offline.

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Sports, Youth and Cultural Activities Department.
