# Marriage Incentive Reward Scheme

**Short Title:** MIRS

**Scheme Brief Description:** The Marriage Incentive Reward scheme for the marriage of disabled persons by the Government of Uttar Pradesh is being operated by the department since the year 1997.

**Launch Date:** 1997-01-01

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Financial Assistance, Widow Marriage, Social Welfare, Empowerment

This is a State Sponsored Scheme and only the citizens who are domiciled in the State of Uttar Pradesh can apply to this scheme. By the Government of Uttar Pradesh, the Marriage Incentive Reward scheme was initiated prizes are given to Divyangjan for getting married. The marriage should be registered with the Registrar of the State and one or both should be a differently able person.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Financial assistance

**Where it applies:** Uttar Pradesh; State

**Benefits / Assistance:** 1. In the case of the young man with the disability: ₹15,000/- is provided. 1. In the case of the girl with the disability ₹20,000/- is provided. 1. In case of disability of both young man and girl ₹35,000/- is provided.

**Benefit Type:** Cash
