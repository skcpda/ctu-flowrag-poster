# National Mission on Edible Oil- Oil Palm: Custom Hiring Centre cum Harvester Groups

**Short Title:** NMOECHCHG

**Scheme Brief Description:** The scheme component "Custom Hiring Centre cum Harvester Groups" under the "National Mission on Edible Oil–Oil Palm" scheme aims to increase the production of oil palm in the state and reduce dependency on imports by supporting farmers with equipment and facilities.

**Scheme For:** Infra

**Tags:** Edible Oil, Oil Palm, Custom Hiring Centre, Harvester Group

The scheme component &amp;quot;Custom Hiring Centre cum Harvester Groups &amp;quot; launched under the &amp;quot;National Mission on Edible Oil–Oil Palm&amp;quot; scheme by the Department of Agriculture, Farmers Welfare and Co-operation, Government of Gujarat, aims to increase the production of oil palm in the state and reduce dependency on imports by supporting farmers with equipment and facilities. It provides financial assistance for establishing Custom Hiring Centres and Harvester Groups to make harvesting more efficient. 

**Who is this for:** Registered Societies, Self Help Groups (SHGS), Individual; Category: Agriculture,Rural & Environment; Sector: Entrepreneurship development

**Where it applies:** Gujarat; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - **Subsidy for Custom Hiring Centre cum Harvester Groups:** ₹25,00,000/- per unit for equipment, tools, training, contingencies, and maintenance. 

**Benefit Type:** Cash
