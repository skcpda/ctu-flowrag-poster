# Physical Research Laboratory Award

**Short Title:** PRLA

**Scheme Brief Description:** The PRL Award, instituted by the late Prof. Devendra Lal, former Director of the Physical Research Laboratory (PRL), is awarded biennially to recognize outstanding Indian scientists for original work in Earth & Planetary systems, including areas like Oceanography, Atmospheric Sciences & Meteorology.

**Scheme For:** Family

**Scheme Type:** Centrally Sponsored Scheme

**Tags:** Award, Scientist, Cash Prize

The PRL Award, instituted by the late Prof. Devendra Lal, former Director of the Physical Research Laboratory (PRL), is awarded biennially to recognize outstanding Indian scientists for original work in Earth and Planetary systems, including areas like Oceanography, Atmospheric Sciences, and Meteorology. The award consists of a cash prize of ₹50,000 and a medal.  **Discipline Areas:** - Earth and Planetary systems, including areas of oceanography, atmospheric sciences, and meteorology.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Education & Learning; Sector: Apprenticeships and training

**Where it applies:** Central

**Benefits / Assistance:** - **Cash Prize of ₹50,000/- and Medal**

**Benefit Type:** Composite

**How to apply:** **Step-01:** The applicant visits the [PRL website](https://www.prl.res.in/prl-eng/) and navigate to the &quot;Opportunities&quot; section. **Step-02:** Click on &quot;Awards Administered By PRL&quot; and select scheme &quot;PRL Award&quot; **Step-03:** The candidate downloads the prescribed format of the [application form](https://view.officeapps.live.com/op/view.aspx?src=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.prl.res.in%2Fprl-eng%2Fsites%2Fdefault%2Ffiles%2Fdocuments%2Fnational-awards%2Fperforma-for-nomination.doc&wdOrigin=BROWSELINK). **Step-04:** In the application form, fill in all the mandatory fields, and attach copies of all the mandatory documents (self-attest, if required). **Step-05:** Nominations should be submitted via email to the Director: DIRECTOR@PRL.RES.IN with the subject line &quot;Nomination for The PRL Award&quot;.
Mode: Online.

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Department of Space; Nodal Ministry: Ministry Of Science And Technology.

**References / Annexures:** - **Guidelines**: https://www.prl.res.in/prl-eng/prl_award
