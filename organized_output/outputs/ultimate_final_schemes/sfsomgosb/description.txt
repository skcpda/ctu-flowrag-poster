# Scheme for Supply of Male Goat on Subsidy Basis

**Short Title:** SFSOMGOSB

**Scheme Brief Description:** "Male Goat Supply Scheme on Grant Basis" aims to improve the breed of indigenous/local goats throughout the state. Through this scheme, one male goat of an improved breed (Jamnapari, Barbari, or Sirohi Bakra) is provided to goat farmers of all categories on a grant basis.

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Animal, Goat, Insurance, Financial Assistance, Subsidy

The scheme &quot;Male Goat Supply Scheme on Grant Basis&quot; by the Animal Husbandry Department, Government of Madhya Pradesh aims to improve the breed of indigenous/local goats throughout the state. Through this scheme, one male goat of an improved breed (Jamnapari, Barbari, or Sirohi Bakra) is provided to goat farmers of all categories on a grant basis. The applications for this scheme are processed through a multi-level approval system involving Gram Sabha, Janpad Panchayat, and District Panchayat. The scheme is implemented across all districts of the state.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Agriculture,Rural & Environment; Sector: Animal husbandry

**Where it applies:** Madhya Pradesh; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** **Male Goat Package** - One improved breed male goat: ₹7,500/- - Insurance coverage (2.75% sum insured for one year): ₹206/- - Mineral mixture: ₹394/- - Training booklet and monitoring card: ₹200/- - Total package value: ₹8,300/- **Grant Component** - Government contribution: 75% of total cost - Beneficiary contribution: 25% of total cost

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**How to apply:** **Initial Approval** by the beneficiaries in Gram Sabha. **Secondary Approval** by the Janpad Panchayat for Gram Sabha-approved beneficiaries. **Final Approval** by the Agriculture Standing Committee of the District Panchayat.  The interested applicants should contact the nearest Veterinary Officer of the concerned District Incharge of Animal Dispensary / Deputy Director of Veterinary.
Mode: Offline.

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Animal Husbandry Department.
