# Distribution of Certified Seeds of Maize

**Short Title:** DOCSOM

**Scheme Brief Description:** The "Distribution of Certified Seeds of Maize" scheme by the Agriculture-Farmers Welfare Department, Tamil Nadu offers a subsidy to maize-growing farmers in specific districts of the state, providing financial assistance for purchasing certified maize seeds.

**Scheme For:** Infra

**Tags:** Agriculture, Farmer, Seed Distribution, Maize, Subsidy

The &amp;quot;Distribution of Certified Seeds of Maize&amp;quot; scheme by the Agriculture-Farmers Welfare Department, Tamil Nadu, is aimed at maize-growing farmers across selected districts in Tamil Nadu, offering a subsidy of the seed cost. The scheme ensures a 24% flow of benefits to SC/ST farmers and 20% to women farmers or women groups, with preference given to small and marginal farmers. &lt;br&gt;

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Agriculture,Rural & Environment; Sector: Agricultural Inputs- seeds, fertilizer etc.

**Where it applies:** Tamil Nadu; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - **Subsidy on Certified Seeds:** Farmers can receive 50% of the cost of certified maize seeds, with a cap of ₹1,200/- per quintal, whichever is less.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**How to apply:** **Step 1:** Collect the application form from the nearest Agricultural Office. **Step 2:** Fill out the application form with the required details and attach mandatory documents (self-attested, if required). **Step 3:** Submit the form to the concerned office: &gt; **Village Level:** Submit the application to the Assistant Agricultural Officer. &gt; **Block Level:** Submit to the Agricultural Officer or Deputy Agricultural Officer. &gt; **District Level:** Submit to the Joint Director of Agriculture. &lt;br&gt;
Mode: Offline.

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Agriculture Department.
