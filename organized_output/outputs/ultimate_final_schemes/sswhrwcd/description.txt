# Subsidized Scheme for Widows: Haryana Women Development Corporation

**Short Title:** SSWHRWCD

**Scheme Brief Description:** The scheme aims to provide financial assistance to Haryana Domiciled Widow, Divorced, and Legally Separated Women. Through this scheme, a subsidy on the total loan is provided.

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Self Reliant, Economic Status, Social Status, Interest, Loan

The scheme &quot;Subsidized Scheme for Widows&quot; by the Women &amp; Child Development Department, Haryana Government, aims to provide financial assistance to Haryana-domiciled widows, divorced, and Legally Separated Women. Through this scheme, a subsidy on the total loan is provided. The scheme is covered without collateral security by the bank. The scheme is implemented by the Haryana Women Development Corporation. 

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Women and Child

**Where it applies:** Haryana; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** 1. A 25% flat subsidy, subject to a maximum of ₹50,000/-, will be provided on the total loan (not as a capital subsidy). 1. The beneficiary&#39;s share is 10% of the total loan. 1. The maximum loan limit is ₹3,00,000/-. 1. The scheme will be covered under without collateral security by the bank. 1. The balance amount will be covered from commercial/nationalised banks.  > **Mode of Disbursement:** The balance amount will be covered from commercial/nationalised banks. > **Moratorium Period:** The moratorium period will be three months after disbursement of the loan. 

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Exclusions / Not eligible:** 1. No subsidy for interest accrued on a delayed period will be allowed in case of default of payment of EMI.
