# Family Pension (DBOCWWB)

**Short Title:** FPDBOCWWB

**Scheme Brief Description:** The scheme "Family Pension" was launched by the Delhi Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board. In this scheme, upon the death of the pensioner, the surviving spouse is provided 50% of the pension amount or ₹1500/-, whichever is higher.

**Scheme For:** Family

**Tags:** Construction, Worker, Pension, Family, Labour

The &quot;Family Pension&quot; scheme was launched by the Delhi Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board, Labour Department, Delhi. In this scheme, upon the death of the pensioner, the surviving spouse is provided 50% of the pension amount or ₹1,500/-, whichever is higher.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Pension

**Where it applies:** Delhi; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** 50% of the pension or ₹1,500/-, whichever is higher.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**How to apply:** **Step 1:** The interested applicant should take a print of the [prescribed format](https://bocw.delhi.gov.in/sites/default/files/generic_multiple_files/form_no._xlv_family_pension_under_rule_283.pdf) of the application form. **Step 2:** In the application form, fill in all the mandatory fields, paste the passport-sized photograph (signed across, if required), and attach copies of all the mandatory documents (self-attest, if required). **Step 3:** Submit the duly filled and signed application form along with the documents, within the prescribed period (if any), to the Labour Incharge of the Board. **Helpline Number (24x7):** 011-41236600 **DBOCWWB Department Number (Office Hours: 9:30AM - 6:00PM) :** 011-23813846
Mode: Offline.

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Labour Department.
