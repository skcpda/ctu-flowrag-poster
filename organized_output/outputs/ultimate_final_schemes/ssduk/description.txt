# Saksham Scholarship

**Short Title:** SSDUK

**Scheme Brief Description:** The scheme “Saksham Scholarship” aims to promote higher education among differently-abled students by extending financial assistance for graduate-level studies.

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Scholarship, Technical Education, Differently Abled Student, PwD

The scheme “Saksham Scholarship” aims to encourage and support students with disabilities by providing financial assistance. It provides ₹50,000/- per year to meritorious differently-abled students pursuing graduate-level studies. 

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Education & Learning; Sector: Scholarships and student finance

**Where it applies:** Uttarakhand; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - **Financial Assistance:** ₹50,000/- per year. 

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**How to apply:** - Scholarship schemes conducted by the All India Council for Technical Education (AICTE) follow the below-mentioned procedure: Applications under the AICTE scheme are invited through the NSP portal between the months of September to December. Scholarships are granted by AICTE on the basis of merit. - Documents required for application – High School and Intermediate mark sheets and certificates, and income certificate. - For Saksham Scheme – A disability certificate of minimum 40% issued by the competent authority is mandatory. - Students must first apply online through their respective institutions, which is then verified by the concerned principal. After that, the online application verified by the State Level Officer (S.L.O.) is forwarded to AICTE, New Delhi (AICTE). 
Mode: Online.

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Directorate of Technical Education .
