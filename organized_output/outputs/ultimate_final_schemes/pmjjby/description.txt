# Pradhan Mantri Jeevan Jyoti Bima Yojana

**Short Title:** PMJJBY

It is a one-year cover, renewable from year to year. The scheme is offered by banks/post offices and administered through life insurance companies.

**Launch Date:** 2015-05-09

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Scheme Type:** Central Sector Scheme

**Tags:** LIC, Consent, Jansuraksha, Claim Form

Pradhan Mantri Jeevan Jyoti Bima Yojana (PMJJBY) is an insurance scheme offering life insurance cover for death due to any reason. It is a one-year cover, renewable from year to year. The scheme is offered by banks/post offices and administered through life insurance companies. All individuals who are account holders of participating banks/ post office in the age group of 18 to 50 years are entitled to join.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Banking,Financial Services and Insurance; Sector: Insurance

**Where it applies:** Central

**Benefits / Assistance:** 1. PMJJBY offers one- year term **life cover of ₹ 2.00 Lakh** to all the subscribers in the age group of 18-50 years.1. It covers death due to any reason. 1. **Premium payable is ₹ 436/- per annum** per subscriber, to be auto debited from the subscriber’s bank/post office account.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**How to apply:** **Offline** **Step 01:** Download and take print of the “CONSENT-CUM-DECLARATION FORM” given in the link below: [https://www.jansuraksha.gov.in/Files/PMJJBY/English/ApplicationForm.pdf#zoom=250](https://www.jansuraksha.gov.in/Files/PMJJBY/English/ApplicationForm.pdf#zoom=250) **Step 02:** Duly fill and sign the application form, attach the self-attested copies of the required documents, and submit the case to the authorised official of Bank / Post Office. The official will return you the “ACKNOWLEDGEMENT SLIP CUM CERTIFICATE OF INSURANCE”.
Mode: Offline.

**Additional Application Processes:** **Process 2:** One can also avail cover under PMJJBY online using their bank&#39;s Net banking facility.

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: Banks; Nodal Department: Department of Financial Service; Nodal Ministry: Ministry Of Finance.
