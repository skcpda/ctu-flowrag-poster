# Sweekruti: Assistance to Parents of Transgender Children

**Short Title:** SATPTC

**Scheme Brief Description:** The scheme aims to provide financial assistance to the parents of transgender children to support them in successfully raising their children against societal intolerance, stigma, discrimination, and violence.

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Transgender, Sweekruti, Parents, Guardians

The scheme &amp;quot;Assistance to Parents of Transgenders&amp;quot; is a component of the “Sweekruti” scheme, implemented by the Social Security and Empowerment of Persons with Disabilities (SSEPD) Department, Government of Odisha. The scheme aims to provide financial assistance to the parents of transgender children to support them in successfully raising their children against societal intolerance, stigma, discrimination, and violence.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Financial assistance

**Where it applies:** Odisha; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - **Financial assistance:** ₹1,500/- per transgender child per month. > **Note:** - Assistance is provided until the child reaches the age of 18 years. - In case of death of the parent, supportive money will be made available to the guardian subject to production of the relevant Certificate of guardianship obtained from Competent Authority. 

**Benefit Type:** Cash
