# Son/Daughter Education Assistance (UKBOCWWB)

**Short Title:** SDEA(UKBOCWWB)

**Scheme Brief Description:** The scheme provides financial assistance to the children of registered building and construction workers pursuing education from Class I up to higher education or professional courses.

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Education Assistance, Construction Worker, Building Worker, Labour, Student, Scholarship

The “Son/Daughter Education Assistance” scheme is implemented by the Uttarakhand Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board (UKBOCWWB) under the Labour Department, Government of Uttarakhand. The scheme provides financial assistance to the children of registered building and construction workers pursuing education from Class I up to higher education or professional courses. 

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Education & Learning; Sector: Scholarships and student finance

**Where it applies:** Uttarakhand; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** **Financial assistance for education will be provided to the children of construction workers at the following rates:** **Sl. No.****Class/Course****Financial Assistance (Yearly)**1Class 1 to 5₹1,800/-2Class 6 to 10₹2,400/-3Class 11 to 12₹3,000/-4Graduation/Post Graduation or its equivalent₹10,000/-5ITI, Polytechnic courses or higher educationThe course fee will be payable to the Government Institutions / College.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**How to apply:** - Construction workers have to apply online through Shramik Suvidha Kendras. - During the application process, the following documents must be attached: Shramik Card, Aadhaar Card of the worker, mobile number, bank account details, Aadhaar Card of the children, attested copy of the children’s last year’s mark sheet (not required for Class 1), certificate of enrollment from the educational institution, self-declaration form, and a certificate confirming that no financial assistance has been received from any other department or government for this purpose. - After submission, the department will conduct a verification. If the information is found correct, financial assistance for the entire academic session will be credited in one installment to the worker’s account. 
Mode: Online - via CSC.

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: Uttarakhand Building And Other Construction Workers’ Welfare Board; Nodal Department: Department Of Labour.
