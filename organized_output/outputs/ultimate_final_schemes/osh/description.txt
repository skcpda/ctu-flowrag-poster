# Operation of Sports Hostels

**Short Title:** OSH

**Scheme Brief Description:** The scheme aims to encourage tribal students to participate in sports and prepare them for national & international competitions. Tribal students from Classes 6 to 12 are provided with free accommodation, specialized training, nutritious meals & other necessary facilities in the sports hostels.

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Hostel, Scheduled Tribe, Sports Training, Sports, Tribal Students

The scheme &amp;quot;Operation of Sports Hostels&amp;quot; was introduced by the Tribal Area Development Department, Government of Rajasthan. It aims to encourage tribal students in Scheduled Areas to participate in sports and prepare them for national and international competitions. Under this scheme, tribal students from Class 6 to 12 are provided with free residential facilities, training in archery and athletics by skilled coaches, nutritious meals, uniforms, and modern sports equipment. The scheme is implemented in the following District: Banswara, Dungarpur, Sirohi, Udaipur &amp;amp; Pratapgarh.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Sports & Culture; Sector: Sports

**Where it applies:** Rajasthan; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** The following facilities are provided in the sports hostel: - Free residential accommodation in sports hostels. - Nutritious meals, snacks, and special dietary arrangements as per approved pattern. - Sports training with modern equipment by expert coaches (especially in archery and athletics). - Free school uniforms and educational materials.

**Benefit Type:** In Kind

**How to apply:** **Step 1:** The applicant should gather copies of the necessary documents needed to apply for the concerned scheme. **Step 2:** The applicant should visit the nearest E-Mitra Center and complete the Biometric Verification Process. **Step 3:** The applicant should ensure that the E-Mitra Center agent completes all mandatory fields in the application form and uploads copies of all required documents. **Step 4:** Keep the acknowledgment receipt for future reference.
Mode: Online - via CSC.

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Tribal Area Development Department.
