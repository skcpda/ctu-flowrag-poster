# Ex-gratia for Death for the Construction Workers

**Short Title:** EGFDFTCW

**Scheme Brief Description:** The "Ex-gratia for Death for the Construction Workers'' by the BOCW Welfare Board, Labour Department, U.T. Administration of DNH & DD, is a welfare scheme that provides financial assistance for death to the nominees/ dependents of a deceased registered member.

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Labour, Building Worker, Construction Worker, Death Assistance, Nominees, Dependents, Ex-gratia

The &amp;quot;Ex-gratia for Death for the Construction Workers&amp;quot; by the Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board, Labour Department, U.T. Administration of Dadra and Nagar Haveli and Daman and Diu, is a welfare scheme for the registered workers of the board. Under this scheme, the Board will provide financial assistance to the nominees/ dependents in case of death of a registered member in the course of employment or due to any other reason.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Financial assistance

**Where it applies:** Dadra & Nagar Haveli and Daman & Diu; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - **For death in the course of employment:** ₹4,00,000/-.- **For death due to any other reason:** ₹2,00,000/-.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**How to apply:** &gt; **Registration Process as a Building/ Construction Worker:** **Step 1:** Application for registration is to be made in the prescribed form and is to be accompanied by prescribed documents and a fee of not more than ₹50/-. **Step 2:** Application for registration has to be made to the officer authorized by the Board on this behalf. &gt; **Application Process of the Welfare Scheme:** **Step 1:** Any registered worker can make an application in the prescribed [Form-XI](https://www.daman.nic.in/websites/Printing-press-Daman/documents/2020/Extraordinary/Extra%20Ordinary%20No.%2029.pdf) (Refer Page No: 16) along with the mandatory documents (self attested, if required). **Step 2:** Application shall be submitted to the Member Secretary (BOCWWB) for this benefit. &gt; **Check Beneficiary Details:** Visit the [official website](https://bocwddd.in/welfare-schemes/dnh/payment-of-death-benefit) and enter the “BOCW Registration No.” of the concerned beneficiary in the Search box. **Helpdesk:** BOCW Department: EMAIL ID: labour-dnh@nic.in PHONE NUMBER : 0260 - 2642374 ADDRESS: Old Circuit House Building, Opposite Bal Bhawan, Silvassa, Dadra and Nagar Haveli Pin:- 396230
