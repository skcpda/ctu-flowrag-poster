# Annual Medical Assistance Scheme (BBOCWWB)

**Short Title:** AMAPBBOCWWB

**Scheme Brief Description:** The "Annual Medical Assistance Scheme" by the Bihar Building & Other Construction Workers Welfare Board, Labour Resources Department, Government of Bihar, provides a yearly financial assistance of ₹3,000/- to the construction workers who are registered with the Board.

**Scheme For:** Infra

**Tags:** Construction, Labour, Medical, Worker, Financial Assistance

The &quot;Annual Medical Assistance Scheme&quot; by the Bihar Building &amp; Other Construction Workers Welfare Board, Labour Resources Department, Government of Bihar, provides a yearly financial assistance of ₹3,000/- to the construction workers who are registered with the Board.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Skills & Employment; Sector: Welfare measures

**Where it applies:** Bihar; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** Financial Assistance of ₹3,000/- per annum.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**How to apply:** &gt; **Registration** **Step 1:** Visit the [Official Website](https://bocw.bihar.gov.in/Website/LabourReg.aspx) of the Bihar Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board, and click &amp;quot;[Apply for New Registration](https://bocw.bihar.gov.in/Registration/AddLabourRegistrationWeb.aspx)&amp;quot;. **Step 4:** On the next page, provide your details including your Aadhaar Number. Then click &amp;quot;Authenticate&amp;quot;. **Step 5:** An online form will appear. In the form, fill in all the mandatory fields. The fields marked with an asterisk (*) are mandatory. **Step 6:** Click &amp;quot;Submit&amp;quot;. Proceed to pay the application fee online. **Step 7:****** Upon successful payment, you will receive your construction worker registration number. &gt; **Application** **Step 1:****** Visit the [Official Website ](https://bocw.bihar.gov.in/Website/SchemeApply.aspx)of the Bihar Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board. **Step 2:** Click on &amp;quot;[Scheme Application](https://bocw.bihar.gov.in/Website/SchemeApply.aspx)&amp;quot; and select &amp;quot;[Apply for Scheme](https://bocw.bihar.gov.in/Registration/SchemeTaggingWeb.aspx)&amp;quot;. **Step 3:****** Enter your registration number and click &amp;quot;Show&amp;quot; to view your details. **Step 4:****** Select the scheme you intend to apply for, enter all mandatory information and upload the necessary documents in the specified format and size. **Step 6:****** Click on &amp;quot;Submit Application&amp;quot; to complete the process. &gt; **Application Tracking** Applicants can track their application status online on the [official website ](https://bocw.bihar.gov.in/Registration/ApplicationStatus.aspx)of the Board by providing their registration number.
Mode: Online.
