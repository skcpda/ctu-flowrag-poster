# Maternity Benefit For Female Workers- JKBOCWWB

**Short Title:** MBFFWJKBOCWWB

**Scheme Brief Description:** The "Maternity Benefit for Female Workers" scheme by the JKBOCW Welfare Board, Labour Department, Jammu & Kashmir, is a welfare program that provides maternity benefits to eligible registered female workers up to two times in their lifetime.

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Building Worker, Construction Worker, Labour, Maternity, Pregnancy Care, Women

The &quot;Maternity Benefit for Female Workers&quot; scheme by the Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board, Labour Department, Jammu &amp; Kashmir, provides financial assistance to registered female workers during maternity. Under this scheme, female beneficiaries are eligible to receive ₹5,000 (for up to two children), provided they have been engaged in building or construction work for at least 90 days in the preceding 12 months.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Financial assistance

**Where it applies:** Jammu and Kashmir; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** Under the scheme, maternity benefits for female workers amount to ₹5,000 for up to two children. Note: The benefit is allowed only twice in the beneficiary&#39;s lifetime. 

**Benefit Type:** Cash
