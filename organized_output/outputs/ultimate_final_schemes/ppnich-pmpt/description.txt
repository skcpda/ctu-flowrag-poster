# Promotion of Patriotism/National Integration/Communal Harmony - Production of Musical Programme or Tiatr

**Short Title:** PPNICH-PMPT

**Scheme Brief Description:** The scheme aims to project patriotism, nationalism, and communal harmony through tiatrs, songs and music. It provides remuneration and venue cost coverage to individuals, organizations, educational institutions and clubs.

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Song, Music, Program, Artist, Culture

The scheme &quot;Promotion of Patriotism/National Integration/Communal Harmony - Production of Musical Programme or Tiatr&quot; was launched by the Tiatr Academy under the Art, Culture and Museums Department, Government of Goa. The scheme aims to project patriotism, nationalism, and communal harmony through tiatrs, songs and music, addressing the need to promote these values through cultural performances and artistic expression. The scheme provides remuneration as fixed by Tiatr Academy of Goa to successful applicants and covers actual charges of hall/auditorium or pays a lump sum amount for performances held in makeshift pandals, along with video recording of the activity with one Digital Video Disc copy provided to the applicant. The applications for this scheme are accepted offline. 

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Sports & Culture; Sector: Culture

**Where it applies:** Goa; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - Remuneration as fixed by Tiatr Academy of Goa will be provided to the applicant. - Reimbursement of actual charges of the hall/auditorium or payment of lump sum amount as fixed by Tiatr Academy of Goa if performance is held in makeshift pandal. - Video recording of the activity/programme will be done by Tiatr Academy of Goa. - One copy of the Digital Video Disc will be provided to the applicant. - Maximum of 4 shows of the programme will be staged by Tiatr Academy of Goa at various places. - One banner will be provided by Tiatr Academy of Goa for compulsory display.  > ***Conditions*** - All shows must be shown to public free of charge. - Duration of the musical programme or tiatr should be not less than two hours. - In all advertisements, oral, print or electronic media, banners, the following must be inserted/published/announced: &#39;This programme is promoted by Tiatr Academy of Goa&#39;. - A few seats should be reserved by the organizer(s) for the representatives of Tiatr Academy of Goa. - The Tiatr Academy of Goa banner should be compulsorily displayed at one of the two front side wings of the stage and returned after all shows. - The organizing institution/individual will have to discuss with Tiatr Academy of Goa and decide about the Chief Guest or Guest of Honour well in advance. 

**Benefit Type:** Composite

**How to apply:** **Step 1:** The interested applicant should take a printout of the [prescribed format](https://www.goa.gov.in/wp-content/uploads/2021/06/Citizen-Charter-TAG.pdf) of the application form. **Step 2:** In the application form, fill in all the mandatory fields, attach copies of all the mandatory documents (self-attest, if required). Sign the application form and mention the date. **Step 3:** Submit the duly filled and signed application form along with the documents to - ***Tiatr Academy of Goa, AS-1 / AS-4, Block A, 2nd Floor, Campal Trade Centre, Opposite Kala Academy, Campal, Panaji, Goa, India – 403001*** _*Applicants are advised to save a copy of the submitted application form and any acknowledgment._ _*The scheme will be implemented on &#39;First Come First Serve&#39; basis._ _*Applications will be considered as per the availability of funds._  ##### ***Post-Application Processes*** **Step 1:****** ***Programme Coordination*** Upon approval, the organizing institution/individual will coordinate with Tiatr Academy of Goa to discuss and decide about the Chief Guest or Guest of Honour well in advance, with the President or Vice President of Tiatr Academy of Goa or nominee serving in this capacity. **Step 2:****** ***Banner Collection and Display*** The applicant must collect the Tiatr Academy of Goa banner from their office and ensure compulsory display at one of the two front side wings of the stage during performances. **Step 3:****** ***Performance Implementation*** The applicant must stage the approved programme ensuring all conditions are met including free public access, reservation of seats for Tiatr Academy of Goa representatives, and proper acknowledgment in all promotional materials. **Step 4:****** ***Post-Performance Requirements*** After completion of all shows, the applicant must return the Tiatr Academy of Goa banner to their office, and the payment cheque will be issued in the name of the applicant as appearing at Serial Number 1 of the application form. 
Mode: Offline.

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Art, Culture and Museums Department.
