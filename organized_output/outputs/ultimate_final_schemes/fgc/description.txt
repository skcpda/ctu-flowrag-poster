**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Financial Asisstance, Festival, Sainik, Ex-Serviceman, Widow

The scheme &quot;Festival Gift Coupons&quot; is a Welfare Scheme by the Armed Forces Flag Day Fund of the Department of Sainik Welfare, Puducherry. The objective of the scheme is to provide Festival Gift Coupons worth ₹4,000/- per annum to the Non-Pensioner ESMs who are 60 years or older in age. To coupons are to be collected in person on intimation from DSW, normally between November and January. This scheme applies only to the Ex-servicemen/Widows of Ex-servicemen registered with the Department of Sainik Welfare, Puducherry. The applications are accepted offline.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Financial assistance

**Where it applies:** Puducherry; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - Festival Gift Coupons worth ₹4,000/- per annum. *The coupons are to be collected in person on intimation from DSW, normally between November and January.

**Benefit Type:** Cash
