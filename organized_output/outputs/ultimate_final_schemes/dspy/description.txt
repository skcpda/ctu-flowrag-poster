# Divyang Shiksha Protsahan Yojana

**Short Title:** DSPY

**Scheme Brief Description:** Under this scheme, the Madhya Pradesh government provides financial support to Disabled children of the state.

**Launch Date:** 2016-01-01

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Education, Disabilities, Children, Financial

Divyang Shiksha Protsahan Yojana under the Department of Social Justice and Empowerment of Persons with Disabilities government of Madhya Pradesh, provides financial support to disabled children. The Divyang Shiksha Protsahan Yojana aims to promote and facilitate inclusive education for persons with disabilities (Divyangjan). This scheme aims to ensure that individuals with disabilities have equal access to quality education and educational opportunities  

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Pension

**Where it applies:** Madhya Pradesh; State/ UT

**DBT Scheme:** Yes (Direct Benefit Transfer)

**Benefits / Assistance:** > Financial assistance : ₹ 600/- per month.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**How to apply:** **Step 1:** Applicants can apply for the scheme using the prescribed format and submitting the necessary documents either to the designated officer&#39;s office, the Gram Panchayat, or a Public Service Center. **Step 2:** Upon submitting a complete application, the applicant will receive a mandatory acknowledgment from the office. **Step 3**: The District Panchayat/Gram Panchayat/Urban Body/Ward Office will scrutinize the documents submitted with the application. **Step 4**: If, after investigation, the documents are found to be incorrect according to the rules, the application will be rejected with written explanation. **Step 5**: If, after investigation, the documents are found to be correct, the pension case will be approved according to the rules. **Step 6:** Once the pension is approved, the beneficiary&#39;s name will be added to the pension proposal for the current month by the District Panchayat/Gram Panchayat/Urban Body/Ward Office. The approved order will be maintained on record. **Step 7**: The Directorate will deposit the monthly pension amount directly into the beneficiary&#39;s bank savings account through a single click, starting from the same month the approval is granted.
Mode: Offline.

**Additional Application Processes:** **Process 2:** **Step 1** Go to the [Samajik Suraksha portal.](https://socialsecurity.mp.gov.in/#) **Step 2**: Click on [Samajik Suraksha pension avm arthik sahayata yojanaen.](https://socialsecurity.mp.gov.in/Home.aspx) **Step 3:** Now click on the left on your screen Pension Yojana hetu online aavedan. **Step 4:** Fill in the required details and submit.

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Department of Social Justice and Empowerment of Persons with Disabilities.
