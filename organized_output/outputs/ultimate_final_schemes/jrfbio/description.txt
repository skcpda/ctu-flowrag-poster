# Junior Research Fellowship

**Short Title:** JRFBIO

**Scheme Brief Description:** The Department of Biotechnology, Ministry of Science & Technology, Government of India provides fellowships for pursuing research in biotechnology and life-sciences through the "DBT-Junior Research Fellowship" (DBT-JRF) Programme.

**Launch Date:** 2004-01-01

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Scheme Type:** Central Sector Scheme

**Tags:** Financial Assistance, Research Scientist, Lecturer, Professor

The Junior Research Fellowship (JRF) from the Department of Biotechnology (DBT) is a fellowship program that provides financial support to students who are pursuing doctoral research in biotechnology and life sciences. The JRF program is open to all Indian nationals who have a master&#39;s degree in biotechnology or a related field. 

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Education & Learning

**Where it applies:** Central

**DBT Scheme:** Yes (Direct Benefit Transfer)

**Benefits / Assistance:** > The fellowship amount for the JRF program is ₹31,000 per month for the first two years and ₹35,000 per month for the remaining three years.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**How to apply:** **Step 1: Qualify in the Biotechnology Eligibility Test (BET)** 1. The BET is a national-level entrance exam conducted by the Department of Biotechnology (DBT) to select candidates for the DBT-JRF program. The BET exam is held once a year, typically in the month of June. 1. To apply for the BET exam, applicant must have a master&#39;s degree in biotechnology or a related field from a recognized university. Applicant must also have a valid GATE score or a NET score in biotechnology or a related field.  **Step 2: Apply for the DBT-JRF program online** 1. Once applicant have qualified in the BET exam, applicant can apply for the DBT-JRF program online. The online application process typically opens in the month of July and closes in the month of August. 1. To apply for the DBT-JRF program online, applicant will need to create an account on the DBT website and fill in the required details. applicant will also need to upload the required documents, such as applicant&#39;s BET exam scorecard, research proposal, and NOC from the current employer (if applicable).  **Step 3: Appear for the interview** 1. Candidates who are shortlisted for the DBT-JRF program will be called for an interview. The interview will be held at a designated venue and will be conducted by a panel of experts. 1. The interview will be based on applicant&#39;s research proposal and applicant&#39;s overall knowledge of biotechnology and life sciences. Applicant will also be asked questions about applicant&#39;s research interests and career goals.  **Step 4: Check the final selection list** 1. The final selection list for the DBT-JRF program is usually announced within a few weeks of the interview. Applicant can check the final selection list online on the DBT website.
Mode: Online.

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Department Of Biotechnology; Nodal Ministry: Ministry Of Science And Technology.
