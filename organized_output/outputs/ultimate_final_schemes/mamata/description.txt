# Mamata Scheme

**Short Title:** MS-ODISHA

**Scheme Brief Description:** In order to improve the process in curbing the high rate of mortality and infant mortality rate, the Department of Women and Child Development, Government of Odisha introduced a scheme named "MAMATA" - a conditional cash transfer maternity benefit scheme.

**Launch Date:** 2011-01-03

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Pregnant Women And New Mothers, Financial Assistance, Nutritional Support, Maternity Benefit

In order to improve the process in curbing the high rate of mortality and infant mortality rate, the Department of Women and Child Development, Government of Odisha introduced a scheme named &amp;quot;**MAMATA**&amp;quot; - a conditional cash transfer maternity benefit scheme in 2011. **Aim of the scheme:** 1. Contribute in reducing the maternal and infant mortality. 1. Improve the health and nutrition status of pregnant and lactating mother and their infants. **Objectives of the scheme:** 1. To provide partial wage loss compensation for the pregnant and nursing mothers with the aim to provide complete rest before and after the child birth. 1. Increase in utilization of child health services especially immunization. 1. Improve mother and child care practices.

**Who is this for:** All; Category: Women and Child; Sector: Pregnancy Care

**Where it applies:** Odisha; State

**Benefits / Assistance:** - Rs.3,000 shall be given during the third trimester of the pregnancy of the mother till delivery on completing the following 5 conditions: 1. Pregnancy registered at AWC/Mini AWC 1. Received at least 2 antenatal checkup (ANC) 1. Received IFA and calcium tablets. 1. Received at least 1 TT vaccination (out of optimal 2) 1. Received at least 1 counselling session at AWC/Village Health/ Home Visit. The first 4 conditions shall be verified from MCP Card. On completing the above requirements first instalment shall be released. - Rs.2,000 shall be after completion of 10 months after delivery on fulfilment of following conditions: 1. Child birth is registered at AWC or HSC. 1. Child has received BCG vaccination. 1. Child has received Pentavalent 1 &amp; 2 &amp; 3 vaccinations. 1. Child has received Polio 1 &amp; 2 &amp; 3 vaccinations. 1. Child has received Rotavirus 1 &amp; 2 &amp; 3 vaccinations. 1. Child has received Measles vaccine before attaining 1 year of age. 1. Child has received Vitamin-A first dose before attaining 1 year of age 1. Mother has attended at least six Growth Monitoring and IYCF counselling sessions after delivery till 9 months 1. Child has been exclusively breastfed for first six months (self-certification by the beneficiary) 1. Child has been introduced to complementary food on completion of six months of age (self- certification by the beneficiary) 1. Child is being fed age-appropriate complementary feeding and continuing (self- certification by the beneficiary) **Note:** Overall, Rs.5,000 in two instalments as incentive is provided to mothers.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**How to apply:** 1. MAMATA mobile Application may be downloaded using URL: https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.emamata.odisha&amp;amp;hl=en_IN&amp;amp;pli=1 1. The beneficiary may also log in on the department portal to apply for the scheme. URL: https://emamata.odisha.gov.in/login 1. In case the beneficiary has forgotten the password, the same can be reset by visiting URL : https://emamata.odisha.gov.in/public/forgot/password
Mode: Online.

**Additional Application Processes:** **Process 2:** 1. The beneficiary is required to register at Anganwadi centers and mini Anganwadi centers to which the beneficiary belongs. 1. Fill the application form to apply for the scheme. &lt;br&gt;

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Women and Child Development Department.
