# Managerial Assistance Scheme for Appointment of Veterinarian

**Short Title:** MASAV

**Scheme Brief Description:** The "Managerial Assistance Scheme for Appointment of Veterinarian" provides financial support to registered Panjrapols for appointing a qualified veterinarian to ensure proper medical care, vaccination, and scientific record-keeping for animals housed in Gaushalas/ Panjrapols.

**Scheme For:** Infra

**Tags:** Agriculture, Animal Husbandry, Veterinarian

The &amp;quot;Managerial Assistance Scheme for Appointment of Veterinarian&amp;quot; launched by the Department of Agriculture, Farmers Welfare &amp;amp; Co-operation, Government of Gujarat, aims to encourage Panjrapols to maintain animal health by appointing veterinarians who will provide medical services, artificial insemination (AI), and vaccinations. The scheme also promotes scientific guidance and technical record maintenance. &lt;br&gt;

**Who is this for:** Registered Societies; Category: Agriculture,Rural & Environment; Sector: Animal husbandry

**Where it applies:** Gujarat; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:**  **Component** **Standard of Assistance****Minimum Time Limit (in Years) for Reclaiming Constituent Benefits**Managerial assistance to appoint VeterinarianThe Beneficiary Panjarapol Institute will pay ₹45,000/- per month to the appointed Veterinarian of which 75% will be paid to the Beneficiary Institute as a maximum of ₹33,750/- per month as subsidy.3 Year

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**How to apply:** **Step 1:** The applicant may visit and apply online at I-Khedut Portal: [https://ikhedut.gujarat.gov.in/](https://ikhedut.gujarat.gov.in/) **Step 2:** On the home page, click on ‘Schemes’ and then click on ‘Animal Husbandry Schemes’. **Step 3:** Now select the scheme, click on the ‘Apply’ button and an application page will open. **Step 4:** Click on &amp;quot;Apply New&amp;quot; button and submit a new application. **Step 5:** Click on the &amp;quot;Update Application&amp;quot; button to add corrections to the application. **Step 6:** Once the application is done, confirm it. **Step 7:** Take a print out of the confirmed application. **Step 8:** Applicant can also check their Application Status/View/Print/Upload Application details from the below link: [https://ikhedut.gujarat.gov.in/public/frm_Applicant_Corner.aspx](https://ikhedut.gujarat.gov.in/public/frm_Applicant_Corner.aspx) &gt; **Contact Us:** [Click here](https://ikhedut.gujarat.gov.in/Public/frm_Public_Contacts.aspx).
