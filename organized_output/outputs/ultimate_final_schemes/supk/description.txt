# Skill Upgradation Program Karnataka

**Short Title:** SUPK

**Scheme Brief Description:** The "Skill Upgradation Program Karnataka" scheme aims to upgrade the skills of artisans in leather technology, footwear/leather goods making, and designing by sending them for advanced training at renowned institutions like CFTI Chennai, CLRI, and Karnataka Institute of Leather Technology.

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Stipend, Skill Development, Training

The &quot;Skill Upgradation Program Karnataka&quot; scheme aims to upgrade the skills of artisans in leather technology, footwear/leather goods making, and designing by sending them for advanced training at renowned institutions like CFTI Chennai, CLRI, and Karnataka Institute of Leather Technology. The program enhances artisans’ skills, allowing them to contribute to the development of the leather industry through advanced training.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Skills & Employment; Sector: Training and Skill Up-gradation

**Where it applies:** Karnataka; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - **Course Sponsorship:** The corporation covers the entire cost of the course for selected artisans.- **Stipend:** A stipend is provided during the course duration to support the participants

**Benefit Type:** Composite

**How to apply:** > **Registration Process:** **Step 01:** Visit the official [Seva Sindhu Portal](https://sevasindhu.karnataka.gov.in/Sevasindhu/English). **Step 02:** Choose &quot;New Users Register Here&quot; and fill in your Aadhaar Number and the Captcha code. **Step 03:** An OTP will be sent to your Aadhaar-linked mobile number. Enter the OTP to continue. **Step 04:** Once your DigiLocker account is created, click &quot;Allow&quot; to proceed. **Step 05:** Fill out your email ID, mobile number, create a password, and enter the captcha, then click &quot;Submit&quot;. **Step 06:** You will receive OTPs on both your email and mobile number. Enter both OTPs and select &quot;Validate&quot;. **Step 07:** Once validated, a confirmation message will indicate your registration is successful.  > **Applying for a Scheme:** **Step 01:** Go to [Seva Sindhu Portal](https://sevasindhu.karnataka.gov.in/Sevasindhu/English) and select &quot;Department &amp; Services&quot;. **Step 02:** Select department &quot;Social Welfare Department&quot; and click on the scheme &quot;Skill Upgradation Program&quot; **Step 03:** Click &quot;Apply Online&quot; and enter your registered email ID/mobile number and the OTP. **Step 04:** Select the scheme and provide your personal, address, and banking details as required. **Step 05:** Once submitted, you will receive an Application ID. You can use this ID to track your application status on the portal.
Mode: Online.

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Social Welfare Department.
