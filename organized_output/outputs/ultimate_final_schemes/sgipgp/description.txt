# Scheme to Give Incentive-Prize to Gaushala/ Panjrapole

**Short Title:** SGIPGP

**Scheme Brief Description:** The "Scheme to Give Incentive-Prize to Gaushala/ Panjrapole" focuses on enhancing the management and operation of Gaushalas and Panjrapoles by offering incentive prizes to the top performers.

**Scheme For:** Infra

**Tags:** Agriculture, Animal Husbandry, Incentive

The &amp;quot;Scheme to Give Incentive-Prize to Gaushala/ Panjrapole&amp;quot; launched by the Department of Agriculture, Farmers Welfare &amp;amp; Co-operation, Government of Gujarat, aims to foster healthy competition among Gaushalas and Panjrapoles in the state. By encouraging better animal management practices, the scheme provides incentive prizes to organizations that rank first, second, and third in terms of their operation and management. The prizes are awarded once every three years.

**Who is this for:** Registered Societies; Category: Agriculture,Rural & Environment; Sector: Animal husbandry

**Where it applies:** Gujarat; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - First Rank: ₹1,50,000/- for the top-ranked Gaushala/Panjrapole. - Second Rank: ₹1,00,000/- for the second-ranked Gaushala/Panjrapole. - Third Rank: ₹50,000/- for the third-ranked Gaushala/Panjrapole. - Frequency: The incentive prizes are awarded once every 3 years.

**Benefit Type:** Cash
