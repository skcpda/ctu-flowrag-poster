# Economic Help To Tribal Girls For Higher Education (College Level)

**Short Title:** EHTTG

**Scheme Brief Description:** Rajasthan Government started a scholarship for tribal girls higher education (College Level) In the year 1994-95.

**Launch Date:** 1995-01-01

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Financial Assistance, Student, Higher Education, Educational Grant

In order to encourage tribal girl students of Rajasthan state to study in higher education and to promote girl education, financial assistance is provided to tribal girl students for higher education (college level).

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Education & Learning; Sector: Scholarships and student finance

**Where it applies:** Rajasthan; State

**Benefits / Assistance:** - Annual financial assistance of Rs. 5,000/- is provided for 10 months at the rate of Rs.500/- per month.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**How to apply:** 1. Applicant to log in to [RajSSO](https://sso.rajasthan.gov.in) Portal using his/her SSOID.1. If SSOID is not available then the applicant to first [register ](https://sso.rajasthan.gov.in/register)on RajSSO Portal to create SSOID and update SSO Profile at first login.1. After successful authentication/login and updation of the SSOID Profile and select the scheme name. 1. Select his/her name, the bank account and fill in personal details. 1. Upload the required document. 1. Click the “SUBMIT” button.
Mode: Online.

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Tribal Area Development Department.

**References / Annexures:** - **Guidelines**: https://schemes.rajasthan.gov.in/scheme/detail/451 - **Registration**: https://sso.rajasthan.gov.in/register
