# Funeral Assistance for the Construction Workers

**Short Title:** FACW

**Scheme Brief Description:** The "Funeral Assistance for the Construction Workers" by the Delhi Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board, Labour Department, Delhi, is a welfare scheme that provides funeral assistance of ₹10,000/- in case of death of a member to their nominees/dependents.

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Labour, Funeral Assistance, Construction Workers, Financial Assistance

The &amp;quot;Funeral Assistance for the Construction Workers&amp;quot; by the Delhi Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board, Labour Department, Delhi, is a welfare scheme for the registered workers of the board. Through this scheme, funeral assistance of ₹10,000/- is provided to the nominees/dependents of the member in case of his/her death from the date of joining membership of the fund.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Citizen empowerment

**Where it applies:** Delhi; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - Assistance of ₹10,000/- is provided to the nominees/dependents of the member in case of his/her death from the date of joining membership of the fund. 

**Benefit Type:** Cash
