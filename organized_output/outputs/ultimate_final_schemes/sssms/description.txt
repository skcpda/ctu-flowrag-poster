# Shridev Suman State Merit Scholarship

**Short Title:** SSSMS

**Scheme Brief Description:** The scheme provides monthly scholarship of ₹1,000/- to meritorious students who qualify in eligibility test conducted by SCERT Uttarakhand. The scholarship is awarded to the next 5 students from each development block of Uttarakhand who are not selected for the Dr. Shivanand Nautiyal Scholarship.

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Scholarship, Secondary Education, Student, Sridev Suman

The &amp;quot;Shridev Suman State Merit Scholarship&amp;quot; scheme is implemented by the Secondary Education Department under the Department of Education, Government of Uttarakhand. The scheme aims to provide financial assistance to meritorious students who qualify in the eligibility test conducted by SCERT Uttarakhand. The scholarship is awarded to the next five students from each development block of Uttarakhand who are not selected for the Dr. Shivanand Nautiyal Scholarship. 

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Education & Learning; Sector: Scholarships and student finance

**Where it applies:** Uttarakhand; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - On the basis of the declared results of the eligibility test conducted by SCERT Uttarakhand, the scholarship is awarded to the next five students from each of the 95 development blocks of Uttarakhand (95 × 5 = 475) who are not selected for the Dr. Shivanand Nautiyal Scholarship. - Each selected student receives ₹1,000/- per month for a duration of 10 months. 

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**How to apply:** - To appear in the scholarship examination, students can obtain the application form from the office of the Block Education Officer or download it from the School Education Department’s website ([https://schooleducation.uk.gov.in](https://schooleducation.uk.gov.in/)) or SCERT Uttarakhand’s website ([https://scert.uk.gov.in](https://scert.uk.gov.in/)) during September–October every year. A photocopy of the application form is also valid. No application or examination fee is charged. - While filling out the application form, students must attach a passport-size photograph, Aadhaar card, Class 7 mark sheet, caste certificate, and disability certificate (if applicable). All information must be filled in correctly, and the completed form should be submitted to the Principal of the school where the student is enrolled. - The Principal will verify the form based on the attached documents and school records, attest the photograph, and forward the verified form to the Block Education Officer’s office by the tentative date of 12 November. - After submission, the Block Education Officer issues the admit card. Students can collect the admit card either from their school or the Block Education Officer’s office. - The examination is conducted every year in the month of January. - After the successful completion of the process, the school Principal informs the student. The student must provide their Aadhaar card and Aadhaar-linked bank account details to the school, following which the scholarship amount is transferred directly to the student’s bank account. 
Mode: Offline.

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: Secondary Education Department, Government Of Uttarakhand; Nodal Department: Department Of Education.
