# Pension Scheme (HPBOCWWB)

**Short Title:** PSHPBOCWWB

**Scheme Brief Description:** The "Pension Scheme" by the Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board, Labour Department, Himachal Pradesh provides registered beneficiaries with a minimum pension of ₹1,000/- per month upon reaching 60 years of age.

**Scheme For:** Family

**Tags:** Building, Construction, Labour, Pension, Senior Citizen, Worker

The &quot;Pension Scheme,&quot; launched by the Himachal Pradesh Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board (HPBOCWWB) under the Government of Himachal Pradesh, provides financial support to registered construction workers. Once they reach the age of 60, beneficiaries are entitled to a monthly pension, with a guaranteed minimum amount of ₹1,000/-.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Pension

**Where it applies:** Himachal Pradesh; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** Pension amount ₹1,000/- per month.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**How to apply:** **The Registration Process for Building and Other Construction Workers is as follows:**  **Step 1:** The interested applicant should visit the Labor Welfare Office (during office hours) and request a hard copy of the prescribed application form from the concerned authority. **Step 2:** In the [application form](https://bocw.hp.nic.in/pdf/formno27_A1b.pdf), fill in all the mandatory fields, paste a passport-sized photograph (signed across, if required), and attach copies of all the mandatory documents (self-attested, if required). **Step 3:** Submit the duly filled and signed application form along with a wage slip or appointment letter from the employer, verifying 90 days of construction work. If unavailable, a certificate from a local authority (such as a councilor, executive officer, or Panchayat Secretary) can be submitted to verify employment. **Step 4:** After submitting the documents and payment, the worker will receive an acknowledgment receipt confirming their registration.  **NOTE 1:** Ensure that the application is submitted within the prescribed period, if any. **NOTE 2:** After three years, the worker must visit the office to pay a renewal fee and submit proof of 90 days of work each year to maintain eligibility for benefits. **NOTE 3:** A one-time registration fee must be paid at the office, and it will be valid for three years.
Mode: Offline.

**Additional Application Processes:** **Process 2:** **Registration Process for Building and Other Construction Workers:** **Step 1:** The applicant must visit the [official website](https://bocw.hp.nic.in/index.aspx) of the Himachal Pradesh Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board. **Step 2:** If you are a new user then click worker services , click on &quot;[New Registration](https://bocw.hp.nic.in/PublicPages/AddBeneficiary.aspx).&quot; **Step 3:** Once the dashboard appears, locate and complete the form by entering all required details. Mandatory fields are marked with an asterisk (*), ensuring that all essential information is provided. **Step 4:** Attach all necessary documents, including ID proof and any other relevant certificates, as specified. Ensure the documents are clear and meet the format requirements. **Step 5:** After completing the form and uploading the documents, click on the &quot;Submit&quot; button. This number serves as confirmation of your application and can be used for future reference.  **Scheme Application Process:** **Step 1:** The applicant must visit the [official website](https://bocw.hp.nic.in/index.aspx) of the Himachal Pradesh Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board. **Step 2:** Click on the option &quot;[Apply for Benefits.](https://bocw.hp.nic.in/PublicPages/ApplyForSchemes.aspx)&quot; **Step 3:** Enter your beneficiary details and date of birth, then click &quot;Search&quot; to view your information. **Step 4:** Select the scheme you wish to apply for to avail of the benefits. **Step 5:** Enter all required information and upload the necessary documents as prompted. **Step 6:** Finally, click on &quot;Submit Application&quot; to complete the process.  **Application Tracking:** Applicants can track their application status online through the[ official website](https://bocw.hp.nic.in/index.aspx) of the Himachal Pradesh Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board.
