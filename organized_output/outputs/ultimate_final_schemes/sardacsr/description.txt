# Scheme for Assistance for Research & Development Activities: Assistance for Contract/Sponsored Research Work

**Short Title:** SARDACSR

**Scheme Brief Description:** The "Scheme for Assistance for Research & Development Activities" under the Gujarat Industrial Policy promotes innovation by encouraging R&D among companies and institutions, fostering technological advancements to drive "Atmanirbhar Gujarat."

**Launch Date:** 2020-08-07

**Scheme For:** Infra

**Tags:** Contract Research Work, Sponsored Research Work

The &amp;quot;Scheme for Assistance for Research &amp;amp; Development Activities&amp;quot; is an umbrella scheme launched by the Industries and Mines Department, Gujarat under the Gujarat Industrial Policy. Effective from August 7th, 2020 to August 6th, 2025, this initiative promotes sustainable industrial growth by supporting industries in adopting eco-friendly practices and ensuring compliance with environmental norms. The component &amp;quot;Assistance for Contract/Sponsored Research Work&amp;quot; provides financial support to eligible institutions for establishing R&amp;amp;D or product development centers. It aims to promote innovation, technological advancements, and product design improvements.

**Who is this for:** University / Institution; Category: Business & Entrepreneurship; Sector: Setting up / start-up / entrepreneurship

**Where it applies:** Gujarat; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - **Financial Assistance:** Up to 50% of the project cost (excluding land and building costs). - **Maximum Funding:** A maximum of ₹50,00,000/- per project. - **Support for Research Development:** Facilitates technological advancements through sponsored research.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**How to apply:** **Step-1:** The R&amp;amp;D institution must apply to the Industries Commissionerate in the [prescribed format](https://ic.gujarat.gov.in/documents/commondoc/2023/2.%20CLAIM%20APPLICATION%20FORM%20SCHEME%202.pdf), along with all necessary [documents](https://ic.gujarat.gov.in/documents/commondoc/2023/1.%20CLAIM%20CHECKLIST%20SCHEME%202.pdf). **Step-2:** The office of the Industries Commissioner will scrutinize the proposals to ensure compliance with the scheme&amp;#39;s eligibility and guidelines. **Step-3:** The proposals will be presented to the State Level Empowered Committee (SLEC) for review and approval. **Step-4:** Upon approval by SLEC, the reimbursement of the sanctioned assistance will be processed, and the claim will be disbursed by the office of the Industries Commissioner.
Mode: Offline.

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Industries and Mines Department.
