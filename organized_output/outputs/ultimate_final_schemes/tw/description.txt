# Theatre Workshop

**Short Title:** TW

**Scheme Brief Description:** The objective of the scheme is to provide scientific knowledge of theatre to upcoming theatre artistes through an expert theatre personality. The workshops are organised on the written request of institutions working in the field of theatre and educational institutions for their students.

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Workshop, Theatre, Artist, Culture, Institution

The scheme &quot;Theatre Workshop&quot; is a state-level cultural scheme by the Kala Academy, Department of Art and Culture, Government of Goa. The objective of the scheme is to provide scientific knowledge of theatre to upcoming theatre artistes through an expert theatre personality. The workshops are organised on the written request of institutions working in the field of theatre and educational institutions for their students. Applications are invited by announcement in local print and electronic media. Certificates are given to the trainees.

**Who is this for:** Individual, University / Institution; Category: Sports & Culture; Sector: Culture

**Where it applies:** Goa; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - Honorarium T.A./D.A. is paid to the conductor of the workshop and the person assisting the conductor. - Certificates are given to the trainees.

**Benefit Type:** Composite

**How to apply:** - The applications for this scheme are invited by Kala Academy, Goa, by announcement in local print and electronic media. - The interested institutions must apply in the prescribed format provided in the local print/ electronic media. - The application must be submitted to the Member Secretary, Kala Academy, providing all the relevant particulars and details. - The application must be submitted within the stipulated date declared by Kala Academy every year. **Authority to Receive Applications:** Member Secretary, Kala Academy. **Authority for Redressal of Grievances:** Member Secretary/Chairman, Kala Academy, Goa.
Mode: Offline.

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Art, Culture and Museums Department.
