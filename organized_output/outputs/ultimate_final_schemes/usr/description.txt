# Urdu Scholarship- Rajasthan

**Short Title:** USR

**Scheme Brief Description:** The scheme aims to promote the Urdu language among female students at the undergraduate and postgraduate levels. Under this scheme, a monthly scholarship is provided to undergraduate and postgraduate students who are studying Urdu as an optional subject.

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Scholarship, Girl Student, Undergraduate, Postgraduate, Urdu

The &amp;quot;Urdu Scholarship&amp;quot; scheme was introduced by the Higher Education Department, Government of Rajasthan, aims to promote the Urdu language by providing financial assistance to students who study Urdu as an optional subject. At the graduation level, the scholarship is exclusively available to girl students who have secured at least 50% marks in their qualifying examination and are studying Urdu as an optional subject. At the postgraduate level, both male and female students are eligible, provided they have passed their graduation or post-graduation examination with at least a second division and have opted for Urdu as a subject. It is mandatory for all applicants to study Urdu as an optional subject to be eligible for this scholarship.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Education & Learning; Sector: Scholarships and student finance

**Where it applies:** Rajasthan; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - A monthly scholarship of ₹100/- for students studying Urdu at the undergraduate level. - A monthly scholarship of ₹150/- for students studying Urdu at the postgraduate level.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**How to apply:** ***Application Process:*** **Step 1:** The applicant should download the [application form](https://hte.rajasthan.gov.in/files/uploads/offline-forms.pdf) from the [official website](https://collegeeducation.rajasthan.gov.in/httpdocs/scholarships/sch_urdu_new.pdf). **Step 2:** In the application form, fill in all the mandatory fields and attach copies of all the mandatory documents (self-attest, if required). **Step 3:** Submit the duly filled and signed application form along with the documents to the related college. **Step 4:** Request a receipt or acknowledgment from the related college to whom the application has been submitted. Ensure that the receipt contains essential details such as the date and time of submission and a unique identification number (if applicable). **NOTE:** Incomplete or outdated applications will be rejected. ***Post Application Process:*** The college will forward the application to the Higher Education Department, Rajasthan. ***Renewal Process:*** - Only those students who were sanctioned a new scholarship in the previous year are eligible for renewal. ([Renewal Form](https://collegeeducation.rajasthan.gov.in/httpdocs/scholarships/sch_urdu_renew.pdf))- To qualify, they must have secured at least 50% marks and passed in second division in their last examination.
Mode: Offline.

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Higher Education Department.
