# Indira Gandhi National Vidhva Pension yojana

**Short Title:** IGNVPYRR

**Scheme Brief Description:** In the Indira Gandhi National Vidhva Pension Yojana (IGNWPS), financial support is provided to the widows of the state of Rajasthan.

**Launch Date:** 2009-10-07

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Pension, Women, Widow

The scheme “Indira Gandhi National Vidhva Pension Yojana (IGNWPS)” Started On 7-10-2009 under the Social Justice and Empowerment Department, Government. of Rajasthan. Under this scheme, financial support is provided to the widows of the state of Rajasthan. A widow whose household is below the poverty line is eligible to get the benefits of the pension scheme. The beneficiary receives a monthly pension amount consisting of the Central and State governments&amp;#39; share.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Banking,Financial Services and Insurance; Sector: Pension

**Where it applies:** Rajasthan; State/ UT

**DBT Scheme:** Yes (Direct Benefit Transfer)

**Benefits / Assistance:** - Women who are more than 40 to less than 75 years old monthly pension of ₹ 1000/-. - Women who are more than 75 years old monthly pension of ₹ 1500/-.

**Benefit Type:** Cash
