# MICRO FINANCE (Gopalak Nigam)

**Short Title:** MFGN

**Scheme Brief Description:** Micro-loan scheme for Rabari and Bharwad caste entrepreneurs in Gujarat. Provides up to ₹1.25 lakh loan at 5% interest with only 5% beneficiary contribution.

**Tags:** Microfinance, Rabari & Bharwad Caste, Small Businesses, Loan

This **microfinance scheme** aims to empower small entrepreneurs from the **Rabari and Bharwad castes** by offering **affordable loans** with minimal beneficiary contribution. Designed to support income-generating activities, the scheme helps beneficiaries fund small businesses with **95%** of the project cost covered by the corporation, while only **5%** is required as their own contribution. The maximum loan offered under the scheme is **₹1.25 lakh**, with a subsidized interest rate of **5% per annum**. The scheme is intended to improve economic participation and entrepreneurship among socially and educationally backward communities in Gujarat.

**Who is this for:** Self Help Groups (SHGS); Category: Business & Entrepreneurship; Sector: Setting up / start-up / entrepreneurship

**Where it applies:** Gujarat; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - **Loan assistance up to ₹1.25 lakh** for small businesses- **Interest rate fixed at 5% per annum** - **95% loan coverage** by the corporation- **Only 5% contribution** needed from the applicant- Promotes economic independence and small-scale entrepreneurship

**Benefit Type:** Composite

**How to apply:** **Step 1:** Visit the official [**DAADC**](https://daadconline.gujarat.gov.in/) online portal. **Step 2:** Register/log in using your credentials. **Step 3:** Fill out the application form with accurate personal and scheme-specific details. **Step 4:** Upload the required documents and submit.
Mode: Online.

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Social Justice and Empowerment Department.
