# Shramik Parivahan Yojana (GBOCWWB)

**Short Title:** SPY-GBOCWWB

**Scheme Brief Description:** The registered construction workers will be provided a bus pass through the Municipal Corporation at a concessional rate for commuting from Kadianaka to the workplace.

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Building Worker, Construction Worker, Labour, Bus Pass

The “Shramik Parivahan Yojana” is implemented by the Gujarat Building and Other Construction Worker’s Welfare Board (GBOCWWB), Labour, Skill Development &amp;amp; Employment Department, Government of Gujarat. Under the scheme, the registered construction workers will be provided a bus pass through the Municipal Corporation at a concessional rate for commuting from Kadianaka to the workplace.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Travel & Tourism

**Where it applies:** Gujarat; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** 1. Under the scheme, registered construction workers will receive a bus pass at a concessional rate for commuting from Kadianaka to the workplace. 1. Under the scheme, the beneficiary is required to contribute 20% of the pass amount, while the board covers the remaining 80%. **Note:** The pass shall be issued at a concessional rate monthly or quarterly or six-monthly or annually as per the demand of the registered construction worker.

**Benefit Type:** In Kind

**How to apply:** **Step 01:** The applicant needs to visit the nearest municipal corporation office. **Step 02:** Collect the application form and fill in all the details as required. **Step 03:** Attach all the required documents and submit it to the concerned official in the municipal corporation office. **Step 04:** After verification of the details and a bus pass may be issued to the applicant by the authority. **Note:** 4 municipalities of the state (Ahmedabad, Vadodara, Surat, and Rajkot) are included in this scheme.
Mode: Offline.

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: Gujarat Building And Other Construction Worker’s Welfare Board; Nodal Department: Labour and Employment Department.
