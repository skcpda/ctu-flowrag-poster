# Loktantra Senani Samman Pension Scheme for Widowed Wife or Widower Husband of Democracy Fighters of the State Imprisoned under MISA/DIR during Emergency Period (from 25.06.1975 to 21.03.1977)

**Short Title:** LSSPS

**Scheme Brief Description:** The scheme provides financial assistance in the form of a monthly pension of ₹20,000/- to the widowed wife or widower husband of democracy fighters of the state who were imprisoned under MISA/DIR during the Emergency period from 25.06.1975 to 21.03.1977.

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Pension, Emergency Period, Widow, Democracy Fighter, Widower

The scheme &amp;quot;Loktantra Senani Samman Pension Scheme&amp;quot; is implemented by the Police Department under the Department of Home, Government of Uttarakhand. The scheme aims to provide financial assistance in the form of a monthly pension to the widowed wife or widower husband of democracy fighters of the state who were imprisoned under MISA/DIR during the Emergency period from 25.06.1975 to 21.03.1977. 

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Pension

**Where it applies:** Uttarakhand; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** **Pension Amount:** ₹20,000/- per month 

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**How to apply:** - The application has to be submitted to the concerned District Magistrate. - Based on the report/recommendation of the District Magistrate, the pension will be sanctioned at the Government level after obtaining the approval of the Hon’ble Departmental Minister or the Hon’ble Chief Minister. - The prescribed application format is not available. As proof, the applicant must submit evidence that his/her late husband or wife was receiving the Loktantra Senani Pension, or that he/she was imprisoned under MISA/DIR during the Emergency period (from 25.06.1975 to 21.03.1977). 
Mode: Offline.

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: Police Department, Government Of Uttarakhand; Nodal Department: Department of Home.
