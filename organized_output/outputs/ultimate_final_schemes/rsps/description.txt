# Retired Sportsperson Pension Scheme

**Short Title:** RSPS

**Scheme Brief Description:** The scheme "Retired Sportsperson Pension Scheme" by the Sports, Youth and Cultural Activities Department, Government of Gujarat aims to ensure financial security to retired state-level medal-winning sportspersons aged above 50 years through monthly pension assistance.

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Sport, Retired Sportsperson, Pension, Athletes

The scheme &amp;quot;Retired Sportsperson Pension Scheme&amp;quot; by the Sports, Youth and Cultural Activities Department, Government of Gujarat aims to recognize and support the contributions of state-level sportspersons who have won medals in national-level sports events by offering them a financial pension after the age of 50 years. The scheme ensures dignified post-retirement sustenance for these individuals, thereby encouraging younger generations to pursue sports professionally. The scheme is implemented by the Sports Authority of Gujarat.   

**Who is this for:** Sportsperson; Category: Sports & Culture; Sector: Sports

**Where it applies:** Gujarat; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - **Financial Assistance:** A Pension of ₹3,000/- per month. 

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**How to apply:** **Step 1:** The applicant should download the prescribed format of the application form. **Step 2:** In the application form, provide all the mandatory details, and attach copies of all the mandatory documents (self-attested, if required). **Step 3:** Submit the duly filled and signed application form along with the supporting documents to the Commissioner of Youth Services and Cultural Activities Office. **Step 4:** Request a receipt or acknowledgement from the concerned authority to whom the application has been submitted. > **Commissioner, Youth Services and Cultural Activities Office,** > **Block No. 11, 3rd Floor, Dr. Jivraj Mehta Bhavan,** > **Gandhinagar, 382010.** 
Mode: Offline.
