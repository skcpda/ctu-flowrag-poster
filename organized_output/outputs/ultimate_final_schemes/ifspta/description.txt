# Assistance to Scheduled Castes for Fisheries

**Short Title:** ATSCF

**Scheme Brief Description:** This scheme has been introduced by the Agriculture, Farmers Welfare and Cooperation Department, Gujarat. It aims to enhance fish production and provide employment opportunities to Scheduled Caste (SC) individuals engaged in inland fisheries.

**Scheme For:** Infra

**Tags:** Agriculture, Farmer, Fishing Activities, Boat-net, Fish Seed

The scheme &amp;quot;Assistance to Scheduled Castes for Fisheries&amp;quot; introduced by the Agriculture, Farmers Welfare and Cooperation Department, Gujarat, is designed to boost inland fisheries by supporting Scheduled Caste fishermen and fish farmers. It provides assistance for fish seed rearing, stocking, and transportation, as well as financial aid for boats, nets, and fish sale equipment. Additionally, training programs with stipends are available to enhance skills and productivity in the fisheries sector.

**Who is this for:** Individual, Registered Societies, Self Help Groups (SHGS); Category: Agriculture,Rural & Environment; Sector: Fishing and hunting

**Where it applies:** Gujarat; State/ UT
