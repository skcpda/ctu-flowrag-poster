# Gaurav Puraskar Award

**Short Title:** GPAG

**Scheme Brief Description:** The scheme "Gaurav Puraskar Award" by the Sports, Youth and Cultural Activities Department, Government of Gujarat, aims to honour artistes for their significant achievements in music, dance, drama, and folk art by providing a cash award, trophy, and appreciation letter.

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Artist Recognition, Music, Dance, Drama

The scheme &quot;Gaurav Puraskar Award&quot; by the Sports, Youth and Cultural Activities Department, Government of Gujarat aims to recognize and encourage individuals who have demonstrated excellence in the fields of music, dance, drama, and folk art through a combination of financial and honorary benefits. The scheme confers a cash award of ₹1,000/-, a trophy, and a letter of appreciation to 12 deserving artistes every year. It also facilitates opportunities for awardees to contribute to state-sponsored cultural events and festivals in various expert capacities. The scheme is implemented by the Gujarat State Sangeet Natak Academy. 

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Sports & Culture; Sector: Culture

**Where it applies:** Gujarat; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** > **Cash Prize:** - Each awardee receives a cash prize of ₹1,000/-. > **Honorary Recognition:** - Awardees are presented with a trophy and a letter of appreciation. > **Post-Award Engagement:** - Awardees may be included as members in committees of the Academy. - Awardees can offer their services as adjudicators or experts in state cultural programs such as Rajya-level Navratri festivals, Kalamahakumbh, and other events. 

**Benefit Type:** Composite
