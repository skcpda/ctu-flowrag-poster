# CA/ICWA CMA /CS Scholarship

**Short Title:** CAICWACS

**Scheme Brief Description:** The scheme aims to provide scholarships to students from minority religious communities studying Chartered Accounts/Cost & Management Accounts/Company Secretaryship courses. Through this scheme, a scholarship is provided to eligible students.

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Chartered Account, Cost & Management Account, Company Secretaryship, Course

The scheme &quot;CA/ICWA CMA /CS Scholarship&quot; by the Kerala State Minority Welfare Department aims to provide scholarships to students from minority religious communities studying Chartered Accounts/Cost &amp; Management Accounts/Company Secretaryship courses. Through this scheme, a scholarship amount is provided to eligible students. The scheme is implemented by the Kerala State Minority Welfare Department. 

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Education & Learning; Sector: Scholarships and student finance

**Where it applies:** Kerala; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** 1. The scholarship amount of ₹15,000/- is provided. 1. The scholarship is provided to students from minority religious communities. 

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Exclusions / Not eligible:** - Applicants who have received this scholarship in previous years are not eligible to apply. 

**How to apply:** **Step 1:** Visit the [official website](https://www.minoritywelfare.kerala.gov.in) of the Directorate of Minority Welfare. **Step 2:** Locate and click on the &quot;Scholarship&quot;. **Step 3:** Navigate to the &quot;Scholarship&quot; menu link and click on &quot;CA/ICWA (CMA)/CS Scholarship (IWCS)&quot;. **Step 4:** Click on &quot;Apply Online&quot;. **Step 5:** For new scholarship applicants, first complete one-time registration and log in with your candidate details. **Step 6:** In the Registration Form, carefully enter the details step-by-step under Examination details (register number/roll number), Personal details, and Scholarship details tabs. **Step 7:** In the Upload details tab, upload the required documents (Photo, Signature, SSLC Certificate, Income Certificate, Ration Card Copy, Allotment Memo) in JPEG format under 100 KB size each. **Step 8:** Only after uploading all required documents, the registration process will be considered complete. **Step 9:** After completing the application submission, use the View/Print Application link to take a printout of the application form.  > **Submission of Application Printout** - After submitting the scholarship application (either through new registration or login), click on &quot;View/Print Application&quot; to get a printout of your registration form. - Submit the printout of the registration form along with all the required supporting documents to the head of the institution where you are studying, within the specified deadline.  
Mode: Online.

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Directorate of Minority Welfare.
