# Maintenance Grant for Children with Disabilities from Birth

**Short Title:** MGCDB

**Scheme Brief Description:** The scheme aims to provide financial assistance of ₹700/- per month to parents or guardians for the care of children below 18 years of age with more than 40% disability.

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Childcare, Grant, Differently Abled, PwD, BPL

The scheme “Maintenance Grant for Children with Disabilities from Birth” is implemented by the Department of Social Welfare, Government of Uttarakhand. It aims to provide financial assistance to parents or guardians for caring for their children below 18 years of age with more than 40% disability. 

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Women and Child; Sector: Childcare

**Where it applies:** Uttarakhand; State

**Benefits / Assistance:** ₹700/- per month is provided to parents or guardians for caring for their children. 

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**How to apply:** - The applicant can apply online either by themselves or through a Common Service Center (CSC) via the Department’s Pension Portal ([https://ssp.uk.gov.in](https://ssp.uk.gov.in)), the [UMANG](https://web.umang.gov.in/) Mobile App, or the Apuni Sarkar Portal ([https://eservices.uk.gov.in](https://eservices.uk.gov.in)). - The following documents are mandatory at the time of applying: Applicant’s mobile number, Passport-size photograph (attested by Gram Pradhan / V.P.D.O. / certified by Councillor), Photocopy of Aadhaar card and Voter ID card, CBS bank account details (linked/seeded with Aadhaar), Valid family income certificate or BPL certificate, Certificate of disability of more than 40%, child&amp;amp;#39;s birth certificate, Copy of family register (for rural areas only), Copy of the resolution passed in the open meeting of Gram Panchayat (for rural areas) / certified by Councillor (for urban areas) - All the above documents must be issued from the district where the application is being submitted. - For verification of the online application form in rural areas, the final decision is taken by the District Social Welfare Officer after recommendations from the Gram Panchayat Development Officer, Assistant Social Welfare Officer, and Block Development Officer, respectively. In urban areas, verification and recommendation are done by the Assistant Social Welfare Officer and Sub-District Magistrate. If the application is found correct, the District Social Welfare Officer approves it, after which the pension payment process is initiated. - But along with the above documents, a certificate of disability of more than 40% and a UID certificate from the Chief Medical Officer will also have to be given. - The pension amount will be credited to the parent&amp;amp;#39;s bank account within one month after the application is approved. 
Mode: Online.
