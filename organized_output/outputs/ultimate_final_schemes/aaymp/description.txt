# Aahar Anudan Yojana

**Short Title:** AAYMP

**Scheme Brief Description:** The scheme "Aahar Anudan Yojana" provides monthly assistance of ₹1,000 to students, girls, and women from backward tribes like Baiga, Bharia, and Saharia. The scheme aims to combat malnutrition among special tribal families by ensuring access to nutritious food through financial assistance.

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Women, Food, Financial Assistance, Child Care, Meal, Nutrition

The scheme &quot;Aahar Anudan Yojana&quot; by the Tribal Welfare and Scheduled Caste (SC) Department, Government of Madhya Pradesh, provides monthly assistance of ₹1,000 to students, girls, and women from backward tribes like Baiga, Bharia, and Saharia. The scheme aims to combat malnutrition among special tribal families in the state by ensuring access to nutritious food through financial assistance.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Citizen empowerment

**Where it applies:** Madhya Pradesh; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** Financial Assistance of ₹1,000/- per month.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**How to apply:** **Registration** **Step 1:** The applicant should visit the Madhya Pradesh Tribal Affairs &amp; Scheduled Caste Welfare Automation System [MPTAAS portal.](https://www.tribal.mp.gov.in/mptaas) **Step 2:** Click on &quot;New Beneficiary Profile [Registration](https://www.tribal.mp.gov.in/PMS/Registration/BeneficiaryRegistration/PersonalDetail).&quot; **Step 3:** Fill in all the required details. Fields marked with * are mandatory. Complete the form by entering all necessary information. **Step 4:** Upload all required documents, such as ID proof and other relevant certificates. **Step 5:** After completing the form and uploading the documents, click &quot;Submit&quot;. **Step 6:** You will receive a registration number after successful payment and submission.  **Application** **Step 1:** The applicant should visit the Madhya Pradesh Tribal Affairs &amp; Scheduled Caste Welfare Automation System [MPTAAS portal.](https://www.tribal.mp.gov.in/mptaas) **Step 2:** Enter your user ID, password, and captcha code, then click &quot;Login.&quot; **Step 3:** Choose the scheme you wish to apply for to avail the benefits. **Step 4:** Enter all required information and upload the necessary documents as prompted. **Step 5:** Finally, click on &quot;Submit Application&quot; to complete the process.
Mode: Online.

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Tribal Welfare and Scheduled Caste (SC) Department.
