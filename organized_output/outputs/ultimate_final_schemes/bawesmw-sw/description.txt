# Book Allowance to the Wards of Ex-Servicemen/Widows (Sainik Welfare)

**Short Title:** BAWESMW-SW

**Scheme Brief Description:** "Book Allowance to the Wards of Ex-Servicemen/Widows" is a Welfare Scheme by the Dept of Sainik Welfare, Puducherry. Through this scheme, the wards of the ESM / Widows of ESM studying in Government-recognized Institutions are provided allowances towards the purchase of books.

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Book, Allowance, Student, Ex-Serviceman, Widow

&quot;Book Allowance to the Wards of Ex-Servicemen/Widows (Sainik Welfare)&quot; is a Welfare Scheme by the Armed Forces Flag Day Fund of the Department of Sainik Welfare, Puducherry. Through this scheme, the wards of the ESM / Widows of ESM studying in Government-recognized Institutions are provided allowances towards the purchase of books. The application should be submitted offline within three months from the date of completion of the education.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Education & Learning; Sector: Scholarships and student finance

**Where it applies:** Puducherry; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** > **Book Allowance (in ₹ per annum) as per the Level of Education** - School / School for Special Children / Diploma in Polytechnic: 2,000 - Bachelor Degree: 3,000 - Master Degree: 4,000 - Professional Course (MBBS/BE/BTech) (Bachelor of Medicine and Bachelor of Surgery / Bachelor of Engineering / Bachelor of Technology): 4,000 _*The rate listed above or the actual cost of the fee, whichever is less, is reimbursed._ _*The amount, as decided by the Management Committee, is credited to the beneficiary’s Bank Account through RTGS/ NEFT._

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Exclusions / Not eligible:** - The scheme does not apply to the ward who is repeating the same class. - The Wards who are already in receipt of an academic scholarship are not eligible. - The Wards who are availing the benefit of reimbursement of tuition fees from other sources are not eligible.
