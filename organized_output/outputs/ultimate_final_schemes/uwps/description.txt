# Destitute Widow Pension Scheme - Uttarakhand

**Short Title:** DWPSUK

**Scheme Brief Description:** Under this scheme, a monthly pension of ₹1,500/- is provided to widows whose family income does not exceed ₹4,000/- per month or who belong to the BPL category, and who are not receiving any other pension benefits.

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Destitute, Widow, Pension, BPL

The &amp;amp;quot;Destitute Widow Pension Scheme&amp;amp;quot; was launched by the Department of Social Welfare, Government of Uttarakhand. The scheme aims to help destitute widows meet their basic needs and improve their quality of life. Under this scheme, a monthly pension is provided to widows whose family monthly income is up to ₹4,000/- or who belong to the BPL category, and who are not availing of any other pension benefits. 

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Pension

**Where it applies:** Uttarakhand; State

**Benefits / Assistance:** A pension of ₹1,500/- per month is provided to eligible beneficiaries. 

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**How to apply:** The applicant can apply online either by themselves or through a Common Service Center (CSC) via the Department’s Pension Portal ([https://ssp.uk.gov.in](https://ssp.uk.gov.in)), the [UMANG](https://web.umang.gov.in) Mobile App, or the Apuni Sarkar Portal ([https://eservices.uk.gov.in](https://eservices.uk.gov.in)). **The following documents are mandatory at the time of applying:** Applicant’s mobile number, Passport-size photograph (attested by Gram Pradhan / V.P.D.O. / certified by Councillor), Photocopy of Aadhaar card and Voter ID card, Husband&amp;#39;s Death Certificate, CBS bank account details (linked/seeded with Aadhaar), Valid family income certificate or BPL certificate, Copy of family register (for rural areas only), Copy of the resolution passed in the open meeting of Gram Panchayat (for rural areas) / certified by Councillor (for urban areas) **All the above documents must be issued from the district where the application is being submitted.** &gt; For verification of the online application form in rural areas, the final decision is taken by the District Social Welfare Officer after recommendations from the Gram Panchayat Development Officer, Assistant Social Welfare Officer, and Block Development Officer, respectively. In urban areas, verification and recommendation are done by the Assistant Social Welfare Officer and Sub-District Magistrate. If the application is found correct, the District Social Welfare Officer approves it, after which the pension payment process is initiated. **The pension is credited to the applicant’s bank account one month after the approval of the application.** 
Mode: Online.

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Department Of Social Welfare.
