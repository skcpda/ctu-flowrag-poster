# Solar Home System (Manohar Jyoti)

**Short Title:** SMS-MJ

**Scheme Brief Description:** The "Solar Home System (Manohar Jyoti)" by the Renewable Energy Department, Government of Haryana, aims to promote renewable energy adoption and ensure sustainable power access through solar solutions. Through this scheme, a subsidized solar home lighting system is provided to families.

**Scheme For:** Family

**Tags:** Solar Home System, Renewable Energy, Solar Subsidy

The scheme &amp;quot;Solar Home System (Manohar Jyoti)&amp;quot; by the Renewable Energy Department, Government of Haryana aims to provide reliable, clean, and affordable electricity to rural households and promote the use of renewable energy across the state. The scheme primarily benefits families with a valid Pariwar Pahichan Patra (PPP), offering them subsidized solar home lighting systems to reduce their dependence on conventional electricity sources and promote environmental sustainability. 

**Who is this for:** Family; Category: Agriculture,Rural & Environment; Sector: Mechanization- solar power, farming systems

**Where it applies:** Haryana; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - **Subsidy for General Category:** A subsidy of ₹15,000/- per solar home system is provided to eligible families under the general category. - **Subsidy under SCSP:** A subsidy covering 90% of the cost is provided to Scheduled Caste families under SCSP component.

**Benefit Type:** Cash
