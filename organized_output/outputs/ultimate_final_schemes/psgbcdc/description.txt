# Parivahan Scheme (GBCDC)

**Short Title:** PSGBCDC

**Scheme Brief Description:** The scheme aims to provide financial assistance for vehicle purchases in the transport sector for SEBC unemployed individuals. A loan is provided under the scheme.

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Transport, Self Employment, Vehicle, Loan

The scheme &quot;Parivahan Scheme (GBCDC)&quot; by the Social Justice and Empowerment Department, Government of Gujarat, aims to provide financial assistance for the purchase of vehicles in the transport sector to promote self-employment among the Socially and Educationally Backward Classes (SEBC). Through this scheme, a loan is provided to unemployed individuals for purchasing vehicles like auto-rickshaws, loading vehicles, etc. The scheme is implemented by the Gujarat Backward Classes Development Corporation (GBCDC). 

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Loan

**Where it applies:** Gujarat; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** 1. The applicant can avail a loan of up to ₹2,00,000/- for purchasing vehicles such as auto-rickshaws, loading vehicles, and other transport vehicles. 

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**How to apply:** **Step 1:** Visit the [official website](https://gbcdconline.gujarat.gov.in/) of the Gujarat Backwards Caste Development Corporation. **Step 2:** On the top menu bar, click on &quot;Apply Online&quot;. **Step 3:** If you’re a new user, click on &quot;New Registration&quot; Enter personal details (name, mobile number, email, aadhaar number, etc.), create a password, and complete OTP verification. **Step 4:** Use your registered credentials (User ID and password) to log in. **Step 5:** Click on “New Application”. Select “Parivahan Scheme” from the list of available schemes. **Step 6:** Fill out the Application Form, enter the following details: - Personal Information: Name, gender, date of birth, caste, etc. - Address Information: Permanent and correspondence address - Income Details: Monthly family income and source - Loan Requirement: Amount needed, proposed use (e.g., business, self-employment) - Bank Account Details: For loan disbursement **Step 7:** Upload scanned copies of the Caste certificate, Income certificate, Aadhaar card, Passport-size photo, Bank passbook (first page), and Residence proof. **Step 8:** Double-check all details. Click on “Preview” to verify. If all details are correct, click “Submit”. **Step 9:** You will receive an Application ID/Reference Number. Save or print the acknowledgement for future tracking. 
Mode: Online.

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: Gujarat Backward Classes Development Corporation (GBCDC); Nodal Department: Social Justice and Empowerment Department.
