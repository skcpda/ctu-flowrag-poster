# Dwarf Pension Scheme

**Short Title:** DPS

**Scheme Brief Description:** The scheme aims to provide financial support to individuals suffering from dwarfism to help them meet their basic needs and improve their quality of life. Under this scheme, a monthly pension of ₹1200/- is provided to persons above 21 years of age whose height is less than 4 feet.

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Dwarf, Pension, Bauna

The &amp;quot;Dwarf Pension Scheme&amp;quot; was launched by the Department of Social Welfare, Government of Uttarakhand. The scheme aims to provide financial support to individuals suffering from dwarfism to help them meet their basic needs and improve their quality of life. Under this scheme, a monthly pension is provided to persons above 21 years of age whose height is less than 4 feet. 

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Pension

**Where it applies:** Uttarakhand; State

**Benefits / Assistance:** A pension of ₹1,200/- per month is provided to eligible beneficiaries. 

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**How to apply:** The applicant can apply online either by themselves or through a Common Service Center (CSC) via the Department’s Pension Portal ([https://ssp.uk.gov.in](https://ssp.uk.gov.in)), the [UMANG](https://web.umang.gov.in) Mobile App, or the Apuni Sarkar Portal ([https://eservices.uk.gov.in](https://eservices.uk.gov.in)). **The following documents are mandatory at the time of applying:** Applicant’s mobile number, Passport-size photograph (attested by Gram Pradhan / V.P.D.O. / certified by Councillor), Photocopy of Aadhaar card and Voter ID card, CBS bank account details (linked/seeded with Aadhaar), Copy of family register (for rural areas only), Copy of the resolution passed in the open meeting of Gram Panchayat (for rural areas) / certified by Councillor (for urban areas) **In the case of dwarfism, a disability certificate and income certificate are not required. The Assistant Social Welfare Officer measures the height of the dwarf person.** **All the above documents must be issued from the district where the application is being submitted.** &amp;gt; For verification of the online application form in rural areas, the final decision is taken by the District Social Welfare Officer after recommendations from the Gram Panchayat Development Officer, Assistant Social Welfare Officer, and Block Development Officer, respectively. In urban areas, verification and recommendation are done by the Assistant Social Welfare Officer and Sub-District Magistrate. If the application is found correct, the District Social Welfare Officer approves it, after which the pension payment process is initiated. **The pension is credited to the applicant’s bank account one month after the approval of the application.** 
Mode: Online.

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Department Of Social Welfare.
