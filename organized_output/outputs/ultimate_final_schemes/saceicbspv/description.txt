# Scheme of Assistance for Common Environment Infrastructure: Common Boiler Project by SPV Constituted by Minimum 10 MSMEs

**Short Title:** SACEICBSPV

**Scheme Brief Description:** The scheme "Scheme of Assistance for Common Environment Infrastructure" under the Gujarat Industrial Policy aims to provide incentives for environmental compliance to ensure long-term economic and ecological balance.

**Launch Date:** 2020-08-07

**Scheme For:** Infra

**Tags:** Entrepreneurship

The scheme &amp;quot;Scheme of Assistance for Common Environment Infrastructure&amp;quot; is an umbrella scheme launched by the Industries and Mines Department, Gujarat under the Gujarat Industrial Policy. Effective from August 7th, 2020 to August 6th, 2025, it aims to boost industrial growth through financial assistance, modern technologies, and enhanced facilities for industrial parks and clusters. The component “Common Boiler Project by SPV Constituted by Minimum 10 MSME’s” aims to promote energy efficiency and cost reduction in MSMEs by setting up a Common Boiler Project. A Special Purpose Vehicle (SPV) consisting of at least 10 MSMEs using steam in their process can apply. The project encourages the use of energy-efficient boilers that consume less fuel while maintaining energy output.

**Who is this for:** Business Entity, Industries; Category: Business & Entrepreneurship; Sector: Setting up / start-up / entrepreneurship

**Where it applies:** Gujarat; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - **For Solid Fuel Boilers:** 35% of the total project cost, with a maximum cap of ₹2,00,00,000/-. - **For Cleaner Fuel Boilers (CNG, PNG, Biofuel, etc.):** 50% of the total project cost, with a maximum cap of ₹2,00,00,000/-.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**How to apply:** **Step 1:** Prepare a Detailed Project Report (DPR). **Step 2:** Fill out the [prescribed application form](https://ic.gujarat.gov.in/documents/commondoc/2023/Scheme-1_Application-form_CEIS-IP-2020.pdf). **Step 3:** Submit the application with the required documents to the [Industries Commissionerate](https://ic.gujarat.gov.in/ic-contact.aspx). **Step 4:** Await evaluation and approval from the State Level Empowered Committee. If approved, assistance will be disbursed in phases based on project progress. &amp;gt; **Helpdesk****:** [Contact Here](https://ifp.gujarat.gov.in/DIGIGOV/IFP-pages/contact.jsp?pagedisp=static)
