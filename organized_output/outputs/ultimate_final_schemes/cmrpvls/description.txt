# Chief Minister Rural Path Vendor Loan Scheme

**Short Title:** CMRPVLS

**Scheme Brief Description:** "Chief Minister Rural Path Vendor Loan Scheme" by the Panchayat and Rural Development Department, Government of Madhya Pradesh provides working capital assistance of up to ₹10,000, promotes regular loan repayment, and trains entrepreneurs in business practices. The scheme targets small traders.

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Loan, Trader, Business, Financial Assistance, Vendor, Rural

&quot;Chief Minister Rural Path Vendor Loan Scheme&quot; by the Panchayat and Rural Development Department, Government of Madhya Pradesh provides working capital assistance of up to ₹10,000, promotes regular loan repayment, and trains entrepreneurs in business practices. Street vendors (rehri wala, cycle wala, thela wala, etc) are an integral part of the rural informal economy. They provide various kinds of goods and services to the rural people at their homes at reasonable prices. They sell items like ice cream, fruits, samosa/kachori, bread-biscuits, chicken &amp; eggs, clothes, small utensils, shoes &amp; slippers, brooms, etc. They provide services like barbering, tool repair, etc. in rural areas.  Low-cost equipment or working capital is made available to poor migrant workers/ small traders of rural areas of the state in the form of loans through banks. Entrepreneurship Development (EDP) training is to be provided to rural migrant workers through the &quot;Self Employment Training Institute (RSET)&quot; for setting up new businesses. After training, the loan is provided by banks for setting up the business.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Business & Entrepreneurship; Sector: Loan

**Where it applies:** Madhya Pradesh; State/ UT

**DBT Scheme:** Yes (Direct Benefit Transfer)

**Benefits / Assistance:** - Low-cost equipment or working capital is made available to poor migrant workers/ small traders of rural areas of the state in the form of loans through banks. - Entrepreneurship Development Training is to be provided to rural migrant workers through the &quot;Self Employment Training Institute (RSET)&quot; for setting up new businesses. - After training, an **Interest-free loan of ₹10,000/-** is provided by banks for setting up the business.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**How to apply:** **Step 1:** Visit the [**Street Vendor Portal**](https://kamgarsetu.mp.gov.in/) and click &quot;Register&quot;. **Step 2:** Verify your mobile number via OTP. Then select the &quot;Rural Path Vendor&quot; option. **Step 3:** Verify your Aadhaar number by providing the OTP received on your Aadhaar-linked mobile number. (If your mobile number is not linked to Aadhaar, visit a kiosk for biometric verification). You will be taken to the online application page. **Step 4:** In the online application form, fill in all the mandatory fields and upload all the mandatory documents in the specified format and size. Carefully review all the information. Make any necessary corrections. **Step 5:** Click the &quot;Submit&quot; or &quot;Apply&quot; button to submit your application. You&#39;ll receive a confirmation message on SMS.
Mode: Online.

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Panchayat and Rural Development Department.
