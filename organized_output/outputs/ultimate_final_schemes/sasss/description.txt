# Sanction of Adult Sticks to Senior Citizens

**Short Title:** SASSS

**Scheme Brief Description:** The "Sanction of Adult Sticks to Senior Citizens" scheme aims to provide walking sticks to senior citizens. This initiative ensures that eligible senior citizens receive the necessary support to enhance their mobility and independence.

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Social Welfare, Empowerment, Walking Sticks

The &quot;Sanction of Adult Sticks to Senior Citizens&quot; scheme aims to provide walking sticks to senior citizens. The A.P. Differently Abled &amp; Senior Citizens Assistance Corporation, Andhra Pradesh, under its Action Plan 2017-18. The walking sticks are distributed by the Assistant Directors, Department for the Welfare of Differently Abled &amp; Senior Citizens, and District Managers, A.P. Differently Abled &amp; Senior Citizens Assistance Corporation.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Aids/Appliances

**Where it applies:** Andhra Pradesh; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - Eligible beneficiaries of this scheme will be provided with a DAISY Player to facilitate accessible learning and information access. 

**Benefit Type:** In Kind

**Exclusions / Not eligible:**  

**How to apply:** **Step 01:** Visit the official website: [https://apdascac.ap.gov.in](https://apdascac.ap.gov.in) **Step 02:** On the homepage, click on &quot;[APDASCAC Schemes](https://apdascac.ap.gov.in/APDASCAC-Schemes/)&quot;. **Step 03:** Select the &quot;SANCTION OF ADULT STICKS&quot; option. **Step 04:** Click on &quot;[Apply Now](https://apdascac.ap.gov.in/Forms/adult-sticks/)&quot; to begin the application process. **Step 05:** Fill in the required details and submit your application. 
Mode: Online.
