# Mukhyamantri Atyant Pichhda Varg Medhavriti Yojana

**Short Title:** MAPVMY

**Scheme Brief Description:** The scheme aims to provide ₹10,000/- as a one-time scholarship to extremely backward class students passing class 10th in the first division from the Bihar School Examination Board.

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Financial Assistance, Student, Extremely Backward Class, Matric

The &amp;quot;Mukhyamantri Atyant Pichhda Varg Medhavriti Yojana&amp;quot; was launched by the Backward and Extremely Backward Class Welfare Department, Government of Bihar. The scheme aims to encourage academic excellence among students belonging to extremely backward classes. A one-time financial incentive is provided to students who have passed the 10th class (Matric) examination in the first division from the Bihar School Examination Board (BSEB).

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Education & Learning; Sector: Education and training grants, fellowship, stipend

**Where it applies:** Bihar; State/ UT

**DBT Scheme:** Yes (Direct Benefit Transfer)

**Benefits / Assistance:** - A one-time scholarship of ₹10,000/- is provided per student. **Note:** Payment will be made via Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) into the beneficiary’s bank account.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**How to apply:** ***Application Process:*** - The eligible students may contact their respective schools and ensure the availability of required documents such as a caste certificate, mark sheet, Aadhaar card, etc. - The Bihar School Examination Board provides the list of eligible students to the Education Department. - Documents and identity of eligible students are verified by the concerned authorities. - The authorized officer of the Education Department verifies and approves the disbursement.
Mode: Offline.

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: Education Department; Nodal Department: BC & EBC Welfare Department.
