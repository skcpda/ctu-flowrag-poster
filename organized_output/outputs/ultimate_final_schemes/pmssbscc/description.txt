# Pre Matric Scholarship for Students belonging to Scheduled Caste Community

**Short Title:** PMSSBSCC

**Scheme Brief Description:** The scheme is to provide financial assistance to students belonging to the Scheduled caste community pursuing Post-Matriculation recognized courses in recognized institutions.

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Mizoram, Scholarship, Scheduled Caste, Education, School

The scheme &quot;Pre Matric Scholarship for Students Belonging to the Scheduled Caste Community&quot; was launched by the Mizoram Scholarship Board, Government of Mizoram. The scheme aims to support parents from the Scheduled Caste community in the education of their children studying in classes 9th and 10th so that the incidence of drop-out, especially in the transition from the elementary to the secondary stage, is minimized. The scheme also aims to improve participation these children in the pre-matric stage of education so that they perform better and have a better chance of progressing to the post-matric stage of education. 

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Education & Learning; Sector: Scholarships and student finance

**Where it applies:** Mizoram; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** Financial assistance is provided to the students. _*The scheme is funded by the Ministry of Tribal Affairs and the Government of Mizoram in 90:10 ratio._ 

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**How to apply:** - Normally application is open during July- September through offline where application form is available in Mizoram Scholarship Board during Office working hours. - Application Form duly filled-in along with documents mentioned above must be submitted to the following address - ***Mizoram Scholarship Board, Kaisara Rd, Aizawl, Mizoram 796001*** 
Mode: Offline.

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Mizoram Scholarship Board.
