# Scholarship for Poor Students

**Short Title:** SFPS

**Scheme Brief Description:** The scheme aims to support poor students in sanskrit education. One student per class receives a scholarship equal to the class tuition fee.

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Financial Assistance, Student, Tuition Fees, Sanskrit

The &quot;Scholarship for Needy Students&quot; scheme was launched by the Department of Sanskrit Education, Government of Uttarakhand. It aims to support economically disadvantaged students enrolled in sanskrit education by covering their tuition fees. Under this scheme, one student (either male or female) from each undergraduate and postgraduate class at Uttarakhand Sanskrit University receives a scholarship equivalent to the tuition fee of their respective class. The scheme is implemented by the Scholarship Cell at Uttarakhand Sanskrit University.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Education & Learning; Sector: Scholarships and student finance

**Where it applies:** Uttarakhand; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - The selected student from each undergraduate and postgraduate class receives a scholarship amount equal to the tuition fee of the respective class. > **NOTE:** The scholarship amount is transferred directly to the beneficiary’s bank account.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**How to apply:** > **Application Process:** **A Scholarship Cell is constituted at the university level to manage this scheme.** **Step 1:** The interested applicant should visit (during office hours) the scholarship cell and request a hard copy of the prescribed format of the application form from the staff exclusively entrusted to issue and collect filled-in applications. **Step 2:** In the application form, fill in all the mandatory fields, and attach copies of all the mandatory documents (self-attested, if required). **Step 3:** Submit the duly filled and signed application form along with the documents to the Scholarship Cell.  > **Post-Application Process:** **Step 1:** The Scholarship Cell scrutinizes all applications and verifies income certificates to identify the student with the lowest annual income in each class. **Step 2:** After administrative and financial approval, the scholarship amount is credited directly to the selected students&#39; bank accounts.
Mode: Offline.

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Department Of Sanskrit Education.
