# Financial Support Scheme

**Short Title:** FSSDU

**Scheme Brief Description:** With a view to extend the benefits of equity and access to quality education to the economically weak students, the University has introduced a financial support scheme for such students which will include fee waiver of all components of fee paid by students (except examination fee & hostel fee).

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Scheme Type:** Central Sector Scheme

**Tags:** Fee Waiver, Financial Assistance, Delhi University, EWS, OBC

“Financial Support Scheme” implemented by the Delhi University aims to provide equity and access to quality education to the economically weak students. This scheme also provides financial support for such students which will include fee waiver of all components of fee paid by students (except examination fee &amp;amp; hostel fee). 

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Education & Learning; Sector: Scholarships and student finance

**Where it applies:** Central

**Benefits / Assistance:**  **S. No.****Family income (in ₹) per annum****Fee waiver (percentage)****Remarks**Category 1Less than or equal to ₹ 4,00,000/Up to 100%Actual fee or up to a maximum of ₹ 10,000/-Category 2Between ₹ 4,00,000/- and ₹ 8,00,000/-Up to 50%Actual fee or up to a maximum of ₹ 8,000/-

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Exclusions / Not eligible:** - Students with Essential Repeat papers are not eligible for the fee waiver. - Students who have availed fee waiver during admission 2023-24 (B.Tech/Five-Year Integrated Law Programs) will not be eligible for this scheme.
