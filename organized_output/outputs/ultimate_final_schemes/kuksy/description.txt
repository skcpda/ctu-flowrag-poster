#  Krishak Utpadak Kshati Sahayata Yojana

**Short Title:** KUKSY

**Scheme Brief Description:** Provides compensation to farmers in Uttarakhand’s notified market areas for losses due to fire, floods, excessive rainfall, and soil erosion affecting crops, property, livestock, and cultivable land, ensuring direct benefit transfer to their bank accounts.

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Agriculture, Financial Assistance, Farmers, Natural Calamities

In the notified market areas of Uttarakhand, Krishak Utpadak Kshati Sahayata Yojana by the Agriculture Department of Uttarakhand provides compensation to farmers for losses due to fire, floods, excessive rainfall, and soil erosion. The compensation amount is approved after investigation and is transferred directly to the farmer’s bank account via DBT. 

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Agriculture,Rural & Environment; Sector: Agricultural Inputs- seeds, fertilizer etc.

**Where it applies:** Uttarakhand; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** **Compensation for:** 1. Damage caused by fire accidents to crops ready for harvest in the field, 1. Harvested crops are stored in the field or threshing yards, 1. Residual parts of crops, 1. Damage caused by fire in the farmer’s house affects property and livestock, 1. Damage to cultivable land due to floods or excessive rainfall, 1. And soil erosion. > Direct transfer of approved compensation amount to farmer’s bank account via Bank Draft. 

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**How to apply:** 1. Submit the prescribed application form (available free of cost at the Mandi office) within 7 days of the incident to the Mandi Secretary/Chairperson/Administrator or the area’s Tehsildar. 1. The Mandi Secretary will investigate and verify the claim within one week. 1. The verified report is submitted to the Mandi Committee, chaired by the Chairman/Administrator. 1. The Committee decides on the claim within 3 days in its meeting and forwards it to the Mandi Council office. 1. The Mandi Council verifies and approves the amount. 1. The approved amount is sent via DBT to the farmer’s bank account within a week of approval. 1. The farmer/producer is notified about the claim and transfer. 
Mode: Offline.

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Agriculture Department.
