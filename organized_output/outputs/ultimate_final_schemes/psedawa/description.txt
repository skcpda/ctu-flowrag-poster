# Pension Scheme for Elderly and Destitute Artists/Writers/Authors of Uttarakhand State

**Short Title:** PSEDAWA

**Scheme Brief Description:** The scheme aims to financially support senior contributors in the arts and literature. It provides a monthly pension to eligible beneficiaries.

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Pension, Financial Assistance, Artists, Writer, Authors

The scheme &quot;Pension Scheme for Elderly and Destitute Artists/Writers/Authors of Uttarakhand State&quot; by the Department of Culture and Endowments, Government of Uttarakhand, aims to provide financial assistance to veteran contributors in the field of art, literature, and writing. Through this scheme, a monthly pension of ₹3,000/- is provided to elderly and destitute artists, writers, and authors who have dedicated their lives to these creative fields.

**Who is this for:** Artists, Individual; Category: Sports & Culture; Sector: Culture

**Where it applies:** Uttarakhand; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** 1. A monthly pension of ₹3,000/- is granted to the beneficiary. 1. The pension is intended to support the livelihood of elderly and destitute artists, writers, and authors who have contributed significantly to their field.  > **Mode of Disbursement:** - The amount is directly transferred to the bank account of the beneficiary. > **Frequency of Disbursement:** - Monthly

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**How to apply:** &gt; **Registration Process:** **Step 1:** Visit [Apuni Sarkar](https://eservices.uk.gov.in/) and click on the “Sign Up Here” button. **Step 2:** Fill in the required details, i.e. Email ID, Gender, Name, Mobile Number, Date of Birth, District, Tehsil, Address of Applicant, and Notifications Language Preference. **Step 3:** Click on “Submit”. &lt;br&gt; &gt; **Post-Registration Process:** **Step 1:** Visit [Apuni Sarkar](https://eservices.uk.gov.in/) portal. **Step 2:** In the “Citizen Login” window, enter your phone number/mail id, correct password and captcha code. **Step 3:** Click on the “Sign In” button. **Step 4:** From the left side menu button click on “DBT Services”, then choose the &amp;quot;Department of Culture.&amp;quot; **Step 5:** Now, click on the scheme “Pension Scheme for Elderly and Destitute Artists/Writers/Authors of Uttarakhand State”. **Step 6:** Enter the mobile number and verify with the OTP. **Step 7:** Click on the checkbox for WhatsApp Verification (for receiving updates regarding your application via WhatsApp). **Step 8:** Now upload all the mandatory documents and click on “Submit”. **Step 8:** The application can be tracked from the “Profile” section.
Mode: Online.

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: Department Of Culture And Endowments; Nodal Department: Department of Culture and Endowments.
