# Promotion of Tiatr Through Academic Activities

**Short Title:** PTTAA

**Scheme Brief Description:** The scheme aims to foster interest in Tiatr among students and enthusiasts through academic activities like talks, symposia, seminars, and workshops. It covers activity expenditures, honorarium for resource persons, and provides materials, benefiting educational institutions and individuals.

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Student, Academic, Workshop, Culture, Awareness, Seminar, Grant

The scheme &quot;Promotion of Tiatr Through Academic Activities&quot; was launched by the Tiatr Academy under the Art, Culture and Museums Department, Government of Goa. The scheme aims to create interest in Tiatr among students of educational institutions and tiatr lovers in villages and cities. The scheme provides for the creation of awareness about tiatr, its song, and music through academic activities such as talks, symposia, seminars, and workshops. These activities, which must have at least 20 participants, can be organized by the Tiatr Academy of Goa alone or in collaboration with other institutions or individuals, with the Academy covering various expenditures and providing necessary materials. The scheme is implemented by the Tiatr Academy of Goa on a &#39;First Come First Serve&#39; basis, and applications for this scheme are accepted as per the availability of funds. 

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Education & Learning; Sector: Scholarships and student finance

**Where it applies:** Goa; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** • **Awareness Creation:** Awareness about Tiatr, its song, and music will be created through academic activities such as talks, symposia, seminars, and workshops. • **Resource Person Honorarium:** Resource persons will be paid an honorarium, as fixed by the Tiatr Academy of Goa, for every session, with the amount dependent upon the distance from their residence to the venue. • **Expenditure Coverage:** The Tiatr Academy of Goa will cover the expenditure incurred on Resource Persons, refreshments, and food (lunch or dinner) served to participants. Additionally, any other miscellaneous expenditure made in organizing the activity by an institution or individual by way of collaboration will be taken care of by the Tiatr Academy of Goa. • **Provision of Materials:** The Tiatr Academy of Goa will provide essential materials, including a backdrop, certificates for the participants, and stationery. • **Documentation Coverage:** The Tiatr Academy of Goa will document the Symposia, Seminar, or Workshop, and the expenditure incurred on this documentation will be borne by the Tiatr Academy of Goa.  > **Conditions** _*Prior approval will be required from the Tiatr Academy of Goa before finalizing the resource persons, if the activity is organized in collaboration with others._ _*The cost of refreshments or food to be served must be verified and approved by the Tiatr Academy of Goa before finalization._ _*The scheme will be implemented strictly on a ‘First Come First Serve’ basis._ _*Applications will be considered, and benefits disbursed as per the availability of funds._ 

**Benefit Type:** In Kind

**How to apply:** **Step 1:** The interested applicant should take a printout of the [prescribed format](https://www.goa.gov.in/wp-content/uploads/2021/06/Citizen-Charter-TAG.pdf) of the application form. **Step 2:** In the application form, fill in all the mandatory fields, and attach copies of all the mandatory documents (self-attest, if required). Sign the application form and mention the date. **Step 3:** Submit the duly filled and signed application form along with the copies of the mandatory documents to - ***Tiatr Academy of Goa, AS-1 / AS-4, Block A, 2nd Floor, Campal Trade Centre, Opposite Kala Academy, Campal, Panaji, Goa, India – 403001*** _*Applicants are advised to save a copy of the submitted application form and any acknowledgment._ *The application will be considered on a &#39;First Come First Serve&#39; basis and is subject to the availability of funds. 
Mode: Offline.
