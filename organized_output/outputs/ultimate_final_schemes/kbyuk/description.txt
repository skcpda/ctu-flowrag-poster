# Kishori Balika Yojana- Uttarakhand

**Short Title:** KBYUK

**Scheme Brief Description:** The scheme aims to create awareness among adolescent girls aged 14 to 18 years about nutrition, health, and life cycle issues, and also provides supplementary nutrition for 300 days in a year.

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Adolescent Girls, Awareness On Nutrition, Awareness On Health, Teenage Girl

The scheme &amp;quot;Kishori Balika Yojana&amp;quot; is implemented by the Department of Women Empowerment &amp;amp; Child Development, Government of Uttarakhand. The scheme aims to create awareness among adolescent girls aged 14 to 18 years about nutrition, health, and life cycle issues, and also provides supplementary nutrition for 300 days in a year. 

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Women and Child; Sector: Health promotion

**Where it applies:** Uttarakhand; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** Under this scheme, awareness is created among adolescent girls about nutrition, health, and life cycle issues. They are also provided supplementary nutrition (fortified rice and wheat for 25 days each month) for 300 days in a year. 

**Benefit Type:** In Kind

**How to apply:** Registration of beneficiaries is carried out through the [Poshan Tracker App](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.poshantracker&amp;pli=1), for which the Aadhaar card and birth certificate of the adolescent girl are required. 
Mode: Offline.

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Department Of Woman Empowerment & Child Development.
