# Term Loan Scheme (GMFDC)

**Short Title:** TLSGMFDC

**Scheme Brief Description:** The scheme aims to promote self-employment among individuals from the minority communities by providing financial assistance for agriculture and allied activities, small-scale businesses, craftsmanship, transport sector, traditional occupations, and service-based businesses/trades.

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Minority, Self Employment, Business, Low Interest Loan

The scheme &amp;quot;Term Loan Scheme&amp;quot; is implemented by Gujarat Minorities Finance and Development Corporation (GMFDC) under the Social Justice and Empowerment Department, Government of Gujarat. The scheme aims to promote self-employment among individuals from the minority communities by providing financial assistance for agriculture and allied activities, small-scale businesses, craftsmanship, transport sector, traditional occupations, and service-based businesses/trades. Under this scheme, beneficiaries can avail loans at concessional interest rates to purchase equipment, raw materials, or working capital. 

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Loan

**Where it applies:** Gujarat; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** 1. **Financial Assistance for Business:** Loans ranging from ₹1,00,000/- to ₹20,00,000/- are provided businesses/trades in various sectors listed below: - Agriculture and allied businesses, such as animal husbandry, fisheries, etc. - Technical &amp; Skilled self-employment businesses. - Service-oriented businesses such as provision stores, ready-made garment shops, cyber cafés, cold drink parlors, auto repairing, beauty parlors, etc. - Handicraft businesses. - Transport sector. 1. **Interest Rates:** - For annual income up to ₹1,20,000/- (rural) and ₹1,20,000/- (urban), the interest rate is 6% per annum. - For families with annual income above ₹1,20,000/- and up to ₹8,00,000/-, the interest rate is 8% for male beneficiaries and 6% for female beneficiaries. **3. Repayment:** The loan amount has to be repaid to the corporation in 60 equal installments over five years, including interest. 

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Exclusions / Not eligible:** &lt;br&gt;
