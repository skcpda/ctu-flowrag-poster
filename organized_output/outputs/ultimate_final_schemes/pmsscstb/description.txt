# Post-Matric Scholarship for Scheduled Castes (SC) and Scheduled Tribes (ST) Students-Bihar

**Short Title:** PMSSCSTB

**Scheme Brief Description:** The “Post-Matric Scholarship for Scheduled Castes (SC) and Scheduled Tribes (ST) Students-Bihar” aims to provide financial assistance to eligible students from Schedule Castes (SC) and Scheduled Tribes (ST) for their post-secondary education.

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Scholarship, Post-Matric, Higher Education Support, Education, Financial Assistance

The “Post-Matric Scholarship for Scheduled Castes (SC) and Scheduled Tribes (ST) Students-Bihar” is designed to support students from disadvantaged communities by covering their educational expenses for post-secondary courses. The scholarship is intended for students belonging to Schedule Castes (SC) and Scheduled Tribes (ST). This initiative promotes access to higher education and helps reduce the financial burden on families from these communities. &lt;br&gt;

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Education & Learning; Sector: Scholarships and student finance

**Where it applies:** Bihar; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - Scholarship and maintenance allowance will be payable on the basis of the criteria prescribed in the resolution issued by the Government of India from time to time.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Exclusions / Not eligible:** - After passing one level course, studying in another equivalent course will not be entitled to scholarship, for example, after passing I.S.C. examination, studying in I.A., after passing B.A. examination, studying in B.Com. and after doing M.Sc., studying in M.A. class will not be given scholarship.
