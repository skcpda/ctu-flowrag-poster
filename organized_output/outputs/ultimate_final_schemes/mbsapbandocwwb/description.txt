# Maternity Benefit Scheme (APB&OCWWB)

**Short Title:** MBSAPB&OCWWB

**Scheme Brief Description:** The Govt. of Arunachal Pradesh has proposed and implemented the scheme “Maternity Benefit Scheme” for Registered Workers of Arunachal Pradesh Building & Other Construction Workers Welfare Board (APB&OCWWB).

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Women, Maternity, Construction Worker, Cash Assistance

The scheme “Maternity Benefit Scheme” was launched by the Department of Labour and Employment, Govt. of Arunachal Pradesh for Registered Workers of Arunachal Pradesh Building &amp;amp; Other Construction Workers Welfare Board (APB&amp;amp;OCWWB). The state government has proposed and implemented this scheme through the APB&amp;amp;OCWWB to its women workers who have registered their names in the Labour Welfare Board. **Objective:** • To mitigate suffering in the course of delivery and to supplement well upbringing of the infant. 

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Health & Wellness

**Where it applies:** Arunachal Pradesh; State

**Benefits / Assistance:** 1. Cash assistance of ₹1,000/- during the period of maternity 1. Payable for up to two adult daughters of registered workers. 

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**How to apply:** **Registration Process:** **Step 01:** The applicant is to register herself as a labour/worker under the Arunachal Pradesh Building &amp;amp; Other Construction Workers Welfare Board (APB&amp;amp;OCWWB). **Step 02:** The applicant can obtain the application form from the Registered Office of the District/Sub-Division, APB&amp;amp;OCWWB which can also be downloaded from the official website of the Department of Labour and Employment. **Step 03:** The applicant must ensure that the filled information should be correct before submitting the application form to the concerned department. **Step 04:** After successful verification, the applicant will be registered as a worker/labour under Board and an Identity Card to be issued to her. &lt;br&gt; **Application Process:** **Step 01:** For taking benefit of this scheme, the applicant needs to visit the nearest registered office of the District/Sub-Division, APB&amp;amp;OCWWB along with all the relevant documents. **Step 02:** Application in the prescribed format may be received from the concerned office. **Step 03:** The applicant needs to fill the application form completely and attach all the relevant documents. Thereafter, the applicant may submit the application form to the concerned office. **Step 04:** After successful verification, the applicant can avail the benefit of the scheme.
Mode: Offline.

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: Office Of District/Sub-Division, APB&OCWWB; Nodal Department: Labour and Employment Department.
