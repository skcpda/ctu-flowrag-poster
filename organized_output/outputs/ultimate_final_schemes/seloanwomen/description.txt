#  Self-Employment loan scheme for Minority Widows, Divorcees / Abandoned Women

**Short Title:** SELOANWOMEN

**Scheme Brief Description:** The scheme aims to provide self-employment loans to women from minority communities. Financial assistance with a 20% subsidy is provided to widows, divorced women, and abandoned women from minority communities. The scheme is implemented by the KSMDFC.

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Divorce, Women, Loan, Subsidy, Employment, Minority

The scheme &quot;Self-Employment loan scheme for Minority Widows, Divorcees / Abandoned Women&quot; was introduced by the State Minority Welfare Department, Government of Kerala. The scheme aims to provide self-employment loans to women from minority communities. Through this scheme, government financial assistance with a 20% subsidy is provided to widows, divorced women, and abandoned women from minority communities. The scheme is implemented by the Kerala State Minority Development Finance Corporation. 

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Employment services and jobs

**Where it applies:** Kerala; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - Financial assistance of up to ₹1,00,000/- as a government subsidy. - Maximum loan amount of ₹5,00,000/-. - The interest rate on the loan amount (excluding subsidy) is 6%.  > **Conditions/Criteria Associated with Benefits:** A 20% subsidy (maximum ₹1,00,000/-) is provided on the loan amount. - For a loan amount of ₹1,00,000/-, the subsidy is ₹20,000/-. - For a loan amount of ₹2,00,000/-, the subsidy is ₹40,000/-. - For a loan amount of ₹3,00,000/-, the subsidy is ₹60,000/-. - For a loan amount of ₹4,00,000/-, the subsidy is ₹80,000. - For a loan amount of ₹5,00,000/-, the subsidy is ₹1,00,000/-. _*At each stage of disbursement, photos and bills must be submitted._ _*Direct inspection will be conducted at each stage of disbursement._ _*An additional interest of 6% will be charged for misuse of funds._ 

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Exclusions / Not eligible:** - The following types of land are not accepted as collateral: Kuzhikanam, Verumpattom, Devaswam Verumpattom, pledged land, and agricultural fields (if patta is not received). - Residential land less than 4 cents will not be accepted as collateral. 

**How to apply:** **Step 1:****** Visit the [official website](https://minoritywelfare.kerala.gov.in/) of the Directorate of Minority Affairs and check on &quot;News &amp; Updates&quot;. Select the &quot;Self-Employment loan scheme for Minority Widows, Divorcees / Abandoned Women&quot; and obtain the application form. Alternatively, you can also visit the [official website ](https://ksmdfc.org/)of KSMDFC and go to the &amp;quot;Application Form&amp;quot; section and select the &amp;quot;[Application Form for Widows&amp;#39; Loan Subsidy&amp;quot;](https://ksmdfc.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/Widows-application-form.pdf). Take a printout and fill out the [application form](https://minoritywelfare.kerala.gov.in/pdfnewsflash/imbichi.pdf). **Step 2:****** Attach the required copies of documents (Income Certificate, Caste/Age Certificate, Certificate proving applicant is a widow/divorced/abandoned woman) for primary verification. **Step 3:****** Submit the filled application form, either directly or by post, to the respective Regional Office of the Kerala State Minority Development Finance Corporation in your district.  _*Attend the interview, bringing all original documents mentioned in the &quot;Documents Required - For Interview&quot; section._ _*If the loan is approved, submit the additional required documents as per the chosen guarantee type (officer guarantee or property guarantee)._ _*The loan will be sanctioned as per the Corporation&#39;s rules and government regulations_ 
Mode: Offline.
