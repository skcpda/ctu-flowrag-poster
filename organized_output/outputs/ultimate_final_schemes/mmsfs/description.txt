# Maanki Munda Scholarship for Female Students

**Short Title:** MMSFS

**Scheme Brief Description:** The "Maanki Munda Scholarship for Female Students" scheme was launched by the Department of Higher & Technical Education, Govt. of Jharkhand. This scholarship program aims to make the girl students aware of enrollment in technical education, making them self-reliant & providing financial assistance.

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Financial Assistance, Girl Student, Technical

The &quot;Maanki Munda Scholarship for Female Students&quot; scheme was launched by the Department of Higher and Technical Education, Government of Jharkhand, a wonderful opportunity for female students (Regular and Lateral Entry) pursuing B.Tech. at BIT Sindri. This Scholarship program aims to make the girls of Jharkhand state aware of enrollment in technical education, making them self-reliant, and providing financial assistance.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Education & Learning; Sector: Scholarships and student finance

**Where it applies:** Jharkhand; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - ₹15,000/- per year for female students enrolled in Diploma courses. - ₹30,000/- per year for female students enrolled in B.Tech/B.E. courses.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**How to apply:** _Applications must be submitted online via the_ [_official portal_](https://www.bitsindri.ac.in/)_._ **Step 01:** The student needs to fill out the Google form and submit a handwritten application along with necessary documents to the Academic Section. **Step 02:** Submit a handwritten application to the Academic Section, forwarded by the HOD. **Step 03:** Submit two sets of the printed Google form and self-attested documents in the Academic Section.
Mode: Online.

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Higher and Techical Education Department.

**References / Annexures:** - **Guidelines**: https://www.bitsindri.ac.in/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/Maanki-Munda-Scholarship-for-Girls-2023-24-1.pdf
