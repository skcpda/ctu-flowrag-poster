# Agriculture Engineering (Mechanical) Scheme

**Short Title:** AE(M)S

**Scheme Brief Description:** The objective of the scheme is to provide agricultural machinery such as power tillers/ bulldozers/ tractors etc. on hiring at subsidised rate.

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Agriculture, Engineering, Mechanical, Agricultural Machinery, Farmer, Subsidy

The “Agriculture Engineering (Mechanical) Scheme” is a State Plan Scheme, which is implemented by the Department of Agriculture &amp;amp; Farmers’ Welfare, Government of Meghalaya. The objective of the scheme is to provide agricultural machinery such as power tillers/bulldozers/tractors etc. on hiring at subsidised rate.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Agriculture,Rural & Environment; Sector: Mechanization- solar power, farming systems

**Where it applies:** Meghalaya; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** > **Pattern of Assistance:** - Providing a 60% subsidy for the purchase of improved farm machinery and equipment (Proposed for Power Tiller and Diesel Water Pumpset). > **Type of Assistance and Entitlement (If any):** - Subsidy for hiring of farm machinery and equipment.

**Benefit Type:** Composite

**How to apply:** > **Application Process:** **Step 01:** The eligible bonafide farmer can apply/write an application on plain paper. **Step 02:** Along with the application, attach copies of all the mandatory documents (self-attest, if required). **Step 03:** Submit the duly filled and signed application along with the relevant documents at the nearest office of the Agriculture Development Officer of a CD Block/Agriculture Circle. &gt; **Mode of Selection of Beneficiary(s):** The bonafide farmers shall be selected on a first come first serve basis. &gt; **Whom to Contact:** 1. District Agriculture Officers of the concerned districts. 1. Sub-divisional Officers of the concerned sub-divisions. 1. Agriculture Development Officers of the concerned Community and Rural Development Blocks. 1. Assistant Agriculture Engineer (Mechanical) of the concerned districts.
Mode: Offline.

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: Department Of Agriculture & Farmers’ Welfare, Government Of Meghalaya; Nodal Department: Agriculture Department.
