# Haryana Pre Matric Scholarship For Minority

**Short Title:** HPMSM

**Scheme Brief Description:** Launched in June 2006 under the Prime Minister's New 15 Point Programme for the Welfare of Minorities, the "Pre-Matric Scholarship for Students Belonging to the Minority Communities" is a Centrally Sponsored Scheme by the Ministry of Minority Affairs, Government of India.

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Minority, Scholarship, Education, Student, Tuition Fees, Admission Fees, Allowance

Launched in June 2006 under the Prime Minister&#39;s New 15 Point Programme for the Welfare of Minorities, the &quot;Pre-Matric Scholarship for Students Belonging to the Minority Communities&quot; is a Centrally Sponsored Scheme by the Ministry of Minority Affairs, Government of India, implemented by the Government of Haryana. Through this scheme, scholarships including admission fees up to ₹500 per annum, tuition fees up to ₹350 per month, and maintenance allowance ranging from ₹100 to ₹600 per month are provided to minority community students studying in classes 1 to 10 whose family income is less than ₹1,00,000 per annum. The applications are accepted through State Government/Union Territory Administration. The scheme is being implemented by the Haryana State Government with a special focus on encouraging parents from minority communities to support their children&#39;s school education.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Education & Learning; Sector: Scholarships and student finance

**Where it applies:** Haryana; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** **Admission Fee (Class 6 to 10):** - Up to ₹500 per annum (for both hostellers and day scholars) **Tuition Fee (Class 6 to 10):** - Up to ₹350 per month (for both hostellers and day scholars) **Maintenance Allowance (for 10 months):** - Class 1 to 5 (Day Scholars): ₹100 per month - **Class 6 to 10:** > Hostellers: ₹600 per month > Day Scholars: ₹100 per month

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**How to apply:** **Step 1:** Keep ready the soft copies of the required documents. Go to [the National Scholarship Portal (NSP)](http://www.scholarships.gov.in/). and click “Apply Now”. Guidelines for [Registration](https://scholarships.gov.in/otrapplication/#/registration-page) will appear. Scroll to the bottom. Read carefully the undertaking. Accept the Terms. Click “Continue”. **Step 2:** A Registration Form will appear. (The fields marked as * are compulsory.) Fill in the details and click “Register”. Your Application ID and password will be displayed. The same will also be sent as an SMS to your registered mobile number.  **Step 3:** Go to [Login with OTR](https://scholarships.gov.in/otrapplication/#/login-page). Click on “Login to Apply”. Enter your Application ID and password. Type the Captcha and click “Login”. On the next screen, provide the OTP received on your registered mobile number. You will be directed to the Password Reset screen. Create a new password and confirm. Click “Submit”. You will be directed to the “Applicant’s Dashboard”.  **Step 4:** On the left pane, click “Application Form.&quot; The fields marked as * are compulsory. Fill in the details and upload the documents. You can either click on “Save as Draft” to complete the application later. Else, click “Final Submit” to submit the application.
Mode: Online.

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Social Justice and Empowerment Department.
