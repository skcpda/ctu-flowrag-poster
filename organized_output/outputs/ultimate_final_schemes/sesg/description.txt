# Self Employment Scheme Gujarat

**Short Title:** SESG

**Scheme Brief Description:** The scheme provides unemployed tribal persons with affordable loans ranging from ₹50,000 to ₹5,00,000 at a 4% annual interest rate, aiming to help them establish self-employment opportunities.

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Self Employment, Opportunity, Scheduled Tribe, Loan

The &quot;Self Employment Scheme&quot; was launched by the Tribal Development Department, Government of Gujarat. The scheme aims to provide financial support to unemployed tribal individuals by offering affordable loans for self-employment opportunities. Under this scheme, loans ranging from ₹50,000 to ₹5,00,000 are provided at a concessional interest rate to enable beneficiaries to start any of the 85 identified businesses. 

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Skills & Employment; Sector: Career information

**Where it applies:** Gujarat; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - **Monetary Benefit:** Loan assistance between ₹50,000 and ₹5,00,000, depending on the chosen business activity.- **Interest Rate:** 4% per annum.- **Coverage:** Applicable for 85 different types of businesses as identified by the department. 

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**How to apply:** ##### ***Registration Process*** - Visit the Gujarat Tribal Development Corporation [official website](https://adijatinigam.gujarat.gov.in/home). - Click on the &quot;[APPLY FOR LOAN](https://adijatinigam.gujarat.gov.in/gtdcloan)&quot;.- Select the scheme name &quot;Foreign Study&quot;. - Fill the registration form: Full Name (as per Aadhaar Card), Gender, Date of Birth, Aadhaar Number, Mobile Number, Uder name, Password for login, Confirm Password. - Click on the &quot;Sign Up. - After successful registration, User ID and Password will be generated. ##### ***Application Process*** - Enter User ID, Password, and Captcha Code. - After login, schemes applicable to your caste will be displayed. - Click on the scheme you want to apply for. - Fill the &quot;Application Form&quot; and upload the required documents - Click Save Application. The system will generate an Application Number. - Click Print Application. 
Mode: Online.
