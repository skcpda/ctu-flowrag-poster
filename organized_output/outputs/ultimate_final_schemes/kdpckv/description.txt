# Riyaayati Dar Par Copies Ka Vitaran

**Short Title:** RDPCKV

**Scheme Brief Description:** The Labor department of Madhya Pradesh state started the scheme "Riyaayati dar par Copies Ka Vitaran" for the children of construction worker.

**Launch Date:** 2018-01-01

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Education Support, Construction Worker, Child Development, Study Material

The &quot;Riyaayati dar par Copies Ka Vitaran&quot; scheme iniatited by the Labor department of Madhya Pradesh with the aim to provide financial assistance to the children of the construction worker. It will reduce the financial burden and help to educate their children.  > **Objective:** - Promoting literacy and access to educational materials. - Reducing the financial burden of buying educational supplies for workers&#39; children. - Encouraging school attendance and participation

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Education & Learning; Sector: Scholarships and student finance

**Where it applies:** Madhya Pradesh; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - 10 Small Copies: ₹10/- per copy - 10 Registers : ₹14/- per register

**Benefit Type:** In Kind

**How to apply:** 1. Applicant need to reach the below office: - Regional Officer (Public Relations Officer, Contribution Collection Officer, Welfare Officer, Welfare Supervisor, Welfare Inspector) 1. Get the prescribed format, fill out the required details and submit. 1. Applicant need to deposit the cost of copies with the managing institution. 1. Upon approval, the copies are distributed to the children through the institution. 
Mode: Offline.

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Labour Department.
