# Saraswati Sadhana Yojana to Economically Backward Class Girls studying in Class 9

**Short Title:** SSYEBC

**Scheme Brief Description:** Under this scheme, free bicycle assistance is provided to economically backward class girls studying in Class 9 in government and grant-in-aid schools across Gujarat.

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Education, Bicycle, Girl Student, Economically Weaker Sections

The &amp;quot;Saraswati Sadhana Yojana to Economically Backward Class Girls studying in Class 9&amp;quot; is a welfare scheme by the Social Justice and Empowerment Department of Gujarat. The scheme offers free bicycles to girls enrolled in Class 9 in government and grant-in-aided schools, facilitating easier access to education and encouraging regular school attendance. This physical aid helps in improving educational participation and empowering female students from economically weaker backgrounds.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Education & Learning; Sector: Resources for schools

**Where it applies:** Gujarat; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - Free bicycle provided to each eligible girl student.  

**Benefit Type:** In Kind
