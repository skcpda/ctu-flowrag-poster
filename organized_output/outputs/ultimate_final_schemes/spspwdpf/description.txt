# Scheme to Provide Stipend to the Persons With Disabilities of the State for Poultry Training

**Short Title:** SPSPWDPF

**Scheme Brief Description:** The "Scheme to Provide Stipend to the Persons With Disabilities of the State for Poultry Training" aims to promote poultry rearing skills among persons with disabilities through a ₹2,000/- stipend for attending a 7-day training program conducted by the Intensive Poultry Development Unit.

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Poultry Training, Disability Empowerment, Skill Development, Animal Husbandry, Stipend

The scheme &amp;quot;Scheme to Provide Stipend to the Persons With Disabilities of the State for Poultry Training&amp;quot; by the Agriculture and Co-operation Department, Government of Gujarat, aims to provide financial assistance and capacity building to physically disabled individuals of the state by equipping them with poultry rearing skills. Under this scheme, eligible beneficiaries receive a stipend of ₹2,000/- for participating in a 7-day training session on poultry farming conducted by the Intensive Poultry Development Unit. The initiative is designed to enhance income opportunities and livelihood development for the physically disabled population across Gujarat. 

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Agriculture,Rural & Environment; Sector: Entrepreneurship development

**Where it applies:** Gujarat; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - **Stipend Amount:** ₹2,000/- per beneficiary.   

**Benefit Type:** Cash
