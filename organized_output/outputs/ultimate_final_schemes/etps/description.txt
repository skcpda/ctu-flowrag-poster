# Educational Travel Promotion Scheme

**Short Title:** ETPS

**Scheme Brief Description:** The "Educational Travel Promotion Scheme" by the Department of Education, aims to promote personality development, observation skills, and curiosity among students through funding educational tours.

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Educational Tour, Personality Development, Student, School

The scheme &amp;quot;Educational Travel Promotion Scheme&amp;quot; by the Department of Education, Government of Gujarat, aims to promote the overall personality development of students, enhance their observation skills, and satisfy their curiosity to learn new things by supporting educational tours within the state. It provides financial assistance of ₹500/- per student per year to government secondary and higher secondary schools (including RMSA and Model/Model Day schools) to enable students to study with pleasure. 

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Education & Learning; Sector: Education and training grants, fellowship, stipend

**Where it applies:** Gujarat; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - **Financial Assistance:** ₹500/- per student per year is allocated to the school for organizing educational tours within the state. 

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**How to apply:** - The applicant school should submit an application as per the prescribed checklist with resolutions related to the plan and five high-resolution photographs related to the educational tour/project. 
Mode: Offline.

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Education Department.
