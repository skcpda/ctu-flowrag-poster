# Indira Gandhi National Disability Pension Scheme - Jharkhand

**Short Title:** IGNDPSJ

**Scheme Brief Description:** Under this scheme, a monthly pension is provided to severely disabled and multi-disabled individuals aged 18 and above who are living below the poverty line (BPL) to support them in living a respectful life.

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Pension, Financial Assistance, Person With Disability

The scheme &quot;Indira Gandhi National Disability Pension Scheme&quot; is implemented by the Department of Women, Child Development &amp; Social Security, Government of Jharkhand. Under this scheme, financial assistance is provided to severely disabled and multi-disabled individuals aged 18 and above who are living below the poverty line (BPL) to support them in living a respectful life.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Pension

**Where it applies:** Jharkhand; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - Under this scheme, eligible beneficiaries will receive a monthly pension of ₹1000/- (Central contribution- ₹300/-, State Contribution- ₹700/-). 

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Exclusions / Not eligible:** 1. Households having motorised two-wheeler/three-wheeler/four-wheeler/fishing boat. 1. Households owning machine operated three/four wheeled agricultural implements. 1. Families holding Kisan Credit Card with standard limit of ₹50 thousand or more. 1. Members of a family of a Government servant. 1. Households engaged in non-agricultural industries registered with the Government. 1. Any member of the family earning ₹10,000/- and above. 1. Income tax paying families. 1. Households paying service tax. 1. Families having three or more rooms with concrete walls and roof in all the rooms. (Not applicable for PMAY &amp; IAY beneficiaries) 1. Households having refrigerators. 1. Households having landline phone. 1. Families having 2.5 acres and above irrigated land with at least one irrigation device. 1. Families holding 5 acres or more of irrigated land for two or more crop seasons. 1. Families owning land of 7.5 acres and above with at least one irrigation equipment.

**How to apply:** **Step 1:** In the application form, fill in all the mandatory fields, and attach copies of all the mandatory documents (self-attest, if required). **Step 2:** Submit the duly filled and signed application form along with the documents to the Block Development Officer in rural areas and the concerned Zonal Officer in urban areas.  
Mode: Offline.

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Women, Child Development & Social Security Department.
