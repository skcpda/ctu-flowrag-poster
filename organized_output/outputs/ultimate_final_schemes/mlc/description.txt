# Mobile Learning Centre (UKBOCWWB)

**Short Title:** MLCUKBOCWWB

**Scheme Brief Description:** The scheme aims to provide education to the children of registered construction workers at construction sites through Mobile Learning Centre buses, ensuring their better and brighter future.

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Building Worker, Construction Worker, Labour, Learning Centre

The “Mobile Learning Centre” scheme is implemented by the Uttarakhand Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board (UKBOCWWB) under the Labour Department, Government of Uttarakhand. The scheme aims to provide education to the children of registered construction workers at construction sites through Mobile Learning Centre buses, ensuring their better and brighter future. 

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Education & Learning

**Where it applies:** Uttarakhand; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** Under this scheme, mobile learning schools are operated to provide education to the children of construction workers. 

**Benefit Type:** In Kind

**How to apply:** For securing a bright future for the children of construction site workers, education is provided at construction sites through Mobile Learning Centre buses. 
Mode: Offline.

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: Uttarakhand Building And Other Construction Workers Welfare Board; Nodal Department: Department Of Labour.
