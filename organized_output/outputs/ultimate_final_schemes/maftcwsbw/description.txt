# Medical Assistance for the Construction Worker

**Short Title:** MAFTCWSBW

**Scheme Brief Description:** The "Medical Assistance for the Construction Workers" by the Sikkim BOCW Welfare Board, Labour Department, is a welfare scheme of the board that provides medical expenses for the treatment of the registered beneficiary and /or his dependents.

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Labour, Medical Support, Construction Workers, Financial Assistance

The &amp;quot;Medical Assistance for the Construction Workers&amp;quot; by the Sikkim Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board, Labour Department, is a welfare scheme for the registered workers of the board. Under this scheme, medical expenses shall be provided for the treatment of the beneficiary and /or his dependents suffering from T.B. Cancer, Heart Disease, Kidney Disease, Eye Disease, Leprosy and Nerve Disease. However where an operation is involved for the treatment of the diseases like heart Disease, Kidney Disease etc. &lt;br&gt;

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Emergency medical assistance

**Where it applies:** Sikkim; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - ₹10,000/- is provided for Medical assistance.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**How to apply:** &gt; **Registration Process as a Building/ Construction Worker:** **Step 1:** The complete application form along with the required documents is required to be submitted to the Registering Officer of the area. **Step 2:** The Registering Officer, after satisfying himself/herself with all the details, may register the worker as a beneficiary under the act and issue an Identity Card in Form XXVIII. &gt; **Application Process of the Welfare Scheme:** **Step 1:** Upon receiving the Identity Card as a Building/ Construction Worker, the applicant needs to download the application form for the concerned welfare scheme and submit the form along with the mandatory documents to the Secretary or any other Officer authorized by him in the District Labour Office (self attested, if required). **Step 2:** Later on, verifying the eligibility of the concerned welfare scheme, the applicant may get the benefits. - **Application form for the Registration as a Beneficiary:** [Form XXVII](https://labour.sikkim.gov.in/Uploads/Bocw/8F1D404A-3EC3-4DA9-A4D6-75880B26D191.pdf) (Refer Page No: 153). - **Application form for the Medical Assistance:** Form [XL ](https://labour.sikkim.gov.in/Uploads/Bocw/8F1D404A-3EC3-4DA9-A4D6-75880B26D191.pdf)(Refer Page No: 167). &gt; **Helpdesk:** For further enquiry, workers can contact the following numbers:- 70769-26998 (BOCW Nodal Officer) 90643-89842 (East) 90028-33969 (Pakyong) 96098-63959 (South) 95477-16798 (West) 96359-98441 (North) Toll Free Helpline for labourers: 18003451474
