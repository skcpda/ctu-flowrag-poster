# Rajasthan Varishth Patrakar Samman Yojana

**Short Title:** RVPSY

**Scheme Brief Description:** Under this scheme, a monthly financial assistance of ₹15,000/- is provided to full-time accredited journalists who are 60 years of age or older & have served continuously for at least 20 years in daily, weekly, fortnightly newspapers, independent publications, electronic media, or news agencies.

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Financial Assistance, Journalist, Senior Citizen, Honorarium

The &amp;quot;Rajasthan Varishth Patrakar Samman Yojana&amp;quot; was implemented by the Information and Public Relations Department, Government of Rajasthan, to recognize and support the contributions of senior accredited journalists. Under this scheme, a monthly financial assistance of ₹15,000/- is provided to full-time accredited journalists who are 60 years of age or older and have served continuously for at least 20 years in daily, weekly, fortnightly newspapers, independent publications, electronic media, or news agencies (including editors, publishers, and owners).

**Who is this for:** Journalist; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Financial assistance

**Where it applies:** Rajasthan; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** **Financial Assistance:** - Monthly Honorarium: ₹15,000/- per month to the senior accredited journalists. - In case of the journalist&#39;s death, 50% of the amount is provided to the widow. **Duration:** - Paid for a maximum period of 5 years, subject to review. **Mode of Disbursement:** - Direct Bank Transfer to the journalist’s savings account.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**How to apply:** > **Application Process** **Step 1:** The interested applicant downloads the [application form](https://jankalyanfile.rajasthan.gov.in//Content/UploadFolder/Advertisement/Achievements/2022/Apr/2602_ACH_0919f4fa-eb86-4c9d-b1b7-a76b45a881c4.pdf) from the official website. **Step 2:** In the application form, fill in all the mandatory fields, and attach copies of all the mandatory documents (self-attested, if required). **Step 3:** Submit the duly filled and signed application form along with the documents to the respective Information and Public Relations Department, Rajasthan or Accreditation Committee. &gt; ***Post-Application Process*** **Step 1:** The Accreditation Committee will verify the documents and credentials. **Step 2:** Upon recommendation, final approval will be given by the State Government. **Step 3:** On approval, the monthly honorarium will be directly credited to the applicant&amp;#39;s bank account. **Note:** The scheme is subject to review and honorarium can be withdrawn in case of misconduct or legal violation.
Mode: Offline.

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Information and Public Relations Department.
