# Incentive for the Inter Caste Marriage Couple

**Short Title:** IFTICMC

**Scheme Brief Description:** The Social Welfare Department of the Government of Karnataka has launched the "Incentive for the Inter Caste Marriage Couple", which offers incentives to couples belonging to the SC community and Non-SC. The scheme aims to promote inter-caste marriages and provide financial assistance to the couples

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Financial Assistance, Inter-caste, Marriage

The Karnataka Social Welfare Department offers an incentive to encourage inter-caste marriages, aiming to reduce caste-based discrimination and promote a more inclusive society. This scheme is targeted at Scheduled Caste (SC) individuals who marry outside their caste, particularly Hindu non-SC individuals.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Financial assistance

**Where it applies:** Karnataka; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** **Incentive Amount:** - ₹2,50,000/- for SC men marrying a woman from another caste. - ₹3,00,000/- for SC women marrying a man from another caste. **Subsidy Disbursement:****** The incentive is provided in two installments. - 50% in cash. - 50% as a fixed deposit with a minimum term of 3 years.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**How to apply:** **Step-01:** First go to the [official website](https://swdservices.karnataka.gov.in/index.aspx) and click &quot; Incentives to Marriages&quot;. **Step-02:** Select &quot;Incentives to Inter-caste Marriages&quot; and Click &quot;Register&quot; **Step-03:** Using your &quot;Aadhaar Card Number&quot; Register with us providing all required Information. **Step-04:** Upload all the relevant documents. **Step-05:** After Registration, an SMS will be sent to the couple and they can print the acknowledgment form as proof of application. **Step-06:** Social Welfare will do spot verification to verify the details provided by the couple and process the application further. **Step-07:** After successful spot verification by the department, the incentive will be credited to the joint account of the couple.
Mode: Online.

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Social Welfare Department.
