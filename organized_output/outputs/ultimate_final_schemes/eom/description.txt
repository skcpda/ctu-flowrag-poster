# PMMSY: Establishment of Medium RAS / Biofloc culture system - Haryana

**Short Title:** EOM

**Scheme Brief Description:** The scheme provides capital assistance to fish farmers and entrepreneurs for establishing medium-scale Recirculatory Aquaculture Systems (RAS) and Biofloc systems.

**Tags:** Aquaculture, RAS, Biofloc Systems, Fish, Farming

The Pradhan Mantri Matsya Sampada Yojana: Establishment of Medium RAS / Biofloc Culture System, introduced by the Ministry Of Fisheries, Animal Husbandry &amp; Dairying, Government of India, aims to provide capital assistance to fish farmers and entrepreneurs for establishing medium-scale Recirculatory Aquaculture Systems (RAS) and Biofloc systems. These models promote high-density fish production in controlled environments, significantly reducing land and water usage. It is a centrally funded scheme with a 50:50 cost-sharing pattern between the Central and State Governments. - For RAS: Minimum 6 tanks of 30 m³ each (10 tons/crop)- For Biofloc: 25 tanks of 4 m diameter and 1 m height

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Agriculture,Rural & Environment; Sector: Fishing and hunting

**Where it applies:** Haryana; State/ UT

**DBT Scheme:** Yes (Direct Benefit Transfer)

**Benefits / Assistance:** - The project cost is ₹25,00,000/- > **** **Subsidy Distribution:** 1. 40% subsidy for General Category beneficiaries. 1. 60% subsidy for SC and Women category beneficiaries.

**Benefit Type:** Cash
