# Pradhan Mantri Matsya Sampada Yojana: Motor Cycle with Ice Box - Haryana

**Short Title:** PMMSYMCIBHR

**Scheme Brief Description:** The scheme aims to strengthen fish transportation infrastructure and ensure the freshness and quality of fish during transit. Under this scheme, financial assistance is provided to fishers for purchasing motorcycles equipped with ice boxes, ensuring efficient and hygienic fish transport.

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Subsidy, Fishermen, Motorcycle, Fish, Ice Box, Fisheries

The “Motor Cycle with Ice Box” is a scheme component under the Pradhan Mantri Matsya Sampada Yojana (PMMSY), implemented by the Fisheries Department, Government of Haryana. This is a Centrally Sponsored Scheme, aims to strengthen fish transportation infrastructure and ensure the freshness and quality of fish during transit. Under this scheme, financial assistance is provided to fishers for purchasing motorcycles equipped with ice boxes, ensuring efficient and hygienic fish transport.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Agriculture,Rural & Environment; Sector: Fishing and hunting

**Where it applies:** Haryana; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** **Financial Assistance provided under the scheme:** - Project Cost: ₹75,000/- per unit - Subsidy will be limited to 40% of the project cost for the General Category of beneficiaries. - Subsidy will be limited to 60% of the project cost for Weaker sections/Scheduled Caste/Scheduled Tribe/Women and their co-operatives of beneficiaries.

**Benefit Type:** Cash
