# Mukhyamantri Gyansetu Merit Scholarship Yojana

**Short Title:** MMGMY

**Scheme Brief Description:** This scheme introduced by the Education Department, Government of Gujarat, aims to support meritorious students by providing scholarships from Class 6th to Class 12th in selected schools.

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Scholarship, Education, Student, Financial Assistance

The scheme &amp;quot;Mukhyamantri Gyansetu Merit Scholarship Yojana&amp;quot; by the Education Department, Government of Gujarat aims to select 30,000 brilliant students who have completed their Class 5 education consecutively in government, aided, or RTE-approved schools and provide them with scholarships to study in selected government, aided, or private schools from Class 6th to Class 12th. The selection is done through a Common Entrance Test conducted by the State Examination Board, Gandhinagar. 

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Education & Learning; Sector: Scholarships and student finance

**Where it applies:** Gujarat; State/ UT

**DBT Scheme:** Yes (Direct Benefit Transfer)

**Benefits / Assistance:** > **Government Schools and Government -aided Schools (Approved by Education Department):** - For Class 6th to 8th: ₹5,000/- per year. - For Class 9th to 10th: ₹6,000/- per year. - For Class 11th to 12th: ₹7,000/- per year. > **Private (Selected) Schools:** - For Class 6th to 8th: ₹20,000/- per year. - For Class 9th to 10th: ₹22,000/- per year. - For Class 11th to 12th: ₹25,000/- per year. > **Frequency of Disbursement:** - Disbursed annually in two installments; the first 50% is given after confirmation of at least 80% attendance in the first semester. 

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**How to apply:** > **Registration Process:** **Step 1:** Visit the official website: [http://gssyguj.in](https://gssyguj.in/). **Step 2:** Register online using the 18-digit CTS Aadhaar UDI number. **Step 3:** Provide mobile number, verify using OTP, and set a password to complete registration. > **Application Process:** **Step 1:** Log in using the registered Aadhaar number and password. **Step 2:** Fill out the online application form carefully, including personal details, school details (UDISE number), category (SC/ST/OBC/General/EWS), and residence type (Urban/Rural). **Step 3:** Upload the required documents including caste certificate (if applicable). **Step 4:** Preview the form, save and submit it online. **Step 5:** Take a printout of the submitted form for future reference. **Step 6:** Regularly check the website and registered mobile number for updates and merit list announcements. > **Contact Us:** **Commissioner of Schools** **Address:** Block-9, 1 st floor, J.M.Bhavan, Old sachivalaya, Gandhinagar **Tel. no. :** 079-23254011 **Other office:** 079-23254014 **E-mail:** gssyguj@gmail.com
