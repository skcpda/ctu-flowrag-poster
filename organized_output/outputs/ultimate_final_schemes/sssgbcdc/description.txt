# Swayam Saksham Yojana (GBCDC)

**Short Title:** SSYGBCDC

**Scheme Brief Description:** This scheme aims to instill a sense of self-reliance in the youth belonging to backward classes after receiving vocational education/training. Under this scheme, eligible youth are provided loans at a low-interest rate to start self-employment ventures.

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Enterpreneurship, Loan, Backward Classes, Youth, Self-employment

The scheme &amp;quot;Swayam Saksham Yojana&amp;quot; is implemented by the Gujarat Backward Classes Development Corporation (GBCDC) under the Social Justice and Empowerment Department, Government of Gujarat. The scheme aims to inculcate the spirit of self-reliance among socially and educationally backward class youth who have received vocational or professional training. Under this scheme, low-interest loans are provided to educated unemployed youth for setting up self-employment ventures like clinics, law offices, engineering units, and more. The initiative focuses on empowering beneficiaries to start independent businesses with financial assistance, promoting economic inclusion and entrepreneurship. 

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Business & Entrepreneurship; Sector: Setting up / start-up / entrepreneurship

**Where it applies:** Gujarat; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** **Loan Amount:** - Up to ₹5,00,000/- per applicant. **Interest Rate:** - 6% per annum. **Loan Share:** Under the scheme, the loan will be distributed in a unit cost ratio where- - 85% will be covered by a national corporation - 10% by State Government - 5% contribution by the beneficiary **Repayment Terms:** - This loan will have to be repaid in 60 equal monthly installments including interest. 

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**How to apply:** **Step 1:** Visit the [official website](https://gbcdconline.gujarat.gov.in/) of the Gujarat Backwards Caste Development Corporation. **Step 2:** On the top menu bar, click on &quot;Apply Online&quot;. **Step 3:** If you’re a new user, click on &quot;New Registration&quot; Enter personal details (name, mobile number, email, aadhaar number, etc.), create a password, and complete OTP verification. **Step 4:** Use your registered credentials (User ID and password) to log in. **Step 5:** Click on “New Application”. Select “Swayam Saksham Yojana” from the list of available schemes. **Step 6:** Fill out the Application Form, enter the following details: - Personal Information: Name, gender, date of birth, caste, etc. - Address Information: Permanent and correspondence address - Income Details: Monthly family income and source - Loan Requirement: Amount needed, proposed use (e.g., business, self-employment) - Bank Account Details: For loan disbursement **Step 7:** Upload scanned copies of the Caste certificate, Income certificate, Aadhaar card, Passport-size photo, Bank passbook (first page), and Residence proof. **Step 8:** Double-check all details. Click on “Preview” to verify. If all details are correct, click “Submit”. **Step 9:** Applicant will receive an Application ID/Reference Number. Save or print the acknowledgement for future tracking. 
Mode: Online.

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: Gujarat Backward Classes Development Corporation (GBCDC); Nodal Department: Social Justice and Empowerment Department.
