# Mukhyamantri Shiksha Protsahan Yojana

**Short Title:** MMSPY

**Scheme Brief Description:** The "Mukhyamantri Shiksha Protsahan Yojana" by the Directorate of Higher Education, Department of Higher and Technical Education, Government of Jharkhand, aims to provide free coaching and financial assistance to students prepare for national level competitive examinations like JEE, NEET, CLAT, NHMC

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Free Coaching, Financial Assistance, Student, Exam

The &quot;Mukhyamantri Shiksha Protsahan Yojana&quot; by the Directorate of Higher Education, Department of Higher and Technical Education, Government of Jharkhand, aims to provide free coaching and financial assistance to students preparing for National Level Competitive Examinations like JEE, NEET, CLAT, NHMCET, NIFT-CET etc,.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Education & Learning; Sector: Coaching

**Where it applies:** Jharkhand; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - ₹2,500/- monthly stipends per student during the coaching period. - Free of cost coaching facility in reputed coaching institutes.

**Benefit Type:** Composite

**How to apply:** ***Registration Process:*** **Step 01:** Go to the official website of [Mukhyamantri Shiksha Protsahan Yojana](https://mukhyamantri.apphost.in/) and click on &quot;Registration&quot;. **Step 02:** Fill the required details name, mobile number email id and complete the process. **Step 03:** The student will get an confirmation SMS registered mobile number and an email id.  ***Application Process:*** **Step 01:** Fill the application form and upload the required documents. **Step 02:** Click on &quot;Submit&quot; to get application Id.  **NOTE****:** - The student must secure at least 75% attendance for the entire duration of the coaching.
Mode: Online.

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Higher and Techical Education Department.
