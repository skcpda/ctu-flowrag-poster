# Transgender Utthan Kosh - Post Matric Scholarship for Studying Transgender

**Short Title:** TUKPMSFST

**Scheme Brief Description:** The scheme aims to support transgender students pursuing post-matric education. Under this scheme, transgender students receive a monthly scholarship of ₹1,000/- to cover educational expenses for their post-matric studies.

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Scholarship, Transgender, Post-matric, Student

The &amp;quot;Transgender Utthan Kosh - Post Matric Scholarship for Studying Transgender&amp;quot; scheme was introduced by the Social Justice and Empowerment Department, Government of Rajasthan. It aims to support transgender students pursuing post-matric education. Under this scheme, transgender students receive a monthly scholarship of ₹1,000/- to cover educational expenses for their post-matric studies.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Education & Learning; Sector: Scholarships and student finance

**Where it applies:** Rajasthan; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** ***Post-Matric Scholarship:*** - Scholarship @ ₹1,000/- per month per student for 10 months. **NOTE:** The scholarship amount is directly transferred to the beneficiary&#39;s bank account.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**How to apply:** ***Application Submission:*** - The applicant/parents/guardians must fill out the scholarship application form and attach the required documents. - Submit the complete application form to their school principal/institution. ***Institutional Verification:*** - The school principal/institution verifies the application and forwards it to the Social Justice and Empowerment Department&#39;s district office. ***Approval Process:*** - The district office verifies the documents and approves/rejects the application within one month. ***Disbursement:*** - Upon approval, the scholarship amount is transferred to the applicant&#39;s bank account.
Mode: Offline.

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Social Justice and Empowerment Department.
