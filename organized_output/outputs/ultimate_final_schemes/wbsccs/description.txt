# West Bengal Student Credit Card Scheme

**Short Title:** WBSCCS

**Scheme Brief Description:** The Higher Education Department, Government of West Bengal has introduced the Student Credit Card Scheme for the students of West Bengal to enable them to pursue higher education without having any financial constraints.

**Launch Date:** 2021-06-30

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Financial Assistance, Education Loan, Higher Study

This scheme is designed to support the students to pursue secondary, higher secondary, madrasah, undergraduate and post graduate studies including professional degree and other equivalent courses in any School, Madrasah, College, University and other affiliated institutes within and outside India. Students studying in various coaching institutions for appearing in different competitive examinations like Engineering, Medical, Law, IAS, IPS, WBCS etc, can also avail the loan under this scheme. 

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Education & Learning; Sector: Scholarships and student finance

**Where it applies:** West Bengal; State

**Benefits / Assistance:** **Points** 1. The Scheme provides a maximum loan of ₹10,00,000/- at a 4% simple annual interest rate after interest subvention. 1. The interest rate for loans up to ₹10,00,000/- is set at the prevailing 3-year MCLR of State Bank of India plus 1%, fixed at the time of sanction. 1. Students can access the loan at any point during their course of study. 1. Both the student and the parent/legal guardian must co-submit the loan application. 1. An agreement between the student, parents/legal guardians, and the bank will formalize the loan terms. **Margin money:** 1. Up to ₹4,00,000/-: Nil 1. Above ₹4,00,000/-: 5% 1. Scholarships/assistance ships included 1. Paid pro-rata as disbursements are made **Security**: 1. No unnecessary restrictions or collateral security 1. No security/collateral other than co-obligation of parents/legal guardians 1. State government to enter separate agreement with banks **Insurance:** 1. Life cover for student up to loan amount 1. Premium paid by student and debited from loan account **Time limits:** 1. The Higher Education Department and the School Education Department will communicate on loan sanctioning and disbursement process timeline after receiving approval from the competent authority.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**How to apply:** 1. Applicant have to visit the official [website ](https://wbscc.wb.gov.in)and click on &quot;STUDENT CREDIT CARD&quot;1. Now Click on “STUDENT REGISTRATION” option and select the one option from the following - For [institutions in India](https://wbscc.wb.gov.in/Applicant_Registration). - For [institutions outside India](https://wbscc.wb.gov.in/Scc_Foreign/Applicant_Registration). 1. Provide the require details for online registration process and click on “Submit” button to generate user id and password. 1. After completion of the registration process a unique id will be generated. 1. Now with the credentials need to log in through clicking on “Student Login” button from the home page 1. Here the candidate need to furnish their all details which can be accessed through the “Applicant Dashboard.” - Personal Details - Co-Borrower And Present Address Details - Permanent Address And Course &amp; Income Details - Bank Details Of Students And Co-Borrower - Upload the required documents. 1. Submit.
Mode: Online.
