# Free Computer Training

**Short Title:** FCTUK

**Scheme Brief Description:** The scheme aims to provide a one-year free training course (Computer / Accounting / Web Designing / Digital Marketing) to ex-servicemen, serving soldiers, their widows, and dependents who have passed the 12th standard.

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Computer Training, Soldier, Ex-serviceman, Sainik

The scheme &amp;quot;Free Computer Training&amp;quot; is implemented by the State Sainik Board under the Sainik Welfare Department, Government of Uttarakhand. The scheme aims to provide a one-year free training course (Computer / Accounting / Web Designing / Digital Marketing) to ex-servicemen, serving soldiers, their widows, and dependents who have passed the 12th standard. 

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Education & Learning; Sector: Education and training grants, fellowship, stipend

**Where it applies:** Uttarakhand; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** Under this scheme, eligible beneficiaries are provided with a one-year free training course in Computer, Accounting, Web Designing, or Digital Marketing. 

**Benefit Type:** In Kind

**How to apply:** Under the scheme, training is provided in all districts in the following subjects: 1. Diploma in Computer Applications and Programming (Duration – 1 year) 1. Diploma in Professional Accounting (Duration – 1 year) 1. Web Designing and Development (Duration – 1 year) 1. Digital Marketing (Duration – 1 year) ***The Director, Sainik Welfare Office, invites applications for these courses from ex-servicemen and their dependents through publicity campaigns. The following documents are required for application:*** 1. General application form 1. Aadhaar Card 1. Ex-serviceman Identity Card 1. Discharge Book 1. Educational certificate (12th pass) ***After verification, the concerned District Sainik Welfare Office forwards the list of eligible applicants to the training institute. The institute then informs the selected candidates and commences the training program.*** 
Mode: Offline.

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: State Sainik Board, Government Of Uttarakhand; Nodal Department: State Sainik Board.
