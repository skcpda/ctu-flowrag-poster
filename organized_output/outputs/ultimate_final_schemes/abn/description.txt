# Anti Bird Net

**Short Title:** ABN

**Scheme Brief Description:** Under the Anti Bird Net Birds will never leave your garden if they are used to pecking on certain trees and plants, but if you install anti-bird nets at the appropriate places, you can prevent them from coming again to peck your plants.

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Farmer, Anti Bird Net, Farming, Agriculture

The Anti-Bird Net Scheme, an initiative by the Agriculture Department of the Rajasthan government, promotes protected cultivation practices among farmers by providing subsidies for installing low-cost plastic tunnels. The subsidy amount may be capped for each farmer, depending on the scheme&#39;s provisions and available funds. > **Important points** 1. The work can be started only after the administrative approval/work order is issued by the Horticulture Department. 1. After establishment, verification can be done by the committee formed. 1. The grant amount can be paid directly into the farmer&#39;s bank account or to the producing firm based on the written consent of the farmer. 

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Agriculture,Rural & Environment

**Where it applies:** Rajasthan; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** > **50 percent subsidy is payable for a maximum area of 5000 square meters.**

**Benefit Type:** Cash
