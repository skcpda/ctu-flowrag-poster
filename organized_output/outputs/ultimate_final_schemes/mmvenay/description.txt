# Mukhyamantri Vidwa Evam Ekal Naari Awas Yojana

**Short Title:** MMVENAY

**Scheme Brief Description:** The "Mukhyamantri Vidwa Evam Ekal Naari Awas Yojana" was launched by the Department of Social Justice and Empowerment, Government of Himachal Pradesh. The scheme aims to provide financial grants for the construction of houses to economically weak widows and single women.

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Financial Assistance, Women, Widow, Housing

The &quot;Mukhyamantri Vidwa Evam Ekal Naari Awas Yojana&quot; was launched by the Department of Social Justice and Empowerment, Government of Himachal Pradesh. The scheme aims to provide financial grants for the construction of houses to economically weak widows and single women. Under this scheme, eligible applicants, who are permanent residents of the state will receive financial assistance for constructing a house.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Women and Child; Sector: Financial Assistance

**Where it applies:** Himachal Pradesh; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - **An assistance amount of ₹1,50,000/- will be given for the construction of the house.**

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**How to apply:** **Step 1:** The interested applicant downloads the application form [Annexure-1](http://esomsa.hp.gov.in/sites/default/files/PDF/Scheme%20Mukhyamantri%20Vidwa%20Evam%20Ekal%20Naari%20Awas%20Yojana.pdf). **Step 2:** In the application form, fill in all the mandatory fields, and attach copies of all the mandatory documents (self-attest, if required). **Step 3:** Submit the duly filled and signed application form along with the documents to the Chief Minister / Chief Minister&#39;s office. **Step 4:** Request a receipt ([Annexure-3](http://esomsa.hp.gov.in/sites/default/files/PDF/Scheme%20Mukhyamantri%20Vidwa%20Evam%20Ekal%20Naari%20Awas%20Yojana.pdf)) or acknowledgment from the Chief Minister / Chief Minister&#39;s office to whom the application has been submitted. Ensure that the receipt contains essential details such as the date and time of submission, and a unique identification number (if applicable).  > **Grant Disbursal:** - Before payment of the grant amount, the beneficiary will have to give an undertaking on the prescribed form [Annexure-4](http://esomsa.hp.gov.in/sites/default/files/PDF/Scheme%20Mukhyamantri%20Vidwa%20Evam%20Ekal%20Naari%20Awas%20Yojana.pdf).
Mode: Offline.

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Department of Social Justice and Empowerment.
