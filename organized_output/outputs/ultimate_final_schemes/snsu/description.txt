# Sanitary Napkin Scheme

**Short Title:** SNSUK

**Scheme Brief Description:** The scheme aims to provide sanitary napkins to adolescent girls and women at subsidized rates through Anganwadi centres across the state. Under this scheme, sanitary napkins are made available to adolescent girls and women at a cost of ₹6.00 through Anganwadi centres.

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Adolescent Girls, Sanitary Pad, Teenage Girls, Woman, Anganwadi Centre

The scheme &amp;quot;Sanitary Napkin Scheme&amp;quot; was launched by the Department of Women Empowerment and Child Development, Government of Uttarakhand. The scheme aims to provide sanitary napkins to adolescent girls and women at subsidized rates through Anganwadi centres across the state. 

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Health & Wellness; Sector: Medicine and health products

**Where it applies:** Uttarakhand; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - Sanitary napkins are distributed to adolescent girls/women at a cost of ₹6/-. 

**Benefit Type:** In Kind

**How to apply:** Sanitary napkins can be purchased at subsidized rates from Anganwadi centres. 
Mode: Offline.

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Department Of Woman Empowerment & Child Development.
