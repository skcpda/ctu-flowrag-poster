# Assistance to Women for Trainer and Coaches Training Course

**Short Title:** ATWTCTC

**Scheme Brief Description:** The scheme “Assistance to Women for Trainer and Coaches Training Course” by the Sports, Youth and Cultural Activities Department, Government of Gujarat aims to encourage women's participation in sports by providing financial assistance for diploma courses in sports education.

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Women Coaches, Girls, Sports

The scheme “Assistance to Women for Trainer and Coaches Training Course” by the Sports, Youth and Cultural Activities Department, Government of Gujarat aims to promote and enhance the participation of women in sports through structured academic training by supporting them financially for pursuing diploma courses in sports. It seeks to enable women to take up professional training to become certified coaches and trainers, thereby boosting employment opportunities and representation in the sports sector. The scheme is implemented by the Sports Authority of Gujarat.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Sports & Culture; Sector: Sports

**Where it applies:** Gujarat; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - **Financial Assistance:** Eligible female candidates receive financial support to enroll in recognized diploma courses in sports to become trainers or coaches.

**Benefit Type:** Cash
