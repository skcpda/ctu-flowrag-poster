# West Bengal Transport Workers’ Social Security Scheme: Maternity Benefit

**Short Title:** WBTWSMB

**Scheme Brief Description:** This scheme provides financial assistance to a beneficiary on successful delivery of a child or miscarriage by such beneficiary or his wife.

**Scheme For:** Family

**Tags:** Social Security, Transport Worker, Health & Wellness, Maternity Benefit, Medical Benefit, Pregnant Women

The “West Bengal Transport Workers’ Social Security Scheme: Maternity Benefit” by the Labour Department, West Bengal, provides financial assistance to a beneficiary on successful delivery of a child or miscarriage by such beneficiary or his wife. The benefit is limited to two instances per beneficiary to promote maternal health and provide support during critical times. 

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Women and Child; Sector: Pregnancy Care

**Where it applies:** West Bengal; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - **Financial Assistance:** ₹6,000/-.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**How to apply:** **Step 1:** Visit the “[eDistrict 2.0](https://edistrict.wb.gov.in/portal/home)” portal. Click on Log In/Sign Up. **Step 2:** In the User Login window, scroll down to the bottom and click on “Register”. **Step 3:** Fill in all the details carefully, enter a username, and click on “Register”. **Step 4:** Upon entering the One Time Password received on your mobile, you will be successfully registered. **Step 5:** Now in the User Login window, enter your Username or email and captcha and click on “Sign In”. **Step 6:** Enter the received O.T.P and click on “Submit”. **Step 7:** Upon successful Login, You will be redirected to the “Dashboard”. From the right side menu apply for the concerned scheme.
Mode: Online.

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Labour Department.
