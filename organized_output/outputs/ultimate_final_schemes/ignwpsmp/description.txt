# Indira Gandhi National Widow Pension Scheme-Madhya Pradesh

**Short Title:** IGNWPSMP

**Scheme Brief Description:** The Indira Gandhi National Widow Pension Scheme is being operated to provide financial support to widowed women living below the poverty line.

**Launch Date:** 2009-04-01

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Women, Empowerment, Widow, Pension

Launched in the year 2009, the scheme “Indira Gandhi National Widow Pension Scheme (IGNWPS)” is a Centrally Sponsored Scheme by the Department of Social Justice and Empowerment of Persons with Disabilities, Government. of Madhya Pradesh. Under this scheme, financial support is provided to the widows of the state of Sikkim. A widow whose household is below the poverty line is eligible to get the benefits of the pension scheme. The beneficiary receives a monthly pension amount consists of the Central and State governments&amp;#39; share.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Pension

**Where it applies:** Madhya Pradesh; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - The beneficiary will get a monthly pension of ₹ 600/-.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**How to apply:** **Step 1:** The Application in the prescribed application form in the rural area of ​​his native residence-Gram Panchayat / Janpad Panchayat, Nagar Panchayat / municipal council/ municipal corporation office in an urban area with three photographs, certificate for confirmation of age, Samagra ID and BPL card Or apply online through the nearest Loksewa Kendra. **Step 02:** Fill in all the mandatory fields of the application form, attach all the (self-attested) mandatory documents, and submit them to the designated receiving authority.
Mode: Offline.

**Additional Application Processes:** **Process 2:** **Step 1**:Applicants have to visit the [official portal.](https://socialsecurity.mp.gov.in/Home.aspx) **Step 2**: [Click ](https://socialsecurity.mp.gov.in/PensionManagement/Public/Online_Request_For_pension.aspx)on the &quot;Online Apply for Pension Scheme&quot;. **Step 3**:Select District, Local Are, and enter Samagram ID. **Step 4**: Select the online application option. **Step 5**: Fill out the application form. **Step 6**: Upload the required documents. **Step 7**: Submit.

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Department of Social Justice and Empowerment of Persons with Disabilities.
