# Funeral Support Scheme (HPBOCWWB)

**Short Title:** FSPHPBOCWWB

**Scheme Brief Description:** The "Funeral Support Scheme" under the Labour and Employment Department, Himachal Pradesh, provides ₹20,000 to the nominees or dependents of registered beneficiaries to support funeral expenses.

**Scheme For:** Family

**Tags:** Construction Workers, Building Worker, Funeral, Death, Financial Assistance

The &quot;Funeral Support Scheme,&quot; launched by the Himachal Pradesh Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board (HPBOCWWB) under the Government of Himachal Pradesh, provides financial assistance of ₹20,000/- (twenty thousand) to the nominees or dependents of a registered beneficiary in the event of the worker&#39;s death.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Financial assistance

**Where it applies:** Himachal Pradesh; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** ₹20,000/- is provided for a registered worker&#39;s death.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**How to apply:** **Step 1:** The interested applicant should visit the Labor Welfare Office (during office hours) and request a hard copy of the prescribed [application form](https://bocw.hp.nic.in/pdf/formno37_A1b.pdf) from the concerned authority. **Step 2:** In the application form, fill in all the mandatory fields, paste a passport-sized photograph (signed across, if required), and attach copies of all the mandatory documents (self-attested, if required). **Step 3:** Submit the duly filled and signed application form along with a wage slip or appointment letter from the employer, verifying 90 days of construction work. If unavailable, a certificate from a local authority (such as a councilor, executive officer, or Panchayat Secretary) can be submitted to verify employment. **Step 4:** After submitting the documents and payment, the worker will receive an acknowledgment receipt confirming their registration.
Mode: Offline.

**Additional Application Processes:** **Process 2:** **Scheme Application Process:** **Step 1:** The applicant must visit the [official website](https://bocw.hp.nic.in/index.aspx) of the Himachal Pradesh Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board. **Step 2:** Click on the option &quot;[Apply for Benefits.](https://bocw.hp.nic.in/PublicPages/ApplyForSchemes.aspx)&quot; **Step 3:** Enter your beneficiary details and date of birth, then click &quot;Search&quot; to view your information. **Step 4:** Select the scheme you wish to apply for to avail of the benefits. **Step 5:** Enter all required information and upload the necessary documents as prompted. **Step 6:** Finally, click on &quot;Submit Application&quot; to complete the process.  **Application Tracking:** Applicants can track their application status online through the[ official website](https://bocw.hp.nic.in/index.aspx) of the Himachal Pradesh Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board. Relevant updates will be sent to the registered mobile number. 

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Labour and Employment Department, Himachal Pradesh.
