# Shram Yogi Prasad Scheme

**Short Title:** SYPS

**Scheme Brief Description:** The "Shram Yogi Prasad Scheme'' by the BOCW Welfare Board, Labour Department, U.T. Administration of DNH & DD, is a welfare scheme that provides mid day meal and breakfast to the BOCW registered construction workers.

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Labour, Building Worker, Construction Worker, Mid Day Meal, Food, Breakfast

The &amp;quot;Shram Yogi Prasad Scheme&amp;quot; by the Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board, Labour Department, U.T. Administration of Dadra and Nagar Haveli and Daman and Diu, is a welfare scheme for the registered workers of the board. Under this scheme, the Board shall provide mid day meal and breakfast to the BOCW registered construction workers under the scheme at a concessional rate of ₹5/- per worker everyday. &lt;br&gt;

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Food Security / Public Distribution System

**Where it applies:** Dadra & Nagar Haveli and Daman & Diu; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - **Mid-Day Meal and Breakfast Supply:** Mid day meal and Breakfast at a concessional rate of ₹5/- per worker everyday. The time for supply of mid day meal and breakfast are as under: - **Supply of Breakfast:** 07:00 A.M to 08:00 A.M. (Place near HDFC Bank, Silvassa and Kilvani Naka, etc). - **Supply of mid day meal:** 12:00 P.M to 02:00 P.M.

**Benefit Type:** In Kind

**How to apply:** &gt; **Registration Process as a Building/ Construction Worker:** **Step 1:** Application for registration is to be made in the prescribed form and is to be accompanied by prescribed documents and a fee of not more than ₹50/-. **Step 2:** Application for registration has to be made to the officer authorized by the Board on this behalf. &gt; **Application Process of the Welfare Scheme:** **Step 1:** Any registered worker can make an application in the prescribed [Form-XII](https://www.daman.nic.in/websites/Printing-press-Daman/documents/2020/Extraordinary/Extra%20Ordinary%20No.%2029.pdf) (Refer Page No: 17) along with the mandatory documents (self attested, if required). **Step 2:** Application shall be submitted to the Member Secretary (BOCWWB) for this benefit. &gt; **Helpdesk:** BOCW Department: EMAIL ID: labour-dnh@nic.in PHONE NUMBER : 0260 - 2642374 ADDRESS: Old Circuit House Building, Opposite Bal Bhawan, Silvassa, Dadra and Nagar Haveli Pin:- 396230
Mode: Offline.

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Labour & Employment Department.
