# NEW SWARNIMA (Gopalak Nigam)

**Short Title:** NSGGN

**Scheme Brief Description:** Self-employment loan scheme for Rabari and Bharwad women in Gujarat, offering up to ₹2 lakh at 5% annual interest to promote economic independence.

**Tags:** Rabari Bharwad Scheme, Gopalak Vikas Nigam, Women Entrepreneurs, Gujarat Loan Scheme

The **New Swarnima Scheme** is a financial empowerment initiative by the **Gujarat Gopalak Vikas Nigam** under the **Social Justice and Empowerment Department**. It aims to provide **100% loan** support to women from the **Rabari and Bharwad communities** to promote self-employment and entrepreneurship. Eligible women between the **age of 21 and 45 years**, with an **annual family income up to ₹3 lakh**, can receive a **loan of up to ₹2 lakh** at a subsidized **interest rate of 5% per annum** to start a business of their choice. The scheme is designed to increase economic independence and livelihood generation among backward class women.

**Who is this for:** Individual, Business Entity; Category: Business & Entrepreneurship; Sector: Setting up / start-up / entrepreneurship

**Where it applies:** Gujarat; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - **100% loan** assistance provided to eligible women- Loan amount up to **₹2.00 lakh**- Interest rate fixed at **5% per annum**- Loan can be used to **start a business** of the beneficiary’s choice- Meant for **economically backward women** of specific castes- **Encourages entrepreneurship and self-reliance**

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**How to apply:** **Step 1:** Visit the official [**DAADC**](https://daadconline.gujarat.gov.in/) online portal. **Step 2:** **Register/log in** using your credentials. **Step 3:** Fill out the application form with accurate personal and scheme-specific details. **Step 4:** Upload the **required documents** and submit.
Mode: Online.

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Social Justice and Empowerment Department.
