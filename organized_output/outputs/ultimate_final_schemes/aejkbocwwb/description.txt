# Academic Education (JKBOCWWB)

**Short Title:** AEJKBOCWWB

**Scheme Brief Description:** The "Academic Education" scheme by the JKBOCW Welfare Board, Labour Department, Jammu & Kashmir, provides financial assistance to regular students from Class 9th to Post-Graduation.

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Worker, Construction, Labour, Student, Education

The &quot;Academic Education&quot; scheme by the Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board, Labour Department, Jammu &amp; Kashmir, provides financial assistance to the children of the registered workers. Under this scheme, regular students in Class 9th to 10th receive ₹4,500/-, Class 11th to 12th receive ₹6,000/-, undergraduate students receive ₹10,000/-, and postgraduate students receive ₹15,000/-. 

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Education & Learning; Sector: Universities and higher education

**Where it applies:** Jammu and Kashmir; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** **Financial Assistance based on the Educational Qualification -** 1. Class 9th to 10th: ₹4,500/-. 1. Class 11th to 12th: ₹ 6,000/-. 1. Undergraduate: ₹10,000/-. 1. Postgraduate: ₹15,000/-.

**Benefit Type:** Cash
