# Free Bus Pass for Media Persons

**Short Title:** FBPMP

**Scheme Brief Description:** "Free Bus Pass for Media Persons" scheme by the Dept of Information & Publicity, Puducherry, aims to provide a free bus pass facility to media persons working in Puducherry in Puducherry Road Transport Corporation buses (except town buses) to undertake journeys for their professional requirements.

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Bus, Road, Transport, Travel, Media

The &quot;Free Bus Pass for Media Persons&quot; scheme, introduced by the Department of Information &amp; Publicity, Union Territory of Puducherry, aims to provide a free bus pass facility to media persons working in Puducherry in Puducherry Road Transport Corporation buses (except town buses) to undertake journeys for their professional requirements.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Transport & Infrastructure; Sector: Public transport and private vehicles

**Where it applies:** Puducherry; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** Free bus pass (valid for one year) for professional travel needs. **Time Limit for Issue of a Fresh Bus Pass:** Two weeks from the date of application. **Time Limit for Renewal of the Expired Bus Pass:** Two days form the date of application.

**Benefit Type:** In Kind

**How to apply:** For both Fresh Applications and Renewal Applications, the applicant should write a formal letter, attach copies of all the mandatory documents (self-attest, if required), and submit the same to - **The Director** **Department of Information &amp; Publicity** **18, Belcombe Street, Puducherry - 605 001** **+91-413-2334398, 2336415, 2337078** **info.pon@nic.in** _*The Office Timings are Monday to Friday, 08:45 am to 01:00 pm &amp; 02:00 pm to 05:45 pm._
Mode: Offline.

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: District Industries Centre Organization.
