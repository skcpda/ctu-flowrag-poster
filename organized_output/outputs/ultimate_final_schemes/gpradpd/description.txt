# Grant for Purchase and Repair of Various Assistive Devices/Equipment for Persons with Disabilities

**Short Title:** GPRVADEPWD

**Scheme Brief Description:** Under this scheme, a grant of up to ₹3,500 is provided for the purchase of individual aids, which may be increased to a maximum of ₹7,000 in special cases. Additionally, ₹500 is provided for the repair of prosthetic limbs and other assistive equipment.

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Financial Assistance, Devices, Person With Disability, Differently-abled Person, PwD, Divyang

The scheme “Grant for Purchase and Repair of Various Assistive Devices/Equipment for Persons with Disabilities” is implemented by the Social Welfare Department, Government of Uttarakhand. The scheme aims to enhance the mobility and functional independence of persons with disabilities by providing financial assistance for the purchase and repair of essential assistive devices. Under this scheme, a grant of up to ₹3,500 (maximum ₹7,000 in special cases) is provided for the purchase of individual aids and ₹500 for the repair of prosthetic limbs and assistive equipment.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Aids/Appliances

**Where it applies:** Uttarakhand; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** > **Grant Amount:** - ₹50 to ₹3,500 for the purchase of assistive devices (maximum ₹7,000 in special cases). - ₹500 for repair of artificial limbs and assistive equipment.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**How to apply:** The medical officer must recommend the applicant for fitting with a prosthetic limb or hearing aid. The applicants should complete the application form himself/herself and submit it to the concerned Assistant Social Welfare Officer. The District Social Welfare Officer’s office will process the application as per the prescribed rules. Thereafter, payment will be made to the beneficiary through the treasury. 
Mode: Offline.

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Department Of Social Welfare.
