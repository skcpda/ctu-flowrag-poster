The National Scheme for Assistance to the Transgender Persons (NSATSP) aims to provide social security and welfare to transgender individuals in India. 

The objective of the scheme is to ensure the overall development and empowerment of transgender persons. 

The benefits include financial assistance for education and skill development. 

Transgender persons are eligible for this scheme.
