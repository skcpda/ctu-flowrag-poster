**Kisan Credit Card Scheme**

The Kisan Credit Card Scheme was launched by the Government of India in 1998. 

It aims to provide farmers with timely access to credit. 

The scheme facilitates easy and affordable credit to farmers for agricultural activities. 

The primary objective is to meet the short-term credit needs of farmers. 

It also aims to provide financial support for the purchase of agricultural inputs. 

The scheme helps in ensuring that farmers do not fall into the trap of moneylenders. 

Farmers can avail of credit for various purposes such as crop production and post-harvest expenses. 

The Kisan Credit Card can also be used for purchasing equipment and livestock. 

Farmers can withdraw cash from ATMs using their Kisan Credit Card. 

The scheme offers a credit limit based on the farmer's landholding and income. 

The interest rates under the scheme are lower compared to traditional loans. 

The scheme also provides an insurance cover for the cardholder. 

The insurance cover is provided under the Pradhan Mantri Fasal Bima Yojana. 

The Kisan Credit Card is available to all farmers including tenant farmers and sharecroppers. 

Eligibility is based on the ownership of agricultural land. 

Farmers must have a valid land ownership document to apply. 

The application process involves submitting an application form to the nearest bank branch. 

Farmers need to provide necessary documents such as identity proof and land records. 

Banks may also require a credit history and income proof. 

The processing time for the application is generally quick. 

Farmers can expect to receive their credit card within a few days of application approval. 

The Kisan Credit Card is valid for five years and can be renewed.
