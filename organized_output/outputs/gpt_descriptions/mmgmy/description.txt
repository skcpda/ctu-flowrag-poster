Mahatma Gandhi National Rural Employment Guarantee Act is also known as MGNREGA. 

It was enacted in 2005 to enhance livelihood security in rural areas. 

The primary objective is to provide at least 100 days of guaranteed wage employment in a financial year to every rural household. 

The scheme aims to improve livelihood security and create durable assets in rural areas. 

Benefits include wage payments for unskilled manual work. 

The scheme also promotes social inclusion and empowerment of marginalized communities. 

Eligibility is limited to adult members of rural households. 

Households must apply for a job card to avail of the benefits. 

The application process involves submitting a request to the local Gram Panchayat. 

The timeline for wage payments is typically within 15 days of work completion. 

The Ministry of Rural Development is the implementing authority for MGNREGA. 

State governments are responsible for the execution of the scheme at the local level. 

The scheme is funded by the central and state governments on a cost-sharing basis. 

MGNREGA also includes provisions for grievance redressal and transparency measures.
