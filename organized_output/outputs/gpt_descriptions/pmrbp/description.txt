The Pradhan Mantri Rasoi Yojana (PMRBP) was launched to provide nutritious meals to the underprivileged sections of society. 

The objective is to ensure that no one goes hungry and to improve the nutritional status of the poor. 

The scheme aims to provide affordable meals at a subsidized rate. 

Beneficiaries can access meals at designated centers. 

Eligibility is primarily targeted towards the economically weaker sections of society. 

The application process involves registration at local centers or through designated online platforms. 

The meals are provided on a daily basis. 

The implementing authority for this scheme is the Ministry of Food and Public Distribution. 

The scheme also emphasizes community participation for effective implementation. 

It aims to create awareness about nutrition and healthy eating habits. 

The initiative is part of a broader effort to combat hunger and malnutrition in India.
