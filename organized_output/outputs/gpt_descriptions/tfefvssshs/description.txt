The scheme is known as the Tribal Forest Empowerment and Forest Rights Scheme for Scheduled Tribes and Scheduled Castes.

It aims to empower tribal communities by recognizing their rights over forest resources.

The objectives include ensuring sustainable livelihoods for tribal populations and promoting conservation of forest resources.

Benefits include financial assistance for forest-based livelihoods and support for sustainable practices.

Eligibility is primarily for Scheduled Tribes and Scheduled Castes residing in forest areas.

The application process involves submitting a proposal to the local forest department or designated authority.

The timeline for assistance may vary based on project approval and implementation schedules.

The implementing authorities include the Ministry of Tribal Affairs and state forest departments.

Additional details may include community participation in decision-making and capacity-building initiatives.
