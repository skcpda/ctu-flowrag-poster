The Pradhan Mantri Swasthya Suraksha Yojana for Special Children with Disabilities and Elderly Persons (PMSSCP) aims to provide healthcare support to special children and elderly persons with disabilities. 

The objective of the scheme is to ensure access to quality healthcare services for vulnerable populations. 

The benefits include financial assistance for medical treatments and rehabilitation services. 

Eligibility is generally determined based on age and disability criteria as defined by the government. 

The application process typically involves submitting a form along with necessary documentation to the designated authority. 

The timeline for assistance may vary based on the specific services required. 

The implementing authorities include the Ministry of Health and Family Welfare and local health departments. 

Additional details may include specific healthcare services covered under the scheme and the process for grievance redressal.
