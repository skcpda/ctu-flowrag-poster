The Pradhan Mantri Matsya Sampada Yojana (PMMSY) is a scheme launched by the Government of India. 

It aims to enhance fish production and productivity in the country. 

The scheme focuses on sustainable development of fisheries and aquaculture. 

It seeks to increase the income of fishers and fish farmers. 

The benefits include financial assistance for various activities related to fisheries. 

This includes infrastructure development and technology support. 

Eligibility criteria include individuals and groups involved in fisheries. 

State governments and Union Territories can also apply for assistance under the scheme. 

The application process involves submitting proposals to the respective state fisheries departments. 

The timeline for the implementation of projects varies based on the nature of activities. 

The scheme is implemented by the Department of Fisheries under the Ministry of Fisheries Animal Husbandry and Dairying. 

It is part of the larger initiative to promote Blue Revolution in India. 

The scheme also emphasizes the importance of fish as a source of nutrition and livelihood. 

It aims to create employment opportunities in the fisheries sector. 

The PMMSY is expected to contribute significantly to the overall economic growth of the country.
