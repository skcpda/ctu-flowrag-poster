The Mukhyamantri Maatru Vandana Yojana (MMVY) is a welfare scheme launched by the Government of Madhya Pradesh. 

It aims to provide financial assistance to pregnant and lactating women. 

The objective is to improve maternal and child health by encouraging institutional deliveries. 

Under this scheme eligible beneficiaries receive a cash incentive of INR 6,000. 

The assistance is provided in three installments. 

The first installment is given at the time of registration of pregnancy. 

The second installment is provided after the birth of the child. 

The third installment is given after the child receives the first vaccination. 

Eligibility criteria include being a resident of Madhya Pradesh. 

Beneficiaries must be pregnant or lactating women. 

They should not be availing benefits under any other similar scheme. 

The application process involves filling out a form available at Anganwadi centers. 

Beneficiaries must provide necessary documents such as identity proof and pregnancy registration. 

The scheme is implemented by the Department of Women and Child Development, Madhya Pradesh. 

The scheme is reviewed periodically to ensure effective implementation and reach.
