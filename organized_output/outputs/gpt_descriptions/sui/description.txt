The SUI scheme stands for the Scheme for Urban Infrastructure. 

It aims to enhance urban infrastructure in various cities across India. 

The objective is to improve the quality of life for urban residents through better facilities. 

Benefits include the development of roads drainage systems and public amenities. 

The scheme provides financial assistance to local bodies for infrastructure projects. 

Eligibility criteria typically include urban local bodies and municipal corporations. 

The application process involves submitting project proposals to the respective state governments. 

The timeline for project implementation varies based on the specific project and funding approval. 

The Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs is the primary implementing authority for this scheme. 

Additional details may include collaboration with state governments and local authorities for effective implementation.
