The scheme is known as the Scheme for Financial Support to Farmers for Purchase of Breeding Bulls and Buffaloes.

It was launched to enhance the availability of quality breeding bulls and buffaloes for farmers.

The objective is to improve livestock productivity and increase farmers' income.

The scheme provides financial assistance to farmers for the purchase of breeding bulls and buffaloes.

Eligible beneficiaries include small and marginal farmers.

Farmers must be registered under the relevant agricultural department.

The application process involves submitting a form along with necessary documents to the local agricultural office.

The timeline for processing applications may vary by state.

The scheme is implemented by the Department of Animal Husbandry and Dairying.

Financial assistance is provided directly to the beneficiaries' bank accounts.

The scheme aims to promote better livestock management practices among farmers.
