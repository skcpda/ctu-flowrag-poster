The Madhya Pradesh Employment Support Scheme (MPESS) aims to provide financial assistance to unemployed youth in Madhya Pradesh. 

The objective of the scheme is to enhance the employability of youth and support them during their job search. 

Benefits include a monthly financial assistance amount to eligible beneficiaries for a specified duration. 

Eligibility criteria include being a resident of Madhya Pradesh and being unemployed. 

The application process involves submitting an online application through the official government portal. 

The timeline for disbursement of benefits is typically monthly for the duration of the assistance period. 

The scheme is implemented by the Department of Skill Development and Employment of Madhya Pradesh.
