The scheme is known as the Swami Kalyan Singh Sewa Ghar Yojana (SKSSGIS). 

It was launched by the Government of Uttar Pradesh. 

The background of the scheme is to provide shelter and support to the homeless and destitute individuals. 

The objective is to ensure that every homeless person has access to basic amenities and a safe place to stay. 

The scheme aims to provide food clothing and medical assistance to the beneficiaries. 

It also focuses on rehabilitation and reintegration of the homeless into society. 

Eligibility criteria include being a homeless individual or a destitute person. 

There may be specific age and income criteria that need to be met. 

The application process typically involves submitting an application form at designated offices. 

Beneficiaries may also need to provide identification and proof of homelessness. 

The scheme operates continuously and does not have a specific timeline for applications. 

The implementing authority is the Department of Social Welfare of the Government of Uttar Pradesh. 

Additional details about the scheme may include the establishment of Sewa Ghars in various districts. 

These centers provide not only shelter but also vocational training and counseling services.
