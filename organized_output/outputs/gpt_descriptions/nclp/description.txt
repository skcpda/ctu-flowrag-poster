The National Child Labour Project (NCLP) is an initiative by the Government of India. 

It was launched in 1988 to address the issue of child labor in the country. 

The primary objective is to eliminate child labor and provide educational opportunities for children engaged in labor. 

The scheme aims to rehabilitate child laborers by providing them with formal education and vocational training. 

Benefits include educational support through special schools and vocational training programs. 

Children are also provided with mid-day meals and health care facilities. 

Eligibility criteria include children aged 6 to 14 years who are engaged in labor. 

The application process involves identification of child laborers by local authorities and enrollment in NCLP schools. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Labour and Employment. 

State governments and local bodies are responsible for executing the program at the grassroots level. 

The NCLP operates continuously with periodic assessments to evaluate its effectiveness.
