SPADDA is an initiative aimed at enhancing the livelihood of the rural population in India. 

The scheme focuses on the development of sustainable agricultural practices and improving the income of farmers. 

It aims to provide financial assistance and support for various agricultural activities. 

The benefits include access to modern farming techniques and resources. 

Farmers are provided with training and capacity-building programs. 

Eligibility is generally limited to farmers and agricultural workers in rural areas. 

The application process typically involves registration through local agricultural offices. 

The timeline for assistance may vary depending on the specific program under SPADDA. 

Implementing authorities include the Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers' Welfare at the central level. 

State governments also play a crucial role in the implementation of the scheme.
