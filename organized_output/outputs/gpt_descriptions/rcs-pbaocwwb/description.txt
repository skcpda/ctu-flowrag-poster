The RCS-PBAOCWWB scheme is the Rajiv Gandhi Shramik Kalyan Yojana for the welfare of construction workers. 

It aims to provide financial assistance and social security to construction workers in India. 

The objectives include improving the living standards of construction workers and ensuring their welfare. 

Benefits include financial assistance for medical emergencies education and housing. 

Eligible beneficiaries are construction workers registered under the Building and Other Construction Workers Act 1996. 

The application process involves registration with the respective state welfare boards. 

The timeline for disbursal of benefits varies based on the specific assistance requested. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Labour and Employment along with state welfare boards. 

Additional details include the requirement for workers to provide proof of employment in the construction sector.
