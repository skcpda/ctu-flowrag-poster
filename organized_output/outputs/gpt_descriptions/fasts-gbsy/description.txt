The Fasts-GBSY scheme is an initiative launched by the Government of India aimed at improving the nutritional status of the population.

The full form of Fasts-GBSY is "Fast Track Scheme for Government of India’s Beneficiary Schemes for Youth."

This scheme is designed to provide a platform for the youth in India to access various government welfare schemes.

The Fasts-GBSY scheme aims to bridge this gap by providing a centralized platform for information dissemination.
