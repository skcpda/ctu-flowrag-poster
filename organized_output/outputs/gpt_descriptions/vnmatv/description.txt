The VNMATV scheme is an initiative by the Government of India aimed at promoting the welfare of farmers. 

It focuses on enhancing the productivity and income of farmers through various agricultural interventions. 

The objectives include improving access to modern agricultural practices and technologies. 

The scheme provides financial assistance for purchasing agricultural inputs and equipment. 

It also offers training and capacity-building programs for farmers. 

Eligibility is generally limited to farmers and agricultural laborers. 

The application process typically involves submitting a form to the local agricultural office. 

The timeline for assistance may vary based on the specific intervention and local implementation. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers' Welfare. 

Additional details regarding specific benefits or assistance may vary by region.
