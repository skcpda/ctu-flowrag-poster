The UEA scheme stands for the Urban Employment Scheme. 

It was launched to provide employment opportunities in urban areas. 

The primary objective is to enhance livelihood security for urban poor. 

The scheme aims to create a demand-driven employment model. 

It focuses on providing work for at least 100 days in a financial year. 

Benefits include wage payments for work done under the scheme. 

Assistance is provided to individuals and families in need of employment. 

Eligibility criteria include being a resident of an urban area. 

Applicants must belong to economically weaker sections. 

The application process typically involves registration with local authorities. 

Individuals may need to provide proof of residence and income. 

The timeline for employment generation is based on demand and availability of work. 

The implementing authorities include state and local urban bodies. 

The scheme is part of a broader initiative to reduce urban poverty. 

It also aims to improve infrastructure and public services in urban areas. 

The UEA scheme is aligned with the goals of sustainable urban development.
