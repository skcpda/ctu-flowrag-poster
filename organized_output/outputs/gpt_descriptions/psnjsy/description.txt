The Pradhan Mantri Shram Yogi Maandhan Yojana (PMSYM) is a pension scheme launched by the Government of India. 

It aims to provide social security to the unorganized sector workers. 

The objective is to ensure a monthly pension of Rs. 3000 after the age of 60 years. 

The scheme benefits workers in the unorganized sector such as street vendors and domestic workers. 

Eligible beneficiaries must be between the ages of 18 and 40 years. 

They should have a monthly income of less than Rs. 15,000. 

The application process involves filling out a form available at Common Service Centers or online through the official portal. 

The pension is paid monthly after the beneficiary reaches the age of 60. 

The Ministry of Labour and Employment is the implementing authority for this scheme. 

The scheme also includes a provision for the spouse to receive the pension in case of the beneficiary's demise. 

The contribution to the pension fund is shared between the beneficiary and the government. 

The scheme was launched in February 2019.
