The Mukhyamantri Swavalamban Yojana (MSESU) aims to promote self-employment among the youth in the state of Rajasthan. 

The objective is to provide financial assistance and support to individuals seeking to start their own businesses. 

The scheme offers a subsidy on the investment made in new enterprises. 

Eligible beneficiaries include youth aged between 18 and 35 years who are residents of Rajasthan. 

They must have a viable business plan and should not be engaged in any other government-sponsored self-employment scheme. 

The application process involves submitting a detailed project report along with necessary documents to the concerned department. 

Applications can be submitted online through the official government portal or at designated offices. 

The timeline for approval and disbursement of funds may vary based on the completeness of the application and verification processes. 

The implementing authority for this scheme is the Department of Industries in Rajasthan. 

Additional details regarding the exact subsidy percentage and the maximum limit of financial assistance may vary and should be checked on official sources.
