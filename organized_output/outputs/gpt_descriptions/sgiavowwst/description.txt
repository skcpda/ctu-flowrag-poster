The scheme SGIAVOWWST stands for the Scheme for Grant of Incentive to the Artisans and Weavers of the Weaving Sector. 

It aims to provide financial assistance to artisans and weavers in the handloom and handicraft sectors. 

The objective is to enhance the income and livelihood of artisans and weavers. 

The scheme offers financial incentives to eligible artisans and weavers for their craftsmanship and production. 

Benefits include direct financial support to improve their economic conditions. 

Eligibility criteria include being a registered artisan or weaver under the relevant government programs. 

The application process typically involves submitting an application form along with necessary documentation to the designated authority. 

The timeline for disbursement of benefits may vary based on the processing of applications. 

The implementing authority for this scheme is the Ministry of Textiles, Government of India.
