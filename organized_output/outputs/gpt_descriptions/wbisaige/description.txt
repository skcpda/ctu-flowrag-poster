The West Bengal Integrated Scheme for Agricultural Income Generation and Employment (Wbisaige) aims to enhance agricultural productivity and generate employment in rural areas. 

The objective of Wbisaige is to provide financial assistance and support to farmers and agricultural workers. 

The scheme offers benefits such as subsidies for agricultural inputs and equipment. 

It also provides training and capacity-building programs for farmers. 

Eligibility for the scheme includes farmers and agricultural laborers from West Bengal. 

The application process typically involves submitting an application form to the local agricultural office.
