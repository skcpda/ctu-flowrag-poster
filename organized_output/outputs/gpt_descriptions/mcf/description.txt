The MCF scheme stands for the Mahatma Gandhi National Rural Employment Guarantee Act (MGNREGA) scheme. 

It was launched to provide a legal guarantee for at least 100 days of unskilled wage employment in a financial year to every rural household. 

The primary objective is to enhance livelihood security in rural areas. 

The scheme aims to create durable assets and strengthen the livelihood resource base of rural households. 

Benefits include the provision of wage employment and the creation of assets that contribute to the rural economy. 

Eligible beneficiaries are rural households that demand unskilled wage employment. 

The application process involves submitting a job card application to the local Gram Panchayat. 

The timeline for employment is based on the demand for work and can be availed throughout the year. 

The Ministry of Rural Development is the implementing authority for this scheme. 

State governments and local bodies are responsible for the execution and monitoring of the scheme at the ground level.
