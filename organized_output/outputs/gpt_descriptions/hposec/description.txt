The Haryana Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana Urban Scheme is a significant initiative under the Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana aimed at providing affordable housing to the urban poor in Haryana.

This scheme is part of the larger national mission to ensure housing for all by the year 2022.

The Haryana government launched this scheme to address the housing shortage in urban areas.

The scheme aims to provide financial assistance to eligible beneficiaries for the construction or enhancement of their houses.

The primary objective of the Haryana Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana Urban Scheme is to promote affordable housing for the economically weaker sections of society.

It seeks to provide a pucca house with basic amenities to all urban poor.

The scheme also aims to improve the living conditions of the urban poor by providing them with proper housing.

Under this scheme, financial assistance is provided in the form of a subsidy on home loans.

The subsidy is provided on the interest rate of the home loan taken by the beneficiaries.

The amount of subsidy can vary based on the income category of the beneficiaries.

There are three income categories defined under the scheme.

These categories are Economically Weaker Section or EWS which includes families with an annual income of up to Rs 3 lakh.

The second category is Low Income Group or LIG which includes families with an annual income between Rs 3 lakh and Rs 6 lakh.

The third category is Middle Income Group or MIG which is further divided into MIG-I and MIG-II.

MIG-I includes families with an annual income between Rs 6 lakh and Rs 12 lakh.

MIG-II includes families with an annual income between Rs 12 lakh and Rs 18 lakh.

The benefits under the scheme include a subsidy of up to Rs 2.67 lakh on home loans for EWS and LIG categories.

For the MIG categories the subsidy is up to Rs 2.35 lakh for MIG-I and Rs 1.50 lakh for MIG-II.

The scheme also provides for the construction of houses on land owned by the beneficiaries.

The beneficiaries can also avail of the benefit for the purchase of ready-to-move-in houses.

Eligibility criteria for the Haryana Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana Urban Scheme include being a resident of Haryana.

The applicant must belong to the EWS or LIG or MIG categories as defined by the scheme.

The applicant must not own a pucca house in their name or in the name of any family member.

The applicant must have a valid Aadhaar number for identification.

The application process for the scheme is online and can be completed through the official website.

Applicants need to fill out the application form available on the website.

They must provide all necessary documents including proof of income and identification.

Once the application is submitted it is reviewed by the concerned authorities.

Eligible applicants are then notified about the approval of their application.

The timeline for the approval of applications can vary based on the number of applications received.

Typically the process may take a few weeks to a few months.

The subsidy amount is credited directly to the bank account of the beneficiary.
