**Scheme Name: Marriage of Daughter of SC Widow**

**Context or Background**  
The Marriage of Daughter of SC Widow scheme is designed to support widows belonging to Scheduled Castes in India.  
This initiative recognizes the social and economic challenges faced by widows in the Scheduled Caste community.  
It aims to provide financial assistance for the marriage of their daughters.  
The scheme is part of the broader efforts by the Indian government to empower marginalized sections of society.  
It seeks to alleviate the financial burden associated with marriage ceremonies.  
The scheme is aligned with the government's commitment to gender equality and women's empowerment.  

**Objectives**  
The primary objective of the scheme is to provide financial support for the marriage of daughters of widows from Scheduled Castes.  
It aims to ensure that financial constraints do not hinder the marriage prospects of these daughters.  
The scheme also seeks to promote social dignity and respect for widows and their families.  
By providing assistance, the government aims to reduce the prevalence of child marriage among vulnerable communities.  
It strives to enhance the socio-economic status of widows and their families.  

**Benefits and Assistance**  
Under this scheme, eligible beneficiaries receive a one-time financial grant for the marriage of their daughters.  
The amount of financial assistance provided is determined by the government and may vary over time.  
This financial support can be used to cover various expenses related to the marriage ceremony.  
The assistance is intended to help families manage costs such as venue, catering, and other wedding-related expenses.  
The scheme also aims to promote the dignity of the marriage ceremony by providing adequate financial resources.  

**Eligibility**  
To be eligible for the scheme, the applicant must be a widow belonging to a Scheduled Caste.  
The daughter for whom the marriage assistance is sought must be of legal marriageable age as per Indian law.  
The family income of the applicant should fall below a certain threshold set by the government.  
The applicant must provide necessary documentation to prove her status as a widow and her SC caste.  
Additional criteria may include residency requirements and other socio-economic factors.  

**Application Process**  
Eligible applicants must submit an application to the designated authority in their district.  
The application form can typically be obtained from local government offices or online portals.  
Applicants are required to provide supporting documents such as identity proof, caste certificate, and income certificate.  
Once the application is submitted, it will be reviewed by the concerned authorities.  
Verification of documents and eligibility criteria will be conducted before approval.  
Upon approval, the financial assistance will be disbursed to the beneficiary's bank account.  

**Timeline or Frequency**  
The application process is generally open throughout the year.  
However, specific timelines for disbursement may vary based on the administrative processes of the implementing agency.  
Beneficiaries are advised to apply well in advance of the marriage date to ensure timely assistance.  
The financial assistance is provided as a one-time grant per eligible daughter.  

**Implementing Authorities or Agencies**  
The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment at the central level.  
State governments may also have their respective departments handling the scheme at the state level.  
Local government bodies such as Panchayati Raj institutions may play a role in the application process.  
Awareness and outreach programs may be conducted by these agencies to inform eligible beneficiaries about the scheme.
