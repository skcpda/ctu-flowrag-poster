The Mukhyamantri Mahila Kalyan Yojana for Rural Families Scheme is aimed at empowering rural women in India. 

The objective of the scheme is to provide financial assistance to women from rural families for various livelihood activities. 

The benefits include financial support for women to start small businesses and engage in income-generating activities. 

Eligibility criteria include being a woman from a rural family and meeting specific income thresholds set by the government. 

The application process involves submitting an application form along with required documents to the designated local authorities. 

The timeline for receiving assistance may vary based on the processing of applications. 

The scheme is implemented by the Department of Women and Child Development at the state level. 

Additional details regarding specific financial assistance amounts and types of eligible activities may vary by state.
