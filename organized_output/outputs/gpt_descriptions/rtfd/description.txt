The RTFD scheme stands for the "Rural Technology Development Fund." 

It was initiated to promote the development and dissemination of rural technologies. 

The objective is to enhance the quality of life in rural areas through technological advancements. 

The scheme aims to support innovative projects that can provide sustainable solutions to rural problems. 

Benefits include financial assistance for research and development of rural technologies. 

Eligible beneficiaries include individuals and organizations involved in rural development and technology. 

The application process involves submitting a project proposal to the relevant authorities. 

The timeline for project approval and funding disbursement varies based on the project specifics. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Rural Development. 

Additional details regarding specific funding amounts and project categories may be available through official sources.
