The scheme SDBWAW stands for the Scheme for Development of Women and Children in Rural Areas. 

It aims to provide comprehensive support for the development of women and children in rural regions. 

The objectives include improving the nutritional status of women and children. 

It also focuses on enhancing the educational and health status of these groups. 

The benefits include financial assistance for various programs aimed at women and children. 

Eligibility criteria typically include being a resident of rural areas and belonging to economically weaker sections. 

The application process involves submitting a form to the local implementing authority. 

The timeline for assistance may vary based on the specific program under the scheme. 

The implementing authorities include the Ministry of Women and Child Development and state government departments. 

Additional details on specific programs under the scheme may be available through official government sources.
