The Scheme for Training of Farmers in Agricultural Practices and Crop Livelihood (STFAPCL) aims to enhance the skills of farmers in agricultural practices. 

The objective is to improve the productivity and income of farmers through skill development and training. 

The scheme provides training programs that cover various aspects of agriculture including crop management and sustainable practices. 

Farmers who wish to participate in the program must be engaged in agricultural activities. 

The application process typically involves registration with local agricultural departments or training centers. 

The timeline for training sessions may vary based on the schedule set by implementing agencies. 

The Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers' Welfare is the primary authority responsible for implementing this scheme. 

Additional details regarding specific training modules and locations may be available through local agricultural offices.
