The One Stop Centre Scheme (OSC) aims to provide support and assistance to women affected by violence. 

It was launched by the Ministry of Women and Child Development in 2015. 

The objective is to provide integrated support and assistance to women in distress. 

This includes medical assistance legal aid psychological counseling and shelter. 

The scheme seeks to empower women and ensure their safety and dignity. 

Benefits include access to a range of services under one roof. 

Women can receive immediate assistance and support in crisis situations. 

Eligibility is open to all women who are victims of violence regardless of age or background. 

The application process can be initiated by the victim or any person on her behalf. 

Women can approach the nearest One Stop Centre for assistance. 

The centres operate in various states and union territories across India. 

They are managed by the respective state governments. 

The frequency of services is continuous as the centres operate 24/7. 

Implementing authorities include the Ministry of Women and Child Development and state governments. 

The scheme also collaborates with local NGOs and other stakeholders for effective implementation.
