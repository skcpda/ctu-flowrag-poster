The Welfare of Persons with Hearing Impairment and Other Disabilities Scheme is designed to support individuals with hearing impairments and other disabilities. 

The objective of the scheme is to enhance the quality of life for persons with hearing impairments and promote their integration into society. 

Benefits include financial assistance for hearing aids and other assistive devices. 

Eligibility is generally based on the degree of hearing impairment and the age of the applicant. 

The application process typically involves submitting a form along with necessary documentation such as medical certificates. 

The timeline for processing applications may vary based on the implementing agency. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment. 

Additional details may include awareness programs and training initiatives for beneficiaries.
