The RTAy-OSDP scheme is an initiative under the Ministry of Rural Development. 

It aims to provide skill development and employment opportunities to rural youth. 

The objective is to enhance the employability of rural populations through skill training. 

The scheme offers various benefits including vocational training and placement assistance. 

Eligible beneficiaries include rural youth aged between 15 and 35 years. 

The application process involves registering online through the official portal. 

The timeline for the training programs varies based on the specific skill set being taught. 

The implementing authority for this scheme is the Ministry of Rural Development. 

State governments may also play a role in the execution of the scheme at the local level.
