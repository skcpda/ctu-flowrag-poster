The scheme SKV SYUKBOCWWB stands for the Scheme for Knowledge and Value-based Skill Development for Youth and Women in the Construction and Building Sector. 

This scheme is aimed at enhancing the skill sets of youth and women in the construction and building sector. 

The objective is to provide training and skill development to improve employability and entrepreneurship opportunities in this sector. 

The benefits include skill training programs that lead to employment or self-employment. 

Eligible beneficiaries are youth and women who are interested in pursuing a career in the construction and building sector. 

The application process typically involves submitting an application form to the designated training centers or agencies. 

The timeline for the training programs may vary based on the specific courses offered. 

Implementing authorities include the Ministry of Skill Development and Entrepreneurship and associated training institutes. 

Additional details may include partnerships with industry stakeholders for better job placements.
