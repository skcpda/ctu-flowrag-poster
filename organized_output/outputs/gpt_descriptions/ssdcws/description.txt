The scheme is known as the Scheme for Skill Development in the Country for Women and Children (SSDCWS). 

It aims to empower women and children through skill development and vocational training. 

The objectives include enhancing employability and promoting self-reliance among women and children. 

Benefits include access to skill training programs and financial assistance for starting small businesses. 

Eligibility criteria typically include women and children from economically weaker sections. 

The application process generally involves submitting an application to the designated implementing agency. 

The timeline for the implementation of the scheme may vary based on the specific training programs offered.
