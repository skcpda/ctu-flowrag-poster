The Pradhan Mantri Matsya Sampada Yojana - Economic Boost to Marine Fisheries and Aquaculture (PMMSY-EBTMFVGUJ) is a government initiative aimed at enhancing the income and livelihood of fishers and fish farmers. 

The scheme focuses on sustainable development of the fisheries sector in India. 

It aims to increase fish production and productivity through various interventions. 

The benefits include financial assistance for infrastructure development and technology adoption. 

Eligible beneficiaries include fishers, fish farmers, and individuals involved in the fisheries sector. 

The application process typically involves submitting a proposal to the relevant state fisheries department. 

The timeline for fund disbursement and project implementation varies based on the specific project and state guidelines. 

The scheme is implemented by the Department of Fisheries under the Ministry of Fisheries Animal Husbandry and Dairying. 

Additional details regarding specific financial assistance amounts and project types may vary by state.
