The PISSUK scheme stands for the Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samman Nidhi Yojana for the State of Uttar Pradesh. 

It aims to provide financial support to small and marginal farmers in Uttar Pradesh. 

The objective is to enhance the income of farmers and ensure their financial security. 

Under this scheme eligible farmers receive direct cash transfers. 

The assistance provided is Rs 6,000 per year. 

This amount is disbursed in three equal installments. 

Eligibility criteria include being a small or marginal farmer. 

Farmers must own cultivable land. 

The scheme is targeted at farmers with landholdings of up to 2 hectares. 

The application process requires farmers to register online or through designated centers. 

Farmers need to provide necessary documents such as land records and identity proof. 

The scheme is implemented by the Department of Agriculture Uttar Pradesh. 

The frequency of assistance is annual with payments made in three installments. 

The scheme is part of a broader initiative to support farmers across India.
