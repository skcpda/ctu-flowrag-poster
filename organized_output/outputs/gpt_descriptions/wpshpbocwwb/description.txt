The Welfare Scheme for the Handicapped Persons of the Backward Classes and Weaker Sections is designed to provide financial assistance to individuals with disabilities. 

The objective is to enhance the quality of life for persons with disabilities from backward classes and weaker sections. 

The benefits include financial assistance for education training and rehabilitation. 

Eligibility is determined based on disability status and belonging to backward classes or weaker sections. 

The application process involves submitting a form along with necessary documents to the designated authority. 

The timeline for processing applications may vary based on the implementing agency. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment at the central level and by respective state governments. 

Additional details may include specific financial aid amounts and types of assistance available under the scheme.
