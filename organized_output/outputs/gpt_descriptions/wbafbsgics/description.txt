The West Bengal Awas Yojana for Backward Classes Scheme is designed to provide housing assistance to economically weaker sections of backward classes in West Bengal. 

The objective of the scheme is to ensure that individuals from backward classes have access to affordable housing. 

The benefits include financial assistance for constructing or purchasing a house. 

Eligibility is limited to individuals belonging to backward classes who meet the income criteria set by the government. 

The application process involves submitting an application form along with required documents to the designated authority. 

The timeline for application processing and disbursement of funds may vary based on the specific case and local administrative procedures. 

The implementing authority for this scheme is the Department of Backward Classes Welfare, Government of West Bengal. 

Additional details regarding the specific amount of assistance or the exact documentation required may vary and should be checked with the local authorities.
