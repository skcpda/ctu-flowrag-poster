The West Bengal Tirtha Sathi Scheme is a welfare initiative by the Government of West Bengal. 

It aims to provide financial assistance to the elderly and the poor for pilgrimage purposes. 

The objective is to promote religious tourism and ensure that economically disadvantaged individuals can undertake pilgrimages. 

Eligible beneficiaries include senior citizens aged 60 years and above and individuals from economically weaker sections. 

The scheme provides a subsidy for travel expenses related to pilgrimage. 

The application process involves submitting an application form along with required documents to the designated authorities. 

The scheme is implemented by the West Bengal Minority Development and Finance Corporation. 

The frequency of assistance is based on the pilgrimage schedule and the availability of funds. 

The scheme also includes provisions for organizing group pilgrimages. 

The government aims to cover various religious sites across India under this scheme.
