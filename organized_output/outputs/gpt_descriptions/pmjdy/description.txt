Pradhan Mantri Jan Dhan Yojana (PMJDY) was launched on August 28 2014. 

The scheme aims to provide universal access to banking facilities. 

It seeks to promote financial inclusion among the underprivileged sections of society. 

The objectives include ensuring access to financial services such as banking savings and deposit accounts. 

It also aims to provide access to credit insurance and pension facilities. 

The benefits include a zero-balance savings account. 

Account holders receive a RuPay debit card with an inbuilt accident insurance cover. 

They are also eligible for an overdraft facility after a certain period. 

The scheme targets all Indian citizens especially those from low-income households. 

There is no specific income limit for eligibility. 

Individuals must be at least 10 years old to open an account. 

The application process involves visiting a bank branch or a business correspondent. 

Applicants need to provide valid identification and address proof. 

The accounts can be opened at any bank branch or through online platforms. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Finance Government of India. 

It is supported by various public sector banks private banks and regional rural banks. 

The scheme is ongoing with no specific end date. 

Regular monitoring and evaluation are conducted to assess its impact.
