The Mukhyamantri Tirth Yatra Scheme is an initiative by the Government of Madhya Pradesh. 

It aims to provide financial assistance to senior citizens for pilgrimage to religious sites. 

The primary objective is to promote spiritual tourism among the elderly population. 

Eligible beneficiaries include senior citizens aged 60 years and above. 

The scheme is specifically for residents of Madhya Pradesh. 

Financial assistance covers travel expenses to designated pilgrimage sites. 

The scheme includes destinations such as Varanasi and Ajmer Sharif. 

The application process requires interested individuals to submit an application form at designated offices. 

Documents such as age proof and identity verification are necessary for application. 

The scheme is implemented by the Department of Religious Trust and Endowments of Madhya Pradesh. 

The frequency of the yatra is determined by the government and may vary. 

Additional details about specific pilgrimage dates and arrangements are provided by the implementing authority.
