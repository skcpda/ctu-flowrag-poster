The OTOWS scheme stands for One Time One Window Scheme. 

It was launched by the Government of India to streamline the process of providing various welfare services to citizens. 

The objective of the scheme is to ensure that beneficiaries can access multiple services through a single window. 

The scheme aims to reduce bureaucratic hurdles and enhance service delivery efficiency. 

Benefits include simplified access to government services and reduced waiting times for beneficiaries. 

Eligibility criteria vary based on the specific services accessed under the scheme. 

The application process typically involves submitting necessary documents at designated service centers. 

The timeline for service delivery depends on the specific service being availed. 

The scheme is implemented by various government departments at both the central and state levels. 

Additional details about specific services and their requirements can be found on the official government portal.
