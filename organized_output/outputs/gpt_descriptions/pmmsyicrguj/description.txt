The Pradhan Mantri Matsya Sampada Yojana (PMMSY) is a flagship scheme launched by the Government of India. 

It aims to bring about the sustainable development of the fisheries sector in India. 

The objectives include enhancing fish production and productivity. 

It also focuses on increasing the income of fishers and fish farmers. 

The scheme promotes the development of infrastructure for fisheries. 

Benefits include financial assistance for various activities related to fisheries. 

This includes support for fish farming and aquaculture. 

It also provides assistance for the establishment of fish processing units. 

Eligibility criteria include being a registered fisher or fish farmer. 

Individuals or groups involved in fisheries activities can apply. 

The application process involves submitting a proposal to the relevant authorities. 

The proposals are evaluated based on the guidelines of the scheme. 

The timeline for disbursement of funds may vary based on project approval. 

The Ministry of Fisheries Animal Husbandry and Dairying is the implementing authority. 

State governments also play a role in the implementation of the scheme. 

The scheme is expected to enhance the livelihoods of fishers. 

It aims to promote sustainable fishing practices. 

The PMMSY is part of the broader vision of the Government of India to boost the blue economy.
