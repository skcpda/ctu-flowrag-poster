The MBS-HBOCWWB scheme is the Mahatma Gandhi Bunkar Bima Yojana. 

It is designed to provide financial assistance and social security to handloom weavers in India. 

The objective of the scheme is to offer life insurance coverage to handloom weavers. 

The scheme aims to ensure the welfare of weavers and their families in times of need. 

Beneficiaries receive a life insurance cover of Rs. 2 lakh. 

In case of the death of the insured weaver, the sum assured is paid to the nominee. 

Eligibility for the scheme includes being a registered handloom weaver. 

The applicant must be a member of a cooperative society or a self-help group. 

The application process involves filling out the prescribed application form. 

The form must be submitted to the respective state handloom department. 

The scheme is implemented by the Handloom and Textiles Department of each state. 

The frequency of benefits is contingent upon the occurrence of the insured event. 

The scheme aims to enhance the financial security of handloom weavers across India.
