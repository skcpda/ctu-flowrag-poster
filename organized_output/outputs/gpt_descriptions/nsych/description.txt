The National Scheme of Welfare for the Children of Persons Engaged in Hazardous Occupations (NSYCH) aims to provide support to children of workers engaged in hazardous occupations. 

The objective is to ensure the welfare and development of these children through various support mechanisms. 

Benefits include financial assistance for education and health care. 

The scheme also provides scholarships for children to encourage their education. 

Eligibility criteria include being a child of a worker engaged in hazardous occupations. 

The age limit for beneficiaries is typically from 0 to 18 years. 

The application process involves submitting a form along with necessary documents to the designated authorities. 

The timeline for processing applications may vary based on the implementing agency. 

The Ministry of Labour and Employment is the primary implementing authority for this scheme. 

Additional details may include provisions for vocational training and skill development for older children.
