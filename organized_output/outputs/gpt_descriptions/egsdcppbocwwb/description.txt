The scheme known as the EGS DC PP BOC WWB is an initiative under the Ministry of Rural Development in India. 

This scheme is designed to provide financial assistance to the beneficiaries who are engaged in the construction of rural infrastructure. 

The full form of the scheme is the Employment Generation Scheme for Development of Construction of Public Property by the Backward Classes Welfare Board. 

It aims to enhance the livelihood opportunities for individuals belonging to backward classes in rural areas.

This scheme seeks to address these challenges by providing them with employment opportunities.
