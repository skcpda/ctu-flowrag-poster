The SEBWODAP scheme stands for the Social Empowerment of Women through the Development of Agricultural Produce. 

This scheme is designed to empower women by enhancing their skills in agriculture and related sectors. 

The main objective is to increase the income and livelihood opportunities for women in rural areas. 

The scheme provides training and resources to women to help them engage in agricultural activities. 

It aims to promote self-help groups and cooperatives among women farmers. 

Eligible beneficiaries are women engaged in agriculture or related activities in rural areas. 

The application process typically involves registration with local agricultural offices or women's development agencies. 

The timeline for the implementation of the scheme varies based on the specific training programs and activities planned. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Women and Child Development in collaboration with state governments. 

Additional details about specific training modules and support services may be available through local offices.
