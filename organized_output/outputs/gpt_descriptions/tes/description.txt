The "TES" scheme refers to the "Technical Education Scheme" initiated by the Government of India. 

The context of the scheme is to enhance the quality of technical education in India. 

The objective is to improve the employability of students through skill development and vocational training. 

Benefits include financial assistance for institutions to upgrade infrastructure and training facilities. 

The scheme also aims to provide scholarships to students pursuing technical education. 

Eligibility criteria typically include students enrolled in recognized technical institutions. 

The application process involves institutions submitting proposals for funding to the relevant government authority. 

The timeline for funding and implementation varies based on the specific project and approval process. 

The implementing authority for the scheme is the Ministry of Education in India. 

Additional details may include partnerships with industry for curriculum development and internships.
