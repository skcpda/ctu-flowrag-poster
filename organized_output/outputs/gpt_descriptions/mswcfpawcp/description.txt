The scheme is known as the Mahatma Gandhi National Rural Employment Guarantee Act (MGNREGA) for Women Self-Help Groups and Farmers' Producer Organizations.

The context of the scheme is to enhance livelihood security in rural areas.

The objective is to provide at least 100 days of guaranteed wage employment in a financial year to every rural household.

The benefits include wage payments for work done and the promotion of self-reliance among women and farmers.

Eligibility is primarily for rural households seeking employment.

The application process involves submitting a job card application to the local Gram Panchayat.

The timeline for wage payments is typically within 15 days of work completion.

The implementing authorities include the Ministry of Rural Development and local Gram Panchayats.

The scheme also aims to empower women by promoting their participation in the workforce.
