The PMOCHRO scheme stands for the Prime Minister's Office for Child Health and Nutrition. 

This scheme was launched to address the issues of child health and nutrition in India. 

The objective of the scheme is to reduce malnutrition and improve health outcomes for children under the age of six. 

The scheme aims to provide nutritional support and health services to children and mothers. 

Benefits include access to supplementary nutrition, health check-ups, and immunization services. 

Eligibility criteria include children under six years of age and pregnant or lactating mothers. 

The application process typically involves registration with the local Anganwadi center. 

The timeline for assistance is continuous as services are provided on an ongoing basis. 

The implementing authority for this scheme is the Ministry of Women and Child Development. 

The scheme is part of a broader initiative to improve health and nutrition indicators in the country.
