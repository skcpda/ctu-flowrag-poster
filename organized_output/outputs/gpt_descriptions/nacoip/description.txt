The National AIDS Control Organization Integrated Programme (NACOIP) is a government initiative aimed at controlling and preventing the spread of HIV/AIDS in India. 

The primary objective of NACOIP is to reduce the incidence of HIV infections and to provide care and support for people living with HIV/AIDS. 

The scheme offers various benefits including access to antiretroviral therapy for those infected with HIV. 

It also provides support for awareness campaigns and education on prevention methods. 

Eligibility for the program includes individuals who are HIV positive and require treatment and support services. 

The application process typically involves registration at designated healthcare facilities or through outreach programs conducted by NACO. 

The program operates continuously with ongoing support and services provided to beneficiaries. 

NACOIP is implemented by the National AIDS Control Organization under the Ministry of Health and Family Welfare. 

The program collaborates with state governments and various non-governmental organizations to reach its objectives.
