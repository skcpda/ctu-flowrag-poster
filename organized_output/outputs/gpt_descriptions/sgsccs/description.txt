The Scheme for Grant of Subsidy for Construction of Houses for Scheduled Castes is known as SGSCCS. 

This scheme aims to provide financial assistance for the construction of houses for individuals belonging to Scheduled Castes. 

The objective is to improve housing conditions and ensure that Scheduled Castes have access to adequate shelter. 

The benefits include a subsidy for the construction of houses which reduces the financial burden on beneficiaries. 

Eligibility is limited to individuals belonging to Scheduled Castes who meet specific income criteria. 

The application process typically involves submitting an application form along with required documents to the relevant authority. 

The timeline for disbursement of subsidies may vary based on the processing of applications. 

The Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment is the implementing authority for this scheme. 

Additional details regarding the specific subsidy amount and other conditions may be available through official government sources.
