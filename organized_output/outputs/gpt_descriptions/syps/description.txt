The scheme is known as the Sardar Patel Yuva Swabhiman Yojana (SYPS). 

It was launched by the Government of Gujarat. 

The scheme aims to provide employment opportunities to the youth of Gujarat. 

It focuses on skill development and entrepreneurship. 

The benefits include financial assistance for self-employment. 

It also provides training programs to enhance skills. 

Eligibility criteria include being a resident of Gujarat. 

Applicants must be between the ages of 18 and 35 years. 

The application process involves online registration through the official portal. 

Applicants need to provide necessary documents such as identity proof and educational certificates. 

The scheme is implemented by the Department of Employment and Training, Government of Gujarat. 

The frequency of assistance is based on the completion of training and project approval. 

The scheme aims to empower youth and reduce unemployment in the state.
