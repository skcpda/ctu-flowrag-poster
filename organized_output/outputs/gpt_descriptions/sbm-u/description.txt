Swachh Bharat Mission - Urban (SBM-U) is a flagship program launched by the Government of India in 2014. 

It aims to achieve universal sanitation coverage and improve solid waste management in urban areas. 

The objective is to eliminate open defecation and promote cleanliness in urban environments. 

The mission focuses on constructing individual household toilets and community toilets. 

It also emphasizes the importance of solid waste management practices. 

Benefits include improved sanitation facilities for urban residents. 

It enhances public health and environmental conditions. 

Eligibility criteria generally include urban local bodies and residents of urban areas. 

The application process involves urban local bodies submitting proposals for funding. 

The timeline for implementation varies by project but generally spans several years. 

The Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs is the primary implementing authority. 

State governments and urban local bodies play crucial roles in execution. 

The mission also promotes awareness and behavioral change regarding sanitation. 

Regular monitoring and evaluation are conducted to assess progress and impact.
