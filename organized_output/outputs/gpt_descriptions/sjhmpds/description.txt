The scheme SJHMPDS stands for the Scheme for the Joint Housing and Micro Finance for Persons with Disabilities. 

This scheme is aimed at providing housing and financial assistance to persons with disabilities. 

The primary objective is to enhance the living conditions of persons with disabilities by facilitating access to housing and microfinance. 

The benefits include financial assistance for purchasing or constructing houses. 

Eligible beneficiaries are persons with disabilities who require housing support. 

The application process involves submitting an application to the designated authority along with necessary documentation. 

The timeline for processing applications and disbursing funds may vary based on the implementing agency. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment. 

Additional details regarding specific financial limits or documentation requirements may be available through official sources.
