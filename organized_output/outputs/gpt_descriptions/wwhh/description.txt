The "Welfare of Women and Handicapped" scheme aims to provide support and assistance to women and persons with disabilities. 

The objective is to empower women and improve the quality of life for persons with disabilities. 

Benefits include financial assistance for education healthcare and vocational training. 

Eligibility criteria typically include being a woman or a person with a disability as defined by the government. 

The application process generally involves submitting a form along with required documents to the designated authority. 

The timeline for assistance may vary based on the specific program under the scheme. 

Implementing authorities include the Ministry of Women and Child Development and relevant state departments. 

Additional details may include specific programs under the scheme focusing on different aspects of welfare.
