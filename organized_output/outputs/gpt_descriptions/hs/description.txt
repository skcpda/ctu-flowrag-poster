The "Housing for All" scheme also known as Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana (PMAY) is a significant initiative launched by the Government of India. 

The scheme was launched on June 25 2015. 

It aims to provide affordable housing to the urban poor. 

The initiative is part of the larger vision of the Government to ensure that every citizen has access to a basic necessity such as housing. 

The background of the scheme stems from the recognition of the housing shortage in urban areas. 

The Government identified that millions of people were living in inadequate housing conditions. 

The scheme is aligned with the broader goal of achieving "Housing for All" by the year 2022. 

The objectives of the PMAY scheme are multifaceted. 

One of the primary objectives is to provide financial assistance for the construction of houses. 

The scheme aims to promote the use of sustainable and eco-friendly building materials. 

It also seeks to enhance the quality of life for urban poor by providing them with decent housing. 

The Government intends to reduce the housing shortage in urban areas significantly. 

The benefits and assistance provided under the PMAY scheme are substantial. 

Eligible beneficiaries can receive financial assistance of up to INR 2.5 lakh. 

This amount is provided as a subsidy on home loans. 

The scheme also offers interest subsidies of up to 6.5 percent on home loans. 

This interest subsidy is available for a tenure of 20 years. 

The assistance is aimed at making housing affordable for economically weaker sections. 

The scheme also encourages the construction of houses with basic amenities. 

These amenities include water supply sanitation and electricity connections. 

The eligibility criteria for the PMAY scheme are clearly defined. 

The scheme targets the Economically Weaker Sections (EWS) and Low-Income Groups (LIG). 

Individuals or families with an annual income of up to INR 3 lakh are classified as EWS. 

Families with an annual income between INR 3 lakh and INR 6 lakh fall under the LIG category. 

The beneficiaries must not own a pucca house in their name. 

The scheme is also open to women applicants. 

Preference is given to women applicants for ownership of houses. 

The application process for the PMAY scheme is straightforward. 

Interested beneficiaries can apply online through the official PMAY website. 

They can also visit the nearest Common Service Centers (CSCs) for assistance. 

The application requires basic personal details and income proof. 

Applicants must provide details of their current housing situation. 

Once the application is submitted it undergoes verification by the concerned authorities. 

The timeline for the disbursement of benefits varies. 

Typically the process from application to approval may take several weeks. 

Once approved beneficiaries can expect to receive the subsidy amount in their bank accounts. 

The frequency of the scheme's implementation is ongoing. 

The PMAY scheme is part of a larger mission that continues to evolve. 

Implementing authorities for the PMAY scheme include the Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs. 

State governments and urban local bodies also play a crucial role in implementation. 

They are responsible for identifying beneficiaries and monitoring the progress of construction. 

The scheme is also supported by various financial institutions. 

These institutions provide the necessary loans to beneficiaries. 

The PMAY scheme has seen significant uptake since its launch. 

It has contributed to the construction of millions of houses across urban areas. 

The scheme also promotes the use of technology in construction. 

This includes the use of prefabricated materials and modern construction techniques. 

The Government has also introduced various other initiatives under the PMAY umbrella. 

These include the Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana - Gramin for rural housing.
