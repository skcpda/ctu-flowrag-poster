The scheme SSIEEMAF stands for the Scheme for Support to State Extension Functionaries for Enhancing Agricultural Marketing and Farmer Welfare. 

It was launched by the Government of India to strengthen the agricultural marketing system in the country. 

The objective of the scheme is to enhance the capacity of state extension functionaries to support farmers in marketing their produce. 

The benefits include training and capacity building for state officials. 

This will enable them to provide better support and services to farmers regarding agricultural marketing. 

Eligibility criteria include state extension functionaries involved in agricultural marketing. 

The application process involves submission of proposals by state governments to the Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers' Welfare. 

The timeline for implementation and frequency of support is determined based on the proposals approved by the Ministry. 

The implementing authority for the scheme is the Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers' Welfare. 

The scheme aims to improve the overall efficiency of agricultural marketing and enhance farmer welfare.
