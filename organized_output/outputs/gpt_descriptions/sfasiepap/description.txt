The Scheme for Financial Assistance for the Skill Development of Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes is known as SFASIEPAP. 

It aims to provide financial assistance for skill development programs targeted at Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes. 

The objective is to enhance the employability of individuals from these communities through skill training. 

Benefits include financial support for training programs and resources to facilitate skill development. 

Eligibility is primarily for individuals belonging to Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes. 

The application process typically involves submitting an application to the designated authority or agency overseeing the scheme. 

The timeline for assistance may vary based on the specific training program and its duration. 

Implementing authorities include the Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment and other relevant state departments. 

Additional details may include specific training programs covered under the scheme and the types of skills targeted.
