The Old Age Allowance Scheme (OAA) is a welfare initiative by the Government of India. 

It aims to provide financial assistance to elderly citizens. 

The objective is to ensure a basic standard of living for senior citizens. 

The scheme offers a monthly allowance to eligible beneficiaries. 

The amount varies by state and is intended to support daily needs. 

Eligibility is generally based on age and income criteria. 

Typically, applicants must be above a certain age, often 60 years. 

Income limits may apply to ensure assistance reaches those in need. 

The application process usually involves submitting a form to the local government office. 

Required documents may include proof of age and income. 

The scheme is implemented by state governments. 

Each state may have its own specific guidelines and procedures. 

The frequency of assistance is usually monthly. 

The timeline for processing applications may vary by state. 

Some states may have additional provisions or benefits under this scheme.
