The scheme TDPSPDSTGMISG stands for the "Tribal Development Programme for Scheduled Tribes (ST) in the State of Gujarat." 

The context of this scheme is to promote the welfare and development of Scheduled Tribes in Gujarat. 

The objectives include enhancing the socio-economic status of tribal communities and ensuring their access to various government services and benefits. 

Benefits provided under the scheme include financial assistance for various developmental activities and support for education and skill development. 

Eligibility criteria typically include being a member of a Scheduled Tribe and residing in the state of Gujarat. 

The application process usually involves submitting an application to the local tribal development office along with required documents. 

The timeline for the disbursement of benefits may vary based on the specific activity or assistance being provided. 

The implementing authority for this scheme is the Tribal Development Department of the Government of Gujarat. 

Additional details about specific benefits or activities may be available through the official government portal or local offices.
