The Pradhan Mantri Formalization of Micro Food Processing Enterprises Scheme is an initiative by the Government of India. 

It aims to enhance the competitiveness of micro food processing enterprises. 

The scheme seeks to promote the formalization of the food processing sector. 

It provides financial assistance and support for technology upgradation. 

The benefits include access to credit, skill development, and marketing support. 

Eligible beneficiaries include micro food processing enterprises in the unorganized sector. 

The application process involves submitting an online application through the official portal. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Food Processing Industries. 

Financial assistance is provided in the form of a one-time grant. 

The timeline for processing applications and disbursing funds varies based on the specific case. 

The scheme also encourages the adoption of quality standards and certifications. 

It aims to create a sustainable ecosystem for food processing enterprises.
