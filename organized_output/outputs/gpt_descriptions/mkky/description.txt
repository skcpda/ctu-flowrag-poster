The Mukhyamantri Kanya Kalyan Yojana (MKKY) is a welfare scheme launched by the Government of Uttar Pradesh. 

The scheme aims to promote the welfare and empowerment of girls in the state. 

It focuses on improving the socio-economic status of girls by providing financial assistance for their education and marriage. 

The benefits include a financial grant for the education of girls and support for their marriage expenses. 

Eligible beneficiaries are girls from economically weaker sections of society. 

The scheme is applicable to girls who are residents of Uttar Pradesh. 

The application process involves filling out an online application form on the official government portal. 

The timeline for disbursement of benefits is generally aligned with the educational milestones and marriage age of the beneficiaries. 

The scheme is implemented by the Department of Women and Child Development, Government of Uttar Pradesh.
