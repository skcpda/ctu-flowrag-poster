The RAD scheme stands for the "Rural Agriculture Development" scheme. 

It aims to enhance agricultural productivity and improve the livelihoods of farmers in rural areas. 

The objective is to provide financial assistance and technical support to farmers for sustainable agricultural practices. 

Benefits include access to modern farming techniques and improved crop yields. 

Eligible beneficiaries are small and marginal farmers residing in rural areas. 

The application process typically involves submitting a form to the local agricultural department. 

The timeline for assistance may vary based on the specific project or initiative under the scheme. 

Implementing authorities include the Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers' Welfare at the central level and state agricultural departments at the state level.
