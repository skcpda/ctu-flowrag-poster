The National Family Benefit Scheme (NFBS) is a welfare initiative by the Government of India. 

It aims to provide financial assistance to families in the event of the death of the primary breadwinner. 

The objective is to alleviate the financial burden faced by families due to the loss of income. 

Under this scheme, a one-time financial assistance of Rs. 20,000 is provided to the bereaved family. 

The assistance is intended to help the family meet immediate needs and expenses following the death. 

Eligibility for the scheme includes families living below the poverty line. 

The deceased must be the primary breadwinner of the family. 

The application process involves submitting a death certificate and an application form to the local government office. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Rural Development at the national level. 

State governments are responsible for the execution and management of the scheme at the state level. 

The assistance is provided on a one-time basis. 

The scheme is available to eligible families across all states and union territories in India.
