The Sardha CSR scheme is an initiative aimed at promoting corporate social responsibility in India. 

It encourages companies to contribute to social welfare activities. 

The primary objective is to enhance the quality of life in communities through sustainable development. 

The scheme facilitates partnerships between corporates and non-governmental organizations. 

Benefits include financial support for various social initiatives. 

Assistance may cover areas such as education health and environment. 

Eligibility criteria typically include registered companies under the Companies Act. 

The application process involves submitting proposals to relevant authorities. 

Timelines for project implementation may vary based on the specific initiative. 

The Ministry of Corporate Affairs is the implementing authority for this scheme. 

Additional details may include guidelines for project selection and monitoring.
