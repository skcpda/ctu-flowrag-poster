The Indian government welfare scheme known as the Indira Gandhi National Widow Pension Scheme is designed to provide financial assistance to widows in the country. 

This scheme is part of the broader social security initiatives aimed at improving the living conditions of vulnerable sections of society. 

The scheme acknowledges the challenges faced by widows who often struggle to support themselves and their families after the loss of their spouses. 

It seeks to empower these women by providing them with a regular source of income. 

The primary objective of the Indira Gandhi National Widow Pension Scheme is to ensure that widows receive financial support to meet their basic needs. 

This financial assistance aims to reduce poverty and improve the quality of life for widows across India. 

The scheme also aims to promote social inclusion and dignity for widows who may face social stigma and economic hardships. 

Under this scheme, eligible widows receive a monthly pension. 

The amount of the pension is determined by the government and may vary from state to state. 

This financial assistance helps widows to meet their daily expenses and support their families. 

The scheme is particularly beneficial for widows who do not have any other source of income. 

It provides them with a sense of security and independence. 

Eligibility for the Indira Gandhi National Widow Pension Scheme is primarily based on the age and marital status of the applicant. 

To qualify for the scheme, the applicant must be a widow. 

The applicant must also be in the age group of 18 to 79 years. 

The scheme is applicable to widows from below poverty line households. 

The income criteria for eligibility may vary from state to state. 

Applicants must provide relevant documents to prove their marital status and income level. 

The application process for the Indira Gandhi National Widow Pension Scheme involves several steps. 

Interested applicants must approach the local government office or the designated authority in their area. 

They need to fill out the application form provided by the authorities. 

The application form requires personal details such as name age and address. 

Applicants must also submit documents such as a death certificate of the deceased husband. 

Proof of age and income must also be included with the application. 

Once the application is submitted it is reviewed by the concerned authorities. 

The authorities verify the information provided in the application. 

Upon successful verification the pension is sanctioned.
