The Pradhan Mantri Drought Relief and Rehabilitation Scheme is designed to provide timely assistance to farmers affected by drought conditions. 

The objective of the scheme is to mitigate the impact of drought on agricultural production and ensure food security. 

The scheme provides financial assistance to farmers for crop loss due to drought. 

Eligibility is generally limited to farmers who have suffered crop losses as a result of declared drought conditions. 

Farmers must provide necessary documentation to prove their crop loss and eligibility for assistance. 

The application process typically involves submitting an application to the local revenue office or agriculture department. 

Assistance is provided based on the assessment of crop loss by government officials. 

The timeline for assistance disbursement may vary based on the assessment and approval process. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers' Welfare along with state governments. 

Additional details may include specific guidelines on the quantum of assistance and the criteria for assessing drought conditions.
