The Mukhyamantri Mahila Samriddhi Yojana (MMSSYJ) is a welfare scheme launched by the Government of Madhya Pradesh. 

The scheme aims to empower women by providing financial assistance and promoting self-employment. 

It focuses on enhancing the socio-economic status of women in the state. 

The benefits include financial support for women to start their own businesses and access to skill development programs. 

Eligible beneficiaries are women who are residents of Madhya Pradesh and belong to economically weaker sections. 

The application process involves submitting a form at designated government offices or through online portals. 

The scheme is implemented by the Department of Women and Child Development in Madhya Pradesh. 

The frequency of assistance and disbursement of funds may vary based on the specific program under the scheme.
