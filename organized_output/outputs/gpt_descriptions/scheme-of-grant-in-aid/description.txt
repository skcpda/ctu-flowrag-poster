The Scheme of Grant-in-Aid is a financial assistance program initiated by the Government of India. 

It aims to support various institutions and organizations in carrying out activities that benefit the public. 

The objectives include promoting social welfare and enhancing the quality of services provided by these institutions. 

Benefits include financial support for projects and initiatives that align with government priorities. 

Eligibility criteria vary based on the specific project and the type of institution applying for assistance. 

The application process typically involves submitting a detailed project proposal to the relevant government authority. 

The timeline for processing applications can vary depending on the complexity of the proposal and the workload of the implementing agency. 

Implementing authorities include various ministries and departments of the Government of India, depending on the focus area of the grant. 

Additional details may include specific guidelines on the types of projects eligible for funding and reporting requirements for recipients.
