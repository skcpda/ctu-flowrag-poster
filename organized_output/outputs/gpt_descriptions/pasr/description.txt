The Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana (PMAY) is a flagship housing scheme of the Government of India. 

It aims to provide affordable housing to the urban poor. 

The objective is to ensure that every family has a pucca house with basic amenities. 

The scheme provides financial assistance for the construction or enhancement of houses. 

Benefits include interest subsidies on home loans and direct financial assistance. 

The assistance varies based on the category of beneficiaries such as Economically Weaker Sections and Lower Income Groups. 

Eligibility criteria include being a citizen of India and belonging to the specified income categories. 

Applicants must not own a house in their name or in the name of any family member. 

The application process involves submitting an online application through the PMAY website or through Common Service Centers. 

The scheme has a continuous timeline with applications accepted throughout the year. 

The Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs is the implementing authority for PMAY. 

State governments and urban local bodies also play a role in implementation. 

The scheme is part of the larger initiative to achieve "Housing for All" by 2022.
