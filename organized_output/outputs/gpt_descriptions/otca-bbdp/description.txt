The OTCA-BBDP scheme is the Odisha Tribal Empowerment and Livelihoods Programme. 

It is designed to enhance the livelihoods of tribal communities in Odisha. 

The main objective is to promote sustainable livelihoods through skill development and capacity building. 

The scheme aims to improve the socio-economic conditions of tribal households. 

It provides financial assistance for various livelihood activities. 

Benefits include access to skill training and support for self-employment initiatives. 

Eligibility is primarily for tribal households in Odisha. 

The application process involves submission of required documents to the local implementing agency. 

The scheme is implemented by the Odisha Tribal Development Cooperative Corporation. 

It operates in collaboration with various government departments and agencies. 

The timeline for assistance may vary based on the specific livelihood activity. 

The scheme focuses on empowering tribal communities through participatory approaches. 

It also aims to ensure food security and improve health and education outcomes.
