The West Bengal Integrated Scheme for the Promotion of Agricultural and Allied Sectors (WBISTPAIS) aims to enhance agricultural productivity and promote sustainable farming practices in West Bengal. 

The objective of the scheme is to improve the livelihoods of farmers by providing them with necessary resources and support. 

It focuses on promoting various agricultural and allied activities such as horticulture, fisheries, and animal husbandry. 

The benefits include financial assistance for purchasing seeds, fertilizers, and equipment. 

Farmers can also receive training and technical support to improve their farming practices. 

Eligibility criteria include being a resident of West Bengal and actively engaged in agricultural activities. 

The application process involves submitting an application form along with necessary documents to the local agricultural office. 

The scheme is implemented by the Department of Agriculture, Government of West Bengal. 

The timeline for assistance varies based on the specific activity and season. 

The scheme aims to promote sustainable agricultural practices and improve food security in the region.
