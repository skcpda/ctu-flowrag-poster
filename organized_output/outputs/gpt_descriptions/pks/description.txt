The Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samman Nidhi (PM-KISAN) scheme was launched by the Government of India in February 2019. 

The scheme aims to provide financial support to small and marginal farmers. 

Its objective is to ensure a steady income for farmers and to enable them to meet their agricultural needs. 

Under this scheme, eligible farmers receive direct cash transfers of Rs. 6000 per year. 

This amount is disbursed in three equal installments of Rs. 2000 each. 

Eligibility for the scheme includes small and marginal farmers who own cultivable land. 

Farmers must have a valid landholding and must not be in the exclusion criteria set by the government. 

The application process involves farmers registering on the PM-KISAN portal or through local revenue offices. 

Farmers must provide necessary documents such as land ownership proof and bank account details. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers' Welfare. 

The financial assistance is provided on a regular basis, typically every four months. 

The scheme is part of the government's broader initiative to support the agricultural sector and improve farmers' welfare.
