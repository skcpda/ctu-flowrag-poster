The Scheme for Welfare of Persons with Disabilities (SWPWD) is aimed at promoting the welfare of persons with disabilities in India. 

The objective of the scheme is to provide financial assistance and support services to enhance the quality of life for persons with disabilities. 

Benefits include financial assistance for various needs such as education vocational training and rehabilitation. 

Eligibility criteria include being a person with a disability as defined under the Rights of Persons with Disabilities Act 2016. 

The application process typically involves submitting an application form along with necessary documents to the designated authority. 

The timeline for processing applications may vary depending on the specific state or implementing agency. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment at the central level along with state governments. 

Additional details may include provisions for awareness programs and capacity building initiatives for caregivers and families of persons with disabilities.
