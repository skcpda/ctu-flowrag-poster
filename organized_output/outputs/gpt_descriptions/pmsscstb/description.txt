The Pradhan Mantri Shram Yogi Maandhan (PM-SYM) scheme is designed for the unorganized sector workers. 

It aims to provide social security and a monthly pension to workers in the unorganized sector. 

The objective is to ensure financial security for workers after they reach the age of 60 years. 

Under this scheme, eligible beneficiaries will receive a monthly pension of Rs. 3000 after reaching the age of 60. 

The scheme also provides for the spouse of the beneficiary to receive 50% of the pension amount after the death of the beneficiary. 

Eligibility criteria include being an unorganized sector worker aged between 18 to 40 years. 

The applicant's monthly income should be less than Rs. 15000. 

The application process involves filling out a registration form available at Common Service Centers (CSCs) or online through the official website. 

The timeline for processing applications may vary but is generally completed within a few weeks. 

The Ministry of Labour and Employment is the implementing authority for this scheme. 

The scheme was launched in February 2019. 

Beneficiaries are required to contribute a nominal monthly amount based on their age at the time of enrollment. 

The scheme is expected to cover a large number of workers in the unorganized sector.
