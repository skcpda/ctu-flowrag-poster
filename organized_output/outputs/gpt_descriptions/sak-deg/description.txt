Sak-Deg is a welfare scheme initiated by the Government of India. 

It aims to provide financial assistance to women entrepreneurs in the food processing sector. 

The objective is to promote self-employment and enhance the income of women. 

The scheme supports women in starting and managing their own food processing units. 

Benefits include financial support for equipment and infrastructure development. 

Assistance is also provided for skill development and training programs. 

Eligibility is primarily for women entrepreneurs who are above 18 years of age. 

Applicants must have a viable business plan in the food processing sector. 

The application process involves submitting a detailed project proposal to the relevant authorities. 

The proposal should outline the business model and expected outcomes. 

The timeline for approval and disbursement of funds varies based on the project. 

Implementing agencies include the Ministry of Food Processing Industries and state-level departments. 

The scheme may also collaborate with financial institutions for funding support. 

Additional details may include specific training programs offered under the scheme.
