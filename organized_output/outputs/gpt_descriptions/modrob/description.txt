The Modrob scheme is a government initiative aimed at enhancing the quality of technical education in India. 

It focuses on upgrading the infrastructure and equipment of engineering colleges and polytechnics. 

The objective is to improve the teaching and learning environment in technical institutions. 

The scheme provides financial assistance for the purchase of modern equipment and tools. 

This assistance helps institutions to meet the requirements of the industry and improve the employability of students. 

Eligible institutions include government and private engineering colleges and polytechnics. 

The application process involves submitting a proposal to the concerned authority. 

The proposal must detail the equipment needed and the expected outcomes. 

The timeline for funding approval and disbursement varies based on the proposal review process. 

The Ministry of Education is the primary implementing authority for the Modrob scheme. 

The scheme aims to ensure that technical education institutions remain relevant to industry needs. 

It also promotes innovation and research in engineering and technology fields.
