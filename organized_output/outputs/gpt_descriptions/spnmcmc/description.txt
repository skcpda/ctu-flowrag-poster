The SPNMCMC scheme stands for the Scheme for Promoting National Mission on Clean Ganga. 

It is aimed at promoting the National Mission for Clean Ganga. 

The objective is to ensure the rejuvenation of the Ganga River and its tributaries. 

The scheme focuses on pollution abatement and river conservation. 

Benefits include financial assistance for projects related to river cleaning and conservation. 

The assistance is provided to various stakeholders including state governments and local bodies. 

Eligibility criteria include entities involved in environmental conservation and river management. 

The application process involves submission of project proposals to the relevant authorities. 

The timeline for project implementation varies based on the specific project approved. 

The Ministry of Water Resources River Development and Ganga Rejuvenation is the implementing authority. 

The scheme also promotes community participation in river conservation efforts. 

Awareness campaigns are conducted to educate the public about the importance of a clean Ganga. 

Funding is provided through the National Ganga River Basin Authority. 

Monitoring and evaluation of projects are conducted to ensure compliance and effectiveness.
