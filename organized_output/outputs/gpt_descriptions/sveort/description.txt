The SVEORT scheme stands for Skill Development for Youth and Employment Opportunities in Rural Areas. 

It was launched to enhance the employability of rural youth through skill development. 

The main objective is to provide training and skill development programs tailored to the needs of the local job market. 

The scheme aims to empower rural youth by equipping them with necessary skills for better employment opportunities. 

Benefits include access to skill development training programs and potential job placements after completion. 

Eligible beneficiaries are rural youth aged between 15 to 29 years. 

The application process typically involves filling out an online application form available on the official website. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Rural Development in collaboration with various skill development agencies. 

Training programs under the scheme are conducted periodically based on demand and availability of resources. 

Additional details such as specific training modules and locations may vary based on state and local requirements.
