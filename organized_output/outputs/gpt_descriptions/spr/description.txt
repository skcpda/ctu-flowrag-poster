The scheme SPR stands for the Skill Development and Entrepreneurship Program. 

It was launched to enhance the employability of youth in India. 

The objective is to provide skill training to individuals to promote self-employment and entrepreneurship. 

The scheme offers various benefits including financial assistance for training and support for starting businesses. 

Eligibility criteria include youth aged between 15 to 29 years. 

The application process involves registering on the official portal and submitting necessary documents. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Skill Development and Entrepreneurship. 

Training programs are conducted through various training partners across the country. 

The frequency of training sessions may vary based on the training partner and location. 

Additional details include a focus on sectors with high employment potential.
