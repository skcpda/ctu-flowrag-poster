The scheme is known as the Scheme for Promotion of Bulk Purchase of Biofuels and Biomass Pellets (SMPBPBS). 

It aims to promote the use of biofuels and biomass pellets for energy generation. 

The objective is to enhance the availability and accessibility of biofuels for various sectors. 

Benefits include financial assistance for the procurement of biofuels and biomass pellets. 

This support is intended to reduce dependence on fossil fuels and promote renewable energy sources. 

Eligibility criteria include entities involved in energy generation and those utilizing biofuels. 

The application process typically involves submitting a proposal to the relevant authorities. 

The timeline for processing applications may vary based on the specific case. 

The Ministry of New and Renewable Energy is the implementing authority for this scheme.
