The Mukhyamantri Sewa Sankalp Yojana (MSSY) is a welfare scheme initiated by the Government of Madhya Pradesh. 

The scheme aims to provide employment opportunities to the youth of the state. 

It focuses on skill development and enhancing employability among young individuals. 

The benefits include training programs in various sectors and financial assistance for self-employment. 

Eligible beneficiaries are youth aged between 18 to 35 years residing in Madhya Pradesh. 

The application process involves online registration through the official portal of the scheme. 

The frequency of training programs is conducted periodically based on demand and availability of resources. 

The scheme is implemented by the Department of Skill Development and Entrepreneurship of Madhya Pradesh. 

Additional details include collaboration with various industries for practical training and job placements.
