The Mukhyamantri Dastakari Craft and Artisans Scheme (MDCAS) aims to support traditional artisans and craftsmen in India. 

The scheme seeks to promote and preserve traditional crafts and enhance the livelihoods of artisans. 

It provides financial assistance for skill development and marketing of handicrafts. 

The benefits include training programs and access to markets for selling products. 

Eligibility is primarily for traditional artisans and craftsmen engaged in handicraft production. 

The application process typically involves submitting an application to the relevant state government department. 

The timeline for assistance may vary based on the specific program under the scheme. 

The implementing authorities include state governments and local bodies responsible for artisan welfare. 

Additional details may include specific training modules and partnerships with various organizations for skill enhancement.
