The Mukhyamantri Tirth Yatra Yojana (MTYYD) is a welfare scheme initiated by the Government of Madhya Pradesh. 

The scheme aims to provide financial assistance to senior citizens for pilgrimage trips to various religious sites. 

The primary objective is to promote religious tourism and ensure that elderly citizens have the opportunity to visit holy places. 

Eligible beneficiaries are senior citizens aged 60 years and above who are residents of Madhya Pradesh. 

The scheme offers free travel and accommodation for the pilgrimage. 

The application process involves filling out a form available at designated offices or online portals. 

The frequency of the yatra is organized periodically based on the schedule of the pilgrimage trips. 

The implementing authority for this scheme is the Department of Religious Trust and Endowments, Government of Madhya Pradesh. 

Additional details include that the scheme covers various religious destinations across India. 

The government also provides meals during the pilgrimage. 

The scheme emphasizes the importance of cultural and spiritual heritage for the elderly population.
