The Mukhyamantri Vatsalya Yojana (MVY) is a welfare scheme launched by the Government of Madhya Pradesh. 

It aims to provide financial assistance to orphaned children and those who have lost their parents due to various reasons. 

The primary objective is to ensure the welfare and development of these vulnerable children. 

Under this scheme, eligible beneficiaries receive financial support for education and basic needs. 

The assistance includes a monthly stipend to help cover living expenses. 

Eligibility is primarily focused on children who are orphans or have lost one or both parents. 

The application process involves submitting a form along with necessary documents to the local authorities. 

The scheme is implemented by the Department of Women and Child Development in Madhya Pradesh. 

The frequency of assistance is monthly for the duration of eligibility. 

Additional details regarding the specific amount of financial assistance and documentation required can be obtained from local government offices.
