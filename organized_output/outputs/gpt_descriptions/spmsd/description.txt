The Scheme for Promotion of Manufacturing of Smart Devices (SPMSD) aims to boost the manufacturing of smart devices in India. 

The objective is to enhance domestic manufacturing capabilities and promote innovation in the smart device sector. 

The scheme provides financial assistance to eligible manufacturers for setting up production facilities. 

Benefits include subsidies for capital expenditure and support for research and development activities. 

Eligibility is primarily for manufacturers of smart devices who meet specific criteria set by the government. 

The application process involves submitting a proposal to the relevant government authority along with necessary documentation. 

The timeline for approval and disbursement of funds varies based on the project and submission. 

The Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology is the implementing authority for this scheme. 

Additional details may include specific guidelines for project proposals and compliance requirements.
