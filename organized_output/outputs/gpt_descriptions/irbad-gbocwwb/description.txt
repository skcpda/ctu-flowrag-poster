The Indian government welfare scheme known as the Irbad-GBOCWWB is designed to provide financial assistance and support to the families of construction workers.

This scheme is particularly relevant in the context of India's rapidly growing construction industry.

The construction sector is a significant contributor to the Indian economy.

However it also poses various challenges for the workforce involved.

The Irbad-GBOCWWB scheme aims to address these challenges.

The primary objective of the scheme is to provide financial assistance to the families of construction workers.

This assistance is particularly crucial in times of emergencies or unforeseen circumstances.

The scheme seeks to ensure that the families of deceased construction workers receive adequate support.

This support is intended to help them cope with the loss of income.

The benefits under this scheme include financial aid to the families.

The financial assistance is provided in the form of a one-time payment.

This payment is made to the nominee of the deceased construction worker.

The amount of financial assistance is determined by the government.

In addition to financial aid the scheme may also provide other forms of support.

These may include access to healthcare and educational resources.

The eligibility criteria for the Irbad-GBOCWWB scheme are specific.

To be eligible the applicant must be a registered construction worker.

The worker must be registered under the Building and Other Construction Workers Act of 1996.

The applicant must also have a valid registration number.

The deceased worker must have been actively engaged in construction work at the time of death.
