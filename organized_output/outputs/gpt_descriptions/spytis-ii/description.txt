It focuses on providing support to students in the field of science and technology. 

The primary objective is to promote research and development in educational institutions. 

The scheme aims to improve the infrastructure and facilities available for students. 

It also seeks to encourage innovation and creativity among students. 

Benefits include financial assistance for educational institutions. 

This assistance can be used for upgrading laboratories and research facilities.
