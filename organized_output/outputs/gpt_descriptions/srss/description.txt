The Scheme for Rehabilitation of Manual Scavengers (SRSS) aims to eliminate the practice of manual scavenging in India. 

It focuses on the rehabilitation of manual scavengers and their dependents. 

The objectives include providing alternative livelihoods and ensuring social dignity for manual scavengers. 

Benefits include financial assistance for skill development and employment opportunities in various sectors. 

Eligible beneficiaries are manual scavengers identified through surveys conducted by state governments. 

The application process involves registration with local authorities and submission of necessary documentation. 

The timeline for assistance varies based on the completion of the application process and the availability of funds. 

The Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment is the primary implementing authority for this scheme. 

State governments also play a crucial role in the implementation and monitoring of the scheme.
