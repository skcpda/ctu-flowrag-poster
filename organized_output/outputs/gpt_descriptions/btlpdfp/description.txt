The BTL PDFP scheme stands for the Below Poverty Line (BPL) Development Fund Program. 

This scheme is part of the Indian government's broader initiative to uplift the socio-economic status of individuals and families living below the poverty line. 

The context of the BTL PDFP scheme is rooted in the recognition of poverty as a significant barrier to development. 

The Indian government has consistently aimed to address poverty through various welfare schemes. 

The BTL PDFP specifically targets the needs of those classified as living below the poverty line. 

The objectives of the BTL PDFP are multi-faceted. 

One primary objective is to provide financial assistance to families living in poverty. 

Another objective is to enhance the living standards of individuals and families through skill development and employment opportunities. 

The scheme also aims to promote self-sufficiency among beneficiaries. 

By providing resources and support, the BTL PDFP seeks to empower individuals to improve their economic conditions. 

The benefits and assistance provided under the BTL PDFP are substantial. 

Eligible beneficiaries can receive direct financial aid to support their basic needs. 

This financial assistance can be utilized for various purposes such as education, healthcare, and housing. 

Additionally, the scheme may offer skill development programs to enhance employability. 

These programs are designed to equip beneficiaries with the necessary skills to secure stable employment. 

The BTL PDFP may also facilitate access to microfinance options for small business ventures. 

This aspect of the scheme encourages entrepreneurship among the poor.

Individuals and families must be officially classified as living below the poverty line.
