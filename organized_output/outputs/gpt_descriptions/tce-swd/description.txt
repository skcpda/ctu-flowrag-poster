The TCE-SWD scheme stands for the Technology and Communication Enabled Social Welfare Development scheme. 

This scheme aims to enhance the delivery of social welfare services through the use of technology and communication. 

The primary objective is to improve access to social welfare programs for marginalized and disadvantaged communities. 

The scheme provides assistance in the form of digital platforms and tools to facilitate better communication between beneficiaries and service providers. 

Eligibility criteria typically include individuals from marginalized communities and those in need of social welfare services. 

The application process generally involves registration on the designated digital platform or through local government offices. 

The timeline for implementation may vary based on the specific services being accessed. 

The scheme is implemented by various government agencies at both the central and state levels. 

These agencies work in coordination to ensure effective outreach and service delivery. 

Additional details may include training programs for beneficiaries to enhance their digital literacy.
