The scheme is known as the Swami Vivekananda Grameen Employment Generation Programme (SVAGMP). 

It was launched by the Government of India to provide self-employment opportunities to rural youth. 

The primary objective is to enhance the income-generating capacity of rural households. 

The scheme aims to promote entrepreneurship among rural youth. 

Benefits include financial assistance for setting up small businesses. 

The assistance is provided in the form of a subsidy and bank credit. 

Eligibility criteria include being a resident of rural areas and being within the age group of 18 to 35 years. 

Applicants must have a viable business plan. 

The application process involves submitting a detailed project proposal to the concerned authority. 

The proposal should include the nature of the business and the estimated cost. 

The timeline for approval may vary depending on the project. 

The implementing agency is the Ministry of Rural Development. 

State governments may also have specific agencies to facilitate the scheme.
