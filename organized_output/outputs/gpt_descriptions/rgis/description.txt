The RGIS scheme stands for the Rajiv Gandhi Grameen Internet Seva scheme. 

It was launched to provide internet connectivity in rural areas of India. 

The objective is to bridge the digital divide between urban and rural regions. 

The scheme aims to empower rural citizens with access to information and services. 

It benefits individuals by providing them with opportunities for education and skill development. 

The scheme also facilitates access to government services and e-governance initiatives. 

Eligibility criteria include being a resident of a rural area. 

The application process typically involves registration through designated local authorities or online platforms. 

The timeline for implementation may vary based on the specific project and location. 

The Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology is the implementing authority for this scheme. 

Additional details about specific project timelines and locations may be available through official government channels.
