Subhadra is a welfare scheme initiated by the Government of India. 

The scheme aims to provide financial assistance to women for their health and nutrition needs. 

It is designed to empower women and improve their overall well-being. 

The benefits include direct cash transfers for health-related expenses. 

Eligible beneficiaries are women from economically weaker sections of society. 

The application process involves filling out a form and submitting it to the designated local authority. 

The timeline for disbursement of funds is typically monthly. 

The implementing authority for the Subhadra scheme is the Ministry of Women and Child Development. 

Additional details about the scheme may vary by state or region.
