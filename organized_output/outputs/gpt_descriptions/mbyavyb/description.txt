The Mukhyamantri Bhavishya Sathi Yojana (MBYAVYB) is a welfare scheme launched by the Government of West Bengal. 

The scheme aims to provide financial assistance to students for their education. 

It focuses on ensuring that students from economically weaker sections can pursue higher education without financial constraints. 

The benefits include financial support for tuition fees and other educational expenses. 

Students will receive a monthly stipend to help cover their costs. 

Eligibility is primarily based on the economic status of the family. 

Students from families with an annual income below a specified threshold are eligible. 

The application process involves filling out an online application form on the official website. 

Applicants must provide necessary documents to verify their eligibility. 

The scheme is implemented by the West Bengal Department of Higher Education. 

The timeline for disbursement of funds is typically aligned with the academic calendar. 

The scheme is aimed at students pursuing undergraduate and postgraduate courses. 

It seeks to promote higher education and reduce dropout rates among disadvantaged students.
