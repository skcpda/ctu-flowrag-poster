The scheme TPSFTBP stands for the "Technology and Professional Skill Development for the Tribal Population" scheme. 

It aims to enhance the skill sets of tribal youth through technology and professional training. 

The objective is to empower tribal communities by providing them with relevant skills for better employment opportunities. 

Benefits include access to skill development programs and training in various trades and professions. 

Eligible beneficiaries are tribal youth who belong to Scheduled Tribes. 

The application process involves submitting an application to the designated authority in the respective state. 

The timeline for the training programs varies based on the specific courses offered. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Tribal Affairs and associated state departments. 

Additional details about specific training programs and locations may be available through official government sources.
