The POCRA scheme stands for the Protection of Children from Sexual Offences Act. 

It was enacted to address the increasing incidents of sexual offenses against children in India. 

The primary objective of the scheme is to provide a legal framework for the protection of children from sexual abuse and exploitation. 

The scheme aims to ensure that offenders are punished and that children receive necessary support and rehabilitation. 

Benefits include legal protection for children and access to counseling and rehabilitation services. 

Eligibility is generally applicable to all children under the age of 18 years. 

The application process involves reporting incidents of abuse to the police or child protection services. 

The timeline for the implementation of legal proceedings is defined under the act to ensure swift justice. 

The Ministry of Women and Child Development is the implementing authority for this scheme. 

Additional details include the establishment of Special Courts for the speedy trial of offenses under the act.
