The SLESVKSPY scheme stands for the State Level Employment Scheme for Vulnerable and Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes Youth. 

This scheme was launched by the Government of India to provide employment opportunities to vulnerable youth from Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes. 

The primary objective is to enhance the employability of these youth through skill development and vocational training. 

The scheme aims to empower marginalized communities by providing them with the necessary skills to secure sustainable employment. 

Benefits include financial assistance for skill training programs and support for self-employment initiatives. 

Eligible beneficiaries are youth from Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes aged between 18 to 35 years. 

The application process typically involves submitting an application form along with necessary documents to the designated authorities. 

The timeline for processing applications may vary based on the implementing agency. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment at the national level. 

State governments may also have specific agencies responsible for the execution of the scheme at the state level.
