The Meghalaya Employment Generation Scheme (MEGP) is an initiative by the Government of Meghalaya. 

It aims to provide self-employment opportunities to the youth of the state. 

The objective is to enhance the livelihood of unemployed youth through entrepreneurship. 

The scheme offers financial assistance for setting up small businesses and enterprises. 

Benefits include subsidies and loans to support the establishment of new ventures. 

Eligibility is primarily focused on unemployed youth in Meghalaya. 

Applicants must be between the ages of 18 and 35 years. 

The application process involves submitting a detailed project report to the concerned department. 

Applications can be submitted online or through designated offices. 

The timeline for approval may vary based on the completeness of the application. 

The implementing authority for MEGP is the Directorate of Employment and Craftsmen Training, Meghalaya. 

Additional details include the provision of skill development training for applicants. 

The scheme also encourages the establishment of micro and small enterprises.
