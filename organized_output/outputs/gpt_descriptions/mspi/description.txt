The Mandi Samiti Prabandhan Initiative (MSPI) is a government welfare scheme aimed at improving the functioning of agricultural markets in India. 

The objective of MSPI is to enhance the efficiency and transparency of agricultural marketing. 

The scheme seeks to provide better price realization for farmers by improving market infrastructure. 

Benefits include increased access to markets and improved marketing practices for agricultural produce. 

Eligibility criteria typically include farmers and agricultural producers who are part of the organized market system. 

The application process involves registration with the relevant agricultural marketing committee or authority. 

The timeline for implementation and frequency of benefits may vary based on state-specific regulations and market conditions. 

The implementing authorities for MSPI include state agricultural marketing boards and local market committees. 

Additional details may include specific training programs for farmers to enhance their marketing skills.
