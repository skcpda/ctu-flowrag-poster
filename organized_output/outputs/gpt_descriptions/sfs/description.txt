The SFS scheme stands for the Scheme for Financial Support to Farmers. 

It was launched to provide financial assistance to farmers in India. 

The objective of the scheme is to enhance the income of farmers and improve their livelihood. 

The scheme aims to provide direct cash transfers to eligible farmers. 

Benefits include financial support that can be used for agricultural expenses. 

Eligibility criteria include being a small or marginal farmer. 

Farmers must possess land ownership documents to qualify. 

The application process involves submitting an application form along with necessary documents to the local agricultural office. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers' Welfare. 

The timeline for disbursement of funds is typically announced by the implementing authorities. 

The frequency of assistance may vary based on government policy. 

Additional details about the scheme can be found on official government websites.
