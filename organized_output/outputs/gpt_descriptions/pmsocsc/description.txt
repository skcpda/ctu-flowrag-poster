The Pradhan Mantri Samagra Shiksha Abhiyan is a centrally sponsored scheme launched by the Government of India. 

It aims to provide quality education to all children from preschool to secondary level. 

The objectives include improving the quality of education and enhancing the learning outcomes of students. 

The scheme focuses on holistic development through a multi-dimensional approach. 

Benefits include financial assistance for infrastructure development and teacher training. 

It also provides resources for the development of educational materials and digital learning tools. 

Eligibility is primarily for schools recognized by the state governments. 

The application process involves schools submitting proposals to the respective state education departments. 

The timeline for implementation is aligned with the academic year. 

The Ministry of Education is the implementing authority for this scheme. 

State governments and local bodies also play a crucial role in execution and monitoring. 

The scheme promotes inclusive education for marginalized and disadvantaged groups. 

It emphasizes the use of technology in education to enhance learning experiences. 

Regular assessments and evaluations are conducted to measure the effectiveness of the scheme.
