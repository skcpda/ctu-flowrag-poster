The scheme SDSUK stands for the Skill Development Scheme for Unemployed Youth of Karnataka. 

It was launched by the Government of Karnataka to address the issue of unemployment among the youth. 

The primary objective is to enhance the employability of unemployed youth through skill development and training programs. 

The scheme provides various benefits including vocational training and skill enhancement programs. 

Financial assistance is also provided to the trainees for transportation and other related expenses. 

Eligibility criteria include being a resident of Karnataka and being between the ages of 18 to 35 years. 

Applicants must be unemployed or underemployed individuals seeking skill development. 

The application process involves filling out an online application form available on the official website. 

The timeline for the training programs varies depending on the specific course chosen by the applicant. 

Implementing authorities include the Department of Skill Development Entrepreneurship and Livelihood of Karnataka. 

The scheme aims to create a skilled workforce that meets the demands of the industry.
