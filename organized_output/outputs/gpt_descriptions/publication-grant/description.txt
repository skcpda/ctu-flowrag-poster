The Publication Grant scheme is designed to support the publication of research and academic works. 

It aims to promote scholarly research and enhance the dissemination of knowledge. 

The scheme provides financial assistance for the publication of books and research papers. 

Eligible applicants include researchers and academicians affiliated with recognized institutions. 

The application process involves submitting a proposal along with the manuscript to the concerned authority. 

The timeline for grant approval may vary based on the review process. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Education and its affiliated agencies. 

Additional details regarding the specific funding amount and conditions may vary based on the project.
