The scheme is known as the Swami Vivekananda Sewa Prakalpa or SBSGGVN. 

It aims to provide financial assistance to the youth for self-employment and skill development. 

The objective is to empower the youth by enhancing their skills and providing them with opportunities for self-employment. 

The benefits include financial support for setting up small enterprises and access to skill development training programs. 

Eligibility criteria include being a resident of the state and belonging to the youth age group as defined by the scheme. 

The application process involves submitting an application form along with required documents to the designated authority. 

The timeline for application processing and fund disbursement varies based on the specific project and implementing agency. 

The implementing authorities include the state government departments responsible for youth affairs and skill development. 

Additional details may include specific training partners and institutions involved in the skill development aspect of the scheme.
