The Mukhyamantri Vatsalya Yojana (MVY) is a welfare scheme initiated by the Government of Madhya Pradesh. 

The scheme aims to provide financial assistance to orphaned children and children in need of care and protection. 

It seeks to ensure the welfare and development of these vulnerable children. 

The benefits include a monthly financial support of Rs. 2000 for each eligible child. 

This assistance is provided until the child reaches the age of 18 years. 

Eligibility criteria include being an orphan or a child in need of care and protection. 

The applicant must be a resident of Madhya Pradesh. 

The application process involves submitting a form along with necessary documents to the local Child Welfare Committee. 

The scheme is implemented by the Department of Women and Child Development, Madhya Pradesh. 

There is no specific timeline mentioned for the application process. 

The assistance is provided on a monthly basis until the child turns 18.
