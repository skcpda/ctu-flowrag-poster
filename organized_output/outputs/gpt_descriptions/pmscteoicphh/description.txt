The Pradhan Mantri Scheme for the Construction of Houses for Economically Weaker Sections (PM-SCTEOI-CPHH) aims to provide housing for the economically weaker sections of society. 

The objective of this scheme is to ensure that every individual has access to affordable housing. 

The benefits include financial assistance for the construction of houses. 

Eligible beneficiaries are those belonging to the economically weaker sections as defined by the government. 

The application process typically involves submitting an application to the local authorities or designated agencies. 

The timeline for disbursement of funds and construction may vary based on local implementation. 

The Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs is the primary implementing authority for this scheme. 

Additional details regarding specific financial assistance amounts or documentation requirements may be available through local government offices.
