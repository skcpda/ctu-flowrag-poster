The National Mission on Oilseeds and Oil Palm (NMOEPPM) is an initiative by the Government of India. 

It aims to reduce the country's dependence on edible oil imports. 

The mission focuses on increasing the production of oilseeds and oil palm in India. 

The objectives include enhancing domestic oilseed production. 

It seeks to improve the income of farmers engaged in oilseed cultivation. 

The scheme provides financial assistance for various activities related to oilseed production. 

This includes support for seeds fertilizers and irrigation. 

Farmers can also receive assistance for setting up oil extraction units. 

Eligibility is primarily targeted at farmers engaged in oilseed cultivation. 

The application process involves registering through the respective state agricultural departments. 

Farmers may need to submit documents proving their land ownership and cultivation activities. 

The timeline for assistance varies based on the specific activity and state guidelines. 

Implementing authorities include the Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers' Welfare. 

State governments also play a crucial role in the implementation of the scheme. 

The mission is part of a broader strategy to achieve self-sufficiency in edible oils. 

It is aligned with the government's goal of doubling farmers' income.
