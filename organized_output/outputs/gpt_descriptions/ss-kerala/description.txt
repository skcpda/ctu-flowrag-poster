The SS-Kerala scheme is a welfare initiative launched by the Government of Kerala. 

It aims to provide social security and financial assistance to the elderly, widows, and differently-abled individuals. 

The objective is to enhance the quality of life for vulnerable populations in the state. 

Benefits include monthly financial aid to eligible beneficiaries. 

The assistance amount varies based on the category of the beneficiary. 

Eligibility criteria include being a resident of Kerala and belonging to specific vulnerable groups such as senior citizens, widows, and persons with disabilities. 

The application process involves submitting a form along with required documents to the local government office or online through the official portal. 

The scheme is implemented by the Department of Social Justice, Government of Kerala. 

Payments are made on a monthly basis to the beneficiaries. 

Additional details may include specific documentation requirements and the need for periodic renewal of eligibility.
