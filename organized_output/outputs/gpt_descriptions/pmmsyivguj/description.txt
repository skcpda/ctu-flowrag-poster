The Pradhan Mantri Matsya Sampada Yojana (PMMSY) is a scheme launched by the Government of India. 

It aims to enhance fish production and promote sustainable fisheries. 

The objectives include increasing fish production to 22 million metric tons by 2024-2025. 

It also aims to generate employment opportunities in the fisheries sector. 

The scheme provides financial assistance for various activities related to fisheries. 

This includes the development of fishing harbors and fish landing centers. 

It also supports the establishment of fish processing units. 

Eligible beneficiaries include individual fishers and fish farmers. 

Self-help groups and cooperatives involved in fisheries are also eligible. 

The application process involves submitting a proposal to the respective state fisheries department. 

The timeline for approval may vary based on the state. 

The Ministry of Fisheries Animal Husbandry and Dairying is the implementing authority. 

State fisheries departments play a crucial role in implementation at the local level. 

The scheme is expected to improve the livelihoods of fishers and boost the economy. 

It also focuses on the conservation of aquatic resources and sustainable fishing practices.
