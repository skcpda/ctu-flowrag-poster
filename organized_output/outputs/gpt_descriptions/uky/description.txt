The Ujjwala Yojana is a government welfare scheme launched in May 2016. 

It aims to provide clean cooking fuel to households in rural and semi-urban areas. 

The primary objective is to reduce the health hazards associated with the use of traditional cooking fuels like firewood and dung. 

The scheme provides financial assistance for the installation of Liquefied Petroleum Gas (LPG) connections. 

Eligible beneficiaries receive a subsidy for the cost of the LPG connection and the first refill. 

The scheme targets Below Poverty Line (BPL) households. 

Women from these households are prioritized as the primary beneficiaries. 

The application process involves submitting a form to the local LPG distributor. 

Applicants must provide proof of identity and address along with a declaration of being a BPL household. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Petroleum and Natural Gas. 

The timeline for receiving the connection can vary based on the distributor's capacity. 

The scheme has been instrumental in promoting the use of clean energy and improving the quality of life for women. 

It has also contributed to reducing indoor air pollution. 

The Ujjwala Yojana has been expanded to include additional benefits such as health and nutrition awareness programs. 

The initiative has received positive feedback for its impact on rural households.
