The VSTSPDEC scheme is aimed at promoting sustainable development in rural areas. 

It focuses on enhancing the livelihood opportunities for rural communities. 

The primary objective is to provide financial assistance for sustainable agricultural practices. 

The scheme benefits farmers by providing them with resources for better crop production. 

It also supports the adoption of eco-friendly farming techniques. 

Eligibility criteria include being a resident of a rural area and being engaged in agricultural activities. 

Farmers must also meet specific income thresholds to qualify for assistance. 

The application process involves submitting a form to the local agricultural office. 

Supporting documents such as identity proof and land ownership records are required. 

The timeline for application processing may vary based on the local authority's workload. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers' Welfare. 

State agricultural departments also play a role in the implementation. 

Regular monitoring and evaluation are conducted to assess the impact of the scheme.
