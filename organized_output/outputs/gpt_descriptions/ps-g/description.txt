The Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samman Nidhi (PM-KISAN) scheme was launched by the Government of India in February 2019. 

It aims to provide financial support to small and marginal farmers. 

The objective is to ensure a steady income for farmers and enhance their livelihood security. 

Under this scheme, eligible farmers receive direct cash transfers of Rs. 6000 per year. 

The amount is disbursed in three equal installments of Rs. 2000 each. 

Eligibility is limited to small and marginal farmers who own cultivable land. 

Farmers must have a landholding of up to 2 hectares. 

The scheme excludes certain categories such as institutional landholders and farmers with higher landholdings. 

The application process involves registering through the PM-KISAN portal or through designated state government offices. 

Farmers must provide necessary documents such as land ownership proof and bank account details. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers' Welfare. 

Funds are transferred directly to the bank accounts of the beneficiaries. 

The disbursement of funds occurs three times a year. 

The scheme is ongoing and has been extended to cover more beneficiaries over time.
