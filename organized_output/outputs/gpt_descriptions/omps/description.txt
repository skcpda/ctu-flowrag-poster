The OMPs scheme stands for the One Nation One Ration Card scheme. 

It was launched to ensure that beneficiaries can access their entitled food grains from any ration shop across the country. 

The primary objective is to provide food security to migrant workers and their families. 

The scheme aims to eliminate the need for multiple ration cards and facilitate the portability of food grains. 

Eligible beneficiaries include individuals who are part of the National Food Security Act. 

The application process involves linking existing ration cards with the Aadhaar number. 

This linkage helps in the identification of beneficiaries and prevents duplication. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Consumer Affairs Food and Public Distribution. 

States and Union Territories are responsible for the operational aspects of the scheme. 

The scheme is expected to be implemented in all states and Union Territories by March 2021. 

It allows beneficiaries to access subsidized food grains from any fair price shop in the country. 

The frequency of assistance is monthly based on the entitlement of beneficiaries. 

The scheme also aims to strengthen the Public Distribution System. 

It promotes transparency and efficiency in the distribution of food grains. 

The initiative is part of the government's broader efforts to enhance food security in India.
