The Pradhan Mantri Shram Yogi Maan-Dhan Yojana is a pension scheme for unorganized workers. 

It aims to provide social security to workers in the unorganized sector. 

The objective is to ensure a monthly pension of Rs. 3000 after the age of 60 years. 

The scheme benefits unorganized workers by providing them financial security in old age. 

Eligibility includes being an unorganized worker aged between 18 to 40 years. 

The applicant must have a monthly income of less than Rs. 15000. 

The application process involves filling out a registration form available online or at designated centers. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Labour and Employment. 

The pension is disbursed monthly after the beneficiary turns 60 years old. 

The scheme also includes provisions for family pension in case of the beneficiary's demise.
