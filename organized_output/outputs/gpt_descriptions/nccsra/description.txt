The National Cooperative Consumer's Store and Retail Association (NCCSRA) scheme aims to promote cooperative consumer stores in India. 

The objective of the scheme is to enhance the availability of essential goods at reasonable prices through cooperative societies. 

The benefits include financial assistance for setting up consumer stores and support for operational costs. 

Eligibility is typically limited to registered cooperative societies involved in consumer retail. 

The application process involves submitting a proposal to the relevant cooperative department or authority. 

The timeline for approval and disbursement of funds may vary based on the specific project and state regulations. 

The implementing authorities include the Ministry of Cooperation and respective state cooperative departments. 

Additional details may include guidelines on the types of goods to be sold and operational standards to be maintained.
