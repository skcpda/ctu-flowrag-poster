PM-POSHAN is a scheme initiated by the Government of India. 

It aims to provide nutritious meals to school children. 

The program is designed to improve the nutritional status of children in primary and upper primary schools. 

The scheme is a continuation of the Mid-Day Meal Scheme. 

The objective is to enhance enrollment and attendance in schools. 

It also aims to improve the retention of children in schools. 

The benefits include the provision of cooked meals to children. 

The meals are designed to meet the nutritional requirements of children. 

The assistance includes the supply of food grains and cooking cost. 

Eligibility is primarily for children enrolled in government and government-aided schools. 

The application process is typically managed at the school level. 

Schools must register under the scheme to receive benefits. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Education. 

State governments and union territories are responsible for execution. 

The frequency of meal provision is daily on school days. 

Additional provisions include the incorporation of local and seasonal foods. 

Monitoring and evaluation are conducted to ensure quality and effectiveness.
