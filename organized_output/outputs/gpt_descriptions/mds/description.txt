Mahatma Gandhi National Rural Employment Guarantee Act is commonly known as MGNREGA. 

It was enacted in 2005 to enhance livelihood security in rural areas. 

The main objective is to provide at least 100 days of unskilled wage employment in a financial year to every rural household. 

The scheme aims to improve the purchasing power of rural households and enhance livelihood security. 

Benefits include guaranteed wage employment and the creation of durable assets in rural areas. 

The assistance is provided in the form of wages for unskilled manual work. 

Eligibility is limited to adult members of rural households who are willing to do unskilled manual work. 

The application process involves submitting a demand for work to the local Gram Panchayat. 

The timeline for wage payment is generally within 15 days of work completion. 

The Ministry of Rural Development is the primary implementing authority for MGNREGA. 

State governments are responsible for the execution of the scheme at the local level. 

The scheme also promotes social inclusion and empowers marginalized groups. 

It has provisions for grievance redressal to address issues faced by beneficiaries. 

MGNREGA has been instrumental in reducing rural poverty and improving living standards.
