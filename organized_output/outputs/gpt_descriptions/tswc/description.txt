The Telangana State Warehousing Corporation (TSWC) scheme aims to provide efficient warehousing facilities in the state of Telangana. 

The objective of the scheme is to promote agricultural productivity by ensuring proper storage of agricultural produce. 

The scheme benefits farmers by providing them with access to modern warehousing facilities. 

It also assists in reducing post-harvest losses and stabilizing market prices for agricultural commodities. 

Eligibility for the scheme primarily includes farmers and agricultural producers in Telangana. 

The application process involves farmers submitting their requests to the nearest TSWC office for warehousing space. 

The timeline for availing the services is based on the availability of warehouse space and the specific requirements of the farmers. 

The TSWC is the implementing authority responsible for managing and operating the warehousing facilities. 

Additional details may include the provision of cold storage facilities for perishable goods. 

The scheme also aims to enhance the overall supply chain management for agricultural products in the state.
