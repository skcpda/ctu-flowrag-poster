The Pradhan Mantri Sahaj Bijli Har Ghar Yojana (PSSLP) aims to provide electricity to all households in rural and urban areas. 

The objective is to ensure access to electricity for every household in India. 

The scheme benefits households by providing free electricity connections. 

Financial assistance is provided for the installation of electricity connections. 

Eligibility includes all households that do not have access to electricity. 

The application process involves filling out a form and submitting it to the local electricity distribution company. 

The timeline for connection varies based on the location and the local electricity distribution company's capacity. 

The implementing authority is the Ministry of Power along with state electricity distribution companies. 

The scheme is part of the government's broader initiative to enhance energy access and promote sustainable development.
