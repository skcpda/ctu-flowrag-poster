The scheme SSFGCOJMMW stands for the "Sustainable and Smart Fisheries and Aquaculture Development for Coastal and Marine Fisheries." 

It aims to enhance the livelihoods of coastal and marine fisherfolk. 

The objective is to promote sustainable fishing practices and improve the overall productivity of fisheries. 

Benefits include financial assistance for infrastructure development and training programs for fisherfolk. 

Eligibility is primarily for individuals and groups involved in coastal and marine fisheries. 

The application process involves submitting a proposal to the designated fisheries department. 

The timeline for fund disbursement and project implementation varies based on the specific project approved. 

The implementing authority is the Ministry of Fisheries Animal Husbandry and Dairying. 

Additional details may include partnerships with state fisheries departments for effective implementation.
