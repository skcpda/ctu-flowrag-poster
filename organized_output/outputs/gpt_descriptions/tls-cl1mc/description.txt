The scheme tls-cl1mc is also known as the "Transport and Logistics Sector Skill Council" initiative. 

It aims to enhance the skill development in the transport and logistics sector. 

The objective is to create a skilled workforce that meets the demands of the industry. 

Benefits include training programs that improve employability in the transport and logistics sector. 

Assistance is provided in the form of skill development training and certification. 

Eligibility criteria typically include individuals seeking employment or skill enhancement in the transport and logistics sector. 

The application process involves registration through the official website of the Transport and Logistics Sector Skill Council. 

The timeline for training programs varies based on the specific course and availability. 

The implementing authority for this scheme is the Transport and Logistics Sector Skill Council. 

This initiative is part of the broader Skill India Mission aimed at promoting skill development across various sectors.
