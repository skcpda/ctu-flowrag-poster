The TMAFA scheme stands for the "Teri Maan Ki Asha Fund for Agriculture." 

It was launched to support farmers in India facing challenges in agriculture. 

The objective of the scheme is to provide financial assistance to farmers for improving agricultural productivity. 

The benefits include grants for purchasing seeds fertilizers and equipment. 

Eligible beneficiaries are small and marginal farmers who own less than two hectares of land. 

The application process involves submitting a form to the local agricultural department along with necessary documents. 

The timeline for fund disbursement is typically within two months of application approval. 

The implementing authority for the scheme is the Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers' Welfare. 

Additional details about the scheme can be found on the official government website.
