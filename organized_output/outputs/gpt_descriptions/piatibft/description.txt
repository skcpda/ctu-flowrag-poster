The Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana - Housing for All (PMAY-HFA) is a flagship scheme of the Government of India. 

It was launched in June 2015. 

The objective is to provide affordable housing to the urban poor. 

The scheme aims to construct 20 million affordable houses by 2022. 

Benefits include financial assistance for housing construction and subsidies on home loans. 

Eligible beneficiaries include Economically Weaker Sections and Low Income Groups. 

The annual income limit for Economically Weaker Sections is up to INR 3 lakh. 

For Low Income Groups the annual income limit is between INR 3 lakh and INR 6 lakh. 

The application process involves online registration through the PMAY website. 

Beneficiaries must provide required documents such as income proof and identity proof. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs. 

The timeline for house construction varies based on the project and location. 

The scheme is monitored at the state and local levels through Urban Local Bodies. 

The scheme also promotes the use of sustainable and eco-friendly construction technologies.
