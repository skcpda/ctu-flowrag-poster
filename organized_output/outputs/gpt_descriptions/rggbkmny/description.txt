The RGGVY scheme stands for Rajiv Gandhi Grameen Vidyutikaran Yojana. 

It was launched in 2005 to provide electricity to rural areas in India. 

The objective is to provide access to electricity for all rural households. 

The scheme aims to enhance the quality of life and promote economic development in rural regions. 

Benefits include free electricity connections to Below Poverty Line households. 

The scheme also provides financial assistance for the electrification of villages. 

Eligibility is primarily based on being a resident of a rural area and belonging to the Below Poverty Line category. 

The application process involves submitting a request to the local electricity distribution company. 

The timeline for the completion of electrification projects varies based on the specific project. 

Implementing authorities include the Ministry of Power and state electricity boards. 

The scheme is part of the broader initiative to achieve 24x7 power supply in rural areas. 

It also promotes the use of renewable energy sources in rural electrification. 

The scheme has been instrumental in reducing the rural-urban electricity gap.
