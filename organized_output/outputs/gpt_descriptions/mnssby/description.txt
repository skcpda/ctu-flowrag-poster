The Mukhyamantri Nirmal Sewa Yojana (MNSSBY) is a welfare scheme initiated by the Government of Madhya Pradesh. 

The scheme aims to provide financial assistance to families for the construction of toilets. 

It seeks to promote sanitation and hygiene in rural areas. 

The benefits include a subsidy for toilet construction to eligible families. 

The assistance amount is typically up to INR 12,000 per toilet. 

Eligibility is primarily for families living below the poverty line. 

The scheme targets rural households without access to proper sanitation facilities. 

The application process involves submitting a form at the local Panchayat office. 

Documents required may include proof of residence and income certificate. 

The timeline for disbursement of funds is generally within a few weeks of application approval. 

The scheme is implemented by the Panchayati Raj Department of Madhya Pradesh. 

Regular monitoring and evaluation are conducted to ensure effective implementation. 

Awareness campaigns are also organized to educate the public about the scheme.
