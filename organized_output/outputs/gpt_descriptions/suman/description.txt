Suman is a government welfare scheme aimed at improving maternal and child health in India. 

The objective of the scheme is to reduce maternal and infant mortality rates by providing financial assistance for safe delivery and postnatal care. 

The scheme offers a financial incentive of INR 5,000 to pregnant women for institutional delivery and postnatal care. 

Eligibility for the scheme includes pregnant women who are residents of India and belong to economically weaker sections. 

The application process requires beneficiaries to register at the nearest health facility and provide necessary documentation. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Health and Family Welfare at the national level. 

The financial assistance is provided during the antenatal period and after delivery. 

The scheme aims to promote institutional deliveries and ensure better health outcomes for mothers and infants.
