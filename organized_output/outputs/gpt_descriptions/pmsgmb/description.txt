The Pradhan Mantri Shram Yogi Maandhan (PMSY Maandhan) scheme is a pension scheme for unorganized sector workers. 

It was launched to provide social security to workers in the unorganized sector. 

The objective is to ensure a monthly pension of Rs. 3000 after the age of 60 years. 

The scheme aims to alleviate poverty among the elderly in the unorganized sector. 

Eligible beneficiaries include unorganized workers aged between 18 to 40 years. 

They must have a monthly income of less than Rs. 15,000. 

The application process involves filling out a registration form available online or at designated centers. 

Beneficiaries must provide necessary documents such as identity proof and income certificate. 

The pension is paid monthly after the age of 60. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Labour and Employment. 

The contribution for the scheme is based on the age of entry into the scheme. 

The government matches the contribution made by the beneficiaries. 

The scheme also provides for family pension in case of the pensioner's demise. 

Additional details regarding the scheme can be found on the official website.
