The MBSBBOCWWB scheme stands for the Madhya Pradesh Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board.

It was launched to provide welfare benefits to construction workers in Madhya Pradesh.

The main objective is to ensure the social and economic security of construction workers.

The scheme offers various benefits including financial assistance for medical treatment and education for children.

It also provides assistance for housing and skill development programs.

Eligibility is primarily for construction workers who are registered with the Madhya Pradesh Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board.

The application process involves registration with the board and submission of required documents.

The timeline for processing applications may vary based on the type of assistance sought.

The implementing authority for this scheme is the Madhya Pradesh Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board.

Additional details may include the need for periodic renewal of registration to continue availing benefits.
