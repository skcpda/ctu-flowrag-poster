This scheme aims to enhance the livelihood opportunities for Scheduled Castes.

The primary objective is to provide vocational training and skill enhancement.

It aims to reduce poverty and improve the socio-economic status of Scheduled Castes.

Participants may receive support for setting up their own businesses. 

The scheme also provides access to various resources and tools necessary for skill development. 

Eligible beneficiaries include individuals belonging to Scheduled Castes. 

They must be above the age of 18 years. 

The scheme is open to both men and women from the Scheduled Caste community.
