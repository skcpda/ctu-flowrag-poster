The UktEps scheme is an initiative by the Government of India aimed at enhancing the employability of youth. 

The scheme focuses on providing skill development and vocational training to young individuals. 

The primary objective is to bridge the gap between education and employment. 

The benefits include access to skill training programs and job placement assistance. 

Eligibility criteria typically include being a resident of India and being within a specific age group. 

The application process generally involves online registration through the official portal. 

The timeline for training programs may vary based on the specific courses offered.
