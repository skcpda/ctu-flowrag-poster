The scheme is known as the State Fund for the Promotion of Backward Classes in Jharkhand. 

It aims to provide financial assistance to individuals from backward classes for their educational and economic upliftment. 

The primary objective is to enhance the socio-economic status of backward classes in Jharkhand. 

Benefits include scholarships for students and financial aid for self-employment ventures. 

Eligibility criteria include being a resident of Jharkhand and belonging to a backward class as defined by the state government. 

The application process involves submitting a duly filled application form along with required documents to the designated authority. 

The scheme is implemented by the Department of Welfare of Backward Classes and Extremely Backward Classes in Jharkhand. 

The frequency of assistance may vary based on the specific type of aid being sought. 

Further details can be accessed through the official state government portals.
