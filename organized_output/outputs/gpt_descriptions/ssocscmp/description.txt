The scheme is known as the Social Security Scheme for the Construction Workers (SSOCSCMP). 

It aims to provide social security and welfare benefits to construction workers in India. 

The objectives include ensuring financial assistance during times of need and promoting the welfare of construction workers. 

Benefits include financial assistance for medical emergencies and support for education of children. 

Eligibility criteria typically include being a registered construction worker and meeting specific income thresholds. 

The application process generally involves registration with the local labor department or relevant authority. 

Applications may require submission of identity proof and proof of employment in the construction sector. 

The timeline for processing applications can vary based on the local authority's procedures. 

The implementing authority is usually the Ministry of Labour and Employment at the central level and respective state labor departments. 

Additional details may include provisions for skill development and training programs for construction workers.
