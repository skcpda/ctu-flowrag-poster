The SACEIASRE scheme is aimed at providing support to the agricultural sector in India. 

The objective of the scheme is to enhance the income of farmers and promote sustainable agricultural practices. 

The scheme offers financial assistance for various agricultural activities and initiatives. 

Eligibility is generally limited to farmers and agricultural workers in specific regions. 

The application process typically involves submitting forms to designated agricultural offices or online portals. 

The timeline for assistance may vary depending on the specific agricultural activity and the season. 

The implementing authorities include the Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers' Welfare and state agricultural departments. 

Additional details about specific benefits and the exact nature of assistance can vary by state and program focus.
