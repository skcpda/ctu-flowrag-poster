The Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana - Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana (PMAY) is a government initiative aimed at providing affordable housing to the urban poor.

The objective of PMAY is to ensure that every family has access to a pucca house with basic amenities.

The benefits include financial assistance for housing construction or purchase through interest subsidies on home loans.

Eligible beneficiaries include Economically Weaker Sections (EWS) Low Income Groups (LIG) and Middle Income Groups (MIG).

The application process involves submitting an online application through the official PMAY website or through Common Service Centers.

The scheme is implemented in a phased manner with a target to complete housing units by 2022.

The Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs is the primary implementing authority for this scheme.
