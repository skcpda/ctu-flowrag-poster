The R-CF scheme is a government initiative aimed at providing financial assistance to farmers. 

The primary objective of the R-CF scheme is to enhance the income of farmers through direct cash transfers. 

The scheme offers benefits in the form of financial support for agricultural activities. 

Eligibility for the R-CF scheme typically includes small and marginal farmers. 

Farmers must be registered under the relevant state agricultural department to apply. 

The application process usually involves submitting an online application through the official government portal. 

The timeline for disbursement of funds is generally aligned with the agricultural calendar. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers' Welfare. 

State governments may also play a role in the implementation and monitoring of the scheme. 

Additional details may include specific financial limits and conditions based on state regulations.
