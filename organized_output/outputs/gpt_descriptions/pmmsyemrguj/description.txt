The Pradhan Mantri Matsya Sampada Yojana (PMMSY) is a scheme launched by the Government of India. 

It aims to promote sustainable and responsible development of the fisheries sector in India. 

The objectives include enhancing fish production and productivity. 

It also seeks to create employment opportunities in the fisheries sector. 

The scheme provides financial assistance for various activities related to fisheries. 

These activities include aquaculture, marine fisheries, and fish processing. 

Beneficiaries can receive subsidies for infrastructure development. 

This includes setting up fish farms and processing units. 

Eligibility criteria include being a resident of India. 

The applicant must be involved in the fisheries sector. 

The application process involves submitting a proposal to the concerned state fisheries department. 

The proposal should detail the intended project and required funding. 

The timeline for approval may vary based on the project scale. 

The scheme is implemented by the Department of Fisheries under the Ministry of Fisheries Animal Husbandry and Dairying. 

Additional details may include the provision for skill development and training programs. 

These programs aim to enhance the skills of individuals in the fisheries sector.
