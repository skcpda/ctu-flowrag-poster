The Vishwa Yuva Kendra (VYK) scheme is an initiative aimed at empowering youth through skill development and employment opportunities. 

The objective of the scheme is to provide vocational training and enhance employability among the youth. 

The benefits include skill development programs that lead to better job prospects. 

Assistance is provided in the form of training sessions and workshops. 

Eligibility criteria typically include age limits and educational qualifications which may vary by program. 

The application process usually involves filling out an online or offline application form. 

The timeline for the training programs may vary based on the specific courses offered. 

Implementing authorities include the Ministry of Youth Affairs and Sports and various state governments. 

Additional details may include partnerships with private organizations for training and placement.
