The Swachh Bharat Mission Gramin (SBM-G) is a government initiative aimed at improving sanitation and hygiene in rural areas of India. 

The mission was launched on October 2, 2014. 

Its primary objective is to eliminate open defecation and promote the construction of individual household latrines in rural households. 

The program aims to create a clean and hygienic environment in rural areas. 

It also focuses on raising awareness about sanitation and hygiene practices among rural communities. 

Beneficiaries of the scheme include rural households that do not have access to toilets. 

Financial assistance is provided to eligible households for the construction of toilets. 

The assistance amount varies by state and is determined by the respective state government. 

Eligibility for the scheme includes being a resident of a rural area and not having a functional toilet. 

The application process typically involves submitting an application to the local Gram Panchayat. 

The Gram Panchayat verifies the application and facilitates the construction of the toilet. 

The timeline for the implementation of the scheme is ongoing. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Jal Shakti in collaboration with state governments and local bodies. 

Monitoring and evaluation of the scheme are conducted to ensure its effectiveness and sustainability. 

The mission also promotes solid waste management and cleanliness in rural areas. 

Awareness campaigns are conducted to educate the public about the importance of sanitation. 

The SBM-G is part of the larger Swachh Bharat Mission which includes urban sanitation initiatives.
