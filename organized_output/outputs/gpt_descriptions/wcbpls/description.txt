The WCBPLS stands for the Women and Child Benefit Program for Livelihood Support. 

This scheme aims to provide financial assistance to women and children in vulnerable situations. 

The primary objective is to enhance the livelihood opportunities for women while ensuring the welfare of children. 

Beneficiaries receive direct cash transfers to support their economic activities and improve their living conditions. 

Eligibility is typically based on socio-economic criteria which may include income levels and family status. 

The application process generally involves submitting a form along with necessary documentation to the designated local authority. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Women and Child Development. 

The frequency of assistance may vary based on the specific guidelines of the program. 

Additional details about the scheme can be found on official government websites.
