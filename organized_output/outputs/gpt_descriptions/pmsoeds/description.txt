The Pradhan Mantri Suraksha Maandhan Yojana (PMSMY) is a welfare scheme launched by the Government of India. 

It aims to provide social security to workers in the unorganized sector. 

The objective is to ensure a monthly pension for individuals after they reach the age of 60. 

The scheme offers a pension of Rs. 3,000 per month to eligible beneficiaries. 

Eligibility criteria include individuals aged between 18 to 40 years. 

Applicants must have a monthly income of less than Rs. 15,000. 

The application process involves registering online through the official portal or visiting designated centers. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Labour and Employment. 

The pension is disbursed monthly after the beneficiary turns 60 years old. 

The scheme also encourages regular savings for a secure future. 

It is part of the government's broader initiative to enhance social security coverage.
