The scheme is known as the Scheme for Financial Assistance to Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes for the National Programme for the Welfare of Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes.

It aims to provide financial assistance to Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes for various welfare activities.

The benefits include financial support for education health and skill development.

Eligibility is primarily based on the individual's caste status as a Scheduled Caste or Scheduled Tribe.

The application process involves submitting the required documents to the concerned department in the respective state or union territory.

The timeline for assistance may vary based on the specific activity and the processing time of applications.

The implementing authorities are the respective state governments and union territory administrations.
