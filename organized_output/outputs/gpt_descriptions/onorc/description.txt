The ONORC scheme stands for One Nation One Ration Card. 

It was launched by the Government of India to ensure food security for migrant workers and their families. 

The objective is to provide access to subsidized food grains to beneficiaries across the country regardless of their location. 

The scheme allows beneficiaries to use their ration cards in any state or union territory. 

This ensures that no one goes hungry due to migration for work. 

Eligible beneficiaries include those who hold a National Food Security Act ration card. 

The application process involves linking the ration card to the Aadhaar number. 

States and union territories are responsible for implementing the scheme. 

The scheme is operational in several states and is being expanded nationwide. 

The frequency of assistance is monthly based on the entitlement of food grains. 

The implementing authorities include the Ministry of Consumer Affairs Food and Public Distribution. 

State governments are also involved in the execution and monitoring of the scheme.
