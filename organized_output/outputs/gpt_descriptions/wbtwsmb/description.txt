The West Bengal Tea Workers' Welfare Scheme is designed to provide social security and welfare benefits to tea garden workers in West Bengal. 

The objective of the scheme is to improve the living conditions of tea workers and their families. 

Benefits include financial assistance for health care education and housing. 

Eligibility is primarily for workers employed in tea gardens in West Bengal. 

The application process involves submitting an application form along with necessary documents to the local tea garden management or designated authorities. 

The timeline for disbursement of benefits may vary based on the specific assistance being provided. 

The implementing authority for this scheme is the West Bengal Labour Department. 

Additional details may include provisions for skill development and vocational training for tea workers.
