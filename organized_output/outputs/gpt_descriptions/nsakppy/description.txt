The National Scheme for Assistance to Persons with Disabilities (NSAPD) is an initiative by the Government of India. 

It aims to provide financial assistance to persons with disabilities. 

The objective is to enhance the quality of life and promote social inclusion. 

Benefits include financial support for individuals with disabilities. 

The assistance is provided on a monthly basis to eligible beneficiaries. 

Eligibility criteria include being a resident of India and having a certified disability. 

The application process involves submitting a form along with required documents to the local authorities. 

The timeline for processing applications may vary based on the local administration. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment. 

Additional details may include provisions for different types of disabilities. 

The scheme also emphasizes awareness and capacity building for stakeholders.
