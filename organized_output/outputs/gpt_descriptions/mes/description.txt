The Maternity Entitlement Scheme (MES) is a government initiative aimed at providing financial assistance to pregnant and lactating women. 

The scheme seeks to improve maternal and child health by providing cash benefits during pregnancy and after childbirth. 

The primary objective is to reduce maternal and infant mortality rates by promoting proper nutrition and healthcare during pregnancy. 

Eligible beneficiaries include pregnant women and lactating mothers who are registered under the scheme. 

The scheme provides a cash incentive to support women during their maternity period. 

The application process typically involves registration through local health centers or online platforms. 

The timeline for receiving benefits may vary based on the specific implementation guidelines of the state or region. 

The Ministry of Women and Child Development is the main implementing authority for this scheme.
