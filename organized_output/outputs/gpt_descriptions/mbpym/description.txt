The Mukhyamantri Bhatta Yojana for Migrant Workers (MBPYM) is a welfare scheme initiated by the Government of India. 

The scheme aims to provide financial assistance to migrant workers who have been affected by the COVID-19 pandemic. 

It seeks to support these workers in meeting their basic needs during times of distress. 

Eligible beneficiaries include migrant workers who have lost their jobs or sources of income due to the pandemic. 

The financial assistance provided under the scheme is typically a one-time payment. 

The amount of assistance may vary based on the specific guidelines set by the implementing authorities. 

To apply for the scheme, eligible workers must fill out an application form available through designated government portals or local offices. 

The application process may require submission of identity proof and documents verifying employment status. 

The timeline for disbursal of funds may vary depending on the processing of applications. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Labour and Employment in collaboration with state governments. 

Additional details regarding the scheme can be found on official government websites and portals.
