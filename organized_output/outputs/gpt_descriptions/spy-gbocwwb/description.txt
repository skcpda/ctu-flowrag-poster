The scheme is known as the "Gujarat Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board" or GBOCWWB. 

It is designed to provide welfare benefits to workers in the building and construction sector in Gujarat. 

The primary objective is to ensure the welfare and financial security of construction workers. 

Benefits include financial assistance for medical treatment education and housing. 

Workers can also receive assistance for the marriage of their daughters and for the education of their children. 

Eligibility criteria include being a registered construction worker under the Gujarat Building and Other Construction Workers Act. 

Applicants must be at least 18 years old and should not be a member of any other welfare scheme. 

The application process involves filling out a form and submitting it to the local GBOCWWB office along with required documents. 

The timeline for processing applications may vary but is generally completed within a few weeks. 

The scheme is implemented by the Gujarat Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board. 

This board is responsible for the disbursement of funds and monitoring the welfare of construction workers.
