The PATS Scheme is a government initiative aimed at providing financial assistance to farmers. 

It is designed to support farmers in the procurement of agricultural inputs. 

The main objective is to enhance agricultural productivity and ensure food security. 

Farmers receive financial aid for purchasing seeds fertilizers and other necessary inputs. 

Eligibility is generally limited to small and marginal farmers. 

The application process typically involves submitting relevant documents to local agricultural offices. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers' Welfare. 

The frequency of assistance may vary based on the agricultural cycle and specific needs. 

Additional details about the scheme can be found on official government websites.
