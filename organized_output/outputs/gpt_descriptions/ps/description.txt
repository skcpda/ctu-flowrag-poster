The Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana (PMAY) is a flagship housing scheme launched by the Government of India in 2015. 

The scheme aims to provide affordable housing to the urban poor and promote the construction of houses for economically weaker sections. 

The objective is to ensure that every family has a pucca house with basic amenities by the year 2022. 

Benefits include financial assistance for house construction or purchase and interest subsidies on home loans. 

Eligible beneficiaries include individuals from economically weaker sections and low-income groups. 

The application process involves submitting an online application through the PMAY website or visiting designated Common Service Centers. 

The scheme is implemented in phases with ongoing applications accepted as per the guidelines. 

The Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs is the primary implementing authority for PMAY. 

State governments and urban local bodies also play a crucial role in the execution of the scheme.
