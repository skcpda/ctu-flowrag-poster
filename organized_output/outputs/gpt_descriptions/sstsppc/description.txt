The scheme is known as the Scheme for Strengthening the State Transport Undertakings for Public Transport Connectivity (SSTSPPC). 

It aims to enhance public transport services across states in India. 

The objective is to improve the quality and accessibility of public transport systems. 

Benefits include financial assistance for the purchase of buses and development of transport infrastructure. 

Eligible beneficiaries include state transport undertakings and other public transport agencies. 

The application process involves submitting a proposal to the Ministry of Road Transport and Highways. 

The timeline for fund disbursement and project implementation varies based on the specific project approved. 

The Ministry of Road Transport and Highways is the implementing authority for this scheme. 

The scheme is part of the broader initiative to promote sustainable urban transport solutions.
