The Skill Enhancement Scheme for Persons with Disabilities (SPESP) is an initiative by the Government of India. 

The scheme aims to enhance the employability of persons with disabilities through skill development. 

It provides training and skill development programs tailored to the needs of individuals with disabilities. 

The benefits include access to vocational training and skill development resources. 

Eligible beneficiaries are persons with disabilities as defined under the Rights of Persons with Disabilities Act 2016. 

The application process typically involves registration with the relevant state or district authorities. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment. 

The timeline for training programs may vary based on the specific courses offered. 

Additional details about specific training programs and locations can be obtained from local implementing agencies.
