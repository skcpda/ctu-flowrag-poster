Pradhan Mantri Suraksha Bima Yojana (PMSBY) is a government-backed accident insurance scheme. 

It was launched on 9th May 2015. 

The scheme aims to provide financial support to the families of individuals who suffer from accidental death or disability. 

The primary objective is to enhance the social security of the poor and underprivileged sections of society. 

Under PMSBY, a sum of Rs. 2 lakh is provided in case of accidental death or total disability. 

In case of partial disability, a sum of Rs. 1 lakh is provided. 

The premium for the scheme is Rs. 12 per annum. 

Eligibility for PMSBY includes individuals aged between 18 and 70 years. 

Applicants must have a savings bank account. 

The application process involves enrolling through the bank where the individual holds an account. 

The enrollment can be done online or offline. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Finance through public sector insurance companies. 

The coverage period is from 1st June to 31st May of the following year. 

Renewal of the scheme is automatic unless the subscriber opts out. 

The scheme is available to all eligible individuals across India.
