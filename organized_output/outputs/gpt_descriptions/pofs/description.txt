The Pradhan Mantri Ujjwala Yojana (PMUY) aims to provide LPG connections to women from Below Poverty Line households. 

The scheme was launched in May 2016 to promote clean cooking fuel and reduce health hazards associated with traditional cooking methods. 

The primary objective is to empower women by providing them with easy access to clean cooking fuel. 

Beneficiaries receive a financial assistance of Rs. 1,600 for each LPG connection. 

The scheme also aims to reduce indoor air pollution and improve the health of women and children. 

Eligibility is limited to women from Below Poverty Line households. 

The application process involves filling out a form available at designated LPG distribution centers. 

Beneficiaries must provide proof of identity and address along with the application. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Petroleum and Natural Gas. 

The timeline for receiving the LPG connection varies but is generally completed within a few weeks of application approval. 

The scheme has been expanded to cover additional households beyond the initial target. 

The initiative also includes a focus on promoting the use of clean cooking gas in rural areas.
