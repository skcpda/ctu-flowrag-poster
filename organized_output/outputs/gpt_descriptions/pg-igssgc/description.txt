The PG-IGSSGC scheme stands for Post Graduate Indira Gandhi Scholarship for Single Girl Child. 

It was launched by the Ministry of Human Resource Development. 

The scheme aims to support single girl children who are pursuing post-graduate education. 

The objective is to promote education among girls and encourage them to pursue higher studies. 

The benefits include a scholarship amount of INR 36,200 per annum. 

This financial assistance is provided for a maximum of two years. 

Eligibility criteria include being a single girl child of parents who are not more than one girl child. 

The applicant must be enrolled in a regular post-graduate course in a recognized university. 

The application process involves filling out an online application form on the National Scholarship Portal. 

Documents required include proof of admission and a certificate of being a single girl child. 

The timeline for application typically aligns with the academic calendar and is announced annually. 

The implementing authority for this scheme is the University Grants Commission. 

The scheme is part of the government's broader initiative to empower women through education.
