The Yuva Integrated Programme for Business (YIPB) is a government initiative aimed at promoting entrepreneurship among the youth of India. 

The objective of YIPB is to provide support and resources to young entrepreneurs to help them establish and grow their businesses. 

The scheme offers various benefits including financial assistance training programs and mentorship. 

Eligibility is primarily targeted at young individuals aged between 18 to 35 years who have a viable business idea. 

The application process involves submitting a detailed business proposal along with personal identification documents. 

The timeline for processing applications may vary but typically takes a few weeks after submission. 

The implementing authorities for this scheme include the Ministry of Youth Affairs and Sports and various state-level entrepreneurship development agencies. 

Additional details about the program may include workshops networking events and access to incubation centers.
