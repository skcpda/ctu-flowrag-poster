The National Scheme for Youth and Child Growth (NSYCG) aims to promote the overall development of children and youth in India. 

The objective of NSYCG is to enhance the physical and mental well-being of children and youth. 

The scheme provides various benefits including nutritional support educational assistance and skill development programs. 

Eligibility for the scheme includes children and youth aged between 6 to 24 years from economically weaker sections of society. 

The application process typically involves filling out a form and submitting it to the local implementing authority. 

The scheme is implemented on a continuous basis with periodic reviews and updates. 

The Ministry of Women and Child Development is the primary implementing authority for NSYCG. 

Additional details about specific programs under the scheme may vary by state or region.
