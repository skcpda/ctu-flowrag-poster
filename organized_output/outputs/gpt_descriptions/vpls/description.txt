The VPLS scheme stands for the Village Public Library Scheme. 

It aims to promote literacy and provide access to information in rural areas. 

The objective is to establish public libraries in villages to encourage reading and learning. 

The scheme provides financial assistance for setting up libraries. 

Benefits include access to books newspapers and other educational materials. 

Eligibility is generally for rural areas with no existing library facilities. 

The application process involves submitting a proposal to the concerned state government. 

The timeline for implementation may vary based on state policies. 

The Ministry of Panchayati Raj is the implementing authority for this scheme. 

The scheme also encourages community participation in library management. 

It aims to foster a culture of reading and knowledge sharing in villages.
