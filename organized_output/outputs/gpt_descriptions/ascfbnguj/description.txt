The scheme known as ASCFBNGUJ stands for the Ayushman Bharat - Scheme for Comprehensive Health Insurance for the Beneficiaries of National Urban Livelihoods Mission.

This scheme is part of the broader Ayushman Bharat initiative which aims to provide accessible and affordable healthcare to the underprivileged sections of society.

The Ayushman Bharat initiative was launched by the Government of India in September 2018.

It aims to provide health insurance coverage of up to five lakh rupees per family per year.

This coverage is intended for secondary and tertiary care hospitalization.

The ASCFBNGUJ specifically targets beneficiaries of the National Urban Livelihoods Mission.

The National Urban Livelihoods Mission is a scheme launched by the Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs.

It aims to reduce poverty by promoting self-employment and wage employment for the urban poor.

The objectives of the ASCFBNGUJ scheme include improving access to quality healthcare services for urban poor.

It aims to provide financial protection to the urban poor against high medical expenses.

The scheme also seeks to enhance the quality of healthcare services available to the urban poor.

It aims to ensure that healthcare services are accessible and affordable for all.

The benefits of the ASCFBNGUJ scheme include cashless treatment for beneficiaries.

Beneficiaries can avail of services in empaneled hospitals across the country.

The scheme covers a wide range of medical procedures and treatments.

It includes pre-hospitalization and post-hospitalization expenses.

The scheme also covers day-care procedures that do not require 24-hour hospitalization.

The assistance provided under the scheme is designed to alleviate the financial burden of healthcare.

Eligibility for the ASCFBNGUJ scheme is primarily based on the criteria set for the National Urban Livelihoods Mission.

Beneficiaries must be part of the urban poor population.

They must be registered under the National Urban Livelihoods Mission.

The scheme is aimed at families living below the poverty line.

The application process for the ASCFBNGUJ scheme involves several steps.

Eligible beneficiaries must first ensure they are registered under the National Urban Livelihoods Mission.

They can then approach the designated authorities for enrollment in the scheme.

The enrollment process may require the submission of certain documents.

These documents typically include proof of identity and proof of residence.

Once the application is submitted it will be verified by the concerned authorities.

Upon successful verification beneficiaries will receive a health card.

This health card will enable them to access the benefits of the scheme.

The timeline for the application process may vary depending on the location.

Generally the verification and issuance of health cards can take a few weeks.

The frequency of benefits under the ASCFBNGUJ scheme is not limited to a specific time frame.

Beneficiaries can avail of the health insurance coverage as needed.
