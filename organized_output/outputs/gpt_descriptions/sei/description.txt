The SEI scheme stands for the Skill Enhancement Initiative. 

It is aimed at enhancing the skills of individuals to improve their employability. 

The objective is to provide training and skill development to the youth. 

The scheme focuses on various sectors to meet the demands of the job market. 

Benefits include access to skill development programs and training sessions. 

Participants may receive certification upon successful completion of the training. 

Eligibility criteria typically include age limits and educational qualifications. 

The application process usually involves filling out an online or offline form. 

Applicants may need to provide identification and educational documents. 

The timeline for training programs varies based on the specific course. 

Implementing authorities include the Ministry of Skill Development and Entrepreneurship. 

The scheme may also involve partnerships with various training institutes. 

Additional details about specific training programs can be found on official government websites.
