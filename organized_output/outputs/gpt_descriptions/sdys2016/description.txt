The scheme sdys2016 refers to the Skill Development for Youth Scheme. 

This scheme was launched by the Government of India to enhance the employability of youth. 

The primary objective is to provide skill development training to young individuals. 

The scheme aims to bridge the gap between the skills required by industries and the skills possessed by the youth. 

Benefits include access to skill training programs and potential job placements for participants. 

Financial assistance may be provided for training and development activities. 

Eligibility criteria typically include age limits and educational qualifications which may vary by program. 

The application process generally involves filling out an online application form through the official portal. 

The timeline for the training programs can vary depending on the specific skill being taught. 

The Ministry of Skill Development and Entrepreneurship is the implementing authority for this scheme. 

Various training partners and institutions may also be involved in delivering the training programs.
