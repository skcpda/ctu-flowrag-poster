The Scheme for Livelihood Support to Small Tea Growers (SLSTT) aims to provide financial assistance to small tea growers in India. 

The objective is to enhance the livelihood of small tea growers by providing them with necessary support. 

The scheme offers financial assistance to small tea growers to improve their productivity and income. 

Eligibility is typically based on the size of the tea plantation and the income level of the grower. 

The application process involves submitting an application form along with necessary documents to the designated authority. 

The timeline for disbursement of funds is generally specified by the implementing agency. 

The implementing authorities include the Ministry of Commerce and Industry and state governments. 

Additional details regarding the specific amount of assistance and the exact eligibility criteria may vary by state.
