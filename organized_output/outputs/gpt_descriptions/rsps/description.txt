The Rashtriya Swasthya Bima Yojana (RSBY) is a health insurance scheme launched by the Government of India. 

It aims to provide health insurance coverage to below poverty line families. 

The objective is to reduce the financial burden of healthcare on poor families. 

The scheme offers a health insurance cover of up to INR 30,000 per family per year. 

This coverage includes hospitalization expenses for various medical conditions. 

Eligible beneficiaries include families identified as below the poverty line by the government. 

The application process typically involves registration at designated centers. 

Beneficiaries must provide necessary documentation to prove their eligibility. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Health and Family Welfare. 

State governments also play a crucial role in the implementation of RSBY. 

The scheme is operational across various states in India. 

Enrollment is usually done on an annual basis. 

Beneficiaries can avail of the insurance benefits at empaneled hospitals. 

The scheme also includes provisions for cashless treatment. 

Additional details may vary by state based on local implementation.
