The scheme SG-SW refers to the "Sustainable Livelihoods and Empowerment of Women" initiative. 

It aims to enhance the livelihoods of women in rural areas. 

The objective is to empower women through skill development and self-employment opportunities. 

Benefits include financial assistance for starting small enterprises. 

Women can also receive training in various skills to improve their employability. 

Eligibility is primarily for women from below poverty line households. 

The application process typically involves submitting a form to the local government office. 

The timeline for processing applications may vary by region. 

The implementing authorities include the Ministry of Rural Development and local self-governments. 

Additional details may include partnerships with NGOs for training and support.
