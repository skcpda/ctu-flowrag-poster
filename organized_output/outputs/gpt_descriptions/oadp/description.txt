The OADP scheme stands for the One-Stop Assistance for Development of Persons with Disabilities. 

This scheme aims to provide comprehensive support to persons with disabilities in India. 

The objective is to enhance the quality of life and promote the inclusion of persons with disabilities in society. 

Benefits include financial assistance for education healthcare and vocational training. 

Assistance is also provided for the purchase of assistive devices and aids. 

Eligibility is primarily for individuals with disabilities as defined under the Rights of Persons with Disabilities Act 2016. 

The application process involves submitting a form along with necessary documents to the designated authorities. 

The timeline for processing applications may vary based on the specific requirements of the local implementing agency. 

The implementing authorities include the Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment and state governments. 

Additional details may include awareness programs and capacity-building initiatives for families of persons with disabilities.
