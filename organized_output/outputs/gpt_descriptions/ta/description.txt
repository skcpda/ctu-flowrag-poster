The "TA" scheme refers to the Transport Allowance provided to government employees in India. 

This allowance is intended to compensate employees for travel expenses incurred while performing official duties. 

The objective of the scheme is to ensure that employees are not financially burdened due to travel requirements in the course of their work. 

Benefits include a fixed monthly allowance based on the employee's grade pay and travel requirements. 

Eligibility is generally limited to central government employees who are required to travel for official purposes. 

The application process typically involves submitting a request through the respective department or office. 

The allowance is disbursed monthly along with the employee's salary. 

Implementing authorities include the Ministry of Finance and respective departmental heads. 

Additional details may include specific rates for different grades of employees and conditions under which the allowance can be claimed.
