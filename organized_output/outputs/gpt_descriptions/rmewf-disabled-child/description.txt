The scheme is known as the "Rashtriya Mahila Kosh for Empowerment of Women with Disabilities." 

It aims to provide financial assistance and support to women with disabilities. 

The objective is to enhance the economic independence of women with disabilities. 

The scheme offers loans for self-employment and income-generating activities. 

It also provides skill development training and capacity building programs. 

Eligibility is primarily for women with disabilities who are above the age of 18. 

Applicants must have a disability certificate issued by a recognized authority. 

The application process involves submitting a completed application form along with necessary documents. 

Documents required may include identity proof and disability certificate. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Women and Child Development. 

Funding is provided through the Rashtriya Mahila Kosh. 

The frequency of assistance is based on the financial needs of the applicants. 

The timeline for processing applications may vary depending on the specific case. 

The scheme aims to empower women with disabilities to achieve financial stability and self-reliance.
