The scheme SNSUKBOCWWB stands for the State National Scheme for Unorganized Workers' Beneficiary of Construction Workers Welfare Board. 

It aims to provide financial assistance and welfare benefits to unorganized construction workers. 

The objectives include improving the living standards of construction workers and ensuring their social security. 

Benefits include financial assistance for medical emergencies education and housing. 

Assistance may also cover skill development training and pension schemes. 

Eligibility criteria typically include being a registered construction worker under the respective state welfare board. 

Applicants must be above a certain age and may need to provide proof of employment in the construction sector. 

The application process usually involves filling out a form and submitting it to the local welfare board office. 

Supporting documents such as identity proof and employment proof may be required. 

The timeline for processing applications can vary by state and may take several weeks. 

The implementing authorities are the respective State Construction Workers Welfare Boards. 

Additional details may include periodic reviews of the scheme to enhance benefits based on the needs of workers.
