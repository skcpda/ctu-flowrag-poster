The SCUCCOCSC scheme stands for the State Credit Union Cooperative Societies. 

It aims to provide financial assistance to cooperative societies in the state. 

The objective is to promote the growth and development of cooperative credit societies. 

Benefits include access to credit facilities and financial support for various activities. 

Eligibility is typically limited to registered cooperative societies operating within the state. 

The application process involves submitting a proposal to the concerned state department. 

The timeline for processing applications may vary based on the specific state regulations. 

Implementing authorities include the state government and the respective cooperative department.
