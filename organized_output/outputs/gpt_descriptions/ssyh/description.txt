The Sukanya Samriddhi Yojana (SSY) is a savings scheme launched by the Government of India. 

It aims to promote the welfare of the girl child in India. 

The primary objective is to encourage parents to save for their daughter's education and marriage. 

The scheme offers a higher interest rate compared to regular savings accounts. 

Deposits made under this scheme qualify for tax benefits under Section 80C of the Income Tax Act. 

The interest earned is also tax-free. 

The scheme is available for girls up to the age of 10 years. 

Parents or legal guardians can open an account in the name of a girl child. 

A maximum of two accounts can be opened for two daughters. 

The minimum deposit required is Rs. 250 per year. 

The maximum deposit limit is Rs. 1.5 lakh per year. 

The account can be maintained until the girl turns 21 years old. 

Partial withdrawal is allowed after the girl turns 18 for educational purposes. 

The application process involves filling out a form and submitting it at designated banks or post offices. 

The account can be opened in any bank or post office that offers the scheme. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Finance. 

The interest rate is reviewed and updated quarterly by the government. 

The scheme encourages financial literacy and empowerment for girls. 

It aims to improve the overall status of girls in society.
