The scheme SSC DNNSN stands for the Social Security Scheme for Disabled Persons. 

It aims to provide financial assistance and support to persons with disabilities. 

The primary objective is to enhance the quality of life and promote social inclusion for disabled individuals. 

Benefits include financial aid to meet daily living expenses and support for rehabilitation services. 

Eligibility criteria typically include being a resident of India and having a certified disability as per government guidelines. 

The application process usually involves submitting an application form along with necessary documents to the relevant authorities. 

The timeline for processing applications may vary depending on the specific state or implementing agency. 

Implementing authorities include the Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment and state government departments responsible for welfare. 

Additional details regarding specific benefits and the amount of financial assistance may vary by state.
