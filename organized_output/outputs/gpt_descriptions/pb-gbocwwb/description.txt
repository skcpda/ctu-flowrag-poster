The scheme is known as the Punjab Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board scheme. 

It was established to provide welfare benefits to construction workers in Punjab. 

The main objective is to ensure the financial and social security of construction workers. 

The scheme offers various benefits including financial assistance for education, health, and housing. 

It also provides assistance for the marriage of daughters of construction workers. 

Eligibility criteria include being a registered construction worker under the Punjab Building and Other Construction Workers Act. 

Workers must have completed the registration process with the board to avail benefits. 

The application process typically involves submitting an application form along with required documents to the local office of the welfare board. 

The timeline for processing applications may vary based on the type of assistance requested. 

The Punjab Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board is the implementing authority for this scheme. 

Additional details on specific benefits and application forms can be accessed through the official website of the Punjab government.
