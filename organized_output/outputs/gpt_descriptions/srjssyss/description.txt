The scheme is known as the Sardar Patel Swachh Bharat Mission Gramin Yojana. 

It was launched to promote sanitation and hygiene in rural areas of India. 

The objective is to eliminate open defecation and improve solid waste management. 

The scheme aims to ensure that every household has access to a toilet. 

Benefits include financial assistance for the construction of toilets. 

Households receive a subsidy to help cover the costs. 

Eligibility is primarily for rural households without access to toilets. 

The application process involves submitting a request to the local gram panchayat. 

The timeline for implementation is ongoing as per the needs of the community. 

The Ministry of Jal Shakti is the implementing authority for this scheme. 

The scheme also focuses on behavioral change regarding sanitation practices. 

Awareness campaigns are conducted to educate communities about hygiene. 

Monitoring and evaluation are carried out to assess the impact of the scheme.
