The Scheme for Financial Inclusion and Skill Development (SFIS) aims to promote financial inclusion and enhance skill development among the marginalized sections of society. 

The primary objective is to provide financial assistance and skill training to improve the employability of individuals. 

Benefits include access to credit facilities and skill development programs that cater to various sectors. 

Eligibility criteria typically include individuals from economically weaker sections and marginalized communities. 

The application process generally involves submitting an application form along with necessary documents to the designated authority. 

The timeline for processing applications and disbursing benefits may vary based on the implementing agency. 

The Ministry of Skill Development and Entrepreneurship is the primary implementing authority for this scheme. 

Additional details on specific training programs and financial assistance amounts may be available through official government sources.
