The Sardar Patel Awas Yojana is a housing scheme initiated by the Government of India. 

It aims to provide affordable housing to the urban poor. 

The objective is to ensure that every individual has access to a safe and secure home. 

The scheme offers financial assistance for the construction or purchase of houses. 

Benefits include subsidies on home loans and direct financial support for housing construction. 

Eligibility is primarily based on income levels and must align with the criteria set by the government. 

The application process typically involves submitting a form along with necessary documentation to the local housing authority. 

The timeline for assistance may vary based on the specific project and location. 

The Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs is the implementing authority for this scheme. 

Additional details may include specific provisions for women and marginalized communities.
