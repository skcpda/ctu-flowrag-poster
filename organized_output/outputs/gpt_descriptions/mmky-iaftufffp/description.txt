The Mukhyamantri Kanya Suraksha Yojana is a welfare scheme launched by the Government of Madhya Pradesh. 

The scheme aims to ensure the safety and security of girls in the state. 

It focuses on empowering girls through education and health initiatives. 

The benefits include financial assistance for the education of girls. 

The scheme provides a one-time financial grant for the marriage of girls.
