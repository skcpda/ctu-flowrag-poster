The Welfare Scheme for Building and Construction Workers (WSBCAS) aims to provide financial assistance and welfare benefits to workers in the building and construction sector. 

The objective of the scheme is to improve the living standards of construction workers and ensure their social security. 

The benefits include financial assistance for medical treatment, education for children, and housing support. 

Workers can also receive assistance for skill development and training programs. 

Eligibility is generally limited to registered construction workers who meet specific criteria set by the implementing authorities. 

The application process typically involves submitting an application form along with necessary documents to the designated office or online portal. 

The timeline for processing applications may vary based on the implementing agency's efficiency. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Labour and Employment in collaboration with state governments. 

Additional details may include the requirement for workers to be registered with the Building and Other Construction Workers' (BOCW) welfare board.
