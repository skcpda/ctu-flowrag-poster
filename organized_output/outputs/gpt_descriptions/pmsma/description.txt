The Pradhan Mantri Surakshit Matritva Abhiyan (PMSMA) was launched in 2016. 

It aims to provide comprehensive and quality antenatal care to pregnant women. 

The objective is to reduce maternal and infant mortality rates in India. 

The scheme offers free antenatal check-ups and essential diagnostic tests. 

Pregnant women can receive care at government health facilities. 

Eligibility includes all pregnant women in the country. 

Women are encouraged to register for the scheme during their first trimester. 

The application process involves visiting a designated health facility. 

The scheme is implemented through the Ministry of Health and Family Welfare. 

Health workers and medical professionals are responsible for providing services. 

The initiative is conducted on the 9th of every month. 

This ensures that pregnant women receive timely and regular check-ups. 

The scheme also focuses on creating awareness about maternal health. 

It promotes the importance of institutional deliveries and postnatal care. 

The PMSMA is part of the broader efforts to improve maternal health in India.
