The Sanfsosms scheme is designed to provide financial assistance to small and marginal farmers in India. 

The objective of the scheme is to enhance the income of farmers and improve their living standards. 

The benefits include direct cash transfers to eligible farmers to support their agricultural activities. 

Eligibility criteria include being a small or marginal farmer as defined by government standards. 

Farmers must also possess land records to qualify for the assistance. 

The application process involves submitting an application form along with necessary documents to the local agricultural office. 

The timeline for disbursement of funds is typically aligned with the agricultural calendar and may vary based on the state. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers' Welfare at the central level. 

State governments may also have their own mechanisms for implementing the scheme.
