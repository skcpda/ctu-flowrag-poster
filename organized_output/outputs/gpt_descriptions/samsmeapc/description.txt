The SAMSMEAPC scheme is an initiative by the Government of India. 

It aims to support small and medium enterprises in the country. 

The primary objective is to enhance the competitiveness of MSMEs. 

The scheme provides financial assistance for various activities. 

These activities include technology upgradation and skill development. 

The benefits include access to credit and subsidies. 

Eligible beneficiaries are small and medium enterprises registered under the MSME Act. 

The application process involves submitting a proposal to the relevant authority. 

The timeline for approval and disbursement varies based on the project. 

The Ministry of Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises is the implementing authority. 

Further details can be accessed through the official government portal.
