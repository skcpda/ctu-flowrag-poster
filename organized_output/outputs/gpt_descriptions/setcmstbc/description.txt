The SETCMSTBC scheme is designed to provide financial assistance to Scheduled Tribe students in India. 

The objective of the scheme is to promote education among Scheduled Tribe students by providing them with financial support. 

The benefits include scholarships for students pursuing higher education. 

Eligible candidates are Scheduled Tribe students who are enrolled in recognized educational institutions. 

The application process typically involves submitting an application form along with necessary documents to the designated authority. 

The timeline for disbursement of funds may vary based on the academic calendar and the processing of applications. 

The implementing authority for this scheme is the Ministry of Tribal Affairs.
