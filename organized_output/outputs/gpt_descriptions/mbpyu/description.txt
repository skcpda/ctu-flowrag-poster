The Mukhyamantri Bhuvaneshwari Yojana (MBPYU) is a welfare scheme initiated by the Government of Karnataka. 

The scheme aims to provide financial assistance to women for self-employment and entrepreneurship. 

It focuses on empowering women by promoting their economic independence. 

Eligible beneficiaries include women from economically weaker sections of society. 

Women aged between 18 to 55 years can apply for this scheme. 

The application process involves submitting an online application through the official government portal. 

The scheme provides financial assistance in the form of a grant to eligible women. 

The assistance amount may vary based on the type of business or self-employment activity. 

The implementing authority for this scheme is the Department of Women and Child Development, Government of Karnataka. 

The scheme is expected to enhance the livelihood opportunities for women in the state. 

Specific timelines for application submission and fund disbursement are determined by the implementing authority.
