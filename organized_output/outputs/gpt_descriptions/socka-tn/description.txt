Socka-TN is a welfare scheme initiated by the Government of Tamil Nadu. 

The scheme aims to provide financial assistance to small and marginal farmers. 

It focuses on enhancing the income and livelihood of farmers in the state. 

The benefits include direct cash transfers to eligible farmers. 

This assistance helps in meeting their agricultural expenses and improving their standard of living. 

Eligibility criteria include being a small or marginal farmer in Tamil Nadu. 

Farmers must possess landholding documents to qualify for the scheme. 

The application process involves submitting an application form along with necessary documents. 

Farmers can apply through the official website or designated government offices. 

The scheme is implemented by the Department of Agriculture in Tamil Nadu. 

The frequency of assistance is typically annual but may vary based on government decisions. 

Additional details about the scheme can be found on the official government website.
