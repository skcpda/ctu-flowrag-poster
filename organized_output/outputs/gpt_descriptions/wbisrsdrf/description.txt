The Welfare of Backward Classes and Scheduled Castes Development and Rehabilitation Fund Scheme is known as WBISRSDRF. 

This scheme is implemented by the Government of India to support the development and rehabilitation of backward classes and scheduled castes. 

The primary objective is to provide financial assistance for the socio-economic upliftment of these communities. 

Benefits include financial aid for education health care and skill development programs. 

Eligibility criteria typically include being a member of a backward class or scheduled caste as defined by the government. 

The application process involves submitting a form along with necessary documentation to the designated authority. 

The timeline for processing applications may vary based on the specific requirements of the implementing agency. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment. 

Additional details regarding specific benefits or assistance amounts may vary based on state regulations and policies.
