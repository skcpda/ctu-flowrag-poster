The RASSNB scheme stands for the Rajiv Awas Yojana for Slum Dwellers. 

It aims to provide housing for the urban poor living in slums. 

The objective is to ensure that every individual has access to affordable housing. 

The scheme focuses on the rehabilitation of slum dwellers by providing them with housing and basic amenities. 

Benefits include financial assistance for constructing houses and access to basic services such as water and sanitation. 

Eligibility criteria include being a resident of a slum area and belonging to the economically weaker sections. 

The application process typically involves submitting a form along with required documents to the local urban development authority. 

The timeline for approval and disbursement of funds may vary based on the local authority's processes. 

The implementing authority for this scheme is the Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs. 

The scheme is part of the larger initiative to improve urban infrastructure and living conditions.
