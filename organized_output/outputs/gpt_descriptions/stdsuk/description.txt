The STDSUK scheme is aimed at providing financial assistance to the Scheduled Tribe community in India. 

The objective of the scheme is to enhance the socio-economic status of Scheduled Tribes through skill development and employment opportunities. 

The benefits include financial support for skill development programs and assistance in starting small businesses. 

Eligibility is primarily restricted to individuals belonging to Scheduled Tribe communities. 

The application process typically involves submitting an application form along with necessary documents to the designated authority. 

The timeline for processing applications may vary but is generally completed within a few weeks. 

The implementing authorities include the Ministry of Tribal Affairs and state-level tribal welfare departments. 

Additional details regarding specific training programs and financial limits may vary by state.
