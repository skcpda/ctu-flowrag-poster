The scheme is known as Pradhan Mantri Ujjwala Yojana. 

It was launched in May 2016. 

The objective is to provide clean cooking fuel to women from Below Poverty Line households. 

The scheme aims to reduce the health hazards associated with the use of traditional cooking fuels. 

It also aims to empower women by providing them with a cleaner and more efficient cooking option. 

Eligible beneficiaries are women from Below Poverty Line households. 

They must be at least 18 years of age. 

The application process involves submitting a form to the local LPG distributor. 

Applicants must provide proof of identity and address. 

The timeline for receiving the connection is generally within a few weeks after application approval. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Petroleum and Natural Gas. 

Financial assistance of Rs. 1,600 is provided for each LPG connection. 

The assistance is transferred directly to the bank account of the beneficiary. 

The scheme has been extended to cover more households over the years. 

It also promotes the use of clean energy sources. 

The scheme contributes to the overall goal of improving health and living standards.
