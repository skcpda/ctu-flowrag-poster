The National Savings Certificate Scheme is a savings scheme offered by the Government of India. 

It is designed to encourage savings among the general public. 

The primary objective is to provide a safe investment option with attractive interest rates. 

The scheme offers guaranteed returns and tax benefits under Section 80C of the Income Tax Act. 

The benefits include fixed interest rates which are compounded annually. 

The current interest rate is set by the government and is subject to change. 

The minimum investment amount is INR 1000 with no maximum limit. 

The maturity period is 5 years. 

Individuals can invest in the scheme. 

The scheme is open to Indian citizens only. 

The application process involves visiting a post office or bank that offers the scheme. 

Investors must fill out an application form and provide necessary identification documents. 

The investment can be made in cash or through cheque. 

Interest is credited to the account at the end of each financial year. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Finance through post offices and designated banks. 

The certificates can be transferred from one person to another under certain conditions. 

Premature withdrawal is allowed after 5 years with certain penalties. 

The scheme also allows for nomination facilities. 

Interest rates are reviewed and updated periodically by the government.
