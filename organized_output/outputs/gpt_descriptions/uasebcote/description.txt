The UASEBCOTE scheme is designed to support the education of children from economically backward classes. 

The objective of the scheme is to provide financial assistance for educational expenses. 

The benefits include scholarships for students pursuing education in various fields. 

Eligibility is limited to students from economically backward classes. 

The application process involves submitting the required documents to the designated authorities. 

The timeline for application and disbursement of funds varies each academic year. 

The implementing authority for this scheme is the Ministry of Education. 

Additional details regarding specific scholarship amounts and application deadlines may vary.
