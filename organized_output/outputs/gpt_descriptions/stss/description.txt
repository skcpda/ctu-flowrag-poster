The Scheme for Transgender Persons (STSS) was launched by the Government of India. 

It aims to provide financial assistance and support to transgender persons. 

The objectives include ensuring social security and promoting the welfare of transgender individuals. 

Benefits include financial assistance for education, skill development, and healthcare. 

Transgender persons are eligible for the scheme. 

They must provide proof of their identity as transgender. 

The application process involves submitting an application form along with necessary documents. 

Applications can be submitted online or through designated offices. 

The timeline for processing applications may vary based on the implementing agency. 

The Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment is the primary implementing authority. 

The scheme also focuses on awareness and sensitization programs for the general public. 

It aims to integrate transgender persons into mainstream society.
