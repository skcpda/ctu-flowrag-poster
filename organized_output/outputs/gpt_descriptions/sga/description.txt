The SGA scheme stands for the Skill Grant Assistance scheme. 

It was launched by the Government of India to promote skill development among the youth. 

The objective is to enhance employability by providing financial assistance for skill training. 

The scheme aims to bridge the skill gap in various sectors. 

Benefits include financial support for training programs. 

Assistance is provided to training institutions and individuals. 

Eligibility criteria include being a resident of India and belonging to specific target groups. 

The application process involves submitting an online application through designated portals. 

The timeline for processing applications varies based on the training program. 

The implementing authority for this scheme is the Ministry of Skill Development and Entrepreneurship. 

Additional details may include partnerships with various training providers.
