The Mukhyamantri Kanya Suraksha Yojana (MKSY) is a welfare scheme initiated by the Government of Bihar. 

It aims to improve the health and education of girls in the state. 

The primary objective is to enhance the survival rate of girls and promote their education. 

The scheme provides financial assistance for the education of girls from birth until they reach the age of 21. 

Eligible beneficiaries receive a total of INR 50,000 which is disbursed in installments. 

The first installment is given at birth. 

The second installment is provided when the girl reaches the age of 6 years. 

The third installment is given when she reaches the age of 11 years. 

The final installment is provided when she turns 21 years old. 

Eligibility is restricted to girls born in families with an annual income below a certain threshold. 

The application process involves submitting a birth certificate and income certificate along with the application form. 

The scheme is implemented by the Department of Women and Child Development in Bihar. 

The scheme is part of a broader initiative to empower women and promote gender equality in the state.
