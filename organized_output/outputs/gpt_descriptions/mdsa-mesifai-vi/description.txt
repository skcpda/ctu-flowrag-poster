The MDSA-MESIFAI-VI scheme is a welfare initiative by the Government of India. 

It aims to provide financial assistance for the treatment of patients suffering from specific diseases. 

The primary objective is to reduce the financial burden on families and ensure access to necessary medical care. 

Beneficiaries receive monetary support for medical expenses related to identified health conditions. 

Eligibility is typically determined based on income levels and the specific health conditions outlined in the scheme. 

The application process generally involves submitting required documents to the designated authorities. 

The timeline for assistance may vary based on the processing of applications and the urgency of medical needs. 

Implementing authorities include state health departments and designated medical institutions. 

Additional details may include specific diseases covered and the maximum financial assistance amount available.
