MCMSEC stands for the Mukhyamantri Chiranjeevi Swasthya Bima Yojana. 

It is a health insurance scheme launched by the Government of Rajasthan. 

The scheme aims to provide financial protection to families against health-related expenses. 

The primary objective is to ensure access to quality healthcare for economically weaker sections. 

Under this scheme families can avail cashless treatment for various medical conditions. 

The scheme covers hospitalization expenses including surgeries and treatments. 

The benefits include coverage for pre-existing diseases and maternity benefits. 

Eligible beneficiaries are families with an annual income of up to Rs. 2.5 lakh. 

The scheme is applicable to families registered under the National Food Security Act. 

The application process involves filling out a form and submitting it to designated offices. 

The timeline for processing applications is typically within a few weeks. 

The scheme is implemented by the Department of Medical and Health Services in Rajasthan. 

Beneficiaries can access services at empaneled hospitals across the state. 

The scheme also includes provisions for health check-ups and preventive care.
