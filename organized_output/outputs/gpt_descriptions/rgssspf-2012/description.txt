The Rajiv Gandhi Shramik Kalyan Sansthan Pension Fund Scheme 2012 is a welfare initiative by the Government of India. 

It aims to provide financial assistance to unorganized sector workers. 

The objective is to ensure social security and financial stability for workers in the informal sector. 

The scheme offers a pension to eligible beneficiaries upon reaching the age of 60 years. 

Benefits include a monthly pension to support the livelihood of retired workers. 

Eligibility criteria include being a worker in the unorganized sector. 

Applicants must be between the ages of 18 and 40 years at the time of enrollment. 

The application process involves filling out a prescribed form and submitting it to the designated authority. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Labour and Employment. 

The frequency of pension disbursement is monthly. 

Additional details regarding the specific amount of pension and contribution requirements may vary.
