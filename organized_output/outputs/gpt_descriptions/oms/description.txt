The One Nation One Ration Card scheme aims to provide food security to all eligible beneficiaries across India. 

It ensures that individuals can access subsidized food grains from any fair price shop in the country. 

The primary objective is to enable portability of ration cards and ensure that no one goes hungry. 

The scheme benefits migrant workers and their families by allowing them to access food grains irrespective of their location. 

Eligible beneficiaries include those holding a ration card issued by any state or union territory in India. 

The application process involves registering for a ration card through the respective state government’s food and civil supplies department. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Consumer Affairs Food and Public Distribution. 

The timeline for the implementation varies by state but aims for continuous access to food grains. 

The scheme is part of a larger initiative to strengthen the public distribution system in India.
