The UDPS scheme stands for the Urban Development Policy Scheme. 

It aims to enhance urban infrastructure and improve living conditions in urban areas. 

The objectives include promoting sustainable urban development and improving access to basic services. 

Benefits include financial assistance for infrastructure projects and capacity building for urban local bodies. 

Eligibility criteria typically include urban local bodies and municipalities. 

The application process involves submitting project proposals to the relevant state government or urban development authority. 

The timeline for project approval and funding disbursement varies based on the specific project and state guidelines. 

Implementing authorities include the Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs and state urban development departments. 

The scheme is part of the broader initiative to address urbanization challenges in India.
