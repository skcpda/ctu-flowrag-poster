The Wage Protection for Unorganised Workers Scheme (WPUP) aims to provide financial security to unorganised workers in India. 

The objective is to ensure timely payment of wages to workers in the unorganised sector. 

The scheme benefits unorganised workers by providing them with a mechanism to receive their wages on time. 

Eligibility criteria include being an unorganised worker aged 18 years or above. 

Workers must be engaged in informal employment and not covered under any other social security scheme. 

The application process involves registering on the designated government portal or through local government offices. 

The timeline for wage payments is determined by the employer but must comply with the stipulated guidelines of the scheme. 

The Ministry of Labour and Employment is the implementing authority for this scheme. 

Additional details regarding specific wage limits or payment methods may vary based on state regulations.
