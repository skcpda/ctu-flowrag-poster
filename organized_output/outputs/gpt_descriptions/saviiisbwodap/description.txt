The Savitribai Phule Pune University Initiative for Skill Development and Entrepreneurship is a government welfare scheme. 

It aims to promote skill development and entrepreneurship among youth. 

The objective is to enhance employability and encourage self-employment. 

The scheme provides training programs in various sectors. 

It also offers financial assistance for starting new ventures. 

Eligibility criteria include being a resident of India and being within a certain age group. 

The application process involves submitting an online application through the official portal. 

The timeline for training programs varies based on the specific course. 

Implementing authorities include the Ministry of Skill Development and Entrepreneurship and affiliated institutions. 

The scheme also collaborates with industry partners for practical training. 

It focuses on both urban and rural youth to ensure inclusive growth. 

The initiative is part of a broader effort to boost the Indian economy through skilled workforce development.
