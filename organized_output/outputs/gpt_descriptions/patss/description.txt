The Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana (PMAY) is a flagship housing scheme initiated by the Government of India. 

It aims to provide affordable housing to the urban poor and promote housing for all by 2022. 

The objectives include ensuring that every family has a pucca house with basic amenities. 

Benefits include financial assistance for constructing or upgrading houses. 

The scheme offers subsidies on home loans for the economically weaker sections and low-income groups. 

Eligibility is based on income criteria and the applicant must not own a house in their name. 

The application process involves submitting an online application through the PMAY website or visiting designated Common Service Centers. 

The timeline for approval and disbursement of funds varies based on the application and project completion. 

The Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs is the primary implementing authority for this scheme. 

State governments and urban local bodies also play a significant role in implementation.
