The THSDSE scheme stands for the "Tripura Handloom and Handicrafts Development Scheme." 

It aims to promote the handloom and handicraft sector in the state of Tripura. 

The objective is to enhance the income of artisans and weavers while preserving traditional crafts. 

The scheme provides financial assistance for the purchase of raw materials and tools. 

It also offers training programs to improve the skills of artisans and weavers. 

Eligibility is generally limited to artisans and weavers residing in Tripura. 

The application process involves submitting a form along with necessary documents to the concerned department. 

The timeline for assistance may vary based on the specific program under the scheme. 

The implementing authority for this scheme is the Handloom and Handicrafts Department of Tripura.
