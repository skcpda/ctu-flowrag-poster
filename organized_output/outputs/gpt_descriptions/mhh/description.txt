The Mukhya Mantri Har Ghar Jal Yojana is a government initiative aimed at providing safe drinking water to every household in rural areas of India. 

The objective of the scheme is to ensure that all rural households have access to piped water supply. 

The benefits include the provision of functional household tap connections to every rural household. 

Eligibility is primarily for households in rural areas that do not have access to safe drinking water. 

The application process typically involves registration through local government offices or online portals. 

The timeline for implementation varies by region but aims for completion within a specified period as per state plans. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Jal Shakti in collaboration with state governments. 

Additional details include a focus on sustainability and community participation in water management.
