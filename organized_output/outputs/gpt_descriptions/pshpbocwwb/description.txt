The Pradhan Mantri Shram Yogi Maan-Dhan (PM-SYM) scheme is designed for unorganized sector workers. 

It aims to provide social security and pension benefits to workers in the unorganized sector. 

The objective is to ensure a monthly pension of Rs. 3,000 after the age of 60 years. 

The scheme benefits workers in the unorganized sector who are between the ages of 18 and 40. 

Eligible beneficiaries must have a monthly income of less than Rs. 15,000. 

The application process involves registering online through the official PM-SYM portal or visiting Common Service Centers. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Labour and Employment. 

Contributions to the pension scheme are made by both the government and the beneficiaries. 

The government contributes a matching amount to the pension fund. 

The pension is payable on a monthly basis after the age of 60. 

The scheme provides financial security to workers who do not have access to formal pension schemes.
