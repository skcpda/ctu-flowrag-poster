The Maternity Benefit Program (MCIP) is an initiative by the Government of India. 

It aims to provide financial assistance to pregnant women and lactating mothers. 

The objective is to improve maternal and child health by providing support during pregnancy and after childbirth. 

The benefits include a cash incentive to eligible women to help meet their nutritional needs. 

Eligible beneficiaries are pregnant women and lactating mothers. 

They must be registered under the scheme and belong to low-income households. 

The application process involves submitting a form along with required documents to the local health department. 

The timeline for receiving benefits is typically during the pregnancy period and up to six months after childbirth. 

The implementing authority for this scheme is the Ministry of Women and Child Development. 

Additional details may include awareness programs to educate beneficiaries about maternal health.
