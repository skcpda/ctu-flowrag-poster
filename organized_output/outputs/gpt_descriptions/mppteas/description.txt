The MPPTEAS scheme stands for Madhya Pradesh Public Transport and Employment Assistance Scheme. 

It was launched by the Government of Madhya Pradesh to support the livelihood of individuals engaged in public transport. 

The main objective of the scheme is to provide financial assistance to drivers and operators of public transport vehicles. 

The scheme aims to enhance the income of beneficiaries by offering them support during challenging times. 

Eligible beneficiaries include drivers and operators of public transport vehicles who are registered with the state transport department. 

The application process involves submitting a form along with necessary documents to the relevant transport authority. 

The scheme provides financial assistance in the form of a one-time grant to eligible beneficiaries. 

The implementing authority for the MPPTEAS scheme is the Transport Department of Madhya Pradesh. 

The frequency of assistance is determined based on the specific guidelines set forth by the scheme.
