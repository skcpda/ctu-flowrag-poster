The SRMIQC scheme stands for the Skill Development in Rural Micro Enterprises and Quality Certification. 

It aims to enhance the quality of products made by rural micro enterprises. 

The objective is to improve the marketability of rural products through skill development and quality certification. 

Benefits include training for artisans and small producers in quality standards and production techniques. 

Assistance is provided in the form of workshops and financial support for quality certification. 

Eligibility is generally limited to artisans and small producers operating in rural areas. 

The application process typically involves submitting a proposal to the implementing agency. 

The timeline for the scheme may vary based on specific training programs and certification processes. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises. 

Additional details on specific training modules and certification bodies may be available through official channels.
