The Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samman Nidhi (PM-KISAN) scheme was launched in February 2019. 

It aims to provide financial support to small and marginal farmers in India. 

The objective is to ensure a minimum income support to farmers and alleviate their financial burden. 

Under this scheme eligible farmers receive direct cash transfers of Rs. 6000 per year. 

The amount is disbursed in three equal installments of Rs. 2000 each. 

Eligibility is limited to small and marginal farmers who own cultivable land. 

Farmers must have a landholding of up to 2 hectares. 

The scheme is not applicable to institutional landholders. 

The application process involves farmers registering through the official PM-KISAN portal or through designated state government offices. 

Farmers need to provide necessary documents such as land ownership proof and bank account details. 

The disbursement of funds occurs three times a year. 

The implementing authority for this scheme is the Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers' Welfare. 

State governments also play a crucial role in the implementation of the scheme. 

The scheme is part of the broader effort to enhance agricultural productivity and farmers' income. 

It is expected to benefit millions of farmers across the country.
