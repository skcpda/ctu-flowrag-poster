The Semi-Agricultural Marketing Development and Promotion Scheme (SemiAMDP) aims to enhance the marketing of agricultural products. 

The objective is to promote the development of agricultural marketing infrastructure. 

The scheme provides financial assistance for the establishment of marketing facilities. 

It also supports the promotion of agricultural products through various marketing channels. 

Eligibility is generally for farmers and agricultural cooperatives. 

The application process involves submitting a proposal to the relevant state agricultural department. 

The timeline for processing applications may vary by state. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers' Welfare. 

Additional details regarding specific financial assistance amounts or project types may vary.
