The Pradhan Mantri Dhan Vantri Yojana is a welfare scheme aimed at providing financial assistance to the families of deceased workers in the unorganized sector. 

The objective of the scheme is to support the families of workers who have lost their lives due to accidents or other unforeseen circumstances. 

The benefits include a financial assistance of a specified amount to the eligible beneficiaries. 

Eligibility is typically limited to the families of workers in the unorganized sector who have died in accidents. 

The application process generally involves submitting a form along with required documents to the designated authority. 

The timeline for disbursing assistance may vary based on the processing of applications. 

Implementing authorities include the Ministry of Labour and Employment and state governments. 

Additional details regarding the scheme may include specific eligibility criteria and documentation requirements.
