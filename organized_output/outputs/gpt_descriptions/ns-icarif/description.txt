The NS-ICARIF scheme is an initiative by the Indian government aimed at enhancing agricultural research and innovation. 

The objective of the scheme is to promote research in agriculture and allied sectors. 

It seeks to strengthen the research capabilities of agricultural institutions. 

The scheme provides financial assistance for various research projects. 

Benefits include funding for research activities and capacity building for researchers. 

Eligibility criteria include being a registered agricultural research institution or organization. 

The application process involves submitting a project proposal to the relevant authorities. 

The timeline for project approval and funding disbursement varies based on the proposal. 

The scheme is implemented by the Indian Council of Agricultural Research (ICAR). 

Additional details about specific funding amounts and project durations may vary.
