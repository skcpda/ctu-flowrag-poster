The PCardTP scheme is a government initiative aimed at providing financial assistance to individuals with disabilities. 

The objective of the scheme is to promote the inclusion and empowerment of persons with disabilities. 

The scheme offers financial support for the purchase of assistive devices and aids. 

Eligible beneficiaries include individuals with disabilities as defined under the Rights of Persons with Disabilities Act 2016. 

The application process involves submitting a request through designated government offices or online platforms. 

The timeline for processing applications may vary based on the specific requirements of the implementing agency. 

The scheme is implemented by the Department of Empowerment of Persons with Disabilities under the Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment. 

Additional details regarding the specific types of assistive devices covered under the scheme may be available through official government resources.
