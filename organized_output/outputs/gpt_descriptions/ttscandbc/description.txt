The TTS and DBC scheme is designed to provide financial assistance to the tribal population in India. 

It aims to promote the socio-economic development of Scheduled Tribes. 

The scheme provides benefits in the form of financial aid for various developmental activities. 

Eligible beneficiaries include individuals and groups belonging to Scheduled Tribes. 

The application process involves submitting a proposal to the concerned state government authority. 

The timeline for assistance may vary based on the specific project and state guidelines. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Tribal Affairs in collaboration with state governments. 

Additional details regarding specific benefits or project types may vary by state.
