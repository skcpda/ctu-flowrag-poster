The Maternity Assistance Program for Pregnant Women and Lactating Mothers (MAPS) is a welfare scheme initiated by the Government of India. 

The scheme aims to provide financial assistance to pregnant women and lactating mothers. 

It is designed to improve maternal and child health indicators in the country. 

The objective of MAPS is to reduce maternal and infant mortality rates. 

The program encourages women to seek institutional delivery and postnatal care. 

Eligible beneficiaries receive a cash incentive to help with expenses during pregnancy and after childbirth. 

The assistance amount is provided in installments. 

The first installment is given during the second trimester of pregnancy. 

The second installment is provided after the birth of the child. 

The third installment is given after the mother completes the prescribed immunization schedule for the child. 

To be eligible for the MAPS scheme, women must be pregnant or lactating mothers. 

They must be residents of India. 

The scheme is primarily targeted at women from economically weaker sections. 

Women who have had a child within the last two years can also apply.
