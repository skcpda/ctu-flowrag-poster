The scheme known as the Direct Benefit Transfer for the Scheme for the Development of Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes in India is commonly referred to as DBSALHSIFC. 

This scheme was launched to provide financial assistance to the Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes in India. 

The background of this scheme is rooted in the need to uplift marginalized communities in the country. 

Historically these communities have faced social and economic disadvantages. 

The Indian government recognized the need for targeted interventions to improve their living conditions. 

The scheme aims to provide direct financial support to eligible beneficiaries. 

One of the primary objectives of the DBSALHSIFC is to enhance the economic status of Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes. 

The scheme seeks to empower these communities through financial inclusion. 

It also aims to promote self-sufficiency among the beneficiaries. 

The benefits of the scheme include direct cash transfers to the bank accounts of eligible beneficiaries. 

This financial assistance can be used for various purposes such as education health and livelihood. 

The scheme also aims to reduce the bureaucratic hurdles that often delay the disbursement of funds. 

By providing direct benefits the scheme ensures that the assistance reaches the intended recipients without any intermediaries. 

Eligibility for the DBSALHSIFC is primarily based on the caste of the applicant. 

Only individuals belonging to Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes are eligible to apply for this scheme. 

Additionally applicants must meet certain income criteria to qualify for the benefits. 

The specific income limits may vary based on the guidelines set by the government. 

Applicants must also be residents of India to be eligible for the scheme.
