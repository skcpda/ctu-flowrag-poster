The SOMNK scheme is an initiative by the Government of India aimed at providing financial assistance to marginalized communities. 

The primary objective of the SOMNK scheme is to enhance the livelihood opportunities for the underprivileged sections of society. 

The scheme offers monetary support to eligible beneficiaries to help them meet their basic needs and improve their standard of living. 

Eligibility for the SOMNK scheme typically includes individuals from economically weaker sections and marginalized communities. 

The application process involves submitting the necessary documents to the designated authorities in the respective states or union territories. 

The timeline for disbursing assistance under the SOMNK scheme may vary based on the state or union territory's implementation schedule. 

The implementing authorities for the SOMNK scheme include the Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment and various state government departments. 

Further details on specific benefits and the exact amount of assistance may vary based on state guidelines.
