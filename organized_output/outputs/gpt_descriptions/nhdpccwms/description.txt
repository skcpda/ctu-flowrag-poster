The National Health Mission for the Prevention and Control of Non-Communicable Diseases and Mental Health (NHDPCCWMS) is a government initiative aimed at addressing the growing burden of non-communicable diseases and mental health issues in India. 

The objective of the scheme is to enhance the prevention and control of non-communicable diseases and improve mental health services across the country. 

The benefits of the scheme include increased access to health services for non-communicable diseases and mental health. 

It aims to provide training and capacity building for healthcare providers to effectively manage these health issues. 

Eligibility criteria may vary based on specific interventions under the scheme but generally target vulnerable populations. 

The application process for beneficiaries typically involves registration at designated health facilities or through local health departments. 

The timeline for implementation and frequency of services may vary based on state-specific plans and local health needs. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Health and Family Welfare along with state health departments. 

The initiative is part of a broader strategy to improve overall public health and reduce mortality and morbidity due to non-communicable diseases and mental health conditions.
