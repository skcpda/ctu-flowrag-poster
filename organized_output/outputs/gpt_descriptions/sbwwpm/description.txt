The scheme is known as the Stand-Up India Scheme for Women and Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes. 

It was launched by the Government of India in April 2016. 

The primary objective is to promote entrepreneurship among women and marginalized communities. 

The scheme aims to provide financial support for setting up greenfield enterprises. 

It offers loans ranging from 10 lakh to 1 crore. 

The assistance is provided through scheduled commercial banks. 

Eligible beneficiaries include women entrepreneurs and individuals from Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes. 

The age of the applicant should be above 18 years. 

The applicant must have a viable business plan. 

The application process involves approaching a bank with the business plan. 

The bank will assess the proposal and provide the loan if it meets the criteria. 

The scheme encourages the establishment of new businesses in various sectors. 

There is no specific timeline mentioned for the application process. 

The implementing authority for the scheme is the Department of Financial Services. 

The scheme also aims to create job opportunities and promote inclusive growth. 

It contributes to the overall economic development of the country.
