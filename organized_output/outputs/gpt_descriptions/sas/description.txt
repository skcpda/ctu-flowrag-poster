The Scheme for Adolescent Girls (SAG) is a government initiative aimed at improving the nutritional and health status of adolescent girls. 

It was launched to address the issues of malnutrition and to empower girls through education and skill development. 

The objectives include enhancing the nutritional intake of adolescent girls and promoting their health and hygiene. 

The scheme provides benefits such as supplementary nutrition, health check-ups, and life skills education. 

Eligible beneficiaries are adolescent girls aged 11 to 19 years. 

The application process typically involves registration through local Anganwadi centers. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Women and Child Development. 

The frequency of assistance is usually monthly for nutritional support. 

Additional details may include community participation and awareness programs to encourage enrollment and participation.
