The scheme SPMFAEEW stands for the Scheme for Promoting Micro Enterprises in the Agricultural and Allied Sectors. 

It aims to promote entrepreneurship among the rural population. 

The objective is to enhance self-employment opportunities and improve livelihoods in rural areas. 

The scheme provides financial assistance to individuals for setting up micro-enterprises. 

It also offers skill development and training programs to beneficiaries. 

Eligible applicants include individuals from rural areas who wish to start a micro-enterprise. 

Priority is given to women and marginalized communities. 

The application process involves submitting a project proposal to the designated authority. 

The proposal must outline the business idea and financial requirements. 

The timeline for approval and disbursement of funds may vary based on the project. 

The implementing authority for this scheme is the Ministry of Rural Development. 

State governments may also play a role in execution and monitoring. 

Additional details on specific eligibility criteria and financial assistance limits can be obtained from official sources.
