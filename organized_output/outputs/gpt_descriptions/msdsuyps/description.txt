The Mukhyamantri Swavalamban Yojana for Skill Development and Entrepreneurship Promotion Scheme (MSDSUYPS) is an initiative by the Government of India. 

It aims to promote skill development and entrepreneurship among the youth. 

The objective is to enhance employability and encourage self-employment. 

The scheme provides financial assistance for skill development training programs. 

It also offers support for setting up small businesses. 

Eligible beneficiaries include youth aged between 18 to 35 years. 

They must be residents of the respective state where the scheme is implemented. 

The application process typically involves submitting an online application form. 

Applicants may need to provide documents such as identity proof and educational qualifications. 

The timeline for processing applications may vary by state. 

The scheme is implemented by the respective State Government's Department of Skill Development. 

Additional details about specific training programs and financial assistance may vary by state.
