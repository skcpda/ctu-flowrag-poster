The Central Sector Scheme for the Development of the Handicrafts Sector is known as CEC-DCS. 

This scheme is aimed at promoting the handicrafts sector in India.

It provides employment to millions of artisans across the country. 

The sector is known for its rich cultural heritage and diversity. 

The CEC-DCS scheme was initiated to enhance the livelihood of artisans.
