The scheme is known as the Social Security Pension for Building and Other Construction Workers Board.

It is implemented by the Ministry of Labour and Employment.

The scheme aims to provide financial assistance to construction workers.

The objective is to ensure social security for workers engaged in the building and construction sector.

Eligible beneficiaries include registered construction workers who are part of the Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board.

The benefits include a monthly pension to support workers in their old age.

The application process involves registering with the Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board.

Workers must provide necessary documentation to complete the registration.

The pension is disbursed monthly.

The implementing authority for this scheme is the respective State Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board.

The scheme is part of the broader initiative to enhance the welfare of laborers in the construction industry.
