The National Scheme of Welfare for the Scheduled Tribes (NSVST) aims to provide welfare measures for Scheduled Tribes in India. 

The objective of the scheme is to enhance the socio-economic conditions of Scheduled Tribes. 

The benefits include financial assistance for education health and livelihood. 

Eligibility is primarily for individuals belonging to Scheduled Tribes. 

The application process involves submitting an application to the concerned state government or union territory administration. 

The timeline for disbursement of benefits varies based on the specific assistance provided. 

The Ministry of Tribal Affairs is the implementing authority for this scheme. 

Additional details include the focus on holistic development through various welfare measures.
