The SSSGBCDC stands for the Self-Sustaining Skill Generation and Business Development Center Scheme. 

This scheme aims to promote entrepreneurship and skill development among the youth in India. 

The primary objective is to create self-employment opportunities and enhance the skill sets of individuals. 

The benefits include financial assistance for setting up businesses and training programs to improve employability. 

Eligible beneficiaries are typically youth and aspiring entrepreneurs. 

The application process involves submitting a proposal to the designated implementing authority. 

The timeline for processing applications may vary based on the specific center and location. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Skill Development and Entrepreneurship. 

Additional details regarding specific financial assistance amounts and training modules may vary by region.
