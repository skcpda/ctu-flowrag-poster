The Central Sector Scheme for the Promotion of the Fit India Movement also known as CPFITP is an initiative launched by the Government of India. 

This scheme is aimed at promoting fitness and wellness among the citizens of India. 

The Fit India Movement was launched on August 29 2019 by the Prime Minister of India. 

The movement seeks to encourage people to prioritize physical fitness and health in their daily lives. 

The CPFITP scheme is a part of this broader initiative to foster a culture of fitness across the nation. 

The primary objective of the CPFITP is to promote physical fitness through various activities and programs. 

The scheme aims to create awareness about the importance of fitness and healthy living. 

It seeks to engage individuals from all walks of life in fitness-related activities. 

The scheme also aims to provide a structured framework for fitness programs in schools colleges and communities.
