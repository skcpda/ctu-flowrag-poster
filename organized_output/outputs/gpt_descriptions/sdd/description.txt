The SDD scheme refers to the Skill Development and Entrepreneurship scheme. 

It aims to promote skill development among the youth of India. 

The objective is to enhance employability and entrepreneurship. 

The scheme provides training and financial assistance to eligible individuals. 

Benefits include skill training programs and access to financial support for starting businesses. 

Eligibility criteria include being a resident of India and being within a certain age group. 

The application process typically involves submitting an online application through designated portals. 

The timeline for training programs may vary based on the specific courses offered. 

The Ministry of Skill Development and Entrepreneurship is the implementing authority for this scheme. 

Additional details may include partnerships with various training institutes and organizations.
