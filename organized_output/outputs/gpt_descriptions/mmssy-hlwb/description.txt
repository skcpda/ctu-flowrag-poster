The Mukhyamantri Mahila Samman Sahay Yojana for Health and Wellness Benefits (MMSSY-HLWB) is a welfare scheme initiated by the Government of India. 

The scheme aims to provide financial assistance to women for health and wellness needs. 

It seeks to empower women by ensuring access to healthcare services. 

The benefits include financial support for medical expenses and health-related services. 

Eligible beneficiaries are women from economically weaker sections of society. 

The application process involves submitting a form along with necessary documents to the designated authorities. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Women and Child Development. 

The frequency of assistance is determined based on the specific health needs of the beneficiaries. 

Additional details regarding the exact amount of assistance and specific eligibility criteria may vary.
