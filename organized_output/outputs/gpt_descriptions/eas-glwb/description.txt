The Eas-GLWB scheme stands for the "Elderly Assistance Scheme - Guaranteed Life Welfare Benefit." 

This scheme is part of the Indian government's broader initiative to support the elderly population in the country. 

The elderly demographic in India faces numerous challenges including financial insecurity, health issues, and social isolation. 

Recognizing these challenges the government has implemented the Eas-GLWB scheme to provide financial assistance to senior citizens. 

The primary objective of the Eas-GLWB scheme is to ensure that elderly individuals have a reliable source of income. 

This income support aims to enhance their quality of life and provide them with a sense of security. 

The scheme is designed to cater specifically to those who do not have adequate financial resources. 

It seeks to alleviate poverty among the elderly and reduce their dependence on family members. 

The benefits provided under the Eas-GLWB scheme include a monthly financial assistance amount. 

This assistance is intended to cover basic needs such as food, healthcare, and other essential expenses. 

The exact amount of financial assistance may vary based on the specific guidelines set by the implementing agency.

These healthcare services are aimed at ensuring that elderly individuals receive necessary medical attention. 

Eligibility criteria for the Eas-GLWB scheme are defined to ensure that the assistance reaches those who need it the most.
