The Asom Ghar Ghar Rojgar Scheme is a welfare initiative launched by the Government of Assam. 

This scheme aims to provide employment opportunities to the unemployed youth of the state. 

The background of this scheme is rooted in the high unemployment rates in Assam. 

The government recognized the need for a structured approach to tackle this issue. 

The primary objective of the Asom Ghar Ghar Rojgar Scheme is to ensure that every household in Assam has at least one employed member. 

This initiative seeks to empower the youth by providing them with skill development and employment opportunities. 

The scheme also aims to reduce poverty levels in the state by enhancing the income-generating capacity of families. 

One of the significant benefits of this scheme is the provision of financial assistance to eligible beneficiaries. 

The scheme offers various training programs to equip individuals with necessary skills. 

These training programs cover a wide range of sectors including agriculture, manufacturing, and services. 

The government also collaborates with various organizations to facilitate skill development. 

This collaboration helps in creating a robust ecosystem for employment generation. 

The eligibility criteria for the Asom Ghar Ghar Rojgar Scheme primarily focus on the youth of Assam. 

Applicants must be residents of Assam and belong to economically weaker sections. 

The age limit for applicants is generally between 18 to 35 years. 

Preference is given to those who have completed their education but are still unemployed. 

The application process for the scheme is designed to be user-friendly. 

Interested candidates can apply online through the official government portal. 

They need to fill out the application form with necessary personal details. 

Supporting documents such as identity proof and educational certificates are also required. 

Once the application is submitted, it undergoes a verification process. 

Successful candidates are then notified about their selection for training programs. 

The timeline for the entire process may vary depending on the number of applications received. 

However, the government aims to process applications promptly to ensure timely assistance. 

The implementing authority for this scheme is the Assam Skill Development Mission. 

This mission operates under the Department of Skill Development and Entrepreneurship. 

They are responsible for the overall management and execution of the scheme. 

The government also conducts regular monitoring and evaluation of the scheme's progress. 

This ensures that the objectives are being met and necessary adjustments are made. 

In addition to employment generation, the scheme also focuses on entrepreneurship development. 

It encourages beneficiaries to start their own businesses after receiving training. 

This aspect of the scheme aims to create self-employment opportunities. 

The government provides guidance and support for those interested in entrepreneurship. 

Financial assistance is also available for setting up small businesses.
