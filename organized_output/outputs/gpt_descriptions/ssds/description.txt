The scheme is known as the Skill Development Scheme for Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes (SSDS). 

It aims to enhance the employability of Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes through skill development and vocational training. 

The primary objective is to provide skill training to marginalized communities to improve their livelihood opportunities. 

Benefits include financial assistance for training programs and support for self-employment initiatives. 

Eligible beneficiaries are individuals from Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes. 

The application process typically involves submitting an application to the designated implementing agency. 

The timeline for the training programs varies based on the specific courses offered. 

The Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment is the primary implementing authority for this scheme. 

Additional details may include partnerships with various training institutes and organizations to facilitate skill development.
