The RCPPPMS stands for the Rajiv Gandhi Grameen Vidyutikaran Yojana. 

This scheme was launched to provide electricity to rural households in India. 

The primary objective is to ensure access to electricity for all rural households. 

The scheme aims to enhance the quality of life in rural areas by providing reliable power supply. 

Benefits include free electricity connections to below poverty line households. 

It also provides financial assistance for the electrification of rural areas. 

Eligibility is primarily targeted at below poverty line households in rural regions. 

The application process involves submitting a request to the local electricity distribution company. 

The timeline for implementation varies based on project completion and local conditions. 

The Ministry of Power is the implementing authority for this scheme. 

State electricity boards and distribution companies are also involved in execution.
