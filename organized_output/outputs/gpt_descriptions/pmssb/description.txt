The Pradhan Mantri Suraksha Bima Yojana (PMSBY) is a government welfare scheme launched in 2015. 

It aims to provide accidental death and disability insurance to individuals. 

The objective is to enhance the social security of the poor and vulnerable sections of society. 

Under this scheme, a sum of Rs. 2 lakh is provided in case of accidental death or total disability. 

In case of partial disability, a sum of Rs. 1 lakh is provided. 

The scheme is available to individuals aged between 18 to 70 years. 

They must have a savings bank account to enroll in the scheme. 

The premium for the scheme is Rs. 12 per annum. 

The application process can be completed through banks and financial institutions. 

Enrollment can be done online or offline. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Finance. 

The coverage period is from June 1 to May 31 of the following year. 

Renewal of the policy is required annually. 

The scheme is available across India.
