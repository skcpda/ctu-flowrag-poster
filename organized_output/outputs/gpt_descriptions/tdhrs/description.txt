The TDHRS scheme stands for the Tribal Development and Housing Rights Scheme. 

It is aimed at providing housing and development assistance to tribal communities in India. 

The objective of the scheme is to ensure that tribal households have access to adequate housing and basic amenities. 

The benefits include financial assistance for constructing houses and improving living conditions. 

Eligibility is generally limited to members of Scheduled Tribes who do not own a house. 

The application process typically involves submitting an application to the local government office or designated authority. 

The timeline for assistance may vary based on the specific project and local implementation. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Tribal Affairs and state government agencies. 

Additional details may include specific guidelines for construction and maintenance of the houses provided.
