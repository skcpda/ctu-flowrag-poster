The Mukhyamantri Maatru Pushtivardhan Yojana (MMSFS) is a welfare scheme launched by the Government of Madhya Pradesh. 

The scheme aims to improve maternal and child nutrition in the state. 

It focuses on reducing malnutrition among pregnant women and lactating mothers. 

The initiative also targets the nutritional needs of children under the age of six. 

Benefits include the provision of nutritious food and supplements to eligible beneficiaries. 

Financial assistance is provided to mothers for purchasing nutritious food items. 

Eligibility criteria include being a resident of Madhya Pradesh. 

Beneficiaries must be pregnant women or lactating mothers. 

Children under the age of six in the household are also eligible. 

The application process involves registering with the local Anganwadi center. 

Beneficiaries must provide necessary identification and proof of residence. 

The scheme is implemented by the Department of Women and Child Development in Madhya Pradesh. 

The timeline for assistance is ongoing throughout the year. 

Regular monitoring and evaluation are conducted to assess the impact of the scheme.
