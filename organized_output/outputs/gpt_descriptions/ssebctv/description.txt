The SSEBCTV scheme stands for the Scheme for Support to State Governments for the Establishment of a Broadcasting and Communication Technology Value Chain. 

It was launched to enhance the broadcasting and communication technology infrastructure in the states. 

The objective is to promote the development of a robust broadcasting ecosystem. 

The scheme aims to support state governments in establishing a value chain for broadcasting and communication technologies. 

Benefits include financial assistance for setting up necessary infrastructure. 

Eligible beneficiaries include state governments and their agencies involved in broadcasting and communication technology. 

The application process involves submission of proposals by state governments to the Ministry of Information and Broadcasting. 

The timeline for implementation is determined based on the project proposals approved by the Ministry. 

The Ministry of Information and Broadcasting is the implementing authority for this scheme. 

Additional details may include specific guidelines for project proposals and funding allocations.
