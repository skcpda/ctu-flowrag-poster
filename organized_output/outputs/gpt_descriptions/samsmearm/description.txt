The Samagra Shiksha Abhiyan is a comprehensive scheme aimed at improving the quality of education in India. 

It was launched in 2018 to ensure inclusive and equitable quality education for all children from preschool to secondary level. 

The primary objective is to enhance the quality of education and promote holistic development of children. 

The scheme aims to provide access to quality education and improve learning outcomes. 

It also focuses on strengthening the school infrastructure and providing necessary resources. 

Benefits include financial assistance for the development of schools and training for teachers. 

The scheme supports various initiatives such as digital education and vocational training. 

Eligibility is primarily for schools and educational institutions recognized by the government. 

The application process involves submission of proposals by state governments to the Ministry of Education. 

The implementation is carried out by the Ministry of Education in collaboration with state governments. 

The scheme is funded through a partnership between the central and state governments. 

The funding is released based on the proposals and plans submitted by the states. 

The scheme is ongoing and has a continuous timeline for implementation and evaluation. 

Regular monitoring and evaluation are conducted to assess the effectiveness of the scheme.
