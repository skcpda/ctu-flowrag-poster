The Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samman Nidhi Yojana (PM-KISAN) is a government scheme aimed at providing financial support to small and marginal farmers in India. 

The objective of the scheme is to provide income support to farmers to ensure their financial stability. 

Under this scheme eligible farmers receive an income support of Rs. 6000 per year. 

The assistance is provided in three equal installments of Rs. 2000 each. 

Eligibility is limited to small and marginal farmers who own cultivable land. 

Farmers must have landholding of up to 2 hectares to qualify for the scheme. 

The application process involves registration through the state government portal or local revenue offices. 

Farmers need to provide necessary documents such as land records and identity proof during the application. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers' Welfare. 

The financial assistance is credited directly to the bank accounts of the beneficiaries. 

The scheme is operational throughout the year with no specific application deadline. 

The funds are disbursed in three installments every financial year. 

The scheme was launched in February 2019. 

The PM-KISAN scheme is part of the government's broader efforts to support the agricultural sector.
