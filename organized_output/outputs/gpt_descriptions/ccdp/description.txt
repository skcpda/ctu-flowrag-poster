The Central Sector Scheme for the Development of the Handicrafts Sector is known as the Comprehensive Handicrafts Development Scheme or CCDP.

This scheme was launched by the Ministry of Textiles of the Government of India.

It aims to promote and develop the handicrafts sector in India.

The handicrafts sector is a significant contributor to the Indian economy.

It provides employment to millions of artisans across the country.

The CCDP seeks to enhance the livelihood of these artisans.

It aims to improve the quality and marketability of handicrafts.

The scheme focuses on skill development and capacity building.

It also emphasizes the promotion of traditional crafts.

The eligibility criteria for the CCDP include artisans engaged in handicrafts.

They must also outline their training needs and marketing strategies.

The proposals are evaluated by a committee.

The committee assesses the feasibility and sustainability of the project.

Successful applicants receive financial assistance.
