The National Savings Certificate Scheme is a savings scheme offered by the Government of India. 

It is designed to encourage small savings among the public. 

The objectives include providing a safe investment option with attractive interest rates. 

The benefits include guaranteed returns and tax benefits under Section 80C of the Income Tax Act. 

The scheme offers a fixed interest rate which is compounded annually. 

The maturity period is five years. 

The minimum investment amount is Rs 100. 

There is no maximum limit for investment. 

Individuals can invest in the scheme. 

Hindu Undivided Families are also eligible to invest. 

The application process involves visiting a post office or bank that offers the scheme. 

Investors need to fill out the application form and provide necessary identification documents. 

The scheme can be availed at any time as there is no specific enrollment period. 

The implementing authority for this scheme is the Ministry of Finance. 

Interest rates are reviewed and updated quarterly by the government. 

The scheme is available across India through designated post offices and banks.
