The scheme "SIC" refers to the "Skill India Campaign."

The Skill India Campaign was launched in 2015 by the Government of India.

The objective of the campaign is to empower the youth of India by providing them with skill development training.

The campaign aims to enhance the employability of the workforce and promote entrepreneurship.

Benefits include access to various skill development programs and training sessions.

Participants can receive certification upon successful completion of training.

Eligibility criteria generally include being a resident of India and being within a certain age group.

The application process typically involves registering online through the official Skill India portal or visiting designated training centers.

The campaign operates continuously with various training programs scheduled throughout the year.

The Ministry of Skill Development and Entrepreneurship is the primary implementing authority for this scheme.

Various state governments and training partners also play a role in implementation.
