The scheme is known as the Self Employment Scheme for the Scheduled Castes (SESg). 

It aims to provide self-employment opportunities to individuals belonging to Scheduled Castes. 

The primary objective is to enhance the economic condition of Scheduled Castes by promoting entrepreneurship. 

The scheme offers financial assistance for setting up small businesses and self-employment ventures. 

Benefits include subsidy on bank loans and skill development training. 

Eligibility is restricted to individuals from Scheduled Castes. 

Applicants must be above the age of 18 years. 

The application process involves submitting a proposal to the concerned bank or financial institution. 

Applicants may also need to provide proof of caste and identity. 

The timeline for approval and disbursement of funds varies by institution. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment. 

State governments may also have a role in the implementation at the local level.
