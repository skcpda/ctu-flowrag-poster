USSRA stands for the Urban Street Vendor Scheme for Rehabilitation and Assistance. 

The scheme was launched to support street vendors in urban areas. 

It aims to provide financial assistance and social security to street vendors. 

The primary objective is to ensure the livelihood of street vendors and improve their working conditions. 

Benefits include access to credit facilities and skill development programs. 

Street vendors can also receive assistance for obtaining licenses and permits. 

Eligibility criteria include being a street vendor operating in urban areas. 

Applicants must be registered under the scheme to avail of benefits. 

The application process typically involves filling out a form and submitting required documents. 

The timeline for processing applications may vary based on the implementing authority. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs. 

Local urban bodies are responsible for the execution at the ground level. 

Additional details on specific benefits and assistance may vary by state or region.
