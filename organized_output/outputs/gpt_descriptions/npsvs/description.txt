The National Pension System for the Unorganized Sector (NPSVS) is a government initiative aimed at providing social security to workers in the unorganized sector. 

The objective of NPSVS is to ensure a pension for individuals who do not have access to formal pension schemes. 

The scheme provides a monthly pension to eligible beneficiaries after they reach the age of 60. 

The benefits include a minimum pension amount which is determined by the government. 

Eligibility criteria include being a citizen of India and being engaged in the unorganized sector. 

Individuals must be between the ages of 18 and 40 to enroll in the scheme. 

The application process involves filling out a registration form and submitting it to the designated authority. 

The timeline for processing applications may vary depending on the implementing agency. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Labour and Employment. 

Additional details include that contributions can be made by the beneficiaries on a voluntary basis. 

The scheme aims to provide financial security and improve the quality of life for workers in the unorganized sector.
