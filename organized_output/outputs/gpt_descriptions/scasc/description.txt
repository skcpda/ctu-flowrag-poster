The SCASC scheme stands for the Scheduled Caste Assistance Scheme. 

It is designed to provide financial assistance to individuals from Scheduled Caste communities. 

The objective of the scheme is to enhance the socio-economic conditions of Scheduled Caste individuals. 

The benefits include financial support for education and skill development. 

Eligible beneficiaries are individuals belonging to Scheduled Caste communities. 

The application process typically involves submitting relevant documents to the designated authority. 

The timeline for disbursement of funds may vary based on the specific program under the scheme. 

The implementing authorities include state governments and local bodies. 

Additional details about the specific programs and financial assistance amounts may vary by state.
