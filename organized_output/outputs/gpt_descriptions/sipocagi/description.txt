The SIPOCAGI scheme is an initiative by the Government of India aimed at enhancing the livelihood of rural households. 

The scheme focuses on providing financial assistance to women self-help groups. 

It aims to promote entrepreneurship among women in rural areas. 

The benefits include access to credit and training for skill development. 

Eligible beneficiaries are women who are members of self-help groups in rural regions. 

The application process involves submitting an application through the local self-help group. 

The timeline for disbursement of funds varies based on the project approval process. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Rural Development. 

Additional details about the scheme can be found on official government websites.
