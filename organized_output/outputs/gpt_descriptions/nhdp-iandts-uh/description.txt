The National Health Mission (NHM) is a government initiative aimed at providing accessible and affordable healthcare to the rural population of India. 

The NHM includes various programs to enhance healthcare services. 

The NHDP-IandTS-UH scheme focuses on improving healthcare infrastructure and services in urban areas. 

Objectives include reducing maternal and infant mortality rates and enhancing healthcare delivery systems. 

Benefits include improved access to quality healthcare services and increased healthcare facilities in urban areas. 

Eligibility criteria typically include residents of urban areas and specific vulnerable populations. 

The application process may vary by state and typically involves registration with local health authorities. 

The timeline for implementation is ongoing with regular assessments and updates. 

The Ministry of Health and Family Welfare is the primary implementing authority for this scheme. 

State health departments also play a significant role in execution and monitoring.
