The scheme SFASISSSIS stands for the Scheme for Financial Assistance for the Skill Development of Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes.

It aims to provide financial assistance for skill development among Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes.

The objective is to enhance the employability of these communities by providing them with necessary skills.

Benefits include financial support for training programs and skill development initiatives.

Eligible beneficiaries are individuals from Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes.

The application process typically involves submitting an application to the designated authority along with required documents.

The timeline for processing applications may vary based on the implementing agency.

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment.

Additional details about specific training programs and financial assistance amounts may vary based on state and local guidelines.
