The Garib Kalyan Rojgar Abhiyaan is a significant initiative launched by the Government of India to provide employment opportunities to migrant workers who have returned to their home states during the COVID-19 pandemic.

This scheme was introduced in June 2020 in response to the economic challenges posed by the pandemic and the subsequent lockdowns.

The primary objective of the Garib Kalyan Rojgar Abhiyaan is to provide at least 125 days of guaranteed wage employment in a financial year to every rural household.

This initiative aims to enhance livelihood security for the rural population who have been adversely affected by the pandemic.

The scheme focuses on creating durable assets in rural areas through the implementation of various works related to infrastructure development.

It also aims to boost the rural economy by providing employment opportunities and ensuring that the money circulates within the local economy.

The benefits of the Garib Kalyan Rojgar Abhiyaan include direct wage employment for migrant workers and local residents.

Participants in the scheme are provided with a minimum wage as per the prevailing rates in their respective states.

The scheme also emphasizes skill development and training for workers to enhance their employability in various sectors.

Eligibility for the scheme is primarily targeted at migrant workers who have returned to their native villages due to the pandemic.

Additionally, local residents in rural areas are also eligible to participate in the scheme.

The application process for the Garib Kalyan Rojgar Abhiyaan is designed to be simple and accessible.

Workers can register for employment through local Panchayati Raj institutions or through designated government officials.

The scheme is implemented in a mission mode across 116 districts in six states that have a high concentration of returning migrant workers.

These states include Bihar, Uttar Pradesh, Madhya Pradesh, Rajasthan, Jharkhand, and Odisha.

The timeline for the implementation of the scheme is flexible and is designed to meet the immediate employment needs of the workers.

The scheme is expected to run until all the targeted employment opportunities are fulfilled.

Implementing authorities for the Garib Kalyan Rojgar Abhiyaan include the Ministry of Rural Development and various state governments.

The scheme is supported by multiple ministries to ensure a holistic approach to employment generation.

These ministries include the Ministry of Panchayati Raj, the Ministry of Skill Development and Entrepreneurship, and the Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs.

The scheme also focuses on the creation of durable assets such as roads, irrigation facilities, and housing.

This infrastructure development is aimed at improving the quality of life in rural areas.

The Garib Kalyan Rojgar Abhiyaan is part of the larger Atmanirbhar Bharat initiative which aims to make India self-reliant.

The scheme not only provides immediate relief to workers but also contributes to long-term economic recovery.

In addition to wage employment, the scheme encourages the use of local materials and labor for construction projects.

This approach aims to promote local entrepreneurship and sustainability.

The scheme has provisions for monitoring and evaluation to ensure transparency and accountability.

Regular assessments are conducted to track the progress of the employment generation activities.

The Garib Kalyan Rojgar Abhiyaan also includes a focus on social distancing and health protocols to ensure the safety of workers during the pandemic.

The initiative has been praised for its timely response to the economic crisis faced by rural communities.

It has also been recognized for its potential to create a significant number of jobs in a short period.

The scheme is expected to benefit millions of households across the targeted states.

The government has allocated substantial funds to support the implementation of the scheme.

These funds are utilized for wage payments and the development of infrastructure projects.
