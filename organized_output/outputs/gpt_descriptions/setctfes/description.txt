The Scheme for Empowerment of Women through Technology and Financial Support (SETCTFES) aims to empower women by providing them with access to technology and financial resources. 

The objective of the scheme is to enhance the skills of women and promote their entrepreneurship. 

The benefits include financial assistance for women-led startups and access to training programs in technology. 

Eligibility criteria include being a woman entrepreneur or a woman seeking to start a business. 

The application process involves submitting an online application through the designated government portal. 

The timeline for processing applications typically ranges from one to three months. 

The implementing authority for this scheme is the Ministry of Women and Child Development. 

Additional details may include specific training modules and partnerships with technology providers to facilitate skill development.
