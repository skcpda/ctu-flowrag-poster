The SERB-TETRA scheme is an initiative by the Government of India. 

It is implemented by the Science and Engineering Research Board (SERB). 

The scheme aims to promote research and development in the field of technology. 

It focuses on supporting innovative projects that have the potential for commercialization. 

The objectives include fostering technology development and enhancing competitiveness. 

The benefits include financial assistance for research projects. 

The assistance is provided to academic institutions and research organizations. 

Eligibility is primarily for Indian researchers and institutions. 

The application process involves submitting a project proposal to SERB. 

The timeline for project approval and funding disbursement varies based on the proposal review process. 

The scheme encourages collaboration between academia and industry. 

It aims to create a conducive environment for technological advancements. 

The SERB-TETRA scheme is part of the larger efforts to boost India's innovation ecosystem.
