The PAAPWD scheme stands for the "Pension for Persons with Disabilities" program. 

It aims to provide financial assistance to individuals with disabilities. 

The objective is to ensure social security and improve the quality of life for persons with disabilities. 

The scheme offers a monthly pension to eligible beneficiaries. 

The amount of pension varies based on the severity of the disability. 

Eligibility is primarily determined by the degree of disability as certified by a medical authority. 

Applicants must be residents of India. 

The application process typically involves submitting a disability certificate along with the application form to the concerned authority. 

Applications can be submitted online or offline depending on the state guidelines. 

The pension is usually disbursed monthly. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment at the central level. 

State governments may also have their own guidelines and processes for implementation. 

Additional details may vary by state.
