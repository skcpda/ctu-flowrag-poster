The SDBPPD scheme stands for the Scheme for Development of Backward Classes and Economically Weaker Sections in the State of Punjab.

The scheme aims to provide financial assistance and support for the development of backward classes and economically weaker sections.

The objectives include improving the socio-economic status of these communities and promoting their educational and employment opportunities.

Benefits under the scheme may include scholarships for students and financial aid for skill development programs.

Eligibility criteria typically include being a member of a backward class or economically weaker section as defined by the state government.

The application process generally involves submitting an application form along with required documents to the designated authority.

The timeline for processing applications and disbursing benefits may vary based on the specific program under the scheme.

The implementing authority for the SDBPPD scheme is the Department of Social Justice and Empowerment in the Government of Punjab.
