The OCPR scheme stands for the One Child Policy Reimbursement scheme. 

This scheme was introduced to provide financial assistance to families who have adopted the one-child policy. 

The primary objective is to encourage population control and promote family planning. 

Eligible families receive financial reimbursement for expenses related to family planning methods. 

The benefits include monetary assistance for contraceptive methods and sterilization procedures. 

Eligibility is typically restricted to families that have adhered to the one-child policy. 

The application process involves submitting relevant documents to the designated local authority. 

The timeline for processing applications may vary based on the local administrative procedures. 

The implementing authorities include the Ministry of Health and Family Welfare at the central level and state health departments.
