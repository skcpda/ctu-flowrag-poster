The Right to Information Act 2005 is a landmark legislation in India. 

It aims to promote transparency and accountability in the functioning of the government. 

The objective is to empower citizens to seek information from public authorities. 

This act facilitates access to information held by public authorities. 

It enhances the ability of citizens to participate in governance. 

The benefits include increased transparency in government operations. 

Citizens can request information on various aspects of government functioning. 

Eligibility is open to all Indian citizens. 

Any citizen can file a request for information. 

The application process involves submitting a written request to the relevant public authority. 

Requests can be made in person or through post. 

There is no specific timeline for the frequency of requests. 

Public authorities are required to respond within 30 days. 

The Central Information Commission and State Information Commissions oversee the implementation. 

They handle appeals and complaints regarding information requests. 

The act has provisions for penalties for non-compliance by public authorities. 

It has significantly contributed to the empowerment of citizens. 

The act has been instrumental in promoting good governance.
