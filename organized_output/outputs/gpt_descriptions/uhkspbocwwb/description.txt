The Utkal Hitaishini Kalyan Scheme for Persons with Disabilities and Other Vulnerable Communities is a welfare initiative by the Government of Odisha. 

The scheme aims to provide financial assistance and support to persons with disabilities and other vulnerable communities. 

It focuses on enhancing the quality of life and promoting social inclusion for these groups. 

Benefits include financial aid for education healthcare and skill development. 

Eligibility is primarily for persons with disabilities and individuals from vulnerable communities in Odisha. 

The application process involves submitting a form along with necessary documents to the designated authorities. 

The scheme is implemented by the Department of Social Security and Empowerment of Persons with Disabilities in Odisha.
