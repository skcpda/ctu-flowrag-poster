The SACEICBSPV scheme stands for the Scheme for Assistance to Central and State Government Institutions for Capacity Building of State and Central Government Institutions in the field of Biodiversity and Sustainable Development. 

This scheme is designed to enhance the capacity of institutions involved in biodiversity conservation and sustainable development. 

The objectives include strengthening institutional frameworks and enhancing skills and knowledge related to biodiversity management. 

Benefits include financial assistance for training programs workshops and capacity-building initiatives.

The application process involves submitting a proposal to the relevant authorities detailing the intended capacity-building activities.
