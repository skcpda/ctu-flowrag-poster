The Scheme for Sustainable and Efficient Energy Consumption in Small Industries (SSECES) aims to promote energy efficiency in small-scale industries. 

The objective of SSECES is to reduce energy consumption and enhance productivity in small industries. 

The scheme provides financial assistance for the adoption of energy-efficient technologies. 

It also offers capacity building and technical support to small industries. 

Eligibility is generally limited to small-scale industries registered under the Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises Development Act. 

The application process involves submitting a proposal to the designated authority for approval. 

The timeline for processing applications may vary based on the specific project and its requirements. 

The Ministry of Power is the primary implementing authority for this scheme. 

Additional details regarding specific funding limits or project types may be available through official channels.
