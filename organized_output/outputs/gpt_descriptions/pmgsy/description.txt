The Pradhan Mantri Gram Sadak Yojana (PMGSY) was launched in 2000. 

It aims to provide all-weather road connectivity to unconnected villages in India. 

The objective is to enhance rural infrastructure and promote economic development. 

The scheme benefits rural communities by improving access to markets, education, and healthcare. 

It provides financial assistance for the construction of rural roads. 

The eligibility criteria include villages that are not connected by all-weather roads. 

The application process is typically initiated by state governments. 

States submit project proposals to the Ministry of Rural Development. 

The timeline for project completion varies based on the specific project. 

The Ministry of Rural Development is the primary implementing authority. 

State governments are responsible for the execution of the projects. 

The scheme is funded through a mix of central and state government contributions. 

The PMGSY is a key component of the government's rural development strategy.
