The scheme Majhi Kanya Bhagyashree Yojana is a welfare initiative launched by the Government of Maharashtra.

It aims to improve the health and education of girls in the state.

The scheme is designed to provide financial assistance to families for the education and welfare of their daughters.

The primary objective is to reduce the dropout rate of girls from schools and promote their higher education.

Under this scheme, financial assistance is provided to families upon the birth of a girl child.

The assistance continues until the girl reaches the age of 18 years.

Families receive a fixed amount deposited in a bank account in the name of the girl child.

This amount can be utilized for her education and other related expenses.

The scheme also encourages families to ensure the health and nutrition of the girl child.

It promotes awareness about the importance of female education and empowerment.

Eligibility for the scheme includes families with a girl child born on or after a specific date.

The family must be residents of Maharashtra.

There may be income criteria that families need to meet to qualify for the assistance.

Families need to submit the birth certificate of the girl child along with the application.

Proof of residence and income may also be required during the application process.

Once the application is verified, the financial assistance is credited to the bank account.

The scheme is implemented by the Women and Child Development Department of Maharashtra.

The department is responsible for monitoring and evaluating the scheme's effectiveness.

These installments are released at different stages of the girl child's education.
