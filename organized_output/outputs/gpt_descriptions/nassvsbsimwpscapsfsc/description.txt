The National Agricultural Market Scheme for Small and Marginal Farmers through the State Agricultural Produce Market Committees (NASSVSBSIMWPSCAPSFSC) aims to enhance the income of small and marginal farmers.

The objective is to create a unified national market for agricultural commodities.

The scheme provides farmers with better price realization for their produce.

It also facilitates direct access to markets and reduces the role of intermediaries.

Eligible beneficiaries include small and marginal farmers who are registered with the State Agricultural Produce Market Committees.

Farmers can apply through their respective State Agricultural Produce Market Committees.

The application process may vary by state but generally involves submitting necessary documents and registration forms.

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers' Welfare.

The frequency of assistance and benefits may vary based on market conditions and state-specific implementations.
