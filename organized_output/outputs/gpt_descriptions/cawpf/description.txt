The Central Armed Police Forces Welfare Fund (CAPF Welfare Fund) is a significant initiative by the Government of India. 

This scheme is designed to provide financial assistance and welfare measures for the personnel of the Central Armed Police Forces. 

The CAPF includes various forces such as the Border Security Force, Central Reserve Police Force, Indo-Tibetan Border Police, and others. 

These forces play a crucial role in maintaining internal security and border protection in India. 

The welfare of these personnel is essential due to the nature of their work which often involves high risks and challenges. 

The primary objective of the CAPF Welfare Fund is to ensure the well-being of the personnel and their families. 

The fund aims to provide financial support in times of need, particularly during emergencies or unforeseen circumstances. 

The welfare measures include assistance for medical treatment, education, and housing. 

The scheme also focuses on providing support in the event of the death or disability of a personnel member. 

Financial assistance is extended to the families of deceased personnel to help them cope with the loss.
