The Pradhan Mantri Rozgar Sewa (PMRS) scheme aims to provide self-employment opportunities to the youth of India. 

It focuses on promoting entrepreneurship and skill development among the unemployed population. 

The primary objective is to create sustainable employment opportunities through financial assistance and training. 

Eligible beneficiaries include unemployed youth aged between 18 to 35 years. 

They must possess a minimum educational qualification of 10th grade. 

The application process involves submitting an online application through the official PMRS portal. 

Applicants may need to provide documents such as identity proof and educational certificates. 

The scheme offers financial assistance in the form of loans for starting small businesses. 

Training programs are also provided to enhance the skills of the beneficiaries. 

The frequency of assistance is determined by the project proposal and its approval. 

The implementing authority for the PMRS scheme is the Ministry of Skill Development and Entrepreneurship. 

State governments may also play a role in the implementation at the local level. 

Additional details regarding specific training programs and loan amounts may vary by state.
