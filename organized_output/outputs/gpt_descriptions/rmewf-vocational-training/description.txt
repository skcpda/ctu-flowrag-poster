The RMEWF Vocational Training scheme is part of the Rashtriya Mahila Ekta Foundation initiative. 

It aims to empower women through skill development and vocational training. 

The objective is to enhance employability and promote self-reliance among women. 

Benefits include access to various vocational training programs tailored to market needs. 

Assistance is provided in the form of training sessions and resources for skill development. 

Eligibility is primarily targeted at women from economically weaker sections of society. 

The application process typically involves submitting a form to the nearest training center or online portal. 

The timeline for training programs may vary based on the specific course selected. 

Implementing authorities include the Ministry of Women and Child Development and affiliated training institutes. 

The scheme also focuses on creating awareness about women's rights and available opportunities.
