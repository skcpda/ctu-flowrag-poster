The Scheme for Assistance to Elderly Senior Citizens (SAESC) is designed to provide financial support to senior citizens in India. 

The objective of SAESC is to enhance the quality of life for elderly individuals by providing them with financial assistance. 

The benefits include monthly financial aid to eligible senior citizens to help meet their basic needs. 

Eligibility criteria typically include being a senior citizen aged 60 years and above. 

The application process generally involves submitting an application form along with required documents to the local government office or designated authority. 

The timeline for disbursal of funds can vary based on the implementing agency and local governance. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment at the central level along with state governments. 

Additional details regarding specific financial assistance amounts and documentation requirements may vary by state.
