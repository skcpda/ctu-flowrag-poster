The Kisan Samman Nidhi (KSS) scheme is a government initiative aimed at providing financial support to farmers in India. 

The scheme was launched by the Government of India in December 2018. 

It was introduced to address the economic challenges faced by small and marginal farmers. 

The primary objective of KSS is to provide direct income support to farmers. 

This support helps them meet their agricultural expenses and improve their livelihoods. 

Under this scheme, eligible farmers receive an income support of ₹6,000 per year. 

The amount is disbursed in three equal installments of ₹2,000 each. 

The assistance is provided directly into the bank accounts of the beneficiaries. 

This ensures transparency and reduces the chances of corruption. 

The scheme aims to enhance the income of farmers and alleviate poverty in rural areas. 

It also seeks to promote sustainable agricultural practices. 

Eligibility for the KSS scheme is primarily based on land ownership. 

Small and marginal farmers who own up to 2 hectares of cultivable land are eligible. 

The scheme is applicable to all farmers across the country. 

However, certain categories of farmers are excluded from the scheme. 

These include institutional landholders and farmers who are ineligible for other government schemes. 

The application process for KSS is straightforward. 

Farmers can apply through the official website or through designated government offices. 

They need to provide necessary documents such as land ownership records and identity proof. 

The application can also be submitted through Common Service Centers (CSCs). 

Once the application is submitted, it is verified by the concerned authorities. 

Eligible farmers are then registered under the scheme. 

The disbursement of funds occurs in a timely manner following the verification process. 

The KSS scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers' Welfare. 

State governments also play a role in the implementation of the scheme. 

They are responsible for identifying eligible beneficiaries and facilitating the application process.
