The Scheme for Assistance to Scheduled Castes and Backward Classes for the Maintenance of Adult Women (SASBMA) aims to provide financial assistance to women from Scheduled Castes and Backward Classes. 

The objective of the scheme is to support the maintenance of adult women who are in need of financial help. 

The benefits include a monthly financial assistance amount to eligible women. 

Eligibility criteria include being a woman from a Scheduled Caste or Backward Class. 

The applicant must be an adult and must demonstrate financial need. 

The application process typically involves submitting a form along with required documents to the relevant authorities. 

The frequency of assistance is monthly. 

The implementing authority for this scheme is the Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment.
