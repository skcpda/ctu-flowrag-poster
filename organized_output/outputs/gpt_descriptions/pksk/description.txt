The Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samman Nidhi (PM-KISAN) scheme was launched by the Government of India in February 2019. 

The scheme aims to provide financial support to small and marginal farmers. 

The objective is to ensure a steady income for farmers to meet their agricultural needs. 

Under this scheme eligible farmers receive direct cash transfers of ₹6,000 per year. 

The amount is disbursed in three equal installments of ₹2,000 each. 

Eligibility is limited to small and marginal farmers who own cultivable land. 

Farmers must have a valid landholding and must not be in the exclusion criteria. 

Exclusion criteria include those holding more than two hectares of land and certain government employees. 

The application process involves farmers registering on the PM-KISAN portal. 

Farmers can also apply through state government offices or local revenue officials. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers' Welfare. 

Payments are made directly to the bank accounts of the eligible farmers. 

The scheme is expected to be ongoing with annual assessments. 

The scheme has been beneficial in providing financial security to millions of farmers across India.
