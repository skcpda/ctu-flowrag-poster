Soapttikaa is a government welfare scheme aimed at promoting the use of natural and herbal products in the soap industry. 

The objective of the scheme is to support local artisans and small-scale manufacturers in producing eco-friendly soaps. 

The benefits of the scheme include financial assistance for raw materials and training in sustainable production techniques. 

Eligibility is primarily focused on small-scale manufacturers and artisans involved in soap production. 

The application process involves submitting a proposal to the relevant government department along with necessary documentation. 

The timeline for processing applications and disbursing funds may vary based on the specific project and its requirements. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises. 

Additional details about the scheme can be found on the official government website.
