The Syss scheme is a government initiative aimed at enhancing the livelihoods of individuals in rural areas. 

The objective of the scheme is to provide financial assistance and support for self-employment opportunities. 

The benefits include access to credit for starting small businesses and enhancing existing livelihoods. 

Eligibility is primarily targeted towards rural youth and women seeking to establish their own enterprises. 

The application process typically involves submitting a proposal along with necessary documentation to the local implementing authority. 

The timeline for application processing may vary based on the specific requirements of the local agency. 

Implementing authorities include the Ministry of Rural Development and various state-level departments. 

Additional details may include training programs and workshops to support beneficiaries in skill development.
