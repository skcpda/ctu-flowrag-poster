The RCMRF-KP scheme stands for the Rajiv Gandhi Chhatravriti Yojana for the children of the families of the martyrs of the armed forces in Karnataka. 

This scheme was initiated to provide financial assistance to the children of martyrs who have sacrificed their lives for the nation. 

The main objective is to support the education of these children and ensure they have access to quality education. 

The benefits include scholarships for students pursuing higher education. 

The assistance amount varies based on the level of education and the specific requirements of the students. 

Eligibility is limited to children of martyrs from the armed forces. 

The application process involves submitting the required documents to the designated authorities in Karnataka. 

The timeline for disbursement of scholarships is typically aligned with the academic calendar. 

The implementing authority for this scheme is the Department of Education in Karnataka.
