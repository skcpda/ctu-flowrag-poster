The Mukhyamantri Maatru Pushtahar Yojana (MMBPYBB) is a welfare scheme launched by the Government of Madhya Pradesh. 

The scheme aims to improve maternal and child nutrition in the state. 

It seeks to provide financial assistance to pregnant and lactating women. 

The benefits include a cash incentive for women to encourage them to maintain proper nutrition during pregnancy and breastfeeding. 

Eligible beneficiaries are pregnant women and lactating mothers in Madhya Pradesh. 

The application process involves registering at the local Anganwadi center or through the online portal. 

The scheme provides assistance during the pregnancy period and extends to the breastfeeding phase. 

The implementing authority for this scheme is the Department of Women and Child Development, Madhya Pradesh. 

The scheme is part of a broader initiative to reduce malnutrition and improve health outcomes for mothers and children in the state.
