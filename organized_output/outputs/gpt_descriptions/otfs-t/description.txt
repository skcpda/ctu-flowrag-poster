The One Time Settlement Scheme for Tribals (OTFS-T) aims to provide financial assistance to tribal communities in India. 

The objective of the scheme is to settle the outstanding dues of tribal beneficiaries under various government schemes. 

The benefits include a one-time financial settlement to clear dues and enable beneficiaries to access further assistance. 

Eligibility is generally limited to members of Scheduled Tribes who have outstanding dues under government schemes. 

The application process typically involves submitting an application form along with required documents to the designated authority. 

The timeline for processing applications and disbursing funds may vary based on the implementing agency. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Tribal Affairs in collaboration with state governments.
