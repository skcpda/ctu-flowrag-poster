The SMMGNU scheme stands for the "Sustainable Livelihoods and Self-Employment for Marginalized Groups" initiative. 

It aims to enhance the livelihoods of marginalized communities through skill development and self-employment opportunities. 

The objectives include promoting sustainable livelihoods and empowering marginalized groups economically. 

Benefits include financial assistance for setting up self-employment ventures and access to skill development training programs. 

Eligibility is typically focused on marginalized groups such as Scheduled Castes Scheduled Tribes and other economically weaker sections. 

The application process generally involves submitting an application form along with required documents to the designated authority. 

The timeline for processing applications may vary based on the implementing agency's efficiency. 

Implementing authorities include state governments and local self-governments. 

The scheme may also involve partnerships with non-governmental organizations for effective implementation.
