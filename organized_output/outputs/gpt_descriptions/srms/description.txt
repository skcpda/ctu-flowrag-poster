The SRMS stands for the Skill Development and Entrepreneurship Scheme. 

It was launched by the Government of India to promote skill development and entrepreneurship among the youth. 

The primary objective is to enhance the employability of individuals through skill training. 

The scheme aims to support the establishment of new enterprises and promote self-employment. 

Benefits include financial assistance for skill training and entrepreneurship development. 

Eligible beneficiaries are youth aged between 15 to 29 years. 

Individuals must belong to economically weaker sections of society. 

The application process involves submitting an online application through the official portal. 

The timeline for skill training varies based on the specific courses offered. 

The implementing authority for the scheme is the Ministry of Skill Development and Entrepreneurship. 

Additional details include partnerships with various training institutes and organizations for effective implementation.
