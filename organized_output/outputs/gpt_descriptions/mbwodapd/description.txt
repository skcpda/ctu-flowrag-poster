The scheme is known as the Mukhyamantri Bhuvaneshwari Kalyan Yojana or MBWODAPD. 

It was launched by the Government of Madhya Pradesh. 

The scheme aims to provide financial assistance to women for their empowerment and self-reliance. 

The main objective is to support women in starting their own businesses or becoming self-employed. 

Eligible beneficiaries include women who are residents of Madhya Pradesh. 

Women from economically weaker sections are prioritized. 

The financial assistance provided under the scheme can be used for various entrepreneurial activities. 

The application process involves filling out a form available at designated government offices or online portals. 

The timeline for disbursement of funds is typically defined by the implementing agency. 

The scheme is implemented by the Department of Women and Child Development, Madhya Pradesh.
