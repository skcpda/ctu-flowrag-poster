The VASS scheme stands for the Vulnerable Agricultural Support Scheme. 

It was launched to provide financial assistance to vulnerable farmers in India. 

The primary objective is to enhance the income security of small and marginal farmers. 

The scheme aims to reduce the economic vulnerability of farmers due to crop failure or natural calamities. 

Eligible beneficiaries include small and marginal farmers who own less than two hectares of land. 

Farmers must be registered under the Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samman Nidhi scheme to qualify. 

The assistance provided under the scheme includes direct cash transfers to the beneficiaries. 

The amount of financial assistance varies based on the specific needs and circumstances of the farmers. 

The application process typically involves submitting an application form to the local agricultural office. 

Farmers may also need to provide proof of land ownership and registration under the PM-KISAN scheme. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers' Welfare. 

The frequency of assistance may vary based on the assessment of the farmers' needs and the availability of funds. 

Additional details regarding the scheme can be found on the official government website.
