The SFBOTHW scheme stands for the Scheme for Financial Assistance for the Benefit of the Other Backward Classes. 

This scheme is aimed at providing financial assistance to individuals belonging to the Other Backward Classes. 

The primary objective is to enhance the socio-economic status of OBC communities through financial support. 

Benefits include financial assistance for education and skill development programs. 

Eligible beneficiaries are individuals from the Other Backward Classes as defined by the government. 

The application process typically involves submitting an application form along with required documents to the designated authority. 

The timeline for processing applications may vary based on the implementing agency. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment. 

Additional details regarding specific financial amounts or program duration may vary based on state policies.
