The ShSSD scheme stands for the Scheme for Skill Development in the Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes. 

It was launched to enhance the employability of individuals from Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes. 

The objective is to provide skill development training to improve job opportunities for these communities. 

Benefits include financial assistance for training programs and support for self-employment initiatives. 

The scheme aims to empower marginalized communities through skill acquisition. 

Eligibility is primarily focused on individuals belonging to Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes. 

The application process typically involves submitting an application to the designated implementing agency. 

The timeline for training programs may vary based on the specific courses offered. 

The Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment is the primary implementing authority for this scheme. 

Additional details on specific training programs and locations may be available through official government sources.
