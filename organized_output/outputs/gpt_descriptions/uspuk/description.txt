The Udyogini Scheme is an initiative aimed at promoting entrepreneurship among women in India. 

It is designed to provide financial assistance and support to women who wish to start their own businesses. 

The primary objective is to empower women economically and enhance their participation in the workforce. 

The scheme offers a subsidy on bank loans for women entrepreneurs. 

Financial assistance is provided in the form of loans up to a certain limit. 

The scheme also includes training and skill development programs for women. 

Eligibility is primarily for women who are above the age of 18 and have a viable business idea. 

Women from Scheduled Castes Scheduled Tribes and other backward classes are given priority. 

The application process involves submitting a detailed project report to the concerned bank. 

The application must also include personal identification and proof of address. 

The timeline for loan approval may vary depending on the bank's processing time. 

The scheme is implemented by various banks and financial institutions across India. 

State governments may also play a role in facilitating the scheme at the local level. 

The Udyogini Scheme aims to create a supportive ecosystem for women entrepreneurs.
