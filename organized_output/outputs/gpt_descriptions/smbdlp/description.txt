The Scheme for the Management of Bio-Diversity and Livelihoods of the Poor (SMBDLP) aims to enhance the livelihoods of the poor through sustainable management of biodiversity. 

The objective is to promote conservation of biodiversity while improving the socio-economic conditions of local communities. 

The scheme provides financial assistance for various activities related to biodiversity management. 

Benefits include capacity building training and support for sustainable livelihood initiatives. 

Eligibility criteria include being a member of a poor household engaged in biodiversity-related activities. 

The application process involves submitting a proposal to the designated implementing agency. 

The timeline for fund disbursement and project implementation varies based on the specific project approved. 

The Ministry of Environment Forest and Climate Change is the primary implementing authority for this scheme. 

Additional details may include partnerships with local NGOs and community-based organizations for effective implementation.
