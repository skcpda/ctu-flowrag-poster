The Pradhan Mantri Kaushal Vikas Yojana - Short Term Training (PMKVY-STT) is a skill development initiative launched by the Government of India. 

It aims to enhance the employability of youth by providing them with skill training. 

The objective is to promote skill development in various sectors to meet the demands of the industry. 

The scheme offers financial assistance to training providers who impart skill training to candidates. 

Candidates receive training in specific job roles as per the National Skill Qualification Framework. 

The benefits include skill development training and certification upon successful completion. 

Eligibility criteria include youth aged 15 to 45 years who are either unemployed or seeking skill enhancement. 

The application process involves enrolling through recognized training centers or online portals. 

The training duration varies depending on the job role and can range from a few weeks to several months. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Skill Development and Entrepreneurship. 

Training providers are required to be accredited by the National Skill Development Corporation. 

The scheme is part of a broader initiative to promote skill development across the country. 

Regular monitoring and evaluation are conducted to ensure the quality of training. 

The PMKVY-STT is aligned with the Make in India initiative to boost manufacturing and employment.
