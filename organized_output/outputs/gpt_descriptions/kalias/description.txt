The KALIA scheme stands for Krushak Assistance for Livelihood and Income Augmentation. 

It was launched by the Government of Odisha in December 2018. 

The primary aim of the KALIA scheme is to provide financial assistance to small and marginal farmers. 

The scheme is designed to enhance their livelihood and improve their income levels. 

KALIA is a significant initiative in the context of Odisha's agricultural landscape. 

The state has a large population of small and marginal farmers who often face financial difficulties. 

These farmers typically lack access to credit and other financial resources. 

The KALIA scheme addresses these challenges by providing direct financial support. 

The scheme is part of a broader strategy to promote sustainable agriculture in Odisha. 

It seeks to empower farmers and improve their economic conditions. 

The KALIA scheme has several key objectives. 

One of the main objectives is to provide financial assistance to farmers for agricultural activities. 

This includes support for purchasing seeds and fertilizers. 

The scheme also aims to provide assistance for the purchase of agricultural equipment. 

Another objective is to enhance the livelihood of farmers through various means. 

This includes promoting income-generating activities beyond agriculture. 

The scheme also focuses on providing support to landless agricultural laborers. 

It aims to improve their living standards and economic conditions. 

The KALIA scheme offers several benefits and forms of assistance. 

Eligible farmers receive a financial assistance of Rs 10,000 per year. 

This amount is provided in two installments of Rs 5,000 each. 

The assistance is intended to support agricultural activities and improve productivity. 

Additionally, the scheme provides support for livelihood activities. 

This includes assistance for livestock rearing and poultry farming. 

The scheme also includes provisions for financial support to landless laborers. 

They receive assistance to help them engage in productive activities. 

The KALIA scheme is designed to benefit small and marginal farmers. 

Farmers with landholdings of up to 2 hectares are eligible for assistance. 

Landless agricultural laborers are also eligible for support under the scheme. 

The eligibility criteria are aimed at targeting the most vulnerable sections of the farming community. 

The application process for the KALIA scheme is straightforward. 

Farmers can apply online through the official website of the Odisha government. 

They can also visit the nearest Gram Panchayat office for assistance. 

The application requires basic information about the farmer and their landholdings. 

Farmers must provide documents to prove their eligibility. 

These documents may include land records and identity proof. 

Once the application is submitted it is verified by the concerned authorities. 

Eligible farmers receive the financial assistance directly into their bank accounts. 

The timeline for the disbursement of funds is typically aligned with the agricultural calendar. 

The assistance is provided in two installments each year. 

The first installment is usually disbursed before the Kharif season. 

The second installment is provided before the Rabi season. 

This timing ensures that farmers have access to funds when they need them the most. 

The KALIA scheme is implemented by the Department of Agriculture and Farmers' Empowerment in Odisha. 

This department is responsible for overseeing the scheme and ensuring its effective implementation.
