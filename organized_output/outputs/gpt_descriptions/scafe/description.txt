The SCAFE scheme stands for the Scheme for Assistance to Children of Artisans and Farmers. 

It aims to provide financial assistance to the children of artisans and farmers for their education and skill development. 

The primary objective is to enhance the educational and vocational opportunities for these children. 

The scheme offers scholarships and financial support for educational expenses. 

Eligibility is generally limited to children of artisans and farmers who meet specific income criteria. 

The application process typically involves submitting an application form along with required documents to the designated authority. 

The timeline for processing applications and disbursing funds may vary based on the implementing agency. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Skill Development and Entrepreneurship. 

Additional details regarding specific eligibility criteria and benefits may be available through official government sources.
