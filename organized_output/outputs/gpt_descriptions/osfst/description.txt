The One Stop Facilitation Centre Scheme (OSFSC) aims to provide comprehensive support to women affected by violence. 

It was launched to create a safe space for women to access various services. 

The objectives include providing legal aid medical assistance and psychological counseling. 

Benefits include access to shelter legal assistance and rehabilitation services. 

Women facing domestic violence sexual assault or trafficking are eligible for assistance. 

The application process involves visiting the nearest One Stop Facilitation Centre. 

The centres operate on a continuous basis to provide timely support. 

The Ministry of Women and Child Development is the implementing authority for this scheme. 

State governments are responsible for establishing and managing the centres. 

The scheme promotes a multi-sectoral approach to address women's issues. 

Collaboration with NGOs and other stakeholders is encouraged to enhance service delivery.
