The Mukhyamantri Maatru Pushtivardhan Yojana (MMYG) is a welfare scheme initiated by the Government of Madhya Pradesh. 

The scheme aims to improve maternal and child nutrition in the state. 

It focuses on reducing malnutrition among pregnant women and lactating mothers. 

The scheme also targets the nutritional needs of children under the age of six. 

Benefits include the provision of nutritional supplements to eligible beneficiaries. 

Assistance is provided in the form of food items and health services. 

Eligibility criteria include pregnant women and lactating mothers residing in Madhya Pradesh. 

Children under the age of six are also eligible for benefits under this scheme. 

The application process typically involves registration at local Anganwadi centers. 

Beneficiaries may need to provide identification and proof of residence. 

The scheme is implemented by the Department of Women and Child Development, Madhya Pradesh. 

The frequency of assistance is generally monthly. 

Additional details may include awareness programs conducted to educate beneficiaries about nutrition.
