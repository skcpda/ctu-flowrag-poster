The Pradhan Mantri Kaushal Vikas Yojana (PMKVY) is a skill development initiative launched by the Government of India. 

It aims to enhance the employability of youth by providing skill training. 

The objective is to promote skill development for the youth in various sectors. 

The scheme offers financial assistance for skill training programs. 

It provides a stipend to trainees during the training period. 

The benefits include improved job opportunities and enhanced skill sets. 

Eligibility criteria include being a citizen of India and aged between 15 to 45 years. 

Candidates should not be enrolled in any other skill training program. 

The application process involves registering on the PMKVY portal or through training centers. 

The training is conducted by accredited training providers. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Skill Development and Entrepreneurship. 

Training programs are conducted on a continuous basis. 

The scheme is aligned with the National Skill Development Mission. 

It focuses on various sectors including manufacturing and services. 

Assessment and certification are provided upon successful completion of training. 

The scheme also emphasizes the importance of soft skills and entrepreneurship.
