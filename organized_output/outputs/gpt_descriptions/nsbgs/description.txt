The National Scheme for the Welfare of Girls and Women (NSBGS) is an initiative by the Government of India. 

It aims to empower girls and women through various welfare measures. 

The objectives include promoting gender equality and ensuring the welfare of girls and women in society. 

Benefits include financial assistance for education health and skill development. 

The scheme also provides support for self-help groups and vocational training programs. 

Eligibility criteria typically include being a girl or woman from a marginalized background. 

The application process may involve submitting forms to designated authorities or agencies. 

The timeline for assistance may vary based on the specific program under the scheme. 

Implementing authorities include the Ministry of Women and Child Development and state governments.
