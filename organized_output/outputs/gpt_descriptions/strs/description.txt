The Scheme for Transfer of Subsidy for Rural Students (STRS) aims to provide financial assistance to students in rural areas. 

The objective is to enhance access to education for underprivileged students. 

The scheme offers direct cash transfers to eligible students to cover educational expenses. 

Benefits include financial support for tuition fees and other educational costs. 

Eligibility criteria include being a student residing in rural areas and belonging to economically weaker sections. 

The application process typically involves submitting an online application along with required documents. 

The timeline for disbursal of funds is usually aligned with the academic calendar. 

The implementing authority for this scheme is the Ministry of Education. 

Additional details may include specific guidelines for fund utilization and monitoring mechanisms.
