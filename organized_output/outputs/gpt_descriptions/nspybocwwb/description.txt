The National Scheme for Welfare of Fishermen and their Dependents is known as NSPYBOCWWB. 

It aims to provide financial assistance and welfare measures to fishermen and their families. 

The objectives include improving the living standards of fishermen and ensuring their social security. 

Benefits include financial assistance for education health and housing. 

Eligible beneficiaries are fishermen registered with the relevant authorities. 

The application process involves submitting forms to the local fisheries department. 

The scheme is implemented by the Department of Fisheries under the Ministry of Fisheries Animal Husbandry and Dairying. 

The scheme is periodically reviewed and updated to meet the needs of the beneficiaries.
