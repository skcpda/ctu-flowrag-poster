The Employment Generation Programme for Scheduled Tribes is known as ESSSTJ. 

This scheme was initiated by the Government of India to address the employment challenges faced by Scheduled Tribes. 

Scheduled Tribes in India often experience socio-economic disadvantages. 

These disadvantages include lower literacy rates and limited access to employment opportunities. 

The ESSSTJ scheme aims to empower Scheduled Tribes through skill development and employment generation. 

The primary objective of the ESSSTJ is to enhance the livelihood opportunities for Scheduled Tribe communities. 

This is achieved by providing them with the necessary skills and resources to engage in productive employment. 

The scheme focuses on promoting self-employment and entrepreneurship among Scheduled Tribes. 

It also aims to create sustainable job opportunities in various sectors. 

The benefits of the ESSSTJ scheme include financial assistance for setting up businesses. 

It provides training programs to enhance the skills of individuals from Scheduled Tribes. 

The scheme also facilitates access to credit and market linkages. 

These benefits are designed to improve the overall economic status of Scheduled Tribe communities.
