The SPABEM scheme stands for the Scheme for Providing Assistance to the Beneficiaries of Ex-Militants. 

It was launched to support the rehabilitation of ex-militants in Jammu and Kashmir. 

The objective of the scheme is to provide financial assistance and support for the reintegration of ex-militants into society. 

The benefits include financial aid for vocational training and self-employment opportunities. 

Eligibility is limited to ex-militants who have surrendered and are residents of Jammu and Kashmir. 

The application process involves submitting an application to the designated authorities in the region. 

The timeline for assistance is subject to the processing of applications by the implementing agencies. 

The scheme is implemented by the Jammu and Kashmir government in coordination with relevant central agencies. 

Additional details regarding specific financial amounts or training programs may vary and should be checked with local authorities.
