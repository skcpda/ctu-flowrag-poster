The Indian government welfare scheme known as the Integrated Fisheries Development and Training Centre for Marine and Coastal Fisheries is abbreviated as IFTDCMTST. 

This scheme is designed to promote sustainable fishing practices and enhance the livelihoods of coastal communities in India. 

The background of this scheme is rooted in the need to support the fishing industry which plays a crucial role in the economy of coastal states. 

India has a vast coastline and a rich marine biodiversity which provides significant opportunities for fishing. 

However the fishing sector faces challenges such as overfishing environmental degradation and lack of modern techniques. 

The scheme aims to address these challenges by providing training and resources to fishermen and fishing communities. 

The primary objective of IFTDCMTST is to improve the socio-economic conditions of those engaged in marine and coastal fisheries. 

It seeks to enhance the skills of fishermen through training programs that focus on sustainable fishing practices. 

The scheme also aims to promote the use of modern fishing technologies and methods. 

By improving the skills and knowledge of fishermen the scheme intends to increase fish production and ensure better income for fishing communities. 

The benefits of the IFTDCMTST scheme are multifaceted. 

Fishermen receive training in various aspects of fishing including sustainable practices and modern techniques. 

The scheme provides access to better fishing equipment and technologies which can lead to increased catch and income. 

It also promotes the establishment of cooperative societies among fishermen which can enhance their bargaining power in the market. 

Additionally the scheme encourages the development of value-added products from fish which can further increase income. 

The assistance provided under this scheme includes financial support for purchasing fishing gear and equipment. 

Training sessions are conducted by experts in the field to ensure that fishermen are well-informed about best practices. 

The scheme also facilitates access to markets for fishermen enabling them to sell their catch at better prices. 

Eligibility for the IFTDCMTST scheme is primarily targeted at individuals and groups involved in marine and coastal fisheries. 

Fishermen and their families residing in coastal areas are eligible to apply for the benefits of this scheme. 

The scheme also targets cooperative societies formed by fishermen to enhance collective bargaining and resource sharing. 

The application process for the IFTDCMTST scheme is designed to be straightforward. 

Interested individuals or groups must submit an application to the designated authority in their respective states. 

The application typically requires details about the applicant's background and involvement in fishing activities.

Once the application is submitted it is reviewed by the relevant authorities. 

Successful applicants are then invited to participate in training programs and receive assistance.
