The Nya scheme is a government initiative aimed at providing financial assistance to eligible beneficiaries. 

The primary objective of the Nya scheme is to support individuals and families in need through direct monetary benefits. 

The scheme offers financial assistance to help beneficiaries meet their basic needs and improve their living conditions. 

Eligibility for the Nya scheme typically includes low-income families and individuals who meet specific criteria set by the government. 

The application process generally involves submitting an online or offline application form along with necessary documents to the designated authorities. 

The timeline for disbursement of benefits under the Nya scheme may vary based on the specific guidelines issued by the implementing authorities. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment along with state government agencies. 

Additional details regarding the specific financial assistance amount and the exact eligibility criteria can be found on official government websites.
