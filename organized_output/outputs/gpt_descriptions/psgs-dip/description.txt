The PSGS-DIP scheme stands for the Pradhan Mantri Sahaj Bijli Har Ghar Yojana - Disha Initiative for Power Supply to Un-electrified Households. 

It was launched to provide electricity to un-electrified households in rural and semi-urban areas. 

The objective is to ensure that every household has access to electricity. 

The scheme aims to improve the quality of life and promote economic development in these regions. 

Benefits include the provision of electricity connections to eligible households. 

Financial assistance is provided for the installation of electricity connections. 

Eligibility is primarily based on being an un-electrified household in rural or semi-urban areas. 

The application process typically involves submitting a request to the local electricity distribution company. 

Timeline for implementation may vary based on the region and the number of applications received. 

The Ministry of Power is the implementing authority for this scheme. 

State electricity distribution companies also play a crucial role in execution. 

The scheme is part of the broader initiative to enhance power supply across India.
