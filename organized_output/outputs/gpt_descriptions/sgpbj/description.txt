The Sardar Patel Gramin Pashudhan Jyoti Yojana (SGPBJ) is a government welfare scheme aimed at promoting livestock development in rural areas. 

The objective of the scheme is to enhance the income of rural households through the improvement of livestock health and productivity. 

The scheme provides financial assistance for the establishment of veterinary hospitals and mobile veterinary units. 

It also aims to facilitate the availability of veterinary services and medicines to farmers. 

Eligibility for the scheme includes individuals or groups involved in livestock farming in rural areas. 

The application process involves submitting a proposal to the concerned state government or implementing agency. 

The timeline for the implementation of the scheme may vary based on state-specific guidelines. 

The scheme is implemented by the Department of Animal Husbandry and Dairying under the Ministry of Fisheries Animal Husbandry and Dairying. 

Additional details regarding specific financial assistance amounts and project guidelines can be obtained from the official state government websites.
