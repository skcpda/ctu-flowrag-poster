The scheme SSY EBC is the Sukanya Samriddhi Yojana for the Economically Backward Classes. 

It was launched by the Government of India to promote savings for the education and marriage of the girl child. 

The objective is to encourage parents to invest in the future of their daughters. 

The scheme offers a higher interest rate compared to regular savings accounts. 

Parents can open an account in the name of their daughter. 

The account can be opened for girls aged up to 10 years. 

The minimum deposit required is Rs. 250 per year. 

The maximum deposit allowed is Rs. 1.5 lakh per year. 

The account matures after 21 years from the date of opening. 

Partial withdrawal is allowed after the girl turns 18 for educational purposes. 

The scheme is available to families with an annual income below a specified threshold. 

The application process involves filling out a form and submitting it at designated banks or post offices. 

Required documents include the birth certificate of the girl child and proof of income. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Finance. 

Interest is compounded annually and is tax-free under Section 80C of the Income Tax Act. 

The scheme aims to empower girls and promote gender equality. 

It encourages savings and financial planning among economically backward families.
