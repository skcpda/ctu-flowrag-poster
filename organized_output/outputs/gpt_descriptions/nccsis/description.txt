The National Cadet Corps Special Incentive Scheme (NCCSIS) aims to encourage and motivate NCC cadets. 

It provides financial assistance to cadets who participate in various NCC activities. 

The scheme is designed to enhance the skills and capabilities of the youth through NCC training. 

Benefits include financial incentives for cadets who excel in various competitions and activities. 

Eligibility is primarily for NCC cadets who meet specific performance criteria set by the NCC authorities. 

The application process involves submission of performance records and recommendations from NCC officers. 

The timeline for disbursement of benefits is determined by the NCC authorities based on the completion of events and competitions. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Defence through the National Cadet Corps. 

Additional details may include specific performance benchmarks and types of competitions recognized under the scheme.
