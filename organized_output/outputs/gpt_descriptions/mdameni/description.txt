The MDA Menni scheme is a welfare initiative launched by the Government of India. 

It aims to provide financial assistance to women from economically weaker sections. 

The objective is to empower women by promoting their economic independence. 

The scheme offers monetary support for various livelihood activities. 

Eligible beneficiaries include women from below poverty line households. 

Women belonging to Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes are also eligible. 

The application process involves submitting a form along with necessary documents. 

Applications can be submitted at designated government offices. 

The timeline for processing applications may vary based on the location. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Women and Child Development. 

It is part of a broader effort to enhance women's participation in the economy. 

Regular monitoring and evaluation are conducted to assess the scheme's impact.
