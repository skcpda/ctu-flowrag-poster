The Atal Pension Yojana is a government welfare scheme launched by the Government of India. 

It was introduced on 9 May 2015. 

The scheme aims to provide social security to citizens in their old age. 

The primary focus is on the unorganized sector workers. 

It seeks to encourage individuals to save for their retirement. 

The scheme is named after former Prime Minister Atal Bihari Vajpayee. 

One of the key objectives is to provide a fixed monthly pension. 

The pension amount varies based on the contributions made by the subscribers.

It targets individuals between the ages of 18 and 40.

The pension can range from Rs. 1,000 to Rs. 5,000 per month.

This co-contribution is applicable for eligible subscribers.
