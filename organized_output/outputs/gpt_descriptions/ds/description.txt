The Deendayal Antyodaya Yojana - National Rural Livelihoods Mission is a flagship program of the Government of India. 

It was launched in 2011 by the Ministry of Rural Development. 

The primary aim of this scheme is to reduce poverty by promoting self-employment and organization of rural poor into self-help groups. 

This initiative is rooted in the philosophy of Antyodaya which means the upliftment of the last person in the socio-economic hierarchy. 

The program recognizes that poverty is a multi-dimensional issue that requires a holistic approach. 

It seeks to provide sustainable livelihood opportunities to the rural population. 

The mission focuses on empowering women and marginalized communities. 

It aims to enhance their income-generating capabilities. 

The objectives of the Deendayal Antyodaya Yojana are multi-faceted. 

One of the key objectives is to promote self-employment and skill development among rural households. 

The program aims to create a conducive environment for the establishment of self-help groups. 

It also seeks to enhance the access of rural poor to financial services. 

The initiative promotes the convergence of various government schemes to provide comprehensive support. 

Another objective is to improve the livelihoods of rural households through sustainable practices. 

The benefits of the Deendayal Antyodaya Yojana are extensive. 

The scheme provides skill training to rural youth to enhance their employability. 

It facilitates the formation of self-help groups which empower women economically. 

These self-help groups also serve as a platform for savings and credit. 

The program provides access to financial assistance for setting up small enterprises. 

It promotes the use of technology in agriculture and other livelihood activities. 

The scheme also provides support for marketing the products produced by self-help groups. 

This helps in ensuring better prices for their goods. 

The assistance provided under this scheme is not limited to financial aid. 

It includes capacity building and skill development training. 

The eligibility criteria for the Deendayal Antyodaya Yojana are clearly defined. 

The scheme primarily targets rural households living below the poverty line. 

Priority is given to women and marginalized communities. 

The beneficiaries must be willing to participate in skill development and self-help group activities. 

Individuals from Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes are also given preference. 

The application process for the Deendayal Antyodaya Yojana is straightforward. 

Interested individuals can approach the local self-help groups or the Gram Panchayat. 

They can also visit the official website of the Ministry of Rural Development for more information. 

The application form can be obtained from these local bodies. 

Once the application is submitted it is reviewed by the concerned authorities. 

The timeline for the approval of applications may vary based on the local context. 

Typically the process is designed to be efficient to ensure timely support. 

The implementing authorities for this scheme include the Ministry of Rural Development. 

State governments also play a crucial role in the implementation of the program. 

They are responsible for the execution of various components at the state level. 

District Rural Development Agencies are involved in the ground-level implementation. 

These agencies coordinate with local self-help groups and beneficiaries. 

The Deendayal Antyodaya Yojana is a part of a larger framework of rural development initiatives. 

It works in conjunction with other schemes like the Mahatma Gandhi National Rural Employment Guarantee Act.
