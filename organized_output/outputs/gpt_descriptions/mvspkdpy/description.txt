The Mukhyamantri Vatsalya Yojana (MVSPKDPY) is a welfare scheme launched by the Government of Madhya Pradesh. 

The scheme aims to provide financial assistance to orphaned children and those who have lost one parent due to various reasons. 

The objective is to ensure the welfare and development of these vulnerable children. 

Under the scheme, eligible beneficiaries receive a monthly financial aid to support their basic needs. 

The assistance amount is provided until the child reaches the age of 18 years. 

Eligibility criteria include being an orphan or having lost one parent. 

The applicant must be a resident of Madhya Pradesh. 

The application process involves submitting a form along with necessary documents to the local child welfare committee. 

The timeline for processing applications may vary but is generally completed within a few weeks. 

The scheme is implemented by the Department of Women and Child Development, Madhya Pradesh. 

Additional details about the scheme can be found on the official government website.
