The GPRADPD scheme stands for the Gramin Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana - Rural Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana. 

This scheme is part of the larger Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana initiative which aims to provide affordable housing to the urban and rural poor in India. 

The GPRADPD specifically focuses on the rural population ensuring that they have access to housing that is not only affordable but also sustainable. 

The background of this scheme is rooted in the recognition of the housing crisis faced by rural communities in India. 

Many rural households live in inadequate housing conditions which lack basic amenities. 

The government identified the need to address this issue to improve the living standards of rural populations. 

The primary objective of the GPRADPD scheme is to provide pucca houses to all rural households. 

A pucca house is defined as a permanent structure made of durable materials. 

The scheme aims to ensure that every rural household has access to a safe and secure living environment. 

By providing housing, the scheme also aims to enhance the overall quality of life for rural residents. 

The benefits of the GPRADPD scheme include financial assistance for constructing or upgrading houses. 

Eligible beneficiaries receive a subsidy that significantly reduces the cost of housing construction. 

The scheme also promotes the use of sustainable building materials and techniques. 

This is intended to ensure that the houses built under the scheme are environmentally friendly. 

Additionally, the scheme aims to create job opportunities in rural areas through construction activities. 

The GPRADPD scheme is designed to benefit economically weaker sections of society. 

Eligibility for the scheme is primarily based on the socio-economic status of the applicants. 

Beneficiaries must belong to the Below Poverty Line category or be part of economically weaker sections. 

The scheme is open to all rural households that do not currently own a pucca house. 

Priority is given to marginalized groups including Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes. 

Women-headed households are also given preference under the scheme. 

The application process for the GPRADPD scheme is straightforward and user-friendly. 

Interested applicants can apply online through the official government portal. 

They can also visit their local Gram Panchayat office to obtain application forms. 

The application requires basic information including details about the applicant and their household. 

Documents such as identity proof and income certificates may also be required. 

Once the application is submitted it undergoes verification by local authorities. 

The timeline for approval and disbursement of funds can vary based on the location and workload of the implementing agencies. 

Typically the process may take a few weeks to a couple of months. 

The frequency of fund disbursement is generally aligned with the financial year. 

The implementing authorities for the GPRADPD scheme include the Ministry of Rural Development. 

State governments also play a crucial role in the implementation at the local level. 

They are responsible for the ground-level execution of the scheme and monitoring its progress.
