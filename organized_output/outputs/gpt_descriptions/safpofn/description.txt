The SAFPONF scheme is aimed at providing financial assistance to the families of deceased farmers. 

The objective is to support the families in times of distress and ensure their economic stability. 

The scheme offers a one-time financial assistance of Rs. 2 lakh to the eligible beneficiaries. 

Eligibility is limited to the families of farmers who have died due to natural or accidental causes. 

The application process involves submitting a claim form along with necessary documents such as death certificates and proof of farming. 

The timeline for disbursement of funds is typically within a few weeks after the application is approved. 

The implementing authority for this scheme is the Department of Agriculture of the respective state governments.
