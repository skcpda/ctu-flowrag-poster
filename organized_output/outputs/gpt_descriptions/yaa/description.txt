The Yaa scheme is an initiative aimed at supporting the welfare of women and children in India. 

The objective of the Yaa scheme is to empower women and improve the health and nutrition of children. 

The scheme provides financial assistance and resources to eligible beneficiaries. 

Benefits include access to healthcare services and nutritional support for children. 

Eligibility criteria include being a woman or a child from a low-income family. 

The application process involves submitting necessary documents to the designated authorities. 

The timeline for assistance is typically annual or as specified by the implementing agency. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Women and Child Development.
