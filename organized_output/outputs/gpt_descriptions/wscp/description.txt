The Welfare Scheme for Children of Persons with Disabilities (WSCP) aims to provide financial assistance to children of persons with disabilities. 

The objective is to support the education and overall development of these children. 

The benefits include financial aid for educational expenses and other necessary support services. 

Eligibility is generally limited to children whose parents or guardians are persons with disabilities. 

The application process typically involves submitting a form along with necessary documents to the relevant authorities. 

The timeline for assistance may vary based on the specific program guidelines. 

Implementing authorities include the Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment and state welfare departments. 

Additional details may include specific eligibility criteria and documentation requirements which can vary by state.
