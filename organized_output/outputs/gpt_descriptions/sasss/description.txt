The Scheme for Assistance to Students for Secondary Education (SASS) is an initiative by the Government of India. 

It aims to provide financial assistance to students from economically weaker sections. 

The objective is to enhance access to secondary education for underprivileged students. 

The scheme offers scholarships to eligible students to cover their educational expenses. 

Benefits include financial support for tuition fees and other related costs. 

Eligibility criteria include being a student of class 9 to class 12. 

Students must belong to families with an annual income below a specified threshold. 

The application process typically involves filling out a form and submitting required documents. 

The timeline for application submission is usually announced at the beginning of the academic year. 

The implementing authority for this scheme is the Ministry of Education.
