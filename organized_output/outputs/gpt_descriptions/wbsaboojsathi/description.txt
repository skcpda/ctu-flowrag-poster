The WBSABOOSathi scheme is an initiative by the Government of West Bengal. 

It aims to provide financial assistance to the economically weaker sections of society for the construction of houses. 

The objective is to ensure that every individual has access to safe and secure housing. 

The scheme offers financial support to eligible beneficiaries for building or improving their homes. 

Eligible beneficiaries include individuals belonging to the economically weaker sections and low-income groups. 

The application process involves submitting a form along with required documents to the local authorities. 

The timeline for the disbursement of funds is determined by the implementing authorities. 

The scheme is implemented by the West Bengal Housing Infrastructure Development Corporation. 

Additional details regarding the specific amount of assistance and documentation required may vary.
