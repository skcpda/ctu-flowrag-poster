The Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samman Nidhi Yojana for PM Kisan Samman Nidhi (PM-KISAN) is a government welfare scheme aimed at providing financial support to farmers. 

The scheme was launched to ensure the welfare of small and marginal farmers in India. 

The primary objective is to provide direct income support to farmers to help them meet their agricultural expenses. 

Under this scheme eligible farmers receive an income support of Rs. 6000 per year. 

This amount is disbursed in three equal installments of Rs. 2000 each. 

Eligibility for the scheme includes small and marginal farmers who own cultivable land. 

Farmers with landholding of up to 2 hectares are eligible to apply. 

The application process involves registering on the PM-KISAN portal or through designated Common Service Centers. 

Farmers need to provide necessary documents such as land ownership proof and bank account details. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers' Welfare. 

The financial assistance is provided on a regular basis as per the defined schedule. 

The scheme aims to enhance the income of farmers and improve their livelihood. 

It also seeks to reduce the financial burden on farmers during the agricultural cycle. 

The initiative is part of the government's broader efforts to support the agricultural sector.
