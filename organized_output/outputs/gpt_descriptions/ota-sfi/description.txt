The OTA-SFI scheme is an initiative aimed at providing financial assistance to the families of deceased soldiers. 

The objective of the scheme is to support the families of soldiers who have lost their lives in the line of duty. 

The benefits include a one-time financial assistance amount to the eligible families. 

Eligibility is limited to the families of soldiers who have died while on active duty. 

The application process requires the submission of necessary documents to the concerned authorities. 

The timeline for disbursement of funds is typically defined by the implementing agency. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Defence. 

Additional details about the specific financial amount and documentation requirements may vary.
