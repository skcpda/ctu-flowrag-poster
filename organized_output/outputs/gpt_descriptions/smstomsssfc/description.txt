The SMSTOMSSSFC scheme is designed to provide financial assistance to small and marginal farmers in India. 

The objective of the scheme is to enhance the income of farmers and improve their living standards. 

The scheme offers financial support for various agricultural activities and investments. 

Eligible beneficiaries include small and marginal farmers who meet specific criteria set by the government. 

The application process involves submitting an application form along with required documents to the designated authority. 

The timeline for disbursal of funds may vary depending on the specific project or investment. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers' Welfare. 

Additional details regarding the specific benefits and assistance provided under the scheme can be found on official government websites.
