The scheme SPMSSC910 is part of the Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana.

It aims to provide affordable housing to the urban poor.

The objective is to ensure that every individual has access to a pucca house with basic amenities.

Benefits include financial assistance for housing construction or enhancement.

Eligible beneficiaries are those belonging to the Economically Weaker Sections.

They must meet the income criteria set by the government.

The application process involves submitting an online application through the official PMAY website.

Documents required include proof of identity and income.

The timeline for approval and disbursement of funds varies based on application processing.

The Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs is the implementing authority.

This scheme is part of the larger initiative to promote housing for all by 2022.
