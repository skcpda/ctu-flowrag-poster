The Skerala scheme is an initiative by the Government of Kerala. 

It aims to provide financial assistance to individuals and families in need. 

The primary objective is to enhance the living standards of the economically weaker sections of society. 

Benefits include direct financial support for various needs such as education healthcare and housing. 

Eligibility criteria include being a resident of Kerala and belonging to a low-income group. 

The application process typically involves submitting relevant documents to the local government office. 

The timeline for processing applications may vary based on the specific requirements and workload of the implementing agency. 

The scheme is implemented by the Department of Social Justice in Kerala.
