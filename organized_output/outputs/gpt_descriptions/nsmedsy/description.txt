The National Scheme for Medical Education and Health Services for the Disabled and Senior Citizens (NSMEDSY) aims to provide health services and medical education to disabled individuals and senior citizens in India. 

The objective of the scheme is to enhance access to healthcare services for marginalized groups. 

The benefits include financial assistance for medical treatments and health services. 

Eligible beneficiaries are disabled individuals and senior citizens who meet specific criteria set by the government. 

The application process involves submitting a form along with necessary documentation to the designated authorities. 

The timeline for receiving assistance may vary based on the specific services requested. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Health and Family Welfare in collaboration with state health departments. 

Additional details about specific benefits and services under the scheme can be found on official government websites.
