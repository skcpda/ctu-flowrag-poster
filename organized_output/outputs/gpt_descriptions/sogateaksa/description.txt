The Sogateaksa scheme is an initiative by the Government of India aimed at providing assistance to specific groups in need. 

The objective of the scheme is to enhance the livelihood opportunities for marginalized communities. 

The benefits include financial assistance and skill development programs. 

Eligibility criteria typically include being a member of a marginalized community or group identified by the government. 

The application process usually involves submitting a form along with necessary documentation to the designated authority. 

The timeline for processing applications may vary based on the implementing agency. 

The scheme is implemented by local government bodies and relevant state departments. 

Additional details about specific benefits and the exact nature of assistance may vary by state.
