The RHS scheme stands for the Rashtriya Gramin Swasthya Mission. 

It was launched to improve healthcare access in rural areas of India. 

The objective of the RHS scheme is to provide comprehensive healthcare services to the rural population. 

It aims to reduce the burden of diseases and improve health indicators in rural regions. 

Benefits include free medical treatment and healthcare services for eligible individuals. 

The scheme also provides essential medicines and diagnostic services at no cost. 

Eligibility is primarily for residents of rural areas who belong to economically weaker sections. 

The application process typically involves registration at local health facilities or through designated government portals. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Health and Family Welfare at the national level. 

State health departments are responsible for local implementation and monitoring. 

The RHS scheme operates continuously to ensure ongoing healthcare support. 

Regular assessments and updates are conducted to enhance service delivery.
