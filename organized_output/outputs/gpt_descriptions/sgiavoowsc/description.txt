The SGIAVOOWSC scheme stands for the Scheme for Grant of Incentive for Adoption of Voluntary Organizations Working for Social Change. 

This scheme is designed to support voluntary organizations that work towards social change in India. 

The objective of the scheme is to provide financial assistance to these organizations to enhance their capacity and outreach. 

Benefits include grants that can be used for various activities aimed at social development. 

Eligibility is typically limited to registered voluntary organizations that meet specific criteria set by the government. 

The application process involves submitting a proposal along with necessary documentation to the relevant government authority. 

The timeline for grant disbursement may vary depending on the approval process and the specific project timelines. 

The Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment is the primary implementing authority for this scheme. 

Further details regarding specific eligibility criteria and application requirements can be found on official government websites.
