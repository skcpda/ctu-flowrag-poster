Samsmeis is a welfare scheme initiated by the Government of India. 

It aims to support the Micro Small and Medium Enterprises sector. 

The objective is to enhance the competitiveness of MSMEs. 

The scheme provides financial assistance and support services. 

Benefits include access to credit and subsidies for technology upgrades. 

Eligibility is primarily for Micro Small and Medium Enterprises. 

The application process involves submitting necessary documents to the relevant authorities. 

The timeline for processing applications may vary based on the specific financial institution involved. 

Implementing authorities include the Ministry of Micro Small and Medium Enterprises. 

The scheme also focuses on skill development and capacity building for entrepreneurs. 

It promotes entrepreneurship among women and marginalized communities. 

Samsmeis is part of a broader initiative to boost economic growth and employment.
