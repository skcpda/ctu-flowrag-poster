The STISRR scheme stands for the Scheme for the Transfer of Subsidy for the Implementation of the Scheme for Rehabilitating the Rural Poor. 

This scheme is aimed at providing financial assistance to rural poor for their rehabilitation. 

The objective is to enhance the living standards of the rural poor by providing them with necessary financial support. 

The benefits include direct financial transfers to eligible beneficiaries for various rehabilitation activities. 

Eligibility criteria generally include being a resident of rural areas and belonging to economically weaker sections. 

The application process typically involves submitting an application form to the designated local authority or agency. 

The timeline for disbursement of funds may vary based on the specific needs and circumstances of the beneficiaries. 

The implementing authorities include the Ministry of Rural Development and local government bodies. 

Additional details about the specific financial assistance amounts and types of rehabilitation activities supported may vary based on state policies.
