The Mukhyamantri Mahila Nyay Yojana (MMNY) is a welfare scheme initiated by the Government of Madhya Pradesh. 

The scheme aims to provide legal aid and support to women facing domestic violence and other forms of injustice. 

It seeks to empower women by ensuring access to justice and legal resources. 

The benefits include free legal assistance and counseling services for women. 

Eligible beneficiaries are women who are victims of domestic violence or other forms of gender-based violence. 

The application process involves submitting a request for legal aid through designated offices or online platforms. 

The timeline for receiving assistance may vary based on individual cases. 

The scheme is implemented by the Department of Women and Child Development in Madhya Pradesh. 

The initiative also includes awareness programs to educate women about their rights.
