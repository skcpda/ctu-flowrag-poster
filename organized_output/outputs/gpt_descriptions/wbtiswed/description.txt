The West Bengal Trade Incentive Scheme for Women Entrepreneurs Development (WBTISWED) aims to promote women entrepreneurship in West Bengal. 

The objective of the scheme is to provide financial assistance and support to women entrepreneurs for starting and expanding their businesses. 

The benefits include financial support for setting up new enterprises and assistance in the form of training and skill development programs. 

Eligibility criteria include being a woman resident of West Bengal and having a viable business plan. 

The application process involves submitting a detailed project report along with the application form to the concerned department. 

The timeline for processing applications and disbursing funds varies based on the specific project and departmental procedures. 

The scheme is implemented by the Department of Micro Small and Medium Enterprises and Textiles, Government of West Bengal.
