The West Bengal Madrasah Waqf Management and Welfare Scheme is an initiative by the Government of West Bengal. 

The scheme aims to provide financial assistance and support for the development of madrasahs and the welfare of their students. 

It seeks to enhance educational facilities and improve the quality of education in madrasahs. 

The benefits include financial grants for infrastructure development and support for educational materials. 

Students from madrasahs may receive scholarships and other forms of assistance under this scheme. 

Eligibility criteria include being enrolled in a recognized madrasah in West Bengal. 

The application process involves submitting a proposal for funding or assistance to the relevant authorities. 

The timeline for processing applications may vary depending on the specific request and the implementing agency. 

The scheme is implemented by the West Bengal Minority Affairs and Madrasah Education Department. 

Additional details about the scheme can be found on the official government website.
