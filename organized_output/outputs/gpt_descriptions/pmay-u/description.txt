Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana - Urban (PMAY-U) is a flagship scheme of the Government of India. 

It was launched in June 2015. 

The scheme aims to provide affordable housing to the urban poor. 

The objective is to ensure housing for all by the year 2022. 

It focuses on providing pucca houses with basic amenities. 

Benefits include financial assistance for construction or enhancement of houses. 

The scheme provides a credit-linked subsidy for home loans. 

Eligible beneficiaries can receive a subsidy of up to 2.67 lakh rupees. 

Eligibility criteria include being a citizen of India. 

Applicants must belong to the Economically Weaker Section or Low Income Group. 

The applicant should not own a house in their name. 

The application process involves submitting an online application through the PMAY-U website. 

Beneficiaries can also apply through Common Service Centers. 

The timeline for approval and disbursement varies based on the application. 

The implementing authority is the Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs. 

State governments and urban local bodies also play a role in implementation. 

The scheme is monitored through the Housing for All Mission. 

It aims to promote sustainable and inclusive urban development.
