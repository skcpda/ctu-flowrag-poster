Mahatma Gandhi Sarva Shiksha Abhiyan is a scheme aimed at enhancing educational opportunities for children in India.

The objective is to provide free and compulsory education to all children aged 6 to 14 years.

The scheme benefits children by ensuring access to quality education and improving enrollment and retention rates.

Eligibility is primarily for children in the age group of 6 to 14 years.

The application process typically involves enrollment in schools that are part of the scheme.

The scheme operates continuously to ensure that children receive education without interruption.

The Ministry of Education is the implementing authority for this scheme.

Additional details may include various initiatives under the scheme to improve infrastructure and teacher training.
