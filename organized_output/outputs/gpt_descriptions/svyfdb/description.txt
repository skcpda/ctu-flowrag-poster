The scheme is known as the Swami Vivekananda Yuva Udyami Yojana. 

It was launched by the Government of Madhya Pradesh. 

The scheme aims to promote entrepreneurship among the youth. 

It provides financial assistance to young entrepreneurs for starting their own businesses. 

The benefits include a subsidy on bank loans and skill development training. 

Eligible beneficiaries are youth aged between 18 to 40 years. 

They must be residents of Madhya Pradesh and have a viable business idea. 

The application process involves submitting a detailed project report to the concerned authorities. 

Applications can be submitted online through the official website or at designated offices. 

The scheme is implemented by the Department of Skill Development and Entrepreneurship, Madhya Pradesh. 

Financial assistance is provided in the form of a subsidy on interest rates for bank loans. 

The timeline for processing applications typically varies based on the completeness of the submitted documents. 

The scheme aims to create self-employment opportunities and reduce unemployment among the youth. 

It also focuses on enhancing the skill sets of the youth to make them job-ready. 

The scheme encourages innovation and supports various sectors including manufacturing and services.
