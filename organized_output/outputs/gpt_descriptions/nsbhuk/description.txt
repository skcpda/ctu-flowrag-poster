The National Scheme for Welfare of Fishermen and Fish Farmers (NSBHF) is an initiative by the Government of India. 

It aims to enhance the welfare of fishermen and fish farmers across the country. 

The objectives include providing social security and improving the living standards of fishermen. 

Benefits include financial assistance for various activities related to fishing and aquaculture. 

Eligible beneficiaries are fishermen and fish farmers registered under the scheme. 

The application process typically involves registration with the local fisheries department. 

The timeline for assistance may vary based on the specific program under the scheme. 

The Ministry of Fisheries Animal Husbandry and Dairying is the implementing authority for this scheme. 

Additional details may include training programs and awareness campaigns for sustainable fishing practices.
