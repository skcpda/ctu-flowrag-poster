The Kisan Vikas Patra Scheme is a savings scheme initiated by the Government of India. 

It was launched in 1988 and reintroduced in 2014. 

The scheme aims to encourage savings among the general public. 

It is particularly targeted towards small investors and the rural population. 

The primary objective is to provide a safe and secure investment option. 

The scheme also aims to promote financial literacy and savings culture. 

Kisan Vikas Patra offers a fixed rate of interest. 

The interest rate is determined by the government and is subject to periodic review. 

The maturity period for Kisan Vikas Patra is 124 months or 10 years and 4 months. 

The scheme allows for the doubling of the investment amount at maturity. 

Investors can start with a minimum investment of INR 1,000. 

There is no maximum limit on the amount that can be invested. 

The scheme can be availed by individuals, minors, and joint accounts. 

The interest earned is compounded annually. 

The investment is backed by the Government of India, ensuring safety. 

Kisan Vikas Patra is available through various authorized banks and post offices. 

The application process is straightforward. 

Interested individuals can visit the nearest post office or bank branch. 

They need to fill out the application form for Kisan Vikas Patra. 

Identity and address proof documents are required for the application. 

Once the application is submitted, the investment can be made. 

The scheme allows for premature encashment under specific conditions. 

However, premature withdrawal may lead to a reduction in interest. 

The scheme is available throughout the year. 

Investors can make investments at any time. 

The Kisan Vikas Patra certificates can be transferred to another person. 

This can be done by following the prescribed procedure at the post office or bank. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Finance. 

The Department of Economic Affairs oversees the operations.
