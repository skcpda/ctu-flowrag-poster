The Mukhyamantri Bhatta Scheme for the Poor and the Construction Workers Welfare Board (MBSPBOCWWB) is an initiative by the Government of India.

It aims to provide financial assistance to construction workers and their families.

The objectives include improving the living standards of construction workers and ensuring their welfare.

Benefits include financial aid for education health and other essential needs.

Assistance is provided in the form of direct cash transfers and subsidies.

Eligibility criteria typically include being a registered construction worker under the relevant state welfare board.

Applicants must provide proof of employment and registration to qualify for benefits.

The application process usually involves filling out a form and submitting it to the local welfare board office.

The timeline for processing applications may vary by state.

The scheme is implemented by the respective State Governments through their Labour Departments.

Additional details may include specific benefits based on the worker's needs and state-specific guidelines.
