The Mahatma Gandhi Bunkar Bima Yojana (MGBBY) is a welfare scheme aimed at providing social security to handloom weavers in India. 

The scheme was launched to support the economic stability of handloom weavers who often face financial hardships. 

It is designed to provide insurance coverage to weavers in case of accidental death or disability. 

The primary objective of the scheme is to enhance the livelihood security of handloom weavers. 

The scheme aims to ensure that the weavers and their families have financial protection against unforeseen circumstances. 

Under this scheme, beneficiaries receive a life insurance cover of Rs. 2 lakh. 

In case of accidental death, the nominee of the deceased weaver receives the insurance amount. 

In case of permanent disability due to an accident, the weaver is entitled to receive a portion of the insurance amount. 

The scheme also provides for a scholarship for the children of the weavers. 

The scholarship is aimed at encouraging education among the children of handloom weavers. 

Eligibility for the scheme is primarily focused on handloom weavers. 

The applicant must be a registered handloom weaver under the Handloom Weavers' Cooperative Societies Act. 

The age of the weaver should be between 18 to 59 years. 

The weaver must be a resident of India. 

The application process involves filling out a prescribed application form. 

The form can be obtained from the local handloom department or cooperative societies. 

The completed application form must be submitted along with necessary documents. 

Documents typically include identity proof and proof of handloom weaving activity. 

The application is then verified by the concerned authorities. 

Once verified, the weaver is enrolled in the scheme. 

The enrollment process is usually completed within a few weeks.
