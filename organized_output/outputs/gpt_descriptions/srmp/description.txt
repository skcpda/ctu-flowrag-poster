The Skill Development in Rural Areas Scheme is also known as the SRMP. 

It aims to enhance the employability of rural youth through skill development and vocational training. 

The objective is to provide skill training to rural youth to enable them to secure employment or become self-employed. 

The scheme offers various benefits including financial assistance for training and support for entrepreneurship. 

Eligibility criteria include being a resident of rural areas and being within a specific age range typically between 15 to 35 years. 

The application process generally involves filling out an application form available at designated centers or online platforms. 

The timeline for training programs may vary based on the specific skill being taught but typically lasts a few months. 

The implementing authorities include the Ministry of Rural Development and various state governments. 

The scheme may also collaborate with training partners and institutions to deliver skill training programs.
