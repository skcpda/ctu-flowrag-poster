The RKSYR scheme stands for the Rajiv Gandhi Kisan Nyay Yojana. 

It was launched by the Government of Chhattisgarh in May 2020. 

The scheme aims to provide financial assistance to farmers for the cultivation of various crops. 

The primary objective is to enhance the income of farmers and promote agricultural productivity. 

Under this scheme, farmers receive direct cash transfers based on the area of land cultivated. 

The benefits include financial support for the cultivation of paddy, maize, sugarcane, and other crops. 

Farmers are eligible if they are registered in the state's revenue records as landowners. 

The application process involves farmers registering through the local revenue office or online portals. 

The assistance is provided annually during the kharif and rabi seasons. 

The implementing authority for this scheme is the Department of Agriculture and Farmers Welfare, Government of Chhattisgarh. 

The scheme is part of the state's broader initiative to support the agricultural sector and improve rural livelihoods.
