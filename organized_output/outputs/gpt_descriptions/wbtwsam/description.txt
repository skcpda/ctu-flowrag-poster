The WBTWSAM scheme stands for West Bengal Tribal Welfare Scheme for Agricultural Marketing. 

This scheme is implemented by the Government of West Bengal. 

It aims to promote agricultural marketing among tribal farmers in the state. 

The objective is to enhance the income of tribal farmers through better marketing of their agricultural produce. 

The scheme provides assistance in the form of financial support for the development of marketing infrastructure. 

It also offers training and capacity building for tribal farmers to improve their agricultural practices. 

Eligibility is primarily focused on tribal farmers residing in West Bengal. 

The application process involves submitting an application to the concerned department of the West Bengal government. 

The timeline for the implementation of the scheme may vary based on the specific activities and projects undertaken. 

The implementing authority for this scheme is the Department of Tribal Development, Government of West Bengal. 

Additional details on specific benefits or assistance may be available through official government channels.
