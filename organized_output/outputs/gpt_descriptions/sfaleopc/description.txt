The SFALEOPC scheme is designed to support farmers in India. 

The scheme aims to enhance the productivity and income of farmers through various interventions. 

It provides financial assistance for the purchase of agricultural inputs and equipment. 

Farmers can also receive training and capacity building to improve their farming practices. 

Eligibility is typically limited to small and marginal farmers. 

The application process usually involves submitting a form to the local agricultural department. 

The timeline for assistance may vary based on the specific interventions. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers' Welfare. 

Additional details about the scheme can be found on the official government website.
