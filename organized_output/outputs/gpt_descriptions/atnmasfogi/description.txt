The Atmanirbhar Bharat Abhiyan is a significant initiative launched by the Government of India in response to the economic challenges posed by the COVID-19 pandemic. 

The scheme aims to make India self-reliant or "Atmanirbhar" by promoting local production and reducing dependency on imports. 

It encompasses various sectors including agriculture, manufacturing, and services. 

The initiative was announced by Prime Minister Narendra Modi on May 12, 2020. 

The context for this scheme arose from the need to revive the economy after the nationwide lockdown imposed due to the pandemic. 

The objectives of the Atmanirbhar Bharat Abhiyan include promoting local industries and boosting the economy. 

It seeks to empower small businesses and entrepreneurs by providing them with necessary financial support. 

The scheme also aims to enhance the production capabilities of various sectors. 

One of the key benefits of the Atmanirbhar Bharat Abhiyan is the financial assistance provided to various sectors. 

The government has announced a stimulus package worth ₹20 lakh crore. 

This package is aimed at supporting micro small and medium enterprises or MSMEs. 

The scheme also includes provisions for farmers and agricultural workers. 

It provides credit guarantees and interest subsidies to eligible businesses. 

The Atmanirbhar Bharat Abhiyan also emphasizes the importance of digitalization. 

It encourages businesses to adopt digital tools and platforms for better efficiency. 

The scheme aims to create a conducive environment for entrepreneurship. 

It promotes innovation and the development of new technologies. 

The eligibility criteria for the Atmanirbhar Bharat Abhiyan vary depending on the specific component of the scheme. 

For MSMEs the eligibility typically includes being registered under the MSME Development Act. 

Farmers must possess valid land ownership documents to avail benefits related to agriculture. 

The application process for the Atmanirbhar Bharat Abhiyan is primarily online. 

Businesses can apply through the official government portal dedicated to the scheme. 

They may need to submit various documents including identity proof and business registration certificates. 

The timeline for receiving benefits under the scheme can vary. 

Generally the processing of applications is done on a priority basis. 

Once approved beneficiaries can expect to receive assistance within a few weeks. 

The implementing authorities for the Atmanirbhar Bharat Abhiyan include various ministries. 

The Ministry of Micro Small and Medium Enterprises plays a crucial role in the implementation. 

The Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers' Welfare is also involved in the agricultural components. 

State governments are responsible for the local implementation of the scheme. 

They work in coordination with central agencies to ensure effective delivery of benefits. 

The Atmanirbhar Bharat Abhiyan also includes various sub-schemes. 

These sub-schemes focus on specific sectors such as agriculture and manufacturing. 

For instance the Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samman Nidhi is part of the agricultural support under this initiative. 

The scheme also aims to enhance the ease of doing business in India. 

It addresses regulatory hurdles that small businesses often face. 

The government has introduced measures to simplify compliance and reduce paperwork.
