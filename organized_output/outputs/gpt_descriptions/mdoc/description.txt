The M-Doc scheme is an initiative aimed at providing digital health services to citizens. 

It is designed to enhance access to healthcare through the use of technology. 

The objectives include improving healthcare delivery and ensuring timely medical assistance. 

Benefits include access to telemedicine services and electronic health records. 

The scheme aims to reduce the burden on physical healthcare facilities. 

Eligibility is generally open to all citizens seeking healthcare services. 

The application process typically involves registration on the designated digital platform. 

The timeline for service availability is continuous as it operates online. 

The implementing authority for the M-Doc scheme is the Ministry of Health and Family Welfare. 

Additional details may include partnerships with healthcare providers to expand service reach.
