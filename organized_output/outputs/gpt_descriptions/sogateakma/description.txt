The Sogateakma scheme is an initiative aimed at providing financial assistance to the marginalized sections of society in India. 

The objective of the scheme is to empower economically weaker communities by providing them with financial support for various needs. 

The benefits include direct cash transfers to eligible beneficiaries to improve their living standards. 

Eligibility criteria typically include being a member of a marginalized community and meeting specific income thresholds. 

The application process usually involves submitting an application form along with required documents to the designated authorities. 

The timeline for disbursement of funds may vary based on the specific provisions of the scheme. 

Implementing authorities include state governments and local administrative bodies responsible for welfare schemes.
