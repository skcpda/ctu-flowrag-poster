PMGDisha stands for Pradhan Mantri Gramin Digital Saksharta Abhiyan. 

It was launched on 6th December 2017. 

The scheme aims to make rural citizens digitally literate. 

Its objective is to empower individuals in rural areas to use digital technology. 

This includes using the internet and digital devices. 

The benefits include enhancing digital literacy among rural populations. 

It provides training to individuals on basic digital skills. 

The assistance includes free training sessions. 

Eligibility is targeted at individuals aged 14 years and above. 

Priority is given to women and marginalized communities. 

The application process involves enrolling through designated training centers. 

Candidates can also register online through the official portal. 

The training program is typically completed within 20 hours. 

The implementing authority is the Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology. 

The scheme is executed through Common Service Centers and other training partners. 

The program aims to cover 6 crore rural citizens over a span of 3 years. 

It is a part of the Digital India initiative. 

The scheme promotes financial inclusion and access to government services. 

It also aims to bridge the digital divide between urban and rural areas.
