The Pradhan Mantri Mudra Yojana (PMMY) is a government initiative aimed at providing financial support to small and micro enterprises. 

It was launched in April 2015 to promote entrepreneurship and self-employment. 

The objective is to provide loans to individuals for starting or expanding small businesses. 

The scheme offers three types of loans categorized as Shishu for up to INR 50,000. 

Kishore for loans between INR 50,001 and INR 5 lakh. 

Tarun for loans between INR 5 lakh and INR 10 lakh. 

Benefits include easy access to credit without the need for collateral. 

It also aims to enhance the income-generating capacity of small businesses. 

Eligibility is open to individuals, proprietorships, partnerships, and small companies. 

Applicants must have a viable business plan and should not have any previous loan defaults. 

The application process can be initiated through banks, microfinance institutions, and non-banking financial companies. 

Applicants need to submit necessary documents such as identity proof, address proof, and business details. 

The timeline for loan approval varies by institution but is generally processed within a few days to weeks. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Finance through various financial institutions. 

It is part of the broader initiative to promote entrepreneurship and boost the economy.
