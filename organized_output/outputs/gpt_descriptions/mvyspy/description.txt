The Mukhyamantri Yuva Swabhiman Yojana (MVYSPY) is a welfare scheme initiated by the Government of Madhya Pradesh. 

The scheme aims to provide skill development and employment opportunities to the youth of the state. 

It focuses on enhancing the employability of young individuals through various training programs. 

The benefits include financial assistance for skill training and support for self-employment initiatives. 

Eligibility is primarily targeted at the youth aged between 18 to 29 years residing in Madhya Pradesh. 

Applicants must be unemployed and should not be enrolled in any other skill development program. 

The application process involves submitting an online application through the official portal of the Madhya Pradesh government. 

The timeline for the implementation of training programs may vary based on the specific course and training center. 

The scheme is implemented by the Department of Skill Development and Entrepreneurship of Madhya Pradesh. 

Additional details regarding specific training programs and partnerships with training institutes may be available on the official website.
