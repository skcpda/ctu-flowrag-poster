The Dr. Ambedkar Foundation is an initiative by the Government of India aimed at promoting the welfare of Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes. 

The foundation was established to honor the legacy of Dr. B.R. Ambedkar who was a prominent social reformer and the principal architect of the Indian Constitution. 

The foundation's primary objective is to uplift marginalized communities through various welfare schemes and programs. 

It aims to provide educational and economic opportunities to individuals from these communities. 

The foundation also seeks to promote social justice and equality in society.

This assistance is aimed at helping them pursue higher education and vocational training. 

The foundation offers scholarships for students at various educational levels. 

These scholarships cover tuition fees and provide stipends for living expenses. 

In addition to scholarships, the foundation also supports skill development programs. 

These programs are designed to enhance the employability of individuals from marginalized communities. 

The foundation collaborates with various organizations to implement these skill development initiatives. 

Another significant benefit of the Dr. Ambedkar Foundation is the provision of financial aid for self-employment. 

This aid is aimed at helping individuals start their own businesses. 

The foundation provides grants and loans to eligible candidates for this purpose.
