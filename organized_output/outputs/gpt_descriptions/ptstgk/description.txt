The Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samman Nidhi (PM-KISAN) scheme provides financial support to small and marginal farmers in India. 

The objective of the scheme is to supplement the financial needs of farmers for purchasing agricultural inputs and meeting other expenses. 

Under this scheme eligible farmers receive direct cash transfers of ₹6,000 per year. 

The assistance is provided in three equal installments of ₹2,000 each. 

Eligibility is limited to small and marginal farmers who own cultivable land. 

Farmers must be registered under the scheme to receive benefits. 

The application process involves registering on the PM-KISAN portal or through designated state government offices. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers' Welfare. 

Payments are made directly to the bank accounts of the beneficiaries. 

The scheme was launched in February 2019 and continues to be operational. 

The frequency of payments is quarterly. 

State governments play a crucial role in identifying eligible beneficiaries and facilitating the application process.
