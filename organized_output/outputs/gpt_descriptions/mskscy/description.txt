The Mukhyamantri Sewa Sankalp Yojana (MSSKY) is a welfare scheme launched by the Government of Madhya Pradesh. 

The scheme aims to provide employment opportunities to the youth of the state. 

It focuses on enhancing the skills of young individuals to make them employable. 

The benefits include skill development training and job placement assistance. 

Eligible beneficiaries are youth aged between 18 to 35 years. 

They must be residents of Madhya Pradesh. 

The application process involves filling out a form available on the official website or designated centers. 

The scheme is implemented by the Department of Skill Development and Entrepreneurship of Madhya Pradesh. 

The timeline for training programs may vary based on the specific courses offered. 

The frequency of training sessions is conducted periodically throughout the year.
