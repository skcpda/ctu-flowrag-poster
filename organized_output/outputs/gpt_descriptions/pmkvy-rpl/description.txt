The Pradhan Mantri Kaushal Vikas Yojana Recognition of Prior Learning (PMKVY-RPL) is an initiative under the PMKVY scheme. 

It aims to enhance the employability of individuals by recognizing their prior skills and knowledge. 

The objective is to provide skill certification to individuals who have prior learning experience but lack formal certification. 

The scheme benefits individuals by providing them with a recognized certification that can improve their job prospects. 

Financial assistance is provided to the training partners for conducting assessments and certifications. 

Eligibility criteria include individuals who have prior experience in a specific skill area and are above the age of 14. 

The application process involves training partners registering individuals for assessment. 

The assessments are conducted by authorized assessment agencies. 

The timeline for the assessment process varies based on the training partner and the specific skill area. 

The Ministry of Skill Development and Entrepreneurship is the implementing authority for this scheme. 

The scheme is operational across various sectors and industries in India. 

It aims to bridge the gap between the skilled workforce and the industry requirements.
