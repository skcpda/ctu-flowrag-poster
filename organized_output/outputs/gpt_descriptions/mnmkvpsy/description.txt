MNMKVPSY stands for Mukhyamantri Nari Shakti Yojana. 

This scheme is implemented by the Government of Madhya Pradesh. 

It aims to empower women and promote their socio-economic development. 

The primary objective is to provide financial assistance to women for self-employment and entrepreneurship. 

Eligible beneficiaries include women who are residents of Madhya Pradesh. 

The scheme targets women from economically weaker sections. 

Financial assistance is provided in the form of grants for starting small businesses. 

The application process involves submitting a form along with necessary documents to the designated authorities. 

The scheme is implemented by the Department of Women and Child Development in Madhya Pradesh. 

The frequency of assistance is determined based on the project proposal submitted by the beneficiaries. 

This initiative also includes skill development programs to enhance women's capabilities. 

It aims to create a supportive environment for women's empowerment in the state.
