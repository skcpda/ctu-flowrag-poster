The SPNMUK scheme stands for the State Program for Nutritional Support to Primary School Children. 

It was launched to address the nutritional needs of children in primary schools. 

The objective is to provide nutritious meals to enhance the health and learning capacity of children. 

The scheme aims to reduce malnutrition among school-going children. 

Benefits include the provision of cooked meals to children attending primary schools. 

This ensures that children receive essential nutrients necessary for their growth and development. 

Eligibility is primarily for children enrolled in government and government-aided primary schools. 

The application process typically involves enrollment in the respective school. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Education at the state level. 

The frequency of meal provision is daily on school days. 

The scheme also encourages community participation in the preparation and distribution of meals. 

Monitoring and evaluation are carried out to ensure quality and effectiveness.
