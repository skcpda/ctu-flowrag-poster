The Mukhyamantri Gramin Pashupalan Yojana (MGPY) is an initiative by the Government of Madhya Pradesh. 

It aims to promote livestock development and enhance the income of rural households. 

The objectives include improving the quality of livestock and providing better facilities for animal husbandry. 

Benefits include financial assistance for purchasing livestock and support for veterinary services. 

Eligible beneficiaries are farmers and rural households engaged in animal husbandry. 

The application process involves submitting an application form to the local government office. 

The scheme is implemented by the Department of Animal Husbandry and Dairying of Madhya Pradesh. 

The frequency of assistance may vary based on the specific components of the scheme. 

Additional details may include training programs for beneficiaries on animal care and management.
