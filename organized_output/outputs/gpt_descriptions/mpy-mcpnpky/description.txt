The Mukhyamantri Pashudhan Yojana is a welfare scheme aimed at enhancing the livestock sector in Madhya Pradesh. 

The objective of the scheme is to promote animal husbandry and improve the quality of livestock. 

The benefits include financial assistance for purchasing livestock and support for veterinary services. 

Eligibility is primarily for farmers and individuals engaged in animal husbandry in Madhya Pradesh. 

The application process involves submitting a form to the local animal husbandry department. 

The timeline for assistance is determined by the availability of funds and the specific needs of the applicants. 

The implementing authority for this scheme is the Department of Animal Husbandry and Dairying, Government of Madhya Pradesh. 

Additional details may include training programs for farmers on animal care and management.
