The Mukhyamantri Sewa Sankalp Yojana (MSSY) is a welfare scheme initiated by the Government of Madhya Pradesh. 

The scheme aims to provide employment opportunities to the youth of the state. 

It focuses on skill development and enhancing employability. 

The benefits include training in various trades and assistance in job placement. 

Eligible beneficiaries are youth aged between 18 to 35 years. 

They must be residents of Madhya Pradesh. 

The application process involves online registration through the official portal. 

Applicants need to provide necessary documents such as identity proof and educational qualifications. 

The scheme is implemented by the Department of Skill Development and Employment. 

The training programs are conducted periodically based on demand and availability. 

Additional details regarding specific training centers and courses may vary.
