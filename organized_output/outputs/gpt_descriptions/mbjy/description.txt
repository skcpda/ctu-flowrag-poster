The Mukhyamantri Bhavishya Jyoti Yojana (MBJY) is a welfare scheme launched by the Government of Madhya Pradesh. 

The scheme aims to provide financial assistance to students pursuing higher education. 

It is designed to encourage students to continue their education and reduce dropout rates. 

The benefits include financial support for tuition fees and other educational expenses. 

Eligible beneficiaries are students from economically weaker sections of society. 

Students must be residents of Madhya Pradesh and enrolled in recognized higher education institutions. 

The application process typically involves filling out a form and submitting required documents to the concerned authorities. 

The timeline for application submission is usually announced at the beginning of the academic year. 

The scheme is implemented by the Department of Higher Education in Madhya Pradesh. 

Additional details may include specific eligibility criteria related to income limits and academic performance.
