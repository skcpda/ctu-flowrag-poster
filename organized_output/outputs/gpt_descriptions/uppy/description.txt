The UPPY scheme stands for the Unnat Jyoti by Affordable LEDs for All. 

It was launched by the Government of India to promote energy efficiency. 

The scheme aims to provide LED bulbs to consumers at a subsidized rate. 

The objective is to reduce electricity bills and promote the use of energy-efficient lighting. 

The scheme also aims to reduce carbon emissions and contribute to environmental sustainability. 

Under this scheme households can purchase LED bulbs at a significantly lower price. 

The benefits include reduced energy consumption and lower electricity costs for consumers. 

Eligibility is generally for all households in India. 

The application process typically involves visiting designated outlets or online platforms. 

Consumers can purchase LED bulbs directly from these outlets. 

The scheme is implemented by the Energy Efficiency Services Limited (EESL). 

The frequency of the scheme is ongoing as long as the stock is available. 

The UPPY scheme contributes to the larger goal of energy conservation in India.
