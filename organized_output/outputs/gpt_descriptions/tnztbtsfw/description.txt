The TNZTBTSFW scheme stands for the Tamil Nadu Zero Interest Loan for Women Self Help Groups. 

It was launched by the Government of Tamil Nadu to empower women through financial assistance. 

The main objective is to provide interest-free loans to women self-help groups to promote entrepreneurship and self-employment. 

The scheme aims to enhance the income levels of women and improve their socio-economic status. 

Eligible beneficiaries are women who are members of registered self-help groups in Tamil Nadu. 

The assistance provided under the scheme includes interest-free loans up to a certain limit for various income-generating activities. 

The application process involves submitting an application through the respective self-help group to the local government authority. 

The frequency of loan disbursement is determined based on the group’s performance and needs. 

The implementing authority for this scheme is the Tamil Nadu Rural Development and Panchayat Raj Department. 

Additional details include that the scheme is part of a broader initiative to promote women’s empowerment in the state.
