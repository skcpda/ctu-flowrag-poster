**Scheme Name: Mahatma Gandhi Bunkar Bima Yojana (MGBBY)**

**Context or Background**  
The Mahatma Gandhi Bunkar Bima Yojana is a welfare scheme initiated by the Government of India.  
It aims to provide social security to handloom weavers across the country.  
Handloom weaving is a significant traditional craft in India.  
Weavers often face financial instability due to various factors such as market fluctuations and health issues.  
The scheme was launched to address these challenges and improve the livelihood of weavers.  

**Objectives**  
The primary objective of the Mahatma Gandhi Bunkar Bima Yojana is to provide life insurance coverage to handloom weavers.  
The scheme aims to ensure financial security for the families of deceased weavers.  
It seeks to promote the welfare of weavers by providing them with a safety net.  
The initiative also aims to encourage weavers to continue their traditional craft without the fear of financial distress.  

**Benefits and Assistance**  
Under the scheme, beneficiaries receive life insurance coverage.  
The insurance amount is set at Rs 2 lakh in case of natural death.  
In case of accidental death, the coverage increases to Rs 4 lakh.  
The scheme also provides for the education of the children of deceased weavers.  
Beneficiaries can avail of a scholarship for their children's education.  
The scheme aims to provide financial assistance during emergencies.  
It also helps in promoting the overall welfare of the weaver community.  

**Eligibility**  
The scheme is specifically designed for handloom weavers.  
Only those weavers who are registered under the Handloom Weavers' Cooperative Societies are eligible.  
Beneficiaries must be between the ages of 18 and 50 years.  
The weaver must be actively engaged in handloom weaving.  
The scheme is open to both male and female weavers.  

**Application Process**  
Interested beneficiaries must apply through the Handloom Weavers' Cooperative Societies.  
The application form can be obtained from the respective societies or online portals.  
Applicants need to submit proof of their identity and age.  
They must also provide documentation of their weaving activity.  
Once the application is submitted, it is verified by the concerned authorities.  
Upon successful verification, beneficiaries are enrolled in the scheme.  

**Timeline or Frequency**  
The scheme operates on a continuous basis.  
Beneficiaries can apply at any time during the year.  
The insurance coverage is effective immediately upon enrollment.  
Claims can be made by the beneficiaries or their families as per the guidelines.  

**Implementing Authorities or Agencies**  
The Ministry of Textiles is the primary authority overseeing the scheme.  
State governments also play a crucial role in implementing the scheme at the local level.  
Handloom Weavers' Cooperative Societies are responsible for the enrollment of beneficiaries.  
Insurance companies designated by the government manage the insurance policies.
