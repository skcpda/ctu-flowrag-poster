The Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana (PMAY) is a flagship housing scheme initiated by the Government of India. 

It was launched on June 25, 2015. 

The primary aim of PMAY is to provide affordable housing to the urban poor. 

This scheme is a significant step towards achieving the vision of "Housing for All" by the year 2022. 

The scheme is divided into two components. 

The first component is for the urban areas and is known as PMAY-U. 

The second component is for the rural areas and is known as PMAY-G. 

The urban component focuses on providing housing to the economically weaker sections of society. 

The rural component aims to provide housing to the rural poor. 

The objectives of PMAY include ensuring that every family has access to a pucca house with basic amenities. 

It seeks to promote sustainable and inclusive urban development. 

The scheme also aims to enhance the quality of life for the urban poor. 

Additionally, it focuses on providing financial assistance for the construction of houses. 

The benefits under PMAY include financial assistance for house construction. 

Beneficiaries can avail of a subsidy on home loans. 

The subsidy is provided under the Credit Linked Subsidy Scheme (CLSS). 

The subsidy amount can be up to INR 2.67 lakh depending on the income category. 

The scheme also provides for the construction of houses with basic amenities like water supply and sanitation. 

Eligibility for PMAY varies based on the component of the scheme. 

For PMAY-U, beneficiaries must be from the Economically Weaker Section (EWS) or Low-Income Group (LIG). 

For PMAY-G, beneficiaries must belong to the Below Poverty Line (BPL) category. 

The annual income limit for EWS is INR 3 lakh. 

For LIG, the income limit is between INR 3 lakh and INR 6 lakh. 

The scheme is also open to women and members of Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes. 

The application process for PMAY is straightforward and can be done online. 

Interested beneficiaries can visit the official PMAY website. 

They need to fill out the application form with required details. 

Documents such as identity proof and income proof are necessary. 

Once the application is submitted, it is scrutinized by the authorities. 

The timeline for approval can vary based on the volume of applications. 

However, the government aims to process applications swiftly. 

The frequency of the scheme is continuous as it is an ongoing initiative. 

The implementing authorities for PMAY include the Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs. 

State governments and local bodies also play a crucial role in implementation. 

They are responsible for identifying beneficiaries and monitoring the progress of the scheme.
