The Seh-TMDUMMAPUY scheme is a welfare initiative by the Government of India. 

It aims to provide financial assistance to marginalized communities. 

The scheme focuses on enhancing the socio-economic conditions of beneficiaries. 

Benefits include direct cash transfers and support for livelihood activities. 

Eligibility criteria include being a member of a marginalized community. 

Applicants must also meet specific income thresholds set by the government. 

The application process typically involves submitting necessary documents to local authorities. 

The timeline for disbursement of benefits is usually announced during the application period. 

Implementing authorities include state government departments and local administrative bodies. 

The scheme is part of a broader effort to promote inclusivity and reduce poverty.
