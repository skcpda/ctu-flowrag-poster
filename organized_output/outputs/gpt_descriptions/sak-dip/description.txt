Sak-DIP is a welfare scheme initiated by the Government of India. 

It is designed to provide financial assistance to women entrepreneurs. 

The objective is to promote women's self-employment and entrepreneurship. 

The scheme aims to enhance the economic empowerment of women. 

Benefits include financial support for starting and expanding businesses. 

Assistance is provided in the form of loans and subsidies. 

Eligibility is primarily for women who are above 18 years of age. 

Applicants must be Indian citizens and should have a viable business plan. 

The application process involves submitting a detailed project report. 

Applications can be submitted online through the official portal. 

The timeline for processing applications may vary based on the project. 

The implementing authorities include the Ministry of Women and Child Development. 

State governments may also be involved in the implementation of the scheme. 

Additional details about specific loan amounts and interest rates may vary.
