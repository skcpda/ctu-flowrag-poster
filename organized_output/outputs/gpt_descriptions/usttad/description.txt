USTTAD stands for Upgrading the Skills and Training in Traditional Arts/Crafts for Development. 

It was launched by the Ministry of Minority Affairs in India. 

The scheme aims to preserve and promote traditional arts and crafts. 

It seeks to enhance the skills of artisans and craftsmen from minority communities. 

The objectives include providing skill development training and improving market access for artisans. 

The scheme provides financial assistance for training programs. 

It also offers support for the development of infrastructure related to traditional crafts. 

Eligibility is primarily for artisans and craftsmen belonging to minority communities. 

The application process involves submitting an application to the Ministry of Minority Affairs or designated agencies. 

The timeline for training programs may vary based on specific courses and locations. 

Implementing authorities include the Ministry of Minority Affairs and various state governments. 

The scheme also encourages collaboration with NGOs and other organizations for effective implementation.
