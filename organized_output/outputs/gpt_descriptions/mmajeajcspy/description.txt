The scheme MMAJEAJCSPY stands for Mukhyamantri Awas Yojana for the Economically Weaker Sections. 

It was launched by the Government of Madhya Pradesh. 

The objective of the scheme is to provide affordable housing to economically weaker sections of society. 

The scheme aims to ensure that every individual has access to a safe and secure home. 

Benefits include financial assistance for the construction or purchase of houses. 

Eligible beneficiaries receive a subsidy on the housing loan. 

The eligibility criteria include being a resident of Madhya Pradesh and belonging to the economically weaker section. 

Applicants must not own a pucca house in their name or in the name of any family member. 

The application process involves submitting an online application through the official portal. 

Beneficiaries must provide necessary documents such as income proof and identity proof. 

The timeline for approval and disbursement of funds may vary based on the application. 

The scheme is implemented by the Madhya Pradesh Housing and Infrastructure Development Board. 

Additional details regarding the scheme can be found on the official government website.
