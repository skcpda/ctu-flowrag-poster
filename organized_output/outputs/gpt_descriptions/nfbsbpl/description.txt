The National Family Benefit Scheme (NFBS) is a welfare initiative by the Government of India. 

It aims to provide financial assistance to families living below the poverty line. 

The objective is to support families in the event of the death of the primary breadwinner. 

Under this scheme, a one-time financial assistance of Rs. 20,000 is provided to the bereaved family. 

The assistance is intended to help the family cope with the loss of income. 

Eligibility criteria include that the deceased must be the primary breadwinner of the family. 

The family must be registered under the Below Poverty Line (BPL) category. 

The applicant must be a resident of the state where the scheme is being implemented. 

The application process requires the submission of a death certificate of the deceased. 

The family must also provide proof of being below the poverty line. 

Applications can be submitted to the local government office or designated authority. 

The timeline for disbursement of funds is typically within a few weeks after application approval. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Rural Development at the national level. 

State governments may have their own implementing agencies for the scheme. 

The NFBS is part of a broader effort to provide social security to vulnerable families.
