The Pradhan Mantri Formalization of Micro Food Processing Enterprises Scheme aims to support micro food processing units in India. 

It was launched to enhance the competitiveness of micro food processing enterprises. 

The objective is to provide financial assistance and support for the formalization of the food processing sector. 

The scheme offers financial support up to INR 10 lakh per unit. 

It also provides assistance for the purchase of equipment and technology. 

Training and skill development programs are included to improve the capabilities of entrepreneurs. 

Eligibility is primarily for micro food processing enterprises. 

The applicants must be registered as micro enterprises under the MSME Development Act. 

The application process involves submitting an online application through the official portal. 

The timeline for approval and disbursal of funds varies based on the application review process. 

The Ministry of Food Processing Industries is the implementing authority for this scheme. 

State governments may also play a role in the implementation at the local level. 

The scheme is part of the larger initiative to promote the Atmanirbhar Bharat vision.
