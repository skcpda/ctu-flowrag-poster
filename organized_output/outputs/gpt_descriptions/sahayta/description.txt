Sahayta is a welfare scheme launched by the Government of India. 

The scheme aims to provide financial assistance to individuals in need. 

It focuses on supporting marginalized and economically weaker sections of society. 

The primary objective is to alleviate poverty and improve living standards. 

Benefits include direct cash transfers and support for essential needs. 

Eligibility criteria typically include being a resident of India and belonging to a low-income group. 

The application process generally involves submitting an online application through the official portal. 

Applicants may need to provide identification and income proof. 

The timeline for assistance may vary based on the specific needs and processing time. 

Implementing authorities include various state and central government departments. 

The scheme may also collaborate with local NGOs for outreach and support.
