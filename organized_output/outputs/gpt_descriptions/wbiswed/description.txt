The WBISWED scheme stands for West Bengal Integrated Scheme for Women’s Economic Development. 

It aims to empower women by promoting their economic independence. 

The scheme focuses on providing financial assistance and skill development to women. 

Benefits include access to credit for self-employment and entrepreneurial ventures. 

Women can receive training in various skills to enhance their employability. 

Eligibility is primarily for women residing in West Bengal. 

The application process involves submitting a form to the concerned department. 

The scheme is implemented by the Department of Women Development and Social Welfare, Government of West Bengal. 

The timeline for assistance may vary based on the specific program under the scheme. 

Additional details about the scheme can be found on the official government website.
