The Saeaspjag scheme is an initiative by the Government of India. 

It aims to provide financial assistance to small and marginal farmers. 

The objective is to enhance agricultural productivity and ensure food security. 

Benefits include direct cash transfers to eligible farmers. 

This assistance helps in purchasing seeds fertilizers and other agricultural inputs. 

Eligibility criteria include being a small or marginal farmer. 

Farmers must also be registered under the relevant agricultural department. 

The application process involves filling out a form available at local agricultural offices. 

Farmers may also apply online through the official government portal. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers' Welfare. 

Funds are disbursed on a periodic basis as per the agricultural cycle. 

The frequency of assistance may vary based on the crop season. 

Additional details on the scheme can be found on the official government website.
