The scheme known as the D.P.B.B.O.C.W.W.B. stands for the Deendayal Antyodaya Yojana - National Rural Livelihoods Mission.

This scheme is designed to enhance the livelihood opportunities for the rural poor in India.

It was launched by the Ministry of Rural Development of the Government of India.

The primary objective of this scheme is to reduce poverty by promoting self-employment and organization of rural poor into self-help groups.

The scheme aims to provide skill development training to rural youth.

It also seeks to promote sustainable livelihoods through various means.

The initiative is part of a broader strategy to empower rural households.

The scheme focuses on the most marginalized and vulnerable sections of society.

This includes women and Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes.

The benefits under this scheme include financial assistance for setting up self-employment ventures.

It also provides access to credit and skill training.

The scheme encourages the formation of self-help groups.

These groups are crucial for promoting savings and providing micro-finance.

The assistance provided under the scheme is not limited to financial support.

It also includes capacity building and training programs.

The eligibility criteria for the scheme are primarily based on socio-economic status.

The beneficiaries must belong to rural areas and fall below the poverty line.

Priority is given to women and marginalized communities.

The application process for the scheme is straightforward.

Interested individuals or groups can apply through the local rural development department.

Applications can also be submitted online through designated portals.

Once the application is submitted it undergoes a verification process.

The timeline for the approval of applications can vary.

Typically it takes a few weeks for the applications to be processed.

The scheme is implemented by various agencies at both the central and state levels.

These include the Ministry of Rural Development and state rural livelihoods missions.

The scheme also collaborates with non-governmental organizations.

These organizations play a crucial role in the implementation of the scheme.

They help in mobilizing the rural poor and facilitating training programs.

The scheme is designed to be inclusive and participatory.

It encourages beneficiaries to actively engage in the decision-making process.

The financial assistance provided under the scheme can vary.

It is based on the type of activity and the scale of the project.

The scheme also promotes the use of technology in enhancing livelihoods.

This includes digital literacy programs for rural youth.

The scheme has a strong focus on sustainability.

It encourages practices that are environmentally friendly.

The impact of the scheme is monitored through various indicators.

These indicators include the increase in income levels of beneficiaries.

The scheme also aims to improve the overall quality of life in rural areas.

It seeks to create a self-reliant rural economy.
