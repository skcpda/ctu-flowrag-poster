The TRSP stands for the Tribal Research and Training Institute Scheme. 

It aims to promote the welfare and development of tribal communities in India. 

The objective is to enhance the socio-economic status of tribal populations through research and training. 

The scheme provides financial assistance for various initiatives related to tribal development. 

Benefits include capacity building and skill development programs for tribal youth. 

Eligibility criteria typically include being a member of a recognized Scheduled Tribe. 

The application process involves submitting a proposal to the relevant state government department. 

The timeline for approval and implementation may vary based on the specific project. 

The Ministry of Tribal Affairs is the primary implementing authority for this scheme. 

State governments also play a crucial role in executing the initiatives under this scheme.
