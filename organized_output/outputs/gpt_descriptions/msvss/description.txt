The Mukhyamantri Swavalamban Yojana (MSVSS) is a welfare scheme initiated by the Government of Rajasthan. 

The scheme aims to promote self-employment and entrepreneurship among the youth of the state. 

It provides financial assistance to individuals for setting up small businesses and self-employment ventures. 

The benefits include a subsidy on the bank loan taken for the business. 

The assistance is available to individuals aged between 18 to 35 years. 

Applicants must be residents of Rajasthan and should not have any existing business. 

The application process involves submitting an online application through the official website of the Rajasthan government. 

Documents required include identity proof, address proof, and a detailed project report. 

The scheme is implemented by the Department of Skill, Employment and Entrepreneurship, Government of Rajasthan. 

The financial assistance is provided in the form of a subsidy of up to 30 percent of the loan amount. 

The timeline for disbursal of funds varies based on the completion of the application process and approval. 

The scheme is reviewed periodically to assess its impact and effectiveness.
