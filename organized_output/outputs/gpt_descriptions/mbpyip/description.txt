The Mukhyamantri Bhatta Yojana for Paryavaran Yatra (MBPYIP) is a welfare scheme initiated by the Government of Madhya Pradesh. 

The scheme aims to promote environmental awareness among the youth. 

It encourages students to participate in environmental conservation activities. 

The benefits include financial assistance for students undertaking environmental projects. 

Eligibility criteria include being a student of a recognized educational institution in Madhya Pradesh. 

Students must also be engaged in projects related to environmental conservation. 

The application process involves submitting a proposal for the environmental project to the concerned authorities. 

The timeline for project completion and fund disbursement is specified in the application guidelines. 

The scheme is implemented by the Department of Environment and Climate Change, Government of Madhya Pradesh.
