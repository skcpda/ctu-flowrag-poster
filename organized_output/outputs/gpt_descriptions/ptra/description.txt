The Pradhan Mantri Tractors Scheme (PMTS) is an initiative by the Government of India. 

It aims to provide financial assistance for the purchase of tractors to enhance agricultural productivity. 

The objective is to support small and marginal farmers in mechanizing their farming operations. 

The scheme offers subsidies on the purchase of tractors. 

Farmers can receive financial assistance to reduce the burden of purchasing tractors. 

Eligibility is generally limited to small and marginal farmers. 

The specific criteria may vary by state. 

Farmers must provide proof of land ownership and income. 

The application process typically involves submitting an application form to the local agricultural department. 

Farmers may need to provide documentation such as identity proof and land records. 

The timeline for approval may vary depending on the state and local authorities. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers' Welfare. 

State governments may also have a role in the implementation and disbursement of funds. 

Additional details about the scheme can be found on official government websites.
