The Natthe scheme is an initiative by the Government of India aimed at promoting the welfare of rural communities. 

The primary objective of the Natthe scheme is to enhance the livelihood opportunities for rural households. 

The scheme provides financial assistance and support for various agricultural and non-agricultural activities. 

Eligibility for the Natthe scheme typically includes rural households engaged in farming or related activities. 

The application process generally involves submitting an application form to the local implementing authority. 

The timeline for assistance under the Natthe scheme may vary based on the specific activity and location. 

The implementing authorities for the Natthe scheme include local government bodies and agricultural departments.
