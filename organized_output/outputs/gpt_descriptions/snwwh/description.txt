The scheme is known as the National Scheme for Welfare of Women in Agriculture (NSWAWA). 

It was launched to address the challenges faced by women in the agricultural sector. 

The primary objective is to empower women in agriculture through various support measures. 

The scheme aims to enhance their livelihood opportunities and improve their socio-economic status. 

Benefits include financial assistance for skill development and training programs. 

Women farmers can receive support for self-help groups and cooperative societies. 

Eligibility is primarily for women engaged in agricultural activities. 

The application process involves submitting a proposal to the relevant agricultural department. 

The timeline for assistance may vary based on the specific program under the scheme. 

The Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers' Welfare is the implementing authority. 

Additional details may include awareness programs and capacity-building initiatives.
