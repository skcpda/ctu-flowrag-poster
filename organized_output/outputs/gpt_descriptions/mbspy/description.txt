The Maternity Benefit Program for Small and Marginal Farmers (MBSPY) aims to provide financial assistance to pregnant women and lactating mothers. 

The objective of the scheme is to improve maternal health and nutrition among women in the agricultural sector. 

The scheme offers a cash incentive to eligible beneficiaries during pregnancy and after childbirth. 

Eligible beneficiaries include pregnant women and lactating mothers who are small and marginal farmers. 

The application process involves submitting a form along with necessary documents to the local agricultural office. 

The timeline for receiving benefits is typically during the pregnancy period and extends into the postpartum phase. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers' Welfare. 

Additional details about the specific amount of assistance and the duration of benefits may vary.
