The Mahatma Gandhi National Rural Employment Guarantee Act scheme provides a legal guarantee for at least 100 days of unskilled wage employment in a financial year to every rural household. 

The objective of the scheme is to enhance livelihood security in rural areas. 

It aims to provide a safety net for the rural poor by offering employment opportunities. 

The benefits include guaranteed wage employment and the creation of durable assets in rural areas. 

Eligible beneficiaries are adult members of rural households who volunteer for unskilled manual work. 

The application process involves submitting a job card application to the local Gram Panchayat. 

The scheme operates throughout the year. 

The Ministry of Rural Development is the implementing authority for this scheme. 

The wages are determined by the state government and are paid directly to the beneficiaries' bank accounts. 

The scheme also promotes social inclusion by providing work to marginalized sections of society. 

It has provisions for grievance redressal to address issues faced by beneficiaries.
