The Janani Suraksha Yojana is a government welfare scheme launched by the Government of India. 

It was initiated in the year 2005 under the National Health Mission. 

The primary aim of the scheme is to promote institutional delivery among pregnant women. 

The scheme seeks to reduce maternal and infant mortality rates in the country. 

It focuses particularly on the states with low rates of institutional deliveries. 

The Janani Suraksha Yojana is part of the broader efforts to improve maternal health in India. 

The scheme provides financial assistance to pregnant women who choose to deliver in a health facility. 

This financial support is intended to encourage women to seek professional medical help during childbirth. 

The objectives of the Janani Suraksha Yojana include increasing the number of institutional deliveries. 

It aims to ensure that all pregnant women receive adequate medical care during pregnancy and childbirth. 

The scheme also seeks to promote safe motherhood practices. 

It aims to enhance the accessibility of healthcare services for women in rural and underserved areas. 

The benefits of the Janani Suraksha Yojana include cash assistance for pregnant women. 

Women who deliver in a government health facility can receive a financial incentive. 

The amount of financial assistance varies based on the type of delivery. 

For normal deliveries in government hospitals, the assistance is typically around INR 5,000. 

For cesarean deliveries, the amount can be higher, often around INR 7,000. 

This financial support helps cover transportation costs and other expenses related to childbirth. 

The assistance is provided to women from below-poverty-line households. 

Women from marginalized communities are also prioritized under this scheme. 

Eligibility for the Janani Suraksha Yojana includes being a resident of India. 

The woman must be pregnant and must choose to deliver in an accredited health facility. 

The scheme primarily targets women from economically disadvantaged backgrounds. 

It is designed to assist women who are either first-time mothers or have had multiple pregnancies. 

The application process for the Janani Suraksha Yojana is straightforward. 

Eligible women must register at the health facility where they plan to deliver. 

They need to provide necessary documentation to prove their eligibility. 

This includes proof of pregnancy and identification documents. 

Health workers often assist women in the registration process. 

They provide guidance on how to access the benefits of the scheme. 

Once registered, women are informed about the financial assistance they will receive. 

The timeline for receiving benefits under the Janani Suraksha Yojana is typically immediate. 

The cash assistance is usually disbursed shortly after the delivery. 

In many cases, the amount is transferred directly to the bank account of the beneficiary. 

The frequency of the benefits is linked to each individual delivery. 

Women can avail of the benefits for each pregnancy. 

The Janani Suraksha Yojana is implemented by various authorities at different levels. 

The Ministry of Health and Family Welfare is the primary governing body for the scheme. 

State governments are responsible for executing the scheme at the local level. 

District health authorities play a crucial role in the implementation process. 

They ensure that the scheme reaches the intended beneficiaries. 

Health workers and community health volunteers are also involved in promoting the scheme. 

They help in educating women about the importance of institutional deliveries. 

The Janani Suraksha Yojana also collaborates with various NGOs and community organizations. 

These partnerships help in reaching out to women in remote areas. 

The scheme is monitored regularly to assess its effectiveness and reach. 

Feedback from beneficiaries is collected to improve the services offered.
