The SASSCDC scheme stands for the Scheme for Assistance to Scheduled Castes Students for Degree Courses. 

It aims to provide financial assistance to students belonging to Scheduled Castes pursuing degree courses. 

The primary objective is to promote higher education among Scheduled Castes by alleviating financial barriers. 

Benefits include scholarships covering tuition fees and other educational expenses. 

Eligible candidates must belong to Scheduled Castes and be enrolled in recognized degree courses. 

The application process typically involves submitting an application form along with necessary documents to the concerned authority. 

The timeline for application submission and disbursement of funds may vary based on the academic calendar. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment. 

Additional details regarding specific eligibility criteria and application deadlines may be available on official government websites.
