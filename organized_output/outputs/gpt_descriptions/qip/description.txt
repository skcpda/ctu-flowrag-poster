The Quality Improvement Programme (QIP) is an initiative aimed at enhancing the quality of technical education in India. 

The primary objective of QIP is to improve the standards of teaching and research in engineering and technology institutions. 

The scheme provides financial assistance for various activities such as faculty development, research projects, and infrastructure improvement. 

Eligible beneficiaries include faculty members of engineering colleges and polytechnics recognized by the All India Council for Technical Education (AICTE). 

The application process typically involves submitting a proposal to the implementing agency along with necessary documentation. 

The timeline for approval and disbursement of funds may vary based on the specific project or activity proposed. 

The implementing authority for the QIP is the Ministry of Education, Government of India, in collaboration with AICTE. 

The programme also emphasizes the importance of collaboration with industry to enhance practical exposure for students. 

QIP aims to foster innovation and research in technical education to meet the evolving needs of the economy.
