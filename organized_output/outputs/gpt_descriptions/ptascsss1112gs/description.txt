The scheme PTASCSSS1112GS is the Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana - Gramin.

It aims to provide housing for the rural poor in India.

The objective is to provide a pucca house with basic amenities to all rural households.

Beneficiaries receive financial assistance to construct their houses.

The assistance is provided in the form of a subsidy.

Eligibility is primarily for families belonging to the Below Poverty Line category.

The scheme targets households without a permanent house.

The application process involves submitting an application through the Gram Panchayat.

The applications are verified by the local authorities.

The timeline for disbursement of funds varies based on the completion of the verification process.

The Ministry of Rural Development is the implementing authority for this scheme.

The scheme is part of the larger initiative to improve housing conditions in rural areas.
