The National Mission for Sustainable Agriculture Pradhan Mantri Krishi Vikas Yojana (NMSA-PKVY) is an initiative by the Government of India. 

It aims to promote sustainable agriculture practices. 

The mission focuses on enhancing productivity while ensuring environmental sustainability. 

The objectives include improving soil health and productivity. 

It also aims to promote organic farming and reduce the reliance on chemical fertilizers. 

The benefits include financial assistance for farmers adopting sustainable practices. 

Farmers receive support for organic farming inputs and training. 

They also gain access to technology and information on sustainable practices. 

Eligibility is primarily for farmers engaged in agricultural activities. 

The application process involves registration through state agricultural departments. 

Farmers may need to provide documentation proving their agricultural status. 

The timeline for assistance varies based on the specific program under the mission. 

Implementing authorities include the Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers' Welfare. 

State governments also play a crucial role in execution and monitoring. 

The scheme promotes collaboration with various agricultural universities and research institutions. 

It encourages the use of traditional knowledge in farming practices. 

The mission aligns with the broader goals of enhancing food security and farmer welfare.
