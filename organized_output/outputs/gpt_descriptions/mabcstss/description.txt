The MABCSTSS stands for the "Mahatma Gandhi National Rural Employment Guarantee Act (MGNREGA) - Aajeevika and Skill Development Scheme."

This scheme is part of the broader initiative to enhance livelihood security in rural areas of India.

It aims to provide at least 100 days of guaranteed wage employment in a financial year to every rural household.

The primary objective of the MABCSTSS is to enhance the livelihood security of rural households.

The scheme also focuses on skill development and promoting self-employment opportunities.

Benefits under this scheme include wage employment for rural households.

The scheme provides a legal guarantee for at least 100 days of unskilled wage employment in a financial year.

The wage rate is determined by the state government and is revised periodically.

In addition to wage employment, the scheme promotes skill development through various training programs.

These training programs are designed to enhance the employability of rural youth.

Eligibility for the MABCSTSS is primarily based on residency in rural areas.

Any adult member of a rural household can apply for employment under this scheme.

The scheme is open to all rural households without any discrimination.

The application process involves submitting a demand for work to the local Gram Panchayat.

The Gram Panchayat is responsible for issuing a job card to eligible households.

Once a job card is issued, households can demand work as per their requirement.

The timeline for the application process is immediate upon demand for work.

The Gram Panchayat must provide employment within 15 days of the application.

If employment is not provided within this period, the household is entitled to an unemployment allowance.

The implementing authorities for the MABCSTSS include the Ministry of Rural Development.

State governments also play a crucial role in the implementation of this scheme.

They are responsible for the execution of the program at the state and local levels.

The scheme is monitored through a decentralized structure involving local bodies.

The MABCSTSS also aims to create durable assets in rural areas.

These assets include irrigation facilities, water conservation structures, and rural connectivity.

The scheme encourages the participation of women and marginalized communities.

It aims to empower women by providing them with equal opportunities for employment.

The MABCSTSS also emphasizes sustainable development through eco-friendly practices.

The scheme is linked with other government initiatives for holistic development.

It aims to reduce rural poverty and enhance the quality of life in rural areas.

The MABCSTSS is a demand-driven scheme.

This means that employment is provided based on the demand from rural households.

The scheme also includes provisions for grievance redressal.

This ensures that beneficiaries can report issues and seek resolutions.

The MABCSTSS has been instrumental in providing social security to rural households.

It has contributed to the economic empowerment of rural communities.

The scheme also promotes transparency and accountability in the implementation process.

Regular audits and evaluations are conducted to assess the effectiveness of the scheme.

The MABCSTSS is part of a larger framework of rural development programs in India.

It works in conjunction with other schemes aimed at poverty alleviation.

The scheme has been successful in creating a safety net for rural workers.

It has also played a significant role in reducing seasonal migration for work.

The MABCSTSS is funded by the central government and state governments.

The funding is shared between the two levels of government.

The scheme has provisions for skill training in various sectors.

These sectors include agriculture, handicrafts, and small-scale industries.

The training programs are designed to meet local employment needs.
