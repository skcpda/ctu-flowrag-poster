The scheme SFASISD stands for the Scheme for Financial Assistance for the Skill Development of Scheduled Castes. 

It aims to provide financial assistance for skill development programs specifically for Scheduled Caste individuals. 

The objectives include enhancing the employability of Scheduled Caste youth and promoting entrepreneurship among them. 

Benefits include financial support for skill development training programs and assistance for setting up small enterprises. 

Eligibility is primarily limited to individuals belonging to Scheduled Castes. 

The application process typically involves submitting an application form along with necessary documents to the designated authority. 

The timeline for the disbursement of funds and the frequency of assistance may vary based on the specific training program. 

The implementing authorities include the Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment and state governments. 

Additional details may include the types of skills covered under the scheme and the specific training providers recognized for the programs.
