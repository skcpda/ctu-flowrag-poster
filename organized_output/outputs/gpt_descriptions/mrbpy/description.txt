The Mukhyamantri Raigarh BPL Yojana (MRBPY) is a welfare scheme initiated by the Government of Chhattisgarh. 

The scheme aims to provide financial assistance to families living below the poverty line in the Raigarh district. 

The objective is to improve the living standards of economically disadvantaged families. 

Under this scheme eligible beneficiaries receive direct financial support. 

This assistance can be used for various needs such as education health and livelihood. 

Eligibility is primarily based on the family's economic status as determined by the Below Poverty Line criteria. 

The application process typically involves submitting an application form along with necessary documents to the local authorities. 

The scheme is implemented by the Department of Social Welfare in Chhattisgarh. 

Specific timelines for application submission and fund disbursement may vary. 

Updates and further details can be accessed through official government portals.
