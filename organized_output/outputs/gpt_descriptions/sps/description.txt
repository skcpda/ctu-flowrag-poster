The Scheme for Promotion of Skill Development in the Service Sector (SPS) aims to enhance the employability of youth in the service sector. 

The objective is to provide skill development training to individuals to meet the demands of the service industry. 

The scheme offers financial assistance for training programs and skill development initiatives. 

Benefits include improved job prospects and enhanced skills for participants. 

Eligibility criteria include individuals aged 15 years and above who are seeking skill development in the service sector. 

The application process involves submitting an application through designated training centers or online portals. 

The timeline for the training programs varies based on the specific courses offered. 

Implementing authorities include the Ministry of Skill Development and Entrepreneurship and affiliated training institutions. 

The scheme also focuses on creating partnerships with industry stakeholders to ensure relevant training.
