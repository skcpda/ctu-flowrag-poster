The PRISM-C1-PCPM scheme is an initiative aimed at improving the efficiency of public distribution systems in India. 

The objective of the scheme is to enhance the transparency and accountability of the public distribution system. 

It seeks to streamline the process of distribution of essential commodities to beneficiaries. 

The benefits include better tracking of food grains and other essential items. 

It also aims to reduce leakages and ensure that the intended beneficiaries receive their entitlements. 

Eligibility is typically based on being a registered beneficiary under the public distribution system. 

The application process involves registration through the local rationing office or online portals. 

The timeline for implementation varies by state and is dependent on local administrative processes. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Consumer Affairs Food and Public Distribution. 

State governments also play a crucial role in the execution of the scheme.
