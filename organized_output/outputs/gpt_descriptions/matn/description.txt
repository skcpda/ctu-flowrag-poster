The "Matru Vandana Yojana" is a government welfare scheme in India. 

It was launched to provide financial assistance to pregnant and lactating women. 

The scheme aims to improve maternal and child health. 

It encourages women to seek healthcare during pregnancy and after childbirth. 

The primary objective is to reduce maternal and infant mortality rates. 

The scheme also aims to promote institutional deliveries. 

Under the Matru Vandana Yojana, eligible women receive a cash incentive. 

The cash benefit is provided in three installments. 

The total financial assistance amounts to INR 5,000. 

The first installment of INR 1,000 is provided at the time of registration of pregnancy. 

The second installment of INR 2,000 is given after six months of pregnancy. 

The final installment of INR 2,000 is provided after the birth of the child. 

The assistance can be used for nutritional support and healthcare needs. 

Eligibility for the scheme includes pregnant women and lactating mothers. 

Women must be at least 18 years old to qualify. 

The scheme is applicable for the first living child only. 

Women who are already receiving benefits under other schemes are not eligible.

Women must provide necessary documents such as identity proof and pregnancy registration.
