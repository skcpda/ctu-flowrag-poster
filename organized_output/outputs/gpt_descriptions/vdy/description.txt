The VDY scheme stands for the Vikas Yojana for Development of Youth. 

It aims to empower the youth of India through various skill development and employment generation initiatives. 

The primary objective is to enhance the employability of young individuals by providing them with necessary skills and training. 

The scheme offers various benefits including vocational training programs and financial assistance for starting small enterprises. 

Eligibility criteria typically include age limits for applicants usually between 15 to 29 years. 

The application process generally involves filling out an online or offline application form and submitting required documents. 

The timeline for the implementation of training programs may vary based on the specific training module selected by the applicant. 

The Ministry of Skill Development and Entrepreneurship is usually the implementing authority for this scheme. 

Additional details may include partnerships with various training institutes and organizations to facilitate skill development.
