The WBISCU scheme stands for West Bengal Integrated Scheme for Child Development. 

It is implemented by the Government of West Bengal. 

The scheme aims to provide comprehensive development for children under the age of six years. 

The objectives include improving nutrition and health status of children. 

It also focuses on enhancing early childhood education and development. 

The benefits include supplementary nutrition for children and pregnant women. 

It provides health check-ups and immunization services. 

Financial assistance is offered for the establishment of Anganwadi centers. 

Eligibility criteria include children aged 0 to 6 years and pregnant or lactating mothers. 

The application process typically involves registration at local Anganwadi centers. 

The scheme operates continuously throughout the year. 

The implementing authority is the Department of Women and Child Development and Social Welfare, Government of West Bengal. 

Additional details may include community participation in the implementation of the scheme.
