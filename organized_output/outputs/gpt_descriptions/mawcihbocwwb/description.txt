The scheme "Mahatma Gandhi National Rural Employment Guarantee Act" is often referred to as MGNREGA. 

It was enacted in 2005 to enhance livelihood security in rural areas. 

The primary objective of MGNREGA is to provide at least 100 days of unskilled wage employment in a financial year to every rural household. 

This scheme aims to improve the purchasing power of rural households. 

It also seeks to create durable assets in rural areas. 

The benefits of MGNREGA include guaranteed wage employment. 

The scheme provides a legal guarantee for at least 100 days of work in a financial year. 

Wages are paid according to the minimum wage set by the state government. 

The assistance is provided in the form of wage payments for the work done. 

The scheme also promotes social inclusion by providing employment to marginalized groups. 

Eligibility for MGNREGA includes all rural households. 

Every adult member of a rural household can apply for work under the scheme. 

There is no discrimination based on caste or gender. 

The application process involves submitting a job card application to the local Gram Panchayat. 

Once the application is approved, a job card is issued to the household. 

The job card contains details of the household and the number of days of work provided. 

The household can then apply for work as per their requirement. 

The timeline for the provision of work is within 15 days of the application. 

If work is not provided within this period, the household is entitled to unemployment allowance. 

The frequency of work can vary based on demand and availability of projects. 

Implementing authorities include the Ministry of Rural Development at the central level. 

State governments are responsible for the implementation at the state level. 

Gram Panchayats play a crucial role in planning and executing projects. 

MGNREGA is funded by the central government and state governments in a ratio of 75:25. 

The scheme also emphasizes the creation of sustainable assets like water conservation structures. 

It aims to enhance the livelihood security of rural households. 

MGNREGA has provisions for social audits to ensure transparency and accountability. 

The scheme has a provision for the participation of women in employment. 

At least one-third of the total beneficiaries are to be women.
