The SMA scheme refers to the Scheme for the Management of Adverse Events Following Immunization. 

It was launched to address and manage any adverse events that may occur after immunization. 

The objective of the scheme is to ensure prompt and effective management of such events to maintain public confidence in vaccination programs. 

The benefits include financial assistance for the treatment of adverse events and compensation for the affected individuals. 

Eligibility is generally determined based on the occurrence of an adverse event following immunization. 

The application process involves reporting the adverse event to the health authorities and submitting necessary documentation for assistance. 

The timeline for assistance may vary based on the severity of the event and the processing of claims. 

The implementing authority for the SMA scheme is the Ministry of Health and Family Welfare. 

The scheme aims to enhance the safety and efficacy of immunization programs across India.
