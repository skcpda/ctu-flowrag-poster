The Scheme for Providing Assistance to Families of Scheduled Castes for Purchase of Tools and Equipment (SPAFSG) aims to support Scheduled Caste families. 

The objective of the scheme is to provide financial assistance for the purchase of tools and equipment. 

This assistance helps improve the livelihoods of Scheduled Caste families engaged in traditional occupations. 

Eligible beneficiaries include families belonging to Scheduled Castes. 

The financial assistance is provided for purchasing tools and equipment necessary for their respective trades. 

The application process typically involves submitting an application to the concerned department. 

The timeline for disbursement of funds may vary based on the processing of applications. 

The implementing authority for this scheme is the Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment. 

Additional details regarding specific amounts of assistance or the types of tools and equipment covered may vary by state.
