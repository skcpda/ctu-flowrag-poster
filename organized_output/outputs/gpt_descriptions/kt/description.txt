The Kisan Samman Nidhi (Kisan Nidhi) scheme is a government initiative aimed at providing financial support to farmers in India. 

The scheme was launched by the Government of India in February 2019. 

It was introduced in the context of the increasing financial distress faced by farmers across the country. 

The primary objective of the scheme is to provide direct income support to small and marginal farmers. 

The scheme aims to enhance the income of farmers and ensure food security in the country. 

Under the Kisan Samman Nidhi scheme, eligible farmers receive an amount of Rs. 6000 per year. 

This amount is disbursed in three equal installments of Rs. 2000 each. 

The assistance is intended to help farmers meet their agricultural expenses and improve their livelihoods. 

The scheme covers all small and marginal farmers who own cultivable land. 

Eligibility is determined based on the ownership of land. 

Farmers with landholdings of up to 2 hectares are eligible for the scheme. 

The scheme is applicable to farmers across all states and Union Territories in India. 

However, certain categories of farmers are excluded from the scheme. 

These include institutional landholders and farmers who are ineligible under the scheme's guidelines. 

The application process for the Kisan Samman Nidhi scheme is straightforward. 

Farmers need to register on the official PM-KISAN portal. 

They must provide necessary details such as their land ownership and bank account information. 

The registration can also be done through Common Service Centers (CSCs) across the country. 

Once registered, the details are verified by the respective state government authorities. 

The financial assistance is credited directly to the bank accounts of the beneficiaries. 

The scheme is implemented in a phased manner. 

The first installment is typically disbursed in April. 

Subsequent installments are released in August and December. 

The frequency of payments is quarterly. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers' Welfare. 

State governments play a crucial role in the identification and verification of beneficiaries.
