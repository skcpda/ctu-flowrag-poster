The Credit Linked Subsidy Scheme for Middle Income Group is commonly referred to as CLSS MIG. 

This scheme is part of the broader Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana initiative. 

The Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana aims to provide affordable housing to the urban poor. 

The CLSS MIG component specifically targets the middle-income group. 

The scheme was launched in June 2015. 

It was introduced to promote home ownership among the middle-income population. 

The scheme provides financial assistance to eligible beneficiaries in the form of interest subsidy on housing loans. 

The primary objective of CLSS MIG is to make housing affordable for the middle-income group. 

The scheme aims to bridge the gap between the income levels and the rising cost of housing. 

It seeks to encourage the middle-income group to invest in housing. 

The CLSS MIG offers an interest subsidy of up to 3 percent on home loans. 

This subsidy is applicable for loans taken for the purchase or construction of a house. 

The maximum loan amount eligible for the subsidy is up to Rs. 2 crore. 

The interest subsidy is available for a tenure of 20 years or the loan tenure whichever is lower. 

The scheme is applicable for two income categories. 

The first category is for individuals with an annual income of Rs. 6 lakh to Rs. 12 lakh. 

The second category is for individuals with an annual income of Rs. 12 lakh to Rs. 18 lakh. 

For the first category the interest subsidy is 3 percent. 

For the second category the interest subsidy is 2 percent. 

The scheme is designed to support both first-time home buyers and those looking to upgrade their housing. 

The benefits of the CLSS MIG scheme are significant. 

The interest subsidy directly reduces the monthly EMI burden on the beneficiaries. 

This makes home loans more affordable for the middle-income group. 

The scheme also promotes the construction of new houses. 

This contributes to the overall development of urban infrastructure. 

The eligibility criteria for the CLSS MIG scheme are clearly defined. 

Applicants must be citizens of India. 

They should belong to the middle-income group as defined by the income limits. 

The applicant must not own a pucca house in their name or in the name of any family member. 

The family is defined as the applicant spouse and children. 

The applicant must be at least 18 years old at the time of applying. 

The application process for the CLSS MIG scheme is straightforward. 

Interested applicants can apply through various channels. 

They can approach banks or housing finance companies. 

They can also apply through the online portal of the Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana. 

The application process involves filling out a form with necessary details. 

Applicants need to provide proof of income and identity. 

They must also submit documents related to the property they wish to purchase or construct. 

Once the application is submitted it is reviewed by the lending institution. 

The lending institution verifies the details and assesses eligibility. 

Upon approval the subsidy amount is credited to the lending institution. 

This reduces the effective interest rate for the beneficiary. 

The timeline for processing applications can vary. 

Typically it takes a few weeks for the verification and approval process. 

The frequency of the subsidy is linked to the loan repayment schedule. 

The implementing authorities for the CLSS MIG scheme include the Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs. 

The scheme is executed through various financial institutions.
