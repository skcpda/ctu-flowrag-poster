The PSEDawa scheme is an initiative by the Government of India. 

It aims to provide financial assistance to individuals affected by the COVID-19 pandemic. 

The objective is to support the livelihood of those who have lost their jobs or faced income reduction due to the pandemic. 

Eligible beneficiaries include daily wage workers and individuals from economically weaker sections. 

The application process typically involves submitting an online application through designated portals. 

The assistance is provided in the form of direct cash transfers to the beneficiaries' bank accounts. 

The frequency of assistance may vary based on government directives. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Labour and Employment. 

Additional details regarding the specific amount of assistance and duration may vary based on state regulations.
