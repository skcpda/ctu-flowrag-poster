The Peacetp scheme is an initiative aimed at promoting peace and social harmony in conflict-affected areas of India.

The primary objective of the scheme is to foster community engagement and reconciliation among diverse groups.

The benefits include financial assistance for community projects and initiatives that promote peace and stability.

Eligibility criteria typically include community-based organizations and local bodies in conflict-affected regions.

The application process involves submitting a proposal detailing the project objectives and expected outcomes.

The timeline for project implementation varies based on the specific initiative approved under the scheme.

The Ministry of Home Affairs is the implementing authority for the Peacetp scheme.

Additional details regarding specific funding amounts or project types may be available through official government channels.
