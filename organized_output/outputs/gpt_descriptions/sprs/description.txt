The Scheme for Promotion of Rural Sports (SPRS) aims to promote sports in rural areas of India. 

The objective is to identify and nurture sports talent in rural regions. 

The scheme provides financial assistance for organizing sports competitions and training programs. 

It also aims to develop sports infrastructure in rural areas. 

Eligibility criteria include individuals and organizations involved in promoting sports at the grassroots level. 

The application process involves submitting proposals to the concerned sports authorities at the state or central level. 

The timeline for funding and support is determined based on the project proposals submitted. 

The Ministry of Youth Affairs and Sports is the implementing authority for this scheme. 

Additional details may include specific guidelines for funding and types of sports supported under the scheme.
