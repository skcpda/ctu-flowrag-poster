The Rural Livelihoods Mission (RLM) is a government initiative aimed at promoting self-employment and organization of rural poor into self-help groups. 

The scheme seeks to reduce poverty by promoting sustainable livelihoods for rural households. 

It aims to enhance the income of rural households through skill development and capacity building. 

The mission provides financial assistance and support for various livelihood activities. 

Eligible beneficiaries include rural poor households who are below the poverty line. 

The application process typically involves registration with the local self-help group or through the designated state rural livelihood mission office. 

The scheme operates continuously with ongoing support and interventions. 

The Ministry of Rural Development is the primary implementing authority for this mission. 

State rural livelihood missions also play a crucial role in the implementation at the state level. 

The scheme emphasizes community participation and empowerment of women through self-help groups. 

It encourages the development of sustainable livelihoods through skill training and access to financial services. 

The mission also focuses on enhancing the capacity of rural institutions to support livelihoods. 

The program is aligned with the broader goals of the National Rural Livelihoods Mission.
