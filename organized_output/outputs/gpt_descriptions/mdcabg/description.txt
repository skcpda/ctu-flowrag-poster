The Mukhyamantri Dastakari Chhatra Abhiyan (MDCABG) is an initiative aimed at promoting entrepreneurship among students in Madhya Pradesh. 

The objective of the scheme is to provide financial assistance and support to students who wish to start their own businesses. 

The benefits include access to financial aid for setting up businesses and training programs to enhance entrepreneurial skills. 

Eligibility criteria include being a student enrolled in a recognized educational institution in Madhya Pradesh. 

The application process involves submitting an online application through the official government portal. 

The timeline for application submission varies based on the academic calendar and announcements by the government. 

The scheme is implemented by the Department of Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises in Madhya Pradesh. 

Additional details may include workshops and mentorship programs to guide students in their entrepreneurial journey.
