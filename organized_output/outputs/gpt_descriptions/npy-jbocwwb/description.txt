The NPY-JBOCWWB scheme is aimed at providing financial assistance to the families of construction workers in India. 

The objective of the scheme is to support the welfare of construction workers and their dependents. 

The benefits include financial assistance for education health and housing for the families of registered construction workers. 

Eligibility is limited to families of registered construction workers under the Building and Other Construction Workers Act. 

The application process involves registration with the appropriate labor department and submission of necessary documents. 

The timeline for assistance is typically determined by the specific needs of the beneficiaries and the processing time of applications. 

The scheme is implemented by the respective state governments through their labor departments.
