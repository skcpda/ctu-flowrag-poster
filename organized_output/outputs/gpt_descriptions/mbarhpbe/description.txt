The scheme referred to as mbarhpbe is the "Mahatma Gandhi National Rural Employment Guarantee Act" or MGNREGA.

MGNREGA was enacted in 2005 to enhance livelihood security in rural areas of India.

The primary objective of MGNREGA is to provide at least 100 days of guaranteed wage employment in a financial year to every rural household.

The scheme aims to create durable assets in rural areas while also enhancing the livelihood security of the rural population.

Under MGNREGA, the government provides financial assistance to rural households for unskilled manual work.

The benefits include the provision of minimum wages for the work done by individuals under the scheme.

The scheme also promotes social inclusion by providing employment to marginalized and disadvantaged groups.

Eligibility for MGNREGA includes all rural households.

Any adult member of a rural household can apply for work under the scheme.

The application process involves submitting a job card application to the local Gram Panchayat.

Once the application is approved, a job card is issued to the household.

The job card serves as proof of registration and entitles the household to demand work.

The demand for work can be made at the Gram Panchayat office.

Once the demand is made, the local authorities are required to provide employment within 15 days.

If employment is not provided within this timeframe, the beneficiaries are entitled to an unemployment allowance.

The scheme operates on a continuous basis throughout the year.

Payments for the work done are made directly to the bank accounts of the beneficiaries.

The implementing authorities for MGNREGA include the Ministry of Rural Development at the central level.

State governments are responsible for the implementation of the scheme at the state level.

The Gram Panchayat plays a crucial role in the execution of the scheme at the local level.

MGNREGA also emphasizes the creation of sustainable assets such as water conservation structures and rural infrastructure.

The scheme has provisions for the participation of women and encourages their involvement in the workforce.

At least one-third of the total beneficiaries under the scheme are required to be women.

The scheme also includes provisions for skill development and training of workers.
