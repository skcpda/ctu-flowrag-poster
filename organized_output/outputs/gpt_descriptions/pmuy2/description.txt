Pradhan Mantri Ujjwala Yojana 2.0 (PMUY 2.0) is an extension of the original PMUY scheme launched in 2016. 

The scheme aims to provide clean cooking fuel to households in India. 

It focuses on improving the health and well-being of women and children. 

The objective is to reduce the use of traditional fuels like firewood and coal. 

Benefits include the provision of LPG connections to eligible households. 

Financial assistance is provided for the initial cost of the LPG connection. 

Eligibility is primarily for women from Below Poverty Line (BPL) households. 

Priority is given to those who have not previously received an LPG connection. 

The application process involves submitting a form to the designated authorities. 

Documents required include proof of identity and BPL status. 

The timeline for receiving the LPG connection may vary based on processing time. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Petroleum and Natural Gas. 

Public Sector Oil Marketing Companies are responsible for the distribution of LPG connections. 

PMUY 2.0 also aims to provide additional benefits such as skill development and financial literacy. 

The scheme is part of the government's broader initiative to promote clean energy. 

It contributes to environmental sustainability by reducing carbon emissions. 

The scheme is expected to enhance the quality of life for millions of households.
