The Scheme for Implementation of Cyber Security Measures in Telecom Sector is abbreviated as SICM TCPD. 

This scheme is initiated by the Ministry of Communications. 

It aims to enhance the security of telecommunications networks in India. 

The objective is to protect the telecom infrastructure from cyber threats. 

The scheme provides financial assistance for implementing cyber security measures. 

Benefits include improved security protocols and reduced risk of cyber attacks. 

Eligibility criteria include telecom service providers and infrastructure providers. 

The application process involves submitting a proposal to the Department of Telecommunications. 

The timeline for implementation varies based on the specific project approved. 

The Department of Telecommunications is the implementing authority for this scheme. 

The scheme also emphasizes the need for regular audits and assessments of security measures. 

It encourages collaboration with cybersecurity agencies and experts. 

The scheme is part of the larger effort to secure critical infrastructure in India.
