The RRS-DMS-MECPU scheme is designed to support the development of micro enterprises in rural areas. 

It aims to enhance the income and employment opportunities for rural youth. 

The scheme provides financial assistance for setting up new micro enterprises. 

It also offers skill development training to beneficiaries. 

Eligibility is primarily focused on rural youth who wish to start a micro enterprise. 

The application process involves submitting a proposal to the concerned department. 

The timeline for processing applications may vary based on the specific requirements of the proposal. 

The implementing authority for this scheme is the Ministry of Rural Development. 

Additional details about the scheme can be found on the official government website.
