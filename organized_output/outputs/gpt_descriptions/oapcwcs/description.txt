The Old Age Pension for Widows and Childless Women Scheme (OAPCWCS) is designed to provide financial assistance to vulnerable groups in society. 

The objective of the scheme is to support widows and childless women who are aged 60 years and above. 

The scheme aims to ensure a basic level of financial security for these women. 

Beneficiaries receive a monthly pension to help meet their basic needs. 

Eligibility for the scheme includes being a widow or childless woman aged 60 years or older. 

Applicants must also meet specific income criteria set by the state government. 

The application process typically involves submitting an application form along with required documents to the local government office. 

The pension is disbursed monthly to the beneficiaries. 

The scheme is implemented by the respective state governments under the Ministry of Rural Development. 

Additional details regarding the exact amount of pension and specific state variations may apply.
