The SDTK scheme stands for the Skill Development Training for Knowledge program. 

It aims to enhance the skill sets of youth in India. 

The objective is to provide training in various sectors to improve employability. 

Benefits include skill development training and job placement assistance. 

Eligibility criteria include youth aged between 15 to 29 years. 

Applicants must be residents of India. 

The application process typically involves online registration through designated portals. 

Training sessions are conducted periodically based on demand and availability. 

The implementing authorities include the Ministry of Skill Development and Entrepreneurship. 

Additional details may include partnerships with various training institutes and organizations.
