The Mahatma Gandhi Awas Yojana (MGAY) is a government welfare scheme aimed at providing housing for the rural poor in India. 

The objective of MGAY is to ensure that every rural household has access to a pucca house with basic amenities. 

The scheme provides financial assistance for the construction of houses. 

Beneficiaries receive a subsidy for building materials and labor costs. 

Eligibility is primarily based on being a member of a Below Poverty Line (BPL) household. 

The application process typically involves submitting an application to the local government office or through designated online portals. 

The timeline for the disbursement of funds varies but is generally processed within a few months of application approval. 

The Ministry of Rural Development is the implementing authority for this scheme. 

The scheme also aims to promote the use of eco-friendly and sustainable building materials. 

MGAY is part of the larger initiative to improve housing conditions and reduce homelessness in rural areas.
