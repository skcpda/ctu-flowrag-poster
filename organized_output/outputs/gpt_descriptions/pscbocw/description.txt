The PSCBOCW scheme stands for the Punjab State Construction and Other Workers Welfare Board. 

It was established to provide welfare benefits to construction workers and other workers in the unorganized sector in Punjab. 

The primary objective of the scheme is to improve the living standards of construction workers and their families. 

The benefits under the scheme include financial assistance for medical treatment, education, and housing. 

Workers can also receive assistance for the marriage of their daughters and for the death of a family member. 

Eligibility for the scheme includes being a registered construction worker or a worker in the unorganized sector in Punjab. 

Applicants must be above the age of 18 and should not be a member of any other welfare board. 

The application process involves registering with the Punjab State Construction and Other Workers Welfare Board. 

Workers need to submit necessary documents such as identity proof and proof of employment. 

The timeline for processing applications may vary but is generally completed within a few weeks. 

The implementing authority for this scheme is the Punjab State Construction and Other Workers Welfare Board. 

The scheme aims to ensure social security and welfare for vulnerable workers in the construction sector.
