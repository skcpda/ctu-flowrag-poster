The PSG BCDC scheme stands for the Pradhan Mantri Svanidhi Yojana for the Beneficiary of Street Vendors. 

It was launched to provide affordable credit to street vendors to help them resume their livelihoods that were affected due to the COVID-19 pandemic. 

The objective of the scheme is to facilitate street vendors in accessing loans for working capital. 

The scheme aims to empower street vendors by providing them with financial assistance to restart their businesses. 

Eligible beneficiaries include street vendors who have been engaged in vending in urban areas. 

The application process involves submitting an online application through the PM Svanidhi portal or through Common Service Centers. 

The loan amount can be up to INR 10,000 with a repayment period of one year. 

The scheme also provides for an interest subsidy of 7 percent per annum on timely repayment of loans. 

The implementing authority for this scheme is the Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs. 

The scheme is part of the larger initiative to promote the socio-economic development of street vendors. 

The loans under the scheme can be availed in a single tranche. 

The scheme is operational until March 2024.
