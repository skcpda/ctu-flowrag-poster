The Atmanirbhar Bharat Abhiyan is a government initiative aimed at promoting self-reliance in India. 

The scheme focuses on providing support to various sectors of the economy. 

It aims to empower small businesses and entrepreneurs. 

The objectives include enhancing the production capacity of local industries. 

It also seeks to create job opportunities and boost the economy. 

Benefits include financial assistance and subsidies for eligible businesses. 

The scheme supports skill development and capacity building. 

Eligibility criteria vary based on the specific components of the scheme. 

Small and medium enterprises are typically prioritized. 

The application process involves submitting proposals to the relevant government departments. 

Timelines for funding and assistance may vary based on project approval. 

The Ministry of Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises is the primary implementing authority. 

Other government agencies may also be involved in specific aspects of the scheme.
