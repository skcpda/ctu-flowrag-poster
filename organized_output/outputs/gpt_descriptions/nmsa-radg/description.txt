The National Mission for Sustainable Agriculture - Rainfed Area Development and Global (NMSA-RADG) is a government initiative aimed at enhancing agricultural productivity in rainfed areas. 

The objective of the scheme is to promote sustainable agriculture practices in rainfed regions. 

It seeks to improve the livelihoods of farmers by increasing the productivity of crops. 

The scheme provides financial assistance for various agricultural activities. 

Benefits include support for soil health management and water conservation techniques. 

Farmers can also receive assistance for crop diversification and integrated farming systems. 

Eligibility is primarily targeted at farmers engaged in agriculture in rainfed areas. 

The application process typically involves submission of relevant documents to local agricultural offices. 

The timeline for assistance may vary based on the specific activities and funding availability. 

The Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers' Welfare is the implementing authority for this scheme. 

State governments may also play a role in the implementation at the local level.
