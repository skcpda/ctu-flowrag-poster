The MBSUKBOCWWB scheme is the Mukhyamantri Bhatta Scheme for Unorganized Workers in Construction and Building Sector. 

It aims to provide financial assistance to workers in the unorganized sector specifically in construction and building. 

The objective is to support the livelihood of workers who often lack social security benefits. 

The scheme offers a monthly financial assistance of a specified amount to eligible beneficiaries. 

Eligibility criteria include being a resident of the respective state and being engaged in the construction or building sector. 

Applicants must also be registered under the unorganized workers' social security scheme. 

The application process typically involves filling out a form and submitting it to the designated authority. 

The timeline for disbursal of benefits is usually monthly. 

The implementing authority for this scheme is the respective state government. 

Additional details may include the requirement of identity proof and proof of employment in the construction sector.
