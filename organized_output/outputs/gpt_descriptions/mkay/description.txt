The MKAY scheme stands for Mukhyamantri Kanya Abhiyan Yojana. 

It is a welfare initiative launched by the Government of Madhya Pradesh. 

The scheme aims to improve the health and education of girls in the state. 

It focuses on reducing the dropout rate among girls in schools. 

The benefits include financial assistance for education and health services. 

The scheme provides incentives for families to educate their daughters. 

Eligibility is generally based on the socio-economic status of the family. 

Families with a low income are prioritized for assistance. 

The application process typically involves submitting forms to local government offices. 

Documentation may include proof of income and details of the girl child. 

The scheme is implemented by the Department of Women and Child Development. 

The timeline for assistance may vary based on the specific benefits availed. 

The scheme is part of a broader effort to empower women and promote gender equality. 

It aims to create a supportive environment for girls to thrive in education and health.
