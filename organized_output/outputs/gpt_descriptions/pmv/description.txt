The Pradhan Mantri Vaya Vandana Yojana (PMVVY) is a pension scheme launched by the Government of India. 

It aims to provide social security to senior citizens aged 60 years and above. 

The objective is to ensure a steady income for the elderly through a guaranteed pension. 

Under this scheme, senior citizens can invest a lump sum amount and receive a fixed monthly pension. 

The pension can be received monthly quarterly half-yearly or annually. 

The maximum investment limit is set at Rs. 15 lakh. 

The scheme offers a guaranteed return of 8 percent per annum. 

The benefits include a pension for a period of 10 years. 

The scheme also provides the option to return the purchase price to the nominee upon the death of the pensioner. 

Eligibility criteria include being a citizen of India and being aged 60 years or above. 

The application process involves filling out the application form available at authorized banks and financial institutions. 

The scheme is implemented by the Life Insurance Corporation of India (LIC). 

The scheme was launched on 21st May 2017. 

It is available for subscription until 31st March 2023. 

The pension payment frequency can be chosen by the subscriber at the time of investment. 

The scheme is designed to provide financial security to the elderly population in India.
