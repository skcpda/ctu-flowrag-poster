The Urban Employment Assurance Scheme (UEAS) is designed to provide guaranteed employment to urban residents. 

The scheme aims to enhance livelihood security in urban areas. 

It seeks to provide at least 100 days of wage employment in a financial year to every urban household. 

The benefits include financial assistance through wages for unskilled manual work. 

Eligible beneficiaries are urban households that are seeking employment. 

The application process typically involves registration through local municipal bodies or designated offices. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs. 

The timeline for employment provision is based on the demand for work and the availability of funds. 

Additional details may include specific guidelines on wage rates and types of work available.
