The Mukhyamantri Maatru Pushtahar Yojana (MMAMS) is a welfare scheme launched by the Government of Madhya Pradesh. 

The scheme aims to improve maternal and child nutrition in the state. 

It focuses on providing nutritional support to pregnant and lactating women. 

The scheme also targets children under the age of six years. 

Eligible beneficiaries receive financial assistance for purchasing nutritious food items. 

The assistance is provided in the form of direct cash transfers. 

Women and children are encouraged to consume a balanced diet. 

The scheme aims to reduce malnutrition and improve health outcomes. 

Eligibility is primarily for pregnant and lactating women and children under six. 

The application process involves registration at local Anganwadi centers. 

Beneficiaries must provide necessary documentation to confirm eligibility. 

The scheme is implemented by the Department of Women and Child Development, Madhya Pradesh. 

The frequency of assistance is monthly. 

The timeline for disbursement is aligned with the beneficiaries' nutritional needs. 

The scheme also includes awareness programs on nutrition and health. 

Monitoring and evaluation are conducted to assess the scheme's impact.
