The Mukhyamantri Maatru Pushtikar Yojana (MMVSKY) is a welfare scheme initiated by the Government of Madhya Pradesh. 

The scheme aims to improve maternal and child nutrition in the state. 

It focuses on providing nutritional support to pregnant and lactating women as well as children under the age of six. 

The benefits include the provision of nutritious food and supplements to eligible beneficiaries. 

Eligible beneficiaries are pregnant women and lactating mothers residing in Madhya Pradesh. 

Children under the age of six years are also eligible for assistance under this scheme. 

The application process involves registration at the local Anganwadi centers. 

Beneficiaries can receive assistance through these centers. 

The scheme is implemented by the Department of Women and Child Development, Madhya Pradesh. 

The frequency of assistance is typically monthly, based on the nutritional needs of the beneficiaries. 

The scheme also aims to create awareness about nutrition and health among the target population.
