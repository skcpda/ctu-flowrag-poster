The Skill Development Training for Minorities (SDTM) scheme aims to enhance the employability of minority communities in India. 

The objective of the scheme is to provide skill development training to individuals from minority communities. 

This training is intended to empower them with skills that can lead to better employment opportunities. 

The benefits include financial assistance for training programs and the development of vocational skills. 

Eligible beneficiaries are individuals from minority communities such as Muslims, Christians, Sikhs, Buddhists, Jains, and Zoroastrians. 

The age limit for applicants is typically between 14 to 35 years. 

The application process involves submitting an application form through designated training institutions. 

The training programs are conducted by various implementing agencies recognized by the Ministry of Minority Affairs. 

The timeline for the training programs varies based on the specific skill being taught. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Minority Affairs at the national level. 

Training is provided in various sectors such as hospitality, information technology, and handicrafts. 

The scheme also aims to promote entrepreneurship among minority communities.
