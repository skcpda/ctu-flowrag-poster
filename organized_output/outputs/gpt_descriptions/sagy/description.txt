The Saansad Adarsh Gram Yojana (SAGY) was launched in 2014. 

It aims to develop model villages in India. 

The objective is to promote holistic development in rural areas. 

The scheme focuses on improving infrastructure and enhancing the quality of life. 

It encourages the participation of Members of Parliament in village development. 

Benefits include financial assistance for various development projects. 

Villages selected under SAGY receive support for sanitation health education and livelihood. 

Eligibility is based on the selection of villages by the respective Members of Parliament. 

The application process involves MPs identifying and adopting villages for development. 

There is no specific timeline for the completion of projects as it varies by village. 

The Ministry of Rural Development is the implementing authority for SAGY. 

Additional details include the emphasis on community participation and sustainable development practices.
