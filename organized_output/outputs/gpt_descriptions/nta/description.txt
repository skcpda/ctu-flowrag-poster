The National Testing Agency (NTA) is an autonomous organization established by the Government of India. 

It was created to conduct entrance examinations for higher education institutions. 

The main objective of NTA is to ensure a standardized and transparent examination process. 

NTA conducts various examinations including the Joint Entrance Examination (JEE) and the National Eligibility cum Entrance Test (NEET). 

The benefits include a streamlined examination process and improved access to higher education opportunities. 

Eligibility criteria vary depending on the specific examination conducted by NTA. 

Candidates must check the official notification for each examination to understand the eligibility requirements. 

The application process typically involves online registration through the NTA website. 

Candidates must fill out the application form and pay the required fees. 

The timeline for application submission varies for each examination. 

NTA releases a detailed schedule for each examination cycle. 

The implementing authority is the National Testing Agency itself. 

NTA also provides facilities for candidates with disabilities to ensure inclusivity. 

The agency aims to enhance the quality of education in India through fair and efficient testing.
