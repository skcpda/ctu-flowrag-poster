The YIP or Youth Internship Programme is an initiative by the Government of India. 

It aims to provide young people with opportunities to gain practical experience in various sectors. 

The objective is to enhance employability skills among youth. 

The programme offers internships across different industries and sectors. 

Participants receive a stipend during their internship period. 

Eligibility is typically for youth aged between 18 to 30 years. 

Applicants must be enrolled in a recognized educational institution or have recently graduated. 

The application process usually involves submitting an online application form. 

Shortlisted candidates are then contacted for further steps. 

The timeline for the internship varies based on the specific programme structure. 

The implementing authority for the YIP is the Ministry of Youth Affairs and Sports. 

Additional details may include specific sectors involved and the duration of internships.
