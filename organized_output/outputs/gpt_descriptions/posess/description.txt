The Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana (PMAY) is a government initiative aimed at providing affordable housing to the urban poor in India. 

The scheme was launched in June 2015. 

Its primary objective is to ensure housing for all by the year 2022. 

The benefits include financial assistance for the construction or purchase of houses. 

Eligible beneficiaries can receive subsidies on home loans. 

The scheme targets economically weaker sections low-income groups and middle-income groups. 

Applicants must be Indian citizens aged 18 years or above. 

They should not own a pucca house in their name or in the name of any family member. 

The application process can be completed online through the official PMAY website. 

Beneficiaries can also apply through Common Service Centers or designated banks. 

The timeline for the disbursement of benefits varies based on the completion of the application process. 

The Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs is the implementing authority for this scheme. 

State governments and urban local bodies also play a role in the implementation. 

The scheme is funded through a mix of government budget allocations and financial institutions. 

The PMAY also includes provisions for slum rehabilitation and the development of affordable housing projects. 

It promotes the use of sustainable and eco-friendly building materials. 

The scheme has been extended to rural areas under the Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana - Gramin (PMAY-G). 

This extension aims to provide housing assistance to the rural poor. 

Overall the PMAY aims to improve the living standards of the urban poor through affordable housing solutions.
