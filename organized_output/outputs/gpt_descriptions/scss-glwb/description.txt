The Senior Citizens Savings Scheme (SCSS) is a government-backed savings instrument in India. 

It is designed to provide financial security to senior citizens. 

The objective is to offer a reliable source of income after retirement. 

The scheme offers an interest rate that is higher than traditional savings accounts. 

The current interest rate is set at 8.6 percent per annum. 

Interest is paid quarterly to the account holder. 

The maximum investment limit is Rs. 15 lakh. 

The minimum investment is Rs. 1,000. 

Only Indian citizens aged 60 years and above are eligible to invest. 

Individuals aged 55 years and above who have retired under the Voluntary Retirement Scheme can also apply. 

The account can be opened at designated banks and post offices. 

The application process involves filling out a form and submitting required documents. 

Documents typically include proof of age and identity. 

The account has a maturity period of five years. 

It can be extended for an additional three years after maturity. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Finance. 

The SCSS is a safe investment option backed by the government. 

Premature withdrawal is allowed under certain conditions with applicable penalties. 

Tax benefits under Section 80C of the Income Tax Act are available. 

Interest earned is taxable. 

The scheme is popular among retirees seeking regular income.
