The VCSG PGY Scheme is an initiative by the Government of Uttarakhand. 

It aims to provide financial assistance to the families of deceased soldiers. 

The objective is to support the families in their time of need. 

The scheme offers a one-time financial aid to the eligible beneficiaries. 

The assistance amount is set at Rs. 5 lakh. 

Eligibility is limited to the families of soldiers who have died in action. 

The application process involves submitting a form along with necessary documents. 

Documents required include the death certificate and proof of relationship. 

The application can be submitted to the District Soldier Welfare Office. 

The scheme is implemented by the Uttarakhand Department of Soldier Welfare. 

There is no specific timeline mentioned for the disbursement of funds. 

The scheme is aimed at providing immediate financial relief to the bereaved families.
