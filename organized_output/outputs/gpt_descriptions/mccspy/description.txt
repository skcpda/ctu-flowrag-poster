The MCCSPY scheme stands for the "Mahatma Gandhi National Rural Employment Guarantee Act - Mahatma Gandhi National Rural Employment Guarantee Scheme for the Poor Youth." 

This scheme is aimed at providing employment opportunities to rural youth. 

The objective is to enhance livelihood security in rural areas by providing at least 100 days of wage employment in a financial year. 

The benefits include guaranteed wage employment and skill development opportunities. 

Eligibility is primarily for rural youth aged between 18 to 35 years. 

The application process involves registration at the local Gram Panchayat. 

The scheme operates on a continuous basis throughout the year. 

The Ministry of Rural Development is the implementing authority for this scheme. 

Additional details may include provisions for skill training and capacity building for the beneficiaries.
