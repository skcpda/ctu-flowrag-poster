The Orukkauk scheme is an initiative by the Government of India aimed at providing financial assistance to families in need. 

The objective of the scheme is to enhance the living standards of economically weaker sections of society. 

The benefits include direct cash transfers to eligible families to support their basic needs. 

Eligibility criteria typically include being a resident of India and belonging to a low-income household. 

The application process generally involves submitting a form along with necessary documentation to the designated government office. 

The timeline for disbursement of funds may vary based on the specific implementation guidelines of the scheme. 

Implementing authorities include local government bodies and relevant state departments responsible for welfare schemes. 

Additional details about the scheme may include specific financial limits and the types of assistance provided.
