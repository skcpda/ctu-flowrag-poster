The West Bengal Integrated Social Security Scheme (WBISSCIS) aims to provide social security to vulnerable sections of society in West Bengal. 

The objective is to ensure financial assistance and support for the elderly, widows, and persons with disabilities. 

The scheme offers monthly financial assistance to eligible beneficiaries. 

Elderly individuals aged 60 years and above are eligible for the scheme. 

Widows and persons with disabilities are also eligible for assistance under this scheme. 

The application process involves submitting a form along with necessary documents to the local government office. 

The timeline for processing applications may vary depending on the local administrative procedures. 

The West Bengal Department of Women and Child Development and Social Welfare is responsible for implementing the scheme. 

The scheme is part of the state's broader efforts to enhance social security and welfare for marginalized communities.
