The West Bengal Talent Support for Students in Science and Technology Scheme (WBTISSCIS) is an initiative by the Government of West Bengal. 

The scheme aims to provide financial assistance to students pursuing higher education in science and technology. 

It seeks to encourage students to continue their studies in these fields and enhance their skills. 

The benefits include financial support for tuition fees and other educational expenses. 

Eligible candidates are students who have secured admission in recognized institutions for undergraduate or postgraduate courses in science and technology. 

The application process involves submitting an online application along with required documents. 

The scheme is implemented by the Department of Higher Education, Government of West Bengal. 

The timeline for disbursement of funds is typically aligned with the academic calendar. 

The scheme is part of the state's broader effort to promote education and skill development in science and technology sectors.
