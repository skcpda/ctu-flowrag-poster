The FASUCCU scheme is an initiative launched by the Government of India aimed at providing financial assistance to underprivileged sections of society. 

The full form of FASUCCU is the Financial Assistance for Sustainable Urban Community and Cultural Upliftment. 

This scheme is designed to address the socio-economic challenges faced by marginalized urban communities.

Urban areas in India often face significant disparities in wealth and access to resources.

The FASUCCU scheme aims to bridge these gaps by providing targeted financial support.
