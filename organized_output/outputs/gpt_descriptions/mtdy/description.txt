The Mukhyamantri Tirth Yatra Yojana (MTDY) is a welfare scheme launched by the Government of Madhya Pradesh. 

The scheme aims to provide financial assistance for pilgrimage trips to senior citizens. 

It promotes the cultural and spiritual heritage of India by facilitating travel to religious sites. 

The benefits include free travel and accommodation for eligible senior citizens. 

Eligible beneficiaries must be residents of Madhya Pradesh and aged 60 years or above. 

The application process involves filling out a form available at designated offices or online portals. 

The scheme is implemented by the Department of Religious Trust and Endowments of Madhya Pradesh. 

The frequency of the yatra is determined based on the schedule of the pilgrimage trips organized by the government. 

Additional details include that the scheme covers various religious destinations across India.
