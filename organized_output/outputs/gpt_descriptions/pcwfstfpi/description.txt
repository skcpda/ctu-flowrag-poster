The scheme is known as the Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samman Nidhi (PM-KISAN) scheme. 

It was launched to provide financial support to small and marginal farmers in India. 

The primary objective is to supplement the income of farmers and ensure food security. 

Under this scheme eligible farmers receive direct cash transfers of Rs. 6000 per year. 

The assistance is provided in three equal installments of Rs. 2000 each. 

Eligibility is limited to small and marginal farmers who own cultivable land. 

Farmers must have landholding of up to 2 hectares to qualify for the scheme. 

The application process involves registration through the State Government's designated authorities. 

Farmers must provide necessary documents such as land ownership proof and Aadhaar number. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers' Welfare. 

The financial assistance is provided directly to the bank accounts of the beneficiaries. 

The scheme is expected to be disbursed on a regular basis throughout the year. 

The timeline for the release of funds is typically every four months. 

The scheme aims to enhance the livelihood of farmers and reduce their financial burden. 

It also seeks to promote agricultural productivity and sustainability.
