The Rashtriya Swasthya Aayog Health Protection Scheme (RSAHPS) aims to provide health insurance coverage to economically vulnerable families in India. 

The objective of the scheme is to reduce out-of-pocket healthcare expenses and improve access to quality healthcare services. 

The benefits include coverage for hospitalization expenses and pre and post-hospitalization costs. 

Eligible beneficiaries are identified based on socio-economic criteria and belong to the economically weaker sections of society. 

The application process involves registration through designated state health authorities or online platforms. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Health and Family Welfare in collaboration with state governments. 

The frequency of coverage and benefits is annual, with renewals based on the eligibility criteria. 

Additional details regarding specific coverage limits and exclusions may vary by state.
