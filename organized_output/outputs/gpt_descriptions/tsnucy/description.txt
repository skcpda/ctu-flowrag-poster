The scheme TSNUCY stands for Telangana State Nutritional Support for Children and Young Women. 

It was launched by the Government of Telangana to address malnutrition among children and young women. 

The main objective is to provide nutritional support to improve health and nutritional status. 

The scheme aims to reduce the prevalence of stunting and wasting among children. 

It also focuses on enhancing the dietary intake of young women to promote better maternal health. 

Eligible beneficiaries include children aged 0 to 6 years and young women aged 15 to 49 years. 

The application process typically involves registration through local Anganwadi centers. 

Beneficiaries receive nutritional supplements and food assistance. 

The assistance is provided on a regular basis to ensure consistent nutritional support. 

The implementing authority for this scheme is the Department of Women Development and Child Welfare, Government of Telangana.
