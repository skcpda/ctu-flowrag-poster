The RCS scheme stands for the Regional Connectivity Scheme. 

It was launched by the Government of India in 2016. 

The scheme aims to enhance air connectivity to underserved and unserved airports in India. 

The objective is to make air travel affordable and convenient for the common man. 

The scheme provides financial support to airlines to operate flights on regional routes. 

Benefits include reduced airfares and increased accessibility to air travel for remote areas. 

The assistance is provided in the form of Viability Gap Funding to airlines. 

Eligibility for the scheme includes airlines that are willing to operate on regional routes. 

The application process involves airlines submitting proposals to the Ministry of Civil Aviation. 

The timeline for the implementation of the scheme is ongoing since its launch. 

The Ministry of Civil Aviation is the implementing authority for the RCS scheme. 

The scheme is also known as UDAN which stands for Ude Desh ka Aam Naagrik. 

It aims to stimulate economic growth in regional areas through improved connectivity. 

The scheme has provisions for the development of airport infrastructure in regional locations. 

It encourages the participation of state governments in promoting regional connectivity. 

The RCS has led to the introduction of several new routes and increased flight frequency. 

The scheme is part of a broader initiative to boost tourism and trade in India.
