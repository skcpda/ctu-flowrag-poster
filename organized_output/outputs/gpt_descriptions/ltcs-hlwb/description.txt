**Scheme Name: LTCs-HLWB (Leave Travel Concession for Home Leave)**

**Context or Background:**  
The Leave Travel Concession for Home Leave scheme is designed to provide financial assistance to government employees for travel expenses incurred while availing of leave.  
This scheme is part of the broader efforts by the Indian government to enhance the welfare of its employees.  
The scheme aims to promote the well-being of government employees by easing the financial burden associated with travel during their leave periods.  
It recognizes the importance of family visits and personal time for the mental and emotional health of employees.

**Objectives:**  
The primary objective of the LTCs-HLWB scheme is to facilitate travel for government employees to their home towns during their leave.  
The scheme aims to ensure that employees can spend quality time with their families without the added stress of travel costs.  
It seeks to promote work-life balance among government employees.  
The scheme also aims to encourage employees to utilize their leave entitlements for personal and family purposes.

Under the scheme, eligible government employees can claim reimbursement for travel expenses incurred during their leave.  
The amount reimbursed is subject to certain limits as defined by the government.  
The scheme covers travel expenses for the employee and their family members.  
Eligible family members typically include spouse, children, and dependent parents.
