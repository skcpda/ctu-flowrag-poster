The Mukhyamantri Doodh Sagar Yojana (MDSCY) is a welfare scheme initiated by the Government of Madhya Pradesh. 

The scheme aims to promote the consumption of milk among children and improve their nutritional status. 

It seeks to provide free milk to children in Anganwadi centers and primary schools. 

The target beneficiaries are children aged between 6 months to 6 years. 

Children enrolled in Anganwadi centers and primary schools are eligible for this scheme. 

The application process involves enrollment in Anganwadi centers or primary schools. 

Milk is provided on a daily basis to the eligible children. 

The scheme is implemented by the Department of Women and Child Development, Madhya Pradesh. 

The initiative is part of the state's efforts to combat malnutrition and promote health among young children.
