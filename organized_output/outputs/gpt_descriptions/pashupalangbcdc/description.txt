The Pashupalak Bima Yojana is a government welfare scheme aimed at providing financial assistance to livestock farmers in India. 

The scheme is designed to support farmers by insuring their livestock against various risks. 

The objectives include reducing the financial burden on farmers due to livestock loss and promoting livestock rearing as a sustainable livelihood option. 

Benefits include compensation for the loss of livestock due to accidents diseases or natural calamities. 

Eligible beneficiaries are livestock farmers who own cattle sheep goats and other livestock. 

The application process typically involves submitting a form along with necessary documents to the local veterinary office or designated authority. 

The timeline for claims processing may vary but is generally addressed within a specified period after the submission of the claim. 

The scheme is implemented by the Department of Animal Husbandry and Dairying under the Ministry of Fisheries Animal Husbandry and Dairying. 

Additional details may include specific coverage limits and premium amounts which can vary based on the type of livestock insured.
