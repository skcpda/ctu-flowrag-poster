The scheme SCYMP stands for the Scheme for Capacity Building of Young Managers in Public Sector Enterprises. 

It was launched by the Government of India to enhance the managerial skills of young professionals in public sector enterprises. 

The primary objective is to improve the efficiency and effectiveness of public sector enterprises through better management practices. 

The scheme provides training and development programs for young managers. 

These programs focus on various aspects of management including leadership skills and strategic planning. 

Eligibility criteria include being a young manager working in a public sector enterprise. 

The application process typically involves submission of an application form through the designated portal or agency. 

The timeline for the training programs may vary based on the specific courses offered. 

Implementing authorities include the Ministry of Heavy Industries and Public Enterprises. 

Additional details regarding specific training modules and schedules may be available on official government websites.
