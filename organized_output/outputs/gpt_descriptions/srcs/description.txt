The Scheme for Residential Schools for Children of Families engaged in Hazardous Occupations (SRCs) aims to provide educational opportunities to children from families involved in hazardous occupations. 

The objective is to ensure that children receive quality education and are protected from the adverse effects of hazardous work environments. 

The benefits include free education, accommodation, and access to basic amenities for children. 

The assistance is provided to children from families engaged in hazardous occupations as defined by the government. 

Eligibility criteria include being a child of a family involved in hazardous work and being within the specified age group for school admission. 

The application process typically involves submitting an application form along with necessary documentation to the designated authorities. 

The timeline for the application process and the frequency of admissions may vary based on the academic calendar and specific school policies. 

Implementing authorities include the Ministry of Labour and Employment and state governments responsible for the operation of residential schools. 

Additional details may include the provision of vocational training and support for higher education opportunities for students.
