The Mukhyamantri Kanya Vivah Yojana (MMKVYMP) is a welfare scheme initiated by the Government of Madhya Pradesh. 

The scheme aims to promote the marriage of girls from economically weaker sections. 

It seeks to reduce the financial burden on families during the marriage of their daughters. 

The benefits include financial assistance for the marriage of eligible girls. 

The assistance amount is typically provided in the form of a one-time grant. 

Eligibility is generally determined by the economic status of the family. 

The scheme primarily targets families with an annual income below a specified threshold. 

The applicant must be a resident of Madhya Pradesh. 

The girl must be of marriageable age as per the legal requirements. 

The application process involves submitting an application form along with required documents. 

Documents may include proof of age and income certificate. 

Applications can usually be submitted online or at designated government offices. 

The timeline for processing applications may vary based on the volume of applications received. 

The scheme is implemented by the Department of Women and Child Development in Madhya Pradesh.
