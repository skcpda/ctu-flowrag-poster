The Mukhyamantri Kanya Yojana (MKY) is a welfare scheme initiated by the Government of Gujarat. 

The scheme aims to promote the education and empowerment of girls in the state. 

It focuses on providing financial assistance to families for the education of their daughters. 

The benefits include a one-time financial grant for the education of girls. 

This grant is provided at various stages of education including after the completion of 10th and 12th grades. 

Eligibility for the scheme includes families with a girl child who are residents of Gujarat. 

The family income must be below a specified threshold to qualify for assistance. 

Applications can be submitted online through the official government portal. 

The application process requires submission of necessary documents including income proof and educational certificates. 

The scheme is implemented by the Women and Child Development Department of Gujarat. 

The timeline for disbursement of funds is typically aligned with the academic calendar. 

The frequency of assistance is based on the educational milestones achieved by the girl child.
