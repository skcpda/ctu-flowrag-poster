The scheme SETCMS-SDC is aimed at providing skill development and entrepreneurship support to the youth of India. 

The objective of the scheme is to enhance the employability of youth through skill training and entrepreneurship development. 

The benefits include access to skill development programs and financial assistance for starting new ventures. 

Eligibility criteria include youth aged between 15 to 29 years who are looking to enhance their skills or start their own business. 

The application process involves filling out an online application form available on the official website. 

The timeline for the skill development programs varies based on the specific training modules selected. 

The implementing authorities for this scheme include the Ministry of Skill Development and Entrepreneurship. 

Additional details may include partnerships with various training institutes and organizations to deliver the skill development programs.
