The Zero Budget Natural Farming scheme is an initiative by the Government of India. 

It aims to promote sustainable farming practices. 

The objective is to reduce the cost of farming and enhance farmers' income. 

The scheme encourages the use of natural inputs instead of chemical fertilizers and pesticides. 

Farmers are trained in techniques that improve soil health and increase crop yield. 

Benefits include reduced expenditure on farming inputs and improved crop resilience. 

The assistance provided includes training programs and resources for natural farming practices. 

Eligibility is primarily for farmers engaged in agriculture. 

The application process typically involves registration with local agricultural departments. 

Farmers may need to attend training sessions to qualify for assistance. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers' Welfare. 

State governments may also have a role in local implementation. 

The timeline for training and implementation varies by state and local conditions. 

The scheme is part of a broader effort to promote organic farming in India.
