The SRS Goa scheme is a welfare initiative by the Government of Goa. 

It aims to provide financial assistance to families affected by the COVID-19 pandemic. 

The objective is to support vulnerable households in regaining their economic stability. 

Eligible beneficiaries include families with a monthly income below a specified threshold. 

The scheme targets those who have lost their livelihoods due to the pandemic. 

Financial assistance is provided directly to the bank accounts of the beneficiaries. 

The application process involves submitting a form along with required documents to the designated authorities. 

The timeline for disbursement of funds is typically within a few weeks of application approval. 

The implementing authority for this scheme is the Department of Social Welfare, Government of Goa. 

Additional details about the scheme can be obtained from the official government website.
