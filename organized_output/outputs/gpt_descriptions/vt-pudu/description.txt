The VT-Pudu scheme is an initiative aimed at providing financial assistance to the beneficiaries in the Puducherry region. 

The objective of the scheme is to support the economically weaker sections of society. 

The benefits include direct financial aid to eligible families to improve their living conditions. 

Eligibility criteria include being a resident of Puducherry and belonging to the economically weaker section. 

The application process involves submitting a form along with necessary documents to the designated government office. 

The timeline for disbursement of benefits is typically announced by the implementing authorities. 

The scheme is implemented by the Department of Social Welfare in Puducherry. 

Additional details regarding the specific amount of assistance and frequency of payments may vary based on government notifications.
