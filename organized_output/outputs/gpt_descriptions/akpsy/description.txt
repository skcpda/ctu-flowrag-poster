The Akshay Patra Foundation is a prominent initiative in India aimed at addressing hunger and malnutrition among school children. 

The scheme is often referred to as the Akshay Patra Scheme or simply Akshay Patra. 

It was initiated in 2000 by the Akshay Patra Foundation. 

The foundation was established by a group of individuals led by Madhu Pandit Dasa. 

The primary objective of the Akshay Patra Scheme is to provide nutritious meals to children in government schools. 

The scheme aims to enhance the nutritional status of children and improve their attendance in schools. 

It also seeks to promote education by ensuring that children receive at least one wholesome meal a day. 

The initiative is aligned with the Mid-Day Meal Scheme launched by the Government of India. 

The Akshay Patra Foundation operates in various states across India. 

It serves millions of children every day through its extensive network of kitchens. 

The foundation's kitchens are equipped with modern technology to ensure the preparation of hygienic and nutritious meals. 

The meals provided under this scheme are designed to meet the dietary needs of growing children.

The foundation then coordinates with the school to determine meal requirements.
