The National Scheme for Welfare of Persons with Disabilities (NSFPGS) aims to provide financial assistance and support to individuals with disabilities. 

The objective of the scheme is to promote the welfare and empowerment of persons with disabilities. 

The benefits include financial assistance for various needs and support services for individuals with disabilities. 

Eligibility criteria include being a person with a disability as defined under the Rights of Persons with Disabilities Act 2016. 

The application process typically involves submitting an application form along with necessary documents to the designated authority. 

The timeline for processing applications may vary based on the implementing agency. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment. 

Additional details include the provision of skill development and rehabilitation services for persons with disabilities.
