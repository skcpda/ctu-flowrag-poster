The Scheme for Financial Assistance for the Scheduled Tribes (SFASiis) is designed to provide financial support to Scheduled Tribe communities in India. 

The objective of the scheme is to enhance the socio-economic status of Scheduled Tribes through financial assistance. 

The benefits include financial aid for various developmental activities and initiatives that promote self-reliance among Scheduled Tribes. 

Eligibility is primarily restricted to individuals belonging to Scheduled Tribe communities as recognized by the government. 

The application process involves submitting the required documents to the designated authorities in the respective states or union territories. 

The timeline for disbursement of funds may vary based on the specific project or activity approved under the scheme. 

The implementing authorities include the Ministry of Tribal Affairs and respective state governments.
