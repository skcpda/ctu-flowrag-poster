The Goa Griha Aadhar Scheme is a welfare initiative launched by the Government of Goa. 

This scheme aims to provide financial assistance to the residents of Goa. 

The primary objective of the Goa Griha Aadhar Scheme is to support households by providing them with a monthly financial aid. 

This financial assistance is intended to help families meet their basic needs and improve their standard of living. 

The scheme is particularly focused on supporting low-income households. 

Under this scheme, eligible households receive a monthly amount of financial assistance. 

The amount provided under the scheme is intended to alleviate the financial burden on families. 

This assistance can be used for various essential expenses such as food, education, and healthcare. 

The scheme is designed to ensure that the benefits reach the most vulnerable sections of society. 

Eligibility for the Goa Griha Aadhar Scheme is primarily determined by the income level of the household. 

Households with an annual income below a specified threshold are eligible to apply for the scheme. 

The scheme is open to all residents of Goa who meet the income criteria.
