The Skill Development Programme for Scheduled Castes (SDPS) aims to enhance the employability of Scheduled Caste youth in India. 

The objective of the scheme is to provide skill development training to Scheduled Caste individuals. 

The benefits include skill training in various sectors to improve job opportunities. 

Assistance is provided in the form of training programs and financial support for skill development. 

Eligibility is primarily for individuals belonging to Scheduled Castes. 

The application process typically involves submitting an application to the designated implementing agency. 

The timeline for the training programs varies based on the specific courses offered. 

The Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment is the primary implementing authority for this scheme. 

Additional details may include partnerships with various training institutes to conduct skill development programs.
