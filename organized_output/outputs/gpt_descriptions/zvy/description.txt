The ZVY scheme refers to the "Zila Vikas Yojana." 

It aims to promote integrated rural development in various districts. 

The objective is to enhance the living standards of rural populations. 

The scheme focuses on improving infrastructure and providing essential services. 

Benefits include financial assistance for development projects. 

It also supports skill development and employment generation initiatives. 

Eligibility criteria typically include residents of the targeted rural areas. 

The application process involves submitting proposals to local authorities. 

Proposals are evaluated based on feasibility and alignment with the scheme's objectives. 

The timeline for project implementation varies based on the specific initiative. 

The Ministry of Rural Development is the primary implementing authority. 

State governments may also play a role in execution and monitoring. 

Additional details on specific projects or funding allocations may vary by district.
