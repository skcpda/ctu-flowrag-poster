The Mukhyamantri Matru Vandana Yojana (MMVY) is a maternity benefit scheme launched by the Government of Madhya Pradesh. 

The scheme aims to provide financial assistance to pregnant women and lactating mothers. 

It is designed to improve maternal and child health by encouraging institutional deliveries. 

The objective is to reduce maternal and infant mortality rates. 

Under this scheme, eligible beneficiaries receive a cash incentive of INR 6,000. 

The assistance is provided in three installments during pregnancy and after childbirth. 

Eligibility criteria include being a resident of Madhya Pradesh. 

Beneficiaries must be pregnant women or lactating mothers. 

They should not be receiving benefits under any other maternity scheme. 

The application process involves filling out a form available at Anganwadi centers or online. 

Beneficiaries must provide necessary documents such as identity proof and pregnancy-related documents. 

The scheme is implemented by the Department of Women and Child Development, Madhya Pradesh. 

The timeline for receiving benefits is linked to the stages of pregnancy and childbirth. 

Payments are made directly to the bank accounts of the beneficiaries. 

The scheme aims to promote health and nutrition among mothers and children. 

It also encourages women to seek healthcare services during pregnancy.
