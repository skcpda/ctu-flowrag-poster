Mahatma Gandhi Kisan Samman Nidhi Yojana (MKYH) is a government welfare scheme aimed at providing financial support to farmers in India. 

The scheme was launched to ensure the welfare of small and marginal farmers. 

The primary objective is to provide direct income support to farmers to enhance their livelihood. 

Under this scheme, eligible farmers receive an income support of Rs. 6000 per year. 

The assistance is provided in three equal installments of Rs. 2000 each. 

Eligibility is restricted to small and marginal farmers who own cultivable land. 

Farmers with landholding up to 2 hectares are eligible to apply. 

The application process involves registration through the State Government's portal. 

Farmers must provide necessary documents such as land records and identity proof. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers' Welfare. 

The financial assistance is credited directly to the bank accounts of the beneficiaries. 

The scheme is expected to be disbursed annually. 

The implementation is monitored by the respective state governments. 

This initiative aims to alleviate the financial burden on farmers and improve their living standards.
