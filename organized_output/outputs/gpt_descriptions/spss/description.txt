The Social Protection Scheme for the Scheduled Castes (SPSS) aims to provide financial assistance and social security to Scheduled Castes. 

The objective of SPSS is to uplift the socio-economic status of Scheduled Castes by providing them with financial support. 

Benefits include direct cash transfers and assistance for education health and employment. 

Eligibility is primarily for individuals belonging to Scheduled Castes as defined by the Government of India. 

The application process typically involves submitting an application form along with necessary documents to the local government office or online through designated portals. 

The timeline for disbursement of benefits may vary based on the specific program under SPSS. 

The implementing authorities include the Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment and state government departments. 

Additional details may include specific schemes under SPSS that target various needs such as education scholarships and skill development programs.
