The Mukhyamantri Pashudhan Mission Scheme (MPMSY) is an initiative by the Government of Madhya Pradesh. 

It aims to enhance livestock productivity and promote animal husbandry in the state. 

The primary objective is to provide financial assistance for the purchase of livestock and improve the overall health of animals. 

The scheme offers benefits such as subsidies on the purchase of livestock and support for veterinary services. 

Eligible beneficiaries include farmers and individuals engaged in animal husbandry in Madhya Pradesh. 

The application process involves submitting a form to the local animal husbandry department along with necessary documents. 

The scheme operates on a continuous basis with no specific application deadline. 

The implementing authority for this scheme is the Department of Animal Husbandry and Dairying, Government of Madhya Pradesh. 

The scheme also focuses on training programs for farmers to improve their skills in animal husbandry. 

It aims to boost the income of farmers through enhanced livestock production.
