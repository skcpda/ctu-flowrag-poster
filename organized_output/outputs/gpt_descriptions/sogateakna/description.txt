The Sogateakna scheme is an initiative aimed at providing financial assistance to marginalized communities in India. 

The primary objective of the scheme is to enhance the livelihood opportunities for economically disadvantaged groups. 

The scheme offers direct financial support for various livelihood activities. 

Eligibility for the scheme includes individuals from marginalized communities who meet specific income criteria. 

The application process involves submitting a form along with required documents to the designated local authority. 

The timeline for processing applications typically ranges from a few weeks to a couple of months. 

The implementing authorities for the Sogateakna scheme are local government bodies and relevant state departments. 

Additional details regarding the specific benefits and frequency of assistance may vary by state.
