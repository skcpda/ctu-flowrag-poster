The AFCGWTB scheme stands for the "Aam Aadmi Financial Assistance for Construction Workers and Their Families" scheme.

This scheme is specifically designed to provide financial assistance to construction workers and their families in India.

The context of this scheme arises from the recognition of the challenges faced by construction workers in the country.

Construction workers often work in precarious conditions with limited job security and benefits.

They are usually employed on a daily wage basis which makes them vulnerable to economic fluctuations.

The scheme aims to alleviate some of the financial burdens faced by these workers.

The objectives of the AFCGWTB scheme include providing direct financial support to construction workers.

It also aims to ensure that the families of these workers have access to necessary financial resources.

The scheme seeks to improve the living standards of construction workers and their families.

Additionally, it aims to promote the welfare of workers in the construction sector.

The benefits provided under this scheme include direct cash transfers to eligible construction workers.

These cash transfers are intended to assist with daily expenses and improve the quality of life.

The assistance can also be used for health care and education needs of the workers' families.

This financial support is crucial for families that rely solely on the income of construction workers.

Eligibility for the AFCGWTB scheme is primarily based on the worker's employment status.

Only registered construction workers are eligible to apply for this scheme.

Workers must be able to provide proof of their employment in the construction sector.

They must also meet certain income criteria which may vary by state.

In addition to employment status, the age of the applicant may also be a factor.

Typically, applicants must be above a certain age to qualify for the assistance.

The frequency of financial assistance under the scheme is typically monthly.
