PM-KISAN is a central sector scheme launched by the Government of India. 

It aims to provide financial assistance to small and marginal farmers. 

The objective is to supplement the financial needs of farmers for agricultural activities. 

Under this scheme farmers receive direct cash transfers. 

Each eligible farmer receives Rs. 6000 per year. 

The amount is disbursed in three equal installments of Rs. 2000. 

Eligibility is limited to small and marginal farmers. 

Farmers must own cultivable land. 

Land ownership must be verified through land records. 

The application process is conducted through state governments. 

Farmers need to register on the PM-KISAN portal or through local revenue offices. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers' Welfare. 

The disbursement of funds occurs three times a year. 

The scheme was first launched in December 2018. 

It has been expanded to cover more farmers over time. 

The scheme aims to enhance the income of farmers and reduce their financial burden. 

It also seeks to ensure food security and promote sustainable agriculture.
