The Maternity Benefit Programme is a government welfare scheme in India. 

It aims to provide financial assistance to pregnant women and lactating mothers. 

The objective is to ensure safe and healthy motherhood. 

The scheme offers a cash incentive of INR 5,000 to eligible beneficiaries. 

This amount is provided in three installments during pregnancy and after childbirth. 

Eligibility includes women who are at least 18 years old. 

They must be registered under the scheme and have a valid Aadhaar number. 

The application process involves filling out a form available at designated centers. 

Beneficiaries must submit required documents such as identity proof and bank details. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Women and Child Development. 

It is operational across all states and union territories in India. 

The timeline for receiving benefits is typically within a few weeks of application approval. 

The scheme is part of the larger National Food Security Act. 

It aims to improve maternal and child health indicators in the country.
