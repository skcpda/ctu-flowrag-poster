The SSDDAP Pudu scheme is a welfare initiative aimed at supporting the development of the Pudu community in India. 

The objective of the scheme is to enhance the socio-economic status of the Pudu community. 

It focuses on providing financial assistance and support for various developmental activities. 

Benefits include access to skill development programs and financial aid for self-employment.
