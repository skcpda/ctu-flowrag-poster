MJSYAKM stands for the Mukhyamantri Jan Kalyan (Sambal) Yojana. 

It is a welfare scheme launched by the Government of Madhya Pradesh. 

The scheme aims to provide financial assistance to the economically weaker sections of society. 

It focuses on various vulnerable groups including laborers and marginalized communities. 

The primary objective is to alleviate poverty and improve the living standards of beneficiaries. 

Under this scheme, eligible families receive a monthly financial aid. 

The amount provided is intended to support basic needs such as food and healthcare. 

Eligibility criteria include being a resident of Madhya Pradesh and belonging to the economically weaker section. 

Applicants must also meet specific income thresholds set by the government. 

The application process involves submitting a form along with required documents to the local authorities. 

Applications can typically be made online through the official government portal. 

The scheme is implemented by the Department of Social Justice and Disabled Persons Welfare in Madhya Pradesh. 

The frequency of assistance is monthly. 

The scheme aims to ensure timely support for the beneficiaries. 

Additional details may include awareness programs to educate potential beneficiaries about the scheme.
