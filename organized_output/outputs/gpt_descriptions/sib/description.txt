The SIB scheme refers to the Scheme for the Implementation of the Budget. 

It is designed to enhance transparency and accountability in the implementation of government schemes. 

The objective is to ensure effective utilization of budgetary resources. 

The scheme aims to improve the delivery of public services. 

It provides a framework for monitoring and evaluating government programs. 

The benefits include better financial management and increased public trust. 

Eligibility criteria are typically aligned with the specific programs being monitored. 

The application process varies depending on the specific initiative under the SIB scheme. 

The timeline for implementation is generally aligned with the annual budget cycle. 

The Ministry of Finance is the primary implementing authority for this scheme. 

Additional details may vary based on specific program requirements.
