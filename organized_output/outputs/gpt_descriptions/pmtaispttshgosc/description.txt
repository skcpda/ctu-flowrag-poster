The PMTASPTTSHGOSC stands for Pradhan Mantri Tayi Awas Yojana - Special Provision for Tribal Households in Scheduled Areas. 

This scheme is part of the Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana aimed at providing housing for the economically weaker sections of society. 

It specifically targets tribal households residing in scheduled areas. 

The objective is to ensure that every tribal household has access to a pucca house with basic amenities. 

The benefits include financial assistance for the construction of houses. 

Eligible beneficiaries receive a subsidy for housing construction. 

The scheme aims to improve the living conditions of tribal communities. 

Eligibility is primarily based on being a member of a Scheduled Tribe and residing in a scheduled area. 

The application process typically involves submitting an application through local government offices or online portals. 

The timeline for disbursal of funds is generally aligned with the annual budget cycle. 

The implementing authorities include the Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs and state governments. 

Additional details regarding specific financial assistance amounts or exact application procedures may vary by state.
