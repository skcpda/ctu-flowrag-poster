The Pradhan Mantri Swasthya Suraksha Yojana (PMSSY) aims to improve healthcare delivery in India. 

It focuses on providing quality healthcare services to underserved areas. 

The objective is to enhance the availability of affordable healthcare. 

The scheme also aims to strengthen the medical education system. 

Benefits include the establishment of new medical colleges and upgrading existing ones. 

Financial assistance is provided for infrastructure development. 

Eligibility criteria include the need for healthcare facilities in specific regions. 

State governments and union territories can apply for funding under this scheme. 

The application process involves submitting a proposal to the Ministry of Health and Family Welfare. 

The timeline for project implementation varies based on the specific proposal. 

The Ministry of Health and Family Welfare is the implementing authority. 

Additional details include a focus on improving access to tertiary healthcare services.
