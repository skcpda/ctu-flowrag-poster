The Sruw scheme is designed to support the welfare of women and children in India. 

The primary objective of the Sruw scheme is to enhance the health and nutrition status of women and children. 

The scheme provides financial assistance for health services and nutritional support. 

Eligibility for the Sruw scheme typically includes women and children from low-income families. 

The application process involves submitting necessary documents to the local government office or designated agency. 

The timeline for assistance under the Sruw scheme may vary based on the specific services availed. 

The implementing authorities for the Sruw scheme include the Ministry of Women and Child Development and state government agencies.
