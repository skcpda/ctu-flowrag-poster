The SAMS (Skill Acquisition and Management System) scheme is designed to enhance the skill development of youth in India. 

It aims to provide skill training to the unemployed and underemployed population. 

The objective is to bridge the skill gap and promote self-employment and entrepreneurship. 

Benefits include access to skill development programs and financial assistance for training. 

Eligibility criteria typically include being a resident of India and belonging to a specific age group. 

The application process involves online registration through the official SAMS portal. 

The timeline for training programs varies based on the specific skill courses offered. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Skill Development and Entrepreneurship. 

Additional details include partnerships with various training institutes and organizations for effective implementation.
