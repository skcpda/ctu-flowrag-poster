The Mukhyamantri Sewa Sankalp Yojana (MSSSY) is a welfare scheme initiated by the Government of Madhya Pradesh. 

The scheme aims to provide employment opportunities to the youth of the state. 

It focuses on skill development and enhancing employability among the youth. 

The benefits include training programs that equip participants with necessary skills. 

Participants may receive financial assistance during the training period. 

Eligibility criteria include being a resident of Madhya Pradesh. 

Applicants must be within a specified age range. 

The application process involves online registration through the official portal. 

The timeline for the training programs is determined by the implementing agencies. 

The scheme is implemented by the Department of Skill Development and Entrepreneurship, Madhya Pradesh. 

Additional details may include partnerships with various training institutes for effective implementation.
