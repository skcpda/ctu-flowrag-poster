The Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samman Nidhi (PM-KISAN) scheme was launched by the Government of India in February 2019. 

It aims to provide financial support to small and marginal farmers. 

The objective is to supplement the financial needs of farmers for purchasing various inputs to ensure proper crop health and appropriate yields. 

Under this scheme eligible farmers receive Rs. 6000 per year in three equal installments. 

The assistance is directly transferred to the bank accounts of the beneficiaries. 

Eligibility is limited to small and marginal farmers who own cultivable land. 

Farmers with landholdings of up to 2 hectares are eligible for the scheme. 

The application process involves the submission of an application form along with necessary documents to the local revenue office or through the online portal. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers' Welfare. 

The financial assistance is provided on a quarterly basis. 

The scheme is applicable across all states and Union Territories in India. 

Farmers must ensure that their land records are updated and linked to their Aadhaar numbers for smooth processing of benefits. 

The scheme also aims to reduce the financial burden on farmers and enhance their income security.
