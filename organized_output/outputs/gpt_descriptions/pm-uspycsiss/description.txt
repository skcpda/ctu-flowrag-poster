The PM-USPYCSISS stands for Pradhan Mantri Udyami Shakti Pahal Yojana for Skill Development and Self-Employment.

This scheme aims to promote entrepreneurship and self-employment among the youth of India.

It focuses on providing skill development training and financial assistance to aspiring entrepreneurs.

The benefits include access to skill development programs and financial support for starting small businesses.

Eligibility criteria typically include being a resident of India and being within a specific age range.

The application process usually involves submitting an online application through the official portal or visiting designated offices.

The timeline for approval and disbursement of funds may vary depending on the specific project and application.

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Micro Small and Medium Enterprises.

Additional details may include specific training partners and institutions involved in skill development.
