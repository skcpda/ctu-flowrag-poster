The RFDS scheme stands for the Rashtriya Fasal Bima Yojana. 

It was launched to provide insurance coverage and financial support to farmers in India. 

The objective is to protect farmers against crop loss due to natural calamities. 

The scheme aims to stabilize the income of farmers and ensure food security. 

Benefits include compensation for crop loss due to adverse weather conditions and other risks. 

Farmers can receive financial assistance based on the extent of damage to their crops. 

Eligibility criteria include being a farmer growing notified crops in the notified area. 

Farmers must also pay a premium to avail of the insurance coverage. 

The application process involves filling out a prescribed form and submitting it to the designated authority. 

Farmers can apply through online and offline modes as specified by the implementing agencies. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers' Welfare. 

Insurance companies authorized by the government also play a role in the execution of the scheme. 

The frequency of premium payment and claims processing is determined by the insurance providers. 

The scheme is operational throughout the agricultural year based on the crop cycle. 

Additional details may include specific provisions for different states and types of crops.
