The PSSCSBG scheme stands for the Pradhan Mantri Sahaj Bijli Har Ghar Yojana. 

It was launched to provide electricity connections to all households in rural and urban areas. 

The objective is to ensure that every household has access to electricity. 

The scheme aims to improve the quality of life and promote economic development. 

Under this scheme, beneficiaries receive free electricity connections. 

The assistance includes the provision of necessary infrastructure for electricity supply. 

Eligibility is primarily based on being a resident of India and not having an existing electricity connection. 

The application process involves filling out a form available at local electricity distribution offices or online platforms. 

The timeline for connection can vary based on the location and availability of infrastructure. 

The implementing authority for this scheme is the Ministry of Power, Government of India. 

State electricity distribution companies are also involved in the execution of the scheme.
