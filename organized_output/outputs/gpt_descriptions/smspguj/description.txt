The scheme SMSPGUJ stands for the Scheme for the Management of School and College Infrastructure in Gujarat. 

It aims to enhance the quality of education by improving infrastructure in schools and colleges in Gujarat. 

The objectives include providing better facilities for students and creating a conducive learning environment. 

Benefits include financial assistance for infrastructure development such as classrooms libraries and laboratories. 

Eligibility criteria typically include educational institutions recognized by the Gujarat government. 

The application process involves submitting a proposal to the relevant educational department in Gujarat. 

The timeline for fund disbursement and project completion may vary based on the specific project approved. 

The implementing authority for this scheme is the Department of Education Government of Gujarat. 

Additional details regarding specific funding amounts and project guidelines may be available through official government channels.
