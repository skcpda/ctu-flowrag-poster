The ELSGTKDC scheme stands for the Employment Linked Skill Training Program for the Youth of Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes.

This scheme is implemented by the Government of India to provide skill development and employment opportunities for the youth belonging to Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes.

The background of this scheme is rooted in the need to uplift marginalized communities in India.

Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes have historically faced social and economic disadvantages.

The Indian government recognizes the importance of skill development in enhancing employability among these communities.

This scheme aims to bridge the skill gap that exists in the labor market.

The primary objective of the ELSGTKDC scheme is to provide skill training to youth from Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes.

The scheme focuses on equipping these individuals with skills that are in demand in various sectors.

By doing so, the government aims to enhance their employability and improve their socio-economic status.

The benefits of the ELSGTKDC scheme include access to skill training programs tailored to the needs of the job market.

Participants receive training in various trades and sectors that are aligned with current employment opportunities.

The scheme also provides financial assistance to cover the costs of training.

This financial support helps to alleviate the burden on families who may not have the resources to pay for skill development programs.

In addition to training, the scheme may also facilitate job placements for successful candidates.

This is a crucial aspect of the program as it directly links skill development with employment opportunities.

Eligibility for the ELSGTKDC scheme is primarily based on the applicant's caste.

Individuals must belong to Scheduled Castes or Scheduled Tribes to qualify for the program.

Age limits may apply but specific details on age eligibility are not always specified.

The scheme is designed to target youth who are unemployed or underemployed.
