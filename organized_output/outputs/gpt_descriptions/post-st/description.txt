The Post-Matric Scholarship Scheme is designed to provide financial assistance to students from Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes. 

The scheme aims to promote higher education among students from these communities. 

It seeks to reduce the dropout rate by providing financial support for their education. 

The benefits include covering tuition fees and maintenance allowances for eligible students. 

Students can receive assistance for various courses including professional and technical courses. 

Eligibility is limited to students belonging to Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes. 

Students must be enrolled in post-matriculation courses in recognized institutions. 

The family income of the applicant should not exceed a specified limit set by the government. 

The application process typically involves submitting an online application through the respective state government portal. 

Students may need to provide documentation such as caste certificates and income certificates. 

The scholarship is usually disbursed on an annual basis. 

The implementing authorities are the respective state governments and the Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment. 

The scheme is part of the efforts to enhance educational opportunities for marginalized communities in India.
