The National Family Benefit Scheme (NFBS) is a welfare initiative by the Government of India. 

It is designed to provide financial assistance to families in the event of the death of the primary breadwinner. 

The objective is to alleviate the financial burden faced by families due to the loss of income. 

Under this scheme, a one-time financial assistance of Rs. 20,000 is provided to the bereaved family. 

The assistance is aimed at helping the family meet immediate needs following the death. 

Eligibility for the scheme includes families belonging to the Below Poverty Line (BPL) category. 

The deceased must be the primary breadwinner of the family. 

The family must submit the application within two years of the death of the breadwinner. 

The application process involves submitting a prescribed application form along with necessary documents. 

Documents required include proof of death and BPL status. 

The scheme is implemented by the Department of Rural Development at the state level. 

The timeline for disbursement of funds is generally within a few weeks after the application is approved. 

The scheme is available across all states and Union Territories in India. 

It aims to provide timely support to vulnerable families during difficult times.
