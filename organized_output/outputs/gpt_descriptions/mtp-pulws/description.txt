The MTP-PULWS scheme is the Medical Termination of Pregnancy - Pre and Post Abortion Care for Women Scheme. 

This scheme aims to provide safe and legal abortion services to women. 

It focuses on reducing maternal mortality and morbidity associated with unsafe abortions. 

The scheme also seeks to ensure that women have access to comprehensive reproductive health services. 

Benefits include access to safe abortion services and post-abortion care. 

Women can receive counseling and medical assistance as part of the program. 

Eligibility is generally based on the need for abortion services as per the Medical Termination of Pregnancy Act. 

The application process involves visiting a registered healthcare facility. 

Women may need to provide necessary documentation as required by the healthcare provider. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Health and Family Welfare. 

Healthcare facilities providing these services must be accredited under the scheme. 

The frequency of services is based on individual needs and circumstances. 

The scheme is part of broader efforts to improve women's health in India.
