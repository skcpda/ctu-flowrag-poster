The Mukhyamantri Bhavishya Sewa Prabandhan Yojana (MBYPVY) is a government welfare scheme in India. 

It aims to provide financial assistance to the youth for skill development and employment opportunities. 

The scheme focuses on enhancing the employability of young individuals through various training programs. 

Eligible beneficiaries include youth aged between 18 to 35 years. 

The application process involves online registration through the official government portal. 

Applicants must provide necessary documents such as identity proof and educational qualifications. 

The scheme is implemented by the respective state governments in coordination with local skill development agencies. 

The frequency of assistance may vary based on the specific training programs offered under the scheme. 

The benefits include financial support for training programs and placement assistance after completion. 

The scheme also promotes entrepreneurship among the youth by providing necessary resources and guidance.
