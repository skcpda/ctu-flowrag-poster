The Mukhyamantri Mahila Samriddhi Yojana (MMMSY) is a welfare scheme launched by the Government of Madhya Pradesh. 

The scheme aims to empower women by providing them with financial assistance and support for self-employment. 

It focuses on enhancing the economic status of women and promoting their participation in various income-generating activities. 

Eligible beneficiaries include women from economically weaker sections of society. 

The scheme provides financial assistance in the form of loans for setting up small businesses or self-employment ventures. 

Women can receive training and skill development support to enhance their employability. 

The application process involves submitting a form to the designated authorities along with required documents. 

The timeline for processing applications and disbursing funds may vary based on the specific circumstances of each case. 

The scheme is implemented by the Department of Women and Child Development in Madhya Pradesh. 

Additional details regarding specific eligibility criteria and the exact amount of financial assistance may be available through official government sources.
