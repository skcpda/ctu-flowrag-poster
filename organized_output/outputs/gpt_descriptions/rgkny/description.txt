The Rajiv Gandhi Kisan Nyay Yojana (RGKNY) is a welfare scheme launched by the Government of Chhattisgarh. 

It was introduced in 2020 to support farmers in the state. 

The primary objective is to provide financial assistance to farmers for the sale of their produce. 

The scheme aims to enhance the income of farmers and promote agricultural productivity. 

Under this scheme, farmers receive direct cash transfers based on the quantity of paddy sold. 

The assistance amount is Rs. 10,000 per acre for paddy cultivation. 

Eligibility is limited to farmers who are registered and have sold paddy in the state. 

The application process involves registering with the local agricultural department. 

Farmers must provide necessary documentation including land records and sale receipts. 

The assistance is provided annually during the paddy procurement season. 

The scheme is implemented by the Department of Agriculture and Farmers Welfare, Government of Chhattisgarh. 

It is part of the state's broader efforts to improve the agricultural sector and support rural livelihoods.
