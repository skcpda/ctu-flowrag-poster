The Mukhyamantri Sewa Nyaya Yojana for Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes is a welfare scheme initiated by the Government of India. 

It aims to provide financial assistance to the Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes for various welfare activities. 

The scheme focuses on improving the socio-economic conditions of marginalized communities. 

Benefits include financial aid for education healthcare and skill development. 

Eligibility is primarily based on caste and income criteria. 

The application process involves submitting relevant documents to the designated authorities. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment. 

The frequency of assistance is determined by the specific needs of the beneficiaries. 

Additional details regarding specific financial limits or documentation requirements are available through official government sources.
