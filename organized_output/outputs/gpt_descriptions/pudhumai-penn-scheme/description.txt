The Pudhumai Penn Scheme is a welfare initiative launched by the Government of Tamil Nadu. 

It aims to empower girl students and promote their education. 

The primary objective is to provide financial assistance to girls pursuing higher education. 

The scheme offers a monthly stipend to eligible beneficiaries. 

The assistance is intended to support their educational expenses. 

Eligibility is restricted to girl students who are residents of Tamil Nadu. 

Applicants must be enrolled in higher secondary schools or colleges. 

The scheme is specifically for those from economically weaker sections. 

The application process involves submitting an online application through the official portal. 

Documents required include proof of residence and educational enrollment. 

The scheme is implemented by the Tamil Nadu Department of School Education. 

The financial assistance is disbursed monthly. 

The scheme aims to reduce dropout rates among girls in education. 

It also seeks to encourage families to prioritize girls' education. 

The initiative reflects the government's commitment to gender equality in education.
