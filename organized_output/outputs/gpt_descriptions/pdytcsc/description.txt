The Pradhan Mantri Dakshta Aur Yogyata Samman (PDYTC) scheme is designed to enhance the skills and capabilities of youth in India. 

The objective of the scheme is to provide skill development and training to young individuals. 

The benefits include access to skill development programs that improve employability. 

Eligibility criteria typically include being a youth aged between 15 to 29 years. 

The application process generally involves online registration through the official portal. 

The timeline for the program may vary based on specific training modules offered. 

The implementing authorities include the Ministry of Skill Development and Entrepreneurship. 

Additional details regarding specific training programs and locations may be available on the official website.
