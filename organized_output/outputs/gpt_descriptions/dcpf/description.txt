The DCPF scheme stands for the Direct Cash Payment Scheme. 

This scheme is designed to provide financial assistance directly to the beneficiaries. 

It aims to alleviate poverty and improve the living standards of economically weaker sections of society. 

The DCPF scheme is part of the broader initiative by the Indian government to promote social welfare. 

The context of the DCPF scheme is rooted in the need to ensure that financial aid reaches the intended recipients without any delays or intermediaries. 

The government recognized that traditional methods of distributing welfare benefits often led to leakages and inefficiencies. 

Thus the DCPF scheme was introduced to streamline the process of delivering financial assistance. 

The primary objective of the DCPF scheme is to provide timely financial support to those in need. 

This support is aimed at empowering individuals and families to meet their basic needs. 

The scheme also seeks to promote financial inclusion by encouraging beneficiaries to open bank accounts.
