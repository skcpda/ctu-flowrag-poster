The PMAY was launched in June 2015. 

It aims to provide affordable housing to the urban poor. 

The objective is to ensure housing for all by the year 2022. 

The scheme offers financial assistance for the construction or enhancement of houses. 

Beneficiaries can receive a subsidy on home loan interest rates. 

The assistance is provided to economically weaker sections and low-income groups. 

Eligibility is based on income criteria and family size. 

The scheme is applicable to individuals who do not own a pucca house. 

The application process involves submitting an online application through the PMAY website. 

Documents required include identity proof and income proof. 

The timeline for approval can vary based on the application volume. 

The Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs is the implementing authority. 

State governments and urban local bodies also play a role in implementation. 

The scheme is part of the larger initiative to improve urban infrastructure and housing. 

It aims to enhance the quality of life for urban residents. 

The PMAY also promotes the use of sustainable and eco-friendly building materials. 

Beneficiaries are encouraged to adopt modern construction techniques. 

The scheme has provisions for the construction of houses in rural areas under PMAY-Gramin.
