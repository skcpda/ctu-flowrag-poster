The Mahatma Gandhi National Rural Employment Guarantee Act is also known as MGNREGA. 

This scheme was launched in 2005 to enhance livelihood security in rural areas. 

The primary objective is to provide at least 100 days of guaranteed wage employment in a financial year to every rural household. 

The benefits include the right to work and the provision of minimum wages for unskilled manual work. 

Assistance is provided in the form of employment opportunities and skill development. 

Eligibility is limited to every rural household that applies for work under the scheme. 

The application process involves submitting a demand for work to the local Gram Panchayat. 

The timeline for employment is within 15 days of the application. 

The implementing authority is the Ministry of Rural Development at the central level. 

State governments are responsible for the execution of the scheme at the state and local levels. 

Additional details include provisions for social audits and grievance redressal mechanisms.
