The Digital Sewa Portal for Migrant Workers Scheme is known as DSPMDSY. 

This scheme was launched by the Government of India to address the challenges faced by migrant workers. 

Migrant workers often face various issues such as lack of access to information and services. 

The scheme aims to provide a digital platform that facilitates the welfare of these workers. 

It seeks to empower migrant workers by providing them with essential services and information. 

The objectives of DSPMDSY include enhancing the welfare of migrant workers. 

It aims to ensure that these workers have access to various government services. 

The scheme also focuses on providing timely information regarding job opportunities. 

Another objective is to create a database of migrant workers for better policy formulation. 

The benefits of DSPMDSY are multifaceted. 

Migrant workers can access a variety of services through the digital portal. 

These services include information on employment opportunities. 

Workers can also find details about skill development programs. 

The portal provides information about housing and accommodation facilities. 

Health services and welfare schemes are also accessible through this platform. 

The scheme aims to reduce the exploitation of migrant workers by providing them with information. 

Workers can receive timely updates about their rights and entitlements. 

The assistance provided under this scheme is crucial for the socio-economic upliftment of migrant workers. 

Eligibility for the DSPMDSY is primarily focused on migrant workers. 

Any individual who is a migrant worker can register on the portal. 

There are no specific income criteria mentioned for eligibility. 

The application process for DSPMDSY is designed to be user-friendly. 

Migrant workers can visit the Digital Sewa Portal online. 

They need to create an account by providing necessary details. 

The registration process requires basic information such as name and contact details. 

Once registered, workers can access various services offered by the portal. 

The application process is straightforward and does not require extensive documentation. 

The timeline for accessing services through DSPMDSY is immediate upon registration. 

Once a worker registers on the portal, they can start using the services right away. 

The frequency of updates on the portal is regular to ensure timely information.
