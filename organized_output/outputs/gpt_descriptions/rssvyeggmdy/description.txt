The scheme is known as the Rajiv Gandhi Scheme for Empowerment of Adolescent Girls or RSSVYEGMDY. 

It was launched by the Ministry of Women and Child Development. 

The scheme aims to empower adolescent girls aged 11 to 18 years. 

It focuses on improving their nutritional and health status. 

The objectives include enhancing their self-esteem and life skills. 

The scheme provides benefits such as nutritional support and health education. 

It also offers skills training and access to vocational opportunities. 

Eligibility is primarily for adolescent girls in the specified age group. 

The application process involves registration through local Anganwadi centers. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Women and Child Development. 

It is executed at the state and district levels through various agencies. 

The timeline for benefits is ongoing as per the program's structure. 

The scheme also emphasizes community participation and awareness.
