The SFG scheme stands for the Self-Financing Group scheme. 

It aims to promote self-employment among individuals in rural areas. 

The objective is to provide financial assistance for setting up small businesses. 

The scheme offers benefits in the form of loans and grants. 

Eligible beneficiaries include individuals from economically weaker sections. 

The application process involves submitting a proposal to the concerned authorities. 

The timeline for processing applications may vary based on the implementing agency. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Rural Development. 

Additional details about specific financial limits and repayment terms may vary.
