MMPYU stands for Mukhyamantri Poshak Yojana for Unemployed Youth.

It aims to provide financial assistance to unemployed youth in the state.

The objective is to support the youth in acquiring skills and employment opportunities.

The scheme offers financial aid for skill development and vocational training.

Eligible beneficiaries are unemployed youth who meet specific criteria set by the state government.

The application process typically involves submitting an application form along with necessary documents to the designated authorities.

The timeline for processing applications may vary based on the state’s administrative procedures.

The scheme is implemented by the respective state government departments responsible for employment and skill development.

Additional details such as specific financial assistance amounts or training programs may vary by state.
