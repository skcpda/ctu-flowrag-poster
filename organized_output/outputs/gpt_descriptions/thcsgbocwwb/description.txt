The scheme is known as the "Thalassemia Control and Sickle Cell Anemia Control Programme" under the "Government of India" initiative. 

The objective of the scheme is to control and manage thalassemia and sickle cell anemia in the population. 

The benefits include awareness programs screening services and treatment support for affected individuals. 

Eligibility criteria include individuals diagnosed with thalassemia or sickle cell anemia. 

The application process involves registration at designated health facilities or through government health programs. 

The timeline for the implementation of the scheme is ongoing with regular updates and activities. 

The implementing authorities include the Ministry of Health and Family Welfare and state health departments. 

The scheme aims to reduce the incidence of these genetic disorders through preventive measures and treatment.
