The Tamil Nadu Chief Minister's Breakfast Scheme (TNCMBS) was launched to address malnutrition among school children. 

The objective is to provide nutritious breakfast to children studying in government schools. 

The scheme aims to improve the health and educational outcomes of children by ensuring they receive a healthy meal at the start of the day. 

Benefits include the provision of a nutritious breakfast that meets dietary requirements for school children. 

The scheme targets students from classes I to V in government and government-aided schools. 

Eligibility is limited to children enrolled in these schools in Tamil Nadu. 

The application process is generally managed at the school level with no separate application required from parents. 

The breakfast is provided daily on school days. 

The scheme is implemented by the School Education Department of the Government of Tamil Nadu. 

The initiative also aims to enhance attendance and reduce dropout rates in schools. 

The scheme was launched in September 2021. 

It is part of a broader effort to combat child malnutrition and promote education in the state.
