The Pradhan Mantri Fasal Bima Yojana (PMFBY) was launched in 2016. 

It aims to provide financial support to farmers in the event of crop failure due to natural calamities. 

The scheme seeks to stabilize the income of farmers and encourage them to adopt modern agricultural practices. 

Benefits include coverage for crop loss due to adverse weather conditions pests and diseases. 

Farmers can receive compensation based on the value of the crop insured. 

The scheme covers all food crops oilseeds and annual commercial crops. 

Eligibility is open to all farmers including tenant farmers and sharecroppers. 

Farmers must have a bank account and must be registered under the scheme. 

The application process involves filling out a form and submitting it to the local agriculture office. 

Farmers must apply for insurance before the sowing of the crop. 

The premium rates are subsidized by the government. 

The scheme operates on a seasonal basis corresponding to the cropping season. 

The Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers' Welfare is the implementing authority. 

State governments also play a role in the implementation and outreach of the scheme. 

The scheme is reviewed annually to assess its effectiveness and make necessary adjustments.
