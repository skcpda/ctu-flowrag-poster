The Mukhyamantri Bhatta Vikas Scheme (MBVS) aims to provide financial assistance to the families of deceased workers in the unorganized sector. 

The objective of the scheme is to support the families of workers who have lost their lives while engaged in unorganized work. 

The benefits include a one-time financial assistance amount to the eligible families. 

Eligibility criteria include being a resident of the state and having a family member who was a worker in the unorganized sector and has died due to any cause. 

The application process typically involves submitting an application form along with necessary documents such as proof of death and identity proof of the applicant. 

The timeline for disbursing assistance may vary based on the processing of applications. 

The scheme is implemented by the respective state government departments responsible for labor and welfare. 

Additional details may include specific documentation requirements and the exact amount of financial assistance provided.
