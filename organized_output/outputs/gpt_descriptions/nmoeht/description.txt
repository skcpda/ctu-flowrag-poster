The National Mission for Clean Ganga is also known as the Namami Gange Programme. 

It was launched in 2014 by the Government of India. 

The mission aims to rejuvenate the Ganga River and improve its water quality. 

It focuses on pollution abatement and river conservation. 

The objectives include the creation of a sustainable ecosystem along the river. 

It also aims to promote public awareness and community participation. 

Benefits include improved water quality and enhanced biodiversity in the Ganga River. 

The mission provides financial assistance for various projects related to sewage treatment and riverfront development. 

Eligibility criteria vary based on specific projects and initiatives under the mission. 

The application process typically involves submitting project proposals to the relevant authorities. 

The timeline for project implementation can vary based on the scale and complexity of the project. 

The National Mission for Clean Ganga is implemented by the National Mission for Clean Ganga (NMCG). 

NMCG operates under the Ministry of Water Resources River Development and Ganga Rejuvenation. 

The mission also collaborates with state governments and local bodies for effective implementation. 

Public participation is encouraged through various awareness campaigns and initiatives. 

The mission is funded through the National Ganga River Basin Authority and other financial mechanisms.
