The Soil Health Management Scheme is known as SMAM. 

It was launched by the Government of India to promote sustainable agriculture. 

The objective is to enhance soil fertility and productivity. 

The scheme aims to provide farmers with information on soil health. 

It encourages the use of organic fertilizers and soil amendments. 

Farmers receive assistance for soil testing and analysis. 

The benefits include improved crop yields and sustainable farming practices. 

Eligibility is primarily for farmers engaged in agriculture. 

The application process involves registering through local agricultural offices. 

Farmers can also apply online through designated portals. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers' Welfare. 

State agricultural departments are responsible for execution at the state level. 

Soil testing services are provided periodically based on demand. 

The scheme promotes awareness through training and workshops for farmers. 

It also supports the establishment of soil health clinics. 

The initiative is part of a broader effort to improve agricultural sustainability.
