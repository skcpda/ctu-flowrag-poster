The State Public Service Scheme Goa is designed to provide financial assistance to eligible beneficiaries in the state of Goa. 

The objective of the scheme is to support individuals and families in need by providing them with a monthly financial aid. 

The benefits include a fixed monthly financial assistance amount to eligible beneficiaries. 

Eligibility criteria include being a resident of Goa and belonging to economically weaker sections of society. 

The application process involves submitting the required documents to the designated authorities in Goa. 

The timeline for processing applications may vary based on the volume of applications received. 

The implementing authority for this scheme is the Department of Social Welfare, Government of Goa. 

Additional details regarding specific financial amounts and documentation requirements can be obtained from official sources.
