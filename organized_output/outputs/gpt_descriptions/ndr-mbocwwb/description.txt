The National Database for Registration of Migrant Workers and Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board scheme is aimed at providing welfare measures for migrant workers and construction workers in India. 

The primary objective of the scheme is to create a comprehensive database of migrant workers and to ensure their access to various welfare benefits. 

The benefits include financial assistance for health care education and skill development. 

Eligibility criteria include being a migrant worker or a worker engaged in the building and construction sector. 

Workers must also be registered with the relevant state welfare board to avail of the benefits. 

The application process typically involves filling out a registration form and submitting it to the local welfare board. 

The timeline for processing applications may vary by state but is generally completed within a few weeks. 

The implementing authorities include the Ministry of Labour and Employment and respective state welfare boards. 

Additional details may include provisions for social security and insurance for registered workers.
