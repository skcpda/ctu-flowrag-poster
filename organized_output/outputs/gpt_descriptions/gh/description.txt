The Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana is a flagship housing scheme initiated by the Government of India. 

It was launched on June 25 2015. 

The scheme aims to provide affordable housing to the urban poor. 

It seeks to achieve the goal of "Housing for All" by the year 2022. 

The initiative is part of the larger vision of the Government of India to enhance urban infrastructure and improve living conditions. 

The scheme is divided into two components. 

The first component is for the Economically Weaker Sections. 

The second component is for the Low-Income Groups. 

The primary objective of the Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana is to provide financial assistance for housing. 

It aims to ensure that every citizen has access to a pucca house with basic amenities. 

The scheme focuses on providing housing to those who are homeless or living in inadequate housing conditions. 

Under this scheme the government provides interest subsidies on home loans. 

The subsidy is available for loans taken for the purchase or construction of houses. 

The interest subsidy can be up to 2.67 lakh rupees depending on the income category. 

Beneficiaries can also avail of financial assistance for the construction of houses. 

The scheme promotes the use of sustainable and eco-friendly building materials. 

It encourages the participation of private developers and builders. 

This is to ensure that housing units are built in a timely manner. 

The eligibility criteria for the Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana are defined based on income levels. 

Individuals or families belonging to the Economically Weaker Section are eligible. 

This includes those with an annual income of up to 3 lakh rupees. 

Low-Income Groups with an annual income of up to 6 lakh rupees can also apply. 

The scheme is open to both men and women. 

It encourages women to be the primary owners of the property. 

This is to promote gender equality in property ownership.

Interested applicants can apply online through the official website.

State governments and urban local bodies also facilitate the application process.
