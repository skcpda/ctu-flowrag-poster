The scheme is known as the Pradhan Mantri Samagra Shiksha Abhiyan.

It aims to provide quality education to all children from pre-school to senior secondary level.

The objective is to improve the quality of education and enhance the learning outcomes of students.

The scheme provides financial assistance for infrastructure development and teacher training.

It also focuses on the holistic development of children through various interventions.

Eligibility criteria include all schools recognized by the respective state governments.

The application process involves submission of proposals by state governments to the Ministry of Education.

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Education in collaboration with state governments.

The funding is provided on a sharing basis between the central and state governments.

The scheme is an ongoing initiative with no specific end date.
