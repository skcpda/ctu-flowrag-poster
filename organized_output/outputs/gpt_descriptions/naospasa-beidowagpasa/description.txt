The Naospasa-Beidowagpasa scheme is designed to provide financial assistance to widows in India. 

The objective of the scheme is to empower widows by providing them with a source of income. 

The benefits include a monthly pension to support their livelihood. 

Eligibility is typically limited to widows who are residents of India and meet specific income criteria. 

The application process usually involves submitting an application form along with necessary documents to the local government office. 

The timeline for disbursement of benefits is monthly. 

The implementing authority for this scheme is the Ministry of Women and Child Development. 

Additional details regarding specific income limits or required documentation may vary by state.
