The SCS Goa scheme is a welfare initiative launched by the Government of Goa. 

It aims to provide financial assistance to Scheduled Castes families in the state. 

The objective is to improve the socio-economic conditions of Scheduled Castes communities. 

Benefits include direct financial support for education housing and self-employment. 

Eligibility is limited to families belonging to Scheduled Castes in Goa. 

The application process involves filling out a prescribed application form available at designated government offices. 

Applicants may need to provide relevant documents such as caste certificates and income proofs. 

The timeline for disbursement of benefits may vary based on the specific assistance being sought. 

The scheme is implemented by the Department of Social Welfare Goa. 

Additional details regarding specific benefits and assistance amounts may vary.
