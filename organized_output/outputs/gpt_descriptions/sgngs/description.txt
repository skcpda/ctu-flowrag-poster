The Sardar Gandhi National Employment Guarantee Scheme (SGNGS) is a government initiative aimed at providing employment to rural households. 

The scheme was launched to enhance livelihood security in rural areas. 

Its primary objective is to provide at least 100 days of guaranteed wage employment in a financial year to every rural household whose adult members volunteer to do unskilled manual work. 

Benefits include wage payments at the statutory minimum wage rate. 

The scheme also aims to create durable assets in rural areas and enhance livelihood security. 

Eligibility is limited to adult members of rural households. 

Households must apply for the scheme through local government offices. 

The application process involves submitting a job card application to the Gram Panchayat. 

The scheme operates throughout the year. 

The Ministry of Rural Development is the implementing authority for SGNGS. 

State governments are responsible for the implementation at the local level. 

The scheme also promotes social inclusion by providing employment to marginalized groups. 

Periodic audits and evaluations are conducted to ensure transparency and accountability.
