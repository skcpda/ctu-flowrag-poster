The OELS scheme stands for the Online Employment Linked Scheme. 

This scheme was launched to provide employment opportunities to the youth in India. 

The primary objective is to link employment opportunities with skill development initiatives. 

The scheme aims to enhance the employability of individuals by providing them with necessary training and job placements. 

Eligible beneficiaries include unemployed youth who are seeking employment. 

The application process typically involves registering on the official OELS portal. 

Applicants may need to provide personal details and educational qualifications during registration. 

The scheme may have a timeline for job placements based on the availability of opportunities. 

Implementing authorities include the Ministry of Labour and Employment and various state governments. 

The scheme also collaborates with private sector organizations for job placements. 

Additional details regarding specific benefits or assistance may vary based on state implementations.
