The MSME (Micro Small and Medium Enterprises) scheme aims to promote entrepreneurship and support small businesses in India. 

The objective is to enhance the competitiveness of MSMEs and facilitate their growth. 

The scheme provides financial assistance for various purposes including technology upgradation and marketing support. 

Benefits include access to credit, subsidies, and training programs for entrepreneurs. 

Eligibility is generally based on the size of the enterprise as defined by the MSME Development Act. 

The application process typically involves submitting an online application along with required documents. 

The timeline for approval and disbursement of funds may vary based on the specific assistance sought. 

The Ministry of Micro Small and Medium Enterprises is the primary implementing authority for this scheme. 

Additional details may include specific programs under the MSME scheme such as credit guarantee schemes and skill development initiatives.
