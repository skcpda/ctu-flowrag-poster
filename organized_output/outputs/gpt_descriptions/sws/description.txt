The SWS scheme stands for the Scheme for Welfare of Street Vendors. 

It was launched to provide support and assistance to street vendors in India. 

The primary objective is to enhance the livelihood and working conditions of street vendors. 

The scheme aims to provide financial assistance and access to credit facilities. 

It also focuses on skill development and capacity building for street vendors. 

Eligible beneficiaries include street vendors who are engaged in vending activities. 

They must be registered with the local authorities to avail the benefits. 

The application process involves submitting an application to the local municipal body. 

The application must include necessary documents such as identity proof and proof of vending activity. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs. 

The timeline for disbursement of benefits may vary based on the local authorities. 

The scheme also encourages the formation of vendor associations for better representation. 

It promotes the integration of street vendors into the urban economy. 

The SWS scheme is part of the larger initiative to support informal sector workers.
