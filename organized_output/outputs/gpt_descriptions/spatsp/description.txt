The SPATSP scheme stands for the Scheme for Promotion of Agricultural Technology and Skill Development Program. 

It aims to enhance agricultural productivity and promote skill development among farmers. 

The objectives include improving the adoption of modern agricultural practices and technologies. 

The scheme provides financial assistance for training programs and workshops. 

It also offers support for the purchase of agricultural equipment and tools. 

Eligibility is primarily for farmers and agricultural laborers. 

The application process typically involves submitting a proposal to the relevant agricultural department. 

The timeline for assistance may vary based on the specific training or equipment procurement. 

The implementing authority is the Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers' Welfare. 

Additional details may include partnerships with local agricultural universities and institutions for training.
