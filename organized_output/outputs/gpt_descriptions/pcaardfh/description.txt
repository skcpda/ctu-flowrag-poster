The PCAARDHF scheme stands for the Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samman Nidhi for Agricultural and Rural Development through Farmers' Health. 

This scheme is part of the Indian government's initiative to support farmers and improve their health and well-being. 

The primary objective is to provide financial assistance to farmers for health-related expenses. 

The scheme aims to enhance the overall quality of life for farmers by ensuring they have access to necessary healthcare services. 

Eligible beneficiaries include small and marginal farmers who are registered under the PM-KISAN scheme. 

Farmers must have a valid landholding and meet the criteria set by the government to qualify for assistance. 

The application process generally involves registering through the PM-KISAN portal. 

Farmers may need to provide relevant documents such as land records and identification proof. 

The assistance is provided on a periodic basis as determined by the implementing authorities. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers' Welfare. 

State governments may also play a role in the execution and monitoring of the scheme at the local level. 

Additional details regarding the specific amount of financial assistance and frequency of disbursement may vary based on government notifications.
