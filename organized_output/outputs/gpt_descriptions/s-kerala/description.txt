The S-Kerala scheme is an initiative by the Government of Kerala. 

It aims to provide financial assistance to the elderly population in the state. 

The objective is to ensure social security and improve the quality of life for senior citizens. 

Benefits include a monthly pension for eligible elderly individuals. 

The amount of assistance varies based on the individual's financial status and age. 

Eligibility criteria include being a resident of Kerala and being above a certain age, typically 60 years. 

The application process involves submitting an application form along with necessary documents to the local authorities. 

The scheme is implemented by the Department of Social Justice in Kerala. 

The frequency of assistance is monthly. 

Additional details about the scheme can be found on the official government website.
