The "National Family Benefit Scheme" is a welfare initiative launched by the Government of India. 

This scheme is aimed at providing financial assistance to families in the event of the death of the primary breadwinner. 

The scheme recognizes the economic challenges that families face when they lose their main source of income. 

It is particularly focused on families living below the poverty line. 

The National Family Benefit Scheme is part of the broader social security measures implemented by the government. 

The objective of this scheme is to alleviate the financial burden on families after the loss of a breadwinner. 

The scheme aims to ensure that families can cope with the immediate financial shock caused by such a loss. 

The financial assistance provided under this scheme is intended to help families meet their basic needs. 

This includes expenses related to food, education, and healthcare. 

The assistance is provided in the form of a one-time cash benefit. 

The amount of financial assistance is set at a specific sum.

Eligibility for the National Family Benefit Scheme is primarily based on the socioeconomic status of the family. 

Families must be identified as living below the poverty line to qualify for the scheme. 

Additionally, the deceased must have been the primary breadwinner of the family. 

The scheme is applicable to families across all states and union territories in India.
