The SPADAP scheme stands for the Scheme for Promotion of Agricultural Diversification and Adaptation to Climate Change. 

It was launched to enhance the resilience of farmers against climate change impacts. 

The objective is to promote sustainable agricultural practices and diversify agricultural production. 

The scheme aims to improve the livelihoods of farmers by encouraging the adoption of climate-resilient crops and practices. 

Benefits include financial assistance for adopting new agricultural practices and technologies. 

Farmers may receive training and support for implementing sustainable agricultural methods. 

Eligibility is primarily for farmers engaged in agriculture in climate-affected regions. 

The application process typically involves submitting a proposal to the relevant agricultural department. 

The timeline for assistance may vary based on the specific project and region. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers' Welfare at the central level. 

State agricultural departments may also play a role in local implementation and support.
