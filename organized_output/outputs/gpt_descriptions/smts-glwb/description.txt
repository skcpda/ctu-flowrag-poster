The scheme SMTS-GLWB stands for the Scheme for Management of Tribal Schools and Government Livelihood and Welfare Boards. 

It aims to enhance the quality of education and livelihood opportunities for tribal communities in India. 

The objectives include improving educational infrastructure and providing vocational training to tribal youth. 

Benefits include financial assistance for educational institutions and support for skill development programs. 

Eligibility criteria typically include being a member of a Scheduled Tribe and residing in designated tribal areas. 

The application process generally involves submitting an application to the local government or relevant authority. 

The timeline for implementation and frequency of benefits may vary based on specific program components. 

Implementing authorities include the Ministry of Tribal Affairs and state governments. 

Additional details may include collaboration with NGOs and community organizations for effective implementation.
