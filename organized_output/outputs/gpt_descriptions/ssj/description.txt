The SSJ scheme stands for the Samarth Samudayik Jivan scheme. 

It aims to provide financial assistance to marginalized communities in India. 

The objective is to enhance the livelihood opportunities for the underprivileged sections of society. 

The scheme offers various benefits including skill development training and financial support for self-employment. 

The assistance provided includes grants for starting small businesses and vocational training programs. 

Eligibility criteria typically include being a member of a marginalized community and meeting specific income thresholds. 

The application process usually involves submitting a form along with required documents to the designated authority. 

The timeline for processing applications may vary based on the implementing agency. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment. 

Additional details regarding specific benefits or training programs may vary by state.
