The Samruddhi Scheme is an initiative by the Government of India aimed at enhancing the income of farmers. 

The scheme focuses on promoting sustainable agricultural practices and improving productivity. 

Its primary objective is to provide financial assistance and support to farmers for adopting modern farming techniques. 

The benefits include access to quality seeds fertilizers and modern irrigation facilities. 

Farmers can also receive training on best agricultural practices and market linkages. 

Eligibility criteria include being a registered farmer and having a valid landholding. 

The application process typically involves submitting an application form to the local agricultural office. 

The scheme may have specific timelines for application submission depending on the agricultural calendar. 

Implementing authorities include the Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers' Welfare at the central level and state agricultural departments at the state level. 

The scheme aims to improve the overall livelihood of farmers and promote agricultural sustainability.
