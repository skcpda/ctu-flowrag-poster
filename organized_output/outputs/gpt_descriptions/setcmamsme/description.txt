The Scheme for the Promotion of MSMEs in North Eastern Region (SPM MSME) aims to promote the growth and development of Micro Small and Medium Enterprises in the North Eastern Region of India. 

The objective is to enhance the competitiveness and sustainability of MSMEs in the region. 

The scheme provides financial assistance for various activities such as capacity building, technology support, and marketing. 

Benefits include access to credit, subsidies for technology upgrades, and support for skill development. 

Eligibility is primarily for Micro Small and Medium Enterprises operating in the North Eastern Region. 

The application process involves submitting a proposal to the concerned implementing agency along with necessary documents. 

The timeline for approval and disbursement of funds varies based on the specific activity and proposal submitted. 

The Ministry of Micro Small and Medium Enterprises is the primary implementing authority for this scheme. 

Additional details include the focus on creating employment opportunities and enhancing the overall economic development of the North Eastern Region.
