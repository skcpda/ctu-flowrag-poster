The Pradhan Mantri Crop Insurance Scheme (PM-CIS) was launched to provide financial support to farmers in the event of crop failure. 

The objective of the scheme is to stabilize farmers' income and ensure their financial security. 

It aims to encourage farmers to adopt innovative and modern agricultural practices. 

The scheme provides insurance coverage and financial support in the event of crop loss due to natural calamities, pests, and diseases. 

Farmers can receive compensation for crop losses based on the area and extent of damage. 

Eligibility for the scheme includes all farmers who own land and cultivate crops. 

The application process involves farmers enrolling through designated banks or insurance companies. 

Farmers must submit relevant documents and details about their land and crops. 

The scheme operates on a yearly basis with farmers required to enroll before the sowing season. 

The Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers' Welfare is the implementing authority for this scheme. 

State governments also play a crucial role in the implementation and outreach of the scheme. 

The premium rates for the insurance are subsidized by the government to make it affordable for farmers. 

Farmers can receive compensation within a specified period after the assessment of crop loss.
