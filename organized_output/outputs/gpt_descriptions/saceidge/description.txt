The SACEIDGE scheme aims to provide financial assistance for the education of children from economically weaker sections. 

It focuses on enhancing educational opportunities and reducing dropout rates among underprivileged students. 

The benefits include scholarships and financial support for tuition fees and educational materials. 

Eligibility is primarily based on the income level of the family and the educational status of the child. 

The application process typically involves submitting an online application along with required documents such as income certificates and educational records. 

The scheme is usually implemented on an annual basis with applications accepted during a specific period each year. 

The implementing authorities include the Ministry of Education and various state education departments. 

Additional details may include provisions for monitoring and evaluation of the scheme's impact on educational outcomes.
