The SOKCOS scheme is an initiative aimed at providing financial assistance to the underprivileged sections of society. 

The primary objective of SOKCOS is to enhance the economic condition of marginalized communities. 

The scheme offers monetary support to eligible beneficiaries to help them meet their basic needs. 

Eligibility for the SOKCOS scheme typically includes individuals from low-income families or marginalized groups. 

The application process involves submitting the required documents to the designated authorities. 

The timeline for disbursement of funds under the SOKCOS scheme is determined by the implementing agencies. 

The scheme is implemented by state governments in collaboration with local authorities. 

Further details on specific benefits and assistance may vary by state.
