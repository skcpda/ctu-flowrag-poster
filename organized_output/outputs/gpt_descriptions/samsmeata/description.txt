Samsmeata is a government welfare scheme aimed at promoting the welfare of Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes in India. 

The objective of the scheme is to provide financial assistance for the education and skill development of individuals from these communities. 

The benefits include scholarships for students and financial support for vocational training programs. 

Eligibility criteria typically include being a member of a Scheduled Caste or Scheduled Tribe and meeting specific income thresholds. 

The application process generally involves submitting an application form along with necessary documents to the designated authority. 

The timeline for disbursement of funds may vary depending on the specific program under the scheme. 

Implementing authorities include the Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment and various state government departments. 

Additional details may include specific training programs or educational institutions associated with the scheme.
