The SRE-RANDR scheme is an initiative by the Government of India. 

It aims to provide financial assistance to beneficiaries affected by natural disasters. 

The objective is to ensure timely relief and rehabilitation for affected individuals and families. 

The scheme offers monetary support for rebuilding homes and restoring livelihoods. 

Eligibility is generally based on being a resident of the affected area and suffering losses due to a natural disaster. 

The application process typically involves submitting a request to the local authorities or designated agencies. 

The timeline for assistance is contingent upon the assessment of damages and the approval of applications. 

Implementing authorities include state governments and local administrative bodies. 

The scheme is part of a broader effort to enhance disaster resilience in vulnerable communities.
