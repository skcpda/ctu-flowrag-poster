The Petro Subsidy for Disabled is a welfare scheme initiated by the Government of India. 

The scheme aims to provide financial assistance to persons with disabilities for their transportation needs. 

The objective is to alleviate the financial burden of fuel costs for disabled individuals. 

Eligible beneficiaries include persons with disabilities who possess a valid disability certificate. 

The application process involves submitting the required documents to the designated government authority. 

The timeline for processing applications may vary based on the implementing agency. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment.
