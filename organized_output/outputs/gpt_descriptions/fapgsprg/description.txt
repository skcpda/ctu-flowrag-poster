The FAPGSPR scheme stands for the Financial Assistance for Persons with Disabilities and Special Needs Program.

This scheme is part of the broader initiatives taken by the Government of India to support individuals with disabilities and special needs.

The context of this scheme arises from the recognition of the challenges faced by persons with disabilities in accessing resources and opportunities.

The Indian government has been actively working to create an inclusive society where individuals with disabilities can lead dignified lives.

This assistance aims to improve their quality of life and promote their independence.

The scheme also seeks to empower individuals with disabilities by providing them with the necessary resources to pursue education and vocational training.

Benefits under the FAPGSPR scheme include direct financial support to eligible individuals.

This financial assistance can be utilized for various purposes such as medical expenses, educational needs, and assistive devices.

The scheme may also cover costs related to rehabilitation and skill development programs.
