The Quality Control of India Scheme (QCIS) aims to enhance the quality of products manufactured in India. 

It focuses on ensuring that goods meet specified standards and regulations. 

The objectives include promoting quality assurance and improving consumer confidence in Indian products. 

Benefits include access to quality testing facilities and certification for manufacturers. 

Assistance is provided in the form of financial support for quality improvement initiatives. 

Eligibility criteria typically include being a registered manufacturer in India. 

The application process involves submitting an application to the relevant authority along with necessary documentation. 

The timeline for processing applications may vary based on the specific requirements of the scheme. 

Implementing authorities include the Bureau of Indian Standards and other relevant government agencies. 

The scheme also encourages participation from industry stakeholders to foster a culture of quality.
