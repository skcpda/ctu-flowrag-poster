The Mukhyamantri Gopabandhu Balika Yojana (MGBBY) is a welfare scheme launched by the Government of Odisha. 

It aims to provide financial assistance for the education and empowerment of girl children in the state. 

The primary objective is to reduce dropout rates among girls and promote their education. 

Under this scheme, financial assistance is provided to girls from economically weaker sections. 

The assistance includes a one-time financial grant for educational purposes. 

Eligibility is limited to girl children from families with an annual income below a specified threshold. 

The scheme targets girls who are enrolled in schools and are in need of financial support for their education. 

The application process involves submitting an application form along with necessary documents to the concerned authorities. 

The application can typically be submitted online or through designated offices. 

The timeline for disbursement of funds is usually aligned with the academic calendar. 

The scheme is implemented by the Department of Women and Child Development, Government of Odisha. 

Additional details may include provisions for monitoring the educational progress of beneficiaries.
