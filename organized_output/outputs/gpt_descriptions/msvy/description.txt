Mahatma Gandhi Sarva Shiksha Abhiyan Yojana (MSVY) is an initiative aimed at promoting education in rural areas of India. 

The objective of MSVY is to ensure that every child in the age group of 6 to 14 years has access to quality education. 

The scheme focuses on improving the infrastructure of schools and enhancing the quality of teaching. 

Benefits include financial assistance for school infrastructure development and training for teachers. 

Eligibility is primarily for children aged 6 to 14 years from economically weaker sections in rural areas. 

The application process involves registration through local education authorities or schools. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Education at the national level and state education departments at the state level. 

The timeline for the implementation of the scheme is ongoing with periodic assessments and updates. 

Additional details may include collaboration with local bodies and NGOs to enhance educational outreach.
