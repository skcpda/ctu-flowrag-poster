The scheme is known as the Swamitva Scheme or Survey of Villages and Mapping with Improvised Technology in Village Areas (SRDSY). 

It was launched by the Government of India in April 2020. 

The primary objective is to provide an integrated property validation solution for rural areas. 

It aims to promote transparency in property ownership and reduce property disputes. 

The scheme also seeks to empower rural residents by providing them with property rights. 

Benefits include the issuance of property cards to villagers. 

This helps in availing loans and financial assistance from banks. 

Eligibility is primarily for residents of rural areas who own land. 

The application process involves a survey conducted by designated authorities. 

Villagers are informed about the survey dates and procedures. 

The timeline for the survey is determined by the implementing authorities. 

The Ministry of Panchayati Raj is the main implementing authority for this scheme. 

State governments also play a crucial role in execution and monitoring. 

The scheme utilizes modern technology such as drones for mapping. 

It enhances the accuracy of land records and property ownership data. 

The initiative contributes to the overall development of rural infrastructure. 

It also supports the Digital India initiative by digitizing land records.
