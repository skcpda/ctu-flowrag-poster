The Skaaeti scheme is an initiative by the Government of India aimed at promoting sustainable agriculture practices. 

The primary objective of the scheme is to enhance the income of farmers through the adoption of eco-friendly agricultural techniques. 

The scheme provides financial assistance for the purchase of organic seeds and other necessary inputs. 

It also offers training programs to educate farmers about sustainable farming practices. 

Eligibility for the scheme includes farmers who are registered under the Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samman Nidhi scheme. 

Farmers must also be engaged in agricultural activities and demonstrate a commitment to adopting sustainable practices. 

The application process involves submitting a form to the local agricultural department along with necessary documentation. 

The timeline for processing applications may vary but is generally completed within a few weeks. 

The implementing authority for the Skaaeti scheme is the Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers' Welfare. 

Additional details about the scheme can be found on the official government website.
