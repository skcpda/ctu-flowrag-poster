The "T-Wallet" scheme is an initiative by the Government of Telangana. 

It aims to provide a digital wallet for citizens to facilitate cashless transactions. 

The objective is to promote digital payments and enhance financial inclusion. 

Benefits include easy access to various government services and payment options. 

Users can make payments for utility bills and government services through the wallet. 

Eligibility is open to all residents of Telangana who possess a mobile phone. 

The application process involves downloading the T-Wallet app from the official website or app store. 

Users must complete the registration process by providing necessary details. 

The T-Wallet can be used for transactions at any time. 

The scheme is implemented by the Government of Telangana through the Department of Information Technology. 

Additional features include the ability to transfer money and receive payments. 

The T-Wallet also supports various payment methods including UPI and QR code scanning.
