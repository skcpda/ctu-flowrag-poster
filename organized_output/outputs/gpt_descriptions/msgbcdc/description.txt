The Mukhyamantri Swavalamban Yojana for Backward Classes Development Corporation is a welfare scheme initiated by the Government of India. 

It aims to provide financial assistance and support to individuals from backward classes. 

The objective is to promote self-employment and entrepreneurship among these communities. 

The scheme offers financial assistance in the form of subsidies for various business ventures. 

It also provides skill development training to enhance employability. 

Eligibility is primarily based on the applicant's caste status as a member of a backward class. 

Applicants must also meet specific income criteria set by the government. 

The application process involves submitting a form along with required documents to the designated authority. 

The timeline for processing applications may vary based on the volume of applications received. 

The scheme is implemented by the Backward Classes Development Corporation at the state level. 

Additional details regarding specific financial limits and training programs may be available through official government sources.
