The Mukhyamantri Swavalamban Yojana (MSVEAY) is a scheme initiated by the Government of Rajasthan. 

It aims to promote self-employment among the youth of the state. 

The objective is to provide financial assistance for setting up small businesses and enterprises. 

The scheme offers a subsidy on the investment made in the business. 

Eligible beneficiaries include youth aged between 18 to 35 years. 

They must be residents of Rajasthan and have a viable business plan. 

The application process involves submitting an online application through the official website. 

Applicants need to provide necessary documents such as identity proof and business plan. 

The scheme is implemented by the Department of Skill, Employment and Entrepreneurship, Government of Rajasthan. 

The subsidy is provided in a one-time payment after the approval of the business plan. 

The frequency of assistance depends on the completion of the application process and approval timelines. 

Additional details regarding specific subsidy amounts and business sectors may vary.
