The Mukhyamantri Poshak Yojana is a welfare scheme launched by the Government of Madhya Pradesh. 

It aims to provide school uniforms to children from economically weaker sections. 

The objective is to enhance school attendance and promote education among underprivileged children. 

The scheme benefits students enrolled in government schools from classes 1 to 8. 

Eligible beneficiaries include students belonging to families with an annual income below a specified threshold. 

The application process typically involves enrollment in a government school and verification of income criteria. 

Uniforms are provided once a year at the beginning of the academic session. 

The scheme is implemented by the Department of School Education, Madhya Pradesh. 

It is part of the state's broader initiative to improve educational outcomes and reduce dropout rates.
