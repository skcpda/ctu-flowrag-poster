The scheme SVSTDP is the Skill Vikas and Self-Employment Training and Development Program. 

It aims to enhance the employability of youth through skill development. 

The program focuses on providing training in various trades and self-employment opportunities. 

Benefits include skill training and support for self-employment initiatives. 

Eligibility criteria typically include youth aged between 18 to 35 years. 

The application process usually involves filling out a form and submitting it to designated training centers. 

The timeline for training programs may vary based on the specific trade and center. 

Implementing authorities include the Ministry of Skill Development and Entrepreneurship. 

Additional details about specific training modules and locations may be available through local training centers.
