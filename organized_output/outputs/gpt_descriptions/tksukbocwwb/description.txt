The scheme TKSUKBOCWWB stands for the Telangana State Unorganized Workers' Compensation and Welfare Board. 

It was launched to provide social security and welfare benefits to unorganized workers in Telangana. 

The main objective is to ensure financial assistance and support for unorganized workers during times of need. 

Benefits include financial aid for medical emergencies and educational assistance for their children. 

The scheme also provides pension schemes for elderly unorganized workers. 

Eligibility criteria include being a resident of Telangana and being engaged in unorganized work. 

Applicants must be registered under the Telangana State Unorganized Workers' Compensation and Welfare Board. 

The application process involves filling out a form and submitting it to the local welfare office. 

The timeline for processing applications may vary based on the specific benefit being applied for. 

The implementing authority for this scheme is the Telangana State Unorganized Workers' Compensation and Welfare Board. 

Additional details may include the need for periodic renewal of registration to continue receiving benefits.
