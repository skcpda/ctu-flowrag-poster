The Scheme for National Skill Development Mission (SNSU) aims to enhance the skill development landscape in India. 

The objective is to provide quality skill training to the youth and improve their employability. 

The scheme focuses on creating a skilled workforce that meets the demands of various sectors. 

Benefits include access to skill training programs and financial assistance for training providers. 

Eligibility criteria typically include youth aged between 15 to 29 years. 

The application process generally involves registration through designated training centers or online portals. 

The timeline for training programs varies based on the specific courses offered. 

The Ministry of Skill Development and Entrepreneurship is the primary implementing authority. 

The scheme also promotes partnerships with industry stakeholders for better job placement opportunities.
