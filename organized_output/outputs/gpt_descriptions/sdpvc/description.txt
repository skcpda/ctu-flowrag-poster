The Scheme for Development of PVC (SDPVC) aims to promote the use of polyvinyl chloride in various applications. 

The objective of the scheme is to enhance the production and utilization of PVC in the country. 

The benefits include financial assistance for setting up PVC production units and promoting research and development in PVC applications. 

Eligibility criteria include entities involved in the manufacturing of PVC products and those engaged in research related to PVC. 

The application process involves submitting a proposal to the relevant government department along with necessary documentation. 

The timeline for processing applications may vary depending on the complexity of the proposal. 

The Ministry of Chemicals and Fertilizers is the implementing authority for this scheme. 

Additional details regarding specific financial assistance amounts or project duration may be available through official government sources.
