Widushi is a welfare scheme launched by the Government of India. 

It aims to provide financial assistance to widows. 

The objective is to support widows in achieving financial independence. 

The scheme offers a monthly pension to eligible widows. 

The amount of assistance provided is Rs 1,000 per month. 

Eligibility criteria include being a widow aged 18 years or above. 

Applicants must be residents of India. 

The scheme targets widows from economically weaker sections. 

The application process involves submitting a form along with required documents. 

Documents typically include proof of widowhood and income certificate. 

The application can be submitted online or at designated government offices. 

The timeline for processing applications may vary by state. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Women and Child Development. 

State governments may also have their own specific guidelines. 

Regular monitoring and evaluation are conducted to ensure effective implementation.
