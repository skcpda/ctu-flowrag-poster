The SMS-MJ scheme is a government initiative aimed at providing financial assistance to marginalized communities in India. 

The primary objective of the scheme is to empower women and promote their economic independence. 

The scheme offers financial support to women entrepreneurs and self-help groups. 

Eligible beneficiaries include women from Scheduled Castes, Scheduled Tribes, and Other Backward Classes. 

The application process typically involves submitting a form along with required documents to the designated authority. 

The timeline for processing applications may vary based on the implementing agency. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Women and Child Development. 

Additional details regarding the specific benefits and assistance provided may vary based on the region and local government policies.
