The SPFASAE scheme stands for the Scheme for Promotion of Flagship Agricultural Schemes and Agricultural Extension. 

It aims to enhance the effectiveness of flagship agricultural schemes through improved agricultural extension services. 

The objectives include increasing agricultural productivity and promoting sustainable agricultural practices. 

The scheme provides financial assistance for capacity building and training of farmers. 

It also supports the development of agricultural infrastructure and technology dissemination. 

Eligibility criteria include farmers and agricultural extension workers engaged in agriculture-related activities. 

The application process involves submitting proposals through the designated state agricultural departments. 

The timeline for implementation varies based on the specific activities and projects undertaken under the scheme. 

The Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers' Welfare is the implementing authority for this scheme. 

Additional details regarding specific funding amounts or project durations may vary based on state guidelines.
