The MLC scheme refers to the Mahatma Gandhi National Rural Employment Guarantee Act. 

It aims to provide at least 100 days of guaranteed wage employment in a financial year to every rural household. 

The objective is to enhance livelihood security in rural areas. 

The scheme also aims to create durable assets and strengthen the livelihood resource base of rural households. 

Eligible beneficiaries are adult members of rural households. 

The application process involves submitting a job card application to the local Gram Panchayat. 

The job card is issued after verification of the application. 

Employment is provided within 15 days of the application. 

The implementing authority is the Ministry of Rural Development. 

Funds are allocated to states based on the number of job cards issued. 

The scheme is funded by the central and state governments. 

Wages are paid according to the minimum wage rates set by the respective state governments. 

The scheme is monitored through a decentralized structure involving local bodies. 

Regular audits and social audits are conducted to ensure transparency and accountability.
