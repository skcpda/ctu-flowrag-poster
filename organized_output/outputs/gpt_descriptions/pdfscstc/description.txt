The PDFSCSTC scheme is the Post Matric Scholarship for Scheduled Castes Students in India. 

It aims to provide financial assistance to Scheduled Caste students pursuing post-matriculation courses. 

The objective is to promote higher education among Scheduled Caste students and reduce dropout rates. 

Benefits include covering tuition fees and other related expenses for eligible students. 

The assistance amount varies based on the level of education and institution type. 

Eligibility is limited to students belonging to Scheduled Castes. 

Students must be enrolled in recognized post-matriculation courses. 

The application process typically involves submitting an online application along with required documents. 

The timeline for application submission usually aligns with the academic calendar. 

The implementing authority for this scheme is the Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment. 

State governments may also play a role in the implementation at the local level.
