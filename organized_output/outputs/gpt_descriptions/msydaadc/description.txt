The scheme MSYDAADC stands for the "Mahatma Gandhi National Rural Employment Guarantee Act" in the context of the Assam state government.

The scheme aims to provide guaranteed employment to rural households in Assam. 

It seeks to enhance livelihood security by providing at least 100 days of unskilled wage employment in a financial year. 

The benefits include wage payments for work done and the creation of durable assets in rural areas. 

Eligibility is primarily for rural households that are registered under the scheme. 

The application process involves registering with the local Gram Panchayat or relevant authority. 

The timeline for employment is based on the demand for work and can be availed throughout the year. 

The implementing authority is the Assam State Government in collaboration with the Ministry of Rural Development. 

Additional details include provisions for work-related grievances and the right to appeal against delays in wage payments.
