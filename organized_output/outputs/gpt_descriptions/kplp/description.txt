The KPLP scheme stands for the Kerala Livelihoods Mission. 

It was launched by the Government of Kerala to enhance the livelihoods of the rural population. 

The scheme aims to promote self-employment and sustainable livelihoods among the marginalized sections of society. 

It focuses on empowering women and disadvantaged groups through skill development and capacity building. 

The objectives of KPLP include reducing poverty and improving the quality of life for rural households. 

The scheme seeks to create a sustainable model for income generation through various livelihood activities. 

Benefits under the KPLP scheme include financial assistance for starting self-employment ventures. 

The scheme provides training programs to enhance skills and improve employability. 

It also facilitates access to credit and markets for the beneficiaries. 

Eligibility for the KPLP scheme primarily includes individuals from below-poverty-line households. 

Women self-help groups and marginalized communities are given priority under the scheme. 

The application process for KPLP involves submitting an application to the local self-government institutions. 

Beneficiaries must provide necessary documents to prove their eligibility. 

The local bodies assess the applications and forward them for approval. 

The timeline for processing applications may vary based on the local self-government's efficiency. 

Financial assistance is disbursed in a timely manner once the application is approved. 

The KPLP scheme is implemented by the Kerala State Rural Livelihoods Mission. 

This agency operates under the Department of Rural Development in Kerala. 

The mission collaborates with various stakeholders including NGOs and financial institutions. 

The scheme also promotes the formation of self-help groups to encourage savings and group activities. 

Capacity building workshops are conducted regularly to enhance the skills of participants.
