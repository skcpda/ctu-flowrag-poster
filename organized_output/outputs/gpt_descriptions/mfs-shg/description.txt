The MFS-SHG scheme is a government initiative aimed at promoting financial inclusion among Self-Help Groups in India. 

The objective of the scheme is to provide micro-finance support to Self-Help Groups for their income-generating activities. 

The benefits of the scheme include access to credit for members of Self-Help Groups. 

It also aims to enhance the livelihood opportunities for women and marginalized communities. 

Eligibility for the scheme typically includes being a member of a registered Self-Help Group. 

The application process involves submitting an application through the respective Self-Help Group to the concerned financial institution. 

The timeline for fund disbursement may vary based on the financial institution's processes. 

The scheme is implemented by various financial institutions in collaboration with state governments and local bodies. 

Additional details about the scheme can be found on official government websites.
