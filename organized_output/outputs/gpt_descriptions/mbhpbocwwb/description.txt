The scheme MBHPBOCWWB stands for the Madhya Pradesh Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board. 

It was established to provide welfare benefits to workers engaged in building and other construction activities in Madhya Pradesh. 

The primary objective is to ensure the welfare and social security of construction workers. 

The scheme offers various benefits including financial assistance for medical treatment, education of children, and housing. 

It also provides financial aid for the marriage of daughters and assistance for the purchase of tools. 

Eligibility for the scheme includes workers who are registered under the Madhya Pradesh Building and Other Construction Workers Act 1996. 

Applicants must be engaged in construction work and must meet the criteria set by the welfare board. 

The application process involves filling out a prescribed application form and submitting it to the local welfare board office. 

Supporting documents such as proof of identity and proof of employment must be attached. 

The timeline for processing applications may vary but is generally completed within a few weeks. 

The Madhya Pradesh Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board is the implementing authority for this scheme. 

Additional details regarding specific benefits and assistance may be available through the official website or local offices.
