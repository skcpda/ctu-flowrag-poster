The SEBPUMMAPUY scheme is designed to provide financial assistance to marginalized communities in India. 

It aims to enhance the livelihood opportunities for the poor and underprivileged sections of society. 

The scheme provides monetary support for various self-employment initiatives. 

Eligible beneficiaries include individuals from Scheduled Castes Scheduled Tribes and Other Backward Classes. 

The application process typically involves submitting a form along with necessary documentation to the local government office. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment at the national level. 

The frequency of assistance may vary based on the specific project or initiative under the scheme. 

Additional details regarding the specific financial assistance amount and project types may be available through local government offices.
