The Atal Pension Yojana (APY) is a government-backed pension scheme in India. 

It was launched by the Government of India on May 9, 2015. 

The scheme is aimed at providing financial security to the elderly population of India. 

It is particularly focused on those who work in the unorganized sector. 

The initiative was introduced in response to the growing need for social security among the aging population. 

The scheme is named after former Prime Minister Atal Bihari Vajpayee. 

The primary objective of the Atal Pension Yojana is to provide a fixed monthly pension to subscribers. 

The pension amount can range from Rs. 1,000 to Rs. 5,000 per month. 

The amount of pension depends on the contributions made by the subscribers. 

The scheme encourages individuals to save for their retirement. 

It aims to create a safety net for individuals who do not have access to formal pension schemes. 

The Atal Pension Yojana is designed to be simple and easy to understand. 

It is available to all Indian citizens aged between 18 and 40 years. 

To be eligible for the scheme, individuals must have a savings bank account. 

They must also provide their mobile number and Aadhaar number for registration. 

The scheme is open to both men and women. 

There are no income criteria for eligibility. 

The benefits of the Atal Pension Yojana include a guaranteed pension. 

The pension is provided after the age of 60 years. 

The amount of pension is predetermined based on the age of entry into the scheme. 

For instance, a person who joins at the age of 18 will have to contribute a lower amount than someone who joins at 40. 

The contributions are made on a monthly basis. 

The government also co-contributes 50% of the total contribution. 

This co-contribution is applicable for the first five years. 

The maximum co-contribution is capped at Rs. 1,000 per annum. 

Subscribers can choose their preferred pension amount at the time of enrollment. 

The scheme also provides for the return of the accumulated corpus to the nominee upon the death of the subscriber. 

This ensures that the family of the subscriber is financially secure. 

The application process for the Atal Pension Yojana is straightforward. 

Interested individuals can visit any bank or post office that offers the scheme. 

They need to fill out the application form and submit the required documents. 

The required documents include proof of identity and address. 

The Aadhaar number is mandatory for enrollment. 

Once the application is submitted, the bank will process it. 

The timeline for processing the application is usually quick. 

Subscribers can start contributing to the scheme immediately after enrollment. 

The contributions can be made through various modes such as online banking or auto-debit. 

The frequency of contributions is monthly. 

Subscribers can also change their contribution amount if needed. 

The Atal Pension Yojana is implemented by the Ministry of Finance. 

The Pension Fund Regulatory and Development Authority (PFRDA) oversees the scheme. 

Various banks and financial institutions act as intermediaries for the scheme. 

They facilitate the enrollment and contribution processes.
