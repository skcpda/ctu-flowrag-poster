The Mfal scheme is an initiative aimed at providing financial assistance to farmers in India. 

The objective of the Mfal scheme is to enhance the income of farmers and ensure their financial stability. 

The scheme offers direct cash transfers to eligible farmers to support their agricultural activities. 

Eligible beneficiaries are small and marginal farmers who own a certain amount of land. 

Farmers must be registered under the relevant agricultural department to apply for the scheme. 

The application process typically involves submitting an online application through the designated portal or visiting local agricultural offices. 

The assistance is provided on a periodic basis as determined by the scheme guidelines. 

The implementing authority for the Mfal scheme is the Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers' Welfare. 

Additional details regarding the specific amount of assistance and the frequency of payments may vary based on state regulations.
