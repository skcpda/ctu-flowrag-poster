The Indian government welfare scheme known as the Integrated Approach for the Development of Scheduled Tribes is commonly referred to as IATS. 

This scheme is designed to address the socio-economic challenges faced by Scheduled Tribes in India. 

Scheduled Tribes represent a significant portion of India's population and often experience marginalization and poverty. 

The IATS scheme aims to provide a holistic approach to the development of these communities. 

The primary objective of the IATS scheme is to enhance the living standards of Scheduled Tribes. 

It seeks to improve access to education health care and employment opportunities. 

The scheme also aims to empower Scheduled Tribes through capacity building and skill development. 

By focusing on sustainable development the IATS scheme promotes self-reliance among tribal communities. 

The benefits of the IATS scheme include financial assistance for various developmental projects. 

It provides support for infrastructure development in tribal areas. 

The scheme also facilitates access to education and vocational training programs. 

Health care services are enhanced through the implementation of the IATS scheme.
