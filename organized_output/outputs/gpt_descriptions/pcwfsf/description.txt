The Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samman Nidhi Yojana is a government welfare scheme launched to provide financial support to farmers in India. 

The objective of the scheme is to ensure a steady income for small and marginal farmers. 

The scheme provides an income support of Rs. 6000 per year to eligible farmers. 

This amount is disbursed in three equal installments of Rs. 2000 each. 

Eligibility for the scheme includes small and marginal farmers who own cultivable land. 

Farmers who are registered under the scheme must have landholding of up to 2 hectares. 

The application process involves farmers registering through the official government portal or through local revenue offices. 

The timeline for disbursement is typically every four months with payments made directly to the bank accounts of beneficiaries. 

The Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers Welfare is the implementing authority for this scheme. 

The scheme was launched in February 2019 and continues to operate as part of the government's ongoing support for the agricultural sector.
