The TCDAP (Tribal Cooperative Development Assistance for Scheduled Tribes in the Country) scheme is designed to promote the socio-economic development of Scheduled Tribes in India. 

The objective of the scheme is to enhance the livelihoods of tribal communities through cooperative development. 

The benefits include financial assistance for various income-generating activities and support for cooperative societies. 

Eligibility is primarily for Scheduled Tribe individuals and cooperative societies formed by them. 

The application process involves submitting proposals to the respective state governments or tribal development departments. 

The timeline for the disbursement of funds and implementation of projects varies based on the specific activities approved. 

The implementing authority for the scheme is the Ministry of Tribal Affairs in collaboration with state governments. 

Additional details may include the focus on sustainable development and capacity building for tribal communities.
