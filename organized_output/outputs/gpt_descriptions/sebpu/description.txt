The SEBPU scheme stands for the Skill Enhancement and Business Promotion for Unemployed scheme. 

It aims to provide skill development and entrepreneurship opportunities to unemployed individuals. 

The objective is to enhance employability and promote self-employment among the youth. 

The scheme offers training programs in various skills along with financial assistance for starting businesses. 

Eligible beneficiaries include unemployed youth and individuals seeking to enhance their skills. 

The application process typically involves submitting an application form along with required documents to the designated authority. 

The timeline for training programs may vary based on the specific skill courses offered. 

The implementing authorities include the Ministry of Skill Development and Entrepreneurship. 

Additional details regarding specific training centers and financial assistance amounts may vary based on state policies.
