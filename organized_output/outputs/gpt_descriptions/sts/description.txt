The STS scheme refers to the Skill Training for School Dropouts program. 

This scheme is designed to provide skill development training to school dropouts. 

The objective is to enhance employability and provide livelihood opportunities for youth who have left formal education. 

The benefits include skill training in various sectors to improve job prospects. 

Assistance is provided in the form of training programs that are aligned with industry requirements. 

Eligibility criteria typically include being a school dropout and being within a specified age group. 

The application process usually involves submitting an application form to designated training centers or online portals. 

The timeline for training programs can vary based on the specific course and training provider. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Skill Development and Entrepreneurship. 

Additional details about specific training programs and locations may be available through official government portals.
