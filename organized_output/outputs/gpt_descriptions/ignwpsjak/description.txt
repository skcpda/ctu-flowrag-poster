The Indian government welfare scheme known as the Indira Gandhi National Widow Pension Scheme is designed to provide financial assistance to widows in India. 

This scheme is part of the larger social security initiatives aimed at improving the living conditions of vulnerable sections of society. 

The scheme was launched to address the specific needs of widows who often face economic hardships after the loss of their spouses. 

Widows are particularly vulnerable due to societal stigma and the loss of a primary breadwinner. 

The objectives of the Indira Gandhi National Widow Pension Scheme are multifaceted. 

One of the primary objectives is to provide financial support to widows to ensure their sustenance and dignity. 

The scheme aims to empower widows by providing them with a regular source of income. 

It also seeks to promote social inclusion by recognizing the rights of widows and ensuring they have access to financial resources. 

The benefits provided under this scheme include a monthly pension to eligible widows. 

As of the latest updates, the pension amount is set at Rs. 300 per month for widows aged between 18 and 79 years. 

For widows above the age of 80 years, the pension amount is increased to Rs. 500 per month. 

This financial assistance is intended to help widows meet their basic needs such as food, clothing, and healthcare. 

The scheme also aims to reduce the dependency of widows on their families or relatives for financial support. 

Eligibility criteria for the Indira Gandhi National Widow Pension Scheme are clearly defined. 

To qualify for the scheme, applicants must be widows aged between 18 and 79 years. 

The applicant must belong to a Below Poverty Line category as defined by the government. 

The scheme is applicable to widows from all communities and religions across India. 

Additionally, the applicant must not be receiving any other pension from the government. 

The application process for the Indira Gandhi National Widow Pension Scheme is straightforward. 

Interested widows must obtain the application form from the local government office or online through designated portals. 

The completed application form must be submitted along with necessary documents such as identity proof and proof of widowhood. 

Documents required typically include a death certificate of the deceased husband and proof of age. 

Once the application is submitted, it is reviewed by the concerned authorities. 

The verification process may involve checking the eligibility criteria and the authenticity of the documents provided. 

After successful verification, the pension amount is disbursed to the eligible widow. 

The disbursement is usually made through direct bank transfers to ensure transparency and efficiency. 

The timeline for processing applications may vary depending on the state and local administrative efficiency. 

In many cases, the pension is disbursed on a monthly basis. 

The frequency of payment ensures that widows receive regular financial support. 

Implementing authorities for the Indira Gandhi National Widow Pension Scheme include the Ministry of Rural Development. 

State governments are also involved in the implementation and management of the scheme at the local level. 

Each state may have its own specific guidelines and processes for administering the scheme.
