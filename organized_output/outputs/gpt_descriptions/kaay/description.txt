The Kaay scheme is a government initiative aimed at providing support and assistance to specific vulnerable sections of society in India.

This scheme is designed to address the needs of marginalized communities and improve their overall quality of life.

The context of the Kaay scheme arises from the recognition that certain groups in society face systemic barriers to accessing essential services and opportunities.

These barriers can include economic disadvantages social discrimination and lack of access to education and healthcare.

The Kaay scheme seeks to bridge these gaps by offering targeted support to those who need it most.

The primary objective of the Kaay scheme is to empower individuals and families from marginalized communities.

This empowerment is achieved through various forms of assistance including financial aid educational support and healthcare services.

The scheme aims to enhance the socio-economic status of beneficiaries and promote their integration into mainstream society.

By providing resources and support the Kaay scheme aspires to uplift communities that have historically been disadvantaged.
