The STKK scheme stands for the "Swasthya Kalyan Kendra" initiative. 

It aims to provide accessible healthcare services to the rural population of India. 

The objective is to improve health outcomes and reduce the burden of diseases in underserved areas. 

Benefits include free medical consultations and essential medicines. 

The scheme also offers diagnostic services and health education to the community. 

Eligibility is generally targeted towards residents of rural areas and economically weaker sections. 

The application process typically involves registration at the local health center or Kendra. 

The timeline for services is ongoing as the scheme is designed to operate continuously. 

Implementing authorities include the Ministry of Health and Family Welfare at the central level and state health departments. 

The scheme also focuses on preventive healthcare and community participation in health initiatives.
