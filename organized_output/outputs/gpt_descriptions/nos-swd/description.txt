The National Outreach Scheme for Social Welfare Development (NOS-SWD) aims to enhance the welfare of marginalized communities in India. 

The primary objective is to provide social security and welfare services to disadvantaged groups. 

The scheme focuses on empowering women children and persons with disabilities. 

Benefits include financial assistance skill development training and access to social services. 

Eligibility criteria typically include being a member of a marginalized community and meeting specific income thresholds. 

The application process involves submitting a form along with necessary documentation to the designated local authority. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment. 

Further details regarding specific timelines or frequency of assistance may vary based on local implementation.
