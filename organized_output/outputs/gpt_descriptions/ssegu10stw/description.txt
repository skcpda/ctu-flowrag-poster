The scheme SSEG10STW is designed to provide financial assistance to Scheduled Tribe women in India. 

The primary objective is to empower Scheduled Tribe women by promoting self-employment and entrepreneurship. 

The scheme offers financial support for setting up small businesses and self-employment ventures. 

Eligible beneficiaries include women belonging to Scheduled Tribes who are above the age of 18. 

The application process involves submitting an application form along with necessary documents to the concerned department. 

The timeline for disbursement of funds is typically within a few weeks after the application is approved. 

The implementing authority for this scheme is the Ministry of Tribal Affairs. 

Additional details regarding specific business sectors or types of assistance may vary based on state guidelines.
