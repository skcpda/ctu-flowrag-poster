The Tuk-Chterts scheme is a welfare initiative aimed at providing financial assistance to marginalized communities in India. 

The primary objective of the scheme is to enhance the livelihood opportunities for the beneficiaries. 

The scheme offers direct cash transfers to eligible households to support their basic needs. 

Eligibility is typically determined based on socio-economic criteria such as income level and family size. 

The application process usually involves submitting a form along with necessary documentation to the local government office. 

The timeline for disbursement of funds is generally on a monthly basis. 

The implementing authorities for the Tuk-Chterts scheme include state governments and local administrative bodies. 

Additional details regarding the specific financial assistance amount and the exact eligibility criteria may vary by state.
