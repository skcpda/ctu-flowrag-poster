The Wise PhD scheme is an initiative aimed at promoting research and innovation in higher education. 

It is designed to provide financial assistance to PhD scholars. 

The objective is to enhance the quality of research output in Indian universities. 

The scheme supports scholars by providing grants for research-related expenses. 

Eligible candidates include registered PhD students in recognized institutions. 

The application process involves submitting a proposal detailing the research project. 

Applications are reviewed by a committee for approval. 

The frequency of funding disbursement is typically annual based on project milestones. 

The implementing authority for this scheme is the Ministry of Education.
