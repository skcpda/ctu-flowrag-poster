The Pradhan Mantri Tirth Yatra Scheme aims to provide financial assistance for pilgrimage trips to senior citizens. 

It was launched to promote religious tourism and ensure that elderly citizens can undertake pilgrimages with ease. 

The scheme covers travel expenses for senior citizens visiting religious sites. 

Eligible beneficiaries are senior citizens aged 60 years and above. 

The scheme is specifically designed for those who belong to the below-poverty-line category. 

Applications can be submitted through designated government offices or online portals. 

The frequency of the scheme is not specified. 

The Ministry of Minority Affairs is the implementing authority for this scheme. 

The scheme also includes provisions for travel companions for senior citizens. 

The financial assistance covers travel costs but does not include accommodation or food expenses. 

The scheme aims to enhance the spiritual well-being of senior citizens.
