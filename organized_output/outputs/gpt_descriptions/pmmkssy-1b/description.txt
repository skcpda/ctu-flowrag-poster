Pradhan Mantri Mudra Yojana (PMMY) is a scheme launched by the Government of India. 

It aims to provide financial support to small and micro enterprises. 

The objective is to promote entrepreneurship and self-employment among individuals. 

The scheme offers loans up to 10 lakh rupees. 

These loans are categorized into three types: Shishu, Kishore, and Tarun. 

Shishu loans are for up to 50,000 rupees. 

Kishore loans range from 50,001 to 5 lakh rupees. 

Tarun loans range from 5,00,001 to 10 lakh rupees. 

Eligibility includes individuals or businesses engaged in manufacturing, trading, or services. 

The applicant must be a citizen of India. 

The application process involves submitting an online or offline application to banks or financial institutions. 

Documents required include identity proof, address proof, and business plan. 

The timeline for loan approval varies by institution. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Finance. 

Participating banks and financial institutions include public sector banks, private banks, and NBFCs. 

The scheme is operational across India. 

It aims to enhance the availability of credit to small businesses. 

The initiative also seeks to create employment opportunities. 

The scheme is part of the government's broader effort to boost the economy.
