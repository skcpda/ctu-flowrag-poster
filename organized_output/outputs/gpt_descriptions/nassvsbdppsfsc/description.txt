The National Scheme for Support to State Governments for the Development of the Fisheries Sector is designed to enhance the fisheries sector in India. 

The objective of this scheme is to provide financial assistance to state governments for the development of fisheries and aquaculture. 

The benefits include support for infrastructure development such as fish landing centers and fish markets. 

It also aims to improve fish production and productivity through various interventions. 

Eligibility criteria include state governments and union territories that seek to enhance their fisheries sector. 

The application process involves submitting a proposal to the Ministry of Fisheries Animal Husbandry and Dairying. 

The timeline for fund disbursement is typically aligned with the financial year. 

The implementing authority for this scheme is the Ministry of Fisheries Animal Husbandry and Dairying. 

This scheme is part of the broader efforts to promote sustainable fisheries and improve the livelihoods of those dependent on fishing.
