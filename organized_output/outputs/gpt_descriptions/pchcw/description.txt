The PCHCW scheme stands for the Pradhan Mantri Chikitsa Kendra for Health and Wellness. 

It was launched to enhance healthcare access in rural and underserved areas of India. 

The objective is to provide comprehensive primary healthcare services to the population. 

The scheme aims to strengthen the healthcare delivery system through the establishment of health and wellness centers. 

Benefits include free essential medicines and diagnostic services. 

It also provides health education and awareness programs to the community. 

Eligibility is primarily for residents of rural and underserved areas. 

The application process details are not specified in official sources. 

The timeline for implementation is ongoing as centers are established based on local needs. 

The Ministry of Health and Family Welfare is the implementing authority for this scheme. 

The scheme is part of the broader National Health Mission initiative. 

It aims to promote preventive healthcare and improve health outcomes.
