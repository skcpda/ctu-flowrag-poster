The Old Age Pension Scheme for Senior Citizens (OAPSP) is a welfare initiative by the Government of India. 

It aims to provide financial assistance to senior citizens who are in need. 

The primary objective is to ensure a dignified life for elderly individuals by providing them with a regular income. 

Under this scheme, eligible senior citizens receive a monthly pension. 

The amount of pension varies by state and is determined by the respective state governments. 

Eligibility criteria typically include age requirements and income limits. 

Senior citizens aged 60 years and above are generally eligible. 

The scheme is targeted at those who belong to economically weaker sections of society. 

The application process usually involves submitting a form along with necessary documents such as age proof and income certificate. 

Applications can often be submitted at local government offices or online through state-specific portals. 

The pension is disbursed monthly. 

The implementing authorities are usually the respective state governments and their designated departments. 

This scheme is part of a broader effort to support the elderly population in India.
