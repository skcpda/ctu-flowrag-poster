The Senior Citizens Savings Scheme (SCSS) is a government-backed savings instrument in India. 

It was launched to provide a safe and secure investment option for senior citizens. 

The primary objective is to offer regular income and financial security to elderly individuals. 

The scheme provides an interest rate that is higher than traditional savings accounts. 

The current interest rate is set at 8.0 percent per annum. 

Interest is paid quarterly to the account holders. 

The maximum investment limit is Rs. 15 lakh. 

The minimum investment amount is Rs. 1,000. 

Eligibility is restricted to Indian citizens aged 60 years and above. 

Individuals aged 55 to 60 years who have opted for voluntary retirement are also eligible. 

The account can be opened at designated banks and post offices. 

The application process involves submitting a duly filled application form along with required documents. 

Documents typically include proof of age and identity. 

The account has a maturity period of five years. 

It can be extended for an additional three years after maturity. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Finance. 

Premature withdrawal is allowed under certain conditions with applicable penalties. 

The scheme is a popular choice among senior citizens for its safety and regular income benefits.
