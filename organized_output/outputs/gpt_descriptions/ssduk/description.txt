The scheme SSDUK stands for the Skill Development Scheme for Unemployed Youth of Karnataka. 

It was launched by the Government of Karnataka to enhance the employability of unemployed youth in the state. 

The primary objective is to provide skill development training to youth to help them secure jobs in various sectors. 

The scheme offers financial assistance for training programs and skill development initiatives. 

Eligible beneficiaries include unemployed youth aged between 18 to 35 years residing in Karnataka. 

The application process involves submitting an online application through the official portal of the Karnataka government. 

The scheme is implemented by the Department of Skill Development Entrepreneurship and Livelihood. 

Training programs under this scheme are conducted periodically based on demand and availability of resources. 

The scheme aims to bridge the skill gap in the labor market and promote self-employment among youth.
