The Pradhan Mantri Laghu Vyapari Maan-Dhan Yojana (PLISAAci) is a government welfare scheme aimed at supporting small traders and self-employed individuals. 

The scheme was launched to provide a social security net for small traders and self-employed persons. 

The primary objective is to ensure financial security for small traders during their old age. 

Under this scheme beneficiaries will receive a monthly pension of Rs. 3000 after reaching the age of 60. 

The scheme also aims to encourage small traders to contribute to the Employees' Provident Fund. 

Eligibility for the scheme includes individuals aged between 18 to 40 years who are engaged in small trading activities. 

Annual income should not exceed Rs. 1.5 million. 

The application process involves filling out a prescribed form and submitting it to the relevant authorities. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Labour and Employment. 

The pension is disbursed monthly after the beneficiary reaches the age of 60. 

The scheme aims to cover a large number of small traders across the country. 

It is part of the government's broader initiative to enhance social security for the informal sector.
