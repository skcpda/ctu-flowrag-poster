The Livelihood Promotion and Agricultural Investment Incentive Scheme (LPAIIS) is an initiative by the Government of India.

The scheme aims to enhance the livelihood opportunities for farmers and agricultural workers.

It focuses on promoting sustainable agricultural practices and improving agricultural productivity.

The primary objective is to provide financial assistance to farmers for investments in agricultural activities.

The scheme also seeks to encourage the adoption of modern farming techniques and technologies.

Benefits under the LPAIIS include direct financial support for purchasing agricultural inputs.

Farmers can receive assistance for acquiring equipment and machinery that enhance productivity.

The scheme also provides training and capacity-building programs for farmers.

Eligible beneficiaries include small and marginal farmers as defined by government criteria.

Agricultural laborers who engage in farming activities are also eligible for assistance.

The application process involves submitting a form to the local agricultural department.

Farmers must provide necessary documentation to prove their eligibility.

The application can often be submitted online through designated government portals.

Once the application is submitted it is reviewed by the local authorities.

The timeline for processing applications may vary based on the region.

Assistance is typically disbursed within a few weeks of application approval.

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers' Welfare.

State governments may also play a role in the implementation at the local level.

Monitoring and evaluation of the scheme are conducted to ensure effective utilization of funds.
