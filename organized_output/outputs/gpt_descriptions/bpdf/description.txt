The BPDF scheme stands for the "Bihar Prashasnikik Dastak" program. 

This scheme was initiated by the Government of Bihar. 

It aims to enhance the efficiency and effectiveness of public service delivery in the state.

The BPDF scheme is designed to address these challenges by streamlining processes and making them more citizen-centric.
