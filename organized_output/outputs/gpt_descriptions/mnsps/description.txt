The Mukhyamantri Nasha Mukti Swasthya Punarvas Yojana (MNSPS) is a welfare scheme launched by the Government of Punjab. 

The scheme aims to provide rehabilitation and support for individuals affected by substance abuse. 

It focuses on creating a drug-free society by promoting awareness and providing treatment facilities. 

The benefits include access to de-addiction centers and counseling services. 

Financial assistance is provided for rehabilitation and reintegration into society. 

Eligibility is primarily for individuals struggling with substance abuse issues. 

The application process involves visiting designated de-addiction centers or health facilities. 

Individuals can also seek help through helplines established by the government. 

The scheme is implemented by the Department of Health and Family Welfare, Punjab. 

Regular monitoring and evaluation of the program are conducted to ensure effectiveness. 

Awareness campaigns are also part of the initiative to educate the public about substance abuse.
