The TAA scheme stands for the Technology Acquisition and Development Fund. 

It was launched to promote indigenous technology development and acquisition in various sectors. 

The main objective is to enhance the competitiveness of Indian industries through technology support. 

The scheme provides financial assistance for technology acquisition and development projects. 

Eligible beneficiaries include small and medium enterprises and research institutions. 

The application process involves submitting a proposal to the concerned authorities for evaluation. 

The timeline for project approval and funding disbursement varies based on the project complexity. 

The Department of Science and Technology is the implementing authority for this scheme.
