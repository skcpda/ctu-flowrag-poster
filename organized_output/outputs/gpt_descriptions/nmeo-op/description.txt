The National Mission for Enhanced Oil Recovery and Enhanced Oil Recovery (NMEOR) is an initiative aimed at increasing oil production in India. 

The objective of NMEOR is to enhance the recovery of oil from existing fields. 

The mission seeks to utilize advanced technologies to improve oil extraction efficiency. 

Benefits include increased domestic oil production and reduced dependence on imports. 

Eligibility criteria include oil fields that are already in production and require enhanced recovery techniques. 

The application process involves submission of proposals by oil companies to the Ministry of Petroleum and Natural Gas. 

The timeline for project implementation varies based on the specific technology and field conditions. 

The Ministry of Petroleum and Natural Gas is the primary implementing authority for this mission. 

The mission also promotes research and development in enhanced oil recovery technologies. 

Collaboration with private sector companies is encouraged to achieve the mission's objectives.
