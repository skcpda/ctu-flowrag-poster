The Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana Gramin (PMAGY) is a government welfare scheme aimed at providing housing for the rural poor in India. 

The scheme was launched to address the housing shortage in rural areas and to improve the living conditions of the underprivileged. 

The primary objective is to provide affordable housing to the rural population by constructing pucca houses with basic amenities. 

Beneficiaries receive financial assistance for the construction of houses. 

The scheme provides a subsidy for the construction of houses under the PMAY-G. 

Eligibility criteria include being a member of a Scheduled Caste or Scheduled Tribe or belonging to a Below Poverty Line household. 

The applicant must not own a house in their name or in the name of any family member. 

The application process involves submitting an application to the local Gram Panchayat or through the official PMAY-G website. 

The timeline for the disbursement of funds is typically aligned with the construction phases of the house. 

The Ministry of Rural Development is the implementing authority for this scheme. 

The scheme aims to provide a house to all rural households by the year 2022. 

The assistance amount varies based on the state and is subject to the guidelines set by the Ministry of Rural Development.
