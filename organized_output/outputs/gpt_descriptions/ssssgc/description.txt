The SSSSGC stands for the "Sustainable Livelihoods and Self-Employment Scheme for Scheduled Castes." 

This scheme aims to provide sustainable livelihoods and self-employment opportunities for Scheduled Castes. 

The primary objective is to enhance the economic conditions of Scheduled Castes through skill development and self-employment initiatives. 

Benefits include financial assistance for setting up self-employment ventures and access to skill development training programs. 

Eligibility is primarily for individuals belonging to Scheduled Castes who are looking to start their own businesses or improve their livelihoods. 

The application process typically involves submitting a form along with required documents to the designated authority. 

The timeline for application processing may vary based on the specific project and location. 

Implementing authorities include the Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment and various state governments. 

Additional details may include specific training programs and financial support limits based on the project.
