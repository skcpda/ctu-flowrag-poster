The Kisan Mandhan Yojana is a pension scheme launched by the Government of India. 

It aims to provide social security to small and marginal farmers. 

The scheme was initiated to ensure that farmers have a stable income after retirement. 

The primary objective is to provide a monthly pension of Rs. 3000 to farmers aged 60 years and above. 

This pension is intended to support farmers in their old age and reduce their financial burden. 

The benefits of the scheme include a guaranteed pension amount. 

Farmers can receive the pension after reaching the age of 60. 

The scheme also includes a provision for the spouse of the farmer. 

In case of the farmer's death, the spouse is eligible to receive 50% of the pension amount. 

Eligibility criteria for the Kisan Mandhan Yojana include being a small or marginal farmer. 

Farmers must be between the ages of 18 and 40 to enroll in the scheme. 

The annual income of the farmer should not exceed Rs. 2 lakh. 

Farmers must also have cultivable land of up to 2 hectares.

Farmers can apply online through the official website or visit the nearest Common Service Center.
