The SPFHAVIP scheme stands for the Special Project for Housing and Urban Development for Vulnerable Individuals and Populations. 

It was launched to address the housing needs of vulnerable populations in urban areas. 

The main objective is to provide affordable housing solutions to marginalized communities. 

The scheme aims to improve living conditions and promote social inclusion. 

Benefits include financial assistance for housing construction and improvement. 

Eligible beneficiaries are individuals from economically weaker sections and marginalized communities. 

The application process involves submitting a proposal to the respective urban local body. 

The timeline for approval and disbursement of funds may vary based on the local authority's procedures. 

The Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs is the implementing authority for this scheme. 

Additional details on specific eligibility criteria and funding amounts may be available through local urban development offices.
