The Tamil Nadu State Housing and Urban Development Corporation Limited implements the Tamil Nadu State Housing Scheme for the Economically Weaker Sections. 

This scheme aims to provide affordable housing to low-income families in urban areas. 

The objective is to ensure that every family has access to a basic dwelling. 

Benefits include financial assistance for housing construction or purchase. 

Eligible beneficiaries are those belonging to the Economically Weaker Sections as defined by the government. 

The application process typically involves submitting a form along with required documents to the local housing authority. 

The scheme is implemented on a continuous basis with no specific timeline for applications. 

The Tamil Nadu State Housing and Urban Development Corporation is the primary implementing agency. 

Additional details about specific financial assistance amounts or housing specifications may vary based on local government policies.
