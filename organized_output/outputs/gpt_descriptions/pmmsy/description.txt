Pradhan Mantri Matsya Sampada Yojana (PMMSY) is a flagship scheme launched by the Government of India in May 2020. 

The scheme aims to enhance fish production and promote sustainable fisheries. 

It seeks to create an integrated fisheries management framework. 

The primary objective is to increase fish production to 22 million metric tons by 2024-25. 

The scheme also aims to provide employment opportunities in the fisheries sector. 

Benefits include financial assistance for various activities related to fish farming and aquaculture. 

Subsidies are provided for the establishment of fish farms and hatcheries. 

The scheme also supports the development of fishing harbors and landing centers. 

Eligibility is open to individuals and groups involved in fishing and aquaculture. 

Self-Help Groups and cooperatives can also apply for assistance under this scheme. 

The application process involves submitting a project proposal to the concerned state fisheries department. 

The proposal must detail the intended activities and the financial requirements. 

The timeline for project approval may vary based on the state and project complexity. 

The scheme is implemented by the Department of Fisheries under the Ministry of Fisheries Animal Husbandry and Dairying. 

Additional details include a focus on promoting modern fishing techniques and technology. 

The scheme also emphasizes the importance of sustainable practices in fisheries management.
