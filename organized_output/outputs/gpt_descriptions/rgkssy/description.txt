The Rajiv Gandhi Kisan Nyay Yojana (RGKSSY) was launched by the Government of Chhattisgarh. 

It aims to provide financial assistance to farmers in the state. 

The objective is to enhance the income of farmers and promote agricultural productivity. 

Under this scheme, farmers receive direct cash transfers for the sale of their produce. 

The assistance is provided for various crops including paddy, maize, and sugarcane. 

Eligible beneficiaries include small and marginal farmers in Chhattisgarh. 

Farmers must be registered under the state’s revenue records to qualify. 

The application process involves registration through the local revenue office. 

The scheme is implemented by the Department of Agriculture and Farmers Welfare of Chhattisgarh. 

The financial assistance is provided annually based on the quantity of crops sold. 

Payments are made directly to the bank accounts of the beneficiaries. 

The scheme aims to support farmers during the harvesting season. 

It also seeks to encourage the cultivation of cash crops. 

The initiative is part of the state's broader efforts to improve the agricultural economy.
