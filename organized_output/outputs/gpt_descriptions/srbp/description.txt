The Scheme for the Rehabilitation of Bonded Labourers (SRBP) was launched by the Government of India. 

It aims to eliminate bonded labour and rehabilitate those who have been subjected to it. 

The primary objective is to provide assistance to bonded labourers for their reintegration into society. 

Benefits include financial assistance for livelihood support and skill development. 

The scheme also provides provisions for educational support for the children of bonded labourers. 

Eligibility is primarily for individuals identified as bonded labourers under the relevant laws. 

The application process involves submission of an application to the district authorities. 

The timeline for assistance is typically determined by the assessment of the applications. 

The Ministry of Labour and Employment is the implementing authority for this scheme. 

State governments also play a crucial role in the implementation and monitoring of the scheme. 

Additional details include the provision for awareness campaigns to educate potential beneficiaries about their rights.
