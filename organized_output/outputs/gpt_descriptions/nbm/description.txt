The National Bamboo Mission (NBM) is a government initiative aimed at promoting the growth and development of the bamboo sector in India. 

The mission was launched to enhance the production and productivity of bamboo. 

It seeks to provide sustainable livelihood opportunities to farmers and artisans. 

The objectives include increasing the area under bamboo cultivation and promoting value addition. 

The scheme aims to improve the income of farmers through bamboo-based enterprises. 

Benefits include financial assistance for establishing bamboo nurseries and plantations. 

Support is also provided for processing units and marketing of bamboo products. 

Eligibility is primarily for farmers and individuals involved in bamboo cultivation and processing. 

The application process involves submitting a proposal to the concerned state government. 

The timeline for implementation varies based on project proposals and state-specific plans. 

The Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers' Welfare is the nodal agency for implementing the NBM. 

State governments also play a crucial role in executing the mission at the local level. 

The scheme promotes the use of bamboo for various purposes including construction and handicrafts. 

It aims to enhance the ecological balance by promoting bamboo as a sustainable resource. 

The mission also focuses on research and development in bamboo cultivation and processing techniques.
