The Odisha State Cash Transfer Program (OSCTP) is a welfare scheme initiated by the Government of Odisha. 

The program aims to provide financial assistance to vulnerable households in the state. 

The primary objective is to reduce poverty and improve the living standards of the economically disadvantaged. 

The scheme offers direct cash transfers to eligible beneficiaries to meet their basic needs. 

Benefits include financial support for food security education and healthcare. 

Eligibility is primarily based on socio-economic criteria determined by the state government. 

Households identified under the Below Poverty Line (BPL) category are typically eligible. 

The application process involves submitting a form to the local government office or online through the official portal. 

Beneficiaries are required to provide necessary documentation to verify their eligibility. 

The timeline for disbursement of funds is usually monthly or quarterly depending on the specific assistance. 

The Department of Social Security and Empowerment of Persons with Disabilities is the implementing authority for this scheme. 

Additional details regarding specific amounts or conditions may vary and should be checked on official sources.
