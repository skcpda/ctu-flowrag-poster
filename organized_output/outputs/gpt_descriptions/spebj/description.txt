The SPEBJ scheme stands for the Scheme for Promotion of Employment in the Bamboo Sector. 

This scheme was launched to promote the bamboo sector in India. 

The objective is to enhance the livelihood opportunities for people involved in bamboo cultivation and processing. 

The benefits include financial assistance for setting up bamboo-based enterprises. 

It also provides skill development and training programs for artisans and entrepreneurs. 

Eligibility criteria include individuals or groups engaged in bamboo cultivation or processing. 

The application process involves submitting a proposal to the concerned implementing agency. 

The timeline for approval and disbursement of funds varies based on the project. 

The Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers' Welfare is the implementing authority for this scheme.
