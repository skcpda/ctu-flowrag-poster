The Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana (PMAY) is a flagship housing scheme launched by the Government of India in 2015. 

The scheme aims to provide affordable housing to the urban poor and promote housing for all by 2022. 

It seeks to ensure that every family has a pucca house with basic amenities. 

The benefits include financial assistance for home construction or purchase. 

Subsidy on home loans is provided under the Credit Linked Subsidy Scheme (CLSS). 

The scheme targets Economically Weaker Sections (EWS) Low Income Groups (LIG) and Middle Income Groups (MIG). 

Eligibility criteria include annual income limits which vary based on the category. 

Applicants must be Indian citizens and should not own a house in their name. 

The application process can be completed online through the PMAY website or through Common Service Centers. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs. 

The timeline for the completion of houses varies based on the project and location. 

The scheme is monitored at both the central and state levels to ensure effective implementation.
