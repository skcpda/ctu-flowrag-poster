The WBTWSSFP stands for the West Bengal Tirtha Sathi Scheme for Senior Citizens. 

This scheme is initiated by the Government of West Bengal. 

It aims to provide financial assistance to senior citizens for pilgrimage trips. 

The objective is to promote spiritual tourism among the elderly population. 

The scheme offers financial support for travel expenses to various pilgrimage sites. 

Eligible beneficiaries are senior citizens aged 60 years and above. 

They must be residents of West Bengal. 

The application process involves filling out a form available at designated government offices. 

Beneficiaries may also apply online through the official government portal. 

The scheme provides assistance for a fixed number of trips per year. 

Implementing authorities include the West Bengal Department of Minority Affairs and Madrasah Education. 

The scheme is part of the government's broader initiative to support the welfare of senior citizens.
