The scheme is known as the Pradhan Mantri Shram Yogi Maan-Dhan Yojana (PMSYM). 

It was launched to provide social security to unorganized sector workers. 

The objective is to provide a monthly pension of Rs. 3000 after the age of 60 years. 

The scheme aims to ensure financial stability for workers in the unorganized sector. 

Eligible beneficiaries include unorganized workers aged between 18 to 40 years. 

They must have a monthly income of less than Rs. 15,000. 

The application process can be completed online through the official portal. 

Applicants need to provide necessary documents such as identity proof and income proof. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Labour and Employment. 

The pension is paid monthly after the beneficiary reaches the age of 60. 

The scheme also provides for the spouse to receive a pension in case of the beneficiary's demise. 

The contribution to the scheme is based on the age of entry into the scheme. 

For example, a worker aged 18 years needs to contribute Rs. 55 per month. 

The contribution increases with age up to Rs. 200 per month for those entering at age 40. 

The scheme is designed to be sustainable and self-financed. 

It is part of the government's broader initiative to enhance social security for workers.
