The scheme SADPFA is the Scheme for Assistance to Disabled Persons for Purchase/Fitting of Aids and Appliances. 

It aims to provide financial assistance to persons with disabilities for the purchase of aids and appliances. 

The objective is to enhance the mobility and independence of persons with disabilities. 

Benefits include financial assistance for the purchase of various aids and appliances. 

Eligible beneficiaries are persons with disabilities as defined under the Rights of Persons with Disabilities Act 2016. 

The application process involves submitting a request through the designated authorities. 

The timeline for assistance may vary based on the processing of applications. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment. 

Additional details include that the financial assistance is provided based on the type of aid or appliance required.
