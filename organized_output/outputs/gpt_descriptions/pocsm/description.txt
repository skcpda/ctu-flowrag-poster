The POCSM scheme stands for the Pradhan Mantri OBC Welfare Scheme for the Other Backward Classes. 

It was launched to provide financial assistance and support to the Other Backward Classes community in India. 

The primary objective is to enhance the socio-economic status of OBCs through various welfare measures. 

The scheme offers benefits such as scholarships for students and financial aid for skill development programs. 

Eligibility criteria include being a member of the Other Backward Classes community as defined by the government. 

Applicants must also meet specific income thresholds set by the government to qualify for assistance. 

The application process typically involves submitting an online application along with required documentation. 

The timeline for processing applications may vary but is generally completed within a few weeks of submission. 

The Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment is the primary implementing authority for this scheme. 

Additional details may include periodic reviews and updates to the eligibility criteria and benefits based on government policies.
