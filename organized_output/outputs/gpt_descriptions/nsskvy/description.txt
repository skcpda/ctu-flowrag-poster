The National Skill Support for Knowledge and Vocational Youth (NSSKVY) is an initiative by the Government of India. 

It aims to enhance the employability of youth through skill development and vocational training. 

The objective is to provide quality training to youth in various sectors. 

The scheme focuses on bridging the skill gap in the labor market. 

Benefits include skill training and certification for participants. 

Assistance may also include job placement support after training completion. 

Eligibility criteria typically include youth aged between 15 and 29 years. 

Applicants should be willing to undergo skill training. 

The application process usually involves registration through designated training centers. 

Participants may need to provide identification and educational qualifications. 

The timeline for training programs varies based on the specific skill set being taught. 

Training programs may be conducted periodically throughout the year. 

The implementing authorities include the Ministry of Skill Development and Entrepreneurship. 

State governments and various training partners also play a role in execution. 

The scheme is part of a broader effort to promote skill development in India. 

It aligns with the government's vision of a skilled India.
