The Atal Bimit Vyakti Kalyan Yojana is a welfare scheme initiated by the Government of India. 

This scheme is primarily aimed at providing financial assistance to workers who become unemployed. 

The context of this scheme is rooted in the need to support individuals during times of job loss. 

Unemployment can lead to significant financial distress for families. 

Recognizing this challenge the Government of India introduced the Atal Bimit Vyakti Kalyan Yojana. 

The scheme was launched by the Employees' State Insurance Corporation. 

It was first announced in December 2018. 

The objectives of the scheme are multifaceted. 

One of the primary objectives is to provide relief to insured persons who lose their jobs. 

The scheme aims to support individuals during their transition to new employment. 

It seeks to reduce the financial burden on families during periods of unemployment. 

The benefits under the Atal Bimit Vyakti Kalyan Yojana are significant. 

Eligible beneficiaries can receive a financial assistance amount. 

This amount is equivalent to 50 percent of their average daily wages. 

The assistance is provided for a maximum period of 90 days. 

The financial support is intended to help individuals meet their basic needs. 

It can be used for essential expenses such as food and housing. 

The scheme is designed to provide a safety net for workers. 

This is particularly important in times of economic uncertainty. 

Eligibility criteria for the Atal Bimit Vyakti Kalyan Yojana are clearly defined. 

The scheme is available to individuals who are insured under the Employees' State Insurance Act. 

Applicants must have been in insurable employment for a minimum period. 

They must have contributed to the Employees' State Insurance Corporation. 

Additionally applicants must have lost their job involuntarily. 

This means that voluntary resignation does not qualify for assistance. 

Individuals must also be registered with the Employment Exchange. 

The application process for the scheme is straightforward. 

Eligible individuals can apply online through the Employees' State Insurance Corporation portal. 

They must fill out the application form with required details. 

Documents such as proof of unemployment and identity must be submitted. 

Once the application is submitted it will be reviewed by the concerned authorities. 

The timeline for processing applications can vary. 

Typically the processing time is around 15 days from the date of application. 

However this may vary based on the volume of applications received. 

The frequency of assistance is limited to one time per individual. 

This means that once an individual receives the benefit they cannot reapply for the same period of unemployment. 

The implementing authority for the Atal Bimit Vyakti Kalyan Yojana is the Employees' State Insurance Corporation. 

This organization is responsible for the administration of the scheme. 

They oversee the processing of applications and disbursement of funds.
