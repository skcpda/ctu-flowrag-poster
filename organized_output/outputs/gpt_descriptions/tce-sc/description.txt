The TCE-SC scheme is designed to provide financial assistance to Scheduled Castes. 

It aims to enhance the educational and employment opportunities for individuals from these communities. 

The scheme offers scholarships for students pursuing higher education. 

It also provides financial support for skill development programs. 

Eligibility is primarily based on the applicant's caste status as a Scheduled Caste. 

Applicants must be enrolled in recognized educational institutions or skill development programs. 

The application process typically involves submitting necessary documents to the relevant authorities. 

The timeline for disbursal of funds may vary based on the specific program within the scheme. 

The implementing authorities include the Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment and state welfare departments. 

Additional details about the scheme can be found on official government websites.
