The SRRNA scheme stands for the Scheme for the Rehabilitation of Persons with Disabilities. 

It aims to provide support and rehabilitation services to persons with disabilities. 

The objectives include enhancing the quality of life for persons with disabilities. 

The scheme provides financial assistance for various rehabilitation services. 

Benefits include vocational training and employment opportunities. 

Eligibility criteria include being a person with a disability as defined under the Rights of Persons with Disabilities Act. 

The application process involves submitting an application to the designated authority in the respective state. 

The timeline for processing applications may vary based on the state and specific circumstances. 

Implementing authorities include the Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment and state governments. 

Additional details may include awareness programs and community-based rehabilitation initiatives.
