The Pradhan Mantri Fasal Bima Yojana is a flagship crop insurance scheme launched by the Government of India. 

The scheme was introduced on January 13, 2016. 

It aims to provide financial support to farmers in the event of crop failure due to unforeseen circumstances. 

The initiative was launched to replace the earlier crop insurance schemes that were often criticized for their inefficiency. 

The scheme is designed to ensure that farmers receive timely compensation for crop losses. 

The primary objective of the scheme is to stabilize the income of farmers. 

It also aims to encourage farmers to adopt innovative and modern agricultural practices. 

The scheme covers a wide range of crops including cereals, pulses, oilseeds, and commercial crops. 

Farmers can avail of insurance coverage for both Kharif and Rabi seasons. 

The benefits of the scheme include financial assistance in the case of crop loss due to natural calamities. 

Farmers are also provided with coverage for crop loss due to pest attacks and diseases. 

The scheme provides for a uniform premium rate of only 2% for Kharif crops. 

For Rabi crops, the premium rate is set at 1.5%. 

Commercial and horticultural crops have a premium rate of 5%. 

The government bears the remaining premium amount to ensure affordability for farmers. 

In the event of crop loss, farmers can receive compensation based on the value of the crop insured. 

The compensation is calculated based on the average yield of the crop in the area. 

Farmers are eligible for the scheme if they own cultivable land. 

Tenant farmers and sharecroppers are also eligible under certain conditions. 

Farmers must be registered under the scheme to avail of its benefits. 

The application process for the scheme is straightforward. 

Farmers can apply through the online portal or through designated local offices. 

They need to provide necessary documents such as land records and identity proof. 

The application must be submitted within a specified time frame after the sowing of crops. 

The timeline for application submission usually varies based on the crop season. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers' Welfare. 

State governments play a crucial role in the implementation of the scheme. 

They are responsible for conducting surveys and assessing crop losses. 

Insurance companies are also involved in the execution of the scheme. 

They are tasked with providing insurance coverage and processing claims. 

The scheme has provisions for timely settlement of claims. 

Farmers can expect to receive compensation within a stipulated period after the claim is filed.

It has significantly increased the number of insured farmers in India.
