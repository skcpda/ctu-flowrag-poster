The SECOII scheme aims to support the development of the Indian economy through the promotion of entrepreneurship. 

The objective is to enhance the competitiveness of small and medium enterprises. 

The scheme provides financial assistance and support services to eligible entrepreneurs. 

Benefits include access to credit and capacity building programs. 

Eligibility is generally restricted to small and medium enterprises as defined by the Ministry of MSME. 

The application process involves submitting a proposal to the relevant government authority. 

The timeline for processing applications may vary based on the specific requirements of the proposal. 

Implementing authorities include the Ministry of Micro Small and Medium Enterprises and associated state departments. 

Additional details regarding specific funding limits and project types may be available through official government channels.
