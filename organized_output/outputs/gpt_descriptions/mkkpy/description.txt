The Mukhyamantri Kanya Suraksha Yojana (MKKPY) is a welfare scheme initiated by the Government of Madhya Pradesh. 

The scheme aims to enhance the safety and security of girls in the state. 

It promotes the education and empowerment of girls. 

The benefits include financial assistance for the education of girls. 

Eligible beneficiaries receive a fixed amount upon reaching certain educational milestones. 

The scheme targets families with a girl child. 

The eligibility criteria include being a resident of Madhya Pradesh and having a girl child. 

The application process involves filling out a form available at designated government offices or online portals. 

The timeline for receiving benefits is linked to the educational milestones of the girl child. 

The implementing authority for this scheme is the Department of Women and Child Development, Madhya Pradesh. 

Additional details may include awareness programs to promote the scheme and its objectives.
