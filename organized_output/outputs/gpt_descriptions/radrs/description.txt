The RADRS scheme stands for the Rapid Assessment of Disability Rehabilitation Services. 

It was launched to assess and improve the quality of rehabilitation services for persons with disabilities in India. 

The main objective is to create a comprehensive database of rehabilitation services available across the country. 

The scheme aims to identify gaps in service delivery and enhance the effectiveness of rehabilitation programs. 

Benefits include improved access to rehabilitation services for persons with disabilities. 

The assistance provided under the scheme focuses on enhancing the quality of life for individuals with disabilities. 

Eligibility criteria include individuals with disabilities seeking rehabilitation services. 

The application process involves registering through designated government portals or agencies. 

The timeline for assessments and implementation may vary based on regional requirements. 

The Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment is the primary implementing authority for this scheme. 

Additional details may include collaboration with state governments and NGOs for effective service delivery.
