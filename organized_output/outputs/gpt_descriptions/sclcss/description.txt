The Scheme for Credit Linked Capital Subsidy for Technology Upgradation (SCLCSS) is an initiative by the Government of India. 

It aims to promote technology upgradation in small-scale industries. 

The objective is to enhance the competitiveness of small-scale industries through modern technology. 

The scheme provides financial assistance in the form of a capital subsidy. 

The subsidy is available for the purchase of plant and machinery. 

Eligible industries can receive a subsidy of up to 15 percent on the investment. 

The maximum limit for the subsidy is set at Rs. 15 lakhs. 

Eligibility is primarily for small-scale industries as defined by the MSME Development Act. 

The applicant must have a valid Udyog Aadhaar registration. 

The application process involves submitting a proposal to the concerned financial institution. 

The financial institution will assess the proposal and forward it to the Ministry of MSME. 

The timeline for subsidy disbursement varies based on the financial institution's processing time. 

The Ministry of Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises is the implementing authority. 

The scheme is part of the broader initiatives to support MSMEs in India. 

It encourages the adoption of modern technologies to improve productivity. 

The scheme is applicable across various sectors of the economy. 

The SCLCSS is crucial for the growth and sustainability of small industries.
