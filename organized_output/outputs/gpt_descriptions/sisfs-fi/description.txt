The Scheme for Implementation of the Scheduled Tribes Component in the Five-Year Plans (SISFS-FI) aims to enhance the socio-economic development of Scheduled Tribes in India. 

The objective of the scheme is to ensure that the benefits of development programs reach the Scheduled Tribes. 

The scheme provides financial assistance for various development projects targeted at Scheduled Tribes. 

Eligibility is primarily based on the applicant's status as a member of a Scheduled Tribe. 

The application process involves submitting proposals for development projects to the concerned state government. 

The timeline for project implementation varies depending on the specific project approved. 

The Ministry of Tribal Affairs is the implementing authority for this scheme. 

The scheme emphasizes the need for community participation in the planning and execution of projects. 

It also focuses on capacity building and skill development among Scheduled Tribe communities.
