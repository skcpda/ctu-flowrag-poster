The PM-YASASVI scheme is a government initiative aimed at providing financial assistance to students from economically weaker sections. 

It is designed to support students pursuing higher education in various fields. 

The primary objective is to enhance the accessibility of quality education for underprivileged students. 

The scheme offers scholarships to meritorious students to help cover their educational expenses. 

Eligible candidates include students from economically weaker sections who have completed their Class 8 or Class 12 examinations. 

The application process involves filling out an online application form available on the official PM-YASASVI portal. 

Candidates must provide necessary documents to verify their eligibility. 

The timeline for application submission is typically announced on the official portal. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Education, Government of India. 

Further details regarding the scholarship amount and specific eligibility criteria can be found on the official website.
