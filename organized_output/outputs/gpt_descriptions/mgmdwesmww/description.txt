The Mahatma Gandhi MGNREGA Wage Employment Scheme for Marginalized Women Workers is designed to provide employment opportunities to marginalized women in rural areas. 

The objective of the scheme is to enhance the livelihood security of women workers by providing them with guaranteed wage employment. 

The scheme aims to empower women by ensuring their participation in the workforce and improving their economic status. 

Eligible beneficiaries include marginalized women workers in rural areas who are willing to work on unskilled manual work. 

The application process typically involves registration under the Mahatma Gandhi National Rural Employment Guarantee Act. 

Once registered beneficiaries can apply for work through local Gram Panchayats. 

The scheme provides a minimum of 100 days of wage employment in a financial year. 

Implementing authorities include the Ministry of Rural Development and local Gram Panchayats. 

The scheme is part of the larger framework of the Mahatma Gandhi National Rural Employment Guarantee Act.
