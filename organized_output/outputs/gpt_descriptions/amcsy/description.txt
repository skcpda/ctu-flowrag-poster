The Aam Aadmi Bima Yojana (AABY) is a government welfare scheme in India aimed at providing social security to the unorganized sector workers. 

This scheme was launched on October 2, 2007. 

It was initiated by the Ministry of Labour and Employment of the Government of India. 

The primary objective of the AABY is to provide life insurance coverage to the members of the unorganized sector. 

The scheme aims to ensure financial security for the families of workers in case of an unfortunate event such as death. 

The AABY is particularly significant as a large portion of India's workforce is engaged in the unorganized sector. 

This sector includes workers in agriculture, construction, and various informal jobs. 

The benefits of the AABY include providing life insurance coverage of Rs. 2 lakh. 

This coverage is provided in the event of the death of the insured member. 

The scheme also offers a scholarship for the children of the insured members. 

The scholarship is provided for up to two children. 

This scholarship is available for students studying in classes 9 to 12. 

The amount of the scholarship is Rs. 1,000 per annum per child. 

The AABY also provides for the payment of premium by the government. 

The premium for the insurance coverage is Rs. 200 per annum. 

The government pays 50% of the premium for the beneficiaries. 

This makes the scheme affordable for low-income families. 

Eligibility for the AABY includes being a member of the unorganized sector. 

The age of the applicant must be between 18 and 59 years. 

The applicant must also be a resident of India. 

The scheme is open to individuals who are not covered under any other social security scheme. 

The application process for the AABY is straightforward. 

Interested individuals can apply through the local insurance office. 

They can also apply through various designated agencies. 

The application form can be obtained from these offices or downloaded online. 

Applicants must provide necessary documents such as identity proof and age proof. 

Once the application is submitted, it is processed by the concerned authorities. 

The timeline for the approval of applications may vary. 

Typically, the processing time is around 30 days. 

The AABY is implemented by the Life Insurance Corporation of India. 

The scheme is also monitored by the Ministry of Labour and Employment. 

The government conducts regular reviews to assess the effectiveness of the scheme.
