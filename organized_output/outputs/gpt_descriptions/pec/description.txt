The Pradhan Mantri Employment Generation Programme (PMEGP) is a credit-linked subsidy scheme. 

It was launched by the Ministry of Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises. 

The scheme aims to promote self-employment through the establishment of micro-enterprises. 

It provides financial assistance to individuals for setting up new ventures. 

The objectives include generating employment opportunities and enhancing the income of beneficiaries. 

The benefits include a subsidy on the project cost and access to bank credit. 

The subsidy varies based on the category of the beneficiary and the location of the project. 

For general category applicants the subsidy is 15 percent in urban areas and 25 percent in rural areas. 

For special category applicants such as SC ST and women the subsidy is 25 percent in urban areas and 35 percent in rural areas. 

Eligibility criteria include being an individual above the age of 18 years. 

Applicants must not be enrolled in any other subsidy scheme. 

The application process involves submitting a project proposal to the nearest bank or nodal agency. 

The proposal should include details of the project and the estimated cost. 

The timeline for approval typically ranges from 15 to 30 days after submission. 

The implementing authorities include the Ministry of Micro Small and Medium Enterprises and various banks. 

State governments also play a role in the implementation of the scheme. 

The scheme is operational throughout India. 

It aims to boost the entrepreneurial spirit among the youth and marginalized sections of society. 

The scheme is reviewed periodically to enhance its effectiveness.
