The Rof scheme is designed to provide financial assistance to the families of deceased workers in the unorganized sector. 

The primary objective is to ensure that families do not face financial hardships after the loss of their breadwinner. 

The benefits include a one-time financial assistance amount to support the family during a difficult time. 

Eligibility criteria typically include being a family member of a deceased worker in the unorganized sector. 

The application process generally involves submitting a death certificate and proof of relationship to the deceased. 

The timeline for disbursement of funds can vary based on the processing of applications. 

Implementing authorities may include state governments and local bodies responsible for welfare schemes. 

Further details on specific eligibility and application requirements may vary by state.
