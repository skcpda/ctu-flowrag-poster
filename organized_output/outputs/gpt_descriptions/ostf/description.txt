The One Stop Centre Scheme is an initiative by the Government of India. 

It aims to provide support and assistance to women affected by violence. 

The scheme seeks to ensure that women have access to a range of services under one roof. 

Benefits include medical assistance legal aid psychological counseling and temporary shelter. 

Eligibility is primarily for women who are victims of violence including domestic violence sexual assault and trafficking. 

Women can access services at designated One Stop Centres located across various states. 

The application process typically involves visiting a One Stop Centre and reporting the incident. 

The timeline for assistance is immediate upon reporting to the Centre. 

The Ministry of Women and Child Development is the implementing authority for this scheme. 

State governments are responsible for establishing and managing the One Stop Centres. 

The scheme also aims to create awareness about women's rights and available services. 

It is part of a broader effort to empower women and ensure their safety in society.
