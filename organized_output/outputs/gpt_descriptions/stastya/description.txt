The Stastya scheme is an initiative by the Government of India aimed at improving health outcomes. 

The scheme focuses on providing quality healthcare services to underserved populations. 

The objective is to enhance access to healthcare facilities and ensure timely medical assistance. 

Benefits include financial assistance for medical treatments and access to healthcare services. 

Eligibility criteria typically include being a resident of the targeted areas and belonging to economically weaker sections. 

The application process generally involves filling out a form and submitting it to designated healthcare centers or online portals. 

The timeline for assistance may vary based on the specific healthcare needs and the processing of applications. 

Implementing authorities include the Ministry of Health and Family Welfare and state health departments. 

The scheme may also involve partnerships with local NGOs and healthcare providers to reach beneficiaries effectively.
