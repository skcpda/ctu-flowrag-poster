The SEFTCW scheme stands for the Scheme for Empowerment of Female Through Child Welfare. 

It aims to empower women by providing support for their children's welfare and education. 

The objectives include enhancing the overall well-being of children and promoting gender equality. 

Benefits include financial assistance for education and health care for children. 

Eligible beneficiaries are women with children who require support for education and health. 

The application process typically involves submitting relevant documents to the local authorities. 

The timeline for assistance is generally determined by the specific needs of the beneficiaries. 

Implementing authorities include the Ministry of Women and Child Development and local government bodies.
