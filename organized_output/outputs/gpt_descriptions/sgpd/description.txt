The Scheme for Grant of Post-Doctoral Fellowship for Women (SGPD) aims to promote research among women scholars. 

It provides financial assistance to women who have completed their Ph.D. and wish to pursue post-doctoral research. 

The objective is to enhance the participation of women in research and development activities. 

The benefits include a monthly fellowship amount along with a contingency grant for research-related expenses. 

Eligible candidates must be women who have completed their Ph.D. within the last five years. 

They should not be more than 50 years of age at the time of application. 

The application process involves submitting an online application along with necessary documents through the official portal. 

The selection is based on the evaluation of the research proposal and the academic credentials of the applicant. 

The fellowship is awarded for a period of two years. 

The implementing authority for this scheme is the Department of Science and Technology, Government of India.
