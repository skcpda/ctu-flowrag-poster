The RPSY scheme stands for the Rajiv Gandhi Panchayat Sashaktikaran Yojana. 

It was launched to strengthen the Panchayati Raj institutions in India. 

The objective of the scheme is to enhance the capacity of Panchayati Raj institutions to deliver services effectively. 

The scheme aims to promote decentralized governance and empower local self-governments. 

Benefits include financial assistance for capacity building and training of elected representatives and officials. 

The scheme also supports the development of infrastructure for Panchayati Raj institutions. 

Eligibility criteria include being a member of the Panchayati Raj system. 

The application process involves submission of proposals by the Panchayati Raj institutions to the respective state governments. 

The timeline for implementation varies based on state-specific plans and requirements. 

The Ministry of Panchayati Raj is the implementing authority for this scheme. 

Additional details may include state-specific guidelines and funding patterns.
