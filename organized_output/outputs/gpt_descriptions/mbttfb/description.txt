The Mukhyamantri Bhatta Tatha Fasal Bima Yojana (MBTTFB) is a welfare scheme initiated by the Government of Madhya Pradesh. 

The scheme aims to provide financial assistance to farmers in the event of crop loss due to natural calamities. 

It seeks to ensure the economic stability of farmers and promote agricultural sustainability. 

Eligible beneficiaries include farmers who own agricultural land in Madhya Pradesh. 

Farmers must also be registered under the state's revenue records. 

The application process typically involves submitting an application form along with required documents to the local revenue office. 

The scheme is implemented by the Department of Farmers Welfare and Agriculture Development, Madhya Pradesh. 

The financial assistance is provided based on the extent of crop loss assessed by the authorities. 

The assistance is disbursed directly to the bank accounts of the eligible farmers. 

The frequency of assistance depends on the occurrence of natural calamities affecting crops. 

The scheme is part of the state's broader efforts to support the agricultural sector and enhance farmers' livelihoods.
