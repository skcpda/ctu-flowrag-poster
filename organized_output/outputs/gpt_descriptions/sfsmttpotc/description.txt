The scheme is known as the Scheme for Financial Support for the Management of Tribal Population in the Context of the 2021 Census (SFSMTTPOTC). 

It was launched to ensure proper representation and management of tribal populations during the 2021 Census. 

The objective is to provide financial support for the effective management of tribal data collection. 

The benefits include funding for state governments and agencies involved in the census process. 

Eligibility criteria include state governments and agencies responsible for census activities. 

The application process involves submission of proposals by eligible entities to the Ministry of Tribal Affairs. 

The timeline for the scheme aligns with the census schedule and related activities. 

The implementing authority is the Ministry of Tribal Affairs. 

This scheme aims to enhance the accuracy of tribal population data for better policy formulation.
