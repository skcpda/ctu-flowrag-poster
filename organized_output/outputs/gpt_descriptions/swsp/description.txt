The Swami Vivekananda Swavalamban Protsahan Yojana (SWSP) aims to promote self-employment among youth. 

It is designed to provide financial assistance to individuals who wish to start their own businesses. 

The primary objective is to enhance the employability of youth and encourage entrepreneurship. 

Under this scheme financial assistance is provided in the form of a subsidy. 

The subsidy is available for various sectors including manufacturing and services. 

Eligibility criteria include being a resident of India and being within the age group of 18 to 35 years. 

Applicants must have a viable business plan and should not be a defaulter on any loans. 

The application process involves submitting a detailed project report along with the application form. 

Applications can be submitted online through designated government portals. 

The timeline for approval and disbursement of funds may vary based on the project and the implementing agency. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Skill Development and Entrepreneurship. 

It aims to empower youth and promote sustainable livelihoods. 

Regular monitoring and evaluation are conducted to assess the impact of the scheme.
