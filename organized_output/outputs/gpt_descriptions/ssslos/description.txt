The SSSLOS scheme stands for the Social Security Scheme for Landless Agricultural Labourers and Small and Marginal Farmers. 

It was launched to provide social security to landless agricultural laborers and small and marginal farmers. 

The primary objective is to ensure financial assistance and social security for vulnerable agricultural workers. 

The scheme offers benefits in the form of financial assistance during emergencies such as illness or accidents. 

Eligible beneficiaries include landless agricultural laborers and small and marginal farmers. 

The application process typically involves submitting an application form along with necessary documents to the local implementing authority. 

The timeline for disbursement of benefits may vary based on the specific circumstances of the applicant. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers' Welfare at the central level. 

State governments may also have a role in the implementation and management of the scheme. 

Additional details regarding specific benefits and the exact amount of assistance may vary by state.
