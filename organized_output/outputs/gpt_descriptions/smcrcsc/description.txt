The SMCRC-SC scheme stands for the Scheme for the Management of Rehabilitation of Children in Special Circumstances. 

It is designed to provide support and rehabilitation for children who are in difficult circumstances. 

The primary objective is to ensure the well-being and development of these children. 

The scheme aims to provide a safe environment and access to education and healthcare. 

Benefits include financial assistance for rehabilitation and support services. 

Eligibility is typically determined based on the child's circumstances and needs. 

The application process involves submitting a request to the relevant authorities. 

Timeline for assistance may vary based on individual cases and needs. 

The implementing authority is the Ministry of Women and Child Development. 

Additional details may include collaboration with NGOs and local bodies for effective implementation.
