The TIFAST scheme stands for Technology Innovation Fund for Agriculture Startups. 

It was launched by the Government of India to promote innovation in the agricultural sector. 

The objective of the scheme is to support startups that are developing innovative technologies for agriculture. 

The scheme aims to enhance productivity and sustainability in the agricultural sector. 

Benefits include financial assistance for research and development activities. 

Startups can receive funding to develop and commercialize their agricultural technologies. 

Eligibility is primarily for startups that are registered in India and focus on agricultural innovations. 

The application process involves submitting a proposal outlining the innovative technology and its potential impact. 

The timeline for funding disbursement varies based on the evaluation of proposals. 

The scheme is implemented by the Department of Agriculture and Farmers' Welfare. 

Other details may include specific focus areas such as precision farming and agro-tech solutions.
