The VS-FAUSMPHS scheme is a welfare initiative by the Government of India. 

It aims to provide financial assistance to farmers for the purchase of agricultural inputs. 

The objective is to enhance agricultural productivity and ensure food security. 

The scheme benefits farmers by providing subsidies on seeds fertilizers and pesticides. 

Eligible beneficiaries include small and marginal farmers. 

Farmers must possess a valid landholding certificate to qualify. 

The application process involves filling out a form available at local agricultural offices. 

Farmers may also apply online through the official government portal. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers' Welfare. 

Financial assistance is disbursed annually based on the crop cycle. 

The scheme is part of the broader efforts to support the agricultural sector in India.
