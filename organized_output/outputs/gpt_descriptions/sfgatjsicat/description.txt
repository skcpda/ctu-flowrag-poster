The SFGATJSICAT scheme is designed to support the skill development of youth in India. 

It aims to enhance employability and promote entrepreneurship among young individuals. 

The scheme provides financial assistance for skill training programs. 

Eligible beneficiaries include youth aged between 15 to 29 years. 

The application process involves submitting an online application through the official portal. 

The timeline for the training programs varies based on the specific course selected. 

The implementing authority for this scheme is the Ministry of Skill Development and Entrepreneurship. 

Additional details about specific training programs and their outcomes can be found on the official government website.
