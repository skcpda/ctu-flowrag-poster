The Scheme for the Support of the Fishermen and Fish Farmers (SOSFSS) aims to enhance the livelihoods of fishermen and fish farmers in India. 

The objective of the scheme is to provide financial assistance and support for sustainable fishing practices. 

Benefits include financial aid for purchasing fishing equipment and support for aquaculture development. 

Eligibility is primarily for fishermen and fish farmers registered under relevant state or central government schemes. 

The application process typically involves submitting an application form along with necessary documents to the local fisheries department. 

The timeline for processing applications may vary by state and is generally completed within a few weeks. 

The Ministry of Fisheries Animal Husbandry and Dairying is the implementing authority for this scheme. 

Additional details may include training programs for sustainable fishing practices and awareness campaigns on fish farming techniques.
