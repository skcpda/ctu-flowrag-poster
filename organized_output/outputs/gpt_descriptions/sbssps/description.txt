The scheme SBSSPS stands for the Skill Development and Entrepreneurship Scheme. 

It was launched to promote skill development and entrepreneurship among the youth of India. 

The primary objective is to enhance the employability of the youth through skill training. 

The scheme aims to support self-employment and entrepreneurship development. 

Benefits include financial assistance for skill development training programs. 

Participants may receive support for setting up their own businesses. 

Eligibility criteria typically include age limits and educational qualifications. 

The scheme is primarily targeted at youth aged between 18 to 35 years. 

The application process generally involves submitting an application form to designated authorities. 

Applications may also require supporting documents related to identity and education. 

The timeline for processing applications may vary based on the implementing agency. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Skill Development and Entrepreneurship. 

Additional details regarding specific training programs and financial assistance may be available through official channels.
