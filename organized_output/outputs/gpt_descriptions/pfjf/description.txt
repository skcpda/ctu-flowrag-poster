The Pradhan Mantri Jan Dhan Yojana (PMJDY) is a financial inclusion program launched by the Government of India. 

It was introduced on August 28 2014. 

The objective of PMJDY is to provide universal access to banking facilities. 

It aims to ensure that every household has at least one bank account. 

The scheme promotes financial literacy and provides access to credit insurance and pension. 

Benefits include zero balance accounts and access to overdraft facilities. 

Account holders are eligible for a RuPay debit card and life insurance cover. 

Eligibility is open to all Indian citizens aged 10 years and above. 

The application process involves visiting a bank branch or a financial institution. 

Applicants must provide valid identification and address proof. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Finance and various public sector banks. 

There is no specific timeline for account opening as it is an ongoing scheme. 

The scheme also includes provisions for direct benefit transfers to account holders. 

Additional benefits may vary based on the bank and location.
