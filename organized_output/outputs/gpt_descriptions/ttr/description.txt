The TTR scheme stands for the Transport and Tourism Development Scheme. 

It aims to promote tourism and enhance transport facilities in various regions of India. 

The objectives include improving infrastructure for tourism and providing better connectivity to tourist destinations. 

Benefits include financial assistance for developing tourism-related infrastructure such as hotels and transport services. 

Assistance is provided in the form of grants and loans to eligible entities. 

Eligibility criteria typically include being a registered business or organization involved in tourism or transport. 

The application process involves submitting a proposal to the relevant government authority along with necessary documentation. 

The timeline for processing applications may vary based on the specific project and the authority involved. 

Implementing authorities include the Ministry of Tourism and various state tourism departments. 

Additional details may include specific guidelines for project proposals and funding limits based on the type of project.
