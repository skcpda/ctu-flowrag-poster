The MRCB SHPB O CWWB scheme is designed to provide financial assistance to construction workers in India. 

The objective of the scheme is to support the welfare of construction workers through various benefits. 

The benefits include financial aid for medical treatment and education for children of construction workers. 

Eligibility is generally limited to registered construction workers who are part of the Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board. 

The application process typically requires submission of relevant documents to the local welfare board. 

The timeline for disbursement of benefits may vary based on the specific assistance requested. 

The scheme is implemented by the respective State Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Boards. 

Additional details about specific benefits and assistance may vary by state.
