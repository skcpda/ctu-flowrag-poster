The SSLS-M scheme stands for the State Skill Livelihood Scheme for Migrants. 

It was launched to provide skill development and livelihood opportunities for migrant workers. 

The objective of the scheme is to enhance the employability of migrants through skill training. 

The scheme aims to support migrants in securing sustainable livelihoods. 

Benefits include skill training programs that are aligned with market demand. 

Financial assistance may be provided for training and placement. 

Eligibility criteria include being a migrant worker who has relocated for employment. 

The application process typically involves submitting an application form to the designated authority. 

The timeline for training and placement may vary based on the specific program. 

Implementing authorities include state governments and skill development agencies. 

The scheme may also focus on creating awareness about available job opportunities. 

Additional details may include partnerships with industries for better placement opportunities.
