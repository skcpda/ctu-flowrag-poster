The SSSPY scheme stands for the "Sambal Scheme for Social Security and Protection of Vulnerable Groups." 

It was launched by the Government of Madhya Pradesh. 

The scheme aims to provide social security and financial assistance to vulnerable groups in society. 

The primary objectives include ensuring the welfare of the poor and marginalized sections of the population. 

The scheme offers various benefits such as financial aid for widows or destitute women. 

It also provides support for the elderly and persons with disabilities. 

Eligibility criteria include being a resident of Madhya Pradesh. 

Applicants must belong to economically weaker sections or be part of vulnerable groups. 

The application process involves submitting an application form along with necessary documents to the local authorities. 

The timeline for processing applications may vary based on the local administrative procedures. 

The scheme is implemented by the Department of Social Justice and Disabled Welfare in Madhya Pradesh. 

Additional details may include specific financial assistance amounts which can vary based on the category of beneficiaries.
