The Mukhyamantri Sewa Sankalp Helpline Scheme is an initiative by the Government of Madhya Pradesh. 

It aims to provide a single-window solution for citizens to access various government services. 

The objectives include enhancing the efficiency of service delivery and reducing the time taken to access government services. 

The scheme benefits citizens by providing them with a dedicated helpline for assistance in availing government services. 

Eligibility is generally open to all residents of Madhya Pradesh seeking government services. 

The application process involves calling the helpline number to receive guidance and support. 

The frequency of assistance is continuous as the helpline operates regularly. 

The scheme is implemented by the Government of Madhya Pradesh through its various departments. 

Additional details about specific services offered may vary based on departmental guidelines.
