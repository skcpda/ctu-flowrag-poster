The UASUKBOCWWB scheme is designed to provide financial assistance to construction workers in India. 

The objective of the scheme is to support the welfare of construction workers by offering them various benefits. 

The benefits include financial aid for medical treatment and education for their children. 

Eligible beneficiaries are registered construction workers who are part of the Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board. 

The application process involves registration with the respective state Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board. 

The timeline for the disbursement of benefits may vary based on the state regulations and the specific requirements of the application. 

The implementing authority for this scheme is the Ministry of Labour and Employment along with the respective state welfare boards. 

Additional details about the scheme can be accessed through the official state welfare board websites.
