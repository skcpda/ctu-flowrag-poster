The UBA scheme stands for the Unnat Bharat Abhiyan. 

It was launched by the Government of India in 2014. 

The scheme aims to promote sustainable development in rural areas through the involvement of higher educational institutions. 

The objective is to address the challenges faced by rural communities and enhance their quality of life. 

The benefits include the development of rural infrastructure and the implementation of innovative solutions to local problems. 

Assistance is provided in the form of research and development projects that cater to the needs of rural areas. 

Eligibility includes higher educational institutions that are recognized by the University Grants Commission. 

The application process involves institutions submitting proposals for projects that align with the scheme's objectives. 

The timeline for project implementation varies based on the specific project and its requirements. 

The Ministry of Education is the primary implementing authority for the UBA scheme. 

Participating institutions are expected to collaborate with local communities to ensure effective implementation. 

The scheme encourages the use of technology and local resources to achieve sustainable development. 

Regular monitoring and evaluation are conducted to assess the impact of the projects undertaken.
