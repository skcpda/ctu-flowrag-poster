The scheme SDVAY stands for the Skill Development and Vocational Training for Youth. 

It aims to enhance the employability of youth through skill development and vocational training. 

The objectives include providing quality training to youth and promoting entrepreneurship. 

Benefits include access to skill development programs and financial assistance for training. 

Eligibility criteria typically include age limits and educational qualifications which may vary by program. 

The application process usually involves filling out an online or offline application form and submitting required documents. 

The timeline for training programs may vary based on the specific courses offered. 

Implementing authorities include the Ministry of Skill Development and Entrepreneurship and various state skill development missions. 

Additional details may include partnerships with industry and educational institutions for better training outcomes.
