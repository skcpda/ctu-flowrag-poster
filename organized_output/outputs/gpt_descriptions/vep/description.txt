The VEP scheme stands for the Vikas Pradhan Mantri Employment Generation Programme. 

It aims to provide self-employment opportunities to individuals in rural and urban areas. 

The objective is to promote entrepreneurship and create sustainable livelihoods. 

The scheme offers financial assistance in the form of subsidies and loans to eligible beneficiaries. 

Benefits include skill development training and access to credit facilities. 

Eligibility criteria include being a resident of India and having a viable business idea. 

The application process involves submitting a detailed project report to the concerned authority. 

The timeline for approval and disbursement of funds varies based on the project and the implementing agency. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Micro Small and Medium Enterprises. 

Additional details on specific funding limits and training programs may be available through official channels.
