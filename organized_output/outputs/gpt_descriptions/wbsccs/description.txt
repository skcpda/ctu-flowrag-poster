The West Bengal State Cooperative Credit Society Scheme (WBSCCS) aims to strengthen the cooperative credit structure in West Bengal. 

The objective of the scheme is to provide financial assistance to cooperative societies for enhancing their operational capabilities. 

The benefits include access to credit facilities for cooperative societies which can help them in their financial transactions and growth. 

Eligibility is primarily for registered cooperative societies operating within West Bengal. 

The application process involves submitting a proposal to the concerned department along with necessary documentation. 

The timeline for processing applications may vary based on the completeness of the submitted documents. 

The implementing authority for this scheme is the Department of Cooperation, Government of West Bengal. 

The scheme is part of the broader initiative to promote cooperative movements in the state.
