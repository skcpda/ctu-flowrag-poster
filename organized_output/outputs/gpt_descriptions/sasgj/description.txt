The Sardar Awas Yojana (SASGJ) is a government welfare scheme aimed at providing housing for the economically weaker sections of society. 

The objective of the scheme is to ensure that every individual has access to affordable housing. 

The benefits include financial assistance for constructing or purchasing a house. 

Eligibility is primarily based on income criteria and ownership of land. 

The application process typically involves submitting an application form along with required documents to the local authorities. 

The timeline for disbursement of funds may vary based on the specific project and location. 

The implementing authority for this scheme is the Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs. 

Additional details may include provisions for interest subsidies on housing loans.
