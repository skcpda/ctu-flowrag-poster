The RTFTSCCW scheme stands for the "Rehabilitation of Transgender Persons and Families of Sex Workers in the Context of COVID-19." 

This scheme was launched in response to the challenges faced by transgender persons and families of sex workers during the COVID-19 pandemic. 

The main objective is to provide financial assistance and support to these marginalized communities. 

The benefits include direct cash transfers and access to essential services such as healthcare and social welfare programs. 

Eligibility is primarily focused on transgender individuals and families of sex workers who have been adversely affected by the pandemic. 

The application process involves submitting an application form along with necessary documentation to the designated authorities. 

The timeline for assistance is typically aligned with the ongoing assessment of needs and may vary based on the situation of the applicants. 

Implementing authorities include the Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment and state welfare departments. 

Additional details may include awareness campaigns to educate the target groups about the scheme and its benefits.
