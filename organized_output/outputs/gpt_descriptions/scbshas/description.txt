The scheme SCBSHAS stands for the State Capital Budget Scheme for Housing for All. 

It aims to provide affordable housing to the economically weaker sections of society. 

The objective is to ensure that every citizen has access to a basic shelter. 

The scheme offers financial assistance for the construction or purchase of houses. 

Benefits include subsidies on housing loans and direct financial support for eligible beneficiaries. 

Eligibility criteria typically include being a member of the economically weaker section or low-income group. 

Applicants must also meet specific income limits set by the state government. 

The application process usually involves submitting a form along with necessary documents to the local housing authority. 

The timeline for processing applications may vary by state but is generally completed within a few months. 

The implementing authorities include the respective state housing boards and urban development departments. 

Additional details may include specific guidelines for construction standards and timelines for project completion.
