The MUPSSE scheme stands for the Mukhyamantri Udyog Sahay Yojana for Small Enterprises. 

It was launched by the Government of Madhya Pradesh to promote small-scale industries. 

The objective of the scheme is to provide financial assistance and support to small enterprises. 

The benefits include financial aid for setting up new businesses and expanding existing ones. 

Assistance is provided in the form of subsidies on capital investment. 

Eligibility is limited to small enterprises registered in Madhya Pradesh. 

The application process involves submitting a proposal to the relevant department. 

The timeline for processing applications may vary based on the department's workload. 

The scheme is implemented by the Department of Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises of Madhya Pradesh. 

Additional details may include specific financial limits and types of industries supported.
