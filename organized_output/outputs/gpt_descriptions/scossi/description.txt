The SCOSSI scheme stands for the Skill Certification of School Students Initiative. 

It was launched by the Ministry of Skill Development and Entrepreneurship. 

The scheme aims to enhance the employability of school students through skill development. 

It provides students with opportunities to acquire skills that are in demand in the job market. 

The benefits include skill certification for students which can aid in their future employment. 

Students can gain practical experience in various sectors through this initiative. 

Eligibility is primarily for school students enrolled in classes 9 to 12. 

The application process involves schools registering for the program. 

Students can then participate in skill training sessions organized by their respective schools. 

The timeline for the skill certification process is aligned with the academic calendar. 

The implementing authority for the SCOSSI scheme is the Ministry of Skill Development and Entrepreneurship. 

The scheme is part of the broader National Skill Development Mission. 

It aims to bridge the gap between education and employment.
