The MSSFSBU scheme stands for the Mukhyamantri Sewa Sankalp and Sewa Bhavishya Udyami scheme. 

It is an initiative by the Government of Madhya Pradesh. 

The scheme aims to promote entrepreneurship among the youth. 

Its objective is to provide financial assistance and skill development to aspiring entrepreneurs. 

The benefits include access to financial support for starting businesses. 

The scheme also offers training programs to enhance skills. 

Eligibility criteria include being a resident of Madhya Pradesh. 

Applicants must be between the ages of 18 and 45. 

They should have a viable business idea or project. 

The application process involves submitting a detailed project proposal. 

Applications can be submitted online through the official government portal. 

The timeline for approval and disbursement of funds varies based on project assessment. 

The implementing authority for this scheme is the Department of Micro Small and Medium Enterprises. 

Additional details may include periodic reviews of the scheme's impact on local entrepreneurship.
