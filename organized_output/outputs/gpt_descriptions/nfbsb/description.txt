The National Family Benefit Scheme (NFBS) is a welfare scheme initiated by the Government of India. 

It aims to provide financial assistance to families in the event of the death of the primary breadwinner. 

The objective is to alleviate the financial burden faced by families due to the loss of income. 

Under this scheme, a one-time financial assistance of Rs. 20,000 is provided to the bereaved family. 

The assistance is intended to help the family meet immediate expenses and maintain their livelihood. 

Eligibility for the scheme includes families belonging to the Below Poverty Line (BPL) category. 

The deceased must be the primary breadwinner of the family. 

The application process involves submitting a death certificate of the deceased along with proof of the family's BPL status. 

Applications can be submitted to the local government office or designated authorities. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Rural Development in collaboration with state governments. 

The financial assistance is generally disbursed within a few weeks of the application being approved. 

The scheme is part of the broader social security measures provided by the Indian government.
