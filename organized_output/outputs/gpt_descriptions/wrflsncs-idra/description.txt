The scheme WRFLNSCS-IDRA is the Welfare Scheme for Rehabilitating Families of Landless Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes.

This scheme aims to provide financial assistance to families of landless Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes.

The objective is to improve the living conditions of these families by providing them with necessary resources for sustainable livelihoods.

Benefits include financial aid for housing and other essential needs.

Eligibility is limited to families belonging to Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes who are landless.

The application process involves submitting an application to the designated authority in the respective state or union territory.

The timeline for disbursement of benefits may vary based on the state or union territory's implementation.

The implementing authorities include the Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment and state government departments.

Additional details regarding specific financial amounts or housing assistance may vary by state.
