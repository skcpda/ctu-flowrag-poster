The SBSDAADC stands for the Special Backward Classes Development and Allied Development Corporation. 

It was established to promote the socio-economic development of the backward classes in India. 

The main objective is to provide financial assistance and support for various developmental activities. 

Benefits include access to loans for self-employment and skill development programs. 

The scheme also aims to enhance the living standards of backward classes through various welfare initiatives. 

Eligibility criteria typically include being a member of a backward class as defined by the government. 

Applicants must also meet specific income limits set by the implementing authorities. 

The application process generally involves submitting a form along with necessary documents to the designated office. 

Timelines for processing applications may vary based on the specific program under the scheme. 

The implementing authority for this scheme is the Special Backward Classes Development and Allied Development Corporation. 

Additional details on specific programs and benefits can be obtained from official government sources.
