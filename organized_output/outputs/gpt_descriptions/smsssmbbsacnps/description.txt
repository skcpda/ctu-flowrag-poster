The scheme SMSSSMBBSACNPS stands for the Scheme for Medical Students of Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes under the National Pension System. 

This scheme is designed to provide financial assistance to medical students from Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes. 

The primary objective is to support these students in pursuing their medical education. 

The benefits include financial assistance for tuition fees and other educational expenses. 

Eligible candidates must belong to Scheduled Castes or Scheduled Tribes. 

They must be enrolled in a recognized medical college or institution. 

The application process typically involves submitting an application form along with required documents to the designated authority. 

The timeline for application submission and disbursement of funds may vary each academic year. 

The implementing authority for this scheme is the Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment. 

Additional details regarding the specific amount of assistance and application deadlines may be available through official government portals.
