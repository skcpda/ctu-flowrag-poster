The Indian government welfare scheme known as the Indian Technical and Economic Cooperation or ITEC is a significant initiative aimed at fostering international cooperation and development. 

ITEC was launched in 1964 by the Government of India. 

The scheme is designed to provide technical assistance and training to developing countries. 

It aims to enhance the capabilities of these countries in various sectors. 

The primary objective of ITEC is to promote mutual cooperation and understanding between India and other developing nations. 

The scheme focuses on capacity building through training programs. 

ITEC offers a wide range of courses in diverse fields such as information technology, agriculture, rural development, and more. 

The benefits of the ITEC scheme extend to participants from partner countries. 

Participants receive training that equips them with skills relevant to their local contexts. 

The training programs are conducted by various Indian institutions and universities. 

These institutions are selected based on their expertise in specific fields. 

Participants are provided with accommodation, travel, and other necessary expenses during their training. 

The scheme also facilitates the exchange of knowledge and best practices. 

This exchange is crucial for fostering long-term relationships between India and other nations. 

Eligibility for the ITEC scheme is primarily targeted at citizens of developing countries. 

Participants must be nominated by their respective governments. 

The selection process is coordinated through Indian diplomatic missions abroad. 

The application process involves filling out a nomination form. 

This form is submitted to the Indian embassy or consulate in the participant's country. 

Once the nomination is received, it is forwarded to the Ministry of External Affairs in India. 

The Ministry reviews the nominations and finalizes the list of participants. 

The timeline for the ITEC training programs varies depending on the specific course. 

Courses can range from a few weeks to several months in duration. 

The frequency of the training programs is generally annual. 

However, specific courses may be offered at different times throughout the year. 

The implementing authority for the ITEC scheme is the Ministry of External Affairs. 

The Ministry is responsible for overseeing the entire process of training and technical cooperation.
