The VS-CAMCD scheme is the "Vulnerability Support for Children Affected by Migration and Climate Disasters" initiative. 

It aims to provide support to children who are vulnerable due to migration and climate-related disasters. 

The objectives include ensuring the safety and well-being of affected children. 

The scheme provides financial assistance and support services to children impacted by such circumstances. 

Eligibility criteria include being a child affected by migration or climate disasters. 

The application process involves submitting a request through designated local authorities or agencies. 

The timeline for assistance is determined based on the specific needs of the affected children. 

Implementing authorities include the Ministry of Women and Child Development and local government bodies. 

Additional details may include collaboration with NGOs and community organizations for effective implementation.
