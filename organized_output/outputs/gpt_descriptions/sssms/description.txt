The SSSMS stands for the State Scheme for the Support of Marginalized Sections. 

It is designed to provide financial assistance to marginalized communities in India. 

The objective of the scheme is to enhance the socio-economic status of marginalized groups. 

The benefits include financial aid for education health and livelihood opportunities. 

Eligibility criteria typically include being a member of a marginalized community and meeting specific income thresholds. 

The application process usually involves submitting a form along with required documents to the designated authority. 

The timeline for disbursement of benefits may vary based on the specific program under the scheme. 

Implementing authorities may include state government departments focused on welfare and social justice. 

Additional details may include specific programs under the scheme that target different sectors such as education or health.
