Petalse is a government welfare scheme aimed at enhancing the livelihoods of farmers in India.

The scheme focuses on promoting the cultivation of flowers and ornamental plants.

Its objectives include increasing the income of farmers and improving their standard of living.

The benefits of the scheme include financial assistance for purchasing seeds and saplings.

Farmers can also receive training on best practices for flower cultivation.

Eligibility criteria include being a registered farmer in India.

The application process involves submitting a form to the local agricultural department.

The timeline for assistance is typically aligned with the planting season.

The implementing authority for the scheme is the Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers' Welfare.

Additional details include support for marketing the produce through government initiatives.
