The Mukhya Mantri Bhatta Yojana for Construction Workers is a welfare scheme launched by the Government of India. 

It aims to provide financial assistance to construction workers who are in need of support. 

The scheme is designed to improve the living conditions of construction workers and their families. 

Eligible beneficiaries include registered construction workers who are part of the Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board. 

The assistance provided under this scheme is in the form of a monthly financial grant. 

The application process typically involves submitting an application form along with required documents to the local welfare board. 

The timeline for disbursement of benefits is usually monthly. 

The scheme is implemented by the respective State Governments through the Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Boards. 

Additional details regarding the specific amount of assistance and documentation requirements may vary by state.
