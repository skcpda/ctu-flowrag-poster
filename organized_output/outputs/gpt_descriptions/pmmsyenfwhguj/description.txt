The scheme PMMSY (Pradhan Mantri Matsya Sampada Yojana) is aimed at enhancing fish production and productivity in India. 

It was launched to promote sustainable development of the fisheries sector. 

The objectives include increasing fish production to 22 million metric tons by 2024-25. 

It also aims to create employment opportunities and increase the income of fishers and fish farmers. 

The benefits include financial assistance for various activities such as fish farming and aquaculture. 

The scheme provides support for the establishment of fish markets and fish processing units. 

Eligibility is primarily for fishers and fish farmers. 

State governments and Union Territories are also eligible to implement the scheme. 

The application process involves submitting proposals to the respective state fisheries departments. 

The timeline for fund disbursement is based on the approval of the proposals. 

The implementing authorities include the Ministry of Fisheries Animal Husbandry and Dairying. 

The scheme is part of the broader initiative to promote the Blue Revolution in India. 

It focuses on sustainable and responsible fishing practices. 

The scheme also emphasizes the use of modern technology in fish farming. 

Financial assistance is provided in the form of grants and subsidies. 

The scheme encourages the participation of cooperatives and self-help groups. 

It aims to improve the livelihoods of rural communities dependent on fisheries. 

The scheme promotes the development of inland and marine fisheries. 

It also supports the establishment of fisheries infrastructure. 

The monitoring and evaluation of the scheme are conducted by the Ministry of Fisheries. 

The scheme is aligned with the National Fisheries Policy. 

It aims to enhance the overall contribution of the fisheries sector to the economy.
