The Bhausaheb Fundkar Horticulture Plantation Scheme is an initiative launched by the Government of Maharashtra. 

This scheme aims to promote horticulture in the state. 

It is named after Bhausaheb Fundkar who was a prominent figure in the horticulture sector. 

The scheme is designed to enhance the income of farmers through the cultivation of horticultural crops. 

Horticulture plays a crucial role in the agricultural economy of Maharashtra. 

The state has diverse climatic conditions suitable for various horticultural crops. 

The objectives of the scheme include increasing the area under horticulture cultivation. 

It also aims to improve the productivity of horticultural crops. 

The scheme seeks to provide financial assistance to farmers for establishing horticulture plantations. 

It encourages the adoption of modern agricultural practices. 

Farmers are provided with high-quality planting materials under this scheme.

Farmers receive financial support for setting up their horticulture plantations. 

The assistance helps in reducing the initial investment burden on farmers. 

The scheme also promotes sustainable agricultural practices.

The eligibility criteria for the scheme are clearly defined. 

Farmers from Maharashtra are eligible to apply for the scheme. 

They must be engaged in agricultural activities. 

The scheme is particularly aimed at small and marginal farmers.

Farmers can apply online through the official government portal.

Once the application is submitted it is reviewed by the concerned authorities. 

The timeline for approval can vary based on the volume of applications.

Financial assistance is typically disbursed in a phased manner. 

Farmers receive funds based on the progress of their plantation activities.

They are responsible for overseeing the implementation of the scheme. 

The department works in collaboration with local agricultural offices. 

These offices provide on-ground support to farmers.
