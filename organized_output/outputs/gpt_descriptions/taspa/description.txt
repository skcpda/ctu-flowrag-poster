The Taspa scheme is an initiative aimed at providing financial assistance to farmers in India. 

The primary objective of the Taspa scheme is to enhance the income of farmers through direct monetary support. 

The scheme offers financial assistance to eligible farmers to help them meet their agricultural expenses. 

Eligible beneficiaries include small and marginal farmers who own agricultural land. 

Farmers must apply through the designated state government offices or online portals. 

The application process typically requires submission of land ownership documents and identity proof. 

The frequency of assistance may vary based on state policies and agricultural cycles. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers' Welfare in collaboration with state governments. 

Additional details regarding the specific amount of assistance and conditions may vary by state.
