The OOSC Scheme stands for the "Out of School Children" Scheme. 

It was launched to address the issue of children who are not enrolled in schools. 

The primary objective is to provide educational opportunities to out-of-school children. 

The scheme aims to bring these children back into the formal education system. 

Benefits include access to education and learning materials. 

Children enrolled under this scheme receive support for their educational needs. 

Eligibility is primarily focused on children who are between the ages of 6 to 14 years. 

The scheme targets children who have never been enrolled in school or have dropped out. 

The application process involves local education authorities identifying out-of-school children. 

Parents or guardians may need to provide necessary documentation for enrollment. 

The timeline for the implementation of the scheme is ongoing. 

It is a continuous effort to ensure that all children receive education. 

The implementing authorities include the Ministry of Education and local education departments. 

Various NGOs and community organizations may also be involved in the implementation. 

The scheme is part of the broader initiative to achieve universal education in India.
