The scheme is known as the Scheme for Financial Inclusion of Fishermen and Women in Aquaculture (SFIFFWA). 

It aims to enhance the financial inclusion of fishermen and women involved in aquaculture. 

The objectives include providing financial assistance to improve livelihoods and promote sustainable fishing practices. 

Benefits include access to credit facilities and financial literacy programs. 

The scheme also provides support for the purchase of fishing equipment and technology. 

Eligibility is primarily for fishermen and women engaged in aquaculture activities. 

The application process involves submitting an application to the designated financial institutions or government agencies. 

The timeline for assistance may vary based on the processing of applications. 

Implementing authorities include the Ministry of Fisheries Animal Husbandry and Dairying. 

The scheme is part of the broader initiative to support the fisheries sector in India.
