The scheme SFSTSUFPC stands for the Scheme for Financial Support to Farmers for Sustainable Practices in Crop Production. 

This scheme is aimed at promoting sustainable agricultural practices among farmers. 

The objective is to provide financial assistance to farmers who adopt eco-friendly farming methods. 

Benefits include financial support for purchasing inputs that promote sustainable practices. 

Eligibility criteria include being a registered farmer and engaging in sustainable agricultural practices. 

The application process involves submitting an application form along with necessary documents to the local agricultural office. 

The timeline for disbursement of funds is typically within a few weeks after application approval. 

The implementing authority for this scheme is the Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers' Welfare. 

Additional details about the specific sustainable practices supported may vary based on state guidelines.
