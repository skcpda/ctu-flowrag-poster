The National Database for Agriculture and Farmers' Welfare (NDAFCWU) is an initiative aimed at creating a comprehensive database for farmers in India. 

The objective is to enhance the welfare of farmers through better access to information and services. 

The scheme aims to provide targeted benefits to farmers based on their specific needs and circumstances. 

Benefits include access to various government schemes and services designed for agricultural development and farmer welfare. 

Eligibility criteria include being a registered farmer in India. 

The application process involves registration on the official portal dedicated to the scheme. 

The timeline for updates and data collection is ongoing to ensure the database remains current. 

The Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers' Welfare is the implementing authority for this scheme. 

The initiative also aims to facilitate better policy-making and resource allocation for agricultural development.
