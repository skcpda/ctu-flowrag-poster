The National Scheme for the Welfare of Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes (NASSVSBPUBSOPWWS) aims to promote the welfare of Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes in India. 

The objective of the scheme is to provide financial assistance and support for the socio-economic development of these communities. 

Benefits include scholarships for students and financial aid for various developmental programs. 

Eligibility criteria typically include being a member of a Scheduled Caste or Scheduled Tribe. 

The application process generally involves submitting an application form along with necessary documents to the designated authorities. 

The timeline for disbursement of benefits may vary based on the specific program under the scheme. 

Implementing authorities include the Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment and various state governments.
