The National Scheme for Welfare of Fishermen (NSWF) aims to enhance the welfare of fishermen in India. 

The objective of NSWF is to provide financial assistance and support to the fishing community. 

Benefits include financial aid for the construction of houses and provision of fishing gear. 

The scheme also offers assistance for skill development and training programs for fishermen. 

Eligibility criteria include being a member of a fishing community and having a valid fishing license. 

The application process involves submitting an application form to the relevant fisheries department. 

The timeline for processing applications may vary based on the specific state or union territory. 

The Ministry of Fisheries Animal Husbandry and Dairying is the implementing authority for this scheme. 

Additional details may include specific state-level adaptations of the scheme.
