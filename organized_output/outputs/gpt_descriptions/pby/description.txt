The Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samman Nidhi (PM-KISAN) scheme was launched by the Government of India in February 2019. 

The scheme aims to provide financial support to small and marginal farmers. 

The objective is to ensure a steady income for farmers and to help them meet their agricultural expenses. 

Under this scheme, eligible farmers receive direct cash transfers of Rs. 6000 per year. 

The amount is disbursed in three equal installments of Rs. 2000 each. 

Eligibility is limited to small and marginal farmers who own cultivable land. 

Farmers must have a landholding of up to 2 hectares. 

The scheme is not applicable to institutional landholders and certain other categories of farmers. 

The application process involves farmers registering on the PM-KISAN portal or through local revenue offices. 

Farmers must provide necessary documentation to verify their eligibility. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers' Welfare. 

The disbursement of funds occurs three times a year. 

The scheme is ongoing and continues to be available to eligible farmers.
