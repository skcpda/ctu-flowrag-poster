The Jagananna Pelli Kanuka Scheme is a welfare initiative launched by the Government of Andhra Pradesh. 

This scheme aims to provide financial assistance to brides from economically weaker sections of society. 

The initiative is part of the broader vision of the Andhra Pradesh government to empower women and support families during the marriage of their daughters. 

The scheme recognizes the financial burden that families often face when arranging weddings. 

It is designed to alleviate some of these financial pressures by providing a monetary benefit to eligible families. 

The primary objective of the Jagananna Pelli Kanuka Scheme is to ensure that no girl is denied a dignified marriage due to financial constraints. 

The scheme aims to promote social equality and support the welfare of women in the state. 

Under this scheme, eligible beneficiaries receive a financial assistance amount. 

As of the latest updates, the assistance provided is Rs. 50,000 per bride. 

This amount is intended to help cover various expenses associated with weddings. 

The scheme is particularly focused on supporting families belonging to the Scheduled Castes, Scheduled Tribes, and Other Backward Classes. 

It also extends to economically weaker sections of the general category. 

To be eligible for the Jagananna Pelli Kanuka Scheme, certain criteria must be met. 

The bride must be a resident of Andhra Pradesh. 

She must belong to a family that falls under the poverty line. 

The bride must be at least 18 years old at the time of marriage. 

The marriage must be registered under the Hindu Marriage Act or the Special Marriage Act. 

The family income should not exceed a specified limit set by the government. 

The application process for the Jagananna Pelli Kanuka Scheme is straightforward. 

Eligible beneficiaries must apply through the official government portal or designated offices. 

Applications can also be submitted through the village or ward secretariats. 

The required documents typically include proof of identity, proof of residence, and income certificate. 

The marriage registration certificate is also necessary to validate the application. 

Once the application is submitted, it undergoes verification by the concerned authorities. 

The timeline for processing applications can vary. 

However, the government aims to ensure timely disbursement of funds to eligible beneficiaries. 

The assistance is generally provided in a single installment. 

The implementing authority for the Jagananna Pelli Kanuka Scheme is the Department of Women Development and Child Welfare.
