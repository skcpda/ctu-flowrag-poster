The TPFKCC scheme stands for the Tamil Nadu Public Distribution System for Kottai and Community Consumption. 

It aims to provide essential commodities to the economically weaker sections of society. 

The objectives include ensuring food security and improving the nutritional status of the population. 

Benefits include subsidized food grains and essential commodities to eligible households. 

Eligibility is primarily based on income criteria and household status as determined by the state government. 

The application process involves submitting a request to the local ration shop or designated government office. 

The frequency of assistance is typically monthly as per the distribution schedule. 

The implementing authority is the Department of Civil Supplies and Consumer Affairs of Tamil Nadu. 

Additional details may include periodic reviews and updates to the eligibility criteria based on socio-economic changes.
