The scheme SISDRDOLPT stands for the Scheme for Integrated Skill Development and Rural Development through Livelihood Promotion and Training. 

It aims to enhance the employability of rural youth by providing skill development training. 

The objective is to promote self-employment and wage employment opportunities in rural areas. 

Benefits include skill training in various sectors and financial assistance for starting small enterprises. 

Eligibility criteria typically include age limits and residency requirements for rural youth. 

The application process generally involves submitting an application form to designated local authorities or training centers. 

The timeline for training programs may vary based on the specific skill set being offered. 

Implementing authorities include the Ministry of Rural Development and various state governments. 

Additional details may include partnerships with NGOs and private sector organizations for training delivery.
