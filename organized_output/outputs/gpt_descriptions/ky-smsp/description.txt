**Context or Background:**
The Kisan Yojana - Soil Management Support Program is an initiative by the Government of India aimed at enhancing soil health and productivity.  
The program recognizes the critical role of soil health in agricultural productivity and sustainability.  
It is part of the broader efforts to promote sustainable agricultural practices across the country.  
The initiative is designed to address the challenges posed by soil degradation and nutrient depletion.

The primary objective of the KY-SMSP is to promote sustainable soil management practices among farmers.  
The program aims to improve soil fertility and productivity through scientific interventions.

The initiative also aims to increase awareness about the importance of soil conservation.  
Another objective is to enhance the overall agricultural output by improving soil conditions.  

**Benefits and Assistance:**
Farmers participating in the KY-SMSP receive assistance in the form of soil health cards.

The program offers training sessions on best practices for soil management.  
Financial assistance may be provided for purchasing soil testing kits and organic fertilizers.
