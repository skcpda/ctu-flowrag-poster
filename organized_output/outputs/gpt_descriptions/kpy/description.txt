The KPY scheme stands for the Kisan Paryatan Yojana. 

This scheme is designed to promote agricultural tourism in India. 

It aims to enhance the income of farmers by encouraging them to showcase their agricultural practices and produce to tourists. 

The initiative is part of a broader effort to boost rural economy and sustainable tourism.

The scheme also aims to educate tourists about Indian agriculture and its significance.
