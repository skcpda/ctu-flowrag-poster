The Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana - Urban (PMAY-U) is a housing scheme launched by the Government of India. 

It aims to provide affordable housing to the urban poor. 

The scheme seeks to ensure housing for all by the year 2022. 

It provides financial assistance to eligible beneficiaries for the construction or enhancement of houses. 

The benefits include a subsidy on home loans for the economically weaker sections. 

The assistance can be up to Rs 2.67 lakh depending on the category of beneficiaries. 

Eligibility is based on income levels and the applicant must be a resident of urban areas. 

The scheme targets Economically Weaker Sections, Low Income Groups, and Middle Income Groups. 

The application process involves filling out an online form on the PMAY website or through Common Service Centers. 

The timeline for the disbursement of benefits varies based on the completion of the application process. 

The Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs is the implementing authority for this scheme. 

State governments and urban local bodies also play a role in the implementation. 

The scheme includes various components such as In-situ Slum Redevelopment and Affordable Housing in Partnership. 

It promotes the use of sustainable and eco-friendly building materials. 

The scheme is part of the larger initiative to improve urban infrastructure and living conditions.
