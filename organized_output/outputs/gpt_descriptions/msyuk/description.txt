The Mukhyamantri Swavalamban Yojana (MSYUK) is a scheme launched by the Government of Rajasthan. 

It aims to promote self-employment among the youth of the state. 

The objective is to provide financial assistance to individuals starting their own businesses. 

The scheme offers a subsidy on the loan amount taken from banks for setting up a new enterprise. 

Eligible beneficiaries include youth aged between 18 to 35 years. 

They must be residents of Rajasthan and should not have any existing business. 

The application process involves submitting an online application through the official website. 

Applicants need to provide necessary documents such as identity proof and project reports. 

The scheme is implemented by the Department of Skill, Employment and Entrepreneurship, Government of Rajasthan. 

The subsidy is provided in the form of a percentage of the loan amount. 

The frequency of assistance is based on the approval of the loan and subsequent disbursement by the bank. 

The scheme also focuses on skill development and training for the beneficiaries. 

It encourages entrepreneurship in various sectors including manufacturing and services. 

The initiative aims to reduce unemployment and promote economic growth in the state.
