The scheme SSNTDNTTV stands for the "Scheme for the Support of National Technical Development for New Technologies in the Textile and Apparel Sector." 

This scheme aims to enhance the technological capabilities of the textile and apparel sector in India. 

The objective is to promote research and development in new technologies. 

It seeks to improve the quality and competitiveness of Indian textiles in the global market. 

Benefits include financial assistance for research projects and technology development. 

Eligible beneficiaries include research institutions and textile manufacturers. 

The application process involves submitting a proposal to the relevant government authority. 

The timeline for project approval and funding disbursement may vary based on the proposal evaluation. 

The Ministry of Textiles is the implementing authority for this scheme. 

Further details regarding specific funding amounts and project duration may be available through official notifications.
