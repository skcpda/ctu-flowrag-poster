The Odisha State Scholarship Portal for Post Matric Students (OSSPPMS) is an initiative by the Government of Odisha. 

It aims to provide financial assistance to students from Scheduled Castes, Scheduled Tribes, and Other Backward Classes. 

The objective is to promote higher education among marginalized communities. 

Benefits include scholarships that cover tuition fees, maintenance allowances, and other educational expenses. 

Eligibility is limited to students belonging to SC, ST, and OBC categories who are pursuing post-matriculation courses. 

Students must be residents of Odisha and enrolled in recognized institutions. 

The application process involves registering on the OSSPPMS portal and submitting required documents online. 

The timeline for application typically coincides with the academic year. 

The implementing authority is the Department of Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes Development, Government of Odisha. 

The scheme aims to reduce dropout rates and enhance educational attainment among disadvantaged groups.
