The SSWHRWCD scheme stands for the Scheme for Support to Women in Hardship and Vulnerability through the Department of Women and Child Development. 

This scheme aims to provide financial assistance and support to women facing hardship and vulnerability. 

The primary objective is to empower women by providing them with the necessary resources to improve their living conditions. 

Benefits include financial assistance for basic needs such as food shelter and healthcare. 

Eligibility criteria typically include women who are widowed divorced or abandoned and are in need of support. 

The application process involves submitting a form along with necessary documentation to the local Department of Women and Child Development office. 

The timeline for assistance may vary based on the processing of applications and availability of funds. 

The implementing authority for this scheme is the Department of Women and Child Development at both state and central levels. 

Additional details may include specific outreach programs and community support initiatives to assist beneficiaries.
