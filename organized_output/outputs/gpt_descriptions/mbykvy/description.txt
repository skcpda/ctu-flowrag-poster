The Mukhyamantri Bhatta Yojana for Kisan Vikas Yatra (MBYKVY) is a welfare scheme launched by the Government of Madhya Pradesh. 

The scheme aims to provide financial assistance to farmers for the purchase of agricultural equipment and tools. 

The objective is to enhance agricultural productivity and improve the livelihoods of farmers in the state. 

Eligible beneficiaries include small and marginal farmers who are residents of Madhya Pradesh. 

The assistance is provided in the form of a subsidy on the purchase of specified agricultural equipment. 

Farmers can apply for the scheme through the official website or designated government offices. 

The application process typically involves filling out a form and submitting necessary documents to prove eligibility. 

The scheme is implemented by the Department of Agriculture of Madhya Pradesh. 

The frequency of assistance may vary based on the availability of funds and the specific requirements of the farmers.
