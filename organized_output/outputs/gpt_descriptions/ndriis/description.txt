The National Dairy Research Institute Innovation and Incubation Scheme (NDRIIS) aims to promote innovation and entrepreneurship in the dairy sector. 

The objective is to support startups and entrepreneurs in developing dairy-related technologies and products. 

The scheme provides financial assistance for research and development activities. 

It also offers mentorship and guidance to startups in the dairy industry. 

Eligibility includes individuals or groups with innovative ideas in dairy technology. 

The application process involves submitting a project proposal to the National Dairy Research Institute. 

The timeline for approval and funding may vary based on the project specifics. 

The National Dairy Research Institute is the implementing authority for this scheme. 

Additional details about specific funding amounts and project duration may vary based on individual proposals.
