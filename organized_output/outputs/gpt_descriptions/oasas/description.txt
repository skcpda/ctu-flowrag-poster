The OASAS scheme stands for Online Agricultural Services and Advisory System. 

It was launched to provide farmers with timely and relevant information related to agriculture. 

The objective is to enhance agricultural productivity through the use of technology. 

The scheme aims to provide advisory services on various agricultural practices. 

Farmers can access information on weather forecasts pest management and market prices. 

The benefits include improved decision-making for farmers and increased crop yields. 

Eligibility is generally open to all farmers in India. 

The application process involves registering on the official portal to access services. 

The services are available throughout the year as per the agricultural calendar. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers' Welfare. 

The initiative also collaborates with state agricultural departments for localized support.
