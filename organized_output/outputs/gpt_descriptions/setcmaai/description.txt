The SETCMAAI scheme stands for the Skill Enhancement Training for Capacity Building of Marginalized and Adverse Impacted Individuals. 

It aims to enhance the skills of marginalized individuals to improve their employability and livelihood opportunities. 

The objectives include providing skill development training and promoting entrepreneurship among the target population. 

Benefits include access to skill training programs and support for self-employment initiatives. 

Eligibility criteria typically include being a marginalized individual or belonging to a disadvantaged community. 

The application process generally involves submitting an application to the designated implementing agency or authority. 

The timeline for the training programs may vary based on the specific training modules offered. 

Implementing authorities include various government departments and skill development agencies at the state and central levels. 

The scheme is part of a broader initiative to empower marginalized communities through skill development and economic support.
