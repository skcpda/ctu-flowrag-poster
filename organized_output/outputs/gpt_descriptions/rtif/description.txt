The RTIF scheme stands for the Rural Technology and Innovation Fund. 

It was launched to promote innovation and technology in rural areas. 

The objective is to enhance the quality of life in rural communities through sustainable solutions. 

The scheme aims to support research and development in rural technologies. 

It provides financial assistance to innovators and entrepreneurs working on rural development projects. 

Eligible beneficiaries include individuals and organizations involved in rural technology innovation. 

The application process involves submitting a proposal outlining the project and its impact. 

The proposals are evaluated based on their feasibility and potential benefits. 

The implementing authority for the RTIF scheme is the Ministry of Rural Development. 

The scheme operates on a project basis with timelines varying according to the specific project. 

Funding is provided based on the approved project proposals.
