The National Scheme for Incentive to Girls for Secondary Education (NSIGSE) aims to promote the education of girls at the secondary level. 

The scheme is designed to reduce the dropout rate among girls in secondary education. 

It encourages families to send their daughters to school by providing financial assistance. 

The benefits include a one-time financial incentive for girls who complete their secondary education. 

Eligible beneficiaries are girls who are enrolled in classes 9 to 12 in government or government-aided schools. 

The scheme is primarily targeted at girls from economically weaker sections. 

The application process involves the submission of required documents to the respective school authorities. 

The financial assistance is disbursed directly to the bank accounts of the beneficiaries. 

The implementing authority for this scheme is the Ministry of Education, Government of India. 

The scheme is part of the broader initiative to empower girls through education. 

It aims to create a conducive environment for girls to pursue their education without financial constraints.
