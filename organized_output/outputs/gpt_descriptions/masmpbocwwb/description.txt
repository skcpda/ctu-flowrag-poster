**Mahatma Gandhi National Rural Employment Guarantee Act (MGNREGA)**

Context or background.  
The Mahatma Gandhi National Rural Employment Guarantee Act was enacted in 2005.  
It aims to enhance livelihood security in rural areas of India.  
The act provides a legal guarantee for at least 100 days of unskilled wage employment in a financial year.  
It was introduced to address rural poverty and provide social security.  
The scheme is a significant step towards empowering rural households.  
It promotes the right to work and ensures a minimum wage.  

Objectives.  
The primary objective is to provide at least 100 days of wage employment in a financial year to every rural household.  
It aims to enhance livelihood security in rural areas.  
The scheme seeks to create durable assets in rural areas.  
It promotes social inclusion and gender equality.  
The act aims to strengthen the rural economy through wage employment.  

Benefits and assistance.  
Eligible households can avail of 100 days of unskilled wage employment.  
The wage rate is determined by the state government and is revised periodically.  
The scheme provides a safety net for rural households during lean seasons.  
It promotes the creation of community assets such as roads and irrigation facilities.  
The scheme encourages participation of marginalized groups including women and Scheduled Castes.  

Eligibility.  
All rural households are eligible to apply for the scheme.  
The scheme is open to all adult members of the household.  
There is no restriction based on income or land ownership.  
Priority is given to households with no adult member employed in government jobs.  

Application process.  
Interested households must apply for a job card at the local Gram Panchayat.  
The job card is issued after verification of the application.  
Once issued, the job card allows households to demand work.  
Households can submit a written request for work to the Gram Panchayat.  
The Gram Panchayat is required to provide work within 15 days of the request.  

Timeline or frequency.  
The scheme operates throughout the year.  
Employment is provided based on the demand from households.  
Wages are paid within 15 days of the completion of work.  
The financial year is the basis for the 100 days of employment guarantee.  

Implementing authorities or agencies.  
The Ministry of Rural Development is the nodal ministry for the implementation of MGNREGA.  
State governments are responsible for the execution of the scheme at the state level.  
Gram Panchayats play a crucial role in the implementation and monitoring of the scheme.  
Local bodies are responsible for planning and execution of works.
