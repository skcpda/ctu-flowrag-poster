The scheme SKHVC stands for the Skill Development Scheme for Handicraft and Handloom Sector. 

It aims to enhance the skills of artisans and weavers in the handicraft and handloom sectors. 

The objective is to promote sustainable livelihoods through skill development and entrepreneurship. 

The benefits include training in various crafts and access to modern techniques and tools. 

Eligible beneficiaries are artisans and weavers from the handicraft and handloom sectors. 

The application process typically involves submitting an application form to the designated authority. 

The timeline for training programs may vary based on the specific courses offered. 

Implementing authorities include the Ministry of Textiles and various state governments. 

The scheme also focuses on preserving traditional crafts while promoting innovation.
