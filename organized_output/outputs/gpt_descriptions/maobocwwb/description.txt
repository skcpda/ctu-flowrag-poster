The MAOBOCWWB stands for the Mahatma Gandhi Bunkar Bima Yojana. 

This scheme is designed to provide social security and welfare benefits to handloom weavers in India. 

It aims to improve the living conditions of handloom weavers who are often economically vulnerable. 

The primary objective of the scheme is to provide financial assistance to the families of weavers in case of accidental death or disability. 

The scheme also seeks to promote the welfare of handloom weavers by providing them with insurance coverage. 

Under this scheme, beneficiaries receive a life insurance cover of a specified amount. 

In addition to life insurance, the scheme also offers benefits in case of permanent disability. 

The financial assistance provided under this scheme helps in securing the future of the weaver's family. 

The scheme is particularly beneficial for those weavers who do not have any other form of social security. 

Eligibility for the MAOBOCWWB is primarily based on the status of the applicant as a handloom weaver. 

Applicants must be registered weavers under the Handloom Weavers' Cooperative Societies or other recognized bodies. 

The age limit for beneficiaries is typically between 18 to 59 years. 

The application process involves filling out a prescribed form available at designated offices. 

Weavers can apply through their respective cooperative societies or directly at the offices of the implementing agencies. 

Documentation required includes proof of identity and proof of being a registered weaver. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Textiles in collaboration with various state governments. 

The implementing agencies include the Handloom Development Corporations and other related organizations. 

The scheme is expected to provide timely assistance to beneficiaries in need. 

The frequency of benefits is contingent upon the occurrence of the insured event.
