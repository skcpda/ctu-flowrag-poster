**Scheme Name: Mahatma Gandhi National Rural Employment Guarantee Act (MGNREGA) - PBAO-CWWB**

**Context or Background:**
The Mahatma Gandhi National Rural Employment Guarantee Act was enacted in 2005.  
It aims to provide a legal guarantee for at least 100 days of unskilled wage employment in a financial year.  
The scheme is designed to enhance livelihood security in rural areas.  
It seeks to create durable assets and strengthen the livelihood resource base of rural households.  
The PBAO-CWWB (Pension for the Beneficiaries of the Aged, Orphans, and Widows under the Central Government) is a component under MGNREGA.  

**Objectives:**
The primary objective of the PBAO-CWWB is to provide financial assistance to vulnerable sections of society.  
It specifically targets the aged, orphans, and widows in rural areas.  
The scheme aims to reduce poverty and improve the quality of life for beneficiaries.  
It seeks to ensure social security for marginalized groups.  

**Benefits and Assistance:**
Beneficiaries receive a monthly pension under this scheme.  
The pension amount is fixed and varies based on the category of the beneficiary.  
The scheme provides financial support to help meet basic needs.  
It aims to empower beneficiaries by providing them with a source of income.  
The assistance helps in reducing dependency on others for financial support.  

**Eligibility:**
Eligibility criteria for the scheme include being a resident of rural areas.  
Applicants must belong to the categories of aged individuals, orphans, or widows.  
There may be an age limit for aged beneficiaries which is typically set at 60 years or above.  
Orphans must be below a certain age limit to qualify for assistance.  
Widows must provide necessary documentation to prove their status.  
Income criteria may also apply to ensure that assistance reaches the most needy.  

**Application Process:**
Interested applicants must approach the local Gram Panchayat.  
They need to fill out the application form provided by the Panchayat.  
Necessary documents must be submitted along with the application form.  
Documents typically include proof of age, identity, and status as an orphan or widow.  
The Gram Panchayat will verify the application and documents.  
Once verified, the application will be forwarded to the relevant authorities for approval.  
Beneficiaries will be informed about the status of their application.  

**Timeline or Frequency:**
The application process can take several weeks depending on the verification process.  
Once approved, beneficiaries will start receiving their pension monthly.  
The pension is disbursed on a fixed date each month.  
There may be periodic reviews of eligibility to ensure continued assistance.  

**Implementing Authorities or Agencies:**
The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Rural Development.  
State governments play a crucial role in executing the scheme at the local level.  
Gram Panchayats are responsible for the initial processing of applications.  
District Rural Development Agencies oversee the implementation in their respective areas.  
Local authorities are tasked with monitoring and evaluation of the scheme.
