The Mukhyamantri Matru Shishu Suraksha Yojana (MMKSSY) is a welfare scheme launched by the Government of Assam. 

It aims to provide financial assistance to pregnant women and lactating mothers. 

The objective is to reduce maternal and infant mortality rates. 

The scheme provides a one-time cash incentive to eligible beneficiaries. 

The amount is intended to support women during pregnancy and after childbirth. 

Eligibility includes pregnant women and lactating mothers residing in Assam. 

Beneficiaries must be registered under the scheme to receive assistance. 

The application process involves filling out a form and submitting it to the designated health department office. 

The scheme is implemented by the Department of Health and Family Welfare, Assam. 

The frequency of assistance is a one-time payment during the specified period of pregnancy or post-delivery. 

Additional details about the scheme can be found on official government websites.
