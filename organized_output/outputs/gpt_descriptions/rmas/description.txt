The Rashtriya Madhyamik Shiksha Abhiyan (RMSA) is a government initiative aimed at enhancing secondary education in India. 

It was launched in 2009 to provide universal access to secondary education. 

The primary objective is to improve the quality of education and increase enrollment rates in secondary schools. 

The scheme aims to reduce the dropout rate and ensure that students complete their secondary education. 

Benefits include financial assistance for infrastructure development in schools. 

It also provides training for teachers and support for innovative teaching methods. 

Eligibility criteria include schools that are recognized by the state education department. 

The application process involves submitting proposals for funding to the respective state government. 

The timeline for implementation varies by state and depends on the specific projects approved under the scheme. 

The Ministry of Education is the central authority responsible for the implementation of RMSA. 

State governments also play a crucial role in executing the scheme at the local level. 

The scheme has provisions for monitoring and evaluation to assess its impact on secondary education.
