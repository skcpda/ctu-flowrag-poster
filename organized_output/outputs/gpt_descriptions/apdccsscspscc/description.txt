The Apna Dal (Sonelal) Central Cooperative Society Scheme for Primary Cooperative Societies is a welfare initiative by the Government of India.

This scheme aims to enhance the socio-economic conditions of the rural population.

It focuses on empowering primary cooperative societies in various sectors.

The background of this scheme is rooted in the need for cooperative development in India.

Cooperatives play a crucial role in the agricultural economy of the country.

They provide a platform for farmers to come together and access resources.

The scheme seeks to strengthen these cooperatives to improve productivity.

The objectives of the Apna Dal Central Cooperative Society Scheme include promoting cooperative societies.

It aims to enhance the capacity of these societies to serve their members.

The scheme also focuses on increasing the income of farmers through better cooperative practices.

Another objective is to ensure the availability of essential services to the rural population.

This includes access to credit, marketing, and other agricultural inputs.

The benefits of the scheme are manifold.

It provides financial assistance to primary cooperative societies.

This financial support helps in the establishment and functioning of these societies.

The scheme also facilitates training and capacity-building programs for members.

These programs aim to enhance skills and knowledge in cooperative management.

Additionally, the scheme promotes the use of technology in cooperative operations.

This helps in improving efficiency and productivity.

The assistance under this scheme is aimed at various sectors including agriculture and dairy.

It also extends to fisheries and other rural enterprises.

Eligibility criteria for the scheme are defined clearly.

Primary cooperative societies registered under the relevant state laws are eligible.

These societies must demonstrate a commitment to serving their members.

They should also have a viable business plan to qualify for assistance.

The application process for the scheme is straightforward.

Interested cooperative societies need to submit an application to the concerned authority.

This application should include details about the society's objectives and plans.

The application must also provide financial projections and expected outcomes.

Once submitted, the application is reviewed by the implementing authority.

The timeline for processing applications can vary.

Typically, it may take a few weeks to a couple of months for approval.

The frequency of assistance under the scheme is not explicitly defined.

However, societies can apply for funding as per their needs.

Implementing authorities for this scheme include the Ministry of Cooperation.

State cooperative departments also play a significant role in implementation.

They are responsible for monitoring and evaluating the progress of the scheme.

Other relevant agencies may also be involved in providing support.

The scheme aligns with the broader goals of rural development in India.

It complements other government initiatives aimed at enhancing agricultural productivity.
