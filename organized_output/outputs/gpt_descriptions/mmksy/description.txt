The Mukhyamantri Mahila Kalyan Yojana (MMKSY) is a welfare scheme initiated by the Government of Madhya Pradesh. 

The scheme aims to empower women and enhance their socio-economic status. 

It provides financial assistance to women for various purposes including education and skill development. 

The benefits include a one-time financial aid to women for starting small businesses or engaging in self-employment activities. 

Eligibility is primarily focused on women residents of Madhya Pradesh. 

Women from economically weaker sections are given preference. 

The application process involves submitting an online application through the official government portal. 

Applicants may need to provide identification and proof of residence. 

The scheme is implemented by the Department of Women and Child Development, Madhya Pradesh. 

The frequency of assistance may vary based on the specific needs outlined in the application. 

Additional details regarding the scheme can be found on the official government website.
