Sardar Patel Awas Yojana is a housing scheme initiated by the Government of India. 

It aims to provide affordable housing to the urban poor. 

The objective is to ensure that every individual has access to a decent living space. 

The scheme offers financial assistance for the construction of houses. 

It also provides subsidies on home loans. 

Eligibility is generally based on income criteria and residency status. 

Beneficiaries typically include economically weaker sections and low-income groups. 

The application process involves submitting a form to the local municipal authority. 

Documentation such as income proof and identity verification is required. 

The timeline for approval may vary based on the local authority's processing speed. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs. 

State governments may also play a role in execution at the local level. 

Additional details regarding specific benefits may vary by state.
