The USKA scheme is an initiative aimed at providing financial assistance to small and marginal farmers in India. 

The objective of the scheme is to enhance the income of farmers and improve their livelihood. 

The benefits include direct cash transfers to eligible farmers to support their agricultural activities. 

Eligibility is generally limited to small and marginal farmers as defined by the government. 

The application process typically involves registration through designated government portals or local agricultural offices. 

The timeline for assistance may vary depending on the specific provisions of the scheme. 

Implementing authorities include the Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers' Welfare and relevant state government departments. 

Additional details may include provisions for training and capacity building for farmers to improve agricultural practices.
