The MFGBCDC stands for the Mahatma Gandhi Bunkar Bima Yojana. 

It is a welfare scheme aimed at providing financial assistance to handloom weavers in India. 

The objective of the scheme is to offer social security to weavers in the event of natural calamities or unforeseen circumstances. 

The benefits include financial assistance for the family of the weaver in case of death or disability. 

The assistance amount is provided as a lump sum to the beneficiaries. 

Eligibility criteria include being a handloom weaver and being registered under the Handloom Weavers' Cooperative Societies. 

The application process involves submitting an application form along with necessary documents to the respective implementing authority. 

The timeline for disbursement of benefits is typically defined by the implementing agency upon approval of the application. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Textiles, Government of India. 

Additional details regarding specific benefits and assistance amounts may vary based on the latest government notifications.
