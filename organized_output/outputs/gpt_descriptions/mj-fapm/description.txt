The Mukhya Mantri Fasal Aarthik Madad Yojana (MJ-FAPM) is a welfare scheme launched by the Government of Madhya Pradesh. 

It aims to provide financial assistance to farmers affected by natural calamities. 

The objective is to mitigate the economic losses faced by farmers due to crop failure. 

Under this scheme, eligible farmers receive monetary support to help them recover from losses. 

The benefits include direct cash transfers to the bank accounts of the farmers. 

Eligibility criteria include being a resident of Madhya Pradesh and being a registered farmer. 

Farmers must have suffered losses due to specific natural disasters as defined by the government. 

The application process typically involves submitting a claim to the local revenue office. 

Farmers may need to provide documentation such as land records and proof of crop loss. 

The timeline for disbursement of funds varies based on the assessment of claims. 

The scheme is implemented by the Department of Farmers Welfare and Agriculture Development in Madhya Pradesh. 

Additional details may include periodic reviews and updates to the scheme based on changing agricultural conditions.
