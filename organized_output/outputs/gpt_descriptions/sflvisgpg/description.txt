The SFLVISGPG scheme is the Scheme for Financial Literacy and Financial Inclusion through Self-Help Groups and Producer Groups. 

It aims to enhance financial literacy and promote financial inclusion among marginalized communities. 

The objectives include empowering individuals through financial education and facilitating access to financial services. 

Benefits include increased awareness of financial products and services. 

Participants may also gain access to credit and savings opportunities. 

Eligibility criteria typically include being a member of a Self-Help Group or Producer Group. 

The application process generally involves registration with the respective Self-Help Group or Producer Group. 

The timeline for implementation may vary based on local conditions and group activities. 

The Ministry of Finance and the National Rural Livelihoods Mission are the primary implementing authorities. 

Additional details may include collaboration with local banks and financial institutions for effective outreach.
