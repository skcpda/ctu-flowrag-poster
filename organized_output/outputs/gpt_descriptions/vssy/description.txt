The VSSY scheme stands for the Vishwa Sewa Sansthan Yojana. 

It is aimed at providing financial assistance to individuals and organizations engaged in social service activities. 

The objective of the scheme is to promote and support voluntary organizations that work for the welfare of society. 

Benefits include financial grants to eligible organizations for various social welfare projects. 

Eligibility criteria typically include registration as a non-profit organization and a demonstrated commitment to social service. 

The application process involves submitting a proposal outlining the intended project and its impact. 

The timeline for approval and disbursement of funds may vary based on the project and the reviewing authority. 

Implementing authorities include the Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment and other relevant state departments. 

The scheme encourages community participation and aims to enhance the capacity of voluntary organizations.
