The SAMSMEASC scheme stands for the Scheme for Assistance to Micro and Small Enterprises in the Agricultural Sector. 

It aims to promote the growth and development of micro and small enterprises in the agricultural sector. 

The objective is to enhance the competitiveness of these enterprises by providing financial assistance and support. 

Benefits include access to credit for investment in technology and infrastructure. 

Assistance may also include training and capacity building for entrepreneurs. 

Eligibility is generally limited to micro and small enterprises operating in the agricultural sector. 

The application process typically involves submitting a proposal to the relevant government authority.
