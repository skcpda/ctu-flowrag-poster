The "Bharat Interface for Money" or "Bhim" is a digital payment application developed by the National Payments Corporation of India. 

It was launched on December 30 2016. 

The application is designed to facilitate easy and secure digital transactions through the Unified Payments Interface or UPI. 

The context of this initiative stems from the Indian government's push towards a cashless economy. 

This push was further emphasized following the demonetization of high denomination currency notes in November 2016. 

The objective of the Bhim application is to promote digital payments among the masses. 

It aims to provide a simple and efficient platform for users to make transactions using their smartphones. 

The application allows users to send and receive money instantly. 

Users can link their bank accounts to the app for seamless transactions. 

Bhim supports various payment methods including QR code scanning and mobile number-based payments. 

One of the significant benefits of using Bhim is its user-friendly interface. 

The application is available in multiple languages to cater to a diverse population. 

Bhim also promotes financial inclusion by enabling users from rural areas to access digital payment systems. 

The application is designed to be secure with features such as two-factor authentication. 

Users can also set a UPI PIN for added security during transactions. 

The app supports transactions of up to Rs. 1 lakh in a single transaction. 

Users can make payments to merchants as well as to other individuals. 

The application also allows for bill payments and recharges. 

In addition to individual users, merchants can also benefit from the Bhim application. 

Merchants can accept payments through QR codes generated by the app. 

This feature simplifies the payment process for small businesses and vendors. 

The eligibility criteria for using the Bhim application are straightforward. 

Any individual with a bank account in India can download and use the app. 

Users must have a mobile number linked to their bank account for registration. 

The application is available for both Android and iOS platforms. 

To apply for using the Bhim application, users must first download it from the Google Play Store or Apple App Store. 

After downloading the app, users need to register using their mobile number. 

Once registered, users must link their bank account by selecting their bank from the list provided. 

The app will prompt users to set a UPI PIN for secure transactions. 

After completing these steps, users can start making transactions. 

The Bhim application is available for use 24/7. 

Transactions are processed in real-time. 

Users can check their transaction history within the app. 

The application is designed to handle a high volume of transactions efficiently. 

The implementing authority for the Bhim application is the National Payments Corporation of India. 

The NPCI is responsible for the development and maintenance of the UPI infrastructure.

The government has been actively promoting the use of digital payments through various campaigns. 

These campaigns aim to educate the public about the benefits of digital transactions. 

Bhim has also been integrated with various government welfare schemes for disbursement of benefits. 

This integration allows for direct transfer of subsidies and benefits to beneficiaries.
