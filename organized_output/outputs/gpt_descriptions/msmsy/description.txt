The Mukhyamantri Swavalamban Yojana (MSMSY) is a welfare scheme launched by the Government of Rajasthan. 

The scheme aims to promote self-employment among the youth and women in the state. 

It seeks to provide financial assistance and support for setting up small businesses. 

The benefits include subsidies on loans for starting new enterprises. 

Eligible beneficiaries include unemployed youth and women who are residents of Rajasthan. 

The age limit for applicants is typically between 18 to 45 years. 

The application process involves submitting a form along with necessary documents to the designated authorities. 

Applications can be submitted online through the official government portal or at designated offices. 

The scheme is implemented by the Department of Skill, Employment and Entrepreneurship, Government of Rajasthan. 

Financial assistance is provided in the form of a subsidy on bank loans. 

The subsidy percentage may vary based on the category of the applicant and the type of business. 

The scheme is reviewed periodically to ensure its effectiveness and reach. 

There may be specific timelines for application submission based on the fiscal year. 

The scheme also includes training and skill development programs for beneficiaries. 

Additional details regarding specific eligibility criteria and benefits can be found on the official website.
