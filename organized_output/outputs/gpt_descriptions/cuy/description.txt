The Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samman Nidhi Yojana also known as PM-KISAN is a government welfare scheme initiated by the Government of India. 

This scheme was launched on February 24 2019 during the interim budget presentation. 

The primary objective of PM-KISAN is to provide financial support to small and marginal farmers. 

The scheme aims to enhance the income of farmers and ensure food security in the country. 

Under this scheme eligible farmers receive direct cash transfers. 

The amount provided is Rs 6000 per year. 

This amount is disbursed in three equal installments of Rs 2000 each. 

The financial assistance is intended to help farmers meet their agricultural expenses. 

It also aims to provide them with a safety net during times of financial distress. 

The scheme is applicable to all small and marginal farmers across the country. 

To be eligible for the scheme farmers must own cultivable land. 

The land ownership must be verified through land records. 

Farmers who own more than two hectares of land are not eligible. 

The scheme is specifically designed for those who are economically vulnerable. 

The application process is straightforward and farmer-friendly. 

Farmers can apply through the official PM-KISAN portal. 

They can also visit the nearest Common Service Centers or Krishi Vigyan Kendras. 

To apply farmers need to provide their Aadhaar number and bank account details. 

The application form requires basic personal information and land ownership details. 

Once the application is submitted it undergoes verification. 

The verification process is conducted by the respective state governments. 

After successful verification the funds are transferred directly to the farmer's bank account. 

The timeline for fund disbursement is typically within a few weeks of application approval. 

The frequency of payments is quarterly with three installments each year. 

The implementing authority for this scheme is the Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers' Welfare. 

State governments play a crucial role in the implementation and monitoring of the scheme. 

They are responsible for the identification of eligible beneficiaries. 

The scheme has been a significant step towards improving the livelihood of farmers. 

It has also contributed to reducing rural poverty in India. 

The financial assistance helps farmers invest in better seeds fertilizers and equipment. 

It also enables them to manage unforeseen expenses such as medical emergencies.
