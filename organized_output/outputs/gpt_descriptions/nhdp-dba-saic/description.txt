The National Highway Development Project - Deen Dayal Upadhyaya Grameen Kaushalya Yojana (NHDP-DBA-SAIC) is a government initiative aimed at enhancing rural livelihoods. 

The objective of this scheme is to provide skill development and employment opportunities to rural youth. 

The benefits include training in various skills that align with market demands. 

Eligible beneficiaries are rural youth aged between 15 to 35 years. 

The application process involves registration through designated skill development centers. 

The timeline for training programs varies based on the specific skill being taught. 

The implementing authorities include the Ministry of Rural Development and various state governments. 

This scheme is part of a broader effort to improve rural infrastructure and economic conditions.
