The SBYGBOCWWB scheme stands for the Swami Vivekananda Yuva Sashaktikaran Yojana for the Building and Other Construction Workers Board. 

This scheme is aimed at providing financial assistance and support to the children of registered building and other construction workers. 

The objective is to enhance the educational and skill development opportunities for the children of these workers. 

Benefits include financial aid for education and vocational training. 

The assistance is provided to children who are studying in recognized institutions. 

Eligibility is limited to the children of registered building and other construction workers under the Building and Other Construction Workers Act. 

The application process involves filling out a prescribed form and submitting it to the relevant authorities along with necessary documents. 

The timeline for the disbursement of benefits may vary based on the processing of applications. 

The scheme is implemented by the respective state welfare boards for building and other construction workers. 

Additional details regarding specific benefits and application forms can be found on the official government website.
