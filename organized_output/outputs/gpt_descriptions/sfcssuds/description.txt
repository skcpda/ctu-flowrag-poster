The scheme is known as the Scheme for Financial Assistance to Scheduled Castes for Self Employment and Development of Skills (SFCS-SUDS). 

It aims to provide financial assistance for self-employment and skill development for Scheduled Castes. 

The objectives include promoting self-reliance among Scheduled Castes through entrepreneurship. 

The scheme offers financial assistance for starting small businesses and vocational training programs. 

Eligible beneficiaries are individuals belonging to Scheduled Castes. 

They must be between the ages of 18 and 55 years. 

The application process involves submitting a proposal along with required documents to the concerned department. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment. 

Assistance is provided in the form of grants and loans. 

The frequency of assistance is determined based on the project proposal and its approval. 

The scheme also aims to enhance the skill sets of Scheduled Castes to improve employability. 

Additional details regarding specific financial limits and project types may vary.
