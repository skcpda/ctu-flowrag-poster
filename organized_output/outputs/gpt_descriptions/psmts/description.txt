The Pradhan Mantri Suraksha Maandhan Yojana (PMSMTS) is a government welfare scheme aimed at providing social security to workers in the unorganized sector. 

The objective of the scheme is to provide a monthly pension to workers after they reach the age of 60 years. 

The benefits include a pension of Rs 3,000 per month to eligible beneficiaries. 

Eligibility criteria include being a worker in the unorganized sector and being between the ages of 18 and 40 years at the time of enrollment. 

The application process involves registering online or through designated centers. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Labour and Employment. 

The pension is disbursed monthly after the beneficiary reaches the age of 60 years. 

The scheme aims to enhance the financial security of unorganized sector workers in their old age.
