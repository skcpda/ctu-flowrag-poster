The NAG scheme stands for the National Agricultural Market. 

It was launched to create a unified national market for agricultural commodities. 

The objective is to enhance farmers' income by providing them with better price discovery. 

The scheme aims to eliminate the barriers of state boundaries in agricultural marketing. 

Farmers can sell their produce directly to buyers through an online platform. 

The benefits include access to a larger market and better price realization for farmers. 

Farmers can also receive information on prices and demand for various crops. 

Eligibility is generally limited to farmers who are registered in the system. 

The application process involves registration on the e-NAM portal. 

Farmers need to provide necessary documents such as identity proof and land ownership details. 

The scheme operates throughout the year as it is an online platform. 

The implementing authority is the Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers' Welfare. 

State Agricultural Marketing Boards are also involved in the implementation. 

The scheme promotes transparency in pricing and reduces the role of intermediaries. 

It encourages the use of technology in agriculture marketing. 

The NAG scheme is part of the larger effort to modernize the agricultural sector in India.
