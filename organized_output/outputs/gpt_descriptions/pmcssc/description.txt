The Pradhan Mantri Chaturvedi Sewa Sansthan Scheme (PMCSSC) aims to provide financial assistance to the families of deceased government employees. 

The objective is to support the families during their time of distress and ensure their welfare. 

The scheme offers a one-time financial assistance of ₹10 lakh to the eligible beneficiaries. 

Eligibility is restricted to the families of government employees who have died in harness. 

The application process involves submitting a request through the designated government department. 

The timeline for the disbursement of funds is typically within a few weeks after the application is approved. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Personnel Public Grievances and Pensions. 

Additional details regarding the specific documentation required for the application may vary by state or department.
