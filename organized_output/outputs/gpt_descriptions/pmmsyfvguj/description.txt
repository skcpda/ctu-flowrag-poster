The Pradhan Mantri Matsya Sampada Yojana (PMMSY) is a government initiative aimed at enhancing fish production and promoting sustainable fisheries in India. 

The scheme was launched to increase fish production and productivity in a sustainable manner. 

It aims to create an integrated fisheries management system. 

The benefits include financial assistance for fish farmers and fishermen. 

It also provides support for infrastructure development in the fisheries sector. 

Eligible beneficiaries include fish farmers and fishermen across the country. 

The application process involves submitting a proposal to the respective state fisheries department. 

The timeline for fund disbursement depends on the approval of the proposal. 

The Ministry of Fisheries Animal Husbandry and Dairying is the implementing authority for this scheme. 

The scheme also focuses on enhancing the livelihoods of fishers and fish farmers. 

It promotes the use of modern technology in fish farming practices. 

The scheme encourages the establishment of fish feed mills and hatcheries. 

It aims to increase the availability of quality fish seed and feed. 

The PMMSY also emphasizes the importance of sustainable fishing practices. 

It seeks to improve the socio-economic conditions of fishing communities. 

The scheme is part of the broader efforts to boost the blue economy in India.
