The Vaioo CFSW scheme is designed to provide financial assistance to the families of deceased workers in the unorganised sector. 

The objective of the scheme is to support the families of workers who have lost their lives while engaged in work. 

The benefits include a one-time financial assistance amount to the eligible beneficiaries. 

Eligibility criteria include being a family member of a deceased worker in the unorganised sector. 

The application process involves submitting the required documents to the designated authority. 

The timeline for disbursement of benefits is typically within a few weeks after the application is approved. 

The implementing authority for this scheme is the Ministry of Labour and Employment.
