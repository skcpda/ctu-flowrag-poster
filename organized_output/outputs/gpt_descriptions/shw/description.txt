The SHW scheme stands for the Scheme for Housing for All. 

It was launched by the Government of India to provide affordable housing to the urban poor. 

The objective of the scheme is to ensure that every citizen has access to a pucca house with basic amenities. 

The benefits include financial assistance for housing construction or purchase. 

Eligible beneficiaries include economically weaker sections and low-income groups. 

The application process involves submitting an application through designated online platforms or local authorities. 

The timeline for approval and disbursement of funds may vary based on the specific state or municipality. 

The Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs is the primary implementing authority for this scheme. 

State governments and urban local bodies also play a crucial role in the implementation. 

The scheme aims to achieve the goal of housing for all by the year 2022.
