The Mukhyamantri Nari Shakti Yojana (MNMSUSY) is a welfare scheme initiated by the Government of Madhya Pradesh. 

The scheme aims to empower women and promote gender equality in the state. 

It focuses on providing financial assistance and support to women for various self-employment and entrepreneurial activities. 

The benefits include skill development training and access to financial aid for starting small businesses. 

Women from economically weaker sections are eligible to apply for this scheme. 

The application process involves submitting a form along with necessary documents to the designated authorities. 

The scheme is implemented by the Department of Women and Child Development in Madhya Pradesh. 

Further details regarding the timeline for application processing or frequency of assistance are not specified.
