The West Bengal Praman Patra for Women Beneficiaries (WPWB) is a welfare scheme initiated by the Government of West Bengal. 

The scheme aims to empower women by providing them with necessary certificates to avail various government benefits and services. 

It facilitates the process of obtaining essential documents for women, thereby enhancing their access to welfare schemes. 

The benefits include the issuance of Praman Patra which serves as a proof of identity and eligibility for various government programs. 

Women who are residents of West Bengal are eligible to apply for this scheme. 

The application process involves submitting a form along with required documents to the local administrative office or online through the official portal. 

The timeline for processing applications may vary but typically takes a few weeks. 

The implementing authority for this scheme is the Department of Women and Child Development and Social Welfare, Government of West Bengal. 

The scheme is part of the broader initiative to promote women's rights and welfare in the state.
