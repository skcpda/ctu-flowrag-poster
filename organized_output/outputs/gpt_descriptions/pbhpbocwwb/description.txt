The Pradhan Mantri Bhartiya Janaushadhi Pariyojana (PMBJP) aims to provide quality medicines at affordable prices. 

It seeks to reduce out-of-pocket expenses for healthcare. 

The scheme promotes the use of generic medicines. 

Benefits include access to over 1,500 medicines and 240 surgical items. 

The scheme also provides a sustainable business model for entrepreneurs. 

Eligibility includes any individual or organization wishing to open a Janaushadhi Kendra. 

The application process involves submitting an online application through the official website. 

The timeline for approval varies based on the completeness of the application. 

The implementing authority is the Bureau of Pharma Public Sector Undertakings of India (BPPI). 

The scheme is operational across all states and Union Territories in India. 

It aims to enhance the availability of quality medicines in rural and remote areas. 

The initiative also focuses on creating awareness about the benefits of generic medicines.
