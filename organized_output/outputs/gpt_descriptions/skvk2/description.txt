The scheme SKVK2 stands for the "Sukanya Samriddhi Yojana - Kendra 2." 

It is a savings scheme launched by the Government of India. 

The scheme aims to promote the welfare of the girl child. 

It encourages parents to save for the education and marriage of their daughters. 

The benefits include a higher interest rate compared to regular savings accounts. 

The scheme also offers tax benefits under Section 80C of the Income Tax Act. 

The eligibility criteria include that the account can be opened for a girl child up to the age of 10 years. 

Only one account can be opened per girl child. 

A maximum of two accounts can be opened for two daughters. 

The application process involves visiting a designated bank or post office. 

Parents must provide necessary documents such as the birth certificate of the girl child. 

The account can be opened with a minimum deposit amount. 

Deposits can be made for a maximum of 15 years. 

The maturity period of the account is 21 years from the date of opening. 

The interest is compounded annually. 

The implementing authority for this scheme is the Ministry of Finance. 

The scheme is operational across various banks and post offices in India. 

Further details can be accessed through official government websites.
