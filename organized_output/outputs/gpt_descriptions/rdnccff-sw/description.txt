The RDNCCFF scheme stands for the Rural Development National Cooperative Credit Fund for Farmers. 

It was launched to provide financial assistance to farmers through cooperative societies. 

The primary objective is to enhance the credit flow to the agricultural sector. 

The scheme aims to support farmers in accessing timely credit for agricultural activities. 

Benefits include providing loans at lower interest rates to farmers. 

This assistance helps in improving agricultural productivity and income levels. 

Eligibility is generally limited to farmers who are members of registered cooperative societies. 

The application process involves submitting a loan application through the cooperative society. 

The timeline for loan disbursement may vary based on the cooperative's internal processes. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers' Welfare. 

It collaborates with various state governments and cooperative banks.
