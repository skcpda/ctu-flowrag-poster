The Old Age Pension Scheme for West Bengal (OAPWB) aims to provide financial assistance to elderly individuals in the state. 

The objective of the scheme is to ensure social security for senior citizens who are in need of financial support. 

Under this scheme, eligible beneficiaries receive a monthly pension to help meet their basic needs. 

The benefits include a fixed pension amount which varies based on the age and category of the beneficiary. 

Eligibility criteria include being a resident of West Bengal and being aged 60 years or above. 

The applicant must also belong to a below-poverty-line category or be economically disadvantaged. 

The application process involves submitting a form along with required documents to the local Panchayat or Municipal office. 

The timeline for processing applications may vary but is generally completed within a few weeks. 

The scheme is implemented by the Department of Panchayati Raj and Rural Development in West Bengal. 

This initiative is part of the state's efforts to enhance the welfare of its elderly population.
