The scheme is known as the Skill Development for the Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes (SSTLSBC). 

It aims to enhance the employability of Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes through skill development. 

The primary objective is to provide vocational training and skill development programs. 

The benefits include improved job opportunities and self-employment for beneficiaries. 

Eligible candidates are individuals belonging to Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes. 

The application process typically involves submitting an application form to the designated authority. 

The timeline for the implementation of training programs varies based on the specific training courses. 

The Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment is the implementing authority for this scheme.
