The Scheme for the Welfare of Children in Vulnerable Situations (SOCFWV) aims to provide support to children who are in difficult circumstances. 

The primary objective is to ensure the survival protection and development of children in vulnerable situations. 

The scheme provides financial assistance for various services including education health care and nutrition. 

It also aims to enhance the capacity of child care institutions and promote community-based care options. 

Eligibility criteria include children who are orphaned abandoned or affected by natural disasters and other vulnerable situations. 

The application process involves submission of relevant documents to the concerned authorities at the district level. 

The timeline for assistance is determined based on the specific needs of the children and the availability of resources. 

The implementing authority for this scheme is the Ministry of Women and Child Development. 

Additional details include the focus on rehabilitation and reintegration of children into society.
