The Scheme for the Liberation and Rehabilitation of Scavengers and their Dependents (SLAPWD) aims to eliminate manual scavenging in India. 

It seeks to provide alternative livelihoods and rehabilitation for those engaged in this profession. 

The objectives include ensuring dignity and social security for scavengers and their families. 

The scheme offers financial assistance for skill development and vocational training. 

It also provides support for self-employment and entrepreneurship opportunities. 

Eligibility is primarily for individuals engaged in manual scavenging and their dependents. 

The application process involves submitting a request to the designated authorities in the respective states. 

The timeline for assistance may vary based on the specific program and state implementation. 

The Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment is the primary implementing authority for this scheme. 

State governments also play a crucial role in the execution and monitoring of the scheme.
