The Mukhyamantri Kanya Suraksha Yojana (MKSYBS) is a welfare scheme initiated by the Government of Madhya Pradesh. 

The scheme aims to ensure the safety and security of girls in the state. 

It focuses on promoting the education and health of girls. 

The scheme provides financial assistance for the education of girls from birth until they reach the age of 21 years. 

The benefits include a fixed deposit amount that is released at different stages of the girl's life. 

The assistance is provided in the form of a lump sum amount upon reaching certain milestones such as enrollment in school and completion of higher education. 

Eligibility criteria include being a resident of Madhya Pradesh and being born after the implementation of the scheme. 

The application process involves filling out a form available at designated government offices or online. 

The timeline for the release of funds is linked to the educational milestones achieved by the girl. 

The scheme is implemented by the Women and Child Development Department of Madhya Pradesh. 

Additional details include awareness programs to educate families about the importance of girl child education and safety.
