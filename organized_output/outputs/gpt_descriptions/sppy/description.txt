The SPPY scheme stands for the Samagra Punarvasan Yojana. 

It was launched by the Government of India to provide support for the rehabilitation of families affected by natural disasters. 

The main objective of the scheme is to ensure that affected families can rebuild their lives and livelihoods. 

The benefits include financial assistance for housing and basic amenities. 

Eligible beneficiaries are families who have lost their homes or livelihoods due to natural calamities. 

The application process typically involves submitting a request to the local government authorities along with necessary documentation. 

The timeline for assistance may vary based on the assessment of damage and the processing of applications. 

The implementing authorities include the Ministry of Home Affairs and state disaster management authorities. 

Additional details regarding specific financial amounts or assistance packages may vary by state.
