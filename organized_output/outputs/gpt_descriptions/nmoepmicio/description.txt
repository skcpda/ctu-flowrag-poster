The National Mission for Clean Ganga (NMCG) is a key initiative under the National Ganga River Basin Authority. 

It aims to rejuvenate the Ganga River and improve water quality. 

The objective is to reduce pollution and promote sustainable management of the river. 

Benefits include improved water quality and enhanced biodiversity. 

The scheme also aims to promote public awareness and community participation. 

Eligibility criteria vary based on specific projects under the mission. 

The application process is typically project-based and involves submission to the NMCG. 

The timeline for project implementation varies depending on the specific initiative. 

The NMCG operates under the Ministry of Water Resources River Development and Ganga Rejuvenation. 

It collaborates with various state governments and local bodies for effective implementation.
