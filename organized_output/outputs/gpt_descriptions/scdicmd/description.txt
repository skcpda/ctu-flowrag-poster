The SCDICMD scheme is designed to support small and medium enterprises in India. 

It aims to promote entrepreneurship and enhance the competitiveness of small businesses. 

The scheme provides financial assistance for various business activities. 

This includes support for project reports and marketing of products. 

Eligibility is primarily for small and medium enterprises registered under the relevant laws. 

The application process involves submitting a detailed project proposal to the concerned authorities. 

The timeline for approval and disbursement of funds varies based on the project and the processing time of the authorities. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises. 

Additional details may include specific financial limits and types of projects supported.
