The ISSS-WID scheme stands for the Integrated Social Security Scheme for Women in Distress. 

This welfare scheme was launched by the Government of India to address the specific needs of women who are in distressing situations. 

The background of this scheme is rooted in the recognition of the vulnerabilities faced by women in various circumstances. 

Women often encounter challenges such as domestic violence, abandonment, and economic hardships. 

The government aimed to create a supportive framework to empower these women and provide them with necessary resources. 

The primary objective of the ISSS-WID scheme is to ensure the social security of women who are in distress. 

It seeks to provide financial assistance and support services to help these women regain their independence. 

The scheme also aims to create awareness about women's rights and available resources. 

By doing so, it intends to promote gender equality and empower women in society. 

The benefits under the ISSS-WID scheme include financial assistance for women in distress. 

This assistance can help cover basic needs such as food, shelter, and healthcare. 

Additionally, the scheme may provide access to vocational training and skill development programs. 

These programs are designed to enhance the employability of women and help them become self-sufficient. 

Counseling services may also be available to support women emotionally and psychologically. 

The scheme may facilitate access to legal aid for women facing legal challenges. 

This is particularly important for women dealing with issues such as domestic violence or divorce. 

Eligibility for the ISSS-WID scheme typically includes women who are in distressing situations. 

This may encompass women who are victims of domestic violence, abandonment, or those who are single mothers.
