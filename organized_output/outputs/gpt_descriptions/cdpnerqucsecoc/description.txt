The scheme known as CDPNERQUCSECOC stands for the Comprehensive Development Plan for North Eastern Region and Quality Control of Essential Commodities. 

This scheme is part of the broader initiative by the Indian government to enhance the socio-economic conditions of the North Eastern states of India. 

The North Eastern region of India comprises eight states which include Assam, Arunachal Pradesh, Manipur, Meghalaya, Mizoram, Nagaland, Sikkim, and Tripura. 

These states have unique geographical and socio-economic challenges that require targeted interventions. 

The Comprehensive Development Plan aims to address these challenges through a multi-faceted approach. 

The primary objective of the scheme is to promote sustainable development in the North Eastern region. 

This includes enhancing infrastructure, improving access to quality education, and promoting health services. 

The scheme also aims to boost economic activities by supporting local industries and agriculture. 

Another key objective is to ensure the quality control of essential commodities. 

This is crucial for maintaining standards and ensuring consumer safety in the region.
