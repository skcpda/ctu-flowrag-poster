The Pamatn scheme is an initiative aimed at providing financial assistance to marginalized communities in India. 

The primary objective of the Pamatn scheme is to enhance the livelihood opportunities for economically weaker sections. 

The scheme offers direct financial support to eligible beneficiaries to improve their living standards. 

Eligibility criteria typically include being a member of a marginalized community and meeting specific income thresholds. 

The application process generally involves submitting an application form along with necessary documentation to the designated authority. 

The timeline for disbursement of funds may vary based on the specific guidelines of the implementing agency. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment at the central level. 

State governments may also have their own guidelines and procedures for the effective implementation of the scheme.
