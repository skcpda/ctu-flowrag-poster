The Stiti scheme is a welfare initiative by the Government of India. 

It aims to provide financial assistance to families affected by the COVID-19 pandemic. 

The objective is to support vulnerable households in overcoming economic hardships. 

Eligible beneficiaries include families with no regular source of income. 

The scheme targets those who have lost their livelihoods due to the pandemic. 

Financial assistance is provided in the form of direct cash transfers. 

The amount and frequency of assistance may vary based on government guidelines. 

Applications can be submitted online through designated government portals. 

Local government bodies may also facilitate the application process. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment. 

State governments may have specific guidelines for implementation. 

Details regarding the exact timeline for assistance disbursement may vary. 

The scheme is part of a broader effort to mitigate the impact of COVID-19 on vulnerable populations.
