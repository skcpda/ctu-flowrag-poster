The SFAC OHCS SEFC scheme is an initiative by the Government of India. 

It is part of the efforts to promote organic farming and enhance the income of farmers. 

The primary objective is to provide financial assistance and support to farmers engaged in organic farming practices. 

The scheme aims to improve the productivity and sustainability of organic farming. 

It also seeks to increase the availability of organic produce in the market. 

Eligible beneficiaries include farmers who are practicing organic farming. 

The application process involves submitting an application to the State Farming and Agricultural Corporation. 

Farmers must provide necessary documentation to prove their engagement in organic farming. 

The timeline for the assistance may vary based on the specific program under the scheme. 

The implementing authority for this scheme is the Small Farmers' Agribusiness Consortium. 

This organization works in collaboration with state governments to facilitate the scheme's objectives. 

Additional details regarding specific benefits may vary based on state-level implementations.
