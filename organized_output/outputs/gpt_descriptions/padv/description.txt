The PADV scheme stands for Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana - Urban.

It was launched to provide affordable housing to the urban poor.

The objective is to ensure housing for all by the year 2022.

The scheme aims to provide financial assistance for the construction of houses.

Beneficiaries receive a subsidy on home loan interest.

The subsidy can be up to 2.67 lakh INR depending on the category of the beneficiary.

Eligibility is based on income levels and the applicant must be a first-time homebuyer.

The scheme is available for Economically Weaker Sections Low Income Groups and Middle Income Groups.

The application process involves submitting an online application through the official PMAY website.

Documents required include identity proof income proof and property details.

The timeline for approval and disbursement can vary based on the application and processing.

The Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs is the implementing authority for this scheme.

State governments and urban local bodies also play a role in implementation.
