The Pradhan Mantri Suraksha Bima Yojana for Senior Citizens (PMSSCSN) is a government welfare scheme aimed at providing social security to senior citizens in India. 

The objective of the scheme is to offer financial assistance to elderly individuals in case of accidental death or disability. 

The benefits include an insurance cover of Rs. 2 lakh for accidental death and permanent total disability. 

In case of permanent partial disability, the insurance cover is Rs. 1 lakh. 

Eligibility criteria include being a resident of India and being aged 60 years or above. 

Individuals must also have a savings bank account to enroll in the scheme. 

The application process involves filling out a form available at designated banks and submitting it along with the required documents. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Finance through various public sector banks and insurance companies. 

The premium for the scheme is Rs. 12 per annum. 

The coverage period is from June 1 to May 31 of the following year. 

Renewal of the policy is required annually to continue receiving benefits.
