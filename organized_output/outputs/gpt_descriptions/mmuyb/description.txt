The Mukhyamantri Mahila Utkarsh Yojana (MMUYB) is a welfare scheme initiated by the Government of Madhya Pradesh. 

The scheme aims to empower women by providing them with financial assistance and support for self-employment. 

It seeks to promote entrepreneurship among women and enhance their economic independence. 

The benefits include financial assistance in the form of loans for starting small businesses. 

Women beneficiaries can receive up to INR 1 lakh as a loan at a subsidized interest rate. 

Eligibility is primarily for women who are residents of Madhya Pradesh. 

The scheme targets women from economically weaker sections and those who are part of self-help groups. 

The application process involves submitting an application form to the designated authorities or through online portals. 

The frequency of assistance is based on the approval of the loan application. 

The scheme is implemented by the Department of Women and Child Development of Madhya Pradesh. 

Additional details may include training programs for skill development associated with the scheme.
