The scheme is known as the Swami Vivekananda Uttar Pradesh Chief Minister's Fellowship Program (SFUGP). 

It was launched by the Government of Uttar Pradesh. 

The objective of the scheme is to provide financial assistance to students pursuing higher education. 

The program aims to promote research and innovation among students. 

Eligible candidates include students enrolled in recognized universities and institutions. 

The fellowship is available for postgraduate and doctoral programs. 

The application process involves submitting an online application through the official website. 

Candidates must provide necessary documents such as academic qualifications and research proposals. 

The timeline for application submissions is typically announced on the official website. 

The fellowship amount varies based on the level of study and research. 

The implementing authority for the scheme is the Department of Higher Education, Government of Uttar Pradesh. 

The scheme also encourages interdisciplinary research and collaboration. 

It aims to enhance the quality of education and research in the state. 

The fellowship is renewable based on the performance of the candidates. 

The scheme contributes to the overall development of higher education in Uttar Pradesh.
