The Vidya scheme is an initiative by the Government of India aimed at promoting education among children. 

The objective of the Vidya scheme is to provide financial assistance for the education of underprivileged children. 

The scheme offers scholarships to students from economically weaker sections to help them pursue their studies. 

Benefits include covering tuition fees and providing additional support for educational materials. 

Eligibility is generally based on the economic status of the family and the educational institution attended by the student. 

The application process typically involves submitting an application form along with necessary documents to the designated authority. 

The timeline for disbursement of funds may vary but is usually aligned with the academic year. 

Implementing authorities may include state education departments and local educational institutions. 

Additional details about the scheme can be found on official government websites and portals.
