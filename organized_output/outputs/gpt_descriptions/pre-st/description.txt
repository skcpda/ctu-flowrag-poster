The Pre-Matric Scholarship Scheme is designed to provide financial assistance to students from Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes. 

It aims to encourage students from these communities to continue their education at the pre-matric level. 

The scheme provides scholarships to students studying in classes 1 to 10. 

The benefits include financial support for tuition fees and maintenance allowances. 

Eligibility is limited to students belonging to Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes. 

Students must be enrolled in recognized schools and should have a family income below a specified threshold. 

The application process typically involves submitting an application form along with required documents to the respective state government or educational institution. 

The scholarship is usually disbursed on an annual basis. 

The implementing authorities include the Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment and respective state governments. 

Additional details may vary by state regarding the application process and specific financial assistance amounts.
