The FAFEFTCW scheme stands for the Financial Assistance for Education of the Children of the Textile Workers Scheme. 

This scheme was initiated by the Government of India to provide financial support to the children of textile workers.

The FAFEFTCW scheme aims to alleviate this burden.

It seeks to promote educational opportunities for these children.
