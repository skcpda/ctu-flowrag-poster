The SUATFA scheme stands for the Scheme for Utilization of Agricultural Technology for Farmers' Advancement. 

It was launched to enhance the productivity and income of farmers through the use of advanced agricultural technologies. 

The main objective is to provide farmers with access to modern farming techniques and innovations. 

The scheme aims to improve agricultural practices and increase crop yields. 

Benefits include training in modern agricultural methods and access to technology. 

Farmers may receive assistance in the form of subsidies for purchasing agricultural equipment. 

Eligibility is generally limited to farmers and agricultural workers. 

The application process typically involves registration with local agricultural offices or online portals. 

The timeline for implementation may vary based on the specific activities and training sessions offered. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers' Welfare. 

Additional details about specific programs or training sessions may be available through local agricultural departments.
