The TCSC scheme stands for the Tribal Cooperative Marketing Development Federation of India Limited. 

It aims to promote the socio-economic development of tribal communities in India. 

The primary objective is to provide fair prices for tribal products and enhance their income. 

The scheme facilitates the marketing of tribal products through cooperatives. 

It also provides financial assistance for the development of tribal artisans and their crafts. 

Eligible beneficiaries include tribal artisans and producers who are members of cooperatives. 

The application process typically involves registration with the relevant cooperative society. 

The frequency of assistance and support may vary based on the specific project or initiative. 

The Ministry of Tribal Affairs is the implementing authority for this scheme. 

Additional details may include capacity-building programs and skill development initiatives for tribal communities.
