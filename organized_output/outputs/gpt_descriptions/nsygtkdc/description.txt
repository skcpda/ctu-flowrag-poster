The National Scheme for Youth and Girls' Training and Development Centers (NSYGTKDC) aims to empower youth and girls through skill development and training programs. 

The objective is to enhance employability and promote entrepreneurship among young women and men. 

The scheme provides various training programs in different sectors to improve skills and knowledge. 

Benefits include access to skill development training, workshops, and resources for entrepreneurship. 

Eligibility criteria typically include being a youth or girl aged between 15 to 29 years. 

The application process generally involves filling out an online or offline application form available at designated centers. 

The timeline for the training programs may vary based on the specific course or workshop offered. 

Implementing authorities include the Ministry of Youth Affairs and Sports and various state governments. 

Additional details may include partnerships with NGOs and private organizations to enhance training quality.
