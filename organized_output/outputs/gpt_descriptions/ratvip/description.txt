The RatVIP scheme is an initiative by the Government of India aimed at providing financial assistance to the families of victims of road accidents. 

The primary objective of the scheme is to offer timely support to families who have lost their breadwinners due to road accidents. 

The benefits include a one-time financial assistance amount to the families of deceased victims. 

Eligibility criteria include being a family member of a person who has died in a road accident. 

The application process involves submitting a claim to the relevant authorities along with necessary documentation such as a death certificate and accident report. 

The timeline for disbursement of assistance is typically within a specified period after the application is approved. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Road Transport and Highways in collaboration with state governments.
