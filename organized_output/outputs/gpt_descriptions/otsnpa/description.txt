The One Time Settlement for Non-Performing Assets (OTS for NPA) scheme is an initiative by the Government of India. 

It aims to provide a one-time settlement option for borrowers who have defaulted on loans. 

The objective is to resolve the issue of non-performing assets in the banking sector. 

This scheme helps in reducing the financial burden on borrowers and improving the health of banks. 

Benefits include the opportunity for borrowers to settle their dues at a reduced amount. 

This can lead to the restoration of creditworthiness for borrowers. 

Eligibility criteria typically include being a borrower of a bank or financial institution. 

The borrower must have a non-performing asset status. 

The application process involves submitting a request to the concerned bank or financial institution. 

The borrower may need to provide necessary documentation related to their financial status. 

The timeline for the settlement process may vary based on the bank's policies. 

Implementing authorities include the Reserve Bank of India and individual banks. 

The scheme may be subject to specific terms and conditions set by the respective banks. 

Further details can be obtained from the official websites of the banks or financial institutions.
