The VPS scheme stands for the Vaya Vandana Yojana. 

It is a pension scheme launched by the Government of India. 

The scheme is aimed at providing financial security to senior citizens. 

The primary objective is to offer a regular income during old age. 

The scheme provides a guaranteed return of 8 percent per annum. 

The benefits are available for a minimum investment of INR 1,00,000. 

The maximum investment limit is INR 15,00,000. 

The scheme is available for individuals aged 60 years and above. 

The pension can be received monthly quarterly half-yearly or annually. 

The application process involves filling out a prescribed form. 

Applicants must submit the form along with necessary documents. 

The scheme is implemented by the Life Insurance Corporation of India. 

The tenure of the scheme is 10 years. 

The scheme allows premature exit under certain conditions. 

The scheme is available until March 31 2023 or until the limit of the scheme is reached.
