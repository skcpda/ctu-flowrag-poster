The Widening Digital Employment Network Program (WDENP) aims to enhance digital employment opportunities in India. 

The objective of the program is to create a robust digital ecosystem that connects job seekers with employers. 

The benefits include skill development training and access to job placements in the digital sector. 

Assistance is provided in the form of workshops and online resources for skill enhancement. 

Eligibility criteria typically include being a job seeker or an individual looking to enhance digital skills. 

The application process usually involves registering on the official portal and submitting relevant documents. 

The timeline for the program may vary based on the specific training modules and job placement opportunities. 

Implementing authorities include the Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology and various state governments. 

The program also collaborates with private sector companies to facilitate job placements.
