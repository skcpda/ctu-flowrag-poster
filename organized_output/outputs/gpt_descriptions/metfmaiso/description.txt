The scheme Metfmaiso is designed to provide financial assistance to farmers in India. 

The objective of the scheme is to enhance the income of farmers and improve their livelihoods. 

The benefits include direct cash transfers to eligible farmers to support their agricultural activities. 

Eligibility criteria include being a registered farmer and having a valid landholding. 

The application process involves submitting an application form along with necessary documents to the local agricultural office. 

The timeline for disbursement of funds is typically aligned with the agricultural calendar and may vary based on the specific circumstances. 

The implementing authorities for this scheme are the Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers' Welfare at the central level along with state agricultural departments. 

Further details on specific benefits and assistance may vary by state and local implementation.
