The scheme is known as the Sikkim Chief Minister's Gramin Bikas Yojana (SKGBYS). 

It was launched to promote rural development in the state of Sikkim. 

The objective is to enhance the living standards of rural households by providing financial assistance for various developmental activities. 

The benefits include financial support for infrastructure development and livelihood enhancement. 

Eligible beneficiaries are rural households in Sikkim who meet specific criteria set by the state government. 

The application process involves submitting an application form to the local government office along with necessary documents. 

The scheme is implemented by the Rural Development Department of the Government of Sikkim. 

Further details regarding the timeline or frequency of assistance are not specified.
