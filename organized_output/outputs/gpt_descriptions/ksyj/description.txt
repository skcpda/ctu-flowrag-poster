The Kisan Samman Nidhi Yojana (Ksyj) is a government welfare scheme launched by the Government of India. 

The scheme was introduced in February 2019. 

It aims to provide financial support to small and marginal farmers. 

The primary objective is to enhance the income of farmers and ensure their economic stability. 

The scheme provides direct cash transfers to eligible farmers. 

Each eligible farmer receives Rs. 6000 per year. 

The amount is disbursed in three equal installments of Rs. 2000 each. 

This financial assistance is intended to help farmers meet their agricultural expenses. 

The scheme also aims to reduce the dependency of farmers on informal loans. 

Eligibility for the Ksyj is limited to small and marginal farmers. 

A small farmer is defined as one who cultivates land up to 1 hectare. 

A marginal farmer is defined as one who cultivates land up to 2 hectares. 

Farmers must be registered under the scheme to receive benefits. 

The scheme excludes certain categories of farmers. 

These include institutional landholders and farmers who are ineligible for other government schemes. 

The application process for Ksyj is straightforward. 

Farmers can apply online through the official government portal. 

They can also visit the nearest Common Service Centers (CSCs) for assistance. 

The application requires basic information such as name and land details. 

Farmers must provide their Aadhaar number for identity verification. 

Once the application is submitted it is verified by the authorities. 

The timeline for receiving benefits can vary. 

Typically the first installment is disbursed shortly after verification. 

Subsequent installments are released every four months. 

The implementing authority for the Ksyj is the Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers' Welfare. 

State governments play a crucial role in the implementation of the scheme. 

They are responsible for the identification of eligible beneficiaries. 

The scheme is funded by the central government.
