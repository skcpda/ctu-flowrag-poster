The Telangana State Development Society (TS-DS) scheme is designed to promote the welfare of the Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes in Telangana. 

The primary objective of the scheme is to provide financial assistance for the socio-economic development of these communities. 

The benefits include financial support for education training and self-employment initiatives. 

Eligibility is primarily for individuals belonging to Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes residing in Telangana. 

The application process involves submitting an online application through the official TS-DS portal. 

The timeline for processing applications may vary based on the specific assistance requested. 

The implementing authority for the TS-DS scheme is the Telangana State Development Society. 

Additional details may include specific programs under the scheme aimed at skill development and entrepreneurship.
