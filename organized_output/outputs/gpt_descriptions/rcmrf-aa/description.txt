The RCMRF-AA scheme is the Rajiv Gandhi Chhatravriti Yojana for the children of the families of the Armed Forces. 

It aims to provide financial assistance to the children of armed forces personnel to support their education. 

The scheme provides scholarships to students pursuing higher education. 

Benefits include financial support for tuition fees and other educational expenses. 

Eligibility is limited to children of armed forces personnel who are studying in recognized institutions. 

The application process involves submitting a prescribed application form along with necessary documents to the concerned authorities. 

The timeline for disbursement of scholarships is typically aligned with the academic calendar. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Defence.
