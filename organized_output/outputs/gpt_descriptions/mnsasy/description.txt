Mahatma Gandhi National Rural Employment Guarantee Act is a welfare scheme aimed at providing guaranteed employment to rural households. 

The objective of the scheme is to enhance livelihood security in rural areas by providing at least 100 days of unskilled wage employment in a financial year to every rural household. 

The benefits include wage payments for unskilled manual work and the creation of durable assets in rural areas. 

Eligibility is limited to rural households that apply for employment under the scheme. 

The application process involves submitting a demand for work to the local Gram Panchayat. 

The timeline for wage payments is typically within 15 days of work completion. 

The implementing authority is the Ministry of Rural Development at the central level along with state governments. 

The scheme also aims to empower marginalized sections of society by providing them with employment opportunities. 

It promotes social inclusion and enhances the livelihood security of rural households.
