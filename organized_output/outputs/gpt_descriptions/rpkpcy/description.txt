The RPKPCY scheme stands for the Rajiv Gandhi Panchayat Sashaktikaran Abhiyan. 

It aims to strengthen the Panchayati Raj institutions in rural areas. 

The objective is to enhance the capacity of local self-governments to deliver services effectively. 

Benefits include improved governance and increased participation of citizens in local decision-making. 

The scheme provides financial assistance for capacity building and training of Panchayat members. 

Eligibility criteria include being a member of a Panchayat in rural areas. 

The application process involves submitting a proposal to the respective state government. 

The timeline for implementation varies by state and specific project. 

The Ministry of Panchayati Raj is the implementing authority for this scheme. 

Additional details may include collaboration with state governments for effective execution.
