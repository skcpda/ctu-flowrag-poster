The RCSMMS stands for the Rajiv Gandhi Chaitanya Yojana. 

It is a welfare scheme initiated by the Government of Madhya Pradesh. 

The scheme aims to provide financial assistance to the marginalized sections of society. 

Its primary objective is to empower women and promote self-reliance among them. 

The benefits include financial support for women to start their own businesses or self-help groups. 

Eligible beneficiaries are women from economically weaker sections. 

The application process involves submitting a form along with necessary documents to the designated authority. 

The timeline for processing applications is typically within a few weeks. 

The implementing authority for this scheme is the Department of Women and Child Development, Madhya Pradesh. 

Additional details regarding specific financial assistance amounts or project types may vary.
