The scheme SGRC-SL stands for the State Government Rural Credit Scheme for Self-Help Groups. 

It was launched to provide financial assistance to self-help groups in rural areas. 

The objective is to promote self-employment and entrepreneurship among rural women. 

The scheme aims to enhance the income-generating capacity of self-help groups. 

Benefits include access to credit facilities at lower interest rates. 

Assistance is provided in the form of loans to eligible self-help groups. 

Eligibility criteria typically include being a registered self-help group. 

The group must have a minimum number of members as specified by the state government. 

The application process involves submitting a proposal to the concerned financial institution. 

The proposal must include details about the group and the intended use of funds. 

The timeline for loan disbursement may vary based on the financial institution's procedures. 

Implementing authorities include the state government and associated financial institutions. 

The scheme may also involve training and capacity-building programs for beneficiaries. 

Monitoring and evaluation are conducted to assess the impact of the scheme on rural livelihoods.
