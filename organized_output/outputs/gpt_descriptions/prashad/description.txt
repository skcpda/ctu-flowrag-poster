The Prashad scheme is an initiative by the Government of India. 

It aims to promote religious tourism in the country. 

The objective is to develop and beautify pilgrimage sites. 

The scheme focuses on providing infrastructure and facilities for pilgrims. 

Benefits include improved access to religious sites and enhanced amenities. 

This includes better roads, sanitation, and accommodation facilities. 

Eligibility is generally open to all religious sites recognized by the government. 

The application process involves submission of proposals by state governments. 

These proposals are evaluated by the Ministry of Tourism. 

The timeline for project implementation varies based on the specific project. 

The Ministry of Tourism is the primary implementing authority for the Prashad scheme. 

The scheme is part of a broader effort to boost tourism and preserve cultural heritage.
