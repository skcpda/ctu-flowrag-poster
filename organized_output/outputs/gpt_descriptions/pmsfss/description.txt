The Pradhan Mantri Suraksha Bima Yojana (PMSBY) is a government welfare scheme launched in 2015. 

It aims to provide accidental death and disability insurance to individuals. 

The objective is to enhance the financial security of the poor and vulnerable sections of society. 

The scheme offers a coverage of ₹2 lakh for accidental death and permanent total disability. 

It provides ₹1 lakh for permanent partial disability. 

The premium for the scheme is ₹12 per annum. 

Eligibility is open to all individuals aged between 18 and 70 years. 

Applicants must have a bank account and provide consent for auto-debit of the premium. 

The application process can be completed through banks and insurance companies. 

Enrollment is done on a yearly basis. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Finance through public sector insurance companies. 

It is available across the country. 

The scheme is part of the broader initiative to promote social security in India.
