The scheme is known as the Scheme for Empowerment of Street Vendors in Smart Cities (SESSCT). 

It aims to provide social security and livelihood support to street vendors in urban areas. 

The objective is to enhance the livelihood opportunities of street vendors and improve their living conditions. 

Benefits include access to credit facilities skill development training and financial assistance. 

Eligible beneficiaries are street vendors operating in smart cities. 

The application process typically involves registration with local municipal authorities. 

The timeline for assistance may vary based on the specific program under the scheme. 

Implementing authorities include the Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs and local municipal bodies. 

The scheme is part of the broader initiative to promote urban development and inclusive growth.
