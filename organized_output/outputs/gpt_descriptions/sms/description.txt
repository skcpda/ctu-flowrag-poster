The SMS scheme is a government initiative aimed at providing financial assistance to farmers. 

It is designed to support farmers during times of distress caused by natural calamities or market fluctuations. 

The objective is to ensure food security and improve the livelihood of farmers. 

Eligible beneficiaries include small and marginal farmers who own land. 

Farmers must provide proof of land ownership and identity to apply. 

Applications can be submitted online through the official government portal or at designated offices. 

The assistance is typically provided on an annual basis or as needed during crises. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers' Welfare. 

Additional details about the specific amount of assistance and the application deadlines may vary by state.
