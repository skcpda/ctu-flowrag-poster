The TSGGVN scheme stands for the Tribal Sub-Plan for the Development of Scheduled Tribes in Gujarat. 

It aims to promote the socio-economic development of Scheduled Tribes in the state. 

The objectives include enhancing livelihood opportunities and improving access to education and health services for tribal communities. 

Benefits include financial assistance for various livelihood projects and skill development programs. 

Eligible beneficiaries are members of Scheduled Tribes residing in Gujarat. 

The application process involves submitting a proposal to the concerned district office along with necessary documents. 

The scheme is implemented by the Tribal Development Department of the Government of Gujarat. 

The timeline for assistance varies based on the specific project or program under the scheme. 

Regular monitoring and evaluation are conducted to assess the impact of the scheme on tribal communities.
