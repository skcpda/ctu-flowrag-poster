The MyYyt scheme is an initiative aimed at enhancing the livelihood opportunities for youth in India. 

The primary objective is to provide skill development and employment opportunities to young individuals. 

The scheme offers training programs in various sectors to improve employability. 

Financial assistance may be provided to participants for training and skill development. 

Eligibility criteria typically include age limits and educational qualifications, focusing on youth between 15 to 29 years. 

The application process generally involves online registration through the official portal or designated centers. 

The timeline for the training programs may vary based on the specific course and institution. 

Implementing authorities include the Ministry of Skill Development and Entrepreneurship and affiliated training partners. 

Additional details may include partnerships with private sector organizations for better job placement opportunities.
