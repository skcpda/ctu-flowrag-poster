The Scheme for Capacity Building in Textile Sector (SCBTS) is an initiative by the Government of India. 

It aims to enhance the skills of the workforce in the textile sector. 

The objective is to improve the employability of individuals and meet the industry's demand for skilled labor. 

Benefits include training programs that provide skill development in various textile-related fields. 

The assistance includes financial support for training institutions and trainers. 

Eligibility is generally for individuals seeking employment in the textile sector. 

The application process involves enrolling in recognized training programs under the scheme. 

The timeline for training programs may vary based on the specific courses offered. 

The Ministry of Textiles is the implementing authority for this scheme. 

Additional details may include partnerships with industry stakeholders for effective implementation.
