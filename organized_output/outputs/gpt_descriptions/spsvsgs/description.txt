The scheme is known as the Samagra Punarvas Yojana for Street Vendors and Street Vendors' Self-Help Groups.

It aims to provide financial assistance and support to street vendors in urban areas.

The objectives include promoting self-employment among street vendors and enhancing their livelihoods.

Benefits include access to credit facilities and skill development training.

Eligibility is primarily for street vendors who are registered under the relevant municipal authorities.

The application process involves submitting an application form along with required documents to the local municipal authority.

The timeline for disbursement of funds and assistance may vary based on the local authority's processing time.

The implementing authority for this scheme is the Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs.

Additional details about the scheme may include provisions for capacity building and access to markets for street vendors.
