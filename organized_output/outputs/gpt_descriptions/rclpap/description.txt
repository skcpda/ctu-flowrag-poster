The RCLPAP stands for the Revamped Central Sector Scheme for the Promotion of Agricultural Mechanization. 

This scheme was launched to promote the use of agricultural machinery among farmers. 

The objective is to enhance productivity and reduce the drudgery of farming. 

It aims to provide financial assistance for the purchase of agricultural machinery and equipment. 

The benefits include subsidies on various types of agricultural machinery. 

Farmers can receive up to 50 percent subsidy on the cost of machinery. 

Eligibility is primarily for farmers and agricultural cooperatives. 

The application process involves submitting a proposal to the relevant agricultural department. 

The timeline for approval and disbursement of subsidies may vary based on the state. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers' Welfare. 

State governments also play a role in the implementation and monitoring of the scheme.
