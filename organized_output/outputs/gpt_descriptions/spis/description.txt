The Scheme for Promoting Innovation and Startups (SPIS) is an initiative by the Government of India. 

It aims to foster innovation and entrepreneurship among youth. 

The objective is to create a conducive ecosystem for startups. 

Benefits include financial assistance and mentorship support. 

Eligible applicants are individuals or groups with innovative ideas. 

The application process involves submitting a proposal through the official portal. 

The timeline for approval and assistance varies based on the proposal. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Commerce and Industry. 

It encourages collaboration between startups and established companies. 

SPIS also aims to enhance job creation through startup growth. 

The scheme promotes research and development in various sectors. 

It supports startups in technology and other emerging fields. 

The initiative aligns with the broader Startup India program. 

SPIS is part of the government's vision to boost the economy through innovation.
