The Women and Child Development (WCD) Scheme is aimed at improving the welfare of women and children in India. 

The primary objective is to provide holistic support for the development and empowerment of women and children. 

The scheme offers various benefits including nutritional support health care education and financial assistance. 

Eligibility criteria typically include being a woman or child from a low-income family or belonging to marginalized communities. 

The application process generally involves submitting forms to local authorities or designated offices under the Women and Child Development Department. 

The timeline for assistance may vary based on the specific program under the WCD scheme. 

Implementing authorities include the Ministry of Women and Child Development at the central level and state departments at the local level. 

Additional details may include specific programs under the scheme such as the Integrated Child Development Services (ICDS) and the Beti Bachao Beti Padhao initiative.
