Prowis-I is a government welfare scheme aimed at promoting the welfare of women and children in India. 

The objective of Prowis-I is to provide financial assistance and support to women and children from marginalized communities. 

The scheme focuses on enhancing the socio-economic status of beneficiaries through various initiatives. 

Benefits include financial aid for education health care and skill development programs. 

Eligibility criteria include being a woman or child from a marginalized or economically weaker section of society. 

The application process typically involves submitting an application form along with necessary documents to the designated authority. 

The timeline for processing applications may vary based on the implementing agency. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Women and Child Development.
