The STSSTS scheme stands for the "Scheme for Transgender Persons" under the Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment. 

This scheme aims to provide social security and welfare measures for transgender persons in India. 

The objectives include promoting the rights and welfare of transgender persons and ensuring their inclusion in society. 

Benefits under the scheme include financial assistance for education, skill development, and healthcare. 

Transgender persons are eligible for the scheme. 

The application process involves submitting an application form along with necessary documents to the designated authorities. 

The timeline for processing applications may vary based on the specific requirements of the implementing agency. 

The Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment is the primary implementing authority for this scheme.
