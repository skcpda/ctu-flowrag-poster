The SFRV scheme stands for the Scheme for Financial Assistance to the Families of the Victims of the 1984 Anti-Sikh Riots. 

It was launched to provide financial support to the families affected by the anti-Sikh riots that occurred in 1984. 

The primary objective of the scheme is to offer monetary assistance to the victims' families to help them rebuild their lives. 

The benefits include a one-time financial assistance amount to the eligible families of the victims. 

Eligibility for the scheme is limited to the families of those who lost their lives or were severely injured during the riots. 

The application process involves submitting a form along with necessary documents to the designated authorities. 

The timeline for the disbursement of funds is typically specified by the implementing agency. 

The Ministry of Home Affairs is the primary authority responsible for implementing the SFRV scheme.
