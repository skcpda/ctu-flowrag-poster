The Tuk-Sept scheme is an initiative aimed at providing financial assistance to the elderly population in India. 

The primary objective of the scheme is to ensure a dignified life for senior citizens by offering them a regular source of income. 

The benefits include a monthly pension that helps cover basic living expenses. 

Eligibility criteria typically include age requirements and residency status. 

The application process usually involves submitting necessary documents to the designated local authorities. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment. 

Further details on the timeline for disbursement and frequency of payments may vary by state.
