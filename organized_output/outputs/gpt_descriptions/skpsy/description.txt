The scheme is known as the Scheme for the Promotion of Skill Development among Youth (SKPSY). 

It aims to enhance the employability of youth through skill development and vocational training. 

The objectives include providing training to youth in various sectors to meet industry demands. 

The benefits include skill training programs that lead to job placements and entrepreneurship opportunities. 

Assistance may include financial support for training and access to resources for starting businesses. 

Eligibility criteria typically include age limits and educational qualifications which may vary by program. 

The application process generally involves filling out an online application form and submitting required documents. 

The timeline for training programs may vary based on the specific courses offered. 

Implementing authorities include the Ministry of Skill Development and Entrepreneurship and affiliated training institutes.
