**Mahatma Gandhi National Rural Employment Guarantee Act (MGNREGA)**

MGNREGA was enacted in 2005 to enhance livelihood security in rural areas of India. 

The scheme provides a legal guarantee for at least 100 days of unskilled wage employment in a financial year to every rural household. 

The primary objective of MGNREGA is to provide social security and improve the livelihood of rural households. 

It aims to create durable assets and infrastructure in rural areas. 

The scheme also seeks to empower rural communities by enhancing their bargaining power and ensuring their participation in the development process. 

Under MGNREGA, wage employment is provided for various activities such as water conservation, drought proofing, land development, and flood control. 

The benefits of MGNREGA include the provision of guaranteed wage employment, which helps in poverty alleviation. 

It also promotes sustainable development through the creation of durable assets. 

The scheme enhances the livelihood security of rural households and contributes to the overall economic development of rural areas. 

Eligibility for MGNREGA is limited to rural households. 

Any adult member of a rural household can apply for employment under the scheme. 

The application process involves submitting a demand for work to the local Gram Panchayat. 

The Gram Panchayat is required to register the demand and provide employment within 15 days. 

If employment is not provided within this timeframe, the applicant is entitled to unemployment allowance. 

The timeline for the scheme is based on the financial year. 

The employment provided under MGNREGA is for a minimum of 100 days per household in a financial year. 

The implementing authorities for MGNREGA include the Ministry of Rural Development at the central level. 

State governments are responsible for the implementation of the scheme at the state and local levels. 

The Gram Panchayats play a crucial role in the execution of the scheme.
