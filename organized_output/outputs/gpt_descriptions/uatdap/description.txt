The UATDAP scheme stands for the Urban Agriculture Technology Development and Promotion scheme. 

This scheme aims to promote urban agriculture in cities across India. 

The objective is to enhance food security and improve the livelihood of urban populations. 

The scheme provides technical assistance and support for urban farming initiatives. 

It encourages the use of innovative agricultural practices and technologies. 

Benefits include access to training programs and resources for urban farmers. 

Financial assistance may be provided for the development of urban agriculture projects. 

Eligibility criteria typically include urban residents interested in agricultural activities. 

The application process involves submitting a proposal to the relevant local authority. 

The timeline for project approval and funding may vary based on the proposal. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers' Welfare. 

Local agricultural departments may also play a role in execution and support.
