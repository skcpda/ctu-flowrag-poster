The Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana - Housing for All (PMAY-HFA) is a government initiative aimed at providing affordable housing to the urban poor.

The objective of the scheme is to ensure that every individual has access to a pucca house with basic amenities.

The benefits include financial assistance for the construction or enhancement of houses.

Eligible beneficiaries include individuals from Economically Weaker Sections and Low-Income Groups.

The application process involves submitting an online application through the official PMAY website or through designated Common Service Centers.

The timeline for approval and disbursement of funds may vary based on the application and verification process.

The Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs is the primary implementing authority for this scheme.

Additional details may include the provision of interest subsidy for housing loans under the Credit Linked Subsidy Scheme.
