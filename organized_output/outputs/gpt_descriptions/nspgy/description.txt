The National Social Protection Scheme for the Garment and Textile Workers (NSPGY) aims to provide social security and welfare benefits to workers in the garment and textile sector. 

The primary objective of NSPGY is to enhance the welfare of garment and textile workers by providing them with financial assistance and social security. 

The scheme offers various benefits including health insurance maternity benefits and pensions to eligible workers. 

Eligibility for the scheme includes workers employed in the garment and textile sector who meet specific criteria set by the implementing authorities. 

The application process typically involves submitting necessary documents and forms to the designated authorities or agencies overseeing the scheme. 

The timeline for processing applications and disbursing benefits may vary based on the specific procedures of the implementing agencies. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Textiles in collaboration with state governments and other relevant agencies.
