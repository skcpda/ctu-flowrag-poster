The Nai Roshni scheme is an initiative by the Government of India. 

It aims to empower and provide skills to minority women. 

The objective is to enhance their confidence and promote self-reliance. 

The scheme offers training in various skills and entrepreneurship. 

Financial assistance is provided for training and capacity building. 

Eligible beneficiaries are women from minority communities. 

Women aged between 18 to 65 years can apply. 

The application process involves filling out a prescribed form. 

Applications can be submitted online or through designated offices. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Minority Affairs. 

Training programs are conducted through NGOs and other organizations. 

The frequency of training sessions varies based on demand. 

The scheme also emphasizes leadership development among women. 

Additional support includes awareness programs on rights and entitlements. 

The Nai Roshni scheme is part of the broader efforts to promote gender equality. 

It contributes to the socio-economic development of minority women in India.
