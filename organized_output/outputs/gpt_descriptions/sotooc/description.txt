The SOTOOC scheme stands for the Scheme for Online Training and Orientation of Officials and Citizens. 

It was launched to enhance the skills and knowledge of government officials and citizens. 

The objective is to provide online training and orientation programs to improve governance and citizen engagement. 

The scheme aims to create a knowledgeable workforce that can effectively implement government policies. 

Benefits include access to various training modules and resources for skill development. 

Participants can gain insights into best practices and innovative solutions in governance. 

Eligibility criteria typically include government officials and citizens interested in enhancing their skills. 

The application process usually involves registration on the official portal. 

Participants may need to complete specific training modules to receive certification. 

The timeline for training programs may vary based on the specific course offered. 

Implementing authorities include various government departments and agencies responsible for training and capacity building. 

The scheme promotes digital literacy and encourages the use of technology in governance. 

It also aims to foster a culture of continuous learning among officials and citizens.
