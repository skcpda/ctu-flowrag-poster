The MT-Pudu scheme is an initiative aimed at enhancing the livelihoods of marginalized communities in India. 

The primary objective of the scheme is to provide financial assistance and skill development to improve the economic conditions of the beneficiaries. 

The benefits include monetary support for starting small businesses and access to training programs for skill enhancement. 

Eligibility is generally focused on individuals from marginalized communities including Scheduled Castes Scheduled Tribes and other backward classes. 

The application process typically involves submitting a form along with necessary documents to the designated local authority or agency. 

The timeline for disbursement of funds and training programs may vary based on the specific implementation plan of the local authorities.
