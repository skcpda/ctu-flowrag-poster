The ELPD scheme stands for the Employment Linked Production Development scheme. 

This scheme is part of the Indian government's broader initiative to enhance employment opportunities in the manufacturing sector. 

The ELPD scheme aims to create a conducive environment for the growth of industries that can generate employment. 

It is designed to encourage production in various sectors by linking employment generation to production activities. 

The scheme focuses on providing financial assistance to industries that are willing to create jobs while enhancing their production capabilities. 

The primary objective of the ELPD scheme is to boost the manufacturing sector in India. 

It aims to create a significant number of jobs through the establishment and expansion of production units. 

The scheme also seeks to promote entrepreneurship by providing support to new and existing businesses.
