Nikshay is a digital platform launched by the Government of India. 

It aims to provide support for tuberculosis patients. 

The primary objective is to ensure timely treatment and care for individuals suffering from tuberculosis. 

Nikshay facilitates the tracking of patients and their treatment progress. 

It provides financial assistance to patients for their nutritional needs. 

The scheme also aims to enhance the overall management of tuberculosis cases in the country. 

Eligible beneficiaries include all tuberculosis patients registered under the Revised National Tuberculosis Control Program. 

Patients must be diagnosed with tuberculosis and enrolled in the treatment program. 

The application process involves registration through healthcare facilities. 

Patients can also be registered by healthcare workers. 

The assistance is provided on a monthly basis during the treatment period. 

The implementing authority for this scheme is the Ministry of Health and Family Welfare. 

State health departments also play a role in the implementation at the local level. 

Nikshay promotes community involvement in supporting tuberculosis patients. 

It encourages donations from individuals and organizations to assist patients. 

The platform provides a transparent mechanism for tracking donations and assistance.
