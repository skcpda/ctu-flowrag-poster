The SPK scheme refers to the Samarthya Pradhan Mantri Kalyan Yojana. 

It was launched to provide financial assistance to marginalized communities. 

The objective is to empower individuals through skill development and financial support. 

The scheme aims to enhance livelihood opportunities for beneficiaries. 

Eligible beneficiaries include individuals from economically weaker sections and marginalized groups. 

The application process involves submitting an online application through the official portal. 

The timeline for disbursement of benefits is typically within a few weeks of application approval. 

The Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment is the implementing authority for this scheme. 

Additional details about specific benefits may vary based on state implementation.
