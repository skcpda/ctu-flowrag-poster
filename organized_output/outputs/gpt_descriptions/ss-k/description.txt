The SS-K scheme is a government initiative aimed at providing social security to the underprivileged sections of society. 

The primary objective of the SS-K scheme is to enhance the quality of life for marginalized communities. 

It seeks to provide financial assistance and support for various needs such as education health and livelihood. 

Eligible beneficiaries include individuals from economically weaker sections and marginalized communities. 

The application process involves submitting a form along with required documents to the designated authority. 

The timeline for processing applications may vary depending on the specific requirements of the implementing agency. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment at the national level. 

State governments may also have their own mechanisms for implementing the scheme at the local level. 

Additional details regarding specific benefits may vary based on the state and local government provisions.
