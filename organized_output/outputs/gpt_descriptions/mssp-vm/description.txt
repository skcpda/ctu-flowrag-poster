The MSSP-VM scheme stands for the Mukhyamantri Sewa Sankalp Programme for Vulnerable Migrants. 

It was launched to address the challenges faced by vulnerable migrant workers in India. 

The objective of the scheme is to provide social security and welfare measures to migrant workers. 

The scheme aims to ensure that migrant workers have access to essential services and support during their migration. 

Benefits include financial assistance for housing health care and skill development. 

Assistance is also provided for transportation and employment opportunities. 

Eligibility criteria include being a migrant worker who is vulnerable and in need of support. 

The application process typically involves submitting an application form along with necessary documents to the designated authority. 

The timeline for processing applications may vary based on the implementing agency. 

The scheme is implemented by the respective state governments in coordination with the Ministry of Labour and Employment. 

Additional details about the scheme may include specific provisions for different categories of migrant workers.
