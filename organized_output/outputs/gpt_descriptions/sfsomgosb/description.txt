The SFSOMGoSB scheme is designed to support the welfare of Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes in India. 

It aims to provide financial assistance for the education and skill development of these communities. 

The benefits include scholarships for students and funding for vocational training programs. 

Eligibility is primarily based on the applicant's caste status as a Scheduled Caste or Scheduled Tribe. 

The application process typically involves submitting relevant documents to the designated authorities.
