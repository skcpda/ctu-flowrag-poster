The SES scheme refers to the Skill Enhancement Scheme. 

It aims to enhance the skill sets of individuals to improve their employability. 

The primary objective is to provide training and skill development opportunities. 

The scheme benefits unemployed youth and underprivileged sections of society. 

Financial assistance is provided for training programs. 

Eligibility criteria typically include age limits and educational qualifications. 

Individuals must be between the ages of 15 to 35 years. 

They should have completed at least the 8th grade. 

The application process usually involves online registration. 

Candidates may need to provide documents such as identity proof and educational certificates. 

The timeline for training programs can vary based on the specific course. 

Training may be conducted in various phases throughout the year. 

Implementing authorities include the Ministry of Skill Development and Entrepreneurship. 

State governments may also play a role in the execution of the scheme. 

Additional details may include partnerships with private training providers. 

The scheme may also focus on specific sectors based on regional needs.
