The Pradhan Mantri Digital India Programme aims to transform India into a digitally empowered society and knowledge economy. 

The objective is to ensure that government services are made available to citizens electronically by improving online infrastructure. 

The programme seeks to enhance digital literacy among citizens and promote the use of technology in various sectors. 

Benefits include access to government services through digital platforms and improved connectivity in rural areas. 

Eligibility criteria vary based on specific initiatives under the programme but generally target all citizens of India. 

The application process is primarily online through designated government portals and mobile applications. 

The timeline for implementation is ongoing with various initiatives being launched periodically. 

The Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology is the primary implementing authority for the programme. 

Other details include partnerships with private sector companies to enhance digital infrastructure and training programs for skill development.
