The scheme is known as the Swachh Bharat Kosh (SBK) Health Care Scheme (SKBHCS). 

It was launched to promote sanitation and hygiene in India. 

The primary objective is to improve health outcomes by ensuring access to clean and safe sanitation facilities. 

The scheme aims to reduce the incidence of sanitation-related diseases. 

It provides financial assistance for the construction of toilets and sanitation facilities. 

The benefits include improved health and hygiene for families and communities. 

Eligibility is generally targeted towards households in rural and urban areas lacking proper sanitation facilities. 

The application process typically involves submitting a request to local authorities or designated agencies. 

The timeline for fund disbursement and project completion may vary based on local governance. 

The implementing authorities include the Ministry of Jal Shakti and local municipal bodies. 

The scheme is part of the broader Swachh Bharat Mission initiative.
