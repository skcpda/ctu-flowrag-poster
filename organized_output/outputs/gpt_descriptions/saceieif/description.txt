The SACEIEIF scheme stands for the Scheme for Assistance to Central Educational Institutions for Infrastructure Development. 

It aims to enhance infrastructure in central educational institutions across India. 

The objective is to provide financial assistance for the development of essential infrastructure facilities. 

Benefits include funding for construction of buildings laboratories libraries and other necessary facilities. 

Eligibility is typically limited to central educational institutions recognized by the government. 

The application process involves submission of proposals by the institutions to the Ministry of Education. 

The timeline for fund disbursement varies based on project approval and completion of required documentation. 

The Ministry of Education is the primary implementing authority for this scheme. 

Additional details regarding specific funding amounts or project types may vary based on government guidelines.
