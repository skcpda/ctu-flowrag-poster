The Ongoing Financial Assistance for Old Age Pension Scheme (OFOJS) is a welfare initiative by the Government of India. 

It aims to provide financial support to elderly individuals who are in need. 

The primary objective is to ensure a dignified life for senior citizens by providing them with a regular income. 

Eligible beneficiaries include senior citizens aged 60 years and above who belong to economically weaker sections. 

The scheme offers a monthly pension amount which varies by state. 

The application process typically involves submitting a form along with necessary documents such as age proof and income certificate. 

Applications can be made through local government offices or online portals depending on the state. 

The pension is disbursed monthly to the beneficiaries. 

The implementing authority varies by state but generally includes the Department of Social Welfare or similar agencies. 

The scheme is part of a broader effort to support vulnerable populations in India.
