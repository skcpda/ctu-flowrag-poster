The scheme kmys2016 refers to the Kanyashree Prakalpa scheme initiated by the Government of West Bengal.

The scheme was launched in 2013 to improve the status and well-being of girls in the state.

It aims to reduce child marriage and promote education among girls.

The primary objective is to empower girls by providing financial assistance for their education.

The scheme also seeks to encourage families to keep their daughters in school and delay marriage until they reach adulthood.

Under this scheme, financial assistance is provided to girls from economically disadvantaged backgrounds.

The benefits include a one-time grant of INR 25,000 for girls who complete their secondary education.

Additionally, there is an annual scholarship of INR 1,000 for girls aged 13 to 18 who are enrolled in school.

The scheme targets girls from families with an annual income of less than INR 1.5 lakh.

Eligible beneficiaries must be residents of West Bengal.

They must also be within the age group of 13 to 18 years.

To apply for the Kanyashree Prakalpa scheme, applicants must fill out an online application form.

The application can be submitted through the official Kanyashree portal.

Applicants are required to provide necessary documents such as age proof and income certificate.

The application process also involves verification by local authorities.

Once the application is verified, the financial assistance is credited directly to the beneficiary's bank account.

The scheme is implemented by the Department of Women Development and Social Welfare, Government of West Bengal.

The Kanyashree Prakalpa scheme is a continuous initiative.
