The Pradhan Mantri Matsya Sampada Yojana (PMMSY) is a scheme aimed at enhancing fish production and promoting sustainable fisheries in India. 

The objective of PMMSY is to increase fish production to 22 million metric tons by 2024-25. 

It aims to create a sustainable and responsible fisheries sector. 

The scheme provides financial assistance for various activities related to fisheries. 

These activities include aquaculture, marine fisheries, and post-harvest management. 

The benefits include increased income for fishers and fish farmers. 

It also aims to generate employment opportunities in the fisheries sector. 

Eligibility criteria include fishers, fish farmers, and individuals or groups involved in fisheries. 

The application process involves submitting a proposal to the relevant state fisheries department. 

The timeline for processing applications may vary by state. 

The scheme is implemented by the Department of Fisheries under the Ministry of Fisheries Animal Husbandry and Dairying. 

PMMSY also emphasizes the use of modern technology and practices in fisheries. 

It supports the development of infrastructure for fisheries and aquaculture. 

The scheme encourages the participation of women and marginalized communities in fisheries. 

It aims to promote the export of fish and fish products. 

The PMMSY is part of the broader initiative to promote the Blue Revolution in India.
