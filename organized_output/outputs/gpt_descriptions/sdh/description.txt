The scheme "SDH" refers to the Skill Development for Handicrafts scheme.

It aims to enhance the skills of artisans and craftsmen in the handicraft sector.

The objective is to improve the quality of handicrafts and increase the employability of artisans.

Benefits include training in various handicraft techniques and access to modern tools and technologies.

Assistance may also involve financial support for training programs.

Eligibility typically includes artisans and craftsmen engaged in handicrafts.

The application process generally involves submitting an application to the relevant authority or agency.

The timeline for training programs may vary based on the specific course and location.

The Ministry of Textiles is the primary implementing authority for this scheme.

Additional details may include partnerships with various training institutes and organizations.
