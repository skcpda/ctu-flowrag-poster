The Uksaptd scheme is an initiative by the Government of India aimed at promoting the welfare of Scheduled Tribes. 

The objective of the scheme is to enhance the socio-economic conditions of tribal communities. 

The scheme provides financial assistance for various development projects in tribal areas. 

Benefits include support for education health and livelihood initiatives. 

Eligibility is primarily focused on individuals and groups belonging to Scheduled Tribes. 

The application process involves submitting a proposal to the relevant government authorities. 

The timeline for project approval and funding disbursement varies based on the specific project. 

The Ministry of Tribal Affairs is the implementing authority for the Uksaptd scheme. 

Additional details regarding specific project types and funding limits may be available through official government channels.
