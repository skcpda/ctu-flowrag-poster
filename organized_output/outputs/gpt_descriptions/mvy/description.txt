The Mukhyamantri Vatsalya Yojana (MVY) is a welfare scheme launched by the Government of Madhya Pradesh. 

It aims to provide financial assistance to orphaned children and those who have lost one or both parents due to various reasons. 

The primary objective is to ensure the welfare and development of these vulnerable children. 

Under this scheme, eligible beneficiaries receive a monthly financial aid of INR 2,000. 

This assistance is intended to support their basic needs and education. 

Eligibility is limited to children who are orphans or have lost one parent. 

The age limit for beneficiaries is typically set between 0 to 18 years. 

The application process involves submitting a form along with necessary documents such as identity proof and death certificates of parents. 

Applications can be submitted online or at designated government offices. 

The scheme is implemented by the Department of Women and Child Development in Madhya Pradesh. 

The financial assistance is provided on a monthly basis. 

The scheme aims to create a supportive environment for the growth and development of orphaned children.
