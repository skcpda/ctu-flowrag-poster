The VCFRSK scheme stands for the Vidhwa Charitable Fund for Rehabilitation of Single Women and Widows in Karnataka. 

This scheme was launched to support single women and widows in the state of Karnataka. 

The objective of the scheme is to provide financial assistance and rehabilitation support to empower single women and widows. 

The benefits include financial aid for livelihood activities and skill development programs. 

Eligible beneficiaries are single women and widows residing in Karnataka. 

The application process involves submitting an application form along with necessary documents to the designated authorities. 

The timeline for assistance is determined based on the processing of applications. 

The implementing authority for this scheme is the Department of Women and Child Development, Government of Karnataka.
