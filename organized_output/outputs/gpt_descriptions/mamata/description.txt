The Mamata scheme is a welfare initiative launched by the Government of West Bengal. 

It aims to provide financial assistance to pregnant women and new mothers. 

The scheme was introduced to improve maternal and child health in the state. 

It addresses the challenges faced by women during pregnancy and childbirth. 

The primary objective is to reduce maternal and infant mortality rates. 

The scheme also seeks to promote institutional deliveries. 

Under the Mamata scheme, eligible beneficiaries receive financial support during pregnancy. 

The assistance is provided in three installments. 

The total financial aid amounts to INR 6,000. 

The first installment of INR 1,000 is given during the first trimester. 

The second installment of INR 2,000 is provided after the second trimester. 

The final installment of INR 3,000 is given after the birth of the child. 

This financial support helps women meet their nutritional and medical needs. 

It also encourages them to seek regular medical check-ups. 

The scheme is designed to empower women by providing them with financial independence. 

Eligibility for the Mamata scheme includes pregnant women and new mothers. 

The beneficiaries must be residents of West Bengal. 

They should be at least 18 years old at the time of application. 

The scheme is applicable for the first two live births. 

Women who are already receiving benefits from other government schemes are not eligible. 

The application process for the Mamata scheme is straightforward. 

Interested beneficiaries must apply through the local health department. 

They need to fill out an application form. 

The form requires basic personal information and details about the pregnancy. 

Beneficiaries must submit the application along with necessary documents. 

These documents include proof of identity and residence. 

The application can also be submitted at the nearest Anganwadi center. 

Once the application is submitted, it is verified by health officials. 

Eligible beneficiaries receive the financial assistance directly into their bank accounts. 

The Mamata scheme operates on a continuous basis. 

There is no specific timeline for applications. 

Women can apply at any time during their pregnancy. 

The scheme is implemented by the Department of Health and Family Welfare, West Bengal. 

Local health workers play a crucial role in outreach and awareness. 

They assist women in understanding the benefits of the scheme.
