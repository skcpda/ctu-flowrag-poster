The Swami Vivekananda Uttar Pradesh Chief Minister Youth Empowerment Scheme (SVUCY) is an initiative by the Government of Uttar Pradesh. 

It aims to empower the youth of the state through skill development and employment opportunities. 

The objectives include enhancing employability among youth and promoting entrepreneurship. 

The scheme provides financial assistance for skill development training programs. 

It also offers support for self-employment ventures and start-ups. 

Eligibility criteria include being a resident of Uttar Pradesh and being within the age group of 15 to 40 years. 

Applicants must have completed at least the 8th grade for certain programs. 

The application process involves submitting an online application through the official portal. 

The scheme is implemented by the Uttar Pradesh Skill Development Mission. 

The frequency of assistance and training programs may vary based on the specific initiative under the scheme. 

Further details can be accessed through the official government website.
