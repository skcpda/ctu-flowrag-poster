The WBRUPASHREE scheme is an initiative by the Government of West Bengal. 

It aims to provide financial assistance to the families of women who are married. 

The primary objective is to promote the welfare of women and ensure their financial security. 

Under this scheme, a one-time financial assistance of INR 25,000 is provided to eligible beneficiaries. 

The assistance is intended to help with the marriage expenses of women from economically disadvantaged backgrounds. 

Eligibility is limited to women who are residents of West Bengal. 

Beneficiaries must belong to families with an annual income of less than INR 1.5 lakh. 

The applicant must be at least 18 years old at the time of marriage. 

The application process involves filling out a form available at designated government offices. 

Documents required include proof of identity and income certificate. 

The scheme is implemented by the Department of Women and Child Development and Social Welfare, West Bengal. 

The financial assistance is disbursed after the marriage is registered. 

The scheme aims to empower women and reduce the financial burden on families during marriage.
