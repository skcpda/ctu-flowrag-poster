The Rajiv Gandhi Scheme for Empowerment of Adolescent Girls is known as SABLA. 

It was launched in 2010 by the Ministry of Women and Child Development. 

The scheme aims to empower adolescent girls aged 11 to 18 years. 

It focuses on improving their nutritional and health status. 

The scheme also aims to provide education and life skills. 

It seeks to promote awareness about health, hygiene, and nutrition. 

The benefits include supplementary nutrition, health check-ups, and education. 

Adolescent girls receive vocational training and life skills education. 

The scheme also provides financial assistance for self-employment. 

Eligibility is limited to girls aged 11 to 18 years. 

They must belong to families below the poverty line. 

The application process typically involves registration at local Anganwadi centers. 

The scheme is implemented through the Integrated Child Development Services. 

State governments and local bodies are responsible for execution. 

The frequency of benefits is monthly for nutrition and periodic for training sessions. 

Additional details may include community participation and awareness campaigns.
