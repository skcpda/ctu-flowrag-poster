The PFS-HSFDC scheme is an initiative by the Government of India. 

It aims to provide financial assistance to the Handicraft and Handloom sector. 

The objective is to promote the welfare of artisans and weavers. 

The scheme seeks to enhance their livelihood opportunities. 

Benefits include financial support for skill development and marketing. 

Assistance is provided in the form of loans and grants. 

Eligibility is primarily for artisans and weavers involved in handicrafts and handlooms. 

The application process involves submitting a proposal to the relevant authorities. 

The timeline for processing applications varies based on the specific request. 

Implementing authorities include the Handicrafts and Handloom Development Corporation. 

Additional details on specific funding amounts and project types may be available through official channels.
