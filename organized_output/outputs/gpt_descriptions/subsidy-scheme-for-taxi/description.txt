The Subsidy Scheme for Taxi aims to provide financial assistance to taxi operators in India. 

The objective is to promote the taxi industry and support drivers in acquiring vehicles. 

The scheme offers subsidies on the purchase of new taxis. 

Financial assistance is provided to eligible taxi operators to reduce the burden of vehicle costs. 

Eligibility criteria typically include being a registered taxi operator or driver. 

Applicants must possess a valid driving license and meet the age requirements set by the scheme. 

The application process involves submitting an application form along with required documents to the designated authority. 

Documents may include proof of identity and registration as a taxi operator. 

The timeline for processing applications may vary based on the implementing agency. 

The scheme is implemented by state transport departments or relevant government agencies. 

Additional details may include specific subsidy amounts and vehicle specifications.
