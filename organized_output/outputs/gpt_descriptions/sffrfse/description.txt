The SFFRFSE stands for the Scheme for Financial Assistance for the Rehabilitation of Families of Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes. 

This scheme was launched to provide financial assistance to families belonging to Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes who are affected by various socio-economic challenges. 

The primary objective is to rehabilitate these families and improve their living conditions. 

The benefits include financial assistance for education health and livelihood support. 

Eligible beneficiaries are families belonging to Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes who are facing economic hardships. 

The application process typically involves submitting a form along with necessary documents to the designated authority in the respective state or union territory. 

The timeline for processing applications may vary by region but is generally aimed to be completed within a few months. 

Implementing authorities include the Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment and respective state government departments. 

The scheme aims to empower marginalized communities and promote their socio-economic development.
