The National Food Security Mission (NFSM) is an initiative by the Government of India. 

It was launched in 2007 to enhance the production of food grains. 

The primary objective is to ensure food security for the population. 

The mission aims to increase the production of rice, wheat, and pulses. 

It also focuses on improving the productivity of these crops. 

The benefits include increased availability of food grains for the public. 

Farmers receive assistance in the form of seeds, fertilizers, and technology. 

Financial support is provided for various agricultural practices. 

Eligibility criteria include farmers engaged in food grain production. 

The application process involves registration with local agricultural departments. 

Farmers must provide necessary documentation to avail of the benefits. 

The mission operates continuously with annual reviews and updates. 

The Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers' Welfare is the implementing authority. 

State governments also play a crucial role in execution. 

The scheme is part of a broader strategy to achieve food security in India. 

It supports sustainable agricultural practices and enhances farmer incomes.
