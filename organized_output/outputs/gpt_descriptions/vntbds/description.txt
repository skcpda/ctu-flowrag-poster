The Vantangia Nodal Tribal Development Scheme (VNTBDS) aims to support the development of tribal communities in India. 

The objective of the scheme is to enhance the socio-economic conditions of tribal populations. 

The benefits include financial assistance for various development projects and initiatives aimed at improving livelihoods. 

Eligibility criteria typically include being a member of a recognized tribal community. 

The application process involves submitting an application to the designated authorities in the respective states. 

The timeline for project implementation may vary based on the specific initiatives undertaken under the scheme. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Tribal Affairs in collaboration with state governments.
