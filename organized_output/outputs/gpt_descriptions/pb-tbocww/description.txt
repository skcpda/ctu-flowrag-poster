The scheme PB-TBOCWW stands for the Punjab Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board. 

It is designed to provide welfare benefits to construction workers in Punjab. 

The main objective is to ensure the social security and welfare of construction workers. 

Benefits include financial assistance for medical treatment, education, and housing. 

Workers can also receive assistance for marriage and funeral expenses. 

Eligibility is limited to registered construction workers in the state of Punjab. 

Workers must have a valid registration with the Punjab Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board. 

The application process involves submitting an application form along with required documents to the local office of the Board. 

The timeline for processing applications may vary based on the type of assistance requested. 

The Punjab Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board is the implementing authority for this scheme. 

Additional details about specific benefits and application requirements can be found on the official website of the Board.
