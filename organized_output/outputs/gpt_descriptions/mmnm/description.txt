The Mukhyamantri Mukhya Mantri Nari Samman Nidhi (MMNM) scheme is an initiative by the Government of Madhya Pradesh. 

It aims to empower women by providing them with financial assistance. 

The objective is to support women in their economic activities and enhance their social status. 

Under this scheme, eligible women receive a monthly financial aid of INR 1,000. 

This assistance is intended to help women meet their basic needs and improve their quality of life. 

Eligibility is generally limited to women who are residents of Madhya Pradesh and belong to economically weaker sections. 

The application process involves submitting a form along with necessary documents at designated government offices. 

The scheme is implemented by the Department of Women and Child Development, Madhya Pradesh. 

The financial assistance is provided on a monthly basis. 

The scheme is part of the state's broader efforts to promote gender equality and women's empowerment.
