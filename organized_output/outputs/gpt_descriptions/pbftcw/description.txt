The Pradhan Mantri Bhartiya Janaushadhi Pariyojana (PMBJP) aims to provide quality medicines at affordable prices. 

It seeks to reduce out-of-pocket expenditure on healthcare for the economically weaker sections. 

The scheme promotes the use of generic medicines which are equivalent in quality to branded medicines. 

Benefits include access to over 1,500 medicines and 240 surgical items at reduced prices. 

The scheme also provides financial assistance to establish Janaushadhi Kendras. 

Eligibility to open a Janaushadhi Kendra includes being a pharmacy owner or a registered pharmacist. 

The application process involves submitting an online application through the official website. 

The timeline for approval may vary based on the completeness of the application. 

The Department of Pharmaceuticals under the Ministry of Chemicals and Fertilizers implements the scheme. 

The scheme is operational across all states and Union Territories in India. 

Regular monitoring and evaluation are conducted to ensure the effectiveness of the scheme.
