The Pradhan Mantri Suraksha Bima Yojana (PMSBY) is a government welfare scheme launched in 2015. 

It aims to provide accidental death and disability insurance to individuals. 

The objective is to offer financial support to families in case of accidental death or disability. 

The scheme provides a coverage of INR 2 lakh for accidental death and permanent total disability. 

It also offers INR 1 lakh for permanent partial disability. 

The premium for the scheme is INR 12 per annum. 

Eligibility includes individuals aged between 18 to 70 years. 

Applicants must have a savings bank account. 

The application process involves enrolling through the bank where the individual holds an account. 

The enrollment can be done online or offline during the stipulated period. 

The scheme is available for renewal on an annual basis. 

The implementing authority for this scheme is the Ministry of Finance. 

Participating banks and insurance companies facilitate the enrollment and claims process.
