The scheme is known as the Scheme for the Management of School of Excellence and Model Schools in Odisha (SMSOPM). 

It was launched by the Government of Odisha to enhance the quality of education in schools. 

The main objective is to establish Model Schools and Schools of Excellence to provide high-quality education. 

The scheme aims to improve infrastructure and provide better teaching-learning resources. 

It also focuses on capacity building of teachers and staff. 

Benefits include improved educational outcomes for students. 

Students will have access to better facilities and resources. 

Eligibility criteria include schools that are recognized by the state government. 

The application process involves submission of proposals by schools to the education department. 

The timeline for implementation is determined by the education department based on the proposals received. 

The implementing authority is the Department of School and Mass Education, Government of Odisha. 

Additional details may include specific guidelines for the selection of schools and funding mechanisms.
