The AMI scheme refers to the Ayushman Bharat - Pradhan Mantri Jan Arogya Yojana.

This scheme was launched by the Government of India on September 23, 2018.

It aims to provide health insurance coverage to economically vulnerable populations.

The scheme is part of the larger Ayushman Bharat initiative.

Ayushman Bharat is a flagship program of the Government of India.

The program aims to provide accessible and affordable healthcare services to all citizens.

The primary objective of the AMI scheme is to reduce the financial burden on families.

It seeks to ensure that no one is deprived of necessary medical treatment due to financial constraints.

The AMI scheme provides health insurance coverage of up to INR 5 lakh per family per year.

This coverage is for secondary and tertiary hospitalization expenses.

The scheme covers over 10 crore families in India.

This translates to approximately 50 crore individuals.

The benefits of the AMI scheme include cashless treatment.

Beneficiaries can access treatment in public and private hospitals.

The scheme covers a wide range of medical procedures.

These include surgeries diagnostics and other essential healthcare services.

The AMI scheme also includes pre and post-hospitalization expenses.

This ensures comprehensive coverage for beneficiaries.

Eligibility for the AMI scheme is primarily based on socio-economic status.

The scheme targets families identified through the SECC 2011 data.

These families typically belong to the lower socio-economic strata.

The scheme is also open to specific vulnerable groups.

These include Scheduled Castes Scheduled Tribes and other marginalized communities.

The application process for the AMI scheme is straightforward.

Eligible families can apply through the official website or designated centers.

They need to provide necessary identification and documentation.

The application can also be initiated through the Ayushman Bharat mobile app.

Once the application is submitted it undergoes verification.

Eligible families receive an Ayushman card.

This card is essential for availing of benefits under the scheme.

The timeline for processing applications may vary.

However the government aims to ensure quick processing.

The AMI scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Health and Family Welfare.

State governments also play a crucial role in implementation.

They are responsible for the operationalization of the scheme at the state level.

The scheme is funded through a mix of central and state government contributions.
