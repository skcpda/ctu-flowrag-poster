The Scheme for Financial Assistance for Children of Deceased Workers (SFACD) is designed to support children who have lost their parents due to work-related incidents. 

The primary objective of the scheme is to provide financial assistance to ensure the education and welfare of children who are orphaned due to the death of their earning parent. 

The benefits include a monthly stipend for the children to support their educational needs. 

Eligibility criteria include being a child of a worker who has died in an accident while on duty. 

The application process typically involves submitting a form along with necessary documents such as the death certificate of the parent and proof of the child's age. 

The timeline for processing applications may vary but is generally aimed at being completed within a few months. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Labour and Employment along with respective state governments. 

Additional details may include specific financial amounts allocated per child and the duration of assistance, which may vary by state.
