The Scheme for Adolescent Girls (SAG) is a government welfare initiative aimed at improving the nutritional and health status of adolescent girls. 

The objective of the scheme is to empower adolescent girls by providing them with essential services and support. 

The benefits include nutritional support through take-home rations and health education. 

Assistance also includes life skills education and vocational training. 

Eligibility is primarily focused on girls aged 11 to 18 years. 

The application process typically involves registration through local Anganwadi centers. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Women and Child Development. 

The program is operational across various states in India. 

The frequency of assistance is generally monthly based on the local implementation schedule. 

Additional details include a focus on reducing dropout rates among girls and promoting their overall development.
