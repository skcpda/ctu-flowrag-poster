The FAFT-CWSB scheme is an initiative launched by the Government of India. 

The full form of FAFT-CWSB is the Financial Assistance for Families of Transgender Persons and Children with Special Needs. 

This scheme aims to provide financial support to marginalized communities. 

It specifically targets transgender individuals and families with children who have special needs.

The scheme was introduced to alleviate some of these challenges.
