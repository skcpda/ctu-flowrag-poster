The SJMMSVY stands for the Swami Vivekananda Sarathi Yojana. 

This scheme was launched by the Government of Madhya Pradesh. 

It aims to provide financial assistance to the youth for self-employment. 

The objective is to empower young individuals to start their own businesses. 

The scheme offers a subsidy on loans taken for self-employment ventures. 

Eligible beneficiaries can receive financial support to cover a portion of their project costs. 

The assistance is primarily targeted at youth aged between 18 and 35 years. 

Applicants must be residents of Madhya Pradesh. 

They should have a viable business plan to qualify for the scheme. 

The application process involves submitting a detailed project report to the concerned authorities. 

Applications can be submitted online through the official portal or at designated offices. 

The scheme is implemented by the Department of Skill Development and Entrepreneurship. 

Financial assistance is provided in the form of a subsidy on loans from banks. 

The frequency of assistance is determined based on the project and loan approval timelines. 

The scheme aims to promote entrepreneurship among the youth in the state. 

It also seeks to reduce unemployment by encouraging self-employment opportunities.
