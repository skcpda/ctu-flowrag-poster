The National Health Insurance Scheme (NHIS) is an initiative by the Government of India. 

It aims to provide accessible and affordable healthcare services to the underprivileged sections of society. 

The objectives include reducing out-of-pocket expenses for healthcare and improving health outcomes for low-income families. 

Benefits under the scheme include coverage for hospitalization expenses and pre-existing diseases. 

Assistance is provided in the form of cashless treatment at empaneled hospitals. 

Eligibility is primarily targeted at families below the poverty line and those without any health insurance coverage. 

The application process typically involves submitting an application form along with necessary documents to the designated authority. 

The timeline for processing applications may vary based on the implementing agency. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Health and Family Welfare in collaboration with state governments. 

Additional details regarding specific coverage limits and empaneled hospitals may vary by state.
