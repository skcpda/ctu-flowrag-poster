The YSR Pelli Kanuka Scheme is a welfare initiative by the Government of Andhra Pradesh. 

It aims to provide financial assistance for the marriage of girls from economically weaker sections. 

The objective is to reduce the financial burden on families for marriage expenses. 

Under this scheme, a financial assistance of INR 1 lakh is provided for the marriage of eligible girls. 

The assistance is directly transferred to the bank account of the beneficiary. 

Eligibility criteria include being a resident of Andhra Pradesh and belonging to a Below Poverty Line family. 

The girl must be at least 18 years old at the time of marriage. 

The application process involves submitting an application form along with necessary documents. 

Documents required may include proof of age and income certificate. 

The application can be submitted online through the official website or at designated offices. 

The scheme is implemented by the Department of Women Development and Child Welfare, Government of Andhra Pradesh. 

The assistance is provided once per eligible girl. 

The scheme is part of the broader initiatives to empower women in the state. 

It aims to promote social welfare and improve the status of women in society.
