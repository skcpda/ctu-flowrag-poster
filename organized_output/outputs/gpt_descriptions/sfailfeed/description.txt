The SFAILFEED scheme is designed to provide financial assistance to farmers. 

The scheme aims to ensure timely availability of quality feed to livestock. 

It supports the livestock sector by enhancing productivity and income for farmers. 

Eligible beneficiaries include farmers engaged in livestock rearing. 

Farmers must be registered with the relevant agricultural department. 

The application process involves submitting forms to local agricultural offices. 

The scheme is implemented by the Department of Animal Husbandry and Dairying. 

Financial assistance is provided based on the quantity of feed purchased. 

The assistance is disbursed directly to the beneficiaries' bank accounts. 

The scheme operates on a continuous basis throughout the year. 

Regular monitoring is conducted to assess the impact on livestock productivity. 

The scheme contributes to the overall development of the dairy and livestock sector.
