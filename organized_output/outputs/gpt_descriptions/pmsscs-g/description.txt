The Pradhan Mantri Samagra Shiksha Abhiyan (PMSSS) is a government initiative aimed at improving the quality of education in India. 

The scheme focuses on providing holistic education from pre-school to senior secondary levels. 

Its objectives include enhancing access to quality education and promoting equity in education. 

The scheme aims to improve learning outcomes through various interventions. 

Benefits include financial assistance for infrastructure development and teacher training. 

Students can receive scholarships under this scheme to encourage higher education. 

Eligibility criteria vary based on the specific component of the scheme. 

Generally, schools and educational institutions must be recognized by the appropriate authorities. 

The application process involves submitting proposals for funding and assistance through the designated online portal. 

The timeline for funding and implementation is typically aligned with the academic year. 

The Ministry of Education is the primary implementing authority for this scheme. 

State governments and local bodies also play a crucial role in execution and monitoring.
