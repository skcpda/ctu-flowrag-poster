The STEU scheme stands for the Skill Training for Employment in Urban Areas. 

It aims to enhance the employability of urban youth through skill development. 

The objective is to provide training in various sectors to facilitate better job opportunities. 

The scheme offers skill training programs that are aligned with industry requirements. 

Benefits include improved job prospects and increased income for participants. 

Eligibility criteria include urban youth aged between 18 to 35 years. 

The application process typically involves online registration through the designated portal. 

Training programs are conducted by recognized training partners. 

The frequency of training sessions may vary based on demand and availability. 

The Ministry of Skill Development and Entrepreneurship is the implementing authority for this scheme. 

Additional details regarding specific training modules and locations may be available on official platforms.
