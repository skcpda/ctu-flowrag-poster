The RV scheme refers to the "Rural Livelihoods Mission" in India. 

It aims to reduce poverty by promoting self-employment and organization of rural poor into self-help groups. 

The objectives include enhancing the livelihood opportunities for rural households and promoting sustainable livelihoods. 

Benefits include skill development training financial assistance and access to credit. 

Eligibility criteria typically include being a member of a rural household and belonging to a marginalized community. 

The application process usually involves submitting an application to the local implementing agency or block office. 

The scheme operates on a continuous basis with no specific timeline for applications. 

Implementing authorities include the Ministry of Rural Development and various state governments. 

The scheme also emphasizes capacity building and financial inclusion through various programs.
