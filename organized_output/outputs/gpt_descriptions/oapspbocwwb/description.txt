The Old Age Pension Scheme for the Poor and the Backward Classes (OAPSPBOCWWB) is a welfare initiative by the Government of India. 

It aims to provide financial assistance to elderly individuals from economically weaker sections. 

The objective is to ensure a basic standard of living for senior citizens. 

The scheme offers a monthly pension to eligible beneficiaries. 

The amount of assistance varies based on state policies. 

Eligibility is generally limited to senior citizens from below poverty line households. 

Applicants must also belong to backward classes. 

The application process typically involves submitting a form along with necessary documents to the local government office. 

The pension is disbursed monthly. 

The implementing authority is usually the state government or local bodies. 

Specific details may vary by state.
