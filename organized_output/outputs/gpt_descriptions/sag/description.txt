The SAG scheme stands for the Swayam Siddha Scheme. 

It was launched by the Ministry of Women and Child Development. 

The scheme aims to empower women through skill development and entrepreneurship. 

The objective is to enhance the socio-economic status of women. 

It provides training in various skills to enable women to become self-reliant. 

The benefits include financial assistance for starting small businesses. 

Women are also provided with access to credit and market linkages. 

Eligibility is primarily for women aged 18 years and above. 

Women from marginalized and economically weaker sections are given priority. 

The application process involves submitting an application to the local implementing agency. 

The timeline for the training programs varies based on the skill being taught. 

The implementing authorities include the Ministry of Women and Child Development and state governments. 

The scheme also promotes self-help groups for women. 

It encourages women to participate in decision-making processes. 

The SAG scheme is part of the broader initiative for women's empowerment in India.
