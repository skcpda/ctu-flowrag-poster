The Atal Community Innovation Centre Scheme is an initiative launched by the Government of India. 

This scheme aims to promote innovation and entrepreneurship among the youth. 

It is part of the larger Atal Innovation Mission. 

The Atal Innovation Mission was established by the NITI Aayog. 

NITI Aayog is the policy think tank of the Government of India. 

The Atal Community Innovation Centre Scheme focuses on fostering a culture of innovation. 

It seeks to create an ecosystem that encourages community-based solutions. 

The scheme targets underserved communities. 

It aims to empower these communities through innovation. 

The primary objective is to promote sustainable development.

The benefits of the Atal Community Innovation Centre Scheme are multifaceted.

The application process for the scheme is structured and systematic.

Once submitted, the proposals are reviewed by a selection committee. 

This committee evaluates the feasibility and impact of the proposed project. 

Successful applicants receive funding to establish their innovation centers.
