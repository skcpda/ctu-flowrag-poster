The Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana - Rural (PMAY-R) is a government welfare scheme aimed at providing housing for all in rural areas. 

The scheme was launched in 2016 under the Ministry of Rural Development. 

Its objective is to provide affordable housing to the rural poor. 

The benefits include financial assistance for the construction of houses. 

Eligible beneficiaries receive a subsidy on interest rates for housing loans. 

The scheme targets the economically weaker sections and low-income groups in rural areas. 

Eligibility criteria include being a resident of rural areas and belonging to the economically weaker section. 

The applicant must not own a pucca house in their name or in the name of any family member. 

The application process involves submitting an application to the local Gram Panchayat. 

The timeline for house construction varies based on individual circumstances and local conditions. 

The implementing authority for the scheme is the Ministry of Rural Development. 

State governments and local bodies also play a role in implementation. 

The scheme is part of the larger initiative to achieve "Housing for All" by 2022. 

Beneficiaries are selected based on a survey conducted by the local authorities. 

The scheme also promotes the use of eco-friendly and sustainable building materials.
