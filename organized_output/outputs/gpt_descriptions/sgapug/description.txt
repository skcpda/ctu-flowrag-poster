The scheme SGAPUG stands for the Special Grant Assistance for Persons with Disabilities in Urban Areas. 

It aims to provide financial assistance to persons with disabilities living in urban areas. 

The primary objective is to enhance the quality of life and promote the independence of individuals with disabilities. 

Benefits include financial support for various needs such as education healthcare and vocational training. 

Eligibility is primarily for persons with disabilities residing in urban areas. 

The application process involves submitting a form along with necessary documents to the designated authority. 

The timeline for processing applications may vary based on the implementing agency. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment. 

Additional details regarding the specific amount of assistance or the frequency of grants may not be provided.
