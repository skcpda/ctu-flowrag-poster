The Mukhyamantri Bhavantar Bhugtan Yojana (MBBSY) is a welfare scheme initiated by the Government of Madhya Pradesh. 

It aims to provide financial support to farmers by ensuring that they receive a minimum support price for their agricultural produce. 

The main objective is to reduce the distress faced by farmers due to fluctuations in market prices. 

Under this scheme farmers are compensated for the difference between the minimum support price and the actual selling price of their crops. 

Eligible beneficiaries include small and marginal farmers who cultivate specific crops as defined by the scheme. 

Farmers must be registered under the scheme to avail of the benefits. 

The application process involves farmers submitting their details along with the required documents to the concerned authorities. 

The scheme is implemented by the Department of Agriculture of Madhya Pradesh. 

The timeline for the disbursement of benefits is generally aligned with the harvesting and selling periods of the crops. 

The scheme is reviewed periodically to ensure its effectiveness and to make necessary adjustments.
