The Mukhyamantri Nari Shakti Yojana (MNSDSY) is a welfare scheme launched by the Government of Madhya Pradesh. 

The scheme aims to empower women and promote their welfare in the state. 

It focuses on enhancing women's participation in various sectors and improving their socio-economic status. 

The benefits include financial assistance for women entrepreneurs and support for skill development programs. 

Eligible beneficiaries are women residents of Madhya Pradesh. 

The application process involves submitting an online application through the official government portal. 

The scheme is implemented by the Department of Women and Child Development, Madhya Pradesh. 

The timeline for disbursement of benefits is determined based on the specific program under the scheme. 

Additional details on specific programs under MNSDSY may vary.
