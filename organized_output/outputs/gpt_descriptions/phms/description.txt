The Pradhan Mantri Kisan Mandhan Yojana (PM-KMY) is a government welfare scheme aimed at providing social security to small and marginal farmers. 

The scheme was launched to ensure a pension for farmers after they reach the age of 60 years. 

The objective is to provide financial support to farmers and improve their livelihood security. 

Under this scheme, eligible farmers will receive a monthly pension of Rs. 3000 after they attain the age of 60. 

The scheme also aims to encourage farmers to save for their old age. 

Eligibility for the scheme includes small and marginal farmers who are between the ages of 18 and 40 years. 

Farmers must have landholding of up to 2 hectares to qualify for the scheme. 

The application process involves farmers registering through the Common Service Centers or online portals. 

Farmers need to provide necessary documents such as identity proof and landholding details during registration. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers' Welfare. 

The pension will be disbursed monthly once the farmer reaches the age of 60. 

The scheme also includes provisions for the family of the farmer in case of the farmer's demise. 

The family will receive the pension amount in such cases. 

The scheme is part of the government's efforts to enhance the welfare of the agricultural community in India.
