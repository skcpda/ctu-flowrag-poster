The SIRE scheme stands for the Scheme for Integrated Rehabilitation of the Elderly. 

It was launched by the Government of India to provide support and assistance to elderly individuals. 

The objective of the scheme is to improve the quality of life of senior citizens. 

It aims to provide financial assistance for their basic needs and enhance their social security. 

Benefits include financial aid for maintenance and support for the elderly. 

The scheme also facilitates the establishment of old age homes and other support services. 

Eligibility criteria include being a senior citizen aged 60 years or above. 

The scheme is targeted at those who are in need of financial assistance and social support. 

The application process typically involves submitting a form to the relevant state or district authorities. 

The timeline for processing applications may vary based on the implementing agency. 

The Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment is the primary implementing authority for the scheme. 

State governments may also play a role in the implementation at the local level. 

The scheme is part of a broader effort to address the needs of the elderly population in India.
