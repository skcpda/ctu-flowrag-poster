The Pradhan Mantri National Apprenticeship Mela is an initiative aimed at promoting apprenticeship opportunities in India. 

The objective of the scheme is to enhance employability among youth by providing them with practical training in various sectors. 

The scheme offers benefits such as skill development and job placement opportunities for participants. 

Eligibility criteria include being a citizen of India and being within the age group specified by the implementing agencies. 

The application process typically involves registering online through designated portals or attending local apprenticeship melas. 

The frequency of the apprenticeship melas may vary but they are conducted regularly across different states. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Skill Development and Entrepreneurship along with various state governments and industry partners. 

Additional details may include partnerships with industries to ensure that the training is aligned with market needs.
