The RCBTTSY scheme stands for the Rajasthan Chief Minister's Bhamashah Techno Hub Scheme. 

It was launched to promote entrepreneurship and innovation in the state of Rajasthan. 

The objective is to provide financial assistance and support to startups and small businesses. 

The scheme aims to create a conducive environment for technology-driven enterprises. 

Benefits include financial support for project development and access to mentorship. 

Assistance is provided in the form of grants and subsidies for eligible projects. 

Eligibility criteria include being a registered startup or small business in Rajasthan. 

The applicant must have a viable business plan and innovative technology. 

The application process involves submitting a detailed project proposal online. 

The proposal is evaluated by a committee for approval. 

The timeline for approval can vary based on the completeness of the application. 

The scheme is implemented by the Department of Information Technology and Communication in Rajasthan. 

Additional details may include workshops and training programs for entrepreneurs.
