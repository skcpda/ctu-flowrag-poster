The SEBI IT DIP scheme is an initiative by the Securities and Exchange Board of India. 

It aims to promote innovation and development in the financial technology sector. 

The objective is to support start-ups and entrepreneurs in the fintech space. 

The scheme provides financial assistance and mentorship to eligible entities. 

Benefits include funding for research and development as well as access to regulatory guidance. 

Eligibility is primarily for start-ups and small businesses in the fintech domain. 

The application process involves submitting a proposal to SEBI for evaluation. 

The timeline for funding and support varies based on the project and its requirements. 

The implementing authority for this scheme is the Securities and Exchange Board of India. 

Additional details about specific funding amounts and project criteria may vary.
