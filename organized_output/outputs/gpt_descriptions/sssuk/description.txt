The SSSUK scheme stands for the "Sustainable Livelihoods and Self-Employment for Urban Poor" initiative. 

It aims to provide sustainable livelihood opportunities to urban poor populations. 

The objective is to enhance the income and living standards of the urban poor through skill development and self-employment. 

Benefits include financial assistance for starting small businesses and access to skill development training programs. 

Eligibility is typically limited to individuals from economically weaker sections residing in urban areas. 

The application process generally involves submitting an application form along with relevant documents to the designated local authority. 

The timeline for processing applications may vary based on the local implementing agency. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs at the national level. 

State and local urban development authorities may also play a role in implementation. 

Additional details on specific training programs and financial assistance amounts may vary by region.
