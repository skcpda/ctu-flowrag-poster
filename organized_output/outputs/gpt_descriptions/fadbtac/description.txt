The FADBTAC scheme stands for the Financial Assistance for Development of Backward Tribes and Communities. 

This scheme is part of the broader initiative by the Government of India to uplift and empower marginalized communities. 

The context of this scheme lies in the recognition of the socio-economic disparities faced by backward tribes and communities in India. 

These communities often lack access to basic resources such as education healthcare and employment opportunities. 

The Government of India aims to bridge this gap through targeted financial assistance and development programs. 

The primary objective of the FADBTAC scheme is to provide financial support to backward tribes and communities. 

This support is intended to facilitate their socio-economic development and enhance their quality of life. 

The scheme seeks to empower these communities by providing them with the necessary resources to improve their living conditions. 

Additionally the scheme aims to promote self-sufficiency and economic independence among these communities.
