The SDRFS stands for the State Disaster Response Fund Scheme. 

It was established to provide financial assistance for disaster management and response. 

The objective of the SDRFS is to ensure timely relief and rehabilitation for victims of natural disasters. 

The scheme aims to strengthen the capacity of state governments to respond to disasters effectively. 

Benefits include financial support for immediate relief measures and long-term rehabilitation efforts. 

The assistance is provided to state governments based on the severity and impact of the disaster. 

Eligibility is determined by the occurrence of a notified disaster as per the guidelines of the scheme. 

The application process involves state governments submitting proposals for funding based on disaster assessments. 

The timeline for disbursement of funds is contingent upon the approval of proposals by the National Disaster Management Authority. 

The implementing authority is the Ministry of Home Affairs in collaboration with the National Disaster Management Authority. 

The SDRFS is funded by contributions from the central government and state governments. 

The scheme is reviewed periodically to assess its effectiveness and make necessary adjustments.
