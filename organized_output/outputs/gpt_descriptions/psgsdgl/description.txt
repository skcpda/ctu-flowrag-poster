The Pradhan Mantri Sahaj Bijli Har Ghar Yojana is also known as Saubhagya. 

It was launched in September 2017. 

The scheme aims to provide electricity connections to all households in rural and urban areas. 

The objective is to ensure that every household has access to electricity. 

Benefits include free electricity connections for economically weaker sections. 

The scheme also provides for the installation of transformers and distribution lines. 

Eligibility includes all households that do not have electricity connections. 

The application process involves filling out a form available at local electricity offices. 

The timeline for implementation is ongoing until all households are electrified. 

The Ministry of Power is the implementing authority for this scheme. 

State electricity distribution companies are responsible for execution at the state level.
