The STLs (Skill Training for Livelihood) scheme aims to enhance the employability of youth in India. 

It focuses on providing skill development training to individuals from economically weaker sections. 

The objective is to empower youth with market-relevant skills that can lead to sustainable livelihoods. 

Benefits include access to skill training programs and potential job placements. 

Eligibility criteria typically include being a resident of India and belonging to a specific age group. 

The application process usually involves filling out an online or offline application form. 

The timeline for training programs may vary based on the specific course and training center. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Skill Development and Entrepreneurship. 

Training is often conducted through various recognized training partners across the country.
