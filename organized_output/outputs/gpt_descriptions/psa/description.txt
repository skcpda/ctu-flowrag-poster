The Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana (PMAY) is a flagship housing scheme launched by the Government of India. 

It aims to provide affordable housing to the urban poor and promote housing for all by 2022. 

The objectives include ensuring that every family has a pucca house with basic amenities. 

Benefits include financial assistance for housing construction and the provision of housing loans at subsidized interest rates. 

Eligible beneficiaries include economically weaker sections low-income groups and middle-income groups. 

The application process involves submitting an online application through the PMAY website or visiting designated Common Service Centers. 

The scheme is implemented in a phased manner with ongoing monitoring and evaluation. 

The Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs is the primary implementing authority for PMAY. 

The scheme also encourages the use of sustainable and eco-friendly construction practices. 

It promotes the development of infrastructure and amenities in urban areas. 

The timeline for the scheme is set to achieve the housing target by March 2022.
