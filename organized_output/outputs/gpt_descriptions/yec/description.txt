The Yuva Entrepreneurship Scheme (YES) is an initiative by the Government of India. 

It aims to promote entrepreneurship among the youth. 

The objective is to provide financial assistance and support to young entrepreneurs. 

The scheme offers a credit guarantee for loans taken by youth for starting their businesses. 

Benefits include access to financial support and mentorship for business development. 

Eligibility criteria include being a citizen of India and aged between 18 to 35 years. 

Applicants must have a viable business plan and the ability to repay the loan. 

The application process involves submitting a detailed project report to the designated bank. 

The timeline for loan approval varies based on the bank's processing time. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Skill Development and Entrepreneurship. 

Additional details include the focus on sectors like manufacturing and services. 

The scheme also encourages innovation and technology adoption in businesses.
