The Pradhan Mantri Shram Yogi Maandhan (PM-SYM) scheme is designed to provide social security to workers in the unorganized sector. 

The objective of the scheme is to provide a monthly pension of Rs. 3000 to workers after they reach the age of 60 years. 

The benefits include a pension for the worker and their spouse in case of the worker's demise. 

Eligibility criteria include being an unorganized sector worker aged between 18 and 40 years. 

The applicant must have a monthly income of less than Rs. 15000. 

The application process involves registering online or visiting the nearest Common Service Center. 

The timeline for processing applications is generally immediate upon submission. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Labour and Employment. 

The contribution to the pension fund is based on the age of entry into the scheme. 

The scheme also allows for the option of withdrawing from the scheme under certain conditions.
