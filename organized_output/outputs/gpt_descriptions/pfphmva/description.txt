The Pradhan Mantri Fasal Bima Yojana (PMFBY) is a government welfare scheme aimed at providing insurance coverage to farmers. 

The scheme was launched to support farmers in case of crop failure due to natural calamities. 

The objective is to stabilize the income of farmers and encourage them to adopt modern agricultural practices. 

The benefits include financial assistance to farmers for crop loss due to adverse weather conditions pests and diseases. 

Farmers can receive compensation based on the area and type of crop affected. 

Eligibility is primarily for farmers who have taken loans from financial institutions or are non-loanee farmers. 

Farmers must grow notified crops in the notified areas to be eligible for the scheme. 

The application process involves farmers applying through their respective state governments or financial institutions. 

The timeline for enrollment is typically before the sowing season of the crop. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers' Welfare in collaboration with state governments. 

Premium rates are subsidized by the government to make the scheme affordable for farmers. 

The scheme also includes provisions for timely settlement of claims to ensure farmers receive assistance promptly.
