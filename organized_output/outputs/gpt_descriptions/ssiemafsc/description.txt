The SSIEMAFSC scheme stands for the Small Scale Industries Employment and Market Assistance for Fishermen and Small Craft. 

This scheme is aimed at providing financial assistance and support to small-scale fishermen and those involved in small craft activities. 

The objective of the scheme is to enhance the livelihood opportunities for fishermen and improve their market access. 

The benefits include financial support for purchasing fishing equipment and improving infrastructure related to fishing activities. 

Eligible beneficiaries are small-scale fishermen and individuals involved in small craft activities. 

The application process typically involves submitting an application form along with required documents to the relevant authorities. 

The timeline for processing applications may vary based on the implementing agency. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Fisheries Animal Husbandry and Dairying. 

Additional details on specific funding amounts and project guidelines may be available through official government sources.
