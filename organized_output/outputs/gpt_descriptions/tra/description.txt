The "TRA" scheme refers to the "Transgender Rights and Affirmation" initiative by the Government of India.

This scheme aims to provide social security and welfare measures for transgender persons.

The objectives include ensuring equal rights and opportunities for transgender individuals.

Benefits under the scheme may include financial assistance for education and healthcare.

Transgender persons are eligible for this scheme.

The application process typically involves submitting necessary documents to the designated authorities.

The timeline for processing applications may vary based on the specific requirements of the implementing agency.
