The Manufacturing Skill Sector Council scheme is aimed at enhancing the skill development of the workforce in the manufacturing sector. 

The objective of the scheme is to promote skill development and entrepreneurship in the manufacturing industry. 

The scheme provides training programs to improve the employability of individuals in various manufacturing sectors. 

It offers financial assistance for skill training and capacity building initiatives. 

Eligibility criteria include individuals seeking skill development in manufacturing and those already employed in the sector looking to upgrade their skills. 

The application process typically involves registering on the official skill development portal and applying for specific training programs. 

The timeline for training programs may vary based on the specific course and institution offering the training. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Skill Development and Entrepreneurship along with various training partners and institutions. 

Additional details may include partnerships with industry stakeholders to ensure training aligns with market needs.
