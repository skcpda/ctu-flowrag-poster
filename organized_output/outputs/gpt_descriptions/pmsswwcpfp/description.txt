The Pradhan Mantri Suraksha Maandhan Yojana for Senior Citizens Welfare and Care Fund (PMSSWCPFP) aims to provide social security to senior citizens. 

The objective is to ensure financial security for elderly individuals in the country. 

The scheme offers a monthly pension to eligible senior citizens. 

Eligibility includes individuals aged 60 years and above. 

Applicants must belong to the below-poverty line category. 

The application process involves filling out a prescribed form available at designated centers. 

The pension is disbursed on a monthly basis. 

The Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment is the implementing authority for this scheme. 

Additional details regarding the exact pension amount and specific application centers may vary.
