The Sayantan Ghosh Yojana for Building and Other Construction Workers' Welfare Board (saygbocwwb) is a welfare scheme aimed at providing support to construction workers in India. 

The scheme is designed to improve the living conditions of construction workers and their families. 

It aims to provide financial assistance for various needs such as education, health care, and housing. 

The benefits include financial aid for medical emergencies, educational scholarships for children, and assistance for housing construction. 

Eligibility criteria typically include being a registered construction worker under the Building and Other Construction Workers Act. 

Workers must also meet specific income thresholds and other conditions set by the implementing authority. 

The application process generally involves submitting an application form along with required documents to the local welfare board. 

The timeline for processing applications may vary based on the local authority's efficiency. 

The scheme is implemented by the respective state Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Boards. 

Additional details may include specific benefits that vary by state and updates on new initiatives under the scheme.
