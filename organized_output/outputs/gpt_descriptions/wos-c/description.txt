The Women of Substance (WOS) scheme is an initiative aimed at empowering women in India. 

The scheme focuses on enhancing the socio-economic status of women. 

It aims to provide financial assistance and support to women entrepreneurs. 

The benefits include access to credit and training programs. 

Women are encouraged to start their own businesses. 

Eligibility is primarily for women who are 18 years and above. 

The application process involves submitting an online application through the designated portal. 

The timeline for processing applications varies based on the specific requirements. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Women and Child Development. 

Additional details regarding specific training programs or financial limits may be available on official platforms.
