The Daris scheme is a significant welfare initiative launched by the Government of India. 

This scheme aims to provide financial assistance and support to the weavers and artisans involved in the handloom and handicraft sectors. 

The background of the Daris scheme is rooted in the need to uplift the traditional crafts and promote the livelihoods of artisans who contribute significantly to India's cultural heritage. 

The Indian handloom and handicraft sectors face numerous challenges including competition from machine-made products and a lack of adequate financial support. 

The Daris scheme seeks to address these challenges by providing targeted assistance to artisans and weavers. 

The primary objective of the Daris scheme is to enhance the income and living standards of artisans and weavers. 

It aims to promote the use of traditional weaving techniques and preserve the rich cultural heritage associated with handloom products. 

The scheme also seeks to empower women artisans who play a crucial role in the handloom and handicraft sectors. 

By providing financial assistance the scheme intends to enable artisans to invest in better tools and materials for their craft. 

The benefits of the Daris scheme include direct financial support to artisans and weavers. 

The scheme provides subsidies for the purchase of raw materials and tools necessary for weaving and handicraft production. 

It also includes skill development programs to enhance the capabilities of artisans. 

Artisans can receive training in modern techniques while still preserving traditional methods. 

The scheme aims to create a sustainable livelihood for artisans by increasing their productivity and market access. 

Eligibility for the Daris scheme is primarily focused on artisans and weavers involved in handloom and handicraft production. 

Applicants must be registered under the relevant artisan or weaver categories as defined by the government. 

Priority is often given to women artisans and those from economically weaker sections of society.
