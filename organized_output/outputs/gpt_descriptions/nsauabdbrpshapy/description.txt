The scheme is known as the National Scheme for Agricultural Development and Betterment of Rural Poor through Self-Help Groups and Producer Organizations.

It aims to enhance the livelihood opportunities for rural poor through the formation of self-help groups and producer organizations.

The benefits include financial assistance for setting up self-help groups and support for capacity building and skill development.

Eligibility is primarily for rural poor individuals who are willing to form or join self-help groups.

The application process involves submitting an application to the local implementing agency or department responsible for rural development.

The timeline for assistance may vary based on the specific project and the implementation schedule set by the local authorities.

The implementing authorities include the Ministry of Rural Development and various state government departments focused on rural welfare.

Additional details may include training programs and workshops aimed at empowering rural communities through collective action.
