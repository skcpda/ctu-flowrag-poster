The SA-ADDF scheme stands for the Skill Acquisition and Development for Differently Abled Persons Fund. 

It aims to enhance the employability of differently-abled individuals through skill development. 

The primary objective is to provide financial assistance for skill development programs tailored for persons with disabilities. 

Benefits include funding for training programs and support for self-employment initiatives. 

Eligibility is generally limited to differently-abled individuals seeking skill development opportunities. 

The application process involves submitting a proposal to the relevant authorities detailing the training program and its objectives. 

The timeline for fund disbursement and program implementation may vary based on the specific training initiative. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment. 

Additional details regarding specific training programs and funding limits may be available through official government sources.
