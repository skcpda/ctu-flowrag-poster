The scheme known as Atmanirbhar Bharat Abhiyan is a significant initiative launched by the Government of India. 

This scheme was introduced in response to the economic challenges posed by the COVID-19 pandemic.

The government recognized the need for a comprehensive economic package to support various segments of society.

One of the primary objectives is to boost the economy by promoting local industries. 

The scheme seeks to enhance the production capabilities of small and medium enterprises. 

It also aims to provide financial support to farmers and laborers affected by the pandemic. 

The initiative focuses on creating job opportunities for the youth.
