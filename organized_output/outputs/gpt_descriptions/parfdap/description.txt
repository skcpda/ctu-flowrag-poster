The Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana - Rural (PMAY-R) is a government welfare scheme aimed at providing housing for the rural poor. 

The objective of the scheme is to provide affordable housing to the economically weaker sections of society in rural areas. 

The benefits include financial assistance for the construction of houses and access to basic amenities such as water and sanitation. 

Eligible beneficiaries are those belonging to the economically weaker sections and low-income groups in rural areas. 

The application process involves submitting an application to the local Gram Panchayat or through the official PMAY website. 

The timeline for the disbursement of funds and completion of housing varies based on the project and location. 

The Ministry of Rural Development is the primary implementing authority for this scheme. 

The scheme also aims to promote the use of sustainable and eco-friendly building materials. 

It encourages the participation of local communities in the construction process. 

The scheme is part of the larger initiative to achieve housing for all by 2022.
