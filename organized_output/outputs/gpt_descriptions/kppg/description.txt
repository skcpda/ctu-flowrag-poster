The Kisan Paryatan Pradhan Mantri Gram Yojana (KPPG) is a government initiative aimed at promoting tourism in rural areas of India. 

The scheme was launched to enhance the livelihood of rural communities through the development of rural tourism.

This may include the construction of guest houses and other facilities for tourists.
