The Vambanadu Bhoodan Suraksha Yojana (VBSY) is a welfare scheme initiated by the Government of Kerala. 

It aims to provide financial assistance to landless agricultural laborers and marginal farmers. 

The objective is to ensure land security and promote sustainable livelihoods among the vulnerable sections of society. 

The scheme offers assistance in the form of financial aid for purchasing land. 

Eligible beneficiaries include landless agricultural laborers and marginal farmers who do not own any agricultural land. 

The application process involves submitting a form along with necessary documents to the local revenue office. 

The timeline for processing applications may vary based on local administrative procedures. 

The implementing authority for this scheme is the Department of Revenue in Kerala. 

Additional details regarding the scheme may include provisions for training and capacity building for beneficiaries.
