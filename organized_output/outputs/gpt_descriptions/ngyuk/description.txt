The National Gramin Yuva Udyami Kendra (NGYUK) scheme is an initiative aimed at promoting entrepreneurship among rural youth. 

The objective of the scheme is to provide skill development and financial assistance to young individuals in rural areas. 

The scheme offers training programs and financial support to help youth start their own businesses. 

Benefits include access to skill development training and financial assistance for setting up enterprises. 

Eligibility criteria typically include age limits and residency requirements for rural youth. 

The application process involves submitting a form to the designated authorities along with necessary documentation. 

The timeline for application processing may vary based on the implementing agency. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Rural Development in collaboration with various state governments.
