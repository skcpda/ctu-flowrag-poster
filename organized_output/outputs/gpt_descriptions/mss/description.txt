The MSS scheme stands for the Mahatma Gandhi National Rural Employment Guarantee Act. 

It was launched to provide a legal guarantee for at least 100 days of unskilled wage employment in a financial year to every rural household. 

The main objective is to enhance livelihood security in rural areas. 

The scheme aims to create durable assets and strengthen the livelihood resource base of rural households. 

Benefits include wage employment for unskilled laborers and the creation of infrastructure in rural areas. 

Eligible beneficiaries are adult members of rural households who are willing to do unskilled manual work. 

The application process involves submitting a job card application at the local Gram Panchayat. 

Once registered the beneficiaries can demand work under the scheme. 

The timeline for employment is based on the demand for work and can be availed throughout the year. 

The Ministry of Rural Development is the primary implementing authority for this scheme. 

State governments and local bodies are also involved in the implementation. 

The scheme promotes social inclusion and empowerment of marginalized communities. 

It also aims to reduce rural-urban migration by providing employment in rural areas.
