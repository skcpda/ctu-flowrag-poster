The RKVY-RAFTAAR scheme is an initiative by the Government of India. 

It aims to promote agricultural development in the country. 

The objective is to enhance farmers' income and promote sustainable agriculture. 

The scheme provides financial assistance for various agricultural projects. 

Benefits include support for infrastructure development and technology adoption. 

Farmers and agricultural entrepreneurs are eligible for assistance under this scheme. 

The application process involves submitting project proposals to the state government. 

The proposals are evaluated based on their potential impact on agricultural productivity. 

The scheme operates on an annual basis with funding allocated each financial year. 

The Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers' Welfare is the implementing authority for this scheme. 

State governments play a crucial role in the execution of the scheme at the local level.
