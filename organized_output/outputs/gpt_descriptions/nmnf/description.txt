The National Mission for Clean Ganga (NMCG) is a key initiative under the National Ganga River Basin Authority. 

It aims to rejuvenate the Ganga River and its tributaries through a multi-faceted approach. 

The objectives include reducing pollution levels in the river and promoting sustainable river basin management. 

The mission seeks to enhance biodiversity and improve the quality of life for communities living along the river. 

Benefits include improved water quality and enhanced ecosystem services. 

Financial assistance is provided for various projects aimed at sewage treatment and riverfront development. 

Eligibility criteria vary based on specific project requirements and target beneficiaries. 

The application process typically involves submission of project proposals to the NMCG. 

The timeline for project implementation varies depending on the scale and complexity of the initiative. 

The NMCG operates under the Ministry of Water Resources River Development and Ganga Rejuvenation. 

It collaborates with state governments and local bodies for effective implementation. 

The mission also focuses on public awareness and community participation in river conservation efforts.
