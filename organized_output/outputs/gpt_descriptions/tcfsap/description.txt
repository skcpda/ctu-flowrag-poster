The TCFSAP stands for the Tribal Cooperative Marketing Development Federation of India Limited. 

It aims to promote the socio-economic development of tribal communities in India. 

The primary objective is to provide fair prices for tribal products and enhance their income. 

The scheme focuses on the marketing of tribal products through cooperatives. 

Benefits include access to financial assistance and support for marketing and production. 

Eligibility is primarily for tribal artisans and producers involved in traditional crafts and products. 

The application process involves registration with the cooperative society and submission of necessary documents. 

The timeline for assistance may vary based on the specific project or initiative under the scheme. 

The Ministry of Tribal Affairs is the implementing authority for this scheme. 

Additional details may include capacity building and skill development programs for tribal communities.
