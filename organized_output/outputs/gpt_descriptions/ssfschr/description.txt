The scheme SSFSCHR stands for the Scheme for Support to Senior Citizens for Health Care. 

It is designed to provide financial assistance for health care services to senior citizens. 

The primary objective is to ensure that elderly individuals have access to necessary health care services. 

The benefits include financial support for medical expenses and health care services. 

Eligible beneficiaries are senior citizens aged 60 years and above. 

The application process typically involves submitting a form along with required documents to the designated authorities. 

The timeline for processing applications may vary based on the implementing agency. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment. 

Additional details regarding specific benefits or assistance amounts may vary based on state provisions.
