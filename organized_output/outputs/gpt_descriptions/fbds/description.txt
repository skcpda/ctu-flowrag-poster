The "Fisheries and Aquaculture Development Scheme" also known as FBDS is an important initiative by the Government of India aimed at promoting sustainable fisheries and aquaculture practices across the country.

It also seeks to ensure food security by promoting the consumption of fish.

The policy emphasizes sustainable development and responsible fishing practices. 

The scheme is implemented by the Department of Fisheries under the Ministry of Fisheries Animal Husbandry and Dairying.
