The scheme is known as the Special Assistance for Persons with Disabilities under the National Trust Act.

It was launched to provide financial assistance and support to persons with disabilities.

The primary objective is to enhance the quality of life for individuals with disabilities.

Benefits include financial assistance for various needs such as education healthcare and rehabilitation.

Eligibility is typically restricted to individuals with disabilities as defined under the Rights of Persons with Disabilities Act.

The application process involves submitting an application form along with necessary documents to the designated authority.

The timeline for processing applications may vary based on the specific requirements of the implementing agency.

The scheme is implemented by the National Trust for the Welfare of Persons with Autism Cerebral Palsy Mental Retardation and Multiple Disabilities.

Additional details regarding specific benefits or assistance amounts may vary based on the individual's circumstances.
