The POFUSOP scheme stands for the Public Distribution System for the Urban Poor. 

It aims to provide subsidized food grains to the urban poor in India. 

The objective is to ensure food security for economically weaker sections of urban society. 

The scheme provides essential commodities such as wheat rice and sugar at subsidized rates. 

Eligible beneficiaries include families living below the poverty line in urban areas. 

The application process typically involves submitting an application form along with necessary documents to the local authorities. 

The distribution of food grains under the scheme occurs on a monthly basis. 

The implementing authorities include the Department of Food and Public Distribution at the state and central levels. 

The scheme is part of the larger framework of the National Food Security Act.
