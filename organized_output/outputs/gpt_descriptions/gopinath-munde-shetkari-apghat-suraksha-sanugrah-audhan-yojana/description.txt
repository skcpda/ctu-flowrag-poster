The Gopinath Munde Shetkari Apghat Suraksha Sanugrah Yojana is a welfare scheme initiated by the Government of Maharashtra.

This scheme is designed to provide financial assistance to the families of farmers who have lost their lives due to various reasons.

The scheme is named after Gopinath Munde who was a prominent political leader and a champion of farmers' rights.

The primary objective of this scheme is to ensure that the families of deceased farmers receive adequate financial support during their time of need.

The scheme aims to alleviate the financial burden on these families and help them sustain their livelihoods after the loss of their primary breadwinner.

The assistance provided under this scheme is intended to support the families in meeting their basic needs and expenses.

The scheme recognizes the sacrifices made by farmers and aims to provide them with a safety net.

The financial assistance is provided in the form of a one-time payment to the eligible families.

This payment is intended to help cover immediate expenses such as funeral costs and other related financial burdens.

The scheme is particularly important in the context of Maharashtra where agriculture is a significant part of the economy.

Farmers in Maharashtra often face challenges such as crop failure and debt which can lead to tragic outcomes.

The Gopinath Munde Shetkari Apghat Suraksha Sanugrah Yojana seeks to address these issues by providing timely support.

Eligibility for the scheme is primarily based on the status of the deceased individual as a farmer.

The applicant must be a family member of the deceased farmer to qualify for the assistance.

The scheme is open to all farmers who have been registered under the relevant agricultural departments.

The financial assistance is provided only to those families who can provide necessary documentation proving the farmer's status.

This documentation typically includes proof of farming activities and the farmer's identity.

The application process for the scheme is designed to be straightforward and accessible.

Interested families must submit an application form to the local agricultural office or designated authority.

The application form requires details about the deceased farmer and the applicant's relationship to them.

Supporting documents must also be attached to the application form.

These documents may include identity proof and any other relevant records.

Once the application is submitted it is reviewed by the concerned authorities.

The review process involves verifying the information provided in the application.

If the application is found to be valid the financial assistance is processed.

The timeline for processing applications can vary based on the volume of applications received.

Typically the assistance is disbursed within a few weeks of the application being approved.

The scheme is implemented by the Department of Agriculture in Maharashtra.

This department is responsible for overseeing the welfare of farmers in the state.

The scheme is part of a broader initiative by the Maharashtra government to support farmers.
