The MDA-MEAO scheme stands for the Market Development Assistance for Micro Enterprises in Agriculture and Other Sectors. 

It aims to promote the marketing of products produced by micro enterprises. 

The objective is to enhance the income of micro entrepreneurs by providing them with marketing support. 

The scheme offers financial assistance for marketing activities. 

This includes assistance for participation in trade fairs and exhibitions. 

It also provides support for promotional activities and marketing materials. 

Eligibility is primarily for micro enterprises engaged in agriculture and related sectors. 

The application process involves submitting a proposal to the concerned implementing authority. 

The timeline for assistance is based on the approval of the proposal. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises. 

Additional details regarding specific funding limits or application formats may vary.
