The PVCPPD scheme stands for the Pradhan Mantri Vaya Vandana Yojana. 

It was launched to provide social security to senior citizens. 

The scheme aims to offer a regular income during old age. 

It provides a guaranteed return on investment. 

The benefits include a monthly pension for the subscribers. 

The pension amount can be chosen based on the investment made. 

The scheme is applicable to individuals aged 60 years and above. 

The investment limit is set at a maximum of Rs. 15 lakh. 

The application process involves filling out a form and submitting it to authorized institutions. 

The scheme is implemented by the Life Insurance Corporation of India. 

The pension is paid monthly quarterly half-yearly or annually as per the subscriber's choice. 

The scheme is available for a limited period until a specified date. 

The scheme is designed to ensure financial security for the elderly population.
