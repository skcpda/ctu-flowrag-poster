The FSSDU stands for the Food Safety and Standards Digital Unit. 

It is an initiative launched by the Food Safety and Standards Authority of India. 

The primary objective of the FSSDU is to enhance food safety and standards across the country. 

This initiative aims to digitize the processes related to food safety regulation and compliance. 

The FSSDU is part of a broader effort to improve transparency and efficiency in food safety governance. 

It is designed to facilitate better monitoring and enforcement of food safety standards.

It aims to reduce the time and effort required for food safety assessments.
