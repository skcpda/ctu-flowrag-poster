The scheme known as the Central Sector Scheme for the Promotion of the Handicrafts Sector is often referred to as the "CKLPR" scheme. 

This scheme is designed to support and promote the handicrafts sector in India.

It provides employment opportunities to millions of artisans and craftsmen across the country. 

The sector is known for its diverse range of products that reflect India's rich cultural heritage.

These challenges include lack of access to modern technology and marketing platforms. 

There is also a need for skill development among artisans. 

The CKLPR scheme aims to address these challenges by providing various forms of assistance.

This includes support for skill development and training programs. 

The scheme also provides assistance for marketing and promotion of handicrafts.
