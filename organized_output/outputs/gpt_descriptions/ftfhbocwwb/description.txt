The scheme known as the "Financial Assistance to the Families of the Deceased Workers in the Unorganised Sector" is often referred to by its acronym FTFHBOCWWB.

This scheme is designed to provide financial support to the families of workers who have died while engaged in unorganised sector employment.

The unorganised sector in India encompasses a vast range of occupations that are not regulated by the government.

Workers in this sector often lack job security and access to social security benefits.

The government recognized the need for a safety net for these vulnerable workers and their families.

The primary objective of the FTFHBOCWWB scheme is to offer financial assistance to the dependents of deceased workers.

This assistance aims to alleviate the financial burden that families face after the loss of their primary breadwinner.

The scheme seeks to ensure that families do not fall into poverty due to the sudden loss of income.

The benefits provided under the scheme include a one-time financial assistance amount.

This amount is intended to help the family manage immediate expenses following the death of the worker.

The financial assistance is typically disbursed directly to the bank account of the eligible family member.

This direct transfer method ensures that the funds reach the intended beneficiaries without unnecessary delays.

Eligibility for the FTFHBOCWWB scheme is primarily determined by the employment status of the deceased worker.

The worker must have been engaged in the unorganised sector at the time of their death.

Additionally, the family members applying for assistance must be direct dependents of the deceased worker.

This includes spouses children and other immediate family members.

To apply for the scheme individuals must follow a specific application process.

The application process generally involves filling out a prescribed form.

This form may require details such as the name of the deceased worker their employment details and the relationship of the applicant to the deceased.

Applicants may also need to submit supporting documents.

These documents typically include proof of death such as a death certificate.

Proof of relationship to the deceased worker may also be required.

This authority is usually a local government office or a specific department responsible for welfare schemes.

The timeline for processing applications can vary.

However the government aims to process applications as quickly as possible to provide timely assistance to families in need.

The frequency of assistance under the scheme is generally a one-time payment.
