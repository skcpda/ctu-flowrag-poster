The Satgmsa scheme is an initiative aimed at providing financial assistance to farmers in India. 

The objective of the scheme is to enhance the income of farmers and improve their livelihoods. 

The scheme offers direct cash transfers to eligible farmers to support their agricultural activities. 

Eligible beneficiaries include small and marginal farmers who own agricultural land. 

Farmers must be registered under the Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samman Nidhi scheme to qualify for assistance under Satgmsa. 

The application process involves registration through the official government portal or local agricultural offices. 

The assistance is provided on a periodic basis as determined by the implementing authorities. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers' Welfare.
