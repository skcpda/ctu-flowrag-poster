The SHS scheme stands for the State Health Society scheme. 

It is implemented by the Ministry of Health and Family Welfare. 

The scheme aims to strengthen the healthcare delivery system in the states. 

It focuses on improving access to quality healthcare services. 

The objectives include enhancing healthcare infrastructure and ensuring the availability of essential medicines. 

Benefits include financial assistance for healthcare facilities and support for health programs. 

Eligibility criteria vary by state and may include healthcare providers and institutions. 

The application process generally involves submitting proposals to the respective State Health Society. 

The timeline for approval and funding may vary based on the state’s processes. 

Implementing authorities include the State Health Society and the Ministry of Health and Family Welfare. 

The scheme is part of the broader National Health Mission initiative. 

It aims to promote universal access to healthcare services.
