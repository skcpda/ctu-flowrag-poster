The MRCBSPBOCWWB stands for the Mahatma Gandhi National Rural Employment Guarantee Act for the Construction Workers in the State of Bihar.

This scheme is aimed at providing social security and welfare benefits to construction workers in rural areas.

The objectives include ensuring financial assistance to construction workers during periods of unemployment and providing them with access to various welfare measures.

Benefits include financial assistance for medical emergencies and educational support for children of construction workers.

Eligibility criteria include being a registered construction worker in Bihar and meeting the age requirements set by the scheme.

The application process involves registering with the local labor department and submitting necessary documentation to prove eligibility.

The timeline for assistance is determined based on the specific needs of the workers and the availability of funds.

The implementing authority for this scheme is the Bihar Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board.

Additional details may include provisions for skill development and training programs for construction workers to enhance their employability.
