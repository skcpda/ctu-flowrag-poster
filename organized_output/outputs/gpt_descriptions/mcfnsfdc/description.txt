The MCFNSFDC stands for the Mahatma Gandhi National Rural Employment Guarantee Act Fund for Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes. 

This scheme is designed to provide employment opportunities to marginalized communities in rural areas. 

The primary objective is to enhance livelihood security for Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes. 

The scheme aims to ensure at least 100 days of wage employment in a financial year to every rural household. 

Benefits include financial assistance in the form of wages for unskilled manual work. 

Eligibility is primarily focused on households belonging to Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes. 

The application process typically involves registration under the Mahatma Gandhi National Rural Employment Guarantee Act. 

The timeline for wage payments is generally within 15 days of work completion. 

The Ministry of Rural Development is the implementing authority for this scheme. 

The scheme also emphasizes the creation of durable assets and the enhancement of livelihood security.
