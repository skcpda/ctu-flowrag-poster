The "DS-AandN" scheme refers to the "Direct Benefit Transfer for Andaman and Nicobar Islands" initiative.

This scheme is part of the broader Direct Benefit Transfer program initiated by the Government of India.

The primary objective of the DS-AandN scheme is to ensure that financial assistance reaches the beneficiaries directly.

This is aimed at minimizing leakages and ensuring that the intended recipients receive the benefits without any intermediaries.

The scheme is designed to support various welfare programs and subsidies provided by the government.

It covers a range of sectors including health education and social welfare.

The benefits under the DS-AandN scheme include direct cash transfers to eligible beneficiaries.

These cash transfers are intended to support individuals and families in need.

The scheme aims to empower the economically weaker sections of society.

It also seeks to improve the efficiency of government welfare programs.

Generally the beneficiaries must be residents of the Andaman and Nicobar Islands.
