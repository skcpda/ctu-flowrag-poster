The scheme is known as the Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana - Gramin (PMAY-G) or the Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana - Rural. 

It was launched to provide housing for the rural poor in India. 

The objective is to ensure that every rural household has a pucca house with basic amenities. 

The scheme aims to provide financial assistance for the construction of houses. 

Under PMAY-G, beneficiaries receive a subsidy for building their homes. 

The assistance amount is typically up to INR 1.20 lakh in plain areas and INR 1.30 lakh in hilly areas. 

Eligibility is primarily based on being a member of a Scheduled Caste or Scheduled Tribe or being a below-poverty-line household. 

The applicant must not own a pucca house in their name or in the name of any family member. 

The application process involves submitting an application to the local Gram Panchayat. 

The application must include necessary documents such as identity proof and income details. 

The timeline for disbursement of funds is generally within a few weeks after the application is approved. 

The implementing authorities include the Ministry of Rural Development and state governments. 

The scheme is part of the larger initiative to promote housing for all by 2022. 

Beneficiaries are also encouraged to use eco-friendly materials in construction. 

The scheme promotes the use of local resources and skills for construction. 

Regular monitoring and evaluation are conducted to ensure effective implementation.
