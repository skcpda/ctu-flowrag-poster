The Tribal Sub-Plan (TSP) is a government initiative aimed at the socio-economic development of tribal communities in India. 

The objective of TSP is to ensure that the benefits of development programs reach the tribal population. 

The scheme focuses on providing financial assistance for various development projects in education health and livelihood. 

Eligible beneficiaries include individuals and families belonging to Scheduled Tribes as defined by the government. 

The application process typically involves submitting a proposal to the concerned state government or tribal welfare department. 

The timeline for the implementation of projects under TSP varies based on the specific project and state guidelines. 

The Ministry of Tribal Affairs is the primary implementing authority for the TSP at the central level. 

State governments also play a crucial role in the implementation of TSP initiatives at the local level. 

TSP aims to bridge the gap between tribal and non-tribal populations in terms of development indicators.
