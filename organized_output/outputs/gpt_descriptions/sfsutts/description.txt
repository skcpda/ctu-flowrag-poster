The SFSUTTS scheme stands for the Skill Development for the Scheduled Tribes and Other Vulnerable Sections of Society.

It was launched by the Ministry of Tribal Affairs.

The scheme aims to enhance the employability of Scheduled Tribes and other vulnerable groups.

It focuses on providing skill development training to these communities.

The benefits include vocational training and skill development programs.

Participants may receive financial assistance for training.

Eligibility is primarily targeted at Scheduled Tribes and other vulnerable sections.

The application process involves submitting an application to the concerned authorities.

The timeline for training programs varies based on the specific courses offered.

The implementing authority for this scheme is the Ministry of Tribal Affairs.

The scheme is part of the broader efforts to promote social and economic development among marginalized communities.
