The PM YASASVI Scheme is aimed at providing financial assistance to students from economically weaker sections. 

It is designed to encourage students to pursue higher education in prestigious institutions. 

The main objective is to enhance the quality of education and provide opportunities for meritorious students. 

The scheme offers scholarships to students who qualify through an entrance test. 

Benefits include financial support for tuition fees and other educational expenses. 

Eligibility criteria include being a student of class 9 or class 11 and belonging to a family with an annual income below a specified limit. 

Students must also meet the academic performance standards set by the scheme. 

The application process involves filling out an online application form on the official website. 

Candidates must also register for the entrance test conducted under the scheme. 

The timeline for application submission is typically announced on the official website. 

The scholarship amount and other details are communicated to the selected candidates after the entrance test results. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Education, Government of India. 

Further details regarding the scheme can be found on the official website.
