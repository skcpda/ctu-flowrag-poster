The SDRS scheme stands for the Skill Development and Entrepreneurship Scheme. 

It aims to enhance the employability of youth through skill development initiatives. 

The objective is to provide training and support to individuals for self-employment and entrepreneurship. 

Benefits include financial assistance for training programs and support for starting new ventures. 

Eligibility criteria typically include age limits and educational qualifications which may vary by program. 

The application process generally involves submitting an online application through designated portals. 

The timeline for processing applications and disbursing funds varies depending on the specific program under the scheme. 

Implementing authorities include the Ministry of Skill Development and Entrepreneurship and various state governments. 

The scheme also focuses on promoting entrepreneurship among marginalized sections of society. 

It encourages collaboration with industry partners to ensure relevant skill training. 

Regular monitoring and evaluation are conducted to assess the impact of the scheme.
