The SSBC or Startup India Seed Fund Scheme is an initiative by the Government of India. 

It was launched to provide financial assistance to startups. 

The objective is to support early-stage startups in their growth and development. 

The scheme aims to facilitate the creation of a robust startup ecosystem in India. 

Benefits include financial support of up to INR 5 crores for eligible startups. 

The assistance can be used for product development market entry and commercialization. 

Eligibility criteria include being a registered startup recognized by the Department for Promotion of Industry and Internal Trade. 

Startups must be less than 2 years old and working towards innovation or development of a new product. 

The application process involves submitting an online application through the Startup India portal. 

Startups need to provide details about their business model and funding requirements. 

The timeline for fund disbursement is typically within 30 days after approval. 

The implementing authority for the scheme is the Ministry of Commerce and Industry. 

The scheme is part of the broader Startup India initiative launched in January 2016. 

It aims to foster innovation and entrepreneurship across the country.
