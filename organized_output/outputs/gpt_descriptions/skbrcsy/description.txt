The scheme is known as the Swami Kalyan Babu Rajendra Choudhary Samajik Yojana (SKBRCY). 

It was launched by the Government of Uttar Pradesh. 

The scheme aims to provide financial assistance to the economically weaker sections of society. 

It focuses on the welfare of the marginalized communities. 

The benefits include financial support for education and skill development. 

Eligible beneficiaries can receive assistance for starting small businesses. 

The scheme targets individuals from Scheduled Castes, Scheduled Tribes, and Other Backward Classes. 

Applicants must belong to economically weaker sections. 

The application process involves submitting a form along with necessary documents. 

The forms can be obtained from designated government offices or online portals. 

The timeline for processing applications may vary based on the volume of applications received. 

The implementing authority for this scheme is the Department of Social Welfare, Government of Uttar Pradesh. 

Additional details about specific benefits or assistance amounts may vary.
