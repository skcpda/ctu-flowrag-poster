The SAMSC scheme stands for the Scheme for Assistance to Marginalized Social Communities. 

It aims to provide financial assistance to marginalized social communities in India. 

The objective is to empower these communities through skill development and livelihood opportunities. 

Benefits include financial support for various livelihood activities and skill training programs. 

Eligibility is typically based on being a member of a marginalized social community as defined by the government. 

The application process generally involves submitting an application form along with required documents to the designated authority. 

The timeline for processing applications may vary depending on the implementing agency. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment. 

Additional details may include specific training programs and livelihood activities supported under the scheme.
