The ePY-CSTS scheme is an initiative launched by the Government of India aimed at enhancing the quality of education in the country. 

The full form of ePY-CSTS is Electronic Payment System for Central Sector Scheme of Scholarships for College and University Students. 

This scheme is designed to provide financial assistance to students pursuing higher education in various colleges and universities across India. 

The background of this scheme is rooted in the need to promote higher education among students from economically weaker sections of society. 

The Indian government recognized that financial constraints often hinder students from pursuing their educational aspirations. 

Thus the ePY-CSTS scheme was introduced to alleviate some of these financial burdens. 

The primary objective of the ePY-CSTS scheme is to provide scholarships to deserving students. 

These scholarships aim to support students in their pursuit of higher education. 

The scheme is particularly focused on students from marginalized communities.

The eligibility criteria for the ePY-CSTS scheme are designed to ensure that the benefits reach the intended beneficiaries.
