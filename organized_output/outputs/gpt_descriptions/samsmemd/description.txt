The SAMSMEMD scheme is a government initiative aimed at promoting the development of micro enterprises in India. 

The primary objective of the scheme is to enhance the competitiveness of micro enterprises by providing them with financial support and capacity building. 

The benefits include access to credit for micro enterprises and support for skill development and entrepreneurship training. 

Eligibility is generally limited to micro enterprises registered under the Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises Development Act. 

The application process typically involves submitting an application form along with required documents to the designated authority. 

The timeline for processing applications may vary based on the specific requirements of the implementing agency. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises. 

Additional details regarding specific financial assistance amounts or training programs may vary and should be checked with the implementing agency.
