The scheme known as the FCHDNNASNC stands for the "Financial Assistance for Children of Deceased or Disabled National Awardees Scheme." 

This scheme was introduced by the Government of India to provide financial support to the children of deceased or disabled national awardees. 

The context of this scheme is rooted in the recognition of the sacrifices made by national awardees who have served the nation in various capacities. 

National awardees include individuals who have received prestigious awards for their contributions to the country. 

These awards can include those for bravery, gallantry, and other significant contributions to society. 

The scheme aims to ensure that the families of these awardees are not left in financial distress after the loss or disability of the awardee. 

The primary objective of the FCHDNNASNC is to provide financial assistance to the children of deceased or disabled national awardees. 

This assistance is intended to support their education and overall well-being. 

The government recognizes that the loss of a parent or guardian can have a profound impact on a child's future. 

By providing financial support, the scheme aims to mitigate some of the hardships faced by these children. 

The benefits of the FCHDNNASNC include a direct financial grant to eligible children. 

This financial assistance can be utilized for various purposes including educational expenses.
