The West Bengal Awas Yojana for Backward Classes and Scheduled Castes Development Mission (WBABFSSDM) aims to provide housing assistance to the economically weaker sections of society. 

The primary objective is to improve living conditions for backward classes and scheduled castes in West Bengal. 

The scheme offers financial assistance for the construction of houses. 

Eligible beneficiaries receive a subsidy for housing construction. 

Eligibility is generally limited to individuals from backward classes and scheduled castes who meet specific income criteria.
