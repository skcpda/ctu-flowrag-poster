The SSE40RBU DP scheme is designed to provide financial assistance to small and marginal farmers in India. 

The objective of the scheme is to enhance the income of farmers through various support measures. 

The benefits include direct financial assistance for agricultural activities and support for purchasing seeds and fertilizers. 

Eligibility is primarily based on the landholding size of the farmers. 

Small and marginal farmers are the primary beneficiaries of this scheme. 

The application process typically involves submitting relevant documents to the local agricultural department. 

The timeline for disbursement of funds is generally aligned with the agricultural calendar. 

The implementing authority for this scheme is the Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers' Welfare. 

Additional details regarding specific financial amounts or application deadlines may vary by state.
