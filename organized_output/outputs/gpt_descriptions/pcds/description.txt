The Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samman Nidhi (PM-KISAN) scheme is designed to provide financial support to farmers in India. 

The objective of the scheme is to supplement the financial needs of small and marginal farmers. 

Under this scheme eligible farmers receive direct cash transfers of Rs 6000 per year. 

The amount is disbursed in three equal installments of Rs 2000 each. 

Eligibility is limited to small and marginal farmers who own cultivable land. 

Farmers must have a valid landholding and must not be in the exclusion criteria. 

The application process involves registration through the state government portal or local revenue offices. 

Farmers need to provide necessary documents such as land records and identification proof. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers' Welfare. 

Payments are made directly to the bank accounts of the beneficiaries. 

The scheme was launched in February 2019. 

It is an ongoing scheme with continuous enrollment and disbursement. 

The scheme aims to enhance the income security of farmers and improve their livelihoods.
