The SPara scheme is an initiative aimed at providing support to small and marginal farmers in India. 

The primary objective of the SPara scheme is to enhance the income of farmers through sustainable agricultural practices. 

The scheme offers financial assistance for various agricultural activities and promotes the use of modern technology in farming. 

Eligible beneficiaries include small and marginal farmers who own land and are engaged in agricultural activities. 

Farmers can apply for the scheme through the official government portal or designated agricultural offices. 

The application process typically involves submitting required documents such as land ownership proof and identity verification. 

The timeline for processing applications may vary based on the implementing agency's efficiency. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers' Welfare along with state agricultural departments. 

Additional details regarding specific benefits and assistance may vary by state and local context.
