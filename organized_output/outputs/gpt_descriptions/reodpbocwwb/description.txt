The Reimbursement of Expenses for the Development of Persons with Disabilities (REODPBOCWWB) is a welfare scheme initiated by the Government of India. 

This scheme aims to provide financial assistance for the development and rehabilitation of persons with disabilities. 

The primary objective is to enhance the quality of life and promote the inclusion of persons with disabilities in society. 

Benefits include reimbursement of expenses incurred for various developmental activities and services related to disability. 

Eligibility is generally defined for persons with disabilities as per the Rights of Persons with Disabilities Act 2016. 

The application process typically involves submitting a request along with necessary documentation to the designated authority. 

The timeline for processing applications and disbursing funds may vary based on the implementing agency. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment. 

Additional details on specific benefits and reimbursement limits may be available through official government sources.
