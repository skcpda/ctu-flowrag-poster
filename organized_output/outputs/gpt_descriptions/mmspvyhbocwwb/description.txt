The scheme is known as the Mukhyamantri Mahila Samriddhi Yojana for the Benefit of Women and Children in the Welfare of Backward Classes.

It aims to empower women and improve the socio-economic status of families from backward classes.

The objectives include providing financial assistance to women for self-employment and enhancing their skills.

Benefits include financial support for starting small businesses and vocational training programs.

Eligibility criteria generally include being a woman from a backward class and meeting specific income thresholds.

The application process typically involves submitting an application form along with necessary documents to the designated authority.

The timeline for disbursement of funds and assistance may vary based on the specific program under the scheme.

Implementing authorities include the Department of Women and Child Development and local government bodies.

Additional details may include awareness programs to educate beneficiaries about the scheme and its benefits.
