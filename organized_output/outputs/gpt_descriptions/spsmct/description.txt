The scheme is known as the Samagra Punarvasan Samarthya Manch (SPSMCT). 

It aims to provide comprehensive rehabilitation and support for marginalized communities. 

The objective is to empower individuals through skill development and livelihood opportunities. 

Benefits include financial assistance for training and support for self-employment initiatives. 

Eligibility criteria typically include being part of marginalized or underprivileged communities. 

The application process involves submitting a form along with necessary documentation to the designated authority. 

The timeline for processing applications may vary based on the implementing agency. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment. 

Additional details regarding specific benefits or support services may vary by region.
