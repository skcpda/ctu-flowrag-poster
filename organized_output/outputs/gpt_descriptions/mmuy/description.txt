The Mukhyamantri Mukhya Mantri Udyog Yojana (MMUY) is a state-level initiative aimed at promoting entrepreneurship in various sectors. 

The objective of MMUY is to provide financial assistance and support to small and medium enterprises. 

The scheme aims to create job opportunities and boost the local economy. 

Benefits include financial aid for setting up new businesses and subsidies on equipment purchases. 

Assistance may also cover training programs for entrepreneurs. 

Eligibility criteria typically include being a resident of the respective state and having a viable business plan. 

The application process generally involves submitting an online application along with necessary documents. 

The timeline for application processing may vary by state. 

Implementing authorities include the respective state government departments responsible for industry and commerce. 

Additional details may include specific sectors targeted by the scheme and any caps on financial assistance.
