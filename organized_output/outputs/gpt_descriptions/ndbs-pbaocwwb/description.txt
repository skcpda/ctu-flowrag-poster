The National Database for Unorganised Workers (NDUW) is a scheme aimed at providing social security benefits to unorganised workers in India. 

The objective is to create a comprehensive database of unorganised workers to facilitate the delivery of various welfare schemes and benefits. 

The benefits include access to social security schemes such as health insurance and pension schemes. 

Eligibility is primarily for unorganised workers across various sectors who do not have formal employment contracts. 

The application process involves registration on the NDUW portal where workers can provide their details for inclusion in the database. 

The timeline for registration is ongoing as the database is continuously updated. 

The Ministry of Labour and Employment is the implementing authority for this scheme. 

Additional details include the integration of the database with various government welfare schemes to ensure targeted delivery of benefits.
