The scheme is known as the Swami Vivekananda Yuva Sashaktikaran Yojana.

It is implemented by the Department of Youth Affairs under the Ministry of Youth Affairs and Sports.

The scheme aims to empower youth through skill development and entrepreneurship.

The objective is to provide financial assistance and support to youth for skill development programs.

Benefits include training in various skills and access to financial support for starting businesses.

Eligibility criteria include being a resident of India and falling within the age group of 15 to 29 years.

The application process involves submitting an online application through the official portal.

The scheme is implemented on a continuous basis with no specific end date.

The implementing authorities include the Ministry of Youth Affairs and Sports and various state governments.

The scheme also promotes self-employment and entrepreneurship among the youth.
