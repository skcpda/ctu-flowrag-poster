The Mukhyamantri Mahila Kalyan Yojana (MMKBY) is a welfare scheme initiated by the Government of Madhya Pradesh. 

The scheme aims to empower women by providing them with financial assistance and support for various needs. 

It focuses on enhancing the socio-economic status of women in the state. 

The benefits include financial aid for women's health and education. 

Women can also receive assistance for self-employment and skill development. 

Eligibility is primarily for women residents of Madhya Pradesh. 

Specific criteria may apply based on the type of assistance requested. 

The application process typically involves submitting an application form along with required documents to the designated authorities. 

Applications can be made online or through local government offices. 

The scheme is implemented by the Department of Women and Child Development, Madhya Pradesh. 

The frequency of assistance may vary based on the specific benefits availed. 

Additional details about the scheme can be accessed through official government websites.
