The ResoPops scheme is an initiative aimed at providing financial assistance to individuals in need. 

The context of the scheme is to support vulnerable populations facing economic hardships. 

The main objective is to enhance the livelihood opportunities for the beneficiaries. 

Benefits include direct financial aid to eligible individuals to improve their living conditions. 

Eligibility criteria typically include being a resident of India and belonging to economically weaker sections. 

The application process involves submitting a form along with necessary documentation to the designated authorities. 

The timeline for disbursement of funds is generally specified by the implementing agency.
