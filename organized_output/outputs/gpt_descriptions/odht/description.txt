The One District One Product scheme aims to promote traditional industries and crafts in each district of India. 

It seeks to enhance the production and marketing of unique products from various districts. 

The objective is to create job opportunities and boost local economies. 

The scheme provides financial assistance and support for skill development. 

It also facilitates market access for local products through various initiatives. 

Eligibility criteria typically include small and medium enterprises and artisans in the respective districts. 

The application process usually involves submitting a proposal to the relevant district authority. 

The timeline for approval and assistance may vary based on the district and the nature of the product. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Micro Small and Medium Enterprises. 

Additional details may include collaboration with state governments and local bodies for effective implementation.
