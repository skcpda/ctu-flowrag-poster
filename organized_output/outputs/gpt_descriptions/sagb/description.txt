The SAGB scheme stands for the "Sustainable Agriculture and Green Belt" initiative. 

It aims to promote sustainable agricultural practices and enhance green cover in rural areas. 

The objectives of the scheme include improving agricultural productivity and promoting environmental sustainability. 

Benefits under the SAGB scheme include financial assistance for farmers and support for the development of green belts. 

Eligible beneficiaries are farmers and agricultural workers in rural areas. 

The application process typically involves submitting an application form to the local agricultural department. 

The timeline for assistance may vary based on the specific project and its implementation schedule. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers' Welfare at the central level. 

State agricultural departments may also play a role in the implementation at the state level.
