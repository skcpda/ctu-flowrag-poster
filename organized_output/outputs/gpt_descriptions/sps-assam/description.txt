The SPS Assam scheme is a welfare initiative launched by the Government of Assam. 

It aims to provide financial assistance to the economically weaker sections of society. 

The primary objective is to enhance the livelihood opportunities for marginalized communities. 

The scheme offers direct cash transfers to eligible beneficiaries. 

It focuses on supporting families in need during challenging economic conditions. 

Eligibility criteria include being a resident of Assam and belonging to the economically weaker sections. 

Applicants must provide necessary documentation to prove their eligibility. 

The application process involves submitting forms through designated government offices or online portals. 

The timeline for disbursement of funds is typically aligned with the financial year. 

The scheme is implemented by the Assam State Government through relevant departments.
