The Naospasa Bircarsadu scheme is an initiative by the Government of Assam. 

The scheme aims to provide financial assistance to the families of deceased individuals who were engaged in the business of driving. 

The primary objective is to support the families in overcoming financial hardships after the loss of their earning member. 

Eligible beneficiaries include families of deceased drivers who were registered and had a valid driving license. 

The application process involves submitting a form along with necessary documents such as death certificate and proof of driving license. 

The scheme provides a one-time financial assistance amount to the eligible families. 

The implementing authority for this scheme is the Transport Department of Assam. 

Details regarding the timeline or frequency of assistance are not specified.
