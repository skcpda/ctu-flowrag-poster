The scheme LPT-GBOCWWB stands for the Labourers' Provident Fund - General Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board.

This scheme is designed to provide financial assistance and welfare benefits to construction workers in India.

The context of this scheme arises from the need to support the welfare of workers in the construction sector.

Construction workers often face financial instability due to the nature of their work which is typically temporary and seasonal.

It also seeks to ensure that workers have access to essential services such as healthcare and education.

Workers can also receive benefits for maternity expenses and funeral assistance.

The scheme provides for the establishment of a provident fund for construction workers.

This fund helps workers save for their future and provides them with financial security.
