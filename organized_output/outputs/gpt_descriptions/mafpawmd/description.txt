The MAFPAWMD scheme stands for the Mahatma Gandhi National Rural Employment Guarantee Act (MGNREGA) for the state of Madhya Pradesh. 

This scheme was launched to provide a legal guarantee for at least 100 days of unskilled wage employment in a financial year to every rural household. 

It aims to enhance livelihood security in rural areas. 

The scheme also seeks to create durable assets in rural areas. 

The primary objective is to improve the economic conditions of rural households. 

It aims to empower rural communities by providing them with a source of income. 

The benefits of the scheme include the provision of at least 100 days of wage employment. 

It ensures that wages are paid within a stipulated time frame. 

The scheme also promotes the creation of assets that can benefit the community. 

Eligible beneficiaries include every rural household. 

All adult members of the household can apply for employment under the scheme. 

The application process involves submitting a job card application at the local Gram Panchayat. 

Once the application is approved a job card is issued to the household. 

The job card serves as proof of entitlement to work under the scheme. 

The timeline for employment is based on the demand for work. 

Once a request for work is made it must be provided within 15 days. 

The implementing authority for the scheme is the Ministry of Rural Development. 

State governments are responsible for the execution of the scheme at the local level. 

The scheme is monitored by various levels of government to ensure compliance and effectiveness. 

The wages are determined by the state government based on the minimum wage policy. 

The scheme also includes provisions for the payment of unemployment allowances. 

If employment is not provided within the stipulated time the household is entitled to receive an unemployment allowance. 

The scheme promotes social inclusion by providing employment to marginalized groups.
