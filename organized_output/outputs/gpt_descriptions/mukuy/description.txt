The Mukhyamantri Udyog Sahayata Yojana (MUY) is a welfare scheme initiated by the Government of Madhya Pradesh. 

The scheme aims to promote entrepreneurship and support small and medium enterprises in the state. 

It provides financial assistance to new and existing businesses to enhance their competitiveness. 

The benefits include subsidies on interest rates and capital investment. 

Eligible beneficiaries are small and medium enterprises registered in Madhya Pradesh. 

The application process involves submitting a detailed project report along with the application form to the relevant department. 

The scheme is implemented by the Department of Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises of Madhya Pradesh. 

Financial assistance is provided based on the project cost and the category of the enterprise. 

The frequency of assistance depends on the evaluation of the project and its alignment with the scheme's objectives. 

Additional details may include specific eligibility criteria based on the type of business and investment levels.
