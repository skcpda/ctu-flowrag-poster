Sangch is a government welfare scheme aimed at providing financial assistance to farmers. 

The scheme focuses on improving the income levels of farmers and enhancing agricultural productivity. 

It aims to support farmers in overcoming financial challenges and ensuring sustainable agricultural practices. 

Eligible beneficiaries include small and marginal farmers who own agricultural land. 

The application process typically involves submitting an application form along with necessary documents to the local agricultural office. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers' Welfare at the central level. 

Financial assistance is provided directly to the beneficiaries' bank accounts. 

The frequency of assistance may vary based on the specific needs of the farmers and the agricultural calendar. 

Additional details regarding the exact amount of assistance and specific eligibility criteria may be available through official government sources.
