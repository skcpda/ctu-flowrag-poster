The SHEAMBGOC scheme is an initiative aimed at empowering women in the state of Maharashtra. 

It focuses on providing financial assistance to women entrepreneurs. 

The objective is to promote self-employment and entrepreneurship among women. 

The scheme offers financial support in the form of loans and grants. 

Eligible beneficiaries include women who are residents of Maharashtra and are above the age of 18. 

Women must also have a viable business plan to qualify for assistance. 

The application process involves submitting a detailed business proposal along with necessary documents. 

Applications can be submitted online through the official government portal or at designated offices. 

The timeline for processing applications may vary but is generally completed within a few weeks. 

The scheme is implemented by the Maharashtra State Women’s Commission in collaboration with various financial institutions. 

Additional details about specific loan amounts and repayment terms may vary based on the business proposal.
