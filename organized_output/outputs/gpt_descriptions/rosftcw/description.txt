The ROSHTCW scheme stands for the "Rehabilitation of Street and Homeless Children in India." 

It aims to provide support and rehabilitation for street children and those who are homeless. 

The objectives include ensuring the right to education health and protection for street children. 

The scheme provides financial assistance for the establishment of rehabilitation centers. 

It also offers educational support vocational training and health services. 

Eligibility is primarily focused on children living on the streets or in homeless conditions. 

The application process typically involves submission of proposals by NGOs or local bodies.
