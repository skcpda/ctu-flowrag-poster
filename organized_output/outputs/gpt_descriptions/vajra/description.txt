The Vajra scheme is an initiative by the Government of India aimed at promoting research and development in various fields. 

The primary objective of the Vajra scheme is to foster collaboration between Indian researchers and global scientific communities. 

The scheme provides financial support to researchers for conducting high-quality research projects. 

It also aims to enhance the visibility and impact of Indian research on the global stage. 

Eligibility for the Vajra scheme includes Indian researchers who are affiliated with recognized institutions. 

The application process involves submitting a detailed project proposal through the designated online portal. 

The timeline for project approval and funding disbursement varies based on the review process. 

The implementing authority for the Vajra scheme is the Department of Science and Technology, Government of India. 

Additional details regarding specific funding amounts and project duration may be available on the official website.
