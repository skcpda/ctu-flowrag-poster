The STPHCSI scheme stands for the Scheme for Technology and Product Development for Handicrafts Sector in India. 

This scheme aims to promote the development of the handicrafts sector through technology and product innovation. 

The primary objective is to enhance the competitiveness of handicraft products in both domestic and international markets. 

Benefits include financial assistance for technology development and product design improvement. 

The scheme also provides support for skill development and capacity building of artisans. 

Eligibility is generally for artisans and organizations involved in the handicrafts sector. 

The application process involves submitting a proposal to the relevant implementing authority. 

The timeline for processing applications may vary based on the specific project and requirements. 

The Ministry of Textiles is the primary implementing authority for this scheme. 

Additional details may include specific guidelines for project proposals and funding limits.
