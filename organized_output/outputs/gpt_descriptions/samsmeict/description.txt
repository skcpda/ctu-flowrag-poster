SAMSAMeICT stands for the Scheme for Assistance to MSMEs for the Development of ICT. 

It aims to promote the growth and development of Micro Small and Medium Enterprises in the Information and Communication Technology sector. 

The objective is to enhance the competitiveness of MSMEs through the adoption of advanced technologies and digital solutions. 

Benefits include financial assistance for technology acquisition training and capacity building. 

Eligible beneficiaries are Micro Small and Medium Enterprises engaged in the ICT sector. 

The application process involves submitting a proposal to the relevant government authority. 

The timeline for processing applications may vary based on the specific requirements of the proposal. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Micro Small and Medium Enterprises. 

Additional details regarding the specific financial assistance limits and training programs may be available through official government sources.
