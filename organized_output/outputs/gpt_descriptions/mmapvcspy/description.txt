The Mukhya Mantri Awas Yojana (MMA) for the Poor and Vulnerable Communities in the State of Punjab (Mmapvcspy) aims to provide housing assistance to economically weaker sections. 

The objective of the scheme is to ensure that all citizens have access to affordable housing. 

The benefits include financial assistance for building or purchasing a house. 

Eligible beneficiaries are individuals or families belonging to economically weaker sections of society. 

The application process involves submitting an application form along with required documents to the designated authority. 

The timeline for disbursement of funds is typically aligned with the approval of applications. 

The scheme is implemented by the Department of Housing and Urban Development in Punjab.
