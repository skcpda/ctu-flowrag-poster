The Pradhan Mantri Pashupalan Yojana (PPMA) is a government welfare scheme aimed at promoting animal husbandry in India. 

The scheme focuses on enhancing the productivity of livestock and improving the livelihoods of farmers. 

It aims to provide financial assistance and support to farmers involved in animal husbandry. 

The benefits include training for farmers on best practices in animal husbandry and access to veterinary services. 

Eligible beneficiaries are farmers and individuals engaged in livestock rearing. 

The application process involves submitting an application to the local animal husbandry department. 

The scheme is implemented by the Department of Animal Husbandry and Dairying under the Ministry of Fisheries Animal Husbandry and Dairying. 

Additional details regarding the timeline or frequency of assistance are not specified.
