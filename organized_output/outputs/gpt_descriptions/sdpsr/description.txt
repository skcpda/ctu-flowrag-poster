The Scheme for Development of Panchayati Raj System in India is known as SDPSR. 

It aims to strengthen the Panchayati Raj institutions in rural areas. 

The objective is to enhance the capacity of local self-governments to deliver services effectively. 

Benefits include financial assistance for capacity building and infrastructure development. 

Eligibility criteria typically include local self-governments and Panchayati Raj institutions. 

The application process involves submitting proposals to the relevant state government authorities. 

The timeline for funding and implementation varies by state and project specifics. 

The Ministry of Panchayati Raj is the primary implementing authority for this scheme. 

Additional details may include training programs and workshops for elected representatives.
