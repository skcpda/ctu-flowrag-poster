The SPUK scheme stands for the Scheme for Providing Urban Knowledge. 

It was launched by the Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs. 

The scheme aims to enhance the capacity of urban local bodies. 

It focuses on improving urban governance and service delivery. 

The objective is to promote knowledge sharing and best practices among urban local bodies. 

The benefits include training programs workshops and knowledge dissemination. 

Eligible participants include officials from urban local bodies and related stakeholders. 

The application process typically involves registration through the official portal. 

The timeline for the implementation of training programs varies based on the schedule set by the implementing agencies. 

The Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs is the primary implementing authority. 

Additional details about specific training modules and schedules may be available on the official website.
