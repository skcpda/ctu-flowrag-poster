The "SH" scheme refers to the "Swasthya Sathi" scheme initiated by the Government of West Bengal.

The scheme aims to provide health insurance coverage to the residents of West Bengal.

Its primary objective is to ensure access to quality healthcare services for economically disadvantaged families.

The scheme offers health insurance coverage of up to INR 5 lakh per family per year.

It covers hospitalization expenses for secondary and tertiary care.

The benefits include cashless treatment in empaneled hospitals.

Eligibility is primarily for families with an annual income of less than INR 1.5 lakh.

The scheme is open to all residents of West Bengal who meet the income criteria.

The application process involves filling out a form and submitting it at designated centers.

Beneficiaries can also apply online through the official website.

The scheme is implemented by the West Bengal Health Department.

The coverage is available for a period of one year and can be renewed annually.

The scheme also includes provisions for pre-existing diseases.

Additional benefits may include coverage for maternity and newborn care.

The scheme aims to reduce out-of-pocket expenses for healthcare.

It seeks to improve the overall health indicators of the state.

Awareness campaigns are conducted to inform eligible families about the scheme.

The scheme is part of the state's broader initiative to enhance healthcare access and affordability.
