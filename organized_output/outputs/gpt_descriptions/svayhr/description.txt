The Svayhr scheme is an initiative launched by the Government of India. 

It aims to promote self-employment and entrepreneurship among women. 

The primary objective is to enhance women's economic empowerment. 

The scheme provides financial assistance and skill development training. 

Eligible beneficiaries include women who are above the age of 18. 

Women from economically weaker sections are prioritized. 

The application process involves submitting a form to the designated authority. 

Applications can typically be submitted online or through local government offices. 

The timeline for processing applications may vary based on the implementing agency. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Women and Child Development. 

Additional details regarding the specific benefits may vary by state. 

The scheme may also include collaboration with local self-help groups. 

The assistance provided can include loans and grants for starting businesses. 

Skill development programs are often conducted in partnership with various training institutes. 

The frequency of assistance may depend on the specific project or business plan.
