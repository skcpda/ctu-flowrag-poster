The Mukhyamantri Mahila Samriddhi Yojana (MMSY) is a welfare scheme launched by the Government of Madhya Pradesh. 

The scheme aims to empower women by providing them with financial assistance and skill development opportunities. 

The primary objective is to enhance the economic status of women and promote self-reliance among them. 

Under this scheme women are provided with financial assistance to start their own businesses or engage in income-generating activities. 

The benefits include a one-time financial grant to eligible women for initiating self-employment ventures. 

Eligibility criteria include being a resident of Madhya Pradesh and belonging to economically weaker sections of society. 

Women aged between 18 to 55 years can apply for the scheme. 

The application process involves filling out a form available at designated government offices or online portals. 

Applicants may need to provide identity proof and income certificates as part of the application. 

The scheme is implemented by the Department of Women and Child Development in Madhya Pradesh. 

The timeline for disbursal of funds is generally within a few weeks after the application is approved. 

The scheme is part of a broader initiative to promote women's empowerment in the state.
