The Mukhyamantri Paryavaran Sanrakshan Yojana (MPPCY) is a welfare scheme initiated by the Government of Madhya Pradesh. 

The scheme aims to promote environmental conservation and sustainable development in the state. 

It focuses on the protection of forests and wildlife. 

The benefits include financial assistance for activities related to environmental conservation. 

Eligible beneficiaries include individuals and organizations involved in conservation efforts. 

The application process involves submitting a proposal to the relevant authorities. 

The timeline for the implementation of the scheme varies based on the specific project approved. 

The scheme is implemented by the Forest Department of Madhya Pradesh. 

Additional details about specific projects and funding can be found on official government websites.
