Prlvishwas is a welfare scheme initiated by the Government of India. 

The scheme aims to provide financial assistance to individuals in need. 

It focuses on enhancing the livelihood opportunities for marginalized communities. 

The benefits include direct cash transfers to eligible beneficiaries. 

Eligibility criteria include being a member of a marginalized community and meeting specific income thresholds. 

The application process involves submitting an online application through the official portal. 

The timeline for disbursement of funds is typically monthly. 

Implementing authorities include the Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment. 

The scheme is part of the government's broader initiative to promote social equity and inclusion.
