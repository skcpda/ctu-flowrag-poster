The MGIOECA stands for the Mahatma Gandhi Integrated Urban Employment Guarantee Scheme. 

This scheme aims to provide employment opportunities to urban residents. 

It is designed to enhance livelihood security in urban areas. 

The objective is to provide at least 100 days of wage employment in a financial year to every urban household. 

The benefits include guaranteed wage employment for eligible households. 

Assistance is provided in the form of wages for unskilled manual work. 

Eligibility criteria include being a resident of an urban area and belonging to a household that requires employment. 

The application process typically involves registration with the local urban local body. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs. 

The timeline for wage disbursement is generally on a monthly basis. 

The scheme also focuses on skill development and capacity building for better employment opportunities.
