The Indian government welfare scheme known as the Integrated Fisheries and Aquaculture Development Scheme is designed to enhance the fisheries sector in India. 

This scheme aims to promote sustainable development in fisheries and aquaculture. 

The context of this scheme is rooted in the need to improve the livelihoods of fishers and fish farmers. 

The fisheries sector plays a crucial role in the Indian economy and provides employment to millions. 

The scheme seeks to address the challenges faced by the fisheries sector including overfishing and environmental degradation. 

One of the primary objectives of the scheme is to increase fish production and productivity. 

It aims to enhance the income of fishers and fish farmers through various interventions. 

The scheme also focuses on promoting the sustainable use of aquatic resources. 

It encourages the adoption of modern technologies in fisheries and aquaculture practices. 

The benefits of the scheme include financial assistance for various activities related to fisheries. 

This includes support for the construction of fish ponds and hatcheries. 

The scheme also provides assistance for the purchase of fishing equipment and gear. 

Fishers can receive training and capacity building to improve their skills. 

The scheme aims to promote the establishment of fish processing units.

The assistance provided under the scheme is aimed at both individual fishers and groups.
