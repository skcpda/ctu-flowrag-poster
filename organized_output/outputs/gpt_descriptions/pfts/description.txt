The Pradhan Mantri Fasal Bima Yojana (PMFBY) is a government welfare scheme aimed at providing insurance coverage to farmers. 

The scheme was launched to support farmers in case of crop failure due to natural calamities. 

The primary objective is to stabilize the income of farmers and encourage them to adopt modern agricultural practices. 

Under this scheme, farmers can receive financial assistance for crop loss due to various reasons such as drought, flood, and pest attacks. 

The benefits include coverage for all food crops, oilseeds, and annual commercial crops. 

Farmers are required to pay a nominal premium which is subsidized by the government. 

The eligibility criteria include being a farmer who has taken a loan from a financial institution or is a non-loanee farmer. 

Farmers must have a valid landholding and must insure their crops under the scheme. 

The application process involves farmers submitting their applications through the nearest bank or insurance company. 

The timeline for enrollment typically occurs at the beginning of the sowing season. 

Claims can be made after the crop harvesting period based on the assessment of crop loss. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers' Welfare in collaboration with state governments. 

Insurance companies are responsible for the execution of the scheme at the ground level. 

The scheme aims to enhance the financial stability of farmers and reduce their vulnerability to crop loss.
