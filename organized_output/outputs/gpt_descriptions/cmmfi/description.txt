The Central Sector Scheme for the Development of the Handicrafts Sector is known as the Craft and Handicraft Market Development Initiative or CMMFI. 

This scheme was launched by the Ministry of Textiles, Government of India. 

The primary aim of the CMMFI scheme is to promote and develop the handicrafts sector in India.
