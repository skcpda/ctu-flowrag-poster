The Msofru scheme is an initiative by the Government of India aimed at enhancing the nutritional status of the population. 

The objective of the scheme is to provide fortified rice to improve the health and nutrition of vulnerable groups. 

The benefits include the distribution of fortified rice to beneficiaries under various government welfare programs. 

Eligibility criteria include individuals and families identified under the National Food Security Act and other relevant schemes. 

The application process typically involves registration through local government offices or online platforms. 

The distribution of fortified rice occurs regularly as part of the public distribution system. 

The implementing authority for the Msofru scheme is the Ministry of Consumer Affairs Food and Public Distribution. 

The scheme also collaborates with state governments for effective implementation and outreach.
