The scheme is known as the Pradhan Mantri Jeevan Bima Yojana for Construction Workers Welfare Board.

It was launched to provide financial assistance and social security to construction workers in India.

The objective is to ensure the welfare of construction workers through various benefits.

Benefits include life insurance coverage and financial assistance for medical emergencies.

The scheme also provides assistance for education and skill development of workers' children.

Eligibility is primarily for construction workers who are registered with the respective state welfare boards.

Workers must meet specific criteria set by the state government to qualify for the benefits.

The application process typically involves registration with the state construction workers welfare board.

Workers may need to submit identity proof and other relevant documents during registration.

The timeline for disbursement of benefits varies by state and specific circumstances.

The implementing authorities are the respective state construction workers welfare boards.

These boards are responsible for the execution and monitoring of the scheme at the state level.
