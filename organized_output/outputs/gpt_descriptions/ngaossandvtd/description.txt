The National Grants for the Advancement of Social Security and Development of Vulnerable and Disadvantaged (NGAOSSANDVTD) scheme aims to provide financial assistance to vulnerable and disadvantaged groups in India. 

The objective of the scheme is to enhance the social security and development of marginalized communities. 

Benefits include financial support for various welfare activities and programs aimed at improving the living conditions of disadvantaged groups. 

Eligibility criteria typically include being part of a vulnerable or disadvantaged community as defined by the government. 

The application process generally involves submitting a proposal to the relevant government authority outlining the intended use of funds. 

The timeline for fund disbursement varies based on the proposal approval process. 

Implementing authorities include the Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment and other designated state-level agencies. 

Additional details regarding specific funding limits and project types may be available through official government resources.
