The Madhya Pradesh Mukhyamantri Sewa Sankalp Scheme (MPMSSC) is a welfare initiative by the Government of Madhya Pradesh. 

It aims to provide employment opportunities to the youth of the state. 

The scheme focuses on skill development and enhancing employability. 

It offers financial assistance for skill training programs. 

Eligible beneficiaries include unemployed youth aged between 18 and 35 years. 

The application process involves online registration through the official portal. 

Candidates must provide necessary documents such as identity proof and educational qualifications. 

The scheme is implemented by the Department of Skill Development and Entrepreneurship, Madhya Pradesh. 

Training programs are conducted periodically based on demand and availability. 

The scheme also collaborates with various training institutes for effective implementation.
