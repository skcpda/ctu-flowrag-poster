The Pradhan Mantri Suraksha Bima Yojana (PSSPY) is an insurance scheme launched by the Government of India. 

It aims to provide accidental death and disability coverage to individuals. 

The objective is to enhance the social security of the poor and the underprivileged. 

The scheme offers a sum assured of INR 2 lakh in case of accidental death or total disability. 

In case of partial disability, the coverage is INR 1 lakh. 

The premium for the scheme is INR 12 per annum. 

Eligibility is open to all individuals aged between 18 and 70 years. 

Applicants must have a savings bank account to enroll in the scheme. 

The application process can be completed through participating banks. 

Enrollment is done annually. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Finance. 

It is managed through various public sector and private sector banks. 

The scheme was launched on May 9 2015. 

It is part of the government's broader initiative to provide financial security to citizens.
