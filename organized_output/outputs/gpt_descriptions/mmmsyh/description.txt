The Mukhyamantri Mahtari Nyay Yojana (MMMSYH) is a welfare scheme launched by the Government of Chhattisgarh. 

It aims to provide financial assistance to pregnant women and lactating mothers. 

The objective is to improve maternal and child health by providing nutritional support. 

Eligible beneficiaries include pregnant women and lactating mothers residing in Chhattisgarh. 

The scheme offers a cash incentive of INR 5,000 to eligible women. 

This amount is provided in two installments during pregnancy and lactation periods. 

The application process involves submitting a form at the local Anganwadi center. 

Beneficiaries must provide necessary documents such as identity proof and bank account details. 

The scheme is implemented by the Department of Women and Child Development, Chhattisgarh. 

The assistance is provided during the pregnancy and up to six months after childbirth. 

The scheme aims to reduce maternal and infant mortality rates in the state.
