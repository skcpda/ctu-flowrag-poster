The Pradhan Mantri Suraksha Maandhan Yojana (PMSM) is a pension scheme launched by the Government of India. 

It aims to provide social security to workers in the unorganized sector. 

The objective is to ensure a minimum monthly pension for eligible beneficiaries upon reaching the age of 60 years. 

The scheme offers a monthly pension of Rs. 3,000 to beneficiaries after they attain the age of 60. 

It also provides a life insurance cover of Rs. 2 lakh to the subscriber. 

Eligibility criteria include being a citizen of India and belonging to the unorganized sector. 

Applicants must be between the ages of 18 to 40 years at the time of enrollment. 

The application process involves filling out a form and submitting it to the designated authority. 

Enrollment can be done through Common Service Centers or online platforms. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Labour and Employment. 

The pension is disbursed monthly after the beneficiary turns 60. 

The scheme is designed to promote financial inclusion and social security for vulnerable populations.
