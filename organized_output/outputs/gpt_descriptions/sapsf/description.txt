The Scheme for Assistance to Persons with Severe Disabilities and their Families (SAPSF) is designed to provide support to individuals with severe disabilities. 

The objective of SAPSF is to enhance the quality of life of persons with severe disabilities and to support their families. 

The scheme offers financial assistance to eligible beneficiaries to help with their basic needs and to promote their social inclusion. 

Benefits include a monthly financial grant for individuals with severe disabilities. 

Eligibility criteria include being a resident of India and having a severe disability as defined by the government. 

The application process involves submitting a completed application form along with required documents to the designated authority. 

The timeline for processing applications may vary depending on the implementing agency. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment. 

Additional details may include periodic reviews of the scheme to assess its effectiveness and to make necessary adjustments.
