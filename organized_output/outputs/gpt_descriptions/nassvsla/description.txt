The National Agricultural Market Scheme for Value Chain Development in North Eastern Region is known as NASSVSLA. 

It aims to promote the development of agricultural marketing in the North Eastern Region of India. 

The objective is to enhance the income of farmers by providing them access to better market facilities. 

The scheme facilitates the establishment of a unified national market for agricultural commodities. 

It provides assistance for the development of agricultural marketing infrastructure. 

Eligible beneficiaries include farmers and farmer producer organizations in the North Eastern Region. 

The application process involves submitting proposals to the relevant state agricultural departments. 

The timeline for implementation varies based on project approval and funding availability. 

The Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers' Welfare is the primary implementing authority for this scheme. 

The scheme also focuses on capacity building and skill development for farmers. 

It encourages the use of technology in agricultural marketing. 

The scheme is part of the larger initiative to improve agricultural productivity and sustainability in the region.
