The National Family Benefit Scheme (NFBS) in Sikkim aims to provide financial assistance to families in case of the death of the primary breadwinner. 

The objective of the scheme is to offer support to families facing economic hardship due to the loss of income from the deceased. 

Under this scheme, a one-time financial assistance of INR 20,000 is provided to the eligible family. 

Eligibility for the scheme includes families residing in Sikkim where the primary breadwinner has died. 

The applicant must be a resident of Sikkim and should not be receiving any other financial assistance for the same purpose. 

The application process involves submitting a completed application form along with necessary documents such as the death certificate of the primary breadwinner. 

The application can be submitted to the local Panchayat office or the District Collector's office. 

The scheme is implemented by the Social Justice and Empowerment Department of the Government of Sikkim. 

The assistance is provided on a one-time basis upon approval of the application. 

The timeline for disbursement of funds may vary based on the processing time of the application.
