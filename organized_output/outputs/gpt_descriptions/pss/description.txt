The Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samman Nidhi (PM-KISAN) scheme was launched in February 2019. 

It aims to provide financial support to small and marginal farmers. 

The objective is to ensure a steady income for farmers and improve their livelihoods. 

Under this scheme eligible farmers receive direct cash transfers of Rs. 6000 per year. 

The amount is disbursed in three equal installments of Rs. 2000 each. 

Eligibility is limited to small and marginal farmers who own cultivable land. 

Farmers must have a landholding of up to 2 hectares to qualify. 

The application process involves registration through the State Government or Union Territory administration. 

Farmers need to provide necessary documentation including land ownership proof. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers' Welfare. 

The financial assistance is provided on a continuous basis. 

The funds are transferred directly to the bank accounts of the beneficiaries. 

The scheme is applicable across all states and Union Territories in India. 

State governments are responsible for identifying eligible beneficiaries. 

The scheme has been widely recognized for its role in supporting farmers during economic distress.
