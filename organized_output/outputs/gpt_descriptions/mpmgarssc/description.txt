The MPMGARSSC scheme stands for Madhya Pradesh Mukhyamantri Gramin Aajeevika Mission for Rural Self Employment and Skill Development. 

It was launched by the Government of Madhya Pradesh to enhance livelihood opportunities in rural areas. 

The primary objective is to promote self-employment and skill development among rural populations. 

The scheme aims to empower women and marginalized communities through skill training and financial assistance. 

Benefits include access to skill development programs and financial support for starting self-employment ventures. 

Eligible beneficiaries include rural youth and women who are interested in skill development and entrepreneurship. 

The application process involves submitting an application to the local implementing agency or department. 

The timeline for the scheme's implementation is ongoing with periodic training sessions and workshops. 

The implementing authority for this scheme is the Department of Rural Development, Government of Madhya Pradesh.
