The STLS scheme stands for the Short Term Loan Scheme. 

It is designed to provide financial assistance to small and marginal farmers. 

The primary objective is to ensure timely credit availability to farmers for agricultural activities. 

The scheme aims to enhance agricultural productivity and improve the income of farmers. 

Eligible beneficiaries include small and marginal farmers who own agricultural land. 

Farmers must have a valid land ownership document to qualify for the scheme. 

The application process typically involves submitting a loan application to the nearest bank or financial institution. 

Farmers may also need to provide necessary documents such as identity proof and land ownership proof. 

The loan amount and terms may vary based on the financial institution's policies. 

The frequency of loan disbursement can depend on the agricultural cycle and the specific needs of the farmers. 

The implementing authorities include various banks and financial institutions authorized by the government. 

The scheme may also be supported by state agricultural departments. 

Additional details regarding interest rates and repayment terms can vary by institution.
