The Atmanirbhar Bharat Rojgar Yojana is a significant welfare scheme launched by the Government of India. 

It was introduced in October 2020 as part of the Atmanirbhar Bharat Abhiyan initiative. 

The scheme aims to promote employment generation in the country. 

It was particularly designed to mitigate the impact of the COVID-19 pandemic on the economy. 

The scheme focuses on providing financial support to employers for creating new jobs. 

The primary objective is to incentivize employers to hire more workers. 

This is especially targeted towards those who lost their jobs during the pandemic. 

The scheme aims to encourage the formalization of employment in the country. 

It seeks to provide social security benefits to workers in the organized sector. 

The benefits under the scheme include the government paying the employer's contribution to the Employees' Provident Fund. 

This contribution is applicable for new employees who are hired after October 1, 2020. 

The government will pay a 12 percent contribution of the employer's share of the EPF for two years. 

This is applicable for all new employees earning less than INR 15,000 per month. 

Additionally, for employees earning above INR 15,000, the government will pay a 12 percent contribution for two years as well. 

This is applicable only if the establishment has not laid off any employees during the pandemic. 

The scheme also aims to provide support to the existing employees. 

Employers are encouraged to retain their employees during the economic downturn. 

The scheme is expected to benefit around 1.5 crore employees. 

It aims to create a conducive environment for job creation. 

The eligibility criteria for the scheme include both employers and employees. 

Employers must be registered under the Employees' Provident Fund Organization. 

They must also be compliant with the EPF Act. 

New employees hired after October 1, 2020, must not have been employed in the past six months. 

The scheme is applicable to all sectors of the economy. 

This includes both organized and unorganized sectors. 

The application process for the scheme is straightforward. 

Employers need to register on the official EPFO portal. 

They must provide details of the new employees hired under the scheme. 

The application must include the employees' Aadhaar numbers and bank account details. 

Employers must also submit their EPF registration details. 

Once the application is submitted, it will be verified by the EPFO. 

The timeline for the disbursement of funds is generally within a few weeks. 

The scheme is expected to run for a period of two years. 

It will continue until the allocated budget for the scheme is exhausted. 

The implementing authority for the scheme is the Employees' Provident Fund Organization. 

The EPFO is responsible for overseeing the implementation and monitoring of the scheme.
