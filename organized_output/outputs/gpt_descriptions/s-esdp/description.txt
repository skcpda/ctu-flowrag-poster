The Skill Enhancement and Development Programme (S-ESDP) is an initiative by the Government of India. 

It aims to enhance the employability of youth through skill development. 

The program focuses on providing training in various sectors. 

The benefits include skill training and certification. 

Participants gain access to job opportunities upon completion of the program. 

Eligibility is generally targeted towards unemployed youth. 

The application process typically involves registration through designated training centers. 

The program is implemented by the Ministry of Skill Development and Entrepreneurship. 

Training sessions may vary in duration depending on the skill set being taught. 

The frequency of the program is ongoing with multiple batches conducted throughout the year. 

Specific details about the training sectors and locations may vary by state.
