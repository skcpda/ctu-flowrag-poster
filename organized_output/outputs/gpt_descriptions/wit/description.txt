The Women in Technology (WIT) scheme aims to promote women's participation in technology and innovation sectors. 

The primary objective is to empower women through skill development and capacity building in technology-related fields. 

The scheme provides financial assistance for training programs and workshops. 

It also offers mentorship and networking opportunities with industry leaders. 

Eligibility is generally focused on women who are students or professionals in technology-related fields. 

The application process typically involves submitting an online application through the official portal. 

The timeline for the program may vary based on specific training sessions and workshops. 

The Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology is the implementing authority for this scheme. 

Additional details may include partnerships with educational institutions and industry stakeholders to enhance the program's reach.
