The WBSISHUSathi scheme is an initiative by the Government of West Bengal. 

It aims to provide financial assistance for the construction of houses for the economically weaker sections of society. 

The objective is to ensure that every individual has access to affordable housing. 

The scheme offers a subsidy for housing construction. 

Eligible beneficiaries include individuals from the economically weaker sections and low-income groups. 

Applicants must be residents of West Bengal. 

The application process involves submitting an online application through the official website. 

Documents required include proof of identity and income. 

The timeline for processing applications is typically within a few weeks. 

The implementing authority for this scheme is the Department of Housing, Government of West Bengal. 

The scheme is part of the state's broader efforts to improve housing conditions. 

It also aligns with national housing initiatives aimed at providing shelter to all.
