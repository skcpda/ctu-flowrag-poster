The SSST-PTS scheme stands for the Social Security Scheme for the Transgender Persons. 

This scheme was launched to provide social security and welfare measures for transgender individuals in India. 

The objective is to ensure the inclusion of transgender persons in the social and economic mainstream. 

Benefits include financial assistance for education and skill development. 

Transgender persons are eligible for this scheme. 

They must provide valid identification and proof of being a transgender individual. 

The application process involves submitting the required documents to the designated authorities. 

The timeline for processing applications may vary based on the implementing agency. 

The Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment is the primary implementing authority for this scheme. 

Additional details about specific benefits and assistance may vary by state.
