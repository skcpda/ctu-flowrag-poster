The Mahatma Gandhi Bunkar Bima Yojana is a welfare scheme initiated by the Government of India. 

This scheme is aimed at providing social security to handloom weavers. 

It is designed to support weavers and their families in times of need. 

The scheme provides financial assistance to the beneficiaries in case of natural or accidental death. 

It also offers support in case of total or partial disability due to accidents. 

The primary objective of the Mahatma Gandhi Bunkar Bima Yojana is to enhance the welfare of handloom weavers. 

The scheme aims to improve their living standards and provide them with a safety net. 

Under this scheme, beneficiaries receive an insurance cover of Rs. 2 lakh. 

This insurance cover is provided in the event of the death of the insured weaver. 

In case of total disability, the beneficiary is entitled to the same amount. 

For partial disability, the insured can claim Rs. 1 lakh. 

The scheme also provides for a scholarship for the children of the insured weavers. 

The scholarship is aimed at encouraging education among the children of weavers. 

The amount of the scholarship is Rs. 300 per month for up to two children. 

Eligibility for the scheme includes being a handloom weaver. 

The applicant must be aged between 18 and 50 years. 

The weaver must be a member of a registered cooperative society or a self-help group. 

The scheme is applicable to weavers across the country. 

The application process involves filling out a prescribed application form. 

The form can be obtained from the nearest handloom office or cooperative society. 

Applicants must submit the completed form along with required documents. 

Documents typically include proof of identity and proof of weaving activity. 

The application must be submitted to the designated authority in the respective state. 

The timeline for processing applications is generally within 30 days. 

The frequency of insurance premium payment is annual. 

The premium for the insurance cover is subsidized by the government. 

The implementing authority for this scheme is the Ministry of Textiles. 

State governments also play a role in the implementation at the local level.
