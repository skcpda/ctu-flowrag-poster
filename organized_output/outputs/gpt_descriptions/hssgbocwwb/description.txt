The HSSGBOCWWB scheme stands for the Haryana State Scheduled Castes and Backward Classes Welfare Board Scheme. 

This scheme is specifically designed to provide financial assistance and support to the Scheduled Castes and Backward Classes in the state of Haryana. 

The background of this scheme is rooted in the need to uplift marginalized communities within the state.

The Haryana government recognized these disparities and aimed to create a framework for targeted support.
