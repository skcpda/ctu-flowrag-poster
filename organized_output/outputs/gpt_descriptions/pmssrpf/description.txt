The Pradhan Mantri Suraksha Bima Yojana (PMSBY) is a government welfare scheme aimed at providing accidental death and disability insurance. 

It was launched on May 9 2015. 

The objective of the scheme is to provide financial security to individuals in case of accidents. 

The scheme offers a coverage of Rs 2 lakh for accidental death and permanent total disability. 

In case of permanent partial disability the coverage is Rs 1 lakh. 

The premium for the scheme is Rs 12 per annum. 

Eligibility for the scheme includes individuals aged between 18 and 70 years. 

Applicants must have a savings bank account. 

The application process is done through banks and financial institutions. 

Individuals can enroll in the scheme through online or offline modes. 

The enrollment period is typically from June 1 to May 31 of the following year. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Finance through public and private sector insurance companies. 

The scheme aims to enhance the insurance penetration in India. 

It also promotes the habit of saving among the population.
