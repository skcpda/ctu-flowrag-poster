The Pradhan Mantri Suraksha Bima Yojana (PMSBY) was launched in 2015. 

It aims to provide accidental death and disability insurance to individuals. 

The objective is to enhance the financial security of individuals in case of accidents. 

The scheme offers a coverage of INR 2 lakh for accidental death and total disability. 

For partial disability, the coverage is INR 1 lakh. 

The premium for the scheme is INR 12 per annum. 

Eligibility includes individuals aged between 18 to 70 years. 

Applicants must have a savings bank account. 

The application process involves enrolling through banks or online platforms. 

The enrollment is typically done during the month of May each year. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Finance. 

The insurance is provided by various public and private sector insurance companies. 

The coverage is renewable annually. 

The scheme aims to provide affordable insurance to the economically weaker sections of society.
