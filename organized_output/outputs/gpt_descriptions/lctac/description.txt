The LCTAC scheme stands for the Livelihood and Skill Training for Adolescent Girls. 

This scheme is part of the broader initiative to empower adolescent girls in India. 

It aims to provide skill development and livelihood opportunities to girls aged between 14 to 18 years. 

The context of the scheme arises from the need to enhance the socio-economic status of adolescent girls. 

The Indian government recognizes the importance of equipping young girls with skills that can lead to sustainable livelihoods.
