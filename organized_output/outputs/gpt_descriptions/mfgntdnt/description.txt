The scheme "Mahatma Gandhi National Rural Employment Guarantee Act" is commonly known as MGNREGA. 

It was enacted in 2005 to enhance livelihood security in rural areas. 

The primary objective is to provide at least 100 days of guaranteed wage employment in a financial year to every rural household. 

The scheme aims to create durable assets and strengthen the livelihood resource base of rural households. 

Benefits include wage payments for unskilled manual work. 

The scheme also provides social security and helps in reducing rural poverty. 

Eligibility is open to every rural household. 

All adult members of the household can apply for employment under the scheme. 

The application process involves submitting a job card application to the local Gram Panchayat. 

Once the job card is issued, households can request work as per their needs. 

The timeline for wage payment is generally within 15 days of the work being completed. 

The implementing authority is the Ministry of Rural Development at the central level. 

State governments are responsible for the implementation at the state level. 

The scheme is funded by the central government and state governments in a specified ratio. 

MGNREGA also emphasizes transparency and accountability through social audits.
