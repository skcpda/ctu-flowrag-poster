The Gobardhan scheme is an initiative launched by the Government of India aimed at promoting the management of organic waste and enhancing the income of farmers and rural households. 

The full form of Gobardhan is Galvanizing Organic Bio-Agro Resources Dhan. 

This scheme was introduced under the Swachh Bharat Mission Gramin. 

It focuses on the conversion of organic waste into biogas and organic fertilizers. 

The scheme is part of a broader effort to promote sustainable agricultural practices. 

It also aims to reduce pollution caused by the improper disposal of organic waste. 

The Gobardhan scheme is particularly relevant in the context of India's growing urbanization and waste management challenges. 

The objectives of the Gobardhan scheme include promoting the use of biogas as a clean energy source. 

It aims to enhance the income of farmers through the sale of bio-manure. 

The scheme also seeks to improve sanitation and hygiene in rural areas. 

By converting organic waste into energy, it contributes to the reduction of greenhouse gas emissions. 

The benefits of the Gobardhan scheme are multifaceted. 

Farmers and rural households can generate biogas from organic waste. 

This biogas can be used for cooking and lighting purposes. 

The scheme also provides an opportunity for farmers to produce organic fertilizers. 

These fertilizers can be used in agricultural practices, promoting sustainable farming. 

The Gobardhan scheme encourages the establishment of biogas plants in rural areas. 

These plants can be set up at the household level or in community clusters. 

The assistance provided under the scheme includes financial support for setting up biogas plants. 

The government offers subsidies to reduce the initial investment burden on farmers. 

The eligibility criteria for the Gobardhan scheme primarily target farmers and rural households. 

Individuals or groups involved in agriculture can apply for the scheme. 

The scheme is particularly beneficial for those who generate organic waste. 

This includes farmers with livestock as well as households producing kitchen waste. 

The application process for the Gobardhan scheme is designed to be straightforward. 

Interested applicants can approach the local government offices or designated agencies. 

They may need to fill out an application form detailing their requirements. 

Documentation may include proof of land ownership or farming activities. 

Once the application is submitted, it will be reviewed by the relevant authorities. 

The timeline for processing applications can vary based on location and demand. 

Successful applicants will receive information regarding the subsidy and installation process. 

The frequency of assistance under the Gobardhan scheme is not fixed. 

Support is provided based on the number of applications received and approved. 

The implementing authorities for the Gobardhan scheme include the Ministry of Jal Shakti. 

State governments also play a crucial role in the implementation of the scheme. 

Local bodies such as panchayats are involved in the ground-level execution. 

The Gobardhan scheme is part of a larger vision for rural development in India. 

It aligns with the goals of the National Biofuel Policy.
