The scheme known as SISNHE stands for the Scheme for Implementation of Scheduled Tribes and Other Traditional Forest Dwellers. 

It aims to provide financial assistance and support to Scheduled Tribes and other traditional forest dwellers. 

The primary objective is to enhance the livelihoods of these communities through sustainable forest management and conservation practices. 

Benefits include financial aid for livelihood activities and support for skill development. 

Eligibility is generally limited to Scheduled Tribes and other traditional forest dwellers who depend on forest resources for their livelihoods. 

The application process typically involves submitting a proposal to the relevant state or district authorities. 

The timeline for assistance may vary based on the specific project and its implementation schedule. 

Implementing authorities include the Ministry of Tribal Affairs and various state government departments. 

The scheme also focuses on capacity building and awareness programs for the beneficiaries.
