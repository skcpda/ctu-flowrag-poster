The RL Barpyu scheme is an initiative aimed at providing financial assistance to farmers in India. 

The objective of the scheme is to support farmers in enhancing their agricultural productivity and ensuring their financial stability. 

The benefits of the scheme include direct financial assistance to eligible farmers.
