The KALIA scheme stands for Krushak Assistance for Livelihood and Income Augmentation. 

It was launched by the Government of Odisha in 2018. 

The primary objective of the KALIA scheme is to provide financial assistance to small and marginal farmers. 

The scheme aims to enhance the livelihood and income of farmers in the state. 

It also seeks to support landless agricultural laborers and vulnerable agricultural households. 

The KALIA scheme is a part of the broader initiative to improve the agricultural sector in Odisha. 

The scheme recognizes the challenges faced by farmers in accessing credit and financial support. 

It is designed to provide timely assistance to farmers to ensure food security and improve their economic condition. 

Under the KALIA scheme, eligible farmers receive direct cash transfers. 

The scheme provides financial assistance of INR 10,000 per family per year. 

This amount is divided into two installments of INR 5,000 each. 

The first installment is provided at the beginning of the Kharif season. 

The second installment is provided at the beginning of the Rabi season. 

The assistance is intended to help farmers with various agricultural expenses. 

These expenses include the purchase of seeds fertilizers and other necessary inputs. 

In addition to cash assistance, the KALIA scheme also offers support for landless laborers. 

Landless agricultural laborers receive financial assistance of INR 12,500 per year. 

This assistance is aimed at improving their livelihood and providing them with better opportunities. 

The KALIA scheme also includes provisions for the empowerment of women. 

Women farmers are encouraged to participate in the scheme. 

The scheme aims to promote gender equality in agriculture. 

Eligibility for the KALIA scheme is primarily based on the landholding status of the farmers. 

Small and marginal farmers with landholdings of up to 2 hectares are eligible. 

Landless agricultural laborers are also eligible for the scheme. 

The scheme is targeted towards the most vulnerable sections of the agricultural community. 

Farmers who have already availed of benefits from other government schemes are still eligible. 

The application process for the KALIA scheme is designed to be simple and accessible. 

Farmers can apply online through the official website of the Odisha government. 

They can also visit the nearest Gram Panchayat office to submit their application. 

The application form requires basic information about the farmer and their landholding. 

Farmers need to provide proof of identity and land ownership. 

Once the application is submitted it is verified by the concerned authorities. 

The verification process ensures that only eligible farmers receive the benefits. 

The timeline for the disbursement of funds is structured around the agricultural calendar. 

The first installment is typically disbursed in June or July. 

The second installment is disbursed in November or December. 

The funds are transferred directly to the bank accounts of the beneficiaries. 

This direct transfer mechanism ensures transparency and reduces the chances of corruption. 

The KALIA scheme is implemented by the Department of Agriculture and Farmers' Empowerment in Odisha. 

The department works in collaboration with various local bodies and agencies. 

These include the Panchayati Raj institutions and other government departments.
