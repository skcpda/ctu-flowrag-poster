The scheme is known as the Scheme for Strengthening of State Health Systems (SSCH). 

It was launched to enhance the capacity of state health systems in India. 

The objective is to improve healthcare delivery and strengthen health infrastructure. 

Benefits include financial assistance for upgrading health facilities and improving healthcare services. 

Eligible beneficiaries include state governments and health departments. 

The application process involves submitting proposals to the Ministry of Health and Family Welfare. 

The timeline for fund disbursement is determined based on project approval. 

The implementing authority is the Ministry of Health and Family Welfare. 

The scheme aims to ensure better health outcomes and access to quality healthcare services.
