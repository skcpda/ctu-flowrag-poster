The scheme SPILTPFACSG stands for the Special Assistance for Livelihood and Technical Support for Persons with Disabilities. 

It aims to provide financial assistance and technical support to individuals with disabilities. 

The objectives include enhancing livelihood opportunities and promoting self-employment among persons with disabilities. 

Benefits include financial assistance for starting small businesses and vocational training programs. 

Eligibility is typically limited to individuals with certified disabilities as per government standards. 

The application process usually involves submitting a disability certificate along with a business proposal to the designated authority. 

The timeline for processing applications may vary but is generally completed within a few weeks. 

Implementing authorities include the Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment and state welfare departments. 

Additional details may include periodic reviews of the scheme to assess its impact and effectiveness.
