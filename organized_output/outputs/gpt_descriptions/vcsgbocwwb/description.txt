The VCSGBOCWWB scheme stands for the Vishwakarma Construction Workers Welfare Board. 

It was established to provide welfare benefits to construction workers in Uttarakhand. 

The main objective is to improve the living standards of construction workers. 

The scheme aims to provide financial assistance for various needs such as health, education, and housing. 

Benefits include financial aid for medical treatment, scholarships for children, and assistance for housing construction. 

Eligibility criteria include being a registered construction worker in Uttarakhand. 

Applicants must also meet certain income thresholds as defined by the board. 

The application process involves filling out a form and submitting it to the local office of the Vishwakarma Construction Workers Welfare Board. 

Documents required may include proof of identity, proof of employment, and income certificate. 

The timeline for processing applications may vary but is generally completed within a few weeks. 

The implementing authority for this scheme is the Vishwakarma Construction Workers Welfare Board in Uttarakhand. 

Additional details may include periodic reviews of the scheme to enhance benefits based on worker needs.
