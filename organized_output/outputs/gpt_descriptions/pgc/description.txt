The Pradhan Mantri Garib Kalyan Yojana (PMGKY) was launched in response to the economic challenges posed by the COVID-19 pandemic. 

The objective of the scheme is to provide financial assistance and support to the poor and vulnerable sections of society during the crisis. 

The benefits include direct cash transfers to the accounts of beneficiaries. 

The scheme also includes provisions for free food grains for the poor. 

Eligibility is primarily based on being part of the economically weaker sections of society. 

The application process is streamlined through existing government databases and does not require separate applications for eligible beneficiaries. 

The assistance is provided in a phased manner as per the guidelines of the scheme. 

The Ministry of Finance is the primary implementing authority for this scheme. 

Various state governments and local bodies also play a role in the implementation and distribution of benefits. 

The scheme has been extended multiple times based on the evolving needs of the population during the pandemic.
