The Mukhyamantri Tirth Yatra Yojana (MTYY) is a welfare scheme initiated by the Government of Madhya Pradesh. 

The scheme aims to provide financial assistance to senior citizens for pilgrimage trips to various religious destinations. 

The objective is to promote spiritual tourism and ensure that elderly citizens can fulfill their religious aspirations. 

Eligible beneficiaries include senior citizens aged 60 years and above who are residents of Madhya Pradesh. 

The scheme offers assistance for travel expenses to selected pilgrimage sites. 

The application process involves filling out a form available at designated offices or online portals. 

The frequency of the yatra is determined by the schedule of the pilgrimage trips organized by the government. 

The scheme is implemented by the Department of Religious Trust and Endowments of Madhya Pradesh. 

Additional details include the provision of free food and accommodation during the pilgrimage. 

The scheme also emphasizes the importance of cultural heritage and community bonding through religious journeys.
