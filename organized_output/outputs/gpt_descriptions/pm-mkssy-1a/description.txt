The Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samman Nidhi Yojana is a government welfare scheme aimed at providing financial support to farmers. 

The scheme was launched to ensure a steady income for farmers and to alleviate poverty in rural areas. 

The main objective is to provide direct income support to small and marginal farmers. 

Under this scheme eligible farmers receive an income support of ₹6000 per year. 

This amount is disbursed in three equal installments of ₹2000 each. 

Eligibility is limited to small and marginal farmers who own cultivable land. 

Farmers must have a landholding of up to 2 hectares to qualify for the scheme. 

The application process involves registering through the official government portal or local government offices. 

Farmers need to provide necessary documents such as land ownership proof and identification. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers' Welfare. 

The financial assistance is provided directly to the bank accounts of the beneficiaries. 

The scheme is expected to be ongoing with regular disbursements as per the defined timeline. 

The scheme aims to enhance the livelihood of farmers and improve their socio-economic conditions.
