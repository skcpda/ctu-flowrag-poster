The SFMCA Scheme is designed to provide financial assistance to women self-help groups in India. 

The objective of the scheme is to promote the economic empowerment of women through self-help groups. 

The scheme provides financial support for various livelihood activities undertaken by these groups. 

Eligible beneficiaries are women who are members of registered self-help groups. 

The application process involves submitting a proposal through the respective state government or implementing agency. 

The timeline for disbursement of funds varies based on the state and the specific project approved. 

The Ministry of Women and Child Development is the primary implementing authority for this scheme. 

Additional details regarding specific eligibility criteria and project types may vary by state.
