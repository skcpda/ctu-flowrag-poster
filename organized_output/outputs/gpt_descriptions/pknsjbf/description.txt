The Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samman Nidhi Yojana (PM-KISAN) is a government welfare scheme aimed at providing financial support to farmers in India. 

The objective of the scheme is to provide direct income support to small and marginal farmers. 

The scheme offers an income support of Rs. 6000 per year to eligible farmers. 

This amount is disbursed in three equal installments of Rs. 2000 each. 

Eligibility for the scheme includes small and marginal farmers who own cultivable land. 

Farmers with a landholding of up to 2 hectares are eligible for the benefits. 

The application process involves farmers registering on the PM-KISAN portal or through designated state government offices. 

Farmers must provide necessary documents such as land ownership proof and bank account details. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers' Welfare. 

The funds are transferred directly to the bank accounts of the beneficiaries. 

The scheme was launched in February 2019. 

The financial assistance is provided on an annual basis. 

The scheme aims to alleviate the financial burden on farmers and enhance their livelihood.
