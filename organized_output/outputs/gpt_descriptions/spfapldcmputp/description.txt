The scheme SPFAPLDCMPUTP stands for the Scheme for the Promotion of Financial Assistance for the Development of Cooperatives in the Multi-Purpose Cooperative Societies.

This scheme is initiated by the Ministry of Cooperation in India.

The primary objective is to promote and strengthen cooperative societies.

It aims to enhance the financial viability of multi-purpose cooperative societies.

The benefits include financial assistance for various developmental activities.

This assistance is provided to improve the operational capabilities of cooperatives.

Eligibility criteria include being a registered multi-purpose cooperative society.

The application process involves submitting a proposal to the relevant authorities.

The proposal must detail the intended use of the financial assistance.

The timeline for the disbursement of funds is subject to the approval of the proposal.

The implementing authority for this scheme is the Ministry of Cooperation.

Additional details regarding specific funding limits or project types may vary.
