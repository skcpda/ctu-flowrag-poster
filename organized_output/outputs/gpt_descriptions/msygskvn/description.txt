The Mukhyamantri Sewa Sankalp Yojana (MSSY) is a welfare scheme initiated by the Government of Madhya Pradesh. 

The scheme aims to provide employment opportunities to the youth of the state. 

It focuses on skill development and vocational training. 

The benefits include financial assistance for skill training and job placements. 

Eligible beneficiaries are youth aged between 18 to 35 years. 

They must be residents of Madhya Pradesh. 

The application process involves online registration through the official website. 

The timeline for the scheme is ongoing with periodic training sessions. 

The implementing authority is the Department of Skill Development and Entrepreneurship, Madhya Pradesh. 

The scheme also collaborates with various training institutes for effective implementation.
