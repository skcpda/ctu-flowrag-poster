The Pradhan Mantri Suraksha Bima Yojana (PMSBY) is a government-backed accident insurance scheme. 

It was launched in May 2015. 

The objective of PMSBY is to provide financial support to families in case of accidental death or disability. 

The scheme aims to enhance the social security of individuals. 

The benefits include a sum assured of INR 2 lakh in case of accidental death or total disability. 

In case of partial disability, the benefit is INR 1 lakh. 

The premium for the scheme is INR 12 per annum. 

Eligibility for the scheme includes individuals aged between 18 and 70 years. 

Applicants must have a savings bank account. 

The application process can be completed through banks and financial institutions. 

Enrollment can be done online or offline. 

The scheme is available for subscription every year. 

The implementing authority for PMSBY is the Ministry of Finance. 

The scheme is managed by public sector insurance companies. 

The coverage period is from June 1 to May 31 of the following year. 

Individuals can renew their coverage annually. 

The scheme is aimed at low-income individuals to provide them with financial security.
