The RSDARC scheme stands for the Rural Skill Development and Agricultural Resource Centre. 

It aims to enhance the skills of rural youth and promote sustainable agricultural practices. 

The objectives include providing vocational training and improving employment opportunities in rural areas. 

The scheme benefits rural youth by equipping them with necessary skills for self-employment and wage employment. 

It also supports farmers by promoting modern agricultural techniques and resource management. 

Eligibility criteria include being a resident of a rural area and being within a specific age range defined by the implementing authorities. 

The application process typically involves submitting an application form to the local implementing agency or online through designated portals. 

The timeline for training programs may vary based on the specific courses offered. 

Implementing authorities include the Ministry of Rural Development and various state government agencies. 

Additional details may include partnerships with local organizations for training and resource provision.
