The "Rural Transport and Technology Upgradation Scheme" (RTTUW) aims to enhance rural transport infrastructure and promote technology adoption in rural areas. 

The objective of RTTUW is to improve connectivity in rural regions and facilitate access to markets and services. 

The scheme benefits rural transport operators by providing financial assistance for upgrading their vehicles and adopting modern technology. 

Eligibility is typically limited to transport operators and service providers in rural areas. 

The application process involves submitting a proposal to the relevant state or central government authority overseeing the scheme. 

The timeline for fund disbursement and project completion varies based on the specific project and state guidelines. 

Implementing authorities include the Ministry of Rural Development and respective state governments. 

Additional details may include specific financial assistance amounts and types of technology supported under the scheme.
