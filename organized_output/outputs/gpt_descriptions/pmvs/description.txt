The Pradhan Mantri Vaya Vandana Yojana (PMVVY) is a pension scheme launched by the Government of India. 

It was introduced to provide social security to senior citizens aged 60 years and above. 

The objective is to offer a steady income stream during retirement. 

The scheme provides a guaranteed return of 8 percent per annum payable monthly. 

The maximum investment limit is Rs. 15 lakh per senior citizen. 

The scheme has a tenure of 10 years. 

Eligibility is restricted to Indian citizens aged 60 years and above. 

The application process involves submitting a duly filled application form along with necessary documents to authorized banks or LIC branches. 

The scheme is implemented by the Life Insurance Corporation of India (LIC). 

The scheme is available for subscription until March 31, 2023. 

The benefits include a lump sum payment at maturity and a death benefit to the nominee. 

The scheme also allows for premature exit under specific conditions. 

The payouts can be received monthly quarterly half-yearly or annually as per the choice of the subscriber. 

The scheme aims to enhance the financial security of the elderly population in India.
