The Mahatma Gandhi Sarva Shiksha Abhiyan for Persons with Disabilities and Other Vulnerable Children Welfare Board (MGSAPBOCWWB) is an initiative aimed at promoting education and welfare for children with disabilities and other vulnerable groups. 

The objective of the scheme is to ensure inclusive education for children with disabilities. 

It aims to provide necessary support and resources to enhance the educational experience of these children. 

Benefits include financial assistance for educational materials and resources. 

The scheme also provides training for teachers to better support children with disabilities. 

Eligibility criteria include being a child with a disability or belonging to a vulnerable group. 

The application process involves submitting relevant documents to the local education authority. 

The timeline for assistance is typically aligned with the academic year. 

The implementing authority for this scheme is the Ministry of Education in collaboration with state governments. 

Additional details may include specific training programs for educators and awareness campaigns to promote inclusivity.
