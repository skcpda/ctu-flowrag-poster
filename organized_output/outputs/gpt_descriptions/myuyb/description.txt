The Mukhyamantri Udyog Yojana (MYUYB) is a government initiative aimed at promoting entrepreneurship among the youth in India. 

The objective of the scheme is to provide financial assistance and support to young entrepreneurs to establish their own businesses. 

The benefits include financial support in the form of subsidies and grants for starting small and medium enterprises. 

Assistance may also include training programs and mentorship to help entrepreneurs develop their business skills. 

Eligibility criteria typically include age limits for applicants and the requirement to be a resident of the state where the scheme is implemented. 

The application process usually involves submitting a detailed business plan along with necessary documents to the designated authority. 

The timeline for processing applications and disbursing funds may vary based on the specific state guidelines. 

The implementing authorities for this scheme are usually the state government departments responsible for industry and entrepreneurship development. 

Additional details may include specific sectors targeted by the scheme and any partnerships with financial institutions for easier access to credit.
