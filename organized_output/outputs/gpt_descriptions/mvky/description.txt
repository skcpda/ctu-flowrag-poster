Mahatma Gandhi National Rural Employment Guarantee Act is commonly known as MG-NREGA.

It was enacted in 2005 to enhance livelihood security in rural areas.

The primary objective is to provide at least 100 days of guaranteed wage employment in a financial year to every rural household.

The scheme aims to create durable assets and improve the livelihood security of rural households.

Benefits include the provision of unskilled manual work and the payment of wages at the minimum wage rate.

Eligible beneficiaries are all rural households that apply for employment under the scheme.

The application process involves submitting a demand for work to the local Gram Panchayat.

The Gram Panchayat is responsible for issuing a job card to eligible households.

The scheme operates throughout the year with no specific timeline for applications.

The Ministry of Rural Development is the implementing authority for this scheme.

State governments also play a crucial role in the implementation of MG-NREGA at the local level.
