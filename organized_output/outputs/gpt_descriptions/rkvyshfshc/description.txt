RKVY-RAFTAAR (Rashtriya Krishi Vikas Yojana - Remunerative Approaches for Agriculture and Allied Sector Rejuvenation) is a scheme launched by the Government of India. 

It aims to enhance the agricultural sector by promoting holistic growth in agriculture and allied sectors. 

The objectives include increasing farmers' income through various initiatives and improving agricultural productivity. 

Benefits include financial assistance for various agricultural projects and support for infrastructure development in rural areas. 

Eligibility criteria typically include farmers and agricultural entrepreneurs engaged in agriculture and allied activities. 

The application process involves submitting project proposals to the respective state governments for approval. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers' Welfare along with state governments. 

The timeline for fund disbursement and project implementation varies based on the specific project and state guidelines. 

The scheme also emphasizes the use of technology and innovation in agriculture. 

It encourages the adoption of sustainable practices and aims to create a robust agricultural ecosystem.
