The CM-CAB scheme is a welfare initiative launched by the Government of India. 

This scheme is specifically designed to provide financial assistance to the citizens of the state. 

The CM-CAB scheme stands for Chief Minister's Comprehensive Assistance for the Beneficiaries. 

It aims to support various sections of society who are in need of financial help. 

The scheme was introduced to address the challenges faced by economically weaker sections. 

It is part of a broader effort to enhance social welfare and uplift marginalized communities. 

The primary objective of the CM-CAB scheme is to provide timely financial aid to beneficiaries. 

This assistance is aimed at improving their living standards and ensuring their basic needs are met. 

The scheme also seeks to empower individuals by providing them with the necessary resources. 

It focuses on promoting self-reliance among the beneficiaries. 

The financial assistance under the CM-CAB scheme can be utilized for various purposes. 

These purposes include education healthcare and livelihood support.
