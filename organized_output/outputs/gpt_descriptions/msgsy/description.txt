The Mahatma Gandhi Sarva Shiksha Abhiyan (MGSY) is a government initiative aimed at promoting universal education in India. 

The scheme focuses on providing quality education to all children aged 6 to 14 years. 

It aims to bridge the gap in educational access and improve literacy rates across the country. 

The benefits include free education for children in primary and upper primary schools. 

It also provides for the training of teachers and the development of school infrastructure. 

Eligibility is primarily for children in the age group of 6 to 14 years. 

The application process typically involves enrollment in a government or government-aided school. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Education at the national level. 

State governments also play a crucial role in the execution of the program. 

The scheme is part of the larger framework of the Right to Education Act. 

It aims to ensure that every child has access to quality education without any discrimination. 

The timeline for implementation is ongoing as it is a continuous initiative. 

The scheme also emphasizes the importance of community participation in the education process. 

It includes provisions for special training for out-of-school children. 

The MGSY seeks to enhance the overall educational environment in rural and urban areas alike.
