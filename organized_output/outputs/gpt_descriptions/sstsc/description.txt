The SSTSC stands for the Skill Development Training Scheme for Scheduled Tribes. 

This scheme aims to enhance the employability of Scheduled Tribe youth through skill development. 

The objective is to provide vocational training to improve skills and create job opportunities. 

Benefits include financial assistance for training and support for self-employment initiatives. 

Eligibility is primarily focused on Scheduled Tribe individuals aged between 18 to 45 years. 

The application process typically involves submitting an application form to the designated training centers or agencies. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Tribal Affairs. 

Training programs are conducted periodically based on the demand and availability of resources. 

The scheme also emphasizes the involvement of local training providers to ensure relevance to local job markets.
