The National Mission on Oilseeds and Oil Palm (NMOEDP) is an initiative by the Government of India. 

It aims to reduce the country's dependence on edible oil imports. 

The objective is to increase domestic production of oilseeds and oil palm. 

The scheme seeks to enhance farmers' income and promote sustainable agricultural practices. 

Benefits include financial assistance for cultivation and support for infrastructure development. 

Farmers engaged in oilseed and oil palm cultivation are eligible for the scheme. 

The application process typically involves registration with the relevant agricultural department. 

The timeline for assistance may vary based on the crop cycle and specific project implementation. 

The Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers' Welfare is the primary implementing authority. 

State governments may also play a role in the execution of the scheme. 

The scheme includes provisions for research and development in oilseed production. 

It promotes the use of high-yielding varieties and modern agricultural techniques. 

The NMOEDP is part of a broader strategy to achieve self-sufficiency in edible oils.
