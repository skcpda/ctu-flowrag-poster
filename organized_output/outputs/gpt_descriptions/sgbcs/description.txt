The SGBCS stands for the Sovereign Gold Bond Scheme. 

It was launched by the Government of India in November 2015. 

The scheme aims to provide an alternative to physical gold investment. 

It seeks to reduce the demand for physical gold and promote financial savings. 

The benefits include interest payments and capital appreciation. 

Investors receive a fixed interest rate of 2.5 percent per annum. 

The bonds are issued by the Reserve Bank of India on behalf of the Government of India. 

The bonds are available in denominations of 1 gram of gold and multiples thereof. 

The maximum limit for investment is 4 kilograms for individuals and 20 kilograms for trusts and similar entities. 

The bonds have a tenure of 8 years with an exit option after the 5th year. 

Eligibility includes Indian residents and entities such as trusts and universities. 

The application process involves filling out a form and submitting it to authorized banks or financial institutions. 

The bonds can also be purchased online through designated platforms. 

The scheme is implemented by the Reserve Bank of India. 

The issuance of bonds occurs in tranches as announced by the Reserve Bank. 

Investors can redeem the bonds at the prevailing market price of gold at maturity. 

The scheme also provides tax benefits on capital gains if held until maturity.
