The MP DIC BR SBFS CC scheme is an initiative by the Government of Madhya Pradesh. 

It aims to provide financial assistance to small and medium enterprises. 

The objective is to promote entrepreneurship and enhance the competitiveness of small businesses. 

The scheme offers benefits such as subsidies on interest rates and financial support for capital investment. 

Eligibility is primarily for small and medium enterprises registered in Madhya Pradesh. 

The application process involves submitting a proposal to the District Industries Centre. 

The timeline for processing applications varies based on the completeness of documentation. 

The implementing authority for this scheme is the Department of Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises of Madhya Pradesh. 

Additional details may include specific financial limits and conditions for assistance.
