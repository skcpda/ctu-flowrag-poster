The scheme TBWODAP stands for the "Transgender Persons (Protection of Rights) Act Welfare and Development Assistance Program." 

It was launched to provide support and assistance to transgender persons in India. 

The objectives of the scheme include promoting the welfare of transgender persons and ensuring their rights are protected. 

The benefits include financial assistance for education health care and skill development. 

Eligibility criteria include being a transgender person as defined under the Transgender Persons (Protection of Rights) Act 2019. 

The application process involves submitting an application to the designated authority in the respective state or union territory. 

The timeline for assistance may vary based on the specific program under the scheme. 

Implementing authorities include the Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment and state welfare departments. 

The scheme aims to empower transgender persons and integrate them into mainstream society.
