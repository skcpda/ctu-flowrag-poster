The Pradhan Mantri Emergency Credit Line Guarantee Scheme (PMECRG) was launched in response to the economic impact of the COVID-19 pandemic. 

The objective of the scheme is to provide additional credit to micro small and medium enterprises (MSMEs) to help them recover from the financial distress caused by the pandemic. 

The scheme offers a guarantee of up to 100 percent on additional working capital loans taken by eligible MSMEs. 

The benefits include access to collateral-free loans up to Rs. 3 lakh crore. 

Eligible beneficiaries are MSMEs that have outstanding loans as of February 29 2020. 

The application process involves approaching banks or financial institutions to apply for the loan under the scheme. 

The scheme is applicable for loans sanctioned up to March 31 2021. 

The Ministry of Finance is the implementing authority for the scheme. 

The scheme aims to support the revival of the economy by ensuring liquidity for MSMEs.
