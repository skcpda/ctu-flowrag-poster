STL Meghalaya is a government welfare scheme launched in the state of Meghalaya. 

The scheme aims to provide financial assistance and support to small and medium enterprises. 

It seeks to promote entrepreneurship and enhance the growth of the MSME sector in the state. 

The benefits include access to credit facilities and financial support for business development. 

Eligibility is primarily for small and medium enterprises registered in Meghalaya. 

The application process involves submitting a proposal to the relevant department for evaluation. 

The timeline for approval and disbursement of funds varies based on the application. 

The scheme is implemented by the Directorate of Industries and Commerce, Government of Meghalaya. 

Additional details about the scheme can be found on the official government website.
