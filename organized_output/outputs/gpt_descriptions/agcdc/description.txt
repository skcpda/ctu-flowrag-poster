The Agri-Clinics and Agri-Business Centres scheme is an initiative launched by the Government of India. 

This scheme was introduced in 2002 under the Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers' Welfare. 

The primary aim of the Agri-Clinics and Agri-Business Centres scheme is to provide self-employment opportunities to agricultural graduates.
