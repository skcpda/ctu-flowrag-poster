The MCUF scheme stands for the Mahatma Gandhi National Rural Employment Guarantee Act. 

It was launched to provide a legal guarantee for at least 100 days of unskilled wage employment in a financial year to every rural household. 

The primary objective is to enhance livelihood security in rural areas. 

The scheme aims to create durable assets and infrastructure in rural areas. 

It also seeks to strengthen the livelihood resource base of the rural poor. 

Eligible beneficiaries include every rural household that applies for work under the scheme. 

The application process involves submitting a demand for work to the local Gram Panchayat. 

The work is provided within 15 days of the application. 

The implementing authority is the Ministry of Rural Development at the central level. 

State governments are responsible for the implementation at the state level. 

The scheme is funded by the central government and state governments in a cost-sharing ratio. 

Payments are made directly to the bank accounts of the beneficiaries. 

The scheme is reviewed and monitored regularly to ensure transparency and accountability.
