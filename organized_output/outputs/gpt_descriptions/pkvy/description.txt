Pradhan Mantri Kaushal Vikas Yojana (PMKVY) is a skill development initiative launched by the Government of India. 

It aims to enhance the employability of youth by providing them with industry-relevant skill training. 

The scheme focuses on promoting skill development in various sectors to meet the demands of the job market. 

Benefits include financial assistance for training providers and stipends for trainees during the training period. 

Eligible beneficiaries are youth aged between 15 to 45 years who are either school dropouts or unemployed. 

The application process involves registration on the PMKVY portal or through affiliated training centers. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Skill Development and Entrepreneurship. 

Training programs under PMKVY are conducted on a short-term basis and can vary in duration. 

The scheme is periodically reviewed and updated to align with industry needs and technological advancements.
