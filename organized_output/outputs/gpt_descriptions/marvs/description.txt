**Scheme Name: MARVS (Mobile Application for Real-time Verification of Services)**

**Context or Background:**
MARVS is an initiative launched by the Government of India to enhance the delivery of welfare services.  
It aims to leverage technology to ensure transparency and accountability in the implementation of various government schemes.  
The initiative is part of the broader Digital India program which seeks to improve the efficiency of public service delivery.  
MARVS is designed to facilitate real-time verification of services provided to beneficiaries.  
The scheme addresses issues related to service delivery gaps and fraudulent practices in welfare schemes.  

**Objectives:**
The primary objective of MARVS is to provide a platform for real-time monitoring of service delivery.  
It aims to empower beneficiaries by enabling them to verify the services they are entitled to.  
The scheme seeks to reduce corruption and enhance transparency in the implementation of welfare programs.  
Another objective is to improve the overall efficiency of service delivery mechanisms across various sectors.  

**Benefits and Assistance:**
MARVS provides beneficiaries with the ability to check the status of services in real-time.  
It enhances the accountability of service providers by allowing beneficiaries to report discrepancies.  
The application facilitates better communication between beneficiaries and government authorities.  
By ensuring timely service delivery, MARVS contributes to improved welfare outcomes for citizens.  
The scheme also aids in the identification of bottlenecks in service delivery systems.  

**Eligibility:**
MARVS is intended for all beneficiaries of government welfare schemes.  
There are no specific eligibility criteria beyond being a registered beneficiary of a government program.  
Users must have access to a smartphone or device capable of running the MARVS application.  

**Application Process:**
Beneficiaries can download the MARVS application from official app stores.  
Users must register on the application using their mobile number and other required details.  
Once registered, beneficiaries can log in to access various features of the application.  
The application provides options to verify services and report issues directly to authorities.  

**Timeline or Frequency:**
The MARVS application operates on a continuous basis.  
Beneficiaries can use the application at any time to verify services or report issues.  
Updates and improvements to the application are made periodically based on user feedback.  

**Implementing Authorities or Agencies:**
The MARVS initiative is implemented by the Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology.  
State governments and local authorities are also involved in the execution of the scheme.  
Various departments responsible for welfare schemes collaborate to ensure effective implementation.
