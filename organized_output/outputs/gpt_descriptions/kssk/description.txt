The Kisan Samman Nidhi Scheme (KSSK) is a government initiative aimed at providing financial support to farmers in India. 

The scheme was launched on February 24, 2019. 

It is part of the government's efforts to enhance the income of farmers and ensure their financial stability. 

The primary objective of KSSK is to provide direct income support to small and marginal farmers. 

The scheme aims to alleviate the financial burden on farmers and improve their living standards. 

Under KSSK, eligible farmers receive an income support of Rs. 6,000 per year. 

This amount is disbursed in three equal installments of Rs. 2,000 each. 

The assistance is intended to help farmers meet their agricultural expenses and improve their productivity. 

The scheme is applicable to small and marginal farmers who own cultivable land. 

To be eligible for KSSK, a farmer must own less than 2 hectares of agricultural land. 

The scheme is available to all small and marginal farmers across India. 

However, certain categories of farmers are excluded from the scheme. 

These exclusions include institutional landholders and farmers who are ineligible for income tax. 

To apply for KSSK, farmers must register on the official government portal. 

Farmers can also visit their local revenue office or agriculture department for assistance. 

The application process requires the submission of relevant documents. 

These documents typically include land ownership records and identification proof. 

Once the application is submitted, it undergoes verification by the concerned authorities. 

The timeline for processing applications may vary depending on the state and local administration. 

Once approved, the financial assistance is credited directly to the bank account of the farmer. 

The frequency of payments is three times a year. 

The implementing authority for KSSK is the Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers' Welfare. 

State governments also play a crucial role in the implementation of the scheme. 

They are responsible for the identification and verification of eligible beneficiaries. 

The scheme is part of a broader strategy to enhance agricultural productivity in India. 

It aims to ensure that farmers have access to necessary financial resources.
