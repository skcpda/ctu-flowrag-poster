The Mid-Himalayan Development Scheme (MIDH) is an initiative by the Government of India. 

It aims to promote the development of horticulture in the mid-hill regions of the country. 

The objective is to enhance the productivity and income of farmers in these areas. 

The scheme provides financial assistance for various horticultural activities. 

This includes the establishment of nurseries and production of quality planting materials. 

It also supports the cultivation of fruits vegetables and flowers. 

Farmers can receive training and technical support under this scheme. 

Eligibility is primarily for farmers engaged in horticultural activities in mid-hill regions. 

The application process typically involves submitting a proposal to the relevant state horticulture department. 

The timeline for assistance may vary based on the specific activity and state guidelines. 

The Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers' Welfare is the implementing authority for this scheme. 

State horticulture departments also play a crucial role in implementation. 

The scheme is part of a broader effort to improve agricultural sustainability and farmer livelihoods.
