The National Pension System - Tier II Scheme for Employees of the State Government (NPS-TSEP) is a government initiative aimed at providing pension benefits to state government employees. 

The objective of NPS-TSEP is to ensure financial security for employees after retirement. 

The scheme allows employees to contribute to a pension fund during their service. 

This fund accumulates over time and provides a regular income post-retirement. 

Benefits include tax exemptions on contributions and the ability to withdraw funds under certain conditions. 

Eligibility is limited to state government employees who are part of the NPS. 

The application process typically involves registration through the official NPS portal. 

Employees must provide necessary documentation and complete the required forms. 

The frequency of contributions is generally monthly as per the employee's salary cycle. 

The Pension Fund Regulatory and Development Authority (PFRDA) oversees the implementation of the scheme. 

State governments may have specific guidelines regarding the operation of the scheme.
