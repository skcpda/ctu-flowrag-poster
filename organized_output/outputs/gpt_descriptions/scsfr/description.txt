The scheme SCSFR stands for the Scheme for the Support of the Central Sector Scheme for Financial Assistance to the Scheduled Castes. 

It aims to provide financial assistance to Scheduled Castes for various developmental activities. 

The objectives include promoting entrepreneurship and self-employment among Scheduled Castes. 

The benefits include financial support for setting up small businesses and enhancing skills through training programs. 

Eligibility is primarily restricted to individuals belonging to Scheduled Castes. 

The application process involves submitting an application to the designated authority along with required documents. 

The timeline for processing applications may vary depending on the implementing agency. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment. 

Additional details regarding specific financial limits and training programs may be available through official notifications.
