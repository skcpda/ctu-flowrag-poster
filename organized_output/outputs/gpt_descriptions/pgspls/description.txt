The Pradhan Mantri Garib Kalyan Anna Yojana (PMGKAY) is a welfare scheme initiated by the Government of India. 

The scheme was launched in response to the economic challenges posed by the COVID-19 pandemic. 

Its primary objective is to provide food security to the poor and vulnerable sections of society. 

Under this scheme, beneficiaries receive free food grains for a specified period. 

Each eligible household is entitled to receive 5 kilograms of food grains per person per month. 

The scheme aims to alleviate hunger and ensure nutritional support during crises. 

Eligibility is based on the National Food Security Act criteria. 

The scheme covers all existing beneficiaries under the Public Distribution System. 

The application process does not require a separate application as it is linked to existing ration cards. 

The distribution of food grains occurs monthly through fair price shops. 

The scheme has been implemented by the Ministry of Consumer Affairs Food and Public Distribution. 

It is executed through state governments and union territory administrations. 

The scheme is expected to continue until the end of the COVID-19 pandemic situation. 

Additional details may vary based on state-specific implementations.
