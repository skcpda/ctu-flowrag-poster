The TEClS scheme stands for Technology Enabled Learning for Children with Special Needs. 

It was launched to enhance educational opportunities for children with disabilities. 

The objective is to provide accessible and inclusive education through technology. 

The scheme aims to improve learning outcomes and facilitate skill development. 

Benefits include access to digital learning resources and assistive technologies. 

The assistance is targeted towards children with disabilities in various educational settings. 

Eligibility is primarily for children with disabilities who are enrolled in schools. 

The application process involves registration through designated educational institutions. 

The timeline for implementation may vary based on the specific educational institution. 

The Ministry of Education is the primary implementing authority for this scheme. 

Additional details may include partnerships with NGOs and educational technology providers.
