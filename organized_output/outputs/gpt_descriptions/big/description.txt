The Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana is a flagship housing scheme initiated by the Government of India. 

It was launched on June 25 2015. 

The scheme aims to provide affordable housing to the urban poor. 

The initiative is part of the broader vision of the Government of India to ensure housing for all by the year 2022. 

The scheme is divided into two components. 

One component focuses on the urban areas while the other focuses on rural areas. 

The urban component is known as Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana Urban or PMAY-U. 

The rural component is known as Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana Gramin or PMAY-G. 

The primary objective of PMAY is to provide pucca houses to the homeless and those living in kutcha houses. 

The scheme aims to enhance the living standards of the urban poor. 

It seeks to provide financial assistance for the construction of houses. 

The scheme also promotes the use of sustainable and eco-friendly building materials. 

Under PMAY-U the government provides a subsidy on home loans. 

This subsidy is available for the economically weaker sections of society. 

The subsidy can be availed for a loan amount of up to Rs 2.67 lakh. 

The interest subsidy is up to 6.5 percent. 

This significantly reduces the burden of monthly repayments for beneficiaries. 

The scheme also provides for the construction of houses under the affordable housing segment. 

The houses constructed under PMAY are typically 30 square meters in size. 

They are designed to be affordable and accessible to low-income families. 

The beneficiaries are also encouraged to participate in the construction process. 

This is to ensure that the houses meet their specific needs and preferences. 

Eligibility for PMAY is primarily based on income levels. 

The scheme targets individuals from the Economically Weaker Section or EWS. 

It also includes those from the Lower Income Group or LIG. 

For EWS the annual income limit is set at Rs 3 lakh. 

For LIG the annual income limit is set between Rs 3 lakh and Rs 6 lakh. 

The scheme also prioritizes women beneficiaries. 

At least one woman member of the family must be the owner of the house. 

This is to promote women's empowerment and financial independence. 

Additionally the scheme gives preference to Scheduled Castes Scheduled Tribes and other backward classes. 

The application process for PMAY is designed to be user-friendly. 

Interested beneficiaries can apply online through the official PMAY website. 

They can also visit the Common Service Centers or CSCs for assistance. 

The application form requires basic personal information and income details. 

Beneficiaries must provide proof of income and identity. 

Once the application is submitted it is reviewed by the concerned authorities. 

The timeline for approval can vary based on the volume of applications. 

However the government aims to process applications as quickly as possible. 

The frequency of disbursement of subsidies is typically linked to the progress of construction. 

Beneficiaries receive the subsidy amount in their bank accounts. 

This is done in phases based on the completion of construction milestones. 

The implementing authority for PMAY is the Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs. 

The scheme is executed through various state and union territory governments. 

Local urban bodies play a crucial role in the implementation of the scheme. 

They are responsible for identifying beneficiaries and monitoring the construction process.

It has been instrumental in providing housing to millions of families. 

The government has set ambitious targets for the completion of houses under PMAY. 

By 2022 the aim is to construct over 20 million houses in urban areas alone.
