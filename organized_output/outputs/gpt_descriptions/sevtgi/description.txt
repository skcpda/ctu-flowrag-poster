The SEVTGI scheme stands for the Skill Enhancement and Vocational Training for the Girl Child Initiative. 

It aims to empower girl children through skill development and vocational training. 

The objective is to enhance employability and promote self-reliance among girls. 

The scheme provides training in various vocational skills to enable girls to secure employment. 

Benefits include skill development training and potential job placements. 

Eligibility criteria typically include being a girl child within a specific age range. 

The application process usually involves filling out a form and may require documentation to verify eligibility. 

The timeline for training programs may vary based on the specific vocational courses offered. 

Implementing authorities include the Ministry of Women and Child Development and associated state agencies.
