The Atal Jyoti Yojana is a significant initiative launched by the Government of India. 

This scheme aims to provide solar street lighting systems in rural and semi-urban areas. 

The initiative is part of the broader effort to promote renewable energy sources. 

It seeks to enhance the quality of life in rural areas by improving safety and security.
