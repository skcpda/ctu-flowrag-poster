The Pradhan Mantri Employment Generation Programme for Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes Welfare (PEGFSW) aims to provide self-employment opportunities to individuals from Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes. 

The objective is to promote entrepreneurship among marginalized communities. 

The scheme offers financial assistance for setting up small businesses and enterprises. 

Eligible beneficiaries include individuals from Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes aged between 18 and 45 years. 

The application process involves submitting a project proposal along with required documents to the designated authorities. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises. 

Financial assistance is provided in the form of a subsidy. 

The timeline for application processing and fund disbursement may vary based on the specific project and location. 

The scheme also emphasizes skill development and training for beneficiaries to enhance their business capabilities. 

Additional details regarding specific financial limits and project types can be obtained from official sources.
