The National Scheme for the Welfare of Children in Need of Care and Protection (NSCG) aims to provide support and welfare for children who are in difficult circumstances. 

The objective of the NSCG is to ensure the care and protection of children who are orphaned abandoned or in need of care. 

The scheme provides financial assistance to non-governmental organizations and institutions working for the welfare of these children. 

Benefits include support for shelter food education and health care for children in need. 

Eligibility criteria include being a child in need of care and protection as defined under the Juvenile Justice Act. 

The application process involves NGOs and institutions submitting proposals to the Ministry of Women and Child Development. 

The timeline for funding and assistance is determined based on the approval of proposals and the availability of funds. 

The implementing authority for the NSCG is the Ministry of Women and Child Development. 

The scheme also emphasizes the importance of rehabilitation and reintegration of children into society.
