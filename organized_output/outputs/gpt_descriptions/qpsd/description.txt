The Quality Public Service Delivery (QPSD) scheme aims to enhance the efficiency and effectiveness of public service delivery in India. 

The objective of the QPSD scheme is to improve the quality of services provided to citizens by government departments. 

The scheme focuses on ensuring transparency accountability and responsiveness in public service delivery. 

Benefits include streamlined processes reduced waiting times and improved access to services for citizens. 

Eligibility criteria may vary depending on the specific services being delivered under the scheme. 

The application process typically involves online submissions through designated government portals. 

The timeline for service delivery is expected to be expedited under the QPSD framework. 

Implementing authorities include various state and central government departments responsible for public service delivery. 

The scheme is part of the broader initiative to digitize and modernize government services in India.
