The scheme SGDTLY stands for the Sardar Ganga Singh Development and Training for Livelihoods of Youth. 

It aims to enhance the skills and employability of youth in rural areas. 

The primary objective is to provide vocational training and promote self-employment opportunities. 

Benefits include skill development training and financial assistance for starting small businesses. 

Eligibility criteria include being a resident of the targeted rural areas and being within a specific age range. 

The application process typically involves submitting an application form along with necessary documents to the designated local authority. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Rural Development in collaboration with state governments. 

Training sessions are conducted periodically based on demand and availability of resources. 

The scheme also focuses on creating awareness about various livelihood options among the youth.
