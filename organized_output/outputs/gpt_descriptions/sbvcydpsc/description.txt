The scheme SBVCYDPPSC stands for the Skill Development and Entrepreneurship Program for Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes.

It aims to enhance the employability of Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes through skill development and entrepreneurship training.

The benefits include training in various skills that are in demand in the job market.

Participants may also receive assistance in starting their own businesses.

Eligibility is primarily focused on individuals belonging to Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes.

The application process typically involves submitting an application form along with necessary documentation to the designated authorities.

The timeline for training programs may vary based on the specific skill being taught.

The implementing authorities include the Ministry of Skill Development and Entrepreneurship and various state governments.

Additional details may include partnerships with training institutes and organizations to facilitate skill development.
