The HRIDAY scheme stands for Heritage City Development and Augmentation Yojana. 

It was launched by the Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs in India. 

The scheme was initiated in January 2015. 

HRIDAY aims to preserve and revitalize the rich cultural heritage of cities in India. 

The scheme focuses on cities that are historically significant and have a rich cultural background. 

The initiative is part of the broader Smart Cities Mission. 

It seeks to enhance the quality of life for residents and promote tourism.

It aims to create a sustainable urban environment while maintaining the historical significance of these cities.
