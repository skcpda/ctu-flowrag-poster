The scheme is known as the Swami Vivekananda Swavalamban Yojana for the Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes.

It aims to provide financial assistance for self-employment and skill development among Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes.

The objective is to enhance the livelihood opportunities for these communities.

The benefits include financial support for setting up small businesses and vocational training programs.

Eligible beneficiaries are individuals from Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes.

The application process involves submitting a proposal to the concerned department or agency.

The timeline for approval and disbursement of funds may vary based on the specific project and application.

The implementing authority is the Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment.

Additional details may include the requirement for a project report and adherence to guidelines set by the implementing agency.
