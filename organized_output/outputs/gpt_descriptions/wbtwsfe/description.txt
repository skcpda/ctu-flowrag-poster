The West Bengal Tribal Welfare Scheme for Education (WBTWSFE) aims to enhance educational opportunities for tribal students in West Bengal. 

The objective is to provide financial assistance for the education of tribal children. 

The scheme offers scholarships to tribal students pursuing education from class 1 to post-graduation. 

Benefits include financial support for tuition fees and other educational expenses. 

Eligibility criteria include being a student belonging to a Scheduled Tribe in West Bengal. 

Students must be enrolled in recognized educational institutions. 

The application process involves submitting an online application through the official portal. 

The timeline for application submission typically aligns with the academic year. 

The implementing authority for this scheme is the West Bengal Tribal Development Department. 

The scheme aims to reduce dropout rates among tribal students and promote higher education.
