The Mukhyamantri Swasthya Bima Yojana (MSGBVN) is a health insurance scheme launched by the Government of Madhya Pradesh. 

The scheme aims to provide financial protection to families from health-related expenses. 

It focuses on improving access to quality healthcare services for economically weaker sections of society. 

The benefits include coverage for hospitalization expenses and pre-existing diseases. 

Eligible beneficiaries include families living below the poverty line and those identified under the Antyodaya scheme. 

The application process involves filling out a form available at designated centers or online. 

The scheme is implemented by the Department of Health and Family Welfare of Madhya Pradesh. 

The coverage is provided annually and is renewable each year. 

The scheme covers a wide range of medical treatments and procedures. 

Beneficiaries can access services at empaneled hospitals across the state. 

The scheme also aims to reduce out-of-pocket expenditure for healthcare.
