The scheme "OPS" refers to the Old Pension Scheme. 

It was reintroduced by various state governments in India to provide financial security to retired government employees. 

The objective is to ensure a guaranteed pension for employees after retirement. 

The benefits include a fixed monthly pension based on the last drawn salary. 

This scheme aims to provide financial stability and support to retired personnel. 

Eligibility typically includes government employees who were appointed before a certain date. 

The application process usually involves submitting a request through the respective state government's pension department. 

The timeline for pension disbursement is generally monthly after retirement. 

Implementing authorities vary by state but typically include the finance department or the pension office. 

The scheme is aimed at restoring the old pension benefits that were replaced by the National Pension System.
