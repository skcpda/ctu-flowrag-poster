The MDIIS scheme stands for the Market Development Initiative for Indian Startups. 

It was launched by the Ministry of Commerce and Industry. 

The scheme aims to promote and support Indian startups in the market. 

The objective is to enhance the competitiveness of startups in the global market. 

The benefits include financial assistance for marketing activities. 

Startups can receive support for participation in trade fairs and exhibitions. 

They can also avail assistance for market research and promotional activities. 

Eligibility is primarily for Indian startups recognized by the Department for Promotion of Industry and Internal Trade. 

The application process involves submitting an online application through the official portal. 

The timeline for receiving assistance varies based on the specific activity and approval process. 

The implementing authority is the Ministry of Commerce and Industry. 

The scheme is part of the broader initiative to boost the startup ecosystem in India.
