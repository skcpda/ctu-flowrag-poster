SAMS stands for Student Academic Management System. 

It is an initiative by the Government of Odisha. 

The scheme aims to streamline the academic management of students in various educational institutions. 

The objectives include improving the quality of education and enhancing the administrative efficiency of schools and colleges. 

Benefits include online admission processes and better tracking of student performance. 

The system provides a centralized platform for managing student data. 

Eligibility is primarily for students seeking admission in educational institutions in Odisha. 

The application process involves registering on the SAMS portal. 

Students must fill in the required details and submit their applications online. 

The timeline for admissions varies based on the academic calendar of the respective institutions. 

The implementing authority is the Directorate of Higher Education in Odisha. 

SAMS also facilitates the allocation of scholarships and financial aid to eligible students. 

It promotes transparency and accountability in the education sector.
