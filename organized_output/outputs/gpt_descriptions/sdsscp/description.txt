The scheme is known as the Skill Development Support for Scheduled Castes (SDSSCP). 

It aims to enhance the employability of Scheduled Caste individuals through skill development initiatives. 

The primary objective is to provide financial assistance for skill development training programs. 

The benefits include training in various sectors to improve job prospects for Scheduled Caste individuals. 

Eligible beneficiaries are individuals belonging to Scheduled Castes who are between the ages of 15 and 45 years. 

The application process typically involves submitting an application form along with necessary documents to the designated authorities. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment. 

The frequency of assistance may vary based on the specific training programs and their duration. 

Additional details may include partnerships with training institutions to facilitate skill development.
