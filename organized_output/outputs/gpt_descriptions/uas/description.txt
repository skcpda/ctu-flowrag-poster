The UAS scheme stands for the Unmanned Aircraft Systems scheme. 

It aims to promote the use of drones in various sectors such as agriculture and disaster management. 

The objective is to enhance efficiency and productivity through the integration of drone technology. 

Benefits include improved crop monitoring and management. 

Farmers can utilize drones for precision agriculture to increase yield. 

The scheme also supports research and development in drone technology. 

Eligibility criteria typically include farmers and agricultural professionals. 

The application process involves registration with the relevant agricultural department. 

Applicants may need to provide details about their farming practices and technology usage. 

The timeline for implementation varies based on specific project proposals. 

The Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers' Welfare is the primary implementing authority. 

State agricultural departments may also play a role in execution. 

The scheme aligns with the government's focus on digital agriculture and modernization.
