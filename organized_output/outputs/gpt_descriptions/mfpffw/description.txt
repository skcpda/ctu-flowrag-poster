The MFPFFW scheme stands for the Mechanism for Marketing of Minor Forest Produce through Minimum Support Price for Minor Forest Produce. 

This scheme was launched to provide a fair price to gatherers of minor forest produce. 

The objective is to enhance the income of forest dwellers and promote sustainable harvesting practices. 

The scheme aims to ensure that the gatherers receive a minimum support price for their produce. 

Benefits include financial security for forest gatherers and improved livelihoods. 

Assistance is provided in the form of a minimum support price for various minor forest produce items. 

Eligibility is primarily for Scheduled Tribes and other forest dwellers involved in the collection of minor forest produce. 

The application process involves registration with the concerned state government authorities. 

The timeline for the implementation of the scheme is ongoing and is reviewed periodically. 

Implementing authorities include the Ministry of Tribal Affairs and state forest departments. 

The scheme also promotes the establishment of a robust market infrastructure for minor forest produce. 

It encourages cooperative societies among gatherers to strengthen their bargaining power. 

The scheme contributes to the conservation of forest resources and biodiversity.
