The SSVY scheme stands for the Satyajit Ray Film and Television Institute's Student Welfare Scheme. 

It was initiated to provide financial assistance to students pursuing courses in film and television. 

The primary objective is to support students from economically weaker sections. 

Benefits include financial aid for tuition fees and other educational expenses. 

Eligibility is typically based on the student's economic background and enrollment in a relevant course. 

The application process usually involves submitting a form along with necessary documents to the institute. 

The timeline for application and disbursement of funds may vary each academic year. 

The implementing authority is the Satyajit Ray Film and Television Institute. 

Further details may be available on the official institute website or through their administration office.
