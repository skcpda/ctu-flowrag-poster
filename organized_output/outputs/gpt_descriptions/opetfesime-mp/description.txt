The Opetfesime scheme is a welfare initiative implemented in Madhya Pradesh. 

It aims to provide financial assistance to marginalized communities. 

The primary objective is to enhance the livelihood opportunities for the beneficiaries. 

The scheme offers various benefits including direct cash transfers and skill development programs. 

Eligibility criteria include being a resident of Madhya Pradesh and belonging to a marginalized community. 

The application process involves filling out a form available at designated government offices or online portals. 

The timeline for disbursement of benefits is typically quarterly.
