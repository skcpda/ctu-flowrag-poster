The Scheme for Financial Assistance for the Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes (SFASISS) aims to provide financial support to individuals from Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes. 

The objective of the scheme is to enhance the socio-economic conditions of these communities. 

The benefits include financial assistance for education and skill development. 

Eligibility is limited to individuals belonging to Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes. 

The application process involves submitting the required documents to the designated authority. 

The timeline for disbursement of funds is determined by the implementing agency. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment. 

Additional details regarding specific financial amounts or types of assistance may vary based on state policies.
