Yuvasree is a welfare scheme launched by the Government of West Bengal. 

It aims to provide financial assistance to unemployed youth in the state. 

The objective is to enhance the employability of young individuals and support them in their job search. 

Under this scheme beneficiaries receive a monthly stipend. 

The amount of the stipend is INR 1500 per month for a duration of 2 years. 

Eligibility criteria include being a resident of West Bengal. 

Applicants must be between the ages of 18 and 45 years. 

They should be unemployed and have completed at least Class VIII. 

The application process involves filling out an online application form. 

Applicants must provide necessary documents such as identity proof and educational certificates. 

The scheme is implemented by the Department of Labour and Employment, Government of West Bengal. 

The application can be submitted through the official website or designated offices. 

The scheme is reviewed periodically to assess its impact and effectiveness. 

Beneficiaries are selected based on the information provided in their applications. 

The scheme is part of the state's efforts to address youth unemployment.
