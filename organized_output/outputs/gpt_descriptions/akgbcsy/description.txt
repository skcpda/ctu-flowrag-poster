The Akanksha Ghar Bhatta Yojana is a welfare scheme initiated by the Government of India.

This scheme is designed to provide financial assistance to the economically weaker sections of society.

The primary objective of the Akanksha Ghar Bhatta Yojana is to support individuals and families who are in need of housing assistance.

The scheme aims to ensure that every citizen has access to affordable housing.

It specifically targets those who are unable to afford housing due to financial constraints.

The Akanksha Ghar Bhatta Yojana is part of the larger initiative to promote housing for all.

This initiative aligns with the government's vision of providing shelter to every citizen.

The scheme offers various benefits and assistance to eligible beneficiaries.

Financial assistance is provided to help cover housing costs.

This assistance can be used for purchasing or constructing a house.

The scheme also aims to reduce homelessness and improve living conditions.

Beneficiaries can receive a monthly housing allowance under this scheme.

This allowance is intended to ease the burden of housing costs.

The amount of assistance varies based on the specific needs of the beneficiaries.

Eligibility for the Akanksha Ghar Bhatta Yojana is determined by several criteria.

Applicants must belong to the economically weaker sections of society.

They should not own any residential property.

The scheme is primarily aimed at individuals and families living in urban areas.

Priority is given to marginalized groups such as scheduled castes and scheduled tribes.

Women and single-parent households are also given preference.
