The Mukhyamantri Swami Vivekananda Yuva Sahayta Yojana (MSVYMP) is a welfare scheme launched by the Government of Madhya Pradesh. 

The scheme aims to promote self-employment among the youth of the state. 

It provides financial assistance to young entrepreneurs to establish their own businesses. 

The benefits include a subsidy on the loan amount taken by the beneficiaries. 

The scheme targets youth aged between 18 to 40 years. 

Eligible applicants must be residents of Madhya Pradesh. 

They should have a viable business plan and must not have any outstanding loans from government schemes. 

The application process involves submitting an online application through the official portal. 

Applicants must provide necessary documents such as identity proof and business plan. 

The scheme is implemented by the Department of Skill Development and Entrepreneurship, Madhya Pradesh. 

Financial assistance is provided in the form of a subsidy on loans taken from banks or financial institutions. 

The subsidy amount varies based on the type of business and the category of the applicant. 

The scheme is reviewed periodically to ensure its effectiveness and reach. 

The frequency of assistance is linked to the loan disbursement schedule.
