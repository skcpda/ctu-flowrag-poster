The Mukhyamantri Mahila Samriddhi Yojana (MMSPY) is a welfare scheme launched by the Government of Madhya Pradesh. 

The scheme aims to empower women by providing them with financial assistance to promote self-employment and entrepreneurship. 

It focuses on enhancing the economic status of women in the state. 

Under this scheme, women can receive financial support for various income-generating activities. 

The assistance can be used for starting small businesses or enhancing existing ones. 

Eligible beneficiaries include women who are residents of Madhya Pradesh. 

Women from economically weaker sections are prioritized for assistance. 

The application process involves submitting a form along with necessary documents to the designated authorities. 

The scheme is implemented by the Department of Women and Child Development in Madhya Pradesh. 

The frequency of assistance disbursement is determined by the specific project or business plan approved. 

Additional details regarding specific financial limits and types of businesses supported may vary.
