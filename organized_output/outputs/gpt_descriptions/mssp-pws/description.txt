The MSSP-PWS scheme stands for the Mahatma Gandhi National Rural Employment Guarantee Act (MGNREGA) - Panchayat Water Supply Scheme. 

This scheme is part of the broader initiative to enhance rural livelihoods and improve water supply in rural areas. 

The objective of the scheme is to provide a legal guarantee for at least 100 days of unskilled wage employment in a financial year to every rural household. 

It aims to create durable assets and enhance livelihood security in rural areas through water supply projects. 

The benefits include the provision of employment opportunities and the development of water supply infrastructure in rural communities. 

Eligible beneficiaries include all rural households seeking employment under the MGNREGA framework. 

The application process typically involves submitting a job card application at the local Panchayat office. 

The timeline for employment is based on the demand for work and can vary throughout the year. 

The implementing authorities include the Ministry of Rural Development and local Panchayat bodies. 

The scheme also focuses on enhancing the quality of life and promoting sustainable development in rural areas.
