The VSSAY scheme stands for the Vidhwa Sahay Yojana. 

It is a welfare initiative aimed at providing financial assistance to widows in India. 

The objective of the scheme is to support widows by providing them with a monthly pension. 

This financial aid helps improve their living conditions and ensures their economic independence. 

Eligible beneficiaries include widows who are residents of India and belong to economically weaker sections. 

The age limit for applicants is generally set at 18 years and above. 

The application process typically involves submitting an application form along with necessary documents such as identity proof and income certificate. 

Applications can be submitted at designated government offices or online through official portals. 

The scheme provides a monthly pension amount which may vary by state. 

The frequency of disbursement is monthly. 

The implementing authority for the scheme varies by state but generally involves the Department of Social Welfare or similar agencies. 

Additional details regarding specific state implementations may vary.
