The SPAY scheme stands for the Scheme for Promotion of Adolescent and Youth. 

It was launched to address the health and development needs of adolescents and youth in India. 

The primary objective is to promote health education and awareness among adolescents. 

The scheme aims to empower young people through skill development and vocational training. 

It also focuses on improving access to healthcare services for adolescents. 

Benefits include access to health services and information on reproductive health. 

Assistance is provided in the form of educational materials and training programs. 

Eligibility is generally targeted towards adolescents and youth aged between 10 to 24 years. 

The application process may vary by state and typically involves registration with local health authorities. 

The timeline for implementation is ongoing with periodic assessments. 

The Ministry of Health and Family Welfare is the primary implementing authority for this scheme. 

State health departments may also play a role in local implementation.
