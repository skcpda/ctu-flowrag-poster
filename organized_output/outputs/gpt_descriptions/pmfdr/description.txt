The Pradhan Mantri Fasal Bima Yojana (PMFBY) is a government welfare scheme launched in 2016. 

It aims to provide financial support to farmers in the event of crop failure due to natural calamities. 

The objective is to stabilize the income of farmers and encourage them to adopt modern agricultural practices. 

The scheme offers insurance coverage for various crops against risks such as droughts floods and pests. 

Farmers can receive compensation based on the area yield and the extent of loss. 

Eligibility is open to all farmers including those who cultivate food crops oilseeds and cash crops. 

Farmers must be registered under the scheme to avail of benefits. 

The application process involves enrolling through the nearest bank or insurance company. 

Farmers need to provide necessary documents such as land records and identity proof. 

The scheme operates on a seasonal basis with farmers required to enroll before the sowing season. 

The premium rates are subsidized by the government to make it affordable for farmers. 

The implementing authorities include the Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers Welfare and state governments. 

The scheme is operational across all states and union territories in India. 

Compensation is paid directly to the bank accounts of the farmers. 

The scheme also promotes the use of technology for assessing crop loss. 

Farmers can track their claims through the official PMFBY portal.
