The Scheme for Development of Agro-Ecosystem Enterprises (SDAEE) aims to promote sustainable agricultural practices. 

It focuses on enhancing the livelihoods of farmers through the development of agro-ecosystem enterprises. 

The objectives include increasing productivity and profitability in agriculture. 

The scheme also aims to promote the use of renewable resources and sustainable practices. 

Benefits include financial assistance for setting up agro-ecosystem enterprises. 

Farmers can receive training and capacity-building support. 

Eligibility criteria typically include being a registered farmer or a cooperative. 

The application process generally involves submitting an application to the relevant agricultural department. 

The timeline for processing applications may vary based on the specific state or region. 

Implementing authorities include the Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers' Welfare. 

State agricultural departments also play a role in the implementation of the scheme.
