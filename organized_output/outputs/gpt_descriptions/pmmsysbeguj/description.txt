The Pradhan Mantri Matsya Sampada Yojana (PMMSY) is a government initiative aimed at enhancing fish production and promoting sustainable fisheries in India. 

The scheme was launched to increase fish production and productivity in a sustainable manner. 

It aims to create a framework for the development of the fisheries sector. 

The benefits include financial assistance for fish farmers and fishermen. 

It also provides support for the establishment of fish processing units and cold chain facilities. 

Eligibility criteria include being a registered fish farmer or a member of a fishing community. 

The application process involves submitting a proposal to the relevant fisheries department. 

The timeline for assistance varies based on the specific project and its approval. 

The Ministry of Fisheries Animal Husbandry and Dairying is the implementing authority for this scheme. 

The scheme also focuses on promoting the welfare of fishermen and fish farmers through skill development and training programs.
