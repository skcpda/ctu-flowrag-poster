The scheme SCY stands for the Skill Certification of Youth. 

It aims to provide skill development and certification to the youth of India. 

The objective is to enhance employability and promote entrepreneurship among young individuals. 

The scheme offers training programs in various sectors. 

Participants receive skill certification upon successful completion of training. 

Eligibility criteria include being a youth aged between 15 to 29 years. 

The application process involves enrolling through designated training centers. 

The timeline for training programs varies based on the specific skill being taught. 

Implementing authorities include the Ministry of Skill Development and Entrepreneurship. 

The scheme is part of the broader initiative to boost skill development in the country.
