MYSY stands for Mukhyamantri Yuva Swavalamban Yojana. 

It is a scheme launched by the Government of Gujarat. 

The scheme aims to provide financial assistance to students for higher education. 

The objective is to promote self-employment and entrepreneurship among youth. 

Eligible beneficiaries include students who have completed their Class 12 education. 

Students must be residents of Gujarat. 

They should have secured admission in professional courses in recognized institutions. 

The scheme provides financial assistance for tuition fees and other educational expenses. 

The assistance amount varies based on the course and institution. 

Students can apply online through the official website of the scheme. 

The application process requires submission of necessary documents such as identity proof and admission letter. 

The scheme is implemented by the Department of Education, Government of Gujarat. 

The timeline for disbursement of funds is typically aligned with the academic calendar. 

The scheme is reviewed annually to assess its impact and make necessary adjustments.
