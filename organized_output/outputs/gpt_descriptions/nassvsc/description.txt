The National Agricultural Science and Technology Management System (NASSVSC) is an initiative aimed at enhancing agricultural research and development in India. 

The objective of NASSVSC is to strengthen the management of agricultural science and technology by promoting effective coordination among various stakeholders. 

The scheme seeks to improve the quality of agricultural research and technology dissemination. 

Benefits include increased productivity and sustainability in agriculture through better research outcomes. 

Eligibility criteria are primarily focused on agricultural research institutions and organizations involved in agricultural science and technology. 

The application process involves submission of proposals by eligible institutions to the relevant authorities. 

The timeline for the implementation of projects under NASSVSC may vary based on the specific project approved. 

The scheme is implemented by the Indian Council of Agricultural Research (ICAR) and other associated agricultural research institutions. 

NASSVSC also aims to facilitate capacity building and training for researchers and practitioners in the agricultural sector.
