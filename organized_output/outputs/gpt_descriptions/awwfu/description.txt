The AWWFU scheme stands for the Anganwadi Workers Welfare Fund Scheme. 

This scheme was introduced by the Government of India to provide financial assistance and support to Anganwadi workers across the country. 

Anganwadi workers play a crucial role in the implementation of the Integrated Child Development Services program. 

They are responsible for delivering essential services related to health nutrition and education to children under the age of six and their mothers. 

The AWWFU scheme aims to enhance the welfare of these workers who often work in challenging conditions and receive minimal financial compensation.
