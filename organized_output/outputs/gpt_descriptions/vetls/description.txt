The Vetls scheme is a welfare initiative aimed at providing financial assistance to livestock farmers in India. 

The primary objective of the scheme is to enhance the productivity and income of livestock farmers. 

The scheme offers benefits such as subsidies for purchasing livestock and financial support for veterinary services. 

Eligibility for the scheme typically includes farmers engaged in livestock rearing. 

The application process generally involves submitting an application form along with necessary documents to the local agricultural or veterinary department. 

The timeline for processing applications may vary based on the implementing agency. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Animal Husbandry and Dairying at the central level along with state governments. 

Additional details regarding specific benefits and assistance may vary by state.
