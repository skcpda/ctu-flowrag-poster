The Pradhan Mantri Bhartiya Jan Aushadhi Pariyojana (PMBJP) was launched to provide quality medicines at affordable prices. 

The objective is to ensure that essential medicines are available to all citizens at low costs. 

The scheme aims to reduce out-of-pocket expenditure on healthcare. 

It promotes the use of generic medicines which are equally effective as branded drugs. 

The benefits include access to over 1,000 medicines and 240 surgical items at reduced prices. 

The scheme also supports the establishment of Jan Aushadhi Kendras across the country. 

Eligibility to purchase medicines under this scheme is open to all citizens. 

The application process for setting up a Jan Aushadhi Kendra requires interested individuals to submit an application to the Bureau of Pharma Public Sector Undertakings of India. 

The timeline for the establishment of a Kendra varies based on the approval process. 

The implementing authority for the scheme is the Department of Pharmaceuticals under the Ministry of Chemicals and Fertilizers. 

The scheme also aims to create awareness about the benefits of generic medicines among the public. 

It contributes to the government's vision of providing affordable healthcare to all.
