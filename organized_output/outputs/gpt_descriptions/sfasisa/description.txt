The Scheme for Financial Assistance to Scheduled Castes for Self Employment is known as SFASISA. 

It was launched to provide financial support to Scheduled Caste individuals for self-employment ventures. 

The objective is to enhance the livelihood opportunities for Scheduled Caste communities. 

The scheme aims to promote entrepreneurship and self-reliance among beneficiaries. 

Financial assistance is provided in the form of a subsidy for setting up small businesses. 

The amount of assistance varies based on the project cost and the category of beneficiaries. 

Eligible beneficiaries include individuals from Scheduled Caste communities. 

Applicants must be between the ages of 18 and 55 years. 

The application process involves submitting a project proposal to the concerned department. 

Applications can be submitted online or through designated offices. 

The timeline for processing applications may vary based on the department's workload. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment. 

State governments may also have their own implementing agencies for the scheme. 

Additional details about specific project costs and subsidy percentages can be obtained from official sources.
