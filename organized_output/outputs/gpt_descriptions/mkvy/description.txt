Mahatma Gandhi National Rural Employment Guarantee Act is commonly known as MGNREGA. 

It was enacted in 2005 to enhance livelihood security in rural areas. 

The primary objective is to provide at least 100 days of unskilled wage employment in a financial year to every rural household. 

The scheme aims to create durable assets and strengthen the livelihood resource base of rural households. 

Benefits include guaranteed wage employment and the creation of public assets like roads and irrigation facilities. 

The assistance is provided in the form of wages for the work done. 

Eligibility is limited to adult members of rural households. 

Households must apply to the local Gram Panchayat to register under the scheme. 

The application process involves filling out a job card application form. 

Once registered job cards are issued to eligible households. 

The scheme operates throughout the year. 

The implementing authority is the Ministry of Rural Development at the central level. 

State governments are responsible for the implementation at the state level. 

The scheme is funded by the central government and state governments in a specified ratio. 

Regular monitoring and evaluation are conducted to ensure effective implementation.
