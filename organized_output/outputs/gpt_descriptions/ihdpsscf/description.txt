The Indian government welfare scheme known as the Integrated Housing Development Programme for Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes Families (IHDPS-SCF) is designed to provide housing assistance to marginalized communities in India. 

This scheme is part of a broader initiative to improve the living conditions of Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes. 

The context of this scheme arises from the recognition of the historical injustices faced by these communities. 

Housing is a fundamental human right and access to adequate housing is essential for the dignity and well-being of individuals and families.

The IHDPS-SCF aims to ensure that Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes families have access to safe and affordable housing. 

The primary objective of the IHDPS-SCF is to provide financial assistance for the construction of houses. 

This assistance is intended to enable beneficiaries to build their own homes. 

The scheme also aims to promote the overall development of these communities by improving their living standards.
