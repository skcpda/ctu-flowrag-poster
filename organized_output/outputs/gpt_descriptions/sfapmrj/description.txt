The scheme is known as the State Financial Assistance for the Purchase of Motorized Rickshaws for the Differently Abled Persons (SFAPMRJ). 

It aims to provide financial assistance to differently-abled individuals for purchasing motorized rickshaws. 

The objective is to enhance mobility and promote self-employment among differently-abled persons. 

The benefits include financial support for the purchase of motorized rickshaws. 

Eligible beneficiaries are differently-abled individuals who meet specific criteria set by the state government. 

The application process typically involves submitting an application form along with required documents to the designated authority. 

The timeline for processing applications may vary based on the state government’s procedures. 

Implementing authorities include the respective state government departments responsible for welfare and social justice. 

Additional details regarding the exact amount of financial assistance and specific eligibility criteria may vary by state.
