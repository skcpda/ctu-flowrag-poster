The Pradhan Mantri Swasthya Suraksha Yojana Maharashtra is a health initiative launched by the Government of Maharashtra. 

The scheme aims to provide accessible and affordable healthcare services to the underprivileged sections of society. 

It focuses on enhancing healthcare infrastructure and improving the quality of healthcare services in the state. 

The benefits include financial assistance for medical treatments and access to various health facilities. 

Eligible beneficiaries are those belonging to economically weaker sections and marginalized communities. 

The application process typically involves submitting relevant documents to designated health authorities or online platforms. 

The scheme is implemented by the Maharashtra State Government through its health department. 

Additional details about specific benefits and the exact application process may vary and should be verified with local health authorities.
