The TFRS scheme stands for the Tribal Forest Rights Scheme. 

It aims to recognize and vest the rights of forest-dwelling Scheduled Tribes and other traditional forest dwellers. 

The objective is to ensure that these communities have access to forest resources for their livelihood. 

The scheme provides legal recognition of forest rights to eligible individuals and communities. 

Benefits include the right to collect non-timber forest products and the right to protect, regenerate, and manage forest resources. 

Eligibility is primarily for Scheduled Tribes and other traditional forest dwellers who have been residing in the forest for at least three generations. 

The application process involves submitting claims to the Gram Sabha or local village council. 

The Gram Sabha is responsible for verifying and recommending the claims. 

The timeline for processing claims can vary based on local governance structures. 

The Ministry of Tribal Affairs is the implementing authority for the scheme. 

State governments also play a crucial role in the implementation at the local level. 

The scheme is part of a broader effort to empower tribal communities and ensure their rights over forest resources.
