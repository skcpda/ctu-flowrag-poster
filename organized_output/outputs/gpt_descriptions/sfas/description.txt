The Scheme for Financial Assistance to Farmers (SFAS) aims to provide financial support to farmers in India. 

The objective of SFAS is to enhance the income of farmers and improve their livelihoods. 

The scheme offers direct financial assistance to eligible farmers to support agricultural activities. 

Benefits include monetary support for purchasing seeds fertilizers and other agricultural inputs. 

Eligibility criteria typically include being a small or marginal farmer. 

Farmers must also possess valid land records to qualify for assistance. 

The application process generally involves submitting an application form along with necessary documents to the local agricultural office. 

The timeline for disbursal of funds may vary based on the specific implementation by state governments. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers' Welfare at the central level. 

State governments may also have their own guidelines for the implementation of SFAS.
