The Mukhyamantri Kanya Abhiyan Pradhan Mantri Suraksha Yojana (MKAPS) is a welfare scheme launched by the Government of Madhya Pradesh. 

The scheme aims to improve the health and education of girls in the state. 

It focuses on empowering girls through financial assistance and support for their education. 

The benefits include financial aid for education and health services for girls. 

Eligible beneficiaries are girls from economically weaker sections of society. 

The scheme targets girls from birth until they complete their education. 

The application process involves submitting necessary documents to the local government office or online through the official portal. 

The scheme is implemented by the Department of Women and Child Development in Madhya Pradesh. 

The timeline for disbursement of benefits is aligned with the academic year and health check-up schedules. 

The scheme also promotes awareness about the importance of girl child education and health.
