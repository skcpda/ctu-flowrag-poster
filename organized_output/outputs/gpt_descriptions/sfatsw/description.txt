The Scheme for Financial Assistance to Scheduled Tribes for Skill Development and Employment (SFATSW) aims to enhance the employability of Scheduled Tribe individuals. 

The objective of the scheme is to provide financial assistance for skill development and promote self-employment among Scheduled Tribe communities. 

The benefits include financial support for skill development training programs and assistance for self-employment ventures. 

Eligibility is primarily limited to individuals belonging to Scheduled Tribe communities. 

The application process typically involves submitting a form along with required documents to the designated implementing authority. 

The timeline for the scheme may vary based on the specific training programs and projects undertaken. 

The implementing authority for this scheme is the Ministry of Tribal Affairs. 

Additional details may include specific training partners and the types of skills being promoted under the scheme.
