The Sogateakva scheme is a government initiative aimed at enhancing the livelihoods of marginalized communities in India. 

The primary objective of the scheme is to provide financial assistance and support for sustainable agricultural practices. 

The scheme benefits farmers by offering subsidies for agricultural inputs and promoting organic farming techniques. 

Eligibility for the scheme typically includes small and marginal farmers from scheduled castes and tribes. 

The application process involves submitting a form to the local agricultural department along with necessary documents. 

The timeline for disbursing benefits may vary based on the specific agricultural cycle and project implementation. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers' Welfare at the central level. 

State agricultural departments also play a crucial role in executing the scheme at the local level.
