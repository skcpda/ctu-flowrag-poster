The Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana (PMAY) aims to provide affordable housing to the urban poor. 

It was launched in June 2015. 

The objective is to ensure that every family has a pucca house with basic amenities by 2022. 

The scheme offers financial assistance for the construction or enhancement of houses. 

Benefits include interest subsidy on home loans and direct financial assistance for housing construction. 

The eligibility criteria include being a citizen of India and belonging to the Economically Weaker Section or Low Income Group. 

Applicants must not own a house in their name or in the name of any family member. 

The application process can be completed online through the official PMAY website or through Common Service Centers. 

The timeline for approval and disbursement of funds varies based on the application and project completion. 

The Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs is the implementing authority for this scheme. 

State governments and urban local bodies also play a role in the implementation. 

The scheme is part of the larger initiative to promote housing for all by 2022.
