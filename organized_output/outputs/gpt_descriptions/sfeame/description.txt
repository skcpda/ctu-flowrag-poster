The SFEAME scheme stands for the Skill Development for the Empowerment of Adolescents and Marginalized Youth. 

It aims to provide skill development training to adolescents and marginalized youth. 

The objective is to enhance employability and empower youth through skill development. 

The scheme offers vocational training programs in various sectors. 

It provides financial assistance for training and skill development activities. 

Eligibility criteria include being an adolescent or a marginalized youth aged between 15 to 29 years. 

The application process typically involves registration through designated training centers or online platforms. 

The timeline for training programs may vary based on the specific vocational courses offered. 

The implementing authorities include the Ministry of Skill Development and Entrepreneurship. 

Additional details may include partnerships with various training institutes and NGOs for effective implementation.
