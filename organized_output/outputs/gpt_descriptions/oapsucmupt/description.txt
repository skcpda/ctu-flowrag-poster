The Old Age Pension Scheme for Unmarried Persons (OAPSUCMUPT) is a welfare initiative by the Government of India. 

It aims to provide financial assistance to unmarried senior citizens. 

The objective is to ensure a dignified life for elderly unmarried individuals. 

Eligible beneficiaries are unmarried persons aged 60 years and above. 

The scheme provides a monthly pension to eligible individuals. 

The amount of pension varies by state and is determined by the respective state governments. 

The application process typically involves submitting an application form along with required documents. 

Documents may include proof of age and unmarried status. 

Applications can usually be submitted at local government offices or online portals. 

The timeline for disbursement of pensions is generally monthly. 

The implementing authorities are usually the state government departments responsible for social welfare. 

Specific details may vary by state as the scheme is administered at the state level.
