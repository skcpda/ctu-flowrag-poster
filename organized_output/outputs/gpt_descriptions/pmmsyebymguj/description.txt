The Pradhan Mantri Matsya Sampada Yojana (PMMSY) aims to enhance fish production and productivity in India. 

It focuses on sustainable development of the fisheries sector. 

The objectives include increasing fish production to 22 million tonnes by 2024-25. 

It also aims to provide employment opportunities and improve the livelihoods of fishers and fish farmers. 

The scheme offers financial assistance for various activities such as aquaculture, fisheries infrastructure, and marketing. 

Beneficiaries can receive subsidies for setting up fish farms and purchasing equipment. 

Eligibility is primarily for fishers, fish farmers, and individuals involved in the fisheries sector. 

The application process involves submitting a proposal to the respective state fisheries department. 

The timeline for fund disbursement may vary based on project approval and implementation. 

The scheme is implemented by the Department of Fisheries under the Ministry of Fisheries Animal Husbandry and Dairying. 

It also involves collaboration with state governments and other stakeholders in the fisheries sector.
