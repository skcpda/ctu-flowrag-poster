The SCPMS scheme stands for the Scheduled Castes Post-Matric Scholarship Scheme. 

It is designed to provide financial assistance to students from Scheduled Castes pursuing post-matriculation education. 

The objective of the scheme is to promote education among Scheduled Caste students and reduce dropout rates. 

The benefits include scholarships that cover tuition fees and maintenance allowances. 

The assistance is provided for various courses including professional and technical courses. 

Eligibility is limited to students belonging to Scheduled Castes who are enrolled in post-matriculation courses. 

The family income of the applicant must not exceed a specified limit set by the government. 

The application process typically involves submitting an online application along with required documents. 

Students must provide proof of caste and income along with their educational details. 

The timeline for scholarship disbursement varies but is generally processed annually. 

The implementing authority for the scheme is the Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment. 

State governments may also have their own implementing agencies for the scheme. 

The scheme aims to ensure that financial constraints do not hinder the education of Scheduled Caste students.
