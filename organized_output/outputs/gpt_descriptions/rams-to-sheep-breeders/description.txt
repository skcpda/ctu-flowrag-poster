The Rams to Sheep Breeders scheme is an initiative by the Government of India. 

It aims to enhance the productivity of sheep farming in the country. 

The objective is to provide quality rams to sheep breeders to improve the genetic quality of their flocks. 

The scheme benefits sheep breeders by increasing wool and meat production. 

It also aims to boost the income of sheep farmers through better breeding practices. 

Eligibility criteria include being a registered sheep breeder or farmer. 

The application process involves submitting a request to the local animal husbandry department. 

The timeline for the distribution of rams may vary based on the breeding season and availability. 

The implementing authority for this scheme is the Department of Animal Husbandry and Dairying. 

Additional details about the specific breeds of rams provided may vary based on regional requirements.
