The Tamil Nadu Construction Workers Welfare Board scheme is designed to provide welfare benefits to construction workers in Tamil Nadu. 

The primary objective is to ensure the welfare and social security of construction workers. 

Benefits include financial assistance for medical treatment education marriage and death of a family member. 

Eligibility is limited to registered construction workers who are members of the Tamil Nadu Construction Workers Welfare Board. 

The application process involves submitting an application form along with required documents to the local welfare board office. 

The timeline for processing applications may vary based on the specific benefit being applied for. 

The Tamil Nadu Construction Workers Welfare Board is the implementing authority for this scheme. 

Additional details may include various schemes under the board such as maternity assistance and pension schemes.
