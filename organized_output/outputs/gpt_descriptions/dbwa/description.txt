The Deen Dayal Antyodaya Yojana - National Rural Livelihoods Mission (DAY-NRLM) is a flagship program of the Government of India. 

It was launched in 2011 with the aim of reducing poverty by promoting self-employment and organization of rural poor into self-help groups. 

The scheme is named after Pandit Deen Dayal Upadhyaya who was a prominent Indian politician and philosopher. 

The mission recognizes that poverty is a multi-dimensional issue that requires a multi-faceted approach. 

The primary objective of the DAY-NRLM is to promote self-reliance among rural households. 

It aims to provide sustainable livelihood opportunities to the rural poor. 

The mission focuses on empowering women by promoting their participation in self-help groups. 

It also seeks to enhance the livelihoods of the rural poor through skill development and capacity building. 

The DAY-NRLM provides various benefits and assistance to eligible beneficiaries. 

Financial assistance is provided to self-help groups to support their income-generating activities. 

The scheme also facilitates access to credit for rural households. 

Training programs are organized to enhance the skills of the rural poor. 

These training programs cover various sectors such as agriculture, animal husbandry, and handicrafts. 

The mission promotes the formation of self-help groups which are crucial for the empowerment of women. 

Self-help groups provide a platform for women to come together and support each other. 

They also help in saving money and accessing credit facilities. 

The eligibility criteria for the DAY-NRLM are primarily based on socio-economic status. 

The scheme targets rural households that are below the poverty line. 

Priority is given to women and marginalized communities. 

The application process for the DAY-NRLM is relatively straightforward. 

Interested individuals can approach the local block office or the district rural development agency. 

They need to provide necessary documents to prove their eligibility. 

The application is then reviewed by the concerned authorities. 

Once approved, beneficiaries are enrolled in the program. 

The mission operates on a continuous basis. 

There are no specific timelines for the application process. 

However, training programs and financial assistance are provided periodically. 

The DAY-NRLM is implemented by the Ministry of Rural Development. 

State governments also play a crucial role in the implementation of the scheme. 

They are responsible for the execution of various components of the mission.
