The National Vayoshri Yojana (NVY) is a welfare scheme launched by the Government of India. 

It aims to provide physical aids and assisted living devices to senior citizens. 

The objective is to improve the quality of life for elderly individuals who are in need of support. 

Benefits include the provision of devices such as walking sticks, wheelchairs, and hearing aids. 

Assistance is provided free of cost to eligible beneficiaries. 

Eligibility criteria include being a senior citizen aged 60 years and above. 

The scheme targets individuals who are below the poverty line. 

The application process involves submitting a form through designated authorities. 

The timeline for receiving assistance may vary based on the location and availability of resources. 

The Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment is the implementing authority for this scheme. 

The scheme is implemented through various state governments and local bodies. 

Awareness programs are conducted to inform eligible beneficiaries about the scheme.
