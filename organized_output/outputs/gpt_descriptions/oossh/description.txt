The OOSSH scheme stands for the Online One Stop Shop for Handloom. 

It was launched to promote the handloom sector and support weavers in India. 

The objective of the scheme is to provide a digital platform for handloom weavers to sell their products directly to consumers. 

The benefits include increased visibility for handloom products and better income opportunities for weavers. 

The scheme also aims to enhance the marketing of handloom products through an online medium. 

Eligibility is primarily for handloom weavers and cooperatives involved in the handloom sector. 

The application process involves registering on the online platform and submitting necessary documentation. 

The timeline for the implementation of the scheme is ongoing as it is a digital platform. 

The Ministry of Textiles is the implementing authority for this scheme. 

The scheme also focuses on skill development and capacity building for weavers. 

It promotes the use of technology in the handloom sector to improve productivity.
