The Public Provident Fund Scheme is a long-term savings scheme initiated by the Government of India. 

It was introduced to encourage savings among the public and provide them with a secure investment option. 

The main objective is to provide financial security and a stable source of income after retirement. 

The scheme offers attractive interest rates which are compounded annually. 

The benefits include tax deductions under Section 80C of the Income Tax Act. 

The scheme is open to all Indian citizens including minors. 

The minimum contribution is ₹500 per year and the maximum is ₹1.5 lakh per year. 

The account can be opened at designated banks and post offices. 

The application process involves filling out a form and submitting it along with the required documents. 

The account has a tenure of 15 years which can be extended in blocks of 5 years. 

The interest rate is reviewed quarterly and is determined by the government. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Finance. 

Partial withdrawals are allowed after the completion of 6 years. 

Loans can also be availed against the balance in the account after the third financial year. 

The scheme is designed to promote long-term savings and financial discipline among individuals.
