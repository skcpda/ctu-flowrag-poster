The RVP scheme refers to the Rural Livelihoods Mission program in India. 

It aims to reduce poverty by promoting self-employment and organization of rural poor into self-help groups. 

The objective is to enhance the livelihood opportunities of rural households. 

The scheme provides financial assistance for self-employment ventures and skill development programs. 

It also offers training and capacity building for rural communities. 

Eligibility is primarily for rural households living below the poverty line. 

The application process involves submitting a proposal through local self-help groups or community-based organizations. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Rural Development at the national level. 

State governments also play a crucial role in execution and monitoring. 

The frequency of assistance varies based on the specific program under the RVP scheme. 

Additional details include a focus on women empowerment and sustainable livelihoods.
