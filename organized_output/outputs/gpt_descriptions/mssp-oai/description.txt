The MSSP-OAI scheme stands for the "Mahatma Gandhi National Rural Employment Guarantee Act - Online Application Interface."

This scheme is part of the Mahatma Gandhi National Rural Employment Guarantee Act.

It aims to provide a legal guarantee for at least 100 days of unskilled wage employment in a financial year to every rural household.

The objective is to enhance livelihood security in rural areas.

The scheme benefits rural households by providing them with employment opportunities.

It also aims to create durable assets in rural areas.

Eligibility is limited to rural households that apply for employment under the scheme.

The application process involves submitting an application through the online interface.

Applicants must provide necessary details as required by the system.

The timeline for employment provision is within 15 days of the application.

The implementing authority for this scheme is the Ministry of Rural Development.

State governments are also involved in the implementation at the local level.

The scheme promotes transparency and accountability through online applications.
