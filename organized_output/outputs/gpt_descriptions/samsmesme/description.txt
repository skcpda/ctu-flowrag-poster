The SAMSMESME scheme is an initiative by the Government of India aimed at promoting small and medium enterprises. 

The objective of the scheme is to enhance the competitiveness of small and medium enterprises in the country. 

The scheme provides financial assistance and support for various business activities. 

Benefits include access to credit support and capacity building for entrepreneurs. 

Eligibility is generally based on the classification of the enterprise as small or medium as defined by the Ministry of MSME. 

The application process typically involves submitting a proposal along with necessary documents to the relevant authority. 

The timeline for processing applications may vary depending on the specific requirements and the implementing agency. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises.
