The Minimum Support Price scheme is a government initiative in India. 

It aims to provide farmers with a guaranteed price for their produce. 

The objective is to ensure that farmers do not sell their crops at a loss. 

The scheme covers various crops including cereals and pulses. 

Farmers receive a price that is at least equal to the cost of production. 

This support helps in stabilizing farmers' income. 

Eligibility is generally based on the type of crop cultivated. 

Farmers must be registered under the scheme to avail benefits. 

The application process involves registering with the local agricultural department. 

Farmers can also check for updates through the official government portals. 

The support price is announced annually before the sowing season. 

The implementing authority is the Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers' Welfare. 

State governments also play a role in the implementation of the scheme. 

The scheme aims to enhance food security and promote sustainable agriculture.
