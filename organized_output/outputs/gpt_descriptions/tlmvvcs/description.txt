The TLMVVCS stands for the Tribal Livelihoods Mission for Vulnerable and Marginalized Communities Scheme. 

It aims to enhance the livelihoods of vulnerable and marginalized tribal communities in India. 

The objective is to provide sustainable livelihood opportunities through skill development and capacity building. 

The scheme focuses on promoting self-employment and entrepreneurship among tribal communities. 

Benefits include financial assistance for setting up small enterprises and access to skill development programs. 

Eligible beneficiaries are members of vulnerable and marginalized tribal communities. 

The application process typically involves submitting an application to the local implementing agency. 

The timeline for assistance may vary based on the project and local implementation schedules. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Tribal Affairs in collaboration with state governments. 

Additional details may include partnerships with NGOs for training and support.
