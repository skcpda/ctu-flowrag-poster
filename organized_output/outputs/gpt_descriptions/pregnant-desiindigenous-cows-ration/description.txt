The Pregnant Desi Indigenous Cows Ration scheme is an initiative aimed at supporting the health and productivity of indigenous cow breeds in India. 

The objective of the scheme is to provide nutritional support to pregnant indigenous cows. 

The scheme aims to improve the overall health of pregnant cows which in turn enhances milk production. 

Benefits include the provision of a specially formulated ration that meets the dietary needs of pregnant cows. 

Eligibility is typically limited to farmers who own indigenous cow breeds and are engaged in dairy farming. 

The application process involves farmers submitting an application to the local animal husbandry department or relevant authority. 

The timeline for assistance may vary based on the local implementation but generally aligns with the gestation period of the cows. 

Implementing authorities include the Department of Animal Husbandry and Dairying at both state and central levels. 

The scheme also promotes the conservation of indigenous cow breeds which are considered vital for sustainable agriculture.
