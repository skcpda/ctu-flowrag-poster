The Pradhan Mantri Samagra Shiksha Abhiyan is a comprehensive scheme aimed at improving the quality of education in India. 

It focuses on ensuring inclusive and equitable quality education for all children. 

The scheme aims to provide holistic education from preschool to secondary level. 

It promotes the use of technology in education and encourages innovative teaching methods. 

The benefits include improved infrastructure in schools and enhanced learning outcomes for students. 

Financial assistance is provided for the development of schools and training of teachers. 

Eligibility criteria include all government and government-aided schools across India. 

The application process involves submission of proposals by state governments to the Ministry of Education. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Education in collaboration with state governments. 

The timeline for the scheme is ongoing with annual reviews and assessments. 

It also emphasizes the importance of community participation in the education system.
