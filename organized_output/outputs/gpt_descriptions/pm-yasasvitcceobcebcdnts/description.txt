The PM YASASVI Scheme is a scholarship program initiated by the Government of India. 

It aims to provide financial assistance to students from economically weaker sections. 

The objective is to enhance access to quality education for students pursuing higher studies. 

The scheme specifically targets students in classes 9 to 12. 

Benefits include financial support for tuition fees and other educational expenses. 

Eligible candidates must belong to the Economically Weaker Sections category. 

They should also be pursuing studies in recognized institutions. 

The application process involves filling out an online form on the official website. 

Candidates must provide necessary documents to verify their eligibility. 

The timeline for application typically coincides with the academic year. 

The National Testing Agency is the implementing authority for this scheme. 

The scholarship is awarded based on the results of an entrance examination. 

Successful candidates receive financial assistance directly into their bank accounts. 

The scheme aims to reduce the dropout rate among students from disadvantaged backgrounds. 

It promotes equal opportunities in education for all sections of society.
