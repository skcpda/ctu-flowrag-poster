The SIASEBCTPCE scheme is aimed at providing financial assistance to students pursuing higher education in India. 

The primary objective is to support students from economically weaker sections to enable them to continue their education. 

The benefits include financial aid for tuition fees and other educational expenses. 

Eligibility criteria include being a student enrolled in a recognized higher education institution and belonging to a low-income family. 

The application process typically involves submitting an online application along with necessary documents such as income certificates and academic records. 

The timeline for application submission and disbursement of funds may vary each academic year. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Education in collaboration with state education departments.
