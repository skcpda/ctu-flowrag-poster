The National Employment Through Skill Training (NEST) scheme aims to enhance the employability of youth in India. 

The primary objective is to provide skill development training to unemployed and underemployed individuals. 

The scheme focuses on various sectors to meet the demands of the job market. 

Benefits include skill training and certification which can lead to better job opportunities. 

Eligible beneficiaries are typically youth aged between 15 to 29 years. 

The application process involves registering through designated training centers or online portals. 

The training is conducted periodically based on the availability of courses and demand. 

The Ministry of Skill Development and Entrepreneurship is the implementing authority for this scheme. 

Additional details may include partnerships with private sector organizations for training delivery.
