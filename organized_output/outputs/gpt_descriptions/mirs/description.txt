MIRS stands for the Mahatma Gandhi National Rural Employment Guarantee Act (MGNREGA) Integrated Reporting System. 

It was launched to enhance the transparency and accountability of the MGNREGA scheme. 

The objective is to provide a reliable mechanism for monitoring and reporting the implementation of MGNREGA. 

Benefits include improved data collection and analysis for better decision-making. 

It assists in tracking employment generation and wage payments under the MGNREGA. 

Eligibility criteria are aligned with the MGNREGA guidelines which target rural households. 

The application process involves local authorities and Panchayati Raj institutions. 

The system is implemented by the Ministry of Rural Development. 

MIRS facilitates real-time reporting and helps in addressing grievances related to MGNREGA. 

It aims to strengthen the overall governance of rural employment schemes.
