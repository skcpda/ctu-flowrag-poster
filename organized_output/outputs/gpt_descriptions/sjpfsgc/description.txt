The scheme SJPFSGC stands for the "Sanjay Gandhi Post Graduate Institute of Medical Sciences Financial Support for General Category." 

It aims to provide financial assistance to students from the general category pursuing postgraduate medical education. 

The objective is to promote higher education in medical sciences and support students who may face financial constraints. 

Benefits include financial support for tuition fees and other educational expenses. 

Eligibility is typically based on the applicant's admission to a postgraduate medical program at the Sanjay Gandhi Post Graduate Institute of Medical Sciences. 

The application process generally involves submitting an application form along with required documents to the institute. 

The timeline for application submission and disbursement of funds may vary each academic year. 

The implementing authority for this scheme is the Sanjay Gandhi Post Graduate Institute of Medical Sciences.
