The SMSUGGS scheme stands for the Smart Manufacturing and Sustainable Urban Growth through Green Solutions. 

It was launched to promote sustainable urban development and smart manufacturing practices in India. 

The objective of the scheme is to enhance urban infrastructure while ensuring environmental sustainability. 

The scheme aims to provide financial assistance for projects that focus on green technologies and smart solutions in urban areas. 

Benefits include funding support for eligible projects and access to technical expertise in sustainable urban planning. 

Eligibility criteria include urban local bodies and private entities involved in smart manufacturing and urban development projects. 

The application process involves submitting a project proposal that meets the scheme's guidelines. 

The timeline for project implementation varies based on the specific project approved under the scheme. 

The Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs is the primary implementing authority for this scheme. 

Additional details regarding specific funding amounts and project types may be available through official government sources.
