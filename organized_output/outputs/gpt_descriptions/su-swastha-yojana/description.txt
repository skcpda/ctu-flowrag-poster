Su Swastha Yojana is an initiative by the Government of India aimed at promoting health and wellness among citizens. 

The objective of the scheme is to provide accessible and affordable healthcare services to the underprivileged sections of society. 

The scheme offers a range of health services including preventive healthcare, health education, and treatment for various ailments. 

Beneficiaries can receive free health check-ups and consultations. 

The eligibility criteria include individuals from economically weaker sections and marginalized communities. 

The application process typically involves registration through designated health centers or online platforms. 

The timeline for services may vary based on the specific health services required. 

Implementing authorities include the Ministry of Health and Family Welfare and state health departments. 

The scheme also aims to create awareness about health issues and promote healthy lifestyles among the population.
