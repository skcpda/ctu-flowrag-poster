The Pradhan Mantri Gramin Awas Yojana (PMAY) Surhula is a rural housing scheme in India. 

It aims to provide affordable housing to the rural poor. 

The objective is to ensure that every rural household has a pucca house with basic amenities. 

The scheme benefits individuals from economically weaker sections and low-income groups. 

Assistance includes financial support for the construction of houses. 

Eligible beneficiaries are those who do not own a house and belong to the designated income categories. 

The application process typically involves submitting an application to the local authorities or through online portals. 

The timeline for disbursement of funds and completion of housing varies based on project implementation. 

The Ministry of Rural Development is the primary implementing authority for this scheme. 

State governments and local bodies also play a role in the execution of the scheme.
