Mefarsi is a welfare scheme initiated by the Government of India. 

It aims to provide financial assistance to farmers for the purchase of agricultural inputs. 

The scheme seeks to enhance agricultural productivity and ensure food security. 

Eligible beneficiaries include small and marginal farmers. 

Farmers must be registered under the Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samman Nidhi scheme. 

The application process involves submitting an online application through the official portal. 

Farmers may also need to provide relevant documents such as land ownership proof. 

The assistance is provided on an annual basis. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers' Welfare. 

State governments may also play a role in the implementation at the local level.
