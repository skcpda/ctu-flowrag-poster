Mahatma Gandhi Kisan Samman Nidhi Yojana (MKSY) is a government welfare scheme aimed at providing financial support to farmers in India. 

The scheme was launched to ensure a steady income for farmers and to alleviate poverty in rural areas. 

The primary objective is to provide direct income support to small and marginal farmers. 

Under this scheme, eligible farmers receive an annual financial assistance of INR 6000. 

The assistance is provided in three equal installments of INR 2000 each. 

Eligibility is limited to small and marginal farmers who own cultivable land. 

Farmers with landholdings of up to 2 hectares are eligible for the scheme. 

The application process involves registration through the State Government's portal or local revenue offices. 

Farmers must provide necessary documents such as land records and identification proof. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers' Welfare. 

The financial assistance is credited directly to the bank accounts of the beneficiaries. 

The scheme is expected to be disbursed annually. 

The implementation is monitored at both the state and central levels.
