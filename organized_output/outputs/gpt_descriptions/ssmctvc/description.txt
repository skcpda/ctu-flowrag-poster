The SSMCTVC scheme stands for the Skill Support for Marginalized Communities through Technical Vocational Courses. 

It aims to provide skill development and vocational training to marginalized communities in India. 

The objective is to enhance employability and promote entrepreneurship among these communities. 

The scheme offers financial assistance for training programs and skill development initiatives. 

Eligible beneficiaries include individuals from marginalized communities who are seeking skill development. 

The application process typically involves submitting an application form through designated training centers or online portals. 

The timeline for the training programs may vary depending on the specific course and training provider. 

The implementing authorities include the Ministry of Skill Development and Entrepreneurship and various state government agencies. 

The scheme may also collaborate with private training organizations to facilitate skill development.
