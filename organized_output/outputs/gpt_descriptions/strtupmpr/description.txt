The Startup India initiative aims to promote and facilitate the growth of startups in India. 

It was launched by the Government of India on January 16 2016. 

The objective is to create a conducive ecosystem for innovation and entrepreneurship. 

The scheme provides various benefits including funding support tax exemptions and regulatory relaxations. 

Financial assistance is available through the Fund of Funds for Startups. 

Startups can also access mentorship and networking opportunities. 

Eligibility criteria include being a registered entity and meeting the definition of a startup as per the government guidelines. 

The application process involves registering on the Startup India portal and submitting the necessary documentation. 

The initiative is implemented by the Department for Promotion of Industry and Internal Trade (DPIIT). 

The scheme is ongoing with no specific end date. 

Additional details include various programs and events organized to promote startup culture in the country.
