The Pradhan Mantri Matsya Sampada Yojana (PMMSY) is a scheme launched by the Government of India. 

It aims to enhance fish production and promote sustainable fisheries in the country. 

The objectives include increasing fish production to 22 million metric tons by 2024-25. 

It also seeks to create employment opportunities and improve the livelihoods of fishers and fish farmers. 

The scheme provides financial assistance for various activities related to aquaculture and fisheries. 

This includes support for fish seed production and fish farming infrastructure. 

Beneficiaries can also receive assistance for the establishment of fish processing units. 

Eligibility is primarily for individuals and groups involved in fisheries and aquaculture. 

This includes fish farmers cooperatives and self-help groups. 

The application process involves submitting a proposal to the respective state fisheries department. 

The timeline for fund disbursement varies based on the project and state guidelines. 

The scheme is implemented by the Department of Fisheries under the Ministry of Fisheries Animal Husbandry and Dairying. 

Additional details include a focus on promoting technology and innovation in the fisheries sector. 

The scheme also emphasizes the need for sustainable practices in fish farming.
