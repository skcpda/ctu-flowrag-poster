This scheme aims to promote innovation and entrepreneurship in the agricultural sector.

Eligibility criteria for the Agri-Startup Grant Scheme are clearly defined. 

The scheme is primarily targeted at micro and small enterprises in the agriculture sector. 

Startups must be registered under the relevant laws of India. 

They should have a viable business plan that focuses on agricultural innovation. 

The startups must demonstrate the potential for scalability and sustainability. 

Preference is given to startups that involve women entrepreneurs or marginalized communities. 

The application process for the Agri-Startup Grant Scheme is structured and straightforward. 

Interested startups must submit an online application through the official portal. 

The application should include detailed information about the business plan. 

It must outline the innovative aspects of the proposed product or service. 

Startups are also required to provide financial projections and funding requirements. 

Once the application is submitted, it undergoes a review process. 

The review is conducted by a committee of experts in the agricultural field. 

Successful applicants are notified and provided with the grant amount. 

The timeline for the application process may vary depending on the number of applications received. 

Typically, the review process takes a few weeks to complete. 

The frequency of grants is not fixed and depends on the availability of funds.
