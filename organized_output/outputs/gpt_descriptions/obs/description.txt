The One Nation One Ration Card scheme aims to provide food security to all eligible beneficiaries across India. 

It ensures that individuals can access subsidized food grains from any fair price shop in the country. 

The primary objective is to enable portability of ration cards to ensure that migrants and their families do not face food insecurity. 

The scheme benefits migrant workers and their families by allowing them to access food grains from any state. 

Eligible beneficiaries include those who possess a valid ration card issued by any state in India. 

The application process involves linking the ration card with the Aadhaar number to ensure transparency and prevent duplication. 

The scheme is implemented by the Department of Food and Public Distribution under the Ministry of Consumer Affairs. 

The timeline for the implementation of the scheme varies by state but aims for nationwide coverage. 

The scheme promotes the use of technology in the public distribution system to enhance efficiency. 

It is part of the larger initiative to strengthen the public distribution system in India.
