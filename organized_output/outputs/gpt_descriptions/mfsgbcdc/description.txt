The Mukhya Mantri Swavalamban Yojana (MFSGBCDC) is a welfare scheme initiated by the Government of Madhya Pradesh. 

The scheme aims to promote self-employment among the youth and women in the state. 

It provides financial assistance for setting up small businesses and enterprises. 

The benefits include subsidies on loans and grants for various business activities. 

Eligible beneficiaries include unemployed youth and women who are residents of Madhya Pradesh. 

The age limit for applicants is typically between 18 to 45 years. 

The application process involves submitting an online application through the official Madhya Pradesh government portal. 

Applicants must provide necessary documents such as identity proof and project reports. 

The scheme is implemented by the Department of Micro Small and Medium Enterprises of Madhya Pradesh. 

Financial assistance is provided in the form of subsidies on loans taken from financial institutions. 

The frequency of assistance is determined by the project and loan approval timelines. 

The scheme also includes training programs to enhance entrepreneurial skills among beneficiaries. 

Additional details regarding specific subsidy percentages and loan amounts may vary based on the type of business.
