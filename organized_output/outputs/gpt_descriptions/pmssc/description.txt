The Pradhan Mantri Suraksha Bima Yojana (PMSBY) is a government welfare scheme launched in 2015. 

It aims to provide accidental death and disability insurance to individuals. 

The objective is to enhance the social security of the poor and underprivileged sections of society. 

The scheme offers a coverage of INR 2 lakh for accidental death and permanent total disability. 

It provides INR 1 lakh for permanent partial disability. 

The premium for the scheme is INR 12 per annum. 

Eligibility includes individuals aged between 18 to 70 years. 

Applicants must have a bank account and must provide consent for auto-debit of the premium. 

The application process is straightforward and can be done through banks and financial institutions. 

Enrollment is available from 1st June to 31st May each year. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Finance through public sector and private sector banks. 

The benefits are provided in case of accidents resulting in death or disability. 

The scheme aims to promote financial inclusion and social security.
