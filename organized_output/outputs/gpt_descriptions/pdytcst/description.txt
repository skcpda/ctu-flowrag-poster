The Pradhan Mantri Dhan Yojana for Tribal Communities Scheme (PDYTCST) aims to promote financial inclusion among tribal communities in India. 

The objective of the scheme is to provide access to banking services and financial literacy to tribal populations. 

The benefits include the provision of savings accounts and access to credit facilities. 

Eligible beneficiaries are members of Scheduled Tribes who are above the age of 18. 

The application process involves submitting a form at designated banks or financial institutions along with required identification documents. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Tribal Affairs in collaboration with various financial institutions. 

The frequency of assistance is determined by the financial institutions involved in the program.
