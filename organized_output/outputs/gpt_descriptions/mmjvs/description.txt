The Mukhyamantri Mahila Jagriti Vikas Sansthan (MMJVS) scheme is an initiative by the Government of Madhya Pradesh. 

It aims to empower women through skill development and entrepreneurship. 

The objectives include enhancing women's self-reliance and improving their socio-economic status. 

The scheme provides training programs in various vocational skills. 

Financial assistance is also available for women to start their own businesses. 

Eligibility is primarily focused on women from economically weaker sections. 

Women who are residents of Madhya Pradesh can apply for the scheme. 

The application process involves submitting a form to the local implementing authority. 

The timeline for training programs may vary based on the specific skill being taught. 

The scheme is implemented by the Department of Women and Child Development in Madhya Pradesh. 

Additional details regarding specific training programs and financial assistance amounts may vary.
