The Digital India initiative is a flagship program of the Government of India aimed at transforming India into a digitally empowered society and knowledge economy. 

Launched on July 1, 2015, the initiative seeks to ensure that government services are made available to citizens electronically by improving online infrastructure. 

The program is designed to bridge the digital divide between urban and rural areas. 

It aims to enhance the digital literacy of citizens and promote the use of technology in various sectors. 

The primary objective of the Digital India initiative is to provide high-speed internet access to all citizens. 

The initiative also focuses on the creation of digital infrastructure, digital literacy, and delivering services digitally. 

One of the significant components of the initiative is the establishment of a robust digital infrastructure. 

This includes the creation of a national optical fiber network to connect all the gram panchayats in the country. 

The initiative has also led to the development of various digital platforms for service delivery. 

These platforms include the Common Service Centers which serve as access points for government services.
