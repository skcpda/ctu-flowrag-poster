The Telangana State Innovation and Growth Challenge (TSIGC) is an initiative by the Government of Telangana. 

It aims to foster innovation and support startups in the state. 

The objective is to provide a platform for entrepreneurs to showcase their innovative ideas. 

The scheme seeks to enhance the startup ecosystem in Telangana. 

Benefits include financial assistance for selected startups. 

Participants may also receive mentorship and networking opportunities. 

Eligibility is generally focused on startups and entrepreneurs based in Telangana. 

The application process typically involves submitting a proposal or business plan. 

Specific details regarding the timeline for applications may vary by year. 

The scheme is implemented by the Telangana State Innovation Cell.
