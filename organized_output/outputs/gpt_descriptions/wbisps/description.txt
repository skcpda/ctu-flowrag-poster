The Women’s Business Incubation Support Program (WBISPS) is an initiative aimed at promoting entrepreneurship among women in India. 

The program seeks to empower women by providing them with the necessary support to start and grow their businesses. 

It aims to enhance women's participation in the economy and reduce gender disparities in entrepreneurship. 

The benefits include financial assistance for business setup and operational costs. 

The program also offers mentorship and training to improve business skills. 

Eligibility is generally limited to women entrepreneurs who are starting or running small businesses. 

Specific eligibility criteria may vary based on the implementing agency. 

The application process typically involves submitting a detailed business plan along with the application form. 

Applicants may also need to provide documentation proving their identity and business concept. 

The timeline for application processing and fund disbursement may vary by agency. 

The program is implemented by various government agencies at both the central and state levels. 

These agencies are responsible for overseeing the application process and providing support services.
