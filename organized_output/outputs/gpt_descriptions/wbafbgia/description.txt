The West Bengal Agricultural and Farm-Based General Insurance Scheme (WBAFBGIA) is designed to provide financial support to farmers in West Bengal. 

The objective of the scheme is to protect farmers against losses due to natural calamities and unforeseen events. 

The benefits include coverage for crop loss due to adverse weather conditions and other risks. 

Eligible beneficiaries are farmers who cultivate crops in West Bengal. 

The application process involves farmers submitting their details to the concerned authorities through designated channels. 

The scheme is implemented by the West Bengal government in collaboration with insurance companies. 

The frequency of assistance is determined based on the occurrence of eligible events affecting crops. 

Additional details include the scheme's focus on promoting sustainable agricultural practices and ensuring food security in the state.
