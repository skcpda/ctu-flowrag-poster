The VTVSCSGIT scheme is a government initiative aimed at providing financial assistance to self-help groups. 

The scheme focuses on promoting self-employment and entrepreneurship among women. 

It aims to empower women by enhancing their skills and providing them with necessary resources. 

The benefits include financial support for starting small businesses and access to training programs. 

Eligible beneficiaries are women who are members of registered self-help groups. 

The application process involves submitting an application form along with required documents to the local implementing agency. 

The timeline for processing applications may vary based on the local authority's procedures. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Women and Child Development. 

Additional details regarding specific training programs or financial limits may be available through local offices.
