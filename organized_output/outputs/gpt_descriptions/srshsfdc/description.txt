The Sr. R. S. H. S. F. D. C. scheme is aimed at providing financial assistance to individuals with disabilities. 

The objective of the scheme is to promote the welfare and empowerment of persons with disabilities. 

The benefits include financial assistance for various needs such as education and vocational training. 

Eligibility is generally limited to individuals with certified disabilities as per government standards. 

The application process typically involves submitting a form along with necessary documentation to the relevant authority. 

The timeline for processing applications may vary based on the specific requirements of the implementing agency. 

The scheme is implemented by the respective State Rehabilitation Societies for Handicapped and Disabled Persons. 

Additional details about specific benefits and assistance may vary by state.
