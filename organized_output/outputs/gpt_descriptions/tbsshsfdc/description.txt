The TBSSHFDC stands for the Tamil Nadu Backward Classes Economic Development Corporation. 

It was established to promote economic development among backward classes in Tamil Nadu. 

The primary objective is to provide financial assistance for self-employment and entrepreneurship. 

The scheme aims to uplift backward classes by enhancing their economic status. 

Benefits include financial support for starting small businesses and self-employment ventures. 

The assistance may cover various sectors including agriculture and services. 

Eligibility criteria include being a member of a backward class as defined by the government. 

Applicants must also meet specific income limits set by the corporation. 

The application process involves submitting a completed application form along with required documents. 

Documents typically include proof of caste and income. 

The application can be submitted at designated offices of the TBSSHFDC. 

The timeline for processing applications may vary based on the volume of applications received. 

The TBSSHFDC operates under the Government of Tamil Nadu. 

It collaborates with various financial institutions to facilitate loans and assistance. 

The scheme also provides skill development training to enhance employability. 

Regular monitoring and evaluation are conducted to assess the impact of the scheme.
