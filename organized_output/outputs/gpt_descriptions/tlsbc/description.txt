The TLSBC scheme stands for the Tribal Livelihoods Support and Capacity Building Programme. 

It aims to enhance the livelihoods of tribal communities in India. 

The primary objective is to provide skill development and capacity building to tribal populations. 

The scheme focuses on promoting sustainable livelihood opportunities. 

It seeks to empower tribal communities through training and support. 

Benefits include access to skill development programs and financial assistance for livelihood activities. 

Eligible beneficiaries are members of Scheduled Tribes. 

They must reside in designated tribal areas. 

The application process typically involves submitting an application to local implementing agencies. 

The timeline for the implementation of the scheme may vary based on local conditions. 

The Ministry of Tribal Affairs is the primary implementing authority for this scheme. 

State governments may also play a role in execution at the local level. 

The scheme is part of a broader effort to improve the socio-economic status of tribal communities.
