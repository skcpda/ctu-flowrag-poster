The scheme known as CISHPES-MESIFAI stands for the Central Integrated Scheme for Health and Nutrition for Persons with Disabilities and the Mentally Ill. 

This scheme is part of the broader efforts by the Indian government to enhance the quality of life for individuals with disabilities and mental health issues. 

The context of this scheme is rooted in the recognition of the challenges faced by persons with disabilities and those suffering from mental health conditions. 

Historically in India, individuals with disabilities have often been marginalized and face significant barriers to accessing healthcare and social services. 

The government has acknowledged the need for a comprehensive approach to address these challenges. 

The objectives of the CISHPES-MESIFAI scheme are multifaceted. 

One of the primary objectives is to provide integrated health and nutrition services to persons with disabilities. 

The scheme aims to ensure that individuals with disabilities have access to essential health services. 

Another objective is to promote mental health awareness and provide necessary support to those with mental health issues. 

The scheme also seeks to empower individuals with disabilities through skill development and vocational training. 

By doing so, it aims to enhance their employability and promote their inclusion in society. 

The benefits and assistance provided under this scheme are extensive. 

Eligible beneficiaries can receive financial assistance for medical treatment and rehabilitation services. 

The scheme also provides nutritional support to ensure that individuals with disabilities receive adequate nutrition. 

In addition to health and nutrition, the scheme offers educational support for children with disabilities. 

This includes provisions for special education and learning materials. 

The scheme also facilitates access to assistive devices and technologies that can aid in daily living. 

Furthermore, it promotes community-based rehabilitation programs to support individuals in their local environments. 

Eligibility for the CISHPES-MESIFAI scheme is determined based on specific criteria. 

Individuals with disabilities as defined under the Rights of Persons with Disabilities Act are eligible. 

This includes persons with physical disabilities, sensory disabilities, intellectual disabilities, and mental illnesses. 

The age of the beneficiaries may also be a factor in determining eligibility. 

In some cases, children with disabilities may have specific provisions under the scheme.
