The Pradhan Mantri Ujjwala Yojana (PMUY) is a government welfare scheme launched in May 2016. 

The scheme aims to provide LPG connections to women from Below Poverty Line households. 

The objective is to promote clean cooking fuel and reduce the health hazards associated with traditional cooking methods. 

Under this scheme, beneficiaries receive a financial assistance of Rs. 1,600 for each LPG connection. 

The scheme also includes a subsidy on the LPG cylinders. 

Eligibility is restricted to women who are at least 18 years old and belong to Below Poverty Line households. 

Applicants must provide proof of identity and address. 

The application process involves filling out a form available at designated outlets or online. 

The timeline for receiving the LPG connection may vary based on the location and availability of resources. 

The Ministry of Petroleum and Natural Gas is the implementing authority for this scheme. 

The scheme has been expanded to cover additional beneficiaries over the years. 

It aims to empower women by reducing their dependence on traditional fuels. 

The initiative is part of the government's broader goal to ensure access to clean energy for all.
