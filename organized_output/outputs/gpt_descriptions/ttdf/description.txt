The TTDF scheme refers to the Tirumala Tirupati Devasthanams Trust Fund. 

It was established to support the welfare of the underprivileged and promote various social initiatives. 

The main objective of the TTDF is to provide financial assistance for education healthcare and other essential services to the needy. 

The benefits include scholarships for students financial aid for medical treatments and support for community development projects. 

Eligibility criteria typically include being a resident of the region and belonging to economically weaker sections. 

The application process generally involves submitting a form along with necessary documents to the designated authorities. 

The timeline for fund disbursement varies based on the specific program under the TTDF. 

The scheme is implemented by the Tirumala Tirupati Devasthanams Trust.
