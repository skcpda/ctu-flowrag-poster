The scheme OAHTN stands for the One-Stop Assistance for Health and Nutrition. 

It aims to provide comprehensive health and nutrition support to vulnerable populations. 

The primary objective is to improve health outcomes and nutritional status among marginalized communities. 

Benefits include access to healthcare services nutritional education and support for maternal and child health. 

Eligible beneficiaries typically include pregnant and lactating women children under six years and other vulnerable groups. 

The application process may involve registration at local health centers or through community health workers. 

The timeline for assistance may vary based on the specific needs of the beneficiaries and local implementation. 

The implementing authorities include the Ministry of Health and Family Welfare and local health departments. 

Additional details on specific services and support may vary by region.
