The STPMSC stands for the Skill Training of Persons with Disabilities Scheme. 

This scheme is designed to promote skill development among persons with disabilities. 

The objective is to enhance the employability of persons with disabilities through skill training. 

The benefits include training in various skills that can lead to self-employment or wage employment. 

Assistance is provided in the form of financial support for training programs. 

Eligibility is primarily for persons with disabilities who are above the age of 14 years. 

The application process typically involves submitting an application to the designated training centers. 

The timeline for training programs may vary based on the specific skill being taught. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment. 

Training is conducted through various recognized training institutions across the country. 

Additional details may include the focus on inclusive training practices and the involvement of NGOs in implementation.
