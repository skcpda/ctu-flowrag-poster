The SAT scheme refers to the Skill Acquisition Training program initiated by the Government of India. 

The context of the SAT scheme is to enhance the employability of youth through skill development. 

The objective of the SAT scheme is to provide vocational training to individuals to equip them with necessary skills for various industries. 

Benefits of the SAT scheme include access to skill development training and improved job opportunities for participants. 

The assistance provided under the scheme includes training in various trades and financial support for training providers. 

Eligibility criteria for the SAT scheme typically include youth aged between 15 to 29 years. 

The application process generally involves registration through designated training centers or online portals. 

The timeline for the training programs can vary based on the specific trade and training provider. 

Implementing authorities for the SAT scheme include the Ministry of Skill Development and Entrepreneurship and various state skill development missions. 

Additional details may include partnerships with industry stakeholders for better job placement opportunities.
