The scheme SJPWDs stands for the Scheme for Justice for Persons with Disabilities. 

It aims to provide legal aid and support to persons with disabilities. 

The objective is to ensure that persons with disabilities have access to justice and legal representation. 

The benefits include free legal aid services and assistance in filing cases related to disability rights. 

Eligibility is typically based on the definition of disability as per the Rights of Persons with Disabilities Act 2016. 

The application process involves contacting designated legal aid authorities or organizations that provide support for persons with disabilities. 

The timeline for receiving assistance may vary based on the specific case and local legal aid processes. 

The implementing authorities include the National Legal Services Authority and State Legal Services Authorities. 

Additional details may include awareness programs and workshops to educate persons with disabilities about their legal rights.
