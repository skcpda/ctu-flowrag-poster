The Central Financial Assistance for the Maintenance of the Family (CFM) scheme is a significant initiative of the Government of India. 

This scheme is designed to provide financial support to families in need. 

The CFM scheme aims to alleviate poverty and improve the living standards of disadvantaged families. 

It is particularly focused on families that are economically weaker and require assistance to meet their basic needs. 

The scheme recognizes the importance of family support in ensuring the well-being of individuals. 

The primary objective of the CFM scheme is to provide financial assistance to families to help them maintain a minimum standard of living. 

This assistance is intended to cover essential expenses such as food, clothing, and education. 

By providing financial support, the scheme aims to reduce the burden on families and promote social welfare.

These groups may include single-parent families, elderly individuals, and persons with disabilities.
