The Pradhan Mantri Poshana Abhiyan (PMPY) is a government welfare scheme aimed at improving nutritional outcomes among children and pregnant women. 

The objective of PMPY is to reduce malnutrition and promote health and nutrition awareness. 

The scheme provides supplementary nutrition to children under six years of age and pregnant and lactating women. 

Eligible beneficiaries include children aged six months to six years and pregnant and lactating women from low-income families. 

The application process typically involves registration through Anganwadi centers or local health departments. 

The scheme operates continuously with regular monitoring and evaluation of nutritional status. 

The Ministry of Women and Child Development is the primary implementing authority for PMPY. 

The scheme is part of the larger National Nutrition Mission aimed at addressing malnutrition in India.
