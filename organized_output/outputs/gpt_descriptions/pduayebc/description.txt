The Pradhan Mantri Ujjwala Yojana (PMUY) is a government welfare scheme launched in May 2016. 

It aims to provide clean cooking fuel to women from Below Poverty Line households. 

The objective is to reduce the health hazards associated with the use of traditional cooking fuels. 

The scheme provides a financial assistance of Rs. 1,600 for each LPG connection. 

Beneficiaries receive a free LPG connection along with a stove and first refill. 

Eligibility is limited to women from Below Poverty Line households. 

Applicants must provide proof of being a BPL household. 

The application process involves filling out a form available at designated outlets. 

The timeline for receiving the connection is generally within a few weeks of application approval. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Petroleum and Natural Gas. 

The scheme is operational across all states and Union Territories in India. 

Additional details include the provision of safety and usage training for beneficiaries.
