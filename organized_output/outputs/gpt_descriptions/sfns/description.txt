The Scheme for the Financial Assistance to the Needy Senior Citizens (SFNS) is a welfare initiative by the Government of India. 

It aims to provide financial support to senior citizens who are in need. 

The objective is to enhance the quality of life for elderly individuals by providing them with a regular income. 

Eligible beneficiaries include senior citizens aged 60 years and above who belong to low-income families. 

The scheme provides a monthly pension to eligible senior citizens. 

The amount of financial assistance varies by state and is determined by the respective state governments. 

The application process typically involves submitting an application form along with necessary documents to the local government office or designated authority. 

The timeline for processing applications may vary by state. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment at the central level. 

State governments are responsible for the execution and management of the scheme at the state level. 

Additional details regarding the specific amount of assistance and state-wise variations can be obtained from local government offices.
