The TSGTKVN scheme stands for the "Tribal Sub-Plan for the Development of Scheduled Tribes in the State of Karnataka."

This scheme is aimed at the socio-economic development of Scheduled Tribes in Karnataka.

The primary objective is to enhance the living standards of tribal communities through various developmental initiatives.

Benefits include financial assistance for education health and livelihood opportunities.

Assistance is provided for skill development training and self-employment ventures.

Eligibility criteria include being a member of a Scheduled Tribe and residing in Karnataka.

The application process typically involves submitting a form to the local government office or designated agency.

The timeline for assistance may vary based on the specific program under the scheme.

Implementing authorities include the Department of Tribal Welfare in Karnataka.

Additional details may include specific programs for health education and housing under the scheme.
