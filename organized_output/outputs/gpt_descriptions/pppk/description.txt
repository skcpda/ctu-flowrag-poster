The Pradhan Mantri Poshana Shakti Nirman Yojana (PPPK) aims to improve the nutritional status of children and women in India. 

The objective of PPPK is to reduce malnutrition among children under six years of age and pregnant and lactating women. 

The scheme provides supplementary nutrition to beneficiaries. 

It also focuses on health education and nutrition awareness. 

Eligible beneficiaries include children aged six months to six years and pregnant and lactating women. 

The application process involves registration at the local Anganwadi center. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Women and Child Development. 

The frequency of assistance is typically monthly. 

The scheme is part of the larger Integrated Child Development Services (ICDS) program. 

It aims to promote holistic development and well-being among vulnerable populations.
