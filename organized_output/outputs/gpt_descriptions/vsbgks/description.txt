The Vishwa Sewa Bharti Gramin Kalyan Scheme (VSBGKS) aims to enhance the quality of life in rural areas. 

The scheme focuses on providing various welfare services to rural communities. 

It seeks to empower rural populations through skill development and employment opportunities. 

The benefits include financial assistance for self-employment and skill training programs. 

Eligibility criteria typically include being a resident of a rural area and belonging to economically weaker sections. 

The application process involves submitting a form to the local implementing authority. 

The timeline for processing applications may vary based on the local administration. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Rural Development in collaboration with local bodies. 

Additional details regarding specific benefits or services may vary by region.
