The scheme pbbwc60y is known as the Pradhan Mantri Bhatta Yojana for Widows and Destitute Women.

This scheme aims to provide financial assistance to widows and destitute women.

The objective is to ensure social security and improve the living conditions of vulnerable women.

Eligible beneficiaries receive a monthly pension to support their livelihood.

Eligibility criteria include being a widow or a destitute woman aged 60 years and above.

The application process involves submitting necessary documents to the local government office or designated agency.

The timeline for disbursement of benefits is typically monthly.

The implementing authority for this scheme is the Ministry of Women and Child Development.
