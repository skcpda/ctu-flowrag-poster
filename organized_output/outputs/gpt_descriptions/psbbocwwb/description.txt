The Pradhan Mantri Shram Yogi Maan-Dhan (PM-SYM) scheme is designed for unorganized sector workers. 

It aims to provide a monthly pension of Rs. 3000 after the age of 60 years. 

The scheme targets workers in the unorganized sector who earn less than Rs. 15,000 per month. 

Eligible beneficiaries include workers in sectors such as agriculture, construction, and domestic work. 

The application process involves registration through the official portal or designated centers. 

Beneficiaries must provide identity proof and bank account details during registration. 

The pension is disbursed monthly after the beneficiary reaches the age of 60. 

The Ministry of Labour and Employment is the implementing authority for this scheme. 

The scheme was launched in February 2019. 

It is a voluntary and contributory pension scheme. 

The government contributes an equal amount to the pension fund. 

The scheme also includes provisions for the nominee in case of the beneficiary's demise.
