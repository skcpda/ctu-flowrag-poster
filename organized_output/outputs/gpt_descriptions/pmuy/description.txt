Pradhan Mantri Ujjwala Yojana (PMUY) was launched in May 2016. 

The scheme aims to provide clean cooking fuel to women from below poverty line households. 

It seeks to reduce the health hazards associated with the use of traditional cooking fuels like firewood and dung. 

The primary objective is to empower women by providing them with access to LPG connections. 

Under PMUY, eligible beneficiaries receive a financial assistance of ₹1,600 for each LPG connection. 

The scheme also includes a subsidy on the LPG cylinder. 

Eligibility is restricted to women from Below Poverty Line households. 

Beneficiaries must be at least 18 years of age and must not already possess an LPG connection. 

The application process involves filling out a form available at designated LPG distributors or online. 

Applicants need to provide identity proof and address proof. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Petroleum and Natural Gas. 

The timeline for providing the LPG connection is typically within a few weeks after application approval. 

The scheme is ongoing and continues to accept applications. 

PMUY has been instrumental in promoting the use of clean energy and improving the quality of life for women.
