The CMMSISUK scheme is a welfare initiative launched by the Government of India. 

The full form of CMMSISUK is Chief Minister's Mahila Shakti Initiative Scheme for Urban Karnataka. 

This scheme is specifically designed to empower women in urban areas of Karnataka. 

The background of this scheme stems from the recognition of the challenges faced by women in urban settings. 

Women in urban areas often encounter issues such as unemployment, lack of skill development, and social discrimination. 

The Government of Karnataka aims to address these challenges through targeted interventions.
