The scheme "ps-tn" refers to the Tamil Nadu Public Distribution System. 

This scheme is part of the larger public distribution system in India aimed at providing essential commodities to the economically weaker sections of society. 

The primary objective of the scheme is to ensure food security and provide subsidized food grains to eligible households. 

Beneficiaries receive essential items such as rice, wheat, sugar, and kerosene at subsidized rates. 

Eligibility for the scheme includes families below the poverty line and those identified under the Antyodaya Anna Yojana. 

The application process involves submitting an application form along with necessary documents to the local ration shop or the designated authority. 

The distribution of food grains occurs on a monthly basis. 

The scheme is implemented by the Department of Consumer Affairs, Food and Civil Supplies of the Government of Tamil Nadu. 

Additional details may include the provision for digital ration cards and the use of technology to streamline the distribution process.
