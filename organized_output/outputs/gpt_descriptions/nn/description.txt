The National Nutrition Mission (NNM) is a government initiative aimed at improving nutritional outcomes for children and women in India. 

The mission was launched in 2018 under the Ministry of Women and Child Development. 

Its primary objective is to reduce the level of stunting, under-nutrition, and low birth weight by promoting proper nutrition practices. 

The NNM aims to achieve a reduction in anemia among young children, adolescent girls, and women. 

The scheme provides various benefits including supplementary nutrition, health check-ups, and nutrition education. 

It also focuses on the convergence of various sectors such as health, education, and sanitation to address malnutrition holistically. 

Eligibility for the program includes children under the age of six, pregnant women, and lactating mothers. 

The application process typically involves registration through Anganwadi centers or local health departments. 

The program is implemented by the Ministry of Women and Child Development in collaboration with state governments and local bodies. 

The mission operates on a continuous basis with regular monitoring and evaluation of its impact on nutritional status. 

The NNM also emphasizes the use of technology for real-time monitoring and data collection. 

Capacity building of frontline workers is a key component to ensure effective implementation. 

The scheme promotes community participation to enhance awareness and involvement in nutritional practices.
