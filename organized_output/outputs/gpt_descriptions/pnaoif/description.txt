The Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana - Urban (PMAY-U) is a flagship housing scheme initiated by the Government of India. 

It aims to provide affordable housing to the urban poor. 

The objective is to ensure that every urban household has access to a pucca house with basic amenities. 

The benefits include financial assistance for housing construction or enhancement. 

The scheme provides a subsidy on home loans for eligible beneficiaries. 

Eligibility is primarily based on income criteria and the applicant must be a citizen of India. 

The scheme targets Economically Weaker Sections (EWS) Low Income Groups (LIG) and Middle Income Groups (MIG). 

The application process involves submitting an online application through the official PMAY website or through designated Common Service Centers. 

The timeline for approval and disbursement of funds varies based on the application and processing time. 

The Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs is the implementing authority for this scheme. 

State governments and urban local bodies also play a role in implementation at the local level. 

The scheme was launched in June 2015 and is part of the larger mission to provide housing for all by 2022.
