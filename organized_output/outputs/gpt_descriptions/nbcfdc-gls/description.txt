The National Backward Classes Finance and Development Corporation General Loan Scheme (NBCFDC GLS) aims to promote self-employment and entrepreneurship among backward classes in India. 

The objective of the scheme is to provide financial assistance to individuals from backward classes for setting up small businesses or self-employment ventures. 

The scheme offers loans at concessional interest rates to eligible beneficiaries. 

The assistance can be used for various purposes including purchasing equipment or working capital for businesses. 

Eligibility is primarily for individuals belonging to backward classes as defined by the government. 

Applicants must also meet certain income criteria which are specified by the implementing agency. 

The application process involves submitting a duly filled application form along with required documents to the nearest NBCFDC office or through designated banks. 

The frequency of loan disbursement is based on the assessment and approval of applications. 

The implementing authority for this scheme is the National Backward Classes Finance and Development Corporation. 

The scheme aims to empower backward classes by enhancing their economic status through self-employment opportunities.
