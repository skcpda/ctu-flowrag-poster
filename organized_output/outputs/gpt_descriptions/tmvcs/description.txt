The Tribal Multi-Purpose Cooperative Societies scheme aims to promote the socio-economic development of tribal communities in India. 

The objective is to enhance the livelihood opportunities of tribal populations through cooperative societies. 

The scheme provides financial assistance for the establishment of cooperative societies that cater to the needs of tribal communities. 

Benefits include access to credit facilities and support for various income-generating activities. 

Eligibility is primarily for tribal individuals and groups who wish to form or join cooperative societies. 

The application process involves submitting a proposal to the concerned state government or cooperative department. 

The timeline for fund disbursement and project implementation varies based on state-specific guidelines. 

The implementing authorities include the Ministry of Tribal Affairs and respective state governments. 

The scheme also focuses on capacity building and skill development for tribal members.
