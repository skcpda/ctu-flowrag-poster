The Pradhan Mantri Suraksha Yojana (PMSY) is a government welfare scheme aimed at providing affordable health insurance coverage to the underprivileged sections of society. 

The objective of PMSY is to ensure that individuals have access to quality healthcare services without the burden of high medical expenses. 

The scheme offers health insurance coverage for hospitalization expenses due to illness or injury. 

Beneficiaries can receive coverage for pre-existing diseases after a waiting period. 

The eligibility criteria for PMSY include individuals from economically weaker sections and those without any health insurance. 

The application process involves filling out a form and submitting it to designated authorities or through online platforms. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Health and Family Welfare in collaboration with state governments. 

PMSY aims to provide financial protection against health-related expenses and improve access to healthcare services for the economically disadvantaged.
