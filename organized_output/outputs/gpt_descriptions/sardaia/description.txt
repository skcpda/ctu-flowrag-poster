The Sardaia scheme is a welfare initiative aimed at supporting the education of children in India. 

The scheme focuses on providing financial assistance for the education of children from economically weaker sections. 

Its primary objective is to reduce dropout rates among students and promote higher education. 

The benefits include scholarships for students to cover tuition fees and other educational expenses. 

Eligibility criteria typically include being a student from a low-income family and meeting specific academic standards. 

The application process involves submitting necessary documents such as income certificates and academic records to the designated authorities. 

The timeline for application submission and scholarship disbursement may vary based on the academic calendar. 

Implementing authorities include state education departments and local educational institutions. 

Additional details may include specific provisions for different educational levels such as primary secondary and higher education.
