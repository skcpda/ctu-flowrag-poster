The STPTC scheme stands for the Scheme for Training of Persons with Disabilities. 

It aims to enhance the employability of persons with disabilities through skill development and vocational training. 

The objective is to provide training that aligns with the needs of the job market. 

Benefits include skill development training and support for self-employment opportunities. 

Eligibility is primarily for persons with disabilities who are above the age of 14 years. 

The application process typically involves submitting an application form along with necessary documentation to the designated authority. 

The timeline for training programs may vary based on the specific training institute and course duration. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment. 

Additional details may include partnerships with various training institutes and organizations to facilitate skill development.
