The TASC Scheme is designed to provide financial assistance to small and marginal farmers in India. 

The objective of the scheme is to enhance the income of farmers and improve their livelihood. 

The benefits include direct cash transfers to eligible farmers to support their agricultural activities. 

Eligibility is primarily based on land ownership and income criteria. 

Farmers must be small and marginal landholders to qualify for the assistance. 

The application process involves submitting necessary documents to the local agricultural department. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers' Welfare. 

Payments are made periodically based on the assessment of the farmers' needs. 

The scheme aims to reduce the financial burden on farmers and promote sustainable agricultural practices.
