The SSPBOCEEB scheme stands for the "Skill Support Program for Persons with Disabilities." 

It aims to enhance the employability of persons with disabilities through skill development. 

The objectives include providing vocational training and skill development opportunities. 

The scheme focuses on empowering individuals with disabilities to achieve economic independence. 

Benefits include access to skill development programs and financial assistance for training. 

Eligibility is primarily for persons with disabilities as defined under the Rights of Persons with Disabilities Act 2016. 

The application process typically involves submitting an application to designated skill development centers. 

These centers are responsible for conducting training programs under the scheme. 

The timeline for the training programs may vary based on the specific courses offered. 

Implementing authorities include the Ministry of Skill Development and Entrepreneurship. 

The program may also involve collaboration with various NGOs and training institutes.
