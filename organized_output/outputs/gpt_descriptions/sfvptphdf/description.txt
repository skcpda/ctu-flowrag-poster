The scheme is known as the Scheme for Financial Assistance to Victims of Trafficking and Commercial Sexual Exploitation. 

It aims to provide rehabilitation and support to victims of trafficking and commercial sexual exploitation. 

The objectives include ensuring the safety and security of victims. 

It also seeks to facilitate their reintegration into society. 

The benefits include financial assistance for rehabilitation. 

Victims may receive support for education and vocational training. 

They may also receive medical assistance and legal aid. 

Eligibility is primarily for individuals who have been trafficked for commercial sexual exploitation. 

The application process typically involves reporting to law enforcement or designated authorities. 

Victims may also be referred by NGOs working in the field. 

The timeline for assistance is generally initiated upon verification of the victim's status. 

Implementing authorities include the Ministry of Women and Child Development. 

State governments and local bodies also play a role in implementation. 

The scheme emphasizes a multi-sectoral approach for effective rehabilitation.
