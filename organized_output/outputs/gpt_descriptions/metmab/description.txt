The Metmab scheme is an initiative by the Government of India. 

It aims to provide financial assistance to farmers. 

The objective is to enhance the income of farmers through direct cash transfers. 

The scheme offers monetary support to eligible farmers based on specific criteria. 

Benefits include direct cash transfers to the bank accounts of beneficiaries. 

Eligibility is generally limited to small and marginal farmers. 

Farmers must possess valid land ownership documents to qualify. 

The application process typically involves submitting an application form along with required documents. 

The timeline for disbursement of funds is usually aligned with the agricultural calendar. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers' Welfare. 

State governments may also play a role in the implementation at the local level. 

Further details can be found on the official government website.
