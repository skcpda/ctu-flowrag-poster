The scheme SGFAARSAAOWDFRS stands for the Scheme for Grant of Financial Assistance for Rehabilitation of Scheduled Castes and Other Weaker Sections under the Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment.

The background of this scheme is to provide financial assistance for the rehabilitation of Scheduled Castes and other weaker sections of society.

The primary objective is to empower these communities by providing them with financial support for their rehabilitation.

The benefits include financial assistance for various rehabilitation activities such as skill development and self-employment.

Eligibility criteria include individuals belonging to Scheduled Castes and other weaker sections as defined by the government.
