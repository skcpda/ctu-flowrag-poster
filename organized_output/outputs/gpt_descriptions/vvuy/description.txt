The VVUY scheme stands for the "Vishesh Vivah Utsav Yojana."

It is a welfare scheme initiated by the Government of India.

The scheme aims to provide financial assistance for the marriage of girls belonging to economically weaker sections.

The objective is to reduce the financial burden on families during the marriage of daughters.

Under this scheme, eligible beneficiaries receive a monetary grant to support marriage expenses.

The benefits include a one-time financial assistance amount for each eligible girl.

Eligibility criteria include being a resident of India and belonging to an economically weaker section.

The applicant must have a valid income certificate to prove their financial status.

The application process involves submitting an application form along with necessary documents to the designated authority.

Documents typically required include identity proof and income certificate.

The timeline for processing applications may vary based on the implementing agency.

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Women and Child Development at the central level.

State governments may also have their own specific guidelines under this scheme.

The scheme aims to promote the welfare of girls and ensure their social security.
