The Mukhyamantri Swasthya Dhan Scheme for Rural Poor (MSDSRP) aims to provide health insurance coverage to the rural poor in India. 

The objective of the scheme is to ensure access to quality healthcare services for economically disadvantaged families. 

The scheme offers financial assistance for medical treatments and hospitalization. 

Eligible beneficiaries include families living below the poverty line in rural areas. 

The application process typically involves submitting an application form along with required documents to the designated local authorities. 

The scheme is implemented by the respective state governments in coordination with health departments. 

The frequency of benefits is determined based on the healthcare needs of the beneficiaries. 

Additional details regarding specific coverage limits and types of treatments may vary by state.
