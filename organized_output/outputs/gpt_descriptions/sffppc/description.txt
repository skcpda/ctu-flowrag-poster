The SFFPPC scheme stands for the Scheme for Financial Assistance to Fishermen for Purchase of Fishing Crafts and Gear. 

This scheme is aimed at providing financial support to fishermen for the purchase of fishing crafts and gear. 

The objective is to enhance the livelihood of fishermen by improving their fishing capabilities. 

The benefits include financial assistance for the purchase of boats nets and other fishing equipment. 

Eligible beneficiaries are registered fishermen or fisherwomen who are part of cooperative societies or self-help groups. 

The application process involves submitting an application to the concerned fisheries department along with required documents. 

The timeline for disbursement of funds may vary based on the processing of applications. 

The implementing authority for this scheme is the Department of Fisheries under the Ministry of Fisheries Animal Husbandry and Dairying. 

This scheme aims to promote sustainable fishing practices and improve the overall income of fishermen.
