The Paaspni scheme is a welfare initiative by the Government of India. 

It aims to provide financial assistance to families in need. 

The objective is to ensure that vulnerable households have access to basic necessities. 

The scheme offers direct cash transfers to eligible beneficiaries. 

This financial support helps in meeting daily expenses and improving living standards. 

Eligibility criteria include being a resident of India and belonging to a low-income household. 

The application process typically involves submitting a form along with necessary documents. 

Beneficiaries may need to provide proof of income and residence. 

The timeline for disbursement of funds is generally monthly. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Rural Development. 

State governments may also play a role in the execution of the scheme. 

Additional details regarding the scheme can be found on official government websites.
