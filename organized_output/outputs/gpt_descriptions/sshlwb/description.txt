The scheme SSHLWB stands for the Scheme for Support to Scheduled Caste Households for Livelihood and Welfare Benefits. 

It was launched to provide financial assistance and support to Scheduled Caste households in India. 

The main objective of the scheme is to enhance the livelihood opportunities and welfare of Scheduled Caste families. 

The benefits include financial assistance for various livelihood activities and access to welfare programs. 

Eligibility is primarily based on being a member of a Scheduled Caste household. 

The application process involves submitting an application to the designated authority in the respective state or union territory. 

The timeline for assistance may vary based on the specific livelihood activity and the processing time by the implementing agency. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment at the central level. 

State governments may also have their own implementing agencies to facilitate the scheme at the local level.
