The Pradhan Mantri Jan Dhan Yojana (PMJDY) is a financial inclusion program launched by the Government of India. 

It was initiated on August 28 2014. 

The objective of the scheme is to provide universal access to banking facilities. 

It aims to ensure that every household has at least one bank account. 

The benefits include access to financial services such as banking savings and deposit accounts. 

It also provides access to credit insurance and pension services. 

Eligible beneficiaries include all Indian citizens aged 18 years and above. 

There is no income limit for eligibility. 

The application process involves visiting a bank branch or financial institution. 

Applicants must provide valid identification and address proof. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Finance. 

Banks and financial institutions are responsible for the execution of the scheme. 

The scheme is ongoing and does not have a specific end date. 

It is reviewed periodically for improvements and updates. 

The PMJDY also includes features like overdraft facilities and direct benefit transfers. 

The scheme has been instrumental in promoting financial literacy among the population. 

It has contributed to the overall goal of achieving financial inclusion in India.
