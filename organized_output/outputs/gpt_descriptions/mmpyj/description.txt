MMPYJ stands for the Mukhyamantri Paryatan Yojana. 

This scheme was launched by the Government of Madhya Pradesh. 

It aims to promote tourism in the state. 

The objective is to enhance the tourism infrastructure and provide better facilities for tourists. 

Benefits include financial assistance for developing tourist spots. 

The scheme also supports local entrepreneurs in the tourism sector. 

Eligibility is primarily for local bodies and tourism stakeholders. 

The application process involves submitting a proposal to the concerned authorities. 

The timeline for project approval and funding may vary based on the proposal. 

The Madhya Pradesh Tourism Board is the implementing agency for this scheme. 

Additional details on specific funding amounts and project types may be available through official sources.
