The SPEOSCELU scheme is designed to support the education of children from economically weaker sections. 

The objective of the scheme is to provide financial assistance for the education of children. 

The benefits include scholarships for students to cover tuition fees and other educational expenses. 

Eligibility is primarily based on the economic status of the family. 

The application process typically involves submitting an application form along with required documents to the designated authority. 

The timeline for disbursement of funds may vary based on the academic calendar. 

The implementing authorities for this scheme include state education departments and local government bodies. 

Additional details regarding the specific amount of assistance and the exact eligibility criteria may vary by state.
