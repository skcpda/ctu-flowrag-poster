**Kushal Yuva Program (KYP)**

The Kushal Yuva Program is an initiative by the Government of Haryana. 

It was launched to enhance the employability of the youth in the state. 

The program aims to provide skill development training to young individuals. 

It focuses on various sectors to meet the demands of the job market. 

The primary objective is to empower youth with relevant skills. 

This is intended to bridge the gap between education and employment. 

The program also aims to promote entrepreneurship among the youth. 

Benefits under the Kushal Yuva Program include free skill training. 

Participants receive training in various trades and sectors. 

The program also provides placement assistance to successful candidates. 

Financial assistance is available for certain courses. 

The program targets youth aged between 15 to 35 years. 

Candidates must be residents of Haryana to be eligible. 

They should have completed at least Class 10 or equivalent education. 

The application process is conducted online through the official portal. 

Interested candidates must register on the Kushal Yuva Program website. 

They need to fill out the application form with required details. 

After registration, candidates can select the desired skill training course. 

The training sessions are conducted at designated training centers. 

The frequency of training sessions varies based on the course. 

Training is provided in both short-term and long-term formats. 

The program is implemented by the Haryana Skill Development Mission. 

The mission collaborates with various training partners and institutions. 

The Kushal Yuva Program also focuses on soft skills development. 

This includes communication skills and interview preparation. 

The program encourages participation from marginalized communities. 

Special provisions are made for women and differently-abled individuals. 

The initiative aligns with the National Skill Development Mission. 

It aims to create a skilled workforce in the country. 

The program also emphasizes the importance of vocational training. 

Certificates are awarded to participants upon successful completion.
