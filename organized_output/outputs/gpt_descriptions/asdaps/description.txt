The "Aam Aadmi Doodh Aahar Yojana" also known as AADAPs is a welfare scheme initiated by the Government of India. 

This scheme is designed to provide nutritional support to children in the age group of six months to six years. 

The context of this scheme arises from the need to address malnutrition and improve the health status of children in India. 

Malnutrition is a significant public health issue in India affecting millions of children. 

The Government of India has recognized the importance of ensuring that children receive adequate nutrition for their growth and development.
