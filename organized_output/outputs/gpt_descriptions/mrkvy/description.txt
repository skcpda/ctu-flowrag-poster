The Mukhyamantri Rojgar Kendra Yojana (MRKVY) is an initiative by the Government of Madhya Pradesh. 

It aims to provide employment opportunities to the youth of the state. 

The scheme focuses on skill development and enhancing employability. 

Benefits include financial assistance for setting up employment generation units. 

The scheme also provides training programs to develop skills in various sectors. 

Eligibility is primarily for the youth of Madhya Pradesh. 

Applicants must be between the ages of 18 and 35 years. 

The application process involves submitting a form to the designated employment office. 

The timeline for processing applications may vary based on the office's workload. 

The scheme is implemented by the Department of Skill Development and Employment, Madhya Pradesh. 

Additional details regarding specific training programs and financial assistance amounts may vary.
