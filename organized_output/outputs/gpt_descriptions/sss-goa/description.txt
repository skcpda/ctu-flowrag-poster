The SSS Goa scheme is a welfare initiative launched by the Government of Goa. 

It aims to provide financial assistance to the elderly and differently-abled individuals in the state. 

The primary objective is to enhance the quality of life for these vulnerable groups. 

Eligible beneficiaries include senior citizens aged 60 years and above and persons with disabilities. 

The scheme offers a monthly pension to eligible individuals. 

The amount of financial assistance varies based on the category of the beneficiary. 

The application process involves submitting a form along with required documents to the local authorities. 

Beneficiaries must provide proof of age or disability as part of their application. 

The scheme is implemented by the Department of Social Welfare, Government of Goa. 

Financial assistance is disbursed on a monthly basis. 

The scheme aims to ensure social security and support for the elderly and differently-abled in Goa.
