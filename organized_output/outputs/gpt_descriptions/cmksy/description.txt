The Chief Minister Kanya Suraksha Yojana (CMKSY) is a welfare scheme initiated by the Government of Haryana. 

This scheme was launched with the primary objective of ensuring the safety and security of girls in the state. 

The initiative aims to address the societal issues surrounding gender discrimination and the declining sex ratio in Haryana. 

The CMKSY is designed to empower girls and promote their education and well-being. 

The scheme recognizes the importance of providing a safe environment for girls to thrive and succeed. 

One of the key objectives of CMKSY is to encourage families to value the birth of a girl child. 

The scheme aims to create awareness about the importance of education for girls. 

It also seeks to provide financial assistance to families for the education of their daughters. 

The benefits of CMKSY include financial support for families with girl children. 

Under this scheme, families receive a fixed amount deposited in the name of the girl child. 

This financial support is intended to help cover educational expenses and other related costs. 

The scheme also provides for health check-ups and immunization for girl children. 

Additionally, it promotes the importance of nutrition and health for girls. 

The eligibility criteria for CMKSY are straightforward. 

Families must be residents of Haryana to qualify for the scheme. 

The scheme is applicable to families with a girl child born on or after January 1, 2016. 

There is no income limit specified for families to apply for the scheme. 

However, families must ensure that they register the birth of their girl child. 

The application process for CMKSY is designed to be user-friendly. 

Parents or guardians of the girl child must visit the nearest Anganwadi center or the designated office. 

They need to fill out the application form provided at these centers. 

Along with the application form, they must submit the necessary documents. 

These documents typically include the birth certificate of the girl child. 

Proof of residence in Haryana is also required. 

Once the application is submitted, it is verified by the concerned authorities. 

After successful verification, the financial assistance is credited to the bank account of the girl child. 

The timeline for the disbursement of benefits under CMKSY can vary. 

Typically, once the application is approved, the financial assistance is provided within a few weeks. 

The scheme is implemented by the Department of Women and Child Development in Haryana. 

This department is responsible for overseeing the execution of various welfare schemes for women and children. 

The government has also set up a monitoring mechanism to ensure the effective implementation of CMKSY. 

Regular audits and assessments are conducted to evaluate the impact of the scheme.
