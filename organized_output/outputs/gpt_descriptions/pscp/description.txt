The Pradhan Mantri Suraksha Bima Yojana (PMSBY) is a government welfare scheme launched in 2015. 

It aims to provide accidental death and disability coverage to individuals. 

The objective is to offer financial security to the poor and vulnerable sections of society. 

The scheme provides a sum assured of Rs. 2 lakh in case of accidental death or total disability. 

In case of partial disability, the coverage is Rs. 1 lakh. 

The premium for the scheme is Rs. 12 per annum. 

Eligibility is open to all individuals aged between 18 to 70 years. 

Applicants must have a bank account and provide their Aadhaar number for enrollment. 

The application process can be completed through banks and insurance companies. 

Enrollment can be done online or offline. 

The scheme operates on a yearly basis with renewals required every year. 

The Ministry of Finance is the implementing authority for this scheme. 

The scheme is administered through various public sector and private sector insurance companies. 

It is part of the government's broader initiative to enhance social security for the unorganized sector.
