The Meghalaya Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board scheme is designed to provide welfare benefits to construction workers in Meghalaya. 

The objective of the scheme is to ensure the social and economic welfare of workers engaged in the building and construction industry. 

The benefits include financial assistance for medical treatment, education for children, and assistance for housing. 

Workers are also eligible for maternity benefits and pension schemes under this initiative. 

Eligibility criteria include being a registered construction worker under the Meghalaya Building and Other Construction Workers Act. 

Workers must also be between the ages of 18 and 60 years to qualify for the benefits. 

The application process involves registering with the Meghalaya Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board. 

Applicants must submit necessary documents such as identity proof and proof of employment in the construction sector. 

The timeline for processing applications may vary but is generally completed within a few weeks. 

The Meghalaya Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board is the implementing authority for this scheme. 

Additional details about specific benefits and assistance can be obtained from the official website of the board.
