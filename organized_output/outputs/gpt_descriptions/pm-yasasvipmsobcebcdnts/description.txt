The PM-YASASVI scheme is designed to provide financial assistance to students from economically weaker sections. 

It aims to enhance the quality of education and support students pursuing higher studies. 

The scheme offers scholarships to meritorious students who qualify for the National Eligibility Test. 

Eligible candidates must belong to the Economically Weaker Sections category. 

They must also be studying in classes 9 to 12 in recognized schools. 

The application process involves filling out an online form on the official PM-YASASVI portal. 

Candidates must provide necessary documents to verify their eligibility. 

The scholarship amount varies based on the level of education and the specific requirements of the students. 

The implementing authority for this scheme is the Ministry of Education, Government of India. 

The scheme is reviewed periodically to ensure its effectiveness and reach. 

The scholarship is disbursed annually to the selected candidates.
