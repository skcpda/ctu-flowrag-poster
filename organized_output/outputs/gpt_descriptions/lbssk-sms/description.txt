**Scheme Name: LBSK-SMS (Lal Bahadur Shastri Kisan Samman Nidhi - Soil Management Scheme)**

**Context or Background:**
The LBSK-SMS is a government initiative aimed at promoting sustainable agricultural practices in India.  
It was launched to address the challenges faced by farmers due to soil degradation and nutrient depletion.  
The scheme is part of the broader efforts by the Indian government to enhance agricultural productivity and ensure food security.  
Soil health is critical for the overall agricultural output and the livelihood of farmers.  
The initiative emphasizes the importance of soil management in achieving sustainable agriculture.

**Objectives:**
The primary objective of the LBSK-SMS is to improve soil health across agricultural lands in India.  
It aims to provide farmers with the necessary tools and knowledge to manage soil effectively.  
The scheme seeks to promote the use of organic fertilizers and sustainable farming practices.  
Another objective is to increase awareness among farmers about soil conservation techniques.  
The initiative also aims to enhance the productivity of crops through better soil management.

**Benefits and Assistance:**
Farmers enrolled in the LBSK-SMS receive financial assistance for soil testing and management.  
The scheme provides subsidies for organic fertilizers and soil amendments.  
Farmers are also offered training sessions on sustainable agricultural practices.  
Access to expert advice on soil management is another benefit of the scheme.  
The initiative promotes the use of technology in soil management, helping farmers make informed decisions.

**Eligibility:**
The scheme is primarily targeted at small and marginal farmers in India.  
Farmers must be registered under the Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samman Nidhi (PM-KISAN) scheme to be eligible.  
Eligibility criteria may vary slightly based on state-specific guidelines.  
Farmers must demonstrate a commitment to sustainable agricultural practices to qualify for assistance.  
The scheme is open to all regions of India, ensuring inclusivity in its implementation.

**Application Process:**
Farmers can apply for the LBSK-SMS through the official website or designated government offices.  
The application form can be downloaded online or obtained from local agricultural offices.  
Farmers must provide necessary documentation such as land ownership proof and PM-KISAN registration details.  
Once the application is submitted, it undergoes verification by the concerned authorities.  
Approved applicants will receive a notification regarding their eligibility and the benefits they can avail.

**Timeline or Frequency:**
The application process for the LBSK-SMS is typically open throughout the year.  
However, specific deadlines may be set for certain phases of the scheme.  
Farmers are encouraged to apply as soon as they meet the eligibility criteria to ensure timely assistance.  
The frequency of benefits disbursement may vary based on the type of assistance being provided.  
Soil testing and training sessions are conducted periodically based on demand and availability of resources.

**Implementing Authorities or Agencies:**
The LBSK-SMS is implemented by the Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers' Welfare.  
State agricultural departments play a crucial role in the execution of the scheme at the local level.  
Various agricultural universities and research institutions are involved in providing technical support.  
Local self-governments may also assist in mobilizing farmers and disseminating information about the scheme.  
Collaboration with non-governmental organizations may occur to enhance outreach and effectiveness.
