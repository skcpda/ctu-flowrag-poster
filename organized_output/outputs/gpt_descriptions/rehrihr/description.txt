The Rehri-Hawker Scheme is an initiative by the Government of India aimed at supporting street vendors. 

It was launched to provide financial assistance and promote the livelihoods of street vendors. 

The objectives include facilitating access to credit and providing a conducive environment for street vendors to operate. 

The scheme offers financial assistance in the form of loans to street vendors. 

It also provides training and capacity building to enhance their skills. 

Eligibility is primarily for street vendors who are engaged in vending activities. 

Applicants must be registered under the scheme to avail of the benefits. 

The application process typically involves submitting an online application along with necessary documents. 

The timeline for disbursement of loans may vary based on the processing time. 

The Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs is the implementing authority for this scheme. 

Local urban bodies may also play a role in the implementation at the ground level.
