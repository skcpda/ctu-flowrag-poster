The Pradhan Mantri Suraksha Maandhan Yojana (PMSMY) is a government welfare scheme aimed at providing social security to unorganized sector workers. 

The scheme was launched to ensure financial stability for workers in the unorganized sector post-retirement. 

The primary objective is to provide a monthly pension of Rs. 3,000 after the age of 60 years. 

Eligible beneficiaries include workers in the unorganized sector who are between the ages of 18 to 40 years. 

The scheme is designed for individuals with an income of up to Rs. 15,000 per month. 

The application process involves registering online through the official government portal or visiting designated centers. 

Beneficiaries must provide necessary identification and income proof during the application. 

The pension will be disbursed monthly after the individual reaches the age of 60. 

The Ministry of Labour and Employment is the implementing authority for this scheme. 

Additional details include that the scheme is contributory, requiring a monthly contribution from the beneficiary based on their age at the time of enrollment.
