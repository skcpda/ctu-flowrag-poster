The Pradhan Mantri Suraksha Bima Yojana (PMSBY) is a government welfare scheme launched in 2015. 

It aims to provide accidental death and disability coverage to individuals. 

The objective is to offer financial support to the family of the insured in case of an accident. 

The scheme provides a sum assured of INR 2 lakh in case of accidental death or total disability. 

In case of partial disability, a sum assured of INR 1 lakh is provided. 

The premium for the scheme is INR 12 per annum. 

Eligibility for the scheme includes individuals aged between 18 and 70 years. 

Applicants must have a savings bank account. 

The application process involves enrolling through the bank where the savings account is held. 

Enrollment can be done online or offline. 

The coverage period is one year and can be renewed annually. 

The implementing authority for the scheme is the Ministry of Finance. 

The scheme is administered through public sector and private sector banks. 

The PMSBY is part of a broader initiative to enhance social security in India.
