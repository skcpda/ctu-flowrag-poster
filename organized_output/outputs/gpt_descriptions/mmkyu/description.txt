The Mukhyamantri Mahila Kalyan Yojana (MMKYU) is a welfare scheme launched by the Government of Madhya Pradesh. 

The scheme aims to empower women and enhance their socio-economic status. 

It focuses on providing financial assistance and support for women's health education and skill development. 

The benefits include financial aid for various purposes such as education health and self-employment. 

Women from economically weaker sections are eligible for this scheme. 

The application process involves submitting a form along with necessary documents at designated centers. 

The scheme is implemented by the Department of Women and Child Development of Madhya Pradesh. 

The timeline for disbursement of benefits is determined based on the specific program under the scheme.
