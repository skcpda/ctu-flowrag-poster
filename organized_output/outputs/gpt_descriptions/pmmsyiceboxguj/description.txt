The PMMSY Ice Box Scheme is part of the Pradhan Mantri Matsya Sampada Yojana. 

This scheme aims to enhance the fisheries sector in India. 

It focuses on providing modern infrastructure for fish preservation and storage. 

The objective is to reduce post-harvest losses in the fisheries sector. 

The scheme provides financial assistance for the purchase of ice boxes. 

These ice boxes are essential for maintaining the quality of fish during transportation. 

Eligible beneficiaries include fishers and fish farmers. 

The application process involves submitting a proposal to the concerned fisheries department. 

The timeline for approval and disbursement of funds varies by state. 

The implementing authorities include the Department of Fisheries at both central and state levels. 

The scheme is part of a broader initiative to promote sustainable fisheries and aquaculture.
