The Kanya Sumangala Yojana is a welfare scheme initiated by the Government of Uttar Pradesh. 

It was launched in 2019 to promote the education and empowerment of girls. 

The primary objective of the scheme is to improve the sex ratio and encourage families to educate their daughters. 

The scheme aims to provide financial assistance to families for the education and upbringing of their daughters. 

Under this scheme, financial assistance is provided at various stages of a girl's life. 

The benefits include a one-time payment at the time of the birth of a girl child. 

Families receive additional financial support when the girl child reaches the age of 1 year. 

Further assistance is provided when the girl child is enrolled in class 1 and again when she reaches class 6. 

The final payment is made when the girl child completes class 10. 

The total financial assistance provided under the scheme amounts to INR 15,000. 

Eligibility for the Kanya Sumangala Yojana includes families with a girl child born on or after March 31, 2019. 

The scheme is applicable to families with an annual income of less than INR 3 lakh. 

The application process involves registering online through the official website of the Uttar Pradesh government. 

Parents or guardians must provide necessary documents such as the birth certificate of the girl child. 

They must also submit proof of income and other relevant identification documents. 

The application can be submitted through the online portal or at designated government offices. 

Once the application is submitted it is verified by the concerned authorities.
