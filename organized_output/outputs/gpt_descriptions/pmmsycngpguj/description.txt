The Pradhan Mantri Mudra Yojana (PMMY) is a government initiative aimed at providing financial support to small businesses and entrepreneurs. 

The scheme was launched to promote entrepreneurship and self-employment among individuals. 

The main objective is to provide loans to micro and small enterprises to help them grow and sustain their operations. 

Benefits include access to affordable credit for business activities. 

The scheme offers loans up to 10 lakh rupees without requiring collateral. 

Eligibility is open to individuals who wish to start or expand a small business. 

The application process involves submitting a loan application to banks or financial institutions. 

The timeline for loan approval may vary depending on the lending institution. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Finance through various banks and financial institutions across India. 

The PMMY also categorizes loans into three categories: Shishu for loans up to 50,000 rupees, Kishore for loans between 50,000 and 5 lakh rupees, and Tarun for loans between 5 lakh and 10 lakh rupees. 

The scheme aims to enhance the overall economic growth by promoting small businesses.
