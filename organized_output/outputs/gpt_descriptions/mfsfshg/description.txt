The MFSF-SHG scheme is designed to promote self-help groups among women in India. 

It aims to enhance women's empowerment and financial inclusion. 

The scheme provides financial assistance to self-help groups for income-generating activities. 

Eligible beneficiaries include women who are members of self-help groups. 

The application process typically involves registration with local authorities or banks. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Rural Development. 

Financial assistance is provided in the form of loans and grants. 

The frequency of assistance may vary based on the project and group performance. 

Additional training and capacity-building programs may be offered to beneficiaries. 

The scheme contributes to poverty alleviation and sustainable development in rural areas.
