The Chhattisgarh Mukhyamantri Kanya Vivah Yojana is a welfare scheme initiated by the Government of Chhattisgarh. 

This scheme aims to promote the marriage of girls from economically weaker sections of society. 

It acknowledges the challenges faced by families in arranging marriages for their daughters. 

The scheme is designed to provide financial assistance to help alleviate the financial burden associated with marriage expenses. 

The primary objective of the CMKVY is to encourage families to marry off their daughters at an appropriate age. 

It seeks to prevent child marriages and promote the education of girls. 

The scheme also aims to empower women by ensuring that they are married with dignity and respect. 

Under this initiative, the government provides a monetary incentive to families for the marriage of their daughters. 

The financial assistance offered is intended to cover various marriage-related expenses. 

This includes costs such as wedding ceremonies and related festivities. 

The scheme is particularly focused on supporting families belonging to Scheduled Castes, Scheduled Tribes, and Other Backward Classes. 

It also extends its benefits to families below the poverty line. 

The financial assistance provided under the scheme is substantial. 

As of the latest updates, the amount provided is Rs 25,000 for each girl who gets married. 

This amount is intended to help families manage the costs associated with the wedding. 

The assistance is provided directly to the bank account of the beneficiary. 

This ensures that the funds are transferred securely and efficiently. 

To be eligible for the CMKVY, certain criteria must be met by the applicants. 

The girl must be a resident of Chhattisgarh. 

She must belong to a family that falls under the economically weaker sections. 

The family income should not exceed the threshold set by the government. 

The girl must have completed the age of 18 years at the time of marriage. 

This age criterion is crucial in promoting the legal age of marriage. 

The scheme is open to girls from Scheduled Castes, Scheduled Tribes, and Other Backward Classes. 

Additionally, girls from families below the poverty line are also eligible. 

The application process for the CMKVY is straightforward. 

Interested applicants must fill out the application form provided by the government. 

The application form can be obtained from local government offices or online portals. 

Applicants must provide necessary documentation to support their eligibility. 

This includes proof of age and income certificates. 

Once the application is completed, it must be submitted to the designated authority. 

The designated authority typically includes local government officials or the Panchayat. 

After submission, the application will be reviewed for eligibility. 

If approved, the financial assistance will be disbursed to the beneficiary's bank account. 

The timeline for the disbursement of funds may vary. 

However, the government aims to process applications promptly. 

The frequency of the scheme's implementation is ongoing. 

There are no specific time limits for the application process. 

Families can apply for the scheme whenever they are planning a marriage.

This department is responsible for overseeing the welfare of women and children in the state.
