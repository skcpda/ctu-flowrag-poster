The National Mission for Empowerment of Women (NMEW) is an initiative by the Government of India. 

It aims to strengthen the overall empowerment of women in the country. 

The mission focuses on enhancing women's social and economic status. 

It seeks to promote gender equality and women's rights. 

The benefits include capacity building and skill development programs for women. 

It also provides support for women's self-help groups and microfinance initiatives. 

Eligibility criteria include being a woman and residing in India. 

The application process varies based on specific programs under the mission. 

Women can typically apply through local government offices or online portals. 

The timeline for various programs may differ based on the specific initiative. 

The Ministry of Women and Child Development is the primary implementing authority. 

The mission collaborates with various state governments and NGOs for effective implementation. 

It also focuses on awareness campaigns to educate women about their rights and available services. 

The NMEW aims to create a supportive environment for women's empowerment across all sectors.
