The Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana - Housing for All (PMAY-HFA) is a government initiative aimed at providing affordable housing to the urban poor. 

The objective of the scheme is to ensure that every individual has access to a pucca house with basic amenities. 

The benefits include financial assistance for housing construction or purchase. 

Eligible beneficiaries include Economically Weaker Sections (EWS) Low-Income Groups (LIG) and Middle-Income Groups (MIG). 

The application process typically involves submitting an online application through the official PMAY website or through designated Common Service Centers. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs. 

The timeline for the disbursement of benefits varies based on the completion of the housing project and the verification of beneficiaries. 

The scheme aims to complete housing for all by the year 2022. 

Additional details may include provisions for interest subsidy on home loans for eligible beneficiaries.
