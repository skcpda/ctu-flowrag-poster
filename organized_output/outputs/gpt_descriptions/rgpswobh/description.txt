The RGPSWOBH scheme stands for the Rajiv Gandhi Poshak Bhatta Yojana for Women of the BPL families. 

This scheme is aimed at providing nutritional support to pregnant and lactating women from Below Poverty Line families. 

The objective is to improve maternal and child health by ensuring adequate nutrition during pregnancy and lactation. 

The scheme provides financial assistance to eligible women for purchasing nutritious food. 

Eligible beneficiaries include pregnant and lactating women belonging to Below Poverty Line families. 

The application process involves submitting a form along with necessary documents to the local authorities or designated offices. 

The timeline for assistance is typically aligned with the pregnancy and lactation period of the beneficiaries. 

The implementing authority for this scheme is the Ministry of Women and Child Development. 

Additional details regarding the specific amount of assistance or frequency of payments may vary based on state guidelines.
