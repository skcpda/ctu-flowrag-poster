The National Scheme for Job Creation and Skill Development for Youth (NSJBSY) aims to enhance employment opportunities for the youth in India. 

The objective of the scheme is to provide skill development training and create job opportunities for young individuals. 

The benefits include skill training in various sectors and financial assistance for self-employment initiatives. 

Eligibility criteria include youth aged between 15 to 29 years who are seeking employment or skill development. 

The application process typically involves registration through designated government portals or local employment offices. 

The timeline for the scheme's implementation varies based on the training programs offered and the specific initiatives undertaken. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Skill Development and Entrepreneurship along with various state governments and local agencies. 

Additional details may include partnerships with private sector organizations for skill training and job placements.
