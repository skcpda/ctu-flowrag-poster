The Aam Admi Bima Yojana (AABY) is a social security scheme launched by the Government of India. 

It was initiated in October 2007. 

The scheme aims to provide life insurance coverage to the rural population. 

The primary objective of AABY is to offer financial assistance to the families of deceased breadwinners. 

This assistance is particularly aimed at those who are below the poverty line. 

The scheme is designed to provide a safety net for families in case of an untimely death. 

The insurance coverage under AABY is provided at a nominal premium. 

The premium is subsidized by the Government of India. 

The scheme covers individuals aged between 18 and 59 years. 

The life insurance coverage amount is set at Rs. 2 lakh. 

This amount is paid to the nominee of the insured in case of death. 

The scheme also includes accidental death and disability benefits. 

In case of accidental death, the nominee receives an additional Rs. 1 lakh. 

The scheme is particularly beneficial for low-income families. 

It helps in reducing the financial burden on families after the loss of a breadwinner. 

The eligibility criteria for AABY are straightforward. 

Individuals must be aged between 18 and 59 years. 

They must belong to a rural household. 

The scheme is open to both men and women. 

There is no requirement for prior insurance coverage to apply. 

The application process for AABY is simple and user-friendly. 

Interested individuals can apply through designated insurance companies. 

They can also approach local government offices for assistance. 

The application form is available at these offices and online. 

Applicants need to provide basic personal information. 

This includes details like name age and address. 

They must also provide information about their family. 

Documents required include proof of identity and residence. 

Once the application is submitted it is processed by the insurance company. 

The timeline for processing applications can vary. 

Typically it takes a few weeks for the insurance company to process. 

Once approved the coverage begins immediately. 

The premium for the scheme is very affordable. 

It is set at Rs. 200 per year. 

The government subsidizes a portion of this premium. 

This makes it accessible for low-income families. 

The implementing authorities for AABY include the Ministry of Finance. 

The scheme is executed through various public sector insurance companies. 

These companies are responsible for managing the insurance policies. 

They also handle claims and disbursements.
