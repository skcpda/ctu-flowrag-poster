The scheme SPFAPPBGA stands for the Special Project for Agricultural and Allied Activities in the Border Areas. 

It aims to enhance agricultural productivity and livelihood opportunities in border areas of India. 

The objective is to promote sustainable agricultural practices and improve the socio-economic conditions of farmers in these regions. 

The benefits include financial assistance for agricultural inputs and infrastructure development. 

Farmers in border areas are eligible to apply for this scheme. 

The application process typically involves submitting a proposal to the concerned state agriculture department. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers' Welfare. 

The timeline for disbursement of funds may vary based on project approval and implementation schedules. 

This scheme is part of a broader initiative to strengthen food security and promote rural development in border areas.
