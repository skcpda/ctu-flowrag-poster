The Rashtriya Swasthya Mission (RHSMS) is a government initiative aimed at providing accessible and affordable healthcare services to the population. 

The objective of RHSMS is to enhance healthcare delivery and improve health outcomes in rural and urban areas. 

The scheme benefits individuals by providing financial assistance for medical treatments and services. 

Eligibility for the scheme typically includes individuals and families from economically weaker sections of society. 

The application process may involve filling out a form and providing necessary documentation to local health authorities. 

The timeline for assistance may vary based on the specific healthcare needs and the processing of applications. 

The implementing authorities include the Ministry of Health and Family Welfare and state health departments. 

Additional details about specific services covered under the scheme may include hospitalization, outpatient services, and preventive care.
