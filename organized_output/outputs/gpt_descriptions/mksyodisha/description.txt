MKSY Odisha is the Mukhyamantri Krushi Udyog Yojana launched by the Government of Odisha. 

The scheme aims to promote entrepreneurship in the agricultural sector. 

It seeks to provide financial assistance to farmers and agripreneurs. 

The objective is to enhance agricultural productivity and create employment opportunities. 

The scheme provides subsidies for setting up agricultural and allied enterprises. 

Financial assistance is available for various activities such as food processing and organic farming. 

The benefits include training support and access to credit facilities. 

Eligibility is primarily for farmers and individuals interested in agribusiness. 

Applicants must be residents of Odisha. 

The application process involves submitting a proposal to the concerned department. 

Applications can be submitted online through the official website. 

The timeline for approval and disbursement varies based on the project. 

The Department of Agriculture and Farmers' Empowerment is the implementing authority. 

The scheme is part of the broader efforts to boost the agricultural economy in Odisha. 

It encourages innovation and sustainable practices in farming. 

The scheme also aims to improve the income levels of farmers. 

Regular monitoring and evaluation are conducted to assess the impact of the scheme.
