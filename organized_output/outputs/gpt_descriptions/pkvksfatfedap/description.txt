The scheme PKVKSFATFEDAP stands for Pradhan Mantri Kisan Vikas Kendra Samarthya Fund for Agricultural Development and Promotion. 

It aims to enhance the income of farmers through various agricultural development initiatives. 

The objectives include promoting sustainable agricultural practices and improving productivity. 

Benefits include financial assistance for agricultural inputs and technology adoption. 

Eligibility criteria typically include being a registered farmer and having a valid landholding. 

The application process generally involves submitting an application form to the local agricultural office. 

The timeline for assistance may vary based on the specific project or initiative under the scheme. 

Implementing authorities include the Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers' Welfare at the central level and state agricultural departments at the state level.
