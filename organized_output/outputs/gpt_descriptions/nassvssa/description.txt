The National Agricultural Market Scheme or e-NAM is an initiative by the Government of India. 

It aims to create a unified national market for agricultural commodities. 

The objective is to enhance farmers' income by providing them access to better prices. 

The scheme facilitates online trading of agricultural produce. 

Farmers can sell their produce directly to buyers through a digital platform. 

Benefits include increased transparency in pricing and reduced intermediaries. 

Farmers can access information on prices and demand across various markets. 

Eligibility includes all farmers who are registered in the e-NAM system. 

The application process involves registering on the e-NAM portal. 

Farmers need to provide necessary documents for verification. 

The scheme operates throughout the year. 

The Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers' Welfare is the implementing authority. 

State Agricultural Marketing Boards also play a role in implementation. 

The scheme has been launched in multiple states across India. 

It promotes the use of technology in agricultural marketing. 

The initiative supports the goal of doubling farmers' income by 2022.
