The One Time Grant for Medical and Surgical Consumables for Vulnerable Sections (OTGMSCVS) scheme aims to provide financial assistance for medical and surgical consumables to vulnerable populations. 

The objective of the scheme is to ensure that individuals from economically weaker sections have access to essential medical supplies. 

The benefits include financial support for purchasing necessary medical and surgical consumables. 

Eligibility is primarily targeted towards individuals belonging to economically weaker sections of society. 

The application process involves submitting a request through designated healthcare facilities or local government offices. 

The timeline for assistance may vary based on the specific circumstances of the applicant and the processing time of the application. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Health and Family Welfare along with state health departments. 

Additional details regarding the specific consumables covered under the scheme may vary by state.
