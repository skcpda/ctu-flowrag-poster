The Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana - Madhya Pradesh (PMAY-MP) is a housing scheme initiated by the Government of India. 

It aims to provide affordable housing to the urban poor in Madhya Pradesh. 

The objective is to ensure that every individual has access to a pucca house with basic amenities. 

The scheme offers financial assistance for the construction or enhancement of houses. 

Beneficiaries can receive a subsidy on home loans under the Credit Linked Subsidy Scheme. 

Eligibility is primarily based on income criteria and the applicant must be a resident of Madhya Pradesh. 

The applicant must not own a house in their name or in the name of any family member. 

The application process involves submitting an online application through the official PMAY website or the state government portal. 

Documents required include identity proof income proof and proof of residence. 

The timeline for approval and disbursement of funds varies based on the completeness of the application. 

The implementing authority for this scheme is the Madhya Pradesh Housing and Infrastructure Development Board. 

The scheme is part of the larger Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana initiative launched by the central government. 

It aims to provide housing for all by the year 2022.
