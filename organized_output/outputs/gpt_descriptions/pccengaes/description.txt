The PCCENGAES scheme stands for the Pradhan Mantri Chandra Shekhar Aazad National Employment Generation Scheme. 

It was launched to provide employment opportunities to the rural population. 

The objective of the scheme is to generate sustainable employment in rural areas. 

It aims to enhance the livelihood security of rural households. 

The scheme provides financial assistance for various employment-generating activities. 

Benefits include skill development training and support for self-employment initiatives. 

Eligibility criteria include being a resident of rural areas and belonging to economically weaker sections. 

The application process involves submitting a form to the local employment office. 

The timeline for processing applications is typically within a few weeks. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Rural Development. 

Additional details about specific activities supported under the scheme may vary.
