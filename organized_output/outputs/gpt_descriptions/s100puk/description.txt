The S100Puk scheme is designed to provide financial assistance to the elderly population in India. 

The objective of the scheme is to ensure a basic standard of living for senior citizens. 

The benefits include a monthly pension to eligible beneficiaries. 

Eligibility criteria include being a resident of India and being above a certain age, typically 60 years. 

The application process involves submitting a form along with necessary documents to the local government office or online through designated portals. 

The timeline for disbursement of funds is usually monthly. 

The implementing authority for this scheme is typically the Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment or state government departments. 

Additional details may include specific provisions for differently-abled individuals or those belonging to marginalized communities.
