The RRF or Rural Road Fund is a government initiative aimed at improving rural infrastructure in India. 

The objective of the RRF is to enhance connectivity in rural areas through the construction and maintenance of rural roads. 

The benefits include improved access to markets, healthcare, and education for rural populations. 

The assistance provided under this scheme is primarily in the form of financial support for road construction projects. 

Eligibility criteria typically include projects that focus on rural connectivity and are proposed by state governments or local bodies. 

The application process involves submission of project proposals by the concerned state governments to the Ministry of Rural Development. 

The timeline for project approval and funding disbursement varies based on the nature of the proposal and the assessment process. 

The implementing authority for the RRF is the Ministry of Rural Development, Government of India. 

Additional details regarding specific funding amounts or project types may vary based on annual budgets and policy updates.
