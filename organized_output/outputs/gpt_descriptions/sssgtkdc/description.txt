The SSSGTKDC scheme stands for the Sardar Sarovar Sewa Samiti Gramin Tirth Yatra Development Committee. 

This scheme is aimed at promoting rural tourism and enhancing the livelihood of local communities. 

The objective is to develop and promote pilgrimage tourism in rural areas. 

The scheme provides financial assistance for the development of infrastructure related to pilgrimage sites. 

Eligible beneficiaries include local communities and organizations involved in tourism activities. 

The application process involves submitting a proposal to the concerned authorities for funding. 

The timeline for project approvals and fund disbursement may vary based on the specific project and its requirements. 

The implementing authorities include the Ministry of Tourism and state tourism departments. 

The scheme also aims to create employment opportunities for local residents. 

It focuses on preserving cultural heritage and promoting sustainable tourism practices.
