The Seloan Women scheme is designed to empower women in rural areas of India. 

The objective of the scheme is to promote self-employment and entrepreneurship among women. 

The scheme provides financial assistance to women for starting their own businesses. 

Eligible beneficiaries include women from rural areas who are above the age of 18. 

The application process involves submitting a proposal for the business along with necessary documents to the local implementing authority. 

The timeline for approval and disbursement of funds varies based on the application review process. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Rural Development in collaboration with state governments. 

Additional details regarding specific financial assistance amounts and types of businesses supported may vary by state.
