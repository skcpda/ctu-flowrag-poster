The scheme TMSHKBOCWWB stands for the Tamil Nadu Maternity Assistance Scheme for the Handicapped and the Backward Classes Women Workers in the Construction Industry. 

This scheme is designed to provide financial assistance to pregnant women workers in the construction sector who belong to backward classes and are handicapped. 

The main objective of the scheme is to ensure that women workers receive adequate support during their maternity period. 

The benefits include a one-time financial assistance of a specified amount to support the health and nutrition of the mother and child. 

Eligibility criteria include being a woman worker in the construction industry belonging to backward classes and having a disability. 

Applicants must provide proof of their employment in the construction sector and documentation of their disability. 

The application process typically involves submitting an application form along with necessary documents to the relevant local authority or department. 

The scheme is implemented by the Tamil Nadu government through its labor welfare department. 

Financial assistance is provided once during the maternity period. 

The scheme aims to improve maternal health and reduce the financial burden on women workers during pregnancy.
