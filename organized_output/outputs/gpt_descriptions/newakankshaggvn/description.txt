New Akanksha GGVN is a government welfare scheme aimed at supporting the education of underprivileged children.

The scheme focuses on providing financial assistance to students from economically weaker sections.

It aims to reduce dropout rates and improve educational outcomes for these children.

Benefits include scholarships for school fees and educational materials.

Eligibility is primarily based on the economic status of the family.

Students from families with an annual income below a specified threshold are eligible.

The application process typically involves submitting an application form along with necessary documents.

Documents may include income certificates and proof of enrollment in school.

The timeline for application submission is usually announced at the beginning of the academic year.

The scheme is implemented by the Department of Education at the state level.

Monitoring and evaluation are conducted to ensure effective utilization of funds.

The scheme may also include provisions for periodic reviews and updates based on feedback.
