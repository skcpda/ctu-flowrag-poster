The Mukhyamantri Kanya Suraksha Yojana (MKUSY) is a welfare scheme initiated by the Government of Madhya Pradesh. 

The scheme aims to improve the survival rate of girl children and promote their education. 

It seeks to empower families to invest in the education and health of their daughters. 

The benefits include financial assistance for the education of girl children from birth until they reach the age of 21. 

Families receive a fixed amount deposited in a bank account for the girl child. 

The assistance is provided at various stages including at birth and upon reaching certain educational milestones. 

Eligibility is primarily based on the girl child's birth in Madhya Pradesh. 

The family must belong to the Below Poverty Line (BPL) category. 

The application process involves filling out a form available at designated government offices. 

Documents required include the birth certificate of the girl child and proof of BPL status. 

The scheme is implemented by the Department of Women and Child Development, Madhya Pradesh. 

The financial assistance is provided in installments at specified ages. 

The scheme aims to encourage families to prioritize the education of their daughters.
