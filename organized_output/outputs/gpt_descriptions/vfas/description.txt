The Vikas Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana (VPMAY) is a government welfare scheme aimed at providing affordable housing to the urban poor in India. 

The objective of the scheme is to ensure that all citizens have access to housing and to promote the construction of affordable housing units. 

The benefits include financial assistance for housing construction and the provision of housing units at subsidized rates. 

Eligibility is primarily targeted at economically weaker sections and low-income groups. 

The application process involves submitting an online application through the official PMAY website or through designated Common Service Centers. 

The timeline for the scheme is ongoing with periodic reviews and updates to the beneficiaries. 

The Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs is the implementing authority for this scheme. 

The scheme also aims to promote sustainable and inclusive urban development.
