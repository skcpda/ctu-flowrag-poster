The TPGFTCW scheme stands for the "Tripura Pratiyogita Gyan Fellowship for Tribal Community Women." 

This scheme is aimed at providing financial assistance and support to tribal women in Tripura. 

The objective is to empower tribal women through education and skill development. 

The scheme offers fellowships to eligible candidates to pursue higher education and vocational training. 

Eligible beneficiaries are tribal women who are residents of Tripura. 

They must belong to Scheduled Tribes and meet specific educational criteria. 

The application process involves submitting an online application form along with required documents. 

The timeline for application submission is usually announced by the implementing authority. 

The scheme is implemented by the Tribal Welfare Department of the Government of Tripura. 

Additional details about the fellowship amount and specific training programs may vary.
