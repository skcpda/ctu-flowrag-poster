The One Time Grant for Micro and Small Enterprises Scheme (OTGMP) is an initiative by the Government of India. 

It aims to provide financial assistance to micro and small enterprises affected by the COVID-19 pandemic. 

The objective is to support businesses in recovering from the economic impact of the pandemic. 

Benefits include a one-time grant to eligible enterprises to help them resume operations. 

The assistance amount varies based on the specific needs of the business. 

Eligibility criteria include being a registered micro or small enterprise. 

The enterprise must have been operational before the pandemic and must have suffered losses due to COVID-19. 

The application process involves submitting an online application through the designated portal. 

Supporting documents such as proof of registration and financial statements may be required. 

The timeline for disbursement of funds is typically defined after the application is approved. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises. 

State governments may also play a role in the implementation at the local level. 

Further details can be accessed through the official government portal.
