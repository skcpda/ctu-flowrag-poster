The National Food Security Act 2013 aims to provide subsidized food grains to the eligible population in India. 

The objective is to ensure food and nutritional security by providing access to adequate food at affordable prices. 

The scheme benefits approximately two-thirds of India's population through the Public Distribution System. 

Eligible beneficiaries include households identified as below the poverty line and priority households as per the guidelines. 

The application process involves the submission of an application to the local authorities for inclusion in the below poverty line list. 

The distribution of food grains occurs on a monthly basis. 

The implementing authority is the Ministry of Consumer Affairs Food and Public Distribution. 

State governments are responsible for the implementation of the scheme at the local level. 

The scheme also includes provisions for maternity benefits and nutritional support for children. 

The act mandates the establishment of grievance redressal mechanisms to address issues faced by beneficiaries.
