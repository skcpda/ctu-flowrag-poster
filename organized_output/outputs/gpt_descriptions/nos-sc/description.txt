The NOS-SC scheme is designed to provide financial assistance to Scheduled Castes students in India. 

The objective of the scheme is to promote education among Scheduled Castes students by providing them with scholarships. 

The benefits include financial support for tuition fees and maintenance allowances. 

Eligibility is limited to students belonging to Scheduled Castes who are pursuing education in recognized institutions. 

The application process typically involves submitting an online application along with required documents to the concerned authorities. 

The timeline for disbursement of scholarships may vary based on the academic calendar and processing time. 

The implementing authority for the NOS-SC scheme is the Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment. 

Additional details may include specific guidelines on the amount of scholarship and the duration of support based on the level of education.
