The Mukhyamantri Chiranjeevi Yojana (MCSBY) is a health insurance scheme launched by the Government of Rajasthan. 

It aims to provide health coverage to families living below the poverty line. 

The objective is to ensure access to quality healthcare services for economically weaker sections. 

The scheme offers health insurance coverage of up to Rs 5 lakh per family per year. 

It covers hospitalization expenses for various medical treatments including surgeries and critical illnesses. 

Beneficiaries can avail cashless treatment at empaneled hospitals. 

Eligibility is primarily based on the socio-economic status of the family. 

Families identified under the Socio-Economic Caste Census are eligible for the scheme. 

The application process involves filling out a form available at designated centers or online through the official website. 

The scheme is implemented by the Department of Medical and Health, Government of Rajasthan. 

The coverage is available on an annual basis. 

Renewal of the coverage is automatic as long as the family remains eligible. 

The scheme aims to reduce out-of-pocket expenses for healthcare among the poor. 

It also focuses on improving health outcomes in the state.
