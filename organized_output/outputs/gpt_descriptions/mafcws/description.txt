**Mahatma Gandhi National Rural Employment Guarantee Act (MGNREGA)**

**Context or Background**  
The Mahatma Gandhi National Rural Employment Guarantee Act was enacted in 2005.  
It aims to enhance livelihood security in rural areas of India.  
The act provides a legal guarantee for at least 100 days of unskilled wage employment in a financial year to every rural household.  
It was introduced to address the issues of rural poverty and unemployment.  
The scheme is a significant step in providing social security to rural households.  
It empowers rural citizens by providing them with a means to earn a livelihood.  

**Objectives**  
The primary objective of MGNREGA is to provide at least 100 days of wage employment in a financial year.  
It aims to enhance livelihood security in rural areas.  
The scheme seeks to create durable assets in rural areas.  
It also aims to strengthen the rural economy by increasing purchasing power.  
Another objective is to promote social inclusion by providing employment to marginalized communities.  

**Benefits and Assistance**  
Under MGNREGA, every rural household is entitled to 100 days of unskilled wage employment.  
The wage rate is determined by the state government and is revised periodically.  
The scheme also provides for the creation of durable assets such as roads, ponds, and irrigation facilities.  
It helps in improving rural infrastructure and enhancing productivity.  
The scheme promotes self-reliance among rural households.  
It also provides a safety net during lean agricultural seasons.  

**Eligibility**  
All rural households are eligible for employment under MGNREGA.  
The scheme does not discriminate based on caste, creed, or gender.  
Households must apply for a job card to avail of the benefits.  
Job cards are issued to every rural household that applies for one.  

**Application Process**  
To apply for MGNREGA, households must fill out an application form for a job card.  
The application can be submitted at the Gram Panchayat office.  
Once the application is verified, a job card is issued to the household.  
Households can then apply for work by submitting a written request to the Gram Panchayat.  
The Gram Panchayat is responsible for providing employment within 15 days of the request.  

**Timeline or Frequency**  
The scheme operates throughout the year.  
Employment is provided based on the demand from households.  
The wage payment is made within 15 days of the completion of work.  
The financial year runs from April to March.  
Households can avail of the 100 days of employment within this period.  

**Implementing Authorities or Agencies**  
The Ministry of Rural Development is the nodal ministry for implementing MGNREGA.  
State governments are responsible for the execution of the scheme at the state level.  
Gram Panchayats play a crucial role in the implementation of the scheme.  
They are responsible for planning and executing works under MGNREGA.  
The scheme is monitored by various levels of government to ensure transparency and accountability.
