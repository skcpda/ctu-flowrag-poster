The TFAPPS scheme stands for the Technology Facilitation and Assistance for Public Procurement System. 

It was launched to enhance the efficiency and transparency of public procurement processes in India. 

The primary objective is to provide technological support to government departments and public sector undertakings. 

The scheme aims to streamline procurement procedures and reduce delays in project implementation. 

Benefits include improved access to technology for government agencies. 

It also facilitates better decision-making through data-driven insights. 

Eligibility criteria include government departments and public sector undertakings involved in procurement activities. 

The application process involves submitting a request for technological assistance through designated channels. 

Implementing authorities include the Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology. 

The scheme is part of the broader initiative to digitize and modernize public services in India.
