The MVP scheme stands for the Mukhyamantri Vatsalya Yojana. 

It was launched by the Government of Madhya Pradesh. 

The scheme aims to provide financial assistance to orphaned children. 

The objective is to ensure the welfare and development of these children. 

Under the scheme, financial support is provided for education and basic needs. 

Eligible beneficiaries include children who have lost both parents. 

Children who have lost one parent may also be considered under specific circumstances. 

The application process involves submitting a request to the local authorities. 

Documents required may include proof of orphanhood and identity. 

The assistance is provided on a monthly basis. 

The implementing authority for this scheme is the Department of Women and Child Development, Madhya Pradesh. 

The scheme is part of the state's broader efforts to support vulnerable children.
