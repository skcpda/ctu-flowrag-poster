The Pradhan Mantri Suraksha Bima Yojana (PMSBY) is a government welfare scheme aimed at providing accidental death and disability coverage. 

It was launched on May 9 2015. 

The objective of the scheme is to provide financial support to families in case of accidental death or disability of the insured person. 

The scheme offers a coverage of INR 2 lakh for accidental death and full disability. 

In case of partial disability the coverage is INR 1 lakh. 

The premium for the scheme is INR 12 per annum. 

Eligibility is open to all individuals aged between 18 and 70 years. 

Applicants must have a savings bank account. 

The application process can be completed through banks or online platforms. 

The coverage period is one year and is renewable. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Finance through public sector banks and private sector banks. 

The scheme is available across India. 

The benefits are provided in case of accidents occurring during the policy period. 

The scheme aims to enhance the financial security of individuals and their families.
