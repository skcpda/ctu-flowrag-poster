The Overseas Employment Certificate Program is a welfare scheme initiated by the Ministry of External Affairs of India. 

The program aims to facilitate the overseas employment of Indian citizens. 

It provides a legal framework for Indians seeking employment abroad. 

The scheme ensures that Indian workers are protected and their rights are upheld in foreign countries. 

Benefits include assistance in obtaining necessary documentation for employment abroad. 

The program also provides guidance on safe migration practices. 

Eligibility criteria include Indian citizens seeking employment in foreign countries. 

Applicants must meet specific requirements set by the Ministry of External Affairs. 

The application process involves submitting an online application through the official portal. 

Applicants may need to provide supporting documents such as identity proof and employment contracts. 

The timeline for processing applications may vary based on individual cases. 

The Ministry of External Affairs is the implementing authority for this scheme. 

Additional details regarding specific requirements and procedures can be found on the official website.
