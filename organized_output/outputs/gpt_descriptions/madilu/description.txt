Madilu is a welfare scheme initiated by the Government of Karnataka. 

The scheme aims to provide financial assistance to pregnant women and lactating mothers. 

It is part of the state's efforts to improve maternal and child health. 

The objective of the Madilu scheme is to reduce maternal and infant mortality rates. 

The scheme also seeks to promote institutional deliveries. 

Under this scheme, eligible beneficiaries receive a financial incentive. 

The assistance is provided to support the nutritional needs of mothers during pregnancy and lactation. 

The amount provided under the scheme is intended to help cover medical expenses. 

It also aims to encourage women to seek regular health check-ups. 

The scheme is particularly focused on women from economically weaker sections. 

Eligibility for the Madilu scheme includes pregnant women and lactating mothers. 

Beneficiaries must be residents of Karnataka. 

They should belong to families with low income levels. 

The scheme is applicable to women who are above 18 years of age. 

Women who have had a previous pregnancy with complications may also be eligible. 

The application process for the Madilu scheme involves several steps. 

Interested beneficiaries must approach the local Anganwadi center. 

They need to fill out an application form provided by the Anganwadi worker. 

The application requires submission of identification documents. 

Documents may include proof of residence and income certificates. 

Once the application is submitted, it is verified by the Anganwadi worker. 

After verification, eligible beneficiaries are registered under the scheme. 

The financial assistance is disbursed directly to the beneficiaries' bank accounts. 

The assistance is typically provided in installments. 

The first installment is given during the first trimester of pregnancy. 

Subsequent installments are provided during the later stages of pregnancy and lactation. 

The timeline for the disbursement of funds is aligned with the stages of pregnancy. 

The scheme is implemented by the Department of Women and Child Development in Karnataka. 

Local Anganwadi centers play a crucial role in the execution of the scheme. 

They are responsible for outreach and awareness among potential beneficiaries. 

The scheme also collaborates with healthcare providers for maternal health services. 

Monitoring and evaluation of the scheme are conducted regularly. 

This ensures that the benefits reach the intended recipients.
