Pradhan Mantri Mudra Yojana (PMMY) was launched on April 8 2015. 

It aims to provide financial support to small and micro enterprises in India. 

The objective is to promote entrepreneurship and self-employment among individuals. 

The scheme offers loans up to 10 lakh rupees. 

Loans are categorized into three types: Shishu for up to 50 000 rupees Tarun for 5 lakh to 10 lakh rupees and Kishore for 50 000 to 5 lakh rupees. 

The benefits include easy access to credit and reduced interest rates. 

Eligibility is open to individuals and businesses engaged in manufacturing trading and services. 

Applicants must be above 18 years of age and have a viable business plan. 

The application process involves approaching a bank or financial institution. 

Applicants need to submit necessary documents including identity proof and business details. 

The timeline for loan approval varies by institution but typically ranges from a few days to a few weeks. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Finance through various banks and financial institutions. 

It is monitored by the Mudra Corporation. 

The scheme also aims to create jobs and boost the economy.
