The OTSDFPC scheme stands for the One Time Settlement of Defaulting Farmers' Cooperative Societies. 

This scheme was introduced to address the financial distress faced by farmers who are members of cooperative societies. 

The primary objective is to provide a one-time settlement option for defaulting farmers to clear their dues. 

The scheme aims to revive the cooperative societies and ensure the financial stability of farmers. 

Benefits include the waiver of interest and penalties on outstanding loans. 

Eligible beneficiaries are farmers who are members of cooperative societies and have defaulted on their loans. 

The application process involves submitting a request to the respective cooperative society. 

The timeline for processing applications may vary based on the cooperative society's internal procedures. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Cooperation and respective state cooperative departments. 

Additional details regarding specific timelines and procedures may be available through the respective state cooperative departments.
