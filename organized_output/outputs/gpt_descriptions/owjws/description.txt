The One Woman One Job Work Scheme (OWJWS) is an initiative by the Government of India. 

It aims to empower women by providing them with employment opportunities. 

The objective is to enhance women's participation in the workforce. 

The scheme focuses on skill development and job placement for women. 

Benefits include financial assistance and support for skill training. 

Eligible beneficiaries are women who are unemployed or underemployed. 

The application process typically involves online registration through designated portals. 

The timeline for processing applications may vary based on the implementing agency. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Women and Child Development. 

Additional details about specific training programs and job placements may be available through official channels.
