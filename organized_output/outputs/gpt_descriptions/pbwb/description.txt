The Punjab Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board (PBWB) is a welfare scheme initiated by the Government of Punjab. 

The scheme aims to provide financial assistance and welfare benefits to construction workers in the state. 

It seeks to improve the living standards of workers engaged in the building and construction industry. 

Benefits under the scheme include financial assistance for medical treatment, education, and marriage of workers' children. 

Workers may also receive assistance for housing and other social security measures. 

Eligibility criteria include being a registered construction worker under the PBWB. 

Workers must have completed the necessary registration process to avail of the benefits. 

The application process involves submitting an application form along with required documents to the PBWB office. 

The timeline for processing applications may vary based on the type of assistance requested. 

The PBWB is the implementing authority for this scheme. 

Additional details regarding specific benefits and assistance can be obtained from the PBWB office or its official website.
