The Rajiv Gandhi Scheme for Rural Housing (RGSRY) aims to provide affordable housing to the rural poor in India. 

The primary objective is to enhance the living conditions of rural households by providing financial assistance for housing construction. 

The scheme offers financial support to eligible beneficiaries for the construction of houses. 

The assistance amount varies based on the state and is determined by the government. 

Eligibility is generally limited to individuals belonging to Below Poverty Line (BPL) families in rural areas. 

Applicants must also meet specific criteria set by the state government. 

The application process typically involves submitting a form along with required documents to the local authorities or designated offices. 

The timeline for fund disbursement and house construction may vary based on state regulations and the applicant's compliance with the process. 

The implementing authorities include the Ministry of Rural Development and state governments. 

The scheme is part of the broader initiative to promote housing for all in rural India.
