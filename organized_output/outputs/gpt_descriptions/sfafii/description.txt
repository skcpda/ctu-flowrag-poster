The SFAFII scheme stands for the Scheme for Financial Assistance for Farmers in India. 

This scheme aims to provide financial support to farmers facing economic hardships. 

The objective is to enhance the income of farmers and ensure food security. 

Benefits include direct financial assistance to eligible farmers. 

The assistance amount varies based on specific criteria set by the implementing authorities. 

Eligibility is generally limited to small and marginal farmers. 

Farmers must possess valid land records to qualify for the scheme. 

The application process typically involves submitting an application form along with necessary documentation. 

Applications can usually be submitted online or at designated government offices. 

The timeline for disbursement of funds may vary depending on the processing of applications. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers' Welfare. 

State governments may also play a role in the implementation at the local level. 

Additional details about the scheme can be found on official government websites.
