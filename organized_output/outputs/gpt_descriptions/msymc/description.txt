The Mukhyamantri Sewa Yojana for Migrant Workers (MSYMC) is an initiative by the Government of India. 

It aims to provide support and assistance to migrant workers facing challenges due to their mobility. 

The objectives include ensuring the welfare of migrant workers and facilitating their access to essential services. 

Benefits include financial assistance for transportation and accommodation. 

Assistance may also cover healthcare services and skill development programs. 

Eligibility criteria typically include being a registered migrant worker and meeting specific income thresholds. 

The application process generally involves submitting an online application through designated portals. 

The timeline for disbursement of benefits may vary based on the specific assistance requested. 

Implementing authorities include state governments and local administrative bodies. 

The scheme is part of a broader effort to enhance the livelihood and welfare of migrant workers in India.
