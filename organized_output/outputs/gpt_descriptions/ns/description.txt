The National Scheme for Welfare of Fishermen and Fish Farmers is designed to support the fishing community in India. 

The objective of the scheme is to enhance the socio-economic conditions of fishermen and fish farmers. 

The benefits include financial assistance for various activities such as fishing, aquaculture, and post-harvest management. 

Eligible beneficiaries are primarily fishermen and fish farmers who are registered with the relevant authorities. 

The application process typically involves submitting an application form along with necessary documents to the local fisheries department. 

The timeline for processing applications may vary based on the specific assistance sought. 

The scheme is implemented by the Department of Fisheries under the Ministry of Fisheries Animal Husbandry and Dairying. 

Additional details may include training programs and workshops aimed at improving skills and knowledge in fishing practices.
