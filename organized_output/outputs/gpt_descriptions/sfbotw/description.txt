The scheme "SFBoTW" stands for "Sustainable Farming and Business Opportunities for Tribal Women."

The scheme aims to empower tribal women by providing them with sustainable farming and business opportunities.

The primary objective is to enhance the livelihood of tribal women through skill development and financial assistance.

Benefits include training in sustainable agricultural practices and access to financial resources for starting businesses.

Eligible beneficiaries are tribal women who are part of Scheduled Tribes.

The application process typically involves submitting an application form along with necessary documents to the local implementing agency.

The timeline for the scheme's implementation varies based on the specific program and location.

Implementing authorities include the Ministry of Tribal Affairs and various state governments.

The scheme also focuses on promoting self-help groups among tribal women to foster entrepreneurship.
