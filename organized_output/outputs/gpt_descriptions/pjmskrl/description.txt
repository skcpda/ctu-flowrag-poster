The Pradhan Mantri Jan Dhan Yojana (PMJDY) is a financial inclusion program launched by the Government of India. 

It was initiated on August 28 2014. 

The objective of PMJDY is to provide universal access to banking facilities. 

It aims to ensure that every household has at least one bank account. 

The scheme promotes financial literacy and access to credit. 

Benefits include zero balance accounts and access to overdraft facilities. 

Account holders are eligible for a RuPay debit card and insurance coverage. 

Eligibility is open to all Indian citizens aged 10 years and above. 

The application process involves filling out a form at any bank branch or online. 

The timeline for account opening is immediate upon submission of required documents. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Finance through various banks. 

Additional details include the provision of financial literacy programs. 

The scheme also facilitates direct benefit transfers for various government schemes.
