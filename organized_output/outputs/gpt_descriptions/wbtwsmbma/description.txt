The WBTWSMBMA stands for the West Bengal Tribal Women Self-Help Group and Micro-Business Management Assistance scheme. 

This scheme is implemented by the Government of West Bengal. 

It aims to empower tribal women by promoting self-help groups. 

The objective is to enhance the socio-economic status of tribal women. 

The scheme provides financial assistance to self-help groups for starting micro-businesses. 

Benefits include training in entrepreneurship and management skills. 

Eligible beneficiaries are tribal women who are members of self-help groups. 

The application process involves submitting a proposal through the self-help group. 

The timeline for assistance is determined based on the project approval. 

The implementing authority is the Department of Tribal Development, Government of West Bengal. 

Additional details may include the requirement for regular monitoring and evaluation of the groups.
