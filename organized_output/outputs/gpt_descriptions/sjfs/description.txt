The scheme SJFS stands for the "Sustainable Livelihoods and Financial Inclusion Scheme."

The scheme aims to enhance the livelihoods of marginalized communities in India.

It focuses on providing financial assistance and promoting self-employment opportunities.

The objectives include reducing poverty and improving the standard of living for beneficiaries.

Benefits include access to credit facilities and skill development training.

Assistance is provided in the form of loans and grants for setting up small enterprises.

Eligibility criteria typically include being a member of a marginalized community or group.

Applicants must also demonstrate a viable business plan or skill set.

The application process usually involves submitting a form to the local implementing agency.

Supporting documents may be required to verify eligibility and business viability.

The timeline for processing applications can vary based on the implementing agency.

The scheme is implemented by various state governments in collaboration with central agencies.

Additional details may include specific training programs and workshops offered under the scheme.
