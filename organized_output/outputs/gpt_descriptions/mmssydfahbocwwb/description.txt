The Mukhyamantri Mahila Samriddhi Yojana for the Self-Help Groups is a welfare scheme initiated by the Government of Madhya Pradesh. 

The scheme aims to empower women through financial assistance and skill development. 

It focuses on enhancing the income and livelihood opportunities for women in rural areas. 

The benefits include financial support for self-help groups and training programs for skill development. 

Women belonging to economically weaker sections are eligible to apply. 

The application process involves submitting a form to the local government office or through designated online platforms. 

The scheme is implemented by the Department of Women and Child Development in Madhya Pradesh. 

The frequency of assistance and training programs is determined by the implementing authorities based on the availability of funds and resources.
