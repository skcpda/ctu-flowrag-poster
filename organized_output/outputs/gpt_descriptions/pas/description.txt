The Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana (PMAY) is a government initiative aimed at providing affordable housing to the urban poor in India. 

The scheme was launched in June 2015. 

Its objective is to ensure that every family has access to a pucca house with basic amenities by the year 2022. 

The benefits include financial assistance for housing construction or purchase. 

The scheme offers interest subsidies on home loans. 

It also provides support for the construction of houses under the Beneficiary-led Construction component. 

Eligibility for the scheme includes being a citizen of India. 

Applicants must belong to the Economically Weaker Section or Low-Income Group. 

The scheme is also applicable to women and members of Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes. 

The application process involves filling out an online form on the official PMAY website. 

Applicants may also need to submit relevant documents such as income proof and identity proof. 

The timeline for processing applications varies but is generally completed within a few months. 

The Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs is the implementing authority for this scheme. 

State governments and urban local bodies also play a role in implementation. 

The scheme is monitored through various online platforms to ensure transparency and efficiency.
