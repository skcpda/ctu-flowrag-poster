The Mukhyamantri Bhatta Yojana (MBYB) is a welfare scheme initiated by the Government of Madhya Pradesh. 

The scheme aims to provide financial assistance to unemployed youth in the state. 

It seeks to support young individuals while they search for employment opportunities. 

The benefits include a monthly stipend provided to eligible youth. 

The amount of the stipend is determined by the state government. 

Eligibility for the scheme includes youth aged between 18 to 35 years. 

Applicants must be residents of Madhya Pradesh. 

They should be registered with the employment exchange. 

The application process involves submitting an online application through the official portal. 

Applicants may need to provide identity proof and other relevant documents. 

The scheme is implemented by the Department of Skill Development and Employment. 

The frequency of stipend disbursement is monthly. 

The duration for which the stipend is provided may vary as per government guidelines.
