The OBC Pre-Matric Scholarship Scheme (OBCPMS) is designed to provide financial assistance to students belonging to Other Backward Classes. 

The scheme aims to encourage education among OBC students by reducing their financial burden. 

It provides scholarships to students studying in classes 1 to 10. 

The assistance includes a monthly stipend and a one-time grant for books and other educational materials. 

Eligibility is limited to students from OBC families whose annual income does not exceed a specified limit. 

Students must be enrolled in recognized schools to qualify for the scholarship. 

The application process typically involves filling out an online application form and submitting required documents. 

The scholarship is disbursed annually based on the academic year. 

The implementing authority for this scheme is the Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment. 

State governments may also have their own specific guidelines and processes for administering the scheme.
