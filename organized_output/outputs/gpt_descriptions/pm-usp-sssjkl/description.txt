The Pradhan Mantri Ujjwala Yojana (PMUY) is a government initiative aimed at providing clean cooking fuel to households in India. 

The scheme was launched to reduce the health hazards associated with the use of traditional cooking fuels such as firewood and coal. 

It aims to empower women by providing them with access to clean energy. 

The primary objective is to provide LPG connections to women from Below Poverty Line households. 

Beneficiaries receive a financial assistance of ₹1,600 for each LPG connection. 

The scheme also includes a subsidy on the LPG cylinders. 

Eligibility is limited to women from Below Poverty Line households. 

Applicants must provide proof of their BPL status and identity. 

The application process involves filling out a form available at designated outlets or online. 

The timeline for receiving the LPG connection can vary based on the service provider. 

The Ministry of Petroleum and Natural Gas is the implementing authority for this scheme. 

The scheme is part of the government's broader initiative to promote clean energy and improve public health.
