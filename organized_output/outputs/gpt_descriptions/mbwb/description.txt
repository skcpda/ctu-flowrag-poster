The Mukhyamantri Bhatta Yojana (MBWB) is a welfare scheme initiated by the Government of Madhya Pradesh. 

The scheme aims to provide financial assistance to the unemployed youth in the state. 

It seeks to support the youth in their pursuit of employment opportunities. 

The benefits include a monthly stipend to eligible beneficiaries. 

The assistance is intended to help them sustain themselves while they search for jobs. 

Eligibility criteria include being a resident of Madhya Pradesh. 

Applicants must be between the ages of 18 and 35 years. 

They should be unemployed and actively seeking employment. 

The application process involves submitting an online application through the official government portal. 

Applicants may need to provide necessary documents such as identity proof and educational qualifications. 

The scheme is implemented by the Department of Skill Development and Employment. 

The financial assistance is provided on a monthly basis. 

The duration of the assistance may vary based on specific guidelines set by the implementing authority. 

The scheme is part of the state government's efforts to reduce unemployment. 

It aims to empower youth by providing them with financial support during their job search.
