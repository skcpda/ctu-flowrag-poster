The SSWC-HLWB scheme is designed to provide financial assistance to the widows of armed forces personnel. 

The objective of the scheme is to support the widows in their financial needs and ensure their welfare. 

The benefits include a monthly pension to eligible widows to help them sustain their livelihoods. 

Eligibility is limited to widows of armed forces personnel who died while in service or due to service-related causes. 

The application process involves submitting a prescribed application form along with necessary documents to the concerned authorities. 

The timeline for processing applications may vary based on the specific circumstances and completeness of the application. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Defence in collaboration with respective armed forces welfare boards. 

Additional details regarding the specific amount of assistance and other support services may be available through official channels.
