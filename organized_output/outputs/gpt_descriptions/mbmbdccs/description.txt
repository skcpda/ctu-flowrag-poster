The Mukhyamantri Bhatta Mukhyamantri Dhan Kalyan Yojana is a welfare scheme initiated by the Government of Madhya Pradesh. 

The scheme aims to provide financial assistance to the economically weaker sections of society. 

The objective is to enhance the living standards of beneficiaries by providing them with a monthly financial support. 

Eligible beneficiaries include individuals from economically weaker sections and those without a stable source of income. 

The application process involves submitting a form along with required documents to the designated government office. 

The scheme provides financial assistance on a monthly basis. 

The implementing authority for this scheme is the Department of Social Justice and Disabled Persons Welfare, Government of Madhya Pradesh. 

Additional details about the specific amount of assistance and required documentation may vary.
