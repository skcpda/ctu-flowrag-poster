The National Social Security Fund for Unorganized Workers (NSSFP) aims to provide social security benefits to unorganized sector workers in India. 

The objective of NSSFP is to enhance the welfare of unorganized workers by providing them with financial assistance and social security benefits. 

The scheme offers various benefits including life insurance, health insurance, and pension schemes. 

Eligibility for the NSSFP includes unorganized workers aged between 18 to 60 years. 

Workers must be engaged in the unorganized sector and should not be covered under any other social security scheme. 

The application process involves registering online through the official portal or visiting designated offices for assistance. 

The timeline for the disbursement of benefits varies based on the specific assistance being provided. 

The Ministry of Labour and Employment is the primary implementing authority for the NSSFP. 

State governments may also play a role in the implementation of the scheme at the local level. 

Additional details about specific benefits and assistance can be found on the official government portal.
