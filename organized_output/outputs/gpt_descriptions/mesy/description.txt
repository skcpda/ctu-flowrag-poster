The Mukhyamantri E-Sewa Yojana (MESY) is a welfare scheme launched by the Government of Madhya Pradesh. 

The scheme aims to provide various government services to citizens through a digital platform. 

It seeks to enhance transparency and efficiency in the delivery of services. 

The benefits include access to services such as certificates licenses and permits online. 

Citizens can avail themselves of these services without the need for physical visits to government offices. 

Eligibility criteria typically include being a resident of Madhya Pradesh. 

The application process involves registering on the official MESY portal. 

Users must provide necessary documents as per the specific service they are applying for. 

The timeline for service delivery varies depending on the type of service requested. 

The scheme is implemented by the Department of Information Technology and the Government of Madhya Pradesh. 

Additional details may include ongoing updates to the services offered under the scheme.
