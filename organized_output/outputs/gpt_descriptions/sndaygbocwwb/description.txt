The scheme is known as the "SNDAYGBOCWWB" which stands for "Sustainable Development of the National Agricultural Market and Development of the Agricultural Sector for the Welfare of the Backward Classes and Weaker Sections."

The background of the scheme is to enhance the livelihood opportunities for the backward classes and weaker sections in rural areas.

The primary objective is to promote sustainable agricultural practices and improve the income of the marginalized farming community.

Benefits include financial assistance for agricultural development and access to modern farming techniques.

Eligibility criteria focus on individuals from backward classes and weaker sections engaged in agriculture.

The application process typically involves submitting an application form along with necessary documentation to the designated authority.

The timeline for the disbursement of benefits may vary based on the specific project or initiative under the scheme.

Implementing authorities include the Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers' Welfare and state agricultural departments.
