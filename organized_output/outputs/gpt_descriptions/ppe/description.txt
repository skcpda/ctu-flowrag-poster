The Personal Protective Equipment (PPE) scheme was initiated in response to the COVID-19 pandemic. 

The objective of the scheme is to ensure the availability of personal protective equipment for healthcare workers and frontline workers. 

The benefits include the provision of quality PPE kits to protect workers from infection. 

Eligibility is primarily for healthcare workers and frontline workers engaged in COVID-19 management. 

The application process involves procurement through designated government channels. 

The timeline for distribution is based on the urgency of need and ongoing assessments of the pandemic situation. 

Implementing authorities include the Ministry of Health and Family Welfare and various state health departments. 

The scheme also aims to promote domestic manufacturing of PPE to reduce dependency on imports. 

It supports local industries and ensures the availability of PPE kits in sufficient quantities.
