The RS-MESIFAI scheme is a welfare initiative launched by the Government of India. 

It aims to provide financial assistance to marginalized communities for skill development and employment opportunities. 

The primary objective is to enhance the employability of individuals from economically weaker sections. 

The scheme offers financial support for training programs and skill development initiatives. 

Eligible beneficiaries include individuals from Scheduled Castes Scheduled Tribes and other backward classes. 

The application process involves submitting an online application through the designated portal. 

The timeline for processing applications may vary based on the specific training program. 

The Ministry of Skill Development and Entrepreneurship is the implementing authority for this scheme. 

Additional details regarding specific training programs and financial assistance amounts may be available on the official portal.
