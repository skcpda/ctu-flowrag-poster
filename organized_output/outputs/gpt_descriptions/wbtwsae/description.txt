The WBTWSAE scheme stands for the West Bengal Tribal Women Self-Help and Empowerment Scheme. 

This scheme is implemented by the Government of West Bengal. 

It aims to empower tribal women through self-help groups. 

The objective is to enhance the socio-economic status of tribal women. 

The scheme provides financial assistance to women for income-generating activities. 

Benefits include access to training programs and skill development initiatives. 

Eligibility is primarily for tribal women residing in West Bengal. 

Applicants must be members of self-help groups. 

The application process involves submitting an application to the concerned department. 

The timeline for fund disbursement may vary based on the project. 

The implementing authority is the Department of Tribal Development, Government of West Bengal. 

Additional details may include the promotion of entrepreneurship among tribal women. 

The scheme also focuses on improving access to markets for their products.
