The Mukhyamantri Kanya Vivah Yojana (MKVB) is a welfare scheme launched by the Government of Madhya Pradesh. 

The scheme aims to provide financial assistance for the marriage of daughters from economically weaker sections of society. 

The objective is to promote the marriage of girls and reduce the financial burden on families. 

Under this scheme, a financial assistance of INR 51,000 is provided for the marriage of eligible girls. 

The benefits are aimed at families with a low income and those who may struggle to afford marriage expenses. 

Eligibility criteria include being a resident of Madhya Pradesh and belonging to a financially weaker section. 

The girl must be at least 18 years old at the time of marriage. 

The application process involves submitting a form along with necessary documents to the local government office. 

Documents required may include proof of age and income certificate. 

The scheme is implemented by the Department of Women and Child Development in Madhya Pradesh. 

The assistance is provided once the marriage is registered and verified by the authorities. 

The frequency of the scheme is ongoing and applications can be submitted throughout the year. 

Additional details may include awareness campaigns to educate families about the scheme.
