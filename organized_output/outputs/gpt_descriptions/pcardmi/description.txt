The Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana - Credit Linked Subsidy Scheme (PMAY-CLSS) is a government initiative aimed at providing affordable housing to the urban poor.

The scheme was launched in June 2015 under the Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana.

The primary objective is to provide financial assistance to eligible beneficiaries for the purchase or construction of houses.

The scheme offers interest subsidies on home loans for the economically weaker sections and low-income groups.

The benefits include an interest subsidy of up to 6.5 percent on home loans for a tenure of 20 years.

Eligible beneficiaries include individuals from the Economically Weaker Section, Low Income Group, and Middle Income Group.

Applicants must have a valid Aadhar number and should not own a pucca house in their name or in the name of any family member.

The application process can be initiated through banks or housing finance companies.

Applicants need to fill out the application form and submit the required documents along with it.

The timeline for disbursal of subsidies varies based on the processing time of the financial institutions.

The Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs is the implementing authority for this scheme.

The scheme is part of the broader initiative to ensure housing for all by 2022.
