Shodhs is a government initiative aimed at promoting research and development in various fields. 

The objective of Shodhs is to enhance the quality of research and innovation in India. 

It seeks to provide financial assistance to researchers and institutions engaged in scientific studies. 

The scheme offers grants for research projects that align with national priorities. 

Eligibility is generally extended to researchers affiliated with recognized institutions and universities. 

The application process typically involves submitting a detailed project proposal. 

The timeline for grant approval may vary depending on the project and the reviewing authority. 

The implementing authority for Shodhs is the Department of Science and Technology. 

Additional details regarding specific funding amounts and project durations may be available through official channels.
