The SWSVY scheme stands for the Skill Development and Self-Employment Scheme for Women. 

It was launched to empower women through skill development and promote self-employment opportunities. 

The main objective is to enhance the employability of women and encourage entrepreneurship. 

The scheme provides training in various skills to women. 

Financial assistance is offered to women who wish to start their own businesses. 

Eligibility criteria include being a woman aged 18 years and above. 

The applicant must belong to a low-income household. 

Women from marginalized communities are given preference. 

The application process involves submitting a form to the designated local authority or agency. 

The timeline for training programs varies based on the skill being taught. 

The implementing authorities include the Ministry of Women and Child Development. 

State governments also play a role in the execution of the scheme.
