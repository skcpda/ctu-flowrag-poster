The Pradhan Mantri Samagra Shiksha Scheme for Education of Backward Classes (PMSSSEBC) aims to enhance educational opportunities for students from backward classes. 

The objective is to provide quality education and promote the overall development of students belonging to socially and economically backward communities. 

The scheme offers financial assistance for educational expenses such as tuition fees and study materials. 

It also provides scholarships to encourage higher education among students from these communities. 

Eligibility is typically based on the socio-economic status of the family and the educational institution attended. 

The application process generally involves filling out a form and submitting it to the relevant educational authority or department. 

The timeline for disbursement of funds and scholarships may vary based on the academic calendar and specific guidelines issued by the implementing authorities. 

The Ministry of Education is the primary implementing authority for this scheme. 

State education departments may also play a role in the execution and monitoring of the scheme at the local level.
