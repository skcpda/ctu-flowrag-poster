The TFPPT scheme stands for the Technology Facilitation for Production and Productivity in Tribal Areas. 

It aims to enhance the production and productivity of tribal communities through the application of technology. 

The objectives include improving agricultural practices and increasing income levels among tribal farmers. 

Benefits include access to modern agricultural techniques and tools. 

Assistance may also involve training programs and workshops for skill development. 

Eligibility criteria typically include being a member of a Scheduled Tribe and residing in designated tribal areas. 

The application process generally involves submitting an application to the local implementing authority or agency. 

The timeline for assistance may vary based on the specific program and its implementation schedule. 

Implementing authorities include the Ministry of Tribal Affairs and state government departments focused on tribal welfare. 

Additional details may include collaboration with NGOs and other organizations to facilitate technology transfer.
