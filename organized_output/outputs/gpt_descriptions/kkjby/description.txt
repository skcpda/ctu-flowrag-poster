The Kisan Credit Card Scheme is also known as KKBY or Kisan Credit Card Yojana. 

It was launched by the Government of India in 1998. 

The scheme aims to provide farmers with timely access to credit. 

It helps them meet their agricultural and allied activities. 

The primary objective is to provide short-term credit support. 

This support is for the purchase of inputs such as seeds fertilizers and pesticides. 

The scheme also aims to provide financial assistance for post-harvest expenses. 

It facilitates the purchase of equipment and machinery for farming. 

The Kisan Credit Card Scheme also aims to reduce the dependence of farmers on informal sources of credit. 

Farmers can avail of credit at lower interest rates through this scheme. 

The benefits of the scheme include easy access to credit. 

Farmers can withdraw money from their Kisan Credit Card as needed. 

The scheme provides a credit limit based on the scale of finance. 

This is determined by the lending institutions. 

The credit limit is generally based on the area under cultivation. 

It also considers the type of crops grown by the farmer. 

Farmers can also avail of a higher credit limit for allied activities. 

These activities include dairy poultry and fisheries. 

The scheme provides insurance coverage for the cardholder. 

This coverage is against crop loss due to natural calamities. 

The Kisan Credit Card also offers a facility for overdraft. 

Farmers can withdraw funds beyond their credit limit in emergencies. 

Eligibility for the Kisan Credit Card includes being an Indian citizen. 

The applicant must be a farmer or a member of a farmer's family. 

The applicant should have a minimum landholding. 

The landholding requirement varies by state. 

Farmers must have a bank account with a scheduled bank. 

The application process involves submitting an application form. 

Farmers must provide necessary documents such as land records. 

They also need to submit identity proof and address proof. 

The application can be submitted at the nearest bank branch. 

Banks may also conduct an on-site inspection of the farm. 

Once approved the Kisan Credit Card is issued to the farmer. 

The timeline for processing applications varies by bank. 

Typically it takes a few days to a few weeks for approval. 

The Kisan Credit Card is valid for a certain period. 

Farmers can renew their credit card upon expiry. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers Welfare. 

State governments also play a role in the implementation. 

Public sector banks and cooperative banks are the primary lending institutions. 

The scheme is supported by various government initiatives. 

These initiatives aim to enhance agricultural productivity.
