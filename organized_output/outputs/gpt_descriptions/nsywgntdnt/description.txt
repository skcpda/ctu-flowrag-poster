The National Scheme for Welfare of Women and Children in Detention (NSYWGNTDNT) aims to support women and children who are in detention facilities. 

The primary objective is to ensure the welfare and rehabilitation of women and children in such facilities. 

The scheme provides various forms of assistance including legal aid health care and vocational training. 

Eligible beneficiaries include women and children who are currently in detention. 

The application process typically involves the concerned authorities in detention facilities submitting requests for assistance on behalf of the inmates. 

The timeline for assistance is determined based on the specific needs of the beneficiaries and the resources available. 

The Ministry of Women and Child Development is the implementing authority for this scheme. 

Additional details regarding specific benefits or operational guidelines may vary based on regional implementations.
