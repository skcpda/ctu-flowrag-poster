The West Bengal Madrasah Welfare Scheme for Children and Adolescents (WBMWWSCA) aims to provide financial assistance to students from minority communities. 

The objective of the scheme is to promote education among children and adolescents studying in madrasahs. 

The benefits include financial assistance for educational materials and support for various educational activities. 

Eligibility criteria include students enrolled in recognized madrasahs in West Bengal. 

The application process involves submitting an application form along with necessary documents to the respective madrasah authorities. 

The timeline for disbursing assistance is typically aligned with the academic calendar. 

The implementing authority for this scheme is the West Bengal Minority Affairs and Madrasah Education Department. 

Additional details may include specific financial amounts allocated per student and the types of educational materials covered.
