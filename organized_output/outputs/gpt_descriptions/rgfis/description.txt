The Rajiv Gandhi Grameen LPG Vitaran Yojana (RGFIS) aims to provide liquefied petroleum gas (LPG) connections to rural households. 

The objective is to promote the use of clean cooking fuel in rural areas. 

The scheme seeks to reduce dependence on traditional fuels such as firewood and kerosene. 

Benefits include financial assistance for purchasing LPG connections and cylinders. 

Eligible beneficiaries are identified from below poverty line households in rural areas. 

The application process involves submitting a form to the designated authorities in the local area. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Petroleum and Natural Gas. 

It aims to improve the health and living standards of rural populations. 

The timeline for connection provision may vary based on local implementation. 

The scheme also focuses on promoting the use of clean energy sources.
