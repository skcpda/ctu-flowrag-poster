The MSME-STEs scheme is designed to support Micro Small and Medium Enterprises in India. 

The objective of the scheme is to promote entrepreneurship and enhance the competitiveness of MSMEs. 

The scheme provides financial assistance for various activities including technology transfer and skill development. 

Eligible beneficiaries include existing MSMEs and new entrepreneurs looking to establish their businesses. 

The application process involves submitting a proposal to the relevant government authority. 

The timeline for processing applications may vary based on the specific requirements of the proposal. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Micro Small and Medium Enterprises.
