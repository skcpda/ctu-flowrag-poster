The Mukhyamantri Amrutum Maitri Yojana (MMACY) is a health insurance scheme initiated by the Government of Gujarat. 

It aims to provide financial assistance for medical treatment to economically weaker sections of society. 

The objective is to reduce the out-of-pocket expenditure on healthcare for low-income families. 

The scheme offers coverage for hospitalization expenses for various medical conditions. 

Beneficiaries receive cashless treatment in empaneled hospitals. 

The insurance coverage is up to a specified amount per family per year. 

Eligibility is primarily based on the income level of the family. 

Families with an annual income below a certain threshold are eligible to apply. 

The application process involves submitting a form along with necessary documents to the designated authorities. 

The scheme is implemented by the Gujarat State Government through the Health and Family Welfare Department. 

The timeline for the scheme is annual with renewals based on the eligibility criteria. 

The scheme also includes provisions for pre-existing diseases after a waiting period. 

Additional details may include specific empaneled hospitals and the types of treatments covered.
