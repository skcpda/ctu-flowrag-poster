The Mranmas-II scheme is an initiative by the Government of India. 

It aims to promote the welfare of marginalized communities. 

The objective is to enhance the socio-economic conditions of these communities. 

The scheme provides financial assistance for various developmental activities. 

Benefits include support for education health and livelihood. 

Eligibility criteria typically include being a member of a marginalized community. 

The application process involves submitting required documents to the designated authorities. 

The timeline for assistance may vary based on the specific activity or project. 

Implementing authorities include state governments and local bodies. 

The scheme is part of a broader effort to ensure inclusive growth.
