The SSGGVN scheme stands for the Sardar Sarovar Gramin Vikas Yojana. 

It was launched to promote rural development and improve living standards in villages. 

The primary objective is to provide sustainable livelihood opportunities and enhance the quality of life in rural areas. 

The scheme aims to empower rural communities through skill development and self-employment initiatives. 

Benefits include financial assistance for setting up small enterprises and access to training programs. 

Eligibility criteria typically include being a resident of a rural area and belonging to economically weaker sections. 

The application process usually involves submitting a form to the local implementing authority along with required documents. 

The timeline for processing applications may vary depending on the local authority's procedures. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Rural Development and various state government agencies. 

Additional details about the specific benefits and assistance may vary by state.
