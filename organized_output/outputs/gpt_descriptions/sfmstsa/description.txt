The scheme is known as the Scheme for Financial Assistance for the Management of Scheduled Tribes in Scheduled Areas (SFMSTSA). 

It aims to provide financial assistance for the development of Scheduled Tribes in Scheduled Areas. 

The objectives include enhancing the socio-economic conditions of Scheduled Tribes. 

The scheme provides financial support for various development activities. 

Eligible beneficiaries are Scheduled Tribe individuals or communities residing in Scheduled Areas. 

The application process involves submitting a proposal to the concerned state government. 

The timeline for disbursement of funds is determined by the implementing agency. 

The Ministry of Tribal Affairs is the primary implementing authority for this scheme. 

Additional details on specific benefits or assistance may vary by state.
