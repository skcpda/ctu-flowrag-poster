The SSGFF stands for the Scheme for Support to State Governments for Financial Assistance to Farmers. 

This scheme is designed to provide financial assistance to farmers facing distress due to various reasons. 

The primary objective is to support farmers in overcoming financial challenges and ensuring their livelihoods. 

Benefits include direct financial assistance to eligible farmers to help them manage their agricultural activities. 

Eligibility criteria typically include being a registered farmer and facing financial distress due to specific circumstances. 

The application process usually involves submitting an application to the relevant state government authorities. 

The timeline for assistance may vary depending on state-specific implementation. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers' Welfare in collaboration with state governments. 

Additional details may include specific guidelines on the amount of assistance and the conditions under which it is provided.
