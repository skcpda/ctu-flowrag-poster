The Pradhan Mantri Poshana Shakti Nirman Yojana (PPNICH-PMPT) is a government welfare scheme aimed at addressing malnutrition among children and women in India. 

The objective of the scheme is to enhance the nutritional status of children aged 0 to 6 years and pregnant and lactating women. 

The benefits include the provision of supplementary nutrition and health education to improve dietary practices. 

Eligible beneficiaries are children aged 0 to 6 years and pregnant and lactating women from low-income families. 

The application process typically involves registration through Anganwadi centers or local health departments. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Women and Child Development at the national level. 

The frequency of assistance is generally monthly, with regular monitoring of beneficiaries' health and nutrition status.
