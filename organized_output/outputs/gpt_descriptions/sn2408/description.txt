The scheme SN2408 is known as the Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana - Urban.

It was launched in June 2015.

The objective of the scheme is to provide affordable housing to the urban poor.

The scheme aims to provide housing for all by the year 2022.

Benefits include financial assistance for the construction or enhancement of houses.

Eligible beneficiaries include individuals from Economically Weaker Sections and Low-Income Groups.

The application process involves submitting an online application through the official PMAY website.

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs.

The timeline for the disbursement of funds varies based on project completion.

The scheme also includes provisions for interest subsidy on housing loans.

Additional details include the provision for slum rehabilitation and the construction of affordable housing in partnership with private developers.
