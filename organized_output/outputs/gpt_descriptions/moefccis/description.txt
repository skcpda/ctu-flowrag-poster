The Ministry of Environment Forest and Climate Change Conservation Information System (MoEFCCIS) is an initiative aimed at enhancing environmental governance in India. 

The objective of MoEFCCIS is to provide a centralized platform for the collection and dissemination of information related to environmental conservation. 

The scheme benefits various stakeholders including government agencies researchers and the general public by providing access to critical environmental data. 

Eligibility criteria are not specified as the scheme is designed for all stakeholders interested in environmental information. 

The application process involves accessing the online platform where information can be retrieved without any formal application.

The implementing authority for this scheme is the Ministry of Environment Forest and Climate Change.
