The Mukhyamantri Bhatta Yojana (MBYAVY) is a welfare scheme initiated by the Government of Madhya Pradesh. 

The scheme aims to provide financial assistance to the unemployed youth of the state. 

It seeks to empower young individuals by offering them a monthly stipend. 

The objective is to alleviate the financial burden on unemployed youth while they seek job opportunities. 

Eligible beneficiaries include unemployed youth aged between 21 to 40 years. 

They must be residents of Madhya Pradesh and should not be enrolled in any educational institution. 

The application process involves online registration through the official website of the Madhya Pradesh government. 

Applicants are required to provide necessary documents such as identity proof and educational qualifications. 

The financial assistance is provided on a monthly basis. 

The implementing authority for this scheme is the Department of Skill Development and Employment, Government of Madhya Pradesh. 

The scheme is part of the state government's efforts to enhance employment opportunities for the youth.
