The RPSBWODAPD scheme stands for the Rajiv Gandhi Scheme for Empowerment of Adolescent Girls. 

It was launched by the Ministry of Women and Child Development. 

The scheme aims to empower adolescent girls by providing them with opportunities for education and skill development. 

It focuses on improving the nutritional and health status of girls aged 11 to 18 years. 

The benefits include access to education and vocational training. 

It also provides nutritional support and health services. 

Eligible beneficiaries are adolescent girls in the age group of 11 to 18 years. 

The application process typically involves registration through local anganwadi centers or schools. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Women and Child Development at the national level. 

State governments and local bodies are responsible for executing the program at the grassroots level. 

The scheme is part of a broader initiative to promote gender equality and empower women in India. 

It aims to create a supportive environment for adolescent girls to thrive.
