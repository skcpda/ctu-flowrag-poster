The Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samman Nidhi (PM-KISAN) scheme is a significant initiative by the Government of India aimed at providing financial support to farmers. 

The scheme was launched on February 24, 2019. 

It was introduced to address the challenges faced by small and marginal farmers in the country. 

The background of the scheme lies in the recognition of the economic difficulties faced by farmers. 

Many farmers struggle with rising costs of agricultural inputs and fluctuating market prices. 

The government aimed to provide a safety net to these farmers to ensure their financial stability. 

The primary objective of the PM-KISAN scheme is to provide direct income support to farmers. 

The scheme intends to enhance the income of farmers and improve their living standards. 

Under this scheme, eligible farmers receive an income support of Rs. 6000 per year. 

This amount is disbursed in three equal installments of Rs. 2000 each. 

The financial assistance is intended to help farmers meet their agricultural expenses. 

It also aims to provide them with the necessary support for their families. 

The benefits of the PM-KISAN scheme extend to all small and marginal farmers. 

This includes those who own cultivable land. 

The scheme is applicable to farmers across all states and Union Territories in India. 

Eligibility criteria for the PM-KISAN scheme include ownership of cultivable land. 

Farmers must possess land records to qualify for the assistance. 

The scheme is specifically designed for small and marginal farmers. 

Those who own more than two hectares of land are not eligible for this scheme. 

Additionally, the scheme excludes certain categories of individuals. 

These include institutional landholders and farmers who are ineligible for other government schemes. 

The application process for PM-KISAN is straightforward. 

Farmers can apply online through the official PM-KISAN portal. 

They can also visit their respective state government offices for assistance. 

The application requires basic information such as name and land details. 

Farmers must provide their bank account information for the direct transfer of funds. 

Once the application is submitted, it undergoes verification by the concerned authorities. 

The timeline for the disbursement of funds is typically aligned with the agricultural calendar. 

Farmers receive the installments directly into their bank accounts. 

The frequency of payments is three times a year. 

This ensures that farmers have a steady flow of income throughout the year. 

The implementing authority for the PM-KISAN scheme is the Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers' Welfare. 

State governments also play a crucial role in the implementation of the scheme. 

They are responsible for the identification of eligible beneficiaries. 

The scheme has been well-received by farmers across the country. 

Many have reported improvements in their financial conditions. 

The direct transfer of funds has reduced the dependency on middlemen. 

Farmers can now make timely purchases of seeds and fertilizers. 

This has led to an increase in agricultural productivity in several regions. 

The PM-KISAN scheme is part of a broader strategy to enhance agricultural sustainability. 

It aligns with the government's vision of doubling farmers' income by 2022. 

The scheme has also been instrumental in promoting financial inclusion among farmers. 

Farmers are encouraged to open bank accounts to receive the benefits.
