The scheme is known as the Swami Vivekananda Punarvasu Mukhya Sewa Sansthan (SVPMSS). 

It aims to provide financial assistance to the marginalized and underprivileged sections of society. 

The objective is to promote self-employment and entrepreneurship among youth. 

The scheme offers skill development training and financial support for starting small businesses. 

Eligibility is primarily focused on youth from economically weaker sections. 

Applicants must be between the ages of 18 to 35 years. 

The application process involves submitting a form along with necessary documents to the designated authority. 

Applications can typically be submitted online or through local government offices. 

The timeline for processing applications may vary based on the implementing agency. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Skill Development and Entrepreneurship. 

Additional details regarding specific financial assistance amounts and training programs may vary.
