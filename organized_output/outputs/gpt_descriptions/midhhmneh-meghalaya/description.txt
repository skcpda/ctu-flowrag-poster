The Mid-Day Meal Scheme in Meghalaya is a government initiative aimed at enhancing the nutritional status of school children. 

It seeks to improve school enrollment and attendance while promoting the overall health of children. 

The scheme provides free meals to students in government and government-aided schools. 

The meals are designed to meet the nutritional requirements of children. 

Eligibility includes all students enrolled in classes I to VIII in the specified schools. 

The application process is typically managed at the school level with no separate application required from parents. 

Meals are provided on all school days. 

The implementing authority for the scheme is the Department of Education in Meghalaya. 

The scheme is part of a larger national initiative to ensure food security and education for children across India.
