The Mahatma Gandhi National Rural Employment Guarantee Act (MGNREGA) is a significant welfare scheme in India. 

It was enacted in 2005 to enhance livelihood security in rural areas. 

The scheme aims to provide at least 100 days of guaranteed wage employment in a financial year to every rural household. 

It seeks to create durable assets and improve the livelihood security of rural households. 

The primary objective is to enhance the purchasing power of rural households. 

It also aims to promote social inclusion and empower marginalized communities. 

Under MGNREGA, every rural household can demand work. 

The work provided must be unskilled manual work. 

The scheme also mandates that the work should be provided within 15 days of the application. 

If work is not provided within this period, the applicant is entitled to an unemployment allowance. 

The benefits of MGNREGA include guaranteed wage employment. 

Wages are paid at the statutory minimum wage rate. 

The scheme also contributes to the creation of rural infrastructure. 

This includes assets like roads, ponds, and irrigation facilities. 

MGNREGA promotes sustainable development in rural areas. 

It enhances the livelihood security of rural households. 

Eligibility for MGNREGA is limited to rural households. 

Every adult member of the household can apply for work. 

Households must be registered under the scheme to avail of benefits. 

The application process involves submitting a job card application. 

The job card is issued by the Gram Panchayat. 

Once issued, households can demand work under MGNREGA. 

The application can be made at the local Gram Panchayat office. 

The timeline for the issuance of job cards is typically within 15 days. 

Work can be demanded at any time throughout the year. 

The implementing authorities for MGNREGA are the Ministry of Rural Development. 

State governments are responsible for the implementation at the state level. 

The scheme is funded by the central government and state governments. 

The funding is shared between the center and states in a specified ratio.
