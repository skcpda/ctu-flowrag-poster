The TIFA scheme stands for Trade Infrastructure for Export Scheme. 

It was launched by the Government of India to enhance export competitiveness. 

The objective of the scheme is to develop infrastructure for trade and exports. 

It aims to create world-class infrastructure facilities to support exporters. 

The benefits include financial assistance for the development of export-related infrastructure. 

Eligible applicants include state governments and their agencies. 

The application process involves submitting a proposal to the relevant authority. 

The timeline for project approval and funding varies based on the proposal. 

The scheme is implemented by the Department of Commerce under the Ministry of Commerce and Industry. 

The TIFA scheme is part of the broader strategy to boost India's exports.
