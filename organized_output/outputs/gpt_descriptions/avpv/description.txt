The Atal Pension Yojana (APY) is a government welfare scheme launched by the Government of India. 

It was introduced on 9 May 2015. 

The scheme aims to provide a safety net for individuals in the unorganized sector. 

It is designed to encourage savings for retirement among workers in this sector. 

The APY is a part of the larger financial inclusion initiative of the government. 

The scheme is named after former Prime Minister Atal Bihari Vajpayee. 

The primary objective of the APY is to provide a fixed pension to subscribers upon reaching the age of 60. 

The pension amount can range from INR 1,000 to INR 5,000 per month. 

The amount of pension depends on the contributions made by the subscribers. 

The scheme targets individuals between the ages of 18 and 40. 

This age range is significant as it allows for a longer contribution period. 

The APY aims to ensure that individuals have a reliable source of income during their retirement years. 

The benefits of the scheme include a guaranteed pension amount. 

The pension is payable monthly and is intended to provide financial security. 

In addition to the pension, the scheme also provides a death benefit. 

In the event of the subscriber's death, the spouse is entitled to receive the pension. 

If both the subscriber and spouse pass away, the nominee will receive the accumulated corpus. 

The APY is particularly beneficial for those who do not have access to formal pension schemes. 

It encourages individuals to save for their future. 

The scheme is also designed to promote financial literacy among the target population. 

Eligibility for the APY is primarily based on age and citizenship. 

Individuals must be Indian citizens. 

They must also be within the age limit of 18 to 40 years. 

There are no specific educational qualifications required to enroll in the scheme. 

The scheme is open to all individuals in the unorganized sector. 

This includes workers in agriculture, construction, and other informal employment sectors. 

To apply for the APY, individuals must visit a designated bank or post office. 

They can also apply through the official website of the scheme. 

The application process involves filling out a form with personal details. 

Applicants must provide proof of identity and age. 

They must also provide bank account details for pension disbursement. 

Once the application is submitted, the bank or post office will process it. 

The contributions to the APY can be made on a monthly basis. 

The amount of contribution depends on the pension amount chosen. 

For example, a subscriber opting for a pension of INR 5,000 will have to contribute a higher amount than one opting for INR 1,000. 

The contributions are made through auto-debit from the subscriber's bank account. 

The scheme has a flexible contribution structure. 

Subscribers can increase or decrease their contributions based on their financial situation. 

The APY is implemented by the Pension Fund Regulatory and Development Authority (PFRDA). 

The PFRDA is responsible for overseeing the functioning of the scheme. 

They ensure that the funds are managed efficiently and transparently. 

The scheme is also supported by various banks and financial institutions. 

These entities play a crucial role in promoting the scheme and facilitating enrollment. 

The APY has a specific timeline for contributions. 

Subscribers are required to make regular contributions until they reach the age of 60. 

The frequency of contributions is monthly. 

The pension is then disbursed monthly after the subscriber turns 60.
