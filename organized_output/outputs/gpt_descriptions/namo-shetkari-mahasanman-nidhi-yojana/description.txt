Namo Shetkari Mahasanman Nidhi Yojana is a welfare scheme launched by the Government of Maharashtra. 

The scheme aims to provide financial assistance to farmers in the state. 

It seeks to enhance the income of farmers and improve their living standards. 

The scheme offers a direct cash benefit to eligible farmers. 

Farmers receive an annual financial assistance of INR 6,000. 

Eligibility is limited to small and marginal farmers. 

Farmers must have landholdings of up to 2 hectares. 

The application process involves online registration through the official portal. 

Farmers need to provide necessary documents such as land records and identity proof. 

The financial assistance is disbursed annually. 

The scheme is implemented by the Department of Agriculture, Government of Maharashtra. 

It is part of the state's commitment to support the agricultural sector. 

The scheme aims to reduce the financial burden on farmers. 

It also promotes sustainable agricultural practices.
