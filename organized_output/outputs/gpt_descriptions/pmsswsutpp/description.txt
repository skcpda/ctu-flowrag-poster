The Pradhan Mantri Suraksha Maandhan Yojana for Senior Citizens is a government welfare scheme aimed at providing social security to senior citizens. 

The objective of the scheme is to provide a monthly pension to senior citizens aged 60 years and above. 

The benefits include a monthly pension of Rs. 3,000 for eligible beneficiaries. 

Eligibility criteria include being a citizen of India and aged 60 years or above. 

The application process involves filling out the prescribed application form and submitting it to the designated authorities. 

The timeline for disbursement of the pension is monthly. 

The implementing authority for this scheme is the Ministry of Finance. 

The scheme aims to enhance the financial security of senior citizens and improve their quality of life.
