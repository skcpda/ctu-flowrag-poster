The Pradhan Mantri Suraksha Bima Yojana (PMSBY) is a government welfare scheme launched in 2015. 

It aims to provide affordable accidental death and disability insurance to the citizens of India. 

The primary objective is to offer financial security to individuals in case of accidents. 

The scheme provides a coverage of INR 2 lakh for accidental death and permanent total disability. 

It offers INR 1 lakh for permanent partial disability. 

The annual premium for the scheme is INR 12. 

Eligibility is open to all Indian citizens aged between 18 and 70 years. 

Applicants must have a savings bank account to enroll in the scheme. 

The application process can be completed through participating banks and financial institutions. 

Enrollment can be done online or offline. 

The scheme is available for subscription every year. 

The implementing authority for PMSBY is the Ministry of Finance. 

The scheme is managed by the Life Insurance Corporation of India and other private insurance companies. 

The coverage period is from June 1 to May 31 of the following year. 

Renewal of the scheme is automatic unless the subscriber opts out. 

The scheme aims to enhance the social security net for the economically vulnerable sections of society.
