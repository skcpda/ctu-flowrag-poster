The scheme is known as the West Bengal Tribal Integrated Development Project for Scheduled Tribes and Scheduled Castes Families through Livelihood and Skill Development.

It is implemented by the Government of West Bengal.

The main objective is to improve the livelihood of Scheduled Tribes and Scheduled Castes families through skill development and employment opportunities.

The scheme aims to provide financial assistance for various livelihood activities.

It also focuses on enhancing the skills of the tribal population to make them employable.

Eligible beneficiaries include Scheduled Tribes and Scheduled Castes families residing in West Bengal.

The application process involves submitting a form to the local implementing agency.

The timeline for the scheme's implementation is ongoing.

The implementing authority is the Department of Tribal Development, Government of West Bengal.
