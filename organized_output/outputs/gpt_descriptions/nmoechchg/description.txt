The National Mission for Clean Ganga is a government initiative aimed at rejuvenating the Ganga River. 

It was launched in 2014 under the Ministry of Water Resources River Development and Ganga Rejuvenation. 

The primary objective is to reduce pollution and restore the ecological health of the Ganga River. 

The mission focuses on sewage treatment infrastructure and river surface cleaning. 

It also aims to promote afforestation along the river banks. 

The benefits include improved water quality and enhanced biodiversity in the Ganga River ecosystem. 

Eligible beneficiaries include local communities and stakeholders involved in river conservation. 

The application process typically involves project proposals submitted to the implementing agencies. 

The timeline for project implementation varies based on the specific initiatives undertaken. 

The National Mission for Clean Ganga is implemented by the National Mission for Clean Ganga (NMCG). 

The mission collaborates with various state governments and local bodies for effective execution. 

It also engages with civil society organizations and academic institutions for awareness and research. 

Additional details include the establishment of monitoring systems to track progress and impact.
