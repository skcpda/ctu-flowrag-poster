The Bihar Chief Minister's Public Grievance Redressal Scheme is known as PyBihar. 

This scheme aims to address public grievances and improve the efficiency of governance in the state of Bihar. 

The objective is to provide a platform for citizens to raise their complaints and seek resolutions in a timely manner. 

Benefits include a systematic approach to grievance redressal and enhanced accountability of government officials. 

Eligibility is generally open to all residents of Bihar who have grievances related to government services. 

The application process involves submitting grievances through the official PyBihar portal or designated offices. 

The timeline for grievance resolution varies depending on the nature of the complaint but is aimed to be prompt. 

The scheme is implemented by the Government of Bihar through various state departments. 

Additional details may include regular monitoring and evaluation of grievances to improve service delivery.
