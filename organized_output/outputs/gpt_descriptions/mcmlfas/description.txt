The MCMLFAS stands for the Mahatma Gandhi National Rural Employment Guarantee Act (MGNREGA) and Livelihood and Food Security Scheme. 

It aims to enhance livelihood security in rural areas by providing at least 100 days of unskilled wage employment in a financial year to every rural household. 

The scheme seeks to create durable assets and improve the livelihood security of rural households. 

Benefits include guaranteed wage employment and the creation of community assets. 

Eligible beneficiaries are rural households that request employment under the scheme. 

The application process involves submitting a demand for work to the local Gram Panchayat. 

The scheme operates throughout the year. 

The Ministry of Rural Development is the implementing authority for this scheme. 

The scheme also emphasizes the importance of social inclusion and empowerment of marginalized groups.
