The Mukhyamantri Bhatta Kalyan Yojana (MBKVY) is a welfare scheme launched by the Government of Madhya Pradesh. 

It aims to provide financial assistance to the unemployed youth of the state. 

The objective is to support young individuals in their pursuit of employment and skill development. 

The scheme offers a monthly stipend to eligible beneficiaries to help them sustain themselves while seeking employment. 

Eligible beneficiaries include unemployed youth aged between 18 to 35 years. 

They must be residents of Madhya Pradesh and should not be enrolled in any educational institution. 

The application process involves filling out an online application form on the official government portal. 

Applicants need to provide necessary documents such as identity proof and educational qualifications. 

The scheme is implemented by the Department of Skill Development and Entrepreneurship of Madhya Pradesh. 

The financial assistance is provided for a specific duration as determined by the scheme guidelines. 

The frequency of stipend disbursement is monthly. 

The scheme aims to enhance the employability of youth through financial support during their job search.
