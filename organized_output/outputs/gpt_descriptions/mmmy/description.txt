The Mukhyamantri Mahtari Nyay Yojana (MMMY) is a welfare scheme launched by the Government of Chhattisgarh. 

The scheme aims to provide financial assistance to pregnant women and lactating mothers. 

It seeks to improve maternal and child health outcomes in the state. 

Under this scheme, eligible beneficiaries receive direct cash transfers. 

The financial assistance is provided during pregnancy and post-delivery. 

The amount is intended to support nutritional needs and healthcare expenses. 

Eligibility is primarily for pregnant women and lactating mothers residing in Chhattisgarh. 

The beneficiaries must meet specific criteria set by the state government. 

The application process involves submitting necessary documents to local health authorities. 

The scheme is implemented by the Department of Health and Family Welfare, Chhattisgarh. 

The timeline for assistance is aligned with the pregnancy and lactation periods. 

The scheme is part of a broader initiative to enhance women's health and well-being.
