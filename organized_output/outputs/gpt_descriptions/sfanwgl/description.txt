The scheme "SFAW-NGL" stands for "Support for Agricultural and Welfare Activities for Needy and Vulnerable Groups." 

It is designed to provide financial assistance and support to vulnerable groups in rural areas. 

The objective of the scheme is to enhance the livelihoods of marginalized communities through various agricultural and welfare activities. 

The benefits include financial support for agricultural inputs and training programs to improve productivity. 

Eligible beneficiaries include small and marginal farmers and landless agricultural laborers from vulnerable sections of society. 

The application process typically involves submitting an application form along with necessary documents to the local implementing agency. 

The timeline for disbursement of benefits may vary based on the specific activity and the implementing agency's schedule. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers' Welfare in collaboration with state governments. 

Additional details about specific activities and support services may be available through local agricultural offices.
