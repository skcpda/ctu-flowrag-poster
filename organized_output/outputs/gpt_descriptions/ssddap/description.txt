The SSDDAP scheme stands for the Skill Development Scheme for Differently Abled Persons. 

It was launched to enhance the employability of differently-abled individuals. 

The primary objective is to provide skill development training to persons with disabilities. 

This training aims to empower them to become self-reliant and economically independent. 

The benefits include skill development training programs tailored to the needs of differently-abled individuals. 

Participants may receive assistance in the form of training materials and stipends during the training period. 

Eligibility is generally limited to individuals with disabilities as defined by the Rights of Persons with Disabilities Act. 

The application process typically involves submitting an application form along with necessary documents to the designated authorities. 

The timeline for the training programs may vary based on the specific courses offered. 

Implementing authorities include the Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment and various state governments. 

The scheme may also involve partnerships with NGOs and other organizations for effective implementation.
