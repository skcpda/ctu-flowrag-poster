The SAMS (Skill Acquisition and Mobility Scheme) is a government initiative aimed at enhancing the employability of youth in India.

The scheme focuses on providing skill development training to individuals from marginalized communities.

The objective is to empower youth through skill development and promote self-employment and entrepreneurship.

Benefits include access to skill training programs and financial assistance for setting up small businesses.

Eligibility criteria typically include being a resident of India and belonging to a marginalized community.

The application process involves submitting an online application through the official portal or visiting designated centers.

The timeline for training programs may vary based on the specific skill being taught.

Implementing authorities include the Ministry of Skill Development and Entrepreneurship and various state governments.

The scheme aims to create a skilled workforce that meets the demands of the job market.
