The scheme is known as the Scheme for the Development of Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes Persons with Disabilities (SDSSPD). 

It aims to provide financial assistance and support to persons with disabilities belonging to Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes. 

The objectives include improving the quality of life of persons with disabilities and promoting their social and economic empowerment. 

Benefits include financial assistance for education rehabilitation and skill development. 

Eligible beneficiaries are persons with disabilities who belong to Scheduled Castes or Scheduled Tribes. 

The application process involves submitting an application form along with necessary documents to the concerned authorities. 

The timeline for processing applications may vary based on the implementing agency. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment. 

Additional details on specific financial assistance amounts and types of support may vary based on state regulations.
