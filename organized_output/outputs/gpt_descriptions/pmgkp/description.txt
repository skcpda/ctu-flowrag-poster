The Pradhan Mantri Garib Kalyan Package (PMGKP) was announced in March 2020 in response to the COVID-19 pandemic. 

The scheme aims to provide financial support and assistance to the poor and vulnerable sections of society affected by the pandemic. 

The objectives include ensuring food security and providing cash transfers to low-income families. 

Benefits under the scheme include free food grains for the poor and cash transfers to women and farmers. 

Eligible beneficiaries include women holding Jan Dhan accounts and farmers with Kisan Samman Nidhi. 

The application process is automatic for eligible beneficiaries based on existing government databases. 

The scheme was implemented in phases with immediate relief measures announced in March 2020. 

The Ministry of Finance is the primary implementing authority for the PMGKP. 

Additional details include provisions for health insurance for frontline healthcare workers.
