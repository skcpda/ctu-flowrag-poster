The Socka scheme is an initiative aimed at providing financial assistance to the marginalized sections of society. 

It focuses on improving the livelihood of individuals engaged in the production of socks and hosiery. 

The primary objective is to enhance the income levels of beneficiaries through skill development and financial support. 

Benefits include access to low-interest loans and training programs for skill enhancement. 

Eligibility criteria typically include being a member of a marginalized community and having a specific interest in the sock production industry. 

The application process generally involves submitting an application form along with necessary documents to the designated authority. 

The timeline for processing applications may vary based on the implementing agency. 

Implementing authorities include local government bodies and specialized agencies focused on skill development and financial inclusion. 

Further details on specific benefits and assistance may vary based on state-level implementations.
