The SSSVPY stands for the Satyajit Ray Film and Television Institute (SRFTI) Scholarship Scheme. 

This scheme is aimed at providing financial assistance to students pursuing courses in film and television. 

The objective is to promote education and skill development in the field of film and television. 

The benefits include scholarships that cover tuition fees and other educational expenses. 

Eligibility is typically limited to students enrolled in courses offered by the Satyajit Ray Film and Television Institute. 

The application process usually involves submitting an online application along with required documents. 

The timeline for application submission is announced by the institute prior to the academic year. 

The implementing authority for this scheme is the Satyajit Ray Film and Television Institute. 

Further details about the specific scholarship amounts and selection criteria may be available on the official institute website.
