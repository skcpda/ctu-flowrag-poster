The Mukhya Mantri Gramin BPL Ghar Sewa Yojana (MGBGHSC) is a welfare scheme initiated by the Government of Madhya Pradesh. 

The scheme aims to provide housing assistance to families living below the poverty line in rural areas. 

It seeks to ensure that every rural household has access to a safe and secure living environment. 

The benefits include financial assistance for the construction of houses. 

Eligible beneficiaries are families identified as below the poverty line in rural areas. 

The application process typically involves submitting an application form through the local Panchayat or designated government office. 

The timeline for assistance may vary based on the availability of funds and project implementation schedules. 

The scheme is implemented by the Department of Rural Development in Madhya Pradesh. 

Additional details about the scheme can be found on the official government website.
