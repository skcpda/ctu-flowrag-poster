The Scheme for Integrated Urban Development (SIUPD) aims to promote sustainable urban development in India. 

The objective of SIUPD is to enhance the quality of life in urban areas through integrated planning and development. 

The scheme provides financial assistance for infrastructure development and urban services. 

It focuses on improving housing sanitation and basic amenities in urban areas. 

Eligibility criteria include urban local bodies and municipalities that meet specific requirements set by the government. 

The application process involves submitting proposals to the respective state government for approval. 

The timeline for project implementation varies based on the specific project and state guidelines. 

The Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs is the primary implementing authority for this scheme. 

Additional details regarding specific projects and funding allocations can be found on official government websites.
