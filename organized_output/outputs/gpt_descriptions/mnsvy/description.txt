Mahatma Gandhi National Rural Employment Guarantee Act is also known as MGNREGA. 

The scheme was launched in 2005 to provide legal guarantee for at least 100 days of unskilled wage employment in a financial year to every rural household. 

The objective is to enhance livelihood security in rural areas. 

It aims to create durable assets and strengthen the livelihood resource base of the rural poor. 

The benefits include wage employment for unskilled manual work. 

It also provides social security and helps in reducing rural poverty. 

All rural households are eligible for the scheme. 

The application process involves submitting an application to the local Gram Panchayat. 

The Gram Panchayat will issue a job card to eligible households. 

The timeline for employment is within 15 days of application. 

The implementing authority is the Ministry of Rural Development. 

State governments are responsible for the implementation at the state level. 

The scheme is funded by the central government and state governments. 

It is monitored through a decentralized system involving local self-governments.
