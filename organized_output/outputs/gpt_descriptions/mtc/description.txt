The MTC scheme refers to the "Mahatma Gandhi National Rural Employment Guarantee Act" or MGNREGA. 

This scheme was launched in 2005 to enhance livelihood security in rural areas. 

The main objective is to provide at least 100 days of unskilled wage employment in a financial year to every rural household. 

The benefits include guaranteed wage employment and the creation of durable assets in rural areas. 

Eligible beneficiaries are adult members of rural households who volunteer for unskilled manual work. 

The application process involves submitting a job card application to the Gram Panchayat. 

The scheme operates throughout the year. 

The Ministry of Rural Development is the implementing authority for this scheme. 

Funds are allocated by the central government and state governments for implementation. 

The scheme also aims to empower rural communities and promote social inclusion.
