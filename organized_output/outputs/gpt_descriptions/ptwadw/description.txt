The Pradhan Mantri Ujjwala Yojana (PMUY) is a government welfare scheme launched in May 2016. 

It aims to provide clean cooking fuel to households below the poverty line. 

The primary objective is to reduce the health hazards associated with the use of traditional cooking fuels. 

The scheme provides a financial assistance of ₹1,600 for each LPG connection. 

Beneficiaries also receive a free gas stove and a first refill of LPG. 

Eligibility is limited to women from Below Poverty Line households. 

Applicants must be at least 18 years of age and must not already possess an LPG connection. 

The application process involves filling out a form available at designated centers or online. 

Documents required include proof of identity and proof of address. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Petroleum and Natural Gas. 

The timeline for receiving the LPG connection can vary based on the location and availability. 

The scheme is ongoing and aims to cover 8 crore households by 2020. 

Additional benefits include the reduction of indoor air pollution and the promotion of clean energy.
