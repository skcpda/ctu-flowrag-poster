The MBSAPBOCWWB scheme is the Mahatma Gandhi Sarva Shiksha Abhiyan for the benefit of the children of construction workers. 

It aims to provide educational support to the children of construction workers to ensure their access to quality education. 

The scheme seeks to reduce the dropout rate among children of construction workers and promote their overall development. 

Benefits include financial assistance for educational materials and scholarships for higher education. 

Eligibility is primarily based on the parent being a registered construction worker under the Building and Other Construction Workers Act. 

The application process typically involves submitting an application to the local welfare board along with necessary documentation. 

The timeline for assistance may vary based on the specific educational needs and the academic calendar. 

The scheme is implemented by the respective State Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Boards. 

Additional details may include provisions for vocational training and skill development for older children.
