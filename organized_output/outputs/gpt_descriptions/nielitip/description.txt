NIELITIP stands for the National Institute of Electronics and Information Technology Internship Program. 

It is an initiative aimed at enhancing the employability of students in the field of electronics and information technology. 

The objective of the scheme is to provide practical exposure and hands-on training to students. 

The program offers internships that help students gain industry-relevant skills. 

Benefits include skill development and increased job opportunities for participants. 

Eligible candidates are typically students pursuing degrees or diplomas in electronics and information technology. 

The application process involves submitting an online application through the official NIELIT website. 

Internships are offered periodically based on the availability of projects and resources. 

The implementing authority for this scheme is the National Institute of Electronics and Information Technology. 

Additional details regarding specific timelines and project availability may vary based on the institute's schedule.
