The VKVVS Swas scheme is an initiative launched by the Government of India. 

It aims to provide health care services to the underprivileged sections of society. 

The objective is to ensure access to quality health care for all, particularly in rural and underserved areas. 

The scheme offers benefits such as free medical treatment and health check-ups. 

It also provides essential medicines and diagnostic services at no cost. 

Eligibility is primarily targeted towards low-income families and individuals without health insurance. 

The application process typically involves registration at designated health centers or online portals. 

The scheme is implemented through local health authorities and community health centers. 

It is part of a broader effort to improve public health infrastructure in India. 

The timeline for services is ongoing as the scheme is designed to be a continuous support system.
