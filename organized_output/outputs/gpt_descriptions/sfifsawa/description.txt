The scheme SFIFSawa is designed to support the financial inclusion of small farmers in India. 

The objective of the scheme is to provide financial assistance to small and marginal farmers. 

The benefits include access to credit facilities and financial products tailored for small farmers. 

Eligibility is primarily limited to small and marginal farmers as defined by government criteria. 

The application process involves submitting necessary documentation to the designated financial institutions. 

The timeline for processing applications may vary based on the financial institution's procedures. 

The implementing authorities include the Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers' Welfare and associated financial institutions.
