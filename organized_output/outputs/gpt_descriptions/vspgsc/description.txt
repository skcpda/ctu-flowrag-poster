The VSPGSC scheme stands for the Vidhya Samiksha Prabandhan Gyan Sewa Chayan. 

This scheme is aimed at enhancing the quality of education in government schools. 

It focuses on the effective management and monitoring of educational resources. 

The objective is to improve student learning outcomes through better governance. 

The scheme provides assistance in the form of training for teachers and educational administrators. 

It also offers resources for curriculum development and assessment tools. 

Eligibility criteria typically include government school teachers and administrative staff. 

The application process involves registration through the official education department portal. 

The timeline for implementation and training sessions is generally announced by the respective state education departments. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Education at the central level along with state education departments. 

Further details regarding specific training modules and resources may vary by state.
