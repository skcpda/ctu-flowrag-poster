The National Scheme for Welfare of Fishermen and Fish Farmers is known as NSKBG. 

It aims to provide social security and welfare measures for fishermen and fish farmers. 

The objectives include enhancing the livelihood of fishermen and promoting sustainable fishing practices. 

Benefits include financial assistance for various activities related to fishing and fish farming. 

Eligible beneficiaries are fishermen and fish farmers registered under the scheme. 

The application process involves submitting a form to the respective state fisheries department. 

The timeline for processing applications varies by state. 

The implementing authorities include the Ministry of Fisheries Animal Husbandry and Dairying at the central level. 

State fisheries departments are responsible for local implementation. 

The scheme also focuses on skill development and capacity building for beneficiaries. 

It promotes the use of modern technology in fishing and aquaculture.
