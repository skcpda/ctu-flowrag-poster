The MIP scheme stands for the Market Intervention Scheme. 

It was launched by the Government of India to support farmers in distress due to falling prices of agricultural produce. 

The objective of the scheme is to provide price support to farmers for certain crops during times of market price fluctuations. 

The scheme aims to stabilize the prices of agricultural commodities and ensure that farmers receive a fair price for their produce. 

Benefits include the provision of financial assistance to farmers when the market prices fall below the predetermined threshold levels. 

Farmers who are affected by price drops can receive compensation through the scheme. 

Eligibility for the MIP scheme typically includes farmers who cultivate specific notified crops. 

The application process involves farmers submitting their grievances regarding price drops to the concerned authorities. 

The implementing authorities for the MIP scheme include the Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers' Welfare and state governments. 

The frequency of intervention depends on market conditions and the specific crops involved. 

Additional details may include the specific crops covered under the scheme and the threshold prices set by the government.
