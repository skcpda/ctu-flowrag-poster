The West Bengal Mukhya Mantri Wards Maternity Welfare Scheme for Sanitary Daughters (WBMWWSD) aims to provide financial assistance to women during pregnancy and childbirth. 

The objective is to improve maternal health and reduce maternal mortality rates in the state of West Bengal. 

The scheme offers a one-time cash assistance of ₹6,000 to eligible beneficiaries. 

This assistance is provided to help cover expenses related to pregnancy and childbirth. 

Eligibility is limited to women who are residents of West Bengal and belong to economically weaker sections. 

The beneficiaries must also be registered under the Pradhan Mantri Matru Vandana Yojana. 

The application process involves submitting a form along with necessary documents to the local health department or designated authority. 

The scheme is implemented by the Department of Health and Family Welfare, Government of West Bengal. 

The assistance is provided once during the pregnancy period. 

The scheme aims to ensure that women receive adequate support during a critical time in their lives.
