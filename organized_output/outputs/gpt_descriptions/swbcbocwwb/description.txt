The Swami Vivekananda Karmachari Sahayak Nidhi Scheme is designed for the welfare of construction workers in West Bengal. 

The objective of the scheme is to provide financial assistance to construction workers for various needs. 

The benefits include financial aid for medical emergencies education and marriage of children. 

Eligibility is limited to registered construction workers in West Bengal. 

Applicants must possess a valid registration as a construction worker under the West Bengal Building and Other Construction Workers Act 1996. 

The application process involves submitting an application form along with required documents to the local authority. 

The timeline for processing applications is typically within a few weeks. 

The scheme is implemented by the West Bengal Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board. 

Additional details include that the scheme aims to improve the living standards of construction workers and their families.
