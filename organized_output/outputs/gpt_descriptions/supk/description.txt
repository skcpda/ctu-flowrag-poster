The SUPK scheme stands for the Scheme for Unemployed Persons in Karnataka. 

It was launched to provide financial assistance to unemployed individuals in the state. 

The objective is to support the livelihood of unemployed persons and help them become self-reliant. 

The scheme offers a monthly financial assistance of INR 1,000 to eligible beneficiaries. 

This assistance is intended to help cover basic living expenses while individuals seek employment. 

Eligibility criteria include being a resident of Karnataka and being unemployed. 

Applicants must be between the ages of 18 and 35 years. 

The application process involves submitting an online application through the official government portal. 

Applicants need to provide identity proof and details of their unemployment status. 

The scheme is implemented by the Department of Employment and Training in Karnataka. 

The financial assistance is provided for a period of six months. 

The scheme aims to reduce the financial burden on unemployed individuals during their job search. 

It also encourages skill development and job readiness among the youth.
