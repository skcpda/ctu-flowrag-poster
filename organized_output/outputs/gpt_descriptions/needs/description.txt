The "Needs" scheme is designed to provide support to vulnerable populations in India. 

The primary objective is to ensure that basic needs such as food, shelter, and healthcare are met for marginalized communities. 

The scheme aims to enhance the quality of life for individuals and families living below the poverty line. 

Benefits include financial assistance for essential services and provisions for food security. 

Eligibility criteria typically include being a resident of India and belonging to a low-income household. 

The application process generally requires submission of relevant documents to local government offices or online portals. 

The timeline for assistance may vary based on the specific needs identified during the application process. 

Implementing authorities include state governments and local administrative bodies. 

Additional details about specific benefits or assistance may vary by state or region.
