The Mukhyamantri Swasthya Suraksha Yojana for Ghar Ghar is a health insurance scheme launched by the Government of Madhya Pradesh. 

The scheme aims to provide health insurance coverage to families living below the poverty line. 

It seeks to reduce out-of-pocket expenditure on health services. 

The scheme provides financial assistance for hospitalization and treatment of various diseases. 

Eligible beneficiaries receive coverage for a range of medical procedures and treatments. 

Families identified as below the poverty line are eligible for the scheme. 

The application process involves registration through designated government health facilities. 

Beneficiaries may need to provide proof of income and family status during registration. 

The scheme is implemented by the Department of Health and Family Welfare, Madhya Pradesh. 

The frequency of coverage is annual, with benefits available for each eligible family every year. 

Additional details regarding specific benefits and coverage limits may vary and should be confirmed through official sources.
