The Mukhyamantri Bhatta Yojana for Construction Workers (MBCYB) aims to provide financial assistance to construction workers in the state.

The objective of the scheme is to support the livelihood of construction workers by providing them with a monthly financial benefit.

Eligible beneficiaries include registered construction workers who are part of the state's Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board.

The application process involves registering with the Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board and submitting the required documentation.

The financial assistance is provided on a monthly basis.

The scheme is implemented by the respective state government's Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board.
