The National Scheme for Support to State Governments for the Development of the Child Care Institutions is known as NSSGBCDC. 

This scheme aims to strengthen the infrastructure and services of child care institutions in India. 

The primary objective is to ensure the protection and care of children in difficult circumstances. 

The scheme provides financial assistance to state governments for the establishment and maintenance of child care institutions. 

Benefits include funding for infrastructure development and operational costs of these institutions. 

Eligibility is primarily for state governments seeking to improve child care facilities. 

The application process involves submitting proposals to the Ministry of Women and Child Development. 

The timeline for funding and implementation is determined by the approval of proposals. 

The Ministry of Women and Child Development is the implementing authority for this scheme. 

The scheme also emphasizes the need for quality care and rehabilitation for children in need. 

It aims to promote a safe and nurturing environment for vulnerable children.
