The UandTSoE scheme is a government initiative aimed at promoting the welfare of the unorganized sector workers. 

The objective of the scheme is to provide social security and welfare benefits to unorganized workers. 

The benefits include financial assistance for health care education and skill development. 

Eligibility is primarily focused on workers in the unorganized sector. 

The application process involves online registration through the designated government portal. 

The timeline for processing applications is typically within a few weeks of submission. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Labour and Employment.
