The Kisan Samman Nidhi Scheme is a government initiative aimed at providing financial support to farmers in India. 

The scheme was launched on December 1, 2018. 

It is designed to supplement the income of small and marginal farmers. 

The primary objective is to ensure that farmers have a stable income to meet their agricultural expenses. 

The scheme aims to alleviate poverty among farmers and improve their living standards. 

Under this scheme, eligible farmers receive direct cash transfers. 

Each eligible farmer receives Rs. 6,000 per year. 

The amount is disbursed in three equal installments of Rs. 2,000 each. 

The assistance is intended to help farmers purchase seeds, fertilizers, and other agricultural inputs. 

It also aims to support farmers during periods of financial distress. 

Eligibility for the Kisan Samman Nidhi Scheme is primarily based on land ownership. 

Small and marginal farmers owning up to 2 hectares of land are eligible. 

Farmers from all states and Union Territories in India can apply. 

The scheme excludes certain categories of farmers. 

These include institutional landholders and those who pay income tax. 

The application process for the scheme is straightforward. 

Farmers can apply online through the official government portal. 

They can also visit their local revenue office or agricultural department. 

The application requires basic information such as name, address, and land details. 

Farmers must provide proof of land ownership. 

Once the application is submitted, it is verified by the concerned authorities. 

The timeline for processing applications can vary. 

Typically, it takes a few weeks for verification and approval. 

The disbursement of funds occurs directly into the bank accounts of the beneficiaries. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers' Welfare. 

State governments also play a crucial role in the implementation process. 

They assist in the identification of eligible farmers and facilitate the application process.
