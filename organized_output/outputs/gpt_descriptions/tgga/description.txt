The "TGGAS" or "Tirupati Ganga Gram Abhiyan Scheme" is an initiative aimed at enhancing rural livelihoods in the Tirupati region. 

The objective of the scheme is to promote sustainable development in rural areas through various interventions. 

The benefits include financial assistance for self-employment projects and skill development programs. 

Eligibility criteria typically include residents of the Tirupati region who belong to economically weaker sections. 

The application process involves submitting a form along with necessary documents to the local government office. 

The timeline for processing applications may vary but is generally completed within a few weeks. 

The implementing authority for this scheme is the Tirupati District Administration. 

Additional details about the scheme may include community participation in project planning and execution.
