SFURTI stands for Scheme of Fund for Regeneration of Traditional Industries. 

It was launched by the Ministry of Micro Small and Medium Enterprises. 

The scheme aims to promote traditional industries by enhancing their competitiveness. 

It seeks to provide support for the development of clusters of traditional industries. 

The objectives include improving productivity and marketability of traditional industries. 

It also aims to create sustainable employment opportunities in these sectors. 

Benefits include financial assistance for infrastructure development. 

The scheme provides support for skill development and capacity building. 

It also facilitates access to modern technology and marketing support. 

Eligibility criteria include traditional industries and artisans engaged in these sectors. 

The application process involves submitting a proposal to the Ministry of MSME. 

The proposal should include details about the cluster and the intended activities. 

The implementing authorities include the Ministry of Micro Small and Medium Enterprises. 

State governments and other designated agencies also play a role in implementation. 

The scheme is expected to be implemented on a continuous basis. 

Funding is provided based on the project proposal and its viability. 

The scheme aims to revitalize traditional industries and enhance their contribution to the economy.
