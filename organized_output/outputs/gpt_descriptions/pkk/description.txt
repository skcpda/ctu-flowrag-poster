The Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samman Nidhi (PM-KISAN) scheme was launched by the Government of India in February 2019. 

It aims to provide financial support to small and marginal farmers. 

The objective is to ensure a decent income for farmers and to alleviate their financial burden. 

Under this scheme eligible farmers receive direct cash transfers of Rs. 6000 per year. 

This amount is disbursed in three equal installments of Rs. 2000 each. 

Eligibility is limited to small and marginal farmers who own cultivable land. 

Farmers must have a valid landholding and must not fall under certain exclusion criteria. 

The application process involves farmers registering on the PM-KISAN portal. 

They must provide necessary documents such as land records and bank details. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers' Welfare. 

The financial assistance is provided on a continuous basis throughout the year. 

The funds are directly transferred to the bank accounts of the beneficiaries. 

The scheme is applicable across all states and union territories in India. 

Regular updates and information can be accessed through the official PM-KISAN website.
