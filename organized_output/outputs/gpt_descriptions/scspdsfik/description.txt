The scheme SCSP-DSFIK stands for Scheduled Castes Sub Plan - Development Support Fund for Infrastructure and Knowledge. 

It is aimed at promoting the socio-economic development of Scheduled Castes in India. 

The objective is to provide financial assistance for infrastructure development and knowledge enhancement in Scheduled Castes communities. 

Benefits include funding for projects that improve living conditions and enhance educational and skill development opportunities. 

Eligible beneficiaries are individuals and groups belonging to Scheduled Castes. 

The application process involves submitting a proposal to the concerned state government or implementing agency. 

The timeline for project approval and funding disbursement varies based on the specific project and state procedures. 

The implementing authorities include the Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment and state governments. 

Additional details may include specific guidelines for project proposals and reporting requirements for funded projects.
