The scheme known as HASCMCWGBOCWWB stands for the Housing Assistance Scheme for Construction of Multi-Clustered Workers' Group Housing for Construction Workers and their Families.

This scheme is part of the broader initiative by the Government of India to provide affordable housing to economically weaker sections of society.

The background of this scheme is rooted in the recognition of the challenges faced by construction workers in urban areas.

Construction workers often lack access to adequate housing and live in substandard conditions.

This situation has been exacerbated by rapid urbanization and the increasing demand for housing in metropolitan areas.

The government has aimed to address these issues through targeted welfare schemes.

The primary objective of HASCMCWGBOCWWB is to provide financial assistance for the construction of housing units specifically for construction workers.

The scheme aims to create multi-clustered housing facilities that can accommodate multiple families.

This approach not only provides shelter but also fosters a sense of community among workers.

The benefits of the scheme include financial assistance for the construction of housing.

Eligible beneficiaries can receive monetary support to build their homes.

The scheme also aims to improve living conditions for construction workers and their families.

By providing adequate housing, the scheme seeks to enhance the overall quality of life for these workers.

In terms of eligibility, the scheme is primarily targeted at construction workers who are registered under the Building and Other Construction Workers Act.

The workers must be part of the unorganized sector and should have a valid registration.

Additionally, the beneficiaries must belong to economically weaker sections of society.

They should not own any residential property in urban areas.
