The scheme is known as the Skill Grant Incentive Program for Graduates and Post Graduates (SGIPGP). 

It aims to enhance the employability of graduates and postgraduates through skill development. 

The program provides financial assistance to institutions that offer skill training to eligible candidates. 

The benefits include monetary incentives for skill training programs and support for infrastructure development. 

Eligible candidates include graduates and postgraduates from recognized institutions. 

The application process involves institutions submitting proposals for skill training programs. 

The timeline for the implementation of the program is determined by the specific training schedules of the institutions. 

The Ministry of Skill Development and Entrepreneurship is the implementing authority for this scheme. 

Further details about the specific financial assistance and training modules may vary based on the institution's proposal.
