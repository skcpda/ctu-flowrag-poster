The Pradhan Mantri Garib Kalyan Anna Yojana (PM-GKAY) was launched in March 2020. 

It was introduced in response to the economic impact of the COVID-19 pandemic. 

The primary objective is to provide food security to the poor and vulnerable sections of society. 

The scheme aims to ensure that no one goes hungry during the crisis. 

Under PM-GKAY, beneficiaries receive free food grains. 

Each eligible household is entitled to 5 kilograms of food grains per person per month. 

The scheme also includes an additional allocation of 1 kilogram of pulses per household per month. 

The benefits are provided for a specified period during the pandemic. 

Eligibility is based on the National Food Security Act criteria. 

This includes individuals and families identified as below the poverty line. 

The application process is not required for those already registered under the National Food Security Act. 

Food grains are distributed through the Public Distribution System. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Consumer Affairs Food and Public Distribution. 

State governments and Union Territories are responsible for the distribution. 

The scheme has been extended multiple times based on the ongoing needs. 

It has been a significant measure to alleviate hunger during the pandemic. 

The initiative has been praised for its swift implementation and outreach.
