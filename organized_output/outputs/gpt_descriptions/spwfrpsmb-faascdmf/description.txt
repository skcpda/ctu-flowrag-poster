The scheme SPWFRPSMB-FAASCDMF is designed to support the welfare of farmers. 

It aims to provide financial assistance to farmers facing challenges due to natural calamities. 

The benefits include direct financial support to mitigate losses incurred from adverse weather conditions. 

Eligibility is primarily based on the farmer's landholding status and the extent of damage caused by the calamity. 

Farmers must submit an application along with necessary documentation to avail of the benefits. 

The application process typically involves filling out a form and providing proof of land ownership and damage assessment. 

The timeline for receiving assistance may vary depending on the assessment and approval process. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers' Welfare at the national level. 

State governments may also play a role in the execution and disbursement of funds.
