The SCFC-CPT scheme is part of the Skill Development initiatives by the Government of India. 

It aims to enhance the employability of youth through skill development and vocational training. 

The scheme focuses on providing training in various sectors to improve job opportunities for participants. 

Benefits include skill training, certification, and support for self-employment or wage employment. 

Eligibility criteria include youth aged between 15 to 29 years who are seeking skill development. 

The application process typically involves enrolling through designated training centers or online platforms. 

The timeline for training programs varies depending on the specific course and training provider. 

Implementing authorities include the Ministry of Skill Development and Entrepreneurship and affiliated training partners. 

The scheme is part of a broader effort to promote skill development in India and reduce unemployment.
