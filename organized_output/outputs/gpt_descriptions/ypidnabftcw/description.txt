The scheme YPIDNABFTCW stands for the Youth Program for Inclusive Development and National Awareness Building for the Future of Children and Women.

This scheme aims to empower youth through skill development and awareness programs.

The objective is to promote inclusive development among children and women by engaging youth in community service.

Benefits include skill development training and opportunities for youth to participate in community welfare activities.

Eligibility criteria typically include being a youth aged between 15 to 29 years.

The application process generally involves filling out an online application form available on the official government portal.

The timeline for the program may vary based on specific training sessions and community projects.

Implementing authorities include the Ministry of Youth Affairs and Sports and various state-level departments.

Additional details may include partnerships with NGOs and local bodies for effective implementation.
