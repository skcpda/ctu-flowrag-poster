The West Bengal Tribal Welfare Scheme for Scheduled Tribes is designed to provide financial assistance and support to the tribal communities in West Bengal. 

The objective of the scheme is to promote the socio-economic development of Scheduled Tribes. 

The benefits include financial aid for education health and livelihood initiatives. 

Eligibility is primarily for individuals belonging to Scheduled Tribes in West Bengal. 

The application process involves submitting an application form to the designated local authority or department. 

The timeline for disbursement of benefits may vary based on the specific assistance provided. 

The implementing authority for this scheme is the Department of Tribal Development Government of West Bengal. 

Additional details may include specific programs under the scheme aimed at enhancing skills and providing employment opportunities.
