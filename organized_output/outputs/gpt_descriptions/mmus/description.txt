The Mukhyamantri Mahila Udyami Scheme (MMUS) aims to promote women's entrepreneurship in Madhya Pradesh. 

The objective is to empower women by providing financial assistance for starting and expanding small businesses. 

The scheme offers financial support in the form of subsidies and loans to eligible women entrepreneurs. 

Benefits include a subsidy on the interest rate for loans taken from banks and financial institutions. 

Women entrepreneurs can receive assistance for various types of businesses including manufacturing and service sectors. 

Eligibility criteria include being a woman resident of Madhya Pradesh and having a viable business plan. 

The applicant must also be between the ages of 18 and 55 years. 

The application process involves submitting a detailed business proposal along with required documents to the concerned department. 

Applications can typically be submitted online through the official state government portal. 

The timeline for approval and disbursement of funds may vary based on the completeness of the application and processing time. 

The scheme is implemented by the Department of Micro Small and Medium Enterprises of Madhya Pradesh. 

Additional details may include specific training programs for women entrepreneurs to enhance their skills.
