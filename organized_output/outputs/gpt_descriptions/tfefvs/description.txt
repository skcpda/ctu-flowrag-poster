The scheme TFEFVS stands for the Tribal Forest Empowerment and Forest Village Scheme. 

It aims to empower tribal communities living in forest areas. 

The objective is to enhance the livelihoods of tribal populations through sustainable forest management. 

The scheme provides financial assistance for the development of forest-based livelihoods. 

It also supports capacity building and skill development for tribal communities. 

Eligibility is generally limited to tribal individuals and communities residing in forest villages. 

The application process typically involves submitting a proposal to the relevant forest department. 

The timeline for assistance may vary based on project approval and implementation phases. 

The implementing authority is usually the Ministry of Tribal Affairs along with state forest departments. 

Additional details may include provisions for community participation in forest conservation efforts.
