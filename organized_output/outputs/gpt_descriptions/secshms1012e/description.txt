The scheme secshms1012e is known as the Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana - Urban.

This scheme was launched to provide affordable housing to the urban poor.

The primary objective is to ensure housing for all by 2022.

The benefits include financial assistance for the construction or enhancement of houses.

Eligible beneficiaries include Economically Weaker Sections and Low-Income Groups.

The application process involves submitting an online application through the official PMAY website.

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs.

The timeline for the disbursement of funds varies based on project completion.

The scheme also includes provisions for interest subsidies on housing loans.

It aims to promote sustainable and inclusive urban development.
