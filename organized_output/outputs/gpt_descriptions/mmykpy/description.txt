Mahatma Gandhi National Rural Employment Guarantee Act is also known as MGNREGA. 

The scheme aims to provide at least 100 days of guaranteed wage employment in a financial year to every rural household. 

It seeks to enhance livelihood security in rural areas. 

The objective is to create durable assets and improve the livelihood security of rural households. 

The benefits include wage payments for unskilled manual work. 

The assistance is provided in the form of employment opportunities. 

Eligibility is limited to adult members of rural households. 

The application process involves submitting a demand for work to the local Gram Panchayat. 

The timeline for wage payment is typically within 15 days of work completion. 

The Ministry of Rural Development is the implementing authority for this scheme. 

The scheme is funded by the Government of India and state governments. 

It also promotes social inclusion and empowerment of marginalized communities. 

The scheme has provisions for grievance redressal. 

It is monitored through a decentralized system involving local bodies. 

MGNREGA also emphasizes the importance of transparency and accountability in implementation.
