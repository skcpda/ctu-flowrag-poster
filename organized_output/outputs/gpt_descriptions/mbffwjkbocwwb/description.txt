The scheme "Mahatma Gandhi National Rural Employment Guarantee Act" is aimed at providing legal guarantee for at least 100 days of unskilled wage employment in a financial year to every rural household. 

The objective is to enhance livelihood security in rural areas and improve the economic condition of rural households. 

The benefits include wage employment for unskilled labor and the creation of durable assets in rural areas. 

Eligibility is limited to adult members of rural households who are willing to do unskilled manual work. 

The application process involves submitting a job card application at the local Gram Panchayat. 

The timeline for employment is based on demand and can be availed throughout the year. 

The implementing authorities include the Ministry of Rural Development and the respective State Governments. 

The scheme also aims to empower rural women and promote social inclusion.
