The Scheme for Assistance to the Inhabitants of the Prisons in the State of Delhi (SAIPRSD) is an initiative by the Government of Delhi. 

It aims to provide support and assistance to prisoners and their families. 

The objectives include improving the living conditions of prisoners and facilitating their rehabilitation. 

Benefits include financial assistance for families of prisoners and support for vocational training. 

Eligibility criteria include being a prisoner in Delhi and having a family that requires financial support. 

The application process involves submitting a request to the relevant authorities in the Delhi government. 

The timeline for assistance is determined by the case specifics and the approval process. 

The implementing authority for this scheme is the Delhi Department of Prisons. 

Additional details may include provisions for mental health support and educational opportunities for prisoners.
