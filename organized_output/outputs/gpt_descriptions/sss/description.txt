The SSS scheme refers to the Pradhan Mantri Shram Yogi Maandhan Yojana. 

This scheme was launched to provide social security to unorganized sector workers. 

The objective is to provide a monthly pension to workers after they reach the age of 60. 

The benefits include a monthly pension of Rs. 3000 for eligible beneficiaries. 

Assistance is provided to workers in the unorganized sector such as construction workers and agricultural laborers. 

Eligibility criteria include being an unorganized sector worker aged between 18 to 40 years. 

The applicant must have a monthly income of less than Rs. 15000. 

The application process involves filling out a registration form available at Common Service Centers or online. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Labour and Employment. 

The pension is disbursed monthly after the beneficiary reaches the age of 60. 

The scheme aims to ensure financial security for workers in their old age.
