NSSKY stands for the National Scheme for Support to the Training and Employment of Youth. 

It was launched to enhance the employability of youth in India. 

The scheme aims to provide skill development and vocational training to young individuals. 

It focuses on creating a skilled workforce that meets the demands of various industries. 

The benefits include training in various sectors and support for self-employment initiatives. 

Eligible beneficiaries are youth aged between 15 to 29 years. 

The application process involves registering online through the official portal. 

Training programs are conducted by recognized training providers. 

The timeline for training varies based on the specific program selected. 

The Ministry of Skill Development and Entrepreneurship is the implementing authority. 

The scheme also promotes entrepreneurship among the youth. 

It encourages partnerships with industry stakeholders for better job placements.
