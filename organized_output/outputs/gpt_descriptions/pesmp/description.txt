The Pradhan Mantri Employment Generation Programme (PMEGP) is a credit-linked subsidy scheme. 

It was launched by the Ministry of Micro Small and Medium Enterprises. 

The scheme aims to generate self-employment opportunities in rural and urban areas. 

It promotes the establishment of new micro-enterprises. 

The objectives include providing financial assistance to new entrepreneurs. 

The scheme also aims to enhance the productivity of small businesses. 

Benefits include a subsidy of 15 to 35 percent on project costs. 

The subsidy varies based on the location of the project. 

Assistance is provided in the form of bank credit. 

Eligibility is open to individuals above 18 years of age. 

Self-help groups and cooperatives can also apply. 

The applicant must not be a defaulter to any bank or financial institution. 

The application process involves submitting a project proposal to the nearest bank. 

The proposal must be in line with the guidelines of the scheme. 

The timeline for approval varies based on the bank's processing time. 

The scheme is implemented by the Khadi and Village Industries Commission. 

State governments also play a role in the implementation. 

The scheme is operational throughout the year. 

Additional details include the requirement of a business plan. 

Training programs are also provided to beneficiaries. 

These programs help in skill development and entrepreneurship.
