The scheme is known as the Self-Supporting Scheme for the Construction of Social Security Structures (SSCSSS). 

It was launched to enhance the infrastructure for social security in India. 

The objective is to provide financial assistance for the construction of social security structures. 

The benefits include funding for the establishment of facilities that support social security programs. 

Eligibility criteria include organizations involved in social welfare activities. 

The application process requires submission of a proposal detailing the project and its expected impact. 

The timeline for approval and funding disbursement varies based on the project specifics. 

The implementing authority for this scheme is the Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment. 

Additional details regarding the funding amount and specific project types may vary.
