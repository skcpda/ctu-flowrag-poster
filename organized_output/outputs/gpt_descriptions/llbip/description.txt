The LLBIP stands for the Livelihood and Livestock Based Income Generation Programme. 

This scheme was launched by the Government of India to enhance the income of rural households. 

The program focuses on promoting sustainable livelihoods through livestock and other income-generating activities. 

It aims to improve the socio-economic conditions of rural families by providing them with financial assistance and skill development.

It seeks to empower rural households by providing them with the necessary resources and training to become self-sufficient. 

The scheme also aims to promote the use of modern technologies in livestock management. 

Benefits under the LLBIP include financial assistance for purchasing livestock. 

The program also provides training in livestock management and other related skills. 

Beneficiaries can receive support for setting up small enterprises related to livestock. 

The assistance is designed to help families increase their income through sustainable practices. 

Eligibility for the LLBIP is primarily focused on rural households. 

The scheme targets Below Poverty Line (BPL) families. 

Women and marginalized communities are given priority under this program.
