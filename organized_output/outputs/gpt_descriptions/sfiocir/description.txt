The SFIOCIR scheme stands for the Special Financial Incentive for the Ongoing Construction of Infrastructure in Rural Areas. 

This scheme was launched to enhance infrastructure development in rural regions of India. 

The primary objective is to provide financial assistance for the construction of essential infrastructure. 

Benefits include financial support for projects that improve connectivity and access to basic services in rural areas. 

Eligible beneficiaries include local bodies and organizations involved in rural infrastructure development. 

The application process typically involves submitting project proposals to the relevant authorities. 

The timeline for fund disbursement and project completion may vary based on the specific project and its requirements. 

The implementing authorities include the Ministry of Rural Development and various state government departments. 

Additional details regarding specific funding amounts and project types may be available through official government sources.
