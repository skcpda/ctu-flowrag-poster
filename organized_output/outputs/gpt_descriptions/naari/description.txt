Naari is a government welfare scheme aimed at empowering women in India. 

The scheme focuses on enhancing women's access to various services and resources. 

It seeks to promote gender equality and improve the socio-economic status of women. 

Benefits under the Naari scheme include financial assistance and skill development programs. 

Eligible beneficiaries are women from economically weaker sections of society. 

The application process typically involves submitting necessary documents to the designated authorities. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Women and Child Development.
