The National Youth Corps (NYC) scheme was launched by the Government of India. 

It aims to engage youth in nation-building activities. 

The objective is to provide opportunities for youth to contribute to society. 

The scheme seeks to promote community service and social development. 

Benefits include a monthly stipend for participants. 

Participants receive training and skill development. 

Eligibility criteria include being an Indian citizen aged between 18 to 25 years. 

Candidates must be willing to work in rural and urban areas. 

The application process involves submitting an online application through the designated portal. 

Selected candidates undergo a selection process conducted by the implementing agencies. 

The NYC scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Youth Affairs and Sports. 

The scheme is operational across various states and union territories. 

Participants are expected to serve for a period of two years. 

The frequency of recruitment and training sessions may vary based on state requirements. 

The NYC scheme also encourages youth to take up leadership roles. 

It promotes awareness of social issues among the youth. 

The scheme fosters a sense of responsibility and civic engagement.
