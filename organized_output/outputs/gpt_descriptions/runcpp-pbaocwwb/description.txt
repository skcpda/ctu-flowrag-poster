The Runcpp-PBAOCWWB scheme is designed to provide financial assistance to construction workers in India. 

The objective of the scheme is to support the welfare of construction workers by offering monetary aid during times of need. 

The benefits include direct cash transfers to eligible construction workers. 

Eligibility is limited to registered construction workers who are part of the Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board. 

The application process requires workers to register with the respective state welfare board and submit necessary documentation. 

The timeline for assistance is determined by the specific needs and circumstances of the applicants. 

The scheme is implemented by the respective state Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Boards. 

Additional details regarding the specific amount of assistance and frequency of payments may vary by state.
