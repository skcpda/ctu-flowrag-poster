The scheme SIOMSBG stands for the Scheme for Implementation of the Online Management System for Beneficiary Grievances. 

It was launched to streamline the grievance redressal process for beneficiaries of various government schemes. 

The objective is to provide a transparent and efficient mechanism for addressing grievances related to government welfare schemes. 

The benefits include timely resolution of complaints and improved accountability of implementing agencies. 

Eligibility criteria typically include being a beneficiary of any government welfare scheme. 

The application process involves registering grievances through the online portal set up for the scheme. 

The timeline for grievance resolution is generally specified on the portal. 

The implementing authority for this scheme is the Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology. 

The scheme aims to enhance the overall effectiveness of welfare programs by ensuring that beneficiaries have a platform to voice their concerns.
