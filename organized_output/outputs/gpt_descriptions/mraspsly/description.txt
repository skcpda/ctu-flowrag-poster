The Mukhyamantri Raigarh Awas Yojana for Slum Dwellers is a welfare scheme launched by the Government of Chhattisgarh. 

This scheme aims to provide housing for slum dwellers in urban areas. 

The objective is to improve living conditions and provide access to basic amenities. 

Under this scheme financial assistance is provided for the construction of houses. 

Beneficiaries receive a subsidy for housing construction. 

Eligibility is generally limited to residents of slum areas who do not own a house. 

The application process involves submitting an application to the local municipal authority. 

The timeline for processing applications and disbursing funds may vary based on local governance. 

The implementing authority for this scheme is the Urban Administration and Development Department of Chhattisgarh. 

Additional details about specific financial assistance amounts or exact eligibility criteria may vary and should be confirmed with local authorities.
