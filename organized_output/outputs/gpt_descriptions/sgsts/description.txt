The Scheme for Grant of Financial Assistance to Scheduled Tribes is known as SGSTS. 

It aims to provide financial assistance to Scheduled Tribe communities for various developmental activities. 

The objective is to promote socio-economic development among Scheduled Tribes. 

The scheme offers financial support for projects related to education health and livelihood. 

Eligible beneficiaries are individuals or groups belonging to Scheduled Tribes. 

The application process involves submitting a proposal to the concerned state government or union territory administration. 

The timeline for disbursement of funds varies based on project approval and implementation. 

The Ministry of Tribal Affairs is the implementing authority for this scheme. 

Additional details regarding specific project types and funding limits may be available through official government sources.
