The National Agricultural Cooperative Marketing Federation of India Limited Scheme is known as NASSCOM. 

It aims to promote cooperative marketing of agricultural produce. 

The objective is to enhance the income of farmers through better marketing strategies. 

The scheme provides assistance in the form of marketing support and infrastructure development. 

Eligible beneficiaries include farmers and agricultural cooperatives. 

The application process involves registration with the cooperative society. 

Specific timelines for assistance may vary based on the project and implementation. 

The Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers' Welfare is the implementing authority. 

Additional details may include training programs for farmers on marketing techniques.
