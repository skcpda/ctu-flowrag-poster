The Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana - Gramin (PMAY-G) is a government welfare scheme aimed at providing housing for the rural poor in India. 

The objective of the scheme is to provide pucca houses to all rural households living in kutcha houses. 

The benefits include financial assistance for the construction of a house. 

Eligible beneficiaries are those who belong to the Below Poverty Line category and do not own a house. 

The application process involves submitting an application through the Gram Panchayat or the designated authority in the village. 

The timeline for the disbursement of funds is generally aligned with the construction phases of the house. 

The Ministry of Rural Development is the implementing authority for this scheme. 

The scheme also aims to promote the use of eco-friendly and sustainable building materials.
