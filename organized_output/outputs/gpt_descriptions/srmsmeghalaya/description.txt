The SRMSME Meghalaya scheme is an initiative by the Government of Meghalaya. 

It aims to promote the growth and development of micro small and medium enterprises in the state. 

The objectives include enhancing entrepreneurship and creating employment opportunities. 

The scheme provides financial assistance to eligible MSMEs for various purposes. 

Benefits include access to credit facilities and subsidies. 

Eligibility criteria include being a registered micro small or medium enterprise in Meghalaya. 

The application process involves submitting the required documents to the designated authority. 

The timeline for processing applications may vary based on the nature of the request. 

The implementing authority for this scheme is the Directorate of Industries and Commerce, Government of Meghalaya. 

Additional details about specific financial assistance and support services may be available through official channels.
