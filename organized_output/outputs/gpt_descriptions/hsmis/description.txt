The Health Sector Management Information System (HSMIS) is an initiative launched by the Government of India. 

It aims to enhance the efficiency and effectiveness of health services across the country. 

The scheme is part of the broader efforts to digitize health services and improve healthcare delivery. 

HSMIS seeks to provide a robust framework for managing health information. 

This system is designed to facilitate the collection analysis and dissemination of health data.

It aims to ensure that health services are accessible to all citizens. 

HSMIS also focuses on improving the quality of healthcare services. 

Another objective is to strengthen the health information system in India.

HSMIS aims to support evidence-based decision-making in health policy.
