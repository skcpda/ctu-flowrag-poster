The SIPPRL scheme stands for the Scheme for Integrated Promotion of Production and Research in Livestock. 

It aims to enhance the productivity of livestock through integrated research and development initiatives. 

The primary objective is to promote sustainable livestock production systems. 

The scheme provides financial assistance for research projects related to livestock. 

It also supports capacity building and training programs for farmers and stakeholders. 

Eligibility criteria include research institutions and organizations involved in livestock research. 

The application process involves submitting a project proposal to the relevant authorities. 

The timeline for project implementation varies based on the specific project approved. 

The scheme is implemented by the Department of Animal Husbandry and Dairying under the Ministry of Fisheries Animal Husbandry and Dairying. 

Additional details regarding specific funding limits and project durations may be available through official notifications.
