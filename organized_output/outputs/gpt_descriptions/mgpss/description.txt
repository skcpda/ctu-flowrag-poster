The Mahatma Gandhi Punyakoti Scheme for Sustainable Livelihoods (MGPS) is an initiative aimed at enhancing the livelihoods of marginalized communities in India. 

The objective of the scheme is to provide sustainable livelihood opportunities through skill development and self-employment. 

The benefits include financial assistance for setting up small businesses and access to training programs for skill enhancement. 

Eligibility is primarily focused on marginalized and economically disadvantaged groups including Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes. 

The application process involves submitting a form to the designated local authority or department responsible for implementing the scheme. 

The timeline for processing applications and disbursing funds may vary based on the local administrative procedures. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Rural Development in collaboration with state governments and local bodies.
