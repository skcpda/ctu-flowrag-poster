The Pradhan Mantri Sardar Patel Fellowship and Scholarship for Foreign Students is a scheme initiated by the Government of India. 

It aims to promote cultural exchange and enhance the understanding of Indian culture among foreign students. 

The objectives include providing financial assistance to foreign students for pursuing higher education in India. 

Benefits include a monthly stipend for living expenses and coverage of tuition fees. 

Eligibility is limited to foreign nationals who have been accepted into recognized Indian educational institutions. 

The application process involves submitting an online application through the designated portal. 

The timeline for application submission and scholarship disbursement varies annually. 

The Ministry of External Affairs is the implementing authority for this scheme. 

Additional details regarding specific eligibility criteria and application deadlines can be found on the official website.
