The Mukhyamantri Maatru Pushtahar Yojana (MMVPY) is a welfare scheme launched by the Government of Madhya Pradesh. 

The scheme aims to improve maternal and child nutrition in the state. 

It focuses on providing nutritional support to pregnant and lactating women. 

The objective is to reduce malnutrition and improve health outcomes for mothers and children. 

Eligible beneficiaries include pregnant women and lactating mothers residing in Madhya Pradesh. 

The scheme provides financial assistance for purchasing nutritious food items. 

Beneficiaries receive a monthly allowance to support their nutritional needs. 

The application process involves registration at local health centers or through Anganwadi workers. 

The scheme is implemented by the Department of Women and Child Development in Madhya Pradesh. 

The frequency of assistance is monthly during the pregnancy and lactation period. 

The scheme also includes awareness programs on nutrition and health. 

Monitoring and evaluation are conducted to assess the impact of the scheme on maternal and child health.
