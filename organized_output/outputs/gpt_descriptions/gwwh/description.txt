The Government of India has launched various welfare schemes aimed at improving the living standards of its citizens. 

One such scheme is the Garib Kalyan Rojgar Abhiyaan. 

This scheme was launched in June 2020 in response to the economic challenges posed by the COVID-19 pandemic. 

The primary context behind the scheme is to provide employment opportunities to migrant workers who returned to their villages due to the lockdown. 

The initiative aims to boost the rural economy by providing wage employment to those who lost their jobs during the pandemic. 

The objectives of the Garib Kalyan Rojgar Abhiyaan are multifaceted. 

Firstly it aims to provide at least 100 days of wage employment in a financial year to every rural household. 

Secondly it seeks to create durable assets in rural areas. 

These assets include roads irrigation facilities and housing. 

The scheme also aims to enhance livelihood opportunities for the rural population. 

In terms of benefits and assistance the scheme provides a range of employment opportunities. 

It focuses on various sectors such as agriculture horticulture animal husbandry and fisheries. 

The wage rate under the scheme is aligned with the Mahatma Gandhi National Rural Employment Guarantee Act. 

This ensures that beneficiaries receive a fair wage for their work. 

Additionally the scheme emphasizes skill development and training. 

This is to ensure that workers are equipped with the necessary skills for better employment opportunities. 

The eligibility criteria for the Garib Kalyan Rojgar Abhiyaan are straightforward. 

Any rural household that has lost its livelihood due to the pandemic is eligible to apply. 

The scheme particularly targets migrant workers who have returned to their native villages. 

Women and marginalized communities are also given priority under this scheme. 

The application process for the scheme is designed to be simple and accessible. 

Interested beneficiaries can approach their local Gram Panchayat or employment office. 

They need to provide necessary documentation to prove their eligibility. 

This may include identity proof and proof of residence. 

Once the application is submitted the local authorities will verify the details. 

After verification eligible beneficiaries will be enrolled in the scheme. 

The timeline for the implementation of the scheme is set for a period of 125 days. 

During this time various employment opportunities will be made available to the beneficiaries. 

The scheme is expected to provide immediate relief and support to those in need. 

The implementing authorities for the Garib Kalyan Rojgar Abhiyaan include multiple government departments. 

These departments work in coordination to ensure the effective execution of the scheme. 

The Ministry of Rural Development plays a pivotal role in overseeing the implementation. 

State governments are also involved in the execution at the local level. 

They facilitate the identification of beneficiaries and the allocation of work. 

The scheme is designed to be flexible and adaptable to the needs of different regions. 

This allows for the inclusion of various local projects that can provide employment. 

In addition to providing immediate employment the scheme also aims to create long-term benefits. 

By developing infrastructure and enhancing skills the scheme contributes to sustainable development. 

Moreover the Garib Kalyan Rojgar Abhiyaan is part of a larger framework of government initiatives. 

These initiatives are aimed at poverty alleviation and economic recovery post-COVID-19. 

The scheme aligns with the government's vision of Atmanirbhar Bharat or self-reliant India. 

This vision emphasizes the importance of local production and employment generation. 

The Garib Kalyan Rojgar Abhiyaan is a significant step towards achieving this vision. 

It reflects the government's commitment to supporting the most vulnerable sections of society.
