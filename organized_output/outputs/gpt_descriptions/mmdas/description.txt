The MMDAS scheme stands for the Mahatma Gandhi National Rural Employment Guarantee Act. 

It aims to provide at least 100 days of wage employment in a financial year to every rural household. 

The primary objective is to enhance livelihood security in rural areas. 

The scheme also seeks to create durable assets and improve the rural infrastructure. 

Benefits include guaranteed wage employment and the provision of social security. 

Eligible beneficiaries are rural households that demand work under the scheme. 

The application process involves submitting a demand for work to the local Gram Panchayat. 

The timeline for employment is based on the demand raised by the households. 

The Ministry of Rural Development is the implementing authority for this scheme. 

The scheme is funded by the Government of India and state governments. 

The MMDAS scheme also promotes social inclusion by providing employment to marginalized communities.
