The National Grameen Employment Guarantee Act is commonly known as NREGA or MGNREGA. 

It was enacted in 2005 to provide a legal guarantee for at least 100 days of unskilled wage employment in a financial year to every rural household. 

The primary objective is to enhance livelihood security in rural areas. 

The scheme aims to provide a safety net for rural households and improve their living standards. 

Benefits include the provision of wage employment and the creation of durable assets in rural areas. 

Eligible beneficiaries are all rural households that apply for work under the scheme. 

The application process involves submitting a job card application to the local Gram Panchayat. 

Once registered the households can demand work as per their needs. 

The scheme operates throughout the year. 

The Ministry of Rural Development is the implementing authority for this scheme. 

State governments are responsible for the implementation at the local level. 

Payments are made directly to the bank accounts of the beneficiaries. 

The scheme also focuses on the participation of women and marginalized communities. 

It promotes social inclusion and empowerment through work. 

The scheme has provisions for grievance redressal to address issues faced by beneficiaries. 

Regular monitoring and evaluation are conducted to ensure effective implementation.
