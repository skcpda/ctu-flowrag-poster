The Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana (PMAY) is a flagship housing scheme launched by the Government of India. 

It aims to provide affordable housing to the urban poor and economically weaker sections of society. 

The objective is to construct 20 million affordable houses by the year 2022. 

The scheme offers financial assistance in the form of interest subsidies on home loans. 

Beneficiaries can receive subsidies of up to 2.67 lakh INR based on their income category. 

Eligibility is based on income criteria which are divided into Economically Weaker Section, Low Income Group, and Middle Income Group. 

The application process involves submitting an online application through the PMAY website or visiting designated Common Service Centers. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs. 

The timeline for the completion of houses is set to be within a specified period based on the project approval. 

The scheme also encourages the use of eco-friendly and sustainable construction materials. 

Beneficiaries are selected through a transparent process based on their socio-economic status. 

The scheme also includes provisions for slum rehabilitation and upgrading of existing housing. 

The PMAY is part of the broader initiative to achieve "Housing for All" by 2022.
