The Old Age Pension (OAP) scheme is a government initiative aimed at providing financial assistance to elderly individuals. 

The scheme is designed to support senior citizens who may not have adequate financial resources for their sustenance. 

The primary objective is to ensure a basic standard of living for the elderly population. 

Benefits under the scheme include a monthly pension amount provided to eligible beneficiaries. 

The amount may vary by state and is intended to cover basic needs such as food and healthcare. 

Eligibility criteria typically include age requirements which usually start from 60 years and above. 

Applicants must also meet certain income and asset criteria which may vary by state. 

The application process generally involves submitting a form along with necessary documents such as age proof and income certificate. 

The timeline for processing applications can vary but is usually completed within a few weeks. 

The scheme is implemented by state governments in coordination with the Ministry of Rural Development and the Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment. 

Additional details may include specific provisions for different states and the possibility of direct bank transfers for pension disbursement.
