The Rescaw scheme is an initiative aimed at providing support for the welfare of stray animals in India. 

The objective of the scheme is to ensure the protection and care of stray animals. 

The scheme provides assistance in the form of medical care food and shelter for stray animals. 

Eligibility criteria include organizations and individuals involved in animal welfare activities. 

The application process typically involves submitting a proposal to the relevant authorities detailing the intended activities for animal care. 

The timeline for assistance may vary based on the specific needs and the approval process of the application. 

Implementing authorities include local animal welfare organizations and government bodies responsible for animal husbandry. 

The scheme aims to promote humane treatment and raise awareness about the welfare of stray animals.
