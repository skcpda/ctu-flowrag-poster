The National Mission for Enhanced Energy Efficiency is known as NMEES. 

It was launched by the Government of India to promote energy efficiency in various sectors. 

The objective is to reduce energy consumption and promote sustainable development. 

The scheme aims to enhance energy efficiency in industries and commercial buildings. 

It provides financial assistance for energy efficiency projects. 

The benefits include reduced energy costs and improved environmental sustainability. 

Eligibility is primarily for industries and commercial establishments that seek to improve energy efficiency. 

The application process involves submitting a proposal for energy efficiency projects to the designated authorities. 

The timeline for project implementation varies based on the specific project and approval process. 

The Bureau of Energy Efficiency is the implementing authority for this scheme. 

The scheme also encourages the adoption of energy-efficient technologies and practices. 

It aims to create awareness about energy conservation among stakeholders. 

The mission supports the development of market-based mechanisms to enhance energy efficiency. 

It promotes the establishment of energy service companies to facilitate energy efficiency projects.
