The TTF scheme refers to the Technology Transfer Fund. 

It was launched to promote the transfer of technology to various sectors. 

The objective is to enhance the competitiveness of Indian industries through technology adoption. 

The scheme provides financial assistance for technology acquisition and adaptation. 

Eligible beneficiaries include small and medium enterprises and startups. 

The application process involves submitting a proposal to the relevant authority. 

The timeline for fund disbursement varies based on project evaluation. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises. 

The TTF aims to bridge the technology gap in various industries. 

It encourages innovation and modernization in manufacturing processes.
