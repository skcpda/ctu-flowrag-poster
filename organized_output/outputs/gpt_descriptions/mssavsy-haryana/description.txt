MSSAVSY Haryana is the Mukhyamantri Sewa Sadan Awas Yojana for the state of Haryana. 

The scheme aims to provide housing assistance to economically weaker sections of society. 

It focuses on ensuring that low-income families have access to affordable housing. 

The benefits include financial assistance for constructing or purchasing houses. 

Eligible beneficiaries include families with an annual income below a specified threshold. 

The application process involves submitting an application form along with required documents to the designated authority. 

The timeline for processing applications and disbursing funds may vary based on the specific case. 

The scheme is implemented by the Haryana Urban Development Authority and other relevant state departments. 

Additional details regarding the exact income threshold and specific documentation requirements may be obtained from official sources.
