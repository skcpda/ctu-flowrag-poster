The S30S scheme is a government initiative aimed at providing financial assistance to small and marginal farmers in India. 

The objective of the scheme is to enhance the income of farmers through direct cash transfers. 

The benefits include financial support to eligible farmers to improve their agricultural productivity. 

Eligibility criteria include being a small or marginal farmer as defined by government standards. 

Farmers must also possess a valid land ownership document to qualify for the scheme. 

The application process involves submitting an application form along with necessary documents to the local agricultural office. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers' Welfare. 

The timeline for disbursement of funds is typically aligned with the agricultural calendar and may vary based on specific circumstances. 

Additional details regarding the scheme may include provisions for training and capacity building for farmers.
