The Pradhan Mantri Suraksha Bima Yojana (PMSBY) is a government welfare scheme launched in 2015. 

It aims to provide accidental death and disability coverage to individuals. 

The objective is to enhance the social security of the poor and underprivileged sections of society. 

The scheme offers a sum assured of INR 2 lakh in case of accidental death or permanent disability. 

In case of partial disability, the coverage is INR 1 lakh. 

The premium for the scheme is INR 12 per annum. 

Eligibility includes individuals aged between 18 and 70 years. 

Applicants must have a savings bank account. 

The application process is simple and can be done through banks or online platforms. 

The coverage is provided for one year and is renewable annually. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Finance through public sector insurance companies. 

The scheme is available to all eligible individuals across India.
