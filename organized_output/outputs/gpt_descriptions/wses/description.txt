The WSES scheme stands for the Women Self Employment Scheme. 

It is designed to empower women by promoting self-employment opportunities. 

The primary objective is to enhance the economic status of women through skill development and entrepreneurship. 

The scheme provides financial assistance for starting small businesses. 

It also offers training programs to develop necessary skills for entrepreneurship. 

Eligible beneficiaries include women from low-income families and those who are unemployed. 

The application process typically involves submitting a form along with required documents to the local government office. 

The timeline for approval and disbursement of funds can vary based on the specific state or region. 

The implementing authorities include the Ministry of Women and Child Development and state government departments. 

Additional details may include specific training partners and local resource centers for support.
