The scheme known as the Fat Cat Dhabha Owners and Workers Welfare Board is a significant initiative by the Government of India aimed at supporting the welfare of individuals involved in the dhaba sector. 

This scheme was introduced in response to the growing recognition of the challenges faced by dhaba owners and workers. 

Dhabas are roadside eateries that play a crucial role in providing affordable food options to travelers and local residents alike. 

The informal nature of the dhaba industry often leaves workers and owners without access to essential social security benefits. 

The primary objective of the Fat Cat Dhabha Owners and Workers Welfare Board is to ensure the welfare and upliftment of dhaba owners and workers across the country. 

The scheme aims to provide financial assistance and social security benefits to those engaged in this sector. 

Benefits under this scheme include health insurance coverage for workers and their families. 

The scheme also provides financial assistance for education to the children of dhaba workers. 

Additionally, it offers support for skill development and training programs aimed at enhancing the employability of dhaba workers. 

The welfare board also focuses on providing financial aid during emergencies and unforeseen circumstances. 

Eligibility criteria for the scheme are designed to ensure that the most vulnerable individuals benefit from the initiative. 

To be eligible for the benefits, applicants must be engaged in the dhaba sector as either owners or workers. 

The scheme is particularly aimed at those who have been working in this sector for a minimum period.
