The scheme is known as the Samagra Kalyan Punarvasan Yojana or SKPCSY. 

It was launched by the Government of India to provide rehabilitation and support for marginalized and vulnerable communities. 

The main objective is to ensure the socio-economic upliftment of these communities through various welfare measures. 

The benefits include financial assistance for education health and livelihood. 

Assistance is also provided for skill development and self-employment opportunities. 

Eligibility criteria include being a member of a marginalized community or a vulnerable group. 

The application process typically involves submitting a form along with necessary documentation to the designated authority. 

The timeline for processing applications may vary depending on the specific implementing agency. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment.
