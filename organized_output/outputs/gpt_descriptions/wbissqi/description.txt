The West Bengal Integrated Scheme for Safe and Quality Drinking Water is known as WBISSQI. 

This scheme was launched by the Government of West Bengal. 

It aims to provide safe and quality drinking water to the residents of West Bengal. 

The objectives include ensuring access to safe drinking water and improving water quality in rural and urban areas. 

The benefits include the provision of clean drinking water through various sources such as piped water supply and water purification systems. 

Assistance is provided for the construction of water supply infrastructure and the installation of water quality testing facilities. 

Eligibility criteria include residents of West Bengal who require access to safe drinking water. 

The application process typically involves submitting a request to the local authorities or relevant government departments. 

The timeline for implementation varies based on project scope and location. 

The implementing authority for this scheme is the Public Health Engineering Department of West Bengal. 

Additional details may include community participation in monitoring water quality and maintenance of water supply systems.
