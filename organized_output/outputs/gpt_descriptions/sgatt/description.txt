The Scheme for Grant of Assistance to Tribal Tribes (SGATT) aims to provide financial assistance to Scheduled Tribes in India. 

The objective of SGATT is to promote the socio-economic development of tribal communities. 

The scheme offers financial support for various developmental activities. 

Benefits include funding for education health infrastructure and livelihood projects. 

Eligibility is primarily restricted to individuals and groups belonging to Scheduled Tribes. 

The application process involves submitting a proposal to the concerned state government. 

The timeline for approval and disbursement of funds varies based on the state and project specifics. 

The Ministry of Tribal Affairs is the implementing authority for this scheme. 

State governments also play a crucial role in the execution of SGATT. 

The scheme is part of the broader efforts to uplift tribal populations in India.
