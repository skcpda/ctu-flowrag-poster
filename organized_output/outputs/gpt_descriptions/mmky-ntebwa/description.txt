The Mukhyamantri Kanya Suraksha Yojana (MMKY-NTEBWA) is a welfare scheme initiated by the Government of Madhya Pradesh. 

The scheme aims to enhance the safety and security of girls in the state. 

It focuses on reducing the instances of crimes against women and promoting gender equality. 

The benefits include financial assistance for education and health care for girls. 

Eligible beneficiaries are girls from economically weaker sections of society. 

The application process involves submitting a form through the designated government offices. 

The timeline for assistance is typically aligned with the academic year and health check-up schedules. 

The scheme is implemented by the Department of Women and Child Development in Madhya Pradesh. 

Additional details may include awareness programs and community engagement initiatives to promote the safety of girls.
