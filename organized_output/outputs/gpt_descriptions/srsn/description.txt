The SRSN scheme stands for the Scheme for Rehabilitation of Street Children. 

It aims to provide support and rehabilitation for street children in India. 

The objectives include ensuring the protection of street children and facilitating their access to education and healthcare. 

Benefits include financial assistance for education and vocational training. 

Street children are eligible for this scheme. 

The application process involves submission of relevant documents to local authorities or designated NGOs. 

The timeline for assistance is determined by the specific needs of the children and the resources available. 

The Ministry of Women and Child Development is the implementing authority for this scheme. 

Additional details may include collaboration with local NGOs for effective implementation.
