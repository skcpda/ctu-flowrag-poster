The West Bengal Employment Generation Scheme for the Rural Youth is known as WBEDRJ. 

This scheme was launched by the Government of West Bengal. 

It aims to provide employment opportunities to rural youth in the state. 

The objective is to enhance the livelihood of rural households through self-employment and wage employment. 

The scheme offers financial assistance for setting up small enterprises. 

It also provides skill development training to the beneficiaries. 

Eligibility is primarily focused on rural youth aged between 18 and 45 years. 

Applicants must be residents of West Bengal. 

The application process involves submitting an application form to the local Block Development Office. 

Supporting documents such as identity proof and project proposals are required. 

The scheme is implemented by the Panchayati Raj and Rural Development Department of West Bengal. 

Financial assistance is provided in the form of a subsidy. 

The frequency of assistance may vary based on project approval and completion. 

The scheme aims to promote sustainable rural development through employment generation.
