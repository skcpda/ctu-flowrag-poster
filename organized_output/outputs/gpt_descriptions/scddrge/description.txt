The scheme is known as the Skill Certification Development and Digital Re-skilling of General Workers in the Economy (SCDDRGE). 

It was launched to enhance the skills of general workers in the economy. 

The objective is to provide skill certification and digital re-skilling to improve employability. 

Benefits include skill development training and certification for workers. 

The assistance aims to equip workers with necessary skills for better job opportunities. 

Eligibility criteria include being a general worker in the economy. 

The application process involves registration through designated portals or agencies. 

The timeline for training and certification may vary based on the specific program. 

Implementing authorities include the Ministry of Skill Development and Entrepreneurship. 

The scheme is part of the larger initiative to boost skill development in India.
