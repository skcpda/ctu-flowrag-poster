The TSLGMFDC scheme stands for Telangana State Scheduled Castes Cooperative Finance Corporation Limited. 

It aims to provide financial assistance to the Scheduled Caste communities in Telangana. 

The primary objective is to empower Scheduled Caste individuals through self-employment and entrepreneurship. 

The scheme offers financial support in the form of loans for various income-generating activities. 

It also provides skill development training to enhance employability. 

Eligibility is generally restricted to individuals belonging to Scheduled Castes in Telangana. 

Applicants must be above a certain age limit as specified by the scheme guidelines. 

The application process typically involves submitting a form along with necessary documents to the concerned authority. 

Applications can be submitted online or at designated offices. 

The timeline for loan disbursement may vary based on the processing time of applications. 

The implementing authority for this scheme is the Telangana State Scheduled Castes Cooperative Finance Corporation Limited. 

Additional details regarding specific loan amounts and interest rates may be available through official sources.
