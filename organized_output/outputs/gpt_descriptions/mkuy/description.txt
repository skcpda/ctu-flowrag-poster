The Mukhyamantri Kanya Utthan Yojana (MKUY) is a welfare scheme launched by the Government of Bihar. 

The scheme aims to promote the education and empowerment of girls in the state. 

It focuses on reducing the dropout rate among girls and encouraging them to pursue higher education. 

The benefits include financial assistance provided to families for the education of their daughters. 

Eligible beneficiaries receive a one-time financial incentive upon the completion of their education. 

The scheme targets girls who are born after a certain date and complete their education up to a specified level. 

Families with a girl child are encouraged to apply for the scheme. 

The application process typically involves submitting necessary documents to the local authorities or online through designated portals. 

The financial assistance is disbursed at various stages of education. 

The implementing authority for this scheme is the Department of Social Welfare, Government of Bihar. 

Additional details may include awareness programs to educate families about the importance of girls' education.
