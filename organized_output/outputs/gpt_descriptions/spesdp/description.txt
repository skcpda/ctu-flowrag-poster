The Special Economic Package for the Self-Employed and Daily Wage Workers (SPESDP) was introduced in response to the economic challenges posed by the COVID-19 pandemic. 

The objective of SPESDP is to provide financial assistance to self-employed individuals and daily wage workers who have been adversely affected by the pandemic. 

The scheme aims to support these individuals by offering them a one-time financial grant to help them sustain their livelihoods. 

Eligible beneficiaries include self-employed individuals and daily wage workers who have lost their income due to the pandemic. 

The application process involves submitting an online application through the designated government portal. 

The timeline for disbursing the financial assistance is typically within a few weeks of the application submission. 

The Ministry of Labour and Employment is the primary implementing authority for this scheme. 

Additional details regarding the specific amount of financial assistance and the exact eligibility criteria may vary based on state regulations.
