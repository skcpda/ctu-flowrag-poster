The scheme SSEG10GCP is designed to provide financial assistance to eligible beneficiaries. 

It aims to support the socio-economic development of marginalized communities. 

The benefits include direct cash transfers to the bank accounts of beneficiaries. 

Eligibility criteria include being a member of a marginalized community and meeting specific income thresholds. 

The application process involves submitting an online form along with required documents. 

The timeline for disbursement of funds is typically quarterly. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment. 

Additional details include regular monitoring and evaluation to ensure effective implementation.
