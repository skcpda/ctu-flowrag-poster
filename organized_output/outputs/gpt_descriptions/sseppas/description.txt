The scheme SSEPPAS stands for the Scheme for Support to Entrepreneurship and Promotion of Startups in the Agriculture Sector. 

It aims to promote entrepreneurship in the agriculture sector by providing financial support and assistance. 

The objectives include fostering innovation and enhancing the competitiveness of agricultural startups. 

Benefits include financial assistance for startups in the form of grants and subsidies. 

Assistance may also cover capacity building and mentorship programs. 

Eligibility is generally for individuals or groups intending to start an agricultural venture. 

The application process typically involves submitting a detailed project proposal to the relevant authorities. 

The timeline for processing applications may vary based on the specific project and the implementing agency. 

Implementing authorities include the Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers' Welfare and associated state departments. 

The scheme encourages collaboration with various stakeholders in the agricultural ecosystem.
