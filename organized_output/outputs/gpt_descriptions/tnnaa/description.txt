The Tamil Nadu Nutritional Anemia Awareness (TNAAA) scheme aims to address the issue of anemia among women and children in Tamil Nadu. 

The objective of the scheme is to reduce the prevalence of anemia by promoting awareness and providing nutritional support. 

The benefits include educational programs on nutrition and health. 

The scheme also provides iron and folic acid supplements to the target population. 

Eligibility includes women of reproductive age and children under the age of five. 

The application process typically involves registration through local health centers or community health workers. 

The scheme is implemented by the Department of Health and Family Welfare, Government of Tamil Nadu. 

The initiative is part of a broader effort to improve maternal and child health in the state.
