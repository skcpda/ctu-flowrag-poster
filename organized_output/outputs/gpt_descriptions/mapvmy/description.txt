The scheme MAPVMY stands for Mukhyamantri Awas Yojana for the People of Madhya Pradesh. 

It was launched by the Government of Madhya Pradesh to provide housing for the economically weaker sections of society. 

The scheme aims to ensure that every citizen has access to a basic shelter. 

It is part of the larger initiative to improve living conditions and promote urban development. 

The primary objective is to provide affordable housing to the underprivileged. 

The scheme focuses on constructing houses with basic amenities.

The benefits of the MAPVMY scheme include financial assistance for housing construction. 

Eligible beneficiaries receive a subsidy for building their homes. 

The scheme also provides for the construction of houses in urban areas. 

The assistance is aimed at low-income families and individuals. 

Eligibility criteria include being a resident of Madhya Pradesh. 

Applicants must belong to the economically weaker sections of society. 

They should not own a pucca house in their name. 

The age of the applicant must be at least 18 years. 

The application process involves filling out a form available at designated offices. 

Applicants can also apply online through the official government portal. 

They need to provide necessary documents such as identity proof and income certificate. 

Once the application is submitted it is reviewed by the concerned authorities. 

Eligible applicants are then notified about the financial assistance. 

The timeline for processing applications may vary based on the volume of applications. 

Typically the processing takes a few weeks to a couple of months. 

The frequency of assistance is generally a one-time grant for house construction.
