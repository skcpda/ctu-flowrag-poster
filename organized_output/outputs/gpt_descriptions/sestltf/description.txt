The scheme is known as the Self Employment and Skill Training for the Low-Income Families (SESTLTF). 

It aims to provide skill training and self-employment opportunities to individuals from low-income families. 

The objective is to enhance employability and promote entrepreneurship among the target population. 

Benefits include skill development training and financial assistance for starting small businesses. 

Eligibility criteria typically include being a member of a low-income family and being of a certain age group. 

The application process usually involves submitting an application form along with necessary documents to the designated authority. 

The timeline for the scheme may vary based on the specific training programs and financial assistance disbursement. 

Implementing authorities include the Ministry of Skill Development and Entrepreneurship and various state government agencies.
