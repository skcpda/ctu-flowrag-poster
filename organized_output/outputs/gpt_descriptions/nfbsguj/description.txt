The National Food Security Act Gujarat Scheme (NFBSGuj) aims to provide food and nutritional security to the citizens of Gujarat. 

The primary objective is to ensure access to adequate food at affordable prices for the vulnerable sections of society. 

The scheme provides subsidized food grains to eligible households. 

Beneficiaries receive rice and wheat at a highly subsidized rate. 

Eligibility is determined based on the socio-economic status of households. 

Priority households and Antyodaya Anna Yojana (AAY) beneficiaries are included. 

The application process involves submitting a form to the local authorities or designated offices. 

Verification of eligibility is conducted by the implementing authorities. 

The scheme operates on a continuous basis with no specific timeline for applications. 

The Food and Civil Supplies Department of Gujarat is responsible for implementing the scheme. 

Regular monitoring and evaluation are conducted to ensure effective delivery of services.
