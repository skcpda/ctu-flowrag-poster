The SWCNCP stands for the Scheme for Women and Child Nutrition and Care Programme. 

This scheme was launched to address the nutritional needs of women and children in India. 

The objective is to improve the nutritional status of women and children. 

The scheme aims to reduce malnutrition and promote health among vulnerable populations. 

Benefits include nutritional support and health care services for women and children. 

Eligible beneficiaries are women and children from low-income families. 

The application process typically involves registration through local government offices or health centers. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Women and Child Development.
