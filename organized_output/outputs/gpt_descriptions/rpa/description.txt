The RPA scheme refers to the Rashtriya Punarvas Yojana. 

It is designed to provide rehabilitation and resettlement for families affected by natural calamities and developmental projects. 

The objective of the scheme is to ensure that displaced families receive adequate support for their resettlement. 

Benefits include financial assistance for housing and livelihood support. 

Eligible beneficiaries are families displaced due to natural disasters or government projects. 

The application process typically involves submitting a request to the local government authorities. 

The timeline for assistance may vary based on the specific circumstances of displacement. 

The implementing authorities include state governments and local administrative bodies.
