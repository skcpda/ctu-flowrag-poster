The scheme SMPACMETF stands for the Scheme for Market Promotion and Assistance for Coir and Marine Export Trade Fund. 

It aims to promote the export of coir and marine products from India. 

The objectives include enhancing the competitiveness of Indian coir and marine products in international markets. 

The scheme provides financial assistance for marketing and promotional activities. 

Eligible beneficiaries include exporters of coir and marine products. 

The application process involves submitting a proposal to the relevant authority. 

The timeline for assistance is project-specific and varies based on the proposal. 

The Ministry of Commerce and Industry is the implementing authority for this scheme. 

Additional details about specific funding limits and project requirements may be available through official sources.
