The context of this scheme arises from the persistent challenges of food insecurity faced by various populations in India.

Firstly, the scheme aims to provide nutritional support to children and pregnant women. 

Secondly, it seeks to improve the overall health outcomes of these vulnerable groups. 

Thirdly, the scheme intends to create awareness about the importance of nutrition and healthy eating habits. 

The benefits and assistance provided under the EAT-T10T12S scheme are substantial. 

Eligible beneficiaries receive fortified food items that are designed to meet their nutritional requirements.

In addition, families with a history of malnutrition or health issues are also considered eligible.
