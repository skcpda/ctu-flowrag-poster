The Pradhan Mantri Surakshit Matritva Abhiyan (PMSMA) is a government initiative aimed at improving maternal health in India. 

The scheme was launched to ensure comprehensive and quality healthcare for pregnant women. 

Its primary objective is to reduce maternal and infant mortality rates. 

The PMSMA provides free antenatal check-ups for pregnant women. 

It offers essential health services such as blood tests and ultrasound. 

The scheme also focuses on educating women about maternal health and nutrition. 

Eligibility includes all pregnant women who are registered in the healthcare system. 

The application process involves visiting designated healthcare facilities for registration and check-ups. 

The services are provided on the 9th of every month at participating health centers. 

The Ministry of Health and Family Welfare is the implementing authority for this scheme. 

The PMSMA is part of the larger National Health Mission. 

It aims to ensure that every pregnant woman receives the necessary healthcare services.
