Mahatma Gandhi National Rural Employment Guarantee Act is also known as MGNREGA. 

The scheme aims to enhance livelihood security in rural areas by providing at least 100 days of unskilled wage employment in a financial year to every rural household. 

The objective is to provide social security and improve the economic condition of rural households. 

Benefits include guaranteed wage employment and the creation of durable assets in rural areas. 

The assistance is provided in the form of wage payments for the work done under the scheme. 

Eligibility is limited to adult members of rural households who are willing to do unskilled manual work. 

The application process involves submitting a job card application to the local Gram Panchayat. 

The timeline for wage payments is generally within 15 days of the completion of work. 

The Ministry of Rural Development is the implementing authority for this scheme. 

State governments are responsible for the implementation at the local level. 

The scheme also aims to empower marginalized sections of society by providing them with employment opportunities. 

It promotes sustainable development through the creation of community assets. 

The scheme is a significant step towards reducing rural poverty.
