The Mahatma Gandhi National Rural Employment Guarantee Act (MGNREGA) is a significant welfare scheme in India. 

It was enacted in 2005 to enhance livelihood security in rural areas. 

The scheme aims to provide at least 100 days of guaranteed wage employment in a financial year to every rural household. 

It seeks to create durable assets and improve the livelihood security of rural households.
