The Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samman Nidhi (PM-KISAN) scheme was launched by the Government of India. 

It aims to provide financial support to small and marginal farmers. 

The objective is to ensure a minimum income support to farmers. 

Under this scheme farmers receive direct cash transfers. 

Each eligible farmer receives Rs. 6000 per year. 

This amount is disbursed in three equal installments of Rs. 2000. 

Eligibility is limited to small and marginal farmers. 

Farmers must own cultivable land up to 2 hectares. 

The scheme excludes certain categories of beneficiaries. 

These include institutional landholders and farmers with higher landholdings. 

The application process involves self-registration through the PM-KISAN portal. 

Farmers can also apply through local revenue officers or agriculture departments. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers' Welfare. 

The financial assistance is provided directly to the bank accounts of the beneficiaries. 

The payments are made on a regular basis throughout the year. 

The scheme was first announced in the Interim Budget of 2019. 

It has been operational since December 2018. 

The scheme aims to alleviate the financial burden on farmers. 

It also seeks to enhance their livelihood security. 

The PM-KISAN scheme is part of a broader initiative to support the agricultural sector in India.
