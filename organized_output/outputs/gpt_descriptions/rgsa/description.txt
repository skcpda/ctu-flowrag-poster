The Rashtriya Gram Swaraj Abhiyan (RGSA) is a government initiative aimed at strengthening the Panchayati Raj institutions in India. 

The scheme was launched to enhance the capacity of local self-governments in rural areas. 

The primary objective is to promote self-governance and empower local bodies to deliver better services to citizens. 

The benefits include financial assistance for capacity building and development of rural infrastructure. 

It also aims to improve the delivery of services in areas such as health education and sanitation. 

Eligibility for the scheme includes all Panchayati Raj institutions across the country. 

The application process involves proposals submitted by state governments for funding and support. 

The timeline for implementation varies based on the specific projects undertaken by the states. 

The Ministry of Panchayati Raj is the implementing authority for the RGSA. 

The scheme also emphasizes the importance of participatory governance and community involvement. 

It aims to create a sustainable framework for rural development through decentralized governance.
