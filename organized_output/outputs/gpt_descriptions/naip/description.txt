The National Agriculture Innovation Project (NAIP) is an initiative launched by the Government of India. 

It aims to promote agricultural innovation and enhance the productivity of the agricultural sector. 

The objectives include improving the competitiveness of Indian agriculture and ensuring sustainable agricultural development. 

Benefits include financial assistance for research projects and capacity building for farmers and agricultural institutions. 

The scheme supports the development of new technologies and practices in agriculture. 

Eligibility criteria typically include agricultural research institutions and universities. 

The application process involves submitting project proposals to the relevant authorities for approval. 

The timeline for project implementation varies depending on the specific project approved. 

The Indian Council of Agricultural Research (ICAR) is the primary implementing agency for NAIP. 

The scheme also emphasizes collaboration between public and private sectors in agricultural research.
