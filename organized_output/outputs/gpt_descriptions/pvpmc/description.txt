The Pradhan Mantri Vaya Vandana Yojana (PMVVY) is a pension scheme launched by the Government of India. 

It aims to provide social security to senior citizens aged 60 years and above. 

The objective is to offer a steady income stream during old age. 

The scheme provides an assured return of 8 percent per annum payable monthly. 

The maximum investment limit is set at INR 15 lakh. 

The scheme has a tenure of 10 years. 

Eligibility is restricted to Indian citizens who are 60 years or older. 

The application process involves filling out the prescribed form available at authorized banks and financial institutions. 

The scheme is implemented by the Life Insurance Corporation of India (LIC). 

The PMVVY is available for subscription until March 31, 2023. 

The scheme allows premature exit under certain conditions. 

The benefits are tax-free under Section 80C of the Income Tax Act. 

The scheme is designed to provide financial security and independence to the elderly population.
