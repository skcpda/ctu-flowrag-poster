The Mukhyamantri Chiranjeevi Yojana (MCBSY) is a health insurance scheme launched by the Government of Rajasthan. 

It aims to provide financial protection to families against health-related expenditures. 

The scheme covers a wide range of medical treatments and hospitalizations. 

It specifically targets families living below the poverty line and those in the economically weaker sections. 

Eligible beneficiaries include families registered under the Socio-Economic Caste Census. 

The application process involves online registration through the official website or designated centers. 

Beneficiaries must provide necessary documents such as identity proof and income certificates. 

The scheme offers an annual health coverage of up to INR 5 lakh per family. 

It covers expenses for secondary and tertiary healthcare services. 

The implementing authority for this scheme is the Department of Medical and Health, Government of Rajasthan. 

The scheme is expected to enhance access to quality healthcare services for the underprivileged. 

It aims to reduce out-of-pocket expenses for medical treatments. 

The scheme is operational throughout the year. 

Beneficiaries can avail of the services at empaneled hospitals across the state.
