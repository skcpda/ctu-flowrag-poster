The Scheme for Implementation of the Scheme for Development of the Scheduled Castes and the Scheduled Tribes (SISDGFT) aims to empower marginalized communities in India. 

The objective is to promote the socio-economic development of Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes. 

The scheme provides financial assistance for various development programs. 

Benefits include skill development training and support for entrepreneurship.
