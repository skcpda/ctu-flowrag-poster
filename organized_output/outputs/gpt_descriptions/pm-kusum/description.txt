PM-KUSUM stands for Pradhan Mantri Kisan Urja Suraksha evam Utthaan Mahabhiyan. 

It was launched in March 2019 by the Government of India. 

The scheme aims to provide financial assistance to farmers for installing solar power plants. 

It promotes the use of solar energy in agriculture. 

The objectives include reducing dependence on conventional energy sources. 

It also aims to enhance farmers' income through the sale of surplus power. 

Benefits include the installation of solar power plants of up to 10 kW capacity for individual farmers. 

Farmers can also set up solar power plants of 500 kW to 2 MW capacity in their fields. 

The scheme provides subsidies for the installation of solar pumps and grid-connected solar power projects. 

Eligibility is limited to farmers who own agricultural land. 

Farmers must also have a valid Kisan Credit Card. 

The application process involves submitting an application to the respective State Nodal Agency. 

Farmers need to provide necessary documents such as land records and identity proof. 

The timeline for installation and subsidy disbursement varies by state. 

The implementing authorities include the Ministry of New and Renewable Energy. 

State Nodal Agencies are responsible for the execution of the scheme at the state level. 

The scheme also aims to promote energy security and sustainability in rural areas.
