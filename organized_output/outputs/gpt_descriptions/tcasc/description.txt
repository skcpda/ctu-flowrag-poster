The TCASC scheme stands for the Tribal Cooperative Agricultural and Rural Development Corporation. 

It aims to promote the economic development of tribal communities in India. 

The main objective is to provide financial assistance and support for agricultural and rural development activities. 

The scheme benefits tribal farmers by providing them with access to credit and resources for agricultural production. 

It also assists in the development of infrastructure and marketing facilities for tribal produce. 

Eligibility is generally limited to individuals and groups belonging to Scheduled Tribes. 

The application process typically involves submitting an application to the respective state tribal welfare department or the TCASC. 

The timeline for processing applications may vary by state and specific project requirements. 

The implementing authorities include the Ministry of Tribal Affairs and various state governments. 

The scheme may also collaborate with cooperative societies and NGOs for effective implementation.
