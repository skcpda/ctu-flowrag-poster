The Usapfos scheme is an initiative by the Government of India. 

It aims to provide financial assistance to farmers. 

The objective is to enhance the income of farmers and ensure their financial stability. 

The scheme offers benefits in the form of direct cash transfers. 

This assistance is intended to support agricultural activities and improve livelihoods.

The application process involves submitting an online application through the official portal. 

Farmers need to provide necessary documentation to verify their status.
