The Dr. P.D. Hegdewar Arogya Yojana is a significant welfare scheme initiated by the Government of India. 

This scheme is named after Dr. P.D. Hegdewar who was a prominent figure in the Indian independence movement and is known for his contributions to society. 

The primary context for the establishment of this scheme is the need for accessible healthcare services for the underprivileged sections of society. 

Recognizing that healthcare is a fundamental right, the Government of India aims to provide financial assistance for medical treatment to those who cannot afford it. 

The objectives of the Dr. P.D. Hegdewar Arogya Yojana are multi-faceted. 

Firstly, the scheme aims to provide financial coverage for hospitalization expenses. 

This includes expenses related to surgeries, treatments, and other necessary medical procedures. 

Secondly, the scheme seeks to reduce the out-of-pocket expenditure on healthcare for economically weaker sections. 

By doing so, it aims to alleviate the financial burden on families that may lead to indebtedness due to medical expenses. 

The benefits and assistance provided under this scheme are substantial. 

Eligible beneficiaries can receive financial coverage for a wide range of medical treatments. 

This includes both inpatient and outpatient services depending on the specific guidelines of the scheme. 

The scheme covers various medical conditions and treatments that are essential for maintaining health and well-being. 

Additionally, the scheme may also provide assistance for pre-existing medical conditions. 

This is crucial as many individuals may require ongoing treatment for chronic illnesses. 

The financial assistance provided can significantly vary based on the type of treatment and the healthcare facility chosen by the beneficiary. 

Eligibility for the Dr. P.D. Hegdewar Arogya Yojana is primarily determined by socio-economic criteria.

This includes individuals from economically weaker sections of society.
