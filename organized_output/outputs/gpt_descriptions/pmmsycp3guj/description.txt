The Pradhan Mantri Matsya Sampada Yojana (PMMSY) is a scheme launched by the Government of India. 

It aims to enhance fish production and promote sustainable fisheries. 

The objective is to increase fish production to 22 million metric tons by 2024-25. 

The scheme also focuses on improving the livelihoods of fishers and fish farmers. 

It provides financial assistance for various activities related to fisheries. 

Benefits include subsidies for fish farming infrastructure and technology. 

Eligible beneficiaries include individual fishers, fish farmers, and cooperatives. 

The application process involves submitting a proposal to the relevant state fisheries department. 

The timeline for fund disbursement varies based on project approval. 

The Ministry of Fisheries Animal Husbandry and Dairying is the implementing authority. 

The scheme also promotes the development of fish markets and value-added products. 

It encourages the adoption of modern fishing techniques and practices. 

The PMMSY aims to create a sustainable and responsible fisheries sector in India.
