The Pradhan Mantri Suraksha Bima Yojana (PMSBY) is a government welfare scheme launched to provide accidental death and disability insurance. 

The scheme aims to offer financial support to individuals in case of accidental death or disability. 

It provides a cover of Rs. 2 lakh for accidental death and Rs. 1 lakh for total and permanent disability. 

The scheme is available to all Indian citizens aged between 18 and 70 years. 

Individuals must have a savings bank account to enroll in the scheme. 

The premium for the scheme is Rs. 12 per annum. 

Enrollment can be done through banks and financial institutions. 

The enrollment period is typically from 1st June to 31st May of the following year. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Finance through various insurance companies. 

The benefits are disbursed directly to the nominee or the insured individual in case of disability. 

The scheme aims to enhance the social security net for the economically weaker sections of society.
