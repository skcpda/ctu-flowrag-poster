The Shrestha M1 SSBCPRS scheme is an initiative by the Government of India. 

It aims to provide support and assistance to students from Scheduled Castes. 

The objective is to enhance the educational and professional opportunities for these students. 

The scheme offers financial assistance for coaching in competitive examinations. 

Students can receive funding for various courses that prepare them for civil services and other competitive exams. 

Eligibility is primarily based on the applicant's caste status as a Scheduled Caste. 

Students must also meet the academic criteria set by the implementing authorities. 

The application process involves submitting an online application through the designated portal. 

Applicants may need to provide documentation to verify their caste status and academic qualifications. 

The timeline for application submission is typically announced by the implementing agency. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment. 

Further details can be accessed through the official government website.
