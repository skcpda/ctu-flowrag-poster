The scheme SNPS-SCPTS stands for the Scheme for National Programme for the Supply of Seeds and Planting Material to Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes.

This scheme aims to enhance the availability of quality seeds and planting materials to Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes.

The objective is to improve agricultural productivity and ensure food security among these marginalized communities.

Benefits include the provision of quality seeds and planting materials at subsidized rates.

Eligible beneficiaries are Scheduled Caste and Scheduled Tribe farmers.

The application process typically involves submitting a form to the local agricultural department or designated authority.

The timeline for assistance may vary based on the agricultural calendar and availability of resources.

Implementing authorities include the Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers' Welfare and state agricultural departments.

The scheme also focuses on capacity building and training for beneficiaries to improve their agricultural practices.
