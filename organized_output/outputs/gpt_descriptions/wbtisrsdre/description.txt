The West Bengal Tribal Integrated Development Scheme for Scheduled Tribes and Rural Development is aimed at improving the socio-economic conditions of tribal communities in West Bengal. 

The objective of the scheme is to provide integrated development for Scheduled Tribes through various welfare measures. 

The benefits include financial assistance for livelihood activities skill development education health care and infrastructure development. 

Eligibility is primarily for Scheduled Tribe individuals and families residing in rural areas of West Bengal. 

The application process typically involves submitting an application to the local block development office or the designated authority. 

The timeline for the disbursement of benefits may vary based on the specific program under the scheme. 

The implementing authority for this scheme is the Department of Tribal Development Government of West Bengal. 

Additional details may include specific programs under the scheme that focus on agriculture animal husbandry and self-employment initiatives.
