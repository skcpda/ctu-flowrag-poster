The Atal Tinkering Labs scheme is an initiative under the Atal Innovation Mission. 

It aims to foster creativity and innovation among students in schools. 

The objective is to cultivate a culture of innovation and entrepreneurship in young minds. 

The scheme provides financial assistance to set up tinkering labs in schools. 

These labs are equipped with tools and equipment to encourage hands-on learning. 

Students can engage in activities related to science technology engineering and mathematics. 

Eligibility includes schools that are recognized by the government. 

Both government and private schools can apply for the scheme. 

The application process involves submitting a proposal to the Atal Innovation Mission. 

The proposal must outline the intended use of funds and the lab's objectives. 

The timeline for fund disbursement is subject to the approval of the proposal. 

The implementing agency for this scheme is the Atal Innovation Mission. 

The scheme promotes collaboration with local industry and educational institutions. 

It aims to create an ecosystem that supports innovation and entrepreneurship. 

The initiative also encourages participation in competitions and exhibitions. 

Schools are expected to provide a conducive environment for experimentation. 

Regular monitoring and evaluation are conducted to assess the impact of the labs.
