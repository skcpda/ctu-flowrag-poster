The Sugamya Bharat Abhiyan is a government initiative aimed at promoting accessibility for persons with disabilities. 

The objective of the scheme is to create an inclusive environment that allows persons with disabilities to participate fully in society. 

The benefits include improved access to public spaces transportation and information technology. 

Eligibility is primarily for persons with disabilities as defined under the Rights of Persons with Disabilities Act 2016. 

The application process typically involves registration with the relevant authorities or agencies that oversee disability services. 

The timeline for implementation varies based on specific projects under the scheme. 

The Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment is the primary implementing authority for this scheme. 

Additional details include a focus on awareness campaigns and training programs to sensitize the public about accessibility issues.
