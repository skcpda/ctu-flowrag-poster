The MRC-DDV scheme stands for the Mahatma Gandhi National Rural Employment Guarantee Act for the Differently Abled and Vulnerable. 

It aims to provide employment opportunities to differently-abled individuals and other vulnerable sections of society in rural areas. 

The primary objective is to enhance the livelihood security of these groups by providing at least 100 days of wage employment in a financial year. 

The scheme offers wage employment to eligible individuals in various rural development projects. 

Benefits include financial assistance through wages for work done and skill development opportunities. 

Eligibility criteria include being a resident of rural areas and being differently-abled or belonging to a vulnerable group. 

The application process typically involves submitting an application to the local Gram Panchayat or relevant authority. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Rural Development at the national level. 

State governments may also have specific guidelines for implementation. 

The frequency of assistance is based on the availability of work and the individual's application. 

The scheme aims to promote social inclusion and economic empowerment for marginalized groups.
