The Dasmari Panchayat Bhatta OBC Welfare Board Scheme is an initiative by the Government of India aimed at providing financial assistance and support to the Other Backward Classes (OBC) community.

This scheme is designed to uplift the socio-economic status of OBC individuals and families who are often marginalized and face various challenges in accessing basic resources and opportunities.

The primary objective of the Dasmari Panchayat Bhatta OBC Welfare Board Scheme is to ensure that the OBC community receives adequate support for their educational and economic development.

The scheme aims to provide financial assistance for various purposes such as education, skill development, and entrepreneurship.

By focusing on these areas, the scheme seeks to empower OBC individuals and help them achieve a better quality of life.

The benefits under this scheme include financial aid for educational expenses such as tuition fees, books, and other necessary materials.

Additionally, the scheme provides support for vocational training and skill development programs to enhance employability among OBC youth.

Entrepreneurial support is also a significant aspect of the scheme, as it aims to encourage self-employment and small business initiatives within the OBC community.
