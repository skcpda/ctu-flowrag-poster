The Pradhan Mantri Shram Yogi Maandhan (PM-SYM) scheme is designed to provide social security to workers in the unorganized sector. 

The objective of the scheme is to provide a monthly pension of Rs. 3000 to workers after they reach the age of 60. 

The benefits include a pension for life to the subscriber and their spouse after the subscriber's death. 

Eligibility criteria include being an unorganized sector worker aged between 18 to 40 years. 

The applicant must have a monthly income of less than Rs. 15000. 

The application process involves filling out a registration form available at Common Service Centers or online through the official portal. 

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Labour and Employment. 

The pension is paid monthly after the subscriber attains the age of 60. 

The scheme also allows for the option of exiting the scheme with a refund of contributions made.
