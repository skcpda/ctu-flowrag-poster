The Mukhyamantri Kanya Yojana (MKY) is a welfare scheme initiated by the Government of India. 

It aims to promote the education and empowerment of girls. 

The scheme provides financial assistance to families for the education of their daughters. 

Benefits include monetary support for educational expenses at various levels. 

Eligibility criteria typically include being a resident of the state and having a girl child. 

The application process usually involves submitting relevant documents to the local government office or online portal. 

The timeline for disbursement of funds varies based on the specific educational milestones achieved by the girl child. 

The scheme is implemented by the respective state government departments responsible for women and child development. 

Additional details may include specific financial amounts and educational levels supported, which can vary by state.
