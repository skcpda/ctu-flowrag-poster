The Tamil Nadu Skill Development Corporation (TNSDC) operates the Tamil Nadu Skill Development Scheme (TNSCR). 

This scheme aims to enhance the employability of youth in Tamil Nadu. 

It focuses on providing skill training in various sectors. 

The objective is to bridge the skill gap in the job market. 

Benefits include free skill training programs. 

Participants receive certification upon successful completion of training. 

The scheme also facilitates job placements for trained candidates. 

Eligibility criteria include being a resident of Tamil Nadu. 

Applicants must be between the ages of 18 and 35. 

The application process involves online registration through the TNSDC website. 

Candidates may also apply through designated training centers. 

The training programs are conducted throughout the year. 

Implementing authorities include the Tamil Nadu Skill Development Corporation. 

The scheme collaborates with various training partners and industries. 

Additional details include a focus on sectors such as manufacturing and services. 

The scheme aims to empower women and marginalized communities through skill development.
