# Scholarship for C.G. Domicile Students Pursuing Engineering Courses Outside Chhattisgarh State

**Short Title:** SFSPBBC

**Scheme Brief Description:** The scheme supports students in their first year and fills vacant awards in subsequent years (II/III/IV). Key benefits include financial aid to cover educational expenses and encouragement for students to pursue quality education. Eligible applicants must be Chhattisgarh domicile.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Education & Learning; Sector: Scholarships and student finance

**Where it applies:** Chhattisgarh; State/ UT

**Detailed Description:** The scheme &quot;Scholarship for C.G. Domicile Students Pursuing Engineering Courses Outside Chhattisgarh State&quot; by the Directorate of Technical Education, Chhattisgarh Government, aims to provide financial assistance to students domiciled in Chhattisgarh who are pursuing B.E./B.Tech courses in recognised engineering colleges outside the state. The scheme supports students in their first year and fills vacant awards in subsequent years (II/III/IV). Key benefits include financial aid to cover educational expenses and encouragement for students to pursue quality education beyond state borders. Eligible applicants must be Chhattisgarh domicile holders and enrolled in AICTE-approved institutions.

**Benefits / Assistance:** Financial assistance of ₹2000/- month to cover educational expenses.

**Application Process:** **Step 1:** The interested applicant should take print of the [prescribed format](https://cgdteraipur.cgstate.gov.in/sites/default/files/admissioninfo0310.pdf) of the application form. **Step 2:** In the application form, fill in all the mandatory fields, paste the passport-sized photograph (signed across, if required), and attach copies of all the mandatory documents (self-attest, if required). **Step 3:** Submit the duly filled and signed application form along with the documents, within the prescribed period (if any), to - ***The Director, Directorate of Technical Education, Chhattisgarh*** ***Indrawati Bhavan, Block-3, 3rd/4th Floor, Nawa Raipur, Atal Nagar, Dist..- Raipur - 492002*** ***Phone: 0771-2331331, 2221376 | Fax: 0771-2253620 | Website: http://www.egdleraipur.ac.in*** _*Applicants are advised to save a copy of the submitted application and acknowledgement for future reference._ _*Ensure that the application is submitted within the prescribed period mentioned in the scheme guidelines._  **Post-Application Processes** The Directorate will verify the submitted documents and approve eligible applications. The scholarship amount will be disbursed to the applicant’s bank account upon approval.
