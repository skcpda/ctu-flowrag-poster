# Kaidi Sahay Yojana

**Short Title:** KSYGSW

**Scheme Brief Description:** The "Kaidi Sahay Yojana" by the Social Justice and Empowerment Department, Gujarat, provides financial assistance of ₹25,000/- to the family of a prisoner who is the sole or main earner, to support them through self-employment.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Financial assistance

**Where it applies:** Gujarat; State/ UT

**Detailed Description:** The Kaidi Sahay Yojana, initiated by the Social Justice and Empowerment Department, Gujarat, aims to provide financial relief to families of prisoners who are undergoing sentences in jails and are the main or sole earners of their households. Under this scheme, a one-time financial assistance of ₹25,000/- is granted to the prisoner’s family, ensuring that they can sustain themselves through self-employment during the imprisonment period.

**Benefits / Assistance:** - **Financial Assistance:** A one-time assistance of ₹25,000/-.

**Application Process:** **Step 1:** Visit the District Jail/ Sub Jail/ District Social Defense Office and obtain the [application form](https://mariyojana.gujarat.gov.in/ViewForm.aspx). **Step 2:** Fill out the form with all required details and attach the necessary documents (Self-attested, if required). **Step 3:** Submit the completed application to the office of the District Welfare Officer/ Sub Jail.
