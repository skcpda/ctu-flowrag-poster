# Mukhyamantri Aanchal Amrit Yojana

**Short Title:** MAAYUK

**Scheme Brief Description:** The scheme aims to improve the health of children between 3 to 6 years of age by providing them fortified and flavored milk containing Vitamins A and D, four days a week, at Anganwadi Centres.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Health & Wellness; Sector: Childcare

**Where it applies:** Uttarakhand; State/ UT

**Detailed Description:** The scheme &amp;quot;Mukhyamantri Anchal Amrit Yojana&amp;quot; was launched by the Department of Women Empowerment &amp;amp; Child Development, Government of Uttarakhand. The scheme aims to improve the health of children between 3 to 6 years of age by providing them fortified and flavored milk containing Vitamins A and D, four days a week, at Anganwadi Centres. 

**Benefits / Assistance:** Under this scheme, children between 3 to 6 years of age are provided fortified and flavored milk enriched with Vitamins A and D, four days a week. 

**Application Process:** Children who go to/are registered at the Anganwadi Centre receive benefits directly at the centre. 
Mode: Offline
