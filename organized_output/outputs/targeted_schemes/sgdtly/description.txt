# 100% State Government Divyangjan Term Loan Yojana

**Short Title:** SGDTLY

**Scheme Brief Description:** The scheme provides provide financial support to native residents of Gujarat who have a disability of 40% or more. The eligible individuals can avail of a term loan ranging from ₹50,000/- to ₹1,00,000/- at a concessional interest rate of 6% per annum for a repayment period of five years.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Loan

**Where it applies:** Gujarat; State/ UT

**Detailed Description:** The scheme &amp;quot;100% State Government Divyangjan Term Loan Yojana&amp;quot; was launched by the Social Justice and Empowerment Department, Government of Gujarat, to provide financial support to native residents of Gujarat who have a disability of 40% or more. The eligible individuals can avail of a term loan ranging from ₹50,000/- to ₹1,00,000/- at a concessional interest rate of 6% per annum for a repayment period of five years. This initiative empowers PwDs to become self-reliant by supporting their livelihood and business ventures.

**Benefits / Assistance:** Loans ranging from ₹50,000/- to ₹1,00,000/- are disbursed at an annual rate of 6% for a period of 5 years.

You will be taken to the Registration Form. **Step 3:** In the form, provide all the mandatory fields, fill in the captcha code, and click &quot;Submit&quot; to complete the registration process. A popup will appear acknowledging successful registration and displaying your User ID for Login.  > **Application** **Step 1:** Visit the Official Website of [eSamajkalyan](https://esamajkalyan.gujarat.gov.in/index.aspx). **Step 2:** In the &quot;Citizen Login&quot; section provided in the right pane of the home page, provide your User ID and Password, fill in the Captcha Code, then click &quot;Login&quot;. **Step 3:** In the ribbon provided at the top of the website, click the &quot;Corporation&quot; menu, then click &quot;Gujarat State Divyangjan Finance and Development Corporation&quot;. A list of schemes will be displayed. Select the scheme you intend to apply for. **Step 4:** You will be taken to the online application form of the scheme. In the form, provide all the mandatory fields and upload all the mandatory documents in the specified format and size. **Step 5:** Carefully review all the information provided and the documents uploaded. Make corrections, if any. **Step 6:** Click &quot;Submit&quot; to submit your application. You&#39;ll receive a confirmation message. Note down your Application Number for future reference. You can also take a printout of the submitted application form.  > **Track Application Status** **Step 1:** Visit the Official Website of [eSamajkalyan](https://esamajkalyan.gujarat.gov.in/index.aspx). **Step 2:** Navigate to the bottom of the home page, and click &quot;[Your Application Status](https://esamajkalyan.gujarat.gov.in/index.aspx)&quot;. **Step 3:** In the next page, provide your Application Number, Date of Birth, and click &quot;View Status&quot;. The current status of your application will be displayed.
