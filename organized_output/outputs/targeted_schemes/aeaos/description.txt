# Assistance for Exhibitions Abroad and Out of State

**Short Title:** AEAOS

**Scheme Brief Description:** The scheme aims to promote fine arts by providing financial assistance of up to ₹1,00,000 to artists from Gujarat for organizing art exhibitions abroad and in other states of India.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Sports & Culture; Sector: Culture

**Where it applies:** Gujarat; State/ UT

**Detailed Description:** The scheme &amp;quot;Assistance for Exhibitions Abroad and Out of State&amp;quot; by the Sports, Youth and Cultural Activities Department, Government of Gujarat aims to encourage and promote artists originally from Gujarat in the field of fine arts—such as painting, sculpture, graphic arts, and photography—by offering financial assistance to organize exhibitions abroad and outside the state. The scheme ensures support for artists with a record of conducting prior solo or group exhibitions, helping them to gain national and international exposure through structured financial aid. The scheme is implemented by the Gujarat State Fine Arts Academy. 

**Benefits / Assistance:** - **Financial Assistance:** Up to ₹1,00,000/- for organizing exhibitions abroad. **Application Process:** **Step 1:** The interested applicant should download the prescribed format of the [application form](https://lalitkalaacademy.gujarat.gov.in/writereaddata/Portal/Magazine/Document/1_12_3_5-1_12_3_Form_1.pdf) for the &amp;quot;Publication Support Scheme for Fine Arts&amp;quot; scheme. **Step 2:** In the application form, provide all the mandatory details, including career details in the field of art, photographs, or color slides of work (self-attested, if required). **Step 3:** Submit the duly filled and signed application form along with the supporting documents to the Gujarat State Fine Arts Academy. **Step 4:** Request a receipt or acknowledgement from the concerned authority at the Academy office to whom the application has been submitted. **Note:** Applications for book publication are invited every year through advertisement in daily newspapers. &amp;gt; **Contact Us:** &amp;gt; Gujarat State Academy of Fine Arts &amp;gt; Ravi Shankar Rawal Kala Bhavan, &amp;gt; Opposite Bhaikaka Bhavan, &amp;gt; Law Garden, Ellisbridge, &amp;gt; Ahmedabad - 380006. &amp;gt; 079-26425562 &amp;gt; 079-26562558
