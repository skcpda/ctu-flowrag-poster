# Gujarat Textile Policy: Payroll Assistance (Fiscal Incentives to Labour Intensive Unit)

**Short Title:** GTCPALIU

**Scheme Brief Description:** The "Gujarat Textile Policy" introduced by the Industries and Mines Department aims to create a vibrant Textile Sector ecosystem and generate enormous employment opportunities in the State.

**Launch Date:** 2024-10-01

**Who is this for:** Business Entity, Industries; Category: Business & Entrepreneurship; Sector: Setting up / start-up / entrepreneurship

**Where it applies:** Gujarat; State/ UT

**Detailed Description:** The &amp;quot;Gujarat Textile Policy&amp;quot; is an umbrella scheme introduced by the Industries and Mines Department, Gujarat. It aimed at augmenting investments in the textile sector and strengthening the textile value chain across each sub-sector, while also focusing on strengthening the garments and apparel as well as technical textiles industry. Effective from October 1st, 2024 to September 29th, 2029, this initiative focuses on reducing the carbon footprint and promoting green growth, thereby making the sector globally competitive and environmentally sustainable. The component &amp;quot;Payroll Assistance (Fiscal Incentives to Labour Intensive Unit)&amp;quot; provides financial support to industrial units by offering monetary assistance for the wages of workers. This scheme aims to promote employment and encourage industries in Gujarat, particularly in sectors like Garments, Apparel, Made-ups, and Technical Textiles, including Composite Units.

**Benefits / Assistance:**  **Activity****Payroll Assistance**Garment, Apparel&amp;amp;amp;amp; Made-ups (including Composite Unit) ₹5,000/- per Female Worker &amp;amp;amp;amp; ₹4,000/- per Male Worker per month for a period of 10 yearsTechnical Textile (including Composite Unit) ₹3,000/- per Female Worker &amp;amp;amp;amp; ₹2,000 per Male Worker per month for a period of 10 years
