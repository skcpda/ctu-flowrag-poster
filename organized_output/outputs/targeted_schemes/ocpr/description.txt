# Organisation of Cultural Programs

**Short Title:** OCPS

**Scheme Brief Description:** The scheme aims to promote regional and national cultural programs. It offers financial assistance and travel allowances to listed cultural artists and troupes.

**Who is this for:** Artists; Category: Sports & Culture; Sector: Culture

**Where it applies:** Uttarakhand; State/ UT

**Detailed Description:** The scheme &quot;Organisation of Cultural Programs&quot; by the Department of Culture and Endowments, Govt of Uttarakhand, aims to promote regional and national cultural events through listed cultural troupes and artists. Through this scheme, honorarium and travel allowances are provided to cultural troupes/artists for participating in various fairs, festivals, and national events both within and outside the state. The scheme is implemented by the Directorate of Culture, Government of Uttarakhand. Cultural groups are selected through auditions and empanelment based on qualifications and domicile.

**Benefits / Assistance:** 1. Group Leader - Honorarium: ₹1,000/- 1. Other Artists Honorarium: ₹800/- 1. Travel Allowance: ₹500/- 1. Other Artists Travel Allowance: ₹400/- 1. A-Grade Folk Singers: ₹7,500/- 1. B-Grade Folk Singers: ₹4,250/- 1. Additional Travel Allowance: ₹300/- > Bus Fare (Ordinary Class) and Second-Class Train Fare are reimbursed  > **Mode of Disbursement:** - Direct payment to artists/troupes by the department > **Frequency of Disbursement:** - Disbursed per event/program participation

**Exclusions / Not eligible:** - Artists or troupes that are not listed or empaneled by the department will be considered ineligible.

**Application Process:** **1. Audition Process:** - The Directorate of Culture conducts auditions from time to time to enlist cultural troupes and artists in the department&#39;s panel. **2. Required Documents:** To be selected, the interested institution or artist must submit the following: - Registration Certificate of the institution (for troupes). - Bio-data of the artist or troupe. - Details and number of members in the troupe. - Permanent Residence Certificate or Domicile Certificate proving that the applicant is a permanent resident of Uttarakhand. **3. Listing in the Department:** - Only those who qualify through the audition and fulfil the documentation requirements are listed as empaneled cultural artists or groups. **4. Participation in Events:** - Once listed, the artists/troupes may be invited by the department to participate in state-level, national, or regional cultural programs, where they receive honorarium and travel reimbursement.
Mode: Offline
