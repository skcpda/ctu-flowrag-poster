# Kishori Balika Yojana (Madhya Pradesh)

**Short Title:** KBY-MP

**Scheme Brief Description:** The scheme "Kishori Balika Yojana" by the Department of Women and Child Development, Government of Madhya Pradesh aims to provide vocational, health, and hygiene training to adolescent girls. Through this scheme, training and material assistance are provided to the girls.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Women and Child; Sector: Menstrual Hygiene

**Where it applies:** Madhya Pradesh; State/ UT

**Detailed Description:** The scheme &quot;Kishori Balika Yojana&quot; by the Department of Women and Child Development, Government of Madhya Pradesh aims to provide vocational, health, and hygiene training to adolescent girls. Through this scheme, girls from each Anganwadi center are selected and trained by master trainers, who in turn provide training at the Gram Panchayat level. The scheme is implemented by Anganwadi Workers and includes Two-day Master Trainer Training Programs provided at monthly intervals.

**Benefits / Assistance:** - Each Anganwadi center selects 2 adolescent girls, 1 Anganwadi worker, and 1 Auxiliary Nurse Midwife (ANM) for a two-day master trainer training at monthly intervals. - Master trainers provide vocational, health, and hygiene training at the Gram Panchayat level. - A two-day master trainer training program is provided at monthly intervals.

**Application Process:** The interested applicants should visit the nearest Anganwadi Center for registration, along with the necessary documents.
Mode: Offline
