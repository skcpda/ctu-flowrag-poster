# Saraswati Vidya Yojana: Free Distribution of Bicycles

**Short Title:** SVYFDB

**Scheme Brief Description:** “Free Distribution of Bicycles'' under “Saraswati Vidya Yojana”, implemented by the Directorate of Education, U.T. Administration Of DNH & DD, aims to provide bicycles free of cost to all the girl students, studying in class VIII(8) in the Govt. & Govt. aided schools/ Institutes.

**Launch Date:** 2021-02-25

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Education & Learning; Sector: Resources for schools

**Where it applies:** Dadra & Nagar Haveli and Daman & Diu; State/ UT

**Detailed Description:** “Free Distribution of Bicycles&amp;#39;&amp;#39; under the “Saraswati Vidya Yojana”has been implemented by the Directorate of Education, U.T. Administration Of DNH &amp;amp; DD. Under this scheme, bicycles are provided free of cost to all the girl students, studying in class VIII(8) each year in the Govt. &amp;amp; Govt. aided schools/ Institutes of the U.T. Administration. &gt; **Objective of the &amp;quot;Saraswati Vidya Yojana”:** The UT Administration of Dadra &amp;amp; Nagar Haveli and Daman &amp;amp; Diu has launched “Saraswati Vidya Yojana” on 25th February, 2021, for the girl students of UT of Dadra &amp;amp; Nagar Haveli and Daman &amp;amp; Diu. The scheme aims to encourage girl students to continue education keeping in view the aim of:- “Beti Bachao Beti Padhao”. &gt; **Objective of the &amp;quot;Free Distribution of Bicycles&amp;quot; Sub-scheme:** The objective of the scheme is to retain the girl students at Secondary &amp;amp; Higher Secondary stage and thereby decreasing the Dropout of the girl students by providing Bicycles to the girl students of class VIII to commute to school.

**Benefits / Assistance:** - Bicycles will be provided to all the girl students.

**Exclusions / Not eligible:** - The girl students once availed the benefit will not be provided with the same in any of the subsequent year.

**Application Process:** - The Head Master/ In-charge Head Master will provide the number of enrolment of Girls in Std. VIII in Government and Government Aided Schools to the District Education Office. **Note:** - The girl student does not required to make any application individually. - Bicycles will be provided to schools at the time of commencement of the New Academic Year as per the requirement of the respective schools. &gt; **Contact for Grievance Redressal:** **Dadra and Nagar Haveli****Daman****Diu**District Education Office (Silvassa), PWD Campus, Silvassa, Pin: 396230, Ph: 0260-2642098, Email: doe-dnh@nic.inDistrict Education (Daman) Fort Area, Daman, Pin: 396220, Ph: 0260 2231126, Email: diredu-dd @nic.inDistrict Education Office (Diu), Collectorate, Diu, Pin: 362520, Ph: 02875-252199, Email: edn-diu@yahoo.in
