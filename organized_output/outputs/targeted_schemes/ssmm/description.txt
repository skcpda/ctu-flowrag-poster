# Swavalambi/Sanchari Marata Malige

**Short Title:** SSMM

**Scheme Brief Description:** The "Swavalambi/Sanchari Marata Malige" aims to assist SC leather artisans in establishing their own showrooms or mobile vans to sell their products directly to consumers. Financial assistance is provided in the form of a subsidy depending on the location of the business.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Business & Entrepreneurship; Sector: Setting up / start-up / entrepreneurship

**Where it applies:** Karnataka; State/ UT

**Detailed Description:** The &quot;Swavalambi/Sanchari Marata Malige&quot; was implemented by Karnataka Leather Industries Development Corporation Ltd, Department of Social Welfare, Govt of Karnataka. The scheme aims to assist SC leather artisans in establishing their own showrooms or mobile vans to sell their products directly to consumers. Financial assistance is provided in the form of a subsidy depending on the location of the business.

**Benefits / Assistance:** **Financial assistance ranging from ₹2,00,000/- to ₹5,00,000/- as a subsidy for opening a showroom or mobile van:** - Taluk Level: ₹2,00,000/- - Taluk &amp; District Centers: ₹3,00,000/- - Mahanagara Palike Limits:**** ₹4,00,000/-- BBMP Limits:**** ₹5,00,000/-

**Application Process:** > **Registration Process:** **Step-01:** Visit the official [Seva Sindhu Portal](https://sevasindhu.karnataka.gov.in/Sevasindhu/English). **Step-02:** Choose &quot;New Users Register Here&quot; and fill in your Aadhaar Number and the Captcha code. **Step-03:** An OTP will be sent to your Aadhaar-linked mobile number. Enter the OTP to continue. **Step-04:** Once your DigiLocker account is created, click &quot;Allow&quot; to proceed. **Step-05:** Fill out your email ID, mobile number, create a password, and enter the captcha, then click &quot;Submit&quot;. **Step-06:** You will receive OTPs on both your email and mobile number. Enter both OTPs and select &quot;Validate&quot;. **Step-07:** Once validated, a confirmation message will indicate your registration is successful.  > **Applying for a Scheme:** **Step-01:** Go to [Seva Sindhu Portal](https://sevasindhu.karnataka.gov.in/Sevasindhu/English) and select &quot;Department &amp; Services&quot;. **Step-02:** Select department &quot;Social Welfare Department&quot; and click on the scheme &quot;Swavalambi/Sanchari Marata Malige&quot; **Step-03:** Click &quot;Apply Online&quot; and enter your registered email ID/mobile number and the OTP. **Step-04:** Select the scheme and provide your personal, address, and banking details as required. **Step-05:** Once submitted, you will receive an Application ID. You can use this ID to track your application status on the portal.
Mode: Online

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: Karnataka Leather Industries Development Corporation Ltd; Nodal Department: Social Welfare Department
