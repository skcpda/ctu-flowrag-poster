# Dr.Babasaheb Ambedkar Udyog Uday Yojana: Assistance in Implementation of Information and Communication Technology (ICT)

**Short Title:** BAUUYICT

**Scheme Brief Description:** The scheme "Dr.Babasaheb Ambedkar Udyog Uday Yojana" aims to support SC/ST entrepreneurs and strengthen MSMEs, making them globally competitive.

**Launch Date:** 2020-08-07

**Who is this for:** Business Entity, Industries; Category: Business & Entrepreneurship; Sector: Setting up / start-up / entrepreneurship

**Where it applies:** Gujarat; State/ UT

**Detailed Description:** The scheme &amp;quot;Dr.Babasaheb Ambedkar Udyog Uday Yojana&amp;quot; is an umbrella scheme launched by the Industries and Mines Department, Gujarat. It aims to empower the state&amp;#39;s dynamic MSME sector, which significantly contributes to employment, GDP, and exports. Effective from August 7th, 2020 to August 6th, 2025, this initiative supports SC/ST entrepreneurs and strengthens MSMEs, making them globally competitive. The component &amp;quot;Assistance in Implementation of Information and Communication Technology (ICT)&amp;quot; offers financial assistance to enterprises for the implementation of Information and Communication Technology (ICT) solutions, including hardware for cloud computing and networking.

**Benefits / Assistance:** - **Financial Assistance:** Covers up to 75% of the capital expenditure for ICT facilities maximum upto ₹5,00,000/-

**Application Process:** > **Registration Process:** **Step-1:** Visit the [Investor Facilitation Portal (IFP)](https://ifp.gujarat.gov.in/DIGIGOV/) portal. Click on &quot;New Investor Registration&quot; to access the registration form. **Step-2:** Fill in the Registration Form. Click on &quot;Submit&quot;. **Step-3:** Verify the E-mail ID using the link sent on your registered E-mail ID. **Step-4:** After verification, you will be registered successfully.  > **Post-Registration Process:** **Step-1:** Visit the [Investor Facilitation Portal (IFP)](https://ifp.gujarat.gov.in/DIGIGOV/) portal. Click on &quot;Login&quot;. **Step-2:** Enter the email address and password you used during registration. **Step-3:** Click the &quot;Login&quot; button to access your account. **Step-4:** Now fill in the required details and mandatory documents about your business to complete the application process.  > **Helpdesk:** [**Contact** ](https://ifp.gujarat.gov.in/DIGIGOV/IFP-pages/contact.jsp?pagedisp=static)
Mode: Online
