# Award Scheme to Encourage Entrepreneurs

**Short Title:** ASTEE

**Scheme Brief Description:** The scheme aims to encourage and recognize the exceptional craftsmanship and weaving skills of registered artisans and weavers in the state. It provides awards at both the state and district levels to outstanding performers in the handicrafts and handloom (cotton and woolen textile) sectors.

**Who is this for:** Individual, Business Entity, NGO, Registered Societies; Category: Business & Entrepreneurship

**Where it applies:** Uttarakhand; State/ UT

**Detailed Description:** The scheme &amp;quot;Award Scheme to Encourage Entrepreneurs&amp;quot; is implemented by the Department of Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises, Government of Uttarakhand. The scheme aims to encourage and recognize the exceptional craftsmanship and weaving skills of registered artisans and weavers in the state. It provides awards at both the state and district levels to outstanding performers in the handicrafts and handloom (cotton and woolen textile) sectors. The scheme promotes traditional arts and incentivizes excellence in the handicraft and textile industries.

**Application Process:** > **Application Process:** **Step 1:** The interested applicant should visit (during office hours) the District Industries Centre (DIC) and request a hard copy of the prescribed format of the application form from the staff exclusively entrusted to issue and collect filled-in applications. **OR** The interested applicant downloads the application form from the [official website](https://doi.uk.gov.in/). **Step 2:** In the application form, fill in all the mandatory fields and attach copies of all the mandatory documents (self-attested, if required). **Step 3:** Submit the duly filled and signed application form along with the documents to the General Manager, District Industries Centre. **Note:** Submit the application in three copies with a ₹50 demand draft to the General Manager, District Industries Center, before the deadline. &gt; **Selection Process:** **District-Level Awards:** - Selected by the District Selection Committee, chaired by the District Magistrate or Chief Development Officer. **State-Level Awards:** - Selected by the State Selection Committee, chaired by the Director of Industries Department. **Note:** Shortlisted candidates are finalized based on product quality, innovation, and adherence to traditional standards.
