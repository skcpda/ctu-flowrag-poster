# Free Travel in Uttarakhand Transport Corporation Buses to Widowed Daughters-in-Law of Freedom Fighters

**Short Title:** FTUKTCWDFF

**Scheme Brief Description:** The scheme aims to provide free travel facilities in Uttarakhand Transport Corporation (UTC) buses to the widowed daughters-in-law of freedom fighters. Beneficiaries under this scheme are entitled to avail complimentary travel across all UTC bus services within the state.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Travel & Tourism

**Where it applies:** Uttarakhand; State/ UT

**Detailed Description:** The &amp;quot;Free Travel in Uttarakhand Transport Corporation Buses to Widowed Daughters-in-Law of Freedom Fighters&amp;quot; scheme is implemented by the Police Department under the Department of Home, Government of Uttarakhand. The scheme aims to provide free travel facilities in Uttarakhand Transport Corporation buses to the widowed daughters-in-law of freedom fighters. 

**Benefits / Assistance:** Under this scheme, widowed daughters-in-law of freedom fighters are entitled to free travel in Uttarakhand Transport Corporation (UTC) buses across the state. 

**Application Process:** - To obtain an identity card, the widowed daughter-in-law of a freedom fighter must contact the concerned District Magistrate&amp;#39;s office, inquire about the required documents, and submit the application. - The District Magistrate&amp;#39;s office will then issue her an identity card, on the basis of which she can travel free of cost in the buses of the Uttarakhand Transport Corporation.
