# Health and Maternity Cover for the Construction Workers

**Short Title:** HAMCFTCW

**Scheme Brief Description:** The "Health and Maternity Cover for the Construction Workers'' by the BOCW Welfare Board, Labour Department, U.T. Administration of DNH & DD, is a welfare scheme that provides maternity benefits in the form of financial assistance to the eligible registered women members of the board.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Financial assistance

**Where it applies:** Dadra & Nagar Haveli and Daman & Diu; State/ UT

**Detailed Description:** The &amp;quot;Health and Maternity Cover for the Construction Workers&amp;quot; by the Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board, Labour Department, U.T. Administration of Dadra and Nagar Haveli and Daman and Diu, is a welfare scheme for the registered workers of the board. Under this scheme, registered beneficiaries of the board shall be provided maternity benefits in the form of financial assistance during the period of maternity.

**Benefits / Assistance:** &gt; ***Cash Benefits:*** - **For Male Members:** ₹30,000/- per delivery. - **For Female Members:** ₹40,000/- per delivery. &gt; **Other Benefits:** - **Enrollment Under PM-JAY:** The beneficiaries shall be enrolled under the Pradhan Mantri Jan Arogya Yojna i.e “Ayushman Bharat”. **Note:** The benefit shall not be allowed for more than two deliveries.

**Application Process:** &gt; **Registration Process as a Building/ Construction Worker:** **Step 1:** Application for registration is to be made in the prescribed form and is to be accompanied by prescribed documents and a fee of not more than ₹50/-. **Step 2:** Application for registration has to be made to the officer authorized by the Board on this behalf. &gt; **Application Process of the Welfare Scheme:** **Step 1:** Any registered worker can make an application in the prescribed [Form-II](https://www.daman.nic.in/websites/Printing-press-Daman/documents/2020/Extraordinary/Extra%20Ordinary%20No.%2029.pdf) (Refer Page No: 7) along with the mandatory documents (self attested, if required). **Step 2:** Application shall be submitted to the Member Secretary (BOCWWB) for this benefit. &gt; **Check Beneficiary Details:** Visit the [official website](https://bocwddd.in/welfare-schemes/dnh/maternity-benefit) and enter the “BOCW Registration No.” of the concerned beneficiary in the Search box. **Helpdesk:** BOCW Department: EMAIL ID: labour-dnh@nic.in PHONE NUMBER : 0260 - 2642374 ADDRESS: Old Circuit House Building, Opposite Bal Bhawan, Silvassa, Dadra and Nagar Haveli Pin:- 396230
