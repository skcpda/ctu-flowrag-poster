# Educational Loan Scheme (GMFDC)

**Short Title:** ELSGMFDC

**Scheme Brief Description:** The scheme aims to provide financial assistance for technical and non-technical education that creates employment or job opportunities for beneficiaries. Through this scheme, loans are provided to eligible students from minority communities for studies within India and abroad.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Education & Learning

**Where it applies:** Gujarat; State/ UT

**Detailed Description:** The &amp;quot;Educational Loan Scheme&amp;quot; is implemented by the Gujarat Minorities Finance and Development Corporation (GMFDC) under the Social Justice and Empowerment Department, Government of Gujarat. The scheme aims to provide financial assistance in the form of loans for all types of education, including various technical and non-technical courses, that help beneficiaries gain employment or enhance their job prospects. Under this scheme, low-interest loans are offered to eligible students from minority communities to pursue graduate and postgraduate courses in technical, vocational, professional, and medical fields, either in India or abroad. 

**Benefits / Assistance:** 1. For studies within India, a maximum loan of ₹1,50,000/- per year and up to ₹7,50,000/- for a five-year course can be obtained. 1. For medical courses within India, ₹2,50,000/- for 1 year and ₹12,50,000/- for 5 years can be obtained. 1. For abroad studies, a maximum loan of ₹3,00,000/- for 1 year and ₹15,00,000/- for 5 years is provided. 1. The annual interest rate on this loan is 3%. **Note 1:** If the applicant’s annual family income is up to ₹98,000 in rural areas or ₹1,20,000 in urban areas, the loan is provided at a concessional interest rate of 3% per annum. **Note 2:** According to the National Corporation&#39;s Credit Line-2, for families with an income of ₹1,20,000/- up to ₹8,00,000, the interest rate is 5% for female students and 8% for male students. **Note 3:** The loan and interest amount is to be repaid in 60 equal monthly installments within five years from the date of completion of studies or from the date of getting a job, whichever is earlier. **Note 4:** Penalty interest at the rate of 2% is payable on delayed payments.

A pop-up will appear acknowledging successful registration and displaying your User ID for Login. > **Application** **Step 1:** Visit the Gujarat Minority Finance and Development Corporation [official website](https://gmfdconline.gujarat.gov.in/). **Step 2:** Log in using your registered credentials. Select the Education Loan Scheme from the available schemes. **Step 3:** Fill in personal details, income information, business details, and bank account details. **Step 4:**[ ](https://gmfdconline.gujarat.gov.in/)Upload required documents: Identity Proof, Address Proof, Income Certificate, Minority Certificate, Educational Qualification Certificate, and Bank Passbook. **Step 5:** You will be taken to the online application form of the scheme. In the form, provide all the mandatory fields and upload all the mandatory documents in the specified format and size. **Step 6:** Carefully review all the information provided and the documents uploaded. Make corrections, if any. **Step 7:** Click &quot;Submit&quot; to submit your application. You&#39;ll receive a confirmation message. Note down your Application Number for future reference. You can also take a printout of the submitted application form. > **Track Application Status** **Step 1:** Visit the Gujarat Minority Finance and Development Corporation [official website](https://gmfdconline.gujarat.gov.in/). **Step 2:** Navigate to the bottom of the home page, and click &quot;Your Application Status&quot;. **Step 3:** On the next page, provide your Application Number, Date of Birth, and click &quot;View Status&quot;. The current status of your application will be displayed. > **Contact:** > For any assistance, contact 079-23253843, 23254583, 23253757 or email md-gmfdcl@gujarat.gov.in.
