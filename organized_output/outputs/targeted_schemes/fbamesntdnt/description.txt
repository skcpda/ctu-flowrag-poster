# Food Bill Assistance for Medical & Engineering Students of Nomadic Tribes & Denotified Tribes

**Short Title:** FBAMESNTDNT

**Scheme Brief Description:** The scheme supports students from Nomadic Tribes and Denotified Tribes pursuing full-time Medical or Engineering courses in Gujarat by reducing their living expenses. Eligible students receive ₹1,500/- per month for up to 10 months each academic year to cover mess or canteen expenses.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Education & Learning; Sector: Scholarships and student finance

**Where it applies:** Gujarat; State/ UT

**Detailed Description:** The “Food Bill Assistance for Medical and Engineering Students of Nomadic Tribes and Denotified Tribes” scheme was launched by the Social Justice and Empowerment Department, Government of Gujarat. The scheme aims to reduce the burden of living expenses for students belonging to the Nomadic Tribes and Denotified Tribes (NTDNT) communities who are pursuing full-time Medical or Engineering courses within the state. Under this scheme, eligible students receive a monthly food allowance of ₹1,500/- for up to 10 months each academic year to help cover mess or canteen expenses. 

**Benefits / Assistance:** - Monthly assistance up to ₹1,500/- for 10 months per academic year.
