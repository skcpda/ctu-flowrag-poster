# Financial Assistance to Permanent Residents of Uttarakhand for Religious Pilgrimages

**Short Title:** FAPRURP

**Scheme Brief Description:** The scheme aims to promote religious tourism by supporting eligible residents who complete the Kailash Mansarovar Yatra. Financial assistance is provided upon completion.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Travel & Tourism; Sector: Tourism in India

**Where it applies:** Uttarakhand; State/ UT

**DBT Scheme:** Yes (Direct Benefit Transfer)

**Detailed Description:** The scheme &quot;Financial Assistance to Permanent Residents of Uttarakhand for Religious Pilgrimages&quot; by the Department of Social Welfare, Government of Uttarakhand, aims to promote religious travel and pilgrimage by providing financial assistance to eligible permanent residents of the state who participate in and complete the Kailash Mansarovar Yatra. Through this scheme, monetary benefits are provided to Scheduled Castes, Scheduled Tribes, and other permanent residents of Uttarakhand. The scheme is implemented by Kumaon Mandal Vikas Nigam. The financial assistance is processed after the submission and verification of required documents and confirmation of successful Yatra completion.

**Benefits / Assistance:** 1. A financial grant of ₹50,000 is provided to eligible Scheduled Caste and Scheduled Tribe residents of Uttarakhand who participate in the Kailash Mansarovar Yatra organised by the Government of India. 1. Permanent residents of the state from other categories who complete the Yatra via the Uttarakhand route are also eligible for financial support through Kumaon Mandal Vikas Nigam.  > **Mode of Disbursement:** - Direct benefit transfer (DBT) post-verification and approval. > **Frequency of Disbursement:** - One-time benefit.

**Application Process:** &gt; **Registration Process:** **Step 1:** Visit [Apuni Sarkar](https://eservices.uk.gov.in/) and click on the “Sign Up Here” button. **Step 2:** Fill in the required details, i.e. Email ID, Gender, Name, Mobile Number, Date of Birth, District, Tehsil, Address of Applicant, and Notifications Language Preference. **Step 3:** Click on “Submit”. &lt;br&gt; &gt; **Post-Registration Process:** **Step 1:** Visit [Apuni Sarkar](https://eservices.uk.gov.in/) portal. **Step 2:** In the “Citizen Login” window, enter your phone number/mail id, correct password and captcha code. **Step 3:** Click on the “Sign In” button. **Step 4:** From the left side menu button click on “DBT Services”, then choose the &amp;quot;Department of Culture.&amp;quot; **Step 5:** Now, click on the scheme “Financial Assistance to Permanent Residents of Uttarakhand for Religious Pilgrimages”. **Step 6:** Enter the mobile number and verify with the OTP. **Step 7:** Click on the checkbox for WhatsApp Verification (for receiving updates regarding your application via WhatsApp). **Step 8:** Now upload all the mandatory documents and click on “Submit”. **Step 8:** The application can be tracked from the “Profile” section.
Mode: Online

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: Kumaon Mandal Vikas Nigam; Nodal Department: Department of Culture and Endowments
