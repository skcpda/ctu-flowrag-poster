# Konkani Drama Competition 

**Short Title:** KDC

**Scheme Brief Description:** The "Konkani Drama Competition" is a cultural scheme by Kala Academy under the Dept. of Art and Culture, Govt. of Goa. It is held annually to promote theatre among the institutions. Eligible participants are those registered under the Societies Registration Act, 1860, with renewed registration.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Financial assistance

**Where it applies:** Goa; State/ UT

**Detailed Description:** The scheme &quot;Konkani Drama Competition&quot; is a state-level cultural initiative by the Kala Academy, under the Department of Art and Culture, Government of Goa. It is organized annually to encourage and promote theatre activities among Goan institutions. Participation is invited through press notes published in local newspapers. Goan institutions registered under the Societies Registration Act, 1860, with renewed registration, are eligible to participate. Under this scheme, participating institutions are reimbursed for to and fro transport charges for two vehicles for carrying artistes and stage materials as per government rates and Kala Academy norms.

**Benefits / Assistance:** - An auditorium with permissible infrastructure is provided free of charge. - 80% of gate collection from ticket sales is given to participating institutions. - Prizes are awarded by a panel of judges. **Main Competition Prizes** - 1st Prize: ₹1,00,000/-; 2nd Prize: ₹75,000/-; 3rd Prize: ₹50,000/- - Two Consolation Prizes (subject to 10+ performances): ₹25,000/- each **Direction Category** 1st Prize: ₹10,000/-; 2nd Prize: ₹7,000/-; 3rd Prize: ₹5,000/- **Individual Acting (Male)** 1st Prize: ₹7,000/-; 2nd Prize: ₹5,000/- **Individual Acting (Female)** 1st Prize: ₹7,000/-; 2nd Prize: ₹5,000/- **Technical Awards** Stage Design/Setting: ₹5,000/-; Lighting: ₹5,000/-; Costumes: ₹5,000/-; Background Music: ₹5,000/-; Make-up: ₹5,000/- **Script Writing Awards** 1st Prize: ₹10,000/-; 2nd Prize: ₹7,000/-; Translated/Adapted Script: ₹10,000/-

**Application Process:** **Step 1:** The interested applicant should take a printout of the [prescribed format](https://kalaacademygoa.co.in/rule-copy-entry-forms-of-konkani-drama-competition-2024-25/) of the application form available on the official website of Kala Academy, Goa. **Step 2:** In the application form, fill in all the mandatory fields, paste the passport-sized photograph (signed across, if required), and attach copies of all the mandatory documents (self-attest, if required). **Step 3:** Submit the duly filled and signed application form along with the documents, within the prescribed period (if any), to the Member Secretary, Kala Academy, Goa. **Step 4:** Request a receipt or acknowledgement from the concerned authority to whom the application has been submitted. Ensure that the receipt contains essential details such as the date and time of submission, and a unique identification number (if applicable). _*Ensure that the application is submitted within the prescribed period, if any._ _*Entries are also invited by releasing press notes in local dailies._ **Entry to Public:** Tickets of ₹ 50/- are charged for admission to each show. **Authority for Redressal of Grievances:** Member Secretary/Chairman, Kala Academy, Goa.
