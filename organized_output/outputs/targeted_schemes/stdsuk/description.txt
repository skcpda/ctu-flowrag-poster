# Scholarship to Disabled Students

**Short Title:** STDSUK

**Scheme Brief Description:** The scheme "Scholarship to Disabled Students" by the Social Justice and Empowerment Department aims to provide financial assistance to students with disabilities. Through this scheme, annual scholarships are offered to support the education of eligible students from Class 1 onwards.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Education & Learning; Sector: Scholarships and student finance

**Where it applies:** Gujarat; State/ UT

**Detailed Description:** The scheme &amp;quot;Scholarship to Disabled Students&amp;quot; by the Social Justice and Empowerment Department, Government of Gujarat, aims to support the educational needs of students with benchmark disabilities of more than 40%, as per the Rights of Persons with Disabilities Act, 2016. Through this scheme, annual scholarships ranging from ₹1,500/- to ₹4,500/- are provided to students with disabilities studying from Class 1 onwards, depending on the level of education. The scheme is implemented by the Social Defense Division of the Social Justice and Empowerment Department. The objective is to encourage continued education among students with disabilities by providing financial assistance, especially for those who have achieved at least 40% marks in their last annual examination and possess a valid disability identity card.  

**Benefits / Assistance:** **Scholarship Assistance:** - ₹1,500/- per year for students with disabilities studying in Class 1st to 8th. - ₹2,000/- to ₹4,500 per year for students studying in Class 9th and above.
