# One Time Cash Incentive towards Completing ITI, Typewriting, Accountancy, Bookkeeping, Tailoring, Computer Training (Sainik Welfare)

**Short Title:** OTCI-CITABTCT-SW

**Scheme Brief Description:** "One Time Cash Incentive towards Completing ITI, Typewriting, Accountancy, Bookkeeping, Tailoring, Computer Training" by the Dept of Sainik Welfare, Puducherry, provides upto ₹10,000/- to the unemployed Ex-servicemen / Widow/Dependent of the ESM to pursue the mentioned programs/courses.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Education & Learning; Sector: Apprenticeships and training

**Where it applies:** Puducherry; State/ UT

**Detailed Description:** &quot;One Time Cash Incentive towards Completing ITI, Typewriting, Accountancy, Bookkeeping, Tailoring, Computer Training&quot; is a Welfare Scheme by the Armed Forces Flag Day Fund of the Department of Sainik Welfare, Puducherry. Through this scheme, up to ₹10,000/- is provided to the unemployed Ex-servicemen (ESM) / Widow of the ESM / Dependent of the Widow of the ESM, to pursue the mentioned programs/courses from a Government-recognized School/Institution, upon prior permission from the Department. The application should be submitted offline within three months from the date of successful completion of the course.

**Benefits / Assistance:** - The actual cost of the program/course or ₹10,000/- is provided to the beneficiary. - The amount, as decided by the Management Committee, is credited to the beneficiary’s Bank Account through RTGS/ NEFT

**Exclusions / Not eligible:** The scope of this scheme will not apply to those ESM / living spouse/widows who are re-employed as regular / deemed to be regular in Government/Public Sector Undertakings/Private Organizations and self-employed IT assesses.

**Application Process:** **Step 1:** The interested applicant should take print of the prescribed format of the application form for the concerned scheme from the Official Website of the Department of Sainik Welfare.
