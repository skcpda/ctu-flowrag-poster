# Samagra Samajik Suraksha Parityakta Pension Yojana

**Short Title:** PSSPPY

**Scheme Brief Description:** Under the Scheme, Madhya Pradesh government provides financial benefits to Be from a family living below the poverty line.

**Launch Date:** 1981-01-01

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Pension

**Where it applies:** Madhya Pradesh; State/ UT

**DBT Scheme:** Yes (Direct Benefit Transfer)

**Detailed Description:** Samagra Samajik Suraksha Parityakta Pension Yojana under the Department of Social Justice government of Madhya Pradesh provides financial benefits to Be from a family living below the poverty line. to fulfill the basic needs of life.

**Benefits / Assistance:** Pension Benefits : ₹ 600/- per month

**Application Process:** **Step 1** Go to the [Samajik Suraksha portal.](https://socialsecurity.mp.gov.in/#) **Step 2**: Click on [Samajik Suraksha pension avm arthik sahayata yojanaen.](https://socialsecurity.mp.gov.in/Home.aspx) **Step 3:** Now click on the left on your screen Pension Yojana hetu online aavedan. **Step 4:** Fill in the required details and submit.
Mode: Online

**Additional Application Processes:** **Process 2:** **Step 1:** Applicants can apply for the scheme using the prescribed format and submitting the necessary documents either to the designated officer&#39;s office, the Gram Panchayat, or a Public Service Center. **Step 2:** Upon submitting a complete application, the applicant will receive a mandatory acknowledgment from the office. **Step 3**: The District Panchayat/Gram Panchayat/Urban Body/Ward Office will scrutinize the documents submitted with the application. **Step 4**: If, after investigation, the documents are found to be incorrect according to the rules, the application will be rejected with written explanation. **Step 5**: If, after investigation, the documents are found to be correct, the pension case will be approved according to the rules. **Step 6:** Once the pension is approved, the beneficiary&#39;s name will be added to the pension proposal for the current month by the District Panchayat/Gram Panchayat/Urban Body/Ward Office. The approved order will be maintained on record. **Step 7**: The Directorate will deposit the monthly pension amount directly into the beneficiary&#39;s bank savings account through a single click, starting from the same month the approval is granted.

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Department of Social Justice and Empowerment of Persons with Disabilities
