# Swaran Jayanti Ashray Yojana - Himachal Pradesh

**Short Title:** SJAY-HP

**Scheme Brief Description:** The "Swaran Jayanti Ashray Yojana" was launched by the Department of Social Justice and Empowerment, Government of Himachal Pradesh aims to provide financial assistance for the construction of houses who are permanent residents of Himachal Pradesh belonging to Scheduled Castes, Scheduled Tribes, OBC

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Housing & Shelter; Sector: Shelter, integrated support and assistance

**Where it applies:** Himachal Pradesh; State/ UT

**Detailed Description:** The &quot;Swaran Jayanti Ashray Yojana&quot; was launched by the Department of Social Justice and Empowerment, Government of Himachal Pradesh aims to provide financial assistance for the construction of houses who are permanent residents of Himachal Pradesh belonging to Scheduled Castes, Scheduled Tribes, Other Backward Classes.

**Benefits / Assistance:** - **Financial assistance of ₹1,50,000/- for construction of new house.**

**Application Process:** &gt; **Application Process:** **Step 1:** The interested applicant can download the [application form](http://esomsa.hp.gov.in/sites/default/files/Swarn%20Jayanti%20Ashray%20Yojna.pdf) from the official website. **Step 2:** In the application form, fill in all the mandatory fields, and attach copies of all the mandatory documents (self-attest, if required). **Step 3:** Submit the duly filled and signed application form along with the documents to the Concerned District Welfare Officer/Tehsil Welfare Officer. **Step 4:** Request a receipt or acknowledgment from the Concerned District Welfare Officer/Tehsil Welfare Officer to whom the application has been submitted. Ensure that the receipt contains essential details such as the date and time of submission, and a unique identification number (if applicable).
