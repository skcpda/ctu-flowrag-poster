# Aatmanirbhar Gujarat Scheme for assistance to MSMEs: Assistance for Reimbursement of CGTMSE Fees

**Short Title:** AGMSMERCGT

**Scheme Brief Description:** The "Aatmanirbhar Gujarat Scheme for assistance to MSMEs" aims to empower the state's dynamic MSME sector, which significantly contributes to employment, GDP, and exports.

**Launch Date:** 2022-10-05

**Who is this for:** Business Entity, Industries; Category: Business & Entrepreneurship; Sector: Setting up / start-up / entrepreneurship

**Where it applies:** Gujarat; State/ UT

**Detailed Description:** The &amp;quot;Aatmanirbhar Gujarat Scheme for MSMEs&amp;quot; is an umbrella scheme launched by the Industries and Mines Department, Gujarat. It aims to empower the state&amp;#39;s dynamic MSME sector, which significantly contributes to employment, GDP, and exports. Effective from October 5, 2022, to October 4, 2027, this initiative supports growth, investments, and the vision of &amp;quot;Aatmanirbhar Gujarat&amp;quot; and &amp;quot;Aatmanirbhar Bharat.&amp;quot; The component &amp;quot;Assistance for Reimbursement of CGTMSE Fees&amp;quot; aims to provide financial support to Micro and Small Enterprises (MSEs) by reimbursing the annual service fees charged by banks or financial institutions for collateral-free loans under the Credit Guarantee Fund Trust for Micro and Small Enterprises (CGTMSE) scheme. &lt;br&gt; &lt;br&gt;

**Benefits / Assistance:** - **Reimbursement of Fees:** 100% reimbursement of the annual service fees paid to banks or financial institutions for collateral-free loans under CGTMSE for a period of 5 years. - **Annual Service Fees:** 1st Year-1.8% + Risk Premium; 2nd Year onwards:0.85%or as amended by CGTMSE (Credit Guarantee Fund Trust for Micro and Small Enterprise) Trust/RBI from time to time.

**Exclusions / Not eligible:** - Enterprises engaged in service or trading activities are not eligible under this scheme. &lt;br&gt;

**Application Process:** > **Registration Process:** **Step-1:** Visit the [Investor Facilitation Portal (IFP)](https://ifp.gujarat.gov.in/DIGIGOV/) portal. Click on &quot;New Investor Registration&quot; to access the registration form. **Step-2:** Fill in the Registration Form. Click on &quot;Submit&quot;. **Step-3:** Verify the E-mail ID using the link sent on your registered E-mail ID. **Step-4:** After verification, you will be registered successfully.  > **Post-Registration Process:** **Step-1:** Visit the [Investor Facilitation Portal (IFP)](https://ifp.gujarat.gov.in/DIGIGOV/) portal. Click on &quot;Login&quot;. **Step-2:** Enter the email address and password you used during registration. **Step-3:** Click the &quot;Login&quot; button to access your account. **Step-4:** Now fill in the required details and mandatory documents about your business to complete the application process.  > **Helpdesk****:** [Contact Here](https://ifp.gujarat.gov.in/DIGIGOV/IFP-pages/contact.jsp?pagedisp=static)
