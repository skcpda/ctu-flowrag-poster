# Mukhyamantri Nishulk Gas Refill Yojana

**Short Title:** MMNGRY

**Scheme Brief Description:** The scheme aims to support poor families by reducing their household fuel expenses. Under this scheme, Antyodaya (pink card) ration card holders in the state are entitled to receive three free gas cylinder refills every year.

**Who is this for:** Family; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment

**Where it applies:** Uttarakhand; State/ UT

**DBT Scheme:** Yes (Direct Benefit Transfer)

**Detailed Description:** The scheme &amp;quot;Mukhyamantri Nishulk Gas Refill Yojana&amp;quot; was launched by the Department of Food, Civil Supplies &amp;amp; Consumer Affairs, Government of Uttarakhand. The scheme aims to support poor families holding Antyodaya (pink) ration cards in the state by reducing their household fuel expenses. 

**Benefits / Assistance:** Under this scheme, Antyodaya ration card (pink card) holders in the state are entitled to receive 03 free gas cylinder refills every year. 

**Application Process:** - The list of Antyodaya ration card holders is provided by the District Supply Officer to the respective gas agency in the area. - An Antyodaya ration card holder can avail a free gas refill after linking their ration card details, Aadhaar, and bank account with the nearest gas agency. - To receive one free refill every four months, the cardholder must first pay the full price of the gas to the gas agency and collect the cylinder. The cost of the gas will then be reimbursed directly to the cardholder’s bank account through DBT. 
Mode: Offline

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Department Of Food & Civil Supplies
