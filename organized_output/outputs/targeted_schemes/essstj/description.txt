# AICTE e-Shodh Sindhu Scheme (eSS)

**Short Title:** ESSSTJ

**Scheme Brief Description:** AICTE e-Shodh Sindhu Scheme (eSS) provides subscriptions of e-Resources like Web of Science, Scopus, ASME, ASCE and Bentham Science to institutions.

**Who is this for:** University / Institution; Category: Education & Learning; Sector: Resources for schools

**Where it applies:** Central

**Detailed Description:** Launched by the All India Council for Technical Education, AICTE e-Shodh Sindhu Scheme (eSS) provides subscriptions of e-Resources like Web of Science, Scopus, The American Society of Mechanical Engineers (ASME), American Society of Civil Engineers (ASCE) and Bentham Science to institutions. **Objective:** Facilitating access to e-Resource to the students, faculty and researchers, a new to augment the culture of research in the institution and overall quality of technical education.

**Benefits / Assistance:** **Benefits:** Subscriptions of e-Resources like Web of Science, Scopus, ASME, ASCE and Bentham Science to institutions.  **Duration of Subscriptions:** Initially for one calendar year, renewable for further year on the basis of usage by institute and fulfilments of other essential eligibility criteria.
