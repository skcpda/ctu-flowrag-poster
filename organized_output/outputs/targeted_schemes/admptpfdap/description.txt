# Assistance For Delivery / Miscarriage Of Pregnancy / Termination Of Pregnancy To Female Differently Abled Person

**Short Title:** ADMPTPFDAP

**Scheme Brief Description:** The Government of Tamil Nadu introduced "Assistance for Delivery/Miscarriage of Pregnancy/Termination of Pregnancy to Female Differently Abled Person.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Health & Wellness; Sector: Financial assistance

**Where it applies:** Tamil Nadu; State

**Detailed Description:** The Government of Tamil Nadu introduced &quot;Assistance for Delivery/ Miscarriage of Pregnancy/ Termination of Pregnancy to Female Differently Abled Person with a sum of ₹6,000/- @ ₹1,000/- per month for delivery and ₹3,000/- for miscarriage/termination of pregnancy is given to female differently abled person.

**Benefits / Assistance:** A sum of Rs.6,000/- @ Rs.1,000/- per month for delivery and Rs.3,000 for miscarriage/termination of pregnancy is given to a female differently abled person.

**Application Process:** Step 1: Visit the official website of the[ Chennai district](https://chennai.nic.in/ta/differently-abled-welfare-schemes/). Step 2: Click on the Departments. Select Differently Abled Welfare, after selecting click on Differently Abled Welfare Schemes. Step 3: You will land on the [List of Scheme page](https://chennai.nic.in/ta/differently-abled-welfare-schemes/) of Differently Abled Welfare Schemes. Step 4: Scroll down to the section &quot;L. Tamilnadu differently abled welfare board schemes&quot;. Step 5: Download the [application form](https://cdn.s3waas.gov.in/s313f3cf8c531952d72e5847c4183e6910/uploads/2021/06/2021060180-1.pdf) of the &quot;S.No. 6 Assistance for delivery/miscarriage of pregnancy/termination of pregnancy to female differently abled person&quot;.
