# Dilli Swarojgar Yojna

**Short Title:** DSY

**Scheme Brief Description:** "Dilli Swarojgar Yojna" provides loans up to ₹5,00,000/- at 6% interest for income-generating activities. The scheme targets individuals from SC, ST, OBC, and Minority communities. The loans are disbursed for activities such as vegetable/fruit/flower shops, dairy products, tailoring shops, etc.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Business & Entrepreneurship; Sector: Setting up / start-up / entrepreneurship

**Where it applies:** Delhi; State/ UT

**Detailed Description:** The scheme &quot;Dilli Swarojgar Yojna&quot; by the Delhi Scheduled Castes, Scheduled Tribes, Other Backward Classes, and Minorities Financial and Development Corporation (DSFDC), Government of National Capital Territory (NCT) of Delhi, aims to provide need-based loans up to ₹5,00,000/- at an interest rate of 6% per annum for various income-generating activities. The scheme targets individuals from Scheduled Castes (SC), Scheduled Tribes (ST), Other Backward Classes (OBC), and Minority communities, enabling them to start or expand small businesses. Eligible applicants must be residents of Delhi for the last five years, aged between 18 to 50 years, and have an annual family income not exceeding ₹2,00,000/-. The loans are disbursed for activities such as vegetable/fruit/flower shops, dairy products, tailoring shops, and other permissible non-polluting manufacturing activities.

**Benefits / Assistance:** Need-based loans up to ₹5,00,000/- at an interest rate of 6% per annum to support an [income-generating activity](https://dsfdc.delhi.gov.in/sites/default/files/dsy_scheme_details.pdf) mentioned in the scheme guidelines.

**Application Process:** **Step 1:** The interested applicant should visit the DSFDC branch offices (Rajpur Road, Mangolpuri, Nand Nagri) or headquarters at Rohini to collect the loan application form for the scheme. Pay the form fee of ₹100/-. Alternatively, take print of the [prescribed format](https://dsfdc.delhi.gov.in/sites/default/files/affidavit.pdf) of the application form from the official website of DSFDC. **Step 2:** In the application form, fill in all the mandatory fields, paste the passport-sized photograph, and attach copies of all the mandatory documents (self-attest, if required). **Step 3:** Submit the duly filled and signed application form along with the documents at the nearest DSFDC branch office or headquarters. Pay the processing fee of ₹500/- via demand draft in favor of DSFDC. **Step 4:** Request a receipt or acknowledgement. _*Once the application is approved, the applicant will receive a loan sanction letter. The applicant must sign the loan agreement and provide post-sanction documents as listed. After completing all formalities, the loan amount will be disbursed to the applicant’s bank account._ _*The applicant must repay the loan as per the agreed terms and conditions. Ensure timely repayment through the Electronic Clearing Service (ECS) or post-dated cheques provided during the application process._
Mode: Offline
