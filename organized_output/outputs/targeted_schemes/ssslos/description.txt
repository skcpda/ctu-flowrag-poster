# Sainik School Scholarship Located Outside the Uttarakhand

**Short Title:** SSSLOUK

**Scheme Brief Description:** The scheme offers financial assistance to students from Uttarakhand enrolled in classes 6 to 12 at Sainik Schools outside the state, with scholarships awarded based on family income.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Education & Learning; Sector: Scholarships and student finance

**Where it applies:** Uttarakhand; State/ UT

**Detailed Description:** The scheme &amp;quot;Sainik School Scholarship Located Outside the Uttarakhand&amp;quot; is implemented by the Secondary Education Department under the Department of Education, Government of Uttarakhand. The scheme aims to provide financial assistance to students from Uttarakhand who are studying in classes 6 to 12 at Sainik Schools located outside the state. 

**Benefits / Assistance:** **Rate of Scholarships:** - ₹40,000/- per annum for students from families with a monthly income up to ₹12,000/-. - ₹30,000/- per annum for students from families with a monthly income of ₹12,001/- to ₹15,000/-. - ₹20,000/- per annum for students from families with a monthly income of ₹15,001/- to ₹18,000/-. - ₹10,000/- per annum for students from families with a monthly income of ₹18,001/- to ₹22,000/-. 

**Application Process:** - The student studying in a Sainik School located outside Uttarakhand will apply for a scholarship in that school. - Along with the application, the student must submit the following documents: a valid family income certificate issued by the tehsil, permanent residence certificate, Aadhaar card, and proof of an Aadhaar-linked bank account. All Sainik Schools located outside the state will forward the applications to Sainik School Ghorakhal. Sainik School Ghorakhal will compile the applications, prepare a proposal, and send it to the department. - The department will allocate funds to the concerned schools through Sainik School Ghorakhal, and the schools will disburse the scholarship to the students via DBT (Direct Benefit Transfer). 
Mode: Offline

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: Secondary Education Department, Government Of Uttarakhand; Nodal Department: Department Of Education
