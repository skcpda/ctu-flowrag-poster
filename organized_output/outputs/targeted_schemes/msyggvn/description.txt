# Mahila Samrudhhi Yojana (Gujarat Gopalak Vikas Nigam)

**Short Title:** MSYGGVN

**Scheme Brief Description:** The scheme aims to create self-employment for women and make them self-reliant. Through this scheme, individual women entrepreneurs are provided with microfinance loans to start a business of their choice.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Women and Child; Sector: Loan

**Where it applies:** Gujarat; State/ UT

**Detailed Description:** The scheme &quot;Mahila Samrudhhi Yojana (Gujarat Gopalak Vikas Nigam)&quot; by the Social Justice and Empowerment Department, Government of Gujarat, aims to provide Microfinance for individual women entrepreneurs to create self-employment for women and make them self-reliant. Under this scheme, loans are provided on an individual basis to women beneficiaries to start a business of their choice. The scheme is implemented by the Gujarat Gopalak Vikas Nigam. 

**Benefits / Assistance:** 1. The scheme provides 100% of the loan amount to eligible women beneficiaries, up to a maximum limit of ₹1,25,000/-. 1. The loan carries an interest rate of 4% per annum. 

**Application Process:** > **Step 1: Register on the Official Portal** - Go to the [Official Website ](https://ggdconline.gujarat.gov.in/)Gujarat Gopalak Vikas Nigam. - Scroll down on the homepage, find the &quot;Mahila Samrudhhi Yojana&quot; and click on &quot;Apply Now&quot;. - You will be directed to the application form. > **Step 2: Access and Fill Out the Application Form** - Select &quot;Mahila Samrudhhi Yojana&quot;. - Provide Aadhaar, Ration, Voter ID, full name (Last, First, Father/Husband), address, district, taluka, village, PIN, date of birth, age, caste, subcaste, phone, mobile, email, gender, and marital status. - Enter income certificate number, issue date, issuing officer&#39;s name, total annual family income (up to ₹ 3,00,000/-), and parent/guardian&#39;s occupation. - If &quot;Physically Handicapped,&quot; select &quot;Yes&quot; or &quot;No&quot;. If you selected &quot;Yes&quot;, provide the percentage (in %). - Indicate if any family member works in a government or private job. If &quot;Yes,&quot; provide their Name, Designation, Place, and Salary details. - Select your Education Qualification: Uneducated, Educated, Graduation, Post Graduation, or Other Degree. - If you have taken a past loan, select &quot;Yes&quot; or &quot;No&quot;. If you selected &quot;Yes&quot;, provide loan details (date, amount, paid, remaining) and your account number. - Provide bank account number, bank name, branch name, name as per account, account type (Saving/Current), MICR code, and IFSC code. > **Step 3: Review and Final Submission** - Carefully review each module. Use the preview option to examine the application before final submission. Fill out the provided &quot;CAPTCHA&quot; and Click on “Submit”. > **Track and Review Status** - Applicant will receive an Application ID/Reference Number. Save or print the acknowledgement for future tracking. - Go to the [Official Website](https://ggdconline.gujarat.gov.in/) of Gujarat Gopalak Development Corporation. - Scroll down and locate the Know App Status. Click on &quot;Know App Status&quot;. - Select the Scheme name, Enter App No., and Birth Date. Fill out the Captcha and click on Submit.  
Mode: Online
