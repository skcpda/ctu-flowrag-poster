# Eklavya Prashikshan Yojana

**Short Title:** EPY

**Scheme Brief Description:** The "Eklavya Prashikshan Yojana" by the Directorate of Higher Education, Government of Jharkhand, aims to provide free coaching and financial aid for competitive exam preparation. Eligible students receive free coaching and ₹2,500/- monthly assistance during the coaching period.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Education & Learning; Sector: Scholarships and student finance

**Where it applies:** Jharkhand; State/ UT

**Detailed Description:** The &quot;Eklavya Prashikshan Yojana&quot; by the Directorate of Higher Education, Department of Higher and Technical Education, Government of Jharkhand, aims to provide free coaching and financial assistance to students preparing for competitive exams conducted by national and state-level agencies. Under this scheme, students from Jharkhand receive free coaching and a monthly stipend of ₹2,500/- to help them succeed in examinations such as UPSC, JPSC, SSC, Railway Recruitment, and banking exams.

**Benefits / Assistance:** - ₹2,500/- monthly stipends per student during the coaching period. - Coaching will be provided by nationally reputed institutions within Jharkhand.

**Application Process:** ***Registration Process:*** **Step 01:** Go to the official website of [Eklavya Prashikshan Yojna](https://eklavya.apphost.in/) and click on &quot;Registration&quot;. **Step 02:** Fill the required details name, mobile number email id and complete the process. **Step 03:** The student will get an confirmation SMS registered mobile number and an email id.  ***Application Process:*** **Step 01:** Fill the application form and upload the required documents. **Step 02:** Click on &quot;Submit&quot; to get application Id.  **NOTE:** - The student must secure at least 75% attendance for the entire duration of the coaching. - Students must pass a screening test conducted by a government-approved agency. Merit lists are prepared based on marks and preferences.
Mode: Online

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Higher and Techical Education Department
