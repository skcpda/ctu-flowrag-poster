# Cash Grant in lieu of Sewing Machine

**Short Title:** CGILSM

**Scheme Brief Description:** "Cash Grant in lieu of Sewing Machine" is a Welfare Scheme by the Armed Forces Flag Day Fund of the Dept of Sainik Welfare, Puducherry. Through this scheme, ₹10,000/- is provided to the Widows of Ex-servicemen for the purchase of a Sewing Machine. The applications are accepted offline.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Aids/Appliances

**Where it applies:** Puducherry; State/ UT

**Detailed Description:** &quot;Cash Grant in lieu of Sewing Machine&quot; is a Welfare Scheme by the Armed Forces Flag Day Fund of the Department of Sainik Welfare, Puducherry. Through this scheme, ₹10,000/- is provided to the Widows of Ex-servicemen for the purchase of a Sewing Machine. This scheme applies only to the Widows of the ESM registered with the Department of Sainik Welfare, Puducherry. The applications are accepted offline.

**Benefits / Assistance:** - A Cash Grant of ₹10,000/- is provided for the purchase of a Sewing Machine. - The amount is credited to the beneficiary’s Bank Account through RTGS/ NEFT.
