# Eja-Boi Shagun Yojana

**Short Title:** EBSY

**Scheme Brief Description:** Under this scheme, a cash gift of ₹2,000 /- would be paid to those women who ensured 48 hours stay for delivery of their child in the government hospitals or health care centres.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Women and Child; Sector: Pregnancy Care

**Where it applies:** Uttarakhand; State/ UT

**Detailed Description:** The scheme &amp;quot;Eja-Boi Shagun Yojana&amp;quot; was launched by the Department of Medical Health and Medical Education, Government of Uttarakhand. The scheme aims to ensure safe postnatal care and encourage institutional deliveries. Under this scheme, a financial incentive of ₹2,000 /- is provided to mothers who stay in government hospitals for at least 48 hours post-delivery.

**Benefits / Assistance:** - Mothers receive a ₹2,000/- incentive for delivering in a government hospital and staying for at least 48 hours post-delivery for postnatal care. > **Note:** The incentive amount shall be transferred directly to the beneficiary’s bank account.

**Application Process:** **Step 1:** After delivery, the woman should stay in the government hospital for a minimum of 48 hours and inform the hospital staff about her eligibility under the scheme. **Step 2:** The hospital authorities will collect the necessary documents from the beneficiary. **Step 3:** The hospital will verify the submitted documents to ensure eligibility and accuracy. **Step 4:** Once the verification is complete, the hospital will initiate the process to disburse the benefit amount of ₹2,000 directly into the woman’s bank account.
