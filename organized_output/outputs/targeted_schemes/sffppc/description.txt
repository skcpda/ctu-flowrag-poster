# PMMSY: Fish Feed Plants of Production Capacity of at least 100 Ton/Day - Haryana

**Short Title:** FFPPC

**Scheme Brief Description:** This scheme provides capital subsidy for setting up fish feed plants with a minimum production capacity of 100 tons per day, under the Pradhan Mantri Matsya Sampada Yojana (PMMSY) to general , schedule caste and women beneficiaries .

**Who is this for:** Business Entity; Category: Agriculture,Rural & Environment; Sector: Fishing and hunting

**Where it applies:** Haryana; State/ UT

**Detailed Description:** The Pradhan Mantri Matsya Sampada Yojana: Fish Feed Plants of Production Capacity of at least 100 Ton/Day, introduced by the Ministry Of Fisheries, Animal Husbandry &amp;amp; Dairying, Government of India, aims to provide capital subsidy for setting up fish feed plants with a minimum production capacity of 100 tons per day. The objective is to enhance the availability of high-quality and affordable feed for fish farmers, supporting the expansion of aquaculture in Haryana. This is a Centrally Sponsored Scheme, under which the project is jointly funded by the Central and State Governments: 50% of the subsidy amount is contributed by the Central Government, and 50% is provided by the State Government. Beneficiaries are expected to operate and maintain the plant independently and ensure the supply of feed to fishers at affordable prices. 

**Benefits / Assistance:** - The project cost is ₹650,00,000/- > **Subsidy Distribution :** 1. A 40% subsidy is available for the General Category. 1. A 60% subsidy is offered for SC (Scheduled Caste) and Women beneficiaries.
