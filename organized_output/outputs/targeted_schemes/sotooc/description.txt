# Sanctioning of Tricycles of Orthopedically Challenged

**Short Title:** SOTOOC

**Scheme Brief Description:** The "Sanctioning of Tricycles of Orthopedically Challenged" scheme aims to provide tricycles to orthopedically challenged individuals to enhance their mobility and independence. This initiative ensures that eligible individuals receive the necessary support to lead active and self-reliant lives.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Aids/Appliances

**Where it applies:** Andhra Pradesh; State/ UT

**Detailed Description:** The A.P. Differently Abled &amp; Senior Citizens Assistance Corporation, Andhra Pradesh, under its Action Plan 2017-18, aims to provide tricycles to orthopedically challenged individuals to enhance their mobility and independence. The tricycles are distributed by the Assistant Directors, Department for the Welfare of Differently Abled &amp; Senior Citizens, and District Managers, A.P. Differently Abled &amp; Senior Citizens Assistance Corporation.

**Benefits / Assistance:** - Eligible beneficiaries receive a free tricycle to improve their mobility and independence. 

**Application Process:** **Step 01:** Visit the official website: [https://apdascac.ap.gov.in](https://apdascac.ap.gov.in) **Step 02:** On the homepage, click on &quot;[APDASCAC Schemes](https://apdascac.ap.gov.in/APDASCAC-Schemes/)&quot;. **Step 03:** Select the &quot;SANCTION OF TRICYCLES&quot; option. **Step 04:** Click on &quot;[Apply Now](https://apdascac.ap.gov.in/Forms/tricycles/)&quot; to begin the application process. **Step 05:** Fill in the required details and submit your application. 
Mode: Online

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Dept of Social Welfare, WCD
