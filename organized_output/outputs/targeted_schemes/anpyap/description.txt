# Atma Nirbhar Plantation Yojana

**Short Title:** ANPYAP

**Scheme Brief Description:** The scheme aims to promote self-reliance in the agriculture sector through sustainable plantation activities across the state. Under this scheme, support in the form of plantation assistance and allied resources is provided to individual farmers, Self-Help Groups & Farmer Producer Organizations.

**Who is this for:** Individual, Self Help Groups (SHGS); Category: Agriculture,Rural & Environment; Sector: Land and water resources

**Where it applies:** Arunachal Pradesh; State/ UT

**Detailed Description:** The scheme &amp;quot;Atma Nirbhar Plantation Yojana (ANPY)&amp;quot; was launched by the Department of Agriculture, Government of Arunachal Pradesh, aims to promote self-reliance in the agriculture sector through sustainable plantation activities across the state&amp;#39;s diverse agro-climatic zones. Under this scheme, support in the form of plantation assistance and allied resources is provided to individual farmers, Self-Help Groups (SHGs), and Farmer Producer Organizations (FPOs).

**Benefits / Assistance:** - Access to plantation support including technical guidance, planting material, and financial assistance.

**Application Process:** ***Interested and eligible students need to apply online on the*** [***Atma Nirbhar Yojana***](https://agri.arunachal.gov.in/atmanirbhar_yojana/) ***portal.*** **New User Registration** 1. Select the &amp;quot;[Login](https://agri.arunachal.gov.in/atmanirbhar_yojana/login)&amp;quot; option. 1. Subsequently, click on the &amp;quot;[Register Here&amp;quot;](https://agri.arunachal.gov.in/atmanirbhar_yojana/user-register) link. 1. Complete the registration form with the requisite details, including your name, Aadhaar card number, email ID, and mobile number. 1. Establish a password for your account. 1. Upon successful registration, the applicant will receive a User ID and password. ***Login &amp;amp; Application Process:*** 1. [Log in](https://agri.arunachal.gov.in/atmanirbhar_yojana/login) using the provided User ID and password to access the application form. 1. Fill in the complete application form. 1. Upload the required documents, including a photograph, caste certificate, residential certificate, and land ownership proof, etc. 1. Submit the application form.
Mode: Online

**Additional Application Processes:** **Process 2:** **Step 1:** The interested applicant should visit (during office hours) the District Agriculture Office and request a hard copy of the prescribed format of the application form from the staff exclusively entrusted to issue and collect filled-in applications. **OR** The interested applicant downloads the [application form](https://cdn.s3waas.gov.in/s3c0c7c76d30bd3dcaefc96f40275bdc0a/uploads/2023/05/2023051921.pdf) from the official website. **Step 2:** In the application form, fill in all the mandatory fields, and attach copies of all the mandatory documents (self-attest, if required). **Step 3:** Submit the duly filled and signed application form along with the documents to the District Agriculture Office.
