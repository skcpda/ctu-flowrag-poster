# Mukhyamantri Gram Parivahan Yojana

**Short Title:** MGPY

**Scheme Brief Description:** The Scheme provides financial assistance for purchasing vehicles to enhance transportation facilities in rural areas. It includes grants for regular vehicles, e-rickshaws, and ambulances under specified conditions.

**Launch Date:** 2021-08-07

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Financial assistance

**Where it applies:** Bihar; State/ UT

**Detailed Description:** The &amp;quot;Mukhyamantri Gram Parivahan Yojana&amp;quot; launched by the Transport Department, Government of Bihar, aimed at improving transportation facilities in all panchayats of the state. It offers financial assistance to beneficiaries for purchasing vehicles.

**Benefits / Assistance:** - **Standard vehicles:** 50% of the purchase price or up to ₹1,00,000/-. - **E-rickshaws:** 50% of the purchase price or up to ₹70,000/-. - **Ambulances:** 50% of the purchase price or up to ₹2,00,000/-.

**Application Process:** **Registration Process:** **Step-1:** Visit the official [Website](https://state.bihar.gov.in/transport/SectionInformation.html?editForm&amp;amp;rowId=6353). Click on the &amp;amp;quot;Registration&amp;amp;quot; button. **Step-2:** Enter your Mobile Number, New Password, Email, and Driving License No. **Step-3:** Click on &amp;amp;quot;Register&amp;amp;quot; to complete the registration process. &amp;lt;br&amp;gt; **Application Process:** **Step-1:** Login with your registered Mobile Number and Password. **Step-2:** On the Application page, fill in all the required details and upload the relevant documents. **Step-3:** Then click on the declaration form and click on &amp;amp;quot;Submit&amp;amp;quot;. **Step-4:** Once all details are filled in correctly, click on &amp;amp;quot;Print Application&amp;amp;quot; to get a copy of the application form.
Mode: Online
