# State Government ST Scholarship (Class 1 to 8)

**Short Title:** SGSTS

**Scheme Brief Description:** The scheme was launched by the Tribal Affairs Department, Government of Madhya Pradesh, aims to give benefits for improving the educational status of scheduled tribe students. Under this scheme, scholarships are provided to Scheduled Tribe students in Classes 1 to 8.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Education & Learning; Sector: Scholarships and student finance

**Where it applies:** Madhya Pradesh; State/ UT

**Detailed Description:** The &amp;quot;State Government ST Scholarship (Class 1 to 8)&amp;quot; scheme, launched by the Tribal Affairs Department, Government of Madhya Pradesh, aims to give benefits for improving the educational status of scheduled tribe students. Under this scheme, scholarships are provided to Scheduled Tribe students in Classes 1 to 8. To be eligible, students must be native of Madhya Pradesh and belong to the Scheduled tribe category. There is no restriction on the annual family income of the students.

**Benefits / Assistance:** Under this scheme, scholarships will be provided to the students belonging to Scheduled Tribes at the following rates: **For Classes 1 to 5:** - ₹250/- per year (for 10 months) to all girls and boys belonging to special backward tribes (Baiga, Saharia, and Bharia). **For Classes 6 to 8:** - ₹200/- per year (for 10 months) for boys. - ₹600/- per year (for 10 months) for girls. **Note:** The scholarship will be deposited directly into the student&amp;#39;s bank account.

**Application Process:** - To avail of the benefits under this scheme, eligible students can contact the Principal of their respective schools/institutions. **Note:** The profile of the students is entered on the education portal [https://shikshaportal.mp.gov.in/ ](https://shikshaportal.mp.gov.in/)by the teacher of the educational institution as per the record/certificate provided by the student.
