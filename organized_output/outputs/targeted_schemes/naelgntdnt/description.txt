# New Akanksha Scheme (Educational Loan) (GNDTDC)

**Short Title:** NASELGNDTDC

**Scheme Brief Description:** Under the scheme, educational loans of up to ₹10 lakh are provided to students belonging to Nomadic and Denotified Tribes communities for pursuing higher professional and technical education at the graduate and postgraduate levels.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Education & Learning; Sector: Loan

**Where it applies:** Gujarat; State/ UT

**Detailed Description:** The &amp;quot;New Akanksha Scheme (Educational Loan)&amp;quot; is implemented by the Gujarat Nomadic and Denotified Tribes Development Corporation (GNDTDC) under the Social Justice and Empowerment Department, Government of Gujarat. Under this scheme, educational loans are provided to students belonging to Nomadic and Denotified Tribes communities for pursuing higher professional and technical education at the graduate and postgraduate levels. 

**Benefits / Assistance:** **1. Loan Amount:** The maximum loan limit for this scheme is up to ₹10,00,000/-. **2. Included Expenses:** The loan amount will include the following: - Admission and tuition fees - Expenses for books, uniforms, essential materials, and exam fees - Accommodation and food expenses - Insurance – Policy premium **3. Interest Rate:** - 4% per annum for male students - 3.5% per annum for female students **4. Contribution:** - 90% contribution from the Central Corporation, - 5% from the State Government, - 5% from the beneficiary **5. Repayment Period:** - The loan will have to be repaid in 60 equal monthly installments including interest. - The loan will be recovered within 6 months of completion of the course or upon getting a job, whichever is earlier. 

**Exclusions / Not eligible:** - Loan is not eligible in case of admission obtained under management quota. 

**Application Process:** **Step 1: Visit the Official Portal** - Go to the [Official Website](https://gndcdconline.gujarat.gov.in/) Gujarat Nomadic and Denotified Tribes Development Corporation.- Scroll down on the homepage, find the &quot;Educational Loan (New Akanksha Yojana)&quot; and click on &quot;Apply Now&quot;. - You will be directed to the [application form](https://gndcdconline.gujarat.gov.in/SchemeEducation/AddEdit). **Step 2: Access and Fill Out the Application Form** - Complete all sections under &quot;Applicant information,&quot; including full name, father/husband&#39;s name, gender, complete address details, contact numbers, Aadhaar No., parents/husband/guardian occupation and income details. Also provide Date of Birth, Age, Caste, and Subspecies. Also, if you are disabled and provide a percentage. - For each applicable qualification (SSC, HSC, Diploma, Degree, Graduation), tick the checkbox and fill in the Degree, Board/University Name, Academic year, and Percentage obtained. - Fill in details about the college/institution admitted to, university affiliation, course name, enrollment number, organisation address, website, course duration (in years and semester), and date of admission. - Specify who accredited the course/institution, their name, and address. Proof of accreditation must be submitted. - Select &quot;Yes&quot; or &quot;No&quot; for &quot;Have you obtained admission through government-mandated admission?&quot;. - If staying in a hostel, provide the Hostel name, Hostel address, and Hostel contact number. - Fill in the requested loan amounts for Admission Fee, Tuition Fee, Examination Fee, Hostel Fee, Estimated cost of books/stationery, and Other fees. - List details of any physical assets owned (land, house, government property, NCC, LIC, KVP, etc.). - Indicate if anyone in the family works in a government/private sector job. If &quot;Yes,&quot; provide Name, Designation, Place, and Salary details. - Fill in the Bank Account Number, select the Bank Name from the dropdown, provide the Branch Name, Name as per Account, select Bank Account Type (Saving/Current), and enter the MICR Code and IFSC Code. **Step 3: Review and Final Submission** - Carefully review each module. Use the preview option to examine the application before final submission. Fill out the provided &quot;CAPTCHA&quot; and Click on “Submit”. ***Track and Review Status*** - Applicant will receive an Application ID/Reference Number. Save or print the acknowledgement for future tracking. - Go to the [Official Website](https://gndcdconline.gujarat.gov.in/) Gujarat Nomadic and Denotified Tribes Development Corporation.- Scroll down and locate the Know App Status. Click on &quot;Know App Status&quot;. - Select the Scheme name, Enter App No., and Birth Date. Fill out the Captcha and click on Submit. 
Mode: Online
