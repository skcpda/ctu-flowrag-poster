# Basic Agriculture Training Centre Scheme

**Short Title:** BATCS

**Scheme Brief Description:** The objective of the scheme is to provide short-term vocational training for rural educated unemployed youths in agriculture and allied sectors to develop entrepreneurship skills and employment generation.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Agriculture,Rural & Environment; Sector: Entrepreneurship development

**Where it applies:** Meghalaya; State/ UT

**Detailed Description:** The “Basic Agriculture Training Centre Scheme” is a State Plan Scheme, which is implemented by the Department of Agriculture &amp;amp; Farmers’ Welfare, Government of Meghalaya. The objective of the scheme is to provide short-term vocational training for rural educated unemployed youths in agriculture and allied sectors to develop entrepreneurship skills and employment generation.

**Benefits / Assistance:** > **Pattern of Assistance:** - Free short-term training to educated unemployed rural youths. > **Type of Assistance and Entitlement (If Any):** - Training and capacity building. - Stipend @ ₹500/- per participant per month. - Seed money @ ₹10,000/- per participant.

**Application Process:** &gt; **Application Process:** **Step 01:** The eligible Educated Rural Youths can apply/write an application on plain paper. **Step 02:** Along with the application, attach copies of all the mandatory documents (self-attest, if required). **Step 03:** Submit the duly filled and signed application along with the relevant documents at the office of the Principal, Basic Agricultural Training Centre (BATC). &gt; **Mode of Selection of Beneficiary(s):** The selection procedure is based on merit and on the condition that applicants own agricultural land on which they can start their farm-based activities. &gt; **Whom to Contact:** 1. District Agriculture Officers of the concerned districts. 1. Sub-Divisional Agricultural Officers of the concerned sub-divisions. 1. District Training Officers, Tura/Shillong. 1. Agricultural Development Officers of the concerned Community and Rural Development Blocks.
Mode: Offline

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: Department Of Agriculture & Farmers’ Welfare, Government Of Meghalaya; Nodal Department: Agriculture Department
