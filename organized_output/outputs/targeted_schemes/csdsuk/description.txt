# Coaching Scheme for Divyang Students

**Short Title:** CSDSUK

**Scheme Brief Description:** The scheme "Coaching Scheme for Divyang Students" by the National Institute for the Empowerment of Persons with Visual Disabilities (NIEPVD), Dehradun, aims to support students with disabilities in preparing for competitive government examinations.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Citizen empowerment

**Where it applies:** Uttarakhand; State/ UT

**Detailed Description:** The scheme &amp;quot;Coaching Scheme for Divyang Students&amp;quot; by the National Institute for the Empowerment of Persons with Visual Disabilities (NIEPVD), Dehradun, aims to empower students with benchmark disabilities by supporting their preparation for competitive exams such as SSC, RRB, Banking, Insurance, and UPSC. Through this scheme, free coaching, book allowance, transport/hostel allowance, disability and other allowances are provided to eligible candidates. The scheme is implemented by NIEPVD.

**Benefits / Assistance:** > **Financial Allowances:** - Coaching fees. - Transport or hostel allowance. - Disability allowance. - Book allowance.

**Exclusions / Not eligible:** - The applicant should not have availed benefits under any other coaching scheme of the Center or State Government.
