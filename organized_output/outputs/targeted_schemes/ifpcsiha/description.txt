# Construction of Ideal Fish Pond Scheme in Hilly Areas

**Short Title:** CIFPSHA

**Scheme Brief Description:** The scheme aims to promote sustainable fish farming through cluster-based pond development. It provides 50% subsidy for pond construction and first-year inputs to eligible individuals and groups.

**Who is this for:** Individual, Self Help Groups (SHGS); Category: Agriculture,Rural & Environment; Sector: Fishing and hunting

**Where it applies:** Uttarakhand; State/ UT

**DBT Scheme:** Yes (Direct Benefit Transfer)

**Detailed Description:** The Scheme &amp;quot;Construction of Ideal Fish Pond Scheme in Hilly Areas&amp;quot; was launched by the Department of Fisheries, Government of Uttarakhand. The scheme aims to promote sustainable fish production by utilizing water resources effectively in hilly regions through cluster-based pond development. Under this scheme, financial support of up to 50% of the cost is provided for the construction of ponds and first-year investments such as fish fingerlings and feed to all caste categories in hilly areas. It also includes free technical assistance, optional free training, and marketing support through departmental platforms like Uttarafish.

**Benefits / Assistance:** **Subsidy for Pond Construction:** - Individual beneficiaries: Up to ₹2,50,000/- for pond capacity up to 500 cubic meters. - Committee/Group/Mahasandh/Federation/Mangal Dal: Up to ₹5,00,000/- for pond capacity up to 1,000 cubic meters. **Subsidy for First-Year Inputs:** - 50% subsidy for purchasing fish fingerlings and feed. **Technical Assistance:** - Free technical guidance for high-growth fish species. - Optional free training in fishery operations. **Marketing Support:** - Option to sell fish independently or via &amp;quot;Uttarafish&amp;quot;, a state-supported fish marketing platform. **Cluster Requirement:** - A minimum of 10 ponds must be constructed in one location, either by an individual or by a group. **Self-Financing/Loan Provision** - The remaining 50% investment is to be made by the beneficiary through personal funds or bank loans. &gt; **NOTE:** 1. The subsidy is paid to the beneficiary&amp;#39;s account through DBT. 1. Applicants can avail grants either individually or through registered groups and cooperatives, with additional funding covered through self-investment or bank loans.

**Application Process:** > **Application Process:** **Step 1:** The interested applicant should visit (during office hours) the Assistant Director Fisheries/District Fisheries In-Charge at the district level and request a hard copy of the prescribed format of the application form from the staff exclusively entrusted to issue and collect filled-in applications. **Step 2:** In the application form, fill in all the mandatory fields and attach copies of all the mandatory documents (self-attest, if required). **Step 3:** Submit the duly filled and signed application form along with the documents to the Assistant Director Fisheries/District Fisheries In-charge at the district level. &gt; **Post-application Process:** **Step 1:** A fisheries inspector/field officer conducts a site survey and prepares a proposal in collaboration with the applicant. **Step 2:** The proposal is reviewed and approved by the concerned district fisheries officer. **Step 3:** Pond construction is carried out under the supervision of field personnel. Technical assistance is provided throughout. **Step 4:** On completion, based on the work completion certificate, the subsidy is released via DBT. In case of loans, subsidy is disbursed through the bank. &gt; **Note:** If the applicant lacks 50% of the required funds, a loan can be availed from nearby cooperative or commercial banks. Department-provided proposals are submitted to the bank for loan approval.
Mode: Offline
