# Special Health Scheme for Primitive Tribes

**Short Title:** SHSPT

**Scheme Brief Description:** The "Special Health Scheme for Primitive Tribes" scheme by the Welfare Department, Government of Jharkhand, aims to improve healthcare services for primitive tribal communities. Financial assistance of ₹38,41,632 is provided for targeted healthcare benefits.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Health & Wellness; Sector: Health care providers and access

**Where it applies:** Jharkhand; State/ UT

**Detailed Description:** The &quot;Special Health Scheme for Primitive Tribes&quot; scheme by the Welfare Department, Government of Jharkhand, aims to improve healthcare access and outcomes for primitive tribal communities. Through this scheme, financial assistance of ₹38,41,632 is allocated for health-related services and programs to ensure better medical support and infrastructure for the targeted groups.

**Benefits / Assistance:** - Focused healthcare services for primitive tribal communities. - Distribution of funds to districts such as Dumka, Sahebganj, Pakur, and Godda. - Utilization for better medical facilities, treatments, and preventive care programs. - Priority to regions with high tribal populations and pressing health needs.

**Application Process:** **Step 01:** Visit the district welfare office in your region and obtain the application form for the Special Health Scheme for Primitive Tribes. **Step 02:** Fill in the required details, including personal, income, and community information. **Step 03:** Attach the necessary documents. **Step 04:** Submit the completed form to the welfare officer and keep the acknowledgment receipt for future reference.
