# National Award For Senior Citizens (Vayoshreshtha Samman): Best State In Implementing The Maintenance And Welfare Of Parents And Senior Citizens Act, 2007, And Providing Services And Facilities To Senior Citizens

**Short Title:** NASS(VS)BSIMWPSCAPSFSC

**Scheme Brief Description:** The "Vayoshreshtha Samman: Best State in Implementing the Maintenance and Welfare of Parents and Senior Citizens Act, 2007, and Providing Services and Facilities to Senior Citizens" award is given to the State/UT which has taken the lead in implementing various provisions of the act.

**Who is this for:** State Government; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Financial assistance

**Where it applies:** Central

**Detailed Description:** Every year, the Department of Social Justice and Empowerment, Government of India confers the &amp;quot;National Award for Senior Citizens (Vayoshreshtha Samman)&amp;quot; to the eminent and outstanding senior citizens and institutions/organizations involved in rendering distinguished service for the cause of elderly persons, especially indigent senior citizens. The awards are conferred on 1st October every year by the Hon’ble President of India at Vigyan Bhawan, New Delhi. The **&amp;quot;Vayoshreshtha Samman: Best State in Implementing the Maintenance and Welfare of Parents and Senior Citizens Act, 2007, and Providing Services and Facilities to Senior Citizens&amp;quot;** award shall be given to the State or Union Territory which has taken the lead in implementing various provisions of the Act and has the highest percentage of settlement of claims of maintenance or best security programme for the elderly. A total of 11 awards are given along with a Certificate, a Memento and also Cash Award. The total number of Awardees is 15 (Fifteen). The Awards are given across 13 categories: &lt;br&gt; &gt; **INSTITUTIONAL CATEGORY** 1. Best Institution for Research in the field of Ageing 1. Best Institution for providing services to Senior Citizens and Awareness Generation 1. Best District Panchayat in providing services and facilities to Senior Citizens 1. Best Urban Local Body in providing services and facilities to Senior Citizens 1. Best State in implementing the Maintenance and Welfare of Parents and Senior Citizens Act, 2007, and providing services and facilities to Senior Citizens 1. Best Private Sector Organisation in promoting the well-being and welfare of Senior Citizens 1. Best Public Sector Organisation in promoting the well-being and welfare of Senior Citizens &lt;br&gt; &gt; **INDIVIDUAL CATEGORY** 1. Centenarian 1. Iconic Mother 1. Lifetime Achievement 1. Creative Art 1. Sports and Adventure (one each for Male and Female) 1. Courage &amp;amp; Bravery (one each for Male and Female)

**Benefits / Assistance:** > **Decoration** The Awardee is given a Citation and Memento.  > **Presentation of Award (Ceremony Details)** 1. The Award shall be presented in New Delhi at a special ceremony/function to be held on the occasion of the International Day of Older Persons on the 1st of October every year. 1. The Awards shall be presented by the President of India or the Prime Minister of India. 1. The **boarding and lodging expenses** of the awardees including one companion for each awardee shall be borne by the Central Government.1. The **to-and-fro travelling expenses** and **accommodation for up to one day** prior to and after the date of the Samman ceremony (depending upon the actual arrival and departure dates) shall be arranged and the expenditure shall be reimbursed by the Central Government.1. The **TA/ DA** admissibility of the awardees and one companion shall be equivalent to that of the Joint Secretary to the Government of India.
