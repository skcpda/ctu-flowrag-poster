# Scheme to Increase Seed Replacement Rate (SRR)

**Short Title:** STISRR

**Scheme Brief Description:** The "Scheme to Increase Seed Replacement Rate (SRR)" by the Agriculture and Co-operation Department, Government of Gujarat, aims to improve crop productivity through providing certified seeds at subsidized rates to all farmers across the state.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Agriculture,Rural & Environment; Sector: Agricultural Inputs- seeds, fertilizer etc.

**Where it applies:** Gujarat; State/ UT

**Detailed Description:** The &amp;quot;Scheme to Increase Seed Replacement Rate (SRR)&amp;quot; by the Agriculture and Co-operation Department, Government of Gujarat, aims to enhance the Seed Replacement Rate among farmers by distributing certified seeds of various agricultural crops. This initiative is supported under the centrally sponsored National Food Security Mission (NFSM) and NFSM-Oilseeds schemes. The goal is to provide access to quality seeds and promote better agricultural practices for increased yield and income. 

**Benefits / Assistance:** - **Seed Distribution:** Certified seeds are distributed to eligible farmers.
