# State Agriculture Thesis Grant for M.Sc. Agriculture

**Short Title:** SATGMSA

**Scheme Brief Description:** The scheme “State Agriculture Thesis Grant for M.Sc. Agriculture” was launched by the Department of Agriculture, Government of Nagaland to provide a Thesis Grant to all the inhabitants of Nagaland who passed the M.Sc. Agriculture programme.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Education & Learning; Sector: Education and training grants, fellowship, stipend

**Where it applies:** Nagaland; State/ UT

**Detailed Description:** The scheme “State Agriculture Thesis Grant for M.Sc. Agriculture” was launched by the Department of Agriculture, Government of Nagaland to provide a Thesis Grant to all the inhabitants of Nagaland who passed the M.Sc. Agriculture programme under the State Plan of Education &amp;amp; Training.

**Benefits / Assistance:** 1. Under the scheme, the Thesis Grant will be given to students according to the course/programme in which they are enrolled.
