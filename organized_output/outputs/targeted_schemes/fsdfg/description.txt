# Free Scooty Distribution for Girls (GEN-CATEGAORY-EWS)

**Short Title:** FSDFG

**Scheme Brief Description:** Under this scheme, meritorious girl students from government and private schools who have scored 85% or above in the Class 10th and 12th (Arts, Commerce, Science streams) examinations are provided with a free scooty, along with a helmet and petrol.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Education & Learning

**Where it applies:** Rajasthan; State/ UT

**Detailed Description:** The &quot;Free Scooty Distribution for Girls&quot; scheme was launched by the Secondary Education Department, Government of Rajasthan in the year 2017-18, aims to encourage meritorious girl students belonging to the economically weaker section of the general category in the state. Under this scheme, meritorious girl students from government and private schools who have scored 85% or above in the Class 10th and 12th (Arts, Commerce, Science streams) examinations are provided with a free scooty, along with a helmet and petrol.

**Benefits / Assistance:** Under this scheme, Eligible girl students will receive a scooty, a helmet, and 2 liters of petrol.

**Exclusions / Not eligible:** - The applicant should not have received a scooty through any other scheme.

**Application Process:** ***Application process:*** **Step 1:** The interested applicant visits the [official website](https://evaluation.rajasthan.gov.in/scheme/detail/337) and clicks on the application form to download it. **Step 2:** In the application form, fill in all the mandatory fields and attach copies of all the mandatory documents (self-attested, if required). **Step 3:** Submit the duly filled and signed application form along with the documents to the respective school where the education is completed.
Mode: Offline
