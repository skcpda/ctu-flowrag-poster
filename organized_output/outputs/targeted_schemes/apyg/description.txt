# Ambubhai Purani Award

**Short Title:** APYG

**Scheme Brief Description:** The scheme "Ambubhai Purani Award" by the Sports, Youth and Cultural Activities Department, Gujarat, aims to recognize outstanding contributions in the field of gymnasiums (Akhadas) through the conferment of the Gymnasium Jyotirdhar Ambubhai Purani Award and financial assistance of ₹51,000/-.

**Who is this for:** Sportsperson, Individual, NGO; Category: Sports & Culture; Sector: Sports

**Where it applies:** Gujarat; State/ UT

**Detailed Description:** The scheme &amp;quot;Ambubhai Purani Award&amp;quot; by the Sports, Youth and Cultural Activities Department, Government of Gujarat, aims to promote the development of gymnasiums and traditional Indian physical culture by honoring individuals who have shown exceptional contribution in this field. The scheme provides financial assistance of ₹51,000/- and state-level recognition to outstanding individuals, fostering motivation and continuity in preserving indigenous physical training traditions. The scheme is implemented by the Sports Authority of Gujarat.

**Benefits / Assistance:** - **Financial Assistance:** ₹51,000/-.
