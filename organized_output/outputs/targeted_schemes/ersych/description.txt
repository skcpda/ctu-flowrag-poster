# E-Rikshaw Sahayta Yojana

**Short Title:** ERSY

**Scheme Brief Description:** Chhattisgarh State Government started a scheme "E-Rikshaw Sahayta Yojana" for unorganized worker in 20 December 2016.

**Launch Date:** 2016-12-20

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Financial assistance

**Where it applies:** Chhattisgarh; State

**Detailed Description:** Chhattisgarh State Government started a scheme &quot;E-Rikshaw Sahayta Yojana&quot; for unorganized worker in 20 December 2016. The main objective of the scheme to empower the unorganized worker through provide them financial assistance.

**Benefits / Assistance:** **Financial Assistance** 1. Registered unorganized labor individuals will need to cover the expenses of ₹ 10,000 for scheme execution. 1. A grant of ₹ 30,000/- will be provided by the Chhattisgarh Unorganized Worker State Social Security Board. 1. The remaining sum will be extended to the Rikshaw/Auto Driver as a bank loan, with the beneficiary responsible for repaying it within a specified timeframe and at the interest rate determined by the bank. 1. The Board will only transfer its contribution to the beneficiary&#39;s account once all the necessary loan-related documents are submitted to the board.
