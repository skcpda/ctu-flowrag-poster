# Chanakya Scheme for Swayam Upadhi

**Short Title:** CSSU

**Scheme Brief Description:** The "Chanakya Scheme for Swayam Upadhi” to encourage Brahmin entrepreneurs in Andhra Pradesh. The grant will be given against relevant approved asset acquisition for setting up new business entities or expanding existing/diversifying business entities in different areas.

**Who is this for:** Business Entity; Category: Business & Entrepreneurship; Sector: Micro finance

**Where it applies:** Andhra Pradesh; State/ UT

**Detailed Description:** Andhra Pradesh Brahmin Welfare Corporation (ABC) Limited, a Government of Andhra Pradesh undertaking is introducing the “Chanakya Scheme for Swayam Upadhi” (CS-SU) to encourage Brahmin entrepreneurs in Andhra Pradesh. Under this scheme, one-time financial assistance upto ₹2,00,000/- (not exceeding 50% of the project cost) for projects costing between ₹3,00,000/- and ₹10,00,000/- will be considered.  **Objective:** To encourage Brahmins to set up Micro Enterprises in Andhra Pradesh in various sectors like Catering, Education, Electronics, Healthcare (Clinic, Diagnostics, Pharmacy, Physiotherapy), Engineering, IT &amp; ITES (Coaching, Development, DTP, Repairs), Photography /Videography, Pooja Materials, Retail, Ritual Tools and equipment, Solar, Telecom, Textiles, etc.

**Benefits / Assistance:** - One-time financial assistance upto ₹2,00,000/-. **NOTE:** Not exceeding 50% of the project cost.

**Application Process:** > **Registration and apply** **Step 01:** Applicants have to visit the official website(https://www.andhrabrahmin.ap.gov.in/schemes/schemes.aspx#) and click on &quot;Scheme&quot; **Step 02:** Click on &quot;Registration&quot; After that application form will open **Step 03:** Fill out the required details: - Personal Details: Name, Residence address, Aadhaar no, Ration card no, Bank detail etc - Upload: Photo, Signature and Certificate Details - Documents: Birth Certificate, Residential Certificate, Aadhar Card, Caste Certificate, Bank Passbook etc - Finalize and &quot;Submit&quot; the application. **Step-4:** After submission receive the reference number.  > **Check Application Status:** **Step 01:** Click on the “Services” link on the menu and select &quot;Know your status&quot;. **Step 02:** Enter the Reference or Mobile or Aadhaar Card Number to get the application status. **Step 03:** Select the &quot;Financial Year&quot; and Fetch the details.  > **Download the Acknowledgement slip:** **Step 01:** Click on the &quot;Acknowledgement Slip&quot; **Step 02:** Enter the Aadhaar Card Number and select the &quot;Scheme&quot; **Step 03:** Click on the &quot;Submit&quot;
Mode: Online
