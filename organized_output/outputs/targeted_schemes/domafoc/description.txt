# Distribution of Minikits At Free of Cost

**Short Title:** DOMAFOC

**Scheme Brief Description:** The "Distribution of Minikits at Free of Cost" scheme provides subsidies for minikits to maize-growing farmers in some of the selected districts of Tamil Nadu.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Agriculture,Rural & Environment; Sector: Agricultural Inputs- seeds, fertilizer etc.

**Where it applies:** Tamil Nadu; State/ UT

**Detailed Description:** The “Distribution of Minikits at Free of Cost” scheme by the Agriculture-Farmers Welfare Department, Tamil Nadu, provides subsidies for minikits to maize-growing farmers in specific districts including Coimbatore, Dindigul, Erode, Dharmapuri, Krishnagiri, Namakkal, Perambalur, Virudhunagar, Theni, Villupuram, Vellore, Salem, Pudukkottai, Madurai, Sivagangai, Trichy, Tirunelveli, and Thoothukudi.

**Benefits / Assistance:** - **Quantum of Assistance:** Subsidy for Minikits. 

**Application Process:** **Step 1:** Collect the application form from the nearest Agricultural Office. **Step 2:** Fill out the application form with the required details and attach mandatory documents (self-attested, if required). **Step 3:** Submit the form to the concerned office: &gt; **Village Level:** Submit the application to the Assistant Agricultural Officer. &gt; **Block Level:** Submit to the Agricultural Officer or Deputy Agricultural Officer. &gt; **District Level:** Submit to the Joint Director of Agriculture. &lt;br&gt;
