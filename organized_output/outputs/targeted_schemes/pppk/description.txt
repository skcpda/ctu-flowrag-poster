# Prak Pariksha Prashikshan Kendra

**Short Title:** PPPK

**Scheme Brief Description:** The scheme provides free pre-examination training to eligible candidates for various competitive exams to enhance their preparation and improve their chances of success.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Education & Learning

**Where it applies:** Bihar; State/ UT

**Detailed Description:** The &amp;quot;Prak Pariksha Prashikshan Kendra&amp;quot; is an initiative launched by the Backward Classes and Extremely Backward Classes Welfare Department, Bihar, aimed at supporting underprivileged candidates aspiring to appear for competitive exams. It offers free coaching for exams like Civil Services, SSC, and others. The program includes expert guidance, study materials, and mentoring, enabling candidates to prepare effectively and achieve their career goals. &lt;br&gt; &lt;br&gt;

**Benefits / Assistance:** - **Free Coaching:** Free coaching in [Prak Pariksha Prashikshan Kendras](https://state.bihar.gov.in/bcebcwelfare/cache/41/30-Dec-24/SHOW_DOCS/Letter%20No-I-179552.pdf). - **Incentive:** Students will be provided an incentive amount of ₹3,000/- (three thousand rupees) based on 75% attendance. **Note:****** There is no hostel provision in the centers.

**Application Process:** **Step-1:** Download the [application form](https://state.bihar.gov.in/bcebcwelfare/cache/41/30-Dec-24/SHOW_DOCS/Letter%20No-I-179552.pdf). **Step-2:** In the application form, fill in all the mandatory fields, paste the passport-sized photograph (signed across, if required), and attach copies of all the mandatory documents (self-attest, if required). **Step-3:** The application must be submitted to the concerned Director, Pre-Exam Training Center, via registered mail or speed post. Applicants may also personally submit the application form at the office of the concerned Pre-Exam Training Center (Refer page no 3 of the [Guidelines ](https://state.bihar.gov.in/bcebcwelfare/cache/41/30-Dec-24/SHOW_DOCS/Letter%20No-I-179552.pdf)to see the training center list).
Mode: Online

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: BC & EBC Welfare Department
