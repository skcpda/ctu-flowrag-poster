# Sanitary Napkin Scheme (UKBOCWWB)

**Short Title:** SNS(UKBOCWWB)

**Scheme Brief Description:** The scheme aims to provide free sanitary napkins to the daughters of registered construction workers and to women construction workers, in order to promote health and hygiene.

**Who is this for:** Individual, Family; Category: Health & Wellness; Sector: Menstrual hygiene

**Where it applies:** Uttarakhand; State/ UT

**Detailed Description:** The scheme “Sanitary Napkin Scheme” is implemented by the Uttarakhand Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board (UKBOCWWB) under the Labour Department, Government of Uttarakhand. The scheme aims to provide free sanitary napkins to the daughters of registered construction workers and to women construction workers, in order to promote health and hygiene. 

**Benefits / Assistance:** 1. Under the scheme, free sanitary napkins will be provided to the daughters of registered construction workers and to women construction workers. 

**Application Process:** - The online application can be submitted through Shramik Suvidha Kendras. - Along with the application form, applicants must attach a self-attested photocopy of the Shramik Card, Aadhaar Card, A Self-Declaration Certificate stating that no similar benefit has been availed from any other department. - After verification, sanitary napkins are provided by the department. 
Mode: Online - via CSC

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: Uttarakhand Building And Other Construction Workers’ Welfare Board; Nodal Department: Department Of Labour
