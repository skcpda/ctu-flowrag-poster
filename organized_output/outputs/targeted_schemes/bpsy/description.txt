# Minimata Mahtari Jatan Yojana

**Short Title:** BPSY

**Scheme Brief Description:** The Labor Department of Chhattisgarh started this scheme on March 4, 2010, under the "Building and Construction Workers Welfare Board" for female construction workers. Under the scheme registered female construction workers will receive benefits during pregnancy.

**Launch Date:** 2010-03-04

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Health & Wellness; Sector: Pregnancy care

**Where it applies:** Chhattisgarh; State

**DBT Scheme:** Yes (Direct Benefit Transfer)

**Detailed Description:** The Labor Department of Chhattisgarh introduced the &quot;Minimata Mahtari Jatan Yojana&quot; on March 4, 2010, through the &quot;Building and Construction Workers Welfare Board.&quot; This initiative is designed to provide benefits to registered female construction workers during pregnancy. The scheme applies to both male and female beneficiaries who are registered and possess a beneficiary identity card at least 90 days before the scheme application date.  **Note:** The name of this scheme was changed from &quot;Bhagini Prasuti Sahayata Yojana&quot; to &quot;Minimata Mahtari Jatan Yojana&quot; on May 12, 2022. 

**Benefits / Assistance:** > A lump sum amount of ₹20,000/- will be provided to the registered female construction worker after the birth of the child.

**Exclusions / Not eligible:** - Spouses of construction workers employed by public or government institutions. - Construction worker beneficiaries who lack valid membership with the Board.

Step-5: Click on submit only after uploading all the important information and documents given in Part 1, Part 2, Part 3.
