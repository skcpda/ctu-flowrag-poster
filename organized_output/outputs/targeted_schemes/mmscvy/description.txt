# Mukhyamantri Samekit Chaur Vikas Yojana

**Short Title:** MMSCVY

**Scheme Brief Description:** The "Mukhyamantri Samekit Chaur Vikas Yojana" under Seven Nishchay-2, aims to develop undeveloped and unutilized private land in Bihar for fish-based integrated aquaculture. With a budget of ₹2972.79 lakh, the scheme targets the sustainable development of 600 hectares of Chaur land, enhancing fish.

**Launch Date:** 2023-07-05

**Who is this for:** Individual, Family, Business Entity; Category: Agriculture,Rural & Environment

**Where it applies:** Bihar; State/ UT

**Detailed Description:** The &quot;Mukhyamantri Samekit Chaur Vikas Yojana&quot; was launched by the Animal and Fisheries Resources Department, Government of Bihar. under Seven Nishchay-2, aims to develop undeveloped and unutilized private land in Bihar for fish-based integrated aquaculture. With a budget of ₹2972.79 lakh, the scheme targets the sustainable development of 600 hectares of Chaur land  ***Objective:*** - Utilization of unutilized private land for fish farming. - Increase in fish production and productivity. - Groundwater level improvement, supporting the &quot;Jal-Jeevan-Hariyali&quot; campaign. - Additional employment opportunities for farmers. - Strengthening of the local economy.

**Benefits / Assistance:** > **Beneficiary-based subsidy:** - 50% for other categories. - 70% for Most Backward Castes, Scheduled Castes, and Scheduled Tribes. - 40% Entrepreneur-based subsidy.

**Application Process:** > ***Registration Process:*** **Step-01:** The applicant visits the [official website](https://fisheries.bihar.gov.in/Default.aspx) and clicks the &quot;Applications are being invited for all the schemes of the financial year (2024-25), [click here](https://fisheries.bihar.gov.in/Accordion.aspx) to apply&quot;. **Step-02:** Select the &quot;[Registration for Fisheries Schemes](https://misfisheries.bihar.gov.in/RegisterFisheries.aspx)&quot; **Step-03:** Select the category: Individual and complete all basic details of the applicant for registration, including category, caste, applicant name, date of birth, address, bank details, etc. **Step-04:** Enter a valid 10-digit mobile number and click to send OTP. **Step-05:** An OTP will be sent to the provided mobile number. **Step-06:** Enter the OTP; after verification of the OTP, you will receive a Registration Number and Password. **Step-07:** Click on the &quot;Login&quot; and enter the Registration Number and Password  > ***Application Form Filling Process:*** **Step-01:** The applicant visits the [official website](https://fisheries.bihar.gov.in/Default.aspx) and clicks the &quot;Applications are being invited for all the schemes of the financial year (2024-25), [click here](https://fisheries.bihar.gov.in/Accordion.aspx) to apply&quot;. **Step-02:** Click &quot;If your already register, [login here](https://misfisheries.bihar.gov.in/login.aspx)&quot;. **Step-03**: Select &quot;Application form&quot; under the &quot;scheme name&quot; option on the top menu. **Step-04:** Applicant need to provide the following details : - Personal details. - Permanent address. - Bank account details. - Land details. - Financial availability of the scheme. - Agriculture, horticulture and agroforestry components **Step-05**: Upload the required documents - Passport size photographs. - Aadhaar card. - Land ownership certificate/revenue receipt or lease agreement, etc. **Step-06:** Click on the submit button and a receipt will be generated.
Mode: Online
