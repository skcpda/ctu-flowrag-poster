# Mukhyamantri Nirman Shramikon Ke Bachon Hetu Nihshulk Coaching Sahaayata Yojana

**Short Title:** MNSKBHNCSY

**Scheme Brief Description:** The Labor Department of Chhattisgarh started this scheme on March 1, 2023, under the "Building and Construction Workers Welfare Board." The scheme provides free coaching assistance for children of construction workers.

**Launch Date:** 2023-03-01

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Education & Learning; Sector: Coaching

**Where it applies:** Chhattisgarh; State/ UT

**Detailed Description:** The Labor Department of Chhattisgarh introduced the &quot;Mukhyamantri Nirman Shramikon Ke Bachon Hetu Nihshulk Coaching Sahaayata Yojana&quot; on March 1, 2023, through the &quot;Building and Construction Workers Welfare Board.&quot; Under this scheme, free coaching assistance will be provided to the children of registered workers. Only the first two children of the worker will be eligible for this scheme.

**Benefits / Assistance:** - Free coaching assistance will be provided to the children of registered workers.  **Note:** Construction workers and their children registered under the Board are allowed to appear for competitive examinations as per educational qualification like PSC (Public Service Commission), CG VYAPAM (Chhattisgarh Professional Examination Board), SSC (Staff Selection Commission), IBPS (Banking), RAILWAY (Railway). ), There is a provision to provide free coaching through selected coaching institutes for POLICE ENTRANCE (Police Recruitment Examination).
