# Transgender Utthan Kosh - College/Higher/Technical Educational Fees Refund for Transgender Students

**Short Title:** TUK-CHTERTS

**Scheme Brief Description:** The scheme aims to promote higher and technical education among transgender individuals by providing financial assistance for their educational expenses. Under this scheme, transgender students receive a refund of their school, college, higher, or technical education fees.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Education & Learning; Sector: Scholarships and student finance

**Where it applies:** Rajasthan; State/ UT

**Detailed Description:** The &amp;quot;Transgender Utthan Kosh - College/Higher/Technical Educational Fees Refund for Transgender Students&amp;quot; scheme was introduced by the Social Justice and Empowerment Department, Government of Rajasthan. It aims to promote higher and technical education among transgender individuals by providing financial assistance for their educational expenses. Under this scheme, transgender students receive a refund of their school, college, higher, or technical education fees.

**Benefits / Assistance:** ***Educational Fee Refund:*** - Full reimbursement of prescribed fees is provided for transgender students enrolled in schools, colleges, or technical institutes pursuing higher or technical education. ***Direct Bank Transfer:*** - The amount is credited directly to the beneficiary&#39;s bank account after verification.

**Application Process:** ***Application Submission:*** - The applicant must fill out the scholarship application form and attach the required documents. - Submit the complete application form to the head of the educational institution. ***Institutional Verification:*** - The head of the educational institution verifies the application and forwards it to the Social Justice and Empowerment Department&#39;s district office. ***Approval Process:*** - The district office verifies the documents and approves/rejects the application within one month. ***Disbursement:*** - Upon approval, the scholarship amount is transferred to the applicant&#39;s bank account.
