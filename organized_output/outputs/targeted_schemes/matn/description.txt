# Marriage Assistance

**Short Title:** MATN

**Scheme Brief Description:** The Government of Tamil Nadu introduced "Marriage Assistance" for the employees or their Son/Daughter who legally attain the age of marriage.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Financial assistance

**Where it applies:** Tamil Nadu; State

**Detailed Description:** The Government of Tamil Nadu introduced &quot;Marriage Assistance&quot; with a sum of Rs.10,000/- for both male and female is given as Marriage Assistance to employees or their Son/Daughter who legally attain the age of marriage.

**Benefits / Assistance:** A sum of Rs.10,000/- for both male and female is given as Marriage Assistance to employees or their Son/Daughter who legally attain the age of marriage.

**Application Process:** Applicants who require the [application form](https://www.lwb.tn.gov.in/forms/Marriage_assistance.pdf) in person or self-addressed Rs.- Postage It can be obtained through the enclosed envelope.
