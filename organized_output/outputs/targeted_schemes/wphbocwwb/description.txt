# Widow Pension (HBOCWWB)

**Short Title:** WPHBOCWWB

**Scheme Brief Description:** Under this scheme, a monthly pension of ₹3,000/- is given to the widow of a registered construction worker who is not receiving a similar benefit from any other Government/ Department/ Board/ Corporation /PSU.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Pension

**Where it applies:** Haryana; State/ UT

**Detailed Description:** The scheme “Widow Pension” is implemented by the Haryana Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board (HBOCWWB), Labour Department, Government of Haryana. Under this scheme, a monthly pension of ₹3,000/- is given to the widow of a registered construction worker who is not receiving a similar benefit from any other Government/ Department/ Board/Corporation/PSU.

**Benefits / Assistance:** 1. Under the scheme, ₹3,000/- per month is given as a widow pension to the widow of the registered construction worker.

**Application Process:** > **Registration for deceased Construction worker under HBOCWWB:** **Step 01:** The applicant should visit the [Official Website ](https://hrylabour.gov.in/)of the Labour Department Haryana. **Step 02:** On the home page, click on the “Building &amp;amp; Ors Const. Workers Welfare Board” link. A new window page will be opened. If you don&amp;#39;t have login details then click on “[Click Here](https://hrylabour.gov.in/bocw/Basicinfo/bocw_terms)” to register. **Step 03:** Applicant has to read all the instructions and then “Tick” the acknowledgment, then click on the “Submit” button. **Step 04:** Select “I have Family ID” and enter Parivar Pehchan Patra Number (Family ID). Relevant updates will be sent to the registered mobile number.
