# Mahila Adhikarita Yojana (Gujarat Safai Kamdar Development Corporation)

**Short Title:** MAYGSKDC

**Scheme Brief Description:** "Mahila Adhikarita Yojana" introduced by the Social Justice and Empowerment Department, Gujarat, is a centrally sponsored financial support scheme offering loans of ₹20,000/- at a subsidized interest rate to women sweepers and their dependents to promote self-reliance and upliftment.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Loan

**Where it applies:** Gujarat; State/ UT

**Detailed Description:** &amp;quot;Mahila Adhikarita Yojana&amp;quot; introduced by the Social Justice and Empowerment Department, Gujarat, is a centrally sponsored financial support scheme targeting women sweepers and their dependents. Under this scheme, eligible beneficiaries are provided a loan of ₹20,000 at a low interest rate of 5%, with a repayment period of 5 years. This initiative aims to empower women engaged in sanitation work by providing them with the financial means to pursue economic activities and improve their quality of life. The scheme is being implemented by the Gujarat Safai Kamdar Development Corporation.

**Benefits / Assistance:** - **Loan Assistance:** An amount of ₹20,000/- at 5% interest rate, with a repayment period of 5 years.

Please Register Here&quot;. **Step 4:** Enter the required details and click Register.  > **Application Process:** **Step 1:** Enter your User ID, Password, and Captcha Code, then click &quot;Login&quot;. **Step 2:** Read the scheme details carefully and click OK. **Step 3:** If your permanent address is correct, confirm it. Otherwise, enter your correct address. **Step 4:** Click Save &amp; Next to proceed. Upload the relevant documents. **Step 5:** If you need to make changes, click &quot;No, Cancel&quot;.
