# Mukhyamantri Mahalakshmi Kit Yojana

**Short Title:** MMMKYUK

**Scheme Brief Description:** The scheme aims to improve the health and well-being of both mothers and their newborn girl children by providing essential item kits after the institutional delivery of pregnant women registered at an Anganwadi Centre.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Health & Wellness; Sector: Childcare

**Where it applies:** Uttarakhand; State/ UT

**Detailed Description:** The scheme &amp;quot;Mukhyamantri Mahalakshmi Kit Yojana&amp;quot; was launched by the Department of Women Empowerment &amp;amp; Child Development, Government of Uttarakhand. The scheme aims to improve the health and well-being of both mothers and their newborn girl children by providing essential item kits after the institutional delivery of pregnant women registered at an Anganwadi Centre. 

**Benefits / Assistance:** In the state, after the institutional delivery of a pregnant woman, the following benefits will be provided: - The woman will receive one Mahalakshmi kit for the birth of each of her first two girl children. - In the case of twins, the woman will be given one Mahalakshmi kit, and each child will receive a separate Mahalakshmi kit. 

**Application Process:** After the institutional delivery of a pregnant woman registered at an Anganwadi Centre, a Mahalakshmi Kit is provided as a benefit. The following items are included in the kit for mothers and their newborn girl children: - **For Mothers:** Dry fruits, socks, scarf, towel, shawl, blanket, bedsheet, sanitary pads, mustard oil, soap, and nail cutter.- **For Newborn Girl Child:** Cotton/warm cap, socks, diaper, towel, baby soap, rubber sheet, warm blanket, and vaccination card. 
Mode: Offline

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Department Of Woman Empowerment & Child Development
