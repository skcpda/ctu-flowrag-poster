# Scheme to Provide Monthly Financial Assistance to Elderly and Eligible Writers

**Short Title:** SPMFAEEW

**Scheme Brief Description:** The scheme aims to support senior writers facing financial hardship by offering monthly assistance to those who have made notable contributions to Gujarati or associated language literature.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Sports & Culture; Sector: Culture

**Where it applies:** Gujarat; State/ UT

**Detailed Description:** The &quot;Scheme to Provide Monthly Financial Assistance to Elderly and Eligible Writers&quot; is a welfare initiative implemented by the Gujarat Sahitya Academy to provide financial security to elderly writers who have significantly contributed to Gujarati, Hindi, Sanskrit, Sindhi, Urdu, or Kutchi literature. The scheme offers monthly assistance to writers who are 60 years of age or older, have no steady pension or major income source, and are economically disadvantaged.

**Benefits / Assistance:** - **Monthly Financial Support:** Eligible elderly writers receive monthly financial assistance as specified by the authority.
