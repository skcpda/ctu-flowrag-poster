# Deen Dayal Swasthya Seva Yojana

**Short Title:** DDSSY

**Scheme Brief Description:** To facilitate and take the medical services to the next level, the State of Goa envisaged the present scheme to provide its people with quality medical care and treatment of diseases, hospitalization and surgery through Government and private hospitals.

**Who is this for:** Family; Category: Health & Wellness; Sector: Health Insurance

**Where it applies:** Goa; State/ UT

**Detailed Description:** To facilitate and take medical services to the next level, the State of Goa envisaged the present scheme to provide its people with quality medical care and treatment of diseases, hospitalization, and surgery through government and private hospitals. 

**Benefits / Assistance:** **The benefit under this scheme shall be provided on a cashless basis to the beneficiaries, up to the limit of their annual coverage, and shall be as under:** 1. The insurance cover shall be up to ₹2,50,000/- per annum for a family of three or fewer members and up to ₹4,00,000/- for a family of four or more members. Under no case, the claim would exceed ₹4,00,000/- annually. 1. The insurance benefit can be availed of individually or collectively by members of the family. 1. An individual is free to take higher coverage from the insurance company by paying higher premiums, without having any impact on Government policy and facilities. 1. The facilities for 447 procedures shall be covered under the scheme. 

**Application Process:** - The beneficiary may approach the Empanelled Health Care Provider (EHCP) as per the list provided on the website. After consulting the doctor, if the surgery is covered under the scheme and is available at the said EHCP, the patient can approach the DDSSY Sahayak at the hospital with the card, and the Sahayak will raise a request for pre-authorization with the ISA pre-authorization doctor. **Or**- Alternatively, if the beneficiary is aware of what the procedure is, he/she can check the website to find out at which EHCP the required procedure is performed and visit the hospital of his/her choice as per the list of EHCPs empaneled for the said procedure.  > Note: Officer to be contacted:  Sr. No.Name &amp;amp;amp;amp; address of the Nodal OfficerDesignationContact No.1Dr. Yogesh Potdar, Directorate of Health Services, Campal PanajiMedical Management &amp;amp;amp;amp; Quality Manager0832-2225646, Extn. 256, 74477975822Dr. Ralph Araujo, MD India Health Insurance TPA Pvt.
