# Widow B.Ed Scheme-Rajasthan

**Short Title:** WBEDRJ

**Scheme Brief Description:** In 2015-16, Rajasthan State started the "Widow B.Ed Scheme" for Widow/abandoned women.

**Launch Date:** 2016-01-01

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Education & Learning; Sector: Scholarships and student finance

**Where it applies:** Rajasthan; State

**Detailed Description:** The state government of Rajasthan reimburses the course fees for B.Ed. in teacher training institutions for widows or abandoned women who enroll in the program, providing them with much-needed support.

**Benefits / Assistance:** - Financial Assistance : ₹17,880 (Fee Reimbursement).

**Application Process:** 1. Applicants have to visit the [official portal.](https://hte.rajasthan.gov.in/scholarship.php)1. If applicants are not registered users, they need to click on register and complete the registration process. 1. If Applicants are already registered users, they need to click on login with their SSOID &amp; password. 1. Fill out the application form. 1. Provided the required documents. 1. Submit.
Mode: Online

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Higher Education Department

**Tags:** Student, Empowerment, Higher Education, Abandoned Women
