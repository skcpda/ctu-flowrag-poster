# Nirmaan Sthal Par Karya Ke Dauran A-panjeekrat Shramik Ke Mratyu Ki Dasha Me Antyeshti  Evan Anugrah Rashi Bhugatan Yojana

**Short Title:** MARBY

**Scheme Brief Description:** This scheme aims to provide financial assistance to the families of unregistered workers who die during construction work in Madhya Pradesh.

**Launch Date:** 2017-10-10

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Financial assistance

**Where it applies:** Madhya Pradesh; State/ UT

**DBT Scheme:** Yes (Direct Benefit Transfer)

**Detailed Description:** The scheme falls under the purview of the Labour Department of Madhya Pradesh. The scheme provides financial assistance in the form of funeral and ex-gratia payments to the legal heirs of unregistered construction workers who die during construction site work under the MP Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board. The deceased worker does not need to be registered with the Madhya Pradesh Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board.

**Benefits / Assistance:** 1. Funeral assistance of ₹ 3,000/- 1. In case of accidental death ₹ 4,00,000/- 1. In case of permanent disability ₹ 2,00,000/-

**Application Process:** 1. The applicant has to submit the application form in the prescribed format and within the time limit to the office of the designated officer. - Rural area - Chief Executive Officer, District Panchayat - Urban area - Commissioner, Municipal Corporation / Chief Municipal Officer, Municipality / Municipal Council 1. Apply duration - Within 06 months from the death of the construction worker
Mode: Offline

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Labour Department
