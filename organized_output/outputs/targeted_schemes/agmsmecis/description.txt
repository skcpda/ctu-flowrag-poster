# Aatmanirbhar Gujarat Scheme for assistance to MSMEs: Assistance of Capital Investment Subsidy to Micro enterprises

**Short Title:** AGMSMECIS

**Scheme Brief Description:** The "Aatmanirbhar Gujarat Scheme for assistance to MSMEs" aims to empower the state's dynamic MSME sector, which significantly contributes to employment, GDP, and exports.

**Launch Date:** 2022-10-05

**Who is this for:** Business Entity, Industries; Category: Business & Entrepreneurship; Sector: Setting up / start-up / entrepreneurship

**Where it applies:** Gujarat; State/ UT

**Detailed Description:** The &amp;quot;Aatmanirbhar Gujarat Scheme for MSMEs&amp;quot; is an umbrella scheme launched by the Industries and Mines Department, Gujarat. It aims to empower the state&amp;#39;s dynamic MSME sector, which significantly contributes to employment, GDP, and exports. Effective from October 5, 2022, to October 4, 2027, this initiative supports growth, investments, and the vision of &amp;quot;Aatmanirbhar Gujarat&amp;quot; and &amp;quot;Aatmanirbhar Bharat.&amp;quot; The component &amp;quot;Assistance of Capital Investment Subsidy to Micro enterprises&amp;quot; provides a capital investment subsidy to support micro-enterprises in manufacturing. &lt;br&gt;

**Benefits / Assistance:** Financial Assistance **Category of Taluka****Quantum of Incentive** Category 1 25% of term loan amount upto ₹35,00,000/-.Category 220% of term loan amount upto ₹30,00,000/-.Category 3 &amp;amp;amp;amp; Municipal 10% of term loan amount upto INR 10 lakhs. Corporation Areas 10% of term loan amount upto ₹10,00,000/-.

**Exclusions / Not eligible:** - Enterprises that have availed incentives for the same GFCI under any other state government scheme or agency are ineligible unless explicitly allowed. - Applications submitted after one year from the relevant dates (loan disbursement, production commencement, or GR issue date) will not be considered. - Enterprises whose term loans are sanctioned more than one year after the commencement of commercial production are ineligible for the subsidy. &gt; **Note:** **Ineligible Capital Expenditure/ Assets:** The following expenditure shall not be considered for calculating the eligible fixed capital investment: 1. Land and Land development cost. 1. Working capital. 1. Goodwill. 1. Royalty. 1. Preliminary and pre-operative expenses. 1. Indigenous second-hand plant and machinery. 1. Interest capitalized. 1. Power generation, except for captive use. 1. Rented or leased property.

**Application Process:** > **Registration Process:** **Step-1:** Visit the [Investor Facilitation Portal (IFP)](https://ifp.gujarat.gov.in/DIGIGOV/) portal. Click on &quot;New Investor Registration&quot; to access the registration form. **Step-2:** Fill in the Registration Form. Click on &quot;Submit&quot;. **Step-3:** Verify the E-mail ID using the link sent on your registered E-mail ID. **Step-4:** After verification, you will be registered successfully.  > **Post-Registration Process:** **Step-1:** Visit the [Investor Facilitation Portal (IFP)](https://ifp.gujarat.gov.in/DIGIGOV/) portal. Click on &quot;Login&quot;. **Step-2:** Enter the email address and password you used during registration. **Step-3:** Click the &quot;Login&quot; button to access your account. **Step-4:** Now fill in the required details and mandatory documents about your business to complete the application process.  > **Helpdesk:** [**Contact Here.**](https://ifp.gujarat.gov.in/DIGIGOV/IFP-pages/contact.jsp?pagedisp=static)
