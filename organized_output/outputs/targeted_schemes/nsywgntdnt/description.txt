# New Swarnim Yojana (for women only) GNTDNT

**Short Title:** NSYWGNTDNT

**Scheme Brief Description:** The scheme aims to achieve economic upliftment by providing self-employment with dignity. Through this scheme, loans for self-employment at an annual interest rate are provided to women beneficiaries of nomadic and denotified castes living below the poverty line.

**Who is this for:** Family; Category: Business & Entrepreneurship; Sector: Setting up / start-up / entrepreneurship

**Where it applies:** Gujarat; State/ UT

**Detailed Description:** The scheme &quot;New Swarnim Yojana (For Women Only)&quot; by the Social Justice and Empowerment Department, Government of Gujarat, aims to empower women of nomadic and denotified castes living below the poverty line to achieve economic upliftment by getting self-employment with dignity. Under this scheme, women beneficiaries will be able to do business/occupation of their choice. The Gujarat Nomadic and Denotified Tribes Development Corporation implement the scheme. 

**Benefits / Assistance:** 1. The maximum loan limit is up to ₹2,00,000/-. 1. The interest rate will be 5% per annum. 1. Under this scheme, 100% loan of the business/occupation amount is provided. 1. The loan amount will consist of 95% National Corporation contribution, 5% State Government contribution and zero beneficiary contribution.  > **Repayment:** The loan amount, including interest, will have to be repaid in 60 equal monthly instalments. 

**Application Process:** > **Step 1: Visit the Official Portal** - Go to the [Official Website](https://gndcdconline.gujarat.gov.in/) Gujarat Nomadic and Denotified Tribes Development Corporation. - Scroll down on the homepage, find the &quot;New Swarnim Yojana&quot; and click on &quot;Apply Now&quot;. - You will be directed to the [application form](https://gndcdconline.gujarat.gov.in/SchemeEducation/AddEdit). > **Step 2: Access and Fill Out the Application Form** - Complete all sections under &quot;Applicant information,&quot; including full name, father/husband&#39;s name, gender, complete address details, contact numbers, Aadhaar No., parents/husband/guardian occupation and income details. Also provide Date of Birth, Age, Caste, and Subspecies. Also, if you are disabled and provide a percentage. - For each applicable qualification (SSC, HSC, Diploma, Degree, Graduation), tick the checkbox and fill in the Degree, Board/University Name, Academic year, and Percentage obtained. - Fill in details about the college/institution admitted to, university affiliation, course name, enrollment number, organisation address, website, course duration (in years and semester), and date of admission. - Specify who accredited the course/institution, their name, and address. Proof of accreditation must be submitted. - Select &quot;Yes&quot; or &quot;No&quot; for &quot;Have you obtained admission through government-mandated admission?&quot;. - If staying in a hostel, provide the Hostel name, Hostel address, and Hostel contact number. - Fill in the requested loan amounts for Admission Fee, Tuition Fee, Examination Fee, Hostel Fee, Estimated cost of books/stationery, and Other fees. - List details of any physical assets owned (land, house, government property, NCC, LIC, KVP, etc.). - Indicate if anyone in the family works in a government/private sector job. If &quot;Yes,&quot; provide Name, Designation, Place, and Salary details. - Fill in the Bank Account Number, select the Bank Name from the dropdown, provide the Branch Name, Name as per Account, select Bank Account Type (Saving/Current), and enter the MICR Code and IFSC Code. > **Step 3: Review and Final Submission** - Carefully review each module. Use the preview option to examine the application before final submission. Fill out the provided &quot;CAPTCHA&quot; and Click on “Submit”. > **Track and Review Status** - Applicant will receive an Application ID/Reference Number. Save or print the acknowledgement for future tracking. - Go to the [Official Website](https://gndcdconline.gujarat.gov.in/) Gujarat Nomadic and Denotified Tribes Development Corporation. - Scroll down and locate the Know App Status. Click on &quot;Know App Status&quot;. - Select the Scheme name, Enter App No., and Birth Date. Fill out the Captcha and click on Submit. 
Mode: Online
