# Livelihood Opportunity Promotion Scheme (Disabled)

**Short Title:** LOPSD

**Scheme Brief Description:** The scheme aims to promote self-reliance among persons with disabilities through grants, term loans, and margin money assistance.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Banking,Financial Services and Insurance; Sector: Banking and money

**Where it applies:** Uttarakhand; State/ UT

**Detailed Description:** The &quot;Livelihood Opportunity Promotion Scheme (Disabled)&quot; scheme was launched by the Social Welfare Department, Government of Uttarakhand. It aims to empower persons with disabilities by providing financial assistance for sustainable self-employment and livelihood generation. Under this scheme, the beneficiaries are supported with a mix of grants, subsidized loans, and margin money for term loans, shop construction, and bank-linked enterprises.

**Benefits / Assistance:** **Term Loan Scheme:** - Project Cost: ₹75,000/- - Grant: 20% or ₹10,000/-, whichever is less - Term Loan: ₹65,000/- at 6% annual interest rate **Shop Construction Scheme:** - Project Cost: ₹50,000/- - Grant: 20% or ₹10,000/-, whichever is less - Margin Money Loan: ₹40,000/- at 4% annual interest rate, repayable in 60 monthly installments **Margin Money Loan Scheme for Bank Supported Loan:** - Project Cost: Up to ₹2,00,000/- - Grant: 20%, maximum of ₹10,000/- - Margin Money: 25% of the project cost at 4% annual interest rate, repayable in 36 monthly installments

**Application Process:** ***Application Process:*** **Step 1:** The interested applicant downloads the [application form](http://www.ukbvvn.org.in/Download/ApplicationFormFormatJeevika.pdf) from the official website. **Step 2:** In the application form, fill in all the mandatory fields and attach copies of all the mandatory documents (self-attested, if required). **Step 3:** Submit the duly filled and signed application form along with the documents to the office of the Vikas Khand (Block Development) Office. &amp;amp;lt;br&amp;amp;gt; ***Selection Process:*** **Step 1:** The application will be forwarded to the District Social Welfare Officer. **Step 2:** A District-Level Committee, headed by the District Social Welfare Officer, will review the proposals and select beneficiaries.
Mode: Offline
