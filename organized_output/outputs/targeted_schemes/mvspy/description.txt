# Mukhyamantri Vriddhjan Samman Pension Yojana

**Short Title:** MVSPY

**Scheme Brief Description:** The Rajasthan government's Social Justice and Empowerment Department launched the "Mukhyamantri Vriddhjan Samman Pension Yojana" program, a social welfare initiative that aims to improve the lives of senior citizens residing in the state by providing them with monthly financial assistance.

**Launch Date:** 2013-04-01

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Pension

**Where it applies:** Rajasthan; State/ UT

**DBT Scheme:** Yes (Direct Benefit Transfer)

**Detailed Description:** In 2 October 2021, The &quot;Mukhyamantri Vriddhjan Samman Pension Yojana&quot; was started by the Labore department of Rajasthan. It will provide the benefits to old age people. By offering financial assistance, the program not only eases the burden of financial worries but also honors the lifelong contributions of seniors to the state&#39;s social fabric. It recognizes their wisdom, experience, and resilience, affirming their rightful place in society and ensuring they can live out their golden years with dignity and respect.

**Benefits / Assistance:** > **Pension amount : ₹1,000/- per month**

Enter the ‘OTP’ and - Click on the ‘Verify OTP’ button to Complete the registration. - Google : Enter the Gmail ID, click on the ‘Next’ button, - Enter the password. - A new link appear on screen, now click on new SSO link. - SSO id will appear on screen, now create the password. - Enter Mobile number, click on registration. > **Apply** **Step-1:** Applicant have to visit the [official portal](https://sso.rajasthan.gov.in/signin). **Step-2:** After login, dashboard will open. **Step-3:** Click on “IFMS-RAJSSP” option. **Step-4:** In &quot;IFMS-RAJSSP&quot;, click on &quot;Application Entry Request&quot;. **Step-5:** Enter the &quot;Bhamashah Family ID&quot; and search. **Step-6:** Select the person name and scheme name. **Step-7:** Complete the Aadhaar Authentication and click on get details. **Step-8:** Provide the required details. - Pensioner Details. - Bank Details. - Disability Details. - Verification Details. - Upload Documents. **Step-9:** Submit.  > **Fees for Chief Minister Old Age Samman Pension Scheme is Rs. 33/-.
