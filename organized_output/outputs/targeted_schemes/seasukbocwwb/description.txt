# Solar Energy Assistance Scheme (UKBOCWWB)

**Short Title:** SEAS(UKBOCWWB)

**Scheme Brief Description:** The scheme aims to provide solar energy plates/panels to the families of construction workers registered with the Uttarakhand Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board.

**Who is this for:** Family; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment

**Where it applies:** Uttarakhand; State/ UT

**Detailed Description:** The scheme “Solar Energy Assistance Scheme” is implemented by the Uttarakhand Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board (UKBOCWWB) under the Labour Department, Government of Uttarakhand. The scheme aims to provide solar energy plates/panels to the families of registered construction workers. 

**Benefits / Assistance:** Under this scheme, solar energy plates/panels will be provided, and for maintenance and service charges, beneficiaries will have to contact the concerned supplier. 

**Application Process:** - The online application can be submitted through Shramik Suvidha Kendras. Along with the application form, applicants are required to attach a self-attested photocopy of their Shramik Card, Aadhaar Card, Self-Declaration Certificate, and a certificate confirming that no similar benefit has been availed from any other department. - After verification during the departmental investigation, solar energy plates/panels are provided to the beneficiary through the concerned supplier/dealer. - This benefit can be availed only once in a lifetime. 
Mode: Online - via CSC

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: Uttarakhand Building And Other Construction Workers’ Welfare Board; Nodal Department: Department Of Labour
