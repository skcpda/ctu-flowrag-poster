# Private Tuition Assistance to Scheduled Caste Students Studying in Standard 11th & 12th (General Stream)

**Short Title:** PTASCSSS1112(GS)

**Scheme Brief Description:** Under the scheme, financial assistance is provided to Scheduled Caste (SC) students who are studying in Standard 11th and 12th (General Stream) and have obtained 75% or more marks in the 10th Standard for private coaching fees, as per merit prepared at state level up to 100 students.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Education & Learning; Sector: Coaching

**Where it applies:** Gujarat; State/ UT

**Detailed Description:** The “Private Tuition Assistance to Scheduled Caste Students Studying in Standard 11th &amp;amp; 12th (General Stream)” is implemented by the Director, Scheduled Caste Welfare, Government of Gujarat. Under the scheme, financial assistance is provided to Scheduled Caste (SC) students who are studying in Standard 11th and 12th (General Stream) and have obtained 75% or more marks in the 10th Standard for private coaching fees, as per merit prepared at state level up to 100 students.

**Benefits / Assistance:** > **Assistance Details:** • Standard 11th: ₹8,000/- per annum as a private tuition fees • Standard 12th: ₹4,000/- per annum as a private tuition fees

**Application Process:** **Step 01:** To avail the benefits of the scheme, the students need to visit the nearest Office of the District Deputy Director (SCW) or Director, Scheduled Caste Welfare Office, Gandhinagar. **Step 02:** The application form may be collected from the concerned office. **Step 03:** In the application form, fill in all the mandatory fields and attach all the mandatory documents. **Step 04:** Submit the application form along with the documents to the concerned office.
Mode: Offline

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: Director, Scheduled Caste Welfare, Gandhinagar; Nodal Department: Social Justice and Empowerment Department
