# Girl Education Promotion (Bicycle Scheme)

**Short Title:** GEPBS

**Scheme Brief Description:** The scheme aims to promote girls' education in the state by providing financial assistance for the purchase of bicycles to girl students studying in Class 9 in government and government-aided secondary schools.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Women and Child

**Where it applies:** Uttarakhand; State/ UT

**Detailed Description:** The scheme &amp;quot;Girl Education Promotion (Bicycle Scheme)&amp;quot; is implemented by the Secondary Education Department under the Department of Education, Government of Uttarakhand. The scheme aims to promote girls’ education in the state by providing financial assistance to Class 9 girl students for purchasing bicycles. 

**Benefits / Assistance:** - Each eligible girl student in class 9 receives ₹2,850/-. - The benefit includes a bicycle for plain areas and an FD (Fixed Deposit) worth ₹2,850/- for hilly areas. 

**Application Process:** - The Uttarakhand Board provides bicycles/FDs to schools based on the number of girl students enrolled in Class 9 in government and government-aided secondary schools. - The schools then distribute the bicycles/FDs to the enrolled girl students. - No student is required to apply for this scheme. 
Mode: Offline

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: Secondary Education Department, Government Of Uttarakhand; Nodal Department: Department Of Education
