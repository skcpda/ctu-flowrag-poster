# Promotion of Acting in Tiatr for School Children

**Short Title:** PATSC

**Scheme Brief Description:** The scheme aims to encourage school children to improve their acting abilities in Goan tiatr through organized competitions. It provides cash prizes and certificates to winners in comedy and non-comedy acting categories.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Sports & Culture; Sector: Culture

**Where it applies:** Goa; State/ UT

**Detailed Description:** The scheme &quot;Promotion of Acting in Tiatr for School Children&quot; by the Tiatr Academy of Goa aims to encourage interested artistes to improve their acting abilities through organized competitions related to Goan tiatr dramatic activity, addressing the need to develop theatrical skills among school students at primary and secondary levels. The scheme provides acting competitions in comedy and non-comedy categories with both monologue and dialogue formats, along with cash prizes ranging from ₹2,000/- to ₹5,000/- and participation certificates for winners and best script writers. The applications for this scheme are accepted offline. 

**Benefits / Assistance:** ##### ***Cash Prizes and Recognition*** - **Monologue (Comedy Acting):** 1st Performance - ₹3,000/-; 2nd Performance - ₹2,000/-- **Best Script Writer:** ₹2,000/-- **Dialogue (Comedy Acting):** 1st Performance - ₹5,000/-; 2nd Performance - ₹3,000/-- **Best Script Writer:** ₹2,000/-- **Monologue (Non-Comedy Acting):** 1st Performance - ₹3,000/-; 2nd Performance - ₹2,000/-- **Best Script Writer:** ₹2,000/-- **Dialogue (Non-Comedy Acting):** 1st Performance - ₹5,000/-; 2nd Performance - ₹3,000/-- **Best Script Writer:** ₹2,000/- ##### ***Additional Benefits*** - Participation certificates awarded to all winners. - Transport charges remuneration paid to each participating school as fixed by Tiatr Academy of Goa. - Development of acting abilities through structured competition and professional judging. ##### ***Conditions*** _*Minimum four entries required in each category for competition to be conducted in that particular category._ _*Performance duration must be between 4 to 7 minutes._ _*Scripts must not contain adverse remarks against any governments, individuals, institutions, or anything against caste, creed and religion._ 

**Application Process:** **Step 1:** The interested applicant should take a printout of the [prescribed format](https://www.goa.gov.in/wp-content/uploads/2021/06/Citizen-Charter-TAG.pdf) of the application form. **Step 2:** In the application form, fill in all the mandatory fields. Attach copies of all the mandatory documents (self-attest, if required). Sign the application form and mention the date. **Step 3:** Submit the duly filled and signed application form along with the documents to - ***Tiatr Academy of Goa, AS-1 / AS-4, Block A, 2nd Floor, Campal Trade Centre, Opposite Kala Academy, Campal, Panaji, Goa, India – 403001*** _*Applicants are advised to save a copy of the submitted application form and any acknowledgment_  ##### ***Post-Application Processes*** **Step 1:****** ***Await Competition Schedule*** Wait for Tiatr Academy of Goa to organize the competition once minimum four entries are received in each category, as competitions will only be conducted when this minimum requirement is met. **Step 2:****** ***Performance Evaluation*** Participate in the competition where your acting abilities will be judged on six criteria: delivery of dialogues, facial expression, reaction to others on the stage, movement on the stage, costumes, and overall impact by a panel of judges well versed in acting and direction. **Step 3:****** ***Receive Results and Benefits*** Accept the final and binding decision of the judges, and if selected as a winner, receive cash prizes and participation certificates as per the category and position achieved in the competition. 
Mode: Offline

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Art, Culture and Museums Department
