# Jan Samuh Bima Yojana

**Short Title:** JSBY

**Scheme Brief Description:** The scheme aims to provide financial assistance to Sahariya families in the event of the death of the heads of the families. Under this scheme, a one-time financial assistance of ₹25,000/- is provided by the insurance company to the deceased's family to help reduce their economic burden.

**Who is this for:** Family; Category: Banking,Financial Services and Insurance; Sector: Insurance

**Where it applies:** Rajasthan; State/ UT

**DBT Scheme:** Yes (Direct Benefit Transfer)

**Detailed Description:** The scheme &amp;quot;Jan Samuh Bima Yojana&amp;quot; was launched in 2019 by the Department of Tribal Area Development, Government of Rajasthan. The scheme aims to provide financial assistance to Sahariya families in the event of the death of the heads of the families (primary earning member). Under this scheme, a one-time financial assistance is provided by the insurance company to the deceased&amp;#39;s family to help reduce their economic burden. The heads of these families are insured under the Swachh Project, and the insurance premium is paid by the Swachh Project.

**Benefits / Assistance:** > **Financial Assistance:** - The insurance company provides financial assistance of ₹25,000/- to the family in case of death of the insured person. > **Insurance Premium:** - Fully paid under the Swachh Project.

**Application Process:** > **Survey &amp; Selection:** - A survey is conducted by the Swachh Project in Sahariya bastis of Baran district to identify eligible families. > **Enrollment:** - Eligible families members are enrolled under the insurance scheme by the Swachh Project. > **Submission of Claim (in case of death of the insured):** - The family must submit the required documents to the Swachh Project office at Shahabad. - The documents are verified and forwarded to the insurance company.
Mode: Offline

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: Swachh Project, Shahabad, Rajasthan; Nodal Department: Tribal Area Development Department
