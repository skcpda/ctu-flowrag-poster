# Dr. Shivanand Nautiyal Scholarship

**Short Title:** DSNS

**Scheme Brief Description:** The scheme provides financial assistance of ₹1,500/- per month for a duration of 10 months to meritorious students from the Garhwal and Kumaon divisions who secure top ranks in the examination conducted by SCERT Uttarakhand.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Education & Learning; Sector: Scholarships and student finance

**Where it applies:** Uttarakhand; State/ UT

**Detailed Description:** The &amp;quot;Dr. Shivanand Nautiyal Scholarship&amp;quot; scheme is implemented by the Secondary Education Department under the Department of Education, Government of Uttarakhand. The scheme aims to provide financial assistance to meritorious students from the Garhwal and Kumaon divisions who secure top ranks in the examination conducted by SCERT Uttarakhand. 

**Benefits / Assistance:** Out of the top 100 students in the qualifying examination conducted by SCERT Uttarakhand, 50 students from the Garhwal Division and 50 students from the Kumaon Division will receive ₹1,500/- per month for a period of 10 months. 

**Application Process:** - To appear in the scholarship examination, students can obtain the application form from the office of the Block Education Officer or download it from the School Education Department’s website ([https://schooleducation.uk.gov.in](https://schooleducation.uk.gov.in/)/) or SCERT Uttarakhand’s website ([https://scert.uk.gov.in](https://scert.uk.gov.in/)/) during September–October every year. A photocopy of the application form is also valid. No application or examination fee is charged. - While filling out the application form, students must attach a passport-size photograph, Aadhaar card, Class 7 mark sheet, caste certificate, and disability certificate (if applicable). All information must be filled in correctly, and the completed form should be submitted to the Principal of the school where the student is enrolled. - The Principal will verify the form based on the attached documents and school records, attest the photograph, and forward the verified form to the Block Education Officer’s office by the tentative date of 12 November. - After submission, the Block Education Officer issues the admit card. Students can collect the admit card either from their school or the Block Education Officer’s office. - The examination is conducted every year in the month of January. - After the successful completion of the process, the school Principal informs the student. The student must provide their Aadhaar card and Aadhaar-linked bank account details to the school, following which the scholarship amount is transferred directly to the student’s bank account. 
Mode: Offline

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: Secondary Education Department, Government Of Uttarakhand; Nodal Department: Department Of Education
