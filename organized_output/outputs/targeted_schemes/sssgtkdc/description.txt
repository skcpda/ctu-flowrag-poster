# Swayam Saksham Yojana (GTKVN)

**Short Title:** SSYGTKVN

**Scheme Brief Description:** The scheme aims to promote economic self-reliance among professionally educated unemployed youth from Thakor & Koli communities, aged between 18 & 35 years, who hold degrees in fields such as Pharmacy, Software/Hardware, Automobile Engineering, Law, Chartered Accountancy, & others.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Banking,Financial Services and Insurance; Sector: Loan

**Where it applies:** Gujarat; State/ UT

**Detailed Description:** The &amp;quot;Swayam Saksham Yojana&amp;quot; is implemented by the Gujarat Thakor and Koli Vikas Nigam (GTKVN) under the Social Justice and Empowerment Department, Government of Gujarat. The scheme aims to promote economic self-reliance among professionally educated unemployed youth (both men and women) from the Thakor and Koli communities, aged between 18 and 35 years, who hold degrees in fields such as Pharmacy, Architecture, Software/Hardware, Automobile Engineering, Law, Chartered Accountancy, and others. Under this scheme, financial assistance in the form of low-interest loans is provided to eligible beneficiaries who have completed higher professional education and wish to become self-reliant by starting their own business in their respective fields. It specifically supports individuals trained in professional fields like medicine, law, engineering, accounting, hotel management, and even wrestling or sculpture, to use their skills for gainful self-employment. 

**Benefits / Assistance:** **Loan Amount:** - Up to ₹5,00,000/-. **Interest Rate:** - 5% per annum. **Contribution Split:** - 95% of the business/project cost is covered under the scheme as a loan. - 5% contribution must be made by the beneficiary. **Loan Repayment Terms:** - Repayable in 60 equal monthly installments (maximum 5 years). - The repayment period depends on the nature of the business plan. 

**Application Process:** **Step 1:** Visit the [official website](https://gtkdconline.gujarat.gov.in/) of the Gujarat Thakor and Koli Vikas Nigam. **Step 2:** On the top menu bar, click on &quot;Apply Now&quot;. **Step 3:** Click on the loan scheme name. **Step 4:** Select the loan category and fill in the loan amount. **Step 5:** Fill out the Application Form, enter the following details: - Personal Information: Name, gender, date of birth, caste, etc. - Address Information: Permanent and correspondence address - Income Details: Monthly family income and source - Loan Requirement: Amount needed, proposed use (e.g., business, self-employment) - Bank Account Details: For loan disbursement **Step 6:** Upload scanned copies of the Caste certificate, Income certificate, Aadhaar card, Passport-size photo, Bank passbook (first page), and Residence proof. **Step 7:** Double-check all details. Click on “Preview” to verify. If all details are correct, click “Submit”. **Step 8:** Applicant will receive an Application ID/Reference Number. Save or print the acknowledgement for future tracking. 
Mode: Online

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: Gujarat Thakor And Koli Vikas Nigam; Nodal Department: Social Justice and Empowerment Department
