# Dental Care Scheme- Haryana Labour Welfare Board

**Short Title:** DCS-HLWB

**Scheme Brief Description:** Under this Scheme, workers & their dependents are entitled to receive financial assistance of upto ₹4,000/- or the actual expenditure, whichever is less, for dental care. Furthermore, assistance of upto ₹10,000/- or the actual expenditure, whichever is less, is provided for fixation of full denture.

**Who is this for:** Family, Individual; Category: Health & Wellness; Sector: Financial assistance

**Where it applies:** Haryana; State/ UT

**Detailed Description:** The scheme “Dental Care Scheme” is implemented by the Haryana Labour Welfare Board (HLWB), Labour Department, Government of Haryana. Under this scheme, financial assistance of ₹4,000/- or the actual cost of dental care, whichever is less, is provided for dental care to the workers employed in the industrial and commercial establishments in the state of Haryana. Furthermore, financial assistance of ₹10,000/- or the actual cost of the jaw, whichever is less, is provided to the workers for getting full denture fitted.

**Benefits / Assistance:** 1. Under this scheme, workers and their dependents are entitled to receive financial assistance of up to ₹4,000/- or the actual expenditure, whichever is less, for dental care. 1. Financial assistance of up to ₹10,000/- or the actual amount of expenditure, whichever is less, is provided for fixation of full dentures to workers and their dependents.
