# Palak Mata-Pita Yojana

**Short Title:** PMPY

**Scheme Brief Description:** The "Palak Mata-Pita Yojana" introduced on 29th August, 2009 by the Social Justice and Empowerment Department, Gujarat, provides a monthly assistance of ₹3000 to foster parents raising orphaned children or children whose mother has remarried after the father's death.

**Launch Date:** 2009-08-29

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Financial assistance

**Where it applies:** Gujarat; State/ UT

**DBT Scheme:** Yes (Direct Benefit Transfer)

**Detailed Description:** The &amp;quot;Palak Mata-Pita Yojana&amp;quot; introduced on 29th August, 2009 by the Social Justice and Empowerment Department, Gujarat, aims to provide monthly assistance of Rs. 3000/- is paid to the close relative, guardian or relative who is taking care of the orphaned children who are studying and whose mother and father are both deceased or whose father is deceased and the mother has remarried. This assistance is paid through DBT.

**Benefits / Assistance:** **Financial Assistance:** ₹3,000/- per month.

**Application Process:** **Step 1:** Go to the [eSamajKalyan portal](https://esamajkalyan.gujarat.gov.in/) and Register/Login. **Step 2:** Fill in the online application form under &quot;Palak Mata-Pita Yojana&quot;. **Step 3:** Upload the required documents and Submit the application.
Mode: Online

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Social Justice and Empowerment Department

**Tags:** Foster Parent, Orphan, Child Welfare, Financial Assistance
