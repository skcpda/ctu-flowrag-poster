# Scheme for Establishment of Hi-Tech and Mini Dairy Units under Mukhya Mantri Antyodaya Parivaar Utthan Yojana

**Short Title:** SEH-TMDUMMAPUY

**Scheme Brief Description:** Under this scheme, a resident of Haryana who is between 18 and 60 years of age can avail a 25% subsidy on the cost of two milch animals. The subsidy can be availed as a loan for the purchase of these milch animals by the beneficiary for the establishment of dairy units with two milch animals.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Agriculture,Rural & Environment; Sector: Animal husbandry

**Where it applies:** Haryana; State/ UT

**Detailed Description:** The scheme “Scheme for Establishment of Hi-Tech and Mini Dairy Units under Mukhya Mantri Antyodaya Parivaar Utthan Yojana (MMAPUY)” is implemented by the Department of Animal Husbandry &amp;amp; Dairying, Government of Haryana. Under this scheme, a resident of Haryana who is between 18 and 60 years of age can avail a 25% subsidy on the cost of two milch animals. The subsidy can be availed as a loan for the purchase of these milch animals by the beneficiary for the establishment of dairy units with two milch animals.

**Benefits / Assistance:** > **Financial Assistance under the Scheme:** **Dairy units of 2 milch animals:** - 25 % subsidy on the cost of milch animals which has been availed as a loan for the purchase of these milch animals by the beneficiary for the establishment of dairy units of 2 milch animals; > **Financial Assistance for Insurance under the Scheme:** - Insurance assistance will be provided as per the available departmental scheme if operational during the year; otherwise, the beneficiaries will have to arrange for insurance of animals and shall pay the insurance premium.

**Exclusions / Not eligible:** 1. Any group/firm/organization will not be permitted to apply under the Scheme, as the Scheme is individual beneficiary-oriented.

**Application Process:** **Step 01:** To avail the benefits of the scheme, applicants should visit the Deputy Director&amp;#39;s office in their district and collect the application form. **Step 02:** Fill out the application form and attach all the relevant documents. **Step 03:** Submit the completely filled application form and attached documents to the concerned official in the office. **Step 04:** The Deputy Director of the concerned district will examine the applications on a first-come, first-served basis and process them accordingly.
Mode: Offline

**Additional Application Processes:** **Process 2:** The applicant may submit the application through Common Service Centres, Antodaya Kendra, Atal Sewa Kendra, e-disha Centres or any other such centre.

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: Department Of Animal Husbandry & Dairying, Government Of Haryana; Nodal Department: Animal Husbandry & Dairying Department
