# Pragati Scholarship (For 81 Girl Students Of Uttarakhand Origin (Diploma) And 50 for Graduation Level)

**Short Title:** PSGSDGL

**Scheme Brief Description:** The scheme “Pragati Scholarship” aims to promote higher education among girl students by providing financial assistance.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Education & Learning; Sector: Scholarships and student finance

**Where it applies:** Uttarakhand; State/ UT

**Detailed Description:** The scheme “Pragati Scholarship” aims to encourage higher education among girl students by offering financial assistance. It supports meritorious girl students of Uttarakhand pursuing diploma and graduate-level studies, providing ₹50,000/- per year to each eligible beneficiary. 

**Benefits / Assistance:** - **Financial Assistance:** ₹50,000/- per year. 

**Application Process:** - Scholarship schemes conducted by the All India Council for Technical Education (AICTE) follow the below-mentioned procedure: Applications under the AICTE scheme are invited through the NSP portal between the months of September to December. Scholarships are granted by AICTE on the basis of merit. - Documents required for application – High School and Intermediate mark sheets and certificates, and income certificate. - For Pragati Scheme – Up to 02 girl students can be granted scholarships, for which an income certificate is required. - Students must first apply online through their respective institutions, which is then verified by the concerned principal. After that, the online application verified by the State Level Officer (S.L.O.) is forwarded to AICTE, New Delhi (AICTE). 
Mode: Online

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Directorate of Technical Education
