# Foreign Study Loan Scheme (GUEEDC)

**Short Title:** FSLS

**Scheme Brief Description:** Loan scheme offering up to ₹15 lakh at 4% simple interest to unreserved category students for MBBS, PG, or equivalent courses abroad with flexible collateral and repayment conditions.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Education & Learning; Sector: International education

**Where it applies:** Gujarat; State/ UT

**Detailed Description:** The scheme provides **financial loan assistance** to students from the **unreserved category** to pursue **higher education abroad.** Loans up to **₹15 lakh** are granted at simple interest of **4% per annum**, with flexible repayment options. Security and repayment conditions depend on the loan amount. This scheme aims to support meritorious, economically weaker students for global education opportunities.

**Benefits / Assistance:** **Loan Amount:** Up to ₹15.00 lakh **Interest Rate:** Simple interest at 4% per annum **Collateral:** **Up to ₹7.50 lakh:** Property valued at 1.5 times the loan amount **Above ₹7.50 lakh:** Property equal to the loan amount **Loan Repayment:** **Up to ₹5 lakh:** repay in 5 years after 1-year moratorium **Above ₹5 lakh**: repay in 6 years after 1-year moratorium  **Borrower can pre-pay without penalty**

Mode: Online
