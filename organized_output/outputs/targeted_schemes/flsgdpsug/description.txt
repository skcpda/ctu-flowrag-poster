# Facilitation of Large Size Godowns in APMC and Taluka-District Purchase Sale Union (General)

**Short Title:** FLSGDPSUG

**Scheme Brief Description:** The scheme aims to strengthen the financial status of district and taluka-level cooperative buying and selling unions by supporting infrastructure projects like storage godowns, marketing facilities, and branding units.

**Who is this for:** Registered Societies; Category: Agriculture,Rural & Environment; Sector: Entrepreneurship development

**Where it applies:** Gujarat; State/ UT

**Detailed Description:** The scheme &amp;quot;Facilitation of Large Size Godowns in APMC and Taluka-District Purchase Sale Union (General)&amp;quot; by the Agriculture and Co-operation Department, Government of Gujarat, aims to financially strengthen district and taluka-level cooperative purchase and sale unions across the state. The scheme is designed to help such unions undertake critical infrastructure projects, including purchasing, processing, grading, cleaning, packaging, storage, branding, and marketing of agricultural produce, which ultimately benefits the farmers with improved services and higher financial returns. 

**Benefits / Assistance:** - **Financial Assistance:** Provides crucial capital assistance for the construction of large-sized godowns. - **Infrastructure Development:** Supports the development of essential infrastructure for agricultural activities like processing, grading, cleaning, washing, shortening, packaging, and branding. - **Enhanced Marketing Capabilities:** Enables unions to strengthen their marketing and selling capabilities, potentially leading to better prices for farmers&amp;#39; produce. - **Increased Financial Strength for Unions:** Aims to make District and Taluka Purchase-Sale Unions more financially robust and self-reliant. - **Improved Services for Farmers:** Facilitates the provision of better quality services to farmers through stronger unions and improved facilities. 

**Application Process:** **Step 1:** The interested applicant should prepare the application on plain paper. **Step 2:** In the application form, provide all the mandatory details, and attach copies of all the mandatory documents (self-attested, if required). **Step 3:** Submit the duly filled and signed application form along with the supporting documents to the District Registrar&#39;s Office. **Step 4:** Request a receipt or acknowledgement from the concerned authority to whom the application has been submitted.
