# Sponsorship for Employment

**Short Title:** SFE

**Scheme Brief Description:** The scheme aims to provide employment opportunities to ex-servicemen and their legal dependents by sponsoring them for contractual posts. Under this scheme, eligible candidates are sponsored for various departmental positions based on demand and their qualifications.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Skills & Employment; Sector: Career information

**Where it applies:** Uttarakhand; State/ UT

**Detailed Description:** The scheme &amp;quot;Sponsorship for Employment&amp;quot; is implemented by Uttarakhand Purv Sainik Kalyan Nigam Limited (UPNL) under the Sainik Welfare Department, Government of Uttarakhand. The scheme aims to provide employment opportunities to ex-servicemen and their legal dependents by sponsoring them for contractual posts. Under this scheme, eligible candidates are sponsored for various departmental positions based on demand and their qualifications. 

**Benefits / Assistance:** Under this scheme, employment opportunities are provided to ex-servicemen and their legal dependents by sponsoring them for contractual posts through outsourcing. 

**Application Process:** - Firstly, the eligible candidates (ex-servicemen and their legal dependents) fill out the online application/enrollment form for the desired post on the UPNL website: [www.upnl.co.in](https://upnl.co.in/default.aspx), based on their district, qualifications, and preferred location. - Based on the district-wise demand for posts received from various departments and the eligibility/qualification criteria for those posts, UPNL forwards the names of enrolled candidates with the relevant qualifications to the concerned department for selection, following district-wise and priority order of nomination. - As per the demand received from the department, UPNL sends the names of six enrolled candidates for each vacant post to the concerned department for consideration. - The final selection of candidates is made by the department according to its rules, and the information is subsequently communicated to UPNL. 
Mode: Online

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: Uttarakhand Purv Sainik Kalyan Nigam Limited; Nodal Department: State Sainik Board
