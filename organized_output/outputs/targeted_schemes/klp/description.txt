# Kalyana Lakshmi Pathakam

**Short Title:** KLP

**Scheme Brief Description:** The scheme is designed to support economically disadvantaged families belonging to the SC/ST and minority communities by providing one-time financial assistance for the marriage of their daughters.

**Launch Date:** 2014-10-02

**Who is this for:** Family; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Marriage

**Where it applies:** Telangana; State

**DBT Scheme:** Yes (Direct Benefit Transfer)

**Detailed Description:** The scheme &amp;quot;Kalyana Lakshmi Pathakam&amp;quot; was launched in 2014 by the Backward Classes Welfare Department, Government of Telangana, aims to alleviate financial distress in SC/ST and minority families by providing financial assistance to unmarried girls at the time of marriage. this scheme, a one-time financial assistance of ₹1,00,116 (₹1,25,145 for disabled girls) is provided to eligible girls at the time of marriage. 

**Benefits / Assistance:** 1. One-time financial assistance of ₹1,00,116/- for unmarried SC/ST and minority girls at the time of marriage. 1. One-time financial assistance of ₹1,25,145/- for disabled girls, irrespective of caste, under the Rights of Persons with Disabilities Act, 2016. 1. The amount is directly credited to the bride’s bank account. 1. Only one-time assistance is allowed in a lifetime. 

**Exclusions / Not eligible:** 1. Applicants who have already availed the benefit once are not eligible. 

**Application Process:** **Step 1:** Visit the official ePASS website at [https://telanganaepass.cgg.gov.in/](https://telanganaepass.cgg.gov.in/) . **Step 2:** Select &amp;quot;Kalyana Lakshmi Pathakam&amp;quot; scheme. **Step 3:** Fill in the bride’s personal, caste, income, and address details. **Step 4:** Upload scanned documents including Aadhaar, caste/income certificates, bank passbook, marriage certificate/photo, etc. **Step 5:** Apply online. **Step 6:** A unique application number will be generated. **Step 7:** Await verification from the Welfare Department. **Step 8:** Upon approval, the sanctioned amount will be credited to the bride’s bank account. **Step 9:** Save acknowledgment/receipt for future reference. 
Mode: Online

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: Backward Classes Welfare Department, Telangana; Nodal Department: Backward Classes Welfare Department

**Tags:** Scheduled Caste, Scheduled Tribe, Backward Class, Economically Backward Class, Marriage
