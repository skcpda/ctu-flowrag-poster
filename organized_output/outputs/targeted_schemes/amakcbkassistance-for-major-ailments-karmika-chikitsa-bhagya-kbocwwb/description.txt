# Assistance For Major Ailments (Karmika Chikitsa Bhagya) (K.B.O.C.W.W.B)

**Short Title:** AFMAKCBKBOCWWB

**Scheme Brief Description:** The "Assistance For Major Ailments (Karmika Chikitsa Bhagya)'' by the BOCW Welfare Board, Department of Labour, Karnataka, is a welfare scheme that provides financial support to registered construction workers and their dependents for medical expenses related to specified major ailments.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Health & Wellness; Sector: Financial assistance

**Where it applies:** Karnataka; State/ UT

**Detailed Description:** The &amp;quot;Assistance For Major Ailments&amp;#39;&amp;#39; by the BOCW Welfare Board, Department of Labour, Karnataka, is a welfare scheme for the registered workers of the board. Under this Scheme, the Board provides financial assistance to registered construction workers and their dependents for medical expenses related to specified major ailments. The scheme ensures that eligible workers receive necessary medical treatment without facing financial hardship. &lt;br&gt;

**Benefits / Assistance:** - **Financial Assistance:** ₹2,00,000/- for major ailment treatments.
