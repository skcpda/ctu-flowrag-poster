# Awasiya Vidyala Yojana

**Short Title:** AVY

**Scheme Brief Description:** "Awasiye Vidyala Yojana" initiated by the Labor Department of the Uttar Pradesh State for the education of the construction worker.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Education & Learning; Sector: Scholarships and student finance

**Where it applies:** Uttar Pradesh; State/ UT

**Detailed Description:** It is often seen that children of construction workers stay with their parents at the work site. Due to poverty and lack of resources of the parents, such children are not able to take admission in any school or are not able to continue their education after taking admission. Realizing the need to start residential schools for such children of construction workers, a residential school scheme has been proposed. The objective of the scheme is to provide quality education to the children of registered construction workers in the age group of 6 to 14 years by providing them the facilities of primary, junior high school and secondary education. &lt;br&gt; **Scope of the plan:** Initially, residential school scheme will be operated in districts Etawah, Bhadohi, Kannauj, Moradabad, Firozabad, Lalitpur, Bahraich, Ghaziabad, Kanpur, Azamgarh, Agra and Meerut. Later, based on the experiences and needs gained, this scheme will be expanded to other districts.

**Benefits / Assistance:** 1. Free residential education for all registered construction workers&#39; sons/daughters. 1. Free accommodation, clothing, food and other facilities available.

**Application Process:** > **How to get register and apply for the scheme** 1. Applicant must visit the below offices - Nearest labor office. - Tehsildar of the concerned tehsil. - Block Development Officer of the concerned development block. 1. Get the application form and fill out the completely. 1. Attach required documents in the prescribed format. 1. Submit the application form in the same office.
