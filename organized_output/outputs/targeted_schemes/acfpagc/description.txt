# Assistance of Concentrate Feed for Pregnant Animals (General Category)

**Short Title:** ACFPAGC

**Scheme Brief Description:** This scheme provides free BIS type II concentrate feed to pregnant cattle owned by General Category livestock owners in Gujarat to ensure their good health, boost immunity, prevent metabolic diseases, and enhance milk production.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Agriculture,Rural & Environment; Sector: Animal husbandry

**Where it applies:** Gujarat; State/ UT

**Detailed Description:** The scheme &quot;Assistance of Concentrate Feed for Pregnant Animals (General Category)&quot; introduced by the Agriculture, Farmers Welfare and Cooperation Department, Gujarat, aims to support livestock owners during the last trimester of cattle pregnancy—a critical period for both the animal and the calf. Through this scheme, 150 kg of BIS Type II concentrate feed is provided free of cost to eligible beneficiaries to enhance milk yield, extend the milking period, and prevent health complications.
