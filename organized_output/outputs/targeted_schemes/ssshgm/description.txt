# Subsidy Scheme for Self-help Groups of Minorities

**Short Title:** SSSHGM

**Scheme Brief Description:** The "Subsidy Scheme for Self-help Groups of Minorities" aimed at empowering minority women by providing financial support to Self-Help Groups (SHGs). The scheme offers a subsidy to SHGs engaged in various self-employment activities, promoting economic self-reliance among minority women.

**Who is this for:** Self Help Groups (SHGS); Category: Women and Child; Sector: Financial Assistance

**Where it applies:** Karnataka; State/ UT

**Detailed Description:** The &quot;Subsidy Scheme for Self-help Groups of Minorities&quot; was launched by the Minorities Welfare Department, Government of Karnataka, and implemented by the Karnataka Minorities Development Corporation. The scheme provides a subsidy of 50% of the unit cost or a maximum of ₹2,00,000/- to the self-help groups of the minority community to engage in various types of self-employment activities and make them economically self-reliant, on the loans obtained from various financial institutions recognized by the Nationalized/Scheduled Bank/RBI. An SHG is to make fixed savings and provide internal lending to the members of the group as per requirement from the savings amount of the SHG.

**Benefits / Assistance:** - **Subsidy Amount:** 50% of the unit cost or a maximum of ₹2,00,000/- to the self-help groups.  **NOTE:** - The subsidy will be adjusted against the repayment of the loan taken by the SHG from recognized financial institutions. - SHGs focusing on good internal loan repayment will be given higher priority for the subsidy.

**Application Process:** **Step-01:** Applicant need to visits the Karnataka Minorities Development Corporation [official website](https://kmdconline.karnataka.gov.in/Portal/home). **Step-02:** Click [&quot;Apply Online&quot;](https://kmdconline.karnataka.gov.in/Portal/login) and Enter &quot;Mobile Number&quot; and &quot;Submit&quot;. **Step-03:** Enter &quot;Aadhaar Number&quot; Fill in the &quot;Captcha&quot; and Select &quot;Next&quot;. **Step-04:** Applicant will get OTP in his/her Aadhaar Linked Mobile Number. **Step-05:** Enter &quot;OTP&quot; and select &quot;Continue&quot;, After that Allow the official notice. **Step-06:** After verifying the Aadhaar again fill in the &quot;OTP&quot; and Submit. **Step-07:** Select the scheme &quot;Sikhligar’s Community Development Scheme&quot; and fill in the basic details father&#39;s name, gender, qualification detail **Step-08:** Select &quot;Next&quot; Fill in the address detail and again select &quot;Next&quot;. **Step-09:** Upload the required document passport-size photo, educational document, income certificate, etc. **Step-10:** Click &quot;Proceed&quot; and Select &quot;Submit Application&quot; and get an Application ID.
Mode: Online
