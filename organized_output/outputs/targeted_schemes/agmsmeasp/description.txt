# Aatmanirbhar Gujarat Scheme for assistance to MSMEs: Assistance for Patent Registration 

**Short Title:** AGMSMEASP

**Scheme Brief Description:** The "Aatmanirbhar Gujarat Scheme for assistance to MSMEs" aims to empower the state's dynamic MSME sector, which significantly contributes to employment, GDP, and exports.

**Launch Date:** 2022-10-05

**Who is this for:** Business Entity, Industries, Individual; Category: Business & Entrepreneurship; Sector: Setting up / start-up / entrepreneurship

**Where it applies:** Gujarat; State/ UT

**Detailed Description:** The &amp;quot;Aatmanirbhar Gujarat Scheme for MSMEs&amp;quot; is an umbrella scheme launched by the Industries and Mines Department, Gujarat. It aims to empower the state&amp;#39;s dynamic MSME sector, which significantly contributes to employment, GDP, and exports. Effective from October 5, 2022, to October 4, 2027, this initiative supports growth, investments, and the vision of &amp;quot;Aatmanirbhar Gujarat&amp;quot; and &amp;quot;Aatmanirbhar Bharat.&amp;quot; The component &amp;quot;Assistance for Patent Registration&amp;quot;, provides financial support for individuals and legal entities to obtain patent registration for their developed products or processes. It covers a portion of the costs incurred for patent application, attorney fees, and associated expenses during the scheme&amp;#39;s operative period.

**Benefits / Assistance:** &gt; **Financial Support for Patent Registration:** - 75% of the cost incurred for patent applications. - Maximum assistance of ₹25,00,000/- per applicant/enterprise. &gt; **Eligible Expenditures:** - Fees for patent attorney, patent service center, and patent registration. - Expenses related to patent equipment development (excluding travel and hotel costs). &gt; **Cap on Attorney Fees:** - Domestic patent attorney fees are capped at ₹50,000/-. - International patent attorney fees are capped at ₹2,00,000/- per country. &gt; **Disbursement of Assistance:** - 50% of assistance disbursed after the publication/notification of the patent. - The remaining 50% will be disbursed after the issuance of the patent certificate.

**Application Process:** > **Registration Process:** **Step-1:** Visit the [Investor Facilitation Portal (IFP)](https://ifp.gujarat.gov.in/DIGIGOV/) portal. Click on &quot;New Investor Registration&quot; to access the registration form. **Step-2:** Fill in the Registration Form. Click on &quot;Submit&quot;. **Step-3:** Verify the E-mail ID using the link sent on your registered E-mail ID. **Step-4:** After verification, you will be registered successfully.  > **Post-Registration Process:** **Step-1:** Visit the [Investor Facilitation Portal (IFP)](https://ifp.gujarat.gov.in/DIGIGOV/) portal. Click on &quot;Login&quot;. **Step-2:** Enter the email address and password you used during registration. **Step-3:** Click the &quot;Login&quot; button to access your account. **Step-4:** Now fill in the required details and mandatory documents about your business to complete the application process.  > **Helpdesk****:** [Contact Here.](https://ifp.gujarat.gov.in/DIGIGOV/IFP-pages/contact.jsp?pagedisp=static)
