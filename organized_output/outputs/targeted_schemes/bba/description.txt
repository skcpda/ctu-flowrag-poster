# Best Book Award

**Short Title:** BBA

**Scheme Brief Description:** The scheme "Best Book Award" by the Sports, Youth and Cultural Activities Department, Government of Gujarat aims to promote literary excellence by providing cash awards, certificates, and shields to authors of outstanding books published in Gujarat.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Sports & Culture; Sector: Culture

**Where it applies:** Gujarat; State/ UT

**Detailed Description:** The Best Book Award scheme by the Sports, Youth and Cultural Activities Department, Government of Gujarat, promotes high-quality literary creation and recognition of literary talent. It awards the best books published each calendar year in various languages through the Gujarat Sahitya Akademi. Under this scheme, the first prize includes ₹11,000 along with a certificate and shield; the second prize includes ₹7,000 with a certificate and shield; and the third prize includes ₹5,000, also with a certificate and shield. The scheme is implemented by the Gujarat Sahitya Akademi.

**Benefits / Assistance:** > **Monetary Benefits:** - **First Prize:** ₹11,000/-.- **Second Prize:** ₹7,000/-.- **Third Prize:** ₹5,000/-. > **Other Benefits:** - Certificate of Achievement - Commemorative Shield
