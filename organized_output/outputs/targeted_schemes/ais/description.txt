# Pradhan Mantri Matsya Sampada Yojana: Accident Insurance Scheme - Uttarakhand

**Short Title:** PMMSYAISUK

**Scheme Brief Description:** Under this scheme, fish farmers are provided insurance coverage of ₹5 lakh in case of accidental death/total permanent disability, ₹2.5 lakh for partial disability, & ₹50,000 for hospitalization. No premium is required to be paid by fish farmers, entire cost of the insurance is fully borne by Dept.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Banking,Financial Services and Insurance; Sector: Insurance

**Where it applies:** Uttarakhand; State/ UT

**Detailed Description:** The scheme &amp;quot;Accident Insurance Scheme&amp;quot;, a sub-component of the Pradhan Mantri Matsya Sampada Yojana, is implemented by the Department of Fisheries, Government of Uttarakhand. The scheme aims to provide financial security to fish farmers in the event of accidental death, permanent disability, or hospitalization. Under this scheme, insurance coverage includes ₹5,00,000/- for death or total permanent disability, ₹2,50,000/- for partial disability, and ₹50,000/- for hospitalization. Fish farmers are not required to pay any insurance premium; the entire premium is borne by the department.

**Benefits / Assistance:** **Insurance Coverage:** - ₹5,00,000/- in case of accidental death or total permanent disability. - ₹2,50,000/- for partial disability (such as loss of limbs, fingers, or partial loss of vision/hearing/speech, etc.). - ₹50,000/- for hospitalization expenses due to accidents. **Other Key Aspects:** - Beneficiaries do not pay any premium; the full premium is paid by the Fisheries Department. - Coverage is active for one year from the date of policy issuance. **Mode of Disbursement:** - Insurance claims are disbursed directly to the nominee&#39;s or beneficiary’s bank account.

**Application Process:** &gt; **Application Process:** **Step 1:** The interested applicant/nominee should contact (during office hours) the field personnel of the Fisheries Department and request a hard copy of the prescribed format of the application form from the staff exclusively entrusted to issue and collect filled-in applications. **Step 2:** In the application form, fill in all the mandatory fields and attach copies of all the mandatory documents (self-attest, if required). **Step 3:** Submit the duly filled and signed application form along with the documents to the field personnel of the Fisheries Department. &gt; **Enrollment Process:** **Step 1:** The Fisheries Department completes the enrollment and issues an insurance certificate to the beneficiary. &gt; **Claim Process:** **Step 1:** In case of an accident, death, or hospitalization, the beneficiary&amp;#39;s family must inform the local fisheries officer immediately. **Step 2:** The family then submits a claim form along with relevant supporting documents (e.g., medical or death certificate, insurance certificate, etc.). **Step 3:** Upon successful verification, the insurance claim is processed and disbursed to the nominee’s or beneficiary’s account.
