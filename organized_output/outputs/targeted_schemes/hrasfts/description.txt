# Hostel Rent Assistance Scheme for Tribal Students

**Short Title:** HRASFTS

**Scheme Brief Description:** The scheme provides financial assistance towards hostel rent for Scheduled Tribe students pursuing undergraduate and postgraduate courses in government colleges, who are staying in rented accommodation for the purpose of regular studies.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Education & Learning; Sector: Hostel service

**Where it applies:** Rajasthan; State/ UT

**Detailed Description:** The scheme &amp;quot;Hostel Rent Assistance Scheme for Tribal Students&amp;quot; was introduced by the Tribal Area Development Department, Government of Rajasthan, aims to encourage tribal students (both boys and girls) of Rajasthan to pursue higher education in undergraduate and postgraduate courses. Under this scheme, hostel rent assistance is provided for a maximum period of five years to Scheduled Tribe students studying in government colleges who reside in rented accommodation for the purpose of regular studies. The scheme is applicable only to students from Scheduled Areas of Rajasthan enrolled in government colleges.

**Application Process:** **Step 1:** Eligible students must fill out the prescribed application form online via the [official portal](https://tad.rajasthan.gov.in/services/136). **Step 2:** Navigate to the “ONLINE PORTAL FOR TAD EDUCATIONAL INCENTIVE SCHEMES” on the homepage. **Step 3:** Upload the application form along with the required documents before the notified deadline. ***Post Application Process:*** **Step 1:** The college will verify the online applications and forward them to the respective sanctioning authorities (TAD Project Officer / CEO, Zila Parishad). **Step 2:** Sanctioning authorities will approve the verified applications online. Incomplete applications, if any, will be returned for correction. **Step 3:** Approved applications will be sent to the district treasury office for disbursement. **Step 4:** The sanctioned amount will be transferred directly to the student&amp;#39;s bank account linked with their Bhamashah card.
