# Mukhyamantri Medhavi Chhatra Protsahan Scholarship Scheme (Secondary Level- Class 11 & 12)

**Short Title:** MMMCPSSSL1112

**Scheme Brief Description:** The scheme aims to encourage academic excellence among students entering Classes 11 and 12 by providing financial assistance to meritorious students who have secured 80% or more marks in the Uttarakhand Board Examination of Class 10.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Education & Learning; Sector: Scholarships and student finance

**Where it applies:** Uttarakhand; State/ UT

**Detailed Description:** The scheme “Mukhyamantri Medhavi Chhatra Protsahan Scholarship Scheme (Secondary Level – Classes 11 &amp;amp; 12)” is implemented by the Secondary Education Department under the Department of Education, Government of Uttarakhand. The scheme aims to encourage academic excellence among students entering Classes 11 and 12 by providing financial assistance to meritorious students who have secured 80% or more marks in the Uttarakhand Board Examination of Class 10 . 

**Benefits / Assistance:** ***Rate of Scholarship:*** - **For Class 11th Students:** ₹1,200/- per month for 1 year.- **For Class 12th Students:** ₹1,200/- per month for 1 year. 

**Application Process:** - To avail this scholarship, students are not required to appear for any examination. The department selects the students based on their board examination results. - To avail the benefits of this scheme, students must submit their Aadhaar card, Aadhaar-linked bank account, caste certificate, and the Uttarakhand Board passing certificate along with the application form. The scholarship amount is transferred to the student’s bank account once the school receives the allocated budget. 
Mode: Offline

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: Secondary Education Department, Government Of Uttarakhand; Nodal Department: Department Of Education
