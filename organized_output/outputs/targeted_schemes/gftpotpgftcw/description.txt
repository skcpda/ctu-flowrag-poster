# Grant for the Purchase of Tools/ Protective Gear for the Construction Workers

**Short Title:** GFTPOTPGFTCW

**Scheme Brief Description:** The "Grant for the Purchase of Tools/ Protective Gear for the Construction Workers'' by the BOCW Welfare Board, Labour Department, U.T. Administration of DNH & DD, is a welfare scheme that provides financial assistance to the eligible registered member for purchase of tools.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Financial assistance

**Where it applies:** Dadra & Nagar Haveli and Daman & Diu; State/ UT

**Detailed Description:** The &amp;quot;Grant for the Purchase of Tools/ Protective Gear for the Construction Workers&amp;quot; by the Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board, Labour Department, U.T. Administration of Dadra and Nagar Haveli and Daman and Diu, is a welfare scheme for the registered workers of the board. Under this scheme, registered beneficiaries of the board shall be provided with financial assistance for purchase of tools.

**Benefits / Assistance:** - An amount not exceeding ₹10,000/-. **Note:** The benefit shall be allowed for once in a three years.

**Application Process:** &gt; **Registration Process as a Building/ Construction Worker:** **Step 1:** Application for registration is to be made in the prescribed form and is to be accompanied by prescribed documents and a fee of not more than ₹50/-. **Step 2:** Application for registration has to be made to the officer authorized by the Board on this behalf. &gt; **Application Process of the Welfare Scheme:** **Step 1:** Any registered worker can make an application in the prescribed [Form-III](https://www.daman.nic.in/websites/Printing-press-Daman/documents/2020/Extraordinary/Extra%20Ordinary%20No.%2029.pdf) (Refer Page No: 8) along with the mandatory documents (self attested, if required). **Step 2:** Application shall be submitted to the Member Secretary (BOCWWB) for this benefit. &gt; **Check Beneficiary Details:** Visit the [official website](https://bocwddd.in/welfare-schemes/dnh/purchase-of-tools) and enter the “BOCW Registration No.” of the concerned beneficiary in the Search box. **Helpdesk:** BOCW Department: EMAIL ID: labour-dnh@nic.in PHONE NUMBER : 0260 - 2642374 ADDRESS: Old Circuit House Building, Opposite Bal Bhawan, Silvassa, Dadra and Nagar Haveli Pin:- 396230
