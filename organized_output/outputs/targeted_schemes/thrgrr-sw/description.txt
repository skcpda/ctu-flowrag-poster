# Thatched House Repair Grant for Re-thatching & Repairs (Sainik Welfare)

**Short Title:** THRGRR-SW

**Scheme Brief Description:** Through the "Thatched House Repair Grant for Re-thatching & Repairs" scheme, up to ₹40,000/- is provided to the registered ESM / Widows of ESM / Dependents of ESM every three years, for the re-thatching & repair of their house. The applicant should be receiving a Subsistence Allowance.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Housing & Shelter; Sector: Shelter, integrated support and assistance

**Where it applies:** Puducherry; State/ UT

**Detailed Description:** &quot;Thatched House Repair Grant for Re-thatching &amp; Repairs&quot; is a Welfare Scheme by the Armed Forces Flag Day Fund of the Department of Sainik Welfare, Puducherry. Through this scheme, up to ₹40,000/- is provided to the registered ESM / Widows of ESM / Dependents of ESM every three years, for the re-thatching &amp; repair of their house. The applicant should be receiving a Subsistence Allowance and living in a thatched house. The applications are accepted offline.

**Benefits / Assistance:** - The actual cost of damage or a maximum of ₹40,000/- is provided for the re-thatching and repair of the house. - The amount, as decided by the Management Committee, is credited to the beneficiary’s Bank Account through RTGS/ NEFT.

**Exclusions / Not eligible:** The scope of this scheme will not apply to those ESM / living spouse/widows who are re-employed as regular / deemed to be regular in Government/Public Sector Undertakings/Private Organizations and self-employed IT assesses.
