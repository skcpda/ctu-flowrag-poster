# Uttarakhand Shaheed Kosh Anudan

**Short Title:** UKSKA

**Scheme Brief Description:** The scheme aims to provide financial assistance to widows/dependents of soldiers & paramilitary personnel who were martyred in wars, border clashes, or internal security operations. Under this scheme, a one-time grant of ₹10 lakh is provided, subject to eligibility criteria.

**Who is this for:** Family; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Financial assistance

**Where it applies:** Uttarakhand; State/ UT

**DBT Scheme:** Yes (Direct Benefit Transfer)

**Detailed Description:** The scheme &amp;quot;Uttarakhand Shaheed Kosh Anudan&amp;quot; is implemented by the State Sainik Board under the Sainik Welfare Department, Government of Uttarakhand. It aims to provide financial assistance to the widows/dependents of soldiers and paramilitary personnel who were martyred in wars, border clashes, or internal security operations. Under this scheme, a one-time lump sum grant of ₹10 lakh is provided, subject to eligibility criteria based on the martyr’s family status. 

**Benefits / Assistance:** - **One-Time Grant:** ₹10,00,000/- per martyr 

**Application Process:** - After obtaining the application form from the District Sainik Welfare Office, the applicant must submit it to the concerned office. - The following documents are required for the application: Bank account passbook, preliminary report related to war, part-two order of the record office/Director General&amp;#39;s office related to the war incident, battle casualty certificate of martyr soldier, relationship certificate issued by the related record office/Director General&amp;#39;s office of dependents, discharge book, identity card of dependents, Certificate of non-payment of ex-gratia grant issued by the District Sainik Welfare and Rehabilitation Office, Certificate of battle casualty/operational casualty issued by the Director General, Paramilitary Forces (for Paramilitary Forces), Certificate of battle casualty/operational casualty issued by the Director General, Central Armed Police Forces (for Central Armed Police Forces) - After verification of the applications at the District Sainik Welfare Office level, the approved amount is paid to the applicants found correct through DBT.
