# Special Incentive Assistance to Socially and Educationally Backward Class Trainees for Pre-preparation of Competitive Examination

**Short Title:** SIASEBCTPCE

**Scheme Brief Description:** Under this scheme, to provide financial assistance of up to ₹20,000/- to socially and educationally backward class students preparing for state and central government job exams.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Education & Learning; Sector: Universities and higher education

**Where it applies:** Gujarat; State/ UT

**DBT Scheme:** Yes (Direct Benefit Transfer)

**Detailed Description:** The scheme “Special Incentive Assistance to Socially and Educationally Backward Class Trainees for Pre-Preparation of Competitive Examination” was launched by the Social Justice and Empowerment Department, Government of Gujarat. The scheme aims to encourage SEBC candidates to pursue coaching for government competitive exams by providing financial support. Under this scheme, eligible trainees receive up to ₹20,000 or the actual coaching fee paid (whichever is lower) for preparing for Class 1, 2, or 3 government job exams conducted by state or central bodies. 

**Benefits / Assistance:** **Financial Assistance:** - ₹20,000/- or actual fee paid (whichever is lower) for coaching related to any one exam. **Exams Include:** - State-Level: Gujarat Public Service Commission (GPSC), Gujarat Secondary Service Selection Board (GSSSB), (3) State Police Recruitment, Gujarat Panchayat Service Selection Board (GPSB), etc. - Central-Level: Banking, Railways, Army Recruitment, Central Police Force (CRPF, BSF, CISF etc), Staff Selection Commission (SSC). **Applicable for Exams:** - Class 1, 2, and 3 level government posts. **Mode of Disbursement:** - Amount is credited via Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) to the trainee&#39;s bank account. 

**Application Process:** **Step 01:** The eligible applicant may visit the e-Samaj Kalyan Portal: [https://esamajkalyan.gujarat.gov.in/](https://esamajkalyan.gujarat.gov.in/) **Step 02:** On the home page, under the tab ‘Citizen Login’, click on New User ‘[Please Register Here](https://esamajkalyan.gujarat.gov.in/Registration.aspx)’. **Step 03:** Enter your Full Name as per Aadhaar Card, Gender, Date of Birth, Aadhaar Card Number, Email ID, Caste etc. and then click on ‘Register’. **Step 04:** After successful registration, the applicants can login through their User ID and Password. **Step 05:** Now, click on ‘User Profile’ to update your profile. **Step 06:** Fill in all the mandatory information and then click on ‘Update’. **Step 07:** After updating the profile successfully, select the scheme appearing on the home page; an application form will open. **Step 08:** Fill in all the mandatory details in the application form and upload all the relevant documents. **Step 09:** Now agree with the terms &amp;amp; conditions and click on ‘Save Application’. **Step 10:** Submit the application form and take out the print of the application for future reference. The applicant can note down the application number to track the application status by logging into the portal using their User ID and Password.
