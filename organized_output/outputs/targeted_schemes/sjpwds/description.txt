# Special Jobs to PwDs - Clerical Assistant

**Short Title:** SJPWDCA

**Scheme Brief Description:** The scheme aims to provide employment opportunities to Persons with Disabilities (PwDs) to enhance their financial independence and social inclusion. Through this scheme, PwDs are employed as Clerical Assistants for 89 Days under the District Panchayat offices of their respective islands.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Skills & Employment; Sector: Employment services and jobs

**Where it applies:** Lakshadweep; State/ UT

**Detailed Description:** The scheme &amp;quot;Special Job to PwDs - Clerical Assistant&amp;quot; was introduced by the Department of Social Welfare &amp;amp; Tribal Affairs, Union Territory of Lakshadweep, with the aim of providing temporary employment opportunities to Persons with Disabilities (PwDs) having a minimum of 40% disability in the UT. Under this scheme, 11 PwD candidates are employed as Clerical Assistants on an 89-day rotation basis in the District Panchayat offices of their respective islands to address issues related to disabled persons and other associated matters.

**Benefits / Assistance:** 1. Persons with Disabilities are employed in the District Panchayat offices of their respective islands. 1. A monthly salary of ₹11,000/-. **Note:** Employment is provided on 89 days rotation basis.

**Application Process:** **Step 1:** Eligible applicants can collect the application form from the Village (Dweep) Panchayat Office or District Panchayat Office of their respective islands. **Step 2**: Carefully fill out the application form and attach all required supporting documents. **Step 3:** Submit the completed application form along with the necessary documents to the concerned officer at the Village (Dweep) Panchayat Office or District Panchayat Office.
Mode: Offline

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: District Panchayat Of Lakshadweep; Nodal Department: Social Welfare and Tribal Affairs Department, Lakshadweep
