# Transportation Scheme (Gujarat Gopalak Vikas Nigam)

**Short Title:** TSGGVN

**Scheme Brief Description:** The scheme aims to provide loans for the purchase of auto rickshaws and loading vehicles. Through this scheme, financial assistance for vehicle purchase is provided to the Rabari and Bharwad castes from the socially and educationally backwards classes.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Transport & Infrastructure; Sector: Public transport and private vehicles

**Where it applies:** Gujarat; State/ UT

**Detailed Description:** The scheme &quot;Transportation Scheme&quot; by the Gujarat Gopalak Vikas Nigam, Government of Gujarat, aims to provide loans for the purchase of auto rickshaws and loading vehicles. Through this scheme, financial assistance for vehicle purchase is offered to the Rabari and Bharwad castes from the socially and educationally backwards classes. The scheme is implemented by the Gujarat Gopalak Vikas Nigam. 

**Benefits / Assistance:** Loans are provided on an individual basis to purchase auto rickshaws and loading vehicles. - Maximum Loan Limit: ₹2,00,000/- - Interest Rate: 6% per annum - Loan Amount: 95% of the vehicle cost - Beneficiary Contribution: 5% of the vehicle cost 

**Application Process:** **Step 1: Register on the Official Portal** - Go to the [Official Website](https://ggdconline.gujarat.gov.in/) Gujarat Gopalak Development Corporation.- Scroll down on the homepage, find the &quot;Transportation Scheme,&quot; and click on &quot;Apply Now&quot;. - You will be directed to the [application form](https://ggdconline.gujarat.gov.in/SchemeTransport/AddEdit). **Step 2: Access and Fill Out the Application Form** - Select &quot;Transport&quot;, enter Unit Cost, and specify the Required Loan Amount. - Provide Aadhaar, Ration, Voter ID, full name (Last, First, Father/Husband), address, district, taluka, village, PIN, date of birth, age (21-45 as of 14/08/2023), caste, subcaste, phone, mobile, email, gender, and marital status. - Enter income certificate number, issue date, issuing officer&#39;s name, total annual family income (up to ₹ 3,00,000/-), and parent/guardian&#39;s occupation. - If &quot;Physically Handicapped,&quot; select &quot;Yes&quot; or &quot;No&quot;. If you selected &quot;Yes&quot;, provide the percentage (in %). - Indicate if any family member works in a government or private job. If &quot;Yes,&quot; provide their Name, Designation, Place, and Salary details. - Select your Education Qualification: Uneducated, Educated, Graduation, Post Graduation, or Other Degree. - If you have taken a past loan, select &quot;Yes&quot; or &quot;No&quot;. If you selected &quot;Yes&quot;, provide loan details (date, amount, paid, remaining) and your account number. - Enter driving license number and expiry date. For passenger vehicles, select &quot;Yes&quot; or &quot;No&quot;. If you selected &quot;Yes&quot;, provide the base number. For transport vehicles, select &quot;Yes&quot; or &quot;No&quot;. If you selected &quot;Yes&quot;, provide the transport license number. - Provide the dealer&#39;s name, address, vehicle name, and vehicle price. - Provide bank account number, bank name, branch name, name as per account, account type (Saving/Current), MICR code, and IFSC code. **Step 3: Review and Final Submission** - Carefully review each module. Use the preview option to examine the application before final submission. Fill out the provided &quot;CAPTCHA&quot; and Click on “Submit”.  **Track and Review Status** - Applicant will receive an Application ID/Reference Number. Save or print the acknowledgement for future tracking. - Go to the [Official Website](https://ggdconline.gujarat.gov.in/) Gujarat Gopalak Development Corporation.- Scroll down and locate the Know App Status. Click on &quot;Know App Status&quot;. - Select the Scheme name, Enter App No., and Birth Date. Fill out the Captcha and click on Submit. 
Mode: Online
