# Assistance for Protected Cultivation

**Short Title:** AFPC

**Scheme Brief Description:** The scheme “Assistance for Protected Cultivation” by the Agriculture and Co-operation Department, Government of Gujarat aims to promote protected horticulture practices by providing financial assistance to farmers for mulching, plug nursery, polyhouse, shed net house, and nursery development.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Agriculture,Rural & Environment; Sector: Financial assistance

**Where it applies:** Gujarat; State/ UT

**Detailed Description:** The scheme “Assistance for Protected Cultivation” by the Agriculture and Co-operation Department, Government of Gujarat aims to promote modern, climate-resilient horticulture practices by providing financial support for protected cultivation infrastructure like mulching, nursery development, and plug nurseries. The scheme encourages self-employment and sustainable income generation for farmers through the use of polyhouses, shed net houses, and starter kits to improve crop productivity and resource efficiency. 

**Benefits / Assistance:** > **Plastic Cover (Mulching):** - **General Area:** ₹32,000/- per hectare. - **Hilly Area:** ₹36,800/- per hectare. - 50% of the expenditure incurred, limited to 1 hectare per beneficiary. - **Self-Employment Oriented Horticulture Nursery Development Programme:** - **Nursery Structure:** ₹3,50,000/- per unit - ₹3,00,000/- per unit for 500 sq.m. area - **Starter Kit:** ₹50,000/- per unit - Assistance up to 65% of the cost or ₹2,27,500/-, whichever is less. > **Shed Net House &amp; Polyhouse:** - **Coverage:** 500 sq.m to 4,000 sq.m - Assistance as per square meter cost based on empanelled company prices - Structures supported: Cylindrical, bamboo, and wooden. > **Plug Nursery:** - **Public sector:** ₹30,00,000/- per hectare for 1 hectare - **Private sector:** Assistance of 50% or ₹15,00,000/- per hectare (whichever is less) for 1 hectare.
