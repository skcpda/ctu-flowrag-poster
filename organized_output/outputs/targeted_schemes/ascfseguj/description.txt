# Assistance to Scheduled Castes for Fisheries: Scheme For Fish Sale Equipment - Gujarat

**Short Title:** ASCFSEGUJ

**Scheme Brief Description:** The scheme aims to increase fish production and provide employment to Scheduled Caste (SC) individuals engaged in inland fisheries. Through this scheme, financial assistance is provided for purchasing fish sale equipment to SC fish farmers and fishermen.

**Who is this for:** Business Entity, Individual, Registered Societies, Self Help Groups (SHGS); Category: Agriculture,Rural & Environment; Sector: Fishing and hunting

**Where it applies:** Gujarat; State/ UT

**Detailed Description:** The scheme &quot;Assistance to Scheduled Castes for Fisheries with the component scheme for Fish Sale Equipments, Gujarat&quot; introduced by the Agriculture, Farmers Welfare and Co-operation Department, Government of Gujarat, aims to increase fish production and provide employment to Scheduled Caste (SC) individuals engaged in inland fisheries. Through this scheme, financial assistance is provided for purchasing fish sale equipment to SC fish farmers and fishermen. The scheme is implemented by the Commissioner of Fisheries, Government of Gujarat. **Objectives:** - To increase fish production by rearing fish in reservoirs and provide employment to scheduled caste (SC) people involved in inland fisheries activities. - To boost fish farming or fishing activities in freshwater bodies by scheduled caste (SC) fish farmers and fishermen. - Fishing in reservoirs, aquaculture in ponds, and catch from rivers increase fish production and boost inland fisheries through scheduled caste (SC) fish farmers and fishermen. - The main objective is to provide employment opportunities to Scheduled Caste fishermen in the state and increase fish production. 

**Benefits / Assistance:** Assistance is provided at 50% of the total unit purchase cost of ₹15,000/-, which amounts to ₹7,500/-. > **This assistance covers four components:** - An insulated box (₹1,000/-), - A box (₹500/-), - A renkadi (₹5,000/-), - A weighing unit (₹1,000/-).  > **Frequency of Disbursement:** This benefit can be availed once in a lifetime.

And for all select the District, Taluka, and Village, choose &#39;what type of account holder you are?&#39;, enter your Account Number and Name, solve the Captcha, and click &#39;Save and Next&#39;. **Step 4:** Enter your Mobile Number and E-mail address, click &#39;Send OTP&#39;, enter the received OTP, create and confirm a Password, solve the Captcha, and click &#39;Submit&#39;. > **Login Process:** **Step 1:** The applicant may visit and apply online at the [I-Khedut Portal](https://ikhedut.gujarat.gov.in/). **Step 2:** Click on &#39;Login&#39; from the top right-hand side of the homepage.
