# Hon’ble Chief Minister’s Merit Scholarship Scheme-Sikkim

**Short Title:** HCMMSS-SIKKIM

**Scheme Brief Description:** This scheme aims to provide free education up to the Graduation level to talented and deserving children from underprivileged families with an opportunity to study in reputed public schools both within and outside the state.

**Launch Date:** 2010-04-01

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Education & Learning; Sector: Scholarships and student finance

**Where it applies:** Sikkim; State/ UT

**Detailed Description:** The scheme &amp;quot;Hon’ble Chief Minister’s Merit Scholarship Scheme&amp;quot; was launched by the Education Department, Government of Sikkim in the year 2010. This scheme aims to provide free education up to the Graduation level to talented and deserving children from underprivileged family with an opportunity to study in reputed Public Schools both within and outside the State. The selected students will be admitted to the Public Schools empanelled with HRD Department within and outside the State.

**Benefits / Assistance:** - **Free Education:** Full waiver of academic fees up to the Graduation level for selected students.- **School Admission:** Admission into reputed public schools empanelled by the HRD Department within and outside the state.

**Application Process:** &gt; ***Application Process:*** **Step 1:** To avail of the benefits of the scheme, the parents/guardians need to contact their respective government schools. **Step 2:** The head of the government school nominates the 1st rank holder of the class 5th Annual Examination. **Step 3:** Nominated students submit required documents to the Human Resource Development (HRD) Department. &gt; ***Selection Test Process:*** **Step 1:** Eligible students appear for a written selection test conducted by the Examination Section, HRD Department. **Step 2:** Selected students are announced based on test performance and verification. **Step 3:** Selected students are allotted seats in the empanelled with the HRD Department within and outside the state.
Mode: Offline

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Education Department
