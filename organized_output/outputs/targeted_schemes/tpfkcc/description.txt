# To Provide Interest Subvention to Fishermen of the State at the Rate of 4% to the Beneficiaries Kishan Credit Card

**Short Title:** TPFKCC

**Scheme Brief Description:** The scheme aims to provide 4% interest subvention to the fishermen in Gujarat on timely repayment of short-term loans taken through the Kisan Credit Card.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Agriculture,Rural & Environment; Sector: Fishing and hunting

**Where it applies:** Gujarat; State/ UT

**Detailed Description:** The scheme &amp;quot;To Provide Interest Subvention to Fishermen of the State at the Rate of 4% to the Beneficiaries Kishan Credit Card&amp;quot; introduced by the Agriculture, Farmers Welfare and Cooperation Department, Gujarat, aims to provide interest subvention to fishermen who avail short-term loans under the Kisan Credit Card scheme. If the loan is repaid on time, the beneficiaries receive a 4% interest relief, which helps reduce their financial burden and support sustainable fisheries-based livelihoods. The benefit can be claimed once per financial year.

**Benefits / Assistance:** **Name of Component** **Standard of Assistance** **Minimum Time Limit (in Year) for Reclaiming Benefits)**To provide interest subvention to fishermen of the state at the rate of 4% to the beneficiaries Kishan Credit Card.4% interest subvention on timely repayment of short-term loans taken by animal husbandry up to ₹2,00,000/-.only once in a financial year

**Application Process:** - The bank from which the short term loan has been availed by the fisherman has to submit the integrated interest subvention proposal of its area of operation to the Registrar, Co-operative Societies, Gujarat State, Gandhinagar.
