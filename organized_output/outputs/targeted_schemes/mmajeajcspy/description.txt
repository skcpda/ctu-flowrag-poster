# Mukhyamantri Anusuchit Jati Ewam Anusuchit Janjati Civil Sewa Protsahan Yojana

**Short Title:** MMAJEAJCSPY

**Scheme Brief Description:** The objective of the scheme is to provide financial assistance to SC & ST candidates who qualify in the preliminary examination of Civil Services Competitive Examination conducted by the BPSC, Patna, and the UPSC, New Delhi, to support their preparation for main examination/interview.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Education & Learning; Sector: Coaching

**Where it applies:** Bihar; State/ UT

**Detailed Description:** The scheme &amp;quot;Mukhyamantri Anusuchit Jati Evam Anusuchit Janjati Civil Sewa Protsahan Yojana&amp;quot; has been initiated by the Scheduled Caste and Scheduled Tribe Welfare Department, Government of Bihar. The primary objective of the scheme is to provide financial assistance to meritorious Scheduled Caste and Scheduled Tribe students who qualify in the preliminary examination of the Civil Services Competitive Examination conducted by the Bihar Public Service Commission, Patna, and the Union Public Service Commission, New Delhi, to support their preparation for the main examination and interview.

**Benefits / Assistance:** Under this scheme, candidates who pass the preliminary examination of Civil Services Competitive Examinations are eligible for the following incentives: 1. Scheduled Caste and Scheduled Tribe candidates who pass the Preliminary Examination of the Combined Competitive Examination conducted by the Bihar Public Service Commission, Patna, will receive a one-time financial assistance of ₹50,000/- for further preparation. 1. Scheduled Caste and Scheduled Tribe candidates who pass the Preliminary Examination of the Civil Services Competitive Examination conducted by the Union Public Service Commission, New Delhi, will receive a one-time financial assistance of ₹1,00,000/- for further preparation.

**Exclusions / Not eligible:** 1. Candidates who are already employed in any Government Department, Public Sector Undertaking, or State-Funded Institution are not eligible for this scheme.
