# Border Areas Scholarship

**Short Title:** BAS

**Scheme Brief Description:** The scheme aims to provide annual scholarships to economically weaker students from border areas, supporting them in pursuing education up to the degree level.

**Launch Date:** 1973-01-01

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Education & Learning; Sector: Scholarships and student finance

**Where it applies:** Meghalaya; State

**Detailed Description:** The scheme &amp;quot;Border Areas Scholarship&amp;quot; was launched by the Border Areas Development Department, Government of Meghalaya, aims to provide financial assistance to students from notified international border areas who face economic hardship. Under this scheme, year-wise scholarships or subsidies are provided to students studying in higher secondary schools and Colleges up to the Degree level. The Border Areas Development Department implements the scheme to help students continue their education without interruption due to financial constraints. 

**Benefits / Assistance:** - Financial assistance in the form of annual scholarships/subsidies. - Awarded year-wise based on promotion examination results. - Intended to support education up to the Degree level for students from notified border area villages. 

**Exclusions / Not eligible:** 1. The applicant whose parents are in government service is not eligible. 1. Students pursuing a Ph.D. or M.Phil. are not entitled to the subsidy/scholarship. 1. The scholarship will be lost if the applicant participates in government political activities or demonstrations. 1. B.Ed and M.Ed students currently pursuing the course are eligible, but those already in service are not entitled to the scholarship. 

**Application Process:** - Students may contact the Head of their respective educational institutions to avail the benefits of the scheme. Note: To download the application form, [click here](https://meghalaya.gov.in/sites/default/files/forms/5border_area.pdf). 
Mode: Offline
