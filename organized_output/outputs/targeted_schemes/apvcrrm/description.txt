# Assistance for Planting Vegetable Crops for Raw and Ripe Mandap

**Short Title:** APVCRRM

**Scheme Brief Description:** The scheme provides financial assistance to farmers in Gujarat for setting up vegetable trellises (Mandap) for raw, semi-ripe, and ripe vegetables, with varying support for General, SC, and ST farmers.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Agriculture,Rural & Environment; Sector: Financial assistance

**Where it applies:** Gujarat; State/ UT

**Detailed Description:** The scheme &amp;quot;Assistance for Planting Vegetable Crops for Raw and Ripe Mandap&amp;quot; introduced by the Agriculture, Farmers Welfare and Cooperation Department, Gujarat, aims to offer financial support to farmers across Gujarat for constructing vegetable trellises (mandaps) using wooden/bamboo supports and GI/plastic wires.

**Benefits / Assistance:** &gt; **Raw Mandap (Tomato/Chilli and other vegetables):** - General Farmers: 50% subsidy, up to ₹26,000/ha. - SC/ST Farmers: 75% subsidy, up to ₹39,000/ha. &gt; **Semi-ripe Mandap (Vine vegetables):** - General Farmers: 50% subsidy, up to ₹40,000/ha. - SC/ST Farmers: 75% subsidy, up to ₹60,000/ha. &gt; **Ripe Mandap (Vine vegetables):** - General Farmers: 50% subsidy, up to ₹80,000/ha. - SC/ST Farmers: 75% subsidy, up to ₹1,20,000/ha. &gt; **Other Support Details:** - Maximum area eligible: 2 hectares per farmer. - Construction material: 1600 wooden/bamboo poles per hectare, 400 kg GI wire (12-18 gauge) or plastic wire. - Recommended spacing: 2.50 x 2.50 meters.
