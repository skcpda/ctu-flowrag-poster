# Sabji Shetra Vistar Yojana

**Short Title:** SSVY

**Scheme Brief Description:** Under the Sabji Shetra Vistar yojana, selection of vegetables through quality high yielding varieties to expand the area of crops and increase production.

**Launch Date:** 2016-08-02

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Agriculture,Rural & Environment; Sector: Financial assistance

**Where it applies:** Madhya Pradesh; State/ UT

**Detailed Description:** The Integrated Horticulture Development Mission Scheme of the Central Government, a subsidy (grant) is provided to the farmers of the state in the sabji shetra vistar yojana with the help of the Madhya Pradesh State Horticulture Mission. Under the scheme, a 50% grant amount is given to the farmers for planting hybrid seed vegetables like okra, guarphali, gilki, loki, tomato, pumpkin, cucumber, capsicum, etc. In this, the farmer will be given the benefit of the scheme up to a maximum of 2 hectares.

**Benefits / Assistance:** - 50% of the cost of seeds in vegetable crops up to a maximum of ₹10,000/- Whatever is less, a grant will be given per hectare. - Root and tuber commercial crops, arbi etc. production, planting material 50% of the cost subject to a maximum of ₹30,000/- per hectare, whichever is less. This grant will be payable.

Step 3: Enter your mobile number and click on OTP Send Step 4: Now, please fill in the details and upload the documents. Step 5: After, fill all the information and upload the document click on the submit button
Mode: Online
