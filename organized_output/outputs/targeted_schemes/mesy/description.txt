# Matritva, Shishu Evam Baalika Madad Yojana

**Short Title:** MSEBMY

**Scheme Brief Description:** "Maatrtv, Shishu Evan Baalika Madad Yojana" is a initiative of Uttar Pradesh state Government to provide the financial assistance to the mother and her girl child.

**Launch Date:** 2018-12-20

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Women and Child; Sector: Financial Assistance

**Where it applies:** Uttar Pradesh; State/ UT

**Detailed Description:** The &quot;Matritva, Shishu Evam Baalika Madad Yojana&quot; is a scheme launched by the Uttar Pradesh government to provide financial assistance to construction worker women and wives of construction workers in the state during pregnancy and childbirth. The aim of this scheme is to ensure safe and healthy pregnancies and deliveries for women.  The objective of the scheme is to provide nutritious food to the newborn babies of eligible beneficiaries working in building and other construction processes from their birth till they complete 02 years of age and to the wives of registered female workers and registered male workers after delivery. Along with this, the objective of the scheme is to create positive thinking in the society towards the birth of girls and to give positive results by encouraging legal system like adult marriage.

**Benefits / Assistance:** **Matritva Hitlabh (Maternity benefits)** 1. Registered male workers: ₹6,000/- lump sum. 1. Female workers for institutional delivery: 3 months&#39; minimum wage equivalent + ₹1,000/- medical bonus. 1. Female workers for miscarriage: 6 weeks&#39; minimum wage equivalent. 1. Female workers for sterilization: 2 weeks&#39; minimum wage equivalent.  **Shishu Hitlabh (Child benefits)** 1. For son: ₹20,000/- lump sum. 1. For daughter: ₹25,000/- lump sum. 1. For first or second daughter, or legally adopted daughter: ₹25,000/- fixed deposit (matures if unmarried at 18). 1. For handicapped daughter: ₹50,000/- fixed deposit (matures if unmarried at 18).

**Application Process:** > **How to get register and apply for the scheme** 1. Applicant must visit the below offices - Nearest labor office. - Tehsildar of the concerned tehsil. - Block Development Officer of the concerned development block. 1. Get the application form and fill out the completely. 1. Attach required documents in the prescribed format. 1. Submit the application form in the same office.
Mode: Offline

Applicant must visit the official [web portal](https://www.uplmis.in/Guest/frm_CreateLogin.aspx?type=2).1. Provide the required details in application form. - Applicant/Worker details - Child details - Bank Account Details - Submit the required documents 1. Submit the application request.
