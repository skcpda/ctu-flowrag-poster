# Scholarship to Divyang Students (Class 1 to 8)

**Short Title:** SDSC18

**Scheme Brief Description:** The scheme aims to provide financial assistance of ₹600/- for students in Class 1 to 5 and ₹960/- for students in Class 6 to 8, to regularly enrolled disable students.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Education & Learning; Sector: Scholarships and student finance

**Where it applies:** Uttarakhand; State/ UT

**Detailed Description:** The scheme “Scholarship to Divyang Students (Class 1 to 8)” is implemented by the Department of Social Welfare, Government of Uttarakhand. The scheme aims to provide financial assistance to regularly enrolled disable students from Class 1 to 8. 

**Benefits / Assistance:** ***Rate of Scholarships:*** - **Class 1 to 5:** ₹600/- - **Class 6 to 8:** ₹960/- 

**Application Process:** - Students can apply online for scholarship by visiting the NSP Portal of Government of India [(https://scholarships.gov.in/]((https://scholarships.gov.in/)). - The following documents will be attached with the application: Aadhaar card of the applicant, mobile number, certified photo and CBS bank account which is Aadhaar linked/seeded, previous class passed mark sheet, disability certificate, income certificate, self-declaration certificate that the applicant is not taking benefit of any other scholarship scheme of the government. - The student sends the application to his/her educational institution; the concerned educational institution verifies the application form and forwards it online to the District Social Welfare Officer. - The District Social Welfare Officer examines the received application form and gets the complete application forms physically verified. Incomplete applications (which can be corrected) are temporarily marked as defective, otherwise they are completely rejected. The scholarship amount is paid to the eligible applicants directly in their bank account in the financial year as per the availability of budget. 
Mode: Online

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Department Of Social Welfare
