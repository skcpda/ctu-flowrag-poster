# Marriage Assistance to Person with Disability / BPL

**Short Title:** MAPDBPL

**Scheme Brief Description:** The scheme aims to support differently-abled brides/bridegrooms from BPL families by providing financial assistance to meet marriage-related expenses. Under this scheme, an amount of ₹30,000/- is provided to Scheduled Tribe, differently-abled brides/bridegrooms for their first marriage.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Financial assistance

**Where it applies:** Lakshadweep; State/ UT

**DBT Scheme:** Yes (Direct Benefit Transfer)

**Detailed Description:** The scheme &amp;quot;Marriage Assistance to Persons with Disability/BPL&amp;quot; was introduced by the Social Welfare &amp;amp; Tribal Affairs Department, Union Territory of Lakshadweep, for the benefit of persons with disabilities (differently-abled) belonging to the Below Poverty Line (BPL) category. The scheme aims to support differently-abled brides or bridegrooms from BPL families by providing financial assistance to meet marriage-related expenses and to encourage them to lead a dignified and normal life. Under this scheme, an amount of ₹30,000/- is provided to Scheduled Tribe, differently-abled brides or bridegrooms, and is applicable only for the first marriage.

**Benefits / Assistance:** - The scheme provides a financial assistance of ₹30,000/- to differently-abled individuals belonging to the BPL category for their first marriage. > **Mode of Disbursement:** The amount will be directly transferred to the beneficiary&#39;s bank account. > **Frequency of Disbursement:** One-time assistance.

**Application Process:** **Step 1:** Eligible couples may collect the application form from the office of the Director (Social Welfare &amp;amp; Tribal Affairs) of their respective island. **Step 2:** Carefully fill out the application form and attach all the required supporting documents. **Step 3:** Submit the completed application form along with the documents to the Director (SW&amp;amp;TA) of the respective island, duly forwarded by the DC/SDO of the concerned island and recommended by the Island Level Committee. Note: The beneficiary can apply for incentive within 30 days from the date of marriage and is applicable up to one year of marriage.
Mode: Offline

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: Directorate Of Social Welfare & Tribal Affairs, Lakshadweep; Nodal Department: Social Welfare and Tribal Affairs Department, Lakshadweep
