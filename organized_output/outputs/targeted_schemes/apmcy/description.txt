# Alpasankhyak Post-Maitrik Chhaatravrtti Yojana

**Short Title:** APMCY

**Scheme Brief Description:** The Alpasankhyak Post-Matric Scholarship Scheme under the Madhya Pradesh Government is to provide financial assistance to students from minority communities in Madhya Pradesh pursuing post-secondary education.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Education & Learning; Sector: Scholarships and student finance

**Where it applies:** Madhya Pradesh; State/ UT

**Detailed Description:** The Alpasankhyak Post-Matric Scholarship Scheme under the Higher Education Department of the Madhya Pradesh goverment provides financial assistance to students from minority communities in Madhya Pradesh pursuing post-secondary education. This scheme aims to alleviate the financial burden on economically disadvantaged minority students, enabling them to access quality education and pursue higher studies. By offering scholarships for tuition fees, maintenance allowances, and other educational expenses, it seeks to promote inclusive growth and empower minority communities through education.

**Benefits / Assistance:** 1. Tuition fee of ₹3,000/- per year for graduate and postgraduate students. 1. Maintenance allowance of ₹570/- per month for undergraduate and postgraduate hostel students. 1. Maintenance allowance of ₹300/- per month for non-hostel students 1. Maintenance allowance M.Phil. And Ph.D. ₹1,200/- per month to hostel students. 1. Maintenance allowance of ₹550/- per month for non-hostel students 1. Maintenance allowance will be payable for 10 months.
