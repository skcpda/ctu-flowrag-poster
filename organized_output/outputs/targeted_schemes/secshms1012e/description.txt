# Scheme to Encourage the Children of Sanitation Workers Who have Passed the Class 10/12 Examination (GSKVN)

**Short Title:** SECSWWHPC1012E

**Scheme Brief Description:** Under this scheme, children of sanitation workers who have achieved first, second, or third rank in the board exams of Class 10 and Class 12 (General Stream and Science Stream) across the state are awarded cash prizes and certificates of appreciation.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Education & Learning; Sector: Scholarships and student finance

**Where it applies:** Gujarat; State/ UT

**Detailed Description:** The &amp;quot;Scheme to Encourage the Children of Sanitation Workers Who Have Passed the Class 10/12 Examination&amp;quot; is implemented by the Gujarat Safai Kamdar Vikas Nigam (GSKVN) under the Social Justice and Empowerment Department, Government of Gujarat. This scheme has been in operation since the year 2012-13. The objective of the scheme is to promote and encourage the academic excellence of children of sanitation workers who have passed the Class 10 or Class 12 board examinations with top ranks. Under this scheme, students securing first, second, or third rank in the board examinations (General and Science streams) across the state are awarded cash prizes and certificates of appreciation. 

**Benefits / Assistance:** > **Cash Prize Details:** **Rank****10th Class****12th Class (Science Stream/General Stream)**First Rank₹ 41,000/-₹ 31,000/-Second Rank₹ 21,000/-₹ 21,000/-Third Rank₹ 11,000/-₹ 11,000/-
