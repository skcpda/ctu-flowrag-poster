# Jan Nayak Pustakalaya evam Digital Adhyan Kendra 

**Short Title:** JNPEDAK

**Scheme Brief Description:** "Jan Nayak Pustakalaya evam Digital Adhyan Kendra" scheme aims to develop all Jannayak Karpoori Thakur Hostels, other backward class welfare hostels, and OBC girls’ hostels across the state.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Education & Learning

**Where it applies:** Bihar; State/ UT

**Detailed Description:** The &quot;Jan Nayak Pustakalaya evam Digital Adhyan Kendra&quot; scheme, under the BC &amp; EBC Welfare Department, Government of Bihar, aims to develop all Jannayak Karpoori Thakur Hostels, other backward class welfare hostels, and OBC girls’ hostels across the state into “Jan Nayak Pustakalaya evam Digital Adhyan Kendra.” **Objectives** (a) To ensure the availability of competitive and high-quality books and study material. (b) To ensure quality education and guidance through digital learning. (c) To organize inspirational guidance sessions through experts and previously successful candidates in competitive examinations. Through this initiative, students—especially those in remote areas—will have access to high-quality study material and expert guidance in a modern educational environment.

**Benefits / Assistance:** The scheme provides students with access to quality study materials, digital learning, and expert guidance to enhance their academic and competitive exam preparation.

**Application Process:** The scheme &quot;Jan Nayak Pustakalaya &amp; Digital Learning Centre&quot; by the BC &amp; EBC Welfare Department, Government of Bihar, does not require any formal registration or application process. Only the students enrolled/admitted to the notified institutions or residing in the notified hostels (like JNKT Hostel , OBC Welfare Hostel &amp; OBC Girls +2 school) can visit the library and/or learning centre and benefit from the scheme.
Mode: Offline

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: BC & EBC Welfare Department
