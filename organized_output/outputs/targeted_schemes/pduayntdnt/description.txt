# Pandit Dindayal Upadhyay Awas Yojana (Developing Caste Welfare) (NT-DNT)

**Short Title:** PDUAYNTDNT

**Scheme Brief Description:** The "Pandit Dindayal Upadhyay Awas Yojana (Developing Caste Welfare) (NT-DNT)" provides financial assistance of up to ₹1,20,000/- to the people belonging to Nomadic Tribes and Denotified Tribes.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Housing & Shelter; Sector: Housing

**Where it applies:** Gujarat; State/ UT

**Detailed Description:** The &amp;quot;Pandit Dindayal Upadhyay Awas Yojana (Developing Caste Welfare) (NT-DNT)&amp;quot; is a welfare scheme by the Social Justice and Empowerment Department of Gujarat. Under this scheme, eligible applicants from the Nomadic Tribe (NT) and Denotified Tribe (DNT) are given up to ₹1,20,000/- for constructing a house. Priority is given to individuals from BPL families.

**Benefits / Assistance:** - **Financial Assistance:** Up to ₹1,20,000/- for house construction.

Please Register Here&quot;. **Step 4:** Enter the required details and click Register. > **Application Process:** **Step 1:** Enter your User ID, Password, and Captcha Code, then click &quot;Login&quot;. **Step 2:** Read the scheme details carefully and click OK. **Step 3:** If your permanent address is correct, confirm it. Otherwise, enter your correct address. **Step 4:** Click Save &amp; Next to proceed. Upload the relevant documents. **Step 5:** If you need to make changes, click &quot;No, Cancel&quot;. If all details are correct, click &quot;Yes, Submit&quot;. **Step 6:** Click Save Application. Note down your Application Number for future reference. You can also take a printout by clicking Print Application. > **Check Application Status:** [Click here](https://esamajkalyan.gujarat.gov.in/ViewApplicationStatus.aspx).
