# Pension Scheme for State Agitators Other Than Category of Agitators Who Went to Jail for 7 Days or got Injured during the Uttarakhand State Movement

**Short Title:** PSSAOTCAJ7DIUSM

**Scheme Brief Description:** The scheme provides a monthly pension of ₹4,500/- to state agitators not imprisoned for 7 days or injured during the Uttarakhand State Movement, who are not eligible for any other government pension and are not employed in government service.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Pension

**Where it applies:** Uttarakhand; State/ UT

**Detailed Description:** The &amp;quot;Pension Scheme for State Agitators Other Than Category of Agitators Who went to Jail for 7 Days or got Injured during the Uttarakhand State Movement&amp;quot; scheme is implemented by the Police Department under the Department of Home, Government of Uttarakhand. The scheme aims to provide a monthly pension to state agitators who do not fall under the category of those imprisoned for 7 days or injured during the Uttarakhand State Movement, and who are not entitled to an agitational pension or any other government pension, nor are employed in government service. 

**Benefits / Assistance:** **Pension Amount:** ₹4,500/- per month 

**Application Process:** - The application must be submitted to the office of the District Magistrate of the concerned district. - The required documents must be attached with the application form, and the pension will be sanctioned only after verification by the District Magistrate. 
Mode: Offline

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: Police Department, Government Of Uttarakhand; Nodal Department: Department of Home

**Tags:** Pension, Agitator, Uttarakhand State Movement
