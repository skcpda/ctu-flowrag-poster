# Medical Assistance for the Construction Workers

**Short Title:** MAFTCW

**Scheme Brief Description:** "Medical Assistance for the Construction Workers" was launched by the Delhi Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board, Labour Dept, Delhi. The registered workers are given medical assistance upto ₹ 10,000/- in case of being hospitalized for 5 or more days due to an accident or disease.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Health & Wellness; Sector: Health care providers and access

**Where it applies:** Delhi; State/ UT

**Detailed Description:** The &amp;quot;Medical Assistance for the Construction Workers&amp;quot; by the Delhi Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board, Labour Department, Delhi, is a welfare scheme for the registered workers of the board. Through this scheme, the eligible workers of the board are provided with medical assistance upto ₹ 10,000/- in case of being hospitalized for 05 or more days due to an accident or any disease. 

**Benefits / Assistance:** - ₹2,000/- for the first five days. - ₹200/- each for remaining days, upto a maximum of ₹10,000/-, available from the date of registration.
