# Scheme for the Award of Scholarship for Post Graduate Students of Various Faculties of Junagadh Agricultural University

**Short Title:** SAPGJAU

**Scheme Brief Description:** The "Scheme for the Award of Scholarship for Post Graduate Students of Various Faculties of Junagadh Agricultural University" provides monthly scholarships to students pursuing Post Graduate in various faculties at Junagadh Agricultural University.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Education & Learning; Sector: Scholarships and student finance

**Where it applies:** Gujarat; State/ UT

**Detailed Description:** The &amp;quot;Scheme for the Award of Scholarship for Post Graduate Students of Various Faculties of Junagadh Agricultural University&amp;quot; launched by the Agriculture, Farmers Welfare and Cooperation Department, Gujarat, aims to provide monthly financial assistance to eligible Under Graduate students at Junagadh Agricultural University. The objective is to attract the bright and talented students to seek admission in various faculties of P.G. level, it is necessary to provide financial assistance.

**Benefits / Assistance:** - **Master’s degree students:** ₹2,000/- per month for 2 years. - **Ph.D. degree students:** ₹3,000/- per month for 3 years.

**Exclusions / Not eligible:** - An in-service candidate of the University or a deputee from any other institute/ organization shall not eligible for the scholarship.

**Application Process:** **Step 1:** Write an application on plain paper addressed to the Director, Junagadh Agricultural University. **Step 2:** Submit the completed application along with the necessary documents to the Principal of the college.
Mode: Offline

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Agriculture, Farmers Welfare and Cooperation Department
