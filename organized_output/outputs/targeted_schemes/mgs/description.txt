# Marriage Grant Scheme

**Short Title:** MGS

**Scheme Brief Description:** In Uttar Pradesh State, a scheme for the marriage of girls from economically weaker families of the state.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment

**Where it applies:** Uttar Pradesh; State

**Detailed Description:** The objective of the scheme is to provide grants for the marriage of the daughters of poor’s families of Other Backward Classes so that the economic condition of that family may improve. Regular efforts to uplift the level of poor families are being made by the Department of Backward Classes Welfare. There is a provision to provide marriage grants for the daughters of helpless, poor, and BPL families of Other Backward Classes.

**Benefits / Assistance:** The assistance of ₹20,000/- is provided per marriage.

**Application Process:** 1. Applicants must have to apply through Jan Suvidha Kendras, District Backward Classes Welfare Officer or on the [official website](https://www.shadianudan.upsdc.gov.in) of the department. 1. The applicant will compulsorily have to apply online prior to 90 days of the date of marriage or till post 90 days of marriage conducted. 1. Applicants will keep the hard copy of the online application along with all the self-attested uploaded documents like Aadhar Card, Income Certificate, Caste Certificate, Certificate showing the date fixed for marriage (Marriage Invitation card), documentary proof in case of widow/PwD certificate and documents related with a bank account in the form of print out.
Mode: Online

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Department of Social Welfare
