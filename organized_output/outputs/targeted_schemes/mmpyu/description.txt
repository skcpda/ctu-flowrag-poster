# Mukhyamantri Mahila Poshan Yojana

**Short Title:** MMPYU

**Scheme Brief Description:** The scheme aims to improve the health of pregnant and lactating women by providing nutritional food such as eggs twice a week and dates twice a week at Anganwadi Centres.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Women and Child

**Where it applies:** Uttarakhand; State/ UT

**Detailed Description:** The scheme &amp;quot;Mukhyamantri Mahila Poshan Yojana&amp;quot; was launched by the Department of Women Empowerment &amp;amp; Child Development, Government of Uttarakhand. The scheme aims to improve the health of pregnant and lactating women by providing nutritional food such as eggs twice a week and dates twice a week at Anganwadi Centres. 

**Benefits / Assistance:** Under the scheme, pregnant and lactating women are provided eggs two days a week and dates two days a week. Dates are given to those registered women who do not consume eggs.  

**Application Process:** Women who are registered at the Anganwadi Centre receive the benefits at the centre itself. 
Mode: Offline

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Department Of Woman Empowerment & Child Development
