# Capital Subsidy Scheme for Setting Up Solar Plant in Milk Cooperatives in Gujarat State (Tribal Area)

**Short Title:** CSSSSPG

**Scheme Brief Description:** The scheme aims to promote renewable energy and reduce electricity expenses. Through this scheme, a 75% subsidy is provided for setting up solar plants (1KW to 40KW) in milk cooperative societies in tribal areas.

**Who is this for:** Registered Societies; Category: Agriculture,Rural & Environment; Sector: Mechanization- solar power, farming systems

**Where it applies:** Gujarat; State/ UT

**Detailed Description:** The &amp;quot;Scheme of Capital Assistance for Setting Up Solar Plants in Milk Cooperative Societies in the State of Gujarat (Tribal Areas)&amp;quot; by the Department of Agriculture, Farmers Welfare and Cooperation, Government of Gujarat aims to promote renewable energy and reduce electricity expenses. Through this scheme, a 75% subsidy is provided for setting up solar plants (1KW to 40KW) in milk cooperative societies in tribal areas. 

**Benefits / Assistance:** - **Financial Assistance:** 75% subsidy on capital cost for installation of solar systems ranging from 1KW to 40KW.  

**Application Process:** **Step 1:** Collect the plain paper application form from the District Registrar&amp;#39;s office. **Step 2:** Fill in all required details in the application form and attach the necessary documents. **Step 3:** Submit the completed application to the District Registrar.
