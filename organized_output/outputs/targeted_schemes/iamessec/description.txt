# Assistance for Purchasing Study Materials for Medical and Engineering Students from Socially and Educationally Backward Classes

**Short Title:** APSMMESSEBC

**Scheme Brief Description:** The scheme aims to provide financial assistance to students from socially and educationally backward classes pursuing medical and engineering diploma or degree courses to support their education. Under this scheme, eligible students are provided a one-time grant for purchasing study materials.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Education & Learning; Sector: Scholarships and student finance

**Where it applies:** Gujarat; State/ UT

**Detailed Description:** The scheme “Assistance for Purchasing Study Materials for Medical and Engineering Students from Socially and Educationally Backward Classes” was launched by the Social Justice and Empowerment Department, Government of Gujarat. The scheme aims to provide financial assistance to students from socially and educationally backward classes pursuing medical and engineering diploma and degree courses, in order to support their education and reduce their financial burden. Under this scheme, eligible students receive a one-time grant for purchasing study materials. 

**Benefits / Assistance:** > **Financial assistance per student:** - For Medical Student: ₹10,000/- - For Engineering Student: ₹8,000/- - For Diploma Student: ₹3,000/- 

**Application Process:** &gt; **Application Process:** **Step 1:** The applicant may visit the Digital Gujarat Portal: [https://www.digitalgujarat.gov.in/](https://www.digitalgujarat.gov.in/) **Step 2:** On the home page, click on ‘Register’ and the applicant will be redirected to the registration form page. **Step 3:** Enter your Mobile Number, Email, Set Password, and then click on ‘Save’. **Step 4:** After successful registration, the applicant can login through his/her User Name (Mobile No./Email ID) and Password. **Step 5:** Now, click on ‘My Profile’ to update your profile and fill in all the mandatory information. **Step 6:** After filling in profile details, click on ‘Update Profile’. The profile will be updated after entering the verification Code (OTP). **Step 7:** Now under services, select ‘Scholarship Services’ and then click on ‘SC Scheme’. **Step 8:** Select the Financial Year for which you want to apply for and then click on ‘Change Financial Year’. **Step 9:** Select the scheme, read the interaction carefully, and then click on ‘Continue to Service’. **Step 10:** Fill in the application form completely and upload all the relevant documents. **Step 11:** Now, click on ‘Save as Draft’ and agree with the terms &amp;amp; conditions, and submit the application form by clicking on ‘Confirm &amp;amp; Final Submit’. &gt; **Instructions:** **Note 1:** Photo uploading for all applicants is mandatory. **Note 2:** Application will be considered complete only when all sections are completed. **Note 3:** Only one application per applicant will be acceptable for a scholarship. If any student submits more than one application either from a single institute or from multiple institutions his/her all applications will be rejected by the competent authority and no request from any side will be entertained. **Note 4:** In case of any wrong/misleading information provided in the application shall lead to rejection of the application by Department Authorities. **Note 5:** All required documents must be carefully scanned as per the instructions given. **Note 6:** The student has to fill his/her application carefully as there is no provision for institution change once the application is submitted successfully. **Note 7:** The unreadable uploaded documents will allow the competent authority to reject the student application. **Note 8:** Direct Individual submission of this application form will not be accepted in case of students studying inside the state. Only outside students have to fill out their form online and submit his/her application form to their native district officer.
