# Stipend To Students Studying at Govt. Hindi Institute Dimapur

**Short Title:** SSSGHID

**Scheme Brief Description:** The scheme aims to provide financial support to students enrolled at the Government Hindi Institute, Dimapur. Under this scheme, a monthly stipend of ₹400/- is given to student every year from the allocated scholarship fund provided by the State Government.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Education & Learning; Sector: Scholarships and student finance

**Where it applies:** Nagaland; State

**Detailed Description:** The scheme “Stipend to Students Studying at Govt. Hindi Institute Dimapur” was launched by the Department of School Education, Government of Nagaland, aims to provide a monthly stipend to students studying at the Government Hindi Institute, Dimapur. The stipend, provided at the rate of ₹400/- per month, is funded annually from the allocated fund under Scholarship by the State Government.

**Benefits / Assistance:** - Students receive a monthly stipend of ₹400/-.

**Application Process:** - Eligible applicants can apply for the scheme by contacting the concerned authority at their respective institute.
Mode: Offline
