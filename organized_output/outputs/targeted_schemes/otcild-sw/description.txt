# One Time Cash Incentive towards Learning Driving (Sainik Welfare)

**Short Title:** OTCILD-SW

**Scheme Brief Description:** "One Time Cash Incentive towards Learning Driving" is a Welfare Scheme by the Dept of Sainik Welfare, Puducherry. Through this scheme, up to ₹4,000/- is provided to the unemployed Ex-servicemen to learn to drive. The driving course should be taken from a Government-recognized School/Institution.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Transport & Infrastructure; Sector: Public transport and private vehicles

**Where it applies:** Puducherry; State/ UT

**Detailed Description:** &quot;One Time Cash Incentive towards Learning Driving&quot; is a Welfare Scheme by the Armed Forces Flag Day Fund of the Department of Sainik Welfare, Puducherry. Through this scheme, up to ₹4,000/- is provided to the unemployed Ex-servicemen (ESM) to learn to drive. The driving course should be taken from a Government-recognized School/Institution, with prior permission from the Department of Sainik Welfare, Puducherry. The applications are accepted offline.

**Benefits / Assistance:** - A one-time cash incentive of ₹4,000/- is provided to take a course on driving. - The amount is credited to the beneficiary’s Bank Account through RTGS/ NEFT.

**Exclusions / Not eligible:** The scope of this scheme will not apply to those ESM / living spouse/widows who are re-employed as regular / deemed to be regular in Government/Public Sector Undertakings/Private Organizations and self-employed IT assesses.
