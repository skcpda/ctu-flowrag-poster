# Operation of Ashram Hostels

**Short Title:** OAHR

**Scheme Brief Description:** Under this scheme, free accommodation, nutritious meals, educational materials, uniforms, personal hygiene items, and other facilities are provided to Scheduled Tribe (ST) students residing in ashram hostels operated by the Tribal Area Development Department.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Education & Learning; Sector: Hostel service

**Where it applies:** Rajasthan; State/ UT

**Detailed Description:** The scheme &amp;quot;Operation of Ashram Hostels&amp;quot; was launched by the Department of Tribal Area Development, Government of Rajasthan. It aims to ensure continued education for tribal students who lack access to nearby higher-level schools due to the poor economic condition of their families. Under this scheme, free accommodation, nutritious meals, educational materials, uniforms, personal hygiene items, and other facilities are provided to Scheduled Tribe (ST) students residing in ashram hostels operated by the Tribal Area Development Department.

**Benefits / Assistance:** > **Students are provided with the following annually and monthly benefits:** **Annually (per student):** - School uniform with stitching - Shoes, socks, towel, and warm sweater **Monthly (per student):** - Nutritious breakfast and meals - Hair oil - Bathing and washing soap - Hair cutting service - Laundry for bed linen (sheet, pillow cover, blanket) - Electricity and water facilities - Newspapers and magazines

**Application Process:** **Step 1:** The applicant should gather copies of the necessary documents needed to apply for the concerned scheme. **Step 2:** The applicant should visit the nearest E-Mitra Center and complete the Biometric Verification Process. **Step 3:** The applicant should ensure that the E-Mitra Center agent completes all mandatory fields in the application form and uploads copies of all required documents. **Step 4:** Keep the receipt or acknowledgment form for future reference. &gt; **Note:** - All new admissions will be made on the basis of merit. - Admission to hostels in specific tribal areas will be given only to tribal students belonging to the same area. - Re-admission in the next academic year will be granted only if the student passes the annual examination.
Mode: Online - via CSC

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Tribal Area Development Department
