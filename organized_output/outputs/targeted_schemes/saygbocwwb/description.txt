# Shramik Annapurna Yojana (GBOCWWB)

**Short Title:** SAY(GBOCWWB)

**Scheme Brief Description:** Under the scheme, registered construction workers are entitled to receive nutritious meals at concessional rates at the workplace or on Kadianaka. This benefit extends to the registered worker's family members as well.

**Who is this for:** Family, Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Food Security / Public Distribution System

**Where it applies:** Gujarat; State/ UT

**Detailed Description:** The “Shramik Annapurna Yojana” is implemented by the Gujarat Building and Other Construction Worker’s Welfare Board (GBOCWWB), Labour, Skill Development &amp;amp; Employment Department, Government of Gujarat. Under the scheme, the registered construction worker is providing nutritious meals at concessional rates at the workplace or on Kadianaka. Registered construction workers and their family members can get the benefit.

**Benefits / Assistance:** - Under this scheme, healthy and nutritious meals will be provided to the construction workers at a concessional rate of ₹5/- per meal.
