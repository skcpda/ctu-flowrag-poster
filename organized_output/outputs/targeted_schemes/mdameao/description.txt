# Market Development Assistance to MSMEs: Assistance to Organizers for Organizing Industrial Exhibitions in Gujarat

**Short Title:** MDAMEAO

**Scheme Brief Description:** The scheme "Assistance to Organizers for Organizing Industrial Exhibitions in Gujarat" under the "Market Development Assistance to MSMEs" aims to provide financial assistance to organizers of industrial exhibitions in Gujarat.

**Launch Date:** 2020-08-07

**Who is this for:** Business Entity, Industries; Category: Business & Entrepreneurship; Sector: Exhibitions/Trade fairs/Buyer-seller meet, market development

**Where it applies:** Gujarat; State/ UT

**Detailed Description:** The scheme &amp;amp;amp;quot;Assistance to Organizers for Organizing Industrial Exhibitions in Gujarat&amp;amp;amp;quot; under the &amp;amp;amp;quot;Market Development Assistance to MSMEs&amp;amp;amp;quot; introduced by the Industries and Mines Department, Gujarat, aims to offer reimbursement for stall rent, product literature, and display materials to MSMEs participating in exhibitions at state, national, and international levels.

**Benefits / Assistance:** - **Electricity Cost Reimbursement:** 60% of the electricity bill incurred during the exhibition and pre-preparation period will be reimbursed.

**Application Process:** > **Registration Process:** **Step-1:** Visit the [Investor Facilitation Portal (IFP)](https://ifp.gujarat.gov.in/DIGIGOV/) portal. Click on &quot;New Investor Registration&quot; to access the registration form. **Step-2:** Fill in the Registration Form. Click on &quot;Submit&quot;. **Step-3:** Verify the E-mail ID using the link sent on your registered E-mail ID. **Step-4:** After verification, you will be registered successfully.  > **Post-Registration Process:** **Step-1:** Visit the [Investor Facilitation Portal (IFP)](https://ifp.gujarat.gov.in/DIGIGOV/) portal. Click on &quot;Login&quot;. **Step-2:** Enter the email address and password you used during registration. **Step-3:** Click the &quot;Login&quot; button to access your account. **Step-4:** Now fill in the required details and mandatory documents about your business to complete the application process.  > **Helpdesk:** [**Contact Here.**](https://ifp.gujarat.gov.in/DIGIGOV/IFP-pages/contact.jsp?pagedisp=static)
Mode: Online
