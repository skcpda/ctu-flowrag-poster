# Workers for Class 9th to 12th Through National Scholarship (JKBOCWWB)

**Short Title:** WFCTNSJKBOCWWB

**Scheme Brief Description:** Launched on 1st January 2018, the "Workers for Class 9th to 12th" scheme, under the MUHAFIZ Scheme by the Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board, Labour Dept., J&K, provides ₹1,200 per year for up to two children of registered BOC workers through the National Scholarship Portal.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Education & Learning; Sector: Scholarships and student finance

**Where it applies:** Jammu and Kashmir; State/ UT

**Detailed Description:** Launched on 1st January 2018, The &quot;Workers for Class 9th to 12th&quot; scheme by the Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board, The scheme provides annual financial assistance of ₹1,200 per child (up to two children) for the education of registered BOC workers&#39; children through the national scholarship portal.

**Benefits / Assistance:** An annual scholarship of ₹1,200 per child (up to two children)

**Application Process:** **Registration of a Construction Worker under JKBOCWWB:** Step 1: Go to the J&amp;K Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board (JKBOCWWB) [official website.](https://www.jkbocw.gov.in/Index.aspx) Step 2: On the homepage, find and click the &quot;[Labour Registration&quot;](https://portal.jkbocw.gov.in/MemberRegistration.aspx) link. Step 3:If you&#39;re a new user, click on &quot;Apply for New Registration.&quot; Step 4: Complete the registration form with your details, submit the form, and send the OTP to your registered mobile number. Enter the OTP and click &quot;Verify OTP &amp; Submit.&quot; Step 5: After the OTP is verified, a confirmation message with your Login ID and password will be sent to your registered mobile number. Step 6:Use the Login ID, password, and captcha to log in (remember, the username and password are case-sensitive). filled are case sensitive)Read all the instruction carefully and Click to continue Step 7: Once logged in, choose the &quot;Construction Worker’s Registration&quot; option and click on &quot;Registration Form.&quot; Step 8: A dashboard will appear. Complete the form by entering all required details (marked with *). Step 9: Upload all the important documents, such as ID proof and other relevant certificates. Step 10: Once the form is completed and documents uploaded, click &quot;Submit&quot; and pay the application fee online. You will receive a registration number after successful payment and submission.  **Registration** **Step1:** Go to [national scholarship portal](https://scholarships.gov.in/) and click on &quot;Student&quot; and then &quot;[One Time Registration (OTR)&quot;.](https://scholarships.gov.in/otrapplication/#/registration-page) **Step2:** Read the guidelines, check the box to provide the undertaking, and click &quot;Continue&quot;. **Step3:** Enter your mobile number, receive an OTP, and verify it by entering the OTP **Step4:** Provide necessary documents like Aadhaar and Bank Account details to complete the registration. **Step5:** Ensure all the details you entered are correct, then click &quot;Verified&quot;. **Step6**: After verification, you will receive your registration number on the registered mobile number.  **Steps to Apply for Scheme Benefits:** **Step1:** Visit the [national scholarship portal .](https://scholarships.gov.in/) **Step2:** Click on the &quot;[Student](https://scholarships.gov.in/Students)&quot; option and select[ &quot;Apply For Scholarship.&quot;](https://scholarships.gov.in/ApplicationForm/#/login) **Step3:** Enter your OTR ([One Time Registration](https://scholarships.gov.in/otrapplication/#/registration-page)) number, password, CAPTCHA, and click &quot;Login.&quot; **Step 4:** In the application form, fill in all the mandatory fields, paste the passport-sized photograph (signed across, if required), and attach copies of all the mandatory documents (self-attest, if required). **Step 5:** Once the form is completed and documents uploaded, click &quot;Submit&quot; and pay the application fee online. You will receive a registration number after successful payment and submission. **Step 6:** Print your application form for future reference.
