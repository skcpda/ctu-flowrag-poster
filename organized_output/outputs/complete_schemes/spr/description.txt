# Super 5000

**Short Title:** SPR

**Launch Date:** 2017-08-16

**Tags:** Financial Assistance, Meritorious, Education, Student

The Super 5000 scheme is a part of the ongoing Jan Kalyan Sambal Yojana in Madhya Pradesh. Under this, an education incentive will be given to the first 5000 students of unorganized sector workers who get the maximum marks in the 10th and 12th board examinations. Meritorious students will be able to use this amount to continue their further studies.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Education & Learning; Sector: Scholarships and student finance

**Where it applies:** Madhya Pradesh; State

**DBT Scheme:** Yes (Direct Benefit Transfer)

**Benefits / Assistance:** Scholarship Amount: Rs. 25,000/- only at once.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Labour Department.
