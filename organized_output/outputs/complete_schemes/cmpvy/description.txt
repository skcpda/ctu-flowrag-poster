# Chief Minister’s Paryatan Vikas Yojana

**Short Title:** CMPVY

**Tags:** Tourism, Development, Tourist, Adventure, Homestays

The scheme “Chief Minister’s Paryatan Vikas Yojna (CMPVY)” was launched by the Government of Arunachal Pradesh to develop tourism facilities in the state to attract more tourism. The scheme has three components – a) Development support to Patisserie and Food courts, b) Development support for Homestays, c) Development of adventure sports by financial support in the procurement of equipment. **Objective:** - To promote tourism in the State and thereby livelihood for the locals. **Important Features:** - Patisserie and food courts in popular tourist spots and important towns/ tourist places of Arunachal Pradesh - Value addition to Home Stay operators, which include renovation of 2 adequately sized well-furnished rooms with attached toilet with shower, geyser, and western toilet - Purchase of various types of adventure tourism equipment such as rafts, kayaks, Quad bikes, lobs, lip lines, Bungee jumps, etc.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Travel & Tourism; Sector: Tourism in India

**Where it applies:** Arunachal Pradesh; State

**Benefits / Assistance:** 1. Direct loan subsidy of ₹5.00 lakh is provided to open Patisserie. 1. Homestays and procure equipment for adventure tourism.

**Benefit Type:** Composite

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: Department Of Tourism, Arunachal Pradesh; Nodal Department: Tourism Department.
