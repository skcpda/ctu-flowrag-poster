# Uttarakhand Shaheed Kosh Anudan

**Short Title:** UKSKA

**Tags:** Shaheed Kosh, Martyrs, Dependents, Sainik, Soldiers

The scheme &amp;quot;Uttarakhand Shaheed Kosh Anudan&amp;quot; is implemented by the State Sainik Board under the Sainik Welfare Department, Government of Uttarakhand. It aims to provide financial assistance to the widows/dependents of soldiers and paramilitary personnel who were martyred in wars, border clashes, or internal security operations. Under this scheme, a one-time lump sum grant of ₹10 lakh is provided, subject to eligibility criteria based on the martyr’s family status. 

**Who is this for:** Family; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Financial assistance

**Where it applies:** Uttarakhand; State/ UT

**DBT Scheme:** Yes (Direct Benefit Transfer)

**Benefits / Assistance:** - **One-Time Grant:** ₹10,00,000/- per martyr 

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: State Sainik Board.
