# Talab Matsyiki Vishesh Sahayata Yojana (Anusuchit Jaati/Janajati/Ati Pichhada)

**Short Title:** TMVSY-AJJAP

**Launch Date:** 2024-07-08

**Tags:** Scheduled Caste, Tribe, Most Backward, Farmers

The &quot;Talab Matsyiki Vishesh Sahayata Yojana (Anusuchit Jaati/Janajati/Ati Pichhada)&quot; was launched by the Animal and Fisheries Resources Department, Government of Bihar. This scheme aims to support fish farmers from Scheduled Castes, Tribes, and Extremely Backward Classes by providing financial and technical assistance for pond construction, installation of necessary equipment, and other related activities. The scheme has a total budget of ₹2994.60 lakh and seeks to enhance livelihood opportunities, ensure food and protein security, and promote employment among these communities.

**Who is this for:** Individual, Family; Category: Agriculture,Rural & Environment; Sector: Fishing and hunting

**Where it applies:** Bihar; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** > **Assistance** - Offers a 70% subsidy on the total cost, reducing the financial burden on beneficiaries.  > **Subsidy Payment:** - Subsidy payments are made in two installments: 60% after pond construction and 40% after installation of related units and purchase of inputs.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Animal & Fisheries Resources Department.
