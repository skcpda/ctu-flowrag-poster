# State Agriculture Thesis Grant for M.Sc. Agriculture

**Short Title:** SATGMSA

**Tags:** Agriculture, Thesis Grant, MSc, Student

The scheme “State Agriculture Thesis Grant for M.Sc. Agriculture” was launched by the Department of Agriculture, Government of Nagaland to provide a Thesis Grant to all the inhabitants of Nagaland who passed the M.Sc. Agriculture programme under the State Plan of Education &amp;amp; Training.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Education & Learning; Sector: Education and training grants, fellowship, stipend

**Where it applies:** Nagaland; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** 1. Under the scheme, the Thesis Grant will be given to students according to the course/programme in which they are enrolled.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: Department Of Agriculture, Government Of Nagaland; Nodal Department: Agriculture Department.
