# Old Age Pension Scheme - Uttarakhand

**Short Title:** OAPS

**Tags:** Old Age, Pension, Senior Citizen, BPL

The &amp;amp;quot;Old Age Pension Scheme&amp;amp;quot; was launched by the Department of Social Welfare, Government of Uttarakhand. The scheme aims to help elderly persons meet their basic needs and improve their quality of life. Under this scheme, a monthly pension is provided to senior citizens (both husband and wife) in the state who are 60 years of age or above. 

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Pension

**Where it applies:** Uttarakhand; State

**Benefits / Assistance:** A pension of ₹1,500/- per month is provided to eligible beneficiaries. 

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Department Of Social Welfare.

**References / Annexures:** - **Guidelines (Page No. 16)**: https://uk.gov.in/department92/library_file/file-04-12-2023-06-02-23.pdf - **Online Application Portal**: https://ssp.uk.gov.in/OnlineRegistration/FrmOnlineRegisApplicationOldage.aspx - **Website**: https://socialwelfare.uk.gov.in/service/old-age-pension/

**Additional Information:**
Briefdescription: Under this scheme, a monthly pension of ₹1,500/- is provided to senior citizens (both husband and wife) in the state who are 60 years of age or above and whose family’s monthly income is up to ₹4,000/- or who belong to the BPL category.
