# Scheme for Assistance to Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises (MSME): Assistance for Raising Capital through SME Exchange 

**Short Title:** SAMSMESME

**Launch Date:** 2020-08-07

**Closing Date:** 2025-08-06

**Tags:** Business, Entrepreneurship, MSMEs, Start Up

The scheme &amp;quot;Scheme for Assistance to Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises (MSME)&amp;quot; is an umbrella scheme launched by the Industries and Mines Department, Gujarat. It aims to empower the state&amp;#39;s dynamic MSME sector, which significantly contributes to employment, GDP, and exports. Effective from August 7th, 2020 to August 6th, 2025, this initiative supports entrepreneurs and strengthens MSMEs, making them globally competitive. The component &amp;quot;Assistance for Raising Capital through SME Exchange&amp;quot; offers a reimbursement of the expenditure incurred in raising funds through the SME Exchange.

**Who is this for:** Business Entity, Industries; Category: Business & Entrepreneurship; Sector: Setting up / start-up / entrepreneurship

**Where it applies:** Gujarat; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - **Financial Assistance:** MSMEs can avail of 25% reimbursement of expenses incurred in raising funds through the SME Exchange, up to ₹5,00,000/-. - **Equity Capital Support:** Helps MSMEs raise equity capital for business expansion and growth. - **Encouragement for Listing:** Incentivizes MSMEs to get listed on the SME Exchange, improving visibility and credibility.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Industries and Mines Department.

**References / Annexures:** - **Guidelines**: https://ic.gujarat.gov.in/documents/commondoc/2020/3-GR_02092020.pdf - **Online Application Portal**: https://ifp.gujarat.gov.in/IC/digigov.htm?actionFlag=loadCitizenRegPage
