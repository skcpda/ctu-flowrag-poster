# Integrated Technology Enabled Agriculture Management System Or iTEAMS

**Short Title:** ITEAMS

**Tags:** ITEAMS, Integrated Technology, Agriculture Management System, Farmer, Telephone Advice

The scheme “Integrated Technology Enabled Agriculture Management System Or iTEAMS” was launched by the Department of Agriculture &amp;amp; Farmers’ Welfare, Government of Meghalaya to address the critical issues of enabling and facilitating access to remunerative and sustainable markets for farmers&amp;#39; produce in the most transparent and effective manner through facilitation service iTEAM Dial 1917, whereby farmers can be assisted on the following activities - 12/6 Physical Connectivity to Markets. - Access to input suppliers and bidders. - Tailor-made advisory solutions on crops, Package of practices, Animal Health Care, etc. - Assured logistics and Transport solutions at the rate of 0.20/kg/km. - Livelihood options through leasing Agri Response Vehicle.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Agriculture,Rural & Environment

**Where it applies:** Meghalaya; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** > **Pattern of Assistance:** - Free Telephonic advisory, Access to Subject Matter Specialist (SMS), Transport services. > **Type of Assistance and Entitlement (If any):** - Telephone advice, free mobile SMS to registered farmers, transport service to evacuate farmers&amp;#39; produce to markets at the rate of INR 2.00 per KM per Quintal.

**Benefit Type:** In Kind

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Agriculture Department.
