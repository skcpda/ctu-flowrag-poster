# Vishesh Samman Pension Yojana for the State Agitators who became Disabled and Completely Bedridden during the Uttarakhand State Movement

**Short Title:** VSPYSADBUSM

**Tags:** Pension, Disability, Disabled Person, Bedridden, Uttarakhand State Movement

The &amp;quot;Vishesh Samman Pension Yojana for the State Agitators who became Disabled and Completely Bedridden during the Uttarakhand State Movement&amp;quot; scheme is implemented by the Police Department under the Department of Home, Government of Uttarakhand. The scheme aims to honor state agitators who became permanently disabled and bedridden during the Uttarakhand Statehood Movement by providing them with monthly financial assistance. 

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Crisis/Disaster/Accident

**Where it applies:** Uttarakhand; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** **Pension Amount:** ₹20,000/- per month 

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: Police Department, Government Of Uttarakhand; Nodal Department: Department of Home.

**References / Annexures:** - **Guidelines (Page No. 65)**: https://uk.gov.in/department92/library_file/file-04-12-2023-06-02-23.pdf

Briefdescription: The scheme aims to honor state agitators who became permanently disabled and bedridden during the Uttarakhand Statehood Movement by providing them with monthly financial assistance of ₹20,000/-.
