# Educational Loan Scheme (GMFDC)

**Short Title:** ELSGMFDC

**Tags:** Higher Education, Loan, Abroad Study, Minority

The &amp;quot;Educational Loan Scheme&amp;quot; is implemented by the Gujarat Minorities Finance and Development Corporation (GMFDC) under the Social Justice and Empowerment Department, Government of Gujarat. The scheme aims to provide financial assistance in the form of loans for all types of education, including various technical and non-technical courses, that help beneficiaries gain employment or enhance their job prospects. Under this scheme, low-interest loans are offered to eligible students from minority communities to pursue graduate and postgraduate courses in technical, vocational, professional, and medical fields, either in India or abroad. 

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Education & Learning

**Where it applies:** Gujarat; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** 1. For studies within India, a maximum loan of ₹1,50,000/- per year and up to ₹7,50,000/- for a five-year course can be obtained. 1. For medical courses within India, ₹2,50,000/- for 1 year and ₹12,50,000/- for 5 years can be obtained. 1. For abroad studies, a maximum loan of ₹3,00,000/- for 1 year and ₹15,00,000/- for 5 years is provided. 1. The annual interest rate on this loan is 3%. **Note 1:** If the applicant’s annual family income is up to ₹98,000 in rural areas or ₹1,20,000 in urban areas, the loan is provided at a concessional interest rate of 3% per annum. **Note 2:** According to the National Corporation&#39;s Credit Line-2, for families with an income of ₹1,20,000/- up to ₹8,00,000, the interest rate is 5% for female students and 8% for male students. **Note 3:** The loan and interest amount is to be repaid in 60 equal monthly installments within five years from the date of completion of studies or from the date of getting a job, whichever is earlier. **Note 4:** Penalty interest at the rate of 2% is payable on delayed payments. 

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: Gujarat Minorities Finance & Development Corporation Limited; Nodal Department: Social Justice and Empowerment Department.
