# Coaching Sahay for the Preparation of NEET, JEE, GUJCET Examination to both Socially and Educationally Backward Class Trainees/Students and Female Students and Economically Backward Class Students

**Short Title:** CSPNJGESEBCEBC

**Tags:** Financial Assistance, Coaching, Student, Competitive Exam, NEET, JEE, GUJCET

The “Coaching Sahay for the Preparation of NEET, JEE, GUJCET Examination to both Socially and Educationally Backward Class Trainees/Students and Female Students and Economically Backward Class Students” scheme was launched by the Social Justice and Empowerment Department, Government of Gujarat. It aims to provide financial aid to underprivileged students for coaching support. Under this scheme, students from SEBC or Economically Backward Class categories receive coaching fee (whichever is lower) for one exam-NEET, JEE, or GUJCET. 

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Education & Learning; Sector: Coaching

**Where it applies:** Gujarat; State/ UT

**DBT Scheme:** Yes (Direct Benefit Transfer)

**Benefits / Assistance:** **Financial Assistance:** - ₹20,000/- or actual coaching fee paid, whichever is lower. **Applicable Exams:** - NEET (National Eligibility cum Entrance Test) - JEE (Joint Entrance Examination) - GUJCET (Gujarat Common Entrance Test) **Mode of Disbursement:** - Amount is credited via Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) into the student’s bank account. 

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Social Justice and Empowerment Department.

**References / Annexures:** - **Guidelines**: https://mariyojana.gujarat.gov.in/MoreDetails.aspx

**Additional Information:**
Briefdescription: The scheme aims to support Socially and Educationally Backward Class (SEBC) and Economically Backward Class (EBC) students with coaching fee assistance. Up to ₹20,000 or actual fee is provided for one exam preparation.
