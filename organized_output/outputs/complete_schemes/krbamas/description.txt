# Kumar Rajratna Bhimrao Ambedkar Medical Assistance Scheme

**Short Title:** KRBAMAS

**Tags:** Medical Assistance, Disease, Scheduled Caste, Medical Aid, Treatment

The scheme &amp;quot;Kumar Rajratna Bhimrao Ambedkar Medical Assistance Scheme&amp;quot; was launched by the Social Justice and Empowerment Department, Government of Gujarat. The scheme aims to provide financial support to individuals belonging to Scheduled Caste category suffering from incurable and serious diseases. Under this scheme, financial assistance is provided to Scheduled Caste patients diagnosed with life-threatening conditions such as Tuberculosis (TB), Leukemia, and other critical illnesses. 

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Health & Wellness; Sector: Financial assistance

**Where it applies:** Gujarat; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - Medical Assistance is provided to Scheduled Caste patients for treatment of incurable and critical diseases like TB, Leukemia, and other serious conditions. 

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Social Justice and Empowerment Department.
