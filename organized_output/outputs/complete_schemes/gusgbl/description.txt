# Goat Unit Scheme On Grants And Bank Loans

**Short Title:** GUSGBL

**Tags:** Animal Husbandry, Agriculture, Loan, Goat Breeding

The &amp;quot;Goat Unit Scheme&amp;quot;, launched by the Animal Husbandry Department, Government of Madhya Pradesh, aims to improve beneficiaries’ economic status by supporting goat rearing. Through this scheme, individuals with goat farming experience can apply for grants and loans to purchase (10+1) goat units for milk and meat production. The scheme covers a 40% subsidy for general applicants and 60% for SC/ST beneficiaries, based on a total unit cost of ₹77,456/-.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Agriculture,Rural & Environment; Sector: Financial assistance

**Where it applies:** Madhya Pradesh; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - **Financial Assistance:** 40% subsidy for General beneficiaries and 60% for SC/ST beneficiaries. - **Economic Gains:** Earn income from the sale of goat milk and meat products.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Animal Husbandry Department.

**References / Annexures:** - **Guidelines**: https://khargone.nic.in/en/scheme/goat-unit-scheme-on-grants-and-bank-loans/
