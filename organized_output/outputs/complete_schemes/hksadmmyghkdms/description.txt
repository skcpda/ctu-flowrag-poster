# Hita-dhikaari Ke Samanye Athava Durghatana Me Mrityu Ya Ghayal Hone Ki Dasha Me Sahayata Yojana

**Short Title:** HKSMD

**Tags:** Financial Assistance, Constrution Worker, Health & Wellness, Workers

The Beneficiary&#39;s Death or Injury Assistance Scheme, established by the Labor department of Rajasthan State, is a compassionate government program designed to offer a helping hand to families of beneficiaries who have experienced the loss of a loved one or the sudden hardship of an injury, regardless of whether the cause was natural or accidental. Recognizing the significant emotional and financial strain that such events can inflict, this initiative aims to provide a measure of financial security during these difficult times, allowing families to focus on healing and rebuilding their lives.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Health & Wellness; Sector: Financial assistance

**Where it applies:** Rajasthan; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** 1. In case of death due to accident : ₹5,00,000/- 1. In case of permanent total disability due to accident : ₹3,00,000/- - Permanent total disability means disabling of two eyes or both hands or both legs due to accident. 1. In case of partial permanent disability due to accident : ₹1,00,000/- - Permanent partial disability means disabling of one hand or one leg. 1. In case of serious injury in an accident : Up to ₹20,000/- - (Serious injury in an accident means the beneficiary being admitted as an in-patient in the hospital for at least 5 days. The determination of serious injury will be decided based on the certificate issued by the Medical Board .) 1. In case of minor injuries in an accident : Up to ₹5,000/- - Minor injuries means hospitalization as an inpatient in a hospital for less than 5 days 1. In case of normal death of a construction worker : ₹75,000/-

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Exclusions / Not eligible:** - Death due to suicide or consumption of drugs or substances or - In case of death or injury due to fighting with each other in violation of the law for committing a crime.

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Labour Department.
