# Scheme to Provide Assistance for Construction of Retail Outlets/ Agricultural Malls in State Market Committees

**Short Title:** SPACROAM

**Tags:** Agriculture, Retail Outlets, Agricultural Malls, Assistance For Construction

The scheme &amp;quot;Scheme to Provide Assistance for Construction of Retail Outlets/ Agricultural Malls in State Market Committees&amp;quot; introduced by the Agriculture, Farmers Welfare and Cooperation Department, Gujarat, aims to provide financial assistance to market committees in Gujarat’s &amp;quot;C&amp;quot; and &amp;quot;D&amp;quot; category areas to set up retail outlets/agricultural malls. These establishments help farmers sell their produce directly, eliminating middlemen and ensuring fair prices.

**Who is this for:** Business Entity; Category: Agriculture,Rural & Environment; Sector: Entrepreneurship development

**Where it applies:** Gujarat; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:**  **Name of the Component****Standard of Assistance**For Retail Outlet /Krishi MallAny one market committee of the &amp;amp;amp;quot;C&amp;amp;amp;quot; and &amp;amp;amp;quot;D&amp;amp;amp;quot; category of the state will be eligible for assistance to the extent of 90% of the total project cost and a maximum of ₹2,00,00,000/- 

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Agriculture, Farmers Welfare and Cooperation Department.
