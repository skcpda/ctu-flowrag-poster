# Grant for Purchase of Bicycle (BBOCWWB)

**Short Title:** GPBBBOCWWB

**Tags:** Bicycle, Construction Worker, Financial Assistance, Building Worker, Labour, Organized Workers

The &amp;quot;Grant for Purchase of Bicycle&amp;quot; scheme is implemented by the Bihar Building &amp;amp; Other Construction Workers Welfare Board (BBOCWWB), Labour Resources Department, Government of Bihar. Under this scheme, financial assistance of up to ₹3,500/- is provided to registered construction workers who have been members of the Board for at least one year, for purchasing a bicycle. The assistance will be provided to workers upon submission of the receipt for the bicycle purchase.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Financial assistance

**Where it applies:** Bihar; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** Under this scheme, financial assistance of up to ₹3,500/- is provided to registered construction workers for purchasing a bicycle.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: Bihar Building & Other Construction Workers Welfare Board; Nodal Department: Labour Resources Department.
