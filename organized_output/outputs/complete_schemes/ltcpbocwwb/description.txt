# Leave Travelling Concession Scheme (P.B.O.C.W.W.B)

**Short Title:** LTCPBOCWWB

**Tags:** Labour, Building Worker, Construction Worker, Leave Travelling Concession

The &amp;quot;Leave Travelling Concession Scheme&amp;#39;&amp;#39; by the BOCW Welfare Board, Department of Labour, Punjab, is a welfare scheme for the registered workers of the board. Under this scheme, the Board will provide a certain amount as leave travel concession to the registered construction workers for journeys to religious/ historical places in India or visiting their hometown. 

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Financial assistance

**Where it applies:** Punjab; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - **Financial Assistance:** An amount of ₹2000/- as Leave Travelling Concession (LTC). &gt; **Note:** - The benefit of L.T.C shall be granted after the return from the journey on submitting Self-declaration/ proof online through the Labour Inspector/Labour Enforcement Officer of the area or through the nearest Suvidha Kendra. - In the event of any incident during the journey, he will not be entitled to any compensation under this scheme. &lt;br&gt;

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Exclusions / Not eligible:** Beneficiaries who are getting the benefit of LTC under any similar scheme from any other Govt. Department, will not be entitled for this benefit.

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Department of Labour.
