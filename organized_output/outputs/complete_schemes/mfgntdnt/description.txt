# Micro Finance Scheme (GNDTDC)

**Short Title:** MFSGNDTDC

**Tags:** Small Scale Business, Self-employment, Loan, Nomadic Tribes, Denotified Tribes

 The scheme &amp;quot;Micro Finance Scheme&amp;quot; is implemented by the Gujarat Nomadic and Denotified Tribes Development Corporation (GNDTDC) under the Social Justice and Empowerment Department, Government of Gujarat. The scheme aims to promote self-employment among individuals from the Nomadic and Denotified Tribes communities by providing financial assistance to start a small-scale business/enterprise. Under this scheme, beneficiaries can avail loans at concessional interest rates to purchase equipment, raw materials, or working capital. 

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Banking,Financial Services and Insurance; Sector: Micro finance

**Where it applies:** Gujarat; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** **Loan Amount Details:** - **Loan Amount:** Up to ₹1,25,000/-.- **Interest Rate:** 5% per annum. - **Loan Contribution:** 90% will be from the National Corporation, 5% from the State Government, 5% from the beneficiary.- **Repayment:** The loan amount will have to be repaid in 48 equal monthly installments, including interest. 

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: Gujarat Nomadic And Denotified Tribes Development Corporation; Nodal Department: Social Justice and Empowerment Department.

**References / Annexures:** - **Guidelines**: https://sje.gujarat.gov.in/gntdnt/Micro-Finance - **Online Application**: https://gndcdconline.gujarat.gov.in/ - **User Manual**: https://gndcdconline.gujarat.gov.in/Home/index

**Additional Information:**
Briefdescription: The scheme promotes self-employment among Nomadic and Denotified Tribes by offering loan up to ₹1,25,000 at a concessional interest rate to help start small-scale businesses or enterprises.
