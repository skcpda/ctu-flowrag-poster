# Vasantrao Naik Tanda / Basti Development Scheme

**Short Title:** VNTBDS

**Tags:** Development, Water, Electrification, Latrine, Toilet, Amenities

The scheme &amp;amp;quot;Vasantrao Naik Tanda/Basti Development&amp;amp;quot; is implemented by the Department of Social Justice &amp;amp;amp; Special Assistance, Government of Maharashtra. The scheme provides essential amenities to the Tandas and Bastis of Vimukta Jatis and Nomadic Tribes. The scheme focuses on improving access to drinking water, electrification, latrines, drainage systems, and approach roads for these communities.

**Who is this for:** Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Financial assistance

**Where it applies:** Maharashtra; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** **Admissible Grant based on the Population of the VJNT Community -** - VJNT Population: 51 to 100; Admissible Grant: 4 Lakhs. - VJNT Population: 101 to 150; Admissible Grant: 6 Lakhs. - VJNT Population: Above 151; Admissible Grant: 10 Lakhs.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Social Justice and Special Assistance Department.

**References / Annexures:** - **Guidelines**: https://sjsa.maharashtra.gov.in/en/scheme-category/social-remedies
