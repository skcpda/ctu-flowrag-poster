# Shramashakthi Special Women's Scheme

**Short Title:** SSWS

**Tags:** Loan, Economic Empowerment, Women, Minority Community

The &quot;Shramashakthi Special Women&#39;s Scheme&quot; was launched by the Minorities Welfare Department, Government of Karnataka, and implemented by the Karnataka Minorities Development Corporation. This is a special scheme for the economic empowerment of widowed, divorced, unmarried women of the minority community. Under this scheme, ₹25,000/- loan and ₹25,000/- subsidy will be provided to undertake various economic activities.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Women and Child; Sector: Loan

**Where it applies:** Karnataka; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** > **Financial Assistance:** - A total of ₹50,000/- is provided, comprising ₹25,000/- as a loan and ₹25,000/- as a subsidy.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: Karnataka Minorities Development Corporation; Nodal Department: Minorities Welfare Department.
