# Assistance for Tools, Equipment, Shorting/Grading Equipment, PHM Equipment for Scheduled Castes (HRT-4)

**Short Title:** ATES/GEPHMSC(HRT4)

**Tags:** Tools, Equipment, Scheduled Caste, HRT-4, Farmer, Financial Assistance, Post-Harvest Management, Farmer Group, Cooperative Society, Horticulture

The scheme “Assistance for Tools, Equipment, Shorting/Grading Equipment, PHM Equipment for Scheduled Castes (HRT-4)” is implemented by the Directorate of Horticulture, Agriculture, Farmers Welfare and Co-operation Department, Government of Gujarat. Under the scheme, financial assistance is provided to farmers belonging to the Scheduled Castes category as well as to farmer groups and cooperative societies for purchasing various tools, equipment, sorting/grading equipment, and Post-Harvest Management (PHM) equipment. This includes plastic crates with equipment such as weights, packing materials, sorting/grading machinery, etc.

**Who is this for:** Registered Societies, Individual, Self Help Groups (SHGS); Category: Agriculture,Rural & Environment; Sector: Mechanization- solar power, farming systems

**Where it applies:** Gujarat; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** 1. The Scheduled Castes farmers will be eligible for assistance up to 75% of the cost of the Tools/Equipment or ₹3,750/-, whichever is less. 1. Farmer groups/cooperative societies will be eligible for assistance of 90% of the cost of equipment or up to ₹7,50,000/-, whichever is less, for the installation of Post-Harvest Management (PHM) Equipment.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: Directorate Of Horticulture, Government Of Gujarat; Nodal Department: Agriculture, Farmers Welfare and Cooperation Department.
