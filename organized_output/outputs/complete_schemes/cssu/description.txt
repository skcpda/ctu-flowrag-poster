# Chanakya Scheme for Swayam Upadhi

**Short Title:** CSSU

**Tags:** Financial Assistance, Micro Enterprises, Opportunity, Brahmin Community

Andhra Pradesh Brahmin Welfare Corporation (ABC) Limited, a Government of Andhra Pradesh undertaking is introducing the “Chanakya Scheme for Swayam Upadhi” (CS-SU) to encourage Brahmin entrepreneurs in Andhra Pradesh. Under this scheme, one-time financial assistance upto ₹2,00,000/- (not exceeding 50% of the project cost) for projects costing between ₹3,00,000/- and ₹10,00,000/- will be considered.  **Objective:** To encourage Brahmins to set up Micro Enterprises in Andhra Pradesh in various sectors like Catering, Education, Electronics, Healthcare (Clinic, Diagnostics, Pharmacy, Physiotherapy), Engineering, IT &amp; ITES (Coaching, Development, DTP, Repairs), Photography /Videography, Pooja Materials, Retail, Ritual Tools and equipment, Solar, Telecom, Textiles, etc.

**Who is this for:** Business Entity; Category: Business & Entrepreneurship; Sector: Micro finance

**Where it applies:** Andhra Pradesh; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - One-time financial assistance upto ₹2,00,000/-. **NOTE:** Not exceeding 50% of the project cost.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Andhra Pradesh Brahmin Welfare Corporation.

**References / Annexures:** - **Guidelines**: https://www.andhrabrahmin.ap.gov.in/chanakya/cs-sus1.pdf

**Additional Information:**
Briefdescription: The "Chanakya Scheme for Swayam Upadhi” to encourage Brahmin entrepreneurs in Andhra Pradesh. The grant will be given against relevant approved asset acquisition for setting up new business entities or expanding existing/diversifying business entities in different areas.
