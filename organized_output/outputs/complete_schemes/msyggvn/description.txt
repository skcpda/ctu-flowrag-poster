# Mahila Samrudhhi Yojana (Gujarat Gopalak Vikas Nigam)

**Short Title:** MSYGGVN

**Tags:** Self Employment, Loan, Entrepreneurs, Self Reliant

The scheme &quot;Mahila Samrudhhi Yojana (Gujarat Gopalak Vikas Nigam)&quot; by the Social Justice and Empowerment Department, Government of Gujarat, aims to provide Microfinance for individual women entrepreneurs to create self-employment for women and make them self-reliant. Under this scheme, loans are provided on an individual basis to women beneficiaries to start a business of their choice. The scheme is implemented by the Gujarat Gopalak Vikas Nigam. 

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Women and Child; Sector: Loan

**Where it applies:** Gujarat; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** 1. The scheme provides 100% of the loan amount to eligible women beneficiaries, up to a maximum limit of ₹1,25,000/-. 1. The loan carries an interest rate of 4% per annum. 

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: Gujarat Gopalak Vikas Nigam; Nodal Department: Social Justice and Empowerment Department.

**References / Annexures:** - **Official Guidelines**: https://sje.gujarat.gov.in/ggvn/Schemes/1563?lang=English - **Online Application**: https://ggdconline.gujarat.gov.in/
