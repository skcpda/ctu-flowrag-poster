# Funeral and Ex-Gratia (K.B.O.C.W.W.B)

**Short Title:** FAEXGKBOCWWB

**Tags:** Labour, Building Worker, Construction Worker, Funeral Assistance, Death, Ex-Gratia

The &amp;quot;Funeral and Ex-Gratia&amp;#39;&amp;#39; by the BOCW Welfare Board, Department of Labour, Karnataka, is a welfare scheme for the registered workers of the board. Under this Scheme, the Board provides financial assistance to the nominee on the event of the death of a registered construction worker. The scheme also provides an ex-gratia payment to help mitigate the financial burden caused by the sudden demise of the worker. &lt;br&gt;

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Financial assistance

**Where it applies:** Karnataka; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - **Funeral Assistance:** ₹4,000/- to cover funeral expenses. - **Ex-Gratia Payment:** ₹71,000/- to provide financial support to the nominee of the deceased.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Labour Department.

**References / Annexures:** - **Guidelines**: https://kbocwwb.karnataka.gov.in/funeral - **Guidelines (For Registration)**: https://karbwwb.karnataka.gov.in/42/schemes/en - **User Manual**: https://kbocwwb.karnataka.gov.in/static/media/user_manual_eng.c0bc6338.pdf
