# Dr. Ambedkar Medhavi Chattar Sansodhit Yojna

**Short Title:** AMCSY

**Launch Date:** 2005-01-01

**Tags:** Social Welfare, Financial Assistance,, The Scheme Of Masses, Parity Among Students, Education

The Haryana government has prepared a scheme to provide scholarship to the meritorious students of Scheduled Castes, Backward Classes, Nomadic, DNTs, Semi-Nomadic, Vimukt Jaati and Tapriwas Jaati of classes 11th to postgraduate level. It aims to produce a feeling of competition amongst them so that they can achieve great milestones in the field of education. Both online and offline applications are invited under this scheme. Under this scheme an incentive from 8000 to 12000 per annum is provided to the meritorious students of classes from class 11th to post graduate level. Student may submit their online application on website i.e https://saralharyana.gov.in/. 

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Financial assistance

**Where it applies:** Haryana; State

**Benefits / Assistance:** • For Students of Backward Classes, Scheduled Castes, Nomadic, DNTs, Semi-Nomadic, Vimukt Jati, and Tapriwas Jaati, scholarship ranging from Rs. 8000/- to 12000/- is awarded for Metric/10th,10+ and Graduation. • For Students of Backward Classes Block ‘A’, scholarship of amount Rs 8000 is awarded for metric qualified students. • For Students of Backward Classes Block ‘B’, scholarship of amount Rs 8000 is awarded for metric qualified students. • For Meritorious students of other social classes to be given scholarship at par with Students of Backward Classes Block ‘B’, scholarship of amount Rs 8000 is awarded for metric qualified students.

**Benefit Type:** In Kind

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Government of Haryana.

**References / Annexures:** - **Portal 1**: http://haryanascbc.gov.in/ - **Portal 2**: https://saralharyana.gov.in/

**Additional Information:**
Briefdescription: This scheme is being implemented to encourage the merit amongst the students of Scheduled Castes, Backward Classes, Nomadic, DNTs, Semi-Nomadic, Vimukt Jaati and Tapriwas Jaati and to produce a feeling of competition amongst them and motivate them to earn more and more marks from the middle to post.
