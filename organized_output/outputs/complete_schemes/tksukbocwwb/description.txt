# Toolkit Assistance (UKBOCWWB)

**Short Title:** TKA(UKBOCWWB)

**Tags:** Tool Kit, Construction Worker, Building Worker, Labour, Unorganized Worker

The scheme “Toolkit Assistance” is implemented by the Uttarakhand Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board (UKBOCWWB) under the Labour Department, Government of Uttarakhand. The scheme aims to provide registered construction workers with a toolkit, including hand gloves and other construction-related tools, worth up to ₹10,000/-. 

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Skills & Employment

**Where it applies:** Uttarakhand; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** Under this scheme, registered construction workers are provided with a toolkit worth up to ₹10,000/-. 

**Benefit Type:** In Kind

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: Uttarakhand Building And Other Construction Workers’ Welfare Board; Nodal Department: Department Of Labour.
