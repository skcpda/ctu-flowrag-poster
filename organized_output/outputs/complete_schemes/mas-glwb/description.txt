# Marriage Assistance Scheme- Gujarat Labour Welfare Board

**Short Title:** MAS-GLWB

**Tags:** Marriage, Industrial Worker, Labour, Organized Worker, Financial Assistance

The “Marriage Assistance Scheme” was launched by the Gujarat Labour Welfare Board, Labour, Skill Development &amp;amp; Employment Department, Government of Gujarat. Under the scheme, financial assistance is provided to female workers who are working in a factory/organization in the state of Gujarat after their marriage.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Marriage

**Where it applies:** Gujarat; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** 1. Under the scheme, female workers will be entitled to receive ₹11,000/- after their marriage.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: Gujarat Labour Welfare Board; Nodal Department: Labour and Employment Department.
