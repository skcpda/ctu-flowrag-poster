# Distribution of Gypsum

**Short Title:** DOG

The “Distribution of Gypsum” scheme by the Agriculture-Farmers Welfare Department, Tamil Nadu, provides financial assistance to farmers by subsidizing 50% of the cost of gypsum material and transportation cost. It is targeted at farmers who grow pulse crops, helping them improve soil fertility and increase yield. The scheme includes special provisions for SC/ST farmers (24%) and farm women (20%).

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Agriculture,Rural & Environment; Sector: Agricultural Inputs- seeds, fertilizer etc.

**Where it applies:** Tamil Nadu; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** > **Financial Assistance:** - 50% on the cost of Material. - Transport cost limited to ₹750/- per hectare. 

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Agriculture Department.

**References / Annexures:** - **Guidelines**: https://www.tn.gov.in/scheme/data_view/7075
