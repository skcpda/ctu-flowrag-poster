# Agriculture Academic Studies Scheme

**Short Title:** AASS

**Tags:** Agriculture Universities, Academic, Student, Under Graduate, Horticulture, Sponsorship

The “Agriculture Academic Studies Scheme” is a State Plan Scheme, which is implemented by the Department of Agriculture &amp;amp; Farmers’ Welfare, Government of Meghalaya. The objective of the Scheme is to sponsor students for undergoing 4 years of Graduate/degree courses in Agriculture/Horticulture against State quota seats in different Agriculture Universities in the country.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Education & Learning; Sector: Scholarships and student finance

**Where it applies:** Meghalaya; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** > **Pattern of Assistance:** - Sponsorship of seats, book grants, and scholarships. > **Type of Assistance and Entitlement (If any):** - Monthly stipend @ ₹1,200/-. - Book grant @₹1,200/- per academic year.

**Benefit Type:** Composite

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: Department Of Agriculture & Farmers’ Welfare, Government Of Meghalaya; Nodal Department: Agriculture Department.
