# Children With Special Needs Under Goa Sarva Shiksha Abhivan

**Short Title:** CWSN

**Tags:** Goa, Disability, Medical, Children, Education

The scheme &quot;Children With Special Needs (CWSN)&quot; by the Directorate of Education, Government of Goa aims to provide comprehensive educational support and resources tailored to the needs of differently-abled children. It is designed to ensure that every child, regardless of their disability type (intellectual, physical, etc.), receives the necessary assistance to facilitate their education and overall development. This includes provisions for medical assessments, the establishment of resource rooms, home-based education, the provision of teaching-learning materials (TLM), and specialized training for educators and volunteers.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Education & Learning; Sector: Education and training grants, fellowship, stipend

**Where it applies:** Goa; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** Grant is released for activities for CWSN, medical assessments, resource rooms, home-based education, TLM, training of educators, volunteer educators, assistive aids, and corrective surgeries.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Education Department.

**References / Annexures:** - **Guideline**: https://education.goa.gov.in/sites/default/files/2020-11/schemes-gssa.pdf
