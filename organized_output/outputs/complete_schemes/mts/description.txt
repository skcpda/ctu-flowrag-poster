# Motorized Tricycle Yojana

**Short Title:** MTCY

**Tags:** Persons With Disabilities, Motorized Tricycle, Locomotor Disability, Self-employment, PwD

The &amp;quot;Motorized Tricycle Yojana&amp;quot; is a scheme implemented by the Department of Social Justice and Empowerment, Government of Rajasthan. Under this scheme, motorized tricycles are provided to eligible applicants for purposes such as self-employment and pursuing higher education from Senior Higher Secondary onwards. To be eligible for the scheme, the applicant must be a resident of Rajasthan, a specially-abled person with 50% or more locomotor disability, and possess driving experience along with a valid driving license.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Skills & Employment

**Where it applies:** Rajasthan; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** Under this scheme, beneficiaries will receive a motorized tricycle free of cost to support self-employment and higher education from the Senior Secondary level onwards.

**Benefit Type:** In Kind

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Social Justice and Empowerment Department.
