# Marriage Assistance

**Short Title:** MATN

**Tags:** Tamil Nadu, Marriage, Employees, Son, Daughter

The Government of Tamil Nadu introduced &quot;Marriage Assistance&quot; with a sum of Rs.10,000/- for both male and female is given as Marriage Assistance to employees or their Son/Daughter who legally attain the age of marriage.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Financial assistance

**Where it applies:** Tamil Nadu; State

**Benefits / Assistance:** A sum of Rs.10,000/- for both male and female is given as Marriage Assistance to employees or their Son/Daughter who legally attain the age of marriage.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Labour and Employment Department.
