# Workers for Class 9th to 12th Through National Scholarship (JKBOCWWB)

**Short Title:** WFCTNSJKBOCWWB

**Tags:** Worker, Construction, Labour, Education, Scholarship

Launched on 1st January 2018, The &quot;Workers for Class 9th to 12th&quot; scheme by the Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board, The scheme provides annual financial assistance of ₹1,200 per child (up to two children) for the education of registered BOC workers&#39; children through the national scholarship portal.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Education & Learning; Sector: Scholarships and student finance

**Where it applies:** Jammu and Kashmir; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** An annual scholarship of ₹1,200 per child (up to two children)

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Department of Labour and Employment.

**References / Annexures:** - **Scheme Details**: https://www.jkbocw.gov.in/StaticPages/SchemeInformation.aspx - **Website**: https://www.jkbocw.gov.in/Index.aspx - **Apply For Scheme**: https://scholarships.gov.in/ - **Worker Registration**: https://portal.jkbocw.gov.in/MemberRegistration.aspx
