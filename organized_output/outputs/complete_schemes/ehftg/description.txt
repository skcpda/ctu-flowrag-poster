# Economic Help To Tribal Girls For Higher Education (Class 11 & 12)

**Short Title:** EHFTG

**Launch Date:** 2011-01-01

**Tags:** Financial Assistance, Educational Grant, Student, Higher Education

Rajasthan state government started a scheme &quot;**Economic Help to Tribal Girls for Higher Education (Class 11 &amp; 12)**&quot;. The main objective is to provide benefits to the tribal girls for their higher education, who are studying in government schools by taking admission as regular students for higher education.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Financial assistance

**Where it applies:** Rajasthan; State

**Benefits / Assistance:** - Rs. 3,500/- One time and fully in July

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Tribal Area Development Department.

**References / Annexures:** - **Details**: https://schemes.rajasthan.gov.in/scheme/detail/457 - **Registration**: https://sso.rajasthan.gov.in/register

**Additional Information:**
Briefdescription: Rajasthan Government started a scholarship for tribal girls higher education (Class 11 & 12) In the year 2011.
