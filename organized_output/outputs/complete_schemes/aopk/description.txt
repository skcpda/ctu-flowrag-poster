# Award of Puduvai Kalaimamani

**Short Title:** AOPK

**Tags:** Artists, Financial Assistance, Drama, Music, Author

The &quot;Award of Puduvai Kalaimamani&quot; scheme was launched by the Arts and Culture Department, Puducherry. The scheme aims to provide prizes of ₹50,000/- and 30 awards to artists or authors/writers in the fields of Literature, Music, Dance, Drama, Painting and Sculpture, and Folk arts. The applications are accepted offline in the prescribed form available at the Director of Art and Culture Department.

**Who is this for:** Artists, Individual; Category: Sports & Culture; Sector: Culture

**Where it applies:** Puducherry; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - Financial Assistance of ₹50,000/-. - No. of Awards: 30

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Arts and Culture Department, Puducherry.
