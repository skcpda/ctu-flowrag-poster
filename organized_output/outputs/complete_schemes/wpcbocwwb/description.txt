# Widow Pension (CBOCWWB)

**Short Title:** WP(CBOCWWB)

**Tags:** Widow, Pension, Construction Worker, Labour

The scheme “Widow Pension” was started by the Chandigarh Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board (CBOCWWB), Labour Department, Chandigarh. Under this scheme, financial assistance in the form of a monthly pension shall be given to the widows of male registered construction workers of the Board.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Pension

**Where it applies:** Chandigarh; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** Financial assistance of ₹1000/- per month in the form of pension shall be given to the widows of male registered construction workers of the Board.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: Chandigarh Building And Other Construction Workers Welfare Board; Nodal Department: Labour Department.
