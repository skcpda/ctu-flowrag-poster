# Pradhan Mantri Matsya Sampada Yojana (PMMSY-Inland): Establishment of Large RAS Culture System Gujarat

**Short Title:** PMMSYELRASGUJ

**Tags:** Fish Farming, Biofloc, Recirculating Aquaculture System, Employment, Large RAS

The scheme &quot;Pradhan Mantri Matsya Sampada Yojana (PMMSY-Inland)&quot; with the component of the Establishment of Large RAS (with 8 tanks of minimum 90 m3/ tank capacity 40 tonnes/ crop) Culture System, introduced by the Agriculture, Farmers Welfare and Co-operation Department, Government of Gujarat, aims to increase fish production by rearing fish in reservoirs and provide employment to people involved in inland fisheries activities. Through this scheme, financial assistance is provided for various inland fisheries activities, including the establishment of large Recirculating Aquaculture Systems (RAS). The scheme is implemented by the Commissioner of Fisheries of the State Governments, with 60% contribution from the Government of India and 40% from the Government of Gujarat.  > **Objective:** - Assistance is provided for accelerating inland fish farming activities in freshwater bodies, increasing inland fish production and boosting inland fisheries. - Except for water bodies or ponds, in less land, less water, Biofloc with source as well, Recirculating Aquaculture System (RAS). By creating fish tanks with this method, more fish production can be obtained in less land and less water. If more beneficiaries get the benefit of the subsidy of this scheme, more fish production can be achieved with less water sources and less land. - Inland fish production capacity can be increased by the establishment of freshwater hatcheries. 

**Who is this for:** Business Entity, Individual, Registered Societies, Self Help Groups (SHGS); Category: Agriculture,Rural & Environment; Sector: Fishing and hunting

**Where it applies:** Gujarat; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** Assistance is provided for the establishment of a Large Recirculating Aquaculture System (RAS) with 8 tanks of a minimum 90 m3/tank capacity, targeting 40 tonnes/crop production. - The unit cost for this component is ₹50,00,000/- per unit. - For the General Category, the scale of assistance is 40% of the unit cost or 40% of the actual cost, whichever is less. - For Women, Scheduled Castes (SC), and Scheduled Tribes (ST) categories, the scale of assistance is 60% of the unit cost or 60% of the actual cost, whichever is less.  > **Mode of Disbursement:** 60% of the government contribution is from the Government of India, and 40% is from the State Government. > **Frequency of Disbursement**: The minimum time limit for reclaiming benefits is specified as &quot;Once in a lifetime&quot;. 

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Agriculture, Farmers Welfare and Cooperation Department.
