# National Mission on Edible Oil–Oil Palm: Planting Materials

**Short Title:** NMOEPPM

**Tags:** Edible Oil, Oil Palm, Planting Materials, Imported Plant, Seedlings, Subsidy

The scheme component &amp;quot;Planting Materials&amp;quot; launched under the &amp;quot;National Mission on Edible Oil–Oil Palm&amp;quot; scheme by the Department of Agriculture, Farmers Welfare and Co-operation, Government of Gujarat, aims to enhance oil palm cultivation in Gujarat by providing subsidized planting materials to farmers, thereby reducing import dependency and promoting self-reliance in edible oil production. The assistance covers both locally sourced and imported seedlings, including transportation costs for imported varieties. 

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Agriculture,Rural & Environment; Sector: Agricultural Inputs- seeds, fertilizer etc.

**Where it applies:** Gujarat; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - **For Locally Sourced Planting Materials.:** ₹20,000/- per hectare - **For Imported Plants (including transportation cost):** ₹29,000/- per hectare > **Note:** For imported seedlings (along with transportation cost) the assistance will be admissible as per the holding of land held by the farmer. 

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Agriculture, Farmers Welfare and Cooperation Department.
