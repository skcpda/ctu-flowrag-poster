# Cash Award to Top Ranking Holders in Public Examinations

**Short Title:** CATRHPE

**Tags:** Cash, Award, Rank, Scholarship, Exam, School, College

The scheme &quot;Cash Award to Top Ranking Holders in Public Examinations&quot; was launched by the Directorate of School Education, Union Territory of Puducherry. Through this scheme, cash awards of ₹50,000/-, ₹30,000/- and ₹20,000/- are granted to the 1st, 2nd and 3rd Rank Holders respectively from Secondary School Leaving Certificate (SSLC) Exam, Secondary School Certificate (SSC) Exam, Matriculation Exam, and for Higher Secondary Certificate (HSC) Exam, and Intermediate (2nd Year) Public Examinations.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Education & Learning; Sector: Scholarships and student finance

**Where it applies:** Puducherry; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** > **Quantum of Award** - 1st Rank: ₹50,000/-. - 2nd Rank: ₹30,000/-. - 3rd Rank: ₹20,000/-. * Every year, the awards are granted separately for each of the top three Rank Holders from Secondary School Leaving Certificate (SSLC) Exam, Secondary School Certificate (SSC) Exam, Matriculation Exam, and for Higher Secondary Certificate (HSC) Exam, and Intermediate (2nd Year) Public Examinations. * The awards are granted separately for each of the four regions of the UT of Puducherry, i.e. Puducherry, Karaikal, Mahe, and Yanam. * The awards are granted separately for Government Schools and for Private Schools.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: School Education Department, Puducherry.
