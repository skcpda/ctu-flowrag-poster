# Supply of Radio to the Blind Persons

**Short Title:** SRBP

**Tags:** Blind, Disability, PwD, Differently Abled

The scheme “Supply of Radio to the Blind Persons&quot; was launched by the Department of Social Welfare, Government of Puducherry. In this scheme, a radio is provided to the blind person for recreation. In order to be eligible, the applicant should have disability of 40% or above, and should be a Native/Resident of the Union Territory of Puducherry for at least 5 years.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Aids/Appliances

**Where it applies:** Puducherry; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** A radio is provided to the blind person for recreation.

**Benefit Type:** In Kind

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Social Welfare Department, Puducherry.
