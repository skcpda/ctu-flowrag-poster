# Pension Scheme for Transgender

**Short Title:** PSFT

**Launch Date:** 2015-08-01

**Tags:** Pension, Transgender, Financial Assistance, Social Welfare

Launched on 1st August 2015, the scheme &quot;Pension Scheme for Transgender&quot; is a Pension Scheme by the Directorate of Social Welfare and Social Education, Government of Tripura. The scheme provides financial assistance of ₹2,000/- to the transgender residents of Tripura who have no source of income.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Pension

**Where it applies:** Tripura; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - ₹2,000/- per month.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Directorate Of Social Welfare & Social Education.
