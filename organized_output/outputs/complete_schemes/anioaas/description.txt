# The Andaman & Nicobar Islands Old Age Assistance Scheme

**Short Title:** ANIOAAS

**Tags:** Pension, Senior Citizen, Financial Assistance, Old Age

The scheme “The Andaman &amp;amp; Nicobar Islands Old Age Assistance Scheme” is implemented by the Directorate of Social Welfare, Union Territory of Andaman and Nicobar Islands to provide financial assistance to senior citizens (Above 60 years of age) in the Andaman and Nicobar Islands. The aim of the scheme is to provide social security by way of financial assistance to destitute, senior citizens who are without any means of subsistence and having no one to give financial support to them for their livelihood. As per the scheme, only those who are permanent residents of A&amp;amp;N Islands or having resided in this territory for not less than 10 years immediately preceding the date of applications are eligible for receiving financial assistance.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Pension

**Where it applies:** Andaman and Nicobar Islands; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** 1. The amount of assistance for persons in the age group 60-79 years shall be ₹2,500/- per month. 1. The amount of assistance for persons aged 80 years &amp;amp; above shall be ₹3,000/-. **Note 01:** The scheme also included the beneficiaries of the Indira Gandhi National Old Age Pension Scheme (IGNOAPS). **Note 02:** Payment of allowance shall be made through Bank Account on a monthly basis.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: Directorate Of Social Welfare; Nodal Department: Social Welfare Department.
