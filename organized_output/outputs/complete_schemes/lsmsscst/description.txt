# Laptop Scheme for Meritorious Students of SC/ST

**Short Title:** LSMSSCST

**Tags:** Student, Education, School, Laptop, Scheduled Caste, Scheduled Tribe

The scheme &quot;Laptop Scheme for Meritorious Students of SC/ST&quot; by the Department of Information Technology, Electronics and Communication, Government of Goa aims to provide laptops to the meritorious students who were born in Goa. The students fulfilling the eligibility criteria may apply for the scheme by filling the prescribed online application form. The call for applications for the scheme will be announced by Info Tech Corporation of Goa Limited (ITG) every year.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Education & Learning; Sector: Scholarships and student finance

**Where it applies:** Goa; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** Laptop is provided to the meritorious student.

**Benefit Type:** In Kind

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Information and Publicity Department.

**References / Annexures:** - **Guidelines**: https://dit.goa.gov.in/schemes_policies.html
