# Integrated Child Development Services- Uttarakhand

**Short Title:** ICDSUK

**Tags:** Child Development, Anganwadi Centre, Health Check-ups, Children, Pregnant Woman, Lactating Mother

The scheme “Integrated Child Development Services” is implemented by the Department of Women Empowerment &amp;amp; Child Development, Government of Uttarakhand. The scheme aims to improve the nutritional and health status of all children aged 6 months to 6 years, along with pregnant women and lactating mothers in the state, by providing supplementary nutrition, immunisation, and regular health check-ups. 

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Women and Child; Sector: Pregnancy Care

**Where it applies:** Uttarakhand; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** **The following services are provided free of cost through Anganwadi Centres:** - Supplementary nutrition, immunisation/vaccination, and related services for children between 6 months and 3 years. - Immunisation/vaccination, pre-school education, and related services for children aged 3 to 6 years. - Immunisation/vaccination, health check-ups, nutrition, and health education services for pregnant and lactating mothers. 

**Benefit Type:** In Kind

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Department Of Woman Empowerment & Child Development.
