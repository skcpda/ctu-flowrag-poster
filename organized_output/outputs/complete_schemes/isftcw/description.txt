# Insurance Scheme for the Construction Workers

**Short Title:** ISFTCW

**Tags:** Labour, Building Worker, Construction Worker, Insurance

The &amp;quot;Insurance Scheme for the Construction Workers&amp;quot; by the Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board, Labour Department, U.T. Administration of Dadra and Nagar Haveli and Daman and Diu, is a welfare scheme for the registered workers of the board. Under this scheme, beneficiaries shall be enrolled under the “Pradhan Mantri Suraksha Bima Yojna” and “Pradhan Mantri Jeevan Jyoti Bima Yojna”. &lt;br&gt;

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Insurance

**Where it applies:** Dadra & Nagar Haveli and Daman & Diu; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** Enrollment of beneficiaries under the following schemes: - Pradhan Mantri Suraksha Bima Yojna. - Pradhan Mantri Jeevan Jyoti BimaYojna.

**Benefit Type:** Composite

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Labour & Employment Department.

**References / Annexures:** - **Guidelines**: https://www.daman.nic.in/websites/Printing-press-Daman/documents/2020/Extraordinary/Extra%20Ordinary%20No.%2029.pdf
