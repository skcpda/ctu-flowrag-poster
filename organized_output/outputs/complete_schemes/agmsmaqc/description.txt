# Aatmanirbhar Gujarat Scheme for assistance to MSMEs: Assistance for Quality Certification

**Short Title:** AGMSMAQC

**Launch Date:** 2022-10-05

**Tags:** Business, Entrepreneurship, MSMEs, Reimbursement

The &amp;quot;Aatmanirbhar Gujarat Scheme for MSMEs&amp;quot; is an umbrella scheme launched by the Industries and Mines Department, Gujarat. It aims to empower the state&amp;#39;s dynamic MSME sector, which significantly contributes to employment, GDP, and exports. Effective from October 5, 2022, to October 4, 2027, this initiative supports growth, investments, and the vision of &amp;quot;Aatmanirbhar Gujarat&amp;quot; and &amp;quot;Aatmanirbhar Bharat.&amp;quot; The component &amp;quot;Assistance for Quality Certification&amp;quot; supports MSMEs in the manufacturing sector to obtain quality certifications and enhance their product standards in a competitive market. The scheme covers the implementation of ERP systems and national/international quality certifications.

**Who is this for:** Business Entity, Industries; Category: Business & Entrepreneurship; Sector: Setting up / start-up / entrepreneurship

**Where it applies:** Gujarat; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** &gt; **ERP Assistance:** 1. 65% of the capital cost for installing an ERP system, up to ₹1,00,000 during the scheme&amp;#39;s operative period. 1. Covers ERP systems with software and services for production, inventory control, sales, purchase, accounting, and HR management. 1. Includes installation charges, software costs, annual service fees, and SaaS-based ERP subscription costs (excluding hardware). &gt; **Quality Certification:** 1. 50% assistance for fees toward national/international certifications (e.g., BIS, ISI, WHO, GMP, Hallmark). 1. 50% cost assistance for testing equipment and machinery required for certification, up to ₹10,00,000/-. 1. Covers certification agency fees (excluding travel/hotel/surveillance charges), testing equipment costs, and calibration charges. 

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Exclusions / Not eligible:** - Enterprises that apply for certifications required as part of statutory provisions are not eligible for assistance.

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Industries and Mines Department.
