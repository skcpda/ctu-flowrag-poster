# Cycle Scheme for the Children of Construction Workers (P.B.O.C.W.W.B)

**Short Title:** CSCCWPBOCWWB

**Tags:** Labour, Building Worker, Construction Worker, Children, Cycle

The &quot;Cycle Scheme for the Children of Construction Workers&#39;&#39; by the BOCW Welfare Board, Department of Labour, Punjab, is a welfare scheme for the registered workers of the board. Under this scheme, the Board will provide free cycles to the children of registered construction workers once during the period they study in 9th to 12th class in the State of Punjab.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Citizen empowerment

**Where it applies:** Punjab; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - **Quantum of Assistance:** One(1) cycle to the children of registered construction workers.

**Benefit Type:** In Kind

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Department of Labour.
