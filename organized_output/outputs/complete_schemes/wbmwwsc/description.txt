# West Bengal Migrant Workers’ Welfare Scheme: Repatriation of Dead Body

**Short Title:** WBMWWSC

**Launch Date:** 2023-04-25

**Tags:** Cremation, Migrant, Social Welfare, Financial Assistance, Labour, Worker, Repatriation

&amp;quot;West Bengal Migrant Workers’ Welfare Scheme: Repatriation of Dead Body&amp;quot; is a social welfare scheme implemented by the West Bengal Migrant Workers’ Welfare Board, Labour Department, Government of West Bengal on 25th April, 2023. Under this scheme, if any registered Migrant Worker dies in the course of employment outside the State of West Bengal, an assistance of ₹ 25,000/- will be provided to the nominee of the worker for bringing the dead body to his/ her permanent address.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Crisis/Disaster/Accident

**Where it applies:** West Bengal; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - ₹ 25,000/- to the nominee of the Migrant Worker.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Exclusions / Not eligible:** No claim shall be admissible to the applicant in receipt of any other financial assistance of similar nature from the Government of West Bengal.

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: West Bengal Migrant Workers’ Welfare Board; Nodal Department: Labour Department.
