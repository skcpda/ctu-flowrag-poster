# Scheme for Supportive Money to the Parents of Transgender Children

**Short Title:** SFSMTTPOTC

**Tags:** Financial Assistance, Transgender, Child

The &quot;Scheme for Supportive Money to the Parents of Transgender Children&quot; was launched by the Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment. The scheme aims to support 5,000 beneficiaries annually, with the potential for higher numbers based on State/UT identification. Transgender persons face stigma and discrimination in almost every sphere of their life including within the group, health facilities, education, employment, etc. Awareness and information are needed among parents to support their gender-nonconforming or transgender children, setting aside their discomfort and deeply held normative attitudes.

**Who is this for:** Family; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Financial assistance

**Where it applies:** Central

**Benefits / Assistance:** - **Financial assistance of ₹1,000/- per transgender parent per child per month.**

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Department of Social Justice & Empowerment.
