# The Meghalaya Victim Compensation Scheme, 2022

**Short Title:** TMVCS

**Launch Date:** 2022-01-01

**Tags:** Victim, Shelter, Rehabilitation, Counselling, Legal Aid

The Meghalaya Victim Compensation Scheme, 2022 is a social welfare scheme introduced by the Social Welfare Department, Govt. of Meghalaya. Under this scheme, financial assistance to the victim along with support services such as shelter, counseling, medical aid, legal assistance, education, and vocational training depend upon the needs of the victim. The scheme shall apply to the victims and their dependent(s), or the member(s) of the family of the victim, who have suffered loss, or injury as the case may be, as a result of the crime and who require rehabilitation.

**Who is this for:** Family, Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Psychosocial support/counselling

**Where it applies:** Meghalaya; State

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Exclusions / Not eligible:** 1. The applicant shall not be eligible if the victim’s death or permanent incapacitation was due to suicide. 1. The applicant shall not be eligible if the injury or trauma was self-inflicted. 1. The applicant shall not be eligible if the injury, trauma, or death was a result of the victim’s own wrongdoing. 1. The applicant shall not be eligible if compensation has already been received for the same loss or injury. 1. The compensation may have been received from any scheme of the Central Government, State Government, insurance company, or any other institution. 1. The applicant shall not be eligible if they are the perpetrator of the crime. 1. The applicant shall not be eligible if they are a dependent of the perpetrator.
