# Yatra Pass in DTC non AC Buses for the Construction Workers

**Short Title:** YPIDNABFTCW

**Tags:** Yatra Pass, Labour, Construction Worker, Travel, Transport

The &quot;Yatra Pass in DTC non AC Buses for the Construction Workers&quot; by the Delhi Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board, Labour Department, Delhi, is a welfare scheme that provides a pass of ₹ 2400/- per three months for non AC DTC bus to the registered workers for purchasing. All types of bus passes i.e. Conventional Bus Pass as well as e-Bus Pass can be made online.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment

**Where it applies:** Delhi; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - A pass of 2400/- per three months for non AC DTC bus is provided.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: Delhi Building And Other Construction Workers Welfare Board; Nodal Department: Labour Department.
