# Mukhmantri Sehat Bima Yojana

**Short Title:** MMSBY

**Launch Date:** 2019-08-20

**Tags:** Insurance, Health, Welfare, Medical

Ayushman Bharat PM-JAY Mukhmantri Sehat Bima Yojana (AB PM-JAY MMSBY), was launched on 20 August 2019 this is a flagship State Health Insurance Scheme for the beneficiaires of State of Punjab. AB PM-JAY MMSBY will provide financial protection to 65% population of state of Punjab. It is entitlement based cashless Health Insurance cover. Under this scheme cashless and paperless treatment is available at Government and empaneled Private hospitals. &lt;br&gt;

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Health & Wellness; Sector: Health Insurance

**Where it applies:** Punjab; State

**Benefits / Assistance:** - A cashless health insurance cover of ₹5,00,000/- rupees per family per year for secondary and tertiary care hospitalization (in-patient services) for treatment of listed diseases and medical conditions.

**Benefit Type:** In Kind

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Department of Health & Family Welfare.

**References / Annexures:** - **Official Portal**: https://sha.punjab.gov.in/shapunjab/index.php
