# Assistance For Delivery / Miscarriage Of Pregnancy / Termination Of Pregnancy To Female Differently Abled Person

**Short Title:** ADMPTPFDAP

**Tags:** Female, Delivery, Miscarriage, Pregnancy, Differently Abled Person

The Government of Tamil Nadu introduced &quot;Assistance for Delivery/ Miscarriage of Pregnancy/ Termination of Pregnancy to Female Differently Abled Person with a sum of ₹6,000/- @ ₹1,000/- per month for delivery and ₹3,000/- for miscarriage/termination of pregnancy is given to female differently abled person.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Health & Wellness; Sector: Financial assistance

**Where it applies:** Tamil Nadu; State

**Benefits / Assistance:** A sum of Rs.6,000/- @ Rs.1,000/- per month for delivery and Rs.3,000 for miscarriage/termination of pregnancy is given to a female differently abled person.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Welfare of Differently Abled Persons Department.

**References / Annexures:** - **Guidelines**: https://www.scd.tn.gov.in/pdf/schemes/pdf/ASSISTANCE%20FOR%20DELIVERY%20%20MISCARRIAGE%20OF%20PREGNANCY.pdf
