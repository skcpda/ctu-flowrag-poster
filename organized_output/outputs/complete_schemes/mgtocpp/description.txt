# "Monthly Grant To Old Crafts Persons (Pension)" Component of the "Development of Handicrafts" Scheme

**Short Title:** MGTOCPP

**Tags:** Pension, Senior Citizen, Handicraft, Death

The scheme “Monthly Grant To Old Crafts Persons (Pension)” Component of the &quot;Development of Handicrafts&quot; Scheme by the Department of Industries and Commerce, Union Territory of Puducherry, aims to provide pension to artisans in their old age, recognizing their contribution to traditional crafts. Under this scheme, eligible craftspersons receive a monthly grant of ₹2,000/-.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Pension

**Where it applies:** Puducherry; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - A monthly grant of ₹2,000/-. This financial assistance continues until the lifetime of the beneficiary, subject to renewal every year. - In the case of the craftsperson&#39;s death, the living spouse is entitled to receive ₹1,000/- per month.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Industries and Commerce Department, Puducherry.
