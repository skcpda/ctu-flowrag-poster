# Fodder Minikits under Tribal Area Sub Plan (TASP) Scheme (ST Category)

**Short Title:** FMTASPS(STC)

**Tags:** Fodder Minikits, Farmer, Fodder Seed, Scheduled Tribe

The scheme “Fodder Minikits under Tribal Area Sub Plan (TASP) Scheme” is implemented by the Directorate of Animal Husbandry, Agriculture, Farmers Welfare and Co-operation Department, Government of Gujarat. Under this scheme, improved varieties of fodder seeds are provided to beneficiaries for widespread publicity, demonstration and adaptation by farmers.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Agriculture,Rural & Environment; Sector: Animal husbandry

**Where it applies:** Gujarat; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** 1. Under this scheme, the fodder seed minikit of 3 Kg, 7 Kg or 10 Kg is given to the eligible beneficiary.

**Benefit Type:** In Kind

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: Directorate Of Animal Husbandry, Government Of Gujarat; Nodal Department: Agriculture, Farmers Welfare and Cooperation Department.
