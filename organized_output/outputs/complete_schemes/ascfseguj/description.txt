# Assistance to Scheduled Castes for Fisheries: Scheme For Fish Sale Equipment - Gujarat

**Short Title:** ASCFSEGUJ

**Tags:** Fish Production, Fish Farming, Fishermen, Fish Farmer, Fish Sale Equipments

The scheme &quot;Assistance to Scheduled Castes for Fisheries with the component scheme for Fish Sale Equipments, Gujarat&quot; introduced by the Agriculture, Farmers Welfare and Co-operation Department, Government of Gujarat, aims to increase fish production and provide employment to Scheduled Caste (SC) individuals engaged in inland fisheries. Through this scheme, financial assistance is provided for purchasing fish sale equipment to SC fish farmers and fishermen. The scheme is implemented by the Commissioner of Fisheries, Government of Gujarat. **Objectives:** - To increase fish production by rearing fish in reservoirs and provide employment to scheduled caste (SC) people involved in inland fisheries activities. - To boost fish farming or fishing activities in freshwater bodies by scheduled caste (SC) fish farmers and fishermen. - Fishing in reservoirs, aquaculture in ponds, and catch from rivers increase fish production and boost inland fisheries through scheduled caste (SC) fish farmers and fishermen. - The main objective is to provide employment opportunities to Scheduled Caste fishermen in the state and increase fish production. 

**Who is this for:** Business Entity, Individual, Registered Societies, Self Help Groups (SHGS); Category: Agriculture,Rural & Environment; Sector: Fishing and hunting

**Where it applies:** Gujarat; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** Assistance is provided at 50% of the total unit purchase cost of ₹15,000/-, which amounts to ₹7,500/-. > **This assistance covers four components:** - An insulated box (₹1,000/-), - A box (₹500/-), - A renkadi (₹5,000/-), - A weighing unit (₹1,000/-).  > **Frequency of Disbursement:** This benefit can be availed once in a lifetime. 

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: Commissioner Of Fisheries; Nodal Department: Agriculture, Farmers Welfare and Cooperation Department.
