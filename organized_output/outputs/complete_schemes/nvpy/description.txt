# Nihshaktajan Vivaah Protsaahan Yojana

**Short Title:** NVPY

**Launch Date:** 2008-08-08

**Tags:** Financial Assistance, Marriage Grant, Empowerment

&amp;quot;Nihshaktajan Vivaah Protsaahan Yojana&amp;quot; started by the Madhya Pradesh government is going to prove to be a very useful scheme for the disabled people of the state because the state government will provide financial assistance amount for the people who are differently abled. This scheme has been started to encourage disabled people to marriage and to ensure social rehabilitation of disabled people.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Financial assistance

**Where it applies:** Madhya Pradesh; State

**Benefits / Assistance:** 1. In the case of a disabled youth marrying a normal girl and in the case of a disabled girl marrying a normal youth, the incentive amount is Rs. 2,00,000/- 1. In case of disability of both the young man and the woman, jointly Rs. 1,00,000/- incentive amount is provided.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Social Welfare Department.
