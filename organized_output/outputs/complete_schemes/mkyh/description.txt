# Mahila Kisan Yojana

**Short Title:** MKYH

**Tags:** Women, Employment, Loan, Subsidy, Enterpreneur, Scheduled Caste

The scheme &amp;quot;Mahila Kisan Yojana&amp;quot; is an economic empowerment scheme by the Scheduled Castes Finance and Development Corporation, Government of Haryana. Through this scheme, loans are provided to the eligible women from Scheduled Castes for the betterment of their economic status through self-employment activities in the Agriculture and Allied Sector. The Corporation in collaboration with NSFDC provide loan for various economic development schemes having unit cost upto ₹ 50,000/-. The Corporation provides margin money @ 10% of the project cost.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Business & Entrepreneurship; Sector: Loan

**Where it applies:** Haryana; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - Loan for various economic development schemes having unit cost up to ₹50,000/-. - Margin Money at 10% of the project cost. - Subsidy at 50% of the total project cost (max. ₹10,000/-).

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Welfare of Scheduled Caste & Backward Classes Department.
