# West Bengal Migrant Workers’ Welfare Scheme: Cremation

**Short Title:** WBMWWSCA

**Launch Date:** 2023-04-25

**Tags:** Social Welfare, Migrant, Financial Assistance, Labour, Cremation, Death, Worker

&amp;quot;West Bengal Migrant Workers’ Welfare Scheme: Cremation&amp;quot; is a social welfare scheme implemented by the West Bengal Migrant Workers’ Welfare Board, Labour Department, Government of West Bengal on 25th April, 2023. Under this scheme, if a registered Migrant Worker dies at his / her work place or the place he /she had to stay for the purpose of his / her job, an assistance amounting to ₹ 3,000/- will be provided to the nominee of the worker for cremation at the place, the worker was employed. 

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Financial assistance

**Where it applies:** West Bengal; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** ₹ 3,000/- to the nominee of the registered Migrant Worker. 

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Exclusions / Not eligible:** - No claim shall be admissible to the beneficiary in receipt of any other financial assistance of similar nature from the Government of West Bengal.  

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: West Bengal Migrant Workers’ Welfare Board; Nodal Department: Labour Department.
