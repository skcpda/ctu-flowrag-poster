# Assistance for Exhibitions Abroad and Out of State

**Short Title:** AEAOS

**Tags:** Art Promotion, Exhibition, Visual Arts

The scheme &amp;quot;Assistance for Exhibitions Abroad and Out of State&amp;quot; by the Sports, Youth and Cultural Activities Department, Government of Gujarat aims to encourage and promote artists originally from Gujarat in the field of fine arts—such as painting, sculpture, graphic arts, and photography—by offering financial assistance to organize exhibitions abroad and outside the state. The scheme ensures support for artists with a record of conducting prior solo or group exhibitions, helping them to gain national and international exposure through structured financial aid. The scheme is implemented by the Gujarat State Fine Arts Academy. 

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Sports & Culture; Sector: Culture

**Where it applies:** Gujarat; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - **Financial Assistance:** Up to ₹1,00,000/- for organizing exhibitions abroad.
