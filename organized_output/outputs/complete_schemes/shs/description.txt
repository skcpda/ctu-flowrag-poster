# State Horticulture Scholarship

**Short Title:** SHS

**Tags:** Horticulture, Scholarship, BSc, MSc, PhD, Stipend, Student

The “State Horticulture Scholarship” is a scholarship scheme, launched by the Department of Horticulture, Government of Nagaland to provide scholarship/stipends to students who are pursuing B.Sc. (Horticulture) M.Sc. (Horticulture) Ph.D. (Horticulture) courses. The department introduced the scheme to ensure that students pursuing Horticulture degrees should have the right to quality education and support their education in order to help achieve their maximum potential.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Education & Learning; Sector: Scholarships and student finance

**Where it applies:** Nagaland; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - Under the scheme, scholarships/Stipends will be given to students according to the course/programme in which they are enrolled. &gt; **Time frame:** Applicant shall be awarded the stipend for a fixed number of years as follows: 1. **B.Sc. (Horticulture): 4 Years** 1. **M.Sc. (Horticulture): 2 Years** 1. **Ph.D. (Horticulture): 3 Years**

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: Department Of Horticulture, Government Of Nagaland; Nodal Department: Horticulture Department, Nagaland.

**References / Annexures:** - **Guidelines**: https://scholarship.nagaland.gov.in/storage/Guidelines//9_Guidelines - **Common Scholarship Portal**: https://scholarship.nagaland.gov.in/
