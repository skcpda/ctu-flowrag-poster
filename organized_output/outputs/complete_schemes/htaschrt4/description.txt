# Harvesting Tools Assistance for Scheduled Castes (HRT-4)

**Short Title:** HTASC(HRT4)

**Tags:** Harvesting Tool, Scheduled Caste, HRT-4, Farmer, Financial Assistance, Cooperative Society, FPO, FPC, FIG, SHG

The scheme “Harvesting Tools Assistance for Scheduled Castes (HRT-4)” is implemented by the Directorate of Horticulture, Agriculture, Farmers Welfare and Co-operation Department, Government of Gujarat. Under the scheme, financial assistance is provided to farmers belonging to the Scheduled Castes category as well as to FPOs (Farmers Producer Organizations), FPCs (Farmers Producer Companies), FIGs (Farmer Interest Groups), SHGs (Self-Help Groups), and Cooperative Societies for purchasing different harvesting and processing equipment.

**Who is this for:** Individual, Registered Societies, Self Help Groups (SHGS); Category: Agriculture,Rural & Environment; Sector: Mechanization- solar power, farming systems

**Where it applies:** Gujarat; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** 1. The Scheduled Castes farmers will be eligible for assistance up to 50% of the cost of the harvesting tool or ₹1,00,000/-, whichever is less. 1. FPO/FPC/FIG/SHG/ Cooperative Society will be eligible for assistance of 75% of the cost of the harvesting tool or ₹1,50,000/-, whichever is less. **Note:** Unit Cost of harvesting tool should be up to ₹2,00,000/-.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: Directorate Of Horticulture, Government Of Gujarat; Nodal Department: Agriculture, Farmers Welfare and Cooperation Department.
