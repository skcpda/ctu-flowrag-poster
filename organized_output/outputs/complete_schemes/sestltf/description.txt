# Self-Employment Scheme: Transport/ Logistics/ Travels/ Food Court Wyaaj Sahaay

**Short Title:** SESTLTF

**Tags:** Business, Entrepreneurship, Self Employment, Transport, Logistics, Food Courts

&amp;quot;Transport/ Logistics/ Travels/ Food Court Wyaaj Sahaay&amp;quot; is a component under the &amp;quot;Self-Employment Scheme&amp;quot; introduced by the Social Justice and Empowerment Department, Gujarat. Under this scheme, eligible beneficiaries can avail of a 5% interest subsidy on a bank loan of ₹6.00 lakh for purchasing vehicles and setting up the required infrastructure for businesses like transport, logistics, travelers, food courts, etc.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Banking,Financial Services and Insurance; Sector: Loan

**Where it applies:** Gujarat; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - **Interest Subsidy:** A 5% subsidy on the interest for a bank loan of ₹6,00,000/-. 

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: Gujarat Unreserved Education And Economical Development Corporation; Nodal Department: Social Justice and Empowerment Department.
