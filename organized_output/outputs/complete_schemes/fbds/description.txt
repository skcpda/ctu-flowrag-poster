# Free Bicycle Distribution Scheme

**Short Title:** FBDS

**Tags:** Financial Assistance, Bicycle, Student, Scheduled Caste

The &quot;Free Bicycle Distribution Scheme&quot; by the Welfare Department, Government of Jharkhand, aims to provide financial support for purchasing bicycles to Scheduled Caste (SC) students studying in Class 8th. Through this scheme, ₹3000 is directly transferred to the bank accounts of eligible students via Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT).  > ***Objective:*** - Promotes education by ensuring better accessibility to schools for students. - Available to both Below Poverty Line (BPL) and Above Poverty Line (APL) students. 

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Education & Learning; Sector: Scholarships and student finance

**Where it applies:** Jharkhand; State/ UT

**DBT Scheme:** Yes (Direct Benefit Transfer)

**Benefits / Assistance:** - Monetary assistance of ₹3000 per student for purchasing bicycles.  > **Note****:** - The amount is disbursed directly to the beneficiary’s bank account via DBT. - Ensures purchase of new bicycles conforming to ISI/ISO standards. 

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Department of Scheduled Tribe, Scheduled Caste, Minority and Backward Class Welfare.

**References / Annexures:** - **Guidelines**: https://www.jharkhand.gov.in/PDepartment/ViewDoc?id=D030DO004SD001115012019053406479
