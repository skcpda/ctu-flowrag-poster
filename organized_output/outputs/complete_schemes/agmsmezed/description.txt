# Aatmanirbhar Gujarat Scheme for assistance to MSMEs: Financial Support to MSMEs in ZED Certification

**Short Title:** AGMSMEZED

**Launch Date:** 2022-10-05

**Tags:** Business, Entrepreneurship, MSMEs, ZED Certification

The &amp;quot;Aatmanirbhar Gujarat Scheme for MSMEs&amp;quot; is an umbrella scheme launched by the Industries and Mines Department, Gujarat. It aims to empower the state&amp;#39;s dynamic MSME sector, which significantly contributes to employment, GDP, and exports. Effective from October 5, 2022, to October 4, 2027, this initiative supports growth, investments, and the vision of &amp;quot;Aatmanirbhar Gujarat&amp;quot; and &amp;quot;Aatmanirbhar Bharat.&amp;quot; The component &amp;quot;Financial Support to MSMEs in ZED Certification&amp;quot; aims to promote Zero Defect and Zero Effect (ZED) manufacturing among MSMEs. It focuses on quality improvement, energy efficiency, environmental sustainability, and the adoption of advanced manufacturing standards to support the &amp;quot;Make in India&amp;quot; campaign.

**Who is this for:** Business Entity, Industries; Category: Business & Entrepreneurship; Sector: Setting up / start-up / entrepreneurship

**Where it applies:** Gujarat; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** &gt; **Financial Assistance:** - Subsidy of 50% of all charges after deducting assistance from the Government of India. - Maximum subsidy capped at ₹50,000 during the scheme&amp;#39;s operative period. &gt; **Quality and Sustainability Enhancement:** - Encourages MSMEs to adopt ZED manufacturing practices. - Promotes energy-efficient manufacturing and eco-friendly production. &gt; **Professional Development:** - Develops professionals skilled in ZED certification and manufacturing. &gt; **Support for Prior Expenses:** - Eligible enterprises can claim assistance for expenses incurred during the previous scheme’s operative period. &lt;br&gt;

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Industries and Mines Department.
