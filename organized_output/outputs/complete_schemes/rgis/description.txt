# Rural Girls’ Incentive Scheme

**Short Title:** RGIS

**Tags:** Student Finance, Scholarship, Incentive, Rural Development

The scheme “Rural Girls’ Incentive Scheme&amp;quot; launched by the Backward Classes, Most Backward Classes and Minorities Welfare Department, Tamil Nadu, offers financial aid to girls students of Most Backward Classes (MBC) and Denotified Communities (DNC), attending government or government-aided schools in rural areas, excluding Chennai District. Priority is given to girls from families headed by widows or destitute parents. The scheme aims to enhance educational opportunities and reduce dropout rates among these communities.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Education & Learning; Sector: Scholarships and student finance

**Where it applies:** Tamil Nadu; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** > **Financial Assistance:** - For 3rd to 5th grade: ₹500/- per annum. - For 6th grade: ₹1,000/- per annum. 

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Backward Classes Most Backward Classes and Minorities Welfare Department.

**References / Annexures:** - **Guidelines**: https://bcmbcmw.tn.gov.in/welfschemes.htm#incentive_scheme - **Community List**: https://bcmbcmw.tn.gov.in/community_list.pdf
