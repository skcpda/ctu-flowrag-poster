# Free Computer Training

**Short Title:** FCTUK

**Tags:** Computer Training, Soldier, Ex-serviceman, Sainik

The scheme &amp;quot;Free Computer Training&amp;quot; is implemented by the State Sainik Board under the Sainik Welfare Department, Government of Uttarakhand. The scheme aims to provide a one-year free training course (Computer / Accounting / Web Designing / Digital Marketing) to ex-servicemen, serving soldiers, their widows, and dependents who have passed the 12th standard. 

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Education & Learning; Sector: Education and training grants, fellowship, stipend

**Where it applies:** Uttarakhand; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** Under this scheme, eligible beneficiaries are provided with a one-year free training course in Computer, Accounting, Web Designing, or Digital Marketing. 

**Benefit Type:** In Kind

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: State Sainik Board, Government Of Uttarakhand; Nodal Department: State Sainik Board.
