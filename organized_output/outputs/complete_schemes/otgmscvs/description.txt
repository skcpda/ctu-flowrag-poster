# One-Time Grant Money to Soldiers Conferred with Vishisht Seva Medal Award

**Short Title:** OTGMSCVSMA

**Tags:** Award, Soldiers, One Time Grant, Vishisht Seva Medal, Ex-servicemen

The scheme “One-Time Grant to Soldiers Conferred with the Vishisht Seva Medal Award” is implemented by the State Sainik Board under the Sainik Welfare Department, Government of Uttarakhand. Under this scheme, a one-time financial assistance/grant is provided to serving soldiers and ex-servicemen of the state who have been conferred with the &amp;#39;Vishisht Seva Medal Award&amp;#39;. 

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Financial assistance

**Where it applies:** Uttarakhand; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - A one-time grant ranging from a minimum of ₹75,000/- to a maximum of ₹2,00,000/- is given to serving soldiers and ex-servicemen of the state. 

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: State Sainik Board.

**References / Annexures:** - **Guidelines (Page No. 28)**: https://uk.gov.in/department92/library_file/file-04-12-2023-06-02-23.pdf
