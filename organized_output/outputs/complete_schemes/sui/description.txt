# Stand-Up India

**Short Title:** SUPI

**Launch Date:** 2016-04-05

**Closing Date:** 2025-03-31

**Tags:** Loan, Entrepreneur, Finance, Business

A scheme by Ministry of Finance for financing SC/ST and/or Women Entrepreneurs by facilitating bank loans for setting up a greenfield project enterprise in manufacturing, services, trading sector and activities allied to agriculture. The objective of this scheme is to facilitate bank loans between Rs. 10 lakh and Rs. 1 Crore to at least one Scheduled Caste (SC) or Scheduled Tribe (ST) borrower and at least one woman borrower per bank branch for setting up a greenfield enterprise. In case of non-individual enterprises, at least 51% of the shareholding and controlling stake should be held by either an SC/ST or Woman entrepreneur.

**Who is this for:** Individual, Business Entity; Category: Business & Entrepreneurship; Sector: Banking and money

**Where it applies:** Central

**Benefits / Assistance:** 1. Facilitation of composite loan (inclusive of term loan and working capital) between ₹10 Lakhs and ₹100 Lakhs. Rupay debit card to be issued for convenience of the borrower. 1. The web portal by SIDBI provides hand-holding support through a network of agencies engaged in training, skill development, mentoring, project report preparation, application filling, work shed / utility support services, subsidy schemes etc.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: Banks; Nodal Department: Department of Financial Service.
