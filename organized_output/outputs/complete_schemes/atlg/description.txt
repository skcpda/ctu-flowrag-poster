# Assistance to Law Graduates

**Short Title:** ATLG

**Tags:** Law Graduate, Financial Assistance, Differently-abled Person, Social Welfare

The &quot;Assistance to Law Graduates&quot; scheme was launched by the Department for the Welfare of Differently Abled Persons, Government of Tamil Nadu. Under this scheme, financial assistance of ₹50,000/- will be given to the differently abled Law graduates for enrolment in the Bar Council as lawyer and for the purchase of law books. 

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Education & Learning; Sector: Financial assistance

**Where it applies:** Tamil Nadu; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - **Financial Assistance of ₹50,000/-.**

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Welfare of Differently Abled Persons Department.

**References / Annexures:** - **Citizen's Charter (2022)**: https://cms.tn.gov.in/sites/default/files/documents/wda_e_cc_2022_23.pdf
