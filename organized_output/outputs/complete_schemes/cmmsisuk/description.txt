# Mukhyamantri Medhavi Chhatra Protsahan Scholarship Scheme (Secondary Level)

**Short Title:** MMCPSSSL

**Tags:** Scholarship, Student, Meritorious Students, Secondary Level

The scheme “Mukhyamantri Medhavi Chhatra Protsahan Scholarship Scheme (Secondary Level)” is implemented by the Secondary Education Department under the Department of Education, Government of Uttarakhand. The scheme aims to promote academic excellence among students entering Classes 9 and 10 by providing financial assistance to meritorious students, based on their performance in a competitive examination conducted by the State Council of Educational Research and Training (SCERT), Uttarakhand. 

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Education & Learning; Sector: Scholarships and student finance

**Where it applies:** Uttarakhand; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** Rate of Scholarship: - **For Class 9th Students:** ₹900/- per month for 1 year.- **For Class 10th Students:** ₹900/- per month for 1 year. 

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: Secondary Education Department, Government Of Uttarakhand; Nodal Department: Department Of Education.
