# Scheme for Assistance to Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises (MSME): Assistance in Implementation of Information and Communication Technology (ICT)

**Short Title:** SAMSMEICT

**Launch Date:** 2020-08-07

**Closing Date:** 2025-08-06

**Tags:** Business, Entrepreneurship, MSMEs, Start Up

The scheme &amp;quot;Scheme for Assistance to Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises (MSME)&amp;quot; is an umbrella scheme launched by the Industries and Mines Department, Gujarat. It aims to empower the state&amp;#39;s dynamic MSME sector, which significantly contributes to employment, GDP, and exports. Effective from August 7th, 2020 to August 6th, 2025, this initiative supports entrepreneurs and strengthens MSMEs, making them globally competitive. The component &amp;quot;Assistance in Implementation of Information and Communication Technology (ICT)&amp;quot; provides financial assistance for the implementation of Information and Communication Technology (ICT) infrastructure. The support covers equipment required for communication, networking, and cloud computing.

**Who is this for:** Business Entity, Industries; Category: Business & Entrepreneurship; Sector: Setting up / start-up / entrepreneurship

**Where it applies:** Gujarat; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - **Financial Support:** Covers 65% of capital expenditure on ICT facilities, up to ₹5,00,000/-.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Industries and Mines Department.

**References / Annexures:** - **Guidelines**: https://ic.gujarat.gov.in/documents/commondoc/2020/3-GR_02092020.pdf - **Online Application Portal**: https://ifp.gujarat.gov.in/IC/digigov.htm?actionFlag=loadCitizenRegPage
