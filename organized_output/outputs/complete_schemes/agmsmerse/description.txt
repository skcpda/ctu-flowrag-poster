# Aatmanirbhar Gujarat Scheme for assistance to MSMEs: Rehabilitation of Sick Enterprise

**Short Title:** AGMSMERSE

**Launch Date:** 2022-10-05

**Tags:** Business, Entrepreneurship, MSMEs, Rehabilitation Of Sick Enterprise

The &amp;quot;Aatmanirbhar Gujarat Scheme for MSMEs&amp;quot; is an umbrella scheme launched by the Industries and Mines Department, Gujarat. It aims to empower the state&amp;#39;s dynamic MSME sector, which significantly contributes to employment, GDP, and exports. Effective from October 5, 2022, to October 4, 2027, this initiative supports growth, investments, and the vision of &amp;quot;Aatmanirbhar Gujarat&amp;quot; and &amp;quot;Aatmanirbhar Bharat.&amp;quot; The component &amp;quot;Rehabilitation of Sick Enterprise&amp;quot; scheme supports MSME sick industrial enterprises that meet RBI&amp;#39;s criteria for sickness by reimbursing part of the costs for preparing a diagnostic report and rehabilitation plan. &lt;br&gt; &lt;br&gt;

**Who is this for:** Business Entity, Industries; Category: Business & Entrepreneurship; Sector: Setting up / start-up / entrepreneurship

**Where it applies:** Gujarat; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** &gt; **Cost Reimbursement:** - **Reimbursement:** 50% of the cost for preparing the Draft Rehabilitation Scheme. - **Financial Assistance:** ₹1,00,000/- during the scheme&amp;#39;s operative period. 

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Industries and Mines Department.
