# Indira Gandhi National Old Age Pension Scheme - Sikkim

**Short Title:** IGNOAPS-SIKKIM

**Launch Date:** 2014-01-01

**Tags:** Old Age, Pension, Below Poverty Line, Direct Benefit Transfer

The scheme “Indira Gandhi National Old Age Pension Scheme (IGNOAPS)” was started by the Department of Women &amp;amp; Child Welfare, Govt. of Sikkim in 2014. Under this scheme, a pension is provided to elderly citizens above the age of 60 years belonging to the Below Poverty Line (BPL) category and the beneficiary will receive a monthly pension of ₹ 1500/-, ₹ 2000/- &amp;amp; ₹ 2500/- as per eligibility criteria.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Pension

**Where it applies:** Sikkim; State

**DBT Scheme:** Yes (Direct Benefit Transfer)

**Benefits / Assistance:** • A monthly pension of ₹ 1500/- to the old aged person between the age group of 60 years – 69 years. • A monthly pension of ₹ 2000/- to the old aged person between the age group of 70 years + and above. • A monthly pension of ₹ 2500/- to the old aged persons having attained the age of 80 years and above. • The beneficiary will get a pension as long as he/she is alive.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Exclusions / Not eligible:** Any person receiving a pension from the Central/State Government or Local/ Statutory Body or from any organization substantially financed by the Central/State Government or Local/ Statutory Body will not be eligible to apply for this scheme.

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: Women & Child Welfare Department, Govt. Of Sikkim; Nodal Department: Women & Child Welfare Department.
