# Pre-Matric Scholarship for the Students of Minority Castes

**Short Title:** PMSSMC

**Tags:** Scholarship, Student, Minority, Pre-matric

The “Pre-Matric Scholarship for the Students of Minority Castes” was launched by the Social Justice and Empowerment Department, Government of Gujarat. It aims to promote inclusive education and reduce dropout rates among minority caste students in primary and secondary education. Under this scheme, financial assistance in the form of annual scholarships is provided to students from minority communities studying in government and aided primary schools from standard 1st to 10th, encouraging continued education and easing financial burdens. 

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Education & Learning; Sector: Scholarships and student finance

**Where it applies:** Gujarat; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** > **Scholarship Amounts:** - Boys (Class 1st to 8th): ₹750/- per year- Boys (Class 9th to 10th): ₹1,000/- per year- Girls (Class 1st to 5th): ₹500/- per year- Girls (Class 6th to 10th): ₹1,000/- per year 

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Social Justice and Empowerment Department.

**References / Annexures:** - **Guidelines (Scheme No. 392)**: https://mariyojana.gujarat.gov.in/MoreDetails.aspx
