# Goa State Cultural Award

**Short Title:** GSCA

**Tags:** Artist, Culture, Award, Lifetime Achievement, Music, Dance, Drama

The scheme &quot;Goa State Cultural Award&quot; by the Directorate of Art and Culture, Government of Goa, has been designed to honour the eminent personalities in the field of Art and culture. The scheme intends to give recognition to the Goan artists par excellence for their lifetime achievements and for their extraordinary contribution to the field of Art and culture in Goa.  > **Objectives** 1. To honour the eminent personalities in the field of Art and culture. 1. To felicitate the artists par excellence for their lifetime achievements. 1. To recognize and appreciate the artists&#39; par excellence as State Artist of Goa.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Sports & Culture; Sector: Culture

**Where it applies:** Goa; State

**Benefits / Assistance:** The awardee shall be honoured with a shawl, a sriphal, a certificate of appreciation and a financial purse to the extent of **₹ 1,00,000/- per individual** and monthly financial assistance as provided under the scheme of Kala Samman.  > **Number of Awards** Every year maximum of 12 (twelve) persons are awarded in the following fields - 1. Music 2. Dance 3. Drama/Theatre including Tiatr 4. Performing Folk Art 5. Fine Art &amp; Craft 6. Bhajan/Kirtan/Choir 7. Film &amp; associated Audio Visual works of Art 8. Overall contribution to the field of art &amp; culture 9. Literature 10. (Creative) Photography  > **Announcement of List of Awardees** Within 6 months from the last date of submission of the application.  > **Presentation of Award** On or before April 30.

**Benefit Type:** Composite

**Exclusions / Not eligible:** 1. No assistance shall be paid to the dependent after the death of the artist. 1. If the awardee is availing pension under any State Government or Central Government scheme, then, the awardee shall not be eligible for financial assistance under the Kala Samman Scheme. 1. No person shall be eligible for a second time to avail of the benefit. 1. No award will be presented posthumously under this scheme.

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Art, Culture and Museums Department.
