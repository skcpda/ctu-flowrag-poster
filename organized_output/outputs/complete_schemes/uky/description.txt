# Usha Kiran Yojana

**Short Title:** UKY

**Launch Date:** 2004-04-01

**Tags:** Physical Abuse, Sexual Abuse, Mental Abuse, Deprivation Of Liberty, Domestic Violence

Violence against women and their children below the age of 18 years by a family member/members, which includes physical, sexual, mental, emotional, economic, threats or coercive or arbitrary deprivation of liberty, whether at home or in a public place, comes under the category of domestic violence. For all these protection and assistance, the state government has started the &quot;Usha Kiran Yojana&quot; to provide protection and assistance against domestic violence. To implement the Women&#39;s Protection from Domestic Violence Act 2005, Rule 2006.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Housing & Shelter; Sector: Shelter, integrated support and assistance

**Where it applies:** Madhya Pradesh; State

**Benefits / Assistance:** - Providing protection and support against domestic violence

**Benefit Type:** In Kind

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Women and Child Development Department.
