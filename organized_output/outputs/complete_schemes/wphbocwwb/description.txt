# Widow Pension (HBOCWWB)

**Short Title:** WPHBOCWWB

**Tags:** Widow, Pension, Construction Worker, Building Worker, Labour

The scheme “Widow Pension” is implemented by the Haryana Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board (HBOCWWB), Labour Department, Government of Haryana. Under this scheme, a monthly pension of ₹3,000/- is given to the widow of a registered construction worker who is not receiving a similar benefit from any other Government/ Department/ Board/Corporation/PSU.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Pension

**Where it applies:** Haryana; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** 1. Under the scheme, ₹3,000/- per month is given as a widow pension to the widow of the registered construction worker.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: Haryana Building & Other Construction Workers Welfare Board; Nodal Department: Labour Department.
