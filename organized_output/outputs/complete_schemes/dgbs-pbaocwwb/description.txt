# Diwali Gift Benefit Scheme (PBAOCWWB)

**Short Title:** DGBS-PBAOCWWB

**Tags:** Construction, Worker, DBT, Financial Assistance

The &quot;Diwali Gift Benefit&quot; is a Welfare Scheme by the Puducherry Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board, Labour Department, Union Territory of Puducherry. Through this scheme, the Board sanction sum of ₹ 2,000/- to each beneficiary enrolled in the Board every year to celebrate the Diwali festival. The amount is credited through Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) into the bank account of the registered construction worker. The applications are accepted offline.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Skills & Employment; Sector: Welfare measures

**Where it applies:** Puducherry; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** ₹ 2,000/- credited as Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) into the bank account.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Labour Department, Puducherry.
