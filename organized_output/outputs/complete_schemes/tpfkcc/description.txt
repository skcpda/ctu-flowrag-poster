# To Provide Interest Subvention to Fishermen of the State at the Rate of 4% to the Beneficiaries Kishan Credit Card

**Short Title:** TPFKCC

**Tags:** Agriculture, Fisherman, Interest Subvention

The scheme &amp;quot;To Provide Interest Subvention to Fishermen of the State at the Rate of 4% to the Beneficiaries Kishan Credit Card&amp;quot; introduced by the Agriculture, Farmers Welfare and Cooperation Department, Gujarat, aims to provide interest subvention to fishermen who avail short-term loans under the Kisan Credit Card scheme. If the loan is repaid on time, the beneficiaries receive a 4% interest relief, which helps reduce their financial burden and support sustainable fisheries-based livelihoods. The benefit can be claimed once per financial year.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Agriculture,Rural & Environment; Sector: Fishing and hunting

**Where it applies:** Gujarat; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** **Name of Component** **Standard of Assistance** **Minimum Time Limit (in Year) for Reclaiming Benefits)**To provide interest subvention to fishermen of the state at the rate of 4% to the beneficiaries Kishan Credit Card.4% interest subvention on timely repayment of short-term loans taken by animal husbandry up to ₹2,00,000/-.only once in a financial year

**Benefit Type:** Cash
