# CM Anuprati Coaching Scheme

**Short Title:** CMACS

**Tags:** Anuprati, Coaching, Competitive Examination, UPSC, RPSC

The &amp;quot;CM Anuprati Coaching Scheme&amp;quot; was introduced by the Social Justice and Empowerment Department, Government of Rajasthan. The objective of the scheme is to encourage talented candidates from Scheduled Caste, Scheduled Tribe, Other Backward Class, Extremely Backward Class, Economically Weaker Section or Minority categories of Rajasthan by providing financial assistance after their selection in various competitive examinations, such as IAS, RAS, IIT, IIM, CPMT, NIT, and other government engineering and medical entrance exams.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Education & Learning; Sector: Universities and higher education

**Where it applies:** Rajasthan; State

**Additional Information:**
Briefdescription: The scheme is an initiative launched by the Government of Rajasthan to provide free coaching to students from economically weaker sections (EWS) and socially backward communities for various competitive exams such as IAS, RAS, and other government job recruitment exams.
