# Scheme for Assistance for Research & Development Activities: Assistance to R&D Institutions/ Laboratories set up by the State Government or by Government of India 

**Short Title:** SARDARDL

**Launch Date:** 2020-08-07

**Tags:** Research And Development, Laboratories

The &amp;quot;Scheme for Assistance for Research &amp;amp; Development Activities&amp;quot; is an umbrella scheme launched by the Industries and Mines Department, Gujarat under the Gujarat Industrial Policy. Effective from August 7th, 2020 to August 6th, 2025, this initiative promotes sustainable industrial growth by supporting industries in adopting eco-friendly practices and ensuring compliance with environmental norms. The component &amp;quot;Assistance to R&amp;amp;D Institutions/ Laboratories set up by the State Government or by Government of India&amp;quot; supports the establishment of new laboratories and the modernization or strengthening of existing facilities to enhance their capabilities in research and innovation.

**Who is this for:** University / Institution; Category: Business & Entrepreneurship; Sector: Setting up / start-up / entrepreneurship

**Where it applies:** Gujarat; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - **Setting Up New Facilities:** Assistance to R&amp;amp;D institutions/ laboratories set up by the State Government or Government of India including setting up of new R&amp;amp;D institutions / laboratories shall be provided as per requirement.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Industries and Mines Department.
