# Intensive Fisheries Development Programme - Subsidy on Solar System for fish/shrimp culture

**Short Title:** IFDP-SSSFSC

**Tags:** Subsidy, Solar System, Fish, Fish Farmer, Shrimp Culture

The scheme &amp;quot;Subsidy on Solar System for Fish/Shrimp Culture&amp;quot; is a component of the Intensive Fisheries Development Programme, implemented by the Fisheries Department, Government of Haryana. The scheme aims to provide financial assistance to fish and shrimp farmers for the purchase/installation of solar systems. Under this scheme, subsidies for solar systems are offered to farmers who cultivate fish or shrimp on a minimum area of 2.5 acres. The subsidy benefit is granted only once per beneficiary for a single unit.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Agriculture,Rural & Environment; Sector: Mechanization- solar power, farming systems

**Where it applies:** Haryana; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** 1. A subsidy of 60% on the total project cost amounting to ₹50,000 per kilowatt (KW) or a subsidy amount of ₹30,000 per KW, whichever is less, is provided. 1. The maximum limit for this subsidy is 30 KW for shrimp farmers. 1. The maximum limit for this subsidy is 10 KW for fish farmers.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Fisheries Department.

Briefdescription: Under this scheme, subsidies on solar systems for fish/shrimp culture are provided to eligible fish and shrimp farmers. The subsidy is given at 60% of the total project cost, or ₹30,000 per kW, whichever is lower. The maximum subsidy is limited to 30 kW for shrimp farmers and 10 kW for fish farmers.
