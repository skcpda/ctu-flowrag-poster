# State Research Scholarship

**Short Title:** SRSN

**Tags:** State Research Scholarship, Nagaland, Financial Assistance

**Detailed** Directorate of Higher Education Nagaland Kohima invites students who are eligible to submit applications for the Nagaland State Research Scholarship. This scholarship is specially designed for students who are pursuing research work. Under the scheme, beneficiaries will get cash awards to conduct their research work and financial aid to complete their studies.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Education & Learning; Sector: Scholarships and student finance

**Where it applies:** Nagaland; State

**Benefits / Assistance:** **Benefits** • Under the Nagaland State Research Scholarship beneficiaries will get financial aid to complete their studies. • This scholarship is specially designed for students who are pursuing research work. Under the scheme, beneficiaries will get cash awards to conduct their research work.

**Benefit Type:** Cash
