# Financial Assistance to Old Age Pensioner Suffering From TB/ Cancer

**Short Title:** FAOAPSTBC

**Tags:** Cancer, Pension, TB, Senior Citizen, Financial Asisstance

The scheme “Financial Assistance to Old Age Pensioner Suffering From TB/ Cancer” by the Department of Women &amp; Child Development, Government of Puducherry, was introduced to extend a helping hand by way of a monthly financial assistance of ₹ 500/- to Old Age/Destitute Pensioners who are suffering from TB/Cancer to meet out the medical expenses.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Health & Wellness; Sector: Financial assistance

**Where it applies:** Puducherry; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** ₹500/- per month in addition to the old age pension amount.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Women and Child Development Department, Puducherry.
