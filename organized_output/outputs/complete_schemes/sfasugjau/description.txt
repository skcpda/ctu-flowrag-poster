# Scheme for the Award of Scholarship for the Under Graduate Meritorious Students of Various Faculties of Junagadh Agricultural University

**Short Title:** SFASUGJAU

**Tags:** Agricultural Scholarship, Fellowship, Student, Undergraduate

The &amp;quot;Scheme for the Award of Scholarship for the Under Graduate Meritorious Students of Various Faculties of Junagadh Agricultural University&amp;quot; launched by the Agriculture, Farmers Welfare and Cooperation Department, Gujarat, aims to provide monthly financial assistance to eligible Under Graduate students at Junagadh Agricultural University. The objective is to attract bright rural students to seek admission in all the faculties, and after completion of their studies, they can join the University and help in the rural development thereby improving Agricultural productivity of the State. &lt;br&gt;

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Education & Learning; Sector: Scholarships and student finance

**Where it applies:** Gujarat; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - **Undergraduate Students:** ₹1,000/- per month for 12 months.- **Free Accommodation:** Free hostel accommodation for girl students in all undergraduate faculties. - **Duration:** The duration of the scholarship will be as per duration (or total period) of Graduation including semester end breaks and vacations.- **Number of Scholarships:** 36

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Exclusions / Not eligible:** - The awardee under this scheme will not be allowed to receive any other regular scholarship, free ship stipend loan or any other financial assistance.  

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Agriculture, Farmers Welfare and Cooperation Department.

**References / Annexures:** - **Guidelines (Scheme No. 522)**: https://mariyojana.gujarat.gov.in/MoreDetails.aspx
