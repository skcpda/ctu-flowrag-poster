# Financial Assistance for Coaching- Haryana Labour Welfare Board

**Short Title:** FAC-HLWB

**Tags:** Financial Assistance, Coaching, Student, Worker, Entrance Examinations, Professional Courses, UPSC, HPSC

The &amp;quot;Financial Assistance for Coaching&amp;quot; scheme is implemented by the Haryana Labour Welfare Board (HLWB), under the Labour Department, Government of Haryana. This scheme was launched by the Haryana Labour Welfare Board on 15.01.2019 with the objective to provide financial assistance up to ₹20,000/- for coaching of entrance examinations for professional courses and ₹1,00,000/- to the children of workers for preparing for the main examination after passing the preliminary examination of UPSC and HPSC. Students seek coaching from private coaching institutes to get admission to professional courses, incurring substantial expenses for the workers. Therefore, as per the announcement of the Hon&amp;#39;ble Chief Minister, the proposal to provide financial assistance to the children of workers for coaching, apart from the existing scholarship scheme, was approved by the Board so that the children of poor workers can also brighten their future by getting good coaching in coaching centers and get admission in good professional courses.

**Who is this for:** Family, Individual; Category: Education & Learning; Sector: Coaching

**Where it applies:** Haryana; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** 1. An amount of ₹20,000/- or 75% of the coaching fee (whichever is less) will be payable to the children of workers for coaching in entrance examinations in professional courses. 1. An amount of ₹1,00,000/- will be provided to the children of workers for coaching to prepare for the main examination on passing the Preliminary Examination of UPSC and HPSC.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: Haryana Labour Welfare Board; Nodal Department: Labour Department.
