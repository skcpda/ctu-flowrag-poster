# Pre-Matric Scholarship for the Students of Socially and Educationally Backward Class

**Short Title:** PMSSSEBC

**Tags:** Backward Class, Primary School Students, Secondary School Students, Financial Assistance, Scholarship

The scheme &quot;Pre-Matric Scholarship for the Students of Socially and Educationally Backward Class&quot; by the Social Justice and Empowerment Department, Government of Gujarat, aims to provide financial assistance to promote education among socially and educationally backwards students. Through this scheme, scholarships per annum are provided to boys and girls studying in Standards 1 to 10 in government and aided schools. The scheme is implemented by the Director, Developing Caste Welfare, Gandhinagar, Gujarat. 

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Education & Learning; Sector: Scholarships and student finance

**Where it applies:** Gujarat; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** 1. **For Boys (Std. 1 to 8):** ₹750/- scholarship.1. **For Boys (Std. 9 to 10):** ₹1000/- scholarship.1. **For Girls (Std. 1 to 5):** ₹500/- scholarship.1. **For Girls (Std. 6 to 10):** ₹1000/- scholarship. 

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: Director, Developing Caste Welfare, Gandhinagar, Gujarat; Nodal Department: Social Justice and Empowerment Department.

**References / Annexures:** - **Guidelines**: https://mariyojana.gujarat.gov.in/Schemeatoz.aspx
