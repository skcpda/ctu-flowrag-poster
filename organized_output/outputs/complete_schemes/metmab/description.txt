# Medical Expenses for Treatment of Major Ailments of a Beneficiary

**Short Title:** METMAB

**Tags:** Financial Assistance, Medical Treatment, Construction Worker, Disease

The Manipur Building and Other Construction Workers&#39; Welfare Board launched a scheme &quot;Medical Expenses for Treatment of Major Ailments of a Beneficiary&quot;. The scheme aims to provide financial support for the treatment of serious illnesses such as Tuberculosis, Cancer, Heart Disease, Kidney Disease, and more, for building workers and their family members.  ***Major Ailment Cover:*** - Tuberculosis (T.B.) - Cancer. - Heart Disease. - Kidney Disease. - Eye Disease. - Leprosy. - HIV Disease. - Nerve Disease.

**Who is this for:** Individual, Family; Category: Health & Wellness; Sector: Disease and conditions

**Where it applies:** Manipur; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** ***Financial Assistance:*** - ₹2,000/- per year for minor treatments. - Up to ₹45,000/- (each operation) for major surgeries.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Labour and Employment Department.
