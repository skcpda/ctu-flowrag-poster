# Funeral Assistance- Haryana Silicosis Board

**Short Title:** FA-HSB

**Tags:** Funeral, Silicosis, Worker, Financial Assistance, Death

The scheme “Funeral Assistance” is implemented by the Haryana Labour Welfare Board, Government of Haryana. Under this scheme, financial assistance of ₹15,000/- is given to the dependent/nominee of a deceased worker, who was confirmed to have Silicosis by the Silicosis Diagnosis Board, Haryana, for performing the funerary rituals.

**Who is this for:** Family; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Financial assistance

**Where it applies:** Haryana; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** 1. Under this scheme, financial assistance of ₹15,000/- is given to the dependent/nominee of the deceased worker for performing the funerary rituals.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: Haryana Labour Welfare Board, Government Of Haryana; Nodal Department: Labour Department.
