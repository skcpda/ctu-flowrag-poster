# Chanakya Swayam Upadhi Scheme for Micro Entrepreneurship - Transport Operator

**Short Title:** CSUSME-TO

**Tags:** Financial Assistance, Employment, Brahmin

Andhra Pradesh Brahmin Welfare Corporation (APBWC), a Government of Andhra Pradesh undertaking, is introducing the “Chanakya Swayam Upadhi Scheme for Micro Entrepreneurship - Transport Operator” a subsidy scheme for unemployed youth for purchasing Four- wheeler (self-Driven) for public transport purposes with the collaboration of AP Brahmin Co-operative Credit Society Ltd., Regd No. ABC-CS/006/2015, Vijayawada.  **Objective:** To provide subsidy for purchase of Four-wheelers to the unemployed Brahmin youth for their livelihood. This scheme is applicable for economically weak Brahmins of Andhra Pradesh, who want to settle as transport operators in Andhra Pradesh.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Skills & Employment; Sector: Financial assistance

**Where it applies:** Andhra Pradesh; State/ UT
