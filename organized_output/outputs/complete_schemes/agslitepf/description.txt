# Aatmanirbhar Gujarat Scheme for assistance to Large Industries and Thrust Sector: EPF Reimbursement

**Short Title:** AGSLITEPF

**Launch Date:** 2022-10-05

**Tags:** Business, Entrepreneurship, Large Industries, Thrust Sector, EPF Reimbursement

The &amp;quot;Aatmanirbhar Gujarat Scheme for assistance to Large Industries and Thrust Sector&amp;quot; is an umbrella scheme launched by the Industries and Mines Department, Gujarat. It aims to empower the state&amp;#39;s dynamic large-industry sector, which significantly contributes to employment, GDP, and exports. Effective from October 5, 2022, to October 4, 2027, this initiative supports growth, investments, and the vision of &amp;quot;Aatmanirbhar Gujarat&amp;quot; and &amp;quot;Aatmanirbhar Bharat.&amp;quot; The component &amp;quot;EPF Reimbursement&amp;quot; offers financial assistance to eligible large industrial undertakings in Gujarat for the employer’s contribution to the Employees&amp;#39; Provident Fund (EPF) for new employees. This assistance is available for a maximum period of 10 years from the date of commencement of commercial production.

**Who is this for:** Business Entity, Industries; Category: Business & Entrepreneurship; Sector: Setting up / start-up / entrepreneurship

**Where it applies:** Gujarat; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - **Full reimbursement of employer’s contribution:** 100% of the employer’s statutory EPF contribution will be reimbursed for eligible new employees. - **Financial ceiling per employee:** The reimbursement amount is limited to 12% of the employee’s basic salary plus Dearness Allowance (DA) and retaining allowance, or ₹1800/- per month, whichever is lower. - **Long-term support:** The reimbursement is available for a period of up to 10 years from the date of commencement of commercial production.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Exclusions / Not eligible:** - An eligible industrial undertaking that has availed EPF reimbursement under any scheme of the central / state government, or any agency of the central/ state government, shall not be eligible for EPF incentive under this scheme for that period.

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Industries and Mines Department.
