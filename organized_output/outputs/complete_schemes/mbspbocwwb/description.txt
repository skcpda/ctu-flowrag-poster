# Maternity Benefit Scheme (P.B.O.C.W.W.B)

**Short Title:** MBSPBOCWWB

**Tags:** Labour, Building Worker, Construction Worker, Women, Maternity Benefit, Pregnancy

The &amp;quot;Maternity Benefit Scheme&amp;#39;&amp;#39; by the BOCW Welfare Board, Department of Labour, Punjab, is a welfare scheme for the registered workers of the board. Under this scheme, the Board shall provide financial assistance to construction workers to support childbirth-related expenses for upto two living children of the registered female construction workers and spouse of the male workers. &lt;br&gt;

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Health & Wellness; Sector: Pregnancy care

**Where it applies:** Punjab; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** **Female Construction Workers:** ₹21,000/- per child, maximum of two children. **Male Construction Workers:** ₹5,000/- per child, maximum of two children (for children of their spouse). 

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Department of Labour.
