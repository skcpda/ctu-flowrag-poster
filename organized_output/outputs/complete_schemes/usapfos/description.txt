# Pension Scheme for State Agitators Other Than Category of Agitators Who Went to Jail for 7 Days or got Injured during the Uttarakhand State Movement

**Short Title:** PSSAOTCAJ7DIUSM

**Tags:** Pension, Agitator, Uttarakhand State Movement

The &amp;quot;Pension Scheme for State Agitators Other Than Category of Agitators Who went to Jail for 7 Days or got Injured during the Uttarakhand State Movement&amp;quot; scheme is implemented by the Police Department under the Department of Home, Government of Uttarakhand. The scheme aims to provide a monthly pension to state agitators who do not fall under the category of those imprisoned for 7 days or injured during the Uttarakhand State Movement, and who are not entitled to an agitational pension or any other government pension, nor are employed in government service. 

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Pension

**Where it applies:** Uttarakhand; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** **Pension Amount:** ₹4,500/- per month 

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: Police Department, Government Of Uttarakhand; Nodal Department: Department of Home.

**References / Annexures:** - **Guidelines (Page No. 64)**: https://uk.gov.in/department92/library_file/file-04-12-2023-06-02-23.pdf

**Additional Information:**
Briefdescription: The scheme provides a monthly pension of ₹4,500/- to state agitators not imprisoned for 7 days or injured during the Uttarakhand State Movement, who are not eligible for any other government pension and are not employed in government service.
