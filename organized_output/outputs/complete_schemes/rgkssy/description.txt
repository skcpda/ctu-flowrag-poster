# Rajiv Gandhi Krishak Sathi Sahayta Yojana

**Short Title:** RGKSSY

**Launch Date:** 1994-08-30

**Tags:** Farmer, Accident, Financial Support

The Rajasthan government&#39;s Rajiv Gandhi Krishak Sathi Sahayta Yojana is a critical safety net for those who keep our food system running smoothly. Farmers, farmworkers, and market staff face inherent risks in their daily activities. This program acknowledges those risks and provides much-needed financial assistance in times of crisis. If an accident occurs while they&#39;re tending to crops, raising livestock, or selling their produce at market, the Yojana can help soften the financial blow. This can mean the difference between a family being able to afford medical care and essential needs, or facing devastating hardship.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Agriculture,Rural & Environment; Sector: Financial assistance

**Where it applies:** Rajasthan; State/ UT

**DBT Scheme:** Yes (Direct Benefit Transfer)
