# Maulana Azad Education Finance Foundation Scheme

**Short Title:** MAEFFS

**Tags:** Student, Higher Education, Overseas Education, Loan, Maulana Azad, Minority Community

The scheme “Maulana Azad Education Finance Foundation Scheme” was launched by the Minority Welfare Department, Government of Uttarakhand. The scheme aims to provide interest-free loans to students from minority communities for pursuing higher education in India and abroad. 

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Education & Learning; Sector: Universities and higher education

**Where it applies:** Uttarakhand; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** Under this scheme, students from minority communities are provided interest-free loans of up to ₹5 lakh for pursuing higher education in India and abroad. 

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Minority Welfare, Uttarakhand.
