# Funeral Assistance Scheme (PULWS)

**Short Title:** FAS-PULWS

**Tags:** Death, Crisis, Funeral, Financial Assistance, Worker, Social Welfare

The &quot;Funeral Assistance Scheme (PULWS)&quot; is a Welfare Scheme for the Unorganized Workers in Puducherry by the &quot;Puducherry Unorganised Labourers&#39; Welfare Society&quot;, Labour Department, Union Territory of Puducherry. The Society extends ₹7,000/- to the nominee of the deceased Unorganised Worker towards meeting the funeral expenses. The amount is credited through Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) into the bank account of the nominee. The applications are accepted offline.

**Who is this for:** Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Crisis/Disaster/Accident

**Where it applies:** Puducherry; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - Financial Assistance of ₹7,000/- to the nominee of the deceased member towards funeral expenses. - The amount is credited through Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) into the bank account of the nominee.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Labour Department, Puducherry.

**References / Annexures:** - **Guidelines (Page No. 100)**: https://labour.py.gov.in/sites/default/files/citizens-charter-2020.pdf
