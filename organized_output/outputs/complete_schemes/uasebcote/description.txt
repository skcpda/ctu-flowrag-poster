# Uniform Assistance for Socially and Educationally Backward Class Students Studying Standard 1st to 8th

**Short Title:** UASEBCOTE

**Tags:** Uniform Assistance, Education, Socially And Educationally Backward Class

The scheme &amp;quot;Uniform Assistance for Socially and Educationally Backward Class Students Studying Standard 1st to 8th&amp;quot; by the Social Justice and Empowerment Department, Government of Gujarat, aims to promote education and reduce the economic burden of students from the Socially and Educationally Backward Class by providing them with uniform assistance. The scheme ensures that eligible students from classes 1 to 8 studying in government and aided primary schools receive ₹900/- to cover the cost of three sets of school uniforms.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Education & Learning; Sector: Resources for schools

**Where it applies:** Gujarat; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - **Financial Assistance:** ₹900/- is provided to each eligible student for the purchase of three pairs of school uniforms.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Social Justice and Empowerment Department.
