# Theka Shramik, Gharelu Mahila Kamgar Evan Hamal Shramik Prasuti Sahayta Yojana

**Short Title:** GMKHSPS

**Launch Date:** 2015-03-11

**Tags:** Health, Financial Assistance, Pregnancy Care

Labor Department of Chhattisgarh State started this scheme &amp;amp;quot;Theka Shramik, Gharelu Mahila Kamgar Evan Hamal Shramik Prasuti Sahayta Yojana&amp;amp;quot; for unorganized worker on 05 June 2018. The main objective of the scheme to provide pregnancy care.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Health & Wellness; Sector: Pregnancy care

**Where it applies:** Chhattisgarh; State

**Benefits / Assistance:** - Female employees will receive a financial assistance of ₹4,200 in the third month of pregnancy and ₹2,800 in the eighth month of pregnancy as maternity benefit. - Male employees will receive a financial assistance of ₹3,000 as paternity leave after the birth of the child.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Labour Department.
