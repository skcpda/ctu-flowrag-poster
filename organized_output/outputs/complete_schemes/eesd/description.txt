# Endeavour To Empower-Skill Development

**Short Title:** EESD

**Tags:** Training, Education, Learn Skills

Mission Youth is mandated to enhance the employability of youth in emerging market Job Markets through targeted market-driven skilling programmes. The objective of the scheme is to sponsor market-driven skill development training for the youth of J&amp;amp;K to facilitate 1. Enhancement of Employability &amp;amp; Marketability of Youth in high potential / emerging Job Markets. 1. Establishment of Independent Livelihood avenues for youth in various economic sectors. 1. Development of a resource pool of skilled youth that can be used for the positive economic transformation of J&amp;amp;K. 1. Promotion of the concept of &amp;quot;Be Vocal for Local&amp;quot;. &lt;br&gt; **Mode of Training** Mission Youth, under this programme, envisions partnering with all associated Government Departments/Agencies to bring moresynergy and efficacy in skilling efforts of the Government. The private market players shall also be endeavored to partner with Mission Youth to complement the Government efforts towardsyouth empowerment and employability. The mode of skill development trainings shall be collaborative and all-inclusive in nature, involving all market players from the Public and Private sectors. &lt;br&gt; **Training Partners** The following types of organizations/Institutions will be eligible to partner with Mission Youth, J&amp;amp;amp;K and receive financial backing under the initiative: 1. All organizations/Institutes in the Government sector engaged in skill development training, including J&amp;amp;amp;K Skill Development Mission, J&amp;amp;amp;K EDI, KVIB, and any other such organizations/Institutes. 1. Organizations/Institutes in Private Sector engaged in skill development training; 1. Trusts, Companies, Partnership Firms, or Societies registered under the Societies Registration Act, of 1860 and engaged in skill development training. 1. Any other organization/agency duly registered under relevant statutes.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Business & Entrepreneurship; Sector: Setting up / start-up / entrepreneurship

**Where it applies:** Jammu and Kashmir; State

**Benefits / Assistance:** - The trainings are given in focus sectors at Tourism, IT, handicrafts, Retail, Agriculture, construction, and other focus sectors of Jammu &amp;amp; Kashmir.

**Benefit Type:** In Kind

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Department of Labour and Employment.
