# Procedure for Admission in Government Child Care Institution, Government Communication Home and Open Shelter Home

**Short Title:** PAGCCIGCHOSH

**Tags:** Child Care Institution, Government Communication Home, Open Shelter Home, Orphan Children, Destitute Children, Abandoned Children

The scheme “Procedure for Admission in Government Child Care Institution, Government Communication Home, and Open Shelter Home” is implemented by the Women Welfare Department, Government of Uttarakhand. The scheme aims to support orphaned, destitute, abandoned, neglected, and vulnerable children by providing them with clothing, food, accommodation, medical care, counselling, education, training, and other essential facilities. 

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Housing & Shelter; Sector: Shelter, integrated support and assistance

**Where it applies:** Uttarakhand; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** In the institutions and homes, children are provided with free facilities such as clothing, food, accommodation, medical care, counselling, entertainment, training, and education. Along with these facilities, children are also provided day care facility based on their needs. 

**Benefit Type:** In Kind

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: Women Welfare Department, Government Of Uttarakhand; Nodal Department: Department Of Woman Empowerment & Child Development.

**References / Annexures:** - **Guidelines (Page No. 39)**: https://uk.gov.in/department92/library_file/file-04-12-2023-06-02-23.pdf
