# Aaloo Ke Kufri Chipsona-1 Prabhed Ke Kshetra Vistaar Yojana

**Short Title:** AKCKVY

**Tags:** Agriculture, Subsidy, Plant Supply, Financial Assistance, Potato, Kufri Chipsona Varient

“Aaloo Ke Kufri Chipsona-1 Prabed Ke Kshetra Vistaar Yojana” launched by the Department of Agriculture, Bihar, aims to increase the production of Kufri Chipsona variety suitable for potato processing in Bihar by expanding its area and to increase the income of farmers by promoting processing. Under this scheme, certified quality seeds of Kufri Chipsona variety suitable for potato processing will be made available to farmers. &lt;br&gt;

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Agriculture,Rural & Environment; Sector: Agricultural Inputs- seeds, fertilizer etc.

**Where it applies:** Bihar; State/ UT

**DBT Scheme:** Yes (Direct Benefit Transfer)

**Benefits / Assistance:** - **Affordable High-Quality Seeds:** Farmers will receive Kufri Chipsona-I potato seeds at an estimated price of ₹4,000/- per quintal or the actual seed price, whichever is lower. - **Financial Assistance:** Total ₹93,863/- per hectare (First year: ₹70,397/- and Second year: ₹23,466/-). 

**Benefit Type:** Composite

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Agriculture Department.

**References / Annexures:** - **Guidelines**: https://horticulture.bihar.gov.in/MainSite/Documents/SOP/1.7_2024-09-25.pdf - **Online Application**: https://horticulture.bihar.gov.in/HortMIS/AreaExpansionStateScheme/OnlineAppPotatoKufriChipsona.aspx
