# Aatmanirbhar Gujarat Scheme for assistance to Large Industries and Thrust Sector: Assistance of Interest Subsidy

**Short Title:** AGSLITSIS

**Launch Date:** 2022-10-05

**Tags:** Business, Entrepreneurship, Large Industries, Thrust Sector, Interest Subsidy

The &amp;quot;Aatmanirbhar Gujarat Scheme for assistance to Large Industries and Thrust Sector&amp;quot; is an umbrella scheme launched by the Industries and Mines Department, Gujarat. It aims to empower the state&amp;#39;s dynamic large-industry sector, which significantly contributes to employment, GDP, and exports. Effective from October 5, 2022, to October 4, 2027, this initiative supports growth, investments, and the vision of &amp;quot;Aatmanirbhar Gujarat&amp;quot; and &amp;quot;Aatmanirbhar Bharat.&amp;quot; The component &amp;quot;Assistance of Interest Subsidy&amp;quot; provides financial support to large industrial undertakings by reimbursing a portion of the interest paid on term loans. This subsidy encourages industrial growth, expansion, and diversification by reducing borrowing costs for eligible projects.

**Who is this for:** Industries, Business Entity; Category: Business & Entrepreneurship; Sector: Setting up / start-up / entrepreneurship

**Where it applies:** Gujarat; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:**  **Category of Taluka****General****Thrust**Category 1Interest subsidy @ 7% on Term Loan for 10 years upto 1% of eFCI p.a.Interest subsidy @ 7% on Term Loan for 10 years upto 1.2% of eFCI p.a.Category 2Interest subsidy @ 7% on Term Loan for 8 years upto 1% of eFCI p.a.Interest subsidy @ 7% on Term Loan for 10 years upto 1% of eFCI p.a.Category 3Interest subsidy @ 7% on Term Loan for 6 years upto 1% of eFCI p.a.Interest subsidy @ 7% on Term Loan for 8 years upto 1% of eFCI p.a. **Note**: [List of Taluka categories.](https://imd.gujarat.gov.in/Document/I%20Branch_2297_02_Nov_2020_98.pdf)

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Exclusions / Not eligible:** - A large industrial undertaking that has received any incentive for the same Gross Fixed Capital Investment (eFCI) under any state government scheme or its agency will not be eligible unless specifically stated.

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Industries and Mines Department.
