# Jharkhand Sportsperson Pension Scheme

**Short Title:** JSPS

**Tags:** Financial Assistance, Sportsperson, Pension

The “Jharkhand Sportsperson Pension Scheme” was launched by the Department of Sports &amp; Youth Affairs, Government of Jharkhand. aims to provide financial support to retired athletes for their lifelong contribution to sports. Athletes, including Arjuna, Dronacharya, and Dhyan Chand awardees, Olympic participants, and Commonwealth or Asian Games medalists, receive a monthly pension.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Sports & Culture; Sector: Sports

**Where it applies:** Jharkhand; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - Athletes receive a lifetime monthly pension of ₹10,000/-. - Dependents of deceased athletes (spouse and minor children) receive ₹5,000/- monthly.  **NOTE:** Pension payments are directly transferred to the beneficiary&#39;s bank account.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Department of Sports & Youth Affairs.
