# Minimata Mahtari Jatan Yojana

**Short Title:** BPSY

**Launch Date:** 2010-03-04

**Tags:** Financial Assistance, Pregnancy Care, Women Wellness, Child Care, Construction Workers

The Labor Department of Chhattisgarh introduced the &quot;Minimata Mahtari Jatan Yojana&quot; on March 4, 2010, through the &quot;Building and Construction Workers Welfare Board.&quot; This initiative is designed to provide benefits to registered female construction workers during pregnancy. The scheme applies to both male and female beneficiaries who are registered and possess a beneficiary identity card at least 90 days before the scheme application date.  **Note:** The name of this scheme was changed from &quot;Bhagini Prasuti Sahayata Yojana&quot; to &quot;Minimata Mahtari Jatan Yojana&quot; on May 12, 2022. 

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Health & Wellness; Sector: Pregnancy care

**Where it applies:** Chhattisgarh; State

**DBT Scheme:** Yes (Direct Benefit Transfer)

**Benefits / Assistance:** > A lump sum amount of ₹20,000/- will be provided to the registered female construction worker after the birth of the child.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Exclusions / Not eligible:** - Spouses of construction workers employed by public or government institutions. - Construction worker beneficiaries who lack valid membership with the Board.

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: Building And Construction Workers Welfare Board; Nodal Department: Labour Department.
