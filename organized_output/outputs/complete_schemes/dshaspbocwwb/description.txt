# Denture, Spectacles & Hearing Aid Scheme (P.B.O.C.W.W.B)

**Short Title:** DSHASPBOCWWB

**Tags:** Labour, Building Worker, Construction Worker, Denture, Spectacles, Hearing Aid

The &amp;quot;Denture, Spectacles &amp;amp; Hearing Aid Scheme&amp;#39;&amp;#39; by the BOCW Welfare Board, Department of Labour, Punjab, is a welfare scheme for the registered workers of the board. Under this scheme, the Board will provide financial aid to the registered beneficiary Construction Workers and their family members for spectacles, dentures and hearing aids.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Health & Wellness; Sector: Financial assistance

**Where it applies:** Punjab; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - **Spectacle:** ₹.800/-.- **Denture:** ₹5,000/-.- **Hearing Aid:** ₹6,000/-. &gt; **Note:** - Financial assistance under this scheme shall be granted on the recommendation of Govt. medical officer/Incharge of the E.S.I Dispensary. - Under this scheme, the beneficiary or his family members shall get benefit only once. - The beneficiary shall get the amount of actual expenditure or amount of financial assistance, whichever is less, on the basis of actual bills produced by him.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Exclusions / Not eligible:** - If the beneficiary or his family member has obtained financial assistance for the purchase of the mentioned articles from any other department, then he shall not be given benefit under this scheme.

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Department of Labour.
