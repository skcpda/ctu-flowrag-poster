# Special Incentive Assistance to Socially and Educationally Backward Class Trainees for Pre-preparation of Competitive Examination

**Short Title:** SIASEBCTPCE

**Tags:** Financial Assistance, Competitive Exam, Student, SSC, GPSC

The scheme “Special Incentive Assistance to Socially and Educationally Backward Class Trainees for Pre-Preparation of Competitive Examination” was launched by the Social Justice and Empowerment Department, Government of Gujarat. The scheme aims to encourage SEBC candidates to pursue coaching for government competitive exams by providing financial support. Under this scheme, eligible trainees receive up to ₹20,000 or the actual coaching fee paid (whichever is lower) for preparing for Class 1, 2, or 3 government job exams conducted by state or central bodies. 

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Education & Learning; Sector: Universities and higher education

**Where it applies:** Gujarat; State/ UT

**DBT Scheme:** Yes (Direct Benefit Transfer)

**Benefits / Assistance:** **Financial Assistance:** - ₹20,000/- or actual fee paid (whichever is lower) for coaching related to any one exam. **Exams Include:** - State-Level: Gujarat Public Service Commission (GPSC), Gujarat Secondary Service Selection Board (GSSSB), (3) State Police Recruitment, Gujarat Panchayat Service Selection Board (GPSB), etc. - Central-Level: Banking, Railways, Army Recruitment, Central Police Force (CRPF, BSF, CISF etc), Staff Selection Commission (SSC). **Applicable for Exams:** - Class 1, 2, and 3 level government posts. **Mode of Disbursement:** - Amount is credited via Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) to the trainee&#39;s bank account. 

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Social Justice and Empowerment Department.

**References / Annexures:** - **Guidelines**: https://mariyojana.gujarat.gov.in/MoreDetails.aspx
