# Madhya Pradesh Professional Talent Examination Award Scheme

**Short Title:** MPPTEAS

**Tags:** Examination, Award, Backward Class, Student, Education

Launched in 2010, the &quot;M.P. Professional Talent Test Award Scheme&quot; by the Backward Classes and Minorities Welfare Department, Government of Madhya Pradesh aims to recognize and reward backward-class students who excel in professional entrance examinations. Through this scheme, monetary awards are provided to backward-class students who secure the top three positions in entrance examinations like PET, PPT, and MCA conducted by the Professional Examination Board. The scheme is implemented by the Assistant Director, Backward Classes and Minorities Welfare.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Education & Learning; Sector: Scholarships and student finance

**Where it applies:** Madhya Pradesh; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** **Prize Money** - First Position: ₹1,00,000/- - Second Position: ₹50,000/- - Third Position: ₹25,000/-

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Backward Classes and Minorities Welfare Department.
