# National Family Benefit Scheme (Punjab)

**Short Title:** NFBSP

**Tags:** Pension, Family, BPL, Death, Financial Assistance, Social Welfare

The scheme &quot;National Family Benefit Scheme (Punjab)&quot; is a social welfare initiative by the Department of Social Security and Development of Women and Children, Government of Punjab. Under this scheme, a lump sum assistance of ₹20,000/- is provided to the bereaved household in the event of the death of their breadwinner.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Financial assistance

**Where it applies:** Punjab; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** Financial assistance of ₹20,000/-.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Department of Social Security and Development of Women and Children.
