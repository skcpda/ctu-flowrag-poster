# Mukhya Mantrir Nijut Moina Aasoni

**Short Title:** MMNM

**Launch Date:** 2024-08-07

**Tags:** Girl, Student, Education, Graduation, Female, Incentive, Scholarship

Launched in August 2024, the scheme &amp;quot;Mukhya Mantrir Nijut Moina Aasoni&amp;quot; by the Higher Education Department, Government of Assam, aims to support ten lakh girl students with financial grants as admission incentives to continue their education in Higher Secondary, Graduation, and Post-Graduation studies, serving as a crucial step towards eradicating child marriage and its associated harmful practices. The scheme provides monthly financial assistance of ₹1,000 for Class 11-12 students, ₹1,250 for graduation students, and ₹2,500 for post-graduation students for a maximum of ten months per year, and ensures regular attendance and discipline among beneficiaries through institutional monitoring. The applicant must be an unmarried girl student domiciled in Assam enrolled in government and venture educational institutions (excluding private institutions) and must maintain 75% attendance in the previous year for existing beneficiaries. The scheme is implemented by the Higher Education Department, Government of Assam through a portal-based system where applications are accepted offline at respective educational institutions and verified online through institutional portals. 

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Education & Learning; Sector: Scholarships and student finance

**Where it applies:** Assam; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** > **Financial Assistance** - Higher Secondary (Class 11-12): ₹1,000 per month for maximum 10 months totaling ₹10,000 per year. - Graduation (Bachelor of Arts/Bachelor of Science/Bachelor of Commerce): ₹1,250 per month for maximum 10 months totaling ₹12,500 per year. - Post-Graduation (Master of Arts/Master of Science/Master of Commerce/Bachelor of Education): ₹2,500 per month for maximum 10 months totaling ₹25,000 per year.  > **Disbursement Details** - **Mode:** Direct Benefit Transfer through bank accounts on monthly basis.- **Frequency:** Monthly for maximum 10 months per academic year.- **Validity:** No incentive during summer vacation or vacations extending more than one month.- **Exclusion Period:** Scheme not effective during June and July every year for academic session 2025-26.  > **Conditions for Availment/Continuation** - Regular class attendance required (75% attendance for existing beneficiaries). - Adherence to institutional discipline and rules and regulations. - No engagement in disruptive behavior, cheating, ragging, vandalism, or misconduct. - Students must not drop out of courses during benefit period. - No arrear amounts will be provided for missed periods. - Violation of rules results in withdrawal of benefits under the scheme.  > **Exclusions from Other Benefits** - Students receiving scooter under Banikanta Kakati Award (Pragyan Bharati Scheme) are not eligible unless they opt out of scooter benefit. - Higher Education Department&#39;s Mobility grant scheme has been merged with this scheme. 

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Exclusions / Not eligible:** - Married girls will not be eligible for the scheme except those in the P.G. and B. Ed. category. - In-service girls, admitted to B.Ed. course on deputation, will not be eligible for the scheme. - Daughters of Ministers/MPs and MLAs are not eligible for the Nijut Moina scheme. 

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: Higher Education Department; Nodal Department: Higher Education Department.
