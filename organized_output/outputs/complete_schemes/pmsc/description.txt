# Post Matric Scholarship for Class 11th 12th And College

**Short Title:** PMSC

**Tags:** Scholarship, Scheduled Tribe, Education, Student

The scheme &quot;Post-Matric Scholarship&quot; started in 1945 by the Tribal Welfare and Scheduled Caste (SC) Department, Madhya Pradesh, provides scholarships to Scheduled Tribe students studying in classes 11th, 12th, Colleges, PhD, and Professional Courses.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Education & Learning; Sector: Scholarships and student finance

**Where it applies:** Madhya Pradesh; State

**Benefits / Assistance:** This scholarship is provided to Scheduled Tribe students.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Tribal Welfare and Scheduled Caste (SC) Department.
