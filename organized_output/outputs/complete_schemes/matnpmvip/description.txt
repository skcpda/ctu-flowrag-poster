# Marriage Assistance to Normal Persons Marrying Orthopedically Differently Abled Persons

**Short Title:** MATNPMODAP

**Tags:** Financial Assistance, Marriage, Differently-abled Person, Gold, Orthopedically

The scheme &#39;Marriage Assistance to Normal Persons Marrying Orthopedically Differently Abled Persons&#39; was launched by the Department for the Welfare of Differently Abled Persons, Government of Tamil Nadu, with the noble objective of supporting and facilitating marriages between normal individuals and orthopedically differently abled persons. This initiative recognizes the importance of inclusivity and equality in relationships, aiming to reduce social barriers and promote harmony within society. By providing financial assistance to normal individuals marrying orthopedically differently abled persons, the scheme seeks to alleviate some of the financial burdens that may arise in such unions, ensuring that all individuals, regardless of physical ability, have the opportunity to experience the joys of marriage.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Financial assistance

**Where it applies:** Tamil Nadu; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** 1. Marriage assistance amount: ₹25,000/- as - ₹12,500/- National Savings Certificate - ₹12,500/- cheque/Demand Draft for marriage expenses 1. Additional benefit if either spouse has a degree/diploma: Increased amount to ₹50,000/- - ₹25,000/- National Savings Certificate - ₹25,000/- cheque/Demand Draft 1. Other Benefits: - Certificate of appreciation - 8 gram gold coin for Tirumangalyam (a traditional South Indian symbol of marriage for women)

**Benefit Type:** Composite

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Welfare of Differently Abled Persons Department.
