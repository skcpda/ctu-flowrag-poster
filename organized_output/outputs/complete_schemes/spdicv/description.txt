# Scheme of Production of Domestic Indigenous Certified Seed of Cotton Varieties

**Short Title:** SPDICV

**Tags:** Agriculture, Cotton Seed, Subsidy, Farmer

The &amp;quot;Scheme of Production of Domestic Indigenous Certified Seed of Cotton Varieties&amp;quot; introduced by the Agriculture, Farmers Welfare and Cooperation Department, Gujarat, aims to promote the cultivation and production of local cotton seed varieties across Gujarat. Under this scheme, eligible farmers receive financial assistance of ₹750/- per quintal for certified seeds, as approved by the Gujarat State Seed Certification Agency.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Agriculture,Rural & Environment; Sector: Agricultural Inputs- seeds, fertilizer etc.

**Where it applies:** Gujarat; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - An amount of 750/- per quintal (for foundation and certified seeds certified by the Gujarat State Seed Certification Agency).

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Agriculture, Farmers Welfare and Cooperation Department.
