# All Goa One-Act Play Competition in Konkani

**Short Title:** AGOAPCK

**Tags:** Culture, Artist, Competition, Drama, Award

The scheme &quot;All Goa One-Act Play Competition in Konkani&quot; was launched by the Tiatr Academy under the Art, Culture and Museums Department, Government of Goa. The scheme aims to attract youth towards dramatic activities and provide ample opportunities to showcase their talents in dramatics while promoting Goan culture through Konkani theatre. The scheme provides cash awards for the top 3 performances along with mementos and certificates, individual awards for various categories like direction, acting, music, and stage setting, and reimbursement of transport costs as per Regional Transport Office norms. The applicant must be an individual, club, or association capable of presenting a One-Act Play in Konkani with duration between 35 to 45 minutes, and must submit a typed copy of the play in Roman script along with written permission from the playwright. The scheme is implemented by Tiatr Academy of Goa. The applications for this scheme are accepted offline. 

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Sports & Culture; Sector: Culture

**Where it applies:** Goa; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - Cash awards for 1st, 2nd, and 3rd best performances (amount as decided by Tiatr Academy of Goa). - Mementos and certificates for top 3 performances. - Individual awards with mementos (no cash) for Direction, Male Actor, Female Actor, Background Music, Stage Setting, Light Effects, Make-up, and Script. - Reimbursement of transport expenses as per Regional Transport Office norms. - Video recording of all plays for preservation in Tiatr Academy of Goa Library. _*Performance must not contain adverse remarks against any governments, individuals, institutions, or anything against caste, creed, and religion._ _*Participants must abide by all competition rules and regulations._ _*Any violation may result in disqualification and forfeiture of benefits._ 

**Benefit Type:** Composite

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Art, Culture and Museums Department.
