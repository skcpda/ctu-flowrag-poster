# Establishment of Broiler Farms

**Short Title:** EBFUK

**Tags:** Broiler Farms, Subsidy, Yard Construction, Broiler Birds, Self Help Groups

The scheme “Establishment of Broiler Farms” was launched by the Department of Animal Husbandry, Government of Uttarakhand, to promote the development of broiler chicken farms across all districts of the state. The primary objective of the scheme is to meet the growing demand for poultry production, generate employment opportunities, and ensure access to protein-rich food for the growing population. To enhance poultry meat production and bring about socio-economic transformation in rural areas, the government aims to implement this scheme using a cluster-based approach. > **Objectives:** 1. To establish broiler farms with a minimum capacity of 500 birds. 1. To generate income levels and provide employment opportunities for small farmers, including women. 1. To increase the state&#39;s poultry meat production. 1. To bring desired social and economic changes in rural areas, which is crucial for rural development and prosperity. 1. To establish broiler farms in a cluster approach, identifying 5-6 villages or areas with high potential to support economies of scale and resource convergence for input supply/procurement and product marketing.

**Who is this for:** Individual, Self Help Groups (SHGS); Category: Agriculture,Rural & Environment; Sector: Animal husbandry

**Where it applies:** Uttarakhand; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** **Subsidy Details:** Each broiler farm (500 birds) will receive a total subsidy of ₹60,000, comprising: - ₹15 per bird for 6 production batches: ₹45,000/- per beneficiary - One-time grant for shed construction: ₹15,000 **Project Cost Breakdown (for 500-bird capacity):** **Capital Costs:** - Shed construction: ₹2,00,000 - Equipment (brooders, feeders, drinkers, etc.): ₹19,600 **Recurring Costs:** - Purchase of 500 DOC (chicks): ₹17,500 - Feed: ₹66,000 - Miscellaneous (medicine, rice husk, etc.): ₹2,000 - Total Project Cost: ₹3,05,100 (Financed via subsidy, bank loan, and self-contribution)

**Benefit Type:** Composite

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: District Level Executive Committee (DLEC), With Assistance From The Veterinary Officer/Livestock Extension Officer And The Chief Veterinary Officer.; Nodal Department: Department Of Animal Husbandry.
