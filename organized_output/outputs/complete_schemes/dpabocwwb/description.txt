# Disability Pension (A.B.O.C.W.W.B)

**Short Title:** DP(ABOCWWB)

**Tags:** Pension, Building Worker, Construction Worker, Financial Assistance, Labour

The scheme “Disability Pension” was started by the Assam Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board (A.B.O.C.W.W.B), Labour Welfare Department, Government of Assam. Under this scheme, the Board provides financial assistance as disability pension to a beneficiary who is permanently disabled due to paralysis leprosy, TB., accident etc. in addition to this pension he/she will be eligible for an one time Ex-gratia payment depending upon the percentage of disability and subject to such conditions as fixed by the Board.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Pension

**Where it applies:** Assam; State/ UT

**DBT Scheme:** Yes (Direct Benefit Transfer)

**Benefits / Assistance:** 1. A monthly disability pension of ₹2,000/- plus ₹100/- per month for every completed year of service from the year of registration will be given to the eligible beneficiary. 1. In addition to this pension, the beneficiary will also be eligible for a one-time Ex-gratia payment of not more than ₹3.00 Lakhs depending upon the percentage of disability and subject to such conditions as fixed by the Board.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: Assam Building And Other Construction Workers Welfare Board; Nodal Department: Labour & Welfare Department.
