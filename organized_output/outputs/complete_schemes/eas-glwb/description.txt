# Educational Award Scheme - Gujarat Labour Welfare Board

**Short Title:** EAS-GLWB

**Tags:** Labour, Student, Education, Award

The “Educational Award Scheme” is implemented by the Gujarat Labour Welfare Board, Labour, Skill Development &amp;amp; Employment Department, Government of Gujarat. The scheme has been implemented to encourage the students of the Labours to increase their enthusiasm and to get higher education through it.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Education & Learning; Sector: Financial assistance

**Where it applies:** Gujarat; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** 1. Under the scheme, children of laborers who have passed 70% or above marks in the 10th Standard will receive ₹2,500/- as an award. 1. The children of laborers who have passed 70% or above marks in the 12th Standard will receive ₹5,000/- as an award.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: Gujarat Labour Welfare Board; Nodal Department: Labour and Employment Department.
