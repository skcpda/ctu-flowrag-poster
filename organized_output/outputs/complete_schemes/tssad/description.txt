# The Scheme Of State Award For Disabled

**Short Title:** TSSAD

**Tags:** Persons With Disability, Employment, Award, Placement

The &quot;The Scheme of State Award for Disabled&quot; is a scheme by the Department of Social Justice &amp; Special Assistance, Govt. of Maharashtra. In this scheme, every year, the State Government felicitates awards to the Best Disabled Employees / Employers / Placement Agencies who have done outstanding work in various fields. Only citizens who are permanent residents of the state of Maharashtra are eligible to apply for this scheme. This scheme is 100% funded by the Govt. of Maharashtra.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Skills & Employment; Sector: Information for employers

**Where it applies:** Maharashtra; State

**Benefits / Assistance:** **Category 1:** Best Employees (Visually Handicapped, Hearing Handicapped, Orthopedically Handicapped, Mentally Retarded) No. of Awards: 12 Composition of Award: ₹10,000/- in Cash, a Citation, and a Certificate. **Category 2:** Best Employer (Govt. / Public / Private Sector) No. of Awards: 2 Composition of Award: ₹25,000/- in Cash, a Citation, and a Certificate.

**Benefit Type:** Composite

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: District Social Welfare Offices Of The Department Of Social Justice & Special Assistance, Govt. Of Maharashtra; Nodal Department: Social Justice and Special Assistance Department.

**References / Annexures:** - **Guidelines**: https://sjsa.maharashtra.gov.in/en/scheme-category/disability-welfare
