# Cycle Sahayata Yojana

**Short Title:** CSY-J

**Tags:** Woman, Construction Worker, Social Welfare

&quot;Cycle Sahayata Yojana&quot; is a scheme by the Department of Labour, Employment, Training &amp; Skill Development Govt of Jharkhand. This is a 100% State Sponsored Scheme. Only the residents/domicile of the state of Jharkhand can apply to this scheme. Applications for this scheme are accepted online through the official website of the department. In this scheme, bicycles are provided to the women workers.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Skills & Employment; Sector: Welfare measures

**Where it applies:** Jharkhand; State

**Benefits / Assistance:** • Bicycles are provided to the women workers.

**Benefit Type:** In Kind

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: Jharkhand Building And Other Construction Worker Welfare Board (JBOCWW Board); Nodal Department: Labour, Employment, Training and Skill Department.
