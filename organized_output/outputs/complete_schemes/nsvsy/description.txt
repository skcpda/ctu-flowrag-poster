# Namo Saraswati Vignan Sadhana Yojana

**Short Title:** NSVSY

**Tags:** Education, Student, Science Stream Scholarship

The scheme &amp;quot;Namo Saraswati Vignan Sadhana Yojana&amp;quot; by the Education Department, Government of Gujarat, aims to create ample opportunities for technologically educated and skilled youth in the state by encouraging students to enter and complete science stream education up to Class 12th. The scheme provides financial assistance of ₹25,000/- over two years to students of Class 11th and 12th to support their education in science and help them complete their board examinations successfully. 

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Education & Learning; Sector: Scholarships and student finance

**Where it applies:** Gujarat; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - **Class 11th Benefit:** ₹10,000/- per annum, paid as a monthly stipend of ₹1,000/- for 10 months. - **Class 12th Benefit:** ₹15,000/- per annum. Students will receive a monthly stipend of ₹1,000/- for 10 months (₹10,000) - **Additional Benefit:** ₹5,000/- after passing the Class 12th board examination. 

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Education Department.

**References / Annexures:** - **Guidelines (Scheme No. 330)**: https://mariyojana.gujarat.gov.in/Schemeatoz.aspx - **Guidelines 2**: https://financedepartment.gujarat.gov.in/Documents/Bud-Eng_1378_2024-2-2_988.pdf
