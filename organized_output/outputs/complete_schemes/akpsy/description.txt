# Asangathit Karmakar Prasuti Sahayata Yojana

**Short Title:** AKPSY

**Launch Date:** 2018-04-10

**Tags:** Financial Assistance, Delivery Assistance, Empowerment

Labor Department of Chhattisgarh State started this scheme &amp;amp;quot;Asangathit Karmakar Prasuti Sahayata Yojana&amp;amp;quot; for unorganized worker on 10 April 2018. The main objective of the scheme to empower unorganized worker by providing financial help for the delivery assistance.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Health & Wellness

**Where it applies:** Chhattisgarh; State

**Benefits / Assistance:** > Financial Assistance of ₹ 20,000/-

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Labour Department.
