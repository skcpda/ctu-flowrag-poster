# Financial Assistance for Construction/ Purchase of Wooden/ FRP Fishing Vessel (Canoe) for Fishermen

**Short Title:** FACPWFRPFVCF

**Tags:** Fisheries, Fisherman, Subsidy, Financial Assistance, Marine

The scheme “Financial Assistance for Construction/ Purchase of Wooden/ FRP Fishing Vessel (Canoe) for Fishermen” is a scheme by the Department of Fisheries, Government of Goa. Through this scheme, 50% of the actual cost of the Wooden/ Fiber-Reinforced Plastic (FRP) Fishing Vessel (Canoe) purchased by the fishermen is covered by the Government of Goa. The applicant will be entitled to a subsidy grant once every four years on one craft per family household.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Agriculture,Rural & Environment; Sector: Fishing and hunting

**Where it applies:** Goa; State

**Benefits / Assistance:** - For General Category, **50% subsidy, limited to ₹ 60,000/-**- For SC/ST Category, **50% subsidy, limited to ₹ 80,000/-** - The applicant will be entitled to a subsidy grant once every four years on one craft per family household.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Fisheries Department.
