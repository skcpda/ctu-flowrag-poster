# Stipend To Students Studying at Govt. Hindi Institute Dimapur

**Short Title:** SSSGHID

**Tags:** Stipend, Student, Scholarship, Hindi

The scheme “Stipend to Students Studying at Govt. Hindi Institute Dimapur” was launched by the Department of School Education, Government of Nagaland, aims to provide a monthly stipend to students studying at the Government Hindi Institute, Dimapur. The stipend, provided at the rate of ₹400/- per month, is funded annually from the allocated fund under Scholarship by the State Government.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Education & Learning; Sector: Scholarships and student finance

**Where it applies:** Nagaland; State

**Benefits / Assistance:** - Students receive a monthly stipend of ₹400/-.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: School Education Department, Nagaland.

**References / Annexures:** - **Guidelines**: https://education.nagaland.gov.in/pre-matric-scholarship/ - **Scheme Details (Page No. 12)**: https://education.nagaland.gov.in/wp-content/uploads/2016/05/Dept-AAR-2021-22-1.pdf

**Additional Information:**
Briefdescription: The scheme aims to provide financial support to students enrolled at the Government Hindi Institute, Dimapur. Under this scheme, a monthly stipend of ₹400/- is given to student every year from the allocated scholarship fund provided by the State Government.
