# Goa State Export Market Development Scheme, 2008

**Short Title:** GSEMDS

**Launch Date:** 2009-01-15

**Closing Date:** 2011-03-31

**Tags:** Export, Market, Exhibition, MSME, Tour

Launched in 2008, the scheme &quot;Goa State Export Market Development Scheme, 2008&quot; by the Directorate of Industries, Trade and Commerce, Government of Goa aimed to encourage Goan industries to improve export markets and support them in establishing their goods firmly in the export market, thereby generating growth and employment. The scheme provided interest-free loans up to ₹5,00,000/- for participating in shows/exhibitions outside India and for market study tours abroad. The scheme was implemented by the Directorate of Industries, Trade and Commerce, Government of Goa. The scheme remained in force until 31st March, 2011.

**Who is this for:** Business Entity; Category: Business & Entrepreneurship; Sector: Setting up / start-up / entrepreneurship

**Where it applies:** Goa; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - Interest-free loan up to ₹5,00,000/- was provided for participating in shows/exhibitions outside India. - Interest-free loan up to ₹5,00,000/- was also provided for market study tours outside India. - The loan was repayable over five years in equal half-yearly installments. _*Delay in payment carried an interest at 14% per annum._

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Commercial Taxes Department.
