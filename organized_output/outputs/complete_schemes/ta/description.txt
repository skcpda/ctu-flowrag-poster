# Tholkappiar Award

**Short Title:** TA

**Tags:** Financial Assistance, Award, Author, Writer

The scheme &amp;quot;Tholkappiar Award&amp;quot; was launched by the Arts and Culture Department, Puducherry. The scheme aims to provide financial assistance of ₹10,000/- to writers and authors for outstanding research publications in Tamil literature who are natives of the Union Territory of Puducherry either by birth or domicile. The application was accepted offline.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Sports & Culture; Sector: Culture

**Where it applies:** Puducherry; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - Financial Assistance of ₹10,000/-. with an award and certificate.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Arts and Culture Department, Puducherry.

**References / Annexures:** - **Citizens Charter**: https://art.py.gov.in//sites/default/files/ecit1.pdf - **Official Website**: https://art.py.gov.in/ - **Application Form**: https://art.py.gov.in/sites/default/files/tholkappier2354.pdf
