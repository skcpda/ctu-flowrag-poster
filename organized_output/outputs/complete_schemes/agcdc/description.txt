# All Goa Classical Dance Competition

**Short Title:** AGCDC

**Tags:** Dance, Student, Competition, Art, Culture

The scheme &quot;All Goa Classical Dance Competition&quot; by the Art, Culture and Museums Department, Goa, is open for Bharatnatyam and Kathak disciplines. Only solo dance will be considered for the competition. Costumes and accompanists are to be arranged by the participants at their own cost. Local students in the age group of 12 and 20 years can participate.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Financial assistance

**Where it applies:** Goa; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - 1st Prize: ₹5,000/-, 2nd Prize: ₹3,000/-, 3rd Prize: ₹2,000/-, Five Consolation Prizes: ₹500/- each. - Each participant will receive ₹500/- (lump sum) towards travel expenses, along with one accompanist.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Art, Culture and Museums Department.

**References / Annexures:** - **Guidelines**: https://artandculture.goa.gov.in/sites/default/files/Kala%20Academy%20Goa%20Schemes.pdf
