# Primary Cooperative Agriculture and Rural Development Bank: For Power Tiller

**Short Title:** PCARDBPT

**Tags:** Power Tiller, Loan, Farmer, Agriculture And Rural Development Bank, Farming Equipment

The &amp;quot;Primary Cooperative Agriculture and Rural Development Bank: Power Tiller&amp;quot; scheme launched by the Co-operation, Food and Consumer Protection Department, Tamil Nadu, aims to provide financial assistance to farmers for purchasing a power tiller, with up to 90% of the cost covered through a loan. This initiative aims to support agricultural productivity by making essential farming equipment more accessible. The interest rate on the loan ranges from 11% to 12.25%.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Banking,Financial Services and Insurance; Sector: Loan

**Where it applies:** Tamil Nadu; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - **Access to Loan:** Up to 90% of the cost of a power tiller. - **Interest Rate:** 11% to 12.25% (subject to change). &lt;br&gt;

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Co-operation, Food and Consumer Protection Department.

**References / Annexures:** - **Guidelines**: https://www.tn.gov.in/scheme/data_view/3560

**Additional Information:**
Briefdescription: The scheme offers loans to farmers covering up to 90% of the cost of a power tiller, with an interest rate between 11% and 12.25%.
