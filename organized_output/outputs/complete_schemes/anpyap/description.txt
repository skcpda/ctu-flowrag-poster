# Atma Nirbhar Plantation Yojana

**Short Title:** ANPYAP

**Tags:** Subsidy, Farmer, SHGs, FPO, Loan, Plantation

The scheme &amp;quot;Atma Nirbhar Plantation Yojana (ANPY)&amp;quot; was launched by the Department of Agriculture, Government of Arunachal Pradesh, aims to promote self-reliance in the agriculture sector through sustainable plantation activities across the state&amp;#39;s diverse agro-climatic zones. Under this scheme, support in the form of plantation assistance and allied resources is provided to individual farmers, Self-Help Groups (SHGs), and Farmer Producer Organizations (FPOs).

**Who is this for:** Individual, Self Help Groups (SHGS); Category: Agriculture,Rural & Environment; Sector: Land and water resources

**Where it applies:** Arunachal Pradesh; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - Access to plantation support including technical guidance, planting material, and financial assistance.

**Benefit Type:** Composite

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Agriculture and Marketing Department.
