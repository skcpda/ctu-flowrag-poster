# Aatmanirbhar Gujarat Scheme for Assistance to Mega Industries: Reimbursement of Stamp Duty and Registration Fees

**Short Title:** AGSMRSD

**Launch Date:** 2022-10-05

**Tags:** Business, Entrepreneurship, Mega Industries, Thrust Manufacturing Sector

The &amp;amp;quot;Aatmanirbhar Gujarat Scheme for Assistance to Mega Industries&amp;amp;quot; is an umbrella scheme launched by the Industries and Mines Department, Gujarat. It aims to empower the state&amp;amp;#39;s dynamic mega-industry sector, which significantly contributes to employment, GDP, and exports. Effective from October 5, 2022, to October 4, 2032, this initiative supports growth, investments, and the vision of &amp;amp;quot;Aatmanirbhar Gujarat&amp;amp;quot; and &amp;amp;quot;Aatmanirbhar Bharat.&amp;amp;quot; The component &amp;amp;quot;Reimbursement of Stamp Duty and Registration Fees&amp;amp;quot; offers reimbursement of stamp duty and registration fees to eligible Mega Industrial Units in Gujarat for land purchases or leases related to their projects. It aims to encourage industrial development and ease the financial burden of acquiring land for business purposes.

**Who is this for:** Business Entity, Industries; Category: Business & Entrepreneurship; Sector: Setting up / start-up / entrepreneurship

**Where it applies:** Gujarat; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - **Reimbursement:** Eligible units can claim 100% reimbursement of stamp duty and registration charges paid to the Government of Gujarat for the purchase and/or lease of land meant for the project.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Industries and Mines Department.

**Additional Information:**
Briefdescription: The "Aatmanirbhar Gujarat Scheme for Assistance to Mega Industries" aims to strengthen the overall industrial ecosystem in the State and also enable a larger set of manufacturing companies to emulate the adoption of clean energy and make them future-ready to compete in global markets.
