# Girl Education Promotion (Bicycle Scheme)

**Short Title:** GEPBS

**Tags:** Bicycle, Cycle, Girl Student

The scheme &amp;quot;Girl Education Promotion (Bicycle Scheme)&amp;quot; is implemented by the Secondary Education Department under the Department of Education, Government of Uttarakhand. The scheme aims to promote girls’ education in the state by providing financial assistance to Class 9 girl students for purchasing bicycles. 

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Women and Child

**Where it applies:** Uttarakhand; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - Each eligible girl student in class 9 receives ₹2,850/-. - The benefit includes a bicycle for plain areas and an FD (Fixed Deposit) worth ₹2,850/- for hilly areas. 

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: Secondary Education Department, Government Of Uttarakhand; Nodal Department: Department Of Education.

**References / Annexures:** - **Guidelines (Page No. 75)**: https://uk.gov.in/department92/library_file/file-04-12-2023-06-02-23.pdf

**Additional Information:**
Briefdescription: The scheme aims to promote girls' education in the state by providing financial assistance for the purchase of bicycles to girl students studying in Class 9 in government and government-aided secondary schools.
