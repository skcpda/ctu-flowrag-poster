# Tripura Scheme For Incentive To Girl Child

**Short Title:** TSIGC

**Launch Date:** 2009-06-01

**Tags:** Incentive, Girl Child, Tripura

The scheme &quot;The Tripura Scheme for Incentive to Girl Child-2009&quot; by the Education (SW &amp; SE) Department, Government of Tripura, aims to provide an incentive to girl children born on or after 01/06/2009 to the parents who are cardholders of listed BPL households. The incentive will be provided per head per month up to the month the girl child attains the age of 15 years. The incentive amount will be paid to the mother of the girl child, preferably to the bank/post office account. Up to two girl children in a family will be covered by the scheme. The scheme is implemented by the Child Development Project Officer (CDPO) of the concerned AMC/Nagar Panchayat/RD Block. 

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Financial assistance

**Where it applies:** Tripura; State

**Benefits / Assistance:** > **Rate of Incentive** - At the rate of ₹300/- per head per month up to the month the girl child attains the age of 15 years. The incentive amount will be paid to the mother of the girl child, preferably to the bank/post office account. 

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: Child Development Project Officer (CDPO); Nodal Department: Directorate Of Social Welfare & Social Education.

**Additional Information:**
Briefdescription: The scheme aims to provide an incentive to girl children. Through this scheme, an incentive is offered to the mother of the girl child or her legal guardian on a monthly basis.
