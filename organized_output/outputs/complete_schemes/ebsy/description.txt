# Eja-Boi Shagun Yojana

**Short Title:** EBSY

**Tags:** Financial Assistance, Pregnancy, Post-delivery, Pregnant Woman, Child Care

The scheme &amp;quot;Eja-Boi Shagun Yojana&amp;quot; was launched by the Department of Medical Health and Medical Education, Government of Uttarakhand. The scheme aims to ensure safe postnatal care and encourage institutional deliveries. Under this scheme, a financial incentive of ₹2,000 /- is provided to mothers who stay in government hospitals for at least 48 hours post-delivery.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Women and Child; Sector: Pregnancy Care

**Where it applies:** Uttarakhand; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - Mothers receive a ₹2,000/- incentive for delivering in a government hospital and staying for at least 48 hours post-delivery for postnatal care. > **Note:** The incentive amount shall be transferred directly to the beneficiary’s bank account.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Department Of Medical Education.

**References / Annexures:** - **Guidelines (Page No. 127)**: https://cdnbbsr.s3waas.gov.in/s339e6f67916920d9419b3d92cf1e9a958/uploads/2025/03/202503051772421971.pdf

**Additional Information:**
Briefdescription: Under this scheme, a cash gift of ₹2,000 /- would be paid to those women who ensured 48 hours stay for delivery of their child in the government hospitals or health care centres.
