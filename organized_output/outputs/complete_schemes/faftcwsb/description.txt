# Funeral Assistance for the Construction Worker

**Short Title:** FAFTCWSB

**Tags:** Labour, Funeral Support, Construction Workers, Death, Financial Assistance

The &amp;quot;Funeral Assistance for the Construction Workers&amp;quot; by the Sikkim Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board, Labour Department, is a welfare scheme for the registered workers of the board. Under this scheme, funeral assistance is provided to the nominees/dependents of the member in case of his/ her death. 

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Financial assistance

**Where it applies:** Sikkim; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - ₹10,000/- is provided for Funeral assistance.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Labour Department.

**References / Annexures:** - **Guidelines**: https://labour.sikkim.gov.in/Uploads/Bocw/02335FDA-88DE-41D3-A0A6-3BC21F091009.pdf
