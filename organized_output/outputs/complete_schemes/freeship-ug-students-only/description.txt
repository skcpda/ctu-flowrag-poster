# Freeship less than ₹1,00,000 (UG students only) 

**Short Title:** FREESHIP (UG STUDENTS ONLY) 

**Tags:** Scholarship, Students, Research, Education

&quot;Freeship Less than ₹1,00,000&quot; Scheme by the Dean Student Welfare, Govind Ballabh Pant Krishi Evam Praudyogiki Vishwavidyalaya, Government of Uttarakhand, aims to provide financial assistance to meritorious undergraduate students from economically weaker backgrounds. Through this scheme, a full tuition fee waiver is granted to selected students whose annual family income is less than ₹1,00,000. The benefit is especially extended to girls for an additional academic year. The scheme is implemented by the Office of the Dean Student Welfare in accordance with university regulations. Applications are scrutinized and the final list is approved by university authorities.  

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Education & Learning; Sector: Universities and higher education

**Where it applies:** Uttarakhand; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - Eligible students get a 100% tuition fee waiver. - Aims to reduce the financial burden on low-income families and encourage academic excellence. - The fee waiver is directly applied during fee processing. - Granted once every academic year, at the beginning of the session. - Disbursed after the final selection list is approved by the Registrar and Vice Chancellor. - Validity: Boys: Up to 3 academic years Girls: Up to 4 academic years  

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Department Of Education.

**References / Annexures:** - **Guidelines**: https://uk.gov.in/department92/library_file/file-30-01-2025-04-25-16.pdf

**Additional Information:**
Briefdescription: The scheme "Freeship Less than ₹1,00,000 only for UG students" by the Dean Student Welfare, GBPUAT, Pantnagar aims to support meritorious undergraduate students from low-income families. This Freeship scheme provides full tuition fee exemption to eligible students.
