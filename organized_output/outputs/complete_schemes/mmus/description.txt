# Mukhyamantri Matrupushti Uphar

**Short Title:** MMU

**Tags:** Women, Pregnant, Medical, Financial Assistance

The scheme “Mukhyamantri Matrupushti Uphar” was launched by the Directorate of Social Welfare and Social Education, Government of Tripura, to provide financial assistance of ₹500/- to pregnant women through Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) for antenatal checkups at any health facility.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Women and Child; Sector: Pregnancy Care

**Where it applies:** Tripura; State/ UT

**DBT Scheme:** Yes (Direct Benefit Transfer)

**Benefits / Assistance:** ₹500/- is transferred into the bank account through DBT, after each antenatal check-up, up to four times.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Directorate Of Social Welfare & Social Education.

**References / Annexures:** - **Guidelines**: https://socialwelfare.tripura.gov.in/sites/default/files/Mukhamantri%20Matrupushti%20Uphar_0.pdf
