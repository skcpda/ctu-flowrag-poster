# Krishak Durghatna Kalyan Yojana

**Short Title:** KDKY

**Launch Date:** 2019-09-14

**Tags:** Financial Assistance, Death Benefits, Disability, Farmers

Under this scheme, while expanding the definition of a farmer, along with the Khatauni registered account holder / co-account holder, such earning members of their family whose main source of livelihood is agricultural income from the land registered in the name of the account holder / co-account holder and such landless persons. Those who do agricultural work on the land obtained on lease or on share and whose main means of livelihood is agriculture on the land taken on such lease or share have also been included.

**Who is this for:** Family; Category: Agriculture,Rural & Environment

**Where it applies:** Uttar Pradesh; State

**Benefits / Assistance:** In this scheme, the Uttar Pradesh government will provide the following amount / financial assistance or compensation amount to the farmers if they are victims of the accident – 1. Compensation for loss of both hands and legs of the farmer – Rs 5 lakh. 1. In case of loss of 1 hand and 1 foot – Rs.5 lakh. 1. In case of disability of one leg and one hand – financial assistance ranges from 2 to 3 lakhs. 1. In case of the death of a farmer in an accident – ​​a compensation amount of up to Rs.5 lakh will be given. 1. In the case of disability which is more than 25% but less than 50% – assistance is between 1 to 2 lakhs. 1. In case of loss of an eye due to the accident – ​​assistance up to Rs.5 lakh.  **Assistance will be given to the victims of these accidents :** Farmers have to apply to get profit compensation under the scheme. Under the scheme, the government will provide benefits to the victims of these accidents – 1. On damage and/or death due to falling tree 1. due to landslide. 1. travel incident. 1. struck by lightning. 1. being swept away by a flood. 1. animal bite. 1. due to electrocution. 1. burning in fire. 1. house collapse. 1. terrorist attack. 1. accident in a fight. 1. falling into the chamber. 1. murder in robbery.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Agriculture Department, Uttar Pradesh.
