# Disablement Benefit Scheme (PBAOCWWB)

**Short Title:** DBS-PBAOCWWB

**Tags:** Disability, PwD, Construction, Worker, DBT

The &quot;Disablement Benefit&quot; is a Welfare Scheme by the Puducherry Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board, Labour Department, Union Territory of Puducherry. Through this scheme, financial assistance of ₹75,000/- is extended based on the Disablement Certificate issued by the Competent Authority to the Registered Construction Worker of the Board. The amount is credited through Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) into the bank account of the worker. The applications are accepted offline.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Financial assistance

**Where it applies:** Puducherry; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - **Financial Assistance:** ₹75,000/-.- **Time of Disbursal:** Within 1 month of the application.- **Mode of Disbursal:** Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) into the bank account.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Labour Department, Puducherry.
