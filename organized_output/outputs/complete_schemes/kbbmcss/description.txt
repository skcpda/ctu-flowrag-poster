# Kali Bai Bheel Medhavi Chhatra Scooty Scheme

**Short Title:** KBBMCSS

**Launch Date:** 2016-01-01

**Tags:** Student, Higher Education, Educational Support

Motivating the meritorious girl students of the state of Rajasthan to study by taking admission in government schools as regular students from class 9th to class 12th and to score maximum marks in the examination conducted by the Board of Secondary Education Rajasthan. It is also announced to increase the number of scooties currently being provided to girls from 20 thousand to 30 thousand under this scheme. It is also proposed to give the option of taking Electric Scooty to these girls.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Education & Learning; Sector: Resources for schools

**Where it applies:** Rajasthan; State

**Benefits / Assistance:** - Scooty distribution to girl students.

**Benefit Type:** In Kind

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Higher Education Department.
