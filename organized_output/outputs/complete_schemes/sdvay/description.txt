# Vishesh Yogyajan Sukhad Dampatya Jeevan Yojana

**Short Title:** VYSDJY

**Launch Date:** 1997-12-24

**Tags:** Marriage Assistance, Differently Abled Person, Marriage Grant, Marriage Grant

The scheme &amp;quot;Vishesh Yogyajan Sukhad Dampatya Jeevan Yojana&amp;quot; was launched by the Social Justice &amp;amp; Empowerment Department, Government of Rajasthan, in 1997. The scheme aims to provide financial assistance to specially-abled individuals to support a happy married life after marriage. Under this scheme, financial assistance of ₹50,000/- per couple is provided. Additionally, ₹20,000/- is given as event expenses to organizations that organize marriage events for specially-abled individuals. The scheme is implemented by the Directorate of Specially-Abled Persons in the state.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Financial assistance

**Where it applies:** Rajasthan; State/ UT

**DBT Scheme:** Yes (Direct Benefit Transfer)

**Benefits / Assistance:** - Financial assistance of ₹50,000/- per couple is provided.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: Directorate Of Specially Abled Person, Government Of Rajasthan; Nodal Department: Social Justice and Empowerment Department.
