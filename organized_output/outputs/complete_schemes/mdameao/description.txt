# Market Development Assistance to MSMEs: Assistance to Organizers for Organizing Industrial Exhibitions in Gujarat

**Short Title:** MDAMEAO

**Launch Date:** 2020-08-07

**Tags:** Start Up, Entrepreneurship, Exhibition, MSMEs

The scheme &amp;amp;amp;quot;Assistance to Organizers for Organizing Industrial Exhibitions in Gujarat&amp;amp;amp;quot; under the &amp;amp;amp;quot;Market Development Assistance to MSMEs&amp;amp;amp;quot; introduced by the Industries and Mines Department, Gujarat, aims to offer reimbursement for stall rent, product literature, and display materials to MSMEs participating in exhibitions at state, national, and international levels.

**Who is this for:** Business Entity, Industries; Category: Business & Entrepreneurship; Sector: Exhibitions/Trade fairs/Buyer-seller meet, market development

**Where it applies:** Gujarat; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - **Electricity Cost Reimbursement:** 60% of the electricity bill incurred during the exhibition and pre-preparation period will be reimbursed.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Industries and Mines Department.

**References / Annexures:** - **Guidelines**: https://ic.gujarat.gov.in/documents/commondoc/2020/5-GR_02092020.pdf

**Additional Information:**
Briefdescription: The scheme "Assistance to Organizers for Organizing Industrial Exhibitions in Gujarat" under the "Market Development Assistance to MSMEs" aims to provide financial assistance to organizers of industrial exhibitions in Gujarat.
