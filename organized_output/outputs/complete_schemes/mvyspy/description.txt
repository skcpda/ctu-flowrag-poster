# Mukhyamantri Vishesh Yogyajan Sammaan Pension Yojana

**Short Title:** MVYSPY

**Launch Date:** 2013-04-01

**Tags:** Pension, Financial, Old Age, Disability

The Mukhyamantri Vishesh Yogyajan Samman Pension Yojana (MVSYS) stands as a testament to the commitment of the Government of Rajasthan towards the welfare of its citizens, particularly those with disabilities. Launched in 2013 under the auspices of the Department of Social Justice and Empowerment, this visionary scheme aims to extend crucial financial assistance to persons with disabilities (PwDs) across the state. It operates as a lifeline for individuals grappling with various disabilities, ensuring their financial security and enabling them to lead dignified lives. By providing a pension to eligible beneficiaries, MVSYS not only addresses the economic challenges faced by PwDs but also emphasizes inclusivity and equal opportunities. Through its implementation, the scheme not just disperses funds but fosters a culture of empathy and support, reaffirming the government&amp;#39;s commitment to uplifting marginalized communities. In essence, MVSYS embodies the principles of social justice and empowerment, serving as a beacon of hope and resilience for individuals with disabilities in Rajasthan.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Pension

**Where it applies:** Rajasthan; State/ UT

**DBT Scheme:** Yes (Direct Benefit Transfer)

**Benefits / Assistance:** **The following monthly pension will be provided:** Age CriteriaAmount75 Years or Less₹1,000/- per Month.75 Years or More₹1,250/- per Month.Leprosy Free Patient₹2,500/- per Month.Silicosis Disease Patient₹1,500/- per Month.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Social Justice and Empowerment Department.
