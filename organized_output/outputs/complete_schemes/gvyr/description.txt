# Gaushala Vikas Yojana

**Short Title:** GVYR

**Tags:** Cattle Welfare, Animal Husbandry

The &amp;quot;Gaushala Vikas Yojana&amp;quot; launched on 26th December 2019, by the Animal Husbandry Department, Rajasthan, aims to support registered gaushalas with ownership or long-term leased land (minimum 20 years) to develop permanent infrastructure. Eligible gaushalas must have maintained at least 100 cattle consistently for the past two years and should not have any financial irregularities under review.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Housing & Shelter; Sector: Shelter, integrated support and assistance

**Where it applies:** Rajasthan; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - Financial aid of up to ₹10,00,000/- for infrastructure development.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Animal Husbandry Department.

**References / Annexures:** - **Guidelines**: https://gopalan.rajasthan.gov.in/scheme/detail/573

**Additional Information:**
Briefdescription: This initiative provides financial assistance to registered gaushalas for building permanent infrastructure. Under this scheme, up to ₹10,000,00/- can be provided, with 90% of the funding from the state government and 10% contributed by the gaushala.
