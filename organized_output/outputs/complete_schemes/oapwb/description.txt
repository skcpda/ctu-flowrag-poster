# Old Age Pension

**Short Title:** OAPWB

**Launch Date:** 2010-01-01

**Tags:** Financial Assistance, Social Welfare, Pension, Old Age, Senior Citizens

In 2010, the West Bengal state government, through the Women and Child Development and Social Welfare Department, launched a compassionate initiative called the &amp;quot;Old Age Pension&amp;quot; scheme. The West Bengal Disability Pension Scheme provides financial assistance to persons who are above 60 years and unable to work and earn a living.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Pension

**Where it applies:** West Bengal; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - ₹1,000/- per month.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Exclusions / Not eligible:** &gt; **Professional Beggars and Mendicants:** - Professional beggars and mendicants of urban areas (cities and towns) are not eligible for pension under this scheme. &gt; **Recipients of Other Pensions:** - Persons receiving “Disability Pension”, “Widow Pension”, “Farmers’ Pension”, “Political Sufferers”, or any other pension of any State Government or the Central Government shall not be eligible for pension under this scheme.

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Women and Child Development and Social Welfare Department.

**References / Annexures:** - **Guidelines**: https://wbswpension.gov.in/pages/wcd_about_scheme.php - **Application Form**: https://wbswpension.gov.in/readwrite/miscellaneous/form-P.pdf
