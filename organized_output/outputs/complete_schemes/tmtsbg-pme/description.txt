# The Meghalaya Tribal Students’ Book Grant (Post-Matric Education)

**Short Title:** TMTSBG-PME

**Tags:** Student, Book Grant, Post-matric Courses, Education, Financial Assistance

&quot;The Meghalaya Tribal Students’ Book Grant (Post-Matric Education)&quot; scheme was launched by the Directorate of Higher and Technical Education, Meghalaya. Through this scheme, financial assistance is provided to the meritorious tribal students for the purchase of books for their studies. The student should be enrolled in a recognized institution in India.

**Who is this for:** Family; Category: Education & Learning; Sector: Scholarships and student finance

**Where it applies:** Meghalaya; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** The amount of grant per student per academic course will be as follows: - Class 11th - 12th (Arts &amp; Commerce): ₹500/- - Class 11th - 12th (Science): ₹700/- - Degree Arts (Pass): ₹600/- - Degree Arts (Honours): ₹750/- - Degree Science (Pass): ₹900/- - Degree Science (Honours): ₹1000/- - Post-Graduate Course (Arts &amp; Commerce): ₹900/- - Post Graduate Course (Science): ₹1200/- - Research studies for (Ph.D. M.Phil, or M.Lit.): ₹1500/- **NOTE:** A student receiving a book grant from the Government for a particular course of study, if changes the course of study will not be granted further grants. > **Disbursement** The grant to the selected candidates shall be paid through their parents/guardians or through the Instructions in which the candidates pursue their studies.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Education Department.

**References / Annexures:** - **Guidelines And Application Form**: https://meghalaya.gov.in/sites/default/files/documents/handbook_of_Scholarship_Schemes_0.pdf
