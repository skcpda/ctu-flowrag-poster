# Panchayat Kalyan Kosh

**Short Title:** PKK

**Launch Date:** 2022-03-30

**Tags:** Panchayat Representative, Death, Financial Assiatance, Panchayat

The &amp;quot;Panchayat Kalyan Kosh&amp;quot; scheme was introduced by the Panchayati Raj Department, Government of Uttar Pradesh. The scheme aims to provide financial assistance to the families or dependents of Gram Pradhans, Kshetra Panchayat Pramukhs, Zila Panchayat Presidents, and all members of the three-tier Panchayat elected as representatives of the people, in the event of their death while in office (excluding cases of suicide or involvement in criminal acts).

**Who is this for:** Family; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Financial assistance

**Where it applies:** Uttar Pradesh; State

**DBT Scheme:** Yes (Direct Benefit Transfer)

**Benefits / Assistance:** In the event of the death of a Panchayat Representative while in office, the following amounts will be provided to their families or dependents: - Pradhan of Gram Panchayat, Pramukh of Kshetra Panchayat, and Zila Panchayat President: ₹10,00,000/- - Member of Zila Panchayat: ₹5,00,000/- - Member of Kshetra Panchayat: ₹3,00,000/- - Member of Gram Panchayat: ₹2,00,000/-

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Panchayati Raj Department, Uttar Pradesh.

**References / Annexures:** - **Guidelines**: https://prdfinance.up.gov.in/dist_pdf_kalyankosh/pk_niyamavli.pdf - **Application Process Details**: https://prdfinance.up.gov.in/dist_pdf_kalyankosh/Avedan_Prakriya.pdf - **Online Application Portal**: https://prdfinance.up.gov.in/self_registration_kalyankosh_ben
