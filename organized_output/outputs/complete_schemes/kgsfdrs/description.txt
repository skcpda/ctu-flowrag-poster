# Kasturba Gandhi Special Term Deposit Receipt Scheme

**Short Title:** KGSTDRS

**Tags:** Term Deposit, Girl Student, Residential School, Kasturba Gandhi

The &amp;quot;Kasturba Gandhi Special Term Deposit Receipt Scheme&amp;quot; was launched by the Department of Education, Government of Rajasthan. Under this scheme, girls who complete Class 8 from Kasturba Gandhi Residential Girls&amp;#39; Schools and continue their regular studies in government schools are eligible for financial support. Those who secure at least 50% marks in Class 10 and enroll in Class 11 will be awarded a Special Term Deposit Receipt (STDR) of ₹2,000/- with a maturity period of five years. Similarly, girls who achieve a minimum of 50% marks in Class 12 and enroll in the first year of a graduation course will receive an STDR of ₹4,000/- with a maturity period of three years.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Education & Learning; Sector: Scholarships and student finance

**Where it applies:** Rajasthan; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** > **Monetary Benefits:** - A Special Term Deposit Receipt (STDR) of ₹2,000/- for girl students enrolled in Class 11. - A Special Term Deposit Receipt (STDR) of ₹4,000/- for girl students pursuing a graduation course. > **Mode of Disbursement:** - STDRs (Special Term Deposit Receipts) in the name of the student. > **Validity:** - An STDR of ₹2,000/- is valid for 5 years. - An STDR of ₹4,000/- is valid for 3 years.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: Directorate Of Secondary Education, Bikaner, Rajasthan; Nodal Department: Education (Elementary and Secondary) Department.
