# Funeral Assistance Scheme (PBAOCWWB)

**Short Title:** FAS-PBAOCWWB

**Tags:** Funeral, Financial Assistance, Construction, Worker, DBT, Death

The &quot;Funeral Assistance&quot; is a Welfare Scheme by the Puducherry Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board, Labour Department, Union Territory of Puducherry. Through this scheme, the Board may sanction an amount of ₹7,000/- to the nominees/dependents of the deceased Construction Worker towards funeral expenses. The applicant should be submitted within two months from the date of death of the Construction Worker.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Crisis/Disaster/Accident

**Where it applies:** Puducherry; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** Financial Assistance of ₹7,000/- credited within 7 days through DBT.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Labour Department, Puducherry.
