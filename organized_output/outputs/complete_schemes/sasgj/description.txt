# Scholarships and Stipends

**Short Title:** SASGJ

**Tags:** Sports Stipend, Scholarship, Medal Winners, Athletes

The scheme &amp;quot;Scholarships and Stipends&amp;quot; by the Sports, Youth and Cultural Activities Department, Government of Gujarat, aims to promote excellence in sports by offering financial support to individuals who have represented the state at national-level events and have won medals. This initiative is designed to reduce the financial burden on athletes and to recognize their achievements at the national level, thereby motivating them to continue their athletic pursuits and strive for excellence. The scheme is implemented by the Sports Authority of Gujarat. 

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Sports & Culture; Sector: Sports

**Where it applies:** Gujarat; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - **Scholarships:** ₹2,500/- per player is awarded to athletes who have won medals at the national level. - **Stipends:** ₹2,000/- per player is awarded to athletes who have participated at the national level, irrespective of winning. 

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: Sports Authority Of Gujarat; Nodal Department: Sports, Youth and Cultural Activities Department.

**References / Annexures:** - **Guidelines (Scheme No. 557)**: https://mariyojana.gujarat.gov.in/Schemeatoz.aspx
