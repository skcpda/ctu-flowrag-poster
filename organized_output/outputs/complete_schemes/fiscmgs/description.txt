# Financial Incentive to SC Meritorious Girls Students

**Short Title:** FISCMGS

**Launch Date:** 2025-07-02

**Tags:** Incentive, Scheduled Caste, Student, Social Welfare, Merit

The &#39;Financial Incentive to SC Meritorious Girls Students&#39; scheme, administered by the Directorate of Welfare of Scheduled Castes and Backward Classes, Government of Assam, provides financial assistance to deserving girl students from Scheduled Castes (SC). Eligibility is extended to those who have achieved 80% or higher marks in the HSLC examination. The scheme&#39;s objective is to motivate and encourage SC students to pursue higher education, thereby fostering their academic parity with the broader population through a substantial incentive. Assistance is contingent upon the applicant satisfying the stipulated eligibility criteria and the successful submission of required documentation. An incentive of ₹5,000/- will be disbursed to students as approved by the Selection Committee.  **The applications will be accepted through the myScheme platform from 04/07/2025 to 31/10/2025.** ***A DigiLocker account is required for application. Please refer to the Application Process section for details.*** 

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Education & Learning; Sector: Scholarships and student finance

**Where it applies:** Assam; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** A financial incentive of ₹5,000/- is provided.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Directorate of Welfare of Scheduled Castes and Backward Classes.
