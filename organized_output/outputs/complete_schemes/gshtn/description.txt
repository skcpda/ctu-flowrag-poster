# Government Service Home - Tamil Nadu

**Short Title:** GSH-TN

**Tags:** Home, Women, Destitute, Deserted, Girl, Food, Education

The scheme &quot;Government Service Home&quot; was launched by the Social Welfare and Women Empowerment Department, Government of Tamil Nadu. This scheme aims to provide institutional care to widows, destitute women, deserted women, adolescent girls and differently-abled women and their children who are economically and socially backward by giving them protection, education and vocational training.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Women and Child; Sector: Childcare

**Where it applies:** Tamil Nadu; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** The beneficiary is provided accommodation, Food, Education, Vocational training, Free supply of textbooks, notebooks, uniforms, chappals, and sanitary napkins. 

**Benefit Type:** In Kind

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Department of Social Welfare and Women Empowerment.
