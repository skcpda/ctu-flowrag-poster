# PMMSY: Construction of New Rearing ponds - Haryana

**Short Title:** CNRPHR

**Tags:** Subsidy, Ponds, Nursery, Seed

The scheme &quot;Pradhan Mantri Matasya Sampada Yojana: Construction of New Rearing Ponds (nursery/seed rearing ponds)&quot; by the Ministry Of Fisheries, Animal Husbandry &amp; Dairying, Government of India, aims to expand fisheries in various urban/semi-urban areas only under the construction of new ponds. Through this scheme, central assistance and subsidy are provided for the construction of new rearing units and initial crop costs in newly constructed ponds. 

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Agriculture,Rural & Environment; Sector: Land and water resources

**Where it applies:** Haryana; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - Provides financial assistance for the construction of New Rearing Ponds (nursery/seed rearing ponds). - The project cost of ₹ 7,00,000/- per Hectare is provided. - The subsidy will be limited to 40% of the project cost for the General Category and 60% of the project cost for SC/Women beneficiaries. 

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Fisheries Department.
