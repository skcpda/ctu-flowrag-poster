# Award of Puducherry State Post-Matric Scholarship Scheme

**Short Title:** APSPMSS

**Tags:** Student, School, Education, Scholarship, DBT

The &quot;Award of Puducherry State Post-Matric Scholarship Scheme&quot; initiated by the Directorate of School Education, Union Territory of Puducherry, is designed to make sure that no brilliant student is prevented on the grounds of poverty alone from pursuing an academic career. Through this scheme, scholarship is awarded to students studying in 11th and 12th standards and have secured promotion to the next higher class with 60% &amp; 50% of marks in 10th and 11th standards respectively.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Education & Learning; Sector: Scholarships and student finance

**Where it applies:** Puducherry; State/ UT

**DBT Scheme:** Yes (Direct Benefit Transfer)

**Benefits / Assistance:** > **Number of Slots** 90 scholarships for 11th Standard and 90 scholarships for 12th Standard are awarded every year. * This number may vary depending on the availability of funds in the budget of the year. > **Rate of Scholarship** - Day Scholar: ₹60/month per student (₹600/year/student). - Hosteller: ₹100/month per student (₹1,000/year/student). * For ten months in an academic year. > **Mode of Disbursement** The amount is disbursed directly into Aadhaar-seeded bank account of the applicant through Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) and Public Financial Management System (PFMS).

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: School Education Department, Puducherry.
