# Establishment of 50 Milch Animals Dairy Unit

**Short Title:** EMADU

**Tags:** Animal Husbandry, Dairy Unit, Cattle Development, Livestock Farming, Self Employment

The &amp;quot;Intensive Castration Scheme&amp;quot; introduced by the Agriculture, Farmers Welfare and Cooperation Department, Gujarat, aims to provide financial assistance to livestock owners for setting up dairy farms with 50 milch animals, offering subsidies on loan interest, shed construction, animal insurance, and essential equipment.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Agriculture,Rural & Environment; Sector: Animal husbandry

**Where it applies:** Gujarat; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - **Interest Subsidy:** 7.5% interest subsidy for up to 5 years on bank loans for purchasing 50 dairy animals.- **Shed Construction:** 50% subsidy on actual expenditure, up to ₹5,00,000/-.- **Animal Insurance:** 75% subsidy for a three-year premium, up to ₹1,80,000/-.- **Equipment Assistance:** 1. 75% subsidy on an electric chaff cutter (up to ₹30,000/-). 1. 75% subsidy on a fogger system (up to ₹22,500/-). 1. 75% subsidy on a milking machine (up to ₹56,250/-).

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: Animal Husbandry Department, Gujarat State And Gujarat Milk Marketing Federation (GCMMF); Nodal Department: Agriculture, Farmers Welfare and Cooperation Department.
