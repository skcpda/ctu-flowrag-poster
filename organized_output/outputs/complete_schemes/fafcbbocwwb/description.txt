# Financial Assistance for Cremation (BBOCWWB)

**Short Title:** FAFCBBOCWWB

**Tags:** Construction, Building, Labour, Financial Assistance, Worker, Death

The scheme &quot;Financial Assistance for Cremation&quot; by the Bihar Building &amp; Other Construction Workers Welfare Board, Labour Resources Department, Government of Bihar, provides financial assistance of ₹5,000/-to the dependents of the deceased worker for performing cremation.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Financial assistance

**Where it applies:** Bihar; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** Financial Assistance of ₹5,000/- is provided for the cremation of the deceased construction worker.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Building Construction Department.

**References / Annexures:** - **Scheme Details**: https://bocw.bihar.gov.in/Website/pdf/Brochure.pdf
