# Mahila Samiriddhi Yojana for Backward Classes- Haryana

**Short Title:** MSYBC-H

**Tags:** Mahila, Woman, Backward Classes, Woman Entrepreneur, Loan, Self-help Group

The scheme &amp;quot;Mahila Samiriddhi Yojana for Backward Classes&amp;quot; is implemented by the Haryana Backward Classes and Economically Weaker Sections Kalyan Nigam. This scheme provides loans up to ₹1,25,000/- per beneficiary exclusively to women entrepreneurs of the target group, either directly or through self-help groups. It applies to women aged between 18 to 55 years who are permanent residents of Haryana. The scheme empowers women by offering financial support to start or expand their businesses.

**Who is this for:** Individual, Self Help Groups (SHGS); Category: Banking,Financial Services and Insurance; Sector: Loan

**Where it applies:** Haryana; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** 1. Under this scheme, Nigam provides loans up to ₹1,25,000/- per beneficiary only to women entrepreneurs of the target group either directly or through Self-Help Groups.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: Haryana Backward Classes And Economically Weaker Sections Kalyan Nigam; Nodal Department: Welfare of Scheduled Caste & Backward Classes Department.
