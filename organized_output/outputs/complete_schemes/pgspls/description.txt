# Post Graduate Scholarship for Ph.D./LLM Students

**Short Title:** PGSPLS

**Tags:** Education, Scholarship, PhD, LLM, Student, Research, Academic

The scheme &quot;Post Graduate Scholarship for Ph.D./LLM Students&quot; by the Higher Education Department, Government of Assam, aims to provide financial assistance to students of Assam pursuing Doctor of Philosophy and Master of Laws degree courses in any recognized colleges or universities in the state and other states of the country, focusing on those with uniformly good academic records. The scheme provides access to poor students of the state and helps them achieve the benefits of higher education through scholarship support for advanced academic pursuits. The applicant must have registered their names as Doctor of Philosophy/Post Doctorate Research Scholar in any recognized universities and must be domiciled in Assam. The scheme is implemented by Higher Education Department. The applications for this scheme are accepted online. 

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Education & Learning; Sector: Universities and higher education

**Where it applies:** Assam; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** **Financial Assistance:** Scholarship is provided to the poor students to help them achieve the benefits of Higher Education. **Mode of Disbursement:** Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) _*The applicant must maintain registration as Doctor of Philosophy/Post Doctorate Research Scholar in recognized universities._ _*Academic performance standards must be maintained as per university requirements._ 

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: Higher Education Department, Assam; Nodal Department: Higher Education Department.

**References / Annexures:** - **Schemes Of Department**: https://highereducation.assam.gov.in/portlets/scholarships-0

**Additional Information:**
Briefdescription: The scheme aims to provide financial assistance to students of Assam pursuing PhD and LLM degree courses in recognized institutions. It benefits academically meritorious students who have obtained at least 55% marks in their last qualifying examination and are domiciled in Assam.
