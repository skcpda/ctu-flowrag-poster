# Swanath Scholarship Scheme

**Short Title:** SSSUK

**Tags:** Scholarship, Technical Education, Diploma Scholarship, Orphan Student

The scheme &amp;quot;Swanath Scholarship Scheme&amp;quot; aims to provide financial support to diploma and graduate-level orphans / whose parents or either parent has died due to COVID-19/ dependent of martyr in any military and central armed force. The scheme provides ₹50,000/- annually to each eligible student. 

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Education & Learning; Sector: Scholarships and student finance

**Where it applies:** Uttarakhand; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - **Financial Assistance:** ₹50,000/- per year. 

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Directorate of Technical Education .

**References / Annexures:** - **Guidelines (Page No. 99)**: https://uk.gov.in/department92/library_file/file-04-12-2023-06-02-23.pdf
