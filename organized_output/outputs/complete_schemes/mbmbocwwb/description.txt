# Maternity Benefit (MBOCWWB)

**Short Title:** MB(MBOCWWB)

**Tags:** Maternity, Building Worker, Construction Worker, Labour, Woman, Pregnancy, Financial Assistance

The “Maternity Benefit” is a social security scheme, implemented by the Meghalaya Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board (MBOCWWB), Labour Department, Government of Meghalaya. Under the scheme, financial assistance in the form of maternity benefits shall be provided to women construction workers registered under the Board during their maternity period.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Women and Child; Sector: Pregnancy Care

**Where it applies:** Meghalaya; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** 1. The registered women construction workers shall receive financial assistance of ₹1,000/- as maternity benefits during their maternity period.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: Meghalaya Building & Other Construction Workers Welfare Board; Nodal Department: Labour and Employment Department.
