# Super-100 Programme - Uttarakhand

**Short Title:** S100PUK

**Tags:** Science, Student, Coaching, Engineering Entrance Examination, Medical Entrance Examinations, Samagra Shiksha

The scheme “Super-100 Programme” is implemented under the Samagra Shiksha Programme by the Secondary Education Department, Government of Uttarakhand. The scheme aims to identify and support the brightest Class 12 Science stream students by providing them with high-quality coaching and guidance for engineering and medical entrance examinations. The programme is a unique state-level initiative approved by the Government of India. 

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Education & Learning

**Where it applies:** Uttarakhand; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** Under this programme, 100 Science students were prepared for engineering and medical entrance examinations in 2023–24. Faculty members were selected from reputed coaching institutes based on their interviews and experience. 

**Benefit Type:** In Kind

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: Secondary Education Department, Government Of Uttarakhand; Nodal Department: Department Of Education.
