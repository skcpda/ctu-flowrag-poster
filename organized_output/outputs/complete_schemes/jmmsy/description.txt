# Jharkhand Mukhyamantri Maiya Samman Yojana

**Short Title:** JMMSY

**Launch Date:** 2024-08-16

**Tags:** Financial Assistance, Women, Rural, Urban

The Department of Women, Child Development &amp; Social Security launched the &quot;Jharkhand Mukhyamantri Maiya Samman Yojana&quot; scheme aims to enhance the financial security and well-being of women across the state by providing financial assistance of ₹1,000/- per month to women beneficiaries. The payments are directly deposited into their bank accounts by the 15th of every month. 

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Women and Child; Sector: Financial Assistance

**Where it applies:** Jharkhand; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - **Financial Assistance ₹1,000/- per month.**

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Exclusions / Not eligible:** 1. The applicant herself or her husband should be employed as a regular/permanent employee/contractual employee/honorarium employee in the Central/State Government or Central/State Public Sector Undertakings, Statutory Body, Local Body, Urban Body and Government Aided Educational Institution or Should be receiving pension/family pension after retirement. 1. The family member is a current or former MP/MLA. 1. Income tax paying families. 1. The beneficiaries who are already getting the benefits of any other social security pension scheme run by the Women, Child Development and Social Security Department, Jharkhand, Ranchi, will not be provided the benefits of this scheme. 1. EPF account holder applicant woman.

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Women, Child Development & Social Security Department.
