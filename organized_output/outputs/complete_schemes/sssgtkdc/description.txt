# Swayam Saksham Yojana (GTKVN)

**Short Title:** SSYGTKVN

**Tags:** Enterpreneurs, Self-employment, Youth, Loan, Thakor, Koli

The &amp;quot;Swayam Saksham Yojana&amp;quot; is implemented by the Gujarat Thakor and Koli Vikas Nigam (GTKVN) under the Social Justice and Empowerment Department, Government of Gujarat. The scheme aims to promote economic self-reliance among professionally educated unemployed youth (both men and women) from the Thakor and Koli communities, aged between 18 and 35 years, who hold degrees in fields such as Pharmacy, Architecture, Software/Hardware, Automobile Engineering, Law, Chartered Accountancy, and others. Under this scheme, financial assistance in the form of low-interest loans is provided to eligible beneficiaries who have completed higher professional education and wish to become self-reliant by starting their own business in their respective fields. It specifically supports individuals trained in professional fields like medicine, law, engineering, accounting, hotel management, and even wrestling or sculpture, to use their skills for gainful self-employment. 

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Banking,Financial Services and Insurance; Sector: Loan

**Where it applies:** Gujarat; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** **Loan Amount:** - Up to ₹5,00,000/-. **Interest Rate:** - 5% per annum. **Contribution Split:** - 95% of the business/project cost is covered under the scheme as a loan. - 5% contribution must be made by the beneficiary. **Loan Repayment Terms:** - Repayable in 60 equal monthly installments (maximum 5 years). - The repayment period depends on the nature of the business plan. 

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: Gujarat Thakor And Koli Vikas Nigam; Nodal Department: Social Justice and Empowerment Department.
