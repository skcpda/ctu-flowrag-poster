# Dr.Babasaheb Ambedkar Udyog Uday Yojana: Assistance for Interest Subsidy (Service Sector)

**Short Title:** BAUUYISSS

**Launch Date:** 2020-08-07

**Tags:** Business, Entrepreneurship, MSMEs, Start Up, Scheduled Caste, Scheduled Tribe

The scheme &amp;quot;Dr.Babasaheb Ambedkar Udyog Uday Yojana&amp;quot; is an umbrella scheme launched by the Industries and Mines Department, Gujarat. It aims to empower the state&amp;#39;s dynamic MSME sector, which significantly contributes to employment, GDP, and exports. Effective from August 7th, 2020 to August 6th, 2025, this initiative supports SC/ST entrepreneurs and strengthens MSMEs, making them globally competitive. The component &amp;quot;Assistance for Interest Subsidy (Service Sector)&amp;quot; provides an interest subsidy on the term loan to support micro-enterprises in the service sector.

**Who is this for:** Business Entity, Industries; Category: Business & Entrepreneurship; Sector: Setting up / start-up / entrepreneurship

**Where it applies:** Gujarat; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:**  > **Financial Assistance:** **Category of Taluka** **Quantum of Incentive** [Category I](https://indextb.com/files/2020/11/a34fc914-6cdd-4fd6-8773-502017da4334_taluka%20category.pdf)8% on Term Loan with a maximum amount of ₹35,00,000/- per annum for a period of 7 years[Category II](https://indextb.com/files/2020/11/a34fc914-6cdd-4fd6-8773-502017da4334_taluka%20category.pdf)7% on Term Loan with a maximum amount of ₹30,00,000/- per annum for a period of 6 years [Category III](https://indextb.com/files/2020/11/a34fc914-6cdd-4fd6-8773-502017da4334_taluka%20category.pdf) &amp;amp;amp;amp; Municipal Corporation Areas6% on Term Loan with a maximum amount of ₹25,00,000/- per annum for a period of 5 years> **Additional Subsidy:** 1% extra interest subsidy for registered startups in the service sector with Udyam/MSME Registration.     

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Exclusions / Not eligible:** - Enterprises whose term loan is sanctioned after one year from the commencement of commercial production. - Enterprises defaulting on loan repayments as per RBI and bank guidelines. - Enterprises seeking reimbursement for penal interest or other bank charges. - Enterprises availing interest subsidy under any Central Government scheme where the total subsidy exceeds the total interest paid.

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Industries and Mines Department.
