# Diggy

**Short Title:** DR

**Tags:** Farming, Farmer, Development

In Agriculture Department, Rajasthan Government has started the Diggy Scheme to provide subsidies to the farmers of Rajasthan for making dig for irrigation. Under the scheme, farmers can prepare ways in their fields collect water in one place, and do more irrigation with the help of sprinklers. By providing this grant amount to the farmers, the government will encourage the farmers to grow good crops by building dig and irrigating the crops without any financial pressure. This scheme is to help farmers improve their farming by using more water resources and encourage them to grow good crops.  > **Other Important Points** - Start the construction of the dig only after the administrative approval of the Agriculture Department. - Verification will be done by the department before and after construction. - Installation of drip/sprinkler set on dig is mandatory. - The grant amount will be deposited directly into the farmer&#39;s account.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Agriculture,Rural & Environment; Sector: Financial assistance

**Where it applies:** Rajasthan; State/ UT

**DBT Scheme:** Yes (Direct Benefit Transfer)

**Benefits / Assistance:** - The farmer must purchase a permanent container with a minimum filling capacity of 4 lakh liters and above. - The state government will provide a 75% to 85% subsidy. - Farmer will be provided a grant amount of up to ₹ 3,00,000/- to ₹ 3,40,000/- by the government for making a dig in fields.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Agriculture Department.
