# Taluka Level Personality Development and Yogasan Training Camp for Scheduled Caste

**Short Title:** TLPDYTC

**Tags:** Scheduled Caste, Personality Development, Yoga Training, Physical And Mental Health

The scheme &amp;quot;Taluka Level Personality Development and Yogasan Training Camp for Scheduled Caste&amp;quot; by the Sports, Youth and Cultural Activities Department, Government of Gujarat, aims to facilitate holistic personality development—physical, mental, and spiritual—among Scheduled Caste youth aged between 14 and 35 years by organizing 7-day free training camps. These camps are conducted across all 250 talukas in 41 Scheduled Caste-majority districts of Gujarat and are designed to empower 50 youth participants per taluka through structured personality enhancement and Yogasana sessions. 

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Sports & Culture; Sector: Sports

**Where it applies:** Gujarat; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - **Personality Development:** Participants undergo structured sessions for enhancing communication, confidence, discipline, and overall character. - **Physical Development:** Daily Yogasana training and exercises aimed at physical fitness. - **Mental and Spiritual Development:** Yoga and related practices to foster mental peace and spiritual well-being. 

**Benefit Type:** In Kind

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Sports, Youth and Cultural Activities Department.

**References / Annexures:** - **Guidelines (Scheme No. 635)**: https://mariyojana.gujarat.gov.in/Schemeatoz.aspx
