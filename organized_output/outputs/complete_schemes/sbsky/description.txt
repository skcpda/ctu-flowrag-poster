# Shubhshakti Yojana

**Short Title:** SBSKY

**Launch Date:** 2016-01-01

**Tags:** Girl, Labor, Financial Assistance

The Shubshakti Yojana has been initiated by the Rajasthan government with the aim of providing financial assistance to regular construction workers, unmarried laborers, and their daughters. The primary objective of this scheme to support and strengthen the daughters of labour families in Rajasthan state. 

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Skills & Employment

**Where it applies:** Rajasthan; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - Receives a financial assistance of ₹55,000/-. - The amount is directly transferred to the beneficiary&#39;s bank account. - The funds can be used for various purposes, including: 1. Higher education 1. Vocational training 1. Starting a business 1. Skill development training 1. Marriage expenses

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Labour Department.
