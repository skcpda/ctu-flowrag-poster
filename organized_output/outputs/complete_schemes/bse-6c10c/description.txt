# Bharati Scheme for Education - 6th Class to 10th Class

**Short Title:** BSE - 6C10C

**Tags:** Financial Assistance, Student, Brahmin

Andhra Pradesh Brahmin Welfare Corporation (ABC) launched the “Bharati Scheme for Education - 6th Class to 10th Class&quot; for the academic year to encourage Brahmin students of Andhra Pradesh to continue their education. Under this scheme, eligible poor Brahmin student applicants will receive financial assistance for the academic year. This scheme is applicable for the Brahmin students of Andhra Pradesh covering all 13 districts.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Education & Learning; Sector: Scholarships and student finance

**Where it applies:** Andhra Pradesh; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - **Financial Assistance for the 6****th** **Class to 10****th** **Class: ₹7,000/-.**

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Andhra Pradesh Brahmin Welfare Corporation.
