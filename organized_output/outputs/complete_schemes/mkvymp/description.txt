# Mukhyamantri Kanya Vivaah / Nikaah Yojana

**Short Title:** MKVYMP

**Launch Date:** 2006-04-01

**Tags:** Financial Assitance, Marriage Grant, Group Marriage, Empowerment

Madhya Pradesh State Government started &quot;Mukhyamantri Kanya Vivaah Yojana&quot; with the objective to provide financial assistance to needy girls/widows (Kalyani)/abandoned sisters at the time of their marriage.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Financial assistance

**Where it applies:** Madhya Pradesh; State

**Benefits / Assistance:** - To the Bride 1. Account Payee Check : Rs: 11,000/- 1. Marriage Gift : Rs. 38,000/- - Amount: Rs.6,000/- per girl, to the mass marriage organizing body.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Social Welfare Department.
