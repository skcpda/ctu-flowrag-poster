# Kunwar Bai Nu Mameru Yojana

**Short Title:** KBNMY

**Launch Date:** 1991-01-01

**Tags:** Scheduled Caste, Financial Assistance, Marriage, Girl

The scheme “Kunwar Bai Nu Mameru Yojana” is implemented by the Director Scheduled Caste Welfare, Department of Social Justice &amp;amp; Empowerment, Government of Gujarat. The scheme was launched in the year 1991. Under the scheme, financial assistance is provided on the occasion of the marriage of two adult girls belonging to the Scheduled Caste category.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Marriage

**Where it applies:** Gujarat; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** 1. Scheduled caste girls who marry after 01/04/2021 will receive financial assistance of ₹12,000/- under the scheme. 1. Scheduled caste girls who marry before 01/04/2021 will receive financial assistance of ₹10,000/- under the scheme.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: District Social Welfare Officer Office, Gujarat; Nodal Department: Social Justice and Empowerment Department.
