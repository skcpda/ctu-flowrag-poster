# Saksham Scheme for Backward Classes

**Short Title:** SSBC

**Tags:** Saksham, Backward Classes, Loan, Young Professional

The scheme &amp;quot;Saksham Scheme for Backward Classes&amp;quot; is implemented by the Haryana Backward Classes and Economically Weaker Sections Kalyan Nigam, Government of Haryana. Under the scheme, loans of up to ₹10,00,000/- are provided to young professionals for setting up Doctors&amp;#39; Clinics, Nursing Homes, Physiotherapy Clinics, Bio-Medical Labs, Lawyers&amp;#39; Offices, Engineering Units, Chemist Shops, Architect Offices, and similar establishments. Applicants must be between 18 and 35 years old, permanent residents of Haryana, and belong to the Backward Class.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Banking,Financial Services and Insurance

**Where it applies:** Haryana; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** 1. Under the scheme, loans of up to ₹10,00,000/- are provided to young professionals for setting up Doctors&amp;#39; Clinics, Nursing Homes, Physiotherapy Clinics, Bio-Medical Labs, Lawyers&amp;#39; Offices, Engineering Units, Chemist Shops, Architect Offices, etc.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: Haryana Backward Classes And Economically Weaker Sections Kalyan Nigam; Nodal Department: Welfare of Scheduled Caste & Backward Classes Department.
