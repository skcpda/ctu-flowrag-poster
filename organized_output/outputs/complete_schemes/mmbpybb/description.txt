# Mukhya Mantri Balika Poshak Yojna

**Short Title:** MMBPYBB

**Tags:** Education, School Uniform, Girls

Introduced in 2006-07, the &amp;quot;Mukhya Mantri Balika Poshak Yojna&amp;quot; aims to promote education among girl children by addressing barriers to school attendance, such as the lack of proper clothing. Under the scheme, girl students enrolled in Classes VI to VII in government schools receive a pair of school uniforms. This initiative seeks to improve enrollment, retention, and educational outcomes for girls. &lt;br&gt; &lt;br&gt;

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Education & Learning; Sector: Resources for schools

**Where it applies:** Bihar; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - A pair of school uniforms to girls in Classes VI to VII.

**Benefit Type:** In Kind

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Education Department.

**References / Annexures:** - **Guidelines**: https://state.bihar.gov.in/educationbihar/Content.html?links&page=FACTS_SCHEMES

**Additional Information:**
Briefdescription: The "Mukhya Mantri Balika Poshak Yojna" is a government initiative launched to encourage the enrollment and retention of girl students in schools by providing them with school uniforms.
