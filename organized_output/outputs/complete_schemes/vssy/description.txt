# Vishesh Shiksha Sahaayata Yojana

**Short Title:** VSSY

**Launch Date:** 2018-07-10

**Tags:** Education, Financial Assistance, Social Welfare

Labor Department of Chhattisgarh State started this scheme &amp;amp;quot;Vishesh Shiksha Sahaayata Yojana&amp;amp;quot; for Building and other construction worker on 10 July 2018.. The main objective of the scheme to provides financial assistance for education.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Education & Learning; Sector: Scholarships and student finance

**Where it applies:** Chhattisgarh; State

**Benefits / Assistance:** 1. For registered workers who pass away after one year of registration, the Board will issue special registration cards. 1. These children will be eligible for the benefits of the Naunihal Scholarship Scheme, Meritorious Students Education Incentive Scheme, and Technical Education Scheme. 1. Benefits will be granted to the first two eligible children of the deceased worker.

**Benefit Type:** In Kind

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Labour Department.
