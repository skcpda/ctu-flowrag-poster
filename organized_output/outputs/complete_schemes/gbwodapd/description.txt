# Goggles by Welfare of Differently Abled Persons Department

**Short Title:** GBWODAPD

**Tags:** Differently Abled Person, Goggles, Visually Challenged Person, Social Welfare

The &#39;Goggles by Welfare of Differently Abled Persons Department&#39; scheme was launched by the Department for the Welfare of Differently Abled Persons, Government of Tamil Nadu. The scheme aims to distribute goggles to needy visually impaired persons to enable them to protect themselves from sunlight and moisture. The applications are accepted offline in the prescribed form available at the District Differently Abled Welfare Office.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Aids/Appliances

**Where it applies:** Tamil Nadu; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - **Free distribution of goggles to visually impaired persons.**

**Benefit Type:** In Kind

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Welfare of Differently Abled Persons Department.
