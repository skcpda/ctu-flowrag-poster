# Old Age Pension Scheme- On Completion Of 65 Years (UKBOCWWB)

**Short Title:** OAPSC65YUKBOCWWB

**Tags:** Labour, Old Age, Construction Worker, Senior Citizen, Pension

The scheme “Old Age Pension Scheme” is implemented by the Uttarakhand Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board (UKBOCWWB) under the Labour Department, Government of Uttarakhand. The scheme aims to provide a monthly pension to registered construction workers who have completed 65 years of age. 

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Pension

**Where it applies:** Uttarakhand; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - **Pension Amount:** ₹1,500/- Per Month 

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: Uttarakhand Building And Other Construction Workers Welfare Board; Nodal Department: Department Of Labour.

**References / Annexures:** - **Guidelines (Page No. 56)**: https://uk.gov.in/department92/library_file/file-04-12-2023-06-02-23.pdf - **Official Website**: https://ukbocw.uk.gov.in/

**Additional Information:**
Briefdescription: The scheme aims to provide a monthly pension of ₹1,500/- to construction workers registered with the Uttarakhand Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board who have attained the age of 65 years.
