# Jharkhand Patrakar Samman Pension Niyamawali

**Short Title:** JPSPN2019

**Tags:** Pension, Financial Assistance, Journalist

The &quot;Jharkhand Patrakar Samman Pension Niyamawali&quot; was launched by the Information and Public Relations Department, Government of Jharkhand. The scheme aims to provide financial assistance to journalists who have served the state for an extended period. It ensures their well-being post-retirement and acknowledges their contribution to media and society.  > ***Objective******:*** To support retired full-time journalists by offering a monthly pension and recognizing their dedication and service in the field of journalism.  > ***Scope:*** The scheme applies to journalists across Jharkhand and includes provisions for family pensions for their dependents after their demise.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Skills & Employment; Sector: Pension

**Where it applies:** Jharkhand; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - A monthly pension of ₹7,500/- for journalists. - The family pension of 50% of the journalist&#39;s pension goes to the spouse after their demise. - Lifetime pension for retired journalists who meet the eligibility criteria.  NOTE: Pension payments are made directly to beneficiaries&#39; bank accounts through an online transfer.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Information and Public Relations Department.
