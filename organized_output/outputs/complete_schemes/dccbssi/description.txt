# District Central Cooperative Banks and through its Branches: For SSI Unit Loan and Other NFS Activities

**Short Title:** DCCBSSI

**Tags:** SSI Unit Loan, Non-Farm Sector Activities, Agriculture, Industries

The &amp;quot;District Central Cooperative Banks and through its Branches: For SSI Unit Loan and Other NFS Activities&amp;quot; scheme launched by the Co-operation, Food and Consumer Protection Department, Tamil Nadu, aims to support small-scale industries (SSI) and other NFS activities by providing loans up to ₹1,00,000 at an interest rate of 13% (subject to change). &lt;br&gt;

**Who is this for:** Industries; Category: Banking,Financial Services and Insurance; Sector: Loan

**Where it applies:** Tamil Nadu; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - **Access to Loan:** Up to ₹1,00,000/-.- **Interest Rate:** 13% (subject to change). 

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Co-operation, Food and Consumer Protection Department.

**References / Annexures:** - **Guidelines**: https://www.tn.gov.in/scheme/data_view/3570

**Additional Information:**
Briefdescription: This scheme provides financial assistance for SSI unit loans and other Non-Farm Sector (NFS) activities through District Central Cooperative Banks and their branches.
