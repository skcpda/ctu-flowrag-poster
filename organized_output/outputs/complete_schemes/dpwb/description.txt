# Disability Pension - West Bengal

**Short Title:** DPWB

**Launch Date:** 2010-01-01

**Tags:** Financial Assistance, Social Welfare, Pension

The West Bengal Disability Pension Scheme 2010 provides financial assistance to persons with disabilities who are unable to work and earn a living. The scheme is implemented by the Department of Women and Child Development and Social Welfare, Government of West Bengal.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Pension

**Where it applies:** West Bengal; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** Financial Assistance

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Exclusions / Not eligible:** **Professional Beggars and Mendicants:** 1. Individuals engaged in begging as a profession are not eligible for pension under this scheme. 1. Individuals who do not beg professionally but receive occasional assistance are eligible if they meet other criteria. **Recipients of Other Pensions:** 1. Disabled persons already receiving &quot;Widow Pension,&quot; &quot;Old Age Pension,&quot; &quot;Farmers’ Pension,&quot; or pension under the &quot;Family Pension Scheme&quot; from the State Government, Central Government, or other concerns are not entitled to the pension under this scheme.

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Women and Child Development and Social Welfare Department.
