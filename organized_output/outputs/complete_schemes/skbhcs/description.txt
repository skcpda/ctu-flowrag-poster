# Safai Karmakar Ke Bachche Hetu Chhatravrtti Yojana

**Short Title:** SKBHCS

**Launch Date:** 2015-03-11

**Tags:** Scholarship, Financial Assistance, Student Finance

Labor Department of Chhattisgarh State started this scheme &amp;amp;quot;Safai Karmakar Ke Bachche Hetu Chhatravrtti Yojana&amp;amp;quot; for unorganized worker on 11 March 2015. The main objective of the scheme to provides financial assistance to the children of sanitation workers in India for higher education.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Education & Learning; Sector: Scholarships and student finance

**Where it applies:** Chhattisgarh; State

**Benefits / Assistance:** - Financial assistance provided according to the below table  ClassesBoy Student (Annual Scholarship amount)Girl Student (Annual Scholarship amount)Class I to V₹ 1,000/-₹ 1,500/-Class VI to VIII₹ 1,5000/-₹ 2,000/-Class IX to XII₹ 2,000/-₹ 3,000/-Graduation (BSc, B.Com, Diploma etc₹ 5,000/-₹ 6,000/-Post-Graduation ( MA, MSc, M.Com)₹ 7,000/-₹ 8,000/-Pursuing graduate level professional course₹ 10,000/-₹ 11,000/-Studying for professional level, doing PhD or research work₹ 14,000/-₹ 15,000/-

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Labour Department.
