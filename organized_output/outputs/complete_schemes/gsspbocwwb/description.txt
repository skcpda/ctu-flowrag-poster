# General Surgery Scheme (P.B.O.C.W.W.B)

**Short Title:** GSSPBOCWWB

**Tags:** Labour, Building Worker, Construction Worker, General Surgery, Health, Operation

The &amp;quot;General Surgery Scheme&amp;#39;&amp;#39; by the BOCW Welfare Board, Department of Labour, Punjab, is a welfare scheme for the registered workers of the board. Under this scheme, the Board shall provide financial assistance for general surgery of the beneficiary and his family members. 

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Health & Wellness; Sector: Financial assistance

**Where it applies:** Punjab; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - **Financial Coverage:** ₹50,000/- or actual expenses, whichever is less. 

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Department of Labour.
