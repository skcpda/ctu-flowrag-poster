# Financial Assistance to ST Students under "Gagan Bharari Shiksha Yojana"

**Short Title:** FASTS-GBSY

**Tags:** Financial Asisstance, Scheduled Tribe, Student, Education, School

&quot;Gagan Bharari Shiksha Yojana&quot; is a Financial Assistance Scheme by the Department of Tribal Welfare, Government of Goa. Under this scheme, additional financial assistance of upto ₹ 15,000/- is provided to the Scheduled Tribe Students as the maintenance allowance given under the Post Matric Scholarship is inadequate to meet the expenses on food &amp; travel.  > **Duration** Scholarships for studying in any class will be available for only one year.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Education & Learning; Sector: Scholarships and student finance

**Where it applies:** Goa; State

**Benefits / Assistance:** 1. An Additional Maintenance Allowance of ₹ 7,500/- to Day Scholars for 10 Months. 1. An Additional Maintenance Allowance of ₹ 15,000/- to Hostellers for 10 Months.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Social Welfare Department.
