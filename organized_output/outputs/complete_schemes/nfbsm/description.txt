# National Family Benefit Scheme (Maharashtra)

**Short Title:** NFBSM

**Tags:** Pension, Family, BPL, Death, Below Poverty Line

The scheme &amp;quot;National Family Benefit Scheme (Maharashtra)&amp;quot; is a social welfare scheme by the Social Justice &amp;amp; Special Assistance Department, Government of Maharashtra. Under this scheme, in the event of the death of the primary breadwinner of a BPL family, the family is provided with a one-time lump sum financial assistance of ₹20,000/-.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Pension

**Where it applies:** Maharashtra; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** Financial assistance of ₹20,000/-.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Social Justice and Special Assistance Department.

**References / Annexures:** - **Guidelines**: https://sjsa.maharashtra.gov.in/scheme/national-family-benefit-scheme/
