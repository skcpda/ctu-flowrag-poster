# Bunkar Parivar Sahayata Yojana

**Short Title:** BPSYC

**Tags:** Weaver, Family, Financial Assistance, Handloom, Death

Launched in 2013-14, the scheme &quot;Bunkar Parivar Sahayata Yojana&quot; by the Chhattisgarh State Handloom Development and Marketing Cooperative Union Limited, Raipur aims to provide financial security to families of weavers working in primary weaver cooperative societies. The scheme provides immediate financial assistance to family members of weavers in case of their untimely death. Eligible beneficiaries include working weaver members of primary weaver cooperative societies affiliated with the State Handloom Union. The scheme is implemented by the Chhattisgarh State Handloom Development and Marketing Cooperative Union Limited, Raipur.

**Who is this for:** Family; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Financial assistance

**Where it applies:** Chhattisgarh; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - Financial assistance of ₹20,000/- is provided to the heir of the working weaver in case of death. - The assistance is meant to address the immediate financial crisis faced by family members of weavers upon their untimely death.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Commerce & Industries Department.

Briefdescription: "Bunkar Parivar Sahayata Yojana" aims to provide immediate financial assistance to families of weavers in case of sudden death. The scheme benefits the families of working weavers who are members of primary weaver cooperative societies by providing financial support of ₹20,000/-.
