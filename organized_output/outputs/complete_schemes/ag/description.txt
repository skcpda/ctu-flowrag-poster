# Advocate Grant

**Short Title:** AG

**Launch Date:** 2012-04-01

**Tags:** Advocate, Grant, OBC, Law Graduate

The Scheme “Advocate Grant” was launched by the Backward Classes Development Department, Government of Kerala during the year 2012-13. Under the scheme, the grant will be provided to law graduates belonging to backward classes facing financial difficulties for starting practice as advocates. The scheme aims to ensure adequate representation of OBC’s in the judiciary.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Education & Learning; Sector: Education and training grants, fellowship, stipend

**Where it applies:** Kerala; State

**Benefits / Assistance:** 1. The grant of ₹12000/- per year will be provided to law graduates who enrolled before the Bar Council. 2. The grant will be provided for 3 consecutive years.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: Backward Classes Development Department, Government Of Kerala; Nodal Department: Backward Classes Development Department.
