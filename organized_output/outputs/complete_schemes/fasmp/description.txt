# Funeral Assistance Scheme (Madhya Pradesh)

**Short Title:** FASMP

**Launch Date:** 2013-08-13

**Tags:** Funeral, Poor, Death, Financial Assistance

Launched on 13th August 2013, the &quot;Funeral Assistance Scheme&quot; by the Department of Social Justice and Empowerment, Government of Madhya Pradesh, provides ₹3,000/- for the last rites of destitute individuals and unclaimed bodies. The scheme also supports families registered under labour schemes, especially those in the unorganized sector, who struggle to afford last rites due to financial constraints. The scheme helps ease this burden by providing essential funds for performing the final rites.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Health & Wellness; Sector: Financial assistance

**Where it applies:** Madhya Pradesh; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** A financial assistance of ₹3,000/- is provided.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Department of Social Justice and Empowerment of Persons with Disabilities.
