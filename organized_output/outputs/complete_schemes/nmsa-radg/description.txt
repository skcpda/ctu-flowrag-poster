# National Mission for Sustainable Agriculture - Reinforced Area Development

**Short Title:** NMSA-RADG

**Tags:** Agriculture, Soil Health, Crop Diversification, Integrated Farming

The scheme &amp;quot;National Mission for Sustainable Agriculture - Reinforced Area Development&amp;quot; is a centrally sponsored scheme that aims to promote location-specific sustainable agriculture production by encouraging improved agronomic practices, soil health management, better rainwater utilization, judicious use of agrochemicals, crop diversification, and the progressive adoption of integrated farming systems involving crops, livestock, and trees. 

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Agriculture,Rural & Environment; Sector: Agricultural Inputs- seeds, fertilizer etc.

**Where it applies:** Gujarat; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - **Financial Help:** Provides financial assistance to eligible farmers to adopt sustainable agricultural practices. - **Improved Agronomic Practices:** Encourages the implementation of location-specific, modern, and efficient farming techniques. - **Soil Health Management:** Supports initiatives for maintaining and enhancing soil fertility and health. - **Enhanced Rainwater Use Efficiency:** Promotes techniques and infrastructure for optimal utilization of rainwater in agriculture. - **Judicious Use of Chemicals:** Advocates for reduced and responsible application of chemical fertilizers and pesticides. - **Crop Diversification:** Encourages farmers to cultivate a variety of crops, promoting resilience and better income opportunities. - **Integrated Farming Systems:** Supports the adoption of integrated crop-livestock-tree farming for holistic farm management. 

**Benefit Type:** In Kind

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Agriculture, Farmers Welfare and Cooperation Department.

**References / Annexures:** - **Guidelines (Scheme No. 393)**: https://mariyojana.gujarat.gov.in/Schemeatoz.aspx

**Additional Information:**
Briefdescription: The scheme "National Mission for Sustainable Agriculture - Reinforced Area Development" aims to promote sustainable agriculture through improved agronomic practices, efficient resource use, and integrated farming systems by providing financial support to all farmers.
