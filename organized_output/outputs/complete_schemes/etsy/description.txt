# E-Thela Sahaayata Yojana

**Short Title:** ETSY

**Launch Date:** 2017-06-19

**Tags:** Financial Assistance, Business, Equipment

Labor Department of Chhattisgarh State started this scheme &amp;quot;E-Thela Sahaayata Yojana&amp;quot; for unorganized worker on 19 June 2017. The main objective of the scheme to empower unorganized worker by providing financial assistance for e-thela.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Business & Entrepreneurship; Sector: Setting up / start-up / entrepreneurship

**Where it applies:** Chhattisgarh; State

**Benefits / Assistance:** 1. Registered unorganized labor individuals will need to cover the expenses of ₹ 10,000 for scheme execution. 1. A grant of ₹ 30,000/- will be provided by the Chhattisgarh Unorganized Worker State Social Security Board. 1. The remaining sum will be extended to the Rikshaw/Auto Driver as a bank loan, with the beneficiary responsible for repaying it within a specified timeframe and at the interest rate determined by the bank. 1. The Board will only transfer its contribution to the beneficiary&#39;s account once all the necessary loan-related documents are submitted to the board.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Exclusions / Not eligible:** > Workers who are receiving benefits from any other parallel scheme of the State Government are not eligible for this scheme.

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Labour Department.
