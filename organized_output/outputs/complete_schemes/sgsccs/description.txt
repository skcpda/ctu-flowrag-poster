# State Government Share Capital Contribution to Co.operative Sugar Factories (Normal Area) (Tribal Area)

**Short Title:** SGSCCS

**Tags:** Agriculture, Sugar Co-operatives Factories

The scheme &amp;quot;State Government Share Capital Contribution to Co.operative Sugar Factories (Normal Area) (Tribal Area)&amp;quot; introduced by the Agriculture, Farmers Welfare and Cooperation Department, Gujarat, aims to facilitates the establishment of new sugar cooperative factories by providing a 30% share capital contribution from the State Government. The objective is to strengthen the cooperative sugar industry and support economic development in the state.

**Who is this for:** Registered Societies; Category: Agriculture,Rural & Environment; Sector: Financial assistance

**Where it applies:** Gujarat; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:**  Name of the ComponentStandard of AssistanceMinimum Time Limit (in Years) for Reclaiming Benefits State Government share capital contribution to Co.operative Sugar Factories Share contribution by the State Government on the basis of 30% of the project cost to the new sugar co-operative factories to be set up in the State. To the extent of 30% of the project cost to the concerned Sugar Co-operative Societies who are eligible

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Agriculture, Farmers Welfare and Cooperation Department.
