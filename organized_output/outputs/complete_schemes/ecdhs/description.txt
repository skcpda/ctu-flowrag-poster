# "Exhibition" Component of the "Development Of Handicrafts" Scheme

**Short Title:** ECDHS

**Tags:** Exhibition, Allowance, Business, Entrepreneurship, Handicraft

The &quot;Exhibition&quot; component of the &quot;Development Of Handicrafts&quot; scheme by the Department of Industries and Commerce, Government of Puducherry, was introduced to boost the marketability of handicrafts and enhance sales through the organization of exhibitions. These exhibitions are conducted locally and in various states of the country.

**Who is this for:** Business Entity; Category: Business & Entrepreneurship; Sector: Exhibitions/Trade fairs/Buyer-seller meet, market development

**Where it applies:** Puducherry; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - Travelling allowance to-and-fro 2nd class (berth) train fare. - For participation in A1 cities&#39; exhibitions: Daily allowance of ₹300 per day per crafts person. - For participation in non-A1 cities&#39; exhibitions: Daily allowance of ₹200 per day per crafts person. - For participation in local exhibitions: Daily allowance of ₹150 per day per crafts person. - Reimbursement of actual expenditure for transporting handicrafts goods from the unit to the exhibition spot.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Industries and Commerce Department, Puducherry.
