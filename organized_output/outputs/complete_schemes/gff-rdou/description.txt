# Grant to Films - Regional Dialects of Uttarakhand

**Short Title:** GTF-RDOU

**Tags:** Regional Dialects, Filmmaker, Producer, Film, Film Grant, Film Shooting

The &amp;quot;Grant to Films – Regional Dialects of Uttarakhand&amp;quot; scheme was introduced under the Uttarakhand Film Policy 2024 by the Information and Public Relations Department, Government of Uttarakhand. The scheme aims to promote regional and children’s cinema, encourage film shooting within Uttarakhand, create local employment opportunities, support local and regional films and artists, and generate revenue for the state. Under this scheme, grants and subsidies are provided to filmmakers and producers to cover expenses incurred during film production. Additional incentives are available for children’s films, and OTT-based projects are also eligible under specific conditions. > **Scheme Implementation:** The scheme is implemented by the Film Development Council, Dehradun, and involves review by technical and financial committees.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Sports & Culture; Sector: Culture

**Where it applies:** Uttarakhand; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** 1. During film shooting in Uttarakhand, 50% of the expenditure incurred in the State or up to ₹2 Crore, whichever is less, may be granted. 1. For children’s films, an additional grant of 10% will be given in addition to the sanctioned amount of subsidy. 1. The maximum sanctioned amount of the grant will be accessed based on the quality and technical aspects of the film. &gt; **Note:** - Grant amount depends on technical quality and creative merit of the film. - In order to qualify for the grant - 90% of total expenses of film must be paid through bank, cheque, online medium, internet banking, UPI, digital mediums, etc. - Cash transactions are strongly discouraged. It will only be permitted in unavoidable circumstances on the Chartered Accountant&amp;#39;s certification. Provided that, in any case the total cash expenditure shall not be allowed more than 10% of the total expenditure.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Exclusions / Not eligible:** - Expenses/Funding for film marketing, promotion, and release will not be eligible for grant.

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Uttarakhand Information Department.
