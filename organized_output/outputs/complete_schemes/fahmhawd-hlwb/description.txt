# Financial Assistance of Hearing Machine/Hearing Aid to Workers and their Dependents- Haryana Labour Welfare Board

**Short Title:** FAHM/HAWD-HLWB

**Tags:** Financial Assistance, Hearing Machine, Hearing Aid, Worker, Deaf Worker

The scheme “Financial Assistance of Hearing Machine/Hearing Aid to Workers and their Dependents” is implemented by the Haryana Labour Welfare Board (HLWB), Labour Department, Government of Haryana. Under this scheme, financial assistance of ₹5,000/- or the actual cost of the hearing aid, whichever is less, is provided to workers employed in industrial and commercial establishments in the state of Haryana and their dependents for the purchase of hearing aid machine in case they lose their hearing power in an accident or for any other reason. The benefit of this scheme is provided to the worker or his/her dependents after every five years.

**Who is this for:** Family, Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Crisis/Disaster/Accident

**Where it applies:** Haryana; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** 1. Under this scheme, financial assistance of ₹5,000/- or the actual cost of the hearing aid, whichever is less, is provided to workers and their dependents for the purchase of hearing aids machine. **Note:** The worker will be provided with a one-time financial assistance in a block of 5 years to purchase hearing aids.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: Haryana Labour Welfare Board; Nodal Department: Labour Department.
