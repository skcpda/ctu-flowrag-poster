# West Bengal Migrant Workers’ Welfare Scheme: Accidental Disability

**Short Title:** WBMWWSAD

**Launch Date:** 2023-04-25

**Tags:** Migrant, Social Welfare, Financial Assistance, Labour, Accident, Disability, Worker

Launched on 25th April 2023, &amp;quot;West Bengal Migrant Workers’ Welfare Scheme: Accidental Disability&amp;quot; is a social welfare scheme by the West Bengal Migrant Workers’ Welfare Board, Labour Department, Government of West Bengal. Under this scheme, One-time assistance is provided to the registered Migrant Worker in case of disability arising out of accidents during work. &lt;br&gt;

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Crisis/Disaster/Accident

**Where it applies:** West Bengal; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - ₹ 50,000/- in case of 40% to 80 % disability. - ₹ 1,00,000/- in case of more than 80% disability.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Exclusions / Not eligible:** The applicant should not be in receipt of any other financial assistance of a similar nature from the Government of West Bengal.  

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: West Bengal Migrant Workers’ Welfare Board; Nodal Department: Labour Department.
