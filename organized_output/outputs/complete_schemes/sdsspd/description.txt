# State Disability Scholarship (Scholarship for Persons with Disabilities)

**Short Title:** SDS(SPD)

**Tags:** Disability, Scholarship, PwD, Student

The scheme “State Disability Scholarship” was launched by the Department of Social Welfare, Government of Nagaland to provide financial assistance to students from Class I to Class VIII who have a disability of 40% and above.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Education & Learning; Sector: Scholarships and student finance

**Where it applies:** Nagaland; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - The physically disabled students from Class I to Class VIII are provided with scholarships ranging from ₹200/-. **Note:** The amount of scholarships may vary as per guidelines of the Government of Nagaland.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: Department Of Social Welfare, Government Of Nagaland; Nodal Department: Social Welfare Department, Nagaland.

**References / Annexures:** - **Guidelines**: https://scholarship.nagaland.gov.in/storage/Guidelines//4_Guidelines - **Official Website**: https://nlsic.nagaland.gov.in/index.php/2020/02/28/social-welfare/ - **Common Scholarship Portal**: https://scholarship.nagaland.gov.in/
