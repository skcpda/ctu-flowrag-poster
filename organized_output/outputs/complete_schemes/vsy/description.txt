# Vivah Sahayata Yojana

**Short Title:** VSY

**Tags:** Financial Assistance, Marriage, Social Welfare

&quot;Vivah Sahayata Yojana&quot; is a scheme by the Department of Labour, Employment, Training &amp; Skill Development Govt of Jharkhand. This is a 100% State Sponsored Scheme. Only the residents/domicile of the state of Jharkhand can apply to this scheme. Applications for this scheme are accepted online through the official website of the department. The scheme provides financial assistance of ₹ 30,000/- (Rupees Thirty Thousand Only) rupees for the marriage of two children / female members of the family.

**Who is this for:** Individual, Family; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Marriage

**Where it applies:** Jharkhand; State

**Benefits / Assistance:** The scheme provides financial assistance of ₹ 30,000/- (Rupees Thirty Thousand Only) rupees for the marriage of two children / female members of the family.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: Jharkhand Building And Other Construction Worker Welfare Board (JBOCWW Board); Nodal Department: Labour, Employment, Training and Skill Department.
