# Award of Fellowship to Most Eminent Artist

**Short Title:** AOFTMEA

**Tags:** Eminent Artist, Fellowship, Visual Arts, Retired Artists

The scheme &amp;quot;Award of Fellowship to Most Eminent Artist&amp;quot; by the Sports, Youth and Cultural Activities Department, Government of Gujarat, aims to recognize and financially assist artists aged 60 years or above who have retired from professional artistic practice and have made distinguished contributions in the field of plastic arts at the national level. The fellowship is intended to support such artists by enabling them to acquire necessary equipment and continue contributing meaningfully to the arts. The scheme is implemented by the Gujarat State Fine Arts Academy. In this scheme, 10 artists are given a monthly fellowship of ₹5,000/- for one year, and it is extendable for a maximum of two years, depending on the quality and impact of the artist’s continued work.

**Who is this for:** Artists; Category: Sports & Culture; Sector: Culture

**Where it applies:** Gujarat; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - **Monthly Fellowship:** ₹5,000/-.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: Gujarat State Fine Arts Academy; Nodal Department: Sports, Youth and Cultural Activities Department.
