# Loan for the Purchase of Work Tool for the Construction Workers

**Short Title:** LPWTCW

**Tags:** Labour, Loan, Work Tool, Construction Workers, Financial Assistance

The &amp;quot;Loan for the Purchase of Work Tool for the Construction Workers&amp;quot; by the Delhi Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board, Labour Department, Delhi, is a welfare scheme for the registered workers of the board. Through this scheme, an amount not exceeding ₹20,000/- may be sanctioned as loan to the members of the fund, for the purchase of work related tools. &lt;br&gt;

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Banking,Financial Services and Insurance; Sector: Banking and money

**Where it applies:** Delhi; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - An amount not exceeding ₹20,000/- may be sanctioned as loan to the members of the fund, for the purchase of work related tools. **Note:** The loan amount shall be recovered in not more than sixty installments. 

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: Delhi Building And Other Construction Workers Welfare Board; Nodal Department: Labour Department.
