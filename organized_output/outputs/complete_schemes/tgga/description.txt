# Travel Grants To Goan Authors

**Short Title:** TGGA

**Tags:** Travel, Grant, Author, Culture, Artist

The scheme &quot;Travel Grants To Goan Authors&quot; is a state-level cultural scheme by the Kala Academy, Department of Art and Culture, Government of Goa. The objective of the scheme is to enable the Goan writers to come in close contact with the senior writers of a language-region other than their own within India to acquaint them with the writers and the people of that region, and to widen their awareness and sensibility about the country as a nation.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Transport & Infrastructure; Sector: Culture

**Where it applies:** Goa; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** The grant amount will be decided by Kala Academy with provision for an advance upto 75% of the total grant for a maximum of two Goan authors.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Art, Culture and Museums Department.

**References / Annexures:** - **Guidelines**: https://artandculture.goa.gov.in/sites/default/files/Kala%20Academy%20Goa%20Schemes.pdf
