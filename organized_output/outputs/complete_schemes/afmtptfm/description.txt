# Assistance to Farmers for Mini Tractor and Power Tiller in Farm Mechanization Program

**Short Title:** AFMTPTFM

**Tags:** Agricultural, Farm Mechanization, Subsidy, Mini Tractor, Power Tiller

The scheme “Assistance to Farmers for Mini Tractor and Power Tiller in Farm Mechanization Program” by the Agriculture and Co-operation Department, Government of Gujarat, aims to improve agricultural productivity and reduce manual labor burden by providing financial assistance for the purchase of mini tractors and power tillers to farmers. The scheme offers a subsidy based on the horsepower of the machinery and the applicant&amp;#39;s category to promote inclusive agricultural mechanization and improve efficiency in farming operations. 

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Agriculture,Rural & Environment; Sector: Mechanization- solar power, farming systems

**Where it applies:** Gujarat; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** > **Mini Tractors up to 20 HP and Power Tillers above 8 BHP:** - **General Category Farmers:** Financial assistance of ₹45,000/- or 40% of the equipment cost, whichever is less.- **SC/ST Farmers:** Financial assistance of ₹60,000/- or 50% of the equipment cost, whichever is less. > **Power Tillers Below 8 BHP:** - **General Category Farmers:** Financial assistance of ₹40,000/- or 40% of the equipment cost, whichever is less.- **SC/ST Farmers:** Financial assistance of ₹50,000/- or 50% of the equipment cost, whichever is less. 

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Agriculture, Farmers Welfare and Cooperation Department.

**References / Annexures:** - **Guidelines (Scheme No. 83)**: https://mariyojana.gujarat.gov.in/Schemeatoz.aspx
