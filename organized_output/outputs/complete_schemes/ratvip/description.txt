# Readers Allowance to Visually Impaired Persons

**Short Title:** RATVIP

**Tags:** Student, Financial Assistance, Differently-abled Person, Readers Allowance, Visually Impaired

&#39;Readers Allowance to Visually Impaired Persons&#39; scheme was launched by the Department for the Welfare of Differently Abled Persons, Government of Tamil Nadu. The scheme aims to provide readers allowance to visually impaired students, specific allowance rates based on the education level ranging from secondary school to postgraduate and professional courses.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Education & Learning; Sector: Financial assistance

**Where it applies:** Tamil Nadu; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** > **Reader&#39;s allowance is being paid at the following rate:** - 9th**** To 10th Standard - ₹3,000/- (per annum).- Degree Courses - ₹5,000/- (per annum). - Post Graduate and Professional Courses - ₹6,000/- (per annum). 

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Welfare of Differently Abled Persons Department.

**References / Annexures:** - **Citizen's Charter (2022)**: https://cms.tn.gov.in/sites/default/files/documents/wda_e_cc_2022_23.pdf
