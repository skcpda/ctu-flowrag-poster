# Bankable Scheme

**Short Title:** BSG

**Tags:** Startup, Business, Loan, Subsidy, Scheduled Caste

The &quot;Bankable Scheme&quot; is implemented by the Gujarat Scheduled Caste Development Corporation (GSCDC) under the Social Justice and Empowerment Department, Government of Gujarat. The scheme aims to provide financial assistance to scheduled caste persons for self-employment opportunities. Under this scheme, loans are provided for business, industry, or service activities, along with a subsidy of 50% of the loan amount or a maximum of ₹50,000/-. For setting up or expanding self-employment ventures in areas like animal husbandry, tailoring, groceries, beauty parlors, carpentry, RCC centering, footwear, bamboo work, embroidery, and other small-scale trades. 

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Business & Entrepreneurship; Sector: Setting up / start-up / entrepreneurship

**Where it applies:** Gujarat; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - Loan assistance up to ₹2,00,000/- for businesses, industries, and service activities. - Maximum loan of ₹1,00,000/- for business and ₹1,00,000/- for the industry/service sector individually. - Subsidy of 50% of the loan amount or up to ₹50,000/-, whichever is lower. 

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Social Justice and Empowerment Department.

**References / Annexures:** - **Guidelines**: https://sje.gujarat.gov.in/gscdc/bankebal-scheme?lang=English - **Scheme Details**: https://mariyojana.gujarat.gov.in/MoreDetails.aspx
