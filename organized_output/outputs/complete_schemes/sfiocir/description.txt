# PMMSY: Installation of Cages in Reservoirs Scheme - Haryana

**Short Title:** IOCIRS

**Tags:** Fish Farming, Fishermen, Subsidy, Scheduled Caste, Women, General Category

The &quot;Installation of Cages in Reservoirs Scheme&quot; was launched by the Ministry Of Fisheries, Animal Husbandry &amp; Dairying, Government of India, under the umbrella scheme &quot;Pradhan Mantri Matsya Sampada Yojana&quot;. It aims to promote sustainable aquaculture practices by supporting cage fish farming in water bodies. Under this scheme, a grant of ₹3,00,000/- per unit to the individuals and groups for setting up cage fish farms, thereby encouraging entrepreneurship, increasing fish production, and generating employment. 

**Who is this for:** Individual, Joint Liability Groups (JLGS), Self Help Groups (SHGS), Registered Societies; Category: Agriculture,Rural & Environment; Sector: Fishing and hunting

**Where it applies:** Haryana; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** **Grant Amount:** - ₹3,00,000/- per unit (per cage). **Grant Proportion:** - 40% of unit cost for General Category beneficiaries. - 60% of unit cost for Scheduled Caste/Women beneficiaries. **Cage Limit for Assistance:** - Up to 5 cages for individual farmers. - Up to 20 cages for registered cooperative societies/self-help groups/joint liability groups (with at least 10 members). 

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Fisheries Department.

**References / Annexures:** - **Guidelines**: https://kms.saralharyana.nic.in/ViewDoc?Id=W%2fsFEMWlJZG3hueCeE3oEg%3d%3d
