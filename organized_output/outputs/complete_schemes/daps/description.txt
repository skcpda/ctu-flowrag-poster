# Differently Abled Pension Scheme

**Short Title:** DAPS

**Tags:** Differently-abled, Pension, Financial Assistance, Clothes, Ration, Meal

The &#39;Differently Abled Pension Scheme&#39; is a state scheme launched by the Social Welfare and Women Empowerment Department, Tamil Nadu. The scheme aims to provide a pension of ₹1,000/- to differently-abled persons with disability 40% and above. One free saree to females and one free dhoti to males is given on Pongal and Diwali. A nutritious meal is provided to the beneficiaries through the Anganwadi Centres.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Pension

**Where it applies:** Tamil Nadu; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** **Pension:** - ₹1,000/- per month. **Free Clothes during Pongal and Deepavali:** - One free dhoti to a male pensioner. - One free saree to a female pensioner. **Free Rice in Anganwadi Centres:** - 2kg/month to those who are taking nutritious meal. - 4kg/month to those who are not taking nutritious meal.

**Benefit Type:** Composite

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Department of Social Welfare and Women Empowerment.
