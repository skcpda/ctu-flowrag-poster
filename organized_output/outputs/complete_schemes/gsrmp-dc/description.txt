# "Grant to Subsidy for Raw Materials Procurement" Component of the "Development of Coir" Scheme

**Short Title:** GSRMP-DC

**Tags:** Coir, Entrepreneur, Entrepreneurship, Business, Subsidy, Grant

The &quot;Grant to Subsidy for Raw Materials Procurement&quot; Component of the &quot;Development of Coir&quot; Scheme by the Department of Industries and Commerce, Union Territory of Puducherry, aims to promote Coir Industries by providing a 15% subsidy on annual raw materials procurement, capped at ₹5,00,000 per annum per unit.

**Who is this for:** Business Entity; Category: Business & Entrepreneurship; Sector: Setting up / start-up / entrepreneurship

**Where it applies:** Puducherry; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - 15% subsidy on annual raw materials procurement. - Maximum subsidy capped at ₹5,00,000 per annum per unit.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: District Industries Centre Organization.
