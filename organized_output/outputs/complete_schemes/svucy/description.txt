# Swami Vivekananda Utkrisht Chhatravriti Yojna

**Short Title:** SVUCY

**Launch Date:** 2001-03-05

**Tags:** School, Student, Financial Assistance, Scholarship, Education

The scheme &quot;Swami Vivekananda Utkrisht Chhatravriti Yojna&quot; is a Scholarship Scheme by the Department of Education, Government of Himachal Pradesh. The scheme aims to support meritorious students in their pursuit of higher education. Under this scheme, an amount of ₹ 18,000/- will be given to the eligible students per year for two successive years after matric. Every year, 2000 scholarships are awarded to excellent students from the general category. 

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Education & Learning; Sector: Scholarships and student finance

**Where it applies:** Himachal Pradesh; State

**DBT Scheme:** Yes (Direct Benefit Transfer)

**Benefits / Assistance:** A scholarship of ₹ 18,000/-.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: National Scholarship Portal; Nodal Department: Education Department, Himachal Pradesh.
