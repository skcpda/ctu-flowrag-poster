# Mentally Retarded Children Benefit Scheme (HPBOCWWB)

**Short Title:** MRCBSHPBOCWWB

**Tags:** Construction, Building, Labour, Child, Financial Assistance, Worker, Disability

In the scheme &quot;Mentally Retarded Children Benefit&quot; by the Himachal Pradesh Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board, Government of Himachal Pradesh, an amount of ₹20,000/- per annum is provided to the registered workers to take care of their mentally retarded/disabled children.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Women and Child; Sector: Childcare

**Where it applies:** Himachal Pradesh; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** ₹20,000/- per annum

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Labour and Employment Department, Himachal Pradesh.

**References / Annexures:** - **Guidelines**: https://bocw.hp.nic.in/pdf/Kalyanikariyojana2021_Ab1.pdf - **Scheme Details**: https://bocw.hp.nic.in/PublicPages/schemedetails.aspx - **Website**: https://bocw.hp.nic.in/index.aspx - **Labour Registration**: https://bocw.hp.nic.in/PublicPages/AddBeneficiary.aspx
