# Scholarship for Engineering and Medical Education

**Short Title:** SEME

**Tags:** Scholarship, Higher Education, Student, Medical Course, Engineering Course, Ex-serviceman

The scheme &amp;quot;Scholarship for Engineering and Medical Education&amp;quot; is implemented by the State Sainik Board under the Sainik Welfare Department, Government of Uttarakhand. The scheme aims to provide financial assistance to the dependents of ex-servicemen pursuing Engineering, Medical, or Ph.D. courses. 

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Education & Learning; Sector: Scholarships and student finance

**Where it applies:** Uttarakhand; State/ UT

**DBT Scheme:** Yes (Direct Benefit Transfer)

**Benefits / Assistance:** **Rate of Scholarship:** - ₹12,000/- per year for Engineering students - ₹15,000/- per year for Medical students - ₹10,000/- per year for PhD students 

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: State Sainik Board, Government Of Uttarakhand; Nodal Department: State Sainik Board.

**References / Annexures:** - **Guidelines (Page No. 29)**: https://uk.gov.in/department92/library_file/file-04-12-2023-06-02-23.pdf

**Additional Information:**
Briefdescription: The scheme aims to support the higher education of dependents of ex-servicemen by providing financial assistance for pursuing engineering, medical, and PhD courses. Under this scheme, annual scholarships are awarded to beneficiaries based on their course of study.
