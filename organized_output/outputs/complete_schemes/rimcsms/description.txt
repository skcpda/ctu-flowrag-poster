# RIMC Scholarship (Military Scholarship)

**Short Title:** RIMCSMS

**Tags:** Scholarship, Student, Rashtriya Indian Military College, Military

The “RIMC Scholarship (Military Scholarship)” scheme is implemented by the Secondary Education Department under the Department of Education, Government of Uttarakhand. The scheme aims to provide financial assistance to students from Uttarakhand who are pursuing their education at the Rashtriya Indian Military College (RIMC). 

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Education & Learning; Sector: Scholarships and student finance

**Where it applies:** Uttarakhand; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** The scholarship is payable to students at the rate of ₹1,000/- per month. 

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: Secondary Education Department, Government Of Uttarakhand; Nodal Department: Department Of Education.

**References / Annexures:** - **Guidelines (Page No. 75)**: https://uk.gov.in/department92/library_file/file-04-12-2023-06-02-23.pdf
