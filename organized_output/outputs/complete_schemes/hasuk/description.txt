# Housing Assistance Grant

**Short Title:** HASUK

**Tags:** Housing Grant, Soldier, Sainik, Martyrs, House Construction, Widow

The &amp;quot;Housing Assistance Grant&amp;quot; scheme is implemented by the State Sainik Board under the Sainik Welfare Department, Government of Uttarakhand. The scheme provides financial support to widows/dependents of soldiers martyred in wars and border clashes, as well as to war-disabled retired soldiers. Under this scheme, eligible beneficiaries receive a one-time grant for the construction or repair of a house during their lifetime. 

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Financial assistance

**Where it applies:** Uttarakhand; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - A one-time grant of ₹2,00,000/- for the construction/ repair of the house.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: State Sainik Board, Government Of Uttarakhand; Nodal Department: State Sainik Board.
