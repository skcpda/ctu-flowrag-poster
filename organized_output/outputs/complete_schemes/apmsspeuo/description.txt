# Award of Pre-matric Scholarship to the Students Whose Parents Are Engaged in Unclean Occupation

**Short Title:** APMSSPEUO

**Tags:** Scholarship, Financial Assistance, Scavengers, Student

The scheme &quot;Award of Pre-matric Scholarship to the Students Whose Parents Are Engaged in Unclean Occupation” by the Adi Dravidar Welfare Department, Government of Puducherry, provides scholarships to the students whose parents are engaged in unclean occupation (scavenger, Flayers &amp; Tarner) to improve the status of the unprivileged society. The scheme provides scholarships irrespective of income and caste.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Education & Learning; Sector: Scholarships and student finance

**Where it applies:** Puducherry; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** ₹1850/- per annum.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Adi Dravidar Welfare Department, Puducherry.
