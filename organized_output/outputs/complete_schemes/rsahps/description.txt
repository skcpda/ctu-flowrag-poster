# Rural Sports and Health Promotion Scheme

**Short Title:** RSAHPS

**Tags:** Financial Assistance, Health Promotion, Sports Promotion, Youth, Mangal Dal

The scheme &amp;quot;Rural Sports and Health Promotion Scheme&amp;quot; was launched by the Department of Youth Welfare and Provincial Protection Corps, Government of Uttarakhand. The scheme aims to promote physical fitness and well-being among rural youth by establishing open gyms in village communities. An amount is provided to Mangal Dals for the installation of community fitness equipment (open gyms) in each Gram Panchayat.

**Who is this for:** Joint Liability Groups (JLGS), Self Help Groups (SHGS); Category: Health & Wellness; Sector: Health promotion

**Where it applies:** Uttarakhand; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - A one-time financial grant of ₹17,960/- is allocated per gram panchayat for installing open gym/community fitness equipment to encourage fitness and well-being.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Department of Youth Welfare and Provincial Protection Corps.
