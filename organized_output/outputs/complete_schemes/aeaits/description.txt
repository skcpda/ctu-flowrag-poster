# Art Exhibition Assistance in the State

**Short Title:** AEAITS

**Tags:** Art Exhibition, Financial Assistance, Group Exhibition, Lalitkala Academy

The scheme &quot;Art Exhibition Assistance in the State&quot; by the Sports, Youth and Cultural Activities Department, Government of Gujarat aims to encourage visual artists of Gujarat to showcase their work by offering financial support for organizing exhibitions across the state. The scheme particularly targets those who have either undergone formal art education or have experience in holding art exhibitions. This initiative helps elevate the reach and recognition of regional art and artists. The scheme is implemented by the Gujarat State Lalitkala Academy.

**Who is this for:** Artists; Category: Sports & Culture; Sector: Culture

**Where it applies:** Gujarat; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - **Financial Assistance:** Up to ₹25,000/- or actual expenses incurred (whichever is less) for organizing an art exhibition.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: Gujarat State Lalit Kala Academy; Nodal Department: Sports, Youth and Cultural Activities Department.
