# Devnarayan Post Matric Scholarship Scheme

**Short Title:** DPMSS

**Launch Date:** 2011-01-01

**Tags:** Student, Scholarship, Financial Assistance, Education

The post-matriculation scholarship scheme is for the educational upgradation of students belonging to the very backward classes from class 11 to PhD level education in the state.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Education & Learning; Sector: Scholarships and student finance

**Where it applies:** Rajasthan; State

**Benefits / Assistance:** - Reimbursement of maintenance allowance and fee according to the time of course.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Social Justice and Empowerment Department.

**References / Annexures:** - **Guideline**: https://schemes.rajasthan.gov.in/scheme/detail/323 - **Details**: https://www.sje.rajasthan.gov.in/Default.aspx?PageID=87 - **Scholarship Portal**: https://sjmsnew.rajasthan.gov.in/scholarship/ - **Registation**: https://sso.rajasthan.gov.in/register?encq=dGQ0I/FDr2k00owwOaXQLi063xfzQeNP6f1L6NhniFIuhMKM+bpgvZwCXGZs7pETdxJjt1bkA5lABoFAnFl63Q==
