# Death Benefit (A.B.O.C.W.W.B)

**Short Title:** DB(ABOCWWB)

**Tags:** Death, Financial Assistance, Building Worker, Construction Worker, Labour

The scheme “Death Benefit” was started by the Assam Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board (A.B.O.C.W.W.B), Labour Welfare Department, Government of Assam. Under this scheme, financial assistance shall be given to the nominees/dependents of a registered worker towards death benefit, in case of normal death or due to an accident during the course of employment.

**Who is this for:** Family; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Financial assistance

**Where it applies:** Assam; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** 1. The financial assistance of ₹50,000/- shall be given to the nominees/dependents of a registered construction worker in case of normal death during the course of employment. 1. The financial assistance of ₹3,00,000/- shall be given to the nominee/dependents of a registered construction worker, in case the death is due to an accident during the course of employment.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: Assam Building And Other Construction Workers Welfare Board; Nodal Department: Labour & Welfare Department.
