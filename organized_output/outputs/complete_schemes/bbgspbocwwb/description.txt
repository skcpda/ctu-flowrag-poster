# Balri Birth Gift Scheme (P.B.O.C.W.W.B)

**Short Title:** BBGSPBOCWWB

**Tags:** Labour, Building Worker, Construction Worker, Fixed Deposit, Insurance, Girls

The &amp;quot;Balri Birth Gift Scheme&amp;#39;&amp;#39; by the BOCW Welfare Board, Department of Labour, Punjab, is a welfare scheme for the registered workers of the board. Under this scheme, the Board shall provide financial assistance in the form of a Fixed Deposit Receipt (FDR) for the birth of a female child, with a maximum of two girls per family. The FDR can be encashed only at the time of the daughter&amp;#39;s marriage.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Financial assistance

**Where it applies:** Punjab; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** **Financial Assistance:** ₹51,000/- in the form of an Fixed Deposit Receipt (FDR) for each female child (up to two girls). &gt; **Note:** The FDR can only be encashed at the time of the daughter&amp;#39;s marriage. 

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Department of Labour.
