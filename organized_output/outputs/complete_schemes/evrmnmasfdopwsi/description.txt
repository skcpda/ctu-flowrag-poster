# E.V.R. Maniammaiyar Ninaivu Marriage Assistance Scheme for Daughter’s of Poor Widows: Scheme-I

**Short Title:** EVRMNMASFDOPWSI

**Tags:** Widow, Marriage, Financial Assistance, Daughter

The &#39;E.V.R. Maniammaiyar Ninaivu Marriage Assistance Scheme for Daughters of Poor Widows: Scheme-I&#39; was launched by the Social Welfare and Women Empowerment Department, Government of Tamil Nadu. The aim is to help poor widow mothers who face financial hardship in conducting their daughter&#39;s marriage. The scheme provides financial assistance for the marriage of their daughters.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Women and Child; Sector: Financial Assistance

**Where it applies:** Tamil Nadu; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - Financial assistance of ₹25,000/- given to the widow to meet the expenses incurred in the marriage of her daughter. - 1 Sovereign (8gm) 22-carat gold coin for making Thirumangalyam (Mangalsutra). **Mode of Disbursal:** Through Electronic Clearing Service. **NOTE:** The benefit can be given to the bride in case of the death of the widowed mother.

**Benefit Type:** Composite

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Department of Social Welfare and Women Empowerment.
