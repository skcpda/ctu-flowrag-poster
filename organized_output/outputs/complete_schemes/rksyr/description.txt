# Khadya Suraksha Yojana

**Short Title:** RKSYR

**Launch Date:** 2013-10-02

**Tags:** Food Security, BPL, Families, Development

The &quot;Rashtriya Khadya Suraksha Yojana&quot; started on 02-10-2013 by the Food and Civil Supplies Department of Rajasthan. This scheme aim is to provide adequate quality food grains at reasonable prices to the people so that they can lead a dignified life. The Act provides for coverage of 75% of the rural population and 50% of the urban population to receive subsidized food grains under the Targeted Public Distribution System (TPDS) which covers about two-thirds of the population.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Food Security / Public Distribution System

**Where it applies:** Rajasthan; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** 1. Antyodaya families are provided with 35 kg of wheat per family. 1. Other eligible beneficiaries receive 5 kg of wheat per unit per month. 1. Ration card holders categorized under Antyodaya and Below Poverty Line (BPL) in the state are entitled to wheat at a subsidized rate of ₹1 per kg. 1. Other eligible families categorized as Priority Household receive food security at ₹2 per kg per month.

**Benefit Type:** In Kind

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Food and Civil Supplies Department.
