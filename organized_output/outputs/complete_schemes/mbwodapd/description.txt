# Magnifiers - Tamil Nadu

**Short Title:** MTN

**Tags:** Magnifiers, Social Welfare, Differently-abled Person, Low Vision

The Department launched the &quot;Magnifiers by Welfare of Differently Abled Persons Department&quot; scheme for differently abled persons. Under this scheme, magnifiers are distributed to needy students with low vision to enable them to read books. The applications are accepted offline in the prescribed form available at the District Differently Abled Welfare Office.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Aids/Appliances

**Where it applies:** Tamil Nadu; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - **Free distribution of magnifiers.**

**Benefit Type:** In Kind

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Welfare of Differently Abled Persons Department.
