# Vishvakarma Durghatana Mrtyu par Antyeshti Evan Anugrah Raashi Bhugataan Yojana

**Short Title:** VDRRY

**Tags:** Death Benefits, Construction Worker, Financial Assistance

Chhattisgarh state Government started a scheme &quot;Vishvakarma Durghatana Mrtyu Par Antyeshti Evan Anugrah Raashi Bhugataan Yojana&quot; for labor workers. Under this scheme, benefits will be provided to the workers and their family members. The main objective of starting the scheme is to provide financial help to the workers of the state. If any accident happens to a worker while working, then he or his family members are provided financial assistance under this scheme.

**Who is this for:** Individual, Family; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Financial assistance

**Where it applies:** Chhattisgarh; State

**Benefits / Assistance:** **Financial Assistance under following circumstances** 1. In the case of normal death ₹ 25,000/- and Funeral assistance of ₹ 5,000/- 1. In the case of accidental death: ₹ 1,00,00/- and Funeral assistance of ₹ 5,000/- 1. In case of permanent disability: ₹ 75,000/-

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Exclusions / Not eligible:** - Funeral assistance and ex-gratia payments are not offered for deaths resulting from intentional suicide, the consumption of intoxicating substances, or fatalities resulting from unlawful altercations intended to commit a crime.

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Labour Department.
