# Medhavi Chhatr/Chhatra Shiksha Protsahan Yojana

**Short Title:** MCCSPY

**Tags:** Financial Assistance, Scholarship, Student Finance, Labor

Chhattisgarh &quot;Medhaavee Chhaatr/ Chhaatra Shiksha Protsaahan Yojana&quot; has been started by the Government of Chhattisgarh and operated by the Labor Department of Chhattisgarh. Benefits will be provided to applicants studying in 10th, 12th, graduation, post graduation, and some selected courses.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Education & Learning; Sector: Scholarships and student finance

**Where it applies:** Chhattisgarh; State

For the boys and girls who are in the top ten of merit in the state level examination of class 10th and 12th held₹ 1,00,00/-₹ 1,00,000/-Government ITI, IIT, Engineering, Medical, Law, Dental, and Nursing.
