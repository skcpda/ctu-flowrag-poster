# Dulari Scheme

**Short Title:** DS-A&N

**Launch Date:** 2013-01-26

**Tags:** Girl Child, New Born, Woman, Scholarship, Financial Assistance

The “Dulari Scheme” was launched by the Health Department, Union Territory of Andaman and Nicobar Islands on 26th January, 2013 to give incentives to newborn baby girls and girl children on passing VIII, X &amp;amp; XII standards. As per the present scheme, the girl child born on or after 26.01.2013 will be eligible to get the benefit under the scheme for birth incentives and the girl children who are below 13 years of age not registered under the Planned family scheme (old scheme) will also get the benefit of this scheme.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Education & Learning; Sector: Scholarships and student finance

**Where it applies:** Andaman and Nicobar Islands; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** As per the scheme, the following benefits are given to the girl child whose parents are domicile of Andaman &amp;amp; Nicobar Islands: &lt;br&gt; **Sl. No.****Descriptions****Amount of Assistance**1Benefits at Birth- Institution deliver₹8,000/-2Benefits at Birth- Home delivery₹5,000/-3Benefits on passing VIII Standard₹5,000/-4Benefits on passing X Standard₹10,000/-5Benefits on passing XII Standard₹10,000/-6Benefits on getting married at the age of 21 years₹5,000/-7Benefits on getting married after age of 25 years₹10,000/-&lt;br&gt; &gt; **Note**- The incentive amount will be fixed in a bank account and must not be withdrawn before the girl attains 18 years of age, unless permitted by the A&amp;amp;N Administration. &lt;br&gt;

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Exclusions / Not eligible:** > **Benefits will cease if:** 1. The applicant or her parents provide false or concealed information. 1. The girl gets married before attaining 21 years of age. 1. The girl drops out of school (as further education-based incentives will not be granted). 1. In case of the girl’s death before reaching the eligible disbursal age, the Directorate of Health Services may permit premature closure of the deposit, and the applicable amount may be released to the parents.

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: Directorate Of Health Services, Andaman & Nicobar Island; Nodal Department: Health Department.
