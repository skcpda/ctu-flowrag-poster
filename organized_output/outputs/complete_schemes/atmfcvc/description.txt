# Assistance in Trellise Mandap for Cultivation of Vegetable Crops

**Short Title:** ATMFCVC

**Tags:** Agriculture, Vegetable Farming, Trellis Mandap, Farming Infrastructure

The scheme &amp;quot;Assistance in Trellise Mandap for Cultivation of Vegetable Crops&amp;quot; by the Agriculture and Co-operation Department, Government of Gujarat, aims to promote sustainable and structured vegetable crop cultivation by extending financial assistance to eligible farmers for building kachha, semi-pucca, and pucca trellis mandaps using materials like wood, bamboo, GI wire, and plastic wire. This initiative not only supports effective vegetable cultivation techniques but also improves the productivity and longevity of vegetable crops grown on trellises. 

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Agriculture,Rural & Environment; Sector: Financial assistance

**Where it applies:** Gujarat; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** > **Monetary Assistance:** - **General Farmers:** 50% of unit cost or ₹80,000/- per hectare- **SC/ST Farmers:** 75% of unit cost or ₹1,20,000/- per hectare- **Devipoojak Farmers:** 90% of cost or ₹46,800/- per hectare > **Input Support:** - **Wood/Bamboo Supports:** 1,600 units per hectare- **GI Wire (12–18 gauge) or Plastic Wire:** 400 kg per hectare- **Recommended Plant Spacing:** 2.5 x 2.5 meters (approximate) > **Coverage Limit:** - **Maximum Area:** 2.00 hectares per Khata **Validity:** - **Assistance duration:** 3 years 

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Agriculture, Farmers Welfare and Cooperation Department.

**References / Annexures:** - **Guidelines (Scheme No. 72)**: https://mariyojana.gujarat.gov.in/Schemeatoz.aspx

**Additional Information:**
Briefdescription: The scheme "Assistance in Trellise Mandap for Cultivation of Vegetable Crops" aims to promote structured vegetable farming through the provision of financial assistance for constructing kachha/semi-pucca/pucca trellis mandaps to all farmers across the state.
