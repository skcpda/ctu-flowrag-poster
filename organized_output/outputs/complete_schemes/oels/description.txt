# Overseas Educational Loan Scheme

**Short Title:** OELS

**Tags:** Laon, Education, Student, Financial Assistance

The &quot;Overseas Educational Loan Scheme&quot; was launched by the Minorities Welfare Department, Government of Karnataka, and implemented by the Karnataka Minorities Development Corporation. The scheme objective is to provide financial/loan assistance to students belonging to minority communities who are pursuing graduate or postgraduate degree courses in recognized universities abroad.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Education & Learning; Sector: Loan

**Where it applies:** Karnataka; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - The loan amount will be provided up to ₹20,00,000/- at a zero interest rate.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: Karnataka Minorities Development Corporation; Nodal Department: Minorities Welfare Department.

**References / Annexures:** - **Guidelines**: https://kmdc.karnataka.gov.in/36/overseas-educational-loan/en - **Login & Apply**: https://kmdconline.karnataka.gov.in/Portal/login - **Track Application**: https://kmdconline.karnataka.gov.in/Portal/track
