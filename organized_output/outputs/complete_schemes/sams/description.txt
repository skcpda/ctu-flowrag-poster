# Supply of Agriculture Machineries Scheme

**Short Title:** SAMS

**Tags:** Farmer, Agriculture Machineries, Subsidy

The “Supply of Agriculture Machineries Scheme” was launched by the Department of Agriculture &amp;amp; Farmers’ Welfare, Government of Meghalaya for the distribution and sale of Agriculture Machineries such as Power Tillers, etc. to farmers at a subsidized rate.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Agriculture,Rural & Environment

**Where it applies:** Meghalaya; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** > **Pattern of Assistance:** - Subsidy on Power tillers, Reapers, Harvesters, etc. > **Type of Assistance and Entitlement (If Any):** - 50% subsidy sale.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: Department Of Agriculture & Farmers’ Welfare, Government Of Meghalaya; Nodal Department: Agriculture Department.

**References / Annexures:** - **Official Website**: https://megagriculture.gov.in/PUBLIC/schemes_ShowSchemesA.aspx?schid=80

**Additional Information:**
Briefdescription: The objective of the scheme is to distribute and sale of agricultural machinery such as Power Tillers, etc. to farmers at subsidized rates.
