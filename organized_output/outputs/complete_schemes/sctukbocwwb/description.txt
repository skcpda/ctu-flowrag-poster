# Financial Assistance for Construction of Toilet (UKBOCWWB)

**Short Title:** FACT(UKBOCWWB)

**Tags:** Toilet, Construction Worker, Labour, Building Worker, Financial Assistance

The “Financial Assistance for Construction of Toilet” scheme is implemented by the Uttarakhand Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board (UKBOCWWB) under the Labour Department, Government of Uttarakhand. The scheme aims to provide financial support to registered construction workers for building toilets. The financial assistance is disbursed in two installments. 

**Who is this for:** Family; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Financial assistance

**Where it applies:** Uttarakhand; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** &gt; Under the scheme, registered construction workers receive financial assistance of ₹12,000/- in two installments for the construction of toilets. - **First Installment:** ₹8,000/- - **Second Installment:** ₹4,000/- 

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: Uttarakhand Building And Other Construction Workers’ Welfare Board; Nodal Department: Department Of Labour.

**References / Annexures:** - **Scheme Details (Page No. 54)**: https://uk.gov.in/department92/library_file/file-04-12-2023-06-02-23.pdf - **Official Website**: https://ukbocw.uk.gov.in/

**Additional Information:**
Briefdescription: The scheme provides financial assistance of up to ₹12,000/- to registered construction workers for building toilets. The assistance is disbursed in two installments: the first installment of ₹8,000/- and the second installment of ₹4,000/-.
