# Ramai Awas (Gharkul) Scheme for SC & Nav-Buddha (Urban and Rural)

**Short Title:** RASSNB

**Tags:** Housing, Scheduled Caste, Nav Buddha, Urban, Rural

The scheme &quot;Ramai Awas (Gharkul) Scheme for SC &amp; Nav-Buddha (Urban and Rural)&quot; is a housing scheme by the Department of Social Justice &amp; Special Assistance of the Government of Maharashtra. The scheme aims to raise the standard of living of the individuals from the Scheduled Caste and Nav-Buddha communities residing in rural &amp; urban areas of Maharashtra.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Housing & Shelter; Sector: Housing

**Where it applies:** Maharashtra; State

**Benefits / Assistance:** The benefits vary with the area that the beneficiary resides in - - **Rural Area:** ₹ 1,00,000/- (Beneficiary&#39;s share is Nil)- **Municipal Area:** ₹ 1,50,000/- (Beneficiary&#39;s share is 7.5%)- **Municipal Corporation:** ₹ 2,00,000/- (Beneficiary&#39;s share is 10%)

**Benefit Type:** In Kind

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Social Justice and Special Assistance Department.

**References / Annexures:** - **Official Website**: https://sjsa.maharashtra.gov.in/en/ramai-awas-gharkul-scheme-sc-nav-buddha-urban-and-rural
