# Goa State Best Library and Best Librarian Award

**Short Title:** GSBLBLA

**Tags:** Library, Librarian, Award, Culture

The scheme &quot;Goa State Best Library and Best Librarian Award&quot; has been designed by the Directorate of Art and Culture, Government of Goa envisaging grant of awards for one meritorious Library and one Librarian serving either in any Government or Government–aided Libraries in the State of Goa, which include the Public/Academic/Special/Research Libraries every year.  > **Objective** To recognize the hard work put in by the management of the Libraries and the Librarians and to give an impetus and encouragement to them by conferring awards on them for their contribution in the field of library movement.

**Who is this for:** Individual, University / Institution; Category: Sports & Culture; Sector: Culture

**Where it applies:** Goa; State

**Benefits / Assistance:** > **Nature and Quantum of Assistance**  **Goa State Best Library Award** A cash prize of ₹ 50,000/-, a Memento, a Citation and a Certificate.  **Goa State Best Librarian Award** A cash prize of ₹ 25,000/-, a Memento, a Citation and a Certificate.  > **Number of Awards** Every year one Library and one Librarian will be selected for the award.

**Benefit Type:** Composite

**Exclusions / Not eligible:** **The award shall not be conferred posthumously.** However, in the event of death of the individual after recommendation of his/her name for the award by the Award Committee, the award shall be conferred posthumously. Further, any illustrious service of any individual in the field of library movement shall also be recognized and considered for the award not beyond one year of death or retirement of the individual.

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Art, Culture and Museums Department.
