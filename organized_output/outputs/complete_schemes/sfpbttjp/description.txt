# Scheme for Pension Benefits to the Journalists/Photojournalists

**Short Title:** SFPBTTJP

**Tags:** Journalist, Pension, Financial Assistance, Retired, Senior Citizen

&quot;Scheme for Pension Benefits to the Journalists/photojournalists&quot; was launched by the Department of Information and Cultural Affairs, Government of Tripura. The objective is to provide financial assistance in the form of a Pension to accredited Journalists/ Photojournalists aged 60 years and above. The application should be submitted to the Director of Information and Cultural Affairs Department, Government of Tripura.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Pension

**Where it applies:** Tripura; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** Pension of ₹10,000/- per month.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Information & Cultural Affairs Department.

**References / Annexures:** - **Guidelines & Application Form**: https://ica.tripura.gov.in/sites/default/files/2022-11/pensionsceme_for_journalists.pdf - **Amendment (2018)**: https://ica.tripura.gov.in/sites/default/files/pension_benifits_of_journalists.pdf
