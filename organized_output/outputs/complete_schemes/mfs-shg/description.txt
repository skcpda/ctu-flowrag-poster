# Microcredit Finance Scheme- Self Help Groups (DAADC)

**Short Title:** MFS-SHG

**Tags:** SHGs, Loan, Self-employment, Business, Unemployment

The &quot;Microcredit Finance Scheme- Self Help Groups&quot; is implemented by the Dr. Ambedkar Antyodaya Development Corporation (DAADC) under the Social Justice and Empowerment Department, Government of Gujarat. It aims to promote self-employment among Scheduled Caste (SC) individuals and Antyodaya Self-Help Groups by providing microloans for starting small-scale businesses. Under this scheme, unemployed individuals from SC communities receive financial assistance to become self-reliant through entrepreneurship.  

**Who is this for:** Self Help Groups (SHGS); Category: Business & Entrepreneurship

**Where it applies:** Gujarat; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - **Loan Amount:** Up to ₹50,000/- (maximum ₹5,00,000/-)- **Interest Rate:** 6% annually- **Loan Tenure:** 36 months (3 years)- **State Government Share:** 95% (₹47,500/-)- **Beneficiary Share:** 5% (₹2,500/-)- **Guarantor:** Not required- **Possible Installment amount :** ₹1,445/- per month. 

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Social Justice and Empowerment Department.

**References / Annexures:** - **Guidelines**: https://sje.gujarat.gov.in/gapb/assets/downloads/micro_fincial_2020.pdf - **Scheme Details**: https://mariyojana.gujarat.gov.in/MoreDetails.aspx - **Official Website**: https://sje.gujarat.gov.in/gapb/Schemes?lang=English

**Additional Information:**
Briefdescription: The scheme aims to promote self-employment for SC individuals. It offers microloans to start small businesses and become economically self-sufficient.
