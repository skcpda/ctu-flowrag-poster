# Schemes for welfare of School Children belonging to BPL and BC(A) families: Monthly Stipend for BPL Students

**Short Title:** WCBPLS

**Launch Date:** 2009-03-09

**Tags:** BPL, Student, Stipend, Scholarship

The scheme &amp;quot;Schemes for Welfare of School Children Belonging to BPL and BC(A) families: Monthly Stipend for BPL Students&amp;quot; was launched by the Government of Haryana during the financial year 2009-10. It aims to provide financial assistance to students from Below Poverty Line (BPL) families to support their education. Under this scheme, a monthly stipend is provided to students from BPL families studying in classes 1 to 12 in Haryana Government schools.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Education & Learning; Sector: Scholarships and student finance

**Where it applies:** Haryana; State/ UT

**DBT Scheme:** Yes (Direct Benefit Transfer)

**Benefits / Assistance:** **A. For Girl Students:** 1. Class 1 to 5: ₹150/- per month 1. Class 6 to 8: ₹200/- per month 1. Class 9 to 12: ₹300/- per month 1. Class 11 to 12 **(Science Students only)**: ₹400/- per month **A. For Boy Students:** 1. Class 1 to 5: ₹75/- per month 1. Class 6 to 8: ₹100/- per month 1. Class 9 to 12: ₹150/- per month 1. Class 11 to 12 **(Science Students only)**: ₹200/- per month > **Mode of Disbursement:** > Direct transfer to students&#39; bank accounts. > **Frequency of Disbursement:** > Monthly basis. > **Expected Time of Disbursement:** > Funds to be transferred at the beginning of each quarter. > **Validity:** > Applicable for the duration of the academic session. > Attendance criteria: Minimum 50% attendance required for next installment

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Exclusions / Not eligible:** 1. The applicant who does not maintain 50% attendance will not receive the next instalment. 1. The applicant not studying in a Haryana Government school is ineligible.

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: School Education Department, Haryana; Nodal Department: School Education Department.
