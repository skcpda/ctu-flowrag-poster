# Operation of SC Senior Hostel

**Short Title:** OOSSH

**Tags:** Hostel, Scheduled Caste, College, Student

The scheme &quot;Operation of SC Senior Hostel&quot; by the Scheduled Caste Welfare Department, Government of Madhya Pradesh aims to provide accommodations and a conducive learning environment for Scheduled Caste students. Through this scheme, separate junior, senior, and college hostels with free boarding and lodging facilities are provided to both boys and girls. The applications for this scheme must be submitted within one month after the fixed date for school admissions. The scheme is implemented at the state level through Divisional District Officers.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Housing & Shelter; Sector: Hostel service

**Where it applies:** Madhya Pradesh; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** **Accommodation** - Free boarding facilities - Free lodging facilities - Separate hostels for boys and girls - Junior, senior, and college-level accommodations  **Additional Facilities** - Sports equipment - Toilet kits - Library facilities - Scholarship (90% paid to joint account of Hostel Superintendent and Mess Secretary, 10% to student&#39;s account)

**Benefit Type:** Composite

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Tribal Welfare and Scheduled Caste (SC) Department.
