# The Andaman & Nicobar Islands Allowance to Destitute Women Scheme

**Short Title:** ANIADWS

**Tags:** Allowance, Destitute, Woman, Financial Assistance

The scheme “The Andaman &amp;amp; Nicobar Islands Allowance to Destitute Women Scheme” is implemented by the Directorate of Social Welfare, Union Territory of Andaman and Nicobar Islands to provide financial assistance to the poorest and destitute women in the Andaman and Nicobar Islands.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Financial assistance

**Where it applies:** Andaman and Nicobar Islands; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** 1. The quantum of Allowance to Destitute Women shall be ₹2500/- per month subject to eligibility. 1. Payment of allowance shall be made through Bank Account on a monthly basis.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: Directorate Of Social Welfare, Union Territory Of Andaman And Nicobar Islands; Nodal Department: Social Welfare Department.
