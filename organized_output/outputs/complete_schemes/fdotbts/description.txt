# Free Distribution of Text Books to Students

**Short Title:** FDOTBTS

**Launch Date:** 2008-01-01

**Tags:** Student, Book, School, Education

Launched on 1st January 2008, the scheme &quot;Free Distribution of Textbooks to Students&quot; by the School Education Department, Government of Madhya Pradesh, provides free textbooks to students studying in classes 9th to 12th in government schools.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Education & Learning; Sector: Resources for schools

**Where it applies:** Madhya Pradesh; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** Free textbooks.

**Benefit Type:** In Kind

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: School Education Department.
