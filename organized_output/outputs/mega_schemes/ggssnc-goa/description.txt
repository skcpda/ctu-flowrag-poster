# Government of Goa State Scholarship to NDA Cadets

**Short Title:** GGSSNC-GOA

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Cadet, Defence, NDA, Scholarship, Financial Assistance

The scheme &amp;quot;State Scholarship to NDA Cadets&amp;quot; is a Scholarship Scheme by the Directorate of Higher Education, Government of Goa. The objective of the scheme is to support NDA cadets from humble financial backgrounds undergoing training at the National Defence Academy, Khadakwasla, Pune. The scheme provides financial assistance to the underprivileged cadets hailing from Goa who have opted to serve the country by joining the Defence Services. The scheme aims to attract cadets from economically disadvantaged sections of society opting to serve the country by taking up such challenging jobs/training.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Education & Learning; Sector: Scholarships and student finance

**Where it applies:** Goa; State

**Benefits / Assistance:** 1. **Scholarship of ₹ 1,000/- per month** during the period of training (subject to a maximum of 24 months or duration of the course whichever is less).1. **One Time Outfit Allowance of ₹ 12,000/-.**

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Higher Education Department.
