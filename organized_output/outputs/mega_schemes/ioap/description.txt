# INDIRAMMA Old Age Pension (Rural)

**Short Title:** IOAP

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Pension, Senior Citizen, Social Welfare, Financial Assistance, Old Age

Launched in 2006-07, the scheme &quot;INDIRAMMA Old Age Pension (Rural)&quot; by the Department of Rural Development, Government of Andhra Pradesh, aims to disburse monthly pensions to old age citizens, both male and female, who are 65 years of age or above and are destitute (with little or no means of subsistence and no family or relative to depend upon). To be eligible, they should be from a BPL family, be a resident of the district, and not be covered under any other Pension Scheme. The eligible citizens will be identified by their respective Gram Sabhas. The Panchayat Secretary disburses the pension to the eligible citizens on the first date of every month.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Pension

**Where it applies:** Andhra Pradesh; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** **Pension Amount:** The scale of benefit under the pension will be notified by the Government of Andhra Pradesh. **Mode of Disbursement:** The Panchayat Secretary disburses the pension to the eligible citizens, in the presence of Sarpanch, Gram Panchayat Members and the Office Bearers of Village Organization. **Time of Disbursement:** The pension is disbursed on the first date of every month.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Panchayat Raj Rural Employment.
