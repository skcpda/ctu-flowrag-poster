# Scheme For Subsidy On Interest For Establishment Of 1 To 20 Milch Animal Farm For Scheduled Tribe

**Short Title:** SSIEMAFST

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Animal Husbandry, Scheduled Tribe, Cattle Farming, Subsidy, Loan

The scheme “Scheme for Subsidy on Interest for Establishment of 1 to 20 Milch Animal Farm for Scheduled Tribe” was started by the Dept. of Agriculture, Farmers Welfare &amp;amp; Co-operation, Government of Gujarat. Under the scheme, the small and poor farmers of the Scheduled Tribe category of Gujarat State can purchase 1 to 20 animals as per their need and capacity to maintain. The eligible beneficiary can get an interest subsidy @ 12 % interest on a bank loan amount for 5 years if he/she has obtained a loan for setting up the unit in the current financial year 2023-24 through a nationalized bank or a financial institution approved by the Reserve Bank of India. It also provides good-quality organic manure for improving crop fertility &amp; crop yields. Dung is also used as fuel for domestic purposes. The surplus fodder agricultural by-products are utilized by animals &amp; converted into value-added products viz. Milk, Meat, etc. 

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Agriculture,Rural & Environment; Sector: Animal husbandry

**Where it applies:** Gujarat; State

**Benefits / Assistance:** 1. The beneficiary can purchase 1 to 20 Milch animals (Any dairy animal cow &amp;amp; buffalo) at a subsidized rate as per their need and capacity to maintain. 1. The applicant can get an interest subsidy @ 12 % interest on the bank loan amount for 5 years. **Note 01:** The interest calculated by the bank or a maximum of 12% on the unit cost of the animal or the loan per unit advanced by the bank to purchase the animal, whichever is lower. **Note 02:** The amount of interest subvention will be deposited in the beneficiary&amp;#39;s loan account in the form of a Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) in installments. There will be no cash assistance for any of the beneficiaries.

**Benefit Type:** In Kind

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: Directorate Of Animal Husbandry, Government Of Gujarat; Nodal Department: Agriculture, Farmers Welfare and Cooperation Department.
