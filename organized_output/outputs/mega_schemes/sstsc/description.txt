# Sanctioning Spectacles to Senior Citizens

**Short Title:** SSTSC

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Senior Citizens, Spectacles, Empowerment

The A.P. Differently Abled &amp; Senior Citizens Assistance Corporation, Andhra Pradesh, launched the scheme &quot;Sanctioning Spectacles to Senior Citizens&quot; under its Action Plan 2017-18, aims to provide spectacles to senior citizens. The spectacles are distributed by the Assistant Directors, Department for the Welfare of Differently Abled &amp; Senior Citizens, and District Managers, A.P. Differently Abled &amp; Senior Citizens Assistance Corporation.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Aids/Appliances

**Where it applies:** Andhra Pradesh; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** > **Distribution of Daisy Players.** - Category I: Parent/Guardian income up to ₹15,000/- per month – full cost covered. - Category II: Parent/Guardian income from ₹15,001 to ₹20,000/- per month – half of the cost covered. - Category III: Parent/Guardian income above ₹20,000/- per month – full cost to be borne by the beneficiary.

**Benefit Type:** In Kind

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Dept of Social Welfare, WCD.

**References / Annexures:** - **Guidelines**: https://apdascac.ap.gov.in/Schemes/schemedetails/7 - **Official Website**: https://apdascac.ap.gov.in/

**Additional Information:**
Briefdescription: The "Sanctioning Spectacles to Senior Citizens" scheme aims to provide spectacles to senior citizens. This initiative ensures that eligible senior citizens receive the necessary support to improve their vision and enhance their quality of life.
