# Social Security Pension Scheme

**Short Title:** SSPS

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Social Security, Pension, PwD, Dwarf, BPL, Disabled

The &amp;quot;Social Security Pension Scheme&amp;quot; was launched by the Social Welfare Department, Government of Chhattisgarh. Under this scheme, financial assistance is provided to Below Poverty Line (BPL) Persons with Disabilities (PwDs) and dwarf individuals to support them in living a respectful life.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Pension

**Where it applies:** Chhattisgarh; State/ UT

**DBT Scheme:** Yes (Direct Benefit Transfer)

**Benefits / Assistance:** - Under this scheme, eligible beneficiaries will receive a pension of ₹500/- per month. **Note:** The pension amount will be directly transferred to the applicant&amp;#39;s bank account.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: Social Welfare Department, Government Of Chhattisgarh; Nodal Department: Social Welfare Department.
