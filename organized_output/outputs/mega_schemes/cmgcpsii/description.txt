# Chief Minister’s Girl Child Protection Scheme-II

**Short Title:** CMGCPSII

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Girl Child, Financial Assistance, Family, Poor, Education

The &#39;Chief Minister’s Girl Child Protection Scheme-I&#39; was launched by the Social Welfare and Women Empowerment Department, Government of Tamil Nadu. The scheme aims to provide financial assistance to girl children of poor families as well as to promote girl child education, small family norms, increase the child sex ratio, discourage the preference for male child &amp; eradicate female infanticide.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Women and Child; Sector: Childcare

**Where it applies:** Tamil Nadu; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - A fixed deposit (FD) of ₹25,000/- each in the name of the two girl children (A copy of the FD receipt is given to the family). - Renewal of FD after five years. - An annual incentive of ₹1,800/- from the 6th year of the FD. - The amount deposited along with accrued interest is given to the girl child when she attains 18 years of age. **NOTE:** The matured amount will be given to the girl child only if she appears for the 10th standard public exam.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Department of Social Welfare and Women Empowerment.
