# West Bengal Handloom and Khadi Weavers Financial Benefit Scheme 2024: Supply of Yarn to Societies etc. at Subsidized Rate

**Short Title:** SYSSR

**Launch Date:** 2024-03-07

**Scheme For:** Infra

**Tags:** Handloom, Khadi Weavers, PWCS, Khadi Societies, Yarn, Subsidized Rate

“Supply of Yarn to Societies etc. at Subsidized Rate&amp;quot; is a sub-scheme under &amp;quot;West Bengal Handloom and Khadi Weavers Financial Benefit Scheme 2024”, launched by the Micro, Small &amp;amp; Medium Enterprise and Textiles Department, Government of West Bengal on 7th March, 2024, and it shall remain in force for a period of five years. The objective of this sub-scheme is to provide supply of subsidized yarn to the Primary Weavers&amp;#39; Cooperative Societies and Khadi Weavers&amp;#39; Societies etc. and Individual weavers. &lt;br&gt;

**Who is this for:** Individual, Registered Societies, Joint Liability Groups (JLGS), Self Help Groups (SHGS), Trust; Category: Business & Entrepreneurship; Sector: Setting up / start-up / entrepreneurship

**Where it applies:** West Bengal; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - TANTUJA will supply yarn to handloom societies at a 10% discounted rate. - TANTUJA will receive a 5% administrative cost from the State Government for transportation, storage, and handling. - The Khadi Society, registered under various acts, will receive a 10% discount on roving or high count cotton fibre purchased from WBKVIB. - The WBKVIB will be reimbursed by the State government for the total concession amount given to the Khadi Societies, along with a 5% administrative cost for transportation, storing, and handling charges. &gt; **Note**: This does not apply to yarn procured under the NHDP Scheme. 

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Micro, Small & Medium Enterprises and Textiles Department.
