# Varishtha Bunkar Samman Yojana

**Short Title:** VBSY

**Scheme For:** Infra

**Tags:** Handloom, Weaver, Textile, Financial Assistance, Senior Citizen

Launched in 2007-08, the scheme &quot;Varishtha Bunkar Samman Yojana&quot; by the Chhattisgarh State Handloom Development and Marketing Cooperative Union Limited, Raipur aims to encourage and honor senior weavers in the handloom sector. The scheme provides financial assistance of ₹11,000/- to senior weavers who are 60 years or older and have produced the highest amount of cloth in their district. The eligible weavers must be active members of primary weaver cooperative societies affiliated with the State Handloom Union. The scheme is implemented by the Chhattisgarh State Handloom Development and Marketing Cooperative Union Limited, Raipur.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Financial assistance

**Where it applies:** Chhattisgarh; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** Financial assistance of ₹11,000/- is provided.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Commerce & Industries Department.
