# Thayi Magu Sahaya Hastha (K.B.O.C.W.W.B)

**Short Title:** TMSHKBOCWWB

**Scheme For:** Family

**Tags:** Labour, Building Worker, Construction Worker, Pre-school, Education, Child, Women

The &amp;quot;Thayi Magu Sahaya Hastha&amp;#39;&amp;#39; by the BOCW Welfare Board, Department of Labour, Karnataka, is a welfare scheme for the registered workers of the board. Under this Scheme, financial support shall be provided to registered women construction workers for pre-school education and nutritional support of their child for up to 3 years after delivery. Assistance can be availed for up to 2 children. &lt;br&gt;

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Education & Learning; Sector: Early childhood

**Where it applies:** Karnataka; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - **Financial Assistance:** ₹6,000/- (at the rate of ₹500/- per month).

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Labour Department.

**References / Annexures:** - **Guidelines**: https://kbocwwb.karnataka.gov.in/thayimagu - **Guidelines (For Registration)**: https://karbwwb.karnataka.gov.in/42/schemes/en - **User Manual**: https://kbocwwb.karnataka.gov.in/static/media/user_manual_eng.c0bc6338.pdf
