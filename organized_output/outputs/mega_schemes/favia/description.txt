# Financial Assistance To Visually Impaired Advocates

**Short Title:** FAVIA

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Advocate, Visually Impaired, Financial Assistance, Court, Blind

The scheme “Financial Assistance to visually impaired Advocates” was launched by the Department of Social Justice, Government of Kerala. Under the scheme, financial assistance will be provided to visually impaired Advocates who are practicing in any one or more Courts in Kerala. The applicant under this scheme shall be eligible for an exgratia non-recurring grant for the purchase of books, provisional suits, etc., and they are being paid a reader&amp;#39;s allowance for a period of 10 years.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Financial assistance

**Where it applies:** Kerala; State

**Benefits / Assistance:** 1. Under the scheme, the Visually Impaired Advocates shall be eligible for an exgratia non-recurring grant of ₹3500/- for the purchase of books, provisional suits, etc. 1. The Visually Impaired Advocates are also being paid a reader&#39;s allowance @ ₹5000/- per month for a period of 10 years.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: District Social Justice Office, Kerala; Nodal Department: Social Justice Department.
