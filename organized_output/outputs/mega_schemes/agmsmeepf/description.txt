# Aatmanirbhar Gujarat Scheme for assistance to MSMEs: EPF Reimbursement to MSMEs

**Short Title:** AGMSMEEPF

**Launch Date:** 2022-10-05

**Scheme For:** Infra

**Tags:** Business, Entrepreneurship, MSMEs, Reimbursement

The &amp;quot;Aatmanirbhar Gujarat Scheme for MSMEs&amp;quot; is an umbrella scheme launched by the Industries and Mines Department, Gujarat. It aims to empower the state&amp;#39;s dynamic MSME sector, which significantly contributes to employment, GDP, and exports. Effective from October 5, 2022, to October 4, 2027, this initiative supports growth, investments, and the vision of &amp;quot;Aatmanirbhar Gujarat&amp;quot; and &amp;quot;Aatmanirbhar Bharat.&amp;quot; The component &amp;quot;Financial Support to MSMEs in ZED Certification&amp;quot; provides reimbursement of the employer&amp;#39;s statutory contribution under the Employees&amp;#39; Provident Fund (EPF) to eligible MSMEs for their new employees in Gujarat. &lt;br&gt;

**Who is this for:** Business Entity, Industries; Category: Business & Entrepreneurship; Sector: Setting up / start-up / entrepreneurship

**Where it applies:** Gujarat; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** 1. **100% Reimbursement:** Covers 100% of the employer’s statutory EPF contribution for new employees. 1. **Maximum Limit:** Reimbursement capped at 12% of basic salary plus Dearness Allowance (DA) or ₹1800 per employee per month, whichever is lower. 1. **Extended Support:** Assistance is available for up to 10 years from the date of commercial production.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Exclusions / Not eligible:** - MSMEs that have availed EPF reimbursement under any scheme of the central/state government, or any agency of the central/ state government, shall not be eligible for EPF reimbursement under this scheme for that period. &lt;br&gt;

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Industries and Mines Department.
