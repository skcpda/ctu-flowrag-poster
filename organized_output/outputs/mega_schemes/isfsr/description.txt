# Incentive Scheme for Sericulture Reelers

**Short Title:** ISFSR

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Laon, Minority Community, Subsidy, Financial Assitance

The &quot;Incentive Scheme for Sericulture Reelers&quot; was launched by the Minorities Welfare Department, Government of Karnataka, and is implemented by the Karnataka Minorities Development Corporation. The scheme aims to improve the financial condition of minority communities involved in silk reeling and spinning by providing financial assistance through loans and subsidies. This support is intended to enhance working capital, improve basic infrastructure, and promote better productivity and economic stability.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Business & Entrepreneurship; Sector: Loan

**Where it applies:** Karnataka; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** > **Financial Assistance:** - Silk reeling/spinning workers can receive a loan of up to ₹2,00,000/-, with 50% of the loan amount being provided as a subsidy.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: Karnataka Minorities Development Corporation; Nodal Department: Minorities Welfare Department.
