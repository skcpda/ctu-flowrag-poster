# Medical Assistance (MBOCWWB)

**Short Title:** MA(MBOCWWB)

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Medical, Building Worker, Construction Worker, Financial Assistance, Labour, Accident, Disease

The scheme “Medical Assistance” is a social security scheme, implemented by the Meghalaya Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board (MBOCWWB), Labour Department, Government of Meghalaya. Under the scheme, the Board shall provide financial assistance to registered construction workers who are hospitalized for five or more days due to an accident or any disease.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Health & Wellness; Sector: Financial assistance

**Where it applies:** Meghalaya; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** 1. The financial assistance shall be ₹200/- for first 5 days and ₹20/- each for the remaining days subject to a minimum ₹1,000/-. 1. This assistance shall also be given to the beneficiary who meets with an accident and is put in plaster at the residence. 1. If disability resulted due to an accident, the worker shall be eligible for financial assistance upto a maximum ₹5,000/- depending upon the percentage of disability.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: Meghalaya Building & Other Construction Workers Welfare Board; Nodal Department: Labour and Employment Department.
