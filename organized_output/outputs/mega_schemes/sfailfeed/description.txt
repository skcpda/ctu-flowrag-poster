# Scheme for Financial Assistance to Integrated Logistics Facilities: Exemption of Electricity Duty

**Short Title:** SFAILFEED

**Launch Date:** 2021-08-02

**Closing Date:** 2026-08-01

**Scheme For:** Infra

**Tags:** Logistics Facilities, Electricity Duty

The &amp;quot;Scheme for Financial Assistance to Integrated Logistics Facilities&amp;quot; is an umbrella scheme launched by the Industries and Mines Department, Gujarat under the Gujarat Integrated Logistics and Logistics Park Policy. Effective from August 2nd, 2021 to August 1st, 2026, this initiative aims to provide financial assistance to the private developer for developing Logistics Facilities and Logistics Park with all required infrastructure and logistic services. The component &amp;quot;Exemption of Electricity Duty&amp;quot; is aimed at promoting industrial growth and economic development. Under this scheme, newly established units or projects can avail of electricity duty exemptions after the commencement of their operations.

**Who is this for:** Business Entity, Industries; Category: Business & Entrepreneurship; Sector: Setting up / start-up / entrepreneurship

**Where it applies:** Gujarat; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** Financial Assistance: Unit/Project is eligible for exemption of Electricity Duty from the date of Commencement of Operation.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Exclusions / Not eligible:** - If any Unit/Project is availing incentive under any other State Government scheme, it will not be eligible under this scheme.

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Industries and Mines Department.

**References / Annexures:** - **Guidelines**: https://ic.gujarat.gov.in/documents/commondoc/2024/Revised_Gui_eline_for_the_%20Scheme_for_Financial_Assistance_to_Integrated_Logistics_Facilities.pdf

Briefdescription: The "Scheme for Financial Assistance to Integrated Logistics Facilities" under the Gujarat Integrated Logistics and Logistics Park Policy, provides financial support to private developers for creating logistics facilities and parks with the necessary infrastructure and services.
