# Financial Assistance for Education of Self/ Children of Beneficiaries 

**Short Title:** FAFEOSCOB

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Labour, Building Worker, Construction Worker, Education, Study, Scholarships

The &amp;quot;Financial Assistance for Education of Self/ Children of Beneficiaries&amp;#39; scheme by the Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board, Labour Department, West Bengal, is a welfare scheme for the registered workers of the board. Under this scheme, a beneficiary registered for a continuous period of 6 months shall be entitled to get financial assistance for himself/ herself and his/ her children studying Higher Secondary and above. This benefit can be availed only twice by a single beneficiary. 

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Education & Learning; Sector: Education and training grants, fellowship, stipend

**Where it applies:** West Bengal; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:**  **Class****Amount**Madhyamik passed &amp;amp;amp;amp; Admitted to class XI ₹4,000/-Promoted to class XII ₹5,000/-H.S. passed &amp;amp;amp;amp; Admitted to Graduation ₹6,000/-Admitted to P.G. ₹8,000/-Admitted to Medical/Engineering/MBA/CA ₹30.000/-

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Labour Department.
