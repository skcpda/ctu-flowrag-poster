# Go Green Scheme- Gujarat Labour Welfare Board

**Short Title:** GGS-GLWB

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Go Green, Labour, Industrial Worker, Subsidy, Electric Two-wheelers

The “Go Green Scheme” is implemented by the Gujarat Labour Welfare Board, Labour, Skill Development &amp;amp; Employment Department, Government of Gujarat. The objective of the scheme is to encourage Labourers/Industrial Workers to purchase of battery-operated electric two-wheelers that reduce carbon emissions. It is also benefitted to Government of India&amp;#39;s &amp;quot;Green India Mission”.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Financial assistance

**Where it applies:** Gujarat; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** 1. Under the scheme, a subsidy will be provided to Labourers/Industrial Workers on the purchase of Battery-Operated Two-Wheelers, 30% of the purchase price or ₹30,000/- whichever is less. 1. In addition to this, a one-time subsidy will also be provided on the RTO Registration Tax and Road Tax of the Two-Wheeler.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: Gujarat Labour Welfare Board; Nodal Department: Labour and Employment Department.
