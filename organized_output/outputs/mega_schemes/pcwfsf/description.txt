# Pipes Carrying Water From Source To Field

**Short Title:** PCWFSF

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Agriculture, Farmer, Irrigation, Pipes

The scheme “Pipes Carrying Water From Source To Field” by the Agriculture-Farmers Welfare Department, Tamil Nadu aims to support farmers by subsidizing the cost of water-carrying pipes up to 800m. The subsidy is either 50% of the total cost or ₹15,000/- whichever is lower. The pipes can be of any material or size as required by the farmer, including PVC and HDPE. The initiative targets ensuring water access to farms.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Agriculture,Rural & Environment; Sector: Financial assistance

**Where it applies:** Tamil Nadu; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** **Financial Assistance:** Subsidy on 50% cost or ₹15,000/- for water carrying pipes upto 800m and all types of pipes(PVC HDPE etc.,) and all sizes as per the requirement of the farmer.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Agriculture Department.

**References / Annexures:** - **Guidelines**: https://www.tn.gov.in/scheme/data_view/6859

**Additional Information:**
Briefdescription: The scheme provides subsidies to farmers for installing pipes to carry water from the source to their fields, offering a 50% subsidy or up to ₹15,000/- for pipes (up to 800m), including PVC, HDPE, and others.
