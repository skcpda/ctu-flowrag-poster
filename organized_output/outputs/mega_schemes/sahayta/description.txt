# Sympathetic Assistance and Handholding for Youth Transformation and Amelioration - SAHAYTA

**Short Title:** SAHAYTA

**Scheme For:** Individual

The scheme &quot;Sympathetic Assistance And Handholding For Youth Transformation And Amelioration&quot; is a Jammu and Kashmir initiative with the aim to provide financial assistance to youth in distress for setting up gainful self-employment ventures.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Financial assistance

**Where it applies:** Jammu and Kashmir; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - The total amount of self employment assistance provided shall be ₹2,00,000/- or the actual cost of self employment unit, which is lower. - The Amoun of ₹2,00,000/- has be compulsorily utilized for the self employment generation only. Refer the list of fields covered under the scheme are mention below: 1. Manufacturing, servicing, and petty businesses for which there is considerable local demand. 1. Retail shops 1. Small IT centers and IT service centers 1. Agriculture and horticulture farms or any retail shop selling equipment/facilities connected to the fields or any incidental activity thereof. 1. Livestock farms including cattle and poultry feed retail shops 1. Tourism Agency. 1. Sericulture Farm. 1. Fish farming. 1. Manufacture/sale of biodegradable disposable items and craft paper 1. Solid waste management and incidental activities thereof. 1. Dairy farm and manufacture/sale of dairy products 1. Restaurant business 1. Food processing units 1. Flower farming and activities incidental thereto - If the total cost project cost of the self employment unit to be set up by the beneficiary exceeds ₹2,00,000/- , the amount over above 2,00,000/- shall be self financed by the applicant himself. The sanctioning authority shall facilitate the applicant to avail this component as Mudra Loan

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Mission Youth.
