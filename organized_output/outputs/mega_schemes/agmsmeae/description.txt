# Aatmanirbhar Gujarat Scheme for assistance to MSMEs: Assistance for Saving in Consumption of Energy and Water 

**Short Title:** AGMSMEAE

**Launch Date:** 2022-10-05

**Scheme For:** Infra

**Tags:** Business, Entrepreneurship, MSMEs, Energy Conservation, Water Conservation

The &amp;quot;Aatmanirbhar Gujarat Scheme for MSMEs&amp;quot; is an umbrella scheme launched by the Industries and Mines Department, Gujarat. It aims to empower the state&amp;#39;s dynamic MSME sector, which significantly contributes to employment, GDP, and exports. Effective from October 5, 2022, to October 4, 2027, this initiative supports growth, investments, and the vision of &amp;quot;Aatmanirbhar Gujarat&amp;quot; and &amp;quot;Aatmanirbhar Bharat.&amp;quot; The component &amp;quot;Assistance for Saving in Consumption of Energy and Water&amp;quot; provides financial assistance to enterprises for taking measures that reduce energy and water consumption. It aims to promote sustainable practices and enhance resource efficiency in industries by providing support for energy/water audits and equipment purchases.

**Who is this for:** Business Entity, Government Organisation, Industries; Category: Business & Entrepreneurship; Sector: Setting up / start-up / entrepreneurship

**Where it applies:** Gujarat; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** > **Energy/Water Audit Reimbursement:** - 75% of the cost of an energy/water audit, up to ₹50,000/- will be reimbursed. - Reimbursement is available once during the operative period of the scheme. > **Equipment Cost Assistance:** - 25% of the cost for equipment recommended by the audit authority, up to ₹20,00,000/-, is eligible for assistance. - Assistance is available once during the scheme&#39;s operative period. > **Energy/Water Savings Requirement:** - Assistance for equipment is available only if the energy or water savings are at least 10% of the average monthly consumption over the last 12 months before the audit.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Industries and Mines Department.
