# Maths Wizard & Spelling Genius - Uttarakhand

**Short Title:** MWSGUK

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Maths Wizard, Spelling Genius, Mathematics, English, Competition, Student

The scheme “Maths Wizard &amp;amp; Spelling Genius” is implemented under the Samagra Shiksha Programme by the Secondary Education Department, Government of Uttarakhand. The scheme aims to enhance the interest of Class 5 students in Mathematics and English by organizing competitions at the school, block, district, and state levels, and by encouraging students through rewards. 

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Education & Learning

**Where it applies:** Uttarakhand; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** Under this scheme, to enhance the interest of Class 5 students in Mathematics and English, the Government of India organized the “Maths Wizard” and “Spelling Genius” competitions at the school, block, district, and state levels under AWP-B for the year 2023-24, wherein participating students were encouraged through rewards. 

**Benefit Type:** Composite

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: Secondary Education Department, Government Of Uttarakhand; Nodal Department: Department Of Education.
