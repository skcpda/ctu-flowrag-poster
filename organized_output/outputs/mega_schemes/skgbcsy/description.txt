# Safai Karmakar Gambhir Bimari Chikitsa Sahayata Yojana

**Short Title:** SKGBCSY

**Launch Date:** 2012-04-11

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Medical Treatment, Financial Assistance, Serious Illness, Health & Wellness

Labor Department of Chhattisgarh State started this scheme &amp;amp;quot;Safai Karmakar Gambhir Bimari Chikitsa Sahayata Yojana&amp;amp;quot; for unorganized worker on 28 April 2012. The main objective of the scheme to provide the financial assistance for medical treatment.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Health & Wellness

**Where it applies:** Chhattisgarh; State

**Benefits / Assistance:** 1. Registered unorganized workers who are suffering from serious illness will be provided medical assistance of ₹ 50,000/- for their treatment. 1. Serious diseases will include kidney, cancer, sickle cell anemia, heart disease, AIDS and paralysis.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Labour Department.
