# Balika Samrithi Yojana

**Short Title:** BSY-PUDU

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Girl, Mother, Child, Financial Assistance, Women

The scheme “Balika Samrithi Yojana” by the Department of Women &amp; Child Development, Government of Pondicherry was introduced to improve the status of the girl child and provide nutritious food to the girl child. Through this scheme, assistance of ₹500 in the form of a National Savings Certificate is provided to lactating mothers. The target beneficiaries are girl children born on or after 15-8-97.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Financial assistance

**Where it applies:** Puducherry; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** Assistance of ₹500 in the form of a National Savings Certificate.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Women and Child Development Department, Puducherry.
