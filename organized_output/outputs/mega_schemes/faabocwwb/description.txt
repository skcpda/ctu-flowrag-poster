# Funeral Assistance (A.B.O.C.W.W.B)

**Short Title:** FA(ABOCWWB)

**Scheme For:** Family

**Tags:** Funeral, Death, Financial Assistance, Construction Worker, Building Worker, Labour

The scheme “Funeral Assistance” was started by the Assam Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board (A.B.O.C.W.W.B), Labour Welfare Department, Government of Assam. Under this scheme, financial assistance shall be given to the nominees/dependents of a deceased registered worker, towards funeral expenses.

**Who is this for:** Family; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Financial assistance

**Where it applies:** Assam; State/ UT

**DBT Scheme:** Yes (Direct Benefit Transfer)

**Benefits / Assistance:** 1. The financial assistance of ₹5,000/- shall be given to the nominees/dependents of a deceased registered worker, towards funeral expenses.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: Assam Building And Other Construction Workers Welfare Board; Nodal Department: Labour & Welfare Department.
