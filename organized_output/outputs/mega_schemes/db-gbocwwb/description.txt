# Death Benefit (GBOCWWB)

**Short Title:** DB-GBOCWWB

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Death, Accident, Labour, Worker, Financial Assistance

&quot;Death Benefit (GBOCWWB)&quot; is a Welfare Scheme by the Goa Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board of the Department of Labour and Employment, Government of Goa. Through this scheme, the nominee/dependant of the deceased worker is provided with financial assistance. The death should have either been natural or caused due to an accident, during the course of employment. The applications are accepted offline.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Crisis/Disaster/Accident

**Where it applies:** Goa; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - Financial Assistance of ₹30,000/-, if the death was natural. - Financial Assistance of ₹1,00,000/-, if the death was due to an accident, during the course of employment.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: Goa Building And Other Construction Workers Welfare Board; Nodal Department: Labour and Employment Department.
