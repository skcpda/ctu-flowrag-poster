# Marriage Grant to Orphan Daughter of Ex-Servicemen

**Short Title:** MGODESM

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Marriage, Orphan, Daughter, Ex-Serviceman, Financial Assistance

The &amp;quot;Marriage Grant to Orphan Daughter of Ex-Servicemen&amp;quot; scheme is implemented by the Sainik and Ardh Sainik Welfare Department, Government of Haryana. Under this scheme, financial assistance of ₹51,000/- is provided for the marriage of each dependent orphan daughter of an ex-serviceman, for up to two daughters, who are at least 18 years old.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Marriage

**Where it applies:** Haryana; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** Financial assistance of ₹51,000/‐ is given for the marriage of orphan daughters of ex-servicemen.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: Sainik And Ardh Sainik Welfare Department, Government Of Haryana; Nodal Department: Sainik and Ardh Sainik Welfare Department.
