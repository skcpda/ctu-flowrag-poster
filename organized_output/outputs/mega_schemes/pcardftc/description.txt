# Primary Cooperative Agriculture and Rural Development Bank: For Tractor Purchase

**Short Title:** PCARDFTC

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Agriculture, Loan, Farmer

The &amp;quot;Primary Cooperative Agriculture and Rural Development Bank: For Tractor Purchase&amp;quot; scheme launched by the Co-operation, Food and Consumer Protection Department, Tamil Nadu, aims to provide financial assistance for acquiring tractors, covering up to 90% of the tractor&amp;#39;s cost. The interest rate ranges from 11% to 12.25%, subject to market conditions. &lt;br&gt; 

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Agriculture,Rural & Environment; Sector: Mechanization- solar power, farming systems

**Where it applies:** Tamil Nadu; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - **Access to Loan:** Covers up to 90% of the tractor cost. - **Interest Rate:** 11% to 12.25% (subject to change). &lt;br&gt;

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Co-operation, Food and Consumer Protection Department.

**References / Annexures:** - **Guidelines**: https://www.tn.gov.in/scheme/data_view/3553

**Additional Information:**
Briefdescription: The scheme offers a specialized loan for farmers to purchase tractors, covering up to 90% of the tractor's cost.
