# Annai Teresa Ninaivu Marriage Assistance Scheme for Orphan Girls-II

**Short Title:** ATNMASFOGII

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Orphan, Girl, Marriage, Gold

The &quot;Annai Teresa Ninaivu Marriage Assistance Scheme for Orphan Girls-I&quot; was launched by the Social Welfare and Women Empowerment Department, Government of Tamil Nadu. The scheme provides financial assistance to the orphan girls for their marriage. The bride should have completed 18 years of age at the time of marriage.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Women and Child; Sector: Financial assistance

**Where it applies:** Tamil Nadu; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - Financial assistance of ₹25,000/-. - One sovereign (8gm) 22-carat gold coin for making Thirumangalyam. > **Mode of Disbursal:** Through Electronic Clearing Service.

**Benefit Type:** Composite

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Department of Social Welfare and Women Empowerment.
