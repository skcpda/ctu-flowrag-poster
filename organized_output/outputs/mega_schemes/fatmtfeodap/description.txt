# Financial Assistance to Meet the Funeral Expenses of Differently Abled Person

**Short Title:** FATMTFEODAP

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Financial Assistance, Death, Disability, Funeral

The scheme &quot;Financial Assistance to Meet the Funeral Expenses of Differently Abled Person&quot; was launched by the Department for the Welfare of Differently Abled Persons, Government of Tamil Nadu. In this scheme, financial assistance of ₹2,000/- is provided to the legal heir of the deceased differently abled person, to meet the expenses in connection with the funeral.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Financial assistance

**Where it applies:** Tamil Nadu; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** Financial assistance of ₹2,000/- is paid to the legal heir of the deceased.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Welfare of Differently Abled Persons Department.

**References / Annexures:** - **Citizen's Charter (2022)**: https://cms.tn.gov.in/sites/default/files/documents/wda_e_cc_2022_23.pdf
