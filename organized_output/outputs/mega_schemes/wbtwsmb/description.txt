# West Bengal Transport Workers’ Social Security Scheme: Maternity Benefit

**Short Title:** WBTWSMB

**Scheme For:** Family

**Tags:** Social Security, Transport Worker, Health & Wellness, Maternity Benefit, Medical Benefit, Pregnant Women

The “West Bengal Transport Workers’ Social Security Scheme: Maternity Benefit” by the Labour Department, West Bengal, provides financial assistance to a beneficiary on successful delivery of a child or miscarriage by such beneficiary or his wife. The benefit is limited to two instances per beneficiary to promote maternal health and provide support during critical times. 

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Women and Child; Sector: Pregnancy Care

**Where it applies:** West Bengal; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - **Financial Assistance:** ₹6,000/-.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Labour Department.

**References / Annexures:** - **Guidelines**: https://employmentbankwb.gov.in/social_security_scheme.php#ans8
