# "Training in Silk" Component of the "Development of Silk" Scheme

**Short Title:** TS-DS

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Skill, Training, Silk, Industry, Employment, Youth

The &quot;Training in Silk&quot; Component of the &quot;Development of Silk&quot; Scheme by the Department of Industries and Commerce, Union Territory of Puducherry, aims to revamp the silk weaving industry and generate employment. It offers comprehensive one-year training to equip individuals with the necessary skills. The objective is to develop silk and silk-based industry, to train rural youth in silk weaving and to generate employment thereby.

**Who is this for:** Category: Skills & Employment; Sector: Training and Skill Up-gradation

**Where it applies:** Puducherry; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** > **For Trainees** - Monthly stipend of ₹1500. > **For Master Trainer** - An honorarium of ₹500 per trainee per month (up to ₹5000). - A compensation of ₹500 per trainee per month for raw materials (up to ₹5000).

**Benefit Type:** Composite

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: District Industries Centre Organization.
