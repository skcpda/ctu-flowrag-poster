# Chief Minister Pension Scheme

**Short Title:** CMPS

**Launch Date:** 2018-05-31

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Pension, Senior Citizen, Widow, Abandoned Woman

The &amp;quot;Chief Minister Pension Scheme&amp;quot; was launched by the Social Welfare Department, Government of Chhattisgarh, in the financial year 2018-19. The scheme targets senior citizens, widows, and abandoned women listed on the basis of the survey list of Social, Economic and Caste Census 2011. This scheme will be an additional scheme of the current Central and State Pension Scheme.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Pension

**Where it applies:** Chhattisgarh; State/ UT

**DBT Scheme:** Yes (Direct Benefit Transfer)

**Benefits / Assistance:** - Under this scheme, eligible beneficiaries will receive a pension of ₹500/- per month. **Note:** The pension amount will be directly transferred to the applicant&amp;#39;s bank account and will be paid during the first week of each month.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: Social Welfare Department, Government Of Chhattisgarh; Nodal Department: Social Welfare Department.

**References / Annexures:** - **Guidelines**: https://sw.cg.gov.in/sites/default/files/cm-pensan-yojana.pdf - **Official Website**: https://sw.cg.gov.in/en/chief-minister-pension-scheme - **Application Form (Page No. 12)**: https://sw.cg.gov.in/sites/default/files/cm-pensan-yojana.pdf

**Additional Information:**
Briefdescription: The scheme provides a monthly pension of ₹500/- to senior citizens, widows, and abandoned women listed on the basis of the survey list of Social, Economic and Caste Census 2011 in the state.
