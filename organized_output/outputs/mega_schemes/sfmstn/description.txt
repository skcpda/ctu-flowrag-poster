# Scheme for Meritorious Students to Pursue Higher Secondary Education in the Best Schools of Tamil Nadu

**Short Title:** SFMSTN

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Minority Welfare, Scholarship, Higher Education, Financial Assistance, Higher Secondary

The &amp;quot;Scheme for Meritorious Students to Pursue Higher Secondary Education in the Best Schools of Tamil Nadu&amp;quot; scheme launched by the Backward Classes, Most Backward Classes and Minorities Welfare Department, Tamil Nadu, aims to support academic excellence by offering financial aid to 10 students from each district who have secured the highest marks in the 10th public examination. Eligible students can attend top private schools in Tamil Nadu for their higher secondary education, ensuring they receive the best resources and opportunities to further their studies.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Education & Learning; Sector: Scholarships and student finance

**Where it applies:** Tamil Nadu; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - **Financial Assistance:** Full financial assistance for pursuing higher secondary education in reputed private schools.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Backward Classes Most Backward Classes and Minorities Welfare Department.

**References / Annexures:** - **Guidelines-1**: https://www.tn.gov.in/scheme/data_view/27514 - **Guidelines-2**: https://cms.tn.gov.in/sites/default/files/schemes/bcmbcmw_2.pdf - **Community List**: https://bcmbcmw.tn.gov.in/community_list.pdf

**Additional Information:**
Briefdescription: The scheme provides financial assistance to the top 10 students from each district (based on 10th-grade public exam results) who study in government high schools, enabling them to pursue higher secondary education in select private schools of their choice across Tamil Nadu.
