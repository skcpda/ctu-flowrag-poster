# ECCE (Early Childhood Care and Education)

**Short Title:** ECCE

**Scheme For:** Infra

**Tags:** Childhood Care, Child Education, Samagra Shiksha, Child Development, Pre-School

The scheme “ECCE (Early Childhood Care and Education)” is an initiative under the Samagra Shiksha Programme, implemented by the Secondary Education Department, Government of Uttarakhand. The scheme aims to provide quality early childhood development, care, and pre-primary education to children between the ages of 3 to 6 years. The scheme also emphasizes safe and secure infrastructure, including the provision of furniture, TLM, and child-friendly facilities in co-located Anganwadi centres operating within the premises of government schools in the state. 

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Transport & Infrastructure; Sector: Infrastructure

**Where it applies:** Uttarakhand; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - Providing infrastructure facilities such as furniture, TLM, and child-friendly furniture in co-located Anganwadi centres functioning within the premises of government schools in the state. - Training of Anganwadi workers and assistants. - Ensuring pre-school readiness so that children acquire cognitive and psychological skills before admission to Class 1. 

**Benefit Type:** Composite

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: Secondary Education Department, Government Of Uttarakhand; Nodal Department: Department Of Education.
