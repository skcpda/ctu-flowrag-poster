# Incentive To Saharia Students For B.Ed

**Short Title:** ISSFB

**Launch Date:** 2005-01-01

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Education, Student, Financial Assistane, Higher Education

The basic objective of the scheme is to provide financial assistance to the students of the Saharia region for B.Ed training so that the tribal students of the Saharia region can keep their participation in the government service. In this scheme, hostel fees, scholarships (stipends), tuition fees, and stationery are given to these students. Provision has been made to give financial assistance in the form of The benefit of the scheme will be payable only for 10 months.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Education & Learning; Sector: Scholarships and student finance

**Where it applies:** Rajasthan; State

**Benefits / Assistance:** Saharia tribe students who are taking regular B.Ed training are given Rs.30,000 in the first year and Rs.30,000 in the second year. Details are given below : 1. Rs. 26,000/- for college fees and purchase of books. 1. Rs. 32,000/- for house rent and food. 1. Rs. 2,000/- for clothes. 

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Tribal Area Development Department.
