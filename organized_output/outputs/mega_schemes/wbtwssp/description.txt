# West Bengal Transport Workers’ Social Security Scheme: Pension

**Short Title:** WBTWSSP

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Social Security, Transport Worker, Monthly Assistance, Pension

The “West Bengal Transport Workers’ Social Security Scheme: Pension” by the Labour Department, is designed to support unorganized transport workers upon retirement. Eligible beneficiaries receive a monthly pension of ₹1500/-, which increases by ₹10/- for each year of enrolment beyond 5 years, provided they have been continuously enrolled in the scheme for at least 5 years. This initiative aims to ensure financial stability and dignity for transport workers during their retirement years. &lt;br&gt;

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Pension

**Where it applies:** West Bengal; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - **Pension Amount:** ₹1,500/- per month for beneficiaries aged 60 and above. - **Incremental Benefit:** Additional ₹10/- per month for every completed year of enrolment beyond five years. &lt;br&gt;

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Labour Department.
