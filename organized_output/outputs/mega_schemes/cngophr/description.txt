# PMMSY: Construction of New Grow Out Ponds - Haryana

**Short Title:** CNGOPHR

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Subsidy, Fish, Farming, PMMSY, Ponds

The scheme &quot;Pradhan Mantri Matasya Sampada Yojana: Construction of New Grow out Ponds&quot; by the Ministry Of Fisheries, Animal Husbandry &amp; Dairying, Government of India, aims to expand fisheries in various urban and semi-urban areas through the construction of new grow-out ponds. Through this scheme, subsidies are provided for the construction of new grow-out ponds to beneficiaries who are engaged in fisheries. 

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Agriculture,Rural & Environment; Sector: Land and water resources

**Where it applies:** Haryana; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - The scheme provides financial assistance for the Construction of the New Grow-out Ponds. The project cost is ₹7,00,000/- per Hectare. - The subsidy will be limited to 40% of the project cost for beneficiaries falling under the General Category. - The subsidy will be limited to 60% of the project cost for beneficiaries who are Scheduled Caste (SC) or Women. 

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Fisheries Department.
