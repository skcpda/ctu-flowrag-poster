# Award for Success in State Public Service Commission, Union Public Service Commission Examination (MPBOCWWB)

**Short Title:** AFSISPSCUPSCE

**Scheme For:** Family

**Tags:** Building, Construction, Education, Exam, Worker, Award

Under the&quot; State Public Service Commission and Union Public Service Commission Examination Success Award Scheme&quot; by the Labour Department, Government of Madhya Pradesh, the sons and daughters of registered construction workers who are selected at various levels in the State Public Service Commission or Union Public Service Commission examinations, are provided incentives ranging from ₹15,000/- to ₹50,000/-.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Education & Learning; Sector: Scholarships and student finance

**Where it applies:** Madhya Pradesh; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** **Incentives Upon Selection in &quot;Madhya Pradesh Public Service Commission&quot; Exam** - Preliminary Exam: ₹15,000/- - Main Exam: ₹25,000/- **Incentives Upon Selection in &quot;Union Public Service Commission&quot; Exam** - Preliminary Exam: ₹25,000/- - Main Exam: ₹50,000/-

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Labour Department.

**References / Annexures:** - **Guidelines**: https://labour.mp.gov.in/Public/Pages/Schemes/RajyaLokSevaAyog.aspx - **Labour Registration**: https://labour.mp.gov.in/Public/Registration/BeneficiaryRegistrationRequest.aspx

**Additional Information:**
Briefdescription: The State Public Service Commission and Union Public Service Commission Examination Success Award Scheme, 2012 provides an incentive to sons and daughters of registered construction workers, up to 45 years of age, selected in State or Union Public Service Commission exams.
