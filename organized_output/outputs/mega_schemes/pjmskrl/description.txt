# Prof. Joseph Mundassery Scholarship Award

**Short Title:** PJMSA

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Scholarship, Award, Education, College, Student

The scheme &quot;Prof. Joseph Mundassery Scholarship Award&quot; was introduced by the Department of Minority Welfare, Government of Kerala. The scheme aims to provide financial assistance to financially backward students from minority communities. Through this scheme, financial assistance in the form of scholarship amounts is provided to eligible students from Christian, Buddhist, Jain, Sikh, Muslim, and Parsi communities to pursue higher education. The scheme is implemented by the Department of Minority Welfare, Government of Kerala. 

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Education & Learning; Sector: Scholarships and student finance

**Where it applies:** Kerala; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - The students who score an A+ in Secondary School Leaving Certificate (SSLC)/Plus Two (12) or Technical High School Leaving Certificate(THSL) will get ₹10,000/-. - The undergraduate students with 80% marks will get ₹15,000/-. - The postgraduate student with 75% marks will get ₹15,000/-. 

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Directorate of Minority Welfare.
