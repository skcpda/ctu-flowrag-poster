# Shiksha Protsaahan Puraskaar yojana

**Short Title:** SPPY

**Launch Date:** 2018-01-01

**Scheme For:** Family

**Tags:** Education, Student, Awards, Financial

The Shiksha Protsahan Puraskar Yojana (Shram Kalyan Mandal) is a scheme launched by the Madhya Pradesh government to provide financial assistance to the children of registered workers of the Madhya Pradesh Shram Kalyan Mandal for their higher education.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Education & Learning; Sector: Scholarships and student finance

**Where it applies:** Madhya Pradesh; State/ UT

**DBT Scheme:** Yes (Direct Benefit Transfer)

**Benefits / Assistance:** 1. General Graduate (B.A., B.Com, B.Sc., B.B.A.) : ₹10,000/- 1. Engineering (B.E./B.Tech.) : ₹15,000/- 1. M.B.B.S./ B.D.S. : ₹25,000/- 1. Pharmacy, Ayurvedic, Homeopathic (U.G., P.G. Diploma) : ₹12,500/- 1. Law Courses : ₹10,000/- 1. General P.G. Courses (M.A, M.Com, M.Sc, MBA) : ₹10,000/- 1. M.E/M.Tech : ₹11,500/-

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Labour Department.
