# Dr. Dharmambal Ammaiyar Ninaivu  Widow Remarriage Assistance Scheme-I

**Short Title:** DDANWRASI

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Remarriage, Widow, Financial Assistance, Gold, Rehabilitate

The &quot;Dr. Dharmambal Ammaiyar Ninaivu Widow Remarriage Assistance Scheme&quot; was launched by the Social Welfare and Women Empowerment Department, Government of Tamil Nadu. The scheme&#39;s objective is to encourage widow remarriage and to rehabilitate widows by giving financial assistance along with 1 sovereign (8gm) 22-carat gold coin. Interested applicants should apply Offline or via CSC.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Women and Child; Sector: Financial Assistance

**Where it applies:** Tamil Nadu; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - Financial Assistance of ₹ 25,000/-. - One sovereign (8gm) 22-carat gold coin for making Thirumangalyam.  > **Mode of Disbursal:** ₹15,000/- through Electronic Clearing Service and ₹10,000/- as a National Savings Certificate.

**Benefit Type:** Composite

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Department of Social Welfare and Women Empowerment.

**References / Annexures:** - **Citizen's Charter 2022**: https://cms.tn.gov.in/sites/default/files/documents/swwe_e_cc_2022_23.pdf - **Application Form & Documents Required**: https://cms.tn.gov.in/sites/default/files/forms/social_welfare_form10.pdf
