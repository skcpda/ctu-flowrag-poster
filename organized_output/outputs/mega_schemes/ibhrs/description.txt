# Imbichi Bawa Housing Renovation Scheme

**Short Title:** IBHRS

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Widow, Divorce, Woman, House, Renovation

The scheme &quot;Imbichi Bawa Housing Renovation Scheme&quot; by the Minority Welfare Department, Government of Kerala, aims to improve the basic facilities of houses belonging to widowed, divorced, or abandoned women from minority religious communities. Through this scheme, financial assistance is provided for house repairs to eligible women belonging to Muslim, Christian, Buddhist, Sikh, Parsi, and Jain communities. The scheme is implemented by the District Collectorates under the Minority Welfare Department. 

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Housing & Shelter; Sector: Housing

**Where it applies:** Kerala; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - A non-refundable grant of ₹50,000/- is provided for the repair of a house. - This financial assistance is intended for improving basic amenities in houses that lack proper windows, doors, roofing, flooring, finishing, plumbing, sanitation, or electrification. 

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Exclusions / Not eligible:** - The applicant should not have children with a permanent income from government or semi-government institutions. - The applicant should not have received financial assistance for house construction from the government or similar agencies within the last 10 years. 

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Directorate of Minority Welfare.

**References / Annexures:** - **Guidelines**: https://minoritywelfare.kerala.gov.in/pdfnewsflash/imbichi.pdf
