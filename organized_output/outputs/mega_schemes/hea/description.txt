# Handicrafts Export Award

**Short Title:** HEA

**Scheme For:** Infra

**Tags:** Exporter, Financial Assistance, Handicrafts, Manufacturer, Prize

The &quot;Handicrafts Export Award&quot; scheme was launched by the Handlooms, Handicrafts, Textiles, and Khadi Dept, Government of Tamil Nadu. This award is given to encourage the exporters of handicraft items with a cash prize of ₹40,000/-, 4 gm gold medal, Thamirapattiram &amp; a certificate. The exporter can apply offline for this award through Poompucar Sales and Manufacturing Plant Managers.

**Who is this for:** Business Entity; Category: Business & Entrepreneurship; Sector: Imports and exports

**Where it applies:** Tamil Nadu; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - Cash Prize of ₹40,000/-. - 4gm Gold Medal. - Thamirapattiram. - Certificate.

**Benefit Type:** Composite

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Handlooms, Handicrafts, Textiles and Khadi Department.

**References / Annexures:** - **Citizen Charter (2021)**: https://cms.tn.gov.in/sites/default/files/documents/tnhdc_e_cc_2021_22.pdf - **Application Form**: https://poompuhar.com/tnhdc/downloads/16355860722104819521.pdf
