# "Integrated Farming for Productivity Improvement Scheme" by the Coconut Development Board

**Short Title:** IFPISBTCDB

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Manure, Construction, Farmer, Subsidy, DBT

The scheme “Integrated Farming for Productivity Improvement Scheme&quot; by the Coconut Development Board was launched by the Department of Agriculture and Farmer&#39;s Welfare, Union Territory of Puducherry. Through this scheme, the coconut farmers are provided financial assistance through DBT (Direct Benefit Transfer) mode for the Laying of the Demonstration Plot, and for the Construction of the Organic Manure Unit.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Agriculture,Rural & Environment; Sector: Agricultural Inputs- seeds, fertilizer etc.

**Where it applies:** Puducherry; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - **Laying of Demonstration Plot (LoDP):** Financial assistance @ ₹ 35,000/‐ Ha in two annual instalments.- **Construction of Organic Manure Unit:** Financial assistance @ ₹ 40,000/‐ to ₹ 60,000/‐ per Unit on Pro‐rata basis. Unit size of 1200 cubic feet to 150 cubic feet (60 x 8 x 2.5 ft to 15 x 5 x 2 ft).- The eligible subsidy amount shall be released to the beneficiary&#39;s account on DBT (Direct Benefit Transfer) mode after obtaining expenditure sanction as per norms. - The final list of the beneficiaries would be displayed on the notice board of Uzhavar Uthaviyagams.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Agriculture Department, Puducherry.
