# Dr. Shivanand Nautiyal Scholarship

**Short Title:** DSNS

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Scholarship, Garhwal, Kumaon, Kumaon

The &amp;quot;Dr. Shivanand Nautiyal Scholarship&amp;quot; scheme is implemented by the Secondary Education Department under the Department of Education, Government of Uttarakhand. The scheme aims to provide financial assistance to meritorious students from the Garhwal and Kumaon divisions who secure top ranks in the examination conducted by SCERT Uttarakhand. 

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Education & Learning; Sector: Scholarships and student finance

**Where it applies:** Uttarakhand; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** Out of the top 100 students in the qualifying examination conducted by SCERT Uttarakhand, 50 students from the Garhwal Division and 50 students from the Kumaon Division will receive ₹1,500/- per month for a period of 10 months. 

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: Secondary Education Department, Government Of Uttarakhand; Nodal Department: Department Of Education.

**References / Annexures:** - **Guidelines (Page No. 75)**: https://uk.gov.in/department92/library_file/file-04-12-2023-06-02-23.pdf
