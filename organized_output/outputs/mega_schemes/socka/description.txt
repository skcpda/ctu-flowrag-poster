# Scheme of Conferring ‘Kalaichemmal’ Awards

**Short Title:** SOCKA

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Award, Financial Assistance, Artist, Exhibition

The &quot;Scheme of Conferring Kalaichemmal Awards&quot; was launched by the Art and Culture Department, Tamil Nadu. The scheme aims to encourage eminent artists in the field of paintings and sculptures in recognition of their meritorious service in the fields of Traditional Art and Contemporary Art. The Kalaichemmal awardees are given a citation and a cash award of ₹1,00,000/-. The exhibitions are conducted by Oviya Nunkalai Kuzhu at the state level and creations works will be on display for public view in the exhibition. 

**Who is this for:** Artists; Category: Skills & Employment; Sector: Apprenticeship

**Where it applies:** Tamil Nadu; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - Financial Assistance of ₹1,00,000/-. - Copper Citation Plaque. 

**Benefit Type:** Composite

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Art and Culture Department.

**References / Annexures:** - **Guidelines**: https://www.tn.gov.in/rti/proactive/inftour/handbook_artculture.pdf - **Revised Rate**: https://artandculture.tn.gov.in/oviya-nunkalai-kuzhu-schemes
