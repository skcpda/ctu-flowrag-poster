# LIDCOM Education Loan Scheme

**Short Title:** LELS

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Loan, Education, Student, Postgraduation, Scheduled Caste

Being implemented since the year 2009, the &quot;Education Loan Scheme&quot; is a scheme by the Leather Industries Development Corporation (LIDCOM), Govt. of Maharashtra. In this scheme, finance of upto ₹ 20,00,000 is provided for post graduate students in the 18-50 years age group from Charmakar Community for studies in India and in abroad. Only the citizens who are a permanent resident of the state of Maharashtra are eligible to apply to this scheme. This is a Central Government Scheme and the funding for this scheme come from the National Scheduled Castes Finance and Development Corporation, New Delhi (NSFDC). The main objective of LIDCOM is to implement various schemes to uplift lifestyle of Charmakars (Dhor, Chambhar, Holar, Mochi etc.) who are economically weak and below poverty line and with an intention to develop them educationally, economically and socially so as to give them respectable place in Society.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Education & Learning; Sector: Scholarships and student finance

**Where it applies:** Maharashtra; State

**Benefits / Assistance:** • Education Loan up to ₹10,00,000 for post graduate students in India. • Education Loan up to ₹20,00,000 for post graduate students in abroad. • Rate of Interest for Male Beneficiary: 4% per annum. • Rate of Interest for Female Beneficiary: 3.5% per annum.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: District Offices Of The Leather Industries Development Corporation (LIDCOM); Nodal Department: Social Justice and Special Assistance Department.

**References / Annexures:** - **Guidelines (LIDCOM)**: https://www.lidcom.co.in/education-loan-scheme.php - **Guidelines (SJSAD)**: https://sjsa.maharashtra.gov.in/en/scheme-category/education-training
