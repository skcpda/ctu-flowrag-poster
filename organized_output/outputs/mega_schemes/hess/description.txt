# Higher Education Scholarship Scheme

**Short Title:** HESS

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Scholarship, Education, Student, School, College

The “Higher Education Scholarship Scheme” was launched by the Education Department, Government of Gujarat. The scheme objective is that students studying in various courses at Government and Grant-In-Aid Schools and Colleges are eligible for this scheme. It begins with students studying in class 11th and extends across different Graduate-level study sequences. 

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Education & Learning; Sector: Universities and higher education

**Where it applies:** Gujarat; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - ₹1,000/- for school students. - ₹1,000/- for college students. - ₹1,000/- each for Arts and Commerce streams students. - ₹2,000/- for the Science stream students. 

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Education Department.
