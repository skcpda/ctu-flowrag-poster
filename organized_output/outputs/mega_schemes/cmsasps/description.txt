# Chief Minister Specially Abled Samman Pension Scheme

**Short Title:** CMSASPS

**Launch Date:** 2013-04-01

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Financial Assistance, Empowerment, Pension, Differently Abled

Chief Minister Specially Abled Samman Pension Scheme has been launched by the Government of Rajasthan. This scheme is an important scheme by the Government of Rajasthan for the Divyangjan of the state. It was started mainly to provide financial assistance to disabled persons. In State, a specially-abled person of any age who is suffering from any one or more disabilities (40% and above disability) will be eligible for a pension. From October 2, 2021, the process of issuing automatic approval of applications received online without human intervention was started using the metadata available on the Jan Aadhaar portal for the pension scheme.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Pension

**Where it applies:** Rajasthan; State

**Benefits / Assistance:** 1. For women below 55 years of age and men below 58 years of age: Rs. 750/- per month. 1. To women aged 55 years and above, men aged 58 years and above but less than 75 years of age: Rs. 1,000/- per month. 1. To the beneficiaries of 75 years and above age ( male and female): Rs. 1,250/- per month. 1. To leprosy-free beneficiaries of all ages ( male and female): Rs. 1,500/- per month.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Social Justice and Empowerment Department.
