# Samman Pension Yojana for All First-Generation Living Legal Heirs of Freedom Fighters of Uttarakhand State

**Short Title:** SPYAFGLHFFUKS

**Scheme For:** Family

**Tags:** Pension, Freedom Fighter, Samman, Living Legal Heirs, First-Generation

The scheme &amp;quot;Samman Pension Yojana&amp;quot; is implemented by the Police Department under the Department of Home, Government of Uttarakhand. The scheme aims to provide financial assistance in the form of a pension to all first-generation living legal heirs of freedom fighters residing in the state of Uttarakhand. 

**Who is this for:** Family; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Pension

**Where it applies:** Uttarakhand; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** **Samman Pension Amount:** ₹4,800/- per month 

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: Police Department, Government Of Uttarakhand; Nodal Department: Department of Home.

**References / Annexures:** - **Guidelines (Page No. 63)**: https://uk.gov.in/department92/library_file/file-04-12-2023-06-02-23.pdf

**Additional Information:**
Briefdescription: The scheme provides financial assistance of ₹4,800/- per month as pension to all first-generation living legal heirs of freedom fighters residing in the state of Uttarakhand.
