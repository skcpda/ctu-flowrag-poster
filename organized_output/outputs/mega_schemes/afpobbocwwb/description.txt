# Assistance for Purchase of Bi-cycle (O.B.O.C.W.W.B)

**Short Title:** AFPOBBOCWWB

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Labour, Building Worker, Construction Worker, Bi-cycle

The “Assistance for Purchase of Bi-cycle” scheme by the Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board, Labour Department, Odisha, is a welfare scheme for the registered workers of the board. Under this scheme, the Board shall make one time reimbursement to each beneficiary who is registered with the Board for purchasing Bi-cycle on production of original bill/ voucher.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Financial assistance

**Where it applies:** Odisha; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - **Quantum of Assistance:** ₹4,000/-. &lt;br&gt;

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Labour and Employees State Insurance Department.
