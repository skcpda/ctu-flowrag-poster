# Assistance to Scheduled Caste Farmers for Installation of Submersible / Centrifugal Motor Pumpsets With Accessories

**Short Title:** ASCFISCMPA

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Motor, Pump, Subsidy, Farmer, Agriculture, Scheduled Caste

The scheme “Assistance to Scheduled Caste Farmers for Installation of Submersible / Centrifugal Motor Pumpsets With Accessories&quot; was launched by the Department of Agriculture and Farmer&#39;s Welfare, Union Territory of Puducherry. Through this scheme, the farmers from the Scheduled Caste Category are provided with a grant of 90% subsidy towards the installation of suitable Submersible / Centrifugal motor pump sets with accessories, subject to a maximum of ₹60,000/‐ per tubewell per individual.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Agriculture,Rural & Environment; Sector: Land and water resources

**Where it applies:** Puducherry; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - 90% subsidy towards the installation of suitable Submersible / Centrifugal motor pump sets with accessories, subject to a maximum of ₹60,000/‐ per tubewell per individual. **NOTE:** The subsidy amount shall be released through the Electronic Clearing System (ECS) if the applicant has undertaken the construction with his/her funds. In case, he/she has availed himself/herself loan from the Nationalized Bank / Co‐operative Bank towards the construction of the tubewell, the subsidy amount shall be remitted to the Bank against the loan account of the beneficiary.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Agriculture Department, Puducherry.
