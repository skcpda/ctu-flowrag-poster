# Jharkhand Petrol Subsidy Scheme

**Short Title:** JPSS

**Scheme For:** Family

**Tags:** Subsidy, Petrol, Rural, Two-wheelers

The &quot;Petrol Subsidy Scheme&quot; implemented by the Food, Public Distribution &amp; Consumer Affairs Department, Government of Jharkhand, aims to provide financial relief to economically disadvantaged families by offering a petrol subsidy for their two-wheelers. Under this scheme, beneficiaries under the National Food Security Act (NFSA) or Jharkhand State Food Security Scheme receive ₹25/- per liter of petrol for up to 10 liters monthly.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Financial assistance

**Where it applies:** Jharkhand; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** ***Subsidy Amount*** - ₹25/- per liter of petrol for up to 10 liters monthly (₹250/month).  ***Mode of Disbursement:*** - Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) to the beneficiary&#39;s bank account.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Food, Public Distribution and Consumer affairs Department.

**References / Annexures:** - **Guidelines**: https://cm.jharkhand.gov.in/node/12697
