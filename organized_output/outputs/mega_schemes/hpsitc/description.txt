# Honorarium for Pradhan Samajpatis of Indigenous Tribal Community

**Short Title:** HPSITC

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Honorarium, Tribal, Financial Assistance, Indigenous

The scheme “Honorarium for Pradhan Samajpatis of Indigenous Tribal Community&quot; was launched by the Directorate of Social Welfare and Social Education, Government of Tripura on 1st July 2021 to provide financial assistance to the Pradhan Samajpatis (Chief Community Headman) of indigenous tribal communities in Tripura. 

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Financial assistance

**Where it applies:** Tripura; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** Honorarium of ₹2000/- per month.  

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Directorate Of Social Welfare & Social Education.
