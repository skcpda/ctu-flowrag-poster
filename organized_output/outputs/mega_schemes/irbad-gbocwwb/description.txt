# Interim Relief to the Beneficiaries Affected by Disaster (GBOCWWB)

**Short Title:** IRBAD-GBOCWWB

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Disaster, Calamity, Catastrophe, Relief, Worker

&quot;Interim Relief to the Beneficiaries Affected by Disaster (GBOCWWB)&quot; is a Welfare Scheme by the Goa Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board of the Department of Labour and Employment, Goa. Through this scheme, the beneficiaries of the Board who are affected by disasters, catastrophes or natural calamities are extended immediate relief of up to ₹10,00,000/-. This shall include cash payment to beneficiaries of the affected area and expenditure towards all the facilities and benefits granted to such beneficiaries.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Crisis/Disaster/Accident

**Where it applies:** Goa; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** An immediate relief of up to ₹10,00,000/-. _*The committee shall decide about the modalities regarding disbursement of such relief, quantum and procedure to be followed for such disbursement._

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: Goa Building And Other Construction Workers Welfare Board; Nodal Department: Labour and Employment Department.
