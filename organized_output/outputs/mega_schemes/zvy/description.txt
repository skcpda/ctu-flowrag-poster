# Zupda Vijlikaran Yojana

**Short Title:** ZVY

**Scheme For:** Family

**Tags:** Household, Free Electricity, BPL, Connection

The “Zupda Vijlikaran Yojana” was launched by the Energy and Petro Chemicals Department, Government of Gujarat. The scheme aims to provide access to electricity for Below Poverty Line (BPL) and low-income families across the state. Under this scheme, free household electricity connections are provided to eligible families. 

**Who is this for:** Family; Category: Utility & Sanitation; Sector: Electricity

**Where it applies:** Gujarat; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - Free electricity household connection is provided to BPL/Lower income group beneficiary. 

**Benefit Type:** In Kind

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Energy and Petro Chemicals Department.

**References / Annexures:** - **Guidelines**: https://mariyojana.gujarat.gov.in/MoreDetails.aspx
