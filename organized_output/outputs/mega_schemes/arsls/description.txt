# Agriculture Research Stations and Laboratories Scheme

**Short Title:** ARSLS

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Agriculture, Research Station, Laboratory, Farmer, Bio Pesticide, Bio Agent

The “Agriculture Research Stations and Laboratories Scheme” was launched by the Department of Agriculture &amp;amp; Farmers’ Welfare, Government of Meghalaya to conduct adaptive research trials on different crops in the Research Farms and in Farmers&amp;#39; Fields for adaptability and use of bio-pesticides/bio-agents.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Agriculture,Rural & Environment; Sector: Soil health

**Where it applies:** Meghalaya; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** > **Pattern of Assistance:** - Adaptive research at farmers&#39; field. > **Type of Assistance And Entitlement (If Any):** - Training on Soil management, Providing Soil Health Cards, Conducting soil surveys, etc.

**Benefit Type:** In Kind

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: Department Of Agriculture & Farmers’ Welfare, Government Of Meghalaya; Nodal Department: Agriculture Department.

**References / Annexures:** - **Official Website**: https://megagriculture.gov.in/PUBLIC/schemes_ShowSchemesA.aspx?schid=82

**Additional Information:**
Briefdescription: The objective of the scheme is to conduct adaptive research trials on different crops in the Research Farms and in Farmers' Field for adaptability and use of bio pesticides/bio agents.
