# Bank Tie-Up Scheme

**Short Title:** BTUS

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Loan, Scheduled Caste, Below Poverty Line, Social Welfare, Subsidy

Through the &quot;Bank Tie-Up Scheme&quot;, the Punjab Scheduled Castes Land Development &amp; Finance Corporation (PSCLDFC) arranges loans through banks, and subsidy is provided by PSCLDFC. The permanent residents of Punjab belonging to Scheduled Castes and living Below Poverty Line are eligible under this scheme. This is a 100% State Sponsored Scheme, and only the permanent residents of the state of Punjab can apply for this scheme.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Banking,Financial Services and Insurance; Sector: Loan

**Where it applies:** Punjab; State

**Benefits / Assistance:** • **Subsidy: ** Capital subsidy upto 50% of the total amount subject to a maximum of ₹ 10,000/-. • **Sources of Funds: ** The subsidy is provided by Govt. of India out of SCA. • **Loan: ** The loan amount is provided by various banks operating in the State. • **Rate of Interest: ** Charged by the Banks as per RBI guidelines.

**Benefit Type:** In Kind

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: Punjab Scheduled Castes Land Development & Finance Corporation (PSCLDFC); Nodal Department: Department of Finance.

**References / Annexures:** - **Guidelines**: https://pbscfc.punjab.gov.in/?q=node/25 - **Loan Application Form - I**: https://pbscfc.punjab.gov.in/sites/default/files/bank_tieup_scheme_form.pdf - **Loan Application Form - II**: https://pbscfc.punjab.gov.in/sites/default/files/CORPORATION-1-6%20PAGES.PDF
