# Financial Assistance to Destitute Elderly and Disabled Person

**Short Title:** FADEDP

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Elderly Pension, Senior Citizen, Disabled Person, Social Welfare

The &amp;quot;Financial Assistance to Destitute Elderly and Disabled Person&amp;quot; is a welfare scheme by the Social Justice and Empowerment Department of Gujarat. The scheme aims to support economically vulnerable elderly individuals and persons with severe disabilities. Elderly individuals aged 60 years or more who do not have an adult son (21 years or older), and persons with more than 75% disability aged 45 or more, are eligible under this scheme. 

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Financial assistance

**Where it applies:** Gujarat; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - **For beneficiaries aged 60 to 79 years:** ₹1,000/- per month- **For beneficiaries aged 80 years or more:** ₹1,250/- per month

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Social Justice and Empowerment Department.

**References / Annexures:** - **Guidelines (Scheme No. 187)**: https://mariyojana.gujarat.gov.in/MoreDetails.aspx

**Additional Information:**
Briefdescription: The scheme "Financial Assistance to Destitute Elderly and Disabled Person" provides monthly financial aid to elderly individuals (60+) and severely disabled persons who meet specific income and dependency criteria.
