# Indira Gandhi National Widow Pension Scheme (Punjab)

**Short Title:** IGNWPSP

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Pension, Widow, Woman, Female

The &quot;Indira Gandhi National Widow Pension Scheme (IGNWPS)&quot; is implemented by the Department of Social Security and Development of Women and Children, Government of Punjab. Under this scheme, widows aged 40 years and above receive a pension of ₹300/- per month. After attaining the age of 80 years, the pension amount increases to ₹500/- per month.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Pension

**Where it applies:** Punjab; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - Pension of ₹300/- per month for widows aged 40 to 79 years. - Pension of ₹500/- per month for widows aged 80 years and above.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Department of Social Security and Development of Women and Children.
