# Nirman Shramik Pension Yojana (NSPY) (O.B.O.C.W.W.B)

**Short Title:** NSPYBOCWWB

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Labour, Building Worker, Construction Worker, Pension, Nirman Shramik, Widow

The “Nirman Shramik Pension Yojana (NSPY)” scheme by the Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board, Labour Department, Odisha, is a social welfare scheme for the registered workers of the board. Under this scheme, the Board shall provide pension to a Construction worker registered with the Board and is above 60 years old (OAP)/ widow (any age) (WP)/ widow of registered worker (any age)/ leprosy patient with deformity (CLP)/ physically challenged (any age) (DP)/ widow of HIV positive worker (WP-AIDS) or HIV positive worker (any age) (DP-AIDS). 

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Pension

**Where it applies:** Odisha; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - **Beneficiary (up to 80 years):** ₹500/- Per Month.- **Beneficiary (80 years and above):** ₹700/- Per Month.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Labour and Employees State Insurance Department.

**References / Annexures:** - **Guidelines**: https://labdirodisha.gov.in/?q=node/15%27%3B - **Guidelines**: https://bocboard.labdirodisha.gov.in/Building/homecontent/NSPY/NSPY_11-01-18_292.pdf - **Amendments**: https://bocboard.labdirodisha.gov.in/Building/homecontent/NSPY/NSPY_pensionbenefit.pdf
