# Kachara Binane Vaale Hetu Suraksha Upakaran Sahaayata Yojana

**Short Title:** KBVHSUSY

**Launch Date:** 2017-06-19

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Equipment, Empowerment, Gloves, Shoes, Rain Coat

Labor Department of Chhattisgarh State started this scheme &amp;quot;Kachara Beenane Vaale Hetu Suraksha Upakaran Sahaayata Yojana&amp;quot; for unorganized worker on 19 June 2017. The main objective of the scheme to empower unorganized worker by providing safety equipment.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Skills & Employment

**Where it applies:** Chhattisgarh; State

**Benefits / Assistance:** - Gloves, Shoes and Rain Coat (OR) - A cash assistance in the amount determined by the CSID.

**Benefit Type:** Composite

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Labour Department.
