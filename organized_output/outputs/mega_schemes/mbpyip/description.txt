# Mukhyamantri Balika Protsahan Yojana (Intermediate Pass)

**Short Title:** MBPYIP

**Tags:** Education, Girl, Social Empowerment, Intermediate

The &amp;quot;Mukhyamantri Balika (Intermediate Pass) Protsahan Yojana&amp;quot;, a component of the &amp;quot;Mukhyamantri Kanya Utthan Yojana&amp;quot;, launched by the Education Department, Bihar, focuses on the socio-economic upliftment of girls in Bihar. The scheme provides monetary incentives to unmarried girls who have cleared their intermediate examinations from recognized schools or madrasas within Bihar.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Education & Learning; Sector: Scholarships and student finance

**Where it applies:** Bihar; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - **Financial Assistance:** ₹10,000/- for girls passing intermediate in 2021; ₹25,000/- in 2022 and later. &lt;br&gt;

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Education Department.

**References / Annexures:** - **Guidelines**: https://medhasoft.bih.nic.in/medhamat2022/(S(zqxinzxfgsdfqvntt1d1fxcz))/Notification.pdf

**Additional Information:**
Briefdescription: The Mukhyamantri Balika (Intermediate Pass) Protsahan Yojana is a scheme by the Bihar government aimed at promoting girl child education, curbing child marriages, reducing fertility rates, and empowering girls to become self-reliant.
