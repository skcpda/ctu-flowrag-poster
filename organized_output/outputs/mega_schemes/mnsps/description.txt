# Mukhyamantri Nirmaan Shramik Pension Sahayata

**Short Title:** MNSPS

**Launch Date:** 2023-08-31

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Financial Assistance, Social Welfare

Labor Department of Chhattisgarh State started this scheme &amp;amp;quot;Mukhyamantri Nirmaan Shramik Pension Sahayata&amp;amp;quot; for Building and other construction worker on 29 March 2023.. The main objective of the scheme to provides financial assistance to compensate the loss in regular income of the beneficiary due to lack of work available to him every day and to ensure smooth functioning of his livelihood.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Financial assistance

**Where it applies:** Chhattisgarh; State

**DBT Scheme:** Yes (Direct Benefit Transfer)

**Benefits / Assistance:** - Pension to the Beneficiary : ₹ 1,500/- per month (or) - Pension to the widow of the beneficiary: ₹ 700/- per month.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Labour Department.
