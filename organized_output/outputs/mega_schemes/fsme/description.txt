# First Self Marriage Expenses

**Short Title:** FSME

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Financial Assistance, Marriage, Construction Worker, Labour

The &quot;First Self Marriage Expenses&quot; scheme was implemented by the Maharashtra Building and Other Construction Workers&#39; Welfare Board, Labour Department Government of Maharashtra. Under this scheme, financial assistance of ₹30,000/- towards the first self-marriage expenses to the registered worker. 

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Marriage

**Where it applies:** Maharashtra; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - Financial assistance of ₹30,000/- provided to registered construction worker for first marriage expenses.  

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Labour Department.
