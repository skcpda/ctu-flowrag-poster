# Swavalambi/Sanchari Marata Malige

**Short Title:** SSMM

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Financial Assistance, Self-employed, Artisans, Youth, Scheduled Caste

The &quot;Swavalambi/Sanchari Marata Malige&quot; was implemented by Karnataka Leather Industries Development Corporation Ltd, Department of Social Welfare, Govt of Karnataka. The scheme aims to assist SC leather artisans in establishing their own showrooms or mobile vans to sell their products directly to consumers. Financial assistance is provided in the form of a subsidy depending on the location of the business.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Business & Entrepreneurship; Sector: Setting up / start-up / entrepreneurship

**Where it applies:** Karnataka; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** **Financial assistance ranging from ₹2,00,000/- to ₹5,00,000/- as a subsidy for opening a showroom or mobile van:** - Taluk Level: ₹2,00,000/- - Taluk &amp; District Centers: ₹3,00,000/- - Mahanagara Palike Limits:**** ₹4,00,000/-- BBMP Limits:**** ₹5,00,000/-

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: Karnataka Leather Industries Development Corporation Ltd; Nodal Department: Social Welfare Department.
