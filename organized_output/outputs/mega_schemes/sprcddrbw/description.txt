# Scheme to Provide Relief in Case of Death or Disability of Registered Building Workers

**Short Title:** SPRCDDRBW

**Launch Date:** 2016-02-04

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Financial Assistance, Accident, Differently-abled Person, Death, Construction Worker

The &quot;Scheme to Provide Relief in Case of Death or Disability of Registered Building Workers&quot; is a Welfare Scheme by the Andhra Pradesh Building &amp; Other Construction Workers LET&amp;F (Labour) Department. In cases of accidental death, permanent disability, or natural death, the scheme provides financial assistance to help mitigate the financial burden on the worker&#39;s family. This scheme aims to protect construction workers and their families during times of crisis.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Crisis/Disaster/Accident

**Where it applies:** Andhra Pradesh; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - Accidental Death: ₹5,00,000/- - Permanent Disability: ₹5,00,000/- - Natural Death Relief: ₹60,000/-

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Labour Department.

**References / Annexures:** - **Guidelines**: https://apbocwwb.ap.nic.in/APBOCWW3/G.O.3.pdf - **Official Website**: https://apbocwwb.ap.nic.in/apbocwwbhome.jsp
