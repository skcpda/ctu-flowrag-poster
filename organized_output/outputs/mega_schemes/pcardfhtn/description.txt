# Primary Cooperative Agriculture and Rural Development Bank: For Handloom and Other Non Farm Sector Activities

**Short Title:** PCARDFHTN

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Handloom Loans, Non-farm Sector Activities, Farmer, Financial Assistance

The &amp;quot;Primary Cooperative Agriculture and Rural Development Bank: For Handloom and Other Non Farm Sector Activities&amp;quot; scheme launched by the Co-operation, Food and Consumer Protection Department, Tamil Nadu, aims to offer financial support for handloom and other non-farm sector activities, enabling farmers to diversify income sources beyond agriculture. Loans under this scheme can be obtained based on the project cost, with a limit of ₹2,00,000/-. Interest rates range from 11% to 12.5%, subject to change. &lt;br&gt;

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Banking,Financial Services and Insurance; Sector: Loan

**Where it applies:** Tamil Nadu; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - **Access to Loan:** Up to ₹2,00,000/-. - **Interest Rate:** From 11% to 12.5%, (subject to change). &lt;br&gt;

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Co-operation, Food and Consumer Protection Department.

**References / Annexures:** - **Guidelines**: https://www.tn.gov.in/scheme/data_view/3558

**Additional Information:**
Briefdescription: The scheme offers loans for handloom and other non-farm sector activities, with a maximum loan amount of up to ₹2,00,000/- at an interest rate of 11% to 12.5%. The scheme is designed to support farmers engaged in non-farm activities.
