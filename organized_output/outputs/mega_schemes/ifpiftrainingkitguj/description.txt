# To Increase Fish Seed Production And Inland Fisheries Equipment In Tribal Areas: Training Kit Gujarat

**Short Title:** IFPIFTRAININGKITGUJ

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Fish, Fish Activities, Fish Production, Scheduled Tribes, Fishermen, Training Kit

The scheme &quot;To Increase Fish Seed Production And Inland Fisheries Equipment In Tribal Areas&quot; with a component of &quot;Training Kit&quot;, introduced by the Agriculture, Farmers Welfare and Co-operation Department, Government of Gujarat, aims to increase fish production and provide employment opportunities to Scheduled Tribe (ST) fishermen by enhancing their skills and access to equipment. Through this scheme, training kits containing fishing and net-weaving equipment are provided to Scheduled Tribe trainees upon completion of training. The scheme is implemented by the Commissioner of Fisheries.  > **Objectives:** - To increase fish production by rearing fish in reservoirs and provide employment to people involved in inland fisheries activities. - To boost fish farming or fishing activities in freshwater bodies. - Fishing in reservoirs, aquaculture in ponds, and catch from rivers increase fish production and boost inland fisheries. - The main objective is to provide employment opportunities to Scheduled Tribe fishermen in the state and increase fish production. 

**Who is this for:** Business Entity, Individual, Registered Societies, Self Help Groups (SHGS); Category: Agriculture,Rural & Environment; Sector: Fishing and hunting

**Where it applies:** Gujarat; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** 1. Scheduled Tribe trainees are given a training kit worth ₹2,500/- after completing three months of training. 1. The kit includes cast nets, gill nets with seekers and floats, nylon twine, cotton ropes, plastic ropes, and net-weaving equipment.  > **Training Kit:** The kit is provided after the successful completion of a three-month training program. > **Frequency of Disbursement:** The benefits for this component can be reclaimed only once in a lifetime. 

**Benefit Type:** Composite

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: Commissioner Of Fisheries; Nodal Department: Agriculture, Farmers Welfare and Cooperation Department.
