# A Program to Provide 90 Percent Assistance in Planting Material of Horticultural Fruit Crops

**Short Title:** PTPAPMH

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Agriculture, Horticultural, Fruit Crops

&amp;quot;A Program to Provide 90 Percent Assistance in Planting Material of Horticultural Fruit Crops&amp;quot; introduced by the Agriculture, Farmers Welfare and Cooperation Department, Gujarat, aims to support farmers by providing 90% subsidy on the cost of planting materials like grafted/tissue culture saplings and seedlings for various fruit crops.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Agriculture,Rural & Environment; Sector: Financial assistance

**Where it applies:** Gujarat; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - Farmers receive up to 90% financial assistance on the cost of planting materials. > **Pre-fixed assistance rates per hectare for different crops:**  Fruit Assistance Mango₹32,000/haSapota (Chiku)₹22,000/haPomegranate₹20,000/haGuava₹16,650/haAonla (Indian Gooseberry)₹5,560/haMandarin / Orange₹5,560/haBer₹2,780/haCoconut₹13,000/ha > **Note:** - Only saplings costing up to ₹250/- per unit are eligible for subsidy. - **For fruit crops and cuttings:** Planting material will have to be purchased from nurseries accredited by NHB/Agricultural University/Horticulture Department.- **For coconut crops:** seedlings prepared from seeds will have to be purchased from nurseries accredited by NHB/Agricultural University/Horticulture Department.- **For tissue culture:** Planting material (planting) will have to be purchased from tissue labs approved/accredited by DBT, GNFC, GSFC, Tissue Labs of Agricultural University, Government Public Institutions like

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Agriculture, Farmers Welfare and Cooperation Department.

**References / Annexures:** - **Guidelines**: https://mariyojana.gujarat.gov.in/MoreDetails.aspx - **User Manual (Applicant Registration)**: https://ikhedut.gujarat.gov.in/site/iKhedut%202.0%20User%20Manual%20PPT_Gujarati.pdf

**Additional Information:**
Briefdescription: This scheme offers 90% financial assistance to farmers for purchasing planting materials of fruit crops to promote horticulture.
