# Shramik Basera Yojana (GBOCWWB)

**Short Title:** SBY(GBOCWWB)

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Building Worker, Construction Worker, Labour, Basera, Accommodation Facility

The “Shramik Basera Yojana” is implemented by the Gujarat Building and Other Construction Worker’s Welfare Board (GBOCWWB), Labour, Skill Development &amp;amp; Employment Department, Government of Gujarat. Under the scheme, accommodation facilities will be provided to registered construction workers in temporary prefabricated housing with basic amenities that can be dismantled.

**Who is this for:** Family, Individual; Category: Housing & Shelter; Sector: Short home Stay

**Where it applies:** Gujarat; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** 1. Under this scheme, accommodation facilities will be provided to the laborers/workers at the rate of ₹5/- per day.

**Benefit Type:** In Kind

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: Gujarat Building And Other Construction Worker’s Welfare Board; Nodal Department: Labour and Employment Department.
