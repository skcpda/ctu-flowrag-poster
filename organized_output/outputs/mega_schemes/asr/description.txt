# Aastha Yojana

**Short Title:** AYR

**Scheme For:** Family

**Tags:** Persons With Disabilities, Medical Treatment, Ration, Welfare Benefits, PwD, Astha Card

The scheme &amp;quot;Aastha Yojana&amp;quot; was launched by the Department of Social Justice and Empowerment, Government of Rajasthan. It aims to provide BPL-equivalent benefits to families with multiple specially-abled members. Under this scheme, eligible families receive free medical treatment, ration supplies, and other welfare benefits. To avail of the benefits, a family must possess an Aastha Card, and their annual income should not exceed ₹1.20 lakh.

**Who is this for:** Family; Category: Health & Wellness; Sector: Health care providers and access

**Where it applies:** Rajasthan; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - Aastha cardholder families receive benefits similar to BPL families, including free medical treatment and ration supplies. **Note:** Aastha cardholders are entitled to benefits from all BPL-related schemes run by different state government departments.

**Benefit Type:** In Kind

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Social Justice and Empowerment Department.
