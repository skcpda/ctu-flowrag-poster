# Sanitary Napkin Scheme (UKBOCWWB)

**Short Title:** SNS(UKBOCWWB)

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Sanitary Napkin, Building Worker, Construction Worker, Labour, Woman, Girls

The scheme “Sanitary Napkin Scheme” is implemented by the Uttarakhand Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board (UKBOCWWB) under the Labour Department, Government of Uttarakhand. The scheme aims to provide free sanitary napkins to the daughters of registered construction workers and to women construction workers, in order to promote health and hygiene. 

**Who is this for:** Individual, Family; Category: Health & Wellness; Sector: Menstrual hygiene

**Where it applies:** Uttarakhand; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** 1. Under the scheme, free sanitary napkins will be provided to the daughters of registered construction workers and to women construction workers. 

**Benefit Type:** In Kind

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: Uttarakhand Building And Other Construction Workers’ Welfare Board; Nodal Department: Department Of Labour.
