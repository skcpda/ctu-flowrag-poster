# COP-5 Financial Assistance to Agricultural Credit Co-operative Banks (PACS/LAMPS) for Reconstruction and Reestablishment (General/Tribal Area)

**Short Title:** CFAACOBRR

**Scheme For:** Infra

**Tags:** Agriculture, Co Operative Banks, Reconstruction

The scheme &amp;quot;COP-5 Financial Assistance to Agricultural Credit Co-operative Banks (PACS/LAMPS) for Reconstruction and Reestablishment (General/Tribal Area)&amp;quot; introduced by the Agriculture, Farmers Welfare and Cooperation Department, Gujarat, aims to make economically weak but functionally efficient PACS/LAMPS financially viable. These societies play a foundational role in the three-tier cooperative credit system, and their performance impacts District and State Cooperative Banks. The scheme incentivizes societies based on their growth in credit disbursement, loan recovery, and deposit mobilization over the past three years (two years for LAMPS).

**Who is this for:** Government Organisation; Category: Agriculture,Rural & Environment; Sector: Financial assistance

**Where it applies:** Gujarat; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:**  **Incentive admissible over and above the average amount of the last three years****Percentage of the grant of incentive on the excess amount**For growth from 10% to 15% 1 %For growth above 15% and up to 20% 1.5 %For growth above 20% 2% or a maximum of ₹.50,000/- whichever is less (on deposits up to a ₹2,00,000/- whichever is less) > **Note:** The benefit of the scheme is available only once in a financial year.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Agriculture, Farmers Welfare and Cooperation Department.

**Additional Information:**
Briefdescription: The scheme provides financial assistance to Primary Agricultural Credit Societies (PACS) and Large-sized Agricultural Multi-Purpose Societies (LAMPS) in General and Tribal areas to strengthen their economic viability and ensure their continued contribution to the cooperative credit structure.
