# Integrated Social Security Scheme: Pension to Physically Challenged Persons

**Short Title:** ISSSPPCP

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Pension, PwD, Disability, ISSS, BPL

The scheme &amp;quot;Pension to Physically Challenged Persons&amp;quot; is a sub-component of the “Integrated Social Security Scheme (IGSSS)” implemented by the Social and Tribal Welfare Department, Administration of Union Territory of Ladakh. Under this scheme, a monthly pension of ₹1,000/- is provided to poor physically handicapped persons (including those who are blind, deaf, or dumb) with a degree of disability 40% or above and having no source of income.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Pension

**Where it applies:** Ladakh; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** **Amount of pension:** ₹1,000/- per month per beneficiary

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: Directorate Of Social & Tribal Welfare, Administration Of Union Territory Of Ladakh; Nodal Department: Social and Tribal Welfare Department.
