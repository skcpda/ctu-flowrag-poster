# Palak Mata-Pita Yojana

**Short Title:** PMPY

**Launch Date:** 2009-08-29

**Scheme For:** Family

**Tags:** Foster Parent, Orphan, Child Welfare, Financial Assistance

The &amp;quot;Palak Mata-Pita Yojana&amp;quot; introduced on 29th August, 2009 by the Social Justice and Empowerment Department, Gujarat, aims to provide monthly assistance of Rs. 3000/- is paid to the close relative, guardian or relative who is taking care of the orphaned children who are studying and whose mother and father are both deceased or whose father is deceased and the mother has remarried. This assistance is paid through DBT.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Financial assistance

**Where it applies:** Gujarat; State/ UT

**DBT Scheme:** Yes (Direct Benefit Transfer)

**Benefits / Assistance:** **Financial Assistance:** ₹3,000/- per month.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Social Justice and Empowerment Department.

**References / Annexures:** - **Guidelines (Scheme No. 406)**: https://mariyojana.gujarat.gov.in/MoreDetails.aspx - **Guidelines 2**: https://sje.gujarat.gov.in/dsd/scheme/palak-mata-sccheme

**Additional Information:**
Briefdescription: The "Palak Mata-Pita Yojana" introduced on 29th August, 2009 by the Social Justice and Empowerment Department, Gujarat, provides a monthly assistance of ₹3000 to foster parents raising orphaned children or children whose mother has remarried after the father's death.
