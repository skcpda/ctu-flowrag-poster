# Refund of Fees

**Short Title:** ROF

**Launch Date:** 2012-01-12

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** College, Student, Education, Financial Assistance

The &quot;Refund of Fees&quot; Scheme under the Higher Education Department, Madhya Pradesh, allows students to claim a refund of the remaining admission fee (including caution money) if they leave the college during the academic session after admission, are declared failed after provisional admission, or if their online admission is cancelled. The refund is processed after deducting an admission processing fee of ₹100.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Education & Learning; Sector: Scholarships and student finance

**Where it applies:** Madhya Pradesh; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** Refund of remaining fees (after deducting ₹100) if the student leaves

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Higher Education Department.

**References / Annexures:** - **Scheme Details**: https://cmhelpline.mp.gov.in/KnowYourEntitleDetail.aspx?status=ByVibhag&pointvalue=2&Schemeid=52
