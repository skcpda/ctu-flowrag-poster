# Health and Maternity Cover for the Construction Workers

**Short Title:** HAMCFTCW

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Labour, Construction Workers, Building Workers, Medical Support, Maternity, Health Cover

The &amp;quot;Health and Maternity Cover for the Construction Workers&amp;quot; by the Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board, Labour Department, U.T. Administration of Dadra and Nagar Haveli and Daman and Diu, is a welfare scheme for the registered workers of the board. Under this scheme, registered beneficiaries of the board shall be provided maternity benefits in the form of financial assistance during the period of maternity.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Financial assistance

**Where it applies:** Dadra & Nagar Haveli and Daman & Diu; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** &gt; ***Cash Benefits:*** - **For Male Members:** ₹30,000/- per delivery. - **For Female Members:** ₹40,000/- per delivery. &gt; **Other Benefits:** - **Enrollment Under PM-JAY:** The beneficiaries shall be enrolled under the Pradhan Mantri Jan Arogya Yojna i.e “Ayushman Bharat”. **Note:** The benefit shall not be allowed for more than two deliveries.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Labour & Employment Department.

**References / Annexures:** - **Guidelines**: https://www.daman.nic.in/websites/Printing-press-Daman/documents/2020/Extraordinary/Extra%20Ordinary%20No.%2029.pdf - **Guidelines**: https://bocwddd.in/welfare-schemes/dnh/maternity-benefit - **Official Website**: https://bocwddd.in/
