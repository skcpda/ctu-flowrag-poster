# Free Education Scholarship for Three-Year Diploma (Polytechnic Courses)

**Short Title:** FESFTYDPC

**Launch Date:** 2012-05-07

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Minority Welfare, Scholarship, Education, Polytechnic Courses, Diploma

The &amp;quot;Free Education Scholarship for Three-Year Diploma (Polytechnic Courses)&amp;quot; launched on 7th May, 2012, by the Backward Classes, Most Backward Classes and Minorities Welfare Department, Tamil Nadu, is designed to support students from Backward Classes (BC), Most Backward Classes (MBC), and Denotified Communities (DNC) who wish to pursue higher education in polytechnic institutions. &lt;br&gt; &lt;br&gt;

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Education & Learning; Sector: Scholarships and student finance

**Where it applies:** Tamil Nadu; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** &gt; **Financial Assistance:** - Full payment of tuition fees. - Coverage of special and other non-refundable compulsory fees. - Full reimbursement of examination fees as per government norms. &lt;br&gt;

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Backward Classes Most Backward Classes and Minorities Welfare Department.

**References / Annexures:** - **Guidelines-1**: https://www.tn.gov.in/scheme/data_view/27483 - **Guidelines-2**: https://cms.tn.gov.in/sites/default/files/schemes/bcmbcmw_5.pdf

**Additional Information:**
Briefdescription: This scheme provides free education scholarships for students from Backward Classes, Most Backward Classes, and Denotified Communities pursuing 3-year Diploma courses in Government and Government-Aided Polytechnics.
