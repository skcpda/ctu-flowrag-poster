# West Bengal Handloom and Khadi Weavers Financial Benefit Scheme 2024: Assistance to Viable and Potentially Viable PWCS

**Short Title:** AVPV

**Launch Date:** 2024-03-07

**Tags:** Handloom, Khadi Weavers, PWCS, Khadi Societies

“Assistance to Viable and Potentially Viable PWCS&amp;quot; is a sub-scheme under &amp;quot;West Bengal Handloom and Khadi Weavers Financial Benefit Scheme 2024”, launched by the Micro, Small &amp;amp; Medium Enterprise and Textiles Department, Government of West Bengal on 7th March, 2024, and it shall remain in force for a period of five years. The objective of this sub-scheme is to provide financial assistance to the Primary Weavers&amp;#39; Cooperative Societies (PWCS) and Khadi Societies who will fulfill the criteria of viability as may be determined by the state level committee established under this scheme. 

**Who is this for:** Registered Societies; Category: Business & Entrepreneurship; Sector: Setting up / start-up / entrepreneurship

**Where it applies:** West Bengal; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** ₹1,00,000/ to ₹10,00,000/- depending on the extent of activity of the PWCS.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Micro, Small & Medium Enterprises and Textiles Department.
