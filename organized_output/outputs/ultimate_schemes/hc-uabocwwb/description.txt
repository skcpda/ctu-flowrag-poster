# Health Check-up (A.B.O.C.W.W.B)

**Short Title:** HC-U(ABOCWWB)

**Tags:** Health, Check-Up, Building Worker, Construction Worker, Labour, Financial Assistance

The “Health Check-up” benefit scheme was started by the Assam Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board (A.B.O.C.W.W.B), Labour Welfare Department, Government of Assam. Under this scheme, financial assistance will be provided annually to registered construction workers with continuous Board membership for their annual health check-up at any hospital.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Health & Wellness; Sector: Financial assistance

**Where it applies:** Assam; State/ UT

**DBT Scheme:** Yes (Direct Benefit Transfer)

**Benefits / Assistance:** 1. Under this scheme, registered construction workers can avail an amount of ₹5,000/- once in a year for his/her annual health check-up in any Hospital.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: Assam Building And Other Construction Workers Welfare Board; Nodal Department: Labour & Welfare Department.
