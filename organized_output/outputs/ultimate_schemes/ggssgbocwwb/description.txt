# Go-Green Shramik Scheme (GBOCWWB)

**Short Title:** GGSS(GBOCWWB)

**Tags:** Go-Green, Building Worker, Construction Worker, Subsidy, Labour, Two-Wheeler

The “Go-Green Shramik Scheme” is implemented by the Gujarat Building and Other Construction Worker’s Welfare Board (GBOCWWB), Labour, Skill Development &amp;amp; Employment Department, Government of Gujarat. The objective of the scheme is to make the &amp;quot;GREEN INDIA MISSION&amp;quot; of the Government of India a reality, the construction workers will also become partners and become aware of the environment in them as well as with the good intention that they will have flexibility in commuting, save money and time and leave the slum area near the construction site and live in a clean residential area.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Transport & Infrastructure; Sector: Public transport and private vehicles

**Where it applies:** Gujarat; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** 1. Under the scheme, a One-time subsidy will be available to the extent of 50% of the price of the battery-operated Two-Wheeler or ₹30,000/- whichever is less. 1. The assistance will also be available for the amount of Registration Fee and Road Tax paid for the first time for a new two-wheeler i.e. RTO Tax and Road Tax will be reimbursed. **Note 01:** After deducting the amount of subsidy payable to the vehicle dealer from the ex-showroom price of the two-wheeler, the remaining amount will have to be paid by the construction contractor. **Note 02:** This additional subsidy will be provided in addition to the subsidy available under the electric vehicle-related schemes of the government.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: Gujarat Building And Other Construction Worker’s Welfare Board; Nodal Department: Labour and Employment Department.
