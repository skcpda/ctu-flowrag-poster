# Girnar Arohan-Avrohan Competition

**Short Title:** GAAC

**Tags:** Mount Girnar Competition, Stair Climbing Competition, Youth Activities

The scheme “Girnar Arohan-Avrohan Competition” by the Department of Sports, Youth and Cultural Activities, Government of Gujarat, aims to encourage physical endurance, sports participation, and cultural spirit among individuals by organizing an annual state-level stair-climbing competition on Mount Girnar. Out of the 9,900 steps of the mountain, the competition mandates male participants to complete 5,500 steps and female participants to complete 2,200 steps. Participants who secure positions among the top 10 are rewarded with substantial cash prizes, thereby motivating individuals of all backgrounds to embrace physical challenges and celebrate Gujarat’s heritage. &lt;br&gt; 

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Sports & Culture; Sector: Sports

**Where it applies:** Gujarat; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - **Cash Prize:** A total of ₹8,40,000/- is distributed among the first 10 winners. 

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Sports, Youth and Cultural Activities Department.
