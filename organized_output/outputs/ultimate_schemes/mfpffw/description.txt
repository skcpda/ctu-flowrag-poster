# Monthly/Family Pension for Freedom Fighters and their Widows of Uttarakhand State

**Short Title:** MFPFFWUS

**Tags:** Pension, Widow, Freedom Fighter

The &amp;quot;Monthly/Family Pension for Freedom Fighters and their Widows of Uttarakhand State&amp;quot; scheme is implemented by the Police Department under the Department of Home, Government of Uttarakhand. The scheme aims to provide financial assistance in the form of a monthly pension to the freedom fighters of Uttarakhand and to the widows of deceased freedom fighters, in recognition of their contribution to the nation. 

**Who is this for:** Individual, Family; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Pension

**Where it applies:** Uttarakhand; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** **Monthly/Family Pension Amount:** ₹25,000/- 

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: Police Department, Uttarakhand; Nodal Department: Department of Home.
