# Fitter Training to Hearing Impaired Persons

**Short Title:** FTTHIP

**Tags:** Hearing Impaired, Training, Stipend, Person With Disability, ITI

The scheme &quot;Fitter Training to Hearing Impaired Persons&quot; was launched by the Department for the Welfare of Differently Abled Persons, Government of Tamil Nadu. The scheme aims to give fitter training to the hearing-impaired candidates at the ITI, Guindy for two years and a stipend of ₹300/- per month. The applications are accepted offline in the prescribed form available at the District Differently Abled Welfare Office.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Financial assistance

**Where it applies:** Tamil Nadu; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - Fitter training is given to the hearing-impaired candidates at the ITI, Guindy for 2 years. - A stipend of ₹300/- per month is given to the trainees.

**Benefit Type:** Composite

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Welfare of Differently Abled Persons Department.
