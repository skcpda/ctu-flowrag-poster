# Sanction of Laptops - Andhra Pradesh

**Short Title:** SOLAP

**Tags:** Empowerment, Student, Speech Impaired, Orthopedically

The Department for the Welfare of Differently Abled &amp; Senior Citizens launched the &quot;Sanction of Laptops&quot; scheme to distribute free laptops to visually challenged, hearing impairments, speech impairments, and orthopedic challenges students pursuing professional courses. The A.P. Differently Abled &amp; Senior Citizens Assistance Corporation proposes to procure 200 laptops at ₹30,000/- each.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Aids/Appliances

**Where it applies:** Andhra Pradesh; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** **Distribution of Laptops.** - Category I: Parent/guardian income up to ₹15,000/- per month - Full cost covered. - Category II: Parent/guardian income from ₹15,000/- to ₹20,000/- per month - Half of the cost covered. - Category III: Parent/guardian income above ₹20,000/- per month - Full cost to be paid by the beneficiary.

**Benefit Type:** In Kind

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Dept of Social Welfare, WCD.

**References / Annexures:** - **Guidelines**: https://apdascac.ap.gov.in/Schemes/laptops/ - **Official Website**: https://apdascac.ap.gov.in/
