# Solar Energy Assistance Scheme (UKBOCWWB)

**Short Title:** SEAS(UKBOCWWB)

**Tags:** Solar Light, Solar Lantern, Solar Energy, Building Worker, Construction Worker

The scheme “Solar Energy Assistance Scheme” is implemented by the Uttarakhand Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board (UKBOCWWB) under the Labour Department, Government of Uttarakhand. The scheme aims to provide solar energy plates/panels to the families of registered construction workers. 

**Who is this for:** Family; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment

**Where it applies:** Uttarakhand; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** Under this scheme, solar energy plates/panels will be provided, and for maintenance and service charges, beneficiaries will have to contact the concerned supplier. 

**Benefit Type:** In Kind

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: Uttarakhand Building And Other Construction Workers’ Welfare Board; Nodal Department: Department Of Labour.
