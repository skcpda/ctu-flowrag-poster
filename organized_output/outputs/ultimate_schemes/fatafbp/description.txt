# Financial Assistance to Authors for Book Publication

**Short Title:** FATAFBP

**Tags:** Financial Assistance, Authors, Book Publication, Writer, Poets, Poets

The &amp;quot;Financial Assistance to Authors for Book Publication&amp;quot; scheme was introduced by the Department of Culture, Government of Uttarakhand. The scheme aims to support the state&amp;#39;s eminent litterateurs, writers, poets, and authors whose literary works remain unpublished due to financial constraints. Only permanent/native residents of the state whose works are not being published for financial reasons are eligible to receive financial assistance to publish their manuscripts.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Sports & Culture; Sector: Culture

**Where it applies:** Uttarakhand; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - Financial assistance of up to ₹2,00,000/- per author to cover book printing/publishing costs for unpublished manuscripts.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Department of Culture and Endowments.
