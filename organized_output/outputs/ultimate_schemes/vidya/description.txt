# Vidya Scheme

**Short Title:** VS

**Launch Date:** 2013-04-01

**Tags:** Girl Student, Financial Assistance, Elementary Education, Secondary Education

Vidya scheme under the Education Department, Government of Arunachal Pradesh is a scheme where a girl child who is studying in Class VI is provided with an incentive of ₹10,000/- as fixed deposit in SBI in the name of an individual girl child till she completes Class – XII. After completing Class XII, the Girl child is entitled to receive the maturity amount of the Fixed Deposit. **Objective:** 1. To encourage girl children to continue school education after elementary and secondary levels. 1. To encourage the girls to increase their presence in schools and reduce their dropout rate at the elementary and secondary stages of schooling. 1. Providing financial assistance/incentive to all female students who successfully pass the Class-V examination and enroll in Class-VI in the State Government Schools. 1. The amount of assistance is ₹10,000 from 2015-16 onwards. 1. The awarded amount will be deposited in the State Bank of India (SBI) in the name of the girl student as Fixed.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Education & Learning; Sector: Financial Assistance

**Where it applies:** Arunachal Pradesh; State

**Benefits / Assistance:** 1. Financial assistance: A sum of ₹10,000/- (Rupees Ten Thousand) would only be deposited under the bank&amp;#39;s Fixed Deposit scheme in the name of every eligible female student. 1. The term/period of deposit may be counted from the date of deposit to the approximate date the girl student passes Class-XII. 1. No premature withdrawal will be allowed. 1. Scheme is beneficial one time on successful completion of Class XII.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Education Department.
