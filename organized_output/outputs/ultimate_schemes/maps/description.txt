# Mukhyamantri Avivahita Pension Yojana

**Short Title:** MAPS

**Launch Date:** 2018-10-01

**Tags:** Financial Assistance, Unmarried, Empowerment

Madhya Pradesh government has started the &#39;Mukhyamantri Avivahit Pension Yojana &#39; under the Department of Social Justice and Empowerment of Persons with Disabilities to help those women who have not married for some reason. Such women are alone and live their life. The government will provide a pension of ₹ 600 every month, which will help them to run their lives easily. With this, they will not be dependent on anyone else for their work and will be able to live happily. With this, women will be able to create a brighter future and improve their lives without any hassle. Under Madhya Pradesh Chief Minister Married Women Pension Scheme, unmarried women above 50 years of age have been included, so that they can improve their social status and economic status.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Financial assistance

**Where it applies:** Madhya Pradesh; State

**Benefits / Assistance:** Pension amount: ₹ 600/- per month.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Department of Social Justice and Empowerment of Persons with Disabilities.
