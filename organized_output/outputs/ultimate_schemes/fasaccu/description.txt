# Financial Assistance to Sports Associations/ Clubs/ Committees of Uttarakhand

**Short Title:** FASACCU

**Tags:** Sports Grant, Sports Club, Athlete Support, Sports Association

The scheme “Financial Assistance to Sports Associations/ Clubs/ Committees of Uttarakhand” implemented by the Department Of Sports, Uttarakhand, aims to provide financial grants to recognized sports associations, clubs, and sports committees in the state for organizing competitions at national, state, and district levels. The assistance includes up to ₹3,50,000/- for national-level competitions, ₹2,50,000/- for state-level competitions, and ₹1,00,00/- for district-level competitions. Additionally, selected athletes for international competitions may receive up to ₹2,50,000/-. The scheme also includes grants for the procurement of sports equipment, supporting nearly 20 sports bodies and athletes annually.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Sports & Culture; Sector: Sports

**Where it applies:** Uttarakhand; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** > **For Organizing Sports Competitions:** - National-level competitions: Up to ₹3,50,000/-. - State-level competitions: Up to ₹2,50,000/-. - District-level competitions: Up to ₹1,00,00/-. > **For Players (Financial Aid/Equipment):** - Financial assistance of up to ₹2,50,000/- is provisioned for selected players, which can be utilized for purposes such as purchasing sports equipment.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Department Of Sports.
