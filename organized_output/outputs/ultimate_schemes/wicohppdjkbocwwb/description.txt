#  Workers In Case Of His Partial Permanent Disablement-JKBOCWWB

**Short Title:** WICOHPPDJKBOCWWB

**Tags:** Building Worker, Construction Worker, Labour, Disability, Financial Assistance

The &quot;Workers In Case of Partial Permanent Disablement&quot; scheme by the Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board, Labour Department, Jammu &amp; Kashmir, under the MUHAFIZ Scheme (PMJJBY: Pradhan Mantri Jeevan Jyoti Bima Yojana, PMSBY: Pradhan Mantri Suraksha Bima Yojana) from 01.01.2018, provides Rs. 1,00,000 to registered BOC workers in the case of partial permanent disablement.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Financial assistance

**Where it applies:** Jammu and Kashmir; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** > Under the scheme, Rs. 1,00,000 is provided to registered BOC workers in case of partial permanent disablement.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Department of Labour and Employment.
