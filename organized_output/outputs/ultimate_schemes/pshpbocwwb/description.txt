# Pension Scheme (HPBOCWWB)

**Short Title:** PSHPBOCWWB

**Tags:** Building, Construction, Labour, Pension, Senior Citizen, Worker

The &quot;Pension Scheme,&quot; launched by the Himachal Pradesh Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board (HPBOCWWB) under the Government of Himachal Pradesh, provides financial support to registered construction workers. Once they reach the age of 60, beneficiaries are entitled to a monthly pension, with a guaranteed minimum amount of ₹1,000/-.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Pension

**Where it applies:** Himachal Pradesh; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** Pension amount ₹1,000/- per month.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Labour and Employment Department, Himachal Pradesh.
