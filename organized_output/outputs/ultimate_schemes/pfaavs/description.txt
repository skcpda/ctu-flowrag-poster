# The Punjab Financial Assistance to Acid Victims Scheme

**Short Title:** PFAAVS

**Launch Date:** 2017-06-20

**Tags:** Financial Assistance, Acid Victim, Woman

The scheme “The Punjab Financial Assistance to Acid Victims Scheme” was launched by the Department of Social Security And Women &amp;amp; Child Development, Government of Punjab in the year 2017. The objective of the Scheme is to provide monthly Financial Assistance to females who have been rendered 40% disabled due to acid attack. This scheme is fully state-sponsored.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Women and Child; Sector: Financial Assistance

**Where it applies:** Punjab; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** 1. The Financial Assistance of ₹8,000/- per month is given to rehabilitate the victims of acid attack and make them self-reliant. **Note:** The amount of payment is directly transferred to the account of the beneficiary by the directorate online.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: District Social Security Office, Punjab; Nodal Department: Department of Social Security and Development of Women and Children.
