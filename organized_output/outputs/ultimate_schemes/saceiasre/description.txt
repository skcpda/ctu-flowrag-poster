# Scheme of Assistance for Common Environment Infrastructure: Scheme of Assistance for Strengthening the Regulation and Environmental Compliance

**Short Title:** SACEIASRE

**Launch Date:** 2020-08-07

**Closing Date:** 2025-08-06

**Tags:** Entrepreneurship, Environment Infrastructure, Environmental Compliance

The scheme &amp;quot;Scheme of Assistance for Common Environment Infrastructure&amp;quot; is an umbrella scheme launched by the Industries and Mines Department, Gujarat under the Gujarat Industrial Policy. Effective from August 7th, 2020 to August 6th, 2025, it aims to boost industrial growth through financial assistance, modern technologies, and enhanced facilities for industrial parks and clusters. The component “Financial Assistance for Common Environmental Infrastructure Facilities” aims to support industries and organizations in establishing and upgrading environmental management infrastructure. It provides financial aid for projects that promote pollution control, waste management, and environmental sustainability.

**Who is this for:** Business Entity, Industries, Government Organisation; Category: Business & Entrepreneurship; Sector: Setting up / start-up / entrepreneurship

**Where it applies:** Gujarat; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - **Quantum of Assistance:** Need based support, maximum ₹50,00,000/-, will be provided as may be decided by the State Level Empowered Committee.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Exclusions / Not eligible:** - The Project availing incentives under this scheme will not be entitled to avail incentives under any other scheme of the State Government for the same component, unless specified otherwise. However, the project will be eligible to avail incentives available under schemes of Government of India or any other institutions.

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Industries and Mines Department.
