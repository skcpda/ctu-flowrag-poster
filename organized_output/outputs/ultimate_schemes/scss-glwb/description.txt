# Shramyogi Cycle Subsidy Scheme- Gujarat Labour Welfare Board

**Short Title:** SCSS-GLWB

**Tags:** Labour, Worker, Subsidy, Shramyogi, Cycle, Bicycle

The “Shramyogi Cycle Subsidy Scheme” is implemented by the Gujarat Labour Welfare Board, Labour, Skill Development &amp;amp; Employment Department, Government of Gujarat. The objective of the scheme is to provide a subsidy for the purchase of a bicycle to laborers working in the organized sector, enabling them to commute between their residence and workplace.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Financial assistance

**Where it applies:** Gujarat; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** 1. Under the scheme, laborers will receive assistance of ₹1,500/- as a subsidy towards the purchase of a bicycle.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: Gujarat Labour Welfare Board; Nodal Department: Labour and Employment Department.
