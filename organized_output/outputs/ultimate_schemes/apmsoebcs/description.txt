# Award of Pre-Matric Scholarship to Other Economically Backward Class (OEBC) Students

**Short Title:** APMSOEBCS

**Tags:** OEBC, Student, School, Education, Scholarship

The &quot;Award of Pre-Matric Scholarship to Other Economically Backward Class (OEBC) Students&quot; initiated by the Directorate of School Education, Union Territory of Puducherry, is designed to extend financial assistance to OEBC students, mitigating the risk of dropouts due to financial constraints. The scheme provides scholarships to students in standards 6 to 10, based on specific eligibility criteria.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Education & Learning; Sector: Scholarships and student finance

**Where it applies:** Puducherry; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** > **Rate of Scholarship** - For standards 6 to 8: ₹50 per month per student (₹500 per year per student). - For standards 9 &amp; 10: ₹75 per month per student (₹750 per year per student). _* For ten months in an academic year._ > **Mode of Disbursement** The amount is disbursed directly into the Aadhaar-seeded bank account of the applicant through Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) and Public Financial Management System (PFMS). 

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: School Education Department, Puducherry.
