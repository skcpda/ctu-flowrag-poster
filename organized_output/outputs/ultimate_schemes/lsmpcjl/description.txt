# Large Sized Multi Purpose Coop. Society: Jewel Loan (Interest Free)

**Short Title:** LSMPCJL

**Tags:** Interest Free Loan, Cooperative Society, Jewel Loan

The &amp;quot;Large Sized Multi Purpose Coop. Society: Jewel Loan (Interest Free)&amp;quot; scheme launched by the Co-operation, Food and Consumer Protection Department, Tamil Nadu, aims to provide interest-free loans for up to ₹1,00,000/- through cooperative societies on jewel loans. The purpose is to offer financial relief without charging any interest on jewel loans, thus aiding citizens in managing expenses or urgent requirements. &lt;br&gt;

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Banking,Financial Services and Insurance; Sector: Loan

**Where it applies:** Tamil Nadu; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - **Access to Loan:** Loan up to ₹1,00,000/-. - **Interest Rate:** No interest.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Co-operation, Food and Consumer Protection Department.

**References / Annexures:** - **Guidelines**: https://www.tn.gov.in/scheme/data_view/3614
