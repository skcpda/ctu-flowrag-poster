# Funeral Assistance (GBOCWWB)

**Short Title:** FA-GBOCWWB

**Tags:** Death, Funeral, Crisis, Financial Assistance, Labour

&quot;Funeral Assistance (GBOCWWB)&quot; is a Welfare Scheme by the Goa Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board of the Department of Labour and Employment, Government of Goa. Through this scheme, the nominee/dependant of the deceased worker is provided with financial assistance of ₹5,000/-. The applications are accepted offline.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Crisis/Disaster/Accident

**Where it applies:** Goa; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** Financial Assistance of ₹5,000/-.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: Goa Building And Other Construction Workers Welfare Board; Nodal Department: Labour and Employment Department.
