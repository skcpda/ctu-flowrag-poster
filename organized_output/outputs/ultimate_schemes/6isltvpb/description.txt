# 6% Interest Subsidy on Loans taken for Vehicle Purchase through Banks

**Short Title:** 6ISLTVPB

**Tags:** Employment, Opportunity, Subsidy, Loan, Scheduled Tribe

The &quot;6% Interest Subsidy on Loans taken for Vehicle Purchase through Banks&quot; scheme was launched by the Tribal Development Department, Government of Gujarat. It aims to support Scheduled Tribe (ST) individuals in purchasing vehicles for self-employment purposes. Under this scheme, tribal beneficiaries availing bank loans for vehicle purchase with interest subsidy. This financial support helps reduce the repayment burden on tribal entrepreneurs while encouraging mobility-based self-employment opportunities. 

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Banking,Financial Services and Insurance; Sector: Loan

**Where it applies:** Gujarat; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - 6% interest subsidy on bank loans for vehicle purchase. - Loan eligibility range: ₹5,00,000 to ₹20,00,000. 

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Tribal Development Department.
