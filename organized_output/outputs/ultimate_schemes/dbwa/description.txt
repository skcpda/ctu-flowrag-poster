# Development of Brackish Water Aquaculture

**Short Title:** DBWA

**Tags:** Agriculture, Fishing Activities, Fishermen, Aquaculture

The scheme &amp;quot;Development of Brackish Water Aquaculture&amp;quot; introduced by the Agriculture, Farmers Welfare and Cooperation Department, Gujarat, aims to enhance the income of brackish water fishermen by promoting modern shrimp farming techniques, improving disease control methods, increasing employment, and boosting shrimp exports. The scheme provides financial assistance in the form of subsidies for shrimp farm development, equipment purchase, and related activities.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Agriculture,Rural & Environment; Sector: Fishing and hunting

**Where it applies:** Gujarat; State/ UT
