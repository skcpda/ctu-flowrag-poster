# New Swarnima Scheme (GBCDC)

**Short Title:** NSSGBCDC

**Tags:** Self Employment, Women, Business, Loan, Backward Class

The scheme &quot;New Swarnima Scheme&quot; by the Social Justice and Empowerment Department, Government of Gujarat, aims to provide a special scheme for self-employment of women from backward classes living below the poverty line. Through this scheme, financial assistance for chosen businesses is provided to female beneficiaries from socially and educationally backward classes. The scheme is implemented by the Gujarat Backward Classes Development Corporation. 

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Women and Child; Sector: Setting up / start-up / entrepreneurship

**Where it applies:** Haryana; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** 1. **Loan Amount:** The maximum loan limit under this scheme is up to ₹200,000/- per beneficiary.1. **Interest Rate:** The interest rate in these schemes will be 5% per annum.1. **Loan Coverage:** 95% of unit cost financed by the national corporation (NBCFDC), 5% by the state government.  > **Repayment Terms:** Repayable in 60 equal monthly installments, including interest. > **Mode of Disbursement:** The loan amount is disbursed, with 95 percent of the unit cost from the national corporation and 5 percent from the state government. 

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: Gujarat Backward Classes Development Corporation; Nodal Department: Social Justice and Empowerment Department.

**References / Annexures:** - **Guidelines**: https://sje.gujarat.gov.in/gbcdc/Schemes/1454 - **Online Application**: https://gbcdconline.gujarat.gov.in/
