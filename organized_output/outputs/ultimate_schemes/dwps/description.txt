# Destitute Widow Pension Scheme

**Short Title:** DWPS

**Tags:** Widow, Women, Pension, Financial Assistance, Destitute

The &#39;Destitute Widow Pension Scheme&#39; is a state scheme launched by the Social Welfare and Women Empowerment Department, Government of Tamil Nadu. The scheme aims to provide a pension of ₹1,000/- to the destitute widows who are 18 years and above in age. The widow is also provided with one free saree during Pongal and Deepavali, and free rice in the Anganwadi Centres. Interested applicants should apply via CSC.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Women and Child; Sector: Financial Assistance

**Where it applies:** Tamil Nadu; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** **Pension:** - ₹1,000/- per month. **Free Clothes during Pongal and Deepavali:** - One free saree. **Free Rice in Anganwadi Centres:** - 2kg/month to those who are taking nutritious meals. - 4kg/month to those who are not taking nutritious meals.

**Benefit Type:** Composite

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Department of Social Welfare and Women Empowerment.
