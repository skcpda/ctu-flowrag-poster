# District Level Sports School

**Short Title:** DLSSG

**Tags:** Sports, Young Athlete Training, School Sports Program

Launched in 2013-14, the scheme &amp;quot;District Level Sports School&amp;quot; by the Sports, Youth and Cultural Activities Department, Government of Gujarat, aims to provide intensive sports training and quality education to young athletes by establishing district-level sports schools under a Public-Private Partnership (PPP) model. The scheme seeks to identify sporting talent from events such as Khel Mahakumbh and train them using modern infrastructure, scientific methods, expert coaching, and proper nutrition, while ensuring access to formal school education. The initiative began with 9 schools (6 residential and 3 non-residential), and expanded to 24 schools by 2017-18, catering to over 2,500 students in 21 sports disciplines. Selected athletes receive benefits such as accommodation, food, school uniforms, books, sports kits, and equipment. An average of ₹2.25 lakh is spent per player annually, including ₹1 lakh Mediclaim security. The scheme is implemented by the Sports Authority of Gujarat (SAG). 

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Sports & Culture; Sector: Sports

**Where it applies:** Gujarat; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** > **Monetary Support &amp; Facilities:** - Players are provided with free education, stationery, books, accommodation, meals, school uniforms, and modern sports equipment and kits. > **Medical Insurance:** - A Mediclaim security cover of up to ₹1,00,000/- is provided to each selected athlete. > **Expert Coaching &amp; Training:** - Intensive sports training is imparted in morning and evening sessions by expert coaches. In-school players receive additional training in 5 Physical Training (PT) periods. 

**Benefit Type:** Composite

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: Sports Authority Of Gujarat; Nodal Department: Sports, Youth and Cultural Activities Department.
