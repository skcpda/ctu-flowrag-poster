# Scheme of Free Supply of Textbooks, Stationery, Uniforms and Footwears to Poor Children

**Short Title:** SFSTSUFPC

**Tags:** Stationery, Uniform, Student, Book, Student

The scheme &quot;Scheme of Free Supply of Textbooks, Stationery, Uniforms and Footwears to Poor Children&quot; is a Scholarship Scheme by the Directorate of School Education, Union Territory of Puducherry. Through this scheme, Text Books, Note Book, Chappals, Uniforms, Dictionary, Stationery items etc. are provided free of cost to all students studying in LKG to 12th Standard of all Government and Government-aided Private Schools in all regions of the Union Territory of Puducherry especially in the age group of 6-14 years. The objective of this scheme is to achieve 100% enrolment and retention of children in Government schools without dropouts by taking various measures.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Education & Learning; Sector: Scholarships and student finance

**Where it applies:** Puducherry; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - Laptop for 12th Standard Passout. - Umbrella for 1st Standard. - Pencil &amp; Eraser for 1st to 5th Standard. - Text Book &amp; Exercise Note Books for 1st to 12th Standard. - Geometry Box for 4th &amp; 6th Standard. - Plastic Scale for 4th Standard. - Crayon, Drawing Note Book for Lower Kindergarten (LKG) &amp; Upper Kindergarten (UKG). - Uniform &amp; Footwear for LKG to 12th Standard. - Copywriting Note Book for UKG. - Ink Pen for 5th to 8th Standard. - Atlas for 6th Standard. - Dictionary for 8th Standard.

**Benefit Type:** In Kind

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Labour Department, Puducherry.
