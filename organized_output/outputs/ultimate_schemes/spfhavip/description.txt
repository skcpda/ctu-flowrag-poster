# Smart Phone for Hearing and Visually Impaired Persons

**Short Title:** SPFHAVIP

**Tags:** Social Welfare, Person With Disability, Hearing Impaired, Visually Impaired

The scheme &#39;Smart Phone for Hearing and Visually Impaired Persons&#39; was launched by the Department for the Welfare of Differently Abled Persons, Government of Tamil Nadu. The scheme aims to distribute smart phones to needy visually impaired and hearing impaired students, unemployed youth, self-employed and women.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Aids/Appliances

**Where it applies:** Tamil Nadu; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - Free distribution of smart phones.

**Benefit Type:** In Kind

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Welfare of Differently Abled Persons Department.

**References / Annexures:** - **Citizen's Charter (2022)**: https://cms.tn.gov.in/sites/default/files/documents/wda_e_cc_2022_23.pdf
