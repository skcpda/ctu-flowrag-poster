# Distribution of Nuclear Poly Hedrosis Virus

**Short Title:** DONPV

**Tags:** Agriculture, Farmer, Nuclear Poly Hedrosis Virus, NPV, Subsidy

The “Distribution of Nuclear Poly Hedrosis Virus” scheme by the Agriculture-Farmers Welfare Department, Tamil Nadu, provides subsidies for the distribution of Nuclear Poly Hedrosis Virus (NPV) to farmers raising seed farms with Pulses crops.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Agriculture,Rural & Environment; Sector: Financial assistance

**Where it applies:** Tamil Nadu; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - **Quantum of Assistance:** 50% cost of input limited to ₹250/- per hectare. 

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Agriculture Department.

**References / Annexures:** - **Guidelines**: https://www.tn.gov.in/scheme/data_view/7078
