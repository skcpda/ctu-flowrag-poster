# Medical Assistance for the Construction Workers

**Short Title:** MAFTCW

**Tags:** Labour, Medical Support, Construction Workers, Hospitalization, Financial Assistance, Accidental Support

The &amp;quot;Medical Assistance for the Construction Workers&amp;quot; by the Delhi Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board, Labour Department, Delhi, is a welfare scheme for the registered workers of the board. Through this scheme, the eligible workers of the board are provided with medical assistance upto ₹ 10,000/- in case of being hospitalized for 05 or more days due to an accident or any disease. 

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Health & Wellness; Sector: Health care providers and access

**Where it applies:** Delhi; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - ₹2,000/- for the first five days. - ₹200/- each for remaining days, upto a maximum of ₹10,000/-, available from the date of registration. 

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: Delhi Building And Other Construction Workers Welfare Board; Nodal Department: Labour Department.
