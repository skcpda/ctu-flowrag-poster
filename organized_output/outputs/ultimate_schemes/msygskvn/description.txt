# Mahila Samruddhi Yojana (GSKVN)

**Short Title:** MSYGSKVN

**Tags:** Small Business, Loan, Interest, Shops

The scheme &quot;Mahila Samruddhi Yojana (GSKVN)&quot; by the Social Justice and Empowerment Department, Gujarat Government, aims to provide financial assistance to female Safai Kamdars and their dependents to start small businesses. Through this scheme, loans for various self-employment ventures are provided to eligible female individuals. The scheme is implemented by the Gujarat Safai Kamdar Vikas Nigam (GSKVN). 

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Business & Entrepreneurship; Sector: Setting up / start-up / entrepreneurship

**Where it applies:** Gujarat; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** 1. An individual loan of ₹1,00,000/- is provided. 1. The interest rate on the loan is 4%. 1. The loan is intended for various businesses/occupations such as Mandap Decoration, Computer, Electrical Shop, Animal Husbandry, Grocery Shop, Bamboo Work, Sewing Work, Embroidery, Beauty Parlour, Mobile Repairing, etc. 

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Exclusions / Not eligible:**  

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: Gujarat Safai Kamdar Vikas Nigam; Nodal Department: Social Justice and Empowerment Department.
