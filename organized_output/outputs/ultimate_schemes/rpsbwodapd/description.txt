# Retrofitted Petrol Scooters - Tamil Nadu

**Short Title:** RPSTN

**Tags:** Differently Abled Person, Social Welfare, Scooter, Orthopedically, Student, Employed

The &#39;Retrofitted Petrol Scooters by Welfare of Differently Abled Persons Department&#39; scheme was launched by the Department for the Welfare of Differently Abled Persons, Government. of Tamil Nadu. The scheme aims to provide a retrofitted petrol scooter to orthopedically differently-abled persons whose legs are affected. The students, employed and self-employed differently abled persons are eligible to get the scheme benefits.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Aids/Appliances

**Where it applies:** Tamil Nadu; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - **Free distribution of retrofitted petrol scooters.**

**Benefit Type:** In Kind

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Welfare of Differently Abled Persons Department.
