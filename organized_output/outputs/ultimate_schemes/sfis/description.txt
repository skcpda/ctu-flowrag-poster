# State Fisheries Input Scheme

**Short Title:** SFIS

**Tags:** Financial Assistance, Subsidy, Fish Farming, Fish Farmer, Fishermen

The scheme &amp;quot;State Fisheries Input Scheme&amp;quot; was launched by the Department of Fisheries, Government of Uttarakhand. The scheme aims to provide financial assistance to fish farmers in the form of input subsidies to enhance productivity and sustainability in fish farming. Under this scheme, fish farmers who are already engaged in fish farming are provided a 50% subsidy on fish feed, medicines, and essential input materials such as hand nets, hapa, nets, mini kits, etc.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Agriculture,Rural & Environment; Sector: Fishing and hunting

**Where it applies:** Uttarakhand; State/ UT

**DBT Scheme:** Yes (Direct Benefit Transfer)

**Benefits / Assistance:** > **Tangible Benefits:** **50% Subsidy on Fish Farming Inputs:** - Subsidy on the market value of fish feed, medicines, hatchnets, hapas, nets, and minikits. **Fish Feed Subsidy Allocation Based on Pond Size:** - 1 quintal feed for 100 sq. meter pond. - 6 quintals feed for 500 to 2,000 sq. meter pond. - 7 quintals feed for R.A.S./Bio floc units. - 10 quintals feed for Trout farming units. **Input Material Subsidy:** - 50% subsidy on market cost of handnet, hapa, net, and minikit. - Limited to 1 unit per item per fish farmer.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Department Of Fisheries.
