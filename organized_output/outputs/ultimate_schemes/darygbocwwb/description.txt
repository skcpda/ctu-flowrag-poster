# Dhanvantari Arogya Rath Yojana (GBOCWWB)

**Short Title:** DARY(GBOCWWB)

**Tags:** Building Worker, Construction Worker, Labour, Common Diseases, Free Medicines, Primary Medical Treatment

The “Dhanvantari Arogya Rath Yojana” was launched by the Gujarat Building and Other Construction Worker’s Welfare Board (GBOCWWB), Labour, Skill Development &amp;amp; Employment Department, Government of Gujarat. The scheme has been implemented to ensure that construction workers and their families get primary medical treatment at their doorsteps, prevent serious diseases, and maintain their health at places including construction sites, labour colonies, and Kadianaka. The scheme is implemented by EMRI Green Health Services Gujarat (GVK-108) through making a tripartite MoU with the Health Department and EMRI Green Health Services Gujarat.

**Who is this for:** Family, Individual; Category: Health & Wellness; Sector: Health care providers and access

**Where it applies:** Gujarat; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - Under this scheme, treatment of primary injuries including common diseases like fever, diarrhea, vomiting, skin diseases and laboratory tests like urine, blood, blood sugar, malaria, and various diagnoses including primary care of women and children of their family and primary care of pregnant women during pregnancy done on the spot. Along with this, they are also given free medicines on the spot.

**Benefit Type:** In Kind

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: EMRI Green Health Services Gujarat; Nodal Department: Labour and Employment Department.
