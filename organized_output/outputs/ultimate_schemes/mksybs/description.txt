# Mukhyamantri Kishori Swasthya Yojna

**Short Title:** MKSYBS

**Tags:** Adolescent Girls, Menstrual Hygiene, Sanitary Napkins, Health And Wellness

The &amp;quot;Mukhyamantri Kishori Swasthya Yojna&amp;quot;, launched by the Education Department, Bihar, addresses the issue of increased school dropout rates among girls during puberty due to a lack of access to sanitary hygiene products. Under this scheme, the State Government distributes free sanitary napkins to adolescent girls in secondary schools, encouraging them to continue their education while promoting awareness of menstrual health and hygiene.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Health & Wellness; Sector: Menstrual hygiene

**Where it applies:** Bihar; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - Free sanitary napkins for adolescent girls in secondary schools. &lt;br&gt;

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Education Department.
