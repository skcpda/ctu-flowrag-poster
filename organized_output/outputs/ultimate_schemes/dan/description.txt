# Delhi Arogya Nidhi

**Short Title:** DAN

**Tags:** Arogya, Nidhi, Health, Patient, Treatment

The &amp;quot;Delhi Arogya Nidhi (DAN)&amp;quot; is a scheme implemented by the Directorate General of Health Services to provide financial assistance of up to ₹1.5 lacs to needy patients who have National Food Security Cards for treatment of diseases in Government hospitals only.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Health & Wellness; Sector: Financial assistance

**Where it applies:** Delhi; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** 1. Under the scheme, financial assistance of up to ₹1.5 lacs will be provided to needy patients who have National Food Security Cards for treatment of diseases in Government hospitals only.

**Benefit Type:** In Kind

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: Directorate General Of Health Services, Government NCT Of Delhi; Nodal Department: Health & Family Welfare.
