# Managerial Assistance Scheme for Appointment of Veterinarian

**Short Title:** MASAV

**Tags:** Agriculture, Animal Husbandry, Veterinarian

The &amp;quot;Managerial Assistance Scheme for Appointment of Veterinarian&amp;quot; launched by the Department of Agriculture, Farmers Welfare &amp;amp; Co-operation, Government of Gujarat, aims to encourage Panjrapols to maintain animal health by appointing veterinarians who will provide medical services, artificial insemination (AI), and vaccinations. The scheme also promotes scientific guidance and technical record maintenance. &lt;br&gt;

**Who is this for:** Registered Societies; Category: Agriculture,Rural & Environment; Sector: Animal husbandry

**Where it applies:** Gujarat; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:**  **Component** **Standard of Assistance****Minimum Time Limit (in Years) for Reclaiming Constituent Benefits**Managerial assistance to appoint VeterinarianThe Beneficiary Panjarapol Institute will pay ₹45,000/- per month to the appointed Veterinarian of which 75% will be paid to the Beneficiary Institute as a maximum of ₹33,750/- per month as subsidy.3 Year

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Agriculture, Farmers Welfare and Cooperation Department.
