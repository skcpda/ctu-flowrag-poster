# Aatmanirbhar Gujarat Scheme for assistance to MSMEs: Assistance in Implementation of Information and Communication Technology 

**Short Title:** AGMSMEICT

**Tags:** Business, Entrepreneurship, MSMEs, Start-up

The &amp;quot;Aatmanirbhar Gujarat Scheme for MSMEs&amp;quot; is an umbrella scheme launched by the Industries and Mines Department, Gujarat. It aims to empower the state&amp;#39;s dynamic MSME sector, which significantly contributes to employment, GDP, and exports. Effective from October 5, 2022, to October 4, 2027, this initiative supports growth, investments, and the vision of &amp;quot;Aatmanirbhar Gujarat&amp;quot; and &amp;quot;Aatmanirbhar Bharat.&amp;quot; The component &amp;quot;Assistance in Implementation of Information and Communication Technology &amp;quot; offers financial assistance to enterprises for the implementation of Information and Communication Technology (ICT) solutions, including hardware for cloud computing and networking.

**Who is this for:** Industries, Business Entity; Category: Business & Entrepreneurship; Sector: Setting up / start-up / entrepreneurship

**Where it applies:** Gujarat; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** &gt; **Capital Expenditure Support:** 1. Up to 65% of the capital expenditure related to ICT facilities is eligible for reimbursement. 1. Maximum assistance is up to ₹5,00,000/- during the operative period of the scheme. &gt; **Coverage for Previous Expenditure:** 1. Enterprises opting for the scheme can also receive assistance for expenses incurred during the previous scheme’s operative period until the date of issue of this GR.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Exclusions / Not eligible:** - Enterprises not engaged in the implementation of ICT solutions are not eligible. - Expenditures incurred after the operative period of the scheme are not eligible for assistance. &lt;br&gt;

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Industries and Mines Department.
