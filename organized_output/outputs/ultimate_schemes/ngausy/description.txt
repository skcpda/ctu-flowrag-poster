# Nai Hetu Avashyak Upkaran Sahayata Yojana

**Short Title:** NGAUSY

**Launch Date:** 2018-01-27

**Tags:** Business, Equipment, Empowerment, Employment

Labor Department of Chhattisgarh State started this scheme &amp;quot;Nai Hetu Avashyak Upkaran Sahayata Yojana&amp;quot; for unorganized worker on 27 January 2018.. The main objective of the scheme to empower unorganized worker by providing required equipment for their work.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Skills & Employment

**Where it applies:** Chhattisgarh; State

**Benefits / Assistance:** > Registered barbers will be provided barber kit including scissors, straight razor, comb, hair cutting cape, water spray bottle and shaving brush.

**Benefit Type:** In Kind

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Labour Department.
