# Financial Assistance for the Education of Children of the Beneficiary (O.B.O.C.W.W.B)

**Short Title:** FAECBOBOCWWB

**Tags:** Labour, Building Worker, Construction Worker, Education, Study, Scholarships

The “Financial Assistance for the Education of Children of the Beneficiary” scheme by the Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board, Labour Department, Odisha, is a welfare scheme for the registered workers of the board. Under this scheme, the Board shall provide financial assistance for education to the children of the registered member of the board for a period of one year or more. This assistance shall be entitled up to two children. In case both the spouses are registered beneficiaries, either of them will be eligible to get the benefit of educational assistance. &lt;br&gt;

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Education & Learning; Sector: Scholarships and student finance

**Where it applies:** Odisha; State/ UT
