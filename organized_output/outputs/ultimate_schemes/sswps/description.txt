# Solar Water Pumping System

**Short Title:** SSWPS

**Tags:** Renewable Energy, Solar Water Pump, Solar Irrigation, Micro Irrigation

The scheme &amp;quot;Solar Water Pumping System&amp;quot; by the Renewable Energy Department, Government of Haryana, aims to encourage farmers and agriculture-based community groups to adopt clean and sustainable energy for irrigation by offering subsidized solar water pumping systems. It helps in improving agricultural productivity, reducing dependency on grid electricity or diesel, and promoting water-use efficiency through integration with micro-irrigation systems. Beneficiaries are eligible for a 75% subsidy on the installation of solar pumps. 

**Who is this for:** Family, Individual, Registered Societies, Government Organisation; Category: Agriculture,Rural & Environment; Sector: Mechanization- solar power, farming systems

**Where it applies:** Haryana; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - **Subsidy Amount:** A 75% subsidy is provided on the cost of solar water pumps.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Exclusions / Not eligible:** - The applicant who has already availed benefits under any previous solar pump scheme is not eligible.  

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Renewable Energy Department.
