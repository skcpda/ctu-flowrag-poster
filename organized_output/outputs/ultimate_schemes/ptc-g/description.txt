# Popular Tiatr Competition

**Short Title:** PTC-G

**Tags:** Artist, Culture, Competition, Award, Acting, Singing

The scheme &quot;Popular Tiatr Competition&quot; was launched by the Tiatr Academy under the Art, Culture and Museums Department, Government of Goa. The scheme aims to bring about all round development of Popular Tiatr by organizing annual competitions in different parts of Goa, preserving and promoting the traditional format of tiatr performances. The scheme provides cash prizes ranging from ₹2,000/- to ₹2,00,000/- for various categories including performance, direction, acting, singing, and technical aspects, along with mementos and certificates for winners. The applicant must have completed at least 5 performances including Kantaram during the period from 1st April 2018 to 31st December 2018 and must perform in specified auditoriums across Goa. The scheme is implemented by Tiatr Academy of Goa. The applications for this scheme are accepted offline through submission of application forms along with required documents. 

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Sports & Culture; Sector: Culture

**Where it applies:** Goa; State/ UT

**Exclusions / Not eligible:** - The applicant cannot include Kantaram presented in the Kala Academy Tiatr Competitions in the Popular Tiatr Competition, unless a tiatr from the Kala Academy&#39;s Competition is admitted in the Popular Tiatr Competition. - The applicant cannot participate artists including technicians and backstage artists in more than one group and one Tiatr, however musicians and stage setting personnel can participate in more than 2 Tiatrs.
