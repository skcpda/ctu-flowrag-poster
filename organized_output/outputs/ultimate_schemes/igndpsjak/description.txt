# Indira Gandhi National Disability Pension Scheme (Jammu and Kashmir)

**Short Title:** IGNDPSJAK

**Launch Date:** 1995-08-15

**Tags:** Below Poverty Line, Disability, Pension, Social Welfare

Launched on 15th August 1995, the scheme &quot;Indira Gandhi National Disability Pension Scheme (IGNDPS)&quot; is a pension scheme by the Social Welfare Department, Union Territory of Jammu and Kashmir. The scheme provides ₹1,000/- per month to BPL individuals aged 18 to 79 who have severe or multiple disabilities. Launched under the National Social Assistance Programme (NSAP), the scheme aims to ensure social justice by providing financial support to the most vulnerable sections of society, fulfilling the Directive Principles of State Policy.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Pension

**Where it applies:** Jammu and Kashmir; State/ UT

**DBT Scheme:** Yes (Direct Benefit Transfer)

**Benefits / Assistance:** **Amount of Pension:** The beneficiary will receive a monthly pension of ₹1000/-. **Mode of Disbursement:** The pension is disbursed to the beneficiary either through bank transfer or money order.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Department Of Social Welfare J and K.
