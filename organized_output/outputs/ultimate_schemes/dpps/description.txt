# Disabled Person Pension Scheme - Punjab

**Short Title:** Dpps

**Tags:** Social Welfare, Differently Abled, Financial Assistance

Disabled People often do not have any permanent source of income. Due to this they are not able to take care of their daily need expenses. Government of Punjab comes to the rescue of disabled people by starting Disability Pension Scheme in State. The main objective behind launching this scheme is to provide financial support to disabled people of Punjab. Social Security and Women &amp;amp; Child Development Department of Punjab Government is the nodal department of this scheme.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Pension

**Where it applies:** Punjab; State

**DBT Scheme:** Yes (Direct Benefit Transfer)

**Benefits / Assistance:** Amount : ₹ 1,500/- per month

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Department of Social Security and Development of Women and Children.
