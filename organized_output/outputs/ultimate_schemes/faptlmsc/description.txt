# Financial Assistance for Preparation of Training Camp and Participation in Land, Mountain & Sea Adventure Sports Competitions

**Short Title:** FAPTLMSC

**Tags:** Adventure Sports, Financial Assistance, Youth Empowerment, Training Camps

The scheme &amp;quot;Financial Assistance for Preparation of Training Camp and Participation in Land, Mountain &amp;amp; Sea Adventure Sports Competitions&amp;quot; by the Sports, Youth and Cultural Activities Department, Government of Gujarat aims to promote adventure sports among the youth of Gujarat by encouraging their participation in training programs, workshops, expeditions, and competitions organized within and outside the state. The scheme offers financial support to cover expenses such as fees, equipment, insurance, transportation, and other relevant costs. 

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Sports & Culture; Sector: Sports

**Where it applies:** Gujarat; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - **Financial Assistance:** Up to ₹30,000/- or 75% of the actual expenditure (whichever is lower). 

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Sports, Youth and Cultural Activities Department.

**References / Annexures:** - **Guidelines (Scheme No. 181)**: https://mariyojana.gujarat.gov.in/Schemeatoz.aspx
