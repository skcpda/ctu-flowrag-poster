# Rajya Pratibha Khoj Pariksha Chhatravriti Yojana

**Short Title:** RPKPCY

**Tags:** Scholarship, Student, Talent Search, Secondary Education, Higher Education

The scheme &amp;quot;Rajya Pratibha Khoj Pariksha Chhatravriti Yojana&amp;quot; was implemented by the Secondary Education Department, Government of Rajasthan. Under this scheme, students are provided with scholarships or a one-time incentive for pursuing education up to the postgraduate level, after being selected through the State Talent Search Examination organized by the Board of Secondary Education Rajasthan, Ajmer. Students studying in Classes 10 and 12 in all government schools, recognized private schools, and Swami Vivekanand Model Schools in the state, who have secured a minimum of 50% marks in Classes 9 and 11 respectively, are eligible to apply for the examination. Students selected through this examination will be entitled to the benefits provided under the scheme.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Education & Learning; Sector: Universities and higher education

**Where it applies:** Rajasthan; State/ UT
