# Nirman Shramikon Ke Bacchon Hetu Nishulk Ganvesh Evam Pustak Copy Hetu Sahaayata Raashi Yojana

**Short Title:** NSKBGC

**Launch Date:** 2022-12-09

**Tags:** School Uniform, Education, Financial Assistance

Labor Department of Chhattisgarh State started this scheme &amp;amp;quot;Nirman Shramikon Ke Bacchon Hetu Nishulk Ganvesh Evam Pustak Copy Hetu Sahaayata Raashi Yojana&amp;amp;quot; for Construction Workers on 09 December 2022. The main objective of the scheme to provide financial assistance for the purpose of providing educational aids like uniforms and stationery. &amp;lt;br&amp;gt;

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Education & Learning

**Where it applies:** Chhattisgarh; State

**DBT Scheme:** Yes (Direct Benefit Transfer)

**Benefits / Assistance:** The proposed assistance amount for school uniform and book copy will be payable in lump sum as mentioned below: ClassesAmountClass 1 to 8₹ 1,000/-Class 9 to 12₹ 2,000/-

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Labour Department.
