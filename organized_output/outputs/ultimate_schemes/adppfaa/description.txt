# Assistance to Disabled Persons for Purchase/Fitting of Aids and Appliances (NIEPVD Dehradun)

**Short Title:** ADPPFAA

**Tags:** Disability Aids, Assistive Devices, Free Aids, Visual Impairment, Hearing Impairment

The scheme &amp;quot;Assistance to Disabled Persons for Purchase/Fitting of Aids and Appliances (ADIP)&amp;quot; implemented by National Institute for Empowerment of Persons with Visual Disabilities (NIEPVD), Dehradun, aims to assist persons with disabilities in enhancing their physical, social, and psychological rehabilitation by providing them with modern, standard aids and appliances. Through this scheme, eligible visually, hearing, locomotor, and mentally disabled individuals receive necessary assistive devices free of cost or at a subsidized rate. The scheme is implemented by the National Institute for the Empowerment of Persons with Visual Disabilities (NIEPVD), Dehradun.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Aids/Appliances

**Where it applies:** Uttarakhand; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - **For Visually Impaired:** Braille Kit, Daisy Player, Refractive Mirror, Mobility Cane, Smart Phone. - **For Hearing Impaired:** Hearing Aid. - **For Locomotor Disabled:** Wheelchair, Tricycle, Walking Sticks, Calipers, Crutches. - **For Mentally Disabled:** MR Kit. &gt; **Note:** - For those with an annual income up to ₹22,500/-, the aid is provided completely free. - For those with an annual income up to ₹30,000/-, 50% of the cost of the aid must be borne by the beneficiary. &lt;br&gt;

**Benefit Type:** In Kind

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: National Institute For The Empowerment Of Persons With Visual Disabilities, Dehradun; Nodal Department: Department Of Social Welfare.
