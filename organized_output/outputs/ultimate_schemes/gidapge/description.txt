# Group Insurance for Differently Abled Person Government Employees

**Short Title:** GIDAPGE

**Tags:** Government, Employee, Insurance, Banking, Disability

The scheme “Group Insurance for Differently Abled Person Government Employees&quot; was launched by the Department of Social Welfare, Government of Puducherry. In this scheme, in the case of death due to accident while in service the sum insured will be given to the family of the deceased employee.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Banking,Financial Services and Insurance; Sector: Insurance

**Where it applies:** Puducherry; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** In case of death due to accident while in service the sum insured as follows will be given to the family of the deceased employee: - **Group A:** ₹ 1,20,000/-.- **Group B:** ₹ 60,000/-.- **Group C:** ₹ 30,000/-.- **Group D:** ₹ 15,000/-.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Social Welfare Department, Puducherry.
