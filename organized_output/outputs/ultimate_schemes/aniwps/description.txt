# The Andaman & Nicobar Islands Widow Pension Scheme

**Short Title:** ANIWPS

**Tags:** Widow, Pension, Woman, Financial Assistance, Poor

The scheme “The Andaman &amp;amp; Nicobar Islands Widow Pension Scheme” is implemented by the Directorate of Social Welfare, Union Territory of Andaman and Nicobar Islands to provide financial assistance to poor and destitute widows in the Andaman and Nicobar Islands. The beneficiary shall cease to receive the assistance if she gets remarried or becomes financially independent afterward, which may be divulged by due enquiry or through records.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Pension

**Where it applies:** Andaman and Nicobar Islands; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** 1. The quantum of Widow Allowance shall be ₹2500/- per month subject to eligibility. 1. Payment of allowance shall be made through the Bank Account on a monthly basis.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: Directorate Of Social Welfare, Union Territory Of Andaman And Nicobar Islands; Nodal Department: Social Welfare Department.
