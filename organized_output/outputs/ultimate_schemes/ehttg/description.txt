# Economic Help To Tribal Girls For Higher Education (College Level)

**Short Title:** EHTTG

**Launch Date:** 1995-01-01

**Tags:** Financial Assistance, Student, Higher Education, Educational Grant

In order to encourage tribal girl students of Rajasthan state to study in higher education and to promote girl education, financial assistance is provided to tribal girl students for higher education (college level).

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Education & Learning; Sector: Scholarships and student finance

**Where it applies:** Rajasthan; State

**Benefits / Assistance:** - Annual financial assistance of Rs. 5,000/- is provided for 10 months at the rate of Rs.500/- per month.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Tribal Area Development Department.

**References / Annexures:** - **Guidelines**: https://schemes.rajasthan.gov.in/scheme/detail/451 - **Registration**: https://sso.rajasthan.gov.in/register
