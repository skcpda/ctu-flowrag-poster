# Dr. Bhim Rao Ambedkar Meritorious Student Award Scheme

**Short Title:** DBRAMSAS

**Tags:** Bhimrao Ambedkar, Meritorious Student, Prize, Award, Scheduled Caste

The &amp;quot;Dr. Bhim Rao Ambedkar Meritorious Student Award Scheme&amp;quot; was launched by the Scheduled Caste Welfare Department, Government of Madhya Pradesh, to encourage students from the Scheduled Caste category to achieve high academic scores and motivate them to pursue higher education. This scheme awards special prizes to meritorious Scheduled Caste students who secure the highest marks in the Class 10th and 12th board examinations conducted by the Board of Secondary Education, CBSE, and ICSE in the state.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Education & Learning

**Where it applies:** Madhya Pradesh; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** **Awards for securing the highest marks in the Class 10th Board examinations:** - **1st prize (Boy/Girl):** ₹30,000/- - **2nd prize (Boy/Girl):** ₹25,000/- - **3rd prize (Boy/Girl):** ₹20,000/- - **4th prize (Only Girl):** ₹15,000/- - **5th prize (Only Girl):** ₹10,000/- - **6th prize (Only Girl):** ₹5,000/- **Awards for securing the highest marks in the Class 12th Board examinations:** - **1st prize (Boy/Girl):** ₹51,000/- - **2nd prize (Boy/Girl):** ₹40,000/- - **3rd prize (Boy/Girl):** ₹30,000/- - **4th prize (Only Girl):** ₹20,000/- - **5th prize (Only Girl):** ₹15,000/- - **6th prize (Only Girl):** ₹10,000/- **Note:** Payments are made into the bank accounts of the applicants.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: Scheduled Caste Welfare Department, Government Of Madhya Pradesh; Nodal Department: Tribal Welfare and Scheduled Caste (SC) Department.
