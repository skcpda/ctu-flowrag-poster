# Free Pre-Recruitment Training

**Short Title:** FPRT

**Tags:** Recruitment Training, Soldier, Ex-serviceman, Military Forces, Paramilitary Forces, Police Forces

The scheme &amp;quot;Free Pre-Recruitment Training&amp;quot; is implemented by the State Sainik Board under the Sainik Welfare Department, Government of Uttarakhand. The scheme aims to provide 8 weeks of free pre-recruitment training for the Military, Paramilitary, and Police forces to the sons of ex-servicemen aged between 16 and 21 years. 

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Skills & Employment; Sector: Training and Skill Up-gradation

**Where it applies:** Uttarakhand; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** Under this scheme, 8 weeks of free pre-recruitment training for recruitment into the Military, Paramilitary, and Police Forces is provided to eligible beneficiaries. 

**Benefit Type:** In Kind

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: State Sainik Board, Government Of Uttarakhand; Nodal Department: State Sainik Board.
