# Indira Gandhi National Disability Pension Scheme- Chhattisgarh

**Short Title:** IGNDPSCHH

**Tags:** Indira Gandhi, Disability, Pension, BPL, PWD

The scheme &amp;quot;Indira Gandhi National Disability Pension Scheme&amp;quot; is implemented by the Social Welfare Department, Government of Chhattisgarh. Under this scheme, financial assistance is provided to severely disabled (with a disability of 80% and above) and multi-disabled individuals aged 18 to 79 years who are living below the poverty line (BPL) to support them in living a respectful life. This scheme is centrally sponsored.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Pension

**Where it applies:** Chhattisgarh; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - Under this scheme, eligible beneficiaries will receive a monthly pension of ₹500/-, (Central contribution- ₹300/-, State Contribution- ₹200/-).

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: Social Welfare Department, Government Of Chhattisgarh; Nodal Department: Social Welfare Department.
