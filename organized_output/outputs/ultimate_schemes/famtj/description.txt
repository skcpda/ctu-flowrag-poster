# Financial Assistance for Medical Treatment of Journalists

**Short Title:** FAMTJ

**Tags:** Medical, Financial Assistance, Daughter, Disease

The Tamil Development, Religious Endowments, and Information Department scheme was launched by the &quot;Financial Assistance for Medical Treatment of Journalists&quot;. Under this scheme, financial assistance was provided to four Journalists for medical treatment and to the daughter of a journalist for surgery. The total financial assistance extended under the scheme was ₹50,000/-.

**Who is this for:** Journalist; Category: Health & Wellness; Sector: Financial assistance

**Where it applies:** Tamil Nadu; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - Medical Assistant of ₹50,000/-.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Tamil Development and Information Department.
