# SUBHADRA

**Tags:** Women Empowerment, Financial Support, Digital Financial Literacy, DBT

“SUBHADRA” is a flagship scheme by the Women And Child Development Department, Government of Odisha aimed at empowering women through financial support, enhanced digital literacy, and access to social safety nets. The scheme provides direct benefits and incentivizes digital transactions, focusing on improving the socio-economic conditions of women and their families. &lt;br&gt;

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Women and Child; Sector: Financial Assistance

**Where it applies:** Odisha; State/ UT

**DBT Scheme:** Yes (Direct Benefit Transfer)

**Benefits / Assistance:** - **SUBHADRA Card:** All beneficiaries receive a SUBHADRA card (ATM-cum-debit card) to enhance identity and empowerment. - **Financial Support:** Eligible beneficiaries receive a total of ₹50,000/- over 5 years (₹10,000/- annually between 2024-25 to 2028-29). - **Digital Transactions Incentive:** Top 100 women in each Gram Panchayat/Urban Local Body with the highest number of digital transactions in a year receive an additional ₹500/-. &lt;br&gt;

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Women and Child Development Department.
