# Financial Assistance to Indigent Tamil Scholars

**Short Title:** FATITS

**Tags:** Financial Assistance, Indigent, Artist, Culture

The Arts and Culture Department, Puducherry launched the scheme &amp;quot;Financial Assistance to Indigent Tamil Scholars&amp;quot;. The scheme aims to provide financial assistance of ₹1,500/- per month to indigent artists aged 58 years and above and contribution to Tamil language and literature must be of significance. The applications are accepted offline.

**Who is this for:** Artists; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Financial assistance

**Where it applies:** Puducherry; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - Financial Assistance of ₹1,500/- per month.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Arts and Culture Department, Puducherry.
