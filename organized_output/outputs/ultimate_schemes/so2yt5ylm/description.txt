# Scheme for the Welfare of Schedule Caste Families in Fisheries Sector: Subsidy on 2nd Year to 5th Year Lease Money

**Short Title:** SWSCFFSS2Y5YLM

**Tags:** Subsidy, Fish Farming, Scheduled Caste, Fisheries, Lease, Fish

The “Subsidy on 2nd Year to 5th Year Lease Money” is a scheme component under the Scheme for the Welfare of Scheduled Caste Families in the Fisheries Sector, launched by the Fisheries Department, Government of Haryana. The objective of the scheme is to provide full-time employment to individuals from Scheduled Caste families in the fisheries sector and uplift their economic status by promoting fish farming through financial assistance for leasing Panchayati ponds or watershed areas. Under this scheme, a subsidy is provided to Scheduled Caste beneficiaries to partially cover the lease amount for Panchayati ponds or watershed areas from the second year to the fifth year, thereby reducing long-term operational costs and facilitating the continuation of pond lease arrangements. 

**Who is this for:** Family, Individual; Category: Agriculture,Rural & Environment; Sector: Fishing and hunting

**Where it applies:** Haryana; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** **Financial Assistance:** - 40% of the actual lease amount OR ₹40,000/- per hectare, whichever is less. - The subsidy is applicable for a maximum of 4 hectares per beneficiary. **Note:** Subsidy will be provided on lease money from 2nd to 5th year of lease. 

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Exclusions / Not eligible:** - The applicant must not be a Government/ Semi-Government employee. 

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Fisheries Department.
