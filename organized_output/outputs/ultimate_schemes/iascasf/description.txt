# Incentive Assistance to Scheduled Caste Farmers to Increase the Area, Production and Productivity of Sugarcane Farming

**Short Title:** IASCASF

**Tags:** Agriculture, Farmer, Incentive, Scheduled Caste, SC, Sugarcane Farming

The scheme &amp;quot;Incentive Assistance to Scheduled Caste Farmers to Increase the Area, Production and Productivity of Sugarcane Farming&amp;quot; introduced by the Agriculture, Farmers Welfare and Cooperation Department, Gujarat, aims to support Scheduled Caste farmers by providing cash incentives for high-quality seeds, modern farming techniques, and agricultural mechanization. The scheme encourages farmers to enhance productivity and profitability.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Agriculture,Rural & Environment; Sector: Agricultural Inputs- seeds, fertilizer etc.

**Where it applies:** Gujarat; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:**  **Name of Component****Standard of Assistance****Minimum Time Limit (in Years) for Reclaiming Benefits**Assistance for sugarcane Sowing₹10,000/- per HectareEvery year10% assistance for excellent production10% extra benefit per metric ton for production exceeding 70 metric Tons/Hectares.Every year> **Note:** This scheme covers a cultivation area of 500 hectares of sugarcane for Scheduled Tribe farmers.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Agriculture, Farmers Welfare and Cooperation Department.
