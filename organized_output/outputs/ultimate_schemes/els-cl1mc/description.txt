# Education Loan Scheme- Credit Line 1 for Minority Community

**Short Title:** ELS-CL1MC

**Tags:** Education Loan, Credit Line, Minority Community, Higher Education

The scheme &amp;quot;Education Loan Scheme- Credit Line 1 for Minority Community&amp;quot; is implemented by the Haryana Backward Classes and Economically Weaker Sections Kalyan Nigam. Under the scheme, education loans are provided for pursuing general/professional/ technical courses or training at the graduate and/or higher level to minority communities (Muslims, Sikhs, Christians, Buddhists, Parsis, and Jains) as notified by the Government of India. The Nigam provides loans up to ₹20,00,000/- for studies in India and up to ₹30,00,000/- for studies abroad at an interest rate of 3%. Beneficiaries must have obtained admission to a professional or technical course approved by an appropriate agency such as AICTE, Medical Council of India, UGC, etc. in a recognized institute and must have secured a minimum of 50% marks in the qualifying exam.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Banking,Financial Services and Insurance; Sector: Loan

**Where it applies:** Haryana; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** 1. Under this scheme, Nigam provides loans up to ₹20,00,000/- for studies in India and up to ₹30,00,000/- for studies abroad at an interest rate of 3%. 1. The education loans are provided for pursuing general/professional/technical courses or training at the graduate and/or higher level to minority communities i.e. Muslims, Sikhs, Christians, Buddhists, Parsis, and Jains.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: Haryana Backward Classes And Economically Weaker Sections Kalyan Nigam; Nodal Department: Welfare of Scheduled Caste & Backward Classes Department.
