# Transportation Scheme (Gujarat Gopalak Vikas Nigam)

**Short Title:** TSGGVN

**Tags:** Auto, Rickshaw, Loading Vehicle, Rabari Caste, Bharwad Caste

The scheme &quot;Transportation Scheme&quot; by the Gujarat Gopalak Vikas Nigam, Government of Gujarat, aims to provide loans for the purchase of auto rickshaws and loading vehicles. Through this scheme, financial assistance for vehicle purchase is offered to the Rabari and Bharwad castes from the socially and educationally backwards classes. The scheme is implemented by the Gujarat Gopalak Vikas Nigam. 

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Transport & Infrastructure; Sector: Public transport and private vehicles

**Where it applies:** Gujarat; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** Loans are provided on an individual basis to purchase auto rickshaws and loading vehicles. - Maximum Loan Limit: ₹2,00,000/- - Interest Rate: 6% per annum - Loan Amount: 95% of the vehicle cost - Beneficiary Contribution: 5% of the vehicle cost 

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Social Justice and Empowerment Department.
