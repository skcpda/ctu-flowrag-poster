# Financial Assistance for Scaling Mount Everest and Other Inaccessible Mountains of the World

**Short Title:** FASMEIMW

**Tags:** Mountaineering, Everest Climbing, Adventure Sports

The scheme &amp;quot;Financial Assistance for Scaling Mount Everest and Other Inaccessible Mountains of the World&amp;quot; by the Sports, Youth and Cultural Activities Department, Government of Gujarat, aims to encourage and reward mountaineers who demonstrate extraordinary courage and determination by scaling some of the most difficult mountain peaks in the world. The scheme offers tiered financial assistance based on the height and difficulty of the peak climbed, ranging from ₹50,000 to ₹15,00,000, with the maximum incentive awarded to individuals who successfully scale Mt. Everest. The scheme is implemented by the Commissioner, Youth Services and Cultural Activities, Gandhinagar. 

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Sports & Culture; Sector: Sports

**Where it applies:** Gujarat; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** > **Financial Support for Mountaineers:** - Financial assistance ranging from ₹50,000/- to ₹10,00,000/- is provided to individuals depending on the height and difficulty of the mountain scaled. - A maximum of ₹15,00,000/- is provided to those who successfully climb Mount Everest.   

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: Commissioner, Youth Services And Cultural Activities, Gandhinagar; Nodal Department: Sports, Youth and Cultural Activities Department.
