# Dilli Swarojgar Yojna

**Short Title:** DSY

**Tags:** Loan, Employment, Entrepreneurship, Scheduled Caste, Scheduled Tribe, Minority, Business

The scheme &quot;Dilli Swarojgar Yojna&quot; by the Delhi Scheduled Castes, Scheduled Tribes, Other Backward Classes, and Minorities Financial and Development Corporation (DSFDC), Government of National Capital Territory (NCT) of Delhi, aims to provide need-based loans up to ₹5,00,000/- at an interest rate of 6% per annum for various income-generating activities. The scheme targets individuals from Scheduled Castes (SC), Scheduled Tribes (ST), Other Backward Classes (OBC), and Minority communities, enabling them to start or expand small businesses. Eligible applicants must be residents of Delhi for the last five years, aged between 18 to 50 years, and have an annual family income not exceeding ₹2,00,000/-. The loans are disbursed for activities such as vegetable/fruit/flower shops, dairy products, tailoring shops, and other permissible non-polluting manufacturing activities.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Business & Entrepreneurship; Sector: Setting up / start-up / entrepreneurship

**Where it applies:** Delhi; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** Need-based loans up to ₹5,00,000/- at an interest rate of 6% per annum to support an [income-generating activity](https://dsfdc.delhi.gov.in/sites/default/files/dsy_scheme_details.pdf) mentioned in the scheme guidelines.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Social Welfare Department.
