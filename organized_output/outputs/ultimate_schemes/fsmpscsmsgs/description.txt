# Fellowship Scheme for M.Phil & Ph.D for Scheduled Caste Students (Maharaja Sayajirao Gayakvad Scholarship)

**Short Title:** FSMPSCS(MSGS)

**Tags:** Scholarship, Fellowship, Thesis, MPhil, PhD, Scheduled Caste, Student

The “Fellowship Scheme for M.Phil &amp;amp; Ph.D for Scheduled Caste Students” scheme is administered by the Director, Scheduled Caste Welfare, Government of Gujarat. Under this scheme, Scheduled Caste students pursuing M.Phil and Ph.D. programs at any recognized universities will receive a monthly fellowship.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Education & Learning; Sector: Scholarships and student finance

**Where it applies:** Gujarat; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** > **Scholarship rate per student:** - For M.Phil: ₹2,500/Per Month (For 10 Months) - For PhD: ₹10,000/Per Month (For 10 Months)

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: District Deputy Director (SCW) Office, Gujarat; Nodal Department: Social Justice and Empowerment Department.
