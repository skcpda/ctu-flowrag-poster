# Financial Loan for Training of Commercial Pilot

**Short Title:** FLFTCP

**Tags:** Loan, Training, Opportunity, Skill Development, Interest Rate, SEBC

The &quot;Financial Loan for Training of Commercial Pilot&quot; scheme was launched by the Social Justice and Empowerment Department, Government of Gujarat. The scheme aims to support students from Socially and Educationally Backward Classes (SEBC) in obtaining Commercial Pilot License (CPL) training through financial assistance. Under this scheme, a loan is offered at a concessional interest rate to eligible students who meet the educational and institutional requirements for CPL training. 

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Banking,Financial Services and Insurance; Sector: Loan

**Where it applies:** Gujarat; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** **Loan Amount:****** - Up to ₹25,00,000 for Commercial Pilot License training. **Interest Rate:****** - 4% simple interest per annum. **Penal Interest:** - 2.5% additional interest for late or defaulted payments. **Repayment Period:** - Principal: To be repaid within 10 years from the date of disbursement. - Interest: To be paid within 2 years, making the total repayment duration 12 years. > **NOTE:** Loan recovery will begin one year after the loan is disbursed to the student. 

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Social Justice and Empowerment Department.
