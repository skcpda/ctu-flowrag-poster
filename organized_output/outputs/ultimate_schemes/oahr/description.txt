# Operation of Ashram Hostels

**Short Title:** OAHR

**Tags:** Scheduled Tribe, Hostel, Student, Tribal, Ashram Hostels, Free Accommodation

The scheme &amp;quot;Operation of Ashram Hostels&amp;quot; was launched by the Department of Tribal Area Development, Government of Rajasthan. It aims to ensure continued education for tribal students who lack access to nearby higher-level schools due to the poor economic condition of their families. Under this scheme, free accommodation, nutritious meals, educational materials, uniforms, personal hygiene items, and other facilities are provided to Scheduled Tribe (ST) students residing in ashram hostels operated by the Tribal Area Development Department.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Education & Learning; Sector: Hostel service

**Where it applies:** Rajasthan; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** > **Students are provided with the following annually and monthly benefits:** **Annually (per student):** - School uniform with stitching - Shoes, socks, towel, and warm sweater **Monthly (per student):** - Nutritious breakfast and meals - Hair oil - Bathing and washing soap - Hair cutting service - Laundry for bed linen (sheet, pillow cover, blanket) - Electricity and water facilities - Newspapers and magazines

**Benefit Type:** In Kind

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Tribal Area Development Department.
