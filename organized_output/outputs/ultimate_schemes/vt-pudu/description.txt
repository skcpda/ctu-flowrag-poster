# "Vocational Training" Component of the "Development of Industries" Scheme

**Short Title:** VT-PUDU

**Tags:** Vocational, Training, Skill, Stipend, Unemployed, Youth

The &quot;Vocational Training&quot; Component of the &quot;Development of Industries&quot; Scheme by the District Industries Centre, Department of Industries and Commerce, Union Territory of Puducherry aims to create more employment opportunities broadly for the unemployed youth particularly women to pursue wide career openings besides self-ventures by giving training in trades like Tailoring &amp; Embroidery, Beauty Parlour, Arts &amp; Design. The training is institution oriented.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Skills & Employment; Sector: Training and Skill Up-gradation

**Where it applies:** Puducherry; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - Institution-oriented training in trades like Tailoring &amp; Embroidery, Beauty Parlour, Arts &amp; Design. - Stipend to Trainee: ₹1,500/- per month. - Honorarium to the Master Trainer: ₹ 300/- per trainee per month. - An amount of ₹150/- per trainee per month will be paid to the training institution towards compensation for wastage of raw materials. **Commencement of Training:** In September. **Period of Training:** 6 months.

**Benefit Type:** Composite

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Industries and Commerce Department, Puducherry.
