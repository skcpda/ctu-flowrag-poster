# Pension Scheme for Cancer Patients

**Short Title:** PSCP

**Tags:** Cancer, Patient, Pension, Medical, Financial Assistance

The scheme “Pension Scheme for Cancer Patients&quot; was launched by the Directorate of Social Welfare and Social Education, Government of Tripura. The scheme aims to provide a monthly pension of ₹2,000/- to persons suffering from cancer.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Health & Wellness; Sector: Disease and conditions

**Where it applies:** Tripura; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** ₹2,000/- per month.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Directorate Of Social Welfare & Social Education.
