# Birsa Harit Gram Yojana

**Short Title:** BHGY

**Tags:** Financial Assistance, Livelihood, Opportunities, Promote Afforestation

The &quot;Birsa Harit Gram Yojana&quot; by the Rural Development Department, Government of Jharkhand, aims to promote afforestation and provide sustainable livelihood opportunities for marginalized communities. Under this scheme, block and linear plantation activities are conducted on common and private lands, benefiting Scheduled Castes (SC), Scheduled Tribes (ST), landless families, and other economically weaker sections. The scheme is implemented by the Mahatma Gandhi National Rural Employment Guarantee Act (MGNREGA) framework.  ***Objective:*** The objectives include improving green cover, generating rural employment, and ensuring environmental sustainability through a structured three-phase plantation model.

**Who is this for:** Family; Category: Agriculture,Rural & Environment; Sector: Land and water resources

**Where it applies:** Jharkhand; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** 1. Plantation of fruit-bearing and fodder trees on common land and private land. 1. Sustainable livelihood generation for marginalized families. 1. Employment guarantee of 100 days under MGNREGA. 1. Usufruct rights over the plantation for beneficiaries. 

**Benefit Type:** Composite

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: Mahatma Gandhi National Rural Employment Guarantee Act; Nodal Department: Rural Development Department.
