# Hon’ble Chief Minister’s Merit Scholarship Scheme-Sikkim

**Short Title:** HCMMSS-SIKKIM

**Launch Date:** 2010-04-01

**Tags:** Free Education, Student, Merit, Underprivileged Family, Scholarship

The scheme &amp;quot;Hon’ble Chief Minister’s Merit Scholarship Scheme&amp;quot; was launched by the Education Department, Government of Sikkim in the year 2010. This scheme aims to provide free education up to the Graduation level to talented and deserving children from underprivileged family with an opportunity to study in reputed Public Schools both within and outside the State. The selected students will be admitted to the Public Schools empanelled with HRD Department within and outside the State.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Education & Learning; Sector: Scholarships and student finance

**Where it applies:** Sikkim; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - **Free Education:** Full waiver of academic fees up to the Graduation level for selected students.- **School Admission:** Admission into reputed public schools empanelled by the HRD Department within and outside the state.

**Benefit Type:** In Kind

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Education Department.
