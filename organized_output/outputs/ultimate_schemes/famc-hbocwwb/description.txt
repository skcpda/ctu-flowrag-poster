# Financial Assistance On Marriage Of Children (HBOCWWB)

**Short Title:** FAMC-(HBOCWWB)

**Tags:** Construction Worker, Children, Marriage, Financial Assistance, Social Welfare

The scheme “Financial Assistance on Marriage of Children (HBOCWWB)” was launched by the Haryana Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board (HBOCWWB), Labour Department Haryana. Under this scheme, a provision has been made to provide financial assistance of ₹21,000/- for a son’s marriage and ₹50,000/- for a daughter&amp;#39;s marriage for making arrangements for the marriage of the son/daughter of a construction worker three days before the marriage. This assistance shall be sanctioned for the marriage of two children of the beneficiary provided that the benefits shall be extended up to three daughters.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Marriage

**Where it applies:** Haryana; State

**Benefits / Assistance:** 1. The financial assistance of ₹21,000/- for the son’s marriage and ₹50,000/- for the daughter&amp;#39;s marriage will be given to a registered construction worker for making arrangements of the marriage of his/her son/daughter. 1. The financial assistance shall be sanctioned for the marriage of two children of the beneficiary provided that the benefits shall be extended up to three daughters.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: Haryana Building And Other Construction Workers Welfare Board (HBOCWWB); Nodal Department: Labour Department.
