# Kalyana Lakshmi Pathakam

**Short Title:** KLP

**Launch Date:** 2014-10-02

**Tags:** Scheduled Caste, Scheduled Tribe, Backward Class, Economically Backward Class, Marriage

The scheme &amp;quot;Kalyana Lakshmi Pathakam&amp;quot; was launched in 2014 by the Backward Classes Welfare Department, Government of Telangana, aims to alleviate financial distress in SC/ST and minority families by providing financial assistance to unmarried girls at the time of marriage. this scheme, a one-time financial assistance of ₹1,00,116 (₹1,25,145 for disabled girls) is provided to eligible girls at the time of marriage. 

**Who is this for:** Family; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Marriage

**Where it applies:** Telangana; State

**DBT Scheme:** Yes (Direct Benefit Transfer)

**Benefits / Assistance:** 1. One-time financial assistance of ₹1,00,116/- for unmarried SC/ST and minority girls at the time of marriage. 1. One-time financial assistance of ₹1,25,145/- for disabled girls, irrespective of caste, under the Rights of Persons with Disabilities Act, 2016. 1. The amount is directly credited to the bride’s bank account. 1. Only one-time assistance is allowed in a lifetime. 

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Exclusions / Not eligible:** 1. Applicants who have already availed the benefit once are not eligible. 

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: Backward Classes Welfare Department, Telangana; Nodal Department: Backward Classes Welfare Department.
