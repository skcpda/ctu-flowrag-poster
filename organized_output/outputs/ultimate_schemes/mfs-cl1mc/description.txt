# Micro Finance Scheme- Credit Line 1 for Minority Community

**Short Title:** MFS-CL1MC

**Tags:** Micro Finance, Credit Line, Minority Community, Loan, SHG

The scheme &amp;quot;Micro Finance Scheme- Credit Line 1 for Minority Community&amp;quot; is implemented by the Haryana Backward Classes and Economically Weaker Sections Kalyan Nigam. Under the scheme, small loans of ₹1,00,000/- per beneficiary are provided to members of Self-Help Groups (SHGs), either directly or through the SHGs, to meet the needs of the poorest among the poor. Applicants must be permanent residents of Haryana and belong to minority communities such as Muslims, Sikhs, Christians, Buddhists, Parsis, and Jains.

**Who is this for:** Individual, Self Help Groups (SHGS); Category: Banking,Financial Services and Insurance; Sector: Loan

**Where it applies:** Haryana; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** 1. Under the scheme, small loans of ₹1,00,000/- per beneficiary are provided to members of Self-Help Groups (SHGs), either directly or through the SHGs.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: Haryana Backward Classes And Economically Weaker Sections Kalyan Nigam; Nodal Department: Welfare of Scheduled Caste & Backward Classes Department.
