# Financial Assistance to OBC Students Appearing in the Mains Examination of UPSC

**Short Title:** FAOSAMEU

**Launch Date:** 2025-07-01

**Tags:** UPSC, Student, Exam, OBC, Financial Assistance

The &#39;Financial Assistance to OBC students appearing in the Mains Examination of UPSC&#39; scheme, administered by the Directorate of Welfare of Scheduled Castes and Backward Classes, Government of Assam, is designed to provide financial grants to Other Backward Classes (OBC) students who qualify for the Union Public Service Commission (UPSC) Mains Examination. Each eligible beneficiary will receive an amount of up to ₹50,000/-, subject to budgetary provisions. The disbursement of assistance is contingent upon the applicant satisfying the stipulated eligibility criteria and the successful submission of required documentation.  ***A DigiLocker account is required for application. Please refer to the Application Process section for details.***

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Scholarships and student finance

**Where it applies:** Assam; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** An amount of up to ₹50,000/- will be provided to each beneficiary, subject to budget provision.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Directorate of Welfare of Scheduled Castes and Backward Classes.
