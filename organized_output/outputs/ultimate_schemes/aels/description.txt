# Arivu Education Loan Scheme 

**Short Title:** AELS

**Tags:** Student, Loan, Minority Community

The &quot;Arivu Education Loan Scheme&quot; was launched by the Minorities Welfare Department, Government of Karnataka, and is implemented by the Karnataka Minorities Development Corporation. The scheme aims to provide loans to students who wish to pursue professional courses such as M.B.B.S., M.D., M.S., B.E., B.Tech., M.E., M.Tech., B.D.S., M.D.S., B.Ayush, M.Ayush, M.B.A., M.C.A., L.L.B., B.Arch, M.Arch, B.Sc. in Horticulture, Agriculture, Dairy Technology, Forestry, Veterinary, Animal Sciences, Food Technology, Biotechnology, Fisheries, Sericulture, Home/Community Sciences, Food Nutrition and Dietetics, B.Pharma, M.Pharma, Pharma.D, and D.Pharma.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Education & Learning; Sector: Loan

**Where it applies:** Karnataka; State/ UT

**DBT Scheme:** Yes (Direct Benefit Transfer)

**Benefits / Assistance:** > ***Loan Amount:*** - The loan amount is granted from ₹50,000/- to ₹5,00,000/- every year until the course duration.  > ***Loan Renewal Process:*** - Renewal and pay 12% of the loan amount issued by the corporation during the previous year at their respective District Office.  > **Loan Repayment:** - Repay the loan with service charges of 2% after 6 months of completion of the course. 

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: Karnataka Minorities Development Corporation; Nodal Department: Minorities Welfare Department.
