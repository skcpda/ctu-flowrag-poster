# Funeral Grant (Sainik Welfare)

**Short Title:** FG-SW

**Tags:** Death, Funeral, Ex-Serviceman, Widow, ESM

&quot;Funeral Grant&quot; is a Welfare Scheme by the Armed Forces Flag Day Fund of the Department of Sainik Welfare, Puducherry. Through this scheme, funeral Assistance of ₹12,000/- or ₹10,000/- is provided in the event of death of the ex-Serviceman (ESM) or of the Widow of the ESM respectively. This scheme applies only to the Ex-servicemen/Widows of Ex-servicemen registered with the Department of Sainik Welfare, Puducherry. The applications are accepted offline.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Crisis/Disaster/Accident

**Where it applies:** Puducherry; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - In case of death of the ESM, ₹12,000/- is provided to the ESM&#39;s Widow. - In the case of the death of the Widow of the ESM, ₹10,000/- is provided to a member of the ESM&#39;s family. - The assistance is provided immediately on occurrence with permission for burial/cremation from the appropriate authority (Death Certificate not required). - The amount is credited to the beneficiary’s Bank Account through RTGS/ NEFT.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: Rajya Sainik Board, Puducherry; Nodal Department: Sainik Welfare Department, Puducherry.
