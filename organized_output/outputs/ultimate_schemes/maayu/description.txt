# Mukhyamantri Aanchal Amrit Yojana

**Short Title:** MAAYUK

**Tags:** Childcare, Fortified Milk, Health, Flavoured Milk, Anganwadi Centre

The scheme &amp;quot;Mukhyamantri Anchal Amrit Yojana&amp;quot; was launched by the Department of Women Empowerment &amp;amp; Child Development, Government of Uttarakhand. The scheme aims to improve the health of children between 3 to 6 years of age by providing them fortified and flavored milk containing Vitamins A and D, four days a week, at Anganwadi Centres. 

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Health & Wellness; Sector: Childcare

**Where it applies:** Uttarakhand; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** Under this scheme, children between 3 to 6 years of age are provided fortified and flavored milk enriched with Vitamins A and D, four days a week. 

**Benefit Type:** In Kind

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Department Of Woman Empowerment & Child Development.
