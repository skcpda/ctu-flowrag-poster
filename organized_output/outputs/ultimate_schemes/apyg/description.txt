# Ambubhai Purani Award

**Short Title:** APYG

**Tags:** Gymnasium, Award, Sports

The scheme &amp;quot;Ambubhai Purani Award&amp;quot; by the Sports, Youth and Cultural Activities Department, Government of Gujarat, aims to promote the development of gymnasiums and traditional Indian physical culture by honoring individuals who have shown exceptional contribution in this field. The scheme provides financial assistance of ₹51,000/- and state-level recognition to outstanding individuals, fostering motivation and continuity in preserving indigenous physical training traditions. The scheme is implemented by the Sports Authority of Gujarat.

**Who is this for:** Sportsperson, Individual, NGO; Category: Sports & Culture; Sector: Sports

**Where it applies:** Gujarat; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - **Financial Assistance:** ₹51,000/-.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Sports, Youth and Cultural Activities Department.
