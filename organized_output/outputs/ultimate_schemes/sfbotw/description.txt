# Subsidy for Battery Operated 2-Wheelers

**Short Title:** SFBOTW

**Tags:** Student, Electric Scooter Subsidy, Electric Vehicle, Renewable Energy

The scheme &amp;quot;Subsidy for Battery Operated 2-Wheelers&amp;quot; by the Climate Change Department, Gujarat, aims to encourage the use of clean energy vehicles among the youth by offering financial support to students from Class 9th to 12th, as well as college students, for purchasing battery-operated two-wheelers of their choice from GEDA-authorized manufacturers through their dealers. The scheme is designed to reduce pollution, promote renewable energy adoption, and create awareness about sustainable transport among the younger generation. 

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Agriculture,Rural & Environment; Sector: Environmental management and protection

**Where it applies:** Gujarat; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - **Subsidy Assistance:** Each eligible student receives a subsidy of ₹12,000/- for the purchase of a battery-operated two-wheeler. 

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Climate Change Department.
