# Asangathit Karmakaar Mrtyu Evan Divyaang Sahaayata Yojana

**Short Title:** AKMEDS

**Launch Date:** 2019-12-09

**Tags:** Financial Assistance, Family Benefits, Empowerment

In 2019, Chhattisgarh State Government started a scheme &amp;amp;quot;Asangathit Karmakaar Mrtyu Evan Divyaang Sahaayata Yojana&amp;amp;quot; for unorganized worker. With this scheme, family will able to get the financials benefits after the death or disability of the unorganized worker.

**Who is this for:** Family; Category: Health & Wellness

**Where it applies:** Chhattisgarh; State

**Benefits / Assistance:** **Situation****Financial Help**On Death₹ 1,00,000/-Permanent Disability₹ 50,000/-

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Labour Department.
