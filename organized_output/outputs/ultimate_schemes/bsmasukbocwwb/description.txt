# Bicycle/Sewing Machine Assistance Scheme (UKBOCWWB)

**Short Title:** BSMAS(UKBOCWWB)

**Tags:** Bicycle, Sewing Machine, Construction Worker, Building Worker, Labour

The “Bicycle/Sewing Machine Assistance Scheme” is a welfare scheme which is implemented by the Uttarakhand Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board (UKBOCWWB) under the Labour Department, Government of Uttarakhand. Under the scheme, bicycles are provided to registered building &amp;amp; construction workers in plain areas, while sewing machines are provided to registered workers or their dependents in mountainous/hilly areas. 

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment

**Where it applies:** Uttarakhand; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** Bicycles are provided to construction workers in the plain areas, while sewing machines are provided to construction workers or their dependents in the hilly areas. 

**Benefit Type:** In Kind

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: Uttarakhand Building And Other Construction Workers’ Welfare Board; Nodal Department: Department Of Labour.
