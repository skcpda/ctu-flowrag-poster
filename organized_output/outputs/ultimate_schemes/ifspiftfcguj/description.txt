# To Increase Fish Seed Production And Inland Fisheries Equipment In Tribal Areas: Fish Cage Culture Gujarat

**Short Title:** IFSPIFTFCGUJ

**Tags:** Fish, Fish Activities, Fish Production, Scheduled Tribes, Fishermen, Fish Cage Culture

The scheme &quot;To Increase Fish Seed Production and Inland Fisheries Equipment in Tribal Areas,&quot; with a component of &quot;Fish Cage Culture&quot; introduced by the Agriculture, Farmers Welfare and Co-operation Department, Government of Gujarat, aims to increase fish production by rearing fish in reservoirs and generate employment opportunities for individuals engaged in inland fisheries. Through this scheme, financial assistance is provided for activities such as fish cage culture and fish seed stocking, benefiting primarily the Scheduled Tribe (ST) communities involved in inland fisheries. The scheme is implemented by the Commissioner of Fisheries.  > **Objectives:** - To increase fish production by rearing fish in reservoirs and provide employment to people involved in inland fisheries activities. - To boost fish farming or fishing activities in freshwater bodies. - Fishing in reservoirs, aquaculture in ponds, and catch from rivers increase fish production and boost inland fisheries. - The main objective is to provide employment opportunities to Scheduled Tribe fishermen in the state and increase fish production. 

**Who is this for:** Individual, Business Entity, Registered Societies, Self Help Groups (SHGS); Category: Agriculture,Rural & Environment; Sector: Fishing and hunting

**Where it applies:** Gujarat; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** Scheduled Tribe beneficiaries are eligible for 60% assistance of the unit cost of ₹3,00,000/- to ₹1,80,000/-, or 60% of the actual cost, whichever is less.  > **Minimum Time Limit for Reclaiming Benefits:** 3 year from the date of availing benefit. 

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Agriculture, Farmers Welfare and Cooperation Department.
