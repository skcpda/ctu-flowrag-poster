# Financial Assistance to Permanent Residents of Uttarakhand for Religious Pilgrimages

**Short Title:** FAPRURP

**Tags:** Kailash, Mansarovar, Yatra, Financial Assistance

The scheme &quot;Financial Assistance to Permanent Residents of Uttarakhand for Religious Pilgrimages&quot; by the Department of Social Welfare, Government of Uttarakhand, aims to promote religious travel and pilgrimage by providing financial assistance to eligible permanent residents of the state who participate in and complete the Kailash Mansarovar Yatra. Through this scheme, monetary benefits are provided to Scheduled Castes, Scheduled Tribes, and other permanent residents of Uttarakhand. The scheme is implemented by Kumaon Mandal Vikas Nigam. The financial assistance is processed after the submission and verification of required documents and confirmation of successful Yatra completion.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Travel & Tourism; Sector: Tourism in India

**Where it applies:** Uttarakhand; State/ UT

**DBT Scheme:** Yes (Direct Benefit Transfer)

**Benefits / Assistance:** 1. A financial grant of ₹50,000 is provided to eligible Scheduled Caste and Scheduled Tribe residents of Uttarakhand who participate in the Kailash Mansarovar Yatra organised by the Government of India. 1. Permanent residents of the state from other categories who complete the Yatra via the Uttarakhand route are also eligible for financial support through Kumaon Mandal Vikas Nigam.  > **Mode of Disbursement:** - Direct benefit transfer (DBT) post-verification and approval. > **Frequency of Disbursement:** - One-time benefit.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: Kumaon Mandal Vikas Nigam; Nodal Department: Department of Culture and Endowments.
