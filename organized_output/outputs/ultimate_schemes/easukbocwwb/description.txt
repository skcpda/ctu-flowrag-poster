# Son/Daughter Education Assistance (UKBOCWWB)

**Short Title:** SDEA(UKBOCWWB)

**Tags:** Education Assistance, Construction Worker, Building Worker, Labour, Student, Scholarship

The “Son/Daughter Education Assistance” scheme is implemented by the Uttarakhand Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board (UKBOCWWB) under the Labour Department, Government of Uttarakhand. The scheme provides financial assistance to the children of registered building and construction workers pursuing education from Class I up to higher education or professional courses. 

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Education & Learning; Sector: Scholarships and student finance

**Where it applies:** Uttarakhand; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** **Financial assistance for education will be provided to the children of construction workers at the following rates:** **Sl. **Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: Uttarakhand Building And Other Construction Workers’ Welfare Board; Nodal Department: Department Of Labour.
