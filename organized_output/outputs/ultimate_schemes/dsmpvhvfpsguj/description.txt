# Distribution of Seeds of more productions varieties/ Hybrids varieties Seeds and fertilizer at subsidies etc. to Adivasi farmers in Tribal Area: Pump Set (TASP & OTASP) Gujarat

**Short Title:** DSMPVHVFPSGUJ

**Tags:** Adivasi Farmers, Crop Production, Livelihood, Seeds, Fertilizer, Subsidies, Pump Set

The scheme &quot;Distribution of Seeds of more productive varieties / Hybrid varieties Seeds and fertilizer at subsidies etc. to Adivasi farmers in Tribal Area (TASP)&quot; with a component &quot;Pump set (TASP &amp; OTASP)&quot; is introduced by the Agriculture, Farmers Welfare and Co-operation Department, Government of Gujarat, aims to provide subsidized agricultural inputs to enhance productivity and livelihood of tribal farmers. Farmers belonging to scheduled tribes and tribal cultivators residing outside tribal areas can achieve higher production of agricultural crops and improve their livelihood by using plant protection equipment, tarpaulins, field demonstrations, micronutrients, agriculture fairs, water carrying pipelines, castor cake/neem cake/LFOM/green manure, pump sets, 100% water-soluble fertilizer, plant protection pesticides, etc. These tools and practices, when used according to scientific recommendations, can significantly increase crop production and, consequently, improve their livelihood. 

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Agriculture,Rural & Environment; Sector: Agricultural Inputs- seeds, fertilizer etc.

**Where it applies:** Gujarat; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** > **Subsidy on oil engines:** - **3.0 to 3.5 H.Pa.:** 75% of purchase price or ₹8,700/- (whichever is less).- **5.0 H.Pa.:** 75% of purchase price or ₹12,000/- (whichever is less).- **7.5 to 8.0 H.Pa.:** 75% of purchase price or ₹13,500/- (whichever is less).- **10.0 H.Pa.:** 75% of purchase price or ₹13,875/- (whichever is less). > **Subsidy on electric motors:** - **1.0 to 3.0 H.P.:** 75% of purchase price or ₹8,600/- (whichever is less).- **5.0 H.Pa.:** 75% of purchase price or ₹9,750/- (whichever is less).- **7.5 H.Pa.:** 75% of purchase price or ₹12,900/- (whichever is less). > **Subsidy on submersible pump sets:** - **3.0 H.Pa.:** 75% of purchase price or ₹15,750/- (whichever is less).- **5.0 H.Pa.:** 75% of purchase price or ₹22,350/- (whichever is less).- **7.5 H.Pa.:** 75% of purchase price or ₹27,975/- (whichever is less).- **10.0 H.Pa.:** 75% of purchase price or ₹33,525/- (whichever is less).  > **Frequency of Disbursement:** The benefit can be availed once every 5 years. > **Remark:** As per conditions No. 5, 16, 17, 19 and 22 of [Appendix-1](https://mariyojana.gujarat.gov.in/Schemeatoz.aspx). (Refer Scheme No. 35)
