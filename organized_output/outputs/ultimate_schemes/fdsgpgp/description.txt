# Fodder Development Scheme For Gram Panchayat, Gaushala And Panjarpol Having Gauchar Land

**Short Title:** FDSGPGP

**Tags:** Agriculture, Animal Husbandry, Fodder Development

The &amp;quot;Fodder Development Scheme For Gram Panchayat, Gaushala And Panjarpol Having Gauchar Land&amp;quot; launched by the Department of Agriculture, Farmers Welfare &amp;amp; Co-operation, Government of Gujarat, aims to improve the fertility and productivity of gauchar land by supporting activities like removal of weeds, ploughing, sowing of fodder seeds, and fencing. By enhancing the availability of green fodder, the scheme not only boosts milk production and cattle health but also improves the rural environment and creates employment.

**Who is this for:** Registered Societies, Government Organisation; Category: Agriculture,Rural & Environment; Sector: Animal husbandry

**Where it applies:** Gujarat; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:**  **Component****Standard of Assistance****Minimum Time Limit (in Years) for Reclaiming Constituent Benefits**Fodder Development Scheme for Gram Panchayat, Gaushala and Panjarapol having gauchar land For developing cow pastures in an area of up to 20 hectares are assisted with 75% of per hectare expenditure up to a maximum of ₹75,000/- i.e. A maximum assistance of ₹15,00,000/- for a total of 20 hectares.The benefit of this scheme will be available only once.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Agriculture, Farmers Welfare and Cooperation Department.
