# PVTGs Dakia Yojana (Chief Minister's Food Security Scheme)

**Short Title:** PDY(CMFSS)

**Tags:** Food, PVTG, Doorstep Delivery

The &quot;PVTGs Dakia Yojana (Chief Minister&#39;s Food Security Scheme)&quot; by the Welfare Department, Government of Jharkhand, aims to ensure food security for Particularly Vulnerable Tribal Groups (PVTGs). Under this scheme, subsidized food grains are provided to PVTG families to improve their living conditions.

**Who is this for:** Individual, Family; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Food Security / Public Distribution System

**Where it applies:** Jharkhand; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** ***Food Security for PTG Families:*** - Free provision of 35 kg of wheat and rice per family per month at ₹1 per kg.  ***Direct Village-Level Distribution:*** - Food grains are distributed at the village level by Block Welfare Officers.  ***Support for National Food Security:*** - Complements the &quot;Antyodaya Yojana&quot; by including all PVTG families in Jharkhand.

**Benefit Type:** In Kind

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Food, Public Distribution and Consumer affairs Department.
