# Indira Gandhi National Old Age Pension Scheme (Punjab)

**Short Title:** IGNOAPSP

**Tags:** Pension, Old Age, Below Poverty Line, BPL, Senior Citizen

The &quot;Indira Gandhi National Old Age Pension Scheme (IGNOAPS)&quot; is a social welfare initiative by the Department of Social Security and Development of Women and Children, Government of Punjab. In this scheme, persons aged between 60 and 79 years receive a pension of ₹200/- per month, while those aged 80 years and above receive ₹500/- per month. The scheme applies only to persons belonging to the Below Poverty Line (BPL) category.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Financial assistance

**Where it applies:** Punjab; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - ₹200/- month, for persons aged 60 to 79 years. - ₹500/- month, for persons aged 80 years and above.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Department of Social Security and Development of Women and Children.
