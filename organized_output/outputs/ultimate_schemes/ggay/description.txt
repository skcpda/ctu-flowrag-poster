# Ghar Ghar Aushodhi Yojana

**Short Title:** GGAY

**Launch Date:** 2021-08-01

**Closing Date:** 2026-08-01

**Tags:** Health, Planting, Disease-free Life, Agriculture

Rajasthan government has planned a new scheme called Rajasthan Ghar Ghar Aushadhi Yojana to promote the use of Ayurvedic and medicinal herbs across the state. The state government is planning to implement the scheme to boost the immunity and defense system of the citizens of the state. Under this Rajasthan Ghar Ghar Aushadhi Yojana, medicinal plants like Tulsi, Ashwagandha, Giloy and Kalmegh will be distributed.

**Who is this for:** Individual, Family; Category: Agriculture,Rural & Environment; Sector: Agricultural Inputs- seeds, fertilizer etc.

**Where it applies:** Rajasthan; State

**Benefits / Assistance:** The main objective of the Rajasthan Ghar Ghar Aushadhi Yojana is to provide medicinal plants like Tulsi, Ashwagandha, Giloy and Kalmegh to everyone. 1. It aims to promote medicinal plants and their use in daily life 1. It aims to help people to boost their immunity, lead a healthy and disease-free life 1. Each family will be provided 24 saplings over a period of 5 years 1. This Rajasthan Ghar Ghar Aushadhi Yojana will help in boosting a strong defence system which will lead to a good health for all and thus overall development in the state.

**Benefit Type:** In Kind

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Forest Department.
