# Publication Support Scheme for Fine Arts

**Short Title:** PSSFFA

**Tags:** Fine Arts, Book Publication, Artist Grant, Art Institutions

The scheme &amp;quot;Publication Support Scheme for Fine Arts&amp;quot; by the Sports, Youth and Cultural Activities Department, Government of Gujarat, aims to increase awareness about fine arts and promote the publication of fine arts literature by supporting artists and art institutions financially. Under this initiative, applications are invited annually through paid newspaper advertisements, and 2 (two) artists/art institutions are provided assistance for the publication of fine arts books.. The scheme is implemented by the Gujarat State Fine Arts Academy. &lt;br&gt; 

**Who is this for:** Artists, University / Institution; Category: Sports & Culture; Sector: Culture

**Where it applies:** Gujarat; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - **Financial Assistance:** 50% of the total printing cost or a maximum of ₹20,000/- (whichever is lower). 

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: Gujarat State Fine Arts Academy; Nodal Department: Sports, Youth and Cultural Activities Department.
