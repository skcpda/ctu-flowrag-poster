# Gunavatta Evam Navaachar Nishulk Paathy Pustak Yojana

**Short Title:** GENNPPY

**Tags:** Student, Free Textbook, Samagra Shiksha

The scheme &amp;quot;Gunavatta Evam Navaachar Nishulk Paathy Pustak&amp;quot; is a scheme under the Samagra Shiksha Programme, implemented by the Secondary Education Department, Government of Uttarakhand. The scheme aims to provide free textbooks to all students from Classes 1 to 8 studying in government and government-aided schools, as well as recognized madrasas that follow the state government curriculum. 

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Education & Learning; Sector: Resources for schools

**Where it applies:** Uttarakhand; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** Every year, in accordance with the guidelines of the Directorate of Elementary and Secondary Education, free textbooks are provided to all students from Classes 1 to 8 in government schools, government-aided schools, and recognized madrasas following the state government curriculum.  

**Benefit Type:** In Kind

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: Secondary Education Department, Government Of Uttarakhand; Nodal Department: Department Of Education.
