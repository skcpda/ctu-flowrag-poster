# Dr.Babasaheb Ambedkar Udyog Uday Yojana: Assistance in Implementation of Information and Communication Technology (ICT)

**Short Title:** BAUUYICT

**Launch Date:** 2020-08-07

**Tags:** Business, Entrepreneurship, MSMEs, Start Up, Scheduled Caste, Scheduled Tribe

The scheme &amp;quot;Dr.Babasaheb Ambedkar Udyog Uday Yojana&amp;quot; is an umbrella scheme launched by the Industries and Mines Department, Gujarat. It aims to empower the state&amp;#39;s dynamic MSME sector, which significantly contributes to employment, GDP, and exports. Effective from August 7th, 2020 to August 6th, 2025, this initiative supports SC/ST entrepreneurs and strengthens MSMEs, making them globally competitive. The component &amp;quot;Assistance in Implementation of Information and Communication Technology (ICT)&amp;quot; offers financial assistance to enterprises for the implementation of Information and Communication Technology (ICT) solutions, including hardware for cloud computing and networking.

**Who is this for:** Business Entity, Industries; Category: Business & Entrepreneurship; Sector: Setting up / start-up / entrepreneurship

**Where it applies:** Gujarat; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - **Financial Assistance:** Covers up to 75% of the capital expenditure for ICT facilities maximum upto ₹5,00,000/-

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Industries and Mines Department.

**References / Annexures:** - **Guidelines**: https://ic.gujarat.gov.in/documents/commondoc/2020/2-GR_02092020.pdf - **Online Application Portal**: https://ifp.gujarat.gov.in/IC/digigov.htm?actionFlag=loadCitizenRegPage
