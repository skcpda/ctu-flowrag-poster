# Perunthalaivar Karma Veerar Kamarajar Scheme of Financial Assistance Towards Funeral Expenses of the Differently Abled Persons

**Short Title:** PKVKSFATFEDAP

**Tags:** Funeral, Death, Disability, Financial Assistance

The scheme “Perunthalaivar Karma Veerar Kamarajar Scheme of Financial Assistance Towards Funeral Expenses of the Differently Abled Persons&quot; was launched by the Department of Social Welfare, Government of Puducherry. In this scheme, financial assistance of ₹ 15,000/- is provided to meet the expenses in connection with funeral, in the event of the death of the differently abled person concerned.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Crisis/Disaster/Accident

**Where it applies:** Puducherry; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** On the death of the differently abled person, financial assistance of ₹ 15,000/- shall be paid to the claimant.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Social Welfare Department, Puducherry.
