# Mukhyamantri Bhavishyalakshi Kaushalya Vikas Yojana

**Short Title:** MBKVY

**Tags:** Youth, Skill, Training, Employment, Technology, Student, Certification

The scheme &quot;Mukhyamantri Bhavishyalakshi Kaushalya Vikas Yojana (MBKVY)&quot; by the Labour, Skill Development and Employment Department, Government of Gujarat, aims to provide skill development training to youth in manufacturing, service and other sectors through new age technology-based courses to meet current industry requirements and create skilled manpower in the state. The scheme provides short-term courses, digital training, future skill courses and new age courses with quality training according to current technology, along with practical training as per the latest technology requirements. The applicant must be a domicile of Gujarat state and must have passed at least Standard 10/12/Industrial Training Institute/Degree-Diploma. The scheme is implemented by Gujarat Skill Development Mission under the guidance of Steering Committee and Implementation and Monitoring Committee. The applications for this scheme are accepted online through skills.gujarat.gov.in portal. 

**Who is this for:** Category: Skills & Employment; Sector: Training and Skill Up-gradation

**Where it applies:** Gujarat; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** **Training Cost Coverage:** Training cost will be directly paid to the Training Provider&#39;s bank account based on Common Skilling Norms for National Skill Qualification Framework courses. **Assessment Fee Coverage:** Assessment fee for National Skill Qualification Framework courses will be paid to the concerned Assessment Agency as per set standards. **Industry-Demand Based Course Fees:** For new courses based on industry demand, training cost will be paid to training centers as determined by the Steering Committee. **Special Course Fees:** For special types of courses conducted by Skill Institutes/Industrial Training Institutes/Kaushalya The Skill University, training cost and method will be as approved by the Steering Committee. **Infrastructure Support:** Raw material, guest faculty, Training of Trainers, tools equipment, office expenses etc. will be covered under training cost for Industrial Training Institutes. **Certification:** Certification will be provided through concerned Sector Skill Councils/Gujarat Council for Vocational Training/Kaushalya The Skill University.  **Payment Milestones** _(as reviewed by Mission Director, Gujarat Skill Development Mission)_ - 30%: After commencement of training batch against valid candidates. - 40%: After certification of trainees (with respect to passed out trainees). - 30%: Based on placement results (final payment as per Pradhan Mantri Kaushal Vikas Yojana guidelines). 

**Benefit Type:** In Kind

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Labour and Employment Department.
