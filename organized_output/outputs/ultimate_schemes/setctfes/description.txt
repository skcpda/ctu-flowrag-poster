# Scheme for Enhancement of Technical Competence and Manpower: Scheme of Financial Assistance for Tuition Fees for Enhancement of Skill

**Short Title:** SETCTFES

**Launch Date:** 2020-08-07

**Closing Date:** 2025-08-06

**Tags:** Technical Competence, Manpower Solution, Industries, Skill Development

The &amp;quot;Scheme for Enhancement of Technical Competence and Manpower&amp;quot; is an umbrella scheme launched by the Industries and Mines Department, Gujarat under the Gujarat Industrial Policy. Effective from August 7th, 2020 to August 6th, 2025, it aims to bridge the industry’s skill gap, boost employment, and align workforce training with evolving technology and business needs. The component &amp;quot;Scheme of Financial Assistance for Tuition Fees for Enhancement of Skill&amp;quot; aims to support individuals in skill development by providing financial aid for training programs. It ensures access to industry-relevant training, enhancing employability and career growth opportunities.

**Who is this for:** Industries; Category: Skills & Employment; Sector: Entrepreneurship development

**Where it applies:** Gujarat; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** > **Financial Assistance:** - Up to ₹15,000 for training programs of 121 hours or more. - Up to ₹10,000 for training programs of 61 to 120 hours. - Up to ₹5,000 for training programs of 40 to 60 hours. > **Full Fee Coverage:** - 100% assistance for BPL, Physically Handicapped, SC, ST, SEBC, and Women candidates. - 75% assistance for other eligible candidates. &gt; **Additional Assistance:** - **Awareness Programs &amp;amp; Seminars:** A maximum support of ₹1,00,000/- per training center per year will be provided for awareness programs and seminars. - **Other Expenditures:** SLEC/IC/CED can approve the expenditure on any study, renovation/refurbishing of existing skill centers/institute, remuneration to third party evaluation of scheme, monitoring/audit expenses, assessment agency, etc. - **Trainee Transportation Reimbursement:** Trainees who complete skill upgradation training under any scheme will be eligible for reimbursement of Gujarat State Road Transport Corporation (GSRTC) bus passes for travel between their residence and training center as per the following rates: 1. **For BPL candidates, Physically Handicapped, SC, ST, SEBC, and Women:** 100% 1. **For Other candidates:** 75%

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Industries and Mines Department.
