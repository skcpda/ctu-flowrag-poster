# Destitute Widow Pension Scheme - Uttarakhand

**Short Title:** DWPSUK

**Tags:** Destitute, Widow, Pension, BPL

The &amp;amp;quot;Destitute Widow Pension Scheme&amp;amp;quot; was launched by the Department of Social Welfare, Government of Uttarakhand. The scheme aims to help destitute widows meet their basic needs and improve their quality of life. Under this scheme, a monthly pension is provided to widows whose family monthly income is up to ₹4,000/- or who belong to the BPL category, and who are not availing of any other pension benefits. 

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Pension

**Where it applies:** Uttarakhand; State

**Benefits / Assistance:** A pension of ₹1,500/- per month is provided to eligible beneficiaries. 

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Department Of Social Welfare.
