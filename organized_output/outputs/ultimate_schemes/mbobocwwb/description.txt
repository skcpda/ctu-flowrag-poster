# Maternity Benefit (O.B.O.C.W.W.B)

**Short Title:** MBOBOCWWB

**Tags:** Labour, Building Worker, Construction Worker, Maternity, Pregnancy Care, Women, Child Delivery

The “Maternity Benefit” scheme by the Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board, Labour Department, Odisha, is a welfare scheme for the registered workers of the board. Under this scheme, the Board shall provide financial assistance in the form of maternity benefits to the female beneficiaries subject to a maximum of two children who are registered beneficiaries for a period of more than one year under the Odisha Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board. &lt;br&gt;

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Health & Wellness; Sector: Pregnancy care

**Where it applies:** Odisha; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - **Quantum of Assistance:** ₹8,000/- to ₹10,000/- per Child. - **For eligible beneficiaries under “Mamata” being implemented by W&amp;amp;CD Department:** ₹15,000/- (₹10,000/- under OB&amp;amp;OCWW Board and ₹5,000/- under “Mamata”). &gt; **Note:** Assistance shall be given for a maximum of two children. 

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Labour and Employees State Insurance Department.
