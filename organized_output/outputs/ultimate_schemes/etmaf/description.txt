# Establishment of 12 Milch Animal Farm

**Short Title:** ETMAF

**Tags:** Animal Husbandry, Rural Employment, Animal Farm

The &amp;quot;Establishment of 12 Milch Animal Farm&amp;quot; scheme introduced by the Agriculture, Farmers Welfare and Cooperation Department, Gujarat, aims to promote animal husbandry as an independent business, encourage livestock owners to adopt dairy farming and enhance rural employment opportunities.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Agriculture,Rural & Environment; Sector: Animal husbandry

**Where it applies:** Gujarat; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - **Interest Subsidy:** 1. **General Category:** 7.5% for up to 5 years after availing of a bank loan. 1. **SC/ST/Female Beneficiaries:** 8.5% for up to 5 years. 1. Gir/Kankrej Breed Units: Up to 12% maximum subsidy. - **Shed Construction Assistance:** 1. General Category: 50% of actual expenditure, up to ₹1,50,000/-. 1. Gir/Kankrej Breed: 75% of actual expenditure, up to ₹2,25,000/-. - **Animal Insurance Premium (for 3 years):** 1. General Category: 75% subsidy, up to ₹43,200/-. 1. Gir/Kankrej Breed: 90% subsidy, up to ₹51,840/-. - **Equipment Subsidy:** 1. Electric Chaff Cutter: 75% subsidy, up to ₹18,000/- (General) ; 90% subsidy, up to ₹21,600/- (Gir/Kankrej). 1. Fogger System: 75% subsidy, up to ₹9,000/- (General) | 90% subsidy, up to ₹10,800/- (Gir/Kankrej). 1. Milking Machine: 75% subsidy, up to ₹33,750/- (General) | 90% subsidy, up to ₹40,500/- (Gir/Kankrej). &gt; **Note:** - Interest subsidy will be available on the amount determined above for the unit cost fixed by NABARD or through the bank for the establishment of units for dairy cattle of up to 12 animals, whichever is less.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Exclusions / Not eligible:** - There is no assistance available for renovation and repair under this scheme.

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: Animal Husbandry Department, Gujarat State And Gujarat Milk Marketing Federation (GCMMF); Nodal Department: Agriculture, Farmers Welfare and Cooperation Department.
