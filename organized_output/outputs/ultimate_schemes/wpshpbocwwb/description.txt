# Widow Pension Scheme (HPBOCWWB)

**Short Title:** WPSHPBOCWWB

**Tags:** Construction Workers, Building Worker, Women, Widow

The &quot;Widow Pension Scheme&quot; launched by the Himachal Pradesh Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board (HPBOCWWB) is designed to support widows of registered beneficiaries. Through this initiative, the Board will disburse a monthly amount of ₹1,500/- to help widows manage their expenses and improve their quality of life.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Pension

**Where it applies:** Himachal Pradesh; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** Monthly Pension of ₹1,500/-

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Labour and Employment Department, Himachal Pradesh.
