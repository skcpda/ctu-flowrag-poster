# Pension for Journalists’ Family

**Short Title:** PFJF

**Tags:** Spouse, Pension, Widow, Death

The &quot;Pension for Journalists’ Family&quot; was launched by the Tamil Development, Religious Endowments, and Information Department, Government of Tamil Nadu in 1998. The scheme aims to give a monthly pension to the wife of a deceased journalist pensioner.

**Who is this for:** Journalist; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment

**Where it applies:** Tamil Nadu; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - **Pension Amount of ₹2,000/- per month.**

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Tamil Development and Information Department.

**References / Annexures:** - **Guidelines**: https://cms.tn.gov.in/sites/default/files/documents/Information_publicity_1_0.pdf - **Official Website**: https://www.tn.gov.in/scheme/data_view/6802 - **Application Form**: https://cms.tn.gov.in/sites/default/files/schemes/journalist_family_pension.pdf
