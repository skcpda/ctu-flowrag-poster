# Relief During Natural Calamities Such as Cyclone, Flood or Fire (Sainik Welfare)

**Short Title:** RDNCCFF-SW

**Tags:** Calamity, Disaster, Relief, Ex-Serviceman, Widow, Dependent

&quot;Relief During Natural Calamities Such as Cyclone, Flood or Fire&quot; is a Welfare Scheme by the Armed Forces Flag Day Fund of the Department of Sainik Welfare, Puducherry. Through this scheme, up to ₹40,000/- is provided to the registered ESM / Widows of ESM / Dependents of ESM as a relief during natural calamities such as cyclones, floods or fires. The applications are accepted offline.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Agriculture,Rural & Environment; Sector: Climate change

**Where it applies:** Puducherry; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - The actual cost of damage or a maximum of ₹40,000/- is provided as a relief during natural calamities such as cyclone, flood or fire. - The amount, as decided by the Management Committee, is credited to the beneficiary’s Bank Account through RTGS/ NEFT.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Exclusions / Not eligible:** The scope of this scheme will not apply to those ESM / living spouse/widows who are re-employed as regular / deemed to be regular in Government/Public Sector Undertakings/Private Organizations and self-employed IT assesses.

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: Rajya Sainik Board, Puducherry; Nodal Department: Sainik Welfare Department, Puducherry.
