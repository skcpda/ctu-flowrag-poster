# Ramie Crop Scheme

**Short Title:** RCS

**Tags:** Ramie Crop, Agriculture, Farmer, Cultivation, Textile Crop, Training

The “Ramie Crop Scheme” is a State Plan Scheme, which is implemented by the Department of Agriculture &amp;amp; Farmers’ Welfare, Government of Meghalaya. The objective of the scheme is to encourage the cultivation of perennial and remunerative textile crops such as Ramie in suitable areas by providing necessary inputs and training.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Agriculture,Rural & Environment; Sector: Agricultural Inputs- seeds, fertilizer etc.

**Where it applies:** Meghalaya; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** > **Pattern of Assistance:** - To provide planting materials, degumming, and decorticating machines to the farmers. > **Type of Assistance And Entitlement (If any):** - Quality planting materials, organic manure, bio-pesticides at subsidised rate and farmers&#39; training.

**Benefit Type:** Composite

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: Department Of Agriculture & Farmers’ Welfare, Government Of Meghalaya; Nodal Department: Agriculture Department.
