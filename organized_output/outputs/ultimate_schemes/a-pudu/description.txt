# ARAVANAIPPU

**Short Title:** A-PUDU

**Tags:** Mother, Child, Girl, Nutrition, Financial Assistance

The scheme &quot;ARAVANAIPPU&quot; is a financial assistance scheme by the Department of Women and Child Development, Government of Puducherry. This scheme aims to improve the status of the girl child and provide nutritious food; financial assistance of ₹1200 is offered to lactating mothers. The girl child of the applicant should be born after 1-4-2005. This assistance is provided only for upto two girl children.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Women and Child; Sector: Hospitalization

**Where it applies:** Puducherry; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** ₹1200 financial assistance to lactating mothers. ***The selection is periodical and depends on the availability of funds.**

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Women and Child Development Department, Puducherry.
