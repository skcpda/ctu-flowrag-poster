# Pension Scheme to the Dependents (Husband/Wife) of Identified State Agitators, Other than those Who either went to Jail for 7 Days or were Injured During the Uttarakhand State Movement, after the Death of the Agitator

**Short Title:** PSDISACAJ7DIUSMADA

**Tags:** Uttarakhand State Movement, Pension, Agitator

The &amp;quot;Pension Scheme to the Dependents (Husband/Wife) of Identified State Agitators, Other than those Who either went to Jail for 7 Days or were Injured During the Uttarakhand State Movement&amp;quot; scheme is implemented by the Police Department under the Department of Home, Government of Uttarakhand. The scheme aims to provide a monthly pension to the dependents (husband/wife) of such identified state agitators who do not fall under the category of agitators jailed for 7 days or injured during the movement, and who were neither receiving agitator pension or any other government pension, nor employed in government service. 

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Pension

**Where it applies:** Uttarakhand; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** **Pension Amount:** ₹4,500/- per month 

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: Police Department, Government Of Uttarakhand; Nodal Department: Department of Home.
