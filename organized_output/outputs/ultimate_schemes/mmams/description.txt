# Mukhya Mantri Agricultural Mechanization Scheme

**Short Title:** MMAMS

**Tags:** Agriculture, Machine, Subsidy

The &amp;quot;Mukhya Mantri Agricultural Mechanization Scheme&amp;quot; launched by the Agriculture, Farmers Welfare and Cooperation Department, Gujarat, aims to provide financial aid to farmers for the purchase of various modern farm implements and machinery. The scheme promotes mechanized farming and ensures timely agricultural operations for better yield and efficiency.  

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Agriculture,Rural & Environment; Sector: Mechanization- solar power, farming systems

**Where it applies:** Gujarat; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - **Financial Assistance:** An amount between ₹1,200/- to ₹11,00,000/- for purchasing various farm implements.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Agriculture, Farmers Welfare and Cooperation Department.

**References / Annexures:** - **Guidelines (Scheme No. 357)**: https://mariyojana.gujarat.gov.in/MoreDetails.aspx - **User Manual (Applicant Registration)**: https://ikhedut.gujarat.gov.in/site/iKhedut%202.0%20User%20Manual%20PPT_Gujarati.pdf
