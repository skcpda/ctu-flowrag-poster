# Cost of Spectacle for the Construction Workers  

**Short Title:** COSFTCW

**Tags:** Labour, Building Worker, Construction Worker, Spectacle Purchase

The “Cost of Spectacle for the Construction Workers” scheme by the Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board, Labour Department, West Bengal, is a welfare scheme for the registered workers of the board. Under this scheme, the Board shall provide reimbursement for purchasing spectacle to each registered beneficiary if required.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Financial assistance

**Where it applies:** West Bengal; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - **Quantum of Assistance:** Upto ₹500/- for purchasing spectacle. 

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Labour Department.
