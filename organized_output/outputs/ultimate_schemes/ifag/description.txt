# International Financial Assistance

**Short Title:** IFAG

**Tags:** International Sports, Athlete, Air Travel Expenses

The scheme &quot;International Financial Assistance&quot; by the Sports, Youth and Cultural Activities Department, Government of Gujarat aims to encourage and support sportspersons from Gujarat participating in international-level competitions by providing a one-time financial assistance of ₹75,000/- to cover costs related to boarding, lodging, air travel, kit, or miscellaneous expenses. This initiative ensures that deserving athletes are not hindered by financial constraints in their pursuit of excellence on global platforms. 

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Sports & Culture; Sector: Sports

**Where it applies:** Gujarat; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - **Financial Assistance:** ₹75,000/- is provided to eligible sportspersons to cover expenses for boarding, lodging, travel (including air tickets), sports kits, and other related miscellaneous expenses. 

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Sports, Youth and Cultural Activities Department.
