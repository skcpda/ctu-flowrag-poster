# Ex-gratia Payment for the Construction Workers

**Short Title:** EPCW

**Tags:** Labour, Ex-gratia Payment, Permanently Disability, Construction Workers, Financial Assistance

The &amp;quot;Ex-gratia Payment for the Construction Workers&amp;quot; by the Delhi Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board, Labour Department, Delhi, is a welfare scheme for the registered workers of the board. Through this scheme, an ex-gratia amount of ₹1,00,000/- is provided to the registered workers of the board in case of permanent disability. 

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Financial assistance

**Where it applies:** Delhi; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - Ex-gratia payment of ₹1,00,000/- in case of permanent disability. 

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: Delhi Building And Other Construction Workers Welfare Board; Nodal Department: Labour Department.
