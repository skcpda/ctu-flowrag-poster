# "Sugarcane Nursery Premium" Sub-Scheme of the "Crop Production Technology" Scheme

**Short Title:** SNPSSCPTS

**Tags:** Sugarcane, Nursery, Seed, Financial Assistance, Farmer

The &quot;Sugarcane Nursery Premium&quot; Sub-Scheme of the &quot;Crop Production Technology&quot; Scheme is introduced by the Department of Agriculture and Farmer&#39;s Welfare, Union Territory of Puducherry. Through this scheme, the farmers are provided Nursery Premium for Raising Seed Material to Plant One Hectare of Sugarcane.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Agriculture,Rural & Environment; Sector: Agricultural Inputs- seeds, fertilizer etc.

**Where it applies:** Puducherry; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** Nursery Premium for Raising Seed Material to Plant One Hectare of Sugarcane (10 Ares (25 Cents) of Nursery @ ₹4,000/-)

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Agriculture Department, Puducherry.
