# Suraksha Laghu Evan Simant Vriddhajan Krishak Samman Pension Yojana

**Short Title:** SLSVKP

**Launch Date:** 2019-03-01

**Tags:** Pension, Old Age, Financial Assistance, Social Welfare

The Rajasthan government has introduced the &amp;quot;Suraksha Laghu Evam Simaant Vriddhajan Krishak Samman Pension Yojana&amp;quot; to uplift the lives of small and marginal farmers in the state. This initiative recognizes the significant contributions of these farmers to the agricultural sector and aims to provide them with much-needed social security during their golden years. By offering a monthly pension to eligible farmers aged 60 and above, the scheme seeks to alleviate financial worries and ensure a degree of economic independence for these individuals. This program serves as a critical step towards improving the overall well-being of small and marginal farmers in Rajasthan, acknowledging their hard work and dedication to food production. &lt;br&gt;

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Pension

**Where it applies:** Rajasthan; State/ UT

**DBT Scheme:** Yes (Direct Benefit Transfer)

**Benefits / Assistance:** 1. To a pensioner below 75 years of age : ₹750/- per month 1. To pensioners aged 75 years and above : ₹1,000/- per month

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Exclusions / Not eligible:** > **Termination of pension:-** (i) The pension will cease to exist on the date of death of the pensioner. The undrawn amount of pension payable till the date of death will lapse. (ii) In case of permanent or temporary migration of the pensioner outside Rajasthan, the pension will normally be terminated. However, the Treasurer/Deputy Treasurer, as the case may be, may resume payment of pension from the date of personal appearance before the pensioner on his return to Rajasthan, but the arrears of accrued pension for the period of his stay outside Rajasthan will be payable. Will not happen. (iii) If a small and marginal farmer pensioner has more land than the prescribed category, i.e. if the farmer is out of the small and marginal category, the pension will be stopped.
