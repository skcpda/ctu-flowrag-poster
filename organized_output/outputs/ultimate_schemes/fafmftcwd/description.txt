# Financial assistance for Marriage for the Construction Worker

**Short Title:** FAFMFTCWD

**Tags:** Labour, Building Worker, Construction Worker, Marriage Assistance, Daughter, Women

The &amp;quot;Financial Assistance for Marriage for the Construction Workers&amp;quot; by the Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board, Labour Department, U.T. Administration of Dadra and Nagar Haveli and Daman and Diu, is a welfare scheme for the registered workers of the board. Under this scheme, the Board will provide financial assistance for marriage of self and for children (upto 2 children) of the building workers. &lt;br&gt;

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Marriage

**Where it applies:** Dadra & Nagar Haveli and Daman & Diu; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:**  1.Marriage of Female registered member₹51,000/-2.Marriage of Male Registered member₹35,000/-3.Marriage of Daughter of Registered member₹51,000/-4.Marriage of Son of Registered member₹35,000/-

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Labour & Employment Department.
