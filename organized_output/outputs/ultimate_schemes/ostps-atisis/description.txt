# "Orthodox or Specialty Tea Production Subsidy" under the "Assam Tea Industries Special Incentives Scheme"

**Short Title:** OSTPS-ATISIS

**Tags:** Subsidy, Tea, Production, Incentive, Industry

Launched in 2020, the scheme &quot;Assam Tea Industries Special Incentives Scheme (ATISIS)&quot; by the Finance (Institutional Finance) Department, Government of Assam, aims to ensure long-term financial sustainability of the Tea industry and to incentivize manufacturers to move from Crush, Tear, Curl tea to Orthodox tea production. The scheme is implemented by the Finance Department and the Industries &amp; Commerce Department, Government of Assam, through multiple implementing agencies, including banks and the District Industries &amp; Commerce Centre. The applications for this scheme are accepted online through a dedicated portal with a Direct Benefit Transfer mechanism for reimbursement. 

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Agriculture,Rural & Environment; Sector: Land and water resources

**Where it applies:** Assam; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** **Benefit Amount:** ₹10/- per kilogram for Orthodox tea, Green tea, White tea, Oolong tea, Singhpo tea, Purple Tea, Yellow tea, or other Specialty tea **Special Rate:** ₹12/- per kilogram for applications of Financial Year 2023-24 only (tea production year 2022-23). **Validity Period:** Extended from Financial Year 2023-24 to 2027-28. **Disbursement Mode:** Direct Benefit Transfer as reimbursement. **Claim Deadline:** Latest by 30th June on completion of every financial year. **Additional Benefit:** Provided in addition to any other subsidy claimed from the Tea Board of India or any other agency. 

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Finance Department.
