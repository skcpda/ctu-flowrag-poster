# Old Age Pension Scheme - Punjab

**Short Title:** Oapsp

**Tags:** Financial Assistance, Welfare, Pension

Punjab State Government started a pension for old age person of the state people. The main objective is to provide financial security to those sections of society who doesn&#39;t have any stable source of income. its a DBT scheme so pension amount will directly transferred in the bank account of beneficiary. 

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Pension

**Where it applies:** Punjab; State

**DBT Scheme:** Yes (Direct Benefit Transfer)

**Benefits / Assistance:** Amount : ₹ 1,500/- per month

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Department of Social Security and Development of Women and Children.
