# Indira Mahila Shakti Prashikshan Evam Koshal Sanvardhan Yojana (RS-CSEP)

**Short Title:** IMSPEKSYRCR

**Launch Date:** 2021-12-17

**Tags:** Education, Learning, Women, Development

The Rajasthan government&#39;s Women and Child Development Department offers a program called Indira Mahila Shakti Prashikshan Evam Koshal Sanvardhan Yojana (RS-CSEP). This free program helps women and girls improve their spoken English and communication skills, along with building their confidence. By learning new skills or sharpening existing ones, participants gain the tools they need to find better jobs, start their own businesses, and achieve financial independence. The program goes beyond just skills training, aiming to empower women by providing support and knowledge in areas like entrepreneurship, leadership, managing money, and personal growth.  > **Training period :** 130 hours (2 hours a day, 5 days a week).

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Education & Learning; Sector: Coaching

**Where it applies:** Rajasthan; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** > **RKCL( Rajasthan Knowledge Corporation Limited), free spoken English and personality development training is given to women and girls.**

**Benefit Type:** In Kind

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Women and Child Development Department.
