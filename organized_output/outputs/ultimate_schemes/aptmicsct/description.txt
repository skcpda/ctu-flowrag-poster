# Assistance for Purchase of Traditional Musical Instruments and Costumes for Scheduled Caste/Tribe Individuals

**Short Title:** APTMICSCT

**Tags:** Musical Instruments, Folk Artists, Costumes, Scheduled Caste, Scheduled Tribe, Artist

The &amp;quot;Assistance for Purchase of Traditional Musical Instruments and Costumes for Scheduled Caste/Tribe Individuals&amp;quot; scheme was launched by the Department of Culture and Endowments, Government of Uttarakhand. The scheme aims to support scheduled caste and scheduled tribe individuals whose livelihood depends on traditional arts by providing them with essential musical instruments and costumes. Under this scheme, folk artists and performers who do not possess traditional musical instruments and costumes are provided these items free of cost. Assistance is limited to one eligible artist per family.

**Who is this for:** Artists; Category: Sports & Culture; Sector: Culture

**Where it applies:** Uttarakhand; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** **Free Traditional Musical Instruments:** - Dhol, Damau, Masakbeen, Ransingha, Turahi, Nagada, Dhal Talwar, and other traditional instruments are provided at no cost. **Free Traditional Costumes:** - Traditional attire required for performances is provided free of cost. **Livelihood Support:** - Enables artists to continue their traditional art practices and maintain an income source. > **Note:** - Only one instrument was provided per artist in their lifetime. - A maximum of one artist per family can benefit from the scheme.

**Benefit Type:** In Kind

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Department of Culture and Endowments.
