# Dr.Babasaheb Ambedkar Udyog Uday Yojana: Assistance of Capital Investment Subsidy

**Short Title:** BAUUYCIS

**Launch Date:** 2020-08-07

**Tags:** Business, Entrepreneurship, MSMEs, Start Up, Scheduled Caste, Scheduled Tribe

The scheme &amp;quot;Dr.Babasaheb Ambedkar Udyog Uday Yojana&amp;quot; is an umbrella scheme launched by the Industries and Mines Department, Gujarat. It aims to empower the state&amp;#39;s dynamic MSME sector, which significantly contributes to employment, GDP, and exports. Effective from August 7th, 2020 to August 6th, 2025, this initiative supports SC/ST entrepreneurs and strengthens MSMEs, making them globally competitive. The component &amp;quot;Assistance of Capital Investment Subsidy to Micro enterprises&amp;quot; provides a capital investment subsidy to support micro-enterprises in manufacturing.

**Who is this for:** Business Entity, Industries; Category: Business & Entrepreneurship; Sector: Setting up / start-up / entrepreneurship

**Where it applies:** Gujarat; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** > **Financial Assistance:** **Category of Taluka****Quantum of Incentive** [Category 1](https://indextb.com/files/2020/11/a34fc914-6cdd-4fd6-8773-502017da4334_taluka%20category.pdf)30% of term loan amount upto ₹40,00,000/-; If the Eligible FCI is over 10,00,00,000/-, additional 10,00,000/- will be given.[Category 2](https://indextb.com/files/2020/11/a34fc914-6cdd-4fd6-8773-502017da4334_taluka%20category.pdf)25% of term loan amount upto ₹35,00,000/-; If the Eligible FCI is over 10,00,00,000/-, additional 7,50,000/- will be given.[Category 3](https://indextb.com/files/2020/11/a34fc914-6cdd-4fd6-8773-502017da4334_taluka%20category.pdf) &amp;amp;amp;amp; Municipal Corporation Areas15% of term loan amount upto ₹15,00,000/-; If the Eligible FCI is over 10,00,00,000/-, additional 5,00,000/- will be given.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Industries and Mines Department.
