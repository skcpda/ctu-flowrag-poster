# Distribution of Seeds of more productions varieties/ Hybrids varieties Seeds and fertilizer at subsidies etc. to Adivasi farmers in Tribal Area: Tarpaulin Gujarat

**Short Title:** DSMPVHVSTGUJ

**Tags:** Adivasi Farmers, Crop Production, Livelihood, Seeds, Fertilizer, Subsidies, Tarpaulin

The scheme &quot;Distribution of Seeds of more productive varieties / Hybrid varieties Seeds and fertilizer at subsidies etc. to Adivasi farmers in Tribal Area (TASP)&quot; with a component &quot;Tarpaulin&quot; is introduced by the Agriculture, Farmers Welfare and Co-operation Department, Government of Gujarat, aims to provide subsidized agricultural inputs and tools to Scheduled Tribe farmers for enhancing agricultural production. Through this scheme, the purchase price of tarpaulin assistance provided to tribal cultivators is used to improve productivity and livelihood. 

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Agriculture,Rural & Environment; Sector: Agricultural Inputs- seeds, fertilizer etc.

**Where it applies:** Gujarat; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** 1. The assistance covers 75% of the purchase price of the tarpaulin or ₹1,875/-, whichever amount is less.  > **Conditions:** A maximum of two tarpaulins can be claimed per account. > **Frequency of Disbursement:** The benefit can be reclaimed once every 3 years. > **Remark:** As per conditions No. 5, 16, 17, 19 and 22 of [Appendix-1](https://mariyojana.gujarat.gov.in/Schemeatoz.aspx). (Refer Scheme No. 35) 

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Agriculture, Farmers Welfare and Cooperation Department.
