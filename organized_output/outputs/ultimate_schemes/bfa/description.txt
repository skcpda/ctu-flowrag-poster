# Buti Foundation Award

**Short Title:** BFA

**Tags:** Award, Scientist, Engineer, Research

The Buti Foundation Award, established in 2007 by the Buti Foundation, New Delhi, is a biennial award recognizing innovative and outstanding research in the field of Plasma Science &amp; Technology. The award aims to honor a Scientist/Engineer under the age of 45 years for their significant contributions to this field. The award includes a medal and a cash prize.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Skills & Employment; Sector: Apprenticeship

**Where it applies:** Central
