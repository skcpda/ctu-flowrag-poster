# Gagan Bharari Shiksha Yojana for OBC (Dhangar) Students

**Short Title:** GBSYOBC

**Tags:** Financial Assistance, OBC, Student, Goa, Dhangar, Allowance, Education, Post Matric, Disability

The scheme &quot;Gagan Bharari Shiksha Yojana for OBC (Dhangar) Students&quot; by the Directorate of Social Welfare, Government of Goa aims to provide additional financial assistance to OBC (Dhangar) students to supplement their Post Matric Scholarship maintenance allowance. Through this scheme, additional monthly allowances are provided to OBC (Dhangar) students to meet their food, travel, and disability-related expenses, as the existing Post Matric Scholarship amounts are inadequate. The applications for this scheme are accepted online. The scheme is being implemented by the Chief Minister Scholarship Portal, Government of Goa.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Scholarships and student finance

**Where it applies:** Goa; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** **Additional Maintenance Allowance** - Day Scholars: ₹750 per month - Hostel Residents: ₹1,500 per month **Duration:** 10 months during the academic year  **Additional Disability Allowance** Amount: ₹750 per month **Duration:** 10 months during the academic year

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Social Welfare Department.
