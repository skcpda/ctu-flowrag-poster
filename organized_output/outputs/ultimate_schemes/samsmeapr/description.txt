# Scheme for Assistance to Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises (MSME): Assistance for Patent Registration

**Short Title:** SAMSMEAPR

**Launch Date:** 2020-08-07

**Closing Date:** 2025-08-06

**Tags:** Business, Entrepreneurship, MSMEs, Start Up

The &amp;quot;Scheme for Assistance to Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises (MSME)&amp;quot; is an umbrella scheme launched by the Industries and Mines Department, Gujarat. It aims to empower the state&amp;#39;s dynamic MSME sector, which significantly contributes to employment, GDP, and exports. Effective from August 7th, 2020 to August 6th, 2025, this initiative supports entrepreneurs and strengthens MSMEs, making them globally competitive. The component &amp;quot;Assistance for Patent Registration&amp;quot; provides financial support to individuals and legal entities for obtaining patent registration.

**Who is this for:** Business Entity, Industries; Category: Business & Entrepreneurship; Sector: Setting up / start-up / entrepreneurship

**Where it applies:** Gujarat; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - **Financial Support:** 75% reimbursement of patent registration expenses up to a maximum ₹25,00,000/- per applicant/enterprise. - **Attorney Fee Cap:** ₹50,000/- (domestic), ₹2,00,000/- per country (international). &gt; **Disbursement:** - 50% after patent publication/notification. - 50% after receiving the patent certificate. &gt; **Note:** Expenses related to travel and accommodation are not covered.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Industries and Mines Department.

**References / Annexures:** - **Guidelines**: https://ic.gujarat.gov.in/documents/commondoc/2020/3-GR_02092020.pdf - **Online Application Portal**: https://ifp.gujarat.gov.in/IC/digigov.htm?actionFlag=loadCitizenRegPage
