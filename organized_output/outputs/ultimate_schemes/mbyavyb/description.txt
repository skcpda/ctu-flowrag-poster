# Mukhyamantri Bagwani Mission: Anjeer Vikas Yojana

**Short Title:** MBYAVYB

**Tags:** Agriculture, Subsidy, Plant Supply, Financial Assistance, Fig Development

The “Anjeer Vikas Yojana” a component of the umbrella scheme “Mukhyamantri Bagwani Mission”, launched by the Department of Agriculture, Bihar. The main objective of the scheme is to promote the commercial cultivation of horticultural crops in the state of Bihar. This component promotes fig cultivation across all districts in Bihar by providing financial assistance and high quality plants. It ensures inclusivity with reserved quotas for SC, ST, and women beneficiaries. Payments are processed through DBT, and only ryot farmers are eligible to apply for this scheme. &lt;br&gt;

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Agriculture,Rural & Environment; Sector: Agricultural Inputs- seeds, fertilizer etc.

**Where it applies:** Bihar; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** > **Financial Support:** - **First year:** ₹30,000/- per hectare. - **Second and third year:** ₹10,000/- per hectare (per year). > **Plant Distribution:** - Supply of high-quality plants. 

**Benefit Type:** Composite

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Agriculture Department.
