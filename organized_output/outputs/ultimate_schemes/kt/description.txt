# Khet Talai

**Short Title:** KT

**Tags:** Farming, Farmer, Khet Talai, Development

Khet Talai Yojana under the Agriculture Department of Rajasthan Government, The biggest problem for farmers in Rajasthan is water for irrigation. One solution to this problem is a farm pond or in other words, a farm pond, by which the farmer can collect rainwater and use it for irrigation, but all the farmers get it constructed. Therefore, the state government gives a subsidy to the farmers on the construction of farm ponds so that the farmers can collect rainwater and irrigate their fields by making farm ponds in their fields.  > **Other important points** 1. After the application, administrative approval for the construction of the farm pond will be issued by the Agriculture Department. 1. Information about this will be received through mobile message/agriculture supervisor. 1. Spot verification will be done by the department before and after the construction of Khet Talai. 1. The grant amount will be deposited directly into the farmer&#39;s account.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Agriculture,Rural & Environment; Sector: Financial assistance

**Where it applies:** Rajasthan; State/ UT

**DBT Scheme:** Yes (Direct Benefit Transfer)

**Benefits / Assistance:** 1. Scheduled Caste (SC) and Scheduled Tribes (ST), small, and marginal farmers will get 70 percent of the unit cost or a maximum of ₹73,500/- on raw form pounds and 90 percent or ₹1,35,000/- on plastic lining form pounds and other category farmers. 1. The subsidy is available on 60 percent of the cost or a maximum of ₹63,000/- on raw form pounds and 80 percent or ₹1,20,000/- on plastic lining form pounds, whichever is less. &lt;br&gt; &gt; **The subsidy is payable only on farm ponds of a minimum 400 cubic meter capacity.**

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Agriculture Department.
