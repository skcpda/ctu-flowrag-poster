# Mukhyamantri Aadharbhut Shiksha Prashikshan Sahayata Yojana

**Short Title:** MASPSY

**Launch Date:** 2023-03-23

**Tags:** Education, Coaching, Learning

Labor Department of Chhattisgarh State started this scheme &amp;amp;quot;Mukhyamantri Aadharbhut Shiksha Prashikshan Sahayata Yojana&amp;amp;quot; for Building and other construction worker on 29 March 2023.. The main objective of the scheme to provides financial assistance for education.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Education & Learning

**Where it applies:** Chhattisgarh; State

**Benefits / Assistance:** - Free coaching assistance to get the admission for 6th and 9th classes.

**Benefit Type:** In Kind

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Labour Department.
