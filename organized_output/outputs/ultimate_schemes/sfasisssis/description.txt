# Scheme or assistance for Start Ups/ Innovation: Assistance to Startups having a Significant Impact on Society

**Short Title:** SFASISSSIS

**Launch Date:** 2020-08-07

**Tags:** Start Up, Innovation, Entrepreneurship

The &amp;amp;quot;Scheme for Assistance for Start Ups/ Innovation&amp;amp;quot; is an umbrella scheme launched by the Industries and Mines Department, Gujarat under the Gujarat Industrial Policy. Effective from August 7th, 2020 to August 6th, 2025, it aims to drive economic growth by generating jobs, fostering competition, and introducing next-gen solutions. The component &amp;amp;quot;Assistance to Startups having a Significant Impact on Society&amp;amp;quot;, provides financial assistance to startups that are developing innovative projects with a significant social impact.

**Who is this for:** Business Entity, Industries; Category: Business & Entrepreneurship; Sector: Setting up / start-up / entrepreneurship

**Where it applies:** Gujarat; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - **Financial Support:** Up to ₹10,00,000/- assistance is provided for innovative projects which have a significant impact on society,

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Exclusions / Not eligible:** - Entities formed by splitting or restructuring an existing business are not considered startups under this scheme. - Startups availing benefits under other startup schemes of the Government of Gujarat are ineligible.

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Industries and Mines Department.

**References / Annexures:** - **Guidelines**: https://ic.gujarat.gov.in/documents/commondoc/2020/13-GR_02092020.pdf - **User Manual**: https://startup.gujarat.gov.in/assets/pdf/Startup%20User%20-%20V%201.0.pdf
