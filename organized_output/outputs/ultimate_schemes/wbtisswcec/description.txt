#  West Bengal Textile Incentive Scheme: Subsidy for Water conservation/ Environment Compliance

**Short Title:** WBTISSWCEC

**Launch Date:** 2022-12-13

**Tags:** Textile, Incentives, Waiver, Entrepreneurship, Subsidy, Reimbursement, Water Conservation, Environment Compliance

The scheme “Subsidy for Water Conservation/ Environment Compliance” is a sub scheme under “West Bengal Textile Incentive Scheme”. It was launched on 13 December, 2022, by the Department of Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises and Textiles, Government of West Bengal, and shall remain valid upto five years. &gt; **Objective of the &amp;quot;West Bengal Textile Incentive Scheme” Scheme:** The object of the scheme is to extend fiscal incentives to textile sector across the entire value chain starting from fibre to stitched garments for setting up such units in the State. The focus is on accelerated and focused development of the textiles sector across the state. This shall enable to maximize the utilization of resources, generate new employment and widen the area of operation to make the state emerge as the preferred destination for investment in the textile sector. &gt; **Objective of the &amp;quot;Subsidy for Water Conservation/ Environment Compliance&amp;quot; Scheme:** Through this incentive, an eligible textile sector enterprise for its approved project will be entitled to a reimbursement of expenditure incurred by it towards the cost of captive Effluent Water Treatment Plant for wastewater recycling and/ or other pollution control devices.

**Who is this for:** Business Entity, Joint Liability Groups (JLGS), Self Help Groups (SHGS), Registered Societies, Trust; Category: Business & Entrepreneurship; Sector: Setting up / start-up / entrepreneurship

**Where it applies:** West Bengal; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - A reimbursement of 25% subject to a maximum of ₹ 1,50,00,000/-. - For a cluster of units with a minimum of 5 units with an investment of minimum Rs. 200 crores, the state Govt. in the MSMET department will set up a Common Effluent Treatment Plants (CETP) to be run by an Special Purpose Vehicle (SPV) of the clustered units and the running cost will be borne by those units.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Exclusions / Not eligible:** - The incentives shall not be applicable to the industrial units/ enterprises that have been registered and for which eligibility certificates have been issued /incentives have been sanctioned and disbursed under the respective Incentive Scheme of the State. - That has been registered and for which eligibility certificates have been issued in terms of the respective Incentive Scheme of the State but no claim and sanction of incentive has been made.

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Micro, Small & Medium Enterprises and Textiles Department.
