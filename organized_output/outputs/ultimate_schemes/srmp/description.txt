# Sales Rebate under 'Marketing And Publicity'

**Short Title:** SRMP

**Tags:** Sales, Rebate, MSME, Industry, Business, Handicrafts

The scheme &quot;Sales Rebate under Marketing And Publicity&quot; was launched by the Department of Industries and Commerce, Union Territory of Puducherry. The scheme aims to provide a sales rebate of 15% to the existing and newly established Sales Emporia / Showrooms recognized by the Union Territory of Puducherry for the sale of Handicrafts, Micro and small enterprises products manufactured by the units in Puducherry. The objective of this is to promote the sale of the products of Micro/Small/Medium Enterprises (MSME) and the Cottage Sector.

**Who is this for:** Business Entity; Category: Business & Entrepreneurship; Sector: Sales of goods, services & data protection

**Where it applies:** Puducherry; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** 15% as a rebate on the sales, subject to a maximum of ₹15,00,000/- per annum.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: District Industries Centre Organization.
