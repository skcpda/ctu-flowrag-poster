# Distribution of Seeds of more productions varieties/ Hybrids varieties Seeds and fertilizer at subsidies etc. to Adivasi farmers in Tribal Area: 100% Soluble Chemical Fertilizer/Nano Fertilizer Gujarat

**Short Title:** DSMPVHSCFGUJ

**Tags:** Adivasi Farmers, Crop Production, Livelihood, Fertilizer Subsidies, Seeds, Nano Fertilizer

The scheme &quot;Distribution of Seeds of more productive varieties / Hybrid varieties Seeds and fertilizer at subsidies, etc,. to Adivasi farmers in Tribal Area (TASP)&quot; with a component &quot;100% Soluble Chemical Fertilizer/Nano Fertilizer&quot; is introduced by the Agriculture, Farmers Welfare and Co-operation Department, Government of Gujarat, aims to assist Adivasi and Scheduled Tribe farmers in tribal and non-tribal areas to achieve higher agricultural production and improve their livelihoods. Through this scheme, subsidies are provided on items such as seeds of more productive/hybrid varieties, fertilizers, plant protection equipment, tarpaulins, micronutrients, water carrying pipelines, and pump sets. 

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Agriculture,Rural & Environment; Sector: Agricultural Inputs- seeds, fertilizer etc.

**Where it applies:** Gujarat; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** > **100% Soluble Chemical Fertilizer/Nano Fertilizer:** - 100% soluble chemical fertilizers/nano-fertilizers of FCO-approved grade subject to 75% of the purchase price or ₹ 750/- per hectare, will be eligible for subsidy. (subject to a maximum of four hectares per account)  > **Remark:** As per conditions No. 5, 16, 17, 19 and 22 of [Appendix-1](https://mariyojana.gujarat.gov.in/Schemeatoz.aspx). (Refer Scheme No. 35). > **Frequency:** Every year. 

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Agriculture, Farmers Welfare and Cooperation Department.
