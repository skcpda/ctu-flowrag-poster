# Fodder Minikits under Integrated Fodder Development Scheme (General Category)

**Short Title:** FMUIFDS(GC)

**Tags:** Fodder Minikits, Farmer, Seed

The scheme “Fodder Minikits under Integrated Fodder Development Scheme” is implemented by the Directorate of Animal Husbandry, Agriculture, Farmers Welfare and Co-operation Department, Government of Gujarat. Under this scheme, improved varieties of fodder seeds are provided to beneficiaries for widespread publicity, demonstration, and adaptation by farmers.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Agriculture,Rural & Environment; Sector: Animal husbandry

**Where it applies:** Gujarat; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** 1. Under this scheme, the fodder seed minikit of 3 Kg, 7 Kg, or 10 Kg is given to the eligible beneficiary.

**Benefit Type:** In Kind

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: Directorate Of Animal Husbandry, Government Of Gujarat; Nodal Department: Agriculture, Farmers Welfare and Cooperation Department.
