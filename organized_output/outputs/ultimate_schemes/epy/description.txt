# Eklavya Prashikshan Yojana

**Short Title:** EPY

**Tags:** Free Coaching, Financial Assistance, Student, UPSC, SSC

The &quot;Eklavya Prashikshan Yojana&quot; by the Directorate of Higher Education, Department of Higher and Technical Education, Government of Jharkhand, aims to provide free coaching and financial assistance to students preparing for competitive exams conducted by national and state-level agencies. Under this scheme, students from Jharkhand receive free coaching and a monthly stipend of ₹2,500/- to help them succeed in examinations such as UPSC, JPSC, SSC, Railway Recruitment, and banking exams.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Education & Learning; Sector: Scholarships and student finance

**Where it applies:** Jharkhand; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - ₹2,500/- monthly stipends per student during the coaching period. - Coaching will be provided by nationally reputed institutions within Jharkhand.

**Benefit Type:** Composite

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Higher and Techical Education Department.
