# Samagra Samajik Suraksha Parityakta Pension Yojana

**Short Title:** PSSPPY

**Launch Date:** 1981-01-01

**Tags:** Pension, Empowerment, Financial Benefits, BPL

Samagra Samajik Suraksha Parityakta Pension Yojana under the Department of Social Justice government of Madhya Pradesh provides financial benefits to Be from a family living below the poverty line. to fulfill the basic needs of life.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Pension

**Where it applies:** Madhya Pradesh; State/ UT

**DBT Scheme:** Yes (Direct Benefit Transfer)

**Benefits / Assistance:** Pension Benefits : ₹ 600/- per month

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Department of Social Justice and Empowerment of Persons with Disabilities.

**References / Annexures:** - **Guidelines**: https://socialsecurity.mp.gov.in/Scheme/SSWP.aspx - **Apply Portal**: https://socialsecurity.mp.gov.in/Home.aspx
