# Chief Ministers’ Education Assistance Scheme

**Short Title:** CMEAS

**Tags:** Education Assistance, Student, Economically Weaker Section, Studying Abroad, Coaching Centre

The &amp;quot;Chief Ministers’ Education Assistance Scheme (CMEAS)&amp;quot; was launched by the Education Department, Government of Sikkim. The objective of scheme is to provide financial assistance to students from economically weaker sections, marginalized communities and specially-abled students. Under this scheme, students receive financial aid for pursuing education within the state, outside the state, abroad, and in registered coaching institutions. > ***Scheme Implementation:*** - The scheme is implemented by the Chief Minister’s Office (CMO), Sikkim, with a dedicated nodal officer for verification, approval, and fund disbursement.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Education & Learning; Sector: Universities and higher education

**Where it applies:** Sikkim; State/ UT
