# Tablet Assistance to Scheduled Caste Students of 12th Standard

**Short Title:** TASCS12S

**Tags:** Tablet, Scheduled Caste, Student, 12th Standard

The “Tablet Assistance to Scheduled Caste Students” scheme is administered by the Director, Scheduled Caste Welfare, Government of Gujarat. Under the scheme, tablets are provided to students in the 12th standard who belong to the Scheduled Caste (SC) category.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment

**Where it applies:** Gujarat; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - Under the scheme, students in the 12th standard belonging to the Scheduled Caste category will receive a tablet by paying a token fee of ₹1,000/-.

**Benefit Type:** In Kind

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: District Deputy Director (SCW) Office, Gujarat; Nodal Department: Social Justice and Empowerment Department.
