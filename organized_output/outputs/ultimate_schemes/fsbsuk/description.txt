# Free Shoe and Bag Scheme

**Short Title:** FSBS

**Tags:** Free Shoes, Free Bags, Student

The scheme &amp;quot;Free Shoes and Bags Scheme&amp;quot; is implemented by the Basic Education Department under the Department of Education, Government of Uttarakhand. The scheme aims to provide financial assistance to students from Classes 1 to 8 studying in all government and government-aided schools for purchasing shoes and bags. 

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Education & Learning; Sector: Resources for schools

**Where it applies:** Uttarakhand; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** Under this scheme, financial assistance is provided for purchasing shoes and bags for boys and girls as follows: - ₹318/- per student for Classes 1 to 5 - ₹462/- per student for Classes 6 to 8 The amount is transferred directly to the students&amp;#39; account through Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT). 

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: Basic Education Department, Government Of Uttarakhand; Nodal Department: Department Of Education.
