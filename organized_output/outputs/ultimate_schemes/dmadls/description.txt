# Disable Marriage Scheme (Divyang Lagna Sahay)

**Short Title:** DMA(DLS)

**Tags:** Marriage, Divyang, PwD, Financial Assistance

The scheme &amp;quot;Disable Marriage Scheme&amp;quot; was launched by the Director Social Defence, Department of Social Justice &amp;amp; Empowerment, Government of Gujarat. Under this scheme, financial assistance is provided to persons with disabilities when a person with a disability marries another person with a disability, as well as when a non-disabled person marries someone with a disability.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Marriage

**Where it applies:** Gujarat; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** 1. Under this scheme, when two individuals with disabilities marry each other, the couple is entitled to a total assistance of ₹1,00,000/-, equating to ₹50,000/- per person. 1. If a person without disabilities marries someone with a disability, the individual with a disability is eligible to receive assistance of ₹50,000/-.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: Office Of The District Social Defence Officer, Gujarat; Nodal Department: Social Justice and Empowerment Department.
