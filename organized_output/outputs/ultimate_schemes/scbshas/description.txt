# Scheduled Caste Boys/Girls Housing Assistance Scheme

**Short Title:** SCBSHAS

**Tags:** Scheduled Caste, Housing, Student, College

The scheme &quot;Scheduled Caste Boys/Girls Housing Assistance Scheme&quot; by the Tribal Welfare and Scheduled Caste (SC) Department, Government of Madhya Pradesh, provides financial support for residential facilities to Scheduled Caste students who are studying in colleges outside their native village/city, enabling them to continue their college education.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Housing & Shelter; Sector: Hostel service

**Where it applies:** Madhya Pradesh; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - ₹2,000/- per month, if the student is residing in a Divisional headquarters (Bhopal, Indore, Gwalior, Jabalpur, Ujjain). - ₹1,250/- per month, if the student is residing in Other District Headquarters. - ₹1,000/- per month, if the student is residing in a Tehsil/Development Block Headquarters.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Tribal Welfare and Scheduled Caste (SC) Department.
