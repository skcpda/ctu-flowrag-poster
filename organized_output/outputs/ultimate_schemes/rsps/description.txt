# Retired Sportsperson Pension Scheme

**Short Title:** RSPS

**Tags:** Sport, Retired Sportsperson, Pension, Athletes

The scheme &amp;quot;Retired Sportsperson Pension Scheme&amp;quot; by the Sports, Youth and Cultural Activities Department, Government of Gujarat aims to recognize and support the contributions of state-level sportspersons who have won medals in national-level sports events by offering them a financial pension after the age of 50 years. The scheme ensures dignified post-retirement sustenance for these individuals, thereby encouraging younger generations to pursue sports professionally. The scheme is implemented by the Sports Authority of Gujarat.   

**Who is this for:** Sportsperson; Category: Sports & Culture; Sector: Sports

**Where it applies:** Gujarat; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - **Financial Assistance:** A Pension of ₹3,000/- per month. 

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: Sports Authority Of Gujarat; Nodal Department: Sports, Youth and Cultural Activities Department.

**References / Annexures:** - **Guidelines (Scheme No. 490)**: https://mariyojana.gujarat.gov.in/Schemeatoz.aspx
