# PMMSY: Construction of Fish Kiosks Including Kiosks of Aquarium/Ornamental Fish-Haryana

**Short Title:** SCFKIKAOFHR

**Tags:** Subsidy, Aquarium, Ornamental, Construction, Koisks

The scheme &quot;Pradhan Mantri Matasya Sampada Yojana: Construction of Fish Kiosks including Kiosks of Aquarium/Ornamental fish&quot; by the Ministry Of Fisheries, Animal Husbandry &amp; Dairying, Government of India, aims to provide central assistance for the construction of fish kiosks, including those for aquarium/ornamental fish. 

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Agriculture,Rural & Environment; Sector: Financial assistance

**Where it applies:** Haryana; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** The project cost is ₹10,00,000/- per unit. Subsidy of 40% of the project cost for General Category beneficiaries. Subsidy of 60% of the project cost for SC/Women beneficiaries. 

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Animal Husbandry & Dairying Department.
