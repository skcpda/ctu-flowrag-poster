# Scheme for Supply of TV Sets to Industrial Establishments/Labour Welfare Centres/Clubs/Recreation Rooms

**Short Title:** SSTVSIELWCCRR

**Tags:** Television, TV, Club, Recreation, Industry, Employee, Worker

&quot;Scheme for Supply of TV Sets to Industrial Establishments/Labour Welfare Centres/Clubs/Recreation Rooms&quot; was launched by the Goa Labour Welfare Board, Department of Labour and Employment, Goa. The scheme aims to provide television sets to enable the workers/employees to witness programmes of national importance and to inculcate in themselves ideas of national integration besides broadening their outlook.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Business & Entrepreneurship; Sector: Technology upgradation

**Where it applies:** Goa; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - One TV Set (coloured) of up to ₹25,000, including all accessories. **NOTE 1:** The incidental expenses incurred in connection/ with the operation of TV Sets etc. will have to be borne by the concerned establishment. **NOTE 2:** If the Management/Union/Government fails to comply with the provisions of the schemes/agreement, the Goa Labour Welfare Board shall have the right to withdraw TV Set if it is supplied departmentally or recover the grant-in-aid amount with interest thereon at the prescribed rate if the TV is provided on grant-in-aid basis.

**Benefit Type:** In Kind

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: Goa Labour Welfare Board; Nodal Department: Labour and Employment Department.
