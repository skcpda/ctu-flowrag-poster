# Pre-Matric Scholarship for the Students of Economically Backward Class

**Short Title:** PMSSEBC

**Tags:** Scholarship, Student, EBC, Pre-matric

The &quot;Pre-Matric Scholarship for the Students of Economically Backward Class&quot; was launched by the Social Justice and Empowerment Department, Government of Gujarat. It aims to provide financial assistance to school-going students from economically backward sections to reduce the dropout rate and promote education from class 1st to 10th. Under this scheme, monetary scholarships are provided to boys and girls studying in government and aided primary schools from Standard 1st to 10th, based on class and gender. 

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Education & Learning; Sector: Scholarships and student finance

**Where it applies:** Gujarat; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** > **Scholarship Amounts:** - Boys (Std. 1st to 8th): ₹750/- per annum.- Boys (Std. 9th to 10th): ₹1,000/- per annum.- Girls (Std. 1st to 5th): ₹500/- per annum.- Girls (Std. 6th to 10th): ₹1,000/- per annum. 

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Social Justice and Empowerment Department.

**References / Annexures:** - **Guidelines (Scheme No. 391)**: https://mariyojana.gujarat.gov.in/Schemeatoz.aspx
