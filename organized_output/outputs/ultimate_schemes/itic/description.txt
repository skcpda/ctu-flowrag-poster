# IT Incubation Center

**Short Title:** ITIC

**Tags:** Startup, Incubation, IT Infrastructure

The scheme &amp;quot;IT Incubation Center&amp;quot; by the Information Technology Development Agency, Government of Uttarakhand aims to nurture and accelerate early-stage startups by providing them with a supportive ecosystem. Through this scheme, startups are provided with mentorship, co-working space, advisory services, and guidance to resolve issues related to business development and innovation. The scheme caters to startups in fields like IT, medical/electronics, health tech, travel-tech, and related sectors. It helps promote entrepreneurship and product innovation, with a focus on enabling entrepreneurs to convert ideas into viable business models.

**Who is this for:** Individual, Business Entity, University / Institution; Category: Business & Entrepreneurship; Sector: Setting up / start-up / entrepreneurship

**Where it applies:** Uttarakhand; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - **Mentorship &amp; Advisory Support:** Guidance on technical, financial, and business development aspects.- **Domain-Specific Support:** Startups in medical/electronics, health tech, travel-tech, and other sectors receive customized mentoring.

**Benefit Type:** In Kind

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Information Technology Development Agency.
