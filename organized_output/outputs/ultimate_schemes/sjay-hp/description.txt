# Swaran Jayanti Ashray Yojana - Himachal Pradesh

**Short Title:** SJAY-HP

**Tags:** Financial Assistance, Housing, Shelter

The &quot;Swaran Jayanti Ashray Yojana&quot; was launched by the Department of Social Justice and Empowerment, Government of Himachal Pradesh aims to provide financial assistance for the construction of houses who are permanent residents of Himachal Pradesh belonging to Scheduled Castes, Scheduled Tribes, Other Backward Classes.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Housing & Shelter; Sector: Shelter, integrated support and assistance

**Where it applies:** Himachal Pradesh; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - **Financial assistance of ₹1,50,000/- for construction of new house.**

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Department of Social Justice and Empowerment.

**References / Annexures:** - **Guidelines**: http://esomsa.hp.gov.in/?q=schemes-3 - **Application Form**: http://esomsa.hp.gov.in/sites/default/files/Swarn%20Jayanti%20Ashray%20Yojna.pdf
