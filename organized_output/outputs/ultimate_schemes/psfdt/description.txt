# Pension Scheme for Destitute Transgender

**Short Title:** PSFDT

**Tags:** Transgender, Pension, Financial Assistance, Destitute

The scheme &quot;Pension Scheme for Destitute Transgender&quot; was launched by the Social Welfare and Women Empowerment Department, Government of Tamil Nadu. The scheme aims to help poor transgender who are above the age of 40 years and are unable to earn their livelihood. Interested and eligible applicants should apply via Common Service Centres (CSC).

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Pension

**Where it applies:** Tamil Nadu; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - Pension of ₹ 1,000/- per month.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Department of Social Welfare and Women Empowerment.
