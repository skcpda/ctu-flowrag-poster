# Maternity Benefit

**Short Title:** MBWB

**Tags:** Labour, Building Worker, Construction Worker, Maternity, Pregnancy Care, Women

The &amp;quot;Maternity Benefit&amp;#39;&amp;#39; scheme by the Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board, Labour Department, West Bengal, is a welfare scheme for the registered workers of the board. Under this scheme, the Board shall provide financial benefit during the period of maternity to the female beneficiary or wife in case of a male beneficiary who is registered for 1 year under the Board, subject to a maximum 2 times in his/ her whole life time.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Pregnancy care

**Where it applies:** West Bengal; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - **Quantum of Assistance:** ₹6,000/-. **Note:** The benefit shall be allowed for two times in beneficiarie&amp;#39;s whole life time. &lt;br&gt;

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Labour Department.
