# Tuition Fees And Examination Fees To Vjnt Students

**Short Title:** Tfefvs

**Tags:** Tuition Fees, Vimukta Jati And Nomadic Tribes, Students, Examination Fees

The Government of Maharashtra launched a scheme namely &quot;Tuition fees and examination fees to Vimukta Jati and Nomadic Tribes (VJNT) students. **Scheme Objective** Under the scheme, 1. Tuition Fees, 1. Admission Fees 1. Term Fees 1. Library Fees 1. Laboratory Fees 1. Gymkhana Fees, and 1. Examination Fees All the students belonging to V.J. and N.T. are being reimbursed for the payment of these mentioned 7 fees at Post Matriculation Education in recognized educational institutions. Students with one failure and those not covered under the Government of India Scholarship are also held eligible for such reimbursement.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Education & Learning; Sector: Scholarships and student finance

**Where it applies:** Maharashtra; State

**Benefits / Assistance:** All types of compulsory fees such as Tuition fees, and Exams. Fees &amp; other fees are reimbursed to the concerned college/institution.

**Benefit Type:** In Kind

**Exclusions / Not eligible:** Students with one failure and those not covered under the Government of India Scholarship are also held eligible for such reimbursement.

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Social Justice and Special Assistance Department.
