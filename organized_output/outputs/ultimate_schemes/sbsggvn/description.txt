# Small Business Scheme (Gujarat Gopalak Vikas Nigam)

**Short Title:** SBSGGVN

**Tags:** Grocery Stores, Dairy Parlors, Animal Feed Sales Centers, Mobile Repair Shop, Computer Repair Shop

The scheme &quot;Small Business Scheme&quot; by the Social Justice &amp; Empowerment Department, Government of Gujarat, aims to support small businesses. Through this scheme, individual loans at an annual interest rate are provided to individuals from the SEBC Rabari and Bharwad communities for establishing businesses such as grocery shops, dairy parlours, animal food sales centres, and mobile-computer repair shops. Gujarat Gopalak Vikas Nigam implements the scheme. 

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Banking,Financial Services and Insurance; Sector: Loan

**Where it applies:** Gujarat; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** 1. The maximum loan limit is ₹2,00,000/- per individual. 1. The interest rate is 6% per annum. 1. The loan amount disbursed is 95% of the total project cost. 1. The beneficiary Contribution is 5% of the total project cost. 

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Social Justice and Empowerment Department.
