# Pension Benefit (TBOCWW)

**Short Title:** PB-TBOCWW

**Tags:** Financial Assistance, Pension, Senior Citizen, Social Welfare

The &quot;Pension Benefit&quot; scheme was launched by the Tripura Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board, Labour Department Tripura. The scheme aims to provide financial assistance to the registered beneficiary who has retired due to old age attaining 60 years. The registered beneficiary will apply in the prescribed form through electronically. 

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Pension

**Where it applies:** Tripura; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - **A pension of ₹1,000/- per month is to be paid till the death of the pensioner.**

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Labour Department .

**References / Annexures:** - **Guidelines**: https://tbocwwb.tripura.gov.in/scheme.php?id=6
