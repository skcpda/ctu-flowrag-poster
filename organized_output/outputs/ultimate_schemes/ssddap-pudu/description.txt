# Supply of Sarees and Dhoties to Differently Abled Persons

**Short Title:** SSDDAP-PUDU

**Tags:** Clothes, Disability, PwD, Social Welfare

The scheme “Supply of Sarees and Dhoties to Differently Abled Persons” is launched by the Department of Social Welfare, Government of Puducherry. In this scheme, free dhothies and sarees or equivalent Direct Benefit Transfer are provided to the differently abled persons who are benefited under the monthly financial assistance schemes in lieu of Diwali / Pongal / local regional festivals.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Culture

**Where it applies:** Puducherry; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** Free Dhothies and Sarees or Equivalent Direct Benefit Transfer during Diwali / Pongal / local regional festivals.

**Benefit Type:** In Kind

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Social Welfare Department, Puducherry.
