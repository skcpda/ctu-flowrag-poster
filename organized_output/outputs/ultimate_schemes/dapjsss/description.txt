# Dr. APJ Abdul Kalam Shramik Pravidhik Shiksha Sahaayata Yojana

**Short Title:** DAPJSSS

**Tags:** Education, Financial Assistance, Student Finance

The Dr. APJ Abdul Kalam Shramik Pravidhik Shiksha Sahayata Yojana (DAKSPSSY) is a scheme launched by the Government of Uttar Pradesh to provide financial assistance to the children of registered workers for pursuing technical education. The scheme aims to promote technical education among the children of workers and help them build a better future.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Education & Learning; Sector: Scholarships and student finance

**Where it applies:** Uttar Pradesh; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** Financial Assistance will be provided as: - Degree Course - ₹25,000/- - Diploma Course - ₹15,000/- - Certificate Course - ₹10,000/-

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Labour Department, Uttar Pradesh.
