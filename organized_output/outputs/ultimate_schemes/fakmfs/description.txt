# Financial Assistance To Kashmiri Migrants Families Scheme

**Short Title:** FAKMFS

**Launch Date:** 2006-04-01

**Tags:** Kashmiri, Migrants, Financial Assistance, Jammu And Kashmir, Family

Launched on 1st April 2006, the scheme “Financial Assistance To Kashmiri Migrants Families Scheme” by the Department of Social Justice and Empowerment, Government of Haryana, financial assistance of ₹1250/- per month per family member is provided for five years to the Kashmiri families which have migrated from Jammu &amp;amp; Kashmir to Haryana and are living in various parts of the State. The financial assistance is subject to a maximum of ₹6,250/- per family.

**Who is this for:** Family, Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Financial assistance

**Where it applies:** Haryana; State

**Benefits / Assistance:** Financial Assistance of ₹1,250/- per month per family member for 5 years, subject to a maximum of ₹6,250/- per family per month, provided for re-settlement.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Exclusions / Not eligible:** Any sub-division of the family after displacement from Jammu &amp; Kashmir will not be recognized for additional financial assistance.

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: Department Of Social Justice And Empowerment, Government Of Haryana; Nodal Department: Social Justice and Empowerment Department.
