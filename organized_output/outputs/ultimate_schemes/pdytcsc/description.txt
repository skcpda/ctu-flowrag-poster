# Personality Development and Yogasan Training Camp for Schedule Caste

**Short Title:** PDYTCSC

**Tags:** Youth Development, Yoga Training, Personality Development, Physical Fitness

The scheme &quot;Personality Development and Yogasan Training Camp for Schedule Caste Youth&quot; by the Sports, Youth and Cultural Activities Department, Government of Gujarat, aims to foster the overall development—physical, mental, and spiritual—of youth 14–35 years)belonging to the Scheduled Castes through structured yogasana and personality development programs. Each camp runs for 7 days and selects 50 beneficiaries per district from across Gujarat. The initiative serves to empower socially disadvantaged youth and enhance their self-esteem, discipline, and holistic well-being. The scheme is implemented by the District Youth Development Officers across all districts of Gujarat. 

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Sports & Culture; Sector: Culture

**Where it applies:** Gujarat; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - **Training:** The selected Scheduled Caste youth will receive 7 days of training focused on personality development and yogasana.   

**Benefit Type:** In Kind

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Sports, Youth and Cultural Activities Department.
