# Private Tuition Assistance to Scheduled Caste Students Studying in Standard 11th & 12th (General Stream)

**Short Title:** PTASCSSS1112(GS)

**Tags:** SC, Coaching, Student, Private Tuition, Financial Assistance, General Stream

The “Private Tuition Assistance to Scheduled Caste Students Studying in Standard 11th &amp;amp; 12th (General Stream)” is implemented by the Director, Scheduled Caste Welfare, Government of Gujarat. Under the scheme, financial assistance is provided to Scheduled Caste (SC) students who are studying in Standard 11th and 12th (General Stream) and have obtained 75% or more marks in the 10th Standard for private coaching fees, as per merit prepared at state level up to 100 students.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Education & Learning; Sector: Coaching

**Where it applies:** Gujarat; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** > **Assistance Details:** • Standard 11th: ₹8,000/- per annum as a private tuition fees • Standard 12th: ₹4,000/- per annum as a private tuition fees

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: Director, Scheduled Caste Welfare, Gandhinagar; Nodal Department: Social Justice and Empowerment Department.
