# Mill Gate Price Subsidy Scheme

**Short Title:** MGPSS

**Tags:** Yarn, Subsidy, Silk, Jute, Freight, Handloom

Launched in 2007, the &quot;Mill Gate Price Subsidy Scheme&quot;, implemented by the Commissioner of Cottage and Rural Industries, Industries &amp; Mines Department, Government of Gujarat, aims to provide quality yarn at subsidized rates to handloom weavers, ensuring affordability and improved product quality. The scheme offers freight reimbursement (1–8.5% based on yarn type and region) and a 15% subsidy on yarn purchases from NHDC-recognized depots. Eligible beneficiaries include handloom cooperatives, NGOs, and development centers. The scheme is implemented through District Industries Centers (DICs) and aims to sustain employment for economically weak weavers, especially from Scheduled Castes.

**Who is this for:** Joint Liability Groups (JLGS), Self Help Groups (SHGS), NGO; Category: Business & Entrepreneurship; Sector: Setting up / start-up / entrepreneurship

**Where it applies:** Gujarat; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** **Yarn Purchase Subsidy** 15% subsidy on yarn bought from NHDC-recognized depots.  **Freight Reimbursement** - Non-Silk/Jute Yarn: Plain Area: 1.00%, Hills/Remote: 1.75%, North-East: 3.00% - Silk Yarn: Plain Area: 1.00%, Hills/Remote: 1.25%, North-East: 1.50% - Jute/Jute Blended Yarn: Plain Area: 7.00%, Hills/Remote: 8.00%, North-East: 8.50% _*Additional 2.5% depot operating charges._  **Disbursement Mode** 15% subsidy credited via RTGS/e-payment. Freight reimbursement is processed separately.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Industries and Mines Department.
