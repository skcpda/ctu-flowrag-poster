# Shramik Annapurna Yojana (GBOCWWB)

**Short Title:** SAY(GBOCWWB)

**Tags:** Building Worker, Construction Worker, Labour, Meal

The “Shramik Annapurna Yojana” is implemented by the Gujarat Building and Other Construction Worker’s Welfare Board (GBOCWWB), Labour, Skill Development &amp;amp; Employment Department, Government of Gujarat. Under the scheme, the registered construction worker is providing nutritious meals at concessional rates at the workplace or on Kadianaka. Registered construction workers and their family members can get the benefit.

**Who is this for:** Family, Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Food Security / Public Distribution System

**Where it applies:** Gujarat; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - Under this scheme, healthy and nutritious meals will be provided to the construction workers at a concessional rate of ₹5/- per meal.

**Benefit Type:** In Kind

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: Gujarat Building And Other Construction Worker’s Welfare Board; Nodal Department: Labour and Employment Department.
