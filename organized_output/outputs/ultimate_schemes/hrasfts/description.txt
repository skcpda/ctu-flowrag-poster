# Hostel Rent Assistance Scheme for Tribal Students

**Short Title:** HRASFTS

**Tags:** Hostel, Student, Rent, Undergraduate, Scheduled Tribes, Postgraduate

The scheme &amp;quot;Hostel Rent Assistance Scheme for Tribal Students&amp;quot; was introduced by the Tribal Area Development Department, Government of Rajasthan, aims to encourage tribal students (both boys and girls) of Rajasthan to pursue higher education in undergraduate and postgraduate courses. Under this scheme, hostel rent assistance is provided for a maximum period of five years to Scheduled Tribe students studying in government colleges who reside in rented accommodation for the purpose of regular studies. The scheme is applicable only to students from Scheduled Areas of Rajasthan enrolled in government colleges.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Education & Learning; Sector: Hostel service

**Where it applies:** Rajasthan; State/ UT
