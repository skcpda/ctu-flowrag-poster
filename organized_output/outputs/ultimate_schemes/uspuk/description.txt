# Uttarakhand Startup Policy

**Short Title:** USPUK

**Tags:** Incubation Center, Entrepreneurship, Startups, Women Entrepreneurs, Student Entrepreneurs, Business

The &amp;quot;Uttarakhand Startup Policy&amp;quot; is implemented by the Department of Industries, Government of Uttarakhand, aims to support the growth of startup ecosystem in Uttarakhand. Through this scheme, comprehensive benefits including monthly allowance, one-time seed funding, patent assistance, need-based assistance, and various financial provisions as per the Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises (MSME) Policy are provided to entrepreneurial startups and specifically enhanced support to women, students, Scheduled Caste (SC), Scheduled Tribe (ST), Persons with Disabilities (PWD), Transgender, or grassroots innovation-based startups. **Objectives:** The objective of this policy is to create the culture of innovation and entrepreneurship by establishing a robust startup ecosystem in the state. The growth of the startup ecosystem will be fueled with the establishment of world-class institutional infrastructure, incubation and mentoring network, enabling access to funds, providing market access, public procurement, business friendly and regulatory environment along with promotion of employment generation and wealth creation opportunities across the state. **Goals:** - Stimulate the growth of 1000 startups including technology driven enterprises in next 5 years - Encourage the growth of startup enterprises working on innovative technologies, processes or procedures, eco-friendly technologies, social impact or sustainability having focus on solving complex problems and stimulating the growth of local economy through employment creation and wealth generations - Establish state of the art infrastructure and facilities for innovators, startups, aspiring students entrepreneurs, women entrepreneurs and other ecosystem stakeholders across the state - Establish 30 new incubation centers across the state with at least 1 incubation center in each district - Establish a mechanism to enable access to funds, key infrastructure and other key resources that are important for the growth of startup enterprises in the state. - Enable collaborations between industry and academia to promote entrepreneurship, innovations and its commercialization and the growth of technology enterprises - Develop an environment conducive to the creation, growth or transformation of startups enterprises in to a highly successful enterprise. **Validity of the Policy:** This Policy is valid for a period of 5 years from the date of its notification or until new Policy is formulated.

**Who is this for:** Business Entity; Category: Business & Entrepreneurship; Sector: Setting up / start-up / entrepreneurship

**Where it applies:** Uttarakhand; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** **1. Monthly Allowance:** - ₹15,000/- per month to entrepreneurial startups for one year. - ₹20,000/- per month to women entrepreneurs, person from Scheduled Caste, Scheduled Tribes, Differently Abled, Transgender, startups which is working towards grassroot innovations or innovations with rural impact or in any startup working on disruptive technologies, for one year. **Note:** The monthly allowances shall be a one-time assistance given to startups at the ideation stage for a maximum period of twelve (12) months. **2. One-Time Seed Funding:** - Up to ₹10,00,000/- to recognized startup, which is at the ideation stage or prototyping or MVP stage. - Up to ₹12,50,000/- for women entrepreneurs, scheduled caste, scheduled tribes, differently abled, transgender or a startups that is working towards grassroot innovations or innovations with rural impact, - Up to ₹12,50,000/- to startups working on disruptive technologies. **3. Patent and International Patent Assistance:** - Reimbursement assistance of ₹1,00,000/- per Indian patent. - Reimbursement assistance of up to ₹5,00,000/- for international patents. - Reimbursement assistance of ₹10,000/- (per trademark) for filing trademark application. **Note:** Reimbursement of expenses shall be made in two stages - 75 % after filing of patent application and - 25 % at the time of grant of patent. **4. Financial Provisions from Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises (MSME) Policy:** - Special Capital Subsidy. - Interest Subsidy. - Stamp Duty Exemption. - State Goods and Services Tax (SGST) Reimbursement Assistance. - Pre-Incubation and Incubation Support: - One-time free assistance for pre-incubation support. - One-time free assistance for incubation support. - Capital subsidy up to ₹1,00,00,000/- for setting up new incubation centres. - Capital subsidy up to ₹50,00,000/- for expansion of existing incubation centres. - Startups recognised by the Startup Council and operated by Scheduled Caste (SC), Scheduled Tribes (ST), Women, or Physically Challenged individuals will be provided incubation space at a discount of 25% of the notified rates at recognised Incubators. **Venture Fund Provision:** - Provision of ₹200,00,00,000/- for setting up a venture fund.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Department Of The Industrial Development.
