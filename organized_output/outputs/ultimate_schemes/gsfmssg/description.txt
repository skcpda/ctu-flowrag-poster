# Grant for Marriage of the Daughter of a Scheduled Caste Individual

**Short Title:** GMDSCI

**Tags:** Marriage Grant, Scheduled Caste, BPL, Marriage Of Daughter

The scheme &amp;quot;Grant for Marriage of the Daughter of a Scheduled Caste Individual&amp;quot; is implemented by the Social Welfare Department, Government of Uttarakhand. The scheme aims to provide financial assistance to Scheduled Caste families to help cover the marriage expenses of up to two daughters. 

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment

**Where it applies:** Uttarakhand; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** **Grant Amount:** ₹50,000/- per daughter, for the marriage of up to two daughters. 

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Department Of Social Welfare.
