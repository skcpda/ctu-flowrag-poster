# Scholarship to Divyang Students (Class 1 to 8)

**Short Title:** SDSC18

**Tags:** Scholarship, Student, PwD, Disabled Student

The scheme “Scholarship to Divyang Students (Class 1 to 8)” is implemented by the Department of Social Welfare, Government of Uttarakhand. The scheme aims to provide financial assistance to regularly enrolled disable students from Class 1 to 8. 

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Education & Learning; Sector: Scholarships and student finance

**Where it applies:** Uttarakhand; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** ***Rate of Scholarships:*** - **Class 1 to 5:** ₹600/- - **Class 6 to 8:** ₹960/- 

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Department Of Social Welfare.
