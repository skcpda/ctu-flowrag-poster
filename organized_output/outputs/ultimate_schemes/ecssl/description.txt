# Employees Cooperative Societies Surety Loan

**Short Title:** ECSSL

**Tags:** Surety Loan, Employees Cooperative Societies

The &amp;quot;Employees Cooperative Societies Surety Loan&amp;quot; scheme launched by the Co-operation, Food and Consumer Protection Department, Tamil Nadu, aims to provide a maximum loan amount of ₹1,50,000/- at an interest rate of 14% (subject to change) through surety loans from the Employees Cooperative Societies. The purpose of this scheme is to extend loans to eligible citizens for financial support. &lt;br&gt;

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Banking,Financial Services and Insurance; Sector: Loan

**Where it applies:** Tamil Nadu; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - **Access to Loan:** Surety loans up to ₹1,50,000/-. - **Interest Rate:** 14% (subject to change). &lt;br&gt;

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Co-operation, Food and Consumer Protection Department.

**References / Annexures:** - **Guidelines**: https://www.tn.gov.in/scheme/data_view/3616
