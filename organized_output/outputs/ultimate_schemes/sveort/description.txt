# Swami Vivekananda Exam Oriented Residential Training

**Short Title:** SVEORT

**Tags:** Training, Defence, Army, Navy, Police, Examination, Recruitment, Employment

The scheme &quot;Swami Vivekananda Exam Oriented Residential Training&quot; was launched by the Directorate of Employment and Training, Department of Labour and Employment, Government of Gujarat. The scheme aims to provide comprehensive residential training to aspiring candidates for recruitment in Army, Air Force, Navy, Paramilitary Forces and Police Forces, addressing the need for specialized coaching and preparation to enhance success rates in defence and police recruitment examinations. The scheme provides exam-oriented residential training programs and coaching facilities specifically designed for defence services examinations and conducts comprehensive preparation sessions covering written examinations, physical fitness training, personality development, and interview preparation. The applicant must be a resident of Gujarat state and must be aspiring for recruitment in Army, Air Force, Navy, Paramilitary Forces or Police Forces with appropriate age and educational qualifications as per the respective service requirements. The scheme is implemented by the Directorate of Employment and Training under the Labour and Employment Department, Government of Gujarat and applications for this scheme are accepted through designated employment exchanges and training centers across the state. 

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Education & Learning; Sector: Apprenticeships and training

**Where it applies:** Gujarat; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** > **Training and Coaching Support** - **Residential Training:** Comprehensive exam-oriented coaching in residential format.- **Defence Examination Preparation:** Specialized training for Army, Air Force, Navy recruitment examinations.- **Police Force Training:** Preparation for Police Force recruitment examinations.- **Paramilitary Force Coaching:** Training for Central Armed Police Forces and other paramilitary recruitment.  > **Training Components** - **Written Examination Preparation:** Subject-wise coaching and mock tests.- **Physical Fitness Training:** Physical preparation as per service requirements.- **Personality Development:** Communication skills and confidence building.- **Interview Preparation:** Mock interviews and counseling sessions.  > **Conditions** - Regular attendance in all training sessions and activities. - Compliance with residential training center rules and regulations. - Participation in mock tests and assessment programs. - Maintenance of discipline and conduct as per training center guidelines. 

**Benefit Type:** In Kind
