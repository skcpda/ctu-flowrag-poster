# Indira Gandhi National Disability Pension Scheme (Maharashtra)

**Short Title:** IGNDPSM

**Tags:** Disability, Pension, PwD, Handicapped

The &amp;quot;Indira Gandhi National Disability Pension Scheme (Maharashtra)&amp;quot; is implemented by the Social Justice &amp;amp; Special Assistance Department, Government of Maharashtra. Under this scheme, differently-abled individuals aged 18 to 65 years with a disability of 80% and above are eligible to receive a pension of ₹600/- per month.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Pension

**Where it applies:** Maharashtra; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** A monthly pension of ₹600/-.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Social Justice and Special Assistance Department.

**References / Annexures:** - **Guidelines**: https://sjsa.maharashtra.gov.in/en/scheme-category/special-assistance - **Scheme Details**: https://pune.gov.in/scheme/indira-gandhi-national-disability-pension-scheme/
