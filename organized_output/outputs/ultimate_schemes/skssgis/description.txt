# Shahidvir Kinariwaala Student Security Group Insurance Scheme

**Short Title:** SKSSGIS

**Tags:** Student, Insurance, Education, Death, Disability

The &quot;Shahidvir Kinariwaala Student Security Group Insurance Scheme&quot; was launched by the Education Department, Government of Gujarat. The scheme provides financial protection in case of accidental death or permanent disability to students or their guardians/heirs to help reduce the burden during unforeseen tragedies. The scheme covers students at universities, Government and Non-Government colleges, Gram Vidyapeeth GBTC colleges, and technical education institutes across the state. 

**Who is this for:** Individual, Family; Category: Banking,Financial Services and Insurance; Sector: Insurance

**Where it applies:** Gujarat; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - Maximum financial assistance of ₹1,00,000/- is provided in case of accidental death or permanent disability. - Coverage includes accidental death, permanent total disability, loss of two limbs or two eyes, or one limb and one eye (100% coverage). - Partial disability such as loss of one limb or one eye may also be covered at 50%. 

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Education Department.
