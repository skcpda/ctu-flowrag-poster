# Scheme for Financial Assistance in Case of Death

**Short Title:** SFACD

**Launch Date:** 2022-10-20

**Tags:** Financial Assistance, Construction Worker, Deceased

The &quot;Scheme for Financial Assistance in Case of Death&quot; scheme was launched by the Tripura Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board, Labour Department Tripura. The scheme aims to provide financial assistance to the nominee or spouse or dependent of the deceased registered beneficiary. The registered beneficiary will apply in the prescribed form through electronically. 

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Financial assistance

**Where it applies:** Tripura; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - One time grant in case of natural death : ₹2,00,000/- - One time grant, if the death is due to accident during the course of employment: ₹4,00,000/-

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Exclusions / Not eligible:** - If any registered beneficiary is covered under the Group Life Insurance scheme and is taken by the Board, the death benefit as envisaged in this scheme will not apply.

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Labour Department .
