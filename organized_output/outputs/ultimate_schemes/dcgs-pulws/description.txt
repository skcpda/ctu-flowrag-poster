# Deepawali Cash Gift Scheme (PULWS)

**Short Title:** DCGS-PULWS

**Tags:** Gift, Festival, Financial Assistance, Worker

The &quot;Deepawali Cash Gift Scheme (PULWS)&quot; is a Welfare Scheme for the Unorganized Workers in Puducherry by the &quot;Puducherry Unorganised Labourers&#39; Welfare Society&quot;, Labour Department, Union Territory of Puducherry. The Society extends ₹500/- to the Unorganised Workers as a Cash Gift during the festival of Diwali. The amount is credited through Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) into the bank account of the worker. The applications are accepted offline.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Financial assistance

**Where it applies:** Puducherry; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - Financial Assistance of ₹500/- as a Cash Gift during the festival of Diwali. - The amount is credited through Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) into the bank account of the nominee.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Labour Department, Puducherry.
