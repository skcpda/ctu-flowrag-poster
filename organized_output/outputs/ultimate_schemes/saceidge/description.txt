# Scheme of Assistance for Common Environment Infrastructure: Scheme for Development of Green Estate

**Short Title:** SACEIDGE

**Launch Date:** 2020-08-07

**Closing Date:** 2025-08-06

**Tags:** Entrepreneurship, Environment Infrastructure, Development Of Green Estate

The &amp;quot;Scheme of Assistance for Common Environment Infrastructure&amp;quot; is an umbrella scheme launched by the Industries and Mines Department, Gujarat under the Gujarat Industrial Policy. Effective from August 7th, 2020 to August 6th, 2025, it aims to boost industrial growth through financial assistance, modern technologies, and enhanced facilities for industrial parks and clusters. The component “Scheme for Development of Green Estate” aims to support the relocation or retrofitting of polluting industrial units to designated green estates. The initiative focuses on reducing environmental pollution by providing necessary infrastructure support, ensuring compliance with environmental norms, and promoting sustainable industrial growth.

**Who is this for:** Business Entity, Industries; Category: Business & Entrepreneurship; Sector: Setting up / start-up / entrepreneurship

**Where it applies:** Gujarat; State/ UT
