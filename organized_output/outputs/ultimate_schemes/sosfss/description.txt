# Scheme of Scholarship for Sainik Schools

**Short Title:** SOSFSS

**Tags:** Scholarship, Sainik, School, Student, Education

&quot;Scheme Of Scholarship For Sainik Schools&quot; was launched by the Directorate Of Higher and Technical Education, Meghalaya, to prepare young boys for a career in the Cadre of the Armed Forces. The scholarships are awarded to students whose parents are permanent residents of Meghalaya and whose family income is less than ₹8,000/- per month.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Education & Learning; Sector: Scholarships and student finance

**Where it applies:** Meghalaya; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - Scholarship of ₹20,000/-, awarded to the students whose family income is below ₹5,000/- per month. - Scholarship of ₹10,000/-, awarded to the students whose family income is between ₹5,000/- to ₹8,000/- per month. 

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Education Department.
