# Promotion of Organic Farming under State Organic Policy

**Short Title:** POFUSOP

**Tags:** Organic Farming, Certification Assistance, Organic Input Subsidy, Farmer

The scheme &amp;quot;Promotion of Organic Farming under State Organic Policy&amp;quot; by the Agriculture and Co-operation Department, Government of Gujarat aims to promote sustainable agricultural practices by encouraging farmers to adopt organic farming methods. The scheme provides financial support for the registration and certification processes conducted by the Gujarat Organic Products Certification Agency (GOPCA) and Agricultural and Processed Food Products Export Development Authority (APEDA)-approved agencies. It also offers input cost assistance per hectare, enabling farmers to reduce chemical usage and enhance soil health. 

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Agriculture,Rural & Environment; Sector: Environmental management and protection

**Where it applies:** Gujarat; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - **Organic Farming Registration and Certification Assistance (GOPCA):** The scheme provides 75% at-source assistance in cost to the farmer for registration and certification when undertaken through the Gujarat Organic Products Certification Agency (GOPCA).- **Organic Farming Registration, Certification, and Transportation Assistance (APEDA-approved agencies):** When certification is done through APEDA-approved organic certification agencies, the scheme offers 50% assistance in registration, certification, and transportation fees. The maximum benefit for certification support is limited to ₹2,000/- per hectare.- **Input Assistance for Organic Farming:** Farmers are also eligible for input assistance amounting to ₹5,000/- per hectare to promote organic farming practices. 

**Benefit Type:** Composite

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Agriculture, Farmers Welfare and Cooperation Department.
