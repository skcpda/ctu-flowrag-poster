# Vahan Aksmat Sahay Scheme

**Short Title:** VASS

**Tags:** Vehicle, Accident, Medical, Treatment, Injury, Health, Emergency

Launched in 2020, the scheme &quot;Vahan Aksmat Sahay Scheme&quot; by the Health and Family Welfare Department, Government of Gujarat, aims to provide immediate medical assistance and financial support to victims of road traffic accidents to ensure timely treatment and reduce mortality rates. The scheme provides medical treatment packages ranging from ₹1,000/- to ₹45,000/- for various types of injuries and covers emergency medical procedures, surgeries, and hospitalization for accident victims. The applicant must be a victim of road traffic accident requiring immediate medical treatment and the treatment must be provided at empanelled hospitals under the scheme. The scheme is implemented by Health and Family Welfare Department, Government of Gujarat and applications are accepted through participating hospitals where treatment is provided. 

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Health & Wellness; Sector: Health care providers and access

**Where it applies:** Gujarat; State/ UT
