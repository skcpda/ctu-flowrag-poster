# Leave Travel Concession Scheme- Haryana Labour Welfare Board

**Short Title:** LTCS-HLWB

**Tags:** Leave Travel, Concession, Labour, Worker, LTC

The scheme “Leave Travel Concession (LTC) Scheme” is implemented by the Haryana Labour Welfare Board (HLWB), Labour Department, Government of Haryana. Under this scheme, an amount of ₹1,500/- is provided to the workers employed in the industrial and commercial institutions of the state of Haryana in the form of Leave Travel Concession (LTC) for travel once in a block of 4 years. The first block will be considered for the year 2012-15.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Travel & Tourism; Sector: Tourism in India

**Where it applies:** Haryana; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** 1. Under this scheme, an amount of ₹1,500/- is given to the worker as LTC once in a block of 4 years.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: Haryana Labour Welfare Board; Nodal Department: Labour Department.
