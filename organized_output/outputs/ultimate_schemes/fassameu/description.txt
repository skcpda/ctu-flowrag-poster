# Financial Assistance to SC Students Appearing in the Mains Examination of UPSC

**Short Title:** FASSAMEU

**Launch Date:** 2025-07-02

**Tags:** Scheduled Caste, Student, UPSC, Exam, Financial Assistance

The &#39;Financial Assistance to SC students appearing in the Mains Examination of UPSC&#39; scheme, administered by the Directorate of Welfare of Scheduled Castes and Backward Classes, Government of Assam, is designed to provide financial grants to Scheduled Caste (SC) students who qualify for the Union Public Service Commission (UPSC) Mains Examination. Each eligible beneficiary will receive an amount of up to ₹50,000/-, subject to budgetary provisions. The disbursement of assistance is contingent upon the applicant satisfying the stipulated eligibility criteria and the successful submission of required documentation.  **The applications will be accepted through the myScheme platform from 04/07/2025 to 31/10/2025.** ***A DigiLocker account is required for application. Please refer to the Application Process section for details.*** 

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Education & Learning; Sector: Scholarships and student finance

**Where it applies:** Assam; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** An amount of up to ₹50,000/- is provided to each beneficiary, subject to budget provision.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Directorate of Welfare of Scheduled Castes and Backward Classes.
