# Mukhyamantri Raut, Charavaaha Evan Doodh Duhane Vaale Saayakal Sahaayata Yojana

**Short Title:** MRCDDV

**Launch Date:** 2013-09-10

**Tags:** Financial Assistance, Empowerment, Vehicle

Labor Department of Chhattisgarh State started this scheme &amp;quot;Mukhyamantri Raut, Charavaaha Evan Doodh Duhane Vaale Bicycle Sahaayata Yojana&amp;quot; for unorganized worker on 10 September 2013. The main objective of the scheme to empower unorganized worker by providing financial assistance.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Financial assistance

**Where it applies:** Chhattisgarh; State

**Benefits / Assistance:** > Financial Assistance of ₹ 3,000/- per person for bicycle or the rate fixed by CSIDC for bicycle which will be lower

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Labour Department.
