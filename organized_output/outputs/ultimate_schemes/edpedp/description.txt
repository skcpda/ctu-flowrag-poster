# "Entrepreneureship Development Programme (EDP)" Component of the "Development of Industries" Scheme

**Short Title:** EDPEDP

**Tags:** Entrepreneurship, Entrepreneur, Business, Skill, Training, Vocational

The scheme &quot;Entrepreneureship Development Programme (EDP)&quot; by the District Industries Centre, Department of Industries and Commerce, Union Territory of Puducherry aims to to develop entrepreneurship skills by providing training for setting up of the projects. The programmes contains motivating the prospective entrepreneurs, highlight respective projects, the course of actions to taken by them for setting up and successful running their unit.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Business & Entrepreneurship; Sector: Entrepreneurship development

**Where it applies:** Puducherry; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - The entire expenditure towards the training will be met. - The period of training will generally be for a week.

**Benefit Type:** Composite

**Exclusions / Not eligible:** Already trained candidates are not eligible.

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Industries and Commerce Department, Puducherry.
