# Fish Marketing Scheme

**Short Title:** FMS

**Tags:** Financial Assistance, Four-wheeler, Marketing

The scheme &quot;Fish Marketing Scheme&quot; by the Fisheries Division, Department of Agriculture, Animal Husbandry &amp; Cooperation, Government of Jharkhand, aims to provide financial assistance for fish marketing and transportation. Through this scheme, subsidies are provided to fisheries cooperative societies and their members for purchasing four-wheeler transport vans and setting up aquarium shops. The scheme is implemented by the Directorate of Fisheries, Jharkhand.

**Who is this for:** Registered Societies; Category: Agriculture,Rural & Environment; Sector: Fishing and hunting

**Where it applies:** Jharkhand; State/ UT

**DBT Scheme:** Yes (Direct Benefit Transfer)

**Benefits / Assistance:** - A 60% subsidy or up to ₹3,60,000/- for purchasing four-wheeler transport vans for fisheries cooperative societies. - Up to ₹1,00,000/- subsidy for setting up aquarium shops with necessary equipment.  **NOTE:** Assistance provided through DBT (Direct Benefit Transfer) into the beneficiary&#39;s Escrow Account.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: Directorate Of Fisheries, Jharkhand; Nodal Department: Department of Agriculture, Animal Husbandry & Co-operative.

**References / Annexures:** - **Guidelines**: https://jharkhandfisheries.org/orders/2023/Rajyadesh-%20Matsya%20Vipanan%20Yojana-%20letter%20no.%2011%20RA%20(VI)%20dated%2016.06.2023.pdf
