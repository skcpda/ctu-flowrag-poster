# Scholarship to Undergo Advanced Training in the Field of Art

**Short Title:** SUATFA

**Tags:** Training, Scholarship, Artist, Culture, Music, Dance, Drama

The scheme &quot;Scholarship to Undergo Advanced Training in the Field of Art&quot; is a state-level cultural scheme by the Kala Academy, Department of Art and Culture, Government of Goa. The objective of the scheme is to provide facilities to Goan talented students to undergo advanced training in the field of various disciplines of Art, i.e. Indian Classical Music and Dance, Drama, Western Music, Folk Art, Puppetry, Painting, Sculpture, etc..

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Skills & Employment; Sector: Culture

**Where it applies:** Goa; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - Advanced training in the field of various disciplines of Art, i.e. Indian Classical Music and Dance, Drama, Western Music, Folk Art, Puppetry, Painting, Sculpture, etc. - Scholarship of ₹3,000/- per month is given, initially for two years and can be extended for another three years.

**Benefit Type:** Composite

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Art, Culture and Museums Department.
