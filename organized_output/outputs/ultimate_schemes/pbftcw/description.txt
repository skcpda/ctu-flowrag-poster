# Pension Benefit for the Construction Worker

**Short Title:** PBFTCW

**Tags:** Labour, Pension Benefit, Construction Workers, Financial Assistance

The &amp;quot;Pension Benefit for the Construction Workers&amp;quot; by the Sikkim Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board, Labour Department, is a welfare scheme for the registered workers of the board. Under this scheme, pension to the registered beneficiaries of the Board who have completed the age of 60 years.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Pension

**Where it applies:** Sikkim; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - ₹10,000/- is provided for Pension Benefit.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Labour Department.
