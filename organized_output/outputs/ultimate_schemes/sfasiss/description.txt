# Scheme for assistance for Start Ups/ Innovation: Seed Support

**Short Title:** SFASISS

**Launch Date:** 2020-08-07

**Tags:** Start Up, Innovation, Entrepreneurship, Seed Support

The &amp;quot;Scheme for Assistance for Start Ups/ Innovation&amp;quot; is an umbrella scheme launched by the Industries and Mines Department, Gujarat under the Gujarat Industrial Policy. Effective from August 7th, 2020 to August 6th, 2025, it aims to drive economic growth by generating jobs, fostering competition, and introducing next-gen solutions. The component &amp;quot;Sustenance Allowance&amp;quot;, offers financial assistance to startups for developing innovative projects, covering costs related to prototype development, market research, testing, trials, and marketing/publicity assistance for attending national/international events. &lt;br&gt; &lt;br&gt;

**Who is this for:** Business Entity, Industries; Category: Business & Entrepreneurship; Sector: Setting up / start-up / entrepreneurship

**Where it applies:** Gujarat; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** &gt; **Total Assistance: ₹30,00,000/-.** - **Prototype Development:** Covers costs for raw materials, consumables, hardware, software, professional services, equipment, or tools. - **Market Research:** Financial aid for conducting market research to ensure product viability. - **Testing &amp;amp; Trials:** Assistance for product testing and trial phases. - **Marketing/Publicity Assistance:** Support for attending national/international events (fees/charges only; travel and accommodation expenses excluded).

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Exclusions / Not eligible:** - Entities formed by splitting or restructuring an existing business are not considered startups under this scheme. - Startups availing benefits under other startup schemes of the Government of Gujarat are ineligible.

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Industries and Mines Department.
