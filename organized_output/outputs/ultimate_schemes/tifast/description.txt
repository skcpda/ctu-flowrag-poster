# Trekking in Forest Area (Scheduled Tribes)

**Short Title:** TIFAST

**Tags:** Trekking, Forest Awareness, Wildlife Exposure, Eco Tourism, Scheduled Tribe

The scheme &amp;quot;Trekking in Forest Area (Scheduled Tribes)&amp;quot; by the Sports, Youth and Cultural Activities Department, Government of Gujarat, aims to instill a deep love for nature, pristine landscapes, wild animals and birds, trees, mountains, springs, and ravines among the Scheduled Tribes youth of the state by directly engaging them in a forest trekking experience. Under this initiative, a forest area rotation program is conducted for 100 selected young men and women, giving them immersive exposure to natural surroundings and biodiversity. 

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Sports & Culture; Sector: Sports

**Where it applies:** Gujarat; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - **Nature Immersion Experience:** The selected beneficiaries will have the opportunity to participate in a guided forest trekking program designed to foster a love for nature and environmental awareness. - **Exposure to Biodiversity:** Participants will get firsthand experience of pristine natural sites, including forests, wildlife, birds, trees, springs, mountains, and ravines. 

**Benefit Type:** In Kind

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Sports, Youth and Cultural Activities Department.
