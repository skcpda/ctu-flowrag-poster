# Mini Sprinkler Sanyantr

**Short Title:** MSS

**Tags:** Farmer, Mini Sprinkler Sanyantr, Farming, Agriculture

The Mini Sprinkler Sanyantr scheme Under the Agriculture Department Rajasthan government will provide 70% to 75% subsidy to farmers for installing sprinkler plants. Through this farmers can irrigate more land by getting sprinkler subsidy. The subsidy given by the state government to the farmers will be sent directly to their bank accounts through DBT. The income of farmers will improve through this scheme. Apart from this, efficient utilization and production will also increase.  > **The important points** 1. After the administrative approval of the Horticulture Department is issued, the plants should be ordered and installed as per the departmental norms. 1. If found correct during physical verification, the grant amount is transferred to the account of the farmer or the supplier manufacturer/dealer with the consent of the farmer.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Agriculture,Rural & Environment; Sector: Financial assistance

**Where it applies:** Rajasthan; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** 1. General farmers: Up to 70% of the grant 1. Small and marginal farmers, including small marginal/SC/ST/women farmers: Up to 75% of the grant

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Agriculture Department.
