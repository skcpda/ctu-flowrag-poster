# Direct Loan Scheme Karnataka

**Short Title:** DLSK

**Tags:** Financial Assistance, Subsidy, Unemployed, Scheduled Caste

The &quot;Direct Loan Scheme&quot; was implemented by Karnataka Leather Industries Development Corporation Ltd, Department of Social Welfare, Govt of Karnataka. The scheme aims to provide financial assistance to unemployed scheduled caste individuals from the leather artisan community to start small businesses and empower artisans and improve their livelihoods by providing access to capital to start or expand small-scale enterprises.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Business & Entrepreneurship; Sector: Setting up / start-up / entrepreneurship

**Where it applies:** Karnataka; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - Financial assistance with a 50% subsidy and 50% loan. - Total unit cost is ₹1,00,000/-. - Loan repayment in 24 equal installments with a 4% interest rate.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: Karnataka Leather Industries Development Corporation Ltd; Nodal Department: Social Welfare Department.
