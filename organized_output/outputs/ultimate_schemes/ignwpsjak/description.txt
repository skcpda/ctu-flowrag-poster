# Indira Gandhi National Widow Pension Scheme (Jammu and Kashmir)

**Short Title:** IGNWPSJAK

**Launch Date:** 1995-08-15

**Tags:** Below Poverty Line, Widow, Pension, Social Welfare

Launched on 15th August 1995, the &quot;Indira Gandhi National Widow Pension Scheme&quot; is a welfare scheme by the Social Welfare Department, Union Territory of Jammu and Kashmir. The scheme provides ₹1,000/- per month to widows aged 40 and above from Below Poverty Line (BPL) families. The pension is funded 70% by the Centre and 30% by the Union Territory.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Pension

**Where it applies:** Jammu and Kashmir; State/ UT

**DBT Scheme:** Yes (Direct Benefit Transfer)

**Benefits / Assistance:** The widow will receive a monthly pension of ₹1,000/-.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Department Of Social Welfare J and K.
