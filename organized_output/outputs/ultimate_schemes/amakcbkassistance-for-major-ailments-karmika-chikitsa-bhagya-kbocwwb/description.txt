# Assistance For Major Ailments (Karmika Chikitsa Bhagya) (K.B.O.C.W.W.B)

**Short Title:** AFMAKCBKBOCWWB

**Tags:** Labour, Building Worker, Construction Worker, Ailments, Medical Assistance

The &amp;quot;Assistance For Major Ailments&amp;#39;&amp;#39; by the BOCW Welfare Board, Department of Labour, Karnataka, is a welfare scheme for the registered workers of the board. Under this Scheme, the Board provides financial assistance to registered construction workers and their dependents for medical expenses related to specified major ailments. The scheme ensures that eligible workers receive necessary medical treatment without facing financial hardship. &lt;br&gt;

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Health & Wellness; Sector: Financial assistance

**Where it applies:** Karnataka; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - **Financial Assistance:** ₹2,00,000/- for major ailment treatments.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Labour Department.
