# Swayam Saksham Scheme (Gujarat Gopalak Vikas Nigam)

**Short Title:** SSGGVN

**Tags:** Vocational Training, Education, Loan, Rabari, Bharwad

The scheme &quot;Swayam Saksham Scheme&quot; by the Social Justice &amp; Empowerment Department, Government of Gujarat, aims to instill a sense of self-reliance among the youth pursuing vocational education/training. For Doctor (Clinic), Physiotherapy Clinic, Biomedical Laboratory, Lawyer Office, Engineering Unit, Chemist Shop, Architect Office, Chartered Accountant, Financial Consulting Level Office, etc. The scheme is implemented by the Gujarat Gopalak Vikas Nigam. 

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Education & Learning

**Where it applies:** Gujarat; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** 1. The scheme provides 95% of the loan amount to eligible youth, up to a maximum limit of ₹5,00,000/-. 1. The loan carries an interest rate of 6% per annum.  > **Note:** Beneficiaries are required to contribute 5% of the project cost. 

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: Gujarat Gopalak Vikas Nigam; Nodal Department: Social Justice and Empowerment Department.

**References / Annexures:** - **Official Guidelines**: https://sje.gujarat.gov.in/ggvn/Schemes/1567 - **Online Application**: https://ggdconline.gujarat.gov.in/
