# Retirement Relief in Quitting Service for the Construction Workers

**Short Title:** RRIQSFTCW

**Tags:** Labour, Building Worker, Construction Worker, Retirement

The &amp;quot;Retirement Relief in Quitting Service for the Construction Workers&amp;quot; by the Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board, Labour Department, U.T. Administration of Dadra and Nagar Haveli and Daman and Diu, is a welfare scheme for the registered workers of the board. Under this scheme, the Board will provide a certain amount to the eligible registered worker who is a member of the Board for a continuous period of 15 years, on quitting service after attaining age of 60 years. &lt;br&gt;

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Provident fund

**Where it applies:** Dadra & Nagar Haveli and Daman & Diu; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** **Quantum of Assistance:** A certain amount would be paid to the Construction Workers/ Beneficiary, who is the member of the Board for a continuous period of 15 years. &lt;br&gt;

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Labour & Employment Department.
