# Scheme to Provide Monthly Financial Assistance to Elderly and Eligible Writers

**Short Title:** SPMFAEEW

**Tags:** Elderly Writer, Literary Welfare, Assistance For Author

The &quot;Scheme to Provide Monthly Financial Assistance to Elderly and Eligible Writers&quot; is a welfare initiative implemented by the Gujarat Sahitya Academy to provide financial security to elderly writers who have significantly contributed to Gujarati, Hindi, Sanskrit, Sindhi, Urdu, or Kutchi literature. The scheme offers monthly assistance to writers who are 60 years of age or older, have no steady pension or major income source, and are economically disadvantaged.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Sports & Culture; Sector: Culture

**Where it applies:** Gujarat; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - **Monthly Financial Support:** Eligible elderly writers receive monthly financial assistance as specified by the authority.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: Gujarat Sahitya Academy; Nodal Department: Sports, Youth and Cultural Activities Department.
