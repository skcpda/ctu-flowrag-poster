# Pension Benefit (GBOCWWB)

**Short Title:** PB-GBOCWWB

**Tags:** Pension, Labour, Financial Assistance, Worker, Construction

&quot;Pension Benefit (GBOCWWB)&quot; is a Welfare Scheme by the Goa Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board of the Department of Labour and Employment, Goa. Through this scheme, a member of the Fund who has been working as a building worker for not less than one year, be eligible for a pension of ₹300/- per month, on completion of sixty years of age. The applications are accepted offline.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Pension

**Where it applies:** Goa; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** Pension of ₹300/- per month, with an increase of ₹10/- for every completed year of service beyond 5 years. _*The pension will be payable from the first day of the succeeding month to the month in which he/she becomes 60 years old._

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: Goa Building And Other Construction Workers Welfare Board; Nodal Department: Labour and Employment Department.
