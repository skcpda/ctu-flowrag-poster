# Private Tuition Assistance to Socially and Educationally Backward Class Students Studying in Science Stream of Class 11 & 12

**Short Title:** PTASEBCSSSS

**Tags:** Financial Assistance, Student, Science, Higher Education

The &quot;Private Tuition Assistance to Socially and Educationally Backward Class Students Studying in Science Stream of Class 11 &amp; 12&quot; scheme was launched by the Social Justice and Empowerment Department, Government of Gujarat. It aims to provide financial assistance for private tuition to socially and educationally backward class (SEBC) students pursuing science education in higher secondary classes. Under this scheme, a total of ₹15,000 is granted to each class of 11th and 12th to cover private tuition fees. 

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Education & Learning; Sector: Scholarships and student finance

**Where it applies:** Gujarat; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - Financial assistance of ₹15,000 for Class 11th- Financial assistance of ₹15,000 for Class 12th 

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Social Justice and Empowerment Department.
