# PMMSY: Construction of New ponds for Saline /Alkaline areas-Haryana

**Short Title:** CNPSAAHR

**Tags:** Construction, Ponds, Fish Crop, Lease

The scheme &quot;Pradhan Mantri Matasya Sampada Yojana: Construction of New Ponds for Saline/Alkaline Areas&quot; by the Department of Fisheries, Government of Haryana, aims to expand fisheries in various urban and semi-urban areas. Through this scheme, subsidies for the construction of new ponds and central financial assistance for the initial fish crop on newly constructed ponds are provided to beneficiaries. The scheme considers ponds with a long duration and a registered lease period of at least 7 years for grants.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Agriculture,Rural & Environment; Sector: Land and water resources

**Where it applies:** Haryana; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - The project cost is ₹8,00,000/- per hectare. - The subsidy is limited to 40% of the project cost for the General Category and 60% of the project cost for Scheduled Caste (SC) and women beneficiaries. 

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Animal Husbandry & Dairying Department.
