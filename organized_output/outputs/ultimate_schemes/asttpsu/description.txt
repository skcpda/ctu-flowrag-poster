# AICTE-SHORT TERM TRAINING PROGRAMME (STTP) (UT-J&K,LADAKH AND NER)

**Short Title:** ASTTPSU

**Tags:** Financial Assistance, Technical Education, Faculty Training

Short Term Training Program (STTP) in North-Eastern Region (NER) and UTs of J&amp;amp;K and Ladakh intends to conduct faculty trainings through financial assistance from AICTE to enable faculty members in the field of technical education introspect learn techniques that can help prepare students for active and successful participants in a knowledge society. &lt;br&gt; **Duration of Programme:** It is a residential programme of six days. The approved STTP shall be conducted within six months from the date of release of funds.

**Who is this for:** University / Institution, Individual; Category: Education & Learning; Sector: Apprenticeships and training

**Where it applies:** Central

**Benefits / Assistance:** - **Funding to Institutions:** ₹5,00,000/- to the host University/Institution for conducting the training for the academic year. - **Disbursement of the Funds:** 1. The full amount of the grant sanctioned shall be released as advance to the University/Institution. 1. The amount spent by the institute on the conduct of STTP shall be adjusted on the basis of utilization certificate and detailed expenditure statement submitted by the University/Institution on the prescribed format along with other mandatory documents viz feedback form, copy of proceedings and completion report etc. 1. The above said amount of grant shall be released on the basis of the Extension of Approval (EOA) issued by AICTE to the institute for the academic year.  - **Training to Teachers/Staffs:** Faculty training in the field of technical education with following type: 1. Pedagogical Training. 1. Subject related Training to teachers employed in disciplines: Engineering &amp; Technology, Management, Pharmacy, Architecture etc. 1. Training related to Moral Values, Ethics, Behavioural Sciences &amp; Attitude etc. 1. Training for teachers regarding orientation of newly admitted students. 1. Training for supporting technical and administrative staff including Librarian.

**Benefit Type:** Composite

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Department Of Higher Education.
