# Procedure for Making "Orphan Certificate" for Getting Horizontal Reservation for Orphan Children

**Short Title:** PMOCHROC

**Tags:** Orphan Child, Orphan Certificate, Horizontal Reservation

“Procedure for Making &amp;quot;Orphan Certificate&amp;quot; for Getting Horizontal Reservation for Orphan Children” is implemented by the Women Welfare Department, Government of Uttarakhand. this certificate provides 5% horizontal reservation in Government/Non-Government services for affected children, including those whose both biological or adoptive parents have died within 21 years from the child’s birth. also applicable to those orphan children residing in voluntary or government-run homes within the state. 

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Women and Child; Sector: Childcare

**Where it applies:** Uttarakhand; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** Through this certificate, a 5% horizontal reservation is provided in Government/Non-Government services to eligible beneficiaries. 

**Benefit Type:** In Kind

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Department Of Woman Empowerment & Child Development.
