# Trekking in Coastal Area for Scheduled Caste

**Short Title:** TCASC

**Tags:** Coastal Trekking, Ocean Industry

The scheme &quot;Trekking in Coastal Area for Scheduled Caste&quot; by the Sports, Youth and Cultural Activities Department, Government of Gujarat, aims to offer Scheduled Caste youth a direct introduction to the culture of Sagarkantha, increase awareness about coastal industries and marine wealth, and motivate them to engage in ocean-related developmental activities. 

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Sports & Culture; Sector: Sports

**Where it applies:** Gujarat; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - **Cultural and Industrial Exposure:** The program provides 100 Scheduled Caste youth annually with a 10-day trekking tour to explore the culture of Sagarkantha, learn about coastal wealth, and understand local industries. 

**Benefit Type:** In Kind

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Sports, Youth and Cultural Activities Department.

**References / Annexures:** - **Guidelines (Scheme No. 662)**: https://mariyojana.gujarat.gov.in/Schemeatoz.aspx
