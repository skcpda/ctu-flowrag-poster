# Total Health Check-up Scheme (GBOCWWB)

**Short Title:** THCS(GBOCWWB)

**Tags:** Health Check-up, Building Worker, Construction Worker, Labour, Illnesses

The “Total Health Check-up Scheme” is implemented by the Gujarat Building and Other Construction Worker’s Welfare Board (GBOCWWB), Labour, Skill Development &amp;amp; Employment Department, Government of Gujarat. This scheme facilitates a complete physical examination of the construction workers, which can diagnose potential illnesses and early treatment can prevent major illnesses. Hence, major illness can be avoided and the construction worker can lead a healthy life.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Health & Wellness; Sector: Disease and conditions

**Where it applies:** Gujarat; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** 1. Under the scheme, preventive health check-ups for workers, with a view to initiate proper treatment/health restoration measures. 1. This health check-up will be recommended regularly to every individual for diagnosis of any disease at its initial stage. 1. This health check-up includes routine doctors, pathology, radiology and cardiology check-ups. In which a limit of ₹1950/- per beneficiary will be paid to the empanelled hospital/institution. &gt; **Terms and Conditions:** 1. The benefits of this scheme will be given to the state employees. In which per beneficiary has to spend within the limit of ₹1950/-. 1. Ayushman Bharat Yojana empanelled hospitals from which workers can go for check-ups and treatment in district-wise hospitals decided by the Government/Board through MOU. &gt; **Where to Benefit from:** - Actions are taken by empanelled institutions through camps in Kadianaka/construction sites/colonies.

**Benefit Type:** In Kind

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: Gujarat Building And Other Construction Worker’s Welfare Board; Nodal Department: Labour and Employment Department.
