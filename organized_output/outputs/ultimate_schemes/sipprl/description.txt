# Summer Internship Programme (PRL)

**Short Title:** SIPPRL

**Tags:** Internship, Students, Teachers, Research, Graduation, Post-Graduation

The Physical Research Laboratory (PRL) Summer Internship Programme was launched by the Department of Space, Government of India. The scheme aims to provide a platform for highly motivated students and teachers to interact with PRL’s scientists, gaining exposure to the lab’s research areas and facilities. Participants will work on projects in fundamental science, technology, and their societal applications. The program offers hands-on experience in real research projects, helping young talents shape their career paths.  **Duration of Internship:** 45 days.  **Training Mode:** Offline/Online. 

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Education & Learning; Sector: Education and training grants, fellowship, stipend

**Where it applies:** Central

**Benefits / Assistance:** - **Certification:** On successful completion of the program, participants will receive a certificate endorsed by PRL.- **Accommodation:** Limited paid accommodation is available at ₹100/- per day for participants attending offline.- **Mentorship:** Participants are mentored by PRL faculty members, offering in-depth learning experiences.- **Exposure to PRL&#39;s World-Class Facilities:** Interns will work in PRL’s advanced laboratories and facilities across four campuses.  > **Note:** - The interns shall not be eligible for any stipend/remuneration/financial assistance. - PRL does not cover medical and transport expenses. 

**Benefit Type:** Composite

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Department of Space.

**References / Annexures:** - **Guidelines**: https://www.prl.res.in/prl-eng/summer_internship
