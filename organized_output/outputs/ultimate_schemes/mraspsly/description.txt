# Mai Ramabai Ambedakar Saat Phera Samuh Lagna Yojana

**Short Title:** MRASPSLY

**Launch Date:** 1998-04-29

**Tags:** Group Marriage, Wedding, Incentive, Financial Assistance, Scheduled Caste

The scheme &amp;quot;Mai Ramabai Ambedakar Saat Phera Samuh Lagna Yojana&amp;quot; was launched by the Director of Scheduled Caste Welfare, Department of Social Justice &amp;amp; Empowerment, Government of Gujarat, in the year 1998. Under the scheme, incentives are provided to couples who participate in group marriages, as well as to the organizing institution. If people adopt the practice of group marriages, they can avoid the unnecessary expenses associated with individual weddings and promote financial growth.

**Who is this for:** Family, Trust, NGO, Registered Societies; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Marriage

**Where it applies:** Gujarat; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** 1. Under the scheme, couples participating in group marriages are given an incentive of ₹12,000/- per couple in the name of the bride. 1. For organizing Group Marriage Functions, the organizer/organizing institution will be paid an incentive of ₹3,000/- per couple up to a maximum of ₹75,000/-.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: Office Of Director Scheduled Caste Welfare, Gujarat; Nodal Department: Social Justice and Empowerment Department.
