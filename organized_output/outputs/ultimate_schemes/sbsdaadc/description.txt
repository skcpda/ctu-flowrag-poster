# Loan for Small Businesses (DAADC)

**Short Title:** LSBDAADC

**Tags:** Loan, Self-employment, Scheduled Caste, Small Business, Antyodaya Family

The &amp;quot;Loan for Small Businesses&amp;quot; scheme is implemented by the Dr. Ambedkar Antyodaya Development Corporation (DAADC) under the Social Justice and Empowerment Department, Government of Gujarat. The scheme aims to promote self-employment among Scheduled Caste (SC) individuals belonging to Antyodaya Anna Yojana (AAY) families by providing financial assistance for starting small businesses or self-employment ventures. Under this scheme, eligible beneficiaries are provided a loan of up to ₹1,25,000 at a concessional interest rate. 

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Banking,Financial Services and Insurance; Sector: Loan

**Where it applies:** Gujarat; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** **Loan Amount:** - Up to ₹1,25,000/- ***Interest Rate***_:_ - 4% per annum ***Loan Tenure:***__ - 60 months (5 years) ***Guarantors Required:***__ - Two guarantors ***State Government Contribution:*** - 100% (no beneficiary share required) ***Approximate Installment Amount:*** - ₹2,302/- per month (maximum) 

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: Dr. Ambedkar Antyodaya Development Corporation; Nodal Department: Social Justice and Empowerment Department.
