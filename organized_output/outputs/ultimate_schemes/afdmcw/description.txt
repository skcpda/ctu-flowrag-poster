# Financial Assistance for Daughter's Marriage (UKBOCWWB)

**Short Title:** FADMUKBOCWWB

**Tags:** Labour, Marriage, Construction Worker, Girl Marriage

The “Financial Assistance for Daughter&amp;#39;s Marriage” scheme is implemented by the Uttarakhand Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board (UKBOCWWB) under the Labour Department, Government of Uttarakhand. The scheme aims to provide financial support for the marriage of registered construction workers and their daughters. 

**Who is this for:** Family; Category: Women and Child; Sector: Marriage

**Where it applies:** Uttarakhand; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - An amount of ₹51,000/- for each marriage is provided. &gt; **Note:** Assistance is provided for the marriage of up to two daughters of the registered worker as well as for the worker’s own marriage. 

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: Uttarakhand Building And Other Construction Workers Welfare Board; Nodal Department: Department Of Labour.
