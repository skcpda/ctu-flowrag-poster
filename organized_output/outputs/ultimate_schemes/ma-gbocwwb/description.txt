# Medical Assistance (GBOCWWB)

**Short Title:** MA-GBOCWWB

**Tags:** Patient, Accident, Disease, Surgery, Medical, Labour

&quot;Medical Assistance (GBOCWWB)&quot; is a Welfare Scheme by the Goa Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board of the Department of Labour and Employment, Government of Goa. Through this scheme, the worker who is hospitalized for 5 or more days due to an accident or any disease is provided with ₹400/- for the first 5 days and ₹40/- for the remaining days, subject to a maximum ₹2,000/-.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Crisis/Disaster/Accident

**Where it applies:** Goa; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - Financial Assistance of ₹400/- for the first 5 days. - Financial Assistance of ₹40/- for remaining days, subject to a maximum of ₹2,000/-.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: Goa Building And Other Construction Workers Welfare Board; Nodal Department: Labour and Employment Department.
