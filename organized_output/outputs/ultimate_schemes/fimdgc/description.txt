# Financial Incentives To Mothers Who Deliver A Girl Child (Mamta)

**Short Title:** FIMDGC

**Tags:** Mother, Pregnant, Birth, Girl Child, Medical Institution, ICDS

The scheme &quot;Financial Incentives to Mothers who Deliver a Girl Child (Mamta)&quot; is a scheme of Financial Assistance by the Directorate of Women And Child Development, Govt. of Goa. The objective of the Mamta Scheme is aimed at improving the Female Child Sex Ratio in the State. The scheme is open to all mothers who deliver a live Girl Child (max. 02 deliveries) in a registered medical institution.  > **Implementing Agency** The scheme shall be implemented through the ICDS i.e. Child Development Project Officer, Mukhya Sevikas, and Anganwadi Worker at Taluka/Block Level and Viliage Level respectively.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Health & Wellness; Sector: Financial assistance

**Where it applies:** Goa; State

**Benefits / Assistance:** 1. ₹ 10,000/- as a one-time financial incentive (at the birth of the child). 1. Those beneficiaries who had applied for the 1st Installment of ₹ 5,000/- (as on the date of this Amended Scheme published in the Official Gazette) shall be eligible for ₹ 10,000/- as per the amended scheme.  **NOTE 1:** Those beneficiaries who were paid the 1st Installment of ₹ 5,000/- under the existing scheme shall be eligible for only the balance amount (2nd instalment) of ₹ 5,000/- (to apply within 45 days from the date of the Amended Scheme published in the official Gazette).  > **Mode of Disbursal** The benefits are directly credited to the declared Bank Account.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Exclusions / Not eligible:** Those beneficiaries who were paid both 1st and 2nd Installments shall not be eligible for any further incentives/benefits under the amended scheme and the case shall be treated as closed.

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: ICDS I.e. Child Development Project Officer, Mukhya Sevikas, And Anganwadi Worker At Taluka/Block Level And Viliage Level Respectively.; Nodal Department: Women and Child Development Department.
