# Death Benefit (ANBOCWWB)

**Short Title:** DB(ANBOCWWB)

**Tags:** Financial Assistance, Building Worker, Construction Worker, Accident, Labour

The scheme “Death Benefit” was started by the Andaman &amp;amp; Nicobar Islands Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board (ANBOCWWB), Department of Labour, Employment &amp;amp; Training, Andaman &amp;amp; Nicobar Administration. Under this scheme, financial assistance shall be provided to the nominees or dependents of workers engaged in Building or Other Construction Works in Andaman &amp;amp; Nicobar Islands in the event of their natural or accidental death.

**Who is this for:** Family; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Financial assistance

**Where it applies:** Andaman and Nicobar Islands; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** 1. **Natural Death:** - ₹1,00,000/- shall be sanctioned to the nominee or dependent of a registered beneficiary. 1. **Accidental Death (During Employment):** - ₹2,00,000/- may be given to the nominee or dependent of the beneficiary. 1. **Accidental Death (Non-registered Construction Workers):** - ₹50,000/- may be provided, subject to a certificate from the concerned Tehsildar confirming the deceased was a construction worker.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: Andaman & Nicobar Islands Building And Other Construction Workers Welfare Board, Port Blair; Nodal Department: Labour, Employment & Training Department.
