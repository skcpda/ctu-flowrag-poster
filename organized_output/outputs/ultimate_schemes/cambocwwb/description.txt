# Cash Award (MBOCWWB)

**Short Title:** CA(MBOCWWB)

**Tags:** Cash, Award, Construction Worker, Building Worker, Student, Labour, SSLC Examination

The scheme “Cash Award” is a social security scheme, implemented by the Meghalaya Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board (MBOCWWB), Labour Department, Government of Meghalaya. Under the scheme, the Board annually awards cash prizes to three male and three female children of registered construction workers in each district who scores the highest marks in the SSLC examination.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Education & Learning

**Where it applies:** Meghalaya; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** 1. Every year, the Board awards a cash prize to three male and three female children of registered construction workers in each district who achieve the highest marks in the SSLC examination. 1. The award amounts are ₹1000/-, ₹750/-, and ₹500/- respectively.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: Meghalaya Building & Other Construction Workers Welfare Board; Nodal Department: Labour and Employment Department.
