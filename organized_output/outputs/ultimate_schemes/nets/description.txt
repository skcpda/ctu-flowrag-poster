# NAMO E-Tablet Scheme

**Short Title:** NETS

**Launch Date:** 2017-07-17

**Tags:** NAMO, E-Tablet, Student, Modern Education, Financial Assistance

**Detailed** Under the Gujarat Namo Tablet Yojana, tablets are provided to students studying in colleges and universities in the state. . The tablets are given to students for free and are preloaded with educational content, including textbooks, e-books, and video lectures, to facilitate their studies. The Gujarat Namo Tablet Yojana is part of the state government&amp;#39;s efforts to promote digital learning and reduce the digital divide among students from different socioeconomic backgrounds. The tablets distributed under the scheme are equipped with 4G connectivity and have a screen size of 7 inches.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Education & Learning

**Where it applies:** Gujarat; State

**Benefits / Assistance:** **Benefits** • Tablets are provided to students studying in colleges and universities in the Gujarat state.

**Benefit Type:** In Kind

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: Directorate Of Technical Education, Government Of Gujarat; Nodal Department: Education Department.
