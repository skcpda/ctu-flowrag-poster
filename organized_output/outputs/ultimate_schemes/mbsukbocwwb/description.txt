# Maternity Financial Assistance (UKBOCWWB)

**Short Title:** MFA(UKBOCWWB)

**Tags:** Maternity, Woman, Construction Worker, Building Worker, Labour, Pregnancy

The “Maternity Financial Assistance” scheme is implemented by the Uttarakhand Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board (UKBOCWWB) under the Labour Department, Government of Uttarakhand. The scheme aims to provide financial support to registered women construction workers during the maternity period. This assistance is admissible only for the birth of up to two children. 

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Financial assistance

**Where it applies:** Uttarakhand; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** ₹6,000/- per child (limited to a maximum of two children). 

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: Uttarakhand Building And Other Construction Workers Welfare Board; Nodal Department: Department Of Labour.
