# Pension to the Persons who lost 100% eye sight

**Short Title:** PTTPWL1ES

**Launch Date:** 2012-04-01

**Tags:** Pension, Blind, Financial Assistance

The scheme “Pension to the Persons Who Lost 100% Eye Sight&quot; was launched by the Directorate of Social Welfare and Social Education, Government of Tripura. The scheme aims to provide a monthly pension of ₹2,000/- to the differently-abled persons who are 10 years and above in age and have 100% blindness.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Pension

**Where it applies:** Tripura; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** Pension of ₹2,000/- per month.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Exclusions / Not eligible:** The Scheme will be terminated automatically in case of the death of the beneficiary. However, the pension for the month in which the beneficiary dies will be paid to his nominee / legal heir.

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Directorate Of Social Welfare & Social Education.
