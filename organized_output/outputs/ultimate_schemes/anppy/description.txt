# Atma Nirbhar Pashu Palan Yojana

**Short Title:** ANPPY

**Tags:** Subsidy, Farmer, SHGs, FPO, Animal Husbandry, Livestock Rearing

The scheme &amp;quot;Atma Nirbhar Pashu Palan Yojna (ANPPY)&amp;quot;, implemented by the Department of Animal Husbandry, Veterinary &amp;amp; Dairy Development, Government of Arunachal Pradesh, aims to enhance rural livelihoods, promote sustainable animal husbandry practices, and contribute to self-reliance in the livestock sector. Under this scheme, financial assistance in the form of credit-linked support is provided to unemployed individuals, Self-Help Groups (SHGs), farmers&amp;#39; clubs, cooperative societies, and Dairy Cooperative Societies (DCS) for livestock rearing and allied activities.

**Who is this for:** Individual, Self Help Groups (SHGS), Registered Societies; Category: Agriculture,Rural & Environment; Sector: Animal husbandry

**Where it applies:** Arunachal Pradesh; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** Under this scheme, financial assistance in the form of credit-linked support is provided to eligible beneficiaries for livestock rearing and allied activities.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Animal husbandry, Veterinary and Dairy Department.
