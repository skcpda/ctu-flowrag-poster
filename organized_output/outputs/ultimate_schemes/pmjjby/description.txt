# Pradhan Mantri Jeevan Jyoti Bima Yojana

**Short Title:** PMJJBY

**Launch Date:** 2015-05-09

**Tags:** LIC, Consent, Jansuraksha, Claim Form

Pradhan Mantri Jeevan Jyoti Bima Yojana (PMJJBY) is an insurance scheme offering life insurance cover for death due to any reason. It is a one-year cover, renewable from year to year. The scheme is offered by banks/post offices and administered through life insurance companies. All individuals who are account holders of participating banks/ post office in the age group of 18 to 50 years are entitled to join.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Banking,Financial Services and Insurance; Sector: Insurance

**Where it applies:** Central

**Benefits / Assistance:** 1. PMJJBY offers one- year term **life cover of ₹ 2.00 Lakh** to all the subscribers in the age group of 18-50 years.1. It covers death due to any reason. 1. **Premium payable is ₹ 436/- per annum** per subscriber, to be auto debited from the subscriber’s bank/post office account.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: Banks; Nodal Department: Department of Financial Service.
