# Calipers and Crutches - Tamil Nadu

**Short Title:** CACTN

**Tags:** Calipers And Crutches, Person With Disability, Social Welfare, Orthopedically

The &#39;Calipers and Crutches by Welfare of Differently Abled Persons&#39; scheme was launched by the Department for the Welfare of Differently Abled Persons, Government of Tamil Nadu. Under this scheme, calipers and crutches are given to the needy and deserving orthopedically differently-abled persons for their easy mobility. The applications are accepted offline in the prescribed form available at the District Differently Abled Welfare Office.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Aids/Appliances

**Where it applies:** Tamil Nadu; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - **Free distribution of calipers and crutches.**

**Benefit Type:** In Kind

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Welfare of Differently Abled Persons Department.
