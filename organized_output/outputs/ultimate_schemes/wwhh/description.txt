# Working Women Hostel

**Short Title:** WWHH

**Tags:** Accommodation, Hostel Facility, Working Women, Women Empowerment

The scheme &amp;quot;Working Women Hostel&amp;quot; by the Women and Child Development Department, Government of Haryana aims to provide secure, affordable, and conveniently located housing to employed women to enable them to become socially and economically independent. The scheme also intends to ensure a supportive environment for working women, particularly those away from their homes. The scheme is implemented by the respective designated implementing organizations managing the hostels. These hostels are meant to serve women who are working professionals and need residential support in cities or towns where they are employed.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Women and Child; Sector: Hostel Service

**Where it applies:** Haryana; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** &gt; **Rental Charges:** - **Single Bedrooms:** Not exceeding 15% of total emoluments/gross salary. - **Double Bedrooms:** Not exceeding 10% of total emoluments/gross salary. - **Dormitories:** Not exceeding 7.5% of total emoluments/gross salary.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Women and Child Development Department.
