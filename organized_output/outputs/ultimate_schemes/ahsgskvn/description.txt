# Pashu Palan Yojana (GSKVN)

**Short Title:** PPYGSKVN

**Tags:** Loan, Animal Husbandry, Sanitation Worker, Self-employment, Scavenger

The scheme &amp;#39;Pashu Palan Yojana&amp;#39; is implemented by the Gujarat Safai Kamdar Vikas Nigam (GSKVN) under the Social Justice and Empowerment Department, Government of Gujarat. The scheme aims to provide loans to sanitation workers and their dependents to promote self-employment through animal husbandry and related activities. 

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Agriculture,Rural & Environment; Sector: Animal husbandry

**Where it applies:** Gujarat; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - Loan up to ₹1,00,000/- is provided at an interest rate of 6% to sanitation workers or their dependents for initiating animal husbandry as a business or occupation. 

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: Gujarat Safai Kamdar Vikas Nigam; Nodal Department: Social Justice and Empowerment Department.
