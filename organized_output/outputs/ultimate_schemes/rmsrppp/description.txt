# Rajya/Mukhyamantri Swarn/Rajat Padak Protsahan Puraskar

**Short Title:** RMSRPPP

**Tags:** Education, Cash Incentive, NCC

&amp;quot;Rajya / Mukhyamantri Swarn / Rajat Padak Protsahan Puraskar&amp;quot; implemented by the Department of Education, Government of Uttarakhand, aims to promote excellence in National Cadet Corps (NCC) training by incentivizing high-performing cadets. Through this scheme, cadets who win gold or silver medals during RDC (Republic Day Camp), TSC (Thal Sainik Camp), VSC (Vayu Sainik Camp), or NSC (Nav Sainik Camp) are awarded cash prizes. The scheme is implemented by NCC Group Headquarters through a structured selection and recommendation process.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Education & Learning; Sector: Scholarships and student finance

**Where it applies:** Uttarakhand; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - **Gold Medal Winners:** A cash award of ₹3,000/- each is given to 6 cadets who achieve gold medals during NCC training and camps.- **Silver Medal Winners:** A cash award of ₹2,000/- each is given to 6 cadets who achieve silver medals during NCC training and camps.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Department Of Education.
