# Satyvadi Raja Harishchandra Marnotar Sahay Yojana

**Short Title:** SRHMSY

**Launch Date:** 2000-11-10

**Tags:** Death, Scheduled Caste, Financial Assistance, Funeral, Family

The &amp;quot;Satyvadi Raja Harishchandra Marnotar Sahay Yojana&amp;quot; scheme was launched by the Director of Scheduled Caste Welfare, Department of Social Justice &amp;amp; Empowerment, Government of Gujarat, in the year 2000. Under the scheme, financial assistance is provided to scheduled caste families at the time of the death of a family member to cover funeral expenses, due to their poor economic conditions.

**Who is this for:** Family; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Crisis/Disaster/Accident

**Where it applies:** Gujarat; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** 1. Under the scheme, scheduled caste families receive financial assistance of ₹5,000/- at the time of a family member&amp;#39;s death to cover funeral expenses.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: Office Of Deputy Director, Scheduled Caste Welfare And Office Of Social Welfare Officer (Panchayat), Gujarat; Nodal Department: Social Justice and Empowerment Department.
