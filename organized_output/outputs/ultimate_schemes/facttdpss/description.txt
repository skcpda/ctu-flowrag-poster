# Financial Assistance to Commercial Tiatr Troupes/Directors/Producers for Stage Setting

**Short Title:** FACTTDPSS

**Tags:** Theatre, Director, Producer, Stage, Financial Assistance

The scheme &quot;Financial Assistance to Commercial Tiatr Troupes/Directors/Producers for Stage Setting&quot; was launched by the Tiatr Academy of Goa. The scheme aims to encourage all round development of the Tiatr in all aspects by providing encouragement to commercial tiatr directors and producers to bring in development in stage setting, addressing the need for improved theatrical infrastructure and production quality. The scheme provides financial assistance to six commercial tiatr troupes/directors/producers annually for stage setting development, with specific conditions including mandatory completion of fifteen performances and display of Tiatr Academy logo on stage sets. 

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Sports & Culture; Sector: Culture

**Where it applies:** Goa; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - Financial assistance as fixed by Tiatr Academy of Goa will be provided to six commercial tiatr troupes/directors/producers annually for stage setting development. - Disbursal is made only upon completion of fifteen performances of the tiatr mentioned in the application. - Payment is processed as a one-time benefit per beneficiary. _*Tiatr Academy of Goa logo must be displayed on the front visible frames of the stage set._ _*The stage set designed under this scheme cannot be given on hire to others._ _*No adverse remarks against any individual, government, or political parties are permitted during the use of the set._ _*Work on the stage set must commence only after receiving written approval from Tiatr Academy of Goa._ 

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Exclusions / Not eligible:** The beneficiary will not be entitled to avail the benefit again under this scheme for the next five years from the date of receiving the financial assistance. 

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Art, Culture and Museums Department.
