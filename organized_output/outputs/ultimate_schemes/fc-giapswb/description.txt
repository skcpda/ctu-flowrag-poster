# “Family Counseling" under Grant-in-Aid to Pondicherry State Social Welfare Board

**Short Title:** FC-GIAPSWB

**Tags:** Counselling, Rehabilitation, Psychological, Family

The sub-scheme “Family Counseling&quot; under the umbrella scheme &quot;Grant-in-Aid to Pondicherry State Social Welfare Board&quot; was launched by the Department of Social Welfare, Government of Puducherry. Through this scheme, the residents of Puducherry are provided with counselling, rehabilitative and referral services.

**Who is this for:** Family; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Rehabilitation

**Where it applies:** Puducherry; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - Counselling, rehabilitative and referral services are provided in the centres for the victims of social problems. - Psychological, Social, Educational and Economic issues of the individuals are also dealt with.

**Benefit Type:** In Kind

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Social Welfare Department, Puducherry.
