# Assistance to Trusts/Institutions for Setting Up Cultural Centers/Auditoriums in the Municipal Area

**Short Title:** ATTISCA

**Tags:** Cultural Infrastructure, Auditorium Construction, Cultural Centre Renovation

The scheme &amp;quot;Assistance to Trusts/Institutions for Setting Up Cultural Centers/Auditoriums in the Municipal Area&amp;quot; by the Sports, Youth and Cultural Activities Department, Government of Gujarat, aims to strengthen cultural development and encourage the preservation and promotion of arts and heritage by supporting the creation and improvement of cultural infrastructure in urban regions. Under this initiative, eligible trusts and institutions are provided financial assistance either for constructing new cultural centres or for renovating existing ones. The scheme is implemented by the Sangeet Natak Academy, Gujarat. 

**Who is this for:** Government Organisation, Registered Societies; Category: Sports & Culture; Sector: Culture

**Where it applies:** Gujarat; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - **Assistance for New Construction:** 50% of the total cost or ₹30,00,000/- whichever is lower. - **Assistance for Renovation:** Up to ₹15,00,000/-. 

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Sports, Youth and Cultural Activities Department.
