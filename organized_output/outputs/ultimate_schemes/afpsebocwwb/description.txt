# Assistance for Purchase of Safety Equipment (O.B.O.C.W.W.B)

**Short Title:** AFPSEBOCWWB

**Tags:** Labour, Building Worker, Construction Worker, Safety Equipment, Helmet, Safety Shoes, Hand Gloves

The “Assistance for Purchase of Safety Equipment” scheme by the Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board, Labour Department, Odisha, is a welfare scheme for the registered workers of the board. Under this scheme, the Board shall make one time reimbursement to each beneficiary who is registered with the Board for purchasing Safety Equipment like Helmet, Safety shoes and Hand-gloves on production of original bill/ voucher. &lt;br&gt;

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Citizen empowerment

**Where it applies:** Odisha; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - **Quantum of Assistance:** ₹1,000/-. 

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Labour and Employees State Insurance Department.
