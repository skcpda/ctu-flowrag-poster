# Indira Gandhi National Widow Pension Scheme- Chhattisgarh

**Short Title:** IGNWPSCHH

**Tags:** Indira Gandhi, Widow, Pension, BPL

The scheme &amp;quot;Indira Gandhi National Widow Pension Scheme&amp;quot; is implemented by the Social Welfare Department, Government of Chhattisgarh. Under this scheme, financial assistance is provided to widows aged 40 to 79 years who are living Below the Poverty Line (BPL) to support them in living a respectful life. This scheme is centrally sponsored.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Pension

**Where it applies:** Chhattisgarh; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - Under this scheme, eligible beneficiaries will receive a monthly pension of ₹500/-, (Central contribution- ₹300/-, State Contribution- ₹200/-).

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: Social Welfare Department, Government Of Chhattisgarh; Nodal Department: Social Welfare Department.
