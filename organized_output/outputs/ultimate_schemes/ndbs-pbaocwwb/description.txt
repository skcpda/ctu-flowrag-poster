# Natural Death Benefit Scheme (PBAOCWWB)

**Short Title:** NDBS-PBAOCWWB

**Tags:** Death, Construction, Worker, Financial Assistance, DBT

The &quot;Natural Death Benefit Scheme&quot; is a Welfare Scheme by the Puducherry Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board, Labour Department, Union Territory of Puducherry. Through this scheme, financial assistance of ₹70,000/- is extended to the nominees/dependents of the deceased Construction Worker, apart from the ₹70,000/- extended through the Life Insurance Corporation (LIC). The amount is credited through Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) into the bank account of the nominee/dependent. The applications are accepted offline.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Crisis/Disaster/Accident

**Where it applies:** Puducherry; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** Financial Assistance: ₹70,000/-. Time of Disbursal: Within 1 month of the application. Mode of Disbursal: Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) into the bank account.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Labour Department, Puducherry.
