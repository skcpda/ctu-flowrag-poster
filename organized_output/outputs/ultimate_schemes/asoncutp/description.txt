# Award of Scholarships to Outstanding NCC Cadets of the UT of Puducherry

**Short Title:** ASONCUTP

**Tags:** Cadet, NCC, Camp, Parade, Scholarship

The &quot;Award of Scholarships to Outstanding NCC Cadets of the UT of Puducherry&quot; by the Directorate of School Education, Union Territory of Puducherry, aims to recognize and support outstanding NCC cadets. The scheme offers scholarships to eligible candidates in various divisions: Senior Division Boys (Up to graduation), Senior Wing Girls, Junior Division Boys (Up to 12th Standard), and Junior Wing Girls.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Education & Learning; Sector: Scholarships and student finance

**Where it applies:** Puducherry; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** > **Rate of Scholarships** - Senior Division Cadets: ₹500 per month. - Junior Division Cadets: ₹250 per month. > **Number of Scholarships** - 34 fresh scholarships are awarded every year.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: School Education Department, Puducherry.
