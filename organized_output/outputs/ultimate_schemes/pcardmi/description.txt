# Primary Cooperative Agriculture and Rural Development Bank: For Minor Irrigation

**Short Title:** PCARDMI

**Tags:** Irrigation, Agriculture, Loan, Farmer, Agriculture And Rural Development Bank

The &amp;quot;Primary Cooperative Agriculture and Rural Development Bank: For Minor Irrigation&amp;quot; scheme launched by the Co-operation, Food and Consumer Protection Department, Tamil Nadu, aims to provide financial assistance to farmers through loans intended for minor irrigation projects. It helps improve water availability for agriculture, enhancing crop productivity and supporting sustainable rural development. &lt;br&gt;

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Banking,Financial Services and Insurance; Sector: Loan

**Where it applies:** Tamil Nadu; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - **Access to Loan:** ₹65,000/- to ₹1,20,000/-. - **Interest Rate:** 11% to 11.75% (subject to change). &lt;br&gt;

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Co-operation, Food and Consumer Protection Department.

**References / Annexures:** - **Guidelines**: https://www.tn.gov.in/scheme/data_view/3549
