# Khel Mahakumbh

**Short Title:** KMK

**Launch Date:** 2010-01-01

**Tags:** Sports, Cash Prize, Sports Talent, Competition

Launched in 2010, the scheme &amp;quot;Khel Mahakumbh&amp;quot; by the Sports, Youth and Cultural Activities Department, Government of Gujarat, aims to foster sportsmanship, encourage mass participation in sporting events, and recognize talent across all age groups through free sports services, structured competitions, and significant monetary rewards. The scheme has witnessed continuous growth in participation since its inception and provides opportunities for outstanding performers to benefit from further sports training and development schemes under the Sports Authority of Gujarat. The scheme is implemented by the Sports Authority of Gujarat.   

**Who is this for:** Sportsperson, Individual; Category: Sports & Culture; Sector: Sports

**Where it applies:** Gujarat; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - **Financial Assistance:** Winners of competitions receive cash prizes worth ₹45,00,00,000/- in total. 

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Sports, Youth and Cultural Activities Department.
