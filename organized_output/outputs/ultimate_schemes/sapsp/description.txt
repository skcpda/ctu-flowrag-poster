# Union Territory of Lakshadweep Pension Scheme for Specially Abled Persons

**Short Title:** UTLPSSAP

**Tags:** Financial Assistance, Pension, PwD, Person With Disabilities, Disabled

​The scheme &amp;quot;Union Territory of Lakshadweep Pension Scheme for Specially Abled Persons&amp;quot; was introduced by the Social Welfare &amp;amp; Tribal Affairs Department, Union Territory of Lakshadweep, aims to provide monthly financial assistance to Persons with Disabilities who have no source of income. The scheme covers persons with disabilities, including those who are deaf, mute, blind, mentally retarded, or orthopedically disabled, with a disability level of 40% or more. The District Panchayat and Village (Dweep) Panchayats (VDPs) of the Union Territory of Lakshadweep implement the scheme to ensure smooth processing and disbursement. The pension amount is directly transferred to the beneficiary’s bank account.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Financial assistance

**Where it applies:** Lakshadweep; State/ UT

**DBT Scheme:** Yes (Direct Benefit Transfer)

**Benefits / Assistance:** Under this scheme, eligible individuals receive a monthly pension of ₹1,500/- (₹18,000/- per annum). - **Mode of Disbursement:** The pension is directly transferred to the beneficiary’s bank account.- **Frequency of Disbursement:** The pension is disbursed on a monthly basis.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Exclusions / Not eligible:** 1. The applicant is not eligible if they receive any other government pension. 1. The applicant is not eligible if they have an alternative source of stable income exceeding the prescribed limit.

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: District Panchayat/VDPs Of The Union Territory Of Lakshadweep; Nodal Department: Social Welfare and Tribal Affairs Department, Lakshadweep.
