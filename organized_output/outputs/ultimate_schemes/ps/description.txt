# Palanhar Scheme

**Short Title:** PS

**Launch Date:** 2004-01-01

**Tags:** Financial Assistance, Orphan Child, Protection, Education

Palanhar Yojana has been started by the state government to benefit the orphan children of the state. Under the Palanhar Yojana, arrangements for the upbringing, education, etc. of orphan children of the state or those whose parents have died will not be institutionalized, but in the family of the closest relative/acquaintance of the boys and girls within the society. Education, food, clothes, and other necessary facilities will be made available by the state in a family environment by making an interested person a foster. &lt;br&gt; This scheme is for different categories of boys/girls with special care and protection from 0 to 18 years of the state by the Government of Rajasthan. Arrangements for the care and upbringing of the boys/girls coming under this are made by a close relative/acquaintance within the family. The caretaker of the boys/girls has been appointed guardian. The government ensures the economic, social, and educational development of boys / girls and provides Monthly basis financial assistance.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Financial assistance

**Where it applies:** Rajasthan; State

**Benefits / Assistance:** 1. Orphan Category : For the age group (0 to 6 years) : Rs. 1500/- per month. For the age group (6 to 18 years) : Rs. 2500/- per month. 1. Other Category: For the age group (0 to 6 years) : Rs. 500/- per month. For the age group (6 to 18 years) : Rs. 1000/- per month. 1. For Books/Stationaries/Dress/Sweater/shoes etc: Rs. 2000/- per year.   

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Social Justice and Empowerment Department.
