# Samagra Samajik Suraksha Vridhavastha Pension Yojana

**Short Title:** SSSVPY

**Launch Date:** 1981-01-01

**Tags:** Old Age, Pension, Financial Assistance, Empower

Samagra Samajik Suraksha Vridhavastha Pension Yojana, under the Department of Social Justice and Empowerment of Persons with Disabilities, the government of Madhya Pradesh. provides Pensions are sanctioned to provide social security to old destitute people. Under this, such destitute elderly people who cannot support themselves are benefited. 

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Pension

**Where it applies:** Madhya Pradesh; State/ UT

**DBT Scheme:** Yes (Direct Benefit Transfer)

**Benefits / Assistance:** > Pension amount: ₹ 600/- per month.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Department of Social Justice and Empowerment of Persons with Disabilities.

**References / Annexures:** - **Guidelines**: https://socialsecurity.mp.gov.in/Scheme/IGNOAP.aspx - **Apply Portal**: https://socialsecurity.mp.gov.in/PensionManagement/Public/Online_Request_For_pension.aspx
