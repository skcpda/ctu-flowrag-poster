# Financial Assistance to Traditional Artisans (Panchavrutthi Scheme) 

**Short Title:** FATAPS

**Tags:** Financial Assistance, Artisans, Vishwakarma Communities, Subsidy, Loan

The &quot;Financial Assistance to Traditional Artisans (Panchavrutthi Scheme)&quot; was launched by the Department of Backward Class Welfare, Government of Karnataka, to provide financial assistance to traditional artisans (Panchavrutthi) of the Vishwakarma community in Karnataka. The scheme aims to help these artisans purchase modern tools and upgrade their technological skills to enhance their trade and craftsmanship. The Karnataka Vishwakarma Communities Development Corporation (KVCDC) facilitates this scheme.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Skills & Employment; Sector: Training and Skill Up-gradation

**Where it applies:** Karnataka; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - **Loan Amount:** Up to ₹80,000/- at an interest rate of 4% per annum.- **Subsidy Amount:** Up to ₹20,000/-.- **Total Assistance:** ₹1,00,000/- (Loan + Subsidy).- **Repayment Period:** The loan is repayable in 34 equal installments over three years, with a grace period of 2 months.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: Karnataka Vishwakarma Communities Development Corporation Ltd; Nodal Department: Backward Classes Department.
