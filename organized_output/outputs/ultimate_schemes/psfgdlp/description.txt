# Pension Scheme for Grade II Deformed Leprosy Patients

**Short Title:** PSFGDLP

**Launch Date:** 2015-08-01

**Tags:** Leprosy, Patients, Pension, Financial Assistance

Launched on 1st August 2015, the scheme &quot;Pension Scheme for Grade II Deformed Leprosy Patients&quot; is a Pension Scheme by the Directorate of Social Welfare and Social Education, Government of Tripura. The scheme provides financial assistance of ₹2,000/- per month to Leprosy Patients who are residents of Tripura. The applications are accepted offline.   

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Health & Wellness; Sector: Health promotion

**Where it applies:** Tripura; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** Pension of ₹2,000/- per month.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Directorate Of Social Welfare & Social Education.
