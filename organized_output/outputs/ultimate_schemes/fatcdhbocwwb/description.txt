# Financial Assistance for Treatment of Chronic Diseases (HBOCWWB)

**Short Title:** FATCD(HBOCWWB)

**Tags:** Building Worker, Construction Worker, Labour, Treatment, Chronic Diseases, Financial Assistance, Cancer, TB, AIDS

The scheme “Financial Assistance for Treatment of Chronic Diseases” is implemented by the Haryana Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board (HBOCWWB), Labour Department, Government of Haryana. Under this scheme, financial assistance is provided to registered workers for indoor treatment of chronic diseases like Cancer, TB, AIDS, etc. as per government rules.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Health & Wellness; Sector: Disease and conditions

**Where it applies:** Haryana; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** 1. Under this scheme, financial assistance up to ₹1,00,000/- is provided to registered workers for indoor treatment of chronic diseases like Cancer, TB, AIDS, etc.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: Haryana Building & Other Construction Workers Welfare Board; Nodal Department: Labour Department.
