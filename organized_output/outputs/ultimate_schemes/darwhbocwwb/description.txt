# Disability Assistance For Registered Worker (HBOCWWB)

**Short Title:** DARWHBOCWWB

**Tags:** Construction Worker, Accident, Disability, Financial Assistance

The scheme “Disability Assistance for Registered Worker (HBOCWWB)” was launched by the Haryana Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board (HBOCWWB), Labour Department Haryana. Under the scheme, a lump sum financial assistance of ₹1.50 lakh to ₹3.00 lakh is given to registered workers on the basis of the percentage of disability which occurred due to an accident at the workplace during the course of employment.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Financial assistance

**Where it applies:** Haryana; State

**Benefits / Assistance:** On the basis of the percentage of disability, a lump sum financial assistance will be given to registered workers as under: **Sl. No.** **Percentage of Disability****Amount to be Paid** 1 Upto 50% Disability ₹1,50,000/-2 51% to 75% Disability ₹2,00,000/- 3 76% or above ₹3,00,000/-

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: Haryana Building And Other Construction Workers Welfare Board; Nodal Department: Labour Department.
