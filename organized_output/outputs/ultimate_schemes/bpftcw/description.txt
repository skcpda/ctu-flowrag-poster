# Bus Passes for the Construction Workers

**Short Title:** BPFTCW

**Tags:** Labour, Building Worker, Construction Worker, Bus Pass

The &amp;quot;Bus Passes for the Construction Workers&amp;quot; by the Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board, Labour Department, U.T. Administration of Dadra and Nagar Haveli and Daman and Diu, is a welfare scheme for the registered workers of the board. Under this scheme, the Board will provide 50% reimbursement of the travelling cost of registered construction workers who travel by bus from various villages in search of work in U.T. of Dadra and Nagar Haveli. &lt;br&gt;

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Citizen empowerment

**Where it applies:** Dadra & Nagar Haveli and Daman & Diu; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** **Quantum of Assistance:** 50% reimbursement of the travelling cost. 

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Labour & Employment Department.
