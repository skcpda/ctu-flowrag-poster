# Special Scholarship to SC Students in Selected Schools

**Short Title:** SSSCSSS

**Tags:** Scholarship, Bright Student, Financial Assistance, Scheduled Caste, Selected Schools

The “Special Scholarship to SC Students in Selected Schools” scheme is administered by the Director, Scheduled Caste Welfare, Government of Gujarat. Under this scheme, financial assistance is provided to Scheduled Caste (SC) students studying in famous private selected schools like Doon School, Dehradun; Sofiya School, Abu; Mayo School, Ajmer; Sainik School, Balachadi; Mahila Sainik School, Kherva; Dist. Mehsana, etc. will be paid actual fees upto limit of ₹50,000/- per annum. One-time financial assistance will be provided to 10 students admitted to Standard VIII and 10 students admitted to Standard XI in any of the above-mentioned schools.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Education & Learning; Sector: Scholarships and student finance

**Where it applies:** Gujarat; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** 1. Under this scheme, eligible students will receive financial assistance of up to ₹50,000 per year or the actual amount of their annual fees, whichever is lower. 1. This assistance will cover admission fees, tuition fees, hostel fees, food bills, books, uniforms, and other related expenses.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: Director, Scheduled Caste Welfare, Gandhinagar; Nodal Department: Social Justice and Empowerment Department.
