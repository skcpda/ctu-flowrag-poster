# Grant for Purchase of Tools (BBOCWWB)

**Short Title:** GPTBBOCWWB

**Tags:** Tools, Construction Worker, Financial Assistance, Building Worker, Labour, Unorganized Workers

The &amp;quot;Grant for Purchase of Tools&amp;quot; scheme is implemented by the Bihar Building &amp;amp; Other Construction Workers Welfare Board (BBOCWWB), Labour Resources Department, Government of Bihar. Under this scheme, registered construction workers who have completed skill upgradation training are provided financial assistance of upto ₹15,000/- for purchasing trade-related tools.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Skills & Employment; Sector: Training and Skill Up-gradation

**Where it applies:** Bihar; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** Under this scheme, financial assistance of upto ₹15,000/- is provided to registered construction workers for purchasing trade-related tools upon completing skill upgradation training.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: Bihar Building & Other Construction Workers Welfare Board; Nodal Department: Labour Resources Department.
