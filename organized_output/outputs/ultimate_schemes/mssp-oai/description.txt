# Mukhyamantri Samajik Sahayata Prakalpa - Old and Infirm

**Short Title:** MSSP-OAI

**Tags:** Pension, Financial Assistance, Senior Citizen

The scheme “Mukhyamantri Samajik Sahayata Prakalpa- Old and Infirm&quot; was launched by the Directorate of Social Welfare and Social Education, Government of Tripura. This scheme aims to provide financial assistance to poor people of Tripura who are old and infirm, such as senior citizens who are 60 years and above in age.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Financial assistance

**Where it applies:** Tripura; State/ UT

**DBT Scheme:** Yes (Direct Benefit Transfer)

**Benefits / Assistance:** The pension amount of ₹2000/- per month will be transferred directly to the bank account of the applicant. **NOTE:** The list of beneficiaries will be made available on the [official website](https://socialwelfare.tripura.gov.in/) of the Social Welfare &amp; Social Education Department, Govt. of Tripura.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Exclusions / Not eligible:** - Income taxpayers are not eligible. - Government employees are not eligible. - The individuals whose family members are already getting benefits under any of the Category/Sub-Category under ‘Mukhyamantri Samajik Sahayata Prakalpa&#39; (except infirm &amp; PwDs) are not eligible. - Pensioners who are getting benefits under the “National Social Assistance Program (NSAP)” or the 32 State Social Pension Schemes run by the Social Welfare &amp; Social Education Department, Govt. of Tripura are not eligible.

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Directorate Of Social Welfare & Social Education.
