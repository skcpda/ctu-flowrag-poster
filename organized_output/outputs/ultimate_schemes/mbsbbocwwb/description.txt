# Maternity Benefits Scheme (BBOCWWB)

**Short Title:** MBSBBOCWWB

**Tags:** Construction, Building, Woman, Labour, Maternity, Worker

&quot;Maternity Benefits Scheme&quot; by the Bihar Building &amp; Other Construction Workers&#39; Welfare Board, Labour Resources Department, Government of Bihar, provides an amount equivalent to 90 days of the minimum wages set by the State Government for unskilled workers at the time of childbirth. This benefit is applicable for the first two deliveries. The applicant should be a female construction worker who has completed one year of membership with the Board.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Women and Child; Sector: Childcare

**Where it applies:** Bihar; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** Financial assistance equivalent to 90 days of the minimum wages set by the State Government for unskilled workers.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Building Construction Department.
