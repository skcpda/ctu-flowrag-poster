# Food Bill Assistance

**Short Title:** FBS

**Tags:** Education, Student, Financal Assistance

The &quot;Food Bill Assistance&quot; introduced by the Social Justice and Empowerment Department, Gujarat, aims to support students who are required to live away from their families due to the unavailability of educational facilities in their home taluka. The scheme is being implemented by the Gujarat Unreserved Education and Economical Development Corporation.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Education & Learning; Sector: Financial assistance

**Where it applies:** Gujarat; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - **Financial Assistance:** ₹1,500/- per month for 10 months.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: Gujarat Unreserved Education And Economical Development Corporation; Nodal Department: Social Justice and Empowerment Department.

**References / Annexures:** - **Guidelines**: https://gueedc.gujarat.gov.in/food-bill-scheme.html - **User Manual**: https://esamajkalyan.gujarat.gov.in/ApplicationDocuments/GUEEDC/HowtoApplyGUEEDC.pdf
