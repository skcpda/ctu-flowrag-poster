# Assistance for Establishment of On farm Storage for Sorting-Grading

**Short Title:** AEOFSG

**Tags:** Horticulture, Agricultural Infrastructure, Farm Storage

The scheme &quot;Assistance for Establishment of On-Farm Storage for Sorting-Grading&quot; by the Agriculture and Co-operation Department, Government of Gujarat aims to reduce post-harvest losses in horticultural crops and improve value addition by providing financial assistance to farmers for establishing on-farm infrastructure, including sorting, grading, packing, and primary value addition. The support includes both construction-related infrastructure and modern equipment/machinery for on-farm operations. 

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Agriculture,Rural & Environment; Sector: Entrepreneurship development

**Where it applies:** Gujarat; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - **For Construction of On-Farm Infrastructure:** Financial aid will be 50% of the unit cost or ₹1,50,000/- per unit, whichever is lower.- **For Machinery or Equipment Purchase:** Financial aid of 50% of the actual cost, subject to a maximum of ₹50,000/- per unit, whichever is less. 

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Agriculture, Farmers Welfare and Cooperation Department.
