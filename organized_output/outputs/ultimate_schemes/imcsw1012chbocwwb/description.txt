# Incentives for Meritorious Children (Students) of Workers (10th and 12th Class) (HBOCWWB)

**Short Title:** IMCSW1012CHBOCWWB

**Tags:** Incentive, Meritorious Student, Construction Worker, Building Worker, Scholarship, Prize Money, Award

The scheme “Incentives for Meritorious Children (Students) of Workers (10th and 12th Class)” is implemented by the Haryana Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board (HBOCWWB), Labour Department, Government of Haryana. Under this scheme, a scholarship (prize money) in the form of a fixed deposit will be provided to meritorious children of construction workers who have achieved academic excellence in the Class X/XII examination. Registered workers with a one-year regular membership are eligible to apply for benefits for up to three of their children.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Education & Learning; Sector: Scholarships and student finance

**Where it applies:** Haryana; State/ UT
