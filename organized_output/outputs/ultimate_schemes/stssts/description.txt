# State Technical Scholarship for ST Student

**Short Title:** STSSTS

**Tags:** Technical, Scholarship, ST, Student, Scheduled Tribe

The “State Technical Scholarship for ST Student” is a scholarship scheme, implemented by the Directorate of Technical Education, Government of Nagaland. Under the scheme, financial assistance is provided to students of Scheduled Tribe Communities who are pursuing Diploma/Degree/Post Graduated/course in Engineering Institutes recognized by All India Council for Technical Education. Assistance is also provided to students who are pursuing UG or PG in Forestry courses.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Education & Learning; Sector: Scholarships and student finance

**Where it applies:** Nagaland; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** 1. Under the scheme, financial assistance/scholarships will be given to students according to the course/programme in which they are enrolled. 1. Scholarship shall be provided for a maximum period of 3 years for Diploma, 4 years for Degree (B.E/B.Tech.), and 2 years for (M.E/M.Tech).

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: Directorate Of Technical Education, Government Of Nagaland; Nodal Department: Department of Higher Education.
