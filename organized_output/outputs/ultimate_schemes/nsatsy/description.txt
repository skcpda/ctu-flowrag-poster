# Nirman Shramik Auzaar Toolkit Sahayata Yojana

**Short Title:** NSATSY

**Launch Date:** 2016-01-01

**Tags:** Finance, Labor, Tools, Empower

The NIRMAN SHRAMIK AUZAAR TOOLKIT SAHAYATA YOJANA is a social welfare scheme launched by the Government of Jharkhand to provide financial assistance to registered construction workers in the state for the purchase of essential tools and equipment required for their work.  > **Objectives** 1. To improve the working conditions and productivity of construction workers in Jharkhand. 1. To reduce the financial burden on construction workers for purchasing essential tools and equipment. 1. To promote the overall socio-economic development of construction workers in the state.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Banking,Financial Services and Insurance; Sector: Personal finance

**Where it applies:** Rajasthan; State/ UT

**DBT Scheme:** Yes (Direct Benefit Transfer)

**Benefits / Assistance:** > Actual cost of purchasing tools/toolkit or ₹ 2,000/-, whichever is less

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Exclusions / Not eligible:** ****

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Labour Department.
