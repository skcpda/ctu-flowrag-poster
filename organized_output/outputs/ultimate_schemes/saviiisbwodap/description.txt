# Scholarship (Above VIII Standard) - Tamil Nadu

**Short Title:** SATN

**Tags:** Student, Scholarship, Education, Different Abled Person

The Department for the Welfare of Differently Abled Persons, Government of Tamil Nadu, offers the &quot;Scholarship for Students with Disabilities (Above Grade 8)&quot; program. This initiative provides financial assistance to differently-abled students enrolled in government-recognized schools, colleges, or training centers to help with educational expenses.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Education & Learning; Sector: Scholarships and student finance

**Where it applies:** Tamil Nadu; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** Scholarships are provided as: - For students studying from 9th to 12th Standard: ₹4,000/- per annum. - For Degree Courses: ₹6,000/- per annum. - For Postgraduate, Medical, Engineering, Vocational, and Other Professional Courses: ₹7,000/- per annum.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Welfare of Differently Abled Persons Department.
