# Subsidy For Battery Operated Three-Wheelers

**Short Title:** SFBOTHW

**Tags:** Battery Operated Three-Wheelers, Electric Scooter Subsidy, Electric Vehicle, Renewable Energy, Student

The scheme &amp;quot;Subsidy For Battery Operated Three-wheelers&amp;quot; by the Climate Change Department, Government of Gujarat, aims to encourage the use of clean energy-based vehicles for the transportation of passengers and goods, thereby reducing dependency on petrol and mitigating pollution. Beneficiaries, including rickshaw drivers, women entrepreneurs, startups, unemployed youth, educational institutions, cooperatives, NGOs, local bodies, and yatras, can avail a subsidy on a reimbursement basis after purchasing battery-operated three-wheelers from Gujarat Energy Development Agency (GEDA) authorized manufacturers and dealers. 

**Who is this for:** Individual, Government Organisation, NGO, Registered Societies; Category: Agriculture,Rural & Environment; Sector: Environmental management and protection

**Where it applies:** Gujarat; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - **Subsidy Assistance:** Subsidy of ₹48,000/- after the purchase of a battery-operated three-wheeler from GEDA-authorized manufacturers/dealers.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Climate Change Department.
