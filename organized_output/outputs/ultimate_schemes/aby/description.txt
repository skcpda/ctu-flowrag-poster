# Aapki Beti Yojana

**Short Title:** ABY

**Tags:** Financial Assistance, Girl Student, BPL, Below Poverty Line, Daughter

The &amp;quot;Aapki Beti Yojana&amp;quot; scheme was launched by the School Education Department, Government of Rajasthan, to support girls in continuing their education in government schools from Class 1 to 12. Under the scheme, financial assistance of ₹2,100/- per year is provided to girls studying in Class 1 to 8 in government schools who belong to families living below the poverty line (BPL) and whose one or both parents have passed away. For girls studying in Class 9 to 12, the assistance is ₹2,500/- per year. The scheme is implemented by the Rajasthan Balika Shiksha Foundation.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Education & Learning; Sector: Scholarships and student finance

**Where it applies:** Rajasthan; State/ UT

**DBT Scheme:** Yes (Direct Benefit Transfer)

**Benefits / Assistance:** > **Financial Assistance:** - ₹2,100/- per annum for girl students in classes 1st to 8th.- ₹2,500/- per annum for girl students in classes 9th to 12th. > ***Mode of Disbursement:*** - Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) to the bank account linked with Jan Aadhaar of the family head or the girl child.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: Rajasthan Balika Shiksha Foundation; Nodal Department: Education (Elementary and Secondary) Department.
