# Scholarship to the Ward of Beneficiary (CBOCWWB)

**Short Title:** SWB(CBOCWWB)

**Tags:** Scholarship, Ward, Construction Worker, Labour, Financial Assistance

The scheme “Scholarship to the Ward of Beneficiary” was started by the Chandigarh Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board (CBOCWWB), Labour Department, Chandigarh. Under this scheme, financial assistance in the form of scholarship shall be granted to the children/wards of workers who are registered under the CBOCWWB on the basis of the percentage of marks obtained in 10th, 12th &amp;amp; Graduation.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Education & Learning; Sector: Scholarships and student finance

**Where it applies:** Chandigarh; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** **Rate of scholarship on the basis of percentage of marks obtained in 10th, 12th &amp; Graduation:** ClassPercentage of marks obtainedAmount of Scholarship10th 75% &amp;amp;amp;amp; above₹5,000/-10th60% &amp;amp;amp;amp; above₹4,000/-10th50% &amp;amp;amp;amp; above₹3,000/-10+275%₹5,000/-10+260% &amp;amp;amp;amp; above₹4,000/-10+250 % &amp;amp;amp;amp; above₹3,000/-Graduation or equivalent (from recognized university)60% &amp;amp;amp;amp; above₹7,500/- (one time)**Note:-** No further scholarship shall be granted thereafter.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: Chandigarh Building And Other Construction Workers Welfare Board; Nodal Department: Labour Department.
