# Rajasthan Chief Minister's Relief Fund - Accident Assistance

**Short Title:** RCMRF-AA

**Scheme For:** Family

**Tags:** Accident, Financial Assistance

Launched in April 1999, &quot;Rajasthan Chief Minister&#39;s Relief Fund - Accident Assistance&quot; is a scheme by the Chief Minister&#39;s Secretariat, Govt. of Rajasthan. In case of death due to accident, ₹ 1,00,000 is provided to the dependent of the deceased. In case of serious injury, assistance should be approved up to ₹ 20,000, keeping in view the seriousness of the injury to the ordinary injured. A revolving fund of ₹20,00,000 each has been made available to all district collectors. The assistance is &quot;one-time full-support&quot; and is sanctioned by the concerned District Collector. For assistance, one has to apply either to the Tehsil/District Collector&#39;s Office or to the Chief Minister&#39;s Office.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Crisis/Disaster/Accident

**Where it applies:** Rajasthan; State

**Benefits / Assistance:** • In case of death due to accident, ₹ 1,00,000 is provided to the dependent of the deceased. • In case of serious injury, assistance should be approved up to ₹ 20,000, keeping in view the seriousness of the injury to the ordinary injured. • A revolving fund of ₹20,00,000 each has been made available to all district collectors. • The assistance is "one-time full-support" and is sanctioned by the concerned District Collector. • In case of death of a seriously injured person in an accident while undergoing treatment, assistance is given on applying within a period of 6 months from the date of death instead of the date of application.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Exclusions / Not eligible:** • Assistance is not payable twice in the same case. • Assistance is not payable in case of loss of property due to fire or other reasons. • Assistance is not payable in cases of suicide. • Assistance should not be given from the Chief Minister's Relief Fund in case assistance is due from other schemes of the state government. • It is not necessary to provide assistance if the family is capable.

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Social Justice and Empowerment Department.
