# Education Assistance - Folk Artist

**Short Title:** EAFA

**Scheme For:** Family

**Tags:** Student, Financial Assistance, Artist

The &quot;Education Assistance - Folk Artist&quot; scheme was launched by the Art and Culture Department, Government of Tamil Nadu. This scheme provides financial assistance for the education of the children of registered folk artists. The assistance aims to support the educational pursuits of their children from school to higher education, including vocational and professional courses.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Education & Learning; Sector: Scholarships and student finance

**Where it applies:** Tamil Nadu; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** **Financial Assistance for the children of registered folk artistes** - Girl and Boy children studying 10th - ₹1,000/- - Girl children studying 11th - ₹1,000/- - Girl and Boy children studying 12th - ₹1,500/- - Regular Degree Course with Hostel facility - ₹3,250/- - Regular Post Graduate Course with Hostel facility - ₹5,000/- - Professional Degree Course with Hostel facility - ₹6,000/- - Professional PG Course with Hostel facility - ₹10,000/- - ITI or Polytechnic Course with Hostel Facility - ₹2,200/- 

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Art and Culture Department.
