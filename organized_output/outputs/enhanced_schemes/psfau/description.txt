# Pension Scheme for Artisans

**Short Title:** PSFAU

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Pension, Senior Citizen, Artisans, BPL, Old-age

The &amp;quot;Pension Scheme for Artisans&amp;quot; was launched by the Department of Industries, Government of Uttarakhand. It aims to provide financial security and dignity to senior traditional artisans aged 60 years and above. Under this scheme, artisans who are already receiving an old-age pension and belong to a family living below the poverty line are granted an additional incentive of ₹400/- per month. > **Objective** The scheme supports artisans involved in traditional crafts like stone, wood, iron, bamboo, ringaal, natural fibres, etc., particularly those from below-poverty-line (BPL) families.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Pension

**Where it applies:** Uttarakhand; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - The monthly incentive of ₹400 is provided to senior artisans who are already receiving old-age pension. > **Note:** Payment is made quarterly (every 3 months) via the bank account or post office account of the beneficiary.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Department Of The Industrial Development.
