# Grant for the Repair of House (BBOCWWB)

**Short Title:** GRHBBOCWWB

**Scheme For:** Infra

**Tags:** Building Worker, Construction Worker, Financial Assistance, Labour, Organized Worker, Repair Of House

The &amp;quot;Grant for the Repair of House&amp;quot; scheme is implemented by the Bihar Building &amp;amp; Other Construction Workers Welfare Board (BBOCWWB), Labour Resources Department, Government of Bihar. Under this scheme, financial assistance of ₹20,000/- is provided to registered construction workers who have been members of the Board for at least three years for the repair of their house. However, workers who have already received financial assistance for house construction, bicycles, or tools are not eligible for this benefit. The assistance is provided only once to each eligible worker.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Housing & Shelter; Sector: Housing

**Where it applies:** Bihar; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - Under this scheme, financial assistance of ₹20,000/- is provided to registered construction workers for house repairs.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: Bihar Building & Other Construction Workers Welfare Board; Nodal Department: Labour Resources Department.
