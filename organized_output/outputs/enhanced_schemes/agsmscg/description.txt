# Aatmanirbhar Gujarat Scheme for Assistance to Mega Industries: Reimbursement of SGST Paid on Capital Goods

**Short Title:** AGSMSCG

**Launch Date:** 2022-10-05

**Scheme For:** Infra

**Tags:** Business, Entrepreneurship, Mega Industries, Capital Goods, SGST, Thrust Manufacturing Sector

The &amp;quot;Aatmanirbhar Gujarat Scheme for Assistance to Mega Industries&amp;quot; is an umbrella scheme launched by the Industries and Mines Department, Gujarat. It aims to empower the state&amp;#39;s dynamic mega-industry sector, which significantly contributes to employment, GDP, and exports. Effective from October 5, 2022, to October 4, 2032, this initiative supports growth, investments, and the vision of &amp;quot;Aatmanirbhar Gujarat&amp;quot; and &amp;quot;Aatmanirbhar Bharat.&amp;quot; The component &amp;quot;Reimbursement of SGST Paid on Capital Goods&amp;quot; offers financial assistance to eligible Mega Industrial Units, providing a 100% reimbursement of SGST paid on capital goods during their investment period. The reimbursement is subject to conditions defined under the Gujarat GST Act 2017. &lt;br&gt;

**Who is this for:** Business Entity, Industries; Category: Business & Entrepreneurship; Sector: Setting up / start-up / entrepreneurship

**Where it applies:** Gujarat; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - **SGST Reimbursement:** Full reimbursement of SGST paid on capital goods for eligible units. - **Long-Term Support:** Reimbursement provided in 20 equal annual installments after a year from commercial production.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Exclusions / Not eligible:** - Goods not considered capital goods under the Gujarat GST Act will not be considered under this scheme.

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Industries and Mines Department.
