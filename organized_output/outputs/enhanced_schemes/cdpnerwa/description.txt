# Coffee Development Programme in North Eastern Region: Water Augmentation

**Short Title:** CDPNER:WA

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Agriculture, Farmer, Subsidy, DBT

The scheme &quot;Coffee Development Programme in North Eastern Region: Water Augmentation&quot; is a Sub-Component of the Scheme &quot;Integrated Coffee Development Project During the Medium Term Framework (MTF) Period: Development Support to Stakeholders&quot; by the Coffee Board, Department of Commerce, Ministry of Commerce and Industry. This scheme aims to improve the productivity and quality of coffee by facilitating the creation of infrastructure for water augmentation for irrigation and wet processing of coffee.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Agriculture,Rural & Environment; Sector: Agricultural Inputs- seeds, fertilizer etc.

**Where it applies:** Central

**DBT Scheme:** Yes (Direct Benefit Transfer)

**Benefits / Assistance:** **Subsidy Details** - The eligible subsidy for water harvesting structures and sprinkler units is 75% of the unit cost, capped at a maximum ceiling of ₹1,25,000 for all related activities combined. - For below-ground water storage tanks, the subsidy is based on the lower value between dimension and unit cost applicable to the size of the holding. - For above-ground water storage tanks, the subsidy is based on the lower value between the actual cost or the unit cost applicable to the below-ground water tank. **Subsidy Availability** Growers can claim subsidies for water harvesting structures and irrigation equipment either simultaneously or at different times.  **Unit Cost and Subsidy for Water Harvesting Structures** **Size of Holding****Dimension of Water Harvesting Structure****Total Unit Cost (₹)****Subsidy Amount**Up to 2 Ha.30&amp;amp;amp;#39; x 30&amp;amp;amp;#39; x 10&amp;amp;amp;#39; (9000 Cft.)43,000.0075% of Unit Cost2 Ha to 4 Ha &amp;amp;amp;amp; above43&amp;amp;amp;#39; x 43&amp;amp;amp;#39; x 10&amp;amp;amp;#39; (18490 Cft.)89,000.0075% of Unit Cost**Unit Cost and Subsidy for Sprinkler/Drip Unit** **Size of Holding****Total Unit Cost (₹)****Subsidy Amount**Up to 1.00 Ha. 60,000 75% of the Unit Cost1.00 to 2.00 Ha. &amp;amp;amp;amp; above 1,20,000 75% of the Unit Cost
