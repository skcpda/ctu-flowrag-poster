# Kunwar Bai Nu Mameru Yojana- SEBC & EBC

**Short Title:** KBNMY-SEBCEBC

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Financial Assistance, Marriage, Girl, SEBC, EBC

The &quot;Kunwar Bai Nu Mameru Yojana- SEBC &amp; EBC&quot; scheme was launched by the Social Justice and Empowerment Department, Government of Gujarat. The scheme aims to provide financial assistance to families from Socially and Educationally Backward Classes and Economically Backward Classes to help cover the wedding expenses of their daughters. Under this scheme, an amount of ₹12,000 is provided for each daughter’s marriage, subject to a maximum of two adult daughters per family. 

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment

**Where it applies:** Gujarat; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** **Monetary Assistance:** - ₹12,000 is provided for the marriage of a daughter. > **NOTE:** If the marriage occurred before April 1, 2021, assistance of ₹10,000 is provided. 

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Social Justice and Empowerment Department.

**References / Annexures:** - **Guidelines**: https://mariyojana.gujarat.gov.in/MoreDetails.aspx

**Additional Information:**
Briefdescription: The scheme aims to provide marriage-related financial aid of ₹12,000 per daughter (up to two daughters per family) to socially and educationally backward classes and economically backward class families.
