# Scheduled Castes Unsalaried Credit Co-operative Society and Credit and Supply Co-operative Scheme for Providing Managerial Assistance and Computer–Furniture to the Society

**Short Title:** SCUCCOCSC

**Scheme For:** Infra

**Tags:** Scheduled Caste, Co-operative Societies, Computer And Furniture Grant, Agriculture

The scheme “Scheduled Castes Unsalaried Credit Co-operative Society and Credit and Supply Co-operative Scheme for Providing Managerial Assistance and Computer–Furniture to the Society” by the Agriculture and Co-operation Department, Government of Gujarat, aims to promote the financial inclusion and operational efficiency of Scheduled Caste Savings and Credit Co-operative Societies by offering managerial support and necessary infrastructure. These societies, formed to serve members of the Scheduled Castes through loan and credit services, often lack resources to appoint qualified managers or procure essential office furniture and computer systems. Through this scheme, the government offers financial assistance to help societies pay managerial salaries and purchase basic office infrastructure. 

**Who is this for:** Registered Societies; Category: Agriculture,Rural & Environment; Sector: Entrepreneurship development

**Where it applies:** Gujarat; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - **Managerial Assistance:** A maximum of ₹8,000/- per month is provided for an employee&#39;s salary, totaling Rs. 0.96 lakh per society on an annual basis.- **Computer and Furniture Assistance:** A maximum of Rs. 0.62 lakh per society is provided for the purchase of computers and furniture. 

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Agriculture, Farmers Welfare and Cooperation Department.

**References / Annexures:** - **Guidelines (Scheme No. 518)**: https://mariyojana.gujarat.gov.in/Schemeatoz.aspx
