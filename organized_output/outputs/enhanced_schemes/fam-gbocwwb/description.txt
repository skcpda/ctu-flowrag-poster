# Financial Assistance for Marriage (GBOCWWB)

**Short Title:** FAM-GBOCWWB

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Marriage, Financial Assistance, Worker, Labour, Bride, Bridegroom

&quot;Financial Assistance for Marriage (GBOCWWB)&quot; is a Welfare Scheme by the Goa Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board of the Department of Labour and Employment, Goa. Through this scheme, the worker registered with the Board, having a continuous membership for 3 years, is provided financial assistance of ₹4,000/- for the marriage of their children. The applications are accepted offline.   

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Marriage

**Where it applies:** Goa; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** Financial Assistance of ₹4,000/-.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: Goa Building And Other Construction Workers Welfare Board; Nodal Department: Labour and Employment Department.
