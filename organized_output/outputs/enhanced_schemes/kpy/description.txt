# Farmer Pension Scheme

**Short Title:** FPSUK

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Farmer, Pension, Kisan, Agriculture

The &amp;quot;Farmer Pension Scheme&amp;quot; was launched by the Department of Social Welfare, Government of Uttarakhand. The scheme aims to provide financial support to elderly farmers residing in the state of Uttarakhand. Under this scheme, a monthly pension is provided to farmers who are above 60 years of age, own up to 2 hectares of land, and cultivate their own land. 

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Pension

**Where it applies:** Uttarakhand; State

**Benefits / Assistance:** A pension of ₹1,200/- per month is provided to eligible farmers. 

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Department Of Social Welfare.
