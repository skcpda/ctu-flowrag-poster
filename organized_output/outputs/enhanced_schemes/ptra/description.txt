# Promotion of Tiatr Related Activities

**Short Title:** PTRA

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Culture, Artist, Music, Competition, Heritage

The scheme &quot;Promotion of Tiatr Related Activities&quot; was launched by the Tiatr Academy under the Art, Culture and Museums Department, Government of Goa. The scheme aims to support cultural institutions and individuals in organizing various activities related to tiatr in their respective villages and towns to preserve and promote the traditional Konkani theatrical art form. The scheme provides financial support for organizing Konkani musical shows, singing competitions, acting competitions, one act plays, and programs celebrating the contribution of local tiatr artists both deceased and living but non-active, while ensuring these activities are made accessible to the public free of charge. The applicant must be a cultural institution or individual willing to organize tiatr-related activities and must submit applications within specified advance notice periods ranging from 8 to 30 days depending on the activity type. The scheme is implemented by Tiatr Academy of Goa and applications for this scheme are accepted offline through prescribed application forms available at the Tiatr Academy of Goa office. 

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Sports & Culture; Sector: Culture

**Where it applies:** Goa; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** ##### ***Financial Support Categories*** - ***Category A1*** ***- Konkani Musical Shows:*** Financial assistance for organizing shows with minimum 14 songs including duets, trios, quartets, choral songs, solos and at least 2 comedy skits.- ***Category A2*** ***- Singing Competitions:*** Financial assistance for organizing competitions with minimum 15 participants singing newly composed kantaram.- ***Category B1*** ***- Competitions:*** Financial assistance for acting competitions including monologue, dialogue, and one act play competitions.- ***Category B2*** ***- Celebrating Tiatr Artists:*** Financial assistance for organizing programs to remember and felicitate prominent tiatr artists. ##### ***Conditions*** - Programs must be staged free of charge to the public. - All promotional materials must acknowledge collaboration with Tiatr Academy of Goa. - Backdrop of minimum size 6 feet x 10 feet displaying Tiatr Academy of Goa logo is mandatory. - No adverse remarks against any governments, individuals, institutions, or content against caste, creed, and religion permitted. - Chief Guest and Guest of Honour must be decided in consultation with Tiatr Academy of Goa. - President or Vice President of Tiatr Academy of Goa will be the Chief Guest or Guest of Honour. 

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Art, Culture and Museums Department.
