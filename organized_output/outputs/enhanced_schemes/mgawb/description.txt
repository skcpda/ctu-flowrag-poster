# Mukhyamantri Gramin Awas Yojana

**Short Title:** MGAWB

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Housing, Social Welfare, Scheduled Castes, Scheduled Tribes, Extremely Backward Classes, Financial Assistance

The &amp;quot;Mukhyamantri Gramin Awas Yojana&amp;quot; was introduced by the Rural Development Department, Bihar, to address the housing needs of beneficiaries belonging to Scheduled Castes, Scheduled Tribes, and Extremely Backward Classes whose cluster housing, built under previous schemes before January 1996, has deteriorated. As these families are ineligible for the Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana due to prior benefits, this scheme provides financial assistance in three installments directly to the beneficiaries&amp;#39; accounts for reconstructing their homes. &lt;br&gt; &lt;br&gt;

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Housing & Shelter; Sector: Housing

**Where it applies:** Bihar; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - **Financial Assistance:** ₹1,20,000/- (Provided in three installments: ₹40,000/- each through Direct Benefit Transfer or DBT).

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Rural Development Department.

**References / Annexures:** - **Guidelines**: https://cdnbbsr.s3waas.gov.in/s31943102704f8f8f3302c2b730728e023/uploads/2022/07/2022072358.pdf

**Additional Information:**
Briefdescription: This scheme aims to provide safe housing for Scheduled Castes, Scheduled Tribes, and Extremely Backward Classes whose existing group housing under previous schemes has become broken down.
