# Scheme for Giving Financial Assistance to the Legal Heirs of the Deceased Artist (Wife/Husband) on the Death of the Indigent Artist Who Was Receiving Financial Assistance

**Short Title:** SFGFATTLHOTDAWHOTDOTIAWWRFS

**Scheme For:** Family

**Tags:** Financial Assistance, Artist, Death, Heir, Crisis

The &quot;Scheme for Giving Financial Assistance to the Legal Heirs of the Deceased Artist (Wife/Husband) on the Death of the Indigent Artist who was Receiving Financial Assistance&quot; was launched by the Art and Culture Department, Government of Tamil Nadu. Through this scheme, financial assistance of ₹3,000/- is given to the eligible legal heir (wife/husband) of the deceased artist.

**Who is this for:** Family; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Crisis/Disaster/Accident

**Where it applies:** Tamil Nadu; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** Financial Assistance of ₹3,000/- per month.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Art and Culture Department.
