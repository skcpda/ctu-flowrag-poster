# Swarnima

**Short Title:** SMA

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Other Backward Class, Women, Employment, Loan, OBC

The scheme &amp;amp;quot;Swarnima by the Department of Social Justice &amp;amp;amp; Special Assistance, Government of Maharashtra, is jointly funded by the National Backward Classes Finance &amp;amp;amp; Development Corporation, New Delhi, and the State Corporation. This scheme provides low-interest loans directly deposited into the bank accounts of female beneficiaries from Other Backward Classes (OBC) to support self-employment activities and enhance financial independence. &amp;lt;br&amp;gt; &amp;lt;br&amp;gt; &amp;lt;br&amp;gt; &amp;lt;br&amp;gt; &amp;lt;br&amp;gt; &amp;lt;br&amp;gt; &amp;lt;br&amp;gt;

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Banking,Financial Services and Insurance; Sector: Banking and money

**Where it applies:** Maharashtra; State/ UT

**DBT Scheme:** Yes (Direct Benefit Transfer)

**Benefits / Assistance:** - Financial assistance of up to ₹5,00,000/- per Self-Help Group (SHG) project, with ₹25,000 allocated per beneficiary (up to 20 members per SHG). - Funding shares include 95% from the Central Corporation and 5% from the State Corporation. - No beneficiary contribution is required. - Low interest rate of 5% per annum. - Repayment period of up to 3 years to ease financial planning.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Social Justice and Special Assistance Department.

**References / Annexures:** - **Guidelines**: https://sjsa.maharashtra.gov.in/en/scheme-category/employment

**Additional Information:**
Briefdescription: The Swarnima Scheme provides loan assistance to female beneficiaries from Other Backward Classes (OBC) residing in Maharashtra to support self-employment activities, empowering them to establish a stable livelihood.
