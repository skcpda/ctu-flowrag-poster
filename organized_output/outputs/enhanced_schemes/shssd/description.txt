# Student Housing Scheme for Students with Disabilities

**Short Title:** SHSSD

**Scheme For:** Infra

**Tags:** Student, PwD, Free Facility Of Accommodation, Hostel

The scheme &amp;quot;Student Housing Scheme for Students with Disabilities&amp;quot; was launched by the Social Welfare Department, Government of Chhattisgarh. Under this scheme, there is a provision to provide free housing facilities to a group of 5 disabled students, to motivate them for higher education, by providing accommodations to them. The accommodations are provided at a rental building. The group of disabled students of the state will be able to avail the benefit of hostels for studying in the other states of the country also.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment

**Where it applies:** Madhya Pradesh; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** 1. Free facility of accommodation to 01 or more than 01 (group of maximum 05 children) within the fixed rate of rent at the rate prescribed by the Rent Control Authority. 1. The maximum monthly rent amount of the building including electricity and other expenses is determined for the following category of cities- - For A category cities, up to ₹10,000/- will be payable. - For B category cities, up to ₹7,000/- will be payable. - For C category cities, up to ₹5,000/- will be payable. **Note:** The amount will be paid directly into the landlord&amp;#39;s account through NEFT/RTGS/DBT as far as possible.

**Benefit Type:** In Kind

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: Social Welfare Department, Government Of Chhattisgarh; Nodal Department: Social Welfare Department.
