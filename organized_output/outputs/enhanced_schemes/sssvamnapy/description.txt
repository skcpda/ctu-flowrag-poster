# Samagra Samajik Suraksha Vridh Ashram Mai Nivaasarat Ant-vasi Pension Yojana

**Short Title:** SSSVAMNAPY

**Launch Date:** 2016-01-01

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Empowerment, Pension, Vridvashram, Financial

Samagra Samajik Suraksha Vridh Ashram Mai Nivastan Athavasi Pension Yojana under the Department of Social Justice government of Madhya Pradesh provides financial support to the citizens of Madhya Pradesh who live in old age homes recognized by the Department of Social Justice.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Pension

**Where it applies:** Madhya Pradesh; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** Pension amount: ₹ 600/- per month.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Department of Social Justice and Empowerment of Persons with Disabilities.

**References / Annexures:** - **Guidelines**: https://socialsecurity.mp.gov.in/Scheme/SSPOAH.aspx - **Apply Portal**: https://socialsecurity.mp.gov.in/Home.aspx

**Additional Information:**
Briefdescription: Under this scheme, the Madhya Pradesh government provides financial support to the citizens who live in old age homes recognized by the Department of Social Justice.
