# Pension for Agitators Who were Jailed for 7 Days or Injured During the Uttarakhand State Movement

**Short Title:** PAJ7IDUKSM

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** State Movement, Andolankari, Agitators, Pension

The &amp;quot;Pension for Agitators Who were Jailed for 7 Days or Injured During the Uttarakhand State Movement&amp;quot; scheme is implemented by the Police Department under the Department of Home, Government of Uttarakhand. The scheme aims to provide a monthly pension to agitators who were jailed for 7 days or injured during the Uttarakhand State Movement and are unable to gain employment due to certain reasons. 

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Pension

**Where it applies:** Uttarakhand; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** **Pension Amount:** ₹6,000/- per month 

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: Police Department, Government Of Uttarakhand; Nodal Department: Department of Home.

**References / Annexures:** - **Guidelines (Page No. 63)**: https://uk.gov.in/department92/library_file/file-04-12-2023-06-02-23.pdf

Briefdescription: The scheme provides a monthly pension of ₹6,000 to agitators who were jailed for 7 days or injured during the Uttarakhand State Movement and are unable to gain employment due to certain reasons.
