# Tool Purchase Grant for the Construction Workers

**Short Title:** TPGFTCW

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Labour, Building Worker, Construction Worker, Tool Purchase

The “Tool Purchase Grant for the Construction Workers” scheme by the Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board, Labour Department, West Bengal, is a welfare scheme for the registered workers of the board. Under this scheme, the Board shall make one time reimbursement to each beneficiary who is registered with the Board for a period of at least 6 months for purchasing tools relating to his profession on production of original bill/ voucher. &lt;br&gt;

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Business & Entrepreneurship; Sector: Machine & skill up-Gradation

**Where it applies:** West Bengal; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - **Quantum of Assistance:** One time reimbursement of ₹2,000/-. 

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Labour Department.
