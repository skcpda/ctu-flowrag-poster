# Boneej Scheme

**Short Title:** BNS

**Launch Date:** 2016-07-26

**Scheme For:** Infra

**Tags:** Boneej, Financial Assistance, Assam

**Objective** The &amp;amp;quot;Boneej Scheme&amp;amp;quot; is a flagship program of the Government of Assam in India, aimed at promoting entrepreneurship and generating employment opportunities for the youth in the state. The scheme is focused on providing financial assistance and support to young entrepreneurs who are willing to start their own ventures. Under the Boneej Scheme, the government provides financial assistance in the form of seed money to selected entrepreneurs. The scheme also offers various support services such as training, mentoring, and networking opportunities to help entrepreneurs develop their skills, build their businesses, and access markets. &lt;br&gt; **Area of Implementation** All districts excluding those within the jurisdiction Karbi- Anglong Autonomous District Council, NC Hills Autonomous District Council and Bodoland Territorial Autonomous Districts would be covered. &lt;br&gt; **District Target** - A district-wise target shall be fixed by the Commissionerate of Industries &amp;amp; Commerce, Assam on the basis of rural population in the district and shall be communicated to the concern DICC. &lt;br&gt;

**Who is this for:** Business Entity; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Micro finance

**Where it applies:** Assam; State

**Benefits / Assistance:** **Benefits** • It is proposed to provide Rs. 25,000 ( Rupees twenty-Five thousand ) as a grant to the industries located in Rural areas only where the annual turnover is less than Rs.5 lakh

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Industries and Commerce Department.

Briefdescription: "Boneej" a special scheme to assist the rural industries of the state and it was announced by the Hon'ble Finance Minister's in his Budget speech on 26th July, 2016
