# Protection Against Domestic Violence

**Short Title:** PADV

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Women Protection, Domestic Violence, Counseling, Mediation

The scheme &amp;quot;Protection Against Domestic Violence&amp;quot; by the Women and Child Development Department, Government of Haryana, aims to support women who are subjected to physical, emotional, verbal, sexual, or economic abuse within a domestic relationship. The scheme facilitates timely and confidential intervention by trained counselors and protection officers. Women victims can receive emotional and legal assistance, mediation, and safety measures, including shelter and medical aid where needed.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Women and Child; Sector: Psychosocial support/counselling

**Where it applies:** Haryana; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - **Counseling and Mediation Support:** Victims are provided with trained counselors for psychological support and help in mediation between the victim and the perpetrator if required.

**Benefit Type:** In Kind

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Women and Child Development Department.
