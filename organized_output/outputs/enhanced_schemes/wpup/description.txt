# Widow Pension - Uttar Pradesh

**Short Title:** WPUP

**Launch Date:** 2017-06-16

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Social Welfare, Financial Assistance

The Uttar Pradesh government, recognizing the unique challenges faced by widows living within the state, has implemented the Widow Pension Scheme. This compassionate initiative, administered by the Women Welfare Department, offers a vital form of financial assistance to these vulnerable women.  The scheme provides a monthly pension, acknowledging the significant impact of losing a spouse on household income and stability. This crucial support empowers widows to navigate the hardships of their situations, rebuild their lives with dignity, and secure a measure of financial security. The Widow Pension Scheme stands as a testament to the government&#39;s commitment to the well-being of its widowed citizens, fostering hope and fostering a path towards a brighter future

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Pension

**Where it applies:** Uttar Pradesh; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** Financial Assistance : ₹1,000/- per month

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Exclusions / Not eligible:** > The applicant should not be receiving pension from any other scheme of the state or central government.

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Department of Women & Child Development.

**References / Annexures:** - **Guidelines**: https://sspy-up.gov.in/docs/widow_Guidlines_2021.pdf - **Department Website**: https://mahilakalyan.up.nic.in/OurProgram.aspx - **Apply Online**: https://sspy-up.gov.in/

**Additional Information:**
Briefdescription: Widow pension in Uttar Pradesh is a social welfare scheme by the Government of Uttar Pradesh to provide financial assistance to widows residing in the state. The scheme is administered by the Women Welfare Department of the state government.
