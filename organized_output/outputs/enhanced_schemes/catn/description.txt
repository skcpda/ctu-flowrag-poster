# Child Adoption - Tamil Nadu

**Short Title:** CATN

**Scheme For:** Family

**Tags:** Adoption, Child, Accommodation, Parents, NGO

The scheme &quot;Child Adoption - Tamil Nadu&quot; was launched by the Social Welfare and Women Empowerment Department, Government of Tamil Nadu. The scheme aims to provide a family for orphaned, abandoned, destitute and neglected children. According to Central Adoption Resource Authority (CARA), “adoption is a process by which a child who is abandoned / separated from biological parents, becomes the legitimate child of a new set of parents referred to as adoptive parents, with all the rights, privileges and responsibilities that are attached to this relationship”.

**Who is this for:** Family; Category: Women and Child; Sector: Childcare

**Where it applies:** Tamil Nadu; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - Accommodation, food, and health care to the babies till they are given in adoption. - A family to the destitute child and a child to childless parents. > **Special concessions are given to female government employees to adopt children:** - Special adoption leave of 270 days is extended to female government servants of the state government who adopt a child below one year. The leave is eligible only from the date of legalization of adoption.

**Benefit Type:** In Kind

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Department of Social Welfare and Women Empowerment.
