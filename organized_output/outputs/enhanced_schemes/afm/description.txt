# Assistance for Marriage

**Short Title:** AFM

**Scheme For:** Family

**Tags:** Financial Assistance, Marriage, Differently-abled Person, Children

The scheme &quot;Assistance for Marriage&quot; was launched by the Department for the Welfare of Differently Abled Persons, Government of Tamil Nadu. Under this scheme, a sum of ₹2,000/- is given to meet the marriage expenses of the differently abled person or his son or daughter. The applications are accepted offline in the prescribed form available at the District Differently Abled Welfare Office.

**Who is this for:** Individual, Family; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Financial assistance

**Where it applies:** Tamil Nadu; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** Financial Assistance of ₹2,000/-.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Welfare of Differently Abled Persons Department.
