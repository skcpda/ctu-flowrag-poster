# Scheme for Assistance to Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises (MSME): Assistance for Interest Subsidy (Manufacturing Sector)

**Short Title:** SAMSMEIS

**Launch Date:** 2020-08-07

**Scheme For:** Infra

**Tags:** Business, Entrepreneurship, MSMEs, Start Up, Interest Subsidy

The scheme &amp;quot;Scheme for Assistance to Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises (MSME)&amp;quot; is an umbrella scheme launched by the Industries and Mines Department, Gujarat. It aims to empower the state&amp;#39;s dynamic MSME sector, which significantly contributes to employment, GDP, and exports. Effective from August 7th, 2020 to August 6th, 2025, this initiative supports entrepreneurs and strengthens MSMEs, making them globally competitive. The component &amp;quot;Assistance for Interest Subsidy (Manufacturing Sector)&amp;quot; aims to support manufacturing enterprises by providing an interest subsidy on term loans. The subsidy percentage and duration vary based on the category of the taluka. Additional benefits are available for differently-abled entrepreneurs, women entrepreneurs, registered startups, and young entrepreneurs below 35 years.

**Who is this for:** Business Entity, Industries; Category: Business & Entrepreneurship; Sector: Setting up / start-up / entrepreneurship

**Where it applies:** Gujarat; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** **Financial Assistance:** **Category of Taluka** **Quantum of Incentive** [Category I](https://imd.gujarat.gov.in/Document/I%20Branch_2297_02_Nov_2020_98.pdf)7% on Term Loan with a maximum amount of ₹35,00,000/- per annum for a period of 7 years[Category II](https://imd.gujarat.gov.in/Document/I%20Branch_2297_02_Nov_2020_98.pdf)6% on Term Loan with a maximum amount of ₹30,00,000/- per annum for a period of 6 years [Category III](https://imd.gujarat.gov.in/Document/I%20Branch_2297_02_Nov_2020_98.pdf) &amp;amp;amp;amp; Municipal Corporation Areas5% on Term Loan with a maximum amount of ₹25,00,000/- per annum for a period of 5 years

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Industries and Mines Department.
