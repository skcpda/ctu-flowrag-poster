# Organisation of Cultural Programs

**Short Title:** OCPS

**Scheme For:** Infra

**Tags:** Fair, National Events, Festivals, Cultural Groups, Artists, Troypes

The scheme &quot;Organisation of Cultural Programs&quot; by the Department of Culture and Endowments, Govt of Uttarakhand, aims to promote regional and national cultural events through listed cultural troupes and artists. Through this scheme, honorarium and travel allowances are provided to cultural troupes/artists for participating in various fairs, festivals, and national events both within and outside the state. The scheme is implemented by the Directorate of Culture, Government of Uttarakhand. Cultural groups are selected through auditions and empanelment based on qualifications and domicile.

**Who is this for:** Artists; Category: Sports & Culture; Sector: Culture

**Where it applies:** Uttarakhand; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** 1. Group Leader - Honorarium: ₹1,000/- 1. Other Artists Honorarium: ₹800/- 1. Travel Allowance: ₹500/- 1. Other Artists Travel Allowance: ₹400/- 1. A-Grade Folk Singers: ₹7,500/- 1. B-Grade Folk Singers: ₹4,250/- 1. Additional Travel Allowance: ₹300/- > Bus Fare (Ordinary Class) and Second-Class Train Fare are reimbursed  > **Mode of Disbursement:** - Direct payment to artists/troupes by the department > **Frequency of Disbursement:** - Disbursed per event/program participation

**Benefit Type:** Composite

**Exclusions / Not eligible:** - Artists or troupes that are not listed or empaneled by the department will be considered ineligible.

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Department of Culture and Endowments.
