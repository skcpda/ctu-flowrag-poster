# Professional Education (PEJKBOCWWB)

**Short Title:** PFJKBOCWWB

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Worker, Construction, Labour, Student, Education

The &quot;Professional Education&quot; scheme by the Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board, Labour Department, Jammu &amp; Kashmir, provides financial assistance to the children of registered workers who have engaged in at least 90 days of building or construction work in the preceding 12 months. Under this scheme, regular students receive ₹10,000/- for Para Medical and ITI diploma courses, ₹30,000/- for 3-year engineering or other recognized diploma courses, and ₹50,000/- for professional or technical degree courses.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Education & Learning; Sector: Scholarships and student finance

**Where it applies:** Jammu and Kashmir; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** > **Financial Assistance based on the Educational Qualification -** - Para Medical Diploma Courses (Regular Students): ₹10,000/-. - ITI Diploma Courses (Regular Students): ₹10,000/-. - 3-Year Engineering / Any Other Recognized Diploma Courses (Regular Students): ₹30,000/-. - Professional / Technical Degree Courses (Regular Students): ₹50,000/-.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Department of Labour and Employment.
