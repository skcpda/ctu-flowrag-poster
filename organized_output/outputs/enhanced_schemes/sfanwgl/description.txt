# Scheme of Financial Assistance to New Writers of Gujarati Language and Literature for Publishing Original Works of Creative and Fine Literature

**Short Title:** SFANWGL

**Tags:** Gujarati Literature, Author, Book Printing Aid

The &amp;quot;Scheme of Financial Assistance to New Writers of Gujarati Language and Literature for Publishing Original Works of Creative and Fine Literature&amp;quot; has been designed to encourage first-time authors who lack financial means to publish original and high-quality creative works in the Gujarati language. Administered by the Gujarat Sahitya Academy, the scheme provides grants to cover partial printing costs for eligible manuscripts. Works eligible for assistance include novels, short stories, essays, poetry, and plays that promote cultural awareness, national unity, and intellectual development. The manuscript must be original, unpublished, and reviewed positively by expert evaluators.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Sports & Culture; Sector: Culture

**Where it applies:** Gujarat; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** > **Financial Assistance:** - **For manuscripts with up to 112 pages:** Up to ₹10,000/-.- **For manuscripts exceeding 112 pages:** Up to ₹15,000/-. > **Printing Size Guidelines:** - **For “Crown” size:** ₹2,100/- (paperback), ₹2,200/- (hardcover).- **For “Demy” size:** ₹2,300/- (paperback), ₹2,500/- (hardcover).- **For “Double Crown” size:** ₹2,700/- (paperback), ₹2,800/- (hardcover). > **Note:** The lower of the printing cost or the above ceiling will be granted as assistance.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: Gujarat Sahitya Academy; Nodal Department: Sports, Youth and Cultural Activities Department.

**References / Annexures:** - **Guidelines**: https://gujaratsahityaacademy.com/assets/yojana_rules/2_Navodit_Niyamo202526.pdf

**Additional Information:**
Briefdescription: The scheme aims to financially support new writers in the Gujarati language who wish to publish original works of creative and literary merit.
