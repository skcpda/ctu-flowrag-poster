#  Burial and Ex-gratia Payment Scheme in Case of Death of Unregistered Laborer During Work at Construction Site

**Short Title:** BAEPSICODOULDWACT

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Building Worker, Construction Workers, Unregistered Laborer, Death

Launched in 2014, the &quot; Burial and Ex-gratia Payment Scheme in Case of Death of Unregistered Laborer During Work at Construction Site&quot; is a welfare initiative by the M.P. Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board, Government of Madhya Pradesh. The scheme provides funeral assistance of ₹3,000 and ex-gratia payments in the event of death or permanent disability of unregistered laborers during construction work. It supports construction workers in the unorganized sector who are not registered under the Board and lack a valid identity card.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Financial assistance

**Where it applies:** Madhya Pradesh; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** 1. **Funeral Assistance:** ₹3,000 payable in case of death of a construction laborer.1. **Grace Amount:** - Death due to an accident during construction work: ₹4,00,000/-. - Permanent disability due to an accident during construction work: ₹2,00,000/-.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Labour Department.
