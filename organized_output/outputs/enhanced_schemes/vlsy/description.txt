# Self-Employment Scheme: Vahan Loan Sahay Yojana

**Short Title:** SESVLSY

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Business, Self Employment, Vehicle Loan

&amp;quot;Vahan Loan Sahay Yojana&amp;quot; is a component under the &amp;quot;Self-Employment Scheme&amp;quot; introduced by the Social Justice and Empowerment Department, Gujarat. Under this scheme, self-employed individuals can avail of financial assistance as on-road unit cost for purchasing vehicles such as rickshaws, loading rickshaws, Maruti cars, jeep taxis, etc. The scheme is being implemented by the Gujarat Unreserved Education and Economical Development Corporation.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Banking,Financial Services and Insurance; Sector: Loan

**Where it applies:** Gujarat; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** **Financial Assistance:** On-road unit cost for vehicles like rickshaws, loading rickshaws, Maruti, jeep-taxi, etc. 

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: Gujarat Unreserved Education And Economical Development Corporation; Nodal Department: Social Justice and Empowerment Department.
