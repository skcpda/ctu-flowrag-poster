# Solar Inverter Charger

**Short Title:** SIC

**Scheme For:** Family

**Tags:** Renewable Energy, Solar Inverter, Subsidy, Green Energy Initiative

The &amp;quot;Solar Inverter Charger&amp;quot; by the Renewable Energy Department, Government of Haryana, aims to promote sustainable energy practices, reduce the dependency on grid electricity, and provide financial support for the installation of solar inverter chargers in domestic households. This initiative targets families with existing inverters who have not yet adopted solar technology, thereby enabling energy-efficient solutions and reducing carbon footprint. 

**Who is this for:** Family; Category: Agriculture,Rural & Environment; Sector: Mechanization- solar power, farming systems

**Where it applies:** Haryana; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - **For 320-watt Solar Inverter Charger System:** A subsidy of ₹6,000/- is provided. - **For 640-watt Solar Inverter Charger System:** A subsidy of ₹10,000/- is provided.  

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Exclusions / Not eligible:** - The applicant should not have installed a Solar Inverter Charger in the past 5 years under the Department Scheme.

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Renewable Energy Department.

**References / Annexures:** - **Guidelines**: https://kms.saralharyana.nic.in/ViewDoc?Id=E1fwq0pgAw75GA3jMlieDg==
