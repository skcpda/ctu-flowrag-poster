# Purchase of Vehicle for Commercial Hiring Purpose for Persons with Disabilities 

**Short Title:** PVCPPD

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Vehicle, Commercial Hiring, PwD, Disability, Loan

The scheme &amp;quot;Purchase of Vehicle for Commercial Hiring Purpose for Persons with Disabilities&amp;quot; is implemented by the Haryana Backward Classes and Economically Weaker Sections Kalyan Nigam, Government of Haryana. Under the scheme, loans are provided to differently-abled persons for commercial hiring purposes up to ₹10.00 lakhs, and for the purchase of special category vehicles like JCBs, trucks, road rollers, etc., loans up to ₹25.00 lakhs are available. Permanent residents of Haryana with a disability of 40% or more are eligible to avail the benefits under this scheme.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Banking,Financial Services and Insurance; Sector: Loan

**Where it applies:** Haryana; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** 1. Under this scheme, loans are provided to differently abled persons for commercial hiring up to ₹10,000,00/- and for purchasing special category vehicles such as JCB, Trucks, Road rollers, etc., with loans up to ₹25,000,00/-.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: Haryana Backward Classes And Economically Weaker Sections Kalyan Nigam; Nodal Department: Welfare of Scheduled Caste & Backward Classes Department.
