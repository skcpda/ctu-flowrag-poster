# Schedule Tribe Pre-Metric Scholarship Class 06 To 08

**Short Title:** STPMSC

**Launch Date:** 1984-04-01

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Financial Assistance, Learning, Student Finance, Education

The Rajasthan government aims to provide educational opportunities to students belonging to Schedule Tribe student, specifically those studying in grades 6 to 8. This program targets students from Schedule Tribe whose parents/guardians are not income taxpayers and not getting any other type of scholarship or allowance for study from central, state / public sources.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Education & Learning; Sector: Scholarships and student finance

**Where it applies:** Rajasthan; State

**DBT Scheme:** Yes (Direct Benefit Transfer)

**Benefits / Assistance:** - For Schedule Caste Boy Students Rs. 75/- per month. - For Schedule Caste Girl Students Rs. 125/- per month.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Education (Elementary and Secondary) Department.

**References / Annexures:** - **Guidelines**: https://schemes.rajasthan.gov.in/scheme/detail/268

**Additional Information:**
Briefdescription: In the year 1984, Rajasthan Government started a scholarship to encourage the education of the Schedule Tribe.
