# Bihar Shatabdi Asangathit Karyakshetra Kamagaar Evan Shilpakar Samajik Suraksha Yojana: Accidental Injuries

**Short Title:** BSAKAI

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Social Security, Unorganised Worker, Craftsmen, Accidental Injuries, Medical Assistance

The “Bihar Shatabdi Asangathit Karyakshetra Kamagaar Evan Shilpakar Samajik Suraksha Yojana” launched by the Labour Resources Department, Bihar, aims to provide social security through various types of financial assistance to workers and artisans in the unorganized sector. &lt;br&gt;

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Health & Wellness; Sector: Financial assistance

**Where it applies:** Bihar; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** **Financial Assistance:** ₹5,000/-.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Labour Resources Department.

**References / Annexures:** - **Guidelines**: https://state.bihar.gov.in/labour/cache/40/14-May-21/SHOW_DOCS/Schemes-1.pdf

**Additional Information:**
Briefdescription: The component “Accidental Injuries” under the “Bihar Shatabdi Asangathit Karyakshetra Kamagaar Evan Shilpakar Samajik Suraksha Yojana” launched by the Labour Resources Department, Bihar, provides financial assistance to the workers or craftsmen in case of accidental injuries.
