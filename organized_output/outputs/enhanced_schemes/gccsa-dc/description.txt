# "Grant to Coir Cluster/Societies/Agency" Component of the "Development of Coir" Scheme

**Short Title:** GCCSA-DC

**Scheme For:** Infra

**Tags:** Coir, Society, Grant, Artisan, Youth

The &quot;Grant to Coir Cluster/Societies/Agency&quot; Component of the &quot;Development of Coir&quot; Scheme by the Department of Industries and Commerce, Union Territory of Puducherry, aims to foster the development of coir industries. The scheme encourages unemployed rural youth and artisan individuals to participate in the manufacturing and sales of coir products.

**Who is this for:** Registered Societies, Business Entity; Category: Business & Entrepreneurship; Sector: Sales of goods, services & data protection

**Where it applies:** Puducherry; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** Grant of ₹2,00,000 per year for five years to cover administrative and developmental expenses.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: District Industries Centre Organization.
