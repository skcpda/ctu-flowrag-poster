# Nirmaan Shramik  ke Bachchon Hetu  Utkrasht Khel Protsaahan Yojana

**Short Title:** NSBHUK

**Launch Date:** 2017-05-23

**Scheme For:** Family

**Tags:** Sports, Financial Assistance

Labor Department of Chhattisgarh State started this scheme &amp;amp;quot;Nirmaan Shramik Ke Bachchon Hetu Utkrasht Khel Protsaahan Yojana&amp;amp;quot; for Construction Workers on 23 May 2017. The main objective of the scheme to provide financial to encourage the studdents for sports. &amp;lt;br&amp;gt; &amp;lt;br&amp;gt;

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Sports & Culture

**Where it applies:** Chhattisgarh; State

**DBT Scheme:** Yes (Direct Benefit Transfer)

**Benefits / Assistance:** Incentive amount will be provided to the children who perform excellently at the district / division / state / national level in all recognized sports as follows - 1. Amount at district level: ₹ 1,000/- 1. Amount at divisional level: ₹ 2,000/- 1. Amount at state level: ₹ 5,000/- 1. Amount at National level : ₹ 50,000/-

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Labour Department.
