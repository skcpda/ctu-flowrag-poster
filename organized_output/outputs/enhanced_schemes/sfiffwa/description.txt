# PMMSY: Inputs for fresh water Aquaculture including Composite fish culture, Scampi, Pangasius, Tilapia etc - Haryana

**Short Title:** IFFWA

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Aquaculture, Fish Farmers, Subsidy, Entrepreneurs

The Pradhan Mantri Matsya Sampada Yojana: Inputs for fresh water Aquaculture, including Composite fish culture, Scampi, Pangasius, Tilapia, etc, introduced by the Ministry Of Fisheries, Animal Husbandry &amp; Dairying, Government of India, aims to expand fisheries production in Haryana. This centrally sponsored scheme is jointly funded 50% by the Central Government and 50% by the State Government. It provides input cost subsidies for the first crop in newly constructed ponds under PMMSY, aiming to promote aquaculture in urban and semi-urban areas. 

**Who is this for:** Business Entity, Individual; Category: Agriculture,Rural & Environment; Sector: Fishing and hunting

**Where it applies:** Haryana; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - The project cost is: 4,00,000/- per hectare > **Subsidy Distribution:** 1. 40% of the project cost for General Category beneficiaries 1. 60% of the project cost for SC/Women beneficiaries

**Benefit Type:** Composite

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Fisheries Department.
