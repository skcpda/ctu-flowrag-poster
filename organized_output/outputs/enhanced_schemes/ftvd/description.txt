# Free Training for Vehicle Drivers

**Short Title:** FTVD

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Free Training, Scheduled Caste, Scheduled Tribe, Driving Skills, Self-employment, Vehicle Driver

The &amp;quot;Free Training for Vehicle Drivers&amp;quot; scheme was implemented by the Transport Department, Government of Uttarakhand. The scheme aims to enhance driving skills and promote self-employment among scheduled caste and scheduled tribe individuals. Under this scheme, free professional driving training is provided to candidates for light, heavy, and refresher vehicle courses. The scheme also aims to reduce road accidents and improve driving capabilities in the SC/ST communities. The scheme is implemented by the Institute of Driving and Traffic Research (IDTR), Dehradun.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Skills & Employment; Sector: Training and Skill Up-gradation

**Where it applies:** Uttarakhand; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** > **Free Driving Training:** - Training is offered in Light Motor Vehicle (Commercial), Heavy Motor Vehicle (Commercial), and Refresher Courses. > **Validity** - There is no fixed validity period. > **Renewal** - No renewal is required. > **Fees** - **Free Training** – Training under this scheme will be completely free of cost. No fee will be charged from the applicant.

**Benefit Type:** In Kind

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: State Transport Department.
