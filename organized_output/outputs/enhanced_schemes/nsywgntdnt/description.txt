# New Swarnim Yojana (for women only) GNTDNT

**Short Title:** NSYWGNTDNT

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Self Employment, Business, Loan, Nomadic And Denotified Castes

The scheme &quot;New Swarnim Yojana (For Women Only)&quot; by the Social Justice and Empowerment Department, Government of Gujarat, aims to empower women of nomadic and denotified castes living below the poverty line to achieve economic upliftment by getting self-employment with dignity. Under this scheme, women beneficiaries will be able to do business/occupation of their choice. The Gujarat Nomadic and Denotified Tribes Development Corporation implement the scheme. 

**Who is this for:** Family; Category: Business & Entrepreneurship; Sector: Setting up / start-up / entrepreneurship

**Where it applies:** Gujarat; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** 1. The maximum loan limit is up to ₹2,00,000/-. 1. The interest rate will be 5% per annum. 1. Under this scheme, 100% loan of the business/occupation amount is provided. 1. The loan amount will consist of 95% National Corporation contribution, 5% State Government contribution and zero beneficiary contribution.  > **Repayment:** The loan amount, including interest, will have to be repaid in 60 equal monthly instalments. 

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: Gujarat Nomadic And Denotified Tribes Development Corporation; Nodal Department: Social Justice and Empowerment Department.

**References / Annexures:** - **Official Guidelines**: https://sje.gujarat.gov.in/gntdnt/New-Swarnima-Yojana - **Online Application**: https://gndcdconline.gujarat.gov.in/

**Additional Information:**
Briefdescription: The scheme aims to achieve economic upliftment by providing self-employment with dignity. Through this scheme, loans for self-employment at an annual interest rate are provided to women beneficiaries of nomadic and denotified castes living below the poverty line.
