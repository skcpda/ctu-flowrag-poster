# Goa Welfare / Pension Scheme For Seafarers

**Short Title:** Gwpss

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Pension, Seafarer, Seaman, Financial Assistance, Social Welfare, Retirement, Senior Citizen

The scheme &quot;Goa Welfare / Pension Scheme for Seafarers&quot; by the Home Department, Government of Goa, provides financial assistance in the form of a Gross Pension of ₹ 2,500/- per month to the beneficiaries. The applicant should be a retired seaman. The age of the retired seaman should be 60 years or above.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Pension

**Where it applies:** Goa; State

**Benefits / Assistance:** Financial Assistance in the form of a **Gross Pension of ₹ 2,500/- per month.** **NOTE:** If the applicant is in receipt of a monthly pension under the Dayanand Social Security Scheme / Griha Aadhaar Scheme / Kala Samman Scheme, the amount whereof is less than the Gross Pension available under these schemes, then he/she will be entitled to receive only the difference, between the amounts of Gross Pension available under the above schemes.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Home Department.

**References / Annexures:** - **Guidelines**: https://www.goa.gov.in/wp-content/uploads/2022/04/seafarers-scheme_compressed-1.pdf
