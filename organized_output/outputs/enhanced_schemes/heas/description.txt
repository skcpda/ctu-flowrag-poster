# Higher Education Assistance Scheme

**Short Title:** HEAS

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Education, Labour, Financial Assistance, Student, Welfare, Merit

The &quot;Higher Education Assistance Scheme&quot; by the Gujarat Labour Welfare Board, Labour &amp; Employment Department, Government of Gujarat, aims to provide encouragement to children of workers and reduce the financial burden of education by offering higher educational assistance to meritorious students, addressing the economic challenges faced by workers&#39; families in pursuing quality education for their children. The scheme provides financial assistance of ₹20,000/- for students securing admission in Bachelor of Medicine Bachelor of Surgery courses and ₹10,000/- for students securing admission in any other graduate level courses for students who have passed Standard 12 board examination in the year 2024 with 70 percent or above percentile. The applicant must be a child of a worker whose parent is working in a factory or institution in Gujarat state and whose parent has been contributing to the Labour Welfare Fund office for the last one year. The applications for this scheme are accepted online. The application deadline is the last date of February of the respective year. 

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Financial assistance

**Where it applies:** Gujarat; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** > **Financial Assistance for Medical Education** Direct Benefit Transfer of ₹20,000/- for students securing admission in Bachelor of Medicine Bachelor of Surgery courses. > **Financial Assistance for Other Graduate Courses** Direct Benefit Transfer of ₹10,000/- for students securing admission in any other graduate level courses. 

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Exclusions / Not eligible:** - The applicant must not have secured admission in any foreign educational institution. - The applicant must not be pursuing education abroad under this scheme. 

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Labour and Employment Department.
