# Divyang Shiksha Protsahan Yojana

**Short Title:** DSPY

**Launch Date:** 2016-01-01

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Education, Disabilities, Children, Financial

Divyang Shiksha Protsahan Yojana under the Department of Social Justice and Empowerment of Persons with Disabilities government of Madhya Pradesh, provides financial support to disabled children. The Divyang Shiksha Protsahan Yojana aims to promote and facilitate inclusive education for persons with disabilities (Divyangjan). This scheme aims to ensure that individuals with disabilities have equal access to quality education and educational opportunities  

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Pension

**Where it applies:** Madhya Pradesh; State/ UT

**DBT Scheme:** Yes (Direct Benefit Transfer)

**Benefits / Assistance:** > Financial assistance : ₹ 600/- per month.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Department of Social Justice and Empowerment of Persons with Disabilities.

**References / Annexures:** - **Guidelines**: https://socialsecurity.mp.gov.in/Scheme/SSCWSNP.aspx - **Apply Portal**: https://socialsecurity.mp.gov.in/Home.aspx

**Additional Information:**
Briefdescription: Under this scheme, the Madhya Pradesh government provides financial support to Disabled children of the state.
