# Grant of Transport Allowance to Differently Abled Persons

**Short Title:** GTADAP

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Transport, Allowance, Disability, PwD

The scheme “Grant of Transport Allowance to Differently Abled Persons&quot; was launched by the Department of Social Welfare, Government of Puducherry. In this scheme, a fixed sum ₹ 300/- per month shall be paid as Transport Allowance to the differently abled individuals.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Transport & Infrastructure; Sector: Financial assistance

**Where it applies:** Puducherry; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** ₹ 300/- per month shall be paid through Anganwadi Centres as Transport Allowance.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Social Welfare Department, Puducherry.
