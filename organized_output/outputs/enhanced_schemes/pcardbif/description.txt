# Primary Cooperative Agriculture and Rural Development Bank: For Inland Fishing

**Short Title:** PCARDBIF

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Inland Fishing, Loan, Farmer, Agriculture And Rural Development Bank

The &amp;quot;Primary Cooperative Agriculture and Rural Development Bank: For Inland Fishing&amp;quot; scheme launched by the Co-operation, Food and Consumer Protection Department, Tamil Nadu, aims to support farmers interested in inland fishing by providing financial assistance up to ₹3,30,000/- per hectare. The loan has an interest rate of 11% - 12%, which is subject to change. This financial aid aims to empower farmers to establish or expand inland fishing operations and increase their income. &lt;br&gt;

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Banking,Financial Services and Insurance; Sector: Loan

**Where it applies:** Tamil Nadu; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - **Access to Loan:** ₹3,30,000/- per hectare. - **Interest Rate:** 11% to 12% (subject to change). 

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Co-operation, Food and Consumer Protection Department.

**References / Annexures:** - **Guidelines**: https://www.tn.gov.in/scheme/data_view/3566

**Additional Information:**
Briefdescription: The scheme offers loans to farmers for inland fishing projects, with a loan amount of up to ₹3,30,000/- per hectare at an interest rate between 11% and 12%.
