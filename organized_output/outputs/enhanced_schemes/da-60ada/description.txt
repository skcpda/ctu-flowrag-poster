# 60% and above Disability Allowances

**Short Title:** 60ADA

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Pension, Disability, PwD, Social Welfare, Allowance

&quot;60% and Disability Allowance&quot; is a pension scheme by the Directorate of Social Welfare and Social Education, Government of Tripura. The scheme aims to provide financial assistance in the form of a monthly pension of ₹2,000/- to differently-abled persons with a disability of 60% and above. The applicant should be submitted in person to the Child Development Project Officer.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Pension

**Where it applies:** Tripura; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** Pension of ₹2,000/- per month.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Directorate Of Social Welfare & Social Education.
