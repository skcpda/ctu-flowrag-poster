# Maintenance Grant to Institutions and Organizations Engaged in Cultural Activities

**Short Title:** MGIOECA

**Scheme For:** Infra

**Tags:** Financial Assistance, Maintenance Grant, Organization

The &amp;quot;Maintenance Grant to Institutions and Organizations Engaged in Cultural Activities&amp;quot; scheme was launched by the Arts and Culture Department, Puducherry. The scheme aims to provide a maintenance grant to the organizations engaged in cultural activities. The applications are accepted offline.

**Who is this for:** Business Entity; Category: Sports & Culture; Sector: Culture

**Where it applies:** Puducherry; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - Financial assistance gives for maintenance ₹3,000/- and cultural activities ₹5,000/-.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Arts and Culture Department, Puducherry.

**References / Annexures:** - **Citizens Charter**: https://art.py.gov.in//sites/default/files/ecit1.pdf - **Official Website**: https://art.py.gov.in/ - **Application Form**: https://art.py.gov.in/sites/default/files/application-13.pdf
