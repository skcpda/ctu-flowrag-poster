# Punjabi Language Scholarship Scheme

**Short Title:** PLSS

**Scheme For:** #N/A

**Tags:** Scholarship, Punjabi Language, Financial Support, Merit, Student

The scheme “Punjabi Language Scholarship Scheme” was introduced during the year 2005-2006 by the Department of Education, Government of Haryana under the Education Encouragement for Excellence (EEE) – Scholarships for High/Senior Secondary School Students. Under this scheme, scholarships are awarded to students on the basis of merit in Punjabi subject in 8th class and 10th Class examinations conducted by Haryana Board of School Education and continuing study Punjabi subject in the 9th Class and 11th Class. These scholarships are renewed in next classes on satisfactory performance. **Objectives:** • To encourage students to excel education. • To promote the Punjabi Language in the State.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Education & Learning; Sector: Scholarships and student finance

**Where it applies:** Haryana; State/ UT

**DBT Scheme:** Yes (Direct Benefit Transfer)

**Benefits / Assistance:** **Scholarship Amount:** 1. ₹ 50/- per month for students of Class IX &amp; X. 1. ₹ 75/- per month for students of Class XI &amp; XII. > **Mode of Disbursement:** > Scholarships are directly transferred to the student&#39;s bank account. > **Frequency of Disbursement:** > Scholarships are disbursed monthly. > **Validity:** > Scholarships are renewed in the next classes based on satisfactory performance.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: School Education Department.
