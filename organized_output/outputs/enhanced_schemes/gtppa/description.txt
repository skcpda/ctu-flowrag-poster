# Gujarat Textile Policy: Payroll Assistance

**Short Title:** GTPPA

**Launch Date:** 2024-10-01

**Closing Date:** 2029-09-29

**Scheme For:** Infra

**Tags:** Business, Entrepreneurship, MSMEs, Textile Industry

The &amp;quot;Gujarat Textile Policy&amp;quot; is an umbrella scheme introduced by the Industries and Mines Department, Gujarat. It aimed at augmenting investments in the textile sector and strengthening the textile value chain across each sub-sector, while also focusing on strengthening the garments and apparel as well as technical textiles industry. Effective from October 1st, 2024 to September 29th, 2029, this initiative focuses on reducing the carbon footprint and promoting green growth, thereby making the sector globally competitive and environmentally sustainable. The component &amp;quot;Payroll Assistance&amp;quot; provides financial support to industrial units by offering monetary assistance for the wages of workers. This scheme aims to promote employment and encourage industries in Gujarat, particularly in sectors like Garments, Apparel, Made-ups, and Technical Textiles, including Composite Units.

**Who is this for:** Business Entity, Industries; Category: Business & Entrepreneurship; Sector: Setting up / start-up / entrepreneurship

**Where it applies:** Gujarat; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** **Financial Assistance** **Activity****Payroll Assistance**Garment, Apparel&amp;amp;amp;amp; Made-ups (including Composite Unit) ₹5,000/- per Female Worker &amp;amp;amp;amp; ₹4,000/- per Male Worker per monthTechnical Textile (including Composite Unit) ₹3,000/- per Female Worker &amp;amp;amp;amp; ₹2,000 per Male Worker per month 

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Exclusions / Not eligible:** - An eligible Industrial Unit that has availed EPF reimbursement under any scheme of the Central / State Government, or any agency of the Central/ State Government for any period shall not be eligible for Payroll Assistance under this Policy for that period.

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Industries and Mines Department.
