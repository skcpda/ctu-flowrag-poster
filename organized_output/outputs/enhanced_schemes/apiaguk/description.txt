# Admission Process in All Government and Non-Government Schools of the State

**Short Title:** APAGNGSS

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Free Education, Student, Anganwadi Bal Vatika, Admission In School

The &amp;quot;Admission Process in All Government and Non-Government Schools of the State&amp;quot; scheme is implemented by the Basic Education Department under the Department of Education, Government of Uttarakhand. The scheme aims to provide free and accessible education for children from Class 1 to 8 by offering free education and essential learning materials, including books, shoe-bags, and meals, across all government and non-government schools in the state. 

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Education & Learning; Sector: Resources for schools

**Where it applies:** Uttarakhand; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** After admission in all Government and Non-Government schools of the state, children from Class 1 to 8 receive free education, meals, books, and shoe-bags. 

**Benefit Type:** In Kind

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: Basic Education Department, Government Of Uttarakhand; Nodal Department: Department Of Education.
