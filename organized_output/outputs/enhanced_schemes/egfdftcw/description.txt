# Ex-gratia for Death for the Construction Workers

**Short Title:** EGFDFTCW

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Labour, Building Worker, Construction Worker, Death Assistance, Nominees, Dependents, Ex-gratia

The &amp;quot;Ex-gratia for Death for the Construction Workers&amp;quot; by the Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board, Labour Department, U.T. Administration of Dadra and Nagar Haveli and Daman and Diu, is a welfare scheme for the registered workers of the board. Under this scheme, the Board will provide financial assistance to the nominees/ dependents in case of death of a registered member in the course of employment or due to any other reason.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Financial assistance

**Where it applies:** Dadra & Nagar Haveli and Daman & Diu; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - **For death in the course of employment:** ₹4,00,000/-.- **For death due to any other reason:** ₹2,00,000/-.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Labour & Employment Department.

**References / Annexures:** - **Guidelines**: https://www.daman.nic.in/websites/Printing-press-Daman/documents/2020/Extraordinary/Extra%20Ordinary%20No.%2029.pdf - **Guidelines**: https://bocwddd.in/welfare-schemes/dnh/payment-of-death-benefit - **Official Website**: https://bocwddd.in/
