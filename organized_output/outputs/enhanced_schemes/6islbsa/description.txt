# 6% Interest Subsidy on Loans taken through Banks for Study Abroad

**Short Title:** 6ISLBSA

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Student, Higher Education, Scheduled Tribe, Loan, Foreign Study

The &quot;6% Interest Subsidy on Loans taken through Banks for Study Abroad&quot; scheme was launched by the Tribal Development Department, Government of Gujarat. The scheme aims to support Scheduled Tribe (ST) students pursuing higher education abroad by reducing their financial burden. Under this scheme, beneficiaries receive an interest subsidy on education loans with a maximum subsidy for three years. 

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Education & Learning; Sector: Universities and higher education

**Where it applies:** Gujarat; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - Interest subsidy of 6% per annum on loans taken through banks for foreign education. - Maximum subsidy of ₹1,50,000 for 3 years. - Applicable on loans up to ₹25,00,000. 

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Tribal Development Department.

**References / Annexures:** - **Guidelines**: https://mariyojana.gujarat.gov.in/MoreDetails.aspx

**Additional Information:**
Briefdescription: The scheme aims to reduce financial stress for ST students pursuing foreign education by providing up to ₹1,50,000 subsidy on interest for 3 years.
