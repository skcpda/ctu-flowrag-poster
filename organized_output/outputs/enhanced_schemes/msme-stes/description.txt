# Msme Skill Training And Employment Scheme

**Short Title:** Msme-stes

**Launch Date:** 2021-04-01

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** MSME, Skill, Training, Employment, Stipend, Trainee

The scheme “MSME Skill Training and Employment Scheme” was launched by the Department of Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises, Government of Tamil Nadu during the years 2021-22. The scheme is implemented by the Commissionerate of Industries and Commerce at the State level, the Regional Joint Director of Industries and Commerce for Chennai district, and General Managers of the District Industries Centre for other districts in Tamil Nadu. **Objective:** The objective of the scheme is to fulfill the skilled manpower requirement of MSMEs by imparting necessary on-the-job training by the MSMEs themselves and thereby creating employment. The scheme will assist the MSME sector by sharing the training cost which otherwise would be borne by the enterprises themselves. **Training provider:** MSMEs will have to give employment based job oriented on the job training which will lead to certification. It will be the responsibility of the MSMEs to ensure that for the proposed training, the relevant capacities are available to them. 

**Who is this for:** Industries, Individual; Category: Skills & Employment; Sector: Training and Skill Up-gradation

**Where it applies:** Tamil Nadu; State

**Benefits / Assistance:** 1. Under the scheme, the subsidy will be provided to the MSMEs for training; subject to a maximum of ₹2000/- per month per candidate for a maximum of 6 months. 1. Certificates will be provided by the National Skill Development Corporation (NSDC), after the assessment of acquired skills by the trained candidates.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: Commissionerate Of Industries And Commerce , Government Of Tamil Nadu; Nodal Department: Micro Small and Medium Enterprises Department.
