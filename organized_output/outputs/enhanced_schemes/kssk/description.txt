# Kayaka Spoorthi Scheme Karnataka 

**Short Title:** KSSK

**Scheme For:** Infra

**Tags:** Financial Assistance, SHG, Business Activities, Leather Industry

The &quot;Kayaka Spoorthi Scheme&quot; aims to provide financial assistance to women Self-Help Groups (SHGs) engaged in the leather industry. The goal is to promote self-sufficiency and economic independence by enabling these women to start or expand business activities related to leatherwork. A group of 10 members receives financial support under this scheme.

**Who is this for:** Self Help Groups (SHGS); Category: Women and Child; Sector: Financial Assistance

**Where it applies:** Karnataka; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - Financial Assistance of ₹2,50,000/- per SHG with 10 members.  **NOTE:** - Each member gets ₹15,000/- as a subsidy and ₹10,000/- as a loan from the corporation. - 33% representation for women artisans and 5% for physically challenged individuals.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Social Welfare Department.
