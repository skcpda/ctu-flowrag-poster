# Mukhya Mantri Vidyarthi Kalyan Yojana

**Short Title:** MMVKY

**Launch Date:** 1991-04-23

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Education, Student, Financial Assistance, Scholarship, Skill Development

The scheme “Mukhya Mantri Vidyarthi Kalyan Yojana” is a Scholarship Scheme by the Department of Education, Government of Himachal Pradesh. The objective of the scheme is to provide financial assistance to school and college students in the form of scholarships. The scheme provides scholarships of upto ₹ 2,400 per month to the students from IRDP/BPL Families bonafide in Himachal Pradesh, and pursuing their study in Government / Government Aided Institutions in Himachal Pradesh.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Education & Learning; Sector: Scholarships and student finance

**Where it applies:** Himachal Pradesh; State

**DBT Scheme:** Yes (Direct Benefit Transfer)

**Benefits / Assistance:** > **Rates of Scholarship (per annum)** - Class 9th &amp; 10th: Boys: ₹ 300/-, Girls: ₹ 600/-. - Class 11th &amp; 12th: Boys: ₹ 800/-, Girls: ₹ 800/-. - College/University (Day Scholar): Boys: ₹ 1,200/-, Girls: ₹ 1,200/-. - College/University (Hostelers): Boys: ₹ 2,400/-, Girls: ₹ 2,400/-. 

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: National Scholarship Portal; Nodal Department: Education Department, Himachal Pradesh.
