# Gadiya Lohar Raw Material Purchase Grant-in-aid Scheme

**Short Title:** GLRMPGS

**Launch Date:** 1991-06-20

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Financial Assistance, Raw Material, Blacksmiths, Self-reliant, Business Opportunity

The scheme &amp;quot;Gadiya Lohar Raw Material Purchase Grant-in-Aid Scheme&amp;quot; was introduced by the Social Justice and Empowerment Department, Government of Rajasthan. Under this scheme, the state government provides a one-time financial assistance of ₹10,000/- to the Gadia Lohar community of Rajasthan for purchasing raw materials, helping them become self-reliant in their business.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Business & Entrepreneurship; Sector: Machine & skill up-Gradation

**Where it applies:** Rajasthan; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - Under this scheme, the member of Gadia Lohar community of Rajasthan receives a one-time financial grant of ₹10,000/- to purchase raw materials for their business.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Social Justice and Empowerment Department.
