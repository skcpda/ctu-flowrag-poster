# Scheme for Financial Assistance to Integrated Logistics Facilities: Interest Subsidy

**Short Title:** SFAILFIS

**Launch Date:** 2021-08-02

**Scheme For:** Infra

**Tags:** Logistics Facilities, Interest Subsidy, Business

The &amp;quot;Scheme for Financial Assistance to Integrated Logistics Facilities&amp;quot; is an umbrella scheme launched by the Industries and Mines Department, Gujarat under the Gujarat Integrated Logistics and Logistics Park Policy. Effective from August 2nd, 2021 to August 1st, 2026, this initiative aims to provide financial assistance to the private developer for developing Logistics Facilities and Logistics Park with all required infrastructure and logistic services. The component &amp;quot;Interest Subsidy&amp;quot; is designed to assist developers by reducing their interest burden on term loans. It is applicable only to new units or projects, enabling developers to improve their financial stability during the project&amp;#39;s development phase.

**Who is this for:** Business Entity, Industries; Category: Business & Entrepreneurship; Sector: Setting up / start-up / entrepreneurship

**Where it applies:** Gujarat; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - **Coverage of Moratorium Period:** Interest repayment during the moratorium period is also eligible for subsidy. - **Assistance on Actual Disbursement:** The subsidy is calculated based on the actual disbursed amount of the sanctioned term loan. - **Encourages Timely Repayment:** Incentivizes developers to maintain regular repayment schedules to avoid being declared defaulters.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Exclusions / Not eligible:** - Periods with default in principal repayment (90 days or more) are not eligible, even if interest is repaid during this time.

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Industries and Mines Department.

**References / Annexures:** - **Guidelines**: https://ic.gujarat.gov.in/documents/commondoc/2024/Revised_Gui_eline_for_the_%20Scheme_for_Financial_Assistance_to_Integrated_Logistics_Facilities.pdf
