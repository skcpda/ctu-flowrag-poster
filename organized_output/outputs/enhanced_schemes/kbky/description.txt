# Krshak Prashikshan Tatha Bhraman Karyakram

**Short Title:** KPBKY

**Launch Date:** 2015-10-20

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Farming, Farmer, Farm, Land, Financial Support

The Krishak Parisharam Bhraman Karyakram Yojana under the Horticulture and Food Processing Department of Madhya Pradesh, provide tour to the different countries and states/divisions/districts/trends outside the development block. To attract the farmers towards the production of horticulture crops by giving tours to the farmers regarding horticulture crops.  > **Objective:** 1. To expose farmers to diverse agricultural technologies, practices, and innovations. 1. Organize visits to different agricultural fields, research institutions, and agricultural universities. 1. Facilitate knowledge and experience exchange among farmers. 1. Promote entrepreneurship and innovation in the agriculture sector.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Agriculture,Rural & Environment; Sector: Financial assistance

**Where it applies:** Madhya Pradesh; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** 1. Farmer training within the state : ₹1,000/- per farmer per month (maximum 7 days) 1. Farmer training outside the state : ₹1,500/- per farmer per day. 1. Exposure visit outside the state to observe new technology (new component) : ₹1,500/- per farmer per day. 1. A total of ₹12,000/- for 20 trainees in one-day farmer training and tour, i.e. maximum expenditure per farmer is ₹500/- and honorarium to the host farmer is ₹100/-, thus the total maximum expenditure is Rs 600/- per farmer.

**Benefit Type:** Composite

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Horticulture and Food Processing Department.
