# Production of Foundation and Certified Seeds

**Short Title:** POFACS

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Agriculture, Farmer, Certified Seeds, Seed Production, Foundation Seeds

The scheme “Production of Foundation and Certified Seeds” by the Agriculture-Farmers Welfare Department, Tamil Nadu, is aimed at encouraging farmers to produce high-quality seeds. Under this initiative, farmers who produce and supply Foundation and Certified Class seeds to the Department of Agriculture are eligible to receive a subsidy.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Agriculture,Rural & Environment; Sector: Financial assistance

**Where it applies:** Tamil Nadu; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - **Financial Assistance:** ₹1,000/- per quintal.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Agriculture Department.

**References / Annexures:** - **Guidelines**: https://www.tn.gov.in/scheme/data_view/7612

**Additional Information:**
Briefdescription: The "Production of Foundation and Certified Seeds" scheme, provides subsidy to farmers who produce and supply Foundation and Certified seeds to the Department of Agriculture.
