# Mahila Samriddhi Yojana (GTKVN)

**Short Title:** MSYGTKVN

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Self-Employment, Business, Thakor Caste, Loan, Woman, Koli Caste

The &amp;quot;Mahila Samriddhi Yojana&amp;quot; is implemented by the Gujarat Thakor and Koli Vikas Nigam (GTKVN) under the Social Justice and Empowerment Department, Government of Gujarat. The scheme aims to empower women entrepreneurs from the Thakor and Koli communities by providing access to micro-loans for self-employment. Under this scheme, financial assistance in the form of low-interest loans is provided to eligible women beneficiaries to help them set up small businesses or trades based on their skills. 

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Women and Child; Sector: Financial Assistance

**Where it applies:** Gujarat; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - **Maximum Loan Amount:** Up to ₹60,000/-.- **Interest Rate:** 4% per annum.- **Loan Coverage:** Up to 100% of the loan will be given for the proposed business/trade.- **Repayment:** The loan amount will have to be repaid in 48 equal monthly installments (4 years) including interest. 

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: Gujarat Thakor And Koli Development Corporation; Nodal Department: Social Justice and Empowerment Department.

**References / Annexures:** - **Guidelines**: https://sje.gujarat.gov.in/gtkvn/Schemes/1450?lang=English
