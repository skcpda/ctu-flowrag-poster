# Kala Samman Scheme

**Short Title:** KSS

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Artist, Culture, Art, Financial Assistance, Award

The scheme &quot;Kala Samman Scheme&quot; is implemented by the Art and Culture Department, Government of Goa, to grant financial assistance to eminent artists who are in indigent circumstances as a token of respect for their contribution to the field of Art and culture.  > **Objectives** 1. To give financial support to the artists in indigent circumstances. 1. To grant monthly financial assistance to the artists who are in indigent conditions to meet their daily needs. 1. To recognize the contribution of artists in the field of Art and culture.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Sports & Culture; Sector: Culture

**Where it applies:** Goa; State

**Benefits / Assistance:** 1. Financial Assistance to the extent of ₹ 2,500/- per month every year. 1. However, the recipient of State Cultural Award shall be entitled for Financial Assistance to the extent of ₹ 3,200/- per month every year.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Art, Culture and Museums Department.
