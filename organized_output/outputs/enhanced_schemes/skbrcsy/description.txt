# Safai Karmakar Bahay Rogi Chikitsa Sahayata Yojana

**Short Title:** SKBRCSY

**Launch Date:** 2015-03-11

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Financial Assistance, Health, Wellness

Labor Department of Chhattisgarh State started this scheme &amp;quot;Safai Karmakar Bahay Rogi Chikitsa Sahayata Yojana&amp;quot; for unorganized worker on 11 March 2015. The main objective of the scheme to empower unorganized worker by providing required financial assistance for medical situation.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Health & Wellness; Sector: Financial assistance

**Where it applies:** Chhattisgarh; State

**Benefits / Assistance:** > Medical assistance up to Rs. 2,000/- in a year.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Labour Department.
