# Primary Cooperative Agriculture and Rural Development Bank: For Plantation (Tea)

**Short Title:** PCARDTP

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Tea Plantation, Loan, Farmer, Agriculture, Agriculture And Rural Development Bank

The &amp;quot;Primary Cooperative Agriculture and Rural Development Bank: For Plantation (Tea)&amp;quot; scheme launched by the Co-operation, Food and Consumer Protection Department, Tamil Nadu, aims to provide financial support to farmers for tea plantations. Under this scheme, eligible farmers can obtain a loan of up to ₹33,000/- per acre, with interest rates ranging from 11% to 12.5%. This initiative aims to empower farmers and promote sustainable tea farming practices. &lt;br&gt;

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Banking,Financial Services and Insurance; Sector: Loan

**Where it applies:** Tamil Nadu; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - **Access to Loan:** ₹33,000/- per acre. - **Interest Rate:** 11% to 11.5% (subject to change). &lt;br&gt;

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Co-operation, Food and Consumer Protection Department.

**References / Annexures:** - **Guidelines**: https://www.tn.gov.in/scheme/data_view/3552

**Additional Information:**
Briefdescription: The scheme offers financial assistance of up to ₹33,000/- per acre to farmers for tea plantations, with an interest rate of 11%-12.5%.
