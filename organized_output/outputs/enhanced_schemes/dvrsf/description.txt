# Diesel VAT Relief Scheme for Fishermen

**Short Title:** DVRSF

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Agriculture, Fishing Activities, Fishermen

The &amp;quot;Diesel VAT Relief Scheme for Fishermen&amp;quot; introduced by the Agriculture, Farmers Welfare and Cooperation Department, Gujarat, aims to support fishermen by subsidizing the VAT on diesel, making fuel more affordable for their mechanized boats. This helps reduce the operational cost of fishermen using mechanized boats.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Agriculture,Rural & Environment; Sector: Fishing and hunting

**Where it applies:** Gujarat; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** **Name of Component****Standard of Assistance****Minimum Time Limit (in Years) for Reclaiming Benefits**Diesel VAT assistancePer litre maximum assistance up to ₹15/-Assistance is available as per fixed diesel quota throughout the year

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Agriculture, Farmers Welfare and Cooperation Department.
