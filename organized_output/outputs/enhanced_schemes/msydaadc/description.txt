# Mahila Samruddhi Yojana (DAADC)

**Short Title:** MSYDAADC

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Loan, Woman, Scheduled Caste, Self-employment, Antyodaya Samaj

The &amp;quot;Mahila Samruddhi Yojana&amp;quot; is implemented by the Dr. Ambedkar Antyodaya Development Corporation (DAADC) under the Social Justice and Empowerment Department, Government of Gujarat. The scheme aims to promote self-employment and financial independence among women from the Antyodaya Samaj, especially from Scheduled Castes. Under this scheme, loans are provided at a concessional interest rate to eligible women for starting small-scale businesses. 

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Banking,Financial Services and Insurance; Sector: Loan

**Where it applies:** Gujarat; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - **Loan Amount:** ₹50,000/- provided to eligible women for starting micro or small businesses.- **Interest Rate:** 6% per annum.- **State Government Contribution:** 95% of the loan amount, i.e., ₹47,500/-, is borne by the state.- **Beneficiary Contribution:** 5% of the loan amount, i.e., ₹2,500/-.- **Possible Monthly Installment Amount:** ₹1,445/- per month.- **Duration:** 36 Months (3 years) 

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Social Justice and Empowerment Department.

**References / Annexures:** - **Guidelines**: https://sje.gujarat.gov.in/gapb/assets/downloads/mahila_samrudhi_2020.pdf - **Official Website**: https://sje.gujarat.gov.in/gapb/home?lang=English - **Scheme Details**: https://mariyojana.gujarat.gov.in/MoreDetails.aspx
