# Subsidy for Developing Business Premises for Persons with Disabilities

**Short Title:** SDBPPD

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Subsidy, Business Premise, Disability, PwD, Loan, Self-employment

The scheme &amp;quot;Subsidy for Developing Business Premises for Persons with Disabilities&amp;quot; is implemented by the Haryana Backward Classes and Economically Weaker Sections Kalyan Nigam, Government of Haryana. Under this scheme, loans up to ₹3 lakh are provided to persons with disabilities to develop business premises on their own land and start self-employment activities. Permanent residents of Haryana, aged 18 or older, with a disability of 40% or more, is eligible to avail the benefits under this scheme.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Business & Entrepreneurship; Sector: Loan

**Where it applies:** Haryana; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** 1. Under this scheme, loans up to ₹3 lakh are provided to persons with disabilities to develop business premises on their own land and start self-employment activities.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: Haryana Backward Classes And Economically Weaker Sections Kalyan Nigam; Nodal Department: Welfare of Scheduled Caste & Backward Classes Department.
