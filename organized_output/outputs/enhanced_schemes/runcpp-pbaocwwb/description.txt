# Relief under Natural Calamity / Pandemic Period (PBAOCWWB)

**Short Title:** RUNCPP-PBAOCWWB

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Pandemic, Calamity, Disaster, Financial Assistance, Relief

The &quot;Relief under Natural Calamity / Pandemic Period&quot; is a Welfare Scheme by the Puducherry Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board, Labour Department, Union Territory of Puducherry. Through this scheme, the Board provides financial assistance of ₹ 2,000/- to the registered worker as a relief during a natural calamity/pandemic. The amount is credited through Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) into the bank account of the registered construction worker. The applications are accepted offline.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Crisis/Disaster/Accident

**Where it applies:** Puducherry; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** ₹ 2,000/- credited as Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) into the bank account.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Labour Department, Puducherry.
