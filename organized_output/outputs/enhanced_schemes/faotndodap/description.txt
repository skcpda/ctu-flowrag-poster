# Financial Assistance on the Natural Death of Differently Abled Person

**Short Title:** FAOTNDODAP

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Financial Assistance, Differently Abled, Death

The scheme &quot;Financial Assistance on the Natural Death of Differently Abled Person&quot; was launched by the Department for the Welfare of Differently Abled Persons, Government of Tamil Nadu. Through this scheme, financial assistance of ₹15,000/- is provided to the legal heir in the event of the natural death of the differently-abled person. The applications are accepted offline in the prescribed form available at the District Differently Abled Welfare Office.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Financial assistance

**Where it applies:** Tamil Nadu; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** Financial Assistance of ₹15,000/- to the legal heir.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Welfare of Differently Abled Persons Department.
