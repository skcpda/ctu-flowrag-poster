# Self-Employment Scheme: Nana Vyavasay Mate Loan Yojana

**Short Title:** SESNVMLY

**Scheme For:** Infra

**Tags:** Business, Entrepreneurship, Self Employment, Loan

&amp;quot;Nana Vyavasay Mate Loan Yojana&amp;quot; is a component under the &amp;quot;Self-Employment Scheme&amp;quot; introduced by the Social Justice and Empowerment Department, Gujarat. Under this scheme, for any self-employment business like a business or grocery shop, medical store, readymade garment store, bookstore, etc., a loan of up to ₹10,00,000/- or the actual expenditure incurred, whichever is less, will be provided by the Corporation. The scheme is being implemented by the Gujarat Unreserved Education and Economical Development Corporation. The scheme is being implemented by the Gujarat Unreserved Education and Economical Development Corporation.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Banking,Financial Services and Insurance; Sector: Loan

**Where it applies:** Gujarat; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - **Financial Assistance:** Loan up to ₹10,00,000/- or actual expenditure, whichever is lower.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: Gujarat Unreserved Education And Economical Development Corporation; Nodal Department: Social Justice and Empowerment Department.
