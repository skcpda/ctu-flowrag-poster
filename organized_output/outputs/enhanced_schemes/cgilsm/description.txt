# Cash Grant in lieu of Sewing Machine

**Short Title:** CGILSM

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Widow, Ex-Serviceman, Grant, Sainik

&quot;Cash Grant in lieu of Sewing Machine&quot; is a Welfare Scheme by the Armed Forces Flag Day Fund of the Department of Sainik Welfare, Puducherry. Through this scheme, ₹10,000/- is provided to the Widows of Ex-servicemen for the purchase of a Sewing Machine. This scheme applies only to the Widows of the ESM registered with the Department of Sainik Welfare, Puducherry. The applications are accepted offline.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Aids/Appliances

**Where it applies:** Puducherry; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - A Cash Grant of ₹10,000/- is provided for the purchase of a Sewing Machine. - The amount is credited to the beneficiary’s Bank Account through RTGS/ NEFT.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: Rajya Sainik Board, Puducherry; Nodal Department: Sainik Welfare Department, Puducherry.
