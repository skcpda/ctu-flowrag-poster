# Ex-gratia Scheme: Disability Compensation (P.B.O.C.W.W.B)

**Short Title:** EGSDCPPBOCWWB

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Labour, Building Worker, Construction Worker, Disability, Compensation

The &amp;quot;Ex-gratia Scheme: Disability Compensation&amp;#39;&amp;#39; by the BOCW Welfare Board, Department of Labour, Punjab, is a welfare scheme for the registered workers of the board. Under this scheme, the Board shall provide compensation to the registered worker in the event of complete or partial disability who do not have Health Cards under Bhagat Puran Singh Sehat Bima Yojna.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Financial assistance

**Where it applies:** Punjab; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - **Compensation for Complete Disability:** Ex-gratia amount of ₹4,00,000/-. - **Compensation for Partial Disability:** Proportionate amount of ₹4,00,000/- shall be given on the basis of percentage of disability i.e., ₹4,000/- for each 1% disability and maximum of ₹4,00,000/-.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Department of Labour.
