# NEC Stipend And Book Grant Scheme

**Short Title:** NSBGS

**Launch Date:** 2011-01-01

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** NEC Stipend, Book Grant, Nagaland, Fellowship

**Detailed** The Directorate of Higher Education and Government of Nagaland is the authority that offers NEC Stipend and Book Grant Scheme, Nagaland. This scholarship scheme is offered to the students pursuing higher professional courses and belongs to ST and general category. Only Nagaland domiciled candidates will be eligible to apply for this scheme. &lt;br&gt; The number of stipends to be awarded every year to candidates may be decided by the State Government keeping in mind the fund provided to the State Govt. However, there may be an increase or decrease in each level of study depending upon the fund availability.,etc.) &lt;br&gt; NEC Stipend and Book Grant Scheme, Nagaland invites applications from Schedule Tribe and General category students pursuing the higher professional course. The main objective is to cultivate the spirit of the national mix, global understanding and social duty, empowering logical temper, imparting imaginative and innovative reasoning among students and so on. &lt;br&gt;

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Education & Learning; Sector: Education and training grants, fellowship, stipend

**Where it applies:** Nagaland; State

**Benefits / Assistance:** **Benefits** • The selection of the students will majorly depend upon their academic performance and the need for the scholarship. • The selected students will receive stipends and book grants for studies in India only.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Department of Higher Education.
