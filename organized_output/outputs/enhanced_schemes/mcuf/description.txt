# Medical Check-Up facility (UKBOCWWB)

**Short Title:** MCUFUKBOCWWB

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Labour, Building Worker, Construction Worker, Medical Check-Up, Health

The scheme “Medical Check-Up Facility” is implemented by the Uttarakhand Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board (UKBOCWWB) under the Labour Department, Government of Uttarakhand. The scheme aims to provide medical check-up facilities to registered construction workers through door-to-door visits, health camps, and collection centers. 

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Health & Wellness; Sector: Workplace health and safety

**Where it applies:** Uttarakhand; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** Under this scheme, medical check-up facilities are provided to construction workers. 

**Benefit Type:** In Kind

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: Uttarakhand Building And Other Construction Workers Welfare Board; Nodal Department: Department Of Labour.
