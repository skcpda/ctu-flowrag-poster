# Financial Assistance to Workers with a Visually, Physically or Mentally Disabled Child- Haryana Labour Welfare Board

**Short Title:** FAWVPMDC-HLWB

**Scheme For:** Family

**Tags:** Financial Assistance, Worker, PwD, Blind, Mentally Retarded, Mute, Deaf, Disability

The scheme “Financial Assistance to Workers with a Visually, Physically or Mentally Disabled Child” is implemented by the Haryana Labour Welfare Board (HLWB), Labour Department, Government of Haryana. Under this scheme, financial assistance is provided to the disabled (blind, mentally retarded, mute and deaf) children of registered workers employed in industrial and commercial establishments in the state of Haryana.

**Who is this for:** Family; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Financial assistance

**Where it applies:** Haryana; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** 1. Financial assistance of ₹20,000/- per year is provided on disability of the children from 70 to 90%. 1. Financial assistance of ₹30,000/- per year is provided on disability of the children from 91 to 100%.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: Haryana Labour Welfare Board; Nodal Department: Labour Department.
