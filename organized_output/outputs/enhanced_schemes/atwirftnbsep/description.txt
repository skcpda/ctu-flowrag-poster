# Assistance Towards Expenses Incurred for Raising of Funds through NSE’s/ BSE’s SME Exchange Platform

**Short Title:** ATWIRFTNBSEP

**Launch Date:** 2024-12-04

**Closing Date:** 2029-03-31

**Scheme For:** Infra

**Tags:** NSE, SME Exchange, MSME Policy, Liquidity Constraint, Small Enterprise, Medium Enterprise, BSE

The “Assistance towards Expenses Incurred for Raising of Funds through NSE’s/BSE’s SME Exchange Platform” scheme has been introduced under the Rajasthan MSME Policy 2024 by the Department of Industries and Commerce, Government of Rajasthan. The scheme aims to support enterprises raising equity through NSE/BSE’s exchange platforms to ease the liquidity constraints. This scheme will remain in force until 31st March 2029. ***Scheme Implementation:*** This scheme component will be implemented through the Department of Industries &amp;amp; Commerce. Office of the Commissioner, Industries &amp;amp; Commerce will be the nodal agency for the implementation and supervision of the scheme at the state level.

**Who is this for:** Business Entity, Industries; Category: Business & Entrepreneurship; Sector: Employee management

**Where it applies:** Rajasthan; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** **Financial Assistance:** - The state shall provide one-time support up to a maximum of ₹15,00,000/- towards expenses incurred for raising of funds through NSE’s/ BSE’s SME exchange platform after successful raising of equity. ***Following expenditure to be covered under the assistance:*** - Stock Exchange Filing Fees - “Registrar to the issue” Expenses - Stock Exchange Software Expenses - SEBI registered merchant banker charges - RTA Fees, CDSL and NSDL joining and admission Fees - Advertising charges - NSE or BSE Fees and other Expenses - Market making fees **Note:** - The enterprise must be commercially active and in regular production at the time of disbursement. - The assistance will be released only after successful listing and verification of documents.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: Office Of The Commissioner, Industries & Commerce; Nodal Department: Industry Department.
