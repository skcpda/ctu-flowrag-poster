# A Scheme to Provide Assistance for Building E-Market Facilities in Market Committees

**Short Title:** SPABEM

**Scheme For:** Infra

**Tags:** Agriculture, E-Market

&quot;A Scheme to Provide Assistance for Building E-Market Facilities in Market Committees&quot; introduced by the Agriculture, Farmers Welfare and Cooperation Department, Gujarat, aims to support market committees to set up e-market facilities, enhancing transparency and efficiency in agricultural marketing across the state.

**Who is this for:** Registered Societies; Category: Agriculture,Rural & Environment; Sector: Entrepreneurship development

**Where it applies:** Gujarat; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - **Financial Assistance:** ₹1,00,000/- (One Lakh Rupees) per eligible market committee.  

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Agriculture, Farmers Welfare and Cooperation Department.

**References / Annexures:** - **Guidelines**: https://mariyojana.gujarat.gov.in/MoreDetails.aspx

**Additional Information:**
Briefdescription: This scheme provides financial assistance to market committees in Gujarat to establish e-market facilities, aligning with the National Agriculture Market Policy for a unified national e-market.
