# Intensive Fisheries Development Programme - Subsidy on Purchase of Fishing Nets (Scheduled Caste)

**Short Title:** IFDPSPFN

**Tags:** Fishing Nets, Subsidy, Fishermen, Scheduled Caste, Fisheries

The &amp;quot;Subsidy on Purchase of Fishing Nets&amp;quot; is a scheme component under the Intensive Fisheries Development Programme, introduced by the Fisheries Department, Government of Haryana. The scheme aims to provide financial assistance to individuals from Scheduled Caste families engaged in fishing activities for the purchase of fishing nets. Under this scheme, eligible beneficiaries can avail a subsidy of up to 60% on the cost of fishing nets.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Agriculture,Rural & Environment; Sector: Fishing and hunting

**Where it applies:** Haryana; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** **Financial Assistance is provided to Fish Farmers under the scheme:** - Financial assistance of 60% subsidy on the purchase of fishing nets of amounting to ₹40,000/-. - The maximum subsidy amount is ₹24,000/-. > **Note:** Subsidy is available only once in two years per beneficiary.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Fisheries Department.
