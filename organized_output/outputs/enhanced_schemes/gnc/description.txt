# Gen Next Competition

**Short Title:** GNC

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Generation, Prize, Artisans, Youngsters, Handicraft

The &quot;Gen Next Competition&quot; scheme was launched by the Handlooms, Handicrafts, Textiles, and Khadi Dept, Government of Tamil Nadu. The scheme aims to ensure that the traditional handicrafts of Tamil Nadu survive and are passed on to the next generation, it is necessary to enthuse the youngsters in the field. To achieve this, a craft competition was introduced from the year 2013-2014 onwards for artisans below 30 years of age.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Skills & Employment; Sector: Apprenticeship

**Where it applies:** Tamil Nadu; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - Cash Prize of ₹2,000/-. - Silver Medal. - Certificate.

**Benefit Type:** Composite

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Handlooms, Handicrafts, Textiles and Khadi Department.
