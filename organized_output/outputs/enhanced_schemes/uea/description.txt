# Unemployment Allowance

**Short Title:** UEA

**Launch Date:** 2023-04-01

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Financial Assistance, Berozgari Bhatta, Unemployment

The main objective of the Chhattisgarh Berozgari Bhatta Yojana is to provide financial assistance to the unemployed youth of the state till they get employment. &lt;br&gt;

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Education & Learning; Sector: Education and training grants, fellowship, stipend

**Where it applies:** Chhattisgarh; State

**Benefits / Assistance:** Under the Chhattisgarh Unemployment Allowance Scheme, financial assistance of Rs.2500/- per month will be provided to the eligible unemployed.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Higher Education Department.

**References / Annexures:** - **Guidelines**: https://berojgaribhatta.cg.nic.in/Berojgaribatta_guidelines.pdf - **FAQ**: https://berojgaribhatta.cg.nic.in/FAQ.pdf

**Additional Information:**
Briefdescription: Chhattisgarh Berozgari Bhatta Yojana is an important initiative of Chhattisgarh Government for the unemployed. It has been announced by the Chief Minister of the state Mr. Bhupesh Baghel in his budget speech on 06-03-2023.
