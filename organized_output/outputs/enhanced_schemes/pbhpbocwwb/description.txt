# Paternity Benefit (HPBOCWWB)

**Short Title:** PBHPBOCWWB

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Building, Construction, Financial Assistance, Labour, Child, Worker

The &quot;Paternity Benefit&quot; scheme, introduced by the Himachal Pradesh Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board (HPBOCWWB), The scheme aims to provide financial assistance to registered workers and their families. Under this scheme, the wife of a registered worker is entitled to receive ₹6,000 per delivery, for up to two deliveries, as a form of maternal support. In addition, the scheme also offers ₹1,000 to the male beneficiary as paternity assistance on the birth of a child.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Financial assistance

**Where it applies:** Himachal Pradesh; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** 1. The scheme gives ₹6,000 to the wife of a registered worker for up to two deliveries. 1. The male worker receives ₹1,000 as paternity assistance.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Labour and Employment Department, Himachal Pradesh.
