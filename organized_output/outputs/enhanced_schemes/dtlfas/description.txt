# Dattopant Thengadi Mratak Shramik Aarthik Sahayata Yojana

**Short Title:** DTLFAS

**Scheme For:** Family

**Tags:** Financial Assistance, Social Welfare, Death Benefits

The &quot;Dattopant Thengadi Mratak Shramik Aarthik Sahayata Yojana&quot; is a scheme launched by the Government of Uttar Pradesh to provide financial assistance to the families of registered workers who die while in service. The scheme aims to provide financial relief to the families of deceased workers and help them meet their immediate needs.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Financial assistance

**Where it applies:** Uttar Pradesh; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** Financial Assistance : ₹1,00,000/-

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Labour Department, Uttar Pradesh.
