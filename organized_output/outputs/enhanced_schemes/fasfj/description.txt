# Financial Assistance Scheme for Journalists and Litterateurs - Rajasthan

**Short Title:** FASJLR

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Financial Assistance, Journalist, Litterateur, Medical, Treatment, Old Age

The &amp;quot;Financial Assistance Scheme for Journalists and Litterateurs&amp;quot; scheme was launched by the Department of Information &amp;amp; Public Relations (DIPR), Government of Rajasthan. The scheme aims to provide financial support to journalists and litterateurs for the treatment of critical illnesses, medical emergencies and no source of income. The scheme is implemented by a committee constituted by the state government and managed under the Rajasthan Journalist and Litterateur Welfare Fund. &gt; **Under the scheme, illness covers:** Cancer, leprosy, valve replacement, angioplasty, bypass surgery, kidney transplant, brain stroke (brain and neurological diseases), epileptic seizures, severe injury in an accident, severe infectious diseases - brain infection, lung infection/pneumonia, severe infection in other organs of the body, general surgery - (hernia, appendix, stones in kidney and gall bladder), pregnancy (cesarean) and serious gynecological diseases, diseases related to excessive bleeding, paralysis/palsy, asthma, Alzheimer&amp;#39;s, COVID-19, thyroid, etc.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Health & Wellness; Sector: Financial assistance

**Where it applies:** Rajasthan; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** > **Monetary Assistance for Medical Treatment:** - Up to ₹1,00,000/- for treatment of critical diseases (e.g., cancer, bypass surgery, kidney transplant, brain stroke, etc.). - In special cases, the committee may approve up to ₹2,00,000/-. > **Monthly Financial Assistance:** - ₹1,000 to ₹2,500 per month for a maximum of one year to unemployed journalists or those without income due to illness or old age.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Information and Public Relations Department.
