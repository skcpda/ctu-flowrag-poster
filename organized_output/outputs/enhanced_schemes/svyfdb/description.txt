# Saraswati Vidya Yojana: Free Distribution of Bicycles

**Short Title:** SVYFDB

**Launch Date:** 2021-02-25

**Scheme For:** Infra

**Tags:** Student, Girls, Bicycles, School, Social Empowerment

“Free Distribution of Bicycles&amp;#39;&amp;#39; under the “Saraswati Vidya Yojana”has been implemented by the Directorate of Education, U.T. Administration Of DNH &amp;amp; DD. Under this scheme, bicycles are provided free of cost to all the girl students, studying in class VIII(8) each year in the Govt. &amp;amp; Govt. aided schools/ Institutes of the U.T. Administration. &gt; **Objective of the &amp;quot;Saraswati Vidya Yojana”:** The UT Administration of Dadra &amp;amp; Nagar Haveli and Daman &amp;amp; Diu has launched “Saraswati Vidya Yojana” on 25th February, 2021, for the girl students of UT of Dadra &amp;amp; Nagar Haveli and Daman &amp;amp; Diu. The scheme aims to encourage girl students to continue education keeping in view the aim of:- “Beti Bachao Beti Padhao”. &gt; **Objective of the &amp;quot;Free Distribution of Bicycles&amp;quot; Sub-scheme:** The objective of the scheme is to retain the girl students at Secondary &amp;amp; Higher Secondary stage and thereby decreasing the Dropout of the girl students by providing Bicycles to the girl students of class VIII to commute to school.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Education & Learning; Sector: Resources for schools

**Where it applies:** Dadra & Nagar Haveli and Daman & Diu; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - Bicycles will be provided to all the girl students.

**Benefit Type:** In Kind

**Exclusions / Not eligible:** - The girl students once availed the benefit will not be provided with the same in any of the subsequent year.

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Education Department.
