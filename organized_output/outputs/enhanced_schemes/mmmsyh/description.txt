# Mukhya Mantri Matritva Sahayata Yojana

**Short Title:** MMMSYH

**Launch Date:** 2022-03-08

**Tags:** Women, Maternity Benefit, Lactating Mothers, Pregnancy Care, Child

Launched on 8th March, 2022, the scheme &amp;quot;Mukhya Mantri Matritva Sahayata Yojana&amp;quot; by the Women and Child Development Department, Government of Haryana, aims to offer partial compensation for wage loss to promote adequate rest before and after childbirth, and to encourage health-seeking behaviour among pregnant and lactating women. This initiative particularly targets women delivering their second child (boy) and intends to promote institutional delivery and immunization adherence. The benefit of ₹5,000/- is disbursed through direct benefit transfer (DBT) into the beneficiaries’ accounts. The is provided after the fulfillment of specified conditions including child registration and first immunization cycle.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Women and Child; Sector: Pregnancy Care

**Where it applies:** Haryana; State/ UT

**DBT Scheme:** Yes (Direct Benefit Transfer)

**Benefits / Assistance:** - **Monetary Benefit:** A cash incentive of ₹5,000/- is provided in one instalment after the birth of a second child (boy).

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Women and Child Development Department.

**References / Annexures:** - **Guidelines**: https://cdnbbsr.s3waas.gov.in/s34c144c47ecba6f8318128703ca9e2601/uploads/2024/11/20241125232046895.pdf
