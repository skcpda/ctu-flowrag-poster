# Scholarship for Ph.D. Students of Socially and Educationally Backward Classes

**Short Title:** SPHDSSEBC

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Scholarship, Higher Education, Student, PhD, MPhil

The “Scholarship for Ph.D. Students of Socially and Educationally Backward Classes” scheme was launched by the Social Justice and Empowerment Department, Government of Gujarat. The scheme aims to provide financial assistance to Socially and Educationally Backward Classes (SEBC) students pursuing M.Phil. or Ph.D. to encourage higher education and research. 

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Education & Learning; Sector: Scholarships and student finance

**Where it applies:** Gujarat; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** **Scholarship Assistance:** - ₹25,000/- for students pursuing M.Phil. - ₹1,00,000/- for students preparing Ph.D thesis. 

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Social Justice and Empowerment Department.

**References / Annexures:** - **Guidelines**: https://mariyojana.gujarat.gov.in/MoreDetails.aspx
