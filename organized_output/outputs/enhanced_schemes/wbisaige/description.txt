#  The West Bengal Incentive Scheme: Additional Incentive on Generation of Employment

**Short Title:** WBISAIGE

**Launch Date:** 2021-02-22

**Scheme For:** Infra

**Tags:** Tourism, Incentives, Waiver, Tourism Units, Entrepreneurship, Subsidy, Reimbursement, Employment Generation

“Additional Incentive on Generation of Employment” is a sub scheme under “The West Bengal Incentive Scheme 2021”. It was launched on 22nd February, 2021, by the Department of Tourism, Government of West Bengal, and shall remain valid upto 31st December, 2025. &gt; **Objective of the “West Bengal Incentive Scheme” scheme:** The objective of the Scheme is to provide financial assistance and help enthusiastic intending or prospective entrepreneurs to start their own business as a self-employed youth who will become an employer in upcoming days and generate more employment. &gt; **Objective of the “Additional Incentive on Generation of Employment” scheme:** Through this incentive, an eligible unit will be entitled to an additional incentive for employment generation for paying its contribution towards Employees State Insurance (ESI) and Employees Provident Fund (EPF) Scheme.

**Who is this for:** Business Entity, Individual; Category: Business & Entrepreneurship; Sector: Setting up / start-up / entrepreneurship

**Where it applies:** West Bengal; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - Group A, B, C &amp; D areas: 60% for a period of 5 years - Special Areas: 60% for a period of 10 years.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Exclusions / Not eligible:** - The West Bengal Incentive Scheme shall stand non-applicable in respect of those tourism units that have been registered for and have been sanctioned incentives under any previous scheme. - Any unit which may have applied for registration with the Directorate of Tourism under WBIS (West Bengal Incentive Scheme ) 2015, before the date of commencement of this Scheme and whose applications may have not been disposed off prior to the coming into effect of this Scheme or deemed to have been disposed of shall be entitled to all benefits under WBIS 2015 only.

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Tourism Department.
