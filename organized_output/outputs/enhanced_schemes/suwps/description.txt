# Sikkim Unmarried Women Pension Scheme

**Short Title:** SUWPS

**Launch Date:** 2013-03-21

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Unmarried Women, Pension, Below Poverty Line, Direct Benefit Transfer

The Govt. of Sikkim launched a scheme named “Sikkim Unmarried Women Pension Scheme (SUWPS)” in 2013 for unmarried women of Below Poverty Line (BPL) families in Sikkim. Under this scheme, the unmarried women of BPL families in the age group 45-59 years without any source of income are eligible for payment of ₹ 2,000 as a monthly pension. This scheme is 100% State Innovative Scheme and the unmarried woman should not have been covered by any other existing pension schemes of the State or Central government.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Pension

**Where it applies:** Sikkim; State

**DBT Scheme:** Yes (Direct Benefit Transfer)

**Benefits / Assistance:** A monthly pension of ₹ 2,000/- per beneficiary.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: Women & Child Welfare Department, Govt. Of Sikkim; Nodal Department: Women & Child Welfare Department.
