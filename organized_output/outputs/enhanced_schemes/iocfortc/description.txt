# Concession in Railway Travel

**Short Title:** CRT

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Artist, Travel, Railway, Culture

The scheme &quot;Concession in Railway Travel&quot; was launched by the Department of Art and Culture, Tamil Nadu. Under the scheme, individual artists and troupes consisting of more than three artists are allowed to travel in the train on concession by the Railways. A concession certificate will be issued by the Mandram.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Financial assistance

**Where it applies:** Tamil Nadu; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - 75% concession in Second Class Fare. - 50% concession in First Class Fare.

**Benefit Type:** In Kind

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: Tamil Nadu Eyal Isai Nataka Mandram; Nodal Department: Art and Culture Department.

**References / Annexures:** - **Guidelines**: https://www.tn.gov.in/rti/proactive/inftour/handbook_artculture.pdf
