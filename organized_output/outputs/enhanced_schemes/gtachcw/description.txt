# Grant of Token Amount for Construction of House for the Construction Workers

**Short Title:** GTACHCW

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Labour, Construction Of House, Construction Workers, Building Workers

The &amp;quot;Grant of Token Amount for Construction of House for the Construction Workers&amp;quot; by the Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board, Labour Department, U.T. Administration of Dadra and Nagar Haveli and Daman and Diu, is a welfare scheme for the registered workers of the board. Under this scheme, registered beneficiaries of the board shall be provided with financial assistance for construction of house in their own land.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Financial assistance

**Where it applies:** Dadra & Nagar Haveli and Daman & Diu; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - **Token Amount:** ₹75,000/-. - **For applicants covered under Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojna (Gramin):** ₹28,000/-.- **For applicants covered under Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojna (Urban):** 1. **For Affordable Housing in Partnership (AHP):** ₹1,30,000/-.1. **For Beneficiary Led Construction (BLC):** ₹50,000/-.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Labour & Employment Department.
