# Tanariri Music Awards Festival and Awards (National Level)

**Short Title:** TMAFA

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** National Music Award, Cultural Recognition, Female

The scheme &amp;quot;Tanariri Music Awards Festival and Awards (National Level)&amp;quot; by the Sports, Youth and Cultural Activities Department, Government of Gujarat, aims to commemorate the legacy of legendary musical artistes Beldi Tana and Riri and promote the cultural heritage of Indian classical music by honoring two exceptional female artistes from across the country. These artists are felicitated at a prestigious national-level award ceremony held at Vadnagar, Gujarat, with a cash prize of ₹5,00,000, a shawl, and a certificate each. &lt;br&gt; 

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Sports & Culture; Sector: Culture

**Where it applies:** Gujarat; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - **Cash Prize:** Each of the two selected female artists is awarded a cash prize of ₹5,00,000/-.- **Other Benefit:** In addition to the monetary award, recipients receive a shawl and a certificate recognizing their contribution. 

**Benefit Type:** Composite

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: Gujarat State Sangeet Natak Academy; Nodal Department: Sports, Youth and Cultural Activities Department.

**References / Annexures:** - **Guidelines (Scheme No. 636)**: https://mariyojana.gujarat.gov.in/Schemeatoz.aspx
