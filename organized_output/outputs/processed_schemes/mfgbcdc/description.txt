
# Micro Finance (GBCDC)


**Short Title:** MFGBCDC









**Tags:** Self Employment, Backward Classes, Educationally Backward Class, Socially Backward Class, Business



The scheme &quot;Micro Finance (GBCDC)&quot; by the Social Justice and Empowerment Department, Government of Gujarat, aims to create self-employment for men from the backwards classes. Through this scheme, low interest rates and financial help are provided to the backwards class men. The scheme is implemented by the Gujarat Backwards Classes Development Corporation. 




**Who is this for:** 
Individual; 
Category: Banking,Financial Services and Insurance; 
Sector: Micro finance



**Where it applies:** 
Gujarat; 
State/ UT









**Benefits / Assistance:** 1. The maximum loan limit under this scheme is up to ₹1,25,000/-. 1. The interest rate in these schemes is 5 percent per annum. 1. In these schemes, 90 per cent of the unit cost will be provided as a loan, with 90 per cent from the national corporation, 5 per cent from the state government, and 5 per cent from the beneficiary contribution. 1. This loan is to be repaid in 48 equal monthly instalments, including interest. 



**Benefit Type:** Cash
