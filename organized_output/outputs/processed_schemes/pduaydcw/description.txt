
# Pandit Dindayal Upadhyay Awas Yojana-Developing Caste Welfare


**Short Title:** PDUAYDCW









**Tags:** Housing, Socially And Educationally Backward Class, Economically Backward Class, Nomadic Exempted Caste



The &amp;quot;Pandit Dindayal Upadhyay Awas Yojana-Developing Caste Welfare&amp;quot; is a welfare scheme by the Social Justice and Empowerment Department of Gujarat. The scheme aims to provide financial aid of up to ₹1,20,000/- to eligible beneficiaries from the socially and educationally backward classes (SEBC), economically backward classes (EBC), and nomadic exempted castes (NEC) for constructing new houses. The scheme prioritizes Below Poverty Line (BPL) households, most backward classes, and nomadic communities.




**Who is this for:** 
Individual; 
Category: Housing & Shelter; 
Sector: Housing



**Where it applies:** 
Gujarat; 
State/ UT









**Benefits / Assistance:** - **Financial Assistance:** Up to ₹1,20,000/- for house construction.  



**Benefit Type:** Cash
