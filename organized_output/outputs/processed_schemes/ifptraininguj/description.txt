
# To Increase Fish Seed Production And Inland Fisheries Equipment In Tribal Areas: Training of Tribal Trainees Gujarat


**Short Title:** IFPTRAININGUJ









**Tags:** Fish, Fish Activities, Fish Production, Scheduled Tribes, Fishermen, Training Of Tribal Trainees



The scheme &quot;To Increase Fish Seed Production And Inland Fisheries Equipment In Tribal Areas&quot;, with a component of &quot;Training of Tribal Trainees&quot;, introduced by the Agriculture, Farmers Welfare and Co-operation Department, Government of Gujarat, the training scholarships are provided to Scheduled Tribe trainees involved in inland fisheries to enhance their capacity and skill set. The training is conducted at tribal area training centres run by the Fisheries Department, located in Khedbrahma, Kadana, Chhota Udepur, Rajpipla, Madhuban, and Ukai. The scheme is implemented by the Commissioner of Fisheries.  > **Objectives:** - To increase fish production by rearing fish in reservoirs and provide employment to people involved in inland fisheries activities. - To boost fish farming or fishing activities in freshwater bodies. - Fishing in reservoirs, aquaculture in ponds, and catch from rivers increase fish production and boost inland fisheries. - The main objective is to provide employment opportunities to Scheduled Tribe fishermen in the state and increase fish production. 




**Who is this for:** 
Business Entity, Individual, Self Help Groups (SHGS), Registered Societies; 
Category: Agriculture,Rural & Environment; 
Sector: Fishing and hunting



**Where it applies:** 
Gujarat; 
State/ UT









**Benefits / Assistance:** A trainee is paid a scholarship of ₹2,000/- per month for three months, amounting to a total of ₹6,000/-.  > **Training Details** > **Duration:** 3 months > **Location:** Tribal area training centers run by the Fisheries Department at Khedbrahma, Kadana, Chhota Udepur, Rajpipla, Madhuban, and Ukai. > **Conditions for Receiving Benefits:** A trainee is eligible for the scholarship only if they maintain a minimum of 60% attendance during the training period. > **Validity:** The benefit is available once in a lifetime for each eligible trainee. 



**Benefit Type:** Cash






















**Contacts & Authorities:** 
Implementing agency: Commissioner Of Fisheries.
Nodal Department: Agriculture, Farmers Welfare and Cooperation Department.
