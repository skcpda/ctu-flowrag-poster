
# Grant of Subsidy for Procurement of Vehicle


**Short Title:** GSPV









**Tags:** Subsidy, Procurement, Vehicle, Fisheries, Fisherman, Financial Asisstance



Launched in 2010, the scheme &quot;Grant of Subsidy for Procurement of Vehicle&quot; is a Sub-Scheme under the Umbrella Scheme &quot;Puducherry Development of Shore Based Facilities, Ice Plants, Service Unit, Infrastructural Facilities and Quick Transport Facilities&quot; is a development scheme by the Department of Fisheries and Fishermen Welfare, Government of Puducherry. The scheme aims to facilitate the quick transportation of fish catches from landing centers to marketing places, thereby preventing/minimizing spoilage of fish. It provides a 50% subsidy for the procurement of specified vehicles.




**Who is this for:** 
Individual; 
Category: Agriculture,Rural & Environment; 
Sector: Fishing and hunting



**Where it applies:** 
Puducherry; 
State/ UT









**Benefits / Assistance:** - 50% subsidy for the purchase of auto goods carrier up to 1-ton capacity (maximum subsidy of ₹75,000). - 50% subsidy for the purchase of a mini truck with 1-3 ton capacity (maximum subsidy of ₹1,50,000). - 50% subsidy for the purchase of a mini lorry with 3-5 ton capacity (maximum subsidy of ₹3,50,000). - 50% subsidy for the purchase of an insulated fish van with 5-10 ton capacity (maximum subsidy of ₹5,00,000). - 50% subsidy for the purchase of a moped with an insulated ice-box (maximum subsidy of ₹15,000). - 50% subsidy for the purchase of engine spares for boats (maximum subsidy of ₹2,000 per boat per annum).  > **Mode of Disbursement** The subsidy amount will be released to the bank and credited to the account of the beneficiary for whom the loan amount was sanctioned by the bank.



**Benefit Type:** Cash
