
# Battery Operated Wheelchair by Welfare of Differently Abled Persons Department


**Short Title:** BOWBWODAPD









**Tags:** Differently Abled Person, Social Welfare, Muscular Dystrophy



The &#39;Battery Operated Wheelchair by Welfare of Differently Abled Persons Department&#39; scheme was launched by the Department for the Welfare of Differently Abled Persons, Government of Tamil Nadu. Under this scheme, battery-operated wheelchairs are given to muscular dystrophy and tetraplegia-affected persons. The applications are accepted offline in the prescribed form available at the District Differently Abled Welfare Office.




**Who is this for:** 
Individual; 
Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; 
Sector: Aids/Appliances



**Where it applies:** 
Tamil Nadu; 
State/ UT









**Benefits / Assistance:** - **Free distribution of Battery-Operated Wheelchairs.**



**Benefit Type:** In Kind
