
# Special Incentive to OBC Meritorious Students (Boys & Girls)


**Short Title:** SIOMSBG





**Launch Date:** 2025-07-02





**Tags:** Student, OBC, Incentive, Merit, Social Welfare



The &#39;Special Incentive to OBC Meritorious Students (Boys &amp; Girls)&#39; scheme, administered by the Directorate of Welfare of Scheduled Castes and Backward Classes, Government of Assam, provides financial assistance to deserving Other Backward Classes (OBC) students. Eligibility is extended to those who have achieved 80% or higher marks in the HSSLC examination. The scheme&#39;s objective is to motivate and encourage OBC students to pursue higher education, thereby fostering their academic parity with the broader population through a substantial incentive. Assistance is contingent upon the applicant satisfying the stipulated eligibility criteria and the successful submission of required documentation. An incentive of up to ₹10,000/- will be disbursed to students as approved by the Selection Committee.  ***The applications will be accepted through the myScheme platform from 04/07/2025 to 31/10/2025.*** ***A DigiLocker account is required for application. Please refer to the Application Process section for details.***
