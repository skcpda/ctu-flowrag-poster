
# Indira Gandhi National Vridhavastha Pension Yojana


**Short Title:** IGNVPY





**Launch Date:** 1995-08-15





**Tags:** Pension, Old Age, Financial Assistance, Below Poverty Line



Indira Gandhi National Vridhavastha Pension Yojana, Under the Department of Social Justice and Empowerment of Persons with Disabilities Government of Madhya Pradesh, provides financial support to the citizens whose families live below the poverty line.




**Who is this for:** 
Individual; 
Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; 
Sector: Pension



**Where it applies:** 
Madhya Pradesh; 
State/ UT









**Benefits / Assistance:** > Pension amount: ₹ 600/- per month. 



**Benefit Type:** Cash






















**Contacts & Authorities:** 

Nodal Department: Department of Social Justice and Empowerment of Persons with Disabilities.
