
# Vrutti Protsaha Loan Scheme


**Short Title:** VPLS









**Tags:** Loan, Self-employment, Subsidy, Minority



The &quot;Vrutti Protsaha Loan Scheme&quot; was launched by the Minorities Welfare Department, Government of Karnataka, and implemented by the Karnataka Minorities Development Corporation. The scheme aims to promote self-employment and economic activities among minority communities and provides financial assistance to eligible individuals from minority communities to start or expand their economic activities, thereby fostering economic empowerment and self-sufficiency.




**Who is this for:** 
Individual; 
Category: Skills & Employment; 
Sector: Citizen empowerment



**Where it applies:** 
Karnataka; 
State/ UT









**Benefits / Assistance:** Financial Assistance provided totals ₹1,00,000/- in two parts: - 50% Loan Amount - 50% Subsidy Amount



**Benefit Type:** Cash






















**Contacts & Authorities:** 

Nodal Department: Minorities Welfare Department.
