
# Mahila Samiriddhi Yojana for Minority Community


**Short Title:** MSYMC









**Tags:** Mahila, Minority Community, Woman, Loan, SHG



The scheme &amp;quot;Mahila Samiriddhi Yojana for Minority Community&amp;quot; is implemented by the Haryana Backward Classes and Economically Weaker Sections Kalyan Nigam. Under this scheme, loans up to ₹1,00,000/- per beneficiary are provided only to women entrepreneurs of the target group, either directly or through Self-Help Groups. Only women beneficiaries who are permanent residents of Haryana and belong to minority communities such as Muslims, Sikhs, Christians, Buddhists, Parsis, and Jains are eligible for the loans.
