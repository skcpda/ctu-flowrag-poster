
# Deserted Women Pension Scheme


**Short Title:** DWPS-UK









**Tags:** Deserted, Pension, Woman, Abandoned, Destitute, BPL



The &amp;quot;Deserted Women Pension Scheme&amp;quot; was launched by the Department of Social Welfare, Government of Uttarakhand. The scheme aims to provide a monthly pension to deserted married women, mentally ill deserted husbands or wives, and destitute unmarried women above 40 years of age residing in the state. 




**Who is this for:** 
Individual; 
Category: Women and Child; 
Sector: Pension



**Where it applies:** 
Uttarakhand; 
State/ UT









**Benefits / Assistance:** - **Pension Amount:** ₹1,200/- per month.- **For Mentally Disabled ill Husband/Wife:** ₹1,400/- per month. 



**Benefit Type:** Cash






















**Contacts & Authorities:** 

Nodal Department: Department Of Social Welfare.
