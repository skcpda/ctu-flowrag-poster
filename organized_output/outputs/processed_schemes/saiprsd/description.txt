
# West Bengal Incentive Scheme for Approved Industrial Park (SAIP) for MSMEs: Reimbursement of Stamp Duty


**Short Title:** SAIPRSD









**Tags:** Entrepreneurship, MSMEs, Stamp Duty, Industrial Park, Fiscal Incentives



The scheme “Reimbursement of Stamp Duty” is a sub scheme under the “West Bengal Incentive Scheme for Approved Industrial Park (SAIP) for MSMEs”. It was launched on 16th September, 2020, by the Department of Micro, Small &amp;amp; Medium Enterprises and Textiles, Government of West Bengal, shall remain in force up to 5 years. &gt; **Objective of the &amp;quot;West Bengal Incentive Scheme for Approved Industrial Park (SAIP) for MSMEs”:** The objective of the Scheme is to provide state support in the form of fiscal and non fiscal incentives in order to encourage private sector to set up industrial parks on their own land with quality infrastructure so as to catalyze and promote growth of MSME units in the state, boost employment generation, and lead to technology advancement and improved environmental compliance. &gt; **Objective of the &amp;quot;Reimbursement of Stamp Duty&amp;quot; Sub-scheme:** Through this incentive, an Approved Industrial Park shall be entitled to reimbursement of stamp duty paid by the SPV for registration of land documents within the State relating to purchase of land for development of the AIP. &lt;br&gt;




**Who is this for:** 
Business Entity; 
Category: Business & Entrepreneurship; 
Sector: Setting up / start-up / entrepreneurship



**Where it applies:** 
West Bengal; 
State/ UT









**Benefits / Assistance:** - Approved Industrial Park shall be entitled to reimbursement of stamp duty paid by the SPV for registration of land documents within the State relating to purchase of land for development up of the AIP.



**Benefit Type:** Cash






















**Contacts & Authorities:** 

Nodal Department: Micro, Small & Medium Enterprises and Textiles Department.
