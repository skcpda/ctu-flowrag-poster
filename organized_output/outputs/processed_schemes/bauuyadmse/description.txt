
# Dr.Babasaheb Ambedkar Udyog Uday Yojana: Assistance to GIDC for Developing Multi-Storied Shed in Estates


**Short Title:** BAUUYADMSE





**Launch Date:** 2020-08-07





**Tags:** Business, Entrepreneurship, MSMEs, Start Up, Multi Storied Shed, Scheduled Caste, Scheduled Tribe



The scheme &amp;quot;Dr.Babasaheb Ambedkar Udyog Uday Yojana&amp;quot; is an umbrella scheme launched by the Industries and Mines Department, Gujarat. It aims to empower the state&amp;#39;s dynamic MSME sector, which significantly contributes to employment, GDP, and exports. Effective from August 7th, 2020 to August 6th, 2025, this initiative supports SC/ST entrepreneurs and strengthens MSMEs, making them globally competitive. The component &amp;quot;Assistance to GIDC for Developing Multi-Storied Shed in Estates&amp;quot; provides assistance for constructing multi-storied industrial sheds to support SC/ST entrepreneurs.




**Who is this for:** 
Business Entity, Individual; 
Category: Business & Entrepreneurship; 
Sector: Setting up / start-up / entrepreneurship



**Where it applies:** 
Gujarat; 
State/ UT









**Benefits / Assistance:** > **Financial Assistance:** 1. SC/ST entrepreneurs can receive up to 70% of the allotment price as a subsidy. 1. Entrepreneurs need to pay only 30% of the allotment price to take possession of the shed.



**Benefit Type:** Cash







**Exclusions / Not eligible:** - Trading activity will not be eligible under the scheme. The service activities as decided by SLEC will only be eligible under this scheme. 
















**Contacts & Authorities:** 

Nodal Department: Industries and Mines Department.
