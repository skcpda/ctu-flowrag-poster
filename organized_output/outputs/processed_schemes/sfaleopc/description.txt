
# Scheme of Financial Assistance to Literary/Educational Institutions for Organizing Programs/Conferences


**Short Title:** SFALEOPC









**Tags:** Educational Conferences, Seminar, Sahitya Sammelan, Cultural Events



The &amp;quot;Scheme of Financial Assistance to Literary/Educational Institutions for Organizing Programs/Conferences&amp;quot; by the Sports, Youth and Cultural Activities Department, Government of Gujarat, aims to provide grants to registered institutions for organizing events such as seminars, dialogues, conventions, workshops, and literary festivals. The scheme is implemented by the Gujarat Sahitya Academy, and financial assistance is disbursed based on actual expenses, subject to a maximum limit. Institutions must comply with language usage norms, use appropriate branding and logos, and submit reports and financial documentation after event completion. Events can be state-level, national, or international, and must involve recognized speakers, literary scholars, and culturally relevant topics.  




**Who is this for:** 
University / Institution; 
Category: Sports & Culture; 
Sector: Culture



**Where it applies:** 
Gujarat; 
State/ UT









**Benefits / Assistance:** &gt; **Funding Assistance for Institutions:** - Up to ₹50,000/- or 75% of the actual expenditure, whichever is lower, is provided for organizing approved literary or educational events. > **Honorarium for Speakers:** - An honorarium of up to ₹2,000/- per speaker can be paid to invited speakers participating in the event. > **Note:** - Any honorarium amount exceeding ₹2,000/- will not be reimbursed by the Gujarat Sahitya Academy and must be borne by the organizing institution. - Events must be reported with bills, photos, invitation cards, and video recordings. 



**Benefit Type:** Cash






















**Contacts & Authorities:** 
Implementing agency: Gujarat Sahitya Academy.
Nodal Department: Sports, Youth and Cultural Activities Department.
