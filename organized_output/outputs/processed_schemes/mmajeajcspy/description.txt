
# Mukhyamantri Anusuchit Jati Ewam Anusuchit Janjati Civil Sewa Protsahan Yojana


**Short Title:** MMAJEAJCSPY









**Tags:** Scheduled Caste, Scheduled Tribe, UPSC, BPSC, Preliminary Examination, Incentive



The scheme &amp;quot;Mukhyamantri Anusuchit Jati Evam Anusuchit Janjati Civil Sewa Protsahan Yojana&amp;quot; has been initiated by the Scheduled Caste and Scheduled Tribe Welfare Department, Government of Bihar. The primary objective of the scheme is to provide financial assistance to meritorious Scheduled Caste and Scheduled Tribe students who qualify in the preliminary examination of the Civil Services Competitive Examination conducted by the Bihar Public Service Commission, Patna, and the Union Public Service Commission, New Delhi, to support their preparation for the main examination and interview.




**Who is this for:** 
Individual; 
Category: Education & Learning; 
Sector: Coaching



**Where it applies:** 
Bihar; 
State/ UT









**Benefits / Assistance:** Under this scheme, candidates who pass the preliminary examination of Civil Services Competitive Examinations are eligible for the following incentives: 1. Scheduled Caste and Scheduled Tribe candidates who pass the Preliminary Examination of the Combined Competitive Examination conducted by the Bihar Public Service Commission, Patna, will receive a one-time financial assistance of ₹50,000/- for further preparation. 1. Scheduled Caste and Scheduled Tribe candidates who pass the Preliminary Examination of the Civil Services Competitive Examination conducted by the Union Public Service Commission, New Delhi, will receive a one-time financial assistance of ₹1,00,000/- for further preparation.



**Benefit Type:** Cash







**Exclusions / Not eligible:** 1. Candidates who are already employed in any Government Department, Public Sector Undertaking, or State-Funded Institution are not eligible for this scheme.
















**Contacts & Authorities:** 
Implementing agency: Scheduled Caste And Scheduled Tribe Welfare Department.
Nodal Department: SC & ST Welfare Department.
