
# State Reward to the Recipient of National Award


**Short Title:** SRRNA









**Tags:** Award, Culture, Artist, Literature, Music, Dance



The scheme &quot;State Reward to the Recipient of National Award&quot; has been designed by the Directorate of Art and Culture, Government of Goa as a token of appreciation in order to support and promote the local artists, who have received recognition at the National and International levels.  > **Objectives** 1. To give State recognition to the local artists receiving the National Award. 1. To appreciate local talents and promote local artists. 1. To give financial support to local artists.




**Who is this for:** 
Individual; 
Category: Sports & Culture; 
Sector: Culture



**Where it applies:** 
Goa; 
State









**Benefits / Assistance:** A memento, a certificate of appreciation and a financial purse to the extent of ₹ 1,00,000/- per individual.



**Benefit Type:** Composite







**Exclusions / Not eligible:** 1. No award shall be given posthumously. 1. The Sahitya Akademi Award for translation will not be considered for this scheme.
















**Contacts & Authorities:** 

Nodal Department: Art, Culture and Museums Department.
