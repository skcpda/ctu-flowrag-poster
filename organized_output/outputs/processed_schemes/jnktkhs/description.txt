
# Jan-Nayak Karpoori Thakur Kalyan Hostel Scheme


**Short Title:** JNKTKHS









**Tags:** Education, Student, Backward Classes, Hostel



The scheme &quot;Jan-Nayak Karpoori Thakur Kalyan Hostel Scheme&quot; is a Welfare Scheme by the BC &amp; EBC Welfare Department, Government of Bihar. Under this scheme, the Backward Classes and Extremely Backward Classes Welfare Department, Bihar, Patna provides residential facilities to students studying in educational institutions near the Saharsa District Headquarters Hostel. To support hostel residents, students receive 9 kg of rice and 6 kg of wheat per month under the Chief Minister&#39;s Hostel Foodgrain Scheme and ₹1000/- per month under the Chief Minister&#39;s Hostel Grant Scheme.




**Who is this for:** 
Individual; 
Category: Education & Learning; 
Sector: Scholarships and student finance



**Where it applies:** 
Bihar; 
State/ UT









**Benefits / Assistance:** - 9 kg rice and 6 kg wheat per student per month. - ₹1000/- monthly grant under the Chief Minister’s Hostel Grant Scheme.



**Benefit Type:** Composite






















**Contacts & Authorities:** 

Nodal Department: BC & EBC Welfare Department.
