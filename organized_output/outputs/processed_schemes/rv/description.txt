
# Resham Vriksharopan


**Short Title:** RV









**Tags:** Financial Assistance, Plantation, Farmer, Clusters, Silk



The “Resham Vriksharopan” scheme was launched by the Sericulture Department, Government of Uttarakhand. Under this scheme, selected farmers organized into clusters are provided a financial grant along with a free supply of mulberry saplings for plantation. 




**Who is this for:** 
Individual; 
Category: Agriculture,Rural & Environment; 
Sector: Environmental grants



**Where it applies:** 
Uttarakhand; 
State/ UT









**Benefits / Assistance:** - Selected farmers in the cluster are provided with a grant assistance of ₹17,244 for mulberry plantation. - 2-Mulberry trees are provided free of charge by the department. 



**Benefit Type:** Cash






















**Contacts & Authorities:** 

Nodal Department: Department Of Sericulture.
