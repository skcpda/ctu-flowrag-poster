
# Promotion of Patriotism/National Integration/Communal Harmony - Interschool Choreographic Dance Competition


**Short Title:** PPNICH-ICDC









**Tags:** School, Dance, Competition, Artist, Culture



The scheme &quot;Promotion of Patriotism/National Integration/Communal Harmony - Interschool Choreographic Dance Competition&quot; was launched by the Tiatr Academy under the Art, Culture and Museums Department, Government of Goa. The scheme aims to inculcate values of patriotism, national integration, and communal harmony in children through an inter-school competition of choreographic dancing on Konkani songs focused on these themes. The scheme provides monetary prizes ranging from ₹3,000/- to ₹10,000/- along with mementos and certificates for winners, and traveling allowances ranging from ₹2,000/- to ₹4,000/- based on distance traveled by participating groups. The applicant must be an educational institution with a dance group consisting of not less than 6 or more than 10 dancers and must perform on only one patriotic song in Konkani. The scheme is implemented by Tiatr Academy of Goa and applications for this scheme are accepted offline with the submission of a physical application form along with a recorded copy of the song on Compact Disc.

**Benefits / Assistance:** ##### ***Monetary Benefits*** **Prize Money for Standard 1st-4th Category** - 1st Prize: ₹7,000/- plus memento and certificate. - 2nd Prize: ₹5,000/- plus memento and certificate. - 3rd Prize: ₹3,000/- plus memento and certificate. - Best Choreographer: ₹3,000/- plus memento and certificate. **Prize Money for Standard 5th-10th Category** - 1st Prize: ₹10,000/- plus memento and certificate. - 2nd Prize: ₹7,000/- plus memento and certificate. - 3rd Prize: ₹5,000/- plus memento and certificate. - Best Choreographer: ₹3,000/- plus memento and certificate. **Traveling Allowance** - Groups from within 10 kilometers radius: ₹2,000/-. - Groups from more than 10 kilometers and less than 20 kilometers: ₹3,000/-. - Groups from 20 kilometers or more distance: ₹4,000/-. ##### ***Non-Monetary Benefits*** - Mementos and certificates for all prize winners. - Refreshments provided for participants and their in-charge. - Recognition and exposure through inter-school competition platform.
