
# Assistance to Pilgrims for Kailash Mansarovar Yatra - Rajasthan


**Short Title:** APKMY





**Launch Date:** 2011-04-01





**Tags:** Financial Assistance, Tourism, Pilgrim, Yatra, Mansarovar, Kailash



The &quot;Assistance to Pilgrims for Kailash Mansarovar Yatra&quot; scheme was introduced by the Devasthan Department, Government of Rajasthan, on 1st April 2011. The objective of this scheme is to provide financial support to residents of Rajasthan who successfully complete the Kailash Mansarovar Yatra through the Ministry of External Affairs, Government of India. Under this scheme, financial assistance of up to ₹1,00,000/- is granted to each pilgrim, with a maximum of 100 beneficiaries selected per year.




**Who is this for:** 
Individual; 
Category: Travel & Tourism; 
Sector: Tourism in India



**Where it applies:** 
Rajasthan; 
State/ UT









**Benefits / Assistance:** > **Financial Assistance** - ₹1,00,000 per selected pilgrim after completing the Yatra. > **Mode of Disbursement** Direct transfer to the beneficiary’s bank account via account payee cheque/demand draft, depending on departmental discretion. > **Note:** - The Yatra must be completed through the Ministry of External Affairs, Government of India. - The benefit is available only once in a lifetime. - A maximum of 100 beneficiaries per year. - Selection via lottery if applications exceed the quota.



**Benefit Type:** Cash
