
# Vocational Training For VJNT And SBC Candidates Studying In Government Industrial Training Institute


**Short Title:** VTVSCSGIT









**Tags:** Vocational Training, Government Industrial Training, Vimukta Jati And Nomadic Tribes, Special Backward Classes



The State Government of Maharashtra introduced in 2003-04 the scheme to create self-employment for unemployed youths and students of VJNT and SBC who apply for Vocational Training. He will be given Vocational Training in Government I.T.I and undergo short-duration vocational training courses. The short-duration vocational courses will enable the youths to create their own employment in the service sector. The training fees ₹ 400/- to ₹ 2400/- (as per course) are paid to the concerned I.T.I. After completion of training, one Tool Kit of ₹ 1000/- is provided to the trainee through the concerned Govt. I.T.I.
