
# Disability Aids and Appliances - Telangana


**Short Title:** DAAA





**Launch Date:** 2016-05-01





**Tags:** Differently Abled Person, Appliances, Tricycle, Artificial Limbs, Construction Worker



The &quot;Disability Aids and Appliances&quot; scheme was launched by the Telangana Building &amp; Other Construction Workers Welfare Board, LET&amp;F (Labour) Department. Telangana. The scheme aims to provide artificial limbs, wheelchairs, tricycles, and other fabricated appliances to registered construction workers who have lost limbs or sustained disabilities due to accidents.




**Who is this for:** 
Individual; 
Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; 
Sector: Aids/Appliances



**Where it applies:** 
Telangana; 
State/ UT









**Benefits / Assistance:** - **Free distribution of artificial limbs, wheelchairs/tricycles, and fabricated appliances.**



**Benefit Type:** In Kind






















**Contacts & Authorities:** 

Nodal Department: Labour, Employment Training and Factories Department.
