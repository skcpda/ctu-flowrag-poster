
# To Increase Fish Seed Production and Inland Fisheries Equipment in Non-Tribal Areas


**Short Title:** IFSIFNTA









**Tags:** Agriculture, Farmer, Fish Farming



The scheme &amp;quot;To Increase Fish Seed Production and Inland Fisheries Equipment in Non-Tribal Areas&amp;quot; introduced by the Agriculture, Farmers Welfare and Cooperation Department, Gujarat, aims to enhance fish seed production and promote inland fisheries in non-tribal areas. It provides financial assistance for fish farming, fish seed rearing, and procurement of essential fisheries equipment, benefiting individuals, societies, and self-help groups involved in inland fisheries.




**Who is this for:** 
Individual, Registered Societies, Self Help Groups (SHGS); 
Category: Agriculture,Rural & Environment; 
Sector: Fishing and hunting



**Where it applies:** 
Gujarat; 
State/ UT
