
# Scheme of Awarding Porkizhi to the Indigent Kalaimamani Awardee Artists


**Short Title:** SOAPTTIKAA









**Tags:** Award, Indigent, Artist, Financial Assistance



&quot;Scheme of Awarding Porkizhi to the Indigent Kalaimamani Awardee Artists&quot; was launched by the Art and Culture Department, Government of Tamil Nadu. The artists who have already received the Kalaimamani Award and who are presently in indigent circumstances are awarded financial assistance of ₹15,000/-. The scheme is implemented by the Tamil Nadu Eyal Isai Nataka Mandram Ponni.




**Who is this for:** 
Artists; 
Category: Skills & Employment; 
Sector: Culture



**Where it applies:** 
Tamil Nadu; 
State/ UT









**Benefits / Assistance:** Financial Assistance of ₹15,000/-.



**Benefit Type:** Cash






















**Contacts & Authorities:** 
Implementing agency: Tamil Nadu Eyal Isai Nataka Mandram Ponni.
Nodal Department: Art and Culture Department.
