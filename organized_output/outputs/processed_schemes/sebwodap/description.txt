
# Special Education - Tamil Nadu


**Short Title:** SETN









**Tags:** Child, Visually Impaired, Hearing Impaired, Different Abled Person, Locomotor



The scheme &#39;Special Education by Welfare of Differently Abled Persons&#39; was launched by the Department for the Welfare of Differently Abled Persons, Government of Tamil Nadu. The scheme aims to provide free special education, vocational training, boarding and lodging to the visually impaired, hearing impaired, intellectual disability and locomotor differently-abled persons. Every year four sets of uniforms and textbooks are given free of cost.




**Who is this for:** 
Individual; 
Category: Education & Learning; 
Sector: Training and Skill Up-gradation



**Where it applies:** 
Tamil Nadu; 
State/ UT









**Benefits / Assistance:** - Free special education, vocational training, boarding and lodging. - Every year four sets of uniforms and textbooks are given free of cost.



**Benefit Type:** In Kind






















**Contacts & Authorities:** 

Nodal Department: Welfare of Differently Abled Persons Department.
