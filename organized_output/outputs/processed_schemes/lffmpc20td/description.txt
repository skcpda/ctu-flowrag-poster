
# PMMSY: Large Fish Feed Mill of Production Capacity of 20 Ton /Day Scheme - Haryana


**Short Title:** LFFMPC20TD









**Tags:** Financial Assistance, Fish Farmer, Production, Subsidy



The &quot;Large Fish Feed Mill with Production Capacity of 20 Ton/Per Day&quot; scheme was launched by the Ministry Of Fisheries, Animal Husbandry &amp; Dairying, Government of India under the Pradhan Mantri Matsya Sampada Yojana (PMMSY), it aims to strengthen post-harvest infrastructure and enhance fish feed production to support sustainable aquaculture. Under this scheme, a grant is provided to beneficiaries for setting up a large fish feed mill, with 40% subsidy for general category and 60% for Scheduled Caste/Women beneficiaries. 




**Who is this for:** 
Individual; 
Category: Agriculture,Rural & Environment; 
Sector: Fishing and hunting



**Where it applies:** 
Haryana; 
State/ UT









**Benefits / Assistance:** **Monetary Assistance:** - Grant of ₹20,00,000/- per unit for establishing a fish feed mill with 20-ton/day capacity. **Subsidy Provisions:** - 40% subsidy on unit cost for General Category beneficiaries. - 60% subsidy on unit cost for Scheduled Caste and women beneficiaries.  



**Benefit Type:** Cash






















**Contacts & Authorities:** 

Nodal Department: Fisheries Department.
