
# Grant of Financial Assistance to the Press Reporters of Puducherry


**Short Title:** GFAPRP









**Tags:** Reporter, Financial Assistance, Newspaper, Magazine



The scheme &quot;Grant of Financial Assistance to the Press Reporters of Puducherry&quot; by the Department of Information &amp; Publicity, Union Territory of Puducherry, aims to provide financial support amounting to ₹25,000/- to eligible press reporters who have served in Newspaper / Magazine duly registered with the &quot;Registrar of Newspapers, Government of India, New Delhi&quot; continuously for atleast 10 years.




**Who is this for:** 
Individual; 
Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; 
Sector: Financial assistance



**Where it applies:** 
Puducherry; 
State/ UT









**Benefits / Assistance:** - **Financial Assistance:** ₹25,000/-.- **Mode of Disbursal:** The Director, Department of Information &amp; Publicity, Puducherry will transmit the cheque to the applicant after obtaining the necessary receipt thereof from the applicant in the [prescribed format](https://py.gov.in/sites/default/files/citizens-charterinfopublicity.pdf).- **Time Limit:** Three weeks from the date of application.



**Benefit Type:** Cash






















**Contacts & Authorities:** 

Nodal Department: Information and Publicity Department, Puducherry.

- Guidelines & Application Form: https://py.gov.in/sites/default/files/citizens-charterinfopublicity.pdf
