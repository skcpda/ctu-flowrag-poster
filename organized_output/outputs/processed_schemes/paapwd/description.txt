
# Prosthetic Aid and Appliances for Person with Disabilities


**Short Title:** PAAPWD









**Tags:** Aids And Appliances, Person With Disabilities, PwD, Self-employment Equipment



The scheme &amp;quot;Prosthetic Aids and Appliances for Person with Disabilities&amp;quot; was launched by the Director Social Defence, Department of Social Justice &amp;amp; Empowerment, Government of Gujarat. Under this scheme, a Person with Disabilities with 40% or more disability is given relief in his/her disability as well as equipment for self-employment within the limit of ₹20,000/-. The scheme is fully funded by the state government.




**Who is this for:** 
Individual; 
Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; 
Sector: Aids/Appliances



**Where it applies:** 
Gujarat; 
State/ UT









**Benefits / Assistance:** Under this scheme, the following equipment is given within a limit of ₹20,000/-. 1. Artificial limb, crutch or calipers, tricycle, folding wheelchair to Person with Disabilities. 1. Hearing aids and other tool assistance for mute and deaf persons. 1. Musical instruments, brail kit for visually impaired persons. 1. M. R. child kit for mentally challenged persons. 1. For self-employment, hand carts, sewing machines, instruments of electric repairing, tools of cycle repairing, hair cutting kit, etc.



**Benefit Type:** In Kind






















**Contacts & Authorities:** 
Implementing agency: Office Of The District Social Defence Officer, Gujarat.
Nodal Department: Social Justice and Empowerment Department.
