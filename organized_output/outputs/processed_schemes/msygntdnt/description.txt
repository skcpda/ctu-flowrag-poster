
# Mahila Samriddhi Yojana (for women only) GNTDNT


**Short Title:** MSYGNTDNT









**Tags:** Entrepreneurs, Self Employment, Business, Micro Finance



The scheme &quot;Mahila Samriddhi Yojana&quot; by the Social Justice and Empowerment Department, Government of Gujarat, aims to create a microfinance scheme for women of target groups and women entrepreneurs doing independent business to create self-employment for women of nomadic and denotified castes. Under this scheme, women beneficiaries will be able to pursue a business/occupation of their choice. Gujarat Nomadic and Denotified Castes Development Corporation implement the scheme. 




**Who is this for:** 
Individual; 
Category: Business & Entrepreneurship; 
Sector: Setting up / start-up / entrepreneurship



**Where it applies:** 
Gujarat; 
State/ UT









**Benefits / Assistance:** 1. The maximum loan limit will be up to ₹1,25,000/-. 1. The interest rate will be 4% per annum. 1. Under this scheme, 100% loan of the business/occupation amount is provided. 1. The loan amount will consist of 95% National Corporation contribution, 5% State Government contribution and zero beneficiary contribution. > **Repayment:** The loan amount will have to be repaid in 48 equal monthly instalments, including interest. 



**Benefit Type:** Cash






















**Contacts & Authorities:** 
Implementing agency: Gujarat Nomadic And Free Castes Development Corporation.
Nodal Department: Social Justice and Empowerment Department.
