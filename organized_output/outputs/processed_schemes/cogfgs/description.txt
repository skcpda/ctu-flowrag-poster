
# Construction of Godown for Grain Storage


**Short Title:** COGFGS





**Launch Date:** 2013-12-24





**Tags:** Agriculture, Farmer, Food Security, Godown, Storage



Launched on 24th December 2013, the scheme &quot;Construction of Warehouse for Grain Storage&quot; by the Panchayat and Rural Development Department, Government of Madhya Pradesh aims to provide modern and scientific food grain storage facilities under Food Security Act 13 to interested applicants in rural areas. The application has to be made to the concerned Gram Panchayat. After depositing the prescribed fee in the Gram Panchayat, permission for the construction work will be issued by the Gram Panchayat. The application can be made online.




**Who is this for:** 
Individual; 
Category: Agriculture,Rural & Environment; 
Sector: Agricultural Inputs- seeds, fertilizer etc.



**Where it applies:** 
Madhya Pradesh; 
State/ UT









**Benefits / Assistance:** Construction of modern and scientific food grain storage facilities.



**Benefit Type:** In Kind






















**Contacts & Authorities:** 

Nodal Department: Panchayat and Rural Development Department.
