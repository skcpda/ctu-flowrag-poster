
# NAMO Shree Scheme


**Short Title:** NSS-G









**Tags:** Women, Maternal, Pregnancy, Financial, Health, Nutrition, Delivery



Launched in 2024, the scheme &quot;NAMO Shree Scheme&quot; by the Women and Child Development Department, Government of Gujarat, aims to ensure that every pregnant woman and lactating mother of the state gets proper nutritional support and also encourages them to have institutional deliveries for women&#39;s empowerment and improving maternal health. The scheme provides financial assistance of ₹12,000/- to eligible women beneficiaries and covers expenses from conception to delivery of the child. The applicant must be Gujarat&#39;s pregnant woman or a lactating mother, whose family income is not more than ₹8,00,000/- per year, and must deliver a child in any government or private hospital. The scheme is implemented by the Women and Child Development Department of the Gujarat Government, and registration under this scheme is done at the time of the first antenatal check-up through an Accredited Social Health Activist or Auxiliary Nurse Midwife Worker at the nearest Anganwadi Centre. 




**Who is this for:** 
Individual; 
Category: Health & Wellness; 
Sector: Children’s health and immunization



**Where it applies:** 
Gujarat; 
State/ UT









**Benefits / Assistance:** > **Financial Assistance Amount** Eligible women beneficiaries receive ₹12,000/- from conception to delivery of the child for their first two live children, distributed in phases.  > **Disbursal for First Pregnancy** - ₹5,000/- at the time of registration (₹2,000/- from State Government and ₹3,000/- from Central Government) - ₹2,000/- after six months of pregnancy (from the State Government) - ₹3,000/- immediately after institutional delivery (from the State Government) - ₹2,000/- after the 14-week vaccination (from the Central Government)  > **Disbursal for Second Pregnancy** - ₹2,000/- at the time of registration - ₹3,000/- after six months of pregnancy - ₹6,000/- after institutional delivery (amount given by the Central Government if the newborn is a girl and by the State Government if the newborn is a boy) - ₹1,000/- from the State Government after the 14-week vaccination  **Mode of Disbursement:** The Amount is transferred directly into the beneficiary woman&#39;s bank account through installments. **Additional Benefits:** Beneficiary women are also eligible to avail the benefits of other Central Government schemes like Pradhan Mantri Matritva Vandana Yojana and Janani Suraksha Yojana.  **Conditions for Availment** A certificate from the Medical Institution of Delivery is required for the last installment. 



**Benefit Type:** Cash
