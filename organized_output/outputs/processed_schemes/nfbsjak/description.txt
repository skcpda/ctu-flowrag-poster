
# National Family Benefit Scheme (Jammu and Kashmir)


**Short Title:** NFBSJAK





**Launch Date:** 1995-08-15





**Tags:** Financial Assistance, Family, Below Poverty Line, Death



Launched on 15th August 1995, the &amp;quot;National Family Benefit Scheme&amp;quot; is a welfare scheme by the Social Welfare Department, Union Territory of Jammu and Kashmir. The scheme provides one-time financial assistance of ₹20,000/- to ensure social security for bereaved families living below the poverty line in case of the death of their breadwinner.




**Who is this for:** 
Family; 
Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; 
Sector: Financial assistance



**Where it applies:** 
Jammu and Kashmir; 
State/ UT



**DBT Scheme:** Yes (Direct Benefit Transfer)







**Benefits / Assistance:** A one-time financial assistance of ₹20,000/-.



**Benefit Type:** Cash






















**Contacts & Authorities:** 

Nodal Department: Department Of Social Welfare J and K.
