
# Maternity Assistance for the Construction Worker


**Short Title:** MAFCWS









**Tags:** Labour, Medical Support, Construction Workers, Maternity, Financial Assistance



The &amp;quot;Maternity Assistance for the Construction Workers&amp;quot; by the Sikkim Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board, Labour Department, is a welfare scheme for the registered women workers of the board. Under this scheme, the women employees who are registered beneficiary for a period of one year shall be given a sum as may be determined by the Board from time to time as maternity benefit during the period of maternity.




**Who is this for:** 
Individual; 
Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; 
Sector: Pregnancy Care



**Where it applies:** 
Sikkim; 
State/ UT









**Benefits / Assistance:** - ₹10,000/- is provided to the registered women members. **Note:** The benefit shall not be allowed more than twice.



**Benefit Type:** Cash






















**Contacts & Authorities:** 

Nodal Department: Labour Department.
