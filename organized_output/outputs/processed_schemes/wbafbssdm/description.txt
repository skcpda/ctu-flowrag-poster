
# West Bengal Artisans Financial Benefit Scheme: Support for Digital Marketing


**Short Title:** WBAFBSSDM





**Launch Date:** 2024-03-07





**Tags:** Artisans, Digital Marketing, E-commerce



“Support for Digital Marketing” is a sub-scheme under “West Bengal Artisans Financial Benefit Scheme&amp;quot;, launched by the Micro, Small &amp;amp; Medium Enterprise and Textiles Department, Government of West Bengal on 7th March, 2024, and it shall remain in force for a period of five years. The objective of the scheme is to provide access to digital markets and e-commerce, which will create sustainable business opportunities for the artisans.




**Who is this for:** 
Artists, Registered Societies; 
Category: Business & Entrepreneurship; 
Sector: Setting up / start-up / entrepreneurship



**Where it applies:** 
West Bengal; 
State/ UT









**Benefits / Assistance:** - **Individual Artist:** ₹2000/-.- **Industrial Cooperative Society:** ₹10,000/-.



**Benefit Type:** Cash







**Exclusions / Not eligible:** - Any central/state govt employee and his/her family members shall not be eligible. - Should not have availed of any assistance /grant from Central /State Govt. for the same purpose. - Handloom and Khadi weavers, and their societies (as they are covered under separate schemes) shall not be eligible.
















**Contacts & Authorities:** 

Nodal Department: Micro, Small & Medium Enterprises and Textiles Department.
