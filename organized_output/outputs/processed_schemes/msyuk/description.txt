
# Mukhyamantri Swarojgar Yojna


**Short Title:** MSYUK









**Tags:** Bank Loans, Upgrade Small Scale Industried, Manufacturing Sector, Service Sector, Uttarakhand



The scheme &quot;Mukhyamantri Swarojgar Yojana&quot; by the Government of Uttarakhand aims to persuade enterprising youth of the State, migrants returned to Uttarakhand due to COVID-19, skilled and unskilled artisans, handicraftsmen and educated urban and rural unemployed persons to establish their own enterprise/business. Through this scheme, funding will be provided for eligible manufacturing, service establishments, and business activities via nationalised banks, regional rural banks, state cooperative banks, and other scheduled banks. The Department of Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises, Government of Uttarakhand, provides margin money as a grant, which is adjusted as a grant upon successful operation of the enterprise for 2 years. > **The main objectives of the scheme are:** 1. For self-employment, the creation of opportunities in rural and urban areas by establishing new services, businesses and micro-industries. 1. To provide employment opportunities near their areas of residence, as far as possible, to the enterprising youth, migrants returned to Uttarakhand due to COVID-19, skilled and unskilled artisans, handicraftsmen and educated urban and rural unemployed persons. 1. To prevent migration from hilly and rural areas in search of jobs.




**Who is this for:** 
Individual, Industries; 
Category: Skills & Employment; 
Sector: Loan



**Where it applies:** 
Uttarakhand; 
State/ UT









**Benefits / Assistance:** **1. Financial Assistance (Loan):** - For the manufacturing sector enterprise, the maximum project cost is ₹25,00,000. - For the service and business sector, the maximum project cost is ₹10,00,000. **2. Margin Money as Grant (Subsidy):** - **Category A:** 25% of the total project cost, with a maximum of ₹6,25,000 for manufacturing and ₹2,50,000 for service and business activity, whichever is lower.- **Category B &amp; B+:** 20% of the total project cost, with a maximum of ₹5,00,000 for manufacturing and ₹2,00,000 for service and business activity, whichever is lower.- **Category C &amp; D:** 15% of the total project cost, with a maximum of ₹3,75,000 for manufacturing and ₹1,50,000 for service and business activity, whichever is lower.  > **Mode of Disbursement:** - Funding will be provided to eligible manufacturing, service establishments, and business activities through nationalized banks, regional rural banks, state cooperative banks, and other scheduled banks. - Margin money is provided as a grant by the Department of Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises, Government of Uttarakhand. > **Frequency of Disbursement:** - The frequency of loan disbursement is not explicitly stated but generally depends on the project&#39;s financing schedule. - The margin money, acting as a grant, is adjusted after 2 years of successful operation. > **Expected Time of Disbursement:** - The margin money will be adjusted as a grant upon successful operation of the enterprise for 2 years. > **Validity:** - The grant adjustment is valid after 2 years of successful enterprise operation.



**Benefit Type:** Composite







**Exclusions / Not eligible:** 1. The applicant or unit should not be a defaulter of any Nationalised Bank, Financial Institution, Cooperative Bank, or Institution. 1. The applicant must not have availed benefits from any self-employment scheme of the Government of India or the State Government in the last 5 years (unless for expansion after 5 years and not a defaulter). 1. The cost of land purchase will not be included in the total project cost.
