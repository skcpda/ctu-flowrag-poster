
# Mukhyamantri Swasthya Sahayata Yojana Jharkhand


**Short Title:** MMSSYJ





**Launch Date:** 2016-11-16





**Tags:** Financial Assistance, Health, Rural, Scheduled Tribe, Scheduled Caste, Backward Class



The &quot;Mukhyamantri Swasthya Sahayata Yojana&quot; was launched by the Department of Scheduled Tribe, Scheduled Caste, Minority and Backward Class Welfare, Government of Jharkhand, and aims to provide financial assistance for medical treatment to eligible beneficiaries. Through this scheme, monetary support is provided to alleviate financial burdens caused by illnesses, surgeries, or other medical needs.




**Who is this for:** 
Individual; 
Category: Health & Wellness; 
Sector: Children’s health and immunization



**Where it applies:** 
Jharkhand; 
State/ UT
