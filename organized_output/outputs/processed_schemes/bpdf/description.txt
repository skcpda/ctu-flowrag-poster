
# Biotechnology Post-Doctoral Fellowship


**Short Title:** BPDF









**Tags:** Biotechnology, Post-Doctoral, Fellowship, Young Researcher, PhD, Stipend



The “Biotechnology Post-Doctoral Fellowship (BPDF)” programme is offered to young researchers who have completed their PhD in relevant areas of Biotechnology for promoting independent research mentored by eminent scientists/faculty in reputed institutions by providing fellowship.




**Who is this for:** 
Individual; 
Category: Education & Learning; 
Sector: Education and training grants, fellowship, stipend



**Where it applies:** 
Kerala; 
State









**Benefits / Assistance:** 1. The Post-Doctoral Fellowship carries an amount of ₹32,000/- per month + HRA (10%) with an annual contingent grant of ₹50,000/-. 1. Candidates who have submitted a thesis for a PhD and are awaiting the award of a PhD, will be paid only ₹22,000/- per month + 10% HRA.



**Benefit Type:** Cash






















**Contacts & Authorities:** 
Implementing agency: Kerala Biotechnology Commission, KSCSTE.
Nodal Department: Science and Technology Department.
