
# Phool Vikash Yojana


**Short Title:** PVY









**Tags:** Agriculture, Subsidy, Plant Supply, Financial Assistance, Flower Development



The “Phool Vikash Yojana” launched by the Department of Agriculture, Bihar, aims to support farmers in 15 districts of the state by offering financial assistance for marigold flower cultivation. The scheme provides a 50% subsidy for up to 4 hectares. &lt;br&gt; &lt;br&gt;




**Who is this for:** 
Individual; 
Category: Agriculture,Rural & Environment; 
Sector: Agricultural Inputs- seeds, fertilizer etc.



**Where it applies:** 
Bihar; 
State/ UT



**DBT Scheme:** Yes (Direct Benefit Transfer)







**Benefits / Assistance:** - **Financial Assistance:** 50% subsidy on ₹80,000/- per hectare for marigold cultivation. &lt;br&gt;



**Benefit Type:** Cash






















**Contacts & Authorities:** 

Nodal Department: Agriculture Department.
