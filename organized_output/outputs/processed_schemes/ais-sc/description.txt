
# Ambedkar International Scholarship - SC


**Short Title:** AIS-SC









**Tags:** Financial Assistance, PhD, Student, Scheduled Caste, International



The &quot;Ambedkar International Scholarship - SC&quot; scheme by the Social Justice and Empowerment Department, Government of Rajasthan aims to support meritorious scheduled caste (SC) students in pursuing Ph.D. studies in foreign universities. Under this scheme, financial assistance is provided to SC students enrolled in Ph.D. programs in Humanities and Social Sciences. The scheme is implemented by the Rajasthan Residential Educational Institutions Society (RAJEDU).




**Who is this for:** 
Individual; 
Category: Education & Learning; 
Sector: Scholarships and student finance



**Where it applies:** 
Rajasthan; 
State/ UT









**Benefits / Assistance:** **Financial Assistance:** ₹25,00,000/- is granted to selected candidates in three installments: - ₹10,00,000/- in the first year - ₹10,00,000/- in the second year - ₹5,00,000/- in the third year **Scholarship Validity:****** Up to three years. 



**Benefit Type:** Cash






















**Contacts & Authorities:**
