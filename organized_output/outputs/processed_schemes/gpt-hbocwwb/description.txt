
# Grant For Purchase Of Tools (HBOCWWB)


**Short Title:** GPT-HBOCWWB









**Tags:** Construction Worker, Labour, Tools, Financial Assistance



The scheme “Grant for Purchase of Tools (HBOCWWB)” was launched by the Haryana Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board (HBOCWWB), Labour Department Haryana. Under the scheme, a grant of ₹8000/- is given to the registered construction workers to buy new and suitable tool kits once in 3 years.




**Who is this for:** 
Individual; 
Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; 
Sector: Financial assistance



**Where it applies:** 
Haryana; 
State









**Benefits / Assistance:** 1. A grant of ₹8000/- is given to the registered construction workers to buy new and suitable tool kits. 2. The grant facility is available once in three years.



**Benefit Type:** Cash






















**Contacts & Authorities:** 
Implementing agency: Haryana Building And Other Construction Workers Welfare Board.
Nodal Department: Labour Department.
