
# Natural Death Relief (MBOCWWB)


**Short Title:** NDR-MBOCWWB









**Tags:** Financial Assistance, Death, Construction Worker, Kith And Kin



The &quot;Natural Death Relief&quot; scheme launched by the Manipur Building and Other Construction Workers&#39; Welfare Board provides financial assistance of ₹30,000/- to the kith and kin of a registered building worker in the event of the worker&#39;s natural death. This amount is paid as a one-time relief.




**Who is this for:** 
Family; 
Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; 
Sector: Financial assistance



**Where it applies:** 
Manipur; 
State/ UT









**Benefits / Assistance:** - Financial assistance of ₹30,000/- is provided for natural death as a free additional benefit (FREE ADD).



**Benefit Type:** Cash






















**Contacts & Authorities:** 

Nodal Department: Labour and Employment Department.
