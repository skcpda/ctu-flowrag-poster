
# Free Scooty Distribution for Girls (GEN-CATEGAORY-EWS)


**Short Title:** FSDFG









**Tags:** Scooty, Girl Student, Economically Weaker Section, Meritorious Student, EWS



The &quot;Free Scooty Distribution for Girls&quot; scheme was launched by the Secondary Education Department, Government of Rajasthan in the year 2017-18, aims to encourage meritorious girl students belonging to the economically weaker section of the general category in the state. Under this scheme, meritorious girl students from government and private schools who have scored 85% or above in the Class 10th and 12th (Arts, Commerce, Science streams) examinations are provided with a free scooty, along with a helmet and petrol.




**Who is this for:** 
Individual; 
Category: Education & Learning




**Where it applies:** 
Rajasthan; 
State/ UT









**Benefits / Assistance:** Under this scheme, Eligible girl students will receive a scooty, a helmet, and 2 liters of petrol.



**Benefit Type:** In Kind







**Exclusions / Not eligible:** - The applicant should not have received a scooty through any other scheme.
















**Contacts & Authorities:** 

Nodal Department: Education (Elementary and Secondary) Department.
