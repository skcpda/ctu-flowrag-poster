
# Mentally Retarded Children Benefit Scheme (P.B.O.C.W.W.B)


**Short Title:** MRCBSPBOCWWB









**Tags:** Labour, Building Worker, Construction Worker, Mentally Retarded, Children



The &amp;quot;Mentally Retarded Children Benefit Scheme&amp;#39;&amp;#39; by the BOCW Welfare Board, Department of Labour, Punjab, is a welfare scheme for the registered workers of the board. Under this scheme, the Board shall provide financial assistance for the care of mentally retarded children (son/ daughter) of construction workers. &lt;br&gt;




**Who is this for:** 
Individual; 
Category: Health & Wellness; 
Sector: Mental health



**Where it applies:** 
Punjab; 
State/ UT









**Benefits / Assistance:** - **Financial Coverage:** ₹20,000/- Per Year.



**Benefit Type:** Cash






















**Contacts & Authorities:** 

Nodal Department: Department of Labour.
