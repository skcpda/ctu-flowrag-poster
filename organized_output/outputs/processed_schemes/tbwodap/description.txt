
# Tricycles - Tamil Nadu


**Short Title:** TTN









**Tags:** Tricycles, Person With Disability, Social Welfare, Orthopedically



The Department launched the &#39;Tricycles by Welfare of Differently Abled Persons&#39; scheme for the Welfare of Differently Abled Persons, Govt. of Tamil Nadu. The scheme aims to distribute tricycles to deserving orthopedically differently-abled persons affected by both lower limbs and upper limbs that are normal for their easy mobility. The applications are accepted offline in the prescribed form available at the District Differently Abled Welfare Office.




**Who is this for:** 
Individual; 
Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; 
Sector: Aids/Appliances



**Where it applies:** 
Tamil Nadu; 
State/ UT









**Benefits / Assistance:** - **Free distribution of tricycles.**



**Benefit Type:** In Kind
