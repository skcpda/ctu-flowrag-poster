
# Gaon Ki Beti


**Short Title:** GKB





**Launch Date:** 2005-06-01





**Tags:** Financial Assistance, Higher Education, Scholarship



This scheme aims to provide financial assistance to talented girls from rural areas to encourage them to pursue higher education. It seeks to motivate young girls to continue their studies and build a bright future.




**Who is this for:** 
Individual; 
Category: Education & Learning; 
Sector: Scholarships and student finance



**Where it applies:** 
Madhya Pradesh; 
State









**Benefits / Assistance:** 1. Each year, girls who pass the 12th standard with first-class marks from a village will be eligible. 1. Selected students will receive a scholarship of ₹500/- per month. 1. The scholarship will be provided for a maximum of 10 months per year.



**Benefit Type:** Cash






















**Contacts & Authorities:** 

Nodal Department: Higher Education Department.
