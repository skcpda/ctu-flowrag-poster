
# Farmers Interest Group (FIG)


**Short Title:** FIG









**Tags:** Agriculture, Farmer, Farmers Interest Group, FIG



The “Farmers Interest Group (FIG)” scheme, by the Agriculture-Farmers Welfare Department, Tamil Nadu, supports every group of 15 farmers with grants for agricultural development. The scheme offers financial assistance for purchasing office equipment, training, ID cards, and district-level meetings. &lt;br&gt;




**Who is this for:** 
Registered Societies, Joint Liability Groups (JLGS); 
Category: Agriculture,Rural & Environment; 
Sector: Financial assistance



**Where it applies:** 
Tamil Nadu; 
State/ UT









**Benefits / Assistance:** - **Office Equipment:** ₹5,000/- per group for purchasing office equipment, technical bulletins, and setting up a library. - **Training and Skill Acquisition:** ₹4000/- per group for training and skill acquisition by farmer members. - **ID Cards:** ₹400/- per group for issuing ID cards to members. - **District-Level Meetings:** ₹20,000/- per group for conducting district-level meetings.



**Benefit Type:** Cash






















**Contacts & Authorities:** 

Nodal Department: Agriculture Department.
