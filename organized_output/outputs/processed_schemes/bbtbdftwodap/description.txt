
# Book Binder Training by Department for the Welfare of Differently Abled Persons


**Short Title:** BBTBDFTWODAP









**Tags:** Visually Impaired, Differently Abled Person, Training



The &#39;Book Binder Training by Department for the Welfare of Differently Abled Persons&#39; was launched by the Department for the Welfare of Differently Abled Persons, Government of Tamil Nadu. The scheme aims to provide imparted book binder training to visually impaired candidates at the government higher secondary school, poonamallee every year.




**Who is this for:** 
Individual; 
Category: Skills & Employment; 
Sector: Training and Skill Up-gradation



**Where it applies:** 
Tamil Nadu; 
State/ UT









**Benefits / Assistance:** - Free book binding training every year is imparted in government higher secondary schools for the visually impaired.



**Benefit Type:** In Kind






















**Contacts & Authorities:** 

Nodal Department: Welfare of Differently Abled Persons Department.
