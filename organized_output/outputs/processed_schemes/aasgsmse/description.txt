
# Award of Attendance Scholarship to Girl Students in the Middle Stage of Education


**Short Title:** AASGSMSE









**Tags:** Girl, Student, School, Scholarship, Education



The &quot;Award of Attendance Scholarship to Girl Students in the Middle Stage of Education&quot; was launched by the Department of Information &amp; Publicity, Union Territory of Puducherry. The objective of the scheme is to raise the enrolment of girls in the schools between the age group of 11-14 years in the middle stage of education and also to avoid dropouts among girl students.




**Who is this for:** 
Individual; 
Category: Education & Learning; 
Sector: Scholarships and student finance



**Where it applies:** 
Puducherry; 
State/ UT



**DBT Scheme:** Yes (Direct Benefit Transfer)







**Benefits / Assistance:** - **Scholarship Amount:** ₹200/- per annum per student.- **Mode of Disbursement:** Disbursed directly into Aadhaar-seeded bank account of the applicant through Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) and Public Financial Management System (PFMS).



**Benefit Type:** Cash






















**Contacts & Authorities:** 

Nodal Department: School Education Department, Puducherry.
