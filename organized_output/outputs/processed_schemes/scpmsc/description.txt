
# Schedule Caste Pre-Metric Scholarship Class 06 To 08


**Short Title:** SCPMSC





**Launch Date:** 1984-04-01





**Tags:** Financial Assitance, Education, Empowerment, Encourage



The Rajasthan government aims to provide educational opportunities to students belonging to Schedule Caste student, specifically those studying in grades 6 to 8. This program targets students from Schedule caste whose parents/guardians are not income taxpayers and not getting any other type of scholarship or allowance for study from central, state / public sources.




**Who is this for:** 
Individual; 
Category: Education & Learning; 
Sector: Scholarships and student finance



**Where it applies:** 
Rajasthan; 
State



**DBT Scheme:** Yes (Direct Benefit Transfer)







**Benefits / Assistance:** - For Schedule Caste Boy Students Rs. 75/- per month. - For Schedule Caste Girl Students Rs. 125/- per month.



**Benefit Type:** Cash






















**Contacts & Authorities:** 

Nodal Department: Education (Elementary and Secondary) Department.
