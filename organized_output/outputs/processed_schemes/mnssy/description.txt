
# Mukhyamantri Noni Sashaktikaran Sahayata Yojana


**Short Title:** MNSSY





**Launch Date:** 2022-01-29





**Tags:** Financial Assistance, Empowerment, Girl Education



In 2022, Chhattisgarh State Government started the &quot;Mukhyamantri Noni Sashaktikaran Sahayata Yojana&quot; for the girl child of the family. With this scheme, daughters of workers will be able to get education, employment and self-employment.




**Who is this for:** 
Family; 
Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; 
Sector: Financial assistance



**Where it applies:** 
Chhattisgarh; 
State









**Benefits / Assistance:** Financial Benefits: ₹ 20,000/- only first two girl child.



**Benefit Type:** Cash






















**Contacts & Authorities:** 

Nodal Department: Labour Department.
