
# Scheme on Promotion of Use of Liquid Fermented Organic Manure (LFOM) for Increasing Organic Carbon in Soil


**Short Title:** SPLFOM









**Tags:** Agriculture, Organic Farming, Liquid Manure, Soil Health



The &amp;quot;Scheme on Promotion of Use of Liquid Fermented Organic Manure (LFOM) for Increasing Organic Carbon in Soil&amp;quot; introduced by the Agriculture, Farmers Welfare and Cooperation Department, Gujarat, aims to encourage farmers to adopt eco-friendly agricultural practices. The scheme provides financial assistance of ₹4,000 per acre to farmers who utilize LFOM or Fermented Organic Manure (FOM) in their farming operations.




**Who is this for:** 
Individual; 
Category: Agriculture,Rural & Environment; 
Sector: Soil health



**Where it applies:** 
Gujarat; 
State/ UT









**Benefits / Assistance:** - An amount of ₹4,000/- per acre for using LFOM or FOM.



**Benefit Type:** Cash






















**Contacts & Authorities:** 

Nodal Department: Agriculture, Farmers Welfare and Cooperation Department.
