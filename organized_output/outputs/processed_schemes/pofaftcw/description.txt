
# Payment of Funeral Assistance for the Construction Workers


**Short Title:** POFAFTCW









**Tags:** Labour, Building Worker, Construction Worker, Funeral Assistance, Nominees, Dependents



The &amp;quot;Payment of Funeral Assistance for the Construction Workers&amp;quot; by the Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board, Labour Department, U.T. Administration of Dadra and Nagar Haveli and Daman and Diu, is a welfare scheme for the registered workers of the board. Under this scheme, the Board will provide financial assistance to the nominees/ dependents towards funeral expenses in case of death of a registered member or spouse. &lt;br&gt;




**Who is this for:** 
Individual; 
Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; 
Sector: Financial assistance



**Where it applies:** 
Dadra & Nagar Haveli and Daman & Diu; 
State/ UT









**Benefits / Assistance:** > **Quantum of Assistance:** ₹20,000/-.



**Benefit Type:** Cash






















**Contacts & Authorities:** 

Nodal Department: Labour & Employment Department.
