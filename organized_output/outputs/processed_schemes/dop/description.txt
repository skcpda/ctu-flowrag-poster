
# Distribution of Pulses


**Short Title:** DOP









**Tags:** Pulse, NFSA, JSFSS, Food, Subsidized Rate



The scheme &quot;Distribution of Pulses&quot; by the Department of Food, Public Distribution, and Consumer Affairs, Government of Jharkhand, aims to provide pulses at affordable rates to eligible households, ensuring nutritional support and food security. Under this scheme, pulses at subsidized rates are made available to beneficiaries holding AAY and PHH cards under NFSA and Green Cards under JSFSS.




**Who is this for:** 
Individual; 
Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; 
Sector: Food Security / Public Distribution System



**Where it applies:** 
Jharkhand; 
State/ UT









**Benefits / Assistance:** 1. Subsidized pulses are provided to the households. 1. The scheme supports food security by reducing the cost of essential pulses for low-income families. 1. Benefits are delivered monthly through designated ration shops. 1. Households holding AAY and PHH cards under NFSA and Green Cards under JSFSS are given Pulses.



**Benefit Type:** In Kind






















**Contacts & Authorities:** 
Implementing agency: Directorate Of Food And Consumer Affairs.
Nodal Department: Food, Public Distribution and Consumer affairs Department.
