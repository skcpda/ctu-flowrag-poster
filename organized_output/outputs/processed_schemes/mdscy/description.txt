
# Mukhyamantri Divyangjan Sashaktikaran Chhatra Yojana


**Short Title:** MDSCY









**Tags:** Persons With Disabilities, Tricycle, Student, Empowerment, Locomotor



The Government of Bihar, under the Directorate of Empowerment of Persons with Disabilities (Social Welfare Department), is committed to the empowerment of persons with disabilities. &quot;Mukhyamantri Divyangjan Sashaktikaran Chhatra Yojana&quot; Umbrella Scheme, also known as the Sambal Yojana, aims to provide comprehensive support to the disabled community in Bihar. A significant component of this scheme involves the distribution of battery-operated tricycles to eligible beneficiaries to enhance their mobility and independence.




**Who is this for:** 
Individual; 
Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; 
Sector: Aids/Appliances



**Where it applies:** 
Bihar; 
State/ UT









**Benefits / Assistance:** - **Free distribution of battery-operated tricycles.**



**Benefit Type:** In Kind






















**Contacts & Authorities:** 

Nodal Department: Social Welfare Department.
