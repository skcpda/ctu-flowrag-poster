
# Financial Assistance to 75% or Permanent Disability


**Short Title:** FAT75OPD









**Tags:** Financial Assistance, Disability, Construction Worker, Medical Treatment



The scheme Financial Assistance to 75% or Permanent Disability was launched by the Maharashtra Building And Other Construction Workers Welfare Board (MBOCWW), Labor Department Maharashtra. Under the scheme, financial assistance of ₹2,00,000/- to the registered worker in case of 75 % or more permanent disability (the benefit can be availed only after 26th July 2014 as the benefit is covered under the Mediclaim &amp; Personal Accident Insurance scheme).




**Who is this for:** 
Individual; 
Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; 
Sector: Financial assistance



**Where it applies:** 
Maharashtra; 
State/ UT









**Benefits / Assistance:** - Financial assistance of ₹2,00,000/- is provided by the Board to the registered worker for 75% or more permanent disability. 



**Benefit Type:** Cash
