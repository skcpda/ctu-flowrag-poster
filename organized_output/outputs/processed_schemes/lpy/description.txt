
# Laado Protsahan Yojana


**Short Title:** LPY





**Launch Date:** 2024-08-01





**Tags:** Financial Assistance, Maternity, Childcare, Girl Child, Education



The Scheme &amp;quot;Laado Protsahan Yojana&amp;quot; was launched on 1st August 2024 by the Department of Women Empowerment, Government of Rajasthan. The scheme aims to encourage the birth of girl children in poor families and promote their holistic development. Under this scheme, financial support is provided in seven installments to the parents or guardians of girls born in Government/authorized Medical Institutions. > **Objectives:** - Promote a positive mindset towards the birth of a girl child and ensure her holistic development. - Eliminate gender discrimination in childcare, education, and health. - Promote institutional deliveries and reduce maternal mortality. - Reduce infant mortality among girls and improve the child sex ratio. - Ensure enrollment and retention of girls in schools. - Encourage higher education for girls and reduce child marriage.

A commitment letter of ₹1.50 lakh will be issued at the birth of a girl child. 1. The entire amount will be disbursed online through Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) in seven installments. 1. Until the girl attains adulthood, the first six installments will be transferred to the bank account of her parents or guardian, and the seventh installment will be transferred to the girl&amp;#39;s own bank account through DBT. &gt; **Notes:** - If the benefit of any installment is not availed at any stage after the first two installments of the scheme, the eligible girl child may still be granted the next installment, provided a valid and reasonable justification is given. - There is no limit on the number of girl children eligible to receive the third and subsequent installments under the scheme. All girls who have already received the first two installments will be entitled to continue receiving benefits under the scheme. - The Rajshree Yojana will be merged with the Laado Protsahan Yojana, and the remaining installments of the Rajshree Yojana will be provided under the Laado Protsahan Yojana as per eligibility.
