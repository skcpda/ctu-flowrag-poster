
# "Advance/High Skill Training in Coir" Component of the "Development of Coir" Scheme


**Short Title:** AHSTC-DC









**Tags:** Training, Skill, Coir, Accomodation, Allowance, Trainee



The &quot;Advance/High Skill Training in Coir&quot; Component of the &quot;Development of Coir&quot; Scheme by the Department of Industries and Commerce, Union Territory of Puducherry, aims to enhance the skills of Coir artisans and interested individuals. The objective is to acquire the latest skills and modern technologies through advanced/high-skilled training available across various locations in India, offering valuable benefits to eligible candidates.




**Who is this for:** 
Individual; 
Category: Skills & Employment; 
Sector: Training and Skill Up-gradation



**Where it applies:** 
Puducherry; 
State/ UT









**Benefits / Assistance:** - Trainees receive a stipend of ₹2,500 per month. - Accommodation charges for trainees are ₹500 per month. - Travelling allowance covers to-and-fro 2nd class sleeper train fare. - Full course fees are paid to the training units/institutions as prescribed.



**Benefit Type:** Composite






















**Contacts & Authorities:** 

Nodal Department: District Industries Centre Organization.
