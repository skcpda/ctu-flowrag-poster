
# Prak Pariksha Prashikshan Kendra


**Short Title:** PPPK









**Tags:** Education, Learning, Coaching, Competitive Exams, Student



The &amp;quot;Prak Pariksha Prashikshan Kendra&amp;quot; is an initiative launched by the Backward Classes and Extremely Backward Classes Welfare Department, Bihar, aimed at supporting underprivileged candidates aspiring to appear for competitive exams. It offers free coaching for exams like Civil Services, SSC, and others. The program includes expert guidance, study materials, and mentoring, enabling candidates to prepare effectively and achieve their career goals. &lt;br&gt; &lt;br&gt;




**Who is this for:** 
Individual; 
Category: Education & Learning




**Where it applies:** 
Bihar; 
State/ UT









**Benefits / Assistance:** - **Free Coaching:** Free coaching in [Prak Pariksha Prashikshan Kendras](https://state.bihar.gov.in/bcebcwelfare/cache/41/30-Dec-24/SHOW_DOCS/Letter%20No-I-179552.pdf). - **Incentive:** Students will be provided an incentive amount of ₹3,000/- (three thousand rupees) based on 75% attendance. **Note:****** There is no hostel provision in the centers.



**Benefit Type:** In Kind






















**Contacts & Authorities:** 

Nodal Department: BC & EBC Welfare Department.
