
# Kushal Shramik Sahay Yojana (GBOCWWB)


**Short Title:** KSSU(GBOCWWB)









**Tags:** Building Worker, Construction Worker, Labour, Skill Education



The “Kushal Shramik Sahay Yojana” is implemented by the Gujarat Building and Other Construction Worker’s Welfare Board (GBOCWWB), Labour, Skill Development &amp;amp; Employment Department, Government of Gujarat. Under the scheme, assistance shall be provided to registered construction workers and their children for career development. By advancing in skill education, the children of construction workers can also progress and avail employment opportunities.




**Who is this for:** 
Individual, Family; 
Category: Skills & Employment; 
Sector: Academic qualification Up-Gradation



**Where it applies:** 
Gujarat; 
State/ UT



**DBT Scheme:** Yes (Direct Benefit Transfer)







**Benefits / Assistance:** 1. Under the scheme, the full course fee will be reimbursed. 1. If the course will be of less than 1 year duration, all fees of the course will be reimbursed. 1. If the course is for more than one year all fees will be reimbursed annually.



**Benefit Type:** Composite






















**Contacts & Authorities:** 
Implementing agency: Gujarat Building And Other Construction Worker’s Welfare Board.
Nodal Department: Labour and Employment Department.
