
# Award of Scholarship to Differently Abled Person Students


**Short Title:** ASDAPS









**Tags:** Scholarship, Student, PwD, Disability, Award



The scheme “Award of Scholarship to Differently Abled Person Students” by the Department of Social Welfare, Government of Puducherry was introduced to provide scholarship to the differently abled persons who are students for enabling them to secure education. The assistance is extended to Student with Disabilities studying from Class 1st upto Postgraduate/ Professional courses.




**Who is this for:** 
Individual; 
Category: Education & Learning; 
Sector: Scholarships and student finance



**Where it applies:** 
Puducherry; 
State/ UT









**Benefits / Assistance:** - 1st to 5th Standard: ₹ 1,000/- per year. - 6th to 8th Standard: ₹ 2,000/- per year. - 9th to 12th Standard: ₹ 3,400/- per year. - Undergraduate Course: ₹ 5,000/- per year. - Postgraduate/Professional Course: ₹ 6,800/- per year.



**Benefit Type:** Cash






















**Contacts & Authorities:** 

Nodal Department: Social Welfare Department, Puducherry.
