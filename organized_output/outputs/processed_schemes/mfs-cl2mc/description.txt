
# Micro Finance Scheme- Credit Line 2 for Minority Community


**Short Title:** MFS-CL2MC









**Tags:** Micro Finance, Credit Line, Minority Community, Loan, SHG



The scheme &amp;quot;Micro Finance Scheme- Credit Line 2 for Minority Community&amp;quot; is implemented by the Haryana Backward Classes and Economically Weaker Sections Kalyan Nigam to fulfill the requirement of small loans for the poor. Under the scheme, small loans of ₹1,50,000/- per beneficiary are provided to members of Self-Help Groups (SHGs), either directly or through the SHGs. Applicants must be permanent residents of Haryana and belong to minority communities such as Muslims, Sikhs, Christians, Buddhists, Parsis, and Jains.




**Who is this for:** 
Individual, Self Help Groups (SHGS); 
Category: Banking,Financial Services and Insurance; 
Sector: Loan



**Where it applies:** 
Haryana; 
State/ UT









**Benefits / Assistance:** 1. This scheme provides small loans of ₹1,50,000/- per beneficiary to the members of Self-Help Groups (SHGs), either directly or through the SHGs.
