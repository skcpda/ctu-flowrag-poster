
# Dr. Babu Jagjivan Ram Living cum work shed Construction Scheme


**Short Title:** DBJRLCWSCS









**Tags:** Financial Assistance, Housing, Subsidy, Artisans



The &quot;Dr. Babu Jagjivan Ram Living cum work shed Construction Scheme&quot; provides financial assistance to houseless leather artisans for constructing Living cum Work Sheds through the Rajiv Gandhi Rural Housing Corporation. The artisans who own a site, old house, or hut in their name can avail the benefit.




**Who is this for:** 
Individual; 
Category: Housing & Shelter; 
Sector: Shelter, integrated support and assistance



**Where it applies:** 
Karnataka; 
State/ UT









**Benefits / Assistance:** - **Financial Assistance:** ₹2,50,000/- for construction.- **Subsidy Amount:** ₹2,20,000/- provided by the government.- **Beneficiary Contribution:** ₹30,000/-.



**Benefit Type:** Cash






















**Contacts & Authorities:** 

Nodal Department: Social Welfare Department.
