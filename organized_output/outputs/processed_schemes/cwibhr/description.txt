
# PMMSY: Cycle with Ice Boxes-Haryana


**Short Title:** CWIBHR









**Tags:** Subsidy, Purchase, Initial Crop, Fish



The scheme &quot;Pradhan Mantri Matasya Sampada Yojana: Cycle with Ice Boxes&quot; by the Ministry Of Fisheries, Animal Husbandry &amp; Dairying, Government of India, aims to provide central assistance for the cost of cycles with ice boxes to beneficiaries involved in fisheries activities. Through this scheme, central assistance for Cycle with Ice Box costs is provided for the initial crop only in the purchase of newly purchased cycles with ice boxes. 




**Who is this for:** 
Individual; 
Category: Agriculture,Rural & Environment; 
Sector: Financial assistance



**Where it applies:** 
Haryana; 
State/ UT









**Benefits / Assistance:** - The scheme provides a subsidy for the cycle with ice boxes for fisheries activities. - The project cost is ₹0.10/- lakh per unit. - The subsidy will be limited to 40% of the project cost for beneficiaries falling under the General Category. - The subsidy will be limited to 60% of the project cost for beneficiaries who are Scheduled Caste (SC) or Women.  > **Note:** The Government of India and the Government of Haryana shall not be responsible for any losses incurred on procurement, operation, maintenance, and management of the fish transport facilities. 



**Benefit Type:** Cash






















**Contacts & Authorities:** 

Nodal Department: Fisheries Department.
