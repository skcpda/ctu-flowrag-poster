
# Disability Relief - Telangana


**Short Title:** DRT





**Launch Date:** 2015-05-18





**Tags:** Financial Assistance, Disability Relief, Accident, Construction Worker



The &quot;Disability Relief - TBOCWWB&quot; is a welfare initiative implemented by the Telangana Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board, LET&amp;F (Labour) Department, Telangana. The scheme aims to provide financial support to registered building and other construction workers who suffer from total or partial permanent disabilities due to accidents. This scheme offers enhanced relief amounts to help workers cope with the financial burden resulting from their disabilities.




**Who is this for:** 
Individual; 
Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; 
Sector: Crisis/Disaster/Accident



**Where it applies:** 
Telangana; 
State/ UT









**Benefits / Assistance:** **Relief for Total Permanent Disability:** - A relief amount of ₹5,00,000/-.  **Relief for Partial Permanent Disability:** - A relief amount of up to ₹4,00,000/-.



**Benefit Type:** Cash






















**Contacts & Authorities:** 

Nodal Department: Labour, Employment Training and Factories Department.
