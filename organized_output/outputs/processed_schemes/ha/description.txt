
# Housing Assistance (Madhya Pradesh)


**Short Title:** HA









**Tags:** Student, Hostel, Higher Education, Housing



The scheme &quot;Housing Assistance (Madhya Pradesh)&quot; by the Tribal Welfare and Scheduled Caste (SC) Department, Government of Madhya Pradesh, provides housing support to tribal boys and girls pursuing higher education outside their hometown, where hostels are not available.




**Who is this for:** 
Individual; 
Category: Housing & Shelter; 
Sector: Hostel service



**Where it applies:** 
Madhya Pradesh; 
State/ UT









**Benefits / Assistance:** The scheme provides housing assistance to tribal boys/girls to continue their higher and higher education outside their home town (for whom hostels are not available).



**Benefit Type:** In Kind






















**Contacts & Authorities:** 

Nodal Department: Tribal Welfare and Scheduled Caste (SC) Department.
