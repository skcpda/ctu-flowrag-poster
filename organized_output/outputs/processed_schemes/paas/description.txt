
# Personal Accident Assistance Scheme


**Short Title:** PAAS









**Tags:** Agricultural Laborers, Farmers, Mandi Workers, Agricultural Accidents, Compensation



Under the Uttarakhand State Mandi Act, the Personal Accident Assistance Scheme provides compensation in cases of injury or death caused by accidents occurring during agricultural activities or the use of farm-related equipment. It covers farmers, agricultural laborers, and mandi workers operating in regulated mandi areas. > **Covered activities include:** - Use of electric agricultural equipment - Digging/deepening of wells and ponds for irrigation - Use of tractors in agriculture - Threshing and harvesting - Any accident while performing farming tasks Compensation is disbursed by Mandi Parishads (Market Committees) under the supervision of the Mandi Board. 




**Who is this for:** 
Individual; 
Category: Agriculture,Rural & Environment; 
Sector: Financial assistance



**Where it applies:** 
Uttarakhand; 
State/ UT









**Benefits / Assistance:** - Compensation for death or physical injury due to agricultural accidents. - Coverage includes regulated mandi area farmers, agri-laborers, and mandi workers. 



**Benefit Type:** Cash







**Exclusions / Not eligible:** - Accidents not related to agricultural activities - Incidents occurring outside the jurisdiction of the Mandi Samiti.
















**Contacts & Authorities:**
