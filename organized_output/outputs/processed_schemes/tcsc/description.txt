
# Tiatr Cantaram Singing Competition 


**Short Title:** TCSC









**Tags:** Academy, Financial Support, Award, Artist, Singing, Competition



The &quot;Tiatr Cantaram Singing Competition&quot;, organised by the Kala Academy under the Department of Art, Culture and Museums, Government of Goa, aims to promote singing talent, preserve and revive the tradition of Tiatr songs, and encourage both traditional and new compositions that reflect Goa’s rich Tiatr heritage. The competition is open to all Goan artists.




**Who is this for:** 
Individual; 
Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; 
Sector: Financial assistance



**Where it applies:** 
Goa; 
State/ UT









**Benefits / Assistance:** - A common music band is provided by Kala Academy free of cost for practice and competition. - Each participant gets ₹150/- as travel and daily allowance. - Winners receive cash prizes in different categories.  **Solo Singing [Male &amp; Female]:** 1st Prize: ₹2,000/-, 2nd Prize: ₹1,500/-, 3rd Prize: ₹1,000/- **Duet Singing:** 1st Prize: ₹4,000/-, 2nd Prize: ₹3,000/-, 3rd Prize: ₹2,000/- **Duo Singing:** 1st Prize: ₹4,000/-, 2nd Prize: ₹3,000/-, 3rd Prize: ₹2,000/- **Trio Singing:** 1st Prize: ₹6,000/-, 2nd Prize: ₹4,500/-, 3rd Prize: ₹3,000/- **Quartet [Group of 4]:** 1st Prize: ₹8,000/-, 2nd Prize: ₹6,000/-, 3rd Prize: ₹4,000/- **Choral [Group Singing]:** 1st Prize: ₹8,000/-, 2nd Prize: ₹6,000/-, 3rd Prize: ₹4,000/-



**Benefit Type:** Cash
