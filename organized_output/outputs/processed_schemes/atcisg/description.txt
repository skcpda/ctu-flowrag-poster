
# Assistance to Co-operative Irrigation Societies M. N. R.-10 (General Area)


**Short Title:** ATCISG









**Tags:** Irrigation, Co-operative Irrigation Societies, Farm Productivity, Water Distribution



The scheme &amp;quot;Assistance to Co-operative Irrigation Societies, M. N. R.-10 (General Area)&amp;quot; by the Agriculture and Co-operation Department, Government of Gujarat, aims to enhance agricultural yield in the state by offering assistance to irrigation cooperative societies formed by farmers of a common area. The support involves establishing water distribution systems using pumps and electric motors on wells and agar creeks to facilitate effective irrigation. 




**Who is this for:** 
Registered Societies, Individual; 
Category: Agriculture,Rural & Environment; 
Sector: Land and water resources



**Where it applies:** 
Gujarat; 
State/ UT









**Benefits / Assistance:** - **Monetary Benefit:** Financial assistance for setting up irrigation infrastructure (such as pumps and motors) for cooperative societies. 



**Benefit Type:** In Kind






















**Contacts & Authorities:** 

Nodal Department: Agriculture, Farmers Welfare and Cooperation Department.
