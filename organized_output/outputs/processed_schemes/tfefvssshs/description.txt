
# Tuition Fees And Examination Fees To Vjnt/sbc Students Studying In High Schools


**Short Title:** Tfefvssshs









**Tags:** Vimukta Jati Nomadic Tribes, Special Backward Class, Tuition Fees, Examination Fees, High School



The Government of Maharashtra introduced Tuition fees and Examination fees to Vimukta Jati Nomadic Tribes (VJNT)/ Special Backward Classes (SBC) students studying in High Schools scheme to improve the interest of VJNT/SBC students in High Schools. To help and encourage the VJNT/SBC parents/ guardians for their children&#39;s education. Govt. has introduced a scheme named Tuition Fee Exam. Fees to VJNT/SBC students studying in High Schools, irrespective of age and income vide Govt. Resolution No. EBC/1068/83567/J dated 24th December 1970 and G.R. No. EBC-1094/PK-91/ BCW-1 dated 29 October 1996. Tuition fees, Examination fees, library fee, laboratory fee, and playground fee is being paid. 




**Who is this for:** 
Individual; 
Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; 
Sector: Financial assistance



**Where it applies:** 
Maharashtra; 
State









**Benefits / Assistance:** 1. BPL students studying in Govt. approved aided, non-aided, permanent non-aided schools in 1st to 10th standard are being paid Rs.100/- to 200/- Education Fee as per Urban and Rural area. 1. Tuition fees, Examination fees, Laboratory fees, Library fees, etc. fees are sanctioned and paid to concerned Govt. approved aided, non-aided, permanent non-aided schools. 1. No other concessional benefit is admissible to such students.



**Benefit Type:** In Kind
