
# Distribution of Horticulture Kit to Urban and Rural Areas


**Short Title:** DHKURA









**Tags:** Horticulture, Agriculture, Farmer, Subsidy, DBT



The scheme “Distribution of Horticulture Kit to Urban and Rural Areas&quot; was launched by the Department of Agriculture and Farmer&#39;s Welfare, Union Territory of Puducherry. Through this scheme, all the horticulture growers in rural &amp; urban areas are provided with a horticulture kit worth ₹ 3,000 per kit to urban and rural areas, during Aadipattam/Thaipattam, through the Additional Director of Agriculture (Horticulture).




**Who is this for:** 
Individual; 
Category: Agriculture,Rural & Environment; 
Sector: Agricultural Inputs- seeds, fertilizer etc.



**Where it applies:** 
Puducherry; 
State/ UT









**Benefits / Assistance:** 75% subsidy, i.e. distribution of horticulture kit worth ₹ 3,000 per kit to urban and rural areas (homestead/backyards), through the Additional Director of Agriculture (Horticulture), during Aadipattam/Thaipattam seasonality.



**Benefit Type:** In Kind






















**Contacts & Authorities:** 

Nodal Department: Agriculture Department, Puducherry.
