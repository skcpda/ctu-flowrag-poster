
# Labour Skill Training Scheme (MPBOCWWB)


**Short Title:** LSTSMPBOCWWB





**Launch Date:** 2018-08-01





**Tags:** Building Worker, Construction Worker, Education, Skill Training



Launched on 1st August 2018, the &quot;Labour Skill Training Scheme&quot; is a skill development initiative by the Building and Other Construction Workers Board, Government of Madhya Pradesh. The scheme offers free skill training to construction workers aged 18-45 years and their dependents aged 16-45 years, with eligibility based on age criteria for both workers and their dependents.




**Who is this for:** 
Individual; 
Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; 
Sector: Financial assistance



**Where it applies:** 
Madhya Pradesh; 
State/ UT









**Benefits / Assistance:** The scheme provides free skill training to construction workers and their dependents.



**Benefit Type:** In Kind
