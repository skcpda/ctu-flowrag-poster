
# Primary Cooperative Agriculture and Rural Development Bank: For Animal Husbandry


**Short Title:** PCARDAH









**Tags:** Animal Husbandry Loan, Agriculture, Farmer



The &amp;quot;Primary Cooperative Agriculture and Rural Development Bank: For Animal Husbandry&amp;quot; scheme launched by the Co-operation, Food and Consumer Protection Department, Tamil Nadu, aims to provide financial support to farmers specifically for animal husbandry activities. This initiative aims to empower farmers by boosting livestock development and enhancing agricultural productivity. &lt;br&gt; &lt;br&gt;




**Who is this for:** 
Individual; 
Category: Banking,Financial Services and Insurance; 
Sector: Loan



**Where it applies:** 
Tamil Nadu; 
State/ UT









**Benefits / Assistance:** - **Access to Loan:** 1. **For purchasing 2 Buffaloes:** ₹20,000/-. 1. **For purchasing 21 Goats:** 24,500/-. - **Interest Rate:** 14.5% to 16.5% (subject to change). 



**Benefit Type:** Cash






















**Contacts & Authorities:** 

Nodal Department: Co-operation, Food and Consumer Protection Department.
