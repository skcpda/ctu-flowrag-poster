
# Safai Karmakaar Putr/Putri Cycle sahayata yojana


**Short Title:** SKPCSY





**Launch Date:** 2015-08-27





**Tags:** Free Bicycle, Vehicle, Empowerment



Labor Department of Chhattisgarh State started this scheme &amp;amp;quot;Safai Karmakaar Putr/Putree Cycle Sahayata Yojana&amp;amp;quot; for unorganized worker on 27 August 2015. The main objective of the scheme to empower the unorganized worker son/daughter worker through provide them free cycle.




**Who is this for:** 
Individual; 
Category: Education & Learning




**Where it applies:** 
Chhattisgarh; 
State









**Benefits / Assistance:** > A free of cost cycle will be provided.



**Benefit Type:** In Kind

Nodal Department: Labour Department.
