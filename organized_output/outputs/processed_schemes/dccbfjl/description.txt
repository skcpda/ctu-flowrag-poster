
# District Central Cooperative Banks and through its Branches: For Jewel Loan


**Short Title:** DCCBFJL









**Tags:** Jewel Loan, Banking



The &amp;quot;District Central Cooperative Banks and through its Branches: For Jewel Loan&amp;quot; scheme launched by the Co-operation, Food and Consumer Protection Department, Tamil Nadu, allows citizens to obtain loans by pledging their gold and jewelry. The maximum loan amount is ₹1,00,000/- with an interest rate of 12%, subject to changes as per bank policies.




**Who is this for:** 
Individual; 
Category: Banking,Financial Services and Insurance; 
Sector: Loan



**Where it applies:** 
Tamil Nadu; 
State/ UT









**Benefits / Assistance:** - **Access to Loan:** Up to ₹1,00,000/-.- **Interest Rate:** 12% (subject to change).



**Benefit Type:** Cash






















**Contacts & Authorities:** 

Nodal Department: Co-operation, Food and Consumer Protection Department.
