
# Assistance for Cattleshed, Gaman, Water Tank and Bucket


**Short Title:** AFCGWTB









**Tags:** Animal Husbandry, Cattleshed, Water Tank, Shelter For Cattle



The scheme &amp;quot;Assistance for Cattleshed, Gaman, Water Tank and Bucket&amp;quot; introduced by the Agriculture, Farmers Welfare and Cooperation Department, Gujarat, aims to provide financial aid to farmers and cattle rearers for the construction of cattlesheds, purchasing gaman (cattle feed containers), water tanks, and buckets.




**Who is this for:** 
Individual; 
Category: Agriculture,Rural & Environment; 
Sector: Animal husbandry



**Where it applies:** 
Gujarat; 
State/ UT
