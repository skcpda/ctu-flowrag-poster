
# "Back Ended Investment Subsidy to Paddy Growers" component of the "Crop Production Technology" scheme


**Short Title:** BEISPGCCPTS









**Tags:** Paddy, Subsidy, Financial Assistance, Farmer, Agriculture



The &quot;Back Ended Investment Subsidy to Paddy Growers&quot; component of the &quot;Crop Production Technology&quot; scheme was introduced by the Department of Agriculture and Farmer&#39;s Welfare, Union Territory of Puducherry. Through this scheme, the paddy farmers residing in the Yanam region are provided a back-ended investment subsidy at ₹4,000/- per hectare.




**Who is this for:** 
Individual; 
Category: Agriculture,Rural & Environment; 
Sector: Financial assistance



**Where it applies:** 
Puducherry; 
State/ UT









**Benefits / Assistance:** Back-ended investment subsidy to paddy growers @ ₹4,000/- per hectare.



**Benefit Type:** Cash






















**Contacts & Authorities:** 

Nodal Department: Agriculture Department, Puducherry.
