
# Post SSC Scholarship for Boys/Girls from Socially and Educationally Backward Classes


**Short Title:** PSSCSBGSEBC









**Tags:** Scholarship, SSC, Student, SEBC, NTDNT



The “Post SSC Scholarship for Boys/Girls from Socially and Educationally Backward Classes” scheme was launched by the Social Justice and Empowerment Department, Government of Gujarat. The scheme aims to provide financial assistance to students from Socially and Educationally Backward Classes (SEBC) and Nomadic and De-notified Tribes (NTDNT) pursuing education after SSC. Under this scheme, eligible students receive yearly scholarships ranging from ₹1,500/ to ₹5,000/-, depending on their academic group (Group A to D).
