
# Vishesh Katan, Vankari & Vanat Sahay (Spinning & Weaving Assistance Scheme)


**Short Title:** VKVVS-SWAS









**Tags:** Artisan, Spinner, Weaver, Yarn, Khadi



The scheme &quot;Vishesh Katan, Vankari &amp; Vanat Sahay (Spinning &amp; Weaving Assistance Scheme)&quot; by the Gujarat Rajya Khadi Gramodyog Board, Industries &amp; Mines Department, Government of Gujarat, aims to provide financial assistance in addition to the central government assistance to spinners and weavers. The scheme provides production incentive for spinning of yarn and weaving of Khadi with additional financial assistance ranging from ₹1/- per hank for general and Scheduled Caste spinners to ₹4/- per square meter for Scheduled Tribe weavers, along with weaving equipment aid of ₹1/- per square meter. The applicant must be Khadi spinners and weavers engaged in production activities. The scheme is implemented by Gujarat Rajya Khadi Gramodyog Board under Industries &amp; Mines Department and applications are accepted as per the board&#39;s procedures. 




**Who is this for:** 
Individual; 
Category: Business & Entrepreneurship; 
Sector: Setting up / start-up / entrepreneurship



**Where it applies:** 
Gujarat; 
State/ UT









**Benefits / Assistance:** > **For Spinners** - General and Scheduled Caste Categories: Additional ₹1/- per hank for spinning of yarn. - Women and Scheduled Tribe Categories: Additional ₹1.5/- per hank for spinning of yarn. > **For Weavers** - General and Scheduled Caste Categories: Additional ₹3/- per square meter for weaving of Khadi. - Scheduled Tribe Category: Additional ₹4/- per square meter for weaving of Khadi. - Weaving Equipment Aid: ₹1/- per square meter weaving equipment aid.  > **Mode and Frequency of Disbursement** - Production incentive based on actual spinning and weaving output. - Payment structure linked to per hank for spinning and per square meter for weaving. > **Conditions for Availment/Continuation** - Beneficiaries must be actively engaged in Khadi spinning and weaving activities. - Incentives are provided in addition to central government assistance. - Must maintain production records for verification purposes. 



**Benefit Type:** Cash
