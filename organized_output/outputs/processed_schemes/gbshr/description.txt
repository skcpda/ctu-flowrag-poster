
# Green Business Scheme - Haryana


**Short Title:** GBSHR









**Tags:** E-rickshaw, Compressed Air Vehicle, Solar Energy Gadgets, Poly Houses, Loan, Green Business



The &amp;quot;Green Business Scheme&amp;quot; is implemented by the Haryana Scheduled Castes Finance and Development Corporation (HSCFDC) in collaboration with the National Safai Karamcharis Finance and Development Corporation (NSFDC) within the state of Haryana. Under this scheme, loans of up to ₹2.00 lakhs are provided to Scheduled Caste residents of Haryana for undertaking income-generating activities such as e-rickshaws, compressed air vehicles, solar energy gadgets, polyhouses, and similar ventures. Activities that contribute to mitigating the greenhouse effect are eligible for assistance under this scheme. 




**Who is this for:** 
Individual; 
Category: Business & Entrepreneurship; 
Sector: Financial assistance



**Where it applies:** 
Haryana; 
State/ UT









**Benefits / Assistance:** 1. The Corporation, in collaboration with NSFDC, provides a loan for various economic development schemes having a unit cost up to ₹2,00,000/-. 1. The Corporation provides margin money @10% of the project cost. 1. The Corporation (HSFDC) provides a subsidy of 50% of the total project cost, with a maximum amount of subsidy being ₹10,000/- of the total project cost. 



**Benefit Type:** Cash






















**Contacts & Authorities:** 
Implementing agency: Haryana Scheduled Castes Finance And Development Corporation.
Nodal Department: Welfare of Scheduled Caste & Backward Classes Department.
