
# Grant for the Purchase of Tools/ Protective Gear for the Construction Workers


**Short Title:** GFTPOTPGFTCW









**Tags:** Labour, Construction Workers, Building Workers, Purchase Of Tools, Protective Gear



The &amp;quot;Grant for the Purchase of Tools/ Protective Gear for the Construction Workers&amp;quot; by the Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board, Labour Department, U.T. Administration of Dadra and Nagar Haveli and Daman and Diu, is a welfare scheme for the registered workers of the board. Under this scheme, registered beneficiaries of the board shall be provided with financial assistance for purchase of tools.




**Who is this for:** 
Individual; 
Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; 
Sector: Financial assistance



**Where it applies:** 
Dadra & Nagar Haveli and Daman & Diu; 
State/ UT









**Benefits / Assistance:** - An amount not exceeding ₹10,000/-. **Note:** The benefit shall be allowed for once in a three years.



**Benefit Type:** Cash






















**Contacts & Authorities:** 

Nodal Department: Labour & Employment Department.
