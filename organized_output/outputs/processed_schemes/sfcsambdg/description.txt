
# Contribution to State Agriculture Marketing Board Development Fund (General)


**Short Title:** SFCSAMBDG









**Tags:** Agricultural Marketing, Market Committees, Development Fund, Co-operative Institutions, Infrastructure Development



The scheme &quot;Contribution to State Agriculture Marketing Board Development Fund (General)&quot; by the Agriculture and Co-operation Department, Government of Gujarat, aims to promote the development of agricultural marketing infrastructure by financially supporting the State Agriculture Marketing Board’s Development Fund. The scheme ensures that the State Government contributes no less than 5% of the total amount deposited in the Development Fund by the Market Committees, thereby enabling cooperative institutions to upgrade and modernize market operations across Gujarat. 




**Who is this for:** 
Registered Societies; 
Category: Agriculture,Rural & Environment; 
Sector: Entrepreneurship development



**Where it applies:** 
Gujarat; 
State/ UT









**Benefits / Assistance:** - **Monetary Benefits:** The scheme ensures a mandatory financial contribution from the State Government, amounting to not less than 5% of the total deposits made by the Market Committees into the Development Fund. 



**Benefit Type:** Cash






















**Contacts & Authorities:** 

Nodal Department: Agriculture, Farmers Welfare and Cooperation Department.
