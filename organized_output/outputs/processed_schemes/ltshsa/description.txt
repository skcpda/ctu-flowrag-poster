
# Loans to Tribal Students for Higher Studies Abroad


**Short Title:** LTSHSA









**Tags:** Student, Loan, Higher Education, Scheduled Tribe, Foreign Study



The &quot;Loans to Tribal Students for Higher Studies Abroad&quot; scheme was launched by the Tribal Development Department, Government of Gujarat. The scheme aims to provide financial assistance to tribal students who wish to pursue higher education in foreign universities. Through this scheme, eligible Scheduled Tribe (ST) students can avail a loan at a concessional interest rate of 4% per annum. 




**Who is this for:** 
Individual; 
Category: Education & Learning; 
Sector: Universities and higher education



**Where it applies:** 
Gujarat; 
State/ UT









**Benefits / Assistance:** **Monetary Benefit:** - Loan of up to ₹15,00,000 or the actual cost of study/training abroad, whichever is lower. **Interest Rate:** - 4% per annum. **Penalty Interest:** - 2.5% per annum for late repayment. **Beneficiary Contribution:** - No contribution is required from the beneficiary. **Repayment Terms:** - Repayment to be made in 60 monthly installments. - Repayment starts 6 months after completion of studies. - Early repayment is permitted. 



**Benefit Type:** Cash






















**Contacts & Authorities:** 

Nodal Department: Tribal Development Department.
