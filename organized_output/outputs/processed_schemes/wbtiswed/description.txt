
#  West Bengal Textile Incentive Scheme: Waiver of Electricity Duty


**Short Title:** WBTISWED





**Launch Date:** 2022-12-13





**Tags:** Textile, Incentives, Waiver, Entrepreneurship, Subsidy, Reimbursement, Electricity Duty



The scheme “Waiver of Electricity Duty” is a sub scheme under “West Bengal Textile Incentive Scheme”. It was launched on 13 December, 2022, by the Department of Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises and Textiles, Government of West Bengal, and shall remain valid upto five years. &gt; **Objective of the &amp;quot;West Bengal Textile Incentive Scheme” Scheme:** The object of the scheme is to extend fiscal incentives to textile sector across the entire value chain starting from fibre to stitched garments for setting up such units in the State. The focus is on accelerated and focused development of the textiles sector across the state. This shall enable to maximize the utilization of resources, generate new employment and widen the area of operation to make the state emerge as the preferred destination for investment in the textile sector. &gt; **Objective of the &amp;quot;Waiver of Electricity Duty&amp;quot; Scheme:** Through this incentive, an eligible textile sector enterprise will be entitled to waiver of electricity duty on the electricity consumed in its approved project.




**Who is this for:** 
Business Entity, Industries, Joint Liability Groups (JLGS), Registered Societies, Trust; 
Category: Business & Entrepreneurship; 
Sector: Setting up / start-up / entrepreneurship



**Where it applies:** 
West Bengal; 
State/ UT









**Benefits / Assistance:** - 100% waiver of electricity duty for 5 years from the date of commencement of production subject to a maximum of ₹1,00,00,000/- per year.



**Benefit Type:** Cash







**Exclusions / Not eligible:** - The incentives shall not be applicable to the industrial units/ enterprises that have been registered and for which eligibility certificates have been issued /incentives have been sanctioned and disbursed under the respective Incentive Scheme of the State. - That has been registered and for which eligibility certificates have been issued in terms of the respective Incentive Scheme of the State but no claim and sanction of incentive has been made.
