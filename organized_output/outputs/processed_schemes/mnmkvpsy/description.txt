
# Mukhyamantri Nirmaan Majadur Kaushal vikaas evan Parivaar Sashaktikaran Yojana


**Short Title:** MNMKVPSY





**Launch Date:** 2012-11-26





**Tags:** Business, Skill Upgrade, Empowerment



In 2012, Chhattisgarh State Government started &quot;Mukhyamantri Nirmaan Majadur Kaushal Vikaas Evan Parivaar Sashaktikaran Yojana&quot; for Building and other construction workers. The objective of the scheme is to enable the laborers of Chhattisgarh state to get more new skills in their work and more employment opportunities and with the skill development programs that generate new employment opportunities for the local people are also included.




**Who is this for:** 
Family; 
Category: Business & Entrepreneurship; 
Sector: Machine & skill up-Gradation



**Where it applies:** 
Chhattisgarh; 
State









**Benefits / Assistance:** 1. Registered construction workers will be given training for skill upgradation. 1. Registered construction workers will be provided certificates of their work trade. 1. Family members of registered construction workers will be given training in various trades as per their interest.



**Benefit Type:** In Kind






















**Contacts & Authorities:** 

Nodal Department: Labour Department.
