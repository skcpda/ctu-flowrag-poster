
# Futkar Sabji, Phal-Phool Vikreta Hetu Taraju, Baat Evan Tokari Sahaayata Yojana


**Short Title:** FSPPV





**Launch Date:** 2016-06-15





**Tags:** Empowerment, Financial Assistance, Equipment



Labor Department of Chhattisgarh State started this scheme &amp;quot;Futkar Sabji, Phal-Phool Vikreta Hetu Taraju, Baat Evan Tokari Sahaayata Yojana&amp;quot; for unorganized worker on 15 June 2016.. The main objective of the scheme to empower unorganized worker by providing financial assistance of Cycle or Torch .




**Who is this for:** 
Individual; 
Category: Business & Entrepreneurship; 
Sector: Setting up / start-up / entrepreneurship



**Where it applies:** 
Chhattisgarh; 
State









**Benefits / Assistance:** > An amount of Rs 700/- (Rs seven hundred only) per beneficiary will be provided for scales, weights and 02 baskets.



**Benefit Type:** Cash







**Exclusions / Not eligible:** > Applicant should not have received the similar benefits from any other schemes.
















**Contacts & Authorities:** 

Nodal Department: Labour Department.
