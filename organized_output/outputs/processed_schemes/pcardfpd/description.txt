
# Primary Cooperative Agriculture and Rural Development Bank: For Poultry Development


**Short Title:** PCARDFPD









**Tags:** Poultry Development, Cattle Development, Loan, Farmer, Agriculture And Rural Development Bank



The &amp;quot;Primary Cooperative Agriculture and Rural Development Bank: For Poultry Development&amp;quot; scheme launched by the Co-operation, Food and Consumer Protection Department, Tamil Nadu, aims to provide financial support to farmers for poultry and cattle development, with loans available up to ₹14,800/- for 200 poultry birds or ₹17,000/- for 2 cows. The interest rate ranges from 14.5% to 16.5%, subject to periodic changes.  




**Who is this for:** 
Individual; 
Category: Banking,Financial Services and Insurance; 
Sector: Loan



**Where it applies:** 
Tamil Nadu; 
State/ UT









**Benefits / Assistance:** - **Access to Loan:** 1. Up to ₹14,800/- for 200 birds (poultry). 1. Up to ₹17,000/- for 2 cows. - **Interest Rate:** 14.5% to 16.5% (subject to change). &lt;br&gt;



**Benefit Type:** Cash






















**Contacts & Authorities:** 

Nodal Department: Co-operation, Food and Consumer Protection Department.
