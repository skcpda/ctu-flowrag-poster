
# Supply of Tool Kits to their Trained Workers 


**Short Title:** STKTW









**Tags:** Tool Kit, Construction Worker, Training, Skill Upgradation



The A.P. Building &amp; Other Construction Workers Welfare Board launched a welfare scheme &quot;Supply of Tool Kits Karnataka&quot; to provide toolkits to construction workers who have undergone skill upgradation training in trades such as masonry, electrical, and plumbing. The scheme aims to improve workers&#39; employability by equipping them with essential tools. Toolkits are procured through an e-procurement process, ensuring quality and cost-effectiveness. Each worker receives a toolkit valued at ₹2,000.




**Who is this for:** 
Individual; 
Category: Skills & Employment; 
Sector: Training and Skill Up-gradation



**Where it applies:** 
Karnataka; 
State/ UT









**Benefits / Assistance:** - **Free toolkits are provided to trained workers.**



**Benefit Type:** In Kind






















**Contacts & Authorities:** 
Implementing agency: Karnataka Leather Industries Development Corporation Ltd.
Nodal Department: Social Welfare Department.
