
# Permanent Disability/ Death Benefit for the Construction Workers


**Short Title:** PDDBFTCW





**Launch Date:** 2024-07-17



**Closing Date:** 2024-07-25



**Tags:** Labour, Death Benefit, Construction Workers, Accidental Support, Financial Assistance, Permanent Disability, Divyang



The &amp;quot;Permanent Disability/ Death Benefit for the Construction Workers&amp;quot; by the Sikkim Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board, Labour Department, is a welfare scheme for the registered workers of the board. Under this scheme, financial assistance is provided to the nominees/ dependents of a member, in case of his/her death or Permanent Disability.




**Who is this for:** 
Individual; 
Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; 
Sector: Financial assistance



**Where it applies:** 
Sikkim; 
State/ UT









**Benefits / Assistance:** - ₹1,50,000/- is provided for Death Benefit.



**Benefit Type:** Cash






















**Contacts & Authorities:** 

Nodal Department: Labour Department.
