
# Tuition Sahay Yojana (Unreserved Category)


**Short Title:** TSYUC









**Tags:** Unreserved Category, Science, Tuition Fee Assistance, Education



The scheme &amp;quot;Tution Sahay Yojana (Unreserved Category)&amp;quot; by the Department of Social Justice and Empowerment, Government of Gujarat aims to support meritorious students from the unreserved category pursuing science in Classes 11 and 12 by providing them annual tuition fee assistance. This scheme ensures that students who scored 70% or more in their Class 10 board examination receive ₹15,000 through Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) to pursue science education. The scheme is implemented by the Gujarat Unreserved Educational &amp;amp; Economic Development Corporation.




**Who is this for:** 
Individual; 
Category: Education & Learning; 
Sector: Education and training grants, fellowship, stipend



**Where it applies:** 
Gujarat; 
State/ UT



**DBT Scheme:** Yes (Direct Benefit Transfer)







**Benefits / Assistance:** - **Monetary Assistance:** ₹15,000/- per academic year is provided as tuition assistance to each eligible student.



**Benefit Type:** Cash






















**Contacts & Authorities:** 
Implementing agency: Ujarat Unreserved Educational & Economic Development Corporation.
Nodal Department: Social Justice and Empowerment Department.
