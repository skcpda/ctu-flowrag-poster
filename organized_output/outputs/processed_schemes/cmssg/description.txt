
# Chief Minister Scholarship Scheme


**Short Title:** CMSSG









**Tags:** Scholarship, Higher Education, Diploma, Degree Scholarship, Medical Education Scholarship



The scheme &quot;Chief Minister Scholarship Scheme&quot; by the Education Department, Government of Gujarat, aims to encourage higher education by providing financial assistance to meritorious students who have passed Class 10th or Class 12th and secured admission in diploma, degree, or higher education courses. The scheme provides varying amounts of financial support based on the course type and merit. 




**Who is this for:** 
Individual; 
Category: Education & Learning; 
Sector: Scholarships and student finance



**Where it applies:** 
Gujarat; 
State/ UT
