
# Construction of Water Storage Tanks for Drip Irrigation


**Short Title:** CWSDI









**Tags:** Agriculture, Drip Irrigation, Farmer, Water Tank



The scheme &amp;quot;Construction of Water Storage Tanks for Drip Irrigation&amp;quot; introduced by the Agriculture, Farmers Welfare and Cooperation Department, Gujarat, aims to offer financial assistance to farmers for setting up cemented water tanks essential for efficient drip irrigation. It supports sustainable agriculture through water conservation, especially for horticultural practices.




**Who is this for:** 
Individual; 
Category: Agriculture,Rural & Environment; 
Sector: Land and water resources



**Where it applies:** 
Gujarat; 
State/ UT









**Benefits / Assistance:** - **Unit cost of construction:** ₹1,00,000/- per tank. - **For General category farmers:** 50% subsidy, up to ₹50,000/-. - **For SC/ST farmers:** 75% subsidy, up to ₹75,000/-.



**Benefit Type:** Cash






















**Contacts & Authorities:** 

Nodal Department: Agriculture, Farmers Welfare and Cooperation Department.
