
# Dr. Savita Ben Ambedkar Antarjatiya Vivah Prothsahan Yojana


**Short Title:** DSBAAVPY





**Launch Date:** 2017-08-01





**Tags:** Financial Assistance, Inter-caste, Marriage



The &quot;Dr. Savita Ben Ambedkar Antarjatiya Vivah Prothsahan Yojana&quot; implemented by the Social Justice and Empowerment Department, Government of Rajasthan. Under this scheme, a financial incentive of ₹5,00,000/- is provided to inter-caste married couples to support their married life and household setup. > ***Objectives:*** - To eliminate caste-based discrimination and untouchability. - To promote social harmony by encouraging inter-caste marriages between Scheduled Caste individuals and upper-caste Hindus. - To provide financial assistance to inter-caste couples for economic stability and social acceptance. 




**Who is this for:** 
Individual; 
Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; 
Sector: Financial assistance



**Where it applies:** 
Rajasthan; 
State/ UT









**Benefits / Assistance:** > **Financial Assistance:** - Financial assistance of ₹5,00,000/- is provided per couple. > **Disbursement Process:** - ₹2,50,000/- is deposited as a fixed deposit in a nationalized bank for eight years (not withdrawable before maturity). - ₹2,50,000/- is directly transferred to the couple’s joint bank account for household and essential needs. 



**Benefit Type:** Cash






















**Contacts & Authorities:** 

Nodal Department: Social Justice and Empowerment Department.
