
# Family Pension (DBOCWWB)


**Short Title:** FPDBOCWWB









**Tags:** Construction, Worker, Pension, Family, Labour



The &quot;Family Pension&quot; scheme was launched by the Delhi Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board, Labour Department, Delhi. In this scheme, upon the death of the pensioner, the surviving spouse is provided 50% of the pension amount or ₹1,500/-, whichever is higher.




**Who is this for:** 
Individual; 
Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; 
Sector: Pension



**Where it applies:** 
Delhi; 
State/ UT









**Benefits / Assistance:** 50% of the pension or ₹1,500/-, whichever is higher.



**Benefit Type:** Cash






















**Contacts & Authorities:** 

Nodal Department: Labour Department.
