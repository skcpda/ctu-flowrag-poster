
# Special Jobs to PwDs - Clerical Assistant


**Short Title:** SJPWDCA









**Tags:** Job, PwD, Clerk, Person With Disabilities, Employment, Disabled Person



The scheme &amp;quot;Special Job to PwDs - Clerical Assistant&amp;quot; was introduced by the Department of Social Welfare &amp;amp; Tribal Affairs, Union Territory of Lakshadweep, with the aim of providing temporary employment opportunities to Persons with Disabilities (PwDs) having a minimum of 40% disability in the UT. Under this scheme, 11 PwD candidates are employed as Clerical Assistants on an 89-day rotation basis in the District Panchayat offices of their respective islands to address issues related to disabled persons and other associated matters.




**Who is this for:** 
Individual; 
Category: Skills & Employment; 
Sector: Employment services and jobs



**Where it applies:** 
Lakshadweep; 
State/ UT









**Benefits / Assistance:** 1. Persons with Disabilities are employed in the District Panchayat offices of their respective islands. 1. A monthly salary of ₹11,000/-. **Note:** Employment is provided on 89 days rotation basis.



**Benefit Type:** Cash






















**Contacts & Authorities:** 
Implementing agency: District Panchayat Of Lakshadweep.
Nodal Department: Social Welfare and Tribal Affairs Department, Lakshadweep.
