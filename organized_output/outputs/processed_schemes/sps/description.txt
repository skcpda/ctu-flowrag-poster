
# Savitribai Phule Scholarship For V.J.N.T And S.B.C Girls Students Studying In 8th To 10 Std


**Short Title:** SPS









**Tags:** Pre-Matric, SBC, Scholarship, Special Backward Classes, Vimukta Jati And Nomadic Tribes



Savitribai Phule Scholarship is a Scholarship Scheme by the Social Justice and Special Assistance Department, Government of Maharashtra. The objective of the scheme is to encourage enrollment and to decrease the dropout ratio of Girls in the education of Maharashtra from Vimukta Jati and Nomadic Tribes (VJNT) and Special Backward Classes (SBC), studying in 8th to 10th standard, the Government has introduced this scheme from the academic year 2003-04.




**Who is this for:** 
Individual; 
Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; 
Sector: Scholarships and student finance



**Where it applies:** 
Maharashtra; 
State









**Benefits / Assistance:** The beneficiary is to provide ₹100/- per month for 10 months i.e. ₹1,000/- for 10 months.



**Benefit Type:** Cash






















**Contacts & Authorities:** 
Implementing agency: Department Of Social Justice & Special Assistance.
Nodal Department: Social Justice and Special Assistance Department.
