
# Sri Lanka's Sita Temple Tour


**Short Title:** SLSTT









**Tags:** Sri Lanka, Sita Temple, Pilgrim, Ashok Vatika, Angkor Wat Temple



The scheme &amp;quot;Sri Lanka&amp;#39;s Sita Temple Tour&amp;quot; was started by the Religious Trusts and Endowments Department, Government of Madhya Pradesh. The objective of this scheme is to provide financial assistance to state pilgrims traveling to the Sita Temple and Ashoka Vatika in Sri Lanka, as well as the Angkor Wat Temple in Cambodia.




**Who is this for:** 
Individual; 
Category: Travel & Tourism; 
Sector: Travel abroad



**Where it applies:** 
Madhya Pradesh; 
State/ UT









**Benefits / Assistance:** 1. Under this scheme, natives of Madhya Pradesh who complete visits to Sita Temple &amp;amp; Ashok Vatika in Sri Lanka, as well as Angkor Wat Temple in Cambodia, are eligible for reimbursement. 1. Upon submission of the certificate detailing the actual expenses incurred, the State Government will reimburse 50% of the total expenses, up to a maximum of ₹30,000/-.



**Benefit Type:** Cash






















**Contacts & Authorities:** 
Implementing agency: Directorate Of Religious Trusts And Endowments, Government Of Madhya Pradesh.
Nodal Department: Religious Trusts and Endowments Department.
