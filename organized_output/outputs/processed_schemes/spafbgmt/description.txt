
# Provide Assistance to Farmers Bringing Goods to Market Committees for Cost of Goods Transportation (Tribal)


**Short Title:** SPAFBGMT









**Tags:** Tribal Farmers, Agricultural Market Access, Transport Subsidy, Co-operative Institutes



The scheme &amp;quot;Provide Assistance to Farmers Bringing Goods to Market Committees for Cost of Goods Transportation (Tribal)&amp;quot; by the Agriculture and Co-operation Department, Government of Gujarat, aims to promote the participation of tribal farmers in formal agricultural markets by reimbursing transportation costs incurred while bringing agricultural goods to market committees. The primary objective is to reduce the financial burden of logistics on tribal farmers and thereby enhance their income through better market access and fair price realization. 




**Who is this for:** 
Individual; 
Category: Agriculture,Rural & Environment; 
Sector: Entrepreneurship development



**Where it applies:** 
Gujarat; 
State/ UT









**Benefits / Assistance:** **Financial Assistance:** ₹500/- per tonne of agricultural produce transported. Assistance is available for up to 10 tonnes per farmer, which amounts to a maximum of ₹5,000/-. 



**Benefit Type:** Cash






















**Contacts & Authorities:** 

Nodal Department: Agriculture, Farmers Welfare and Cooperation Department.
