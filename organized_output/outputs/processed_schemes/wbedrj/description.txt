
# Widow B.Ed Scheme-Rajasthan


**Short Title:** WBEDRJ





**Launch Date:** 2016-01-01





**Tags:** Student, Empowerment, Higher Education, Abandoned Women



The state government of Rajasthan reimburses the course fees for B.Ed. in teacher training institutions for widows or abandoned women who enroll in the program, providing them with much-needed support.




**Who is this for:** 
Individual; 
Category: Education & Learning; 
Sector: Scholarships and student finance



**Where it applies:** 
Rajasthan; 
State









**Benefits / Assistance:** - Financial Assistance : ₹17,880 (Fee Reimbursement).



**Benefit Type:** Cash






















**Contacts & Authorities:** 

Nodal Department: Higher Education Department.
