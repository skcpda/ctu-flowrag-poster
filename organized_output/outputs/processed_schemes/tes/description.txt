
# Testing Equipment Scheme


**Short Title:** TES





**Launch Date:** 2016-01-29





**Tags:** Financial Assistance, Business



The Government of Haryana launched the Testing Equipment Assistance Scheme to support Micro and Small Enterprises (MSEs) in enhancing product quality through financial aid for procuring testing equipment. Eligible units can receive a 50% subsidy, up to ₹5,00,000/- annually, for purchasing new testing equipment.  In order to ensure, Zero Defect‟ in the quality of products to make globally competitive, the Micro and small enterprises have to upgrade the quality of the products for which they are required to create testing facilities for testing of raw-materials and end products. The Micro and Small entrepreneurs find it difficult to purchase the testing equipment&#39;s because of limited resources. In order to meet this objective, State Govt. has made provision for Testing Equipment Assistance Scheme. 




**Who is this for:** 
Business Entity; 
Category: Business & Entrepreneurship; 
Sector: Financial regulation



**Where it applies:** 
Haryana; 
State/ UT









**Benefits / Assistance:** 1. **50% Subsidy on Purchase of Testing Equipment :** - Financial support of 50% of the cost, limited to a maximum of ₹5 lakh per year, for purchase of new testing equipment. 1. **Support for Quality Enhancement** - Assists Micro and Small Enterprises in establishing testing facilities for raw materials and end products to ensure zero defect and global competitiveness. 1. **Encouragement to Upgrade Infrastructure** - Aims to overcome resource constraints faced by MSEs in acquiring essential testing infrastructure. 



**Benefit Type:** Composite
