
# Grant to Films - Foreign Language Films


**Short Title:** GFF-FLF









**Tags:** Film Grant, Film Shooting, Filmmaker, Producer, Foreign Language



The “Grant to Films - Foreign Language Films” scheme was launched under the &amp;quot;Uttarakhand Film Policy 2024&amp;quot; by the Department of Information and Public Relations, Government of Uttarakhand. It aims to attract foreign film productions to the state, create employment opportunities, promote regional culture and artists, and generate revenue for the state. Under this scheme, financial grants of 50% of in‑state production expenditure or up to ₹3 crore, whichever is less, are provided to the foreign language film producers who prominently showcase Uttarakhand.




**Who is this for:** 
Individual; 
Category: Sports & Culture; 
Sector: Culture



**Where it applies:** 
Uttarakhand; 
State/ UT









**Benefits / Assistance:** **Film Grant:** - Under this scheme, financial grants of up to 50% of in-state production expenditure or ₹3 crore, whichever is lower, are provided to foreign language films shot in Uttarakhand. - An additional 5% of the sanctioned grant will be awarded, on using the authentic/real names of Uttarakhand locations in the film. &gt; **Note:** - The maximum sanctioned amount of the grant will be accessed based on the quality and technical aspects of the film. - 90% of total expense must be paid through bank, cheque, online medium, internet banking, UPI, digital mediums, etc. - Cash transactions are strongly discouraged. It will only be permitted in unavoidable circumstances on the Chartered Accountant&amp;#39;s certification: Provided that, in any case the total cash expenditure shall not be allowed more than 10% of the total expenditure.



**Benefit Type:** Cash







**Exclusions / Not eligible:** 1. Films that do not incur expenses within Uttarakhand are not eligible. 1. Expense/Funding for film marketing, promotion, and release will not be eligible for grant funding.
















**Contacts & Authorities:** 
Implementing agency: Uttarakhand Film Development Council.
Nodal Department: Uttarakhand Information Department.
