
# Financial Assistance to Industrial Parks: Stamp Duty


**Short Title:** FAITSD





**Launch Date:** 2020-08-07





**Tags:** Industrial Parks, Entrepreneurship, Stamp Duty



The scheme &amp;quot;Financial Assistance to Industrial Parks&amp;quot; is an umbrella scheme launched by the Industries and Mines Department, Gujarat under the Gujarat Industrial Policy. Effective from August 7th, 2020 to August 6th, 2025, it aims to boost industrial growth through financial assistance, modern technologies, and enhanced facilities for industrial parks and clusters. The component &amp;quot;Stamp Duty&amp;quot;, is aimed at providing financial support to the developers of Industrial Parks and individual units located within those parks.




**Who is this for:** 
Business Entity, Industries; 
Category: Business & Entrepreneurship; 
Sector: Setting up / start-up / entrepreneurship



**Where it applies:** 
Gujarat; 
State/ UT









**Benefits / Assistance:** - **Assistance to Institutes:** 100% reimbursement of stamp duty on the purchase of land required for the industrial park. - **Assistance to Individual Units:** Individual units located within the Industrial Park are eligible for a 50% reimbursement of the stamp duty paid on the purchase of plots within the park



**Benefit Type:** Cash






















**Contacts & Authorities:** 

Nodal Department: Industries and Mines Department.
