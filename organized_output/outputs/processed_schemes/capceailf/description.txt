
# Coaching Assistance for Pre-preparation for Competitive Exams of ALL INDIA level like IIM, CEPT, NIFT, NLU and IELTS, TOFEL, GRE for going Foreign


**Short Title:** CAPCEAILF









**Tags:** Financial Assistance, Student, Foreign, Competitive Exam



The scheme &quot;Coaching Assistance for Pre-Preparation for Competitive Exams of All India Level like IIM, CEPT, NIFT, NLU and IELTS, TOEFL, GRE for Going Foreign&quot; was launched by the Social Justice and Empowerment Department, Government of Gujarat. The scheme aims to financially support SEBC and economically backward students preparing for national-level and international entrance exams. Under this scheme, eligible candidates receive up to ₹20,000/- or the actual tuition fee paid, whichever is lower, as assistance for coaching fees. 




**Who is this for:** 
Individual; 
Category: Education & Learning; 
Sector: International education



**Where it applies:** 
Gujarat; 
State/ UT









**Benefits / Assistance:** **Financial Assistance:** - ₹20,000/- or actual coaching fee paid (whichever is lower) for pre-preparation of one competitive exam (IIM, CEPT, NIFT, NLU, IELTS, TOEFL, GRE) **Mode of Disbursement:** - Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) to trainee’s bank account. 



**Benefit Type:** Cash






















**Contacts & Authorities:** 

Nodal Department: Social Justice and Empowerment Department.
