
# Rock Climbing Adventure Training Course


**Short Title:** RCATC









**Tags:** Adventure Sports, Rock Climbing, Training, Scheduled Tribes, Outdoor Activities, Children Development



The scheme &amp;quot;Rock Climbing Adventure Training Course&amp;quot; by the Sports, Youth and Cultural Activities Department, Government of Gujarat aims to develop a spirit of adventure, encourage physical endurance, and boost confidence and discipline among children belonging to Scheduled Tribes through a structured training program. The scheme provides selected students with an opportunity to participate in a 6-day residential rock climbing adventure course conducted at either Mount Abu or Junagadh. It focuses on holistic development by engaging youth in outdoor sports activities, thereby fostering physical and mental growth. &lt;br&gt; 




**Who is this for:** 
Individual; 
Category: Sports & Culture; 
Sector: Sports



**Where it applies:** 
Gujarat; 
State/ UT









**Benefits / Assistance:** - **Training:** A 6-day rock climbing adventure course for 100 children aged 9 to 12 years, conducted at Mount Abu or Junagadh Center. 



**Benefit Type:** In Kind






















**Contacts & Authorities:** 

Nodal Department: Sports, Youth and Cultural Activities Department.
