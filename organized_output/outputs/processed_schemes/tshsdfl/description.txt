
# Trainning Scheme for Horticulture Skill Development to Landless farm labour at Centre of Excelence of Department


**Short Title:** TSHSDFL









**Tags:** Horticulture Training, Skill Development, Farmer



The &amp;quot;Training Scheme for Horticulture Skill Development to Landless Farm Labour at Centre of Excellence of Department&amp;quot; introduced by the Agriculture, Farmers Welfare and Cooperation Department, Gujarat, is designed to uplift landless agricultural workers by equipping them with essential horticulture skills through training. Conducted across Gujarat, the training spans a minimum of three days to a maximum of five days and includes modules like experiential learning tours, training kits, stipend, expert-led sessions, and more.




**Who is this for:** 
Individual; 
Category: Agriculture,Rural & Environment; 
Sector: Financial assistance



**Where it applies:** 
Gujarat; 
State/ UT









**Benefits / Assistance:** - **Stipend:** ₹250/- per trainee.- **Other Facilities:** Dining facility, training literature kit, experiential education tour, trainee kit, etc.



**Benefit Type:** Composite






















**Contacts & Authorities:** 

Nodal Department: Agriculture, Farmers Welfare and Cooperation Department.
