
# Technical Education Scholarship Scheme for ITI Level


**Short Title:** TESSITIL









**Tags:** Technical Education, Scholarship, ITI, ST, SC, OBC



The scheme “Technical Education Scholarship Scheme for ITI Level” was started by the Department of Technical Education, Himachal Pradesh. The objective of awarding this scholarship is two-fold, firstly to improve the quality of training by attracting promising trainees belonging to Scheduled Caste, Scheduled Tribe, and Other Backward Classes, who otherwise would not be in a position to pursue training due to financial constraints and secondly to popularize technical training among these categories, so as to make them able to earn their livelihood respectably and honourably.




**Who is this for:** 
Individual; 
Category: Education & Learning; 
Sector: Education and training grants, fellowship, stipend



**Where it applies:** 
Himachal Pradesh; 
State









**Benefits / Assistance:** 1. The scholarships shall be paid at the rate of ₹250/- per month per trainee subject to the condition that the corresponding decrease shall be affected in the rate of scholarship if the trainees receive a stipend from any other department of the Government or any organization.



**Benefit Type:** Cash






















**Contacts & Authorities:** 
Implementing agency: Department Of Technical Education, Himachal Pradesh.
Nodal Department: Technical Education Department, Himachal Pradesh.
