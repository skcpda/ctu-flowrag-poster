
# Swami Vivekanand Scholarship for Technical Diploma in Professional Courses


**Short Title:** SVSTDPC









**Tags:** Stipend, Student, Scheduled Tribe, Technical Diploma, Courses



The &quot;Swami Vivekanand Scholarship for Technical Diploma in Professional Courses&quot; scheme was launched by the Tribal Development Department, Government of Gujarat. It aims to promote educational development among scheduled tribe (ST) students by supporting them in technical and professional training. Under this scheme, stipends are provided to the students undergoing training in state government or government-recognized Private industrial training institutes (ITIs) and centers, enabling them to meet academic and living expenses. 




**Who is this for:** 
Individual; 
Category: Education & Learning; 
Sector: Universities and higher education



**Where it applies:** 
Gujarat; 
State/ UT









**Benefits / Assistance:** - A monthly stipend of ₹400 for 12 months for the ITI and professional courses. 



**Benefit Type:** Cash






















**Contacts & Authorities:** 

Nodal Department: Tribal Development Department.
