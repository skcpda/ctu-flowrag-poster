
# Term Loan Scheme- Credit Line 2 for Minority Community


**Short Title:** TLSCL2MC









**Tags:** Term Loan, Credit Line, Minority Community, Employment



The scheme &amp;quot;Term Loan Scheme- Credit Line 2 for Minority Community&amp;quot; is implemented by the Haryana Backward Classes and Economically Weaker Sections Kalyan Nigam, Government of Haryana. Under this scheme, loans of up to ₹30,00,000/- are provided at a concessional interest rate to individuals from minority communities to enhance their employability. This applies to those with an annual family income between ₹98,001/- and ₹8,00,000/- in rural areas and ₹1,20,001/- and ₹8,00,000/- in urban areas. Applicants must be permanent residents of Haryana and belong to minority communities, including Muslims, Sikhs, Christians, Buddhists, Parsis, and Jains.
