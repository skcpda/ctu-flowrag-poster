
# A Scheme to Provide Free Tablets to Std.12th Pass Students


**Short Title:** ASPFTS12PS









**Tags:** Student, Tablet, Schedule Tribe, 12th Standard



The “A Scheme to Provide Free Tablets to Std.12th Pass Students” scheme was implemented by the Tribal Development Department, Government of Gujarat. Under this scheme, provide free tablets to ST students who have passed the Higher Secondary Certificate (HSC) examination and are admitted to the first year of a graduate-level course in a recognized university are reimbursed for the token amount of ₹1,000 deposited for receiving tablets from the Education Department. 




**Who is this for:** 
Individual; 
Category: Education & Learning; 
Sector: Universities and higher education



**Where it applies:** 
Gujarat; 
State/ UT









**Benefits / Assistance:** - Under the scheme, students in the 12th standard belonging to the Scheduled Caste category will receive a tablet by paying a token fee of ₹1,000/-. 



**Benefit Type:** In Kind






















**Contacts & Authorities:** 

Nodal Department: Tribal Development Department.
