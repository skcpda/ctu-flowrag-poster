
# Medical Assistance for the Treatment of Critical Illness


**Short Title:** MATCI









**Tags:** Financial Assistance, Illness, Construction Worker, Medical Treatment



The &quot;Medical Assistance for the Treatment of Critical Illness&quot; scheme was launched by the Maharashtra Building And Other Construction Workers Welfare Board (MBOCWW), Labour Department Maharashtra. Under this scheme, medical assistance of ₹1,00,000/- for the treatment of serious ailments for registered worker and his/her family members. (the benefit can be availed only after 26th July 2014 as the benefit is covered under the Mediclaim &amp; Personal Accident Insurance scheme). 




**Who is this for:** 
Individual; 
Category: Health & Wellness; 
Sector: Financial assistance



**Where it applies:** 
Maharashtra; 
State/ UT









**Benefits / Assistance:** - **Financial assistance of ₹1,00,000/- is provided for medical treatment.**



**Benefit Type:** Cash
