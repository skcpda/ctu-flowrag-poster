
# Financial Assistance to Indigent Artistes


**Short Title:** FATIA









**Tags:** Indigent, Artist, Financial Assistance, Culture



The scheme &quot;Financial Assistance to Indigent Artistes&quot; was launched by the Arts and Culture Department, Puducherry. The scheme aims to provide financial assistance of ₹1,000/- per month to indigent artists aged 50 years and above and contributions to music, dance, drama, folk, painting and sculpture etc. The indigent artists resident of the Union Territory of Puducherry either by birth or by domicile. 




**Who is this for:** 
Artists; 
Category: Skills & Employment; 
Sector: Financial assistance



**Where it applies:** 
Puducherry; 
State/ UT









**Benefits / Assistance:** - **Financial Assistance of ₹1,000/- per month.**



**Benefit Type:** Cash






















**Contacts & Authorities:** 

Nodal Department: Arts and Culture Department, Puducherry.
