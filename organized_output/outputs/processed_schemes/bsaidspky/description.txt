
# Bihar Shatabdi AIDS Pirit Kalyan Yojana


**Short Title:** BSAIDSPKY









**Tags:** AIDS Patients, Financial Assistance



The scheme &amp;quot;Bihar Shatabdi AIDS Pirit Kalyan Yojana&amp;quot; was initiated by the Social Welfare Department, Government of Bihar. Under this scheme, financial assistance of ₹1,500/- per month is given to AIDS patients in the state. This scheme is fully funded by the Government of Bihar. **Objective:** The objective of this scheme is to provide financial assistance to AIDS patients in the state.




**Who is this for:** 
Individual; 
Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; 
Sector: Financial assistance



**Where it applies:** 
Bihar; 
State/ UT



**DBT Scheme:** Yes (Direct Benefit Transfer)







**Benefits / Assistance:** Under this scheme, financial assistance of ₹1,500/- per month is given to AIDS patients.



**Benefit Type:** Cash






















**Contacts & Authorities:** 
Implementing agency: Bihar State AIDS Control Society.
Nodal Department: Social Welfare Department.
