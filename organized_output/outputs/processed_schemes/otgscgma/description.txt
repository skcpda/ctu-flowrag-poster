
# One-time and Annual Financial Grant to Soldiers Awarded with Gallantry Medals


**Short Title:** OTAFGSAGM









**Tags:** Grant, Soldiers, Financial Assistance, Gallantry Award, Awards



The scheme “One-time and Annual Financial Grant to Soldiers Awarded with Gallantry Medals” is implemented by the State Sainik Board under the Sainik Welfare Department, Government of Uttarakhand. Under this scheme, a one-time financial assistance/annuity is provided to serving or ex-servicemen who have been conferred with the “Gallantry Medal Award.” In the event of martyrdom or death of the soldier, the grant is extended to the eligible family members or dependents. 




**Who is this for:** 
Individual; 
Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; 
Sector: Financial assistance



**Where it applies:** 
Uttarakhand; 
State/ UT









**Benefits / Assistance:** - A one time grant of maximum of ₹50,00,000/- and minimum ₹4,00,000/- is given. - Annual financial assistance of a maximum of ₹3,00,000/- and a minimum of ₹40,000/- is given. 



**Benefit Type:** Cash






















**Contacts & Authorities:** 

Nodal Department: State Sainik Board.
