
# Marriage Assistance - Folk Artist


**Short Title:** MAFA









**Tags:** Financial Assistance, Marriage, Folk Artist



The scheme &quot;Marriage Assistance&quot; was launched by the Art and Culture Department, Government of Tamil Nadu. This scheme provides financial assistance of ₹2,000/- for the marriage of registered folk artists, or their son or daughter. The assistance aims to support the cultural community by helping with marriage expenses.




**Who is this for:** 
Artists; 
Category: Women and Child; 
Sector: Financial Assistance



**Where it applies:** 
Tamil Nadu; 
State/ UT









**Benefits / Assistance:** - **Financial Assistance of ₹2,000/-.**



**Benefit Type:** Cash






















**Contacts & Authorities:** 

Nodal Department: Art and Culture Department.
