
# Registered BOC Workers in case of an Accidental Death (JKBOCWWB)


**Short Title:** RBWICOAADJKBOCWWB









**Tags:** Worker, Construction, Labour, Financial Assistance, Accident, Death



Launched on 1st January 2018, the scheme &quot;Registered BOC Workers in Case of Accidental Death&quot; is a Welfare Scheme by the Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board, Labour Department, Jammu &amp; Kashmir. Through this scheme, financial assistance of ₹4,00,000/- is provided to the nominee or dependents of registered BOC workers in the event of an accidental death. The applications are accepted online as well as offline.




**Who is this for:** 
Individual; 
Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; 
Sector: Financial assistance



**Where it applies:** 
Jammu and Kashmir; 
State/ UT









**Benefits / Assistance:** ₹4,00,000/- is provided to the nominee or dependents of the deceased worker.



**Benefit Type:** Cash






















**Contacts & Authorities:** 

Nodal Department: Department of Labour and Employment.
