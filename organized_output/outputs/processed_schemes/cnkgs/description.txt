
# Cluster Nutritional Kitchen Garden Scheme


**Short Title:** CNKGS









**Tags:** Malnutrition, Vegetable Cultivation, Livestock Rearing



The Cluster Nutritional Kitchen Garden Scheme (CNKGS) aims to empower marginal farmers and the general public by promoting the cultivation of vegetables and the rearing of livestock within their own households. The scheme seeks to enhance self-sufficiency and improve nutritional requirements by enabling individuals to grow their own fresh and healthy produce. This initiative will be implemented at the district level under the guidance of the respective MLAs (Members of Legislative Assembly) and Deputy Commissioners.




**Who is this for:** 

Category: Agriculture,Rural & Environment




**Where it applies:** 
Arunachal Pradesh; 
State









**Benefits / Assistance:** Reduced dependence on other states for vegetables and livestock. Improved self-sufficiency and availability of fresh and nutritious produce. Increased dietary diversity and improved nutritional intake. Generation of additional income through surplus produce. Promotion of sustainable farming practices and agro-ecological techniques. Enhancement of food security at the household level. Empowerment of marginalized farmers and the general public through skill development and entrepreneurship opportunities.



**Benefit Type:** In Kind







**Exclusions / Not eligible:** Large-scale commercial farmers who already have established farming operations. Individuals residing in urban areas without adequate space for kitchen gardening. Those who do not have the necessary time, resources, or interest in maintaining a kitchen garden.
