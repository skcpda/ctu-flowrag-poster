
# West Bengal Transport Workers’ Social Security Scheme: Family Pension


**Short Title:** WBTWSSFP









**Tags:** Social Security, Transport Worker, Monthly Assistance, Family Pension



The “West Bengal Transport Workers’ Social Security Scheme: Family Pension” by the Labour Department, is aimed at ensuring financial support for the families of unorganized transport workers in the state. Beneficiaries, upon retirement or unfortunate demise, will have 50% of their last drawn amount transferred monthly to their families through a Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) system. &lt;br&gt;




**Who is this for:** 
Individual; 
Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; 
Sector: Pension



**Where it applies:** 
West Bengal; 
State/ UT









**Benefits / Assistance:** - **Monthly Family Pension:** 50% of the amount last drawn by the beneficiary. &lt;br&gt;



**Benefit Type:** Cash






















**Contacts & Authorities:** 

Nodal Department: Labour Department.
