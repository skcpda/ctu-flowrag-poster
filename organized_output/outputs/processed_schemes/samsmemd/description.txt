
# Scheme for Assistance to MSMEs for Market Development


**Short Title:** SAMSMEMD





**Launch Date:** 2024-12-04





**Tags:** MSME, National Exhibition, International Exhibition, MSME Policy, Market Development



The scheme &amp;quot;Scheme for Assistance to MSMEs for Market Development&amp;quot; was introduced under the Rajasthan MSME Policy 2024 by the Department of Industries and Commerce, Government of Rajasthan. The scheme aims to promote and financially support the participation of MSMEs in national and international trade fairs, exhibitions, and buyer-seller meets, etc. This scheme will remain in force until 31st March 2029. ***Scheme Implementation:*** This scheme component will be implemented through the concerned DICC under the aegis of Department of Industries &amp;amp; Commerce. Office of the Commissioner, Industries &amp;amp; Commerce will be the nodal agency for the implementation and supervision of the scheme at the state level.




**Who is this for:** 
Business Entity; 
Category: Business & Entrepreneurship; 
Sector: Exhibitions/Trade fairs/Buyer-seller meet, market development



**Where it applies:** 
Rajasthan; 
State/ UT









**Benefits / Assistance:** **Financial assistance for participation in fairs/exhibitions:** **S. No.****Type of Event****Financial Assistance**1Participation in National/International Fairs/Exhibitions in Rajasthan₹37,500/- or 75% of the stall rent per event, whichever is less, for a maximum of 3 events in a financial year. (Actual Fare of 3-AC Class Train or AC Bus for 2 persons)2Participation in National/international fairs/Exhibitions in the country (other than Rajasthan)₹1,12,500/- or 75% of the stall rent per event, whichever is less, for a maximum of 2 events in a financial year. (Actual Fare of 3-AC Class Train or AC Bus for 2 persons)3Participation in Fairs/Exhibitions organized abroad₹1,50,000/- or 75% of the stall rent, whichever is less, for a maximum of 1 event in a financial year. (Actual Fare on Travel through Airplane in Economy Class for 2 persons)***Note:*** After availing the benefit in a particular category in one financial year, the beneficiary cannot avail it in the same category for the next 2 financial years.



**Benefit Type:** Cash







**Exclusions / Not eligible:** - Preference will be given to those MSMEs who have not availed the benefit under this scheme earlier.
















**Contacts & Authorities:** 
Implementing agency: District Industries And Commerce Centres.
Nodal Department: Industry Department.
