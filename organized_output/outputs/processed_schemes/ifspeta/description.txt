
# To Increase Fish Seed Production and Inland Fisheries Equipment in Tribal Areas


**Short Title:** IFSPETA









**Tags:** Agriculture, Farmer, Fishing Activities, Boat-net, Fish Seed, Scheduled Tribe, Training



The scheme &amp;quot;To Increase Fish Seed Production and Inland Fisheries Equipment in Tribal Areas&amp;quot; introduced by the Agriculture, Farmers Welfare and Cooperation Department, Gujarat, is designed to boost inland fisheries by supporting Scheduled Tribes fishermen. It provides assistance for fish seed rearing, stocking, and transportation, as well as financial aid for boats, nets, and fish sale equipment. Additionally, training programs with stipends are available to enhance skills and productivity in the fisheries sector.




**Who is this for:** 
Individual, Registered Societies, Self Help Groups (SHGS); 
Category: Agriculture,Rural & Environment; 
Sector: Fishing and hunting



**Where it applies:** 
Gujarat; 
State/ UT
