
# Pandit Dindayal Upadhyay Awas Yojana (Developing Caste Welfare) (NT-DNT)


**Short Title:** PDUAYNTDNT









**Tags:** Housing, Nomadic And Denotified Tribe, Social Welfare



The &amp;quot;Pandit Dindayal Upadhyay Awas Yojana (Developing Caste Welfare) (NT-DNT)&amp;quot; is a welfare scheme by the Social Justice and Empowerment Department of Gujarat. Under this scheme, eligible applicants from the Nomadic Tribe (NT) and Denotified Tribe (DNT) are given up to ₹1,20,000/- for constructing a house. Priority is given to individuals from BPL families.




**Who is this for:** 
Individual; 
Category: Housing & Shelter; 
Sector: Housing



**Where it applies:** 
Gujarat; 
State/ UT









**Benefits / Assistance:** - **Financial Assistance:** Up to ₹1,20,000/- for house construction.



**Benefit Type:** Cash






















**Contacts & Authorities:** 

Nodal Department: Social Justice and Empowerment Department.
