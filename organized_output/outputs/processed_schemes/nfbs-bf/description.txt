
# National Family Benefit Scheme- Uttarakhand


**Short Title:** NFBS-U









**Tags:** BPL, Financial Assistance, Death, Breadwinner



The &amp;quot;National Family Benefit Scheme&amp;quot; is a centrally sponsored scheme implemented by the Department of Social Welfare, Government of Uttarakhand. The scheme aims to provide financial assistance to Below Poverty Line (BPL) families upon the death of the primary breadwinner (main earning member of the family), helping them cope with immediate financial hardships. 




**Who is this for:** 
Family; 
Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; 
Sector: Financial assistance



**Where it applies:** 
Uttarakhand; 
State/ UT



**DBT Scheme:** Yes (Direct Benefit Transfer)







**Benefits / Assistance:** A one-time financial assistance of ₹20,000/- is provided to the family in the event of the death of the primary earning member. 



**Benefit Type:** Cash






















**Contacts & Authorities:** 

Nodal Department: Department Of Social Welfare.
