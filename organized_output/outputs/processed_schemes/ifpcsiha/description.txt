
# Construction of Ideal Fish Pond Scheme in Hilly Areas


**Short Title:** CIFPSHA









**Tags:** Financial Assistance, Pond Construction, Fish Farming, Pond, Hilly Areas



The Scheme &amp;quot;Construction of Ideal Fish Pond Scheme in Hilly Areas&amp;quot; was launched by the Department of Fisheries, Government of Uttarakhand. The scheme aims to promote sustainable fish production by utilizing water resources effectively in hilly regions through cluster-based pond development. Under this scheme, financial support of up to 50% of the cost is provided for the construction of ponds and first-year investments such as fish fingerlings and feed to all caste categories in hilly areas. It also includes free technical assistance, optional free training, and marketing support through departmental platforms like Uttarafish.




**Who is this for:** 
Individual, Self Help Groups (SHGS); 
Category: Agriculture,Rural & Environment; 
Sector: Fishing and hunting



**Where it applies:** 
Uttarakhand; 
State/ UT



**DBT Scheme:** Yes (Direct Benefit Transfer)







**Benefits / Assistance:** **Subsidy for Pond Construction:** - Individual beneficiaries: Up to ₹2,50,000/- for pond capacity up to 500 cubic meters. - Committee/Group/Mahasandh/Federation/Mangal Dal: Up to ₹5,00,000/- for pond capacity up to 1,000 cubic meters. **Subsidy for First-Year Inputs:** - 50% subsidy for purchasing fish fingerlings and feed. **Technical Assistance:** - Free technical guidance for high-growth fish species. - Optional free training in fishery operations. **Marketing Support:** - Option to sell fish independently or via &amp;quot;Uttarafish&amp;quot;, a state-supported fish marketing platform. **Cluster Requirement:** - A minimum of 10 ponds must be constructed in one location, either by an individual or by a group. **Self-Financing/Loan Provision** - The remaining 50% investment is to be made by the beneficiary through personal funds or bank loans. &gt; **NOTE:** 1. The subsidy is paid to the beneficiary&amp;#39;s account through DBT. 1. Applicants can avail grants either individually or through registered groups and cooperatives, with additional funding covered through self-investment or bank loans.



**Benefit Type:** Composite
