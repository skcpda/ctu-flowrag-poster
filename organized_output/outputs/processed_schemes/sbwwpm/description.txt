
# Samman Bhatta to War Widows and Parents of Martyrs


**Short Title:** SBWWPM





**Launch Date:** 1998-01-01





**Tags:** Financial Assistance, War, Widow, Martyrs, Sainik



The scheme &amp;quot;Samman Bhatta to War Widows and Parents of Martyrs&amp;quot; was introduced by the Department of Sainik Welfare, Government of Rajasthan in the year 1998. The scheme aims to provide lifelong financial assistance to war widows and parents of unmarried martyrs who sacrificed their lives in various wars, counter-insurgency operations, and aid to civil authorities before 31st March 1999. The amount is deposited directly into the beneficiary’s bank account every month.




**Who is this for:** 
Individual; 
Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; 
Sector: Pension



**Where it applies:** 
Rajasthan; 
State/ UT









**Benefits / Assistance:** - Samman Bhatta is provided at ₹5000/- per month for a lifetime. **Note:** The amount of assistance is deposited in beneficiary’s bank account every month.



**Benefit Type:** Cash






















**Contacts & Authorities:** 

Nodal Department: Department of Sainik Welfare.
