
# Mukhyamantri Medhavi Chhatra Protsahan Scholarship Scheme (Secondary Level- Class 11 & 12)


**Short Title:** MMMCPSSSL1112









**Tags:** Higher Secondary, Student, Scholarship, Meritorious Students



The scheme “Mukhyamantri Medhavi Chhatra Protsahan Scholarship Scheme (Secondary Level – Classes 11 &amp;amp; 12)” is implemented by the Secondary Education Department under the Department of Education, Government of Uttarakhand. The scheme aims to encourage academic excellence among students entering Classes 11 and 12 by providing financial assistance to meritorious students who have secured 80% or more marks in the Uttarakhand Board Examination of Class 10 . 




**Who is this for:** 
Individual; 
Category: Education & Learning; 
Sector: Scholarships and student finance



**Where it applies:** 
Uttarakhand; 
State/ UT









**Benefits / Assistance:** ***Rate of Scholarship:*** - **For Class 11th Students:** ₹1,200/- per month for 1 year.- **For Class 12th Students:** ₹1,200/- per month for 1 year. 



**Benefit Type:** Cash






















**Contacts & Authorities:** 
Implementing agency: Secondary Education Department, Government Of Uttarakhand.
Nodal Department: Department Of Education.
