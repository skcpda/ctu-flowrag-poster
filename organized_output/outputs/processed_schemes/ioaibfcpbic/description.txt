
# Incentive To Owners Of Artificial Insemination Born Female Calf Through Pure Breeding In Indigenous Cow


**Short Title:** IOAIBFCPBIC









**Tags:** Incentive, Artificial Insemination, Indigenous Cow, Livestock



The scheme “Incentive to Owners of Artificial Insemination Born Female Calf through Pure Breeding in Indigenous Cow” was started by the Department of Agriculture, Farmers Welfare &amp;amp; Co-operation, Government of Gujarat. The objective of this scheme is to avoid a decrease in the number of local pure breeds of Cow namely Gir and Kankrej and increase it to be properly maintained in the state.




**Who is this for:** 
Individual; 
Category: Agriculture,Rural & Environment; 
Sector: Animal husbandry



**Where it applies:** 
Gujarat; 
State









**Benefits / Assistance:** 1. The incentive of ₹3,000/- will be given to owners of a female calf born through Artificial Insemination by pure breeding in indigenous cows. 2. The incentive/assistance shall be given once per year for one calf per beneficiary.



**Benefit Type:** Cash






















**Contacts & Authorities:** 
Implementing agency: Directorate Of Animal Husbandry, Government Of Gujarat.
Nodal Department: Agriculture, Farmers Welfare and Cooperation Department.
