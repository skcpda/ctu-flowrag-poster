
# Mukhyamantri Asangathit Karmakar Cycle Sahayata Yojana


**Short Title:** MAKCSY





**Launch Date:** 2012-04-12





**Tags:** Empowerment, Vehicle, Women



Labor Department of Chhattisgarh State started this scheme &quot;Mukhyamantri Asangathit Karmakaar Saayakal Sahaayata Yojana&quot; for unorganized worker on 28 April 2012. Under this scheme, One cycle will be provided free of cost to each women beneficiary. The main objective of the scheme to empower the unorganized female worker through provide them free cycle.




**Who is this for:** 
Individual; 
Category: Transport & Infrastructure; 
Sector: Public transport and private vehicles



**Where it applies:** 
Chhattisgarh; 
State









**Benefits / Assistance:** > A free of cost cycle will be provided.



**Benefit Type:** In Kind







**Exclusions / Not eligible:** > Applicant must not have received a sewing machine under the Sewing Machine Assistance Scheme implemented by the Board or any other government scheme.
















**Contacts & Authorities:** 

Nodal Department: Labour Department.
