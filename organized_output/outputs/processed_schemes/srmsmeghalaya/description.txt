
# State Rice Mission Scheme


**Short Title:** SRMSMEGHALAYA









**Tags:** Rice, Farmer, Agriculture, Subsidy, Training



The “State Rice Mission Scheme” is a State Plan Scheme, which is implemented by the Department of Agriculture &amp;amp; Farmers’ Welfare, Government of Meghalaya. The objective of the scheme is to increase productivity and production of rice through improved technologies and extension methods and, to supply the quality/certified seeds/organic fertilizers at 50% subsidy.




**Who is this for:** 
Individual; 
Category: Agriculture,Rural & Environment; 
Sector: Agricultural Inputs- seeds, fertilizer etc.



**Where it applies:** 
Meghalaya; 
State/ UT









**Benefits / Assistance:** > **Pattern of Assistance:** - Training, Awareness program, extension, Technology support, Subsidy on inputs. > **Type of Assistance and Entitlement (If any):** 1. 50 % Subsidy for quality paddy seeds to farmers. 1. Minor irrigation (Check dam, shallow tube well, etc.) 1. 50 % sale of Power Tillers SRI Tools for demonstration. 1. Trainings



**Benefit Type:** Composite






















**Contacts & Authorities:** 
Implementing agency: Department Of Agriculture & Farmers’ Welfare, Government Of Meghalaya.
Nodal Department: Agriculture Department.
