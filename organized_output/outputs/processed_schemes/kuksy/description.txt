
#  Krishak Utpadak Kshati Sahayata Yojana


**Short Title:** KUKSY









**Tags:** Agriculture, Financial Assistance, Farmers, Natural Calamities



In the notified market areas of Uttarakhand, Krishak Utpadak Kshati Sahayata Yojana by the Agriculture Department of Uttarakhand provides compensation to farmers for losses due to fire, floods, excessive rainfall, and soil erosion. The compensation amount is approved after investigation and is transferred directly to the farmer’s bank account via DBT. 




**Who is this for:** 
Individual; 
Category: Agriculture,Rural & Environment; 
Sector: Agricultural Inputs- seeds, fertilizer etc.



**Where it applies:** 
Uttarakhand; 
State/ UT









**Benefits / Assistance:** **Compensation for:** 1. Damage caused by fire accidents to crops ready for harvest in the field, 1. Harvested crops are stored in the field or threshing yards, 1. Residual parts of crops, 1. Damage caused by fire in the farmer’s house affects property and livestock, 1. Damage to cultivable land due to floods or excessive rainfall, 1. And soil erosion. > Direct transfer of approved compensation amount to farmer’s bank account via Bank Draft. 



**Benefit Type:** Cash






















**Contacts & Authorities:** 

Nodal Department: Agriculture Department.
