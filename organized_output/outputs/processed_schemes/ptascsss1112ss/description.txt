
# Private Tuition Assistance to Scheduled Caste Students Studying in Standard 11th & 12th (Science Stream)


**Short Title:** PTASCSSS1112(SS)









**Tags:** SC, Private Tuition, Coaching, Student, Science Stream, Financial Assistance



The “Private Tuition Assistance to Scheduled Caste Students Studying in Standard 11th &amp;amp; 12th (Science Stream)” is implemented by the Director, Scheduled Caste Welfare, Government of Gujarat. Under the scheme, financial assistance is provided to Scheduled Caste (SC) students who are studying in Standard 11th and 12th (Science Stream) and have obtained 70% or more marks in the 10th Standard for private coaching.




**Who is this for:** 
Individual; 
Category: Education & Learning; 
Sector: Coaching



**Where it applies:** 
Gujarat; 
State/ UT









**Benefits / Assistance:** > **Assistance Details:** • 11th Standard: ₹15,000/- per annum as a private tuition fees • 12th Standard: ₹15,000/- per annum as a private tuition fees



**Benefit Type:** Cash






















**Contacts & Authorities:** 
Implementing agency: Office Of The Concerned District Deputy Director.
Nodal Department: Social Justice and Empowerment Department.
