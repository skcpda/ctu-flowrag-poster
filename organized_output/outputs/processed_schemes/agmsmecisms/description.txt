
# Aatmanirbhar Gujarat Scheme for assistance to MSMEs: Assistance for Interest Subsidy to Micro, Small and Medium Enterprise (Manufacturing Sector)


**Short Title:** AGMSMECISMS





**Launch Date:** 2022-05-10





**Tags:** Business, Entrepreneurship, MSMEs, Interest Subsidy



The &amp;quot;Aatmanirbhar Gujarat Scheme for MSMEs&amp;quot; is an umbrella scheme launched by the Industries and Mines Department, Gujarat. It aims to empower the state&amp;#39;s dynamic MSME sector, which significantly contributes to employment, GDP, and exports. Effective from October 5, 2022, to October 4, 2027, this initiative supports growth, investments, and the vision of &amp;quot;Aatmanirbhar Gujarat&amp;quot; and &amp;quot;Aatmanirbhar Bharat.&amp;quot; The component &amp;quot;Assistance for Interest Subsidy to Micro, Small and Medium Enterprise (Manufacturing Sector)&amp;quot; provides an interest subsidy on the term loan to support micro-enterprises in manufacturing. &lt;br&gt; &lt;br&gt; &lt;br&gt;




**Who is this for:** 
Business Entity, Industries; 
Category: Business & Entrepreneurship; 
Sector: Setting up / start-up / entrepreneurship



**Where it applies:** 
Gujarat; 
State/ UT









**Benefits / Assistance:** **Financial Assistance** **Category of Taluka** **Quantum of Incentive** Category 17% on Term Loan with a maximum amount of ₹35,00,000/- per annum for a period of 7 yearsCategory 2 6% on Term Loan with a maximum amount of ₹30,00,000/- per annum for a period of 6 years Category 3 &amp;amp;amp;amp; Municipal Corporation Areas5% on Term Loan with a maximum amount of ₹25,00,000/- per annum for a period of 5 years



**Benefit Type:** Cash







**Exclusions / Not eligible:** - Enterprises that have availed incentives for the same GFCI under any other state government scheme or agency are ineligible unless specifically allowed. - Enterprises with term loans sanctioned more than one year after starting commercial production/services are ineligible. - Enterprises defaulting on loan repayments will not receive interest subsidies for the default period. - Enterprises seeking reimbursement for penal interest or other charges are ineligible for such claims.
















**Contacts & Authorities:** 

Nodal Department: Industries and Mines Department.
