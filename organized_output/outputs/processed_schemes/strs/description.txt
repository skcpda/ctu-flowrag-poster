
# Scheduled Tribe Relief Scheme 


**Short Title:** STRS









**Tags:** Marriage, Scheduled Tribe, Financial Assistance, Disability



Launched on 31st March 2017, the &quot;Scheduled Tribe Relief Scheme&quot; by the Tribal Welfare and Scheduled Caste Department, Government of Madhya Pradesh, provides immediate support for destitute tribal brides and assistance to disabled, elderly, blind, and impoverished individuals.




**Who is this for:** 
Individual; 
Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; 
Sector: Financial assistance



**Where it applies:** 
Madhya Pradesh; 
State/ UT



**DBT Scheme:** Yes (Direct Benefit Transfer)







**Benefits / Assistance:** - ₹15,000/- in the case of house burning. - ₹5,000/- in the case of loss of livelihood-related movable property. - ₹5,000/- as relief by the Sub-Divisional Officer. - ₹5,000/-in the case of damages to wells, tube wells, motors, or economic resources.



**Benefit Type:** Cash






















**Contacts & Authorities:** 

Nodal Department: Tribal Welfare and Scheduled Caste (SC) Department.
