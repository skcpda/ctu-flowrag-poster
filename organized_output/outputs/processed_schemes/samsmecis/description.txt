
# Scheme for Assistance to Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises (MSME): Assistance of Capital Investment Subsidy (Manufacturing Sector)


**Short Title:** SAMSMECIS





**Launch Date:** 2020-08-07





**Tags:** Business, Entrepreneurship, MSMEs, Start Up, Capital Investment Subsidy



The &amp;quot;Scheme for Assistance to Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises (MSME)&amp;quot; is an umbrella scheme launched by the Industries and Mines Department, Gujarat. It aims to empower the state&amp;#39;s dynamic MSME sector, which significantly contributes to employment, GDP, and exports. Effective from August 7th, 2020 to August 6th, 2025, this initiative supports entrepreneurs and strengthens MSMEs, making them globally competitive. The component &amp;quot;Assistance of Capital Investment Subsidy (Manufacturing Sector)&amp;quot; provides a capital investment subsidy to support MSMEs in the manufacturing sector. 




**Who is this for:** 
Business Entity, Industries; 
Category: Business & Entrepreneurship; 
Sector: Setting up / start-up / entrepreneurship



**Where it applies:** 
Gujarat; 
State/ UT









**Benefits / Assistance:** **Financial Assistance:** **Category of Taluka****Quantum of Incentive** [Category 1](https://imd.gujarat.gov.in/Document/I%20Branch_2297_02_Nov_2020_98.pdf)25% of term loan amount upto ₹35,00,000/-; If the Eligible FCI is over ₹10,00,00,000/-, additional ₹10,00,000/- will be given.[Category 2](https://imd.gujarat.gov.in/Document/I%20Branch_2297_02_Nov_2020_98.pdf)20% of term loan amount upto ₹30,00,000/-; If the Eligible FCI is over ₹10,00,00,000/-, additional ₹7,50,000/- will be given.[Category 3](https://imd.gujarat.gov.in/Document/I%20Branch_2297_02_Nov_2020_98.pdf) &amp;amp;amp;amp; Municipal Corporation Areas10% of term loan amount upto ₹15,00,000/-; If the Eligible FCI is over ₹10,00,00,000/-, additional ₹5,00,000/- will be given.



**Benefit Type:** Cash







**Exclusions / Not eligible:** - Loan sanctioned by Non-Banking Financial Institutes will not be eligible for incentives under this resolution.
