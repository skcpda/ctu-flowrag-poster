
# The Post-Matric Scholarship For Scheduled Tribes Students


**Short Title:** PMSSTS









**Tags:** Scholarship, Student Finance, Financial Assistance, Empowerment



The scheme &amp;quot;Post-Matric Scholarship for Scheduled Tribes Students&amp;quot; by the Ministry of Tribal Affairs, Government of India, and the Government of Mizoram aims to provide financial assistance to Scheduled Tribe students pursuing post-matriculation education. Through this scheme, financial support is provided to ST students enrolled in recognized professional, technical, non-professional, and non-technical courses, including correspondence, distance, and continuing education. The scheme is implemented by the Mizoram Scholarship Board, Government of Mizoram. It ensures that students can pursue higher education without financial constraints. The assistance supports education at various levels and promotes educational advancement among Scheduled Tribes. The funding is shared in a 90:10 ratio between the Ministry of Tribal Affairs and the Mizoram State Government. 




**Who is this for:** 
Individual; 
Category: Education & Learning




**Where it applies:** 
Mizoram; 
State









**Benefits / Assistance:** 1. Financial assistance for post-matric education. 1. Support for ST students pursuing recognized professional, technical, non-professional, non-technical, and correspondence courses. 



**Benefit Type:** In Kind
