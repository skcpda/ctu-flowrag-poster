
# National Mission on Edible Oil- Oil Palm: Nursery


**Short Title:** NMOEN









**Tags:** Edible Oil, Nursery, Farmer, Subsidy



The scheme component &amp;quot;Nursery&amp;quot; launched under the &amp;quot;National Mission on Edible Oil–Oil Palm&amp;quot; scheme by the Department of Agriculture, Farmers Welfare and Co-operation, Government of Gujarat, aims to promote oil palm cultivation in the state by providing financial assistance to farmers for establishing oil palm nurseries. This initiative seeks to increase domestic production, thereby reducing the import of oil palm from abroad and strengthening the agricultural economy. 




**Who is this for:** 
Individual; 
Category: Agriculture,Rural & Environment; 
Sector: Entrepreneurship development



**Where it applies:** 
Gujarat; 
State/ UT









**Benefits / Assistance:** - **Subsidy:** ₹40,00,000/- per nursery.- **Capacity:** 10 hectares for ₹1,00,000 worth of saplings. 



**Benefit Type:** Cash






















**Contacts & Authorities:** 

Nodal Department: Agriculture, Farmers Welfare and Cooperation Department.
