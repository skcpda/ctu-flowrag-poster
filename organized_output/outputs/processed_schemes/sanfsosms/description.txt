
# Sathiyavanimuthu Ammaiyar Ninaivu Free Supply of Sewing Machine Scheme


**Short Title:** SANFSOSMS









**Tags:** EWS, Destitute, Widow, Differently Abled, Employment



The &#39;Sathiyavanimuthu Ammaiyar Ninaivu Free Supply of Sewing Machine Scheme&#39; is a state scheme launched by the Social Welfare and Women Empowerment Department, Government of Tamil Nadu. The scheme objective is to supply sewing machines free of cost to increase the self-employment potential for rehabilitation of destitute women/widows, deserted women, differently abled men and women, socially affected women and women from economically weaker sections.




**Who is this for:** 
Individual; 
Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; 
Sector: Aids/Appliances



**Where it applies:** 
Tamil Nadu; 
State/ UT









**Benefits / Assistance:** Sewing machines and accessories are provided at free of cost.



**Benefit Type:** In Kind






















**Contacts & Authorities:** 

Nodal Department: Department of Social Welfare and Women Empowerment.
