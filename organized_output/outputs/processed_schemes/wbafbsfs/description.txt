
# West Bengal Artisans Financial Benefit Scheme: Facilitation Supports


**Short Title:** WBAFBSFS





**Launch Date:** 2024-03-07





**Tags:** Artisans, Facilitation Support



“Facilitation Supports” is a sub-scheme under “West Bengal Artisans Financial Benefit Scheme 2024&amp;quot;, launched by the Micro, Small &amp;amp; Medium Enterprise and Textiles Department, Government of West Bengal on 7th March, 2024, and it shall remain in force for a period of five years. The objective of the scheme is to provide various types of facilitation support to the artisans and Industrial Cooperative Societies.




**Who is this for:** 
Artists; 
Category: Business & Entrepreneurship; 
Sector: Setting up / start-up / entrepreneurship



**Where it applies:** 
West Bengal; 
State/ UT









**Benefits / Assistance:** - Facilitation for enlistment on the State Portal for Artisans. - Facilitation for formation of Industrial Cooperative Society. - Facilitation for obtaining statutory requirements.



**Benefit Type:** Composite







**Exclusions / Not eligible:** - Any central/state govt employee and his/her family members shall not be eligible. - Should not have availed of any assistance /grant from Central /State Govt. for the same purpose. - Handloom and Khadi weavers, and their societies shall not be eligible(as they are covered under separate schemes).
















**Contacts & Authorities:** 

Nodal Department: Micro, Small & Medium Enterprises and Textiles Department.
