
# Mukhyamantri Gambhir Bimari Upchar Yojana


**Short Title:** MGBUY









**Tags:** Financial Assistance, Illnesses, Medical, Hospital



The “Mukhyamantri Gambhir Bimari Upchar Yojana” is implemented by the Department of Health, Medical Education &amp; Family Welfare, Government of Jharkhand, aims to provide financial assistance for the treatment of critical illnesses such as cancer, kidney transplant, and severe liver diseases, as well as support to acid attack victims. Through this scheme, eligible individuals receive medical grants of up to ₹5,00,000/- to cover treatment costs.




**Who is this for:** 
Individual; 
Category: Health & Wellness; 
Sector: Disease and conditions



**Where it applies:** 
Jharkhand; 
State/ UT









**Benefits / Assistance:** - Financial Assistance: Up to ₹5,00,000/- per case for the treatment of critical illnesses such as cancer, kidney transplant, and severe liver diseases. - Valid Hospitals: Treatment costs are covered only in Jharkhand&#39;s medical colleges, central medical institutions, or empaneled hospitals. (Link) - Expense Reimbursement: Covers bed charges, medicines, surgeries, and other related costs. **NOTE:** Disbursal: Grants are disbursed directly to hospitals via electronic transfer or bank draft. 



**Benefit Type:** Composite







**Exclusions / Not eligible:** - Applicants receiving aid from similar government health schemes are excluded unless the medical condition is non-overlapping.  
















**Contacts & Authorities:** 

Nodal Department: Department of Health, Medical Education & Family Welfare.
