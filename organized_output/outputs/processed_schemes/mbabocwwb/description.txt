
# Maternity Benefit (A.B.O.C.W.W.B)


**Short Title:** MB(ABOCWWB)









**Tags:** Maternity, Construction Worker, Financial Assistance, Woman, Labour



The scheme “Maternity Benefit” was started by the Assam Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board (A.B.O.C.W.W.B), Labour Welfare Department, Government of Assam. Under this scheme, financial assistance shall be given to the registered women beneficiaries as maternity benefits during the period of maternity. This benefit shall be allowed twice only.




**Who is this for:** 
Individual; 
Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; 
Sector: Financial assistance



**Where it applies:** 
Assam; 
State/ UT



**DBT Scheme:** Yes (Direct Benefit Transfer)







**Benefits / Assistance:** The registered woman beneficiary shall be given ₹20,000/- each as maternity benefit during the period of maternity.



**Benefit Type:** Cash






















**Contacts & Authorities:** 
Implementing agency: Assam Building And Other Construction Workers Welfare Board.
Nodal Department: Labour & Welfare Department.
