
# Financial Assistance for Try Cycle to Workers and their Dependents - Haryana Labour Welfare Board


**Short Title:** FATCWD-HLWB









**Tags:** Financial Assistance, Try Cycle, Worker, Labour, PwD, Disability



The scheme “Financial Assistance for Try Cycle to Workers and their Dependents” is implemented by the Haryana Labour Welfare Board (HLWB), Labour Department, Government of Haryana. Under this scheme, financial assistance of ₹7,000/- or the actual price, whichever is less, will be provided to workers and their dependents employed in industrial and commercial units of Haryana for purchasing a tricycle if they lose their legs in an accident or due to any other reason. The benefit of this scheme is provided to the worker or his/her dependents after every five years.




**Who is this for:** 
Family, Individual; 
Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; 
Sector: Crisis/Disaster/Accident



**Where it applies:** 
Haryana; 
State/ UT









**Benefits / Assistance:** 1. Under this scheme, financial assistance of ₹7,000/- or the actual price, whichever is less, will be provided to workers and their dependents for purchasing a tricycle. **Note:** The worker will be provided with a one-time financial assistance in a block of 5 years to purchase a Try Cycle.



**Benefit Type:** Cash






















**Contacts & Authorities:** 
Implementing agency: Haryana Labour Welfare Board.
