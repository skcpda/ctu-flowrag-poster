
# Organization of Sports Competitions and Training Programs


**Short Title:** OSCTP









**Tags:** Cash Prizes, Sportsperson, Sports Competition, Training Program, Free Training, Sports



The scheme &amp;quot;Organization of Sports Competitions and Training Programs&amp;quot; was launched by the Department of Youth Welfare and Provincial Protection Corps, Government of Uttarakhand. The scheme aims to promote sports and identify grassroots-level talent among youth through structured competitions and training. Under this scheme, cash prizes and free training are provided to talented players and encourage participation at multiple levels like Gram Panchayat, Development Block, District, and state while providing an opportunity for free training to meritorious players before competing at the state level.
