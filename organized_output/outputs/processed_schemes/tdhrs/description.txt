
# Temporary Disability (Hospitalization) Relief Scheme


**Short Title:** TDHRS





**Launch Date:** 2016-02-04





**Tags:** Financial Assistance, Temporary Disability, Construction Worker



The &quot;Temporary Disability (Hospitalization) Relief Scheme&quot; welfare scheme by the Andhra Pradesh Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board offers financial assistance to registered construction workers who face temporary disability due to accidents or diseases. The scheme aims to help workers and their families manage the financial impact of temporary disability by offering timely relief.




**Who is this for:** 
Individual; 
Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; 
Sector: Financial assistance



**Where it applies:** 
Andhra Pradesh; 
State/ UT









**Benefits / Assistance:** - **Financial Assistance of ₹3,000/- per month.**



**Benefit Type:** Cash






















**Contacts & Authorities:** 

Nodal Department: Labour Department.
