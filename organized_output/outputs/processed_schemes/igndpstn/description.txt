
# Indira Gandhi National Disability Pension Scheme - Tamil Nadu


**Short Title:** IGNDPSTN









**Tags:** PwD, Pension, BPL, Clothes, Meal, Disability



The &#39;Indira Gandhi National Disability Pension Scheme&#39; is a state initiative by the Social Welfare and Women Empowerment Department, Government of Tamil Nadu. Its goal is to offer a ₹1,000 pension to individuals aged 18 and above, who belong to households below the poverty line. Eligibility criteria include a disability percentage of 80% or above, or severe/multiple disabilities.




**Who is this for:** 
Individual; 
Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; 
Sector: Pension



**Where it applies:** 
Tamil Nadu; 
State/ UT









**Benefits / Assistance:** **Pension:** - ₹1,000/- per month. **Free Clothes during Pongal and Deepavali:** - One free dhoti to a male pensioner. - One free saree to a female pensioner. **Free Rice in Anganwadi Centres:** - 2kg/month to those who are taking nutritious meal. - 4kg/month to those who are not taking nutritious meal.



**Benefit Type:** Composite






















**Contacts & Authorities:** 

Nodal Department: Department of Social Welfare and Women Empowerment.
