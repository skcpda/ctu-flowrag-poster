
# Medical Expenses for Treatment of Major Ailments (O.B.O.C.W.W.B)


**Short Title:** METFMAISO









**Tags:** Labour, Building Worker, Construction Worker, Treatment, Ailments, Surgery, Medical Expenses



The “Medical Expenses for Treatment of Major Ailments” scheme by the Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board, Labour Department, Odisha, is a welfare scheme for the registered workers of the board. Under this scheme, the Board shall provide financial assistance as medical expenses for the treatment of beneficiary or his/ her dependents suffering from disease of Nervous system (Brain &amp;amp; spine related), Cardiovascular System, Kidney and Cancer. The beneficiary should be registered at least for one year in lines of the Odisha State Treatment Fund. &lt;br&gt;




**Who is this for:** 
Individual; 
Category: Health & Wellness; 
Sector: Disease and conditions



**Where it applies:** 
Odisha; 
State/ UT









**Benefits / Assistance:** - **Quantum of Assistance:** ₹3,00,000/-.



**Benefit Type:** Cash






















**Contacts & Authorities:** 

Nodal Department: Labour and Employees State Insurance Department.
