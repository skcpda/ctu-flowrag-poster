
# Keetpalan Upkaran


**Short Title:** KU









**Tags:** Financial Assistance, Farmer, Equipment, Silkworm, Rearing



The “Keetpalan Upkaran” scheme by the Sericulture Department, Government of Uttarakhand. The scheme aims to provide financial assistance to farmers for procuring essential equipment required for silkworm rearing. 




**Who is this for:** 
Individual; 
Category: Agriculture,Rural & Environment; 
Sector: Financial assistance



**Where it applies:** 
Uttarakhand; 
State/ UT









**Benefits / Assistance:** - An assistance of ₹40,000 (80:10:10) is provided to selected beneficiaries for purchasing necessary equipment for insect farming. - Out of this amount, 10% is to be spent by the beneficiary themselves to establish a leaf chamber, while other insect farming materials are provided by the department. 



**Benefit Type:** Cash






















**Contacts & Authorities:** 

Nodal Department: Department Of Sericulture.
