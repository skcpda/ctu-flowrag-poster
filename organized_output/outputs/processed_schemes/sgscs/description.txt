
# State Government SC Scholarship (Class 1 to 10)


**Short Title:** SGSCS









**Tags:** Scholarship, Student, Girl Student, Scheduled Caste



The &amp;quot;State Government SC Scholarship (Class 1 to 10)&amp;quot; scheme, launched by the School Education Department, Government of Madhya Pradesh, aims to improve the educational status of Scheduled Caste students by providing financial assistance. Under this scheme, scholarships are provided to Scheduled Caste girl students in Classes 1 to 5, and to both boys and girls in the Scheduled Caste category in Classes 6 to 10. To be eligible, students must be domicile of Madhya Pradesh and belong to the Scheduled Caste category. There is no restriction on the annual family income of the students.




**Who is this for:** 
Individual; 
Category: Education & Learning; 
Sector: Scholarships and student finance



**Where it applies:** 
Madhya Pradesh; 
State/ UT









**Benefits / Assistance:** Under this scheme, scholarships for Scheduled Caste students are provided at the following rates: - **Class 1 to 5:** ₹250/- per year (for girls only) - **Class 6 to 8:** ₹200/- per year (for boys) and ₹600/- per year (for girls) - **Class 9 to 10:** ₹600/- per year (for boys) and ₹1,200/- per year (for girls) **Note:** The scholarship will be deposited directly into the student&amp;#39;s bank account.



**Benefit Type:** Cash






















**Contacts & Authorities:** 
Implementing agency: School Education Department, Government Of Madhya Pradesh.
Nodal Department: School Education Department.
