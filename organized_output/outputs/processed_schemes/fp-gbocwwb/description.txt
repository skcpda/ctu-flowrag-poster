
# Family Pension (GBOCWWB)


**Short Title:** FP-GBOCWWB









**Tags:** Pension, Spouse, Widow, Widower, Financial Assistance



&quot;Family Pension (GBOCWWB)&quot; is a Welfare Scheme by the Goa Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board of the Department of Labour and Employment, Goa. Through this scheme, in the event of the death of the pensioner of the Board, a family pension is given to his/her surviving spouse. The pension to the spouse will be 50% of the pension that used to be provided to the pensioner (the deceased), or ₹200/-, whichever is higher.




**Who is this for:** 
Individual; 
Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; 
Sector: Pension



**Where it applies:** 
Goa; 
State/ UT









**Benefits / Assistance:** 50% of the pension that used to be provided to the pensioner (the deceased) or ₹200/-, whichever is higher.



**Benefit Type:** Cash






















**Contacts & Authorities:** 
Implementing agency: Goa Building And Other Construction Workers Welfare Board.
Nodal Department: Labour and Employment Department.
