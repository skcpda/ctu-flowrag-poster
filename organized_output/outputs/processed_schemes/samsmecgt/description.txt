
# Scheme for Assistance to Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises (MSME):  Assistance for Reimbursement of CGTMSE Fees


**Short Title:** SAMSMECGT





**Launch Date:** 2020-08-07



**Closing Date:** 2025-08-06



**Tags:** Business, Entrepreneurship, MSMEs, Start Up



The scheme &amp;quot;Scheme for Assistance to Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises (MSME)&amp;quot; is an umbrella scheme launched by the Industries and Mines Department, Gujarat. It aims to empower the state&amp;#39;s dynamic MSME sector, which significantly contributes to employment, GDP, and exports. Effective from August 7th, 2020 to August 6th, 2025, this initiative supports entrepreneurs and strengthens MSMEs, making them globally competitive. The component &amp;quot;Assistance for Reimbursement of CGTMSE Fees&amp;quot; provides financial assistance for the implementation of Information and Communication Technology (ICT) infrastructure. The support covers equipment required for communication, networking, and cloud computing.




**Who is this for:** 
Business Entity, Industries; 
Category: Business & Entrepreneurship; 
Sector: Setting up / start-up / entrepreneurship



**Where it applies:** 
Gujarat; 
State/ UT









**Benefits / Assistance:** &gt; **Collateral-Free Loans:** - Government of India has instructed financial institutions to sanction collateral-free loans up to ₹2,00,00,000/-. &gt; **Support for MSEs:** - Assistance is provided to Micro and Small Enterprises (MSEs) under this scheme. - Service fees charged by banks/financial institutions are eligible for reimbursement. &gt; **Annual Service Fees:** - 1st Year: 1.8% + Risk Premium. - 2nd Year Onwards: 0.85% or as amended by CGTMSE/RBI. &gt; **Reimbursement Assistance:** - 100% reimbursement of the annual service fees paid by entrepreneurs for up to 5 years.



**Benefit Type:** Cash







**Exclusions / Not eligible:** - Enterprises engaged in service or trading activities are not eligible. - Enterprises that have not availed of CGTMSE-backed loans are not eligible.
















**Contacts & Authorities:** 

Nodal Department: Industries and Mines Department.
