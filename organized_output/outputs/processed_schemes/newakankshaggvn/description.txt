
# New Akanksha Scheme (Higher Education Loan) (GGDC)


**Short Title:** NASHELGGDC









**Tags:** Higher Education, Loan, Rabari Caste, Bharwad Caste, Student



The &amp;quot;New Akanksha Scheme (Higher Educational Loan)&amp;quot; is implemented by the Gujarat Gopalak Development Corporation (GGDC) under the Social Justice and Empowerment Department, Government of Gujarat. Under this scheme, educational loans of up to ₹15 lakh are provided to students belonging to the Rabari and Bharwad castes from socially and educationally backward classes for pursuing higher professional courses such as medical, dental, engineering, pharmacy, etc. 




**Who is this for:** 
Individual; 
Category: Banking,Financial Services and Insurance; 
Sector: Loan



**Where it applies:** 
Gujarat; 
State/ UT









**Benefits / Assistance:** **Educational Loan Details:** **1. Loan Amount:** - Up to ₹15,00,000/- **2. Interest Rate:** - 3.5% for female students - 4% for male students **3. Contribution:** - 95% contribution from the Corporation/State Government, - 5% from the beneficiary 



**Benefit Type:** Cash






















**Contacts & Authorities:** 
Implementing agency: Gujarat Gopalak Development Corporation.
Nodal Department: Social Justice and Empowerment Department.
