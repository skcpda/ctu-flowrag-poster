
# Integrated Social Security Scheme: Old Age Pension


**Short Title:** ISSSOAP









**Tags:** Old Age, Pension, ISSS, BPL



The scheme &amp;quot;Old Age Pension (OAP)&amp;quot; is a sub-component of the “Integrated Social Security Scheme (IGSSS)” implemented by the Social and Tribal Welfare Department, Administration of Union Territory of Ladakh. Under this scheme, pension is provided to old aged persons males (60 years &amp;amp; above) and females (55 and above) and who are destitute in the sense of having no means of subsistence from their own source of income or through financial support from family members or other sources.




**Who is this for:** 
Individual; 
Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; 
Sector: Pension



**Where it applies:** 
Ladakh; 
State/ UT









**Benefits / Assistance:** **Amount of pension:** ₹1,000/- per month per beneficiary



**Benefit Type:** Cash






















**Contacts & Authorities:** 
Implementing agency: Directorate Of Social & Tribal Welfare, Administration Of Union Territory Of Ladakh.
Nodal Department: Social and Tribal Welfare Department.
