
# Adi Dravidar and Tribal Welfare Department: Incentive/Award of Prizes-For Each Subjects


**Short Title:** ADTWIP









**Tags:** Tribal Welfare, Adi Dravidar, Students, Incentive, Education, Prizes



The scheme “Adi Dravidar and Tribal Welfare Department: Incentive/Award of Prizes-For Each Subjects” by the Adi Dravidar and Tribal Welfare Department, Tamil Nadu, provides financial incentives to promote academic achievement among students from underprivileged communities. One Student each from Adi Dravidar/Tribal/Adi Dravider converted to Christianity for each subject is selected for the awards. &lt;br&gt; &lt;br&gt;




**Who is this for:** 
Individual; 
Category: Education & Learning; 
Sector: Scholarships and student finance



**Where it applies:** 
Tamil Nadu; 
State/ UT









**Benefits / Assistance:** - **For plus 2 Examination:** ₹2,000/-. - **For 10th Examination:** ₹1,000/-.



**Benefit Type:** Cash






















**Contacts & Authorities:** 

Nodal Department: Adi Dravidar and Tribal Welfare Department.
