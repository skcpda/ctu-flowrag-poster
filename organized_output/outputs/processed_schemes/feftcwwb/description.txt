
# Funeral Expenses for the Construction Workers


**Short Title:** FEFTCWWB









**Tags:** Labour, Building Worker, Construction Worker, Funeral Expenses, Death



The “Funeral Expenses for the Construction Workers” scheme by the Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board, Labour Department, West Bengal, is a welfare scheme for the registered workers of the board. Under this scheme, financial assistance shall be provided to the nominee of each deceased registered member to meet their funeral expenses. 




**Who is this for:** 
Individual; 
Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; 
Sector: Financial assistance



**Where it applies:** 
West Bengal; 
State/ UT









**Benefits / Assistance:** - **Quantum of Assistance:** ₹3,000/- for each deceased beneficiary payable to the nominee.



**Benefit Type:** Cash






















**Contacts & Authorities:** 

Nodal Department: Labour Department.
