
# Uniform Allowance to the Wards of Ex-Servicemen/Widows (Sainik Welfare)


**Short Title:** UAWESMW-SW









**Tags:** Uniform, Allowance, Student, Ex-Serviceman, Widow



&quot;Uniform Allowance to the Wards of Ex-Servicemen/Widows (Sainik Welfare)&quot; is a Welfare Scheme by the Armed Forces Flag Day Fund of the Department of Sainik Welfare, Puducherry. Through this scheme, the wards of the ESM / Widows of ESM studying in Government-recognized Institutions are provided allowances towards purchase of their uniform. The application should be submitted offline within three months from the date of completion of the education.




**Who is this for:** 
Individual; 
Category: Education & Learning; 
Sector: Scholarships and student finance



**Where it applies:** 
Puducherry; 
State/ UT









**Benefits / Assistance:** > **Allowance (in ₹ per annum) according to the Level of Qualification** - School Level: 2,000 - Other Courses: 2,500 _*The rate listed above or the actual cost, whichever is less, is reimbursed._ _*The amount is credited to the beneficiary’s Bank Account through RTGS/ NEFT._



**Benefit Type:** Cash







**Exclusions / Not eligible:** - The scheme is not applicable to the ward who is repeating the same class. - The Wards who are already in receipt of an academic scholarship are not eligible. - The Wards who are availing benefit of reimbursement of tuition fees from other source(s) are not eligible.
















**Contacts & Authorities:** 

Nodal Department: Sainik Welfare Department, Puducherry.
