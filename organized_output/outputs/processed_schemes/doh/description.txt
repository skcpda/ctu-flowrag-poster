
# DEVELOPMENT OF HANDICRAFTS


**Short Title:** DOH









**Tags:** Handicraft, Puducherry, Training, Skills, Trades



The government of Puducherry takes the initiative to revive the vanishing crafts and restore the significance of traditional crafts. Objective: (i) To promote and develop the Handicrafts in the Union Territory of Puducherry. (ii) To revive the vanishing crafts and to restore the traditional Handicrafts /trades under the Viswakarma category. (iii) To develop the skills in order to create better employment opportunities and to boost the livelihood of rural poor, who have contributed a significant share to exports by the U.T. of Puducherry.  > Training Objective: (i) To revive the vanishing crafts and to restore the traditional Handicrafts. (ii) To develop the skills in order to create better employment opportunities and to boost the livelihood of rural poor, who have contributed a significant share to exports by the U.T. of Puducherry.




**Who is this for:** 
Individual; 
Category: Skills & Employment; 
Sector: Training and Skill Up-gradation



**Where it applies:** 
Puducherry; 
State









**Benefits / Assistance:** > To revive the vanishing crafts and to restore the traditional Handicrafts.  > Financial Assistance:  - To the trainee – (i) Stipend: Rs. 1,500 /- per month/ per trainee. - To the trainer – (i) Honorarium Rs. 300/-per month/ per trainee (maximum Rs.3, 000/- per month). (ii) Compensation for wastage of raw materials Rs.150 /- per month/ per trainee (maximum Rs.1,500/- per month).  Note:- Period of Training: 6 months



**Benefit Type:** Composite
