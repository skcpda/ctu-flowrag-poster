
# Compensation Amount Towards Cattle Injury Caused by Forest Animals


**Short Title:** CATCICBFA





**Launch Date:** 2014-02-06





**Tags:** Animal, Forest, Financial Assistance, Death, Compensation, Injury, Cattle



Launched on 6th February 2024, the scheme &quot;Compensation Amount towards Cattle Injury Caused by Forest Animals&quot; by the Forest Department, Government of Madhya Pradesh, provides compensation in case of death of cattle due to attacks by wild animals. The cattle owner will receive a relief amount. The nearest forest officer must be informed in writing or verbally within 48 hours of the incident, and the dead cattle should not be removed from the place of death.




**Who is this for:** 
Individual; 
Category: Agriculture,Rural & Environment; 
Sector: Animal husbandry



**Where it applies:** 
Madhya Pradesh; 
State/ UT



**DBT Scheme:** Yes (Direct Benefit Transfer)







**Benefits / Assistance:** - ₹1,000/- as immediate assistance for wild animal injury. - ₹30,000/- for medical expenses (normal injury). - ₹1,00,000/- for permanent disability and full treatment cost.



**Benefit Type:** Cash






















**Contacts & Authorities:** 

Nodal Department: Forest Department.
