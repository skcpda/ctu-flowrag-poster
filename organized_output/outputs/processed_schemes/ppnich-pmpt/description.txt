
# Promotion of Patriotism/National Integration/Communal Harmony - Production of Musical Programme or Tiatr


**Short Title:** PPNICH-PMPT









**Tags:** Song, Music, Program, Artist, Culture



The scheme &quot;Promotion of Patriotism/National Integration/Communal Harmony - Production of Musical Programme or Tiatr&quot; was launched by the Tiatr Academy under the Art, Culture and Museums Department, Government of Goa. The scheme aims to project patriotism, nationalism, and communal harmony through tiatrs, songs and music, addressing the need to promote these values through cultural performances and artistic expression. The scheme provides remuneration as fixed by Tiatr Academy of Goa to successful applicants and covers actual charges of hall/auditorium or pays a lump sum amount for performances held in makeshift pandals, along with video recording of the activity with one Digital Video Disc copy provided to the applicant. The applications for this scheme are accepted offline. 




**Who is this for:** 
Individual; 
Category: Sports & Culture; 
Sector: Culture



**Where it applies:** 
Goa; 
State/ UT









**Benefits / Assistance:** - Remuneration as fixed by Tiatr Academy of Goa will be provided to the applicant. - Reimbursement of actual charges of the hall/auditorium or payment of lump sum amount as fixed by Tiatr Academy of Goa if performance is held in makeshift pandal. - Video recording of the activity/programme will be done by Tiatr Academy of Goa. - One copy of the Digital Video Disc will be provided to the applicant. - Maximum of 4 shows of the programme will be staged by Tiatr Academy of Goa at various places. - One banner will be provided by Tiatr Academy of Goa for compulsory display.  > ***Conditions*** - All shows must be shown to public free of charge. - Duration of the musical programme or tiatr should be not less than two hours. - In all advertisements, oral, print or electronic media, banners, the following must be inserted/published/announced: &#39;This programme is promoted by Tiatr Academy of Goa&#39;. - A few seats should be reserved by the organizer(s) for the representatives of Tiatr Academy of Goa. - The Tiatr Academy of Goa banner should be compulsorily displayed at one of the two front side wings of the stage and returned after all shows. - The organizing institution/individual will have to discuss with Tiatr Academy of Goa and decide about the Chief Guest or Guest of Honour well in advance. 



**Benefit Type:** Composite
