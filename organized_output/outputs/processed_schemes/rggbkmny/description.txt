
# Rajiv Gandhi Gramin Bhoomihin Krishi Majdoor Nyay Yojana


**Short Title:** RGGBKMNY





**Launch Date:** 2021-04-01





**Tags:** Financial Assistance, Agricultural, Landless, Laborer



The objective of the scheme:- 1. To identify landless agricultural laborer families in Gram Panchayat and Nagar Panchayat and provide financial grants to landless agricultural laborer families on an annual basis. 1. To increase the net income of landless agricultural labor families through financial grants.




**Who is this for:** 
Individual; 
Category: Agriculture,Rural & Environment; 
Sector: Financial assistance



**Where it applies:** 
Chhattisgarh; 
State









**Benefits / Assistance:** Under the scheme, an amount of Rs.7000/- per year will be provided as a grant-in-aid in installments to the head of the eligible identified beneficiary family. The grant-in-aid amount will be made by Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) directly into the bank account of the head of the identified empaneled household.



**Benefit Type:** Cash






















**Contacts & Authorities:** 

Nodal Department: Revenue and Disaster Department.
