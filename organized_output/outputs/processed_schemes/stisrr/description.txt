
# Scheme to Increase Seed Replacement Rate (SRR)


**Short Title:** STISRR









**Tags:** Seed Distribution, Seed Replacement Rate, Subsidy, Certified Seeds



The &amp;quot;Scheme to Increase Seed Replacement Rate (SRR)&amp;quot; by the Agriculture and Co-operation Department, Government of Gujarat, aims to enhance the Seed Replacement Rate among farmers by distributing certified seeds of various agricultural crops. This initiative is supported under the centrally sponsored National Food Security Mission (NFSM) and NFSM-Oilseeds schemes. The goal is to provide access to quality seeds and promote better agricultural practices for increased yield and income. 




**Who is this for:** 
Individual; 
Category: Agriculture,Rural & Environment; 
Sector: Agricultural Inputs- seeds, fertilizer etc.



**Where it applies:** 
Gujarat; 
State/ UT









**Benefits / Assistance:** - **Seed Distribution:** Certified seeds are distributed to eligible farmers.  



**Benefit Type:** In Kind






















**Contacts & Authorities:** 

Nodal Department: Agriculture, Farmers Welfare and Cooperation Department.
