
# Mukhyamantri Kanya Vivah Yojana- Madhya Pradesh


**Short Title:** MMKVYMP





**Launch Date:** 2006-04-01





**Tags:** Vivah, Widow, Girl, Abandoned Woman, Destitute



The scheme &amp;quot;Mukhyamantri Kanya Vivah Yojana&amp;quot; was started by the Social Justice and Empowerment Department, Government of Madhya Pradesh in 2006. The objective of this scheme is to provide financial assistance for the mass marriage of girls, widows, and abandoned women from destitute or poor families in the state.




**Who is this for:** 
Individual; 
Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; 
Sector: Marriage



**Where it applies:** 
Madhya Pradesh; 
State/ UT









**Benefits / Assistance:** Under this scheme, the following assistance is provided: - Each eligible bride (including girls, widows, or abandoned women) participating in the mass marriage ceremony will receive a total of ₹49,000/- through an Account Payee Cheque, on the day of the mass marriage ceremony. - The organizing body that conducts the mass marriage ceremony will receive ₹6,000/- per beneficiary. **Note:** A total of ₹55,000/- is provided per beneficiary under the scheme.



**Benefit Type:** Cash






















**Contacts & Authorities:** 
Implementing agency: Social Justice And Empowerment Department, Government Of Madhya Pradesh.
Nodal Department: Department of Social Justice and Empowerment of Persons with Disabilities.
