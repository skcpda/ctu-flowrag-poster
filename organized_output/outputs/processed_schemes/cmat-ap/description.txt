
# Chief Ministergi Artistesinggi Tengbang (CMAT) – Artiste’s Pension


**Short Title:** CMAT-AP









**Tags:** Artist, Pension, Financial Assistance, Manipur, Culture



The scheme &quot;Chief Ministergi Artistesinggi Tengbang (CMAT) – Artiste’s Pension&quot; by the Dept. of Art and Culture, Govt. of Manipur provides a monthly pension of up to ₹ 4,000/- to the aged reputed artists who have contributed to making the tradition, culture, and art forms of Manipur more vibrant by working in various fields of arts such as Theatre (traditional/modern) / Shumang Leela / Nupi Leela / Mime/ Classical Dance / Traditional Dance / Folk Dance / Tribal Dance, etc.




**Who is this for:** 
Individual; 
Category: Sports & Culture; 
Sector: Culture



**Where it applies:** 
Manipur; 
State









**Benefits / Assistance:** Category A: Awardees & Gurus (150 number) @ ₹ 4,000/- per month will be supported. Category B: Non-Awardees (850 number) @ ₹ 2,500/- per month will be supported.



**Benefit Type:** Cash







**Exclusions / Not eligible:** Any artistes receiving assistance from any State Government or Central Government under Artistes’ Pension Scheme and Medical Aid to Artistes are not eligible for assistance under CMAT-Artistes’ Pension. There must be no duplicity.
















**Contacts & Authorities:** 

Nodal Department: Art and Culture Department.
