
# Marriage Assistance (ANBOCWWB)


**Short Title:** MA-ANBOCWWB









**Tags:** Marriage, Financial Assistance, Building Worker, Construction Worker, Labour



The scheme “Marriage Assistance” was started by the Andaman &amp;amp; Nicobar Islands Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board (ANBOCWWB), Department of Labour, Employment &amp;amp; Training, Andaman &amp;amp; Nicobar Administration. Under this scheme, registered construction workers will receive a financial allowance to cover either their own marriage expenses or those of their two dependent children.




**Who is this for:** 
Family, Individual; 
Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; 
Sector: Marriage



**Where it applies:** 
Andaman and Nicobar Islands; 
State/ UT









**Benefits / Assistance:** 1. Under this scheme, registered construction workers will receive financial assistance of ₹20,000/- to cover either their own marriage expenses or those of their two dependent children.



**Benefit Type:** Cash






















**Contacts & Authorities:** 
Implementing agency: Andaman & Nicobar Islands Building And Other Construction Workers Welfare Board, Port Blair.
Nodal Department: Labour, Employment & Training Department.
