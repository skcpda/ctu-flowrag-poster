
# Skill Development and Economic Empowerment


**Short Title:** SDAEE









**Tags:** Skill Development, Vocational Training, Visually Challenged, Divyang, Computer Literacy



The scheme &amp;quot;Skill Development and Economic Empowerment&amp;quot; by the is a central sector scheme that aims to provide inclusive vocational training and empower visually challenged individuals for economic self-reliance. Through this scheme, training is provided in various trades such as computer operations, stenography, programming assistance, secretarial assistance, and other technical fields. Additionally, students are provided with free meals, hostel accommodation, library facilities, teaching aids, and medical care. The scheme is implemented by the National Institute for the Empowerment of Persons with Visual Disabilities, Dehradun.  




**Who is this for:** 
Individual; 
Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; 
Sector: Training and Skill Up-gradation



**Where it applies:** 
Uttarakhand; 
State/ UT









**Benefits / Assistance:** &gt; **Free Vocational Training:** - Provides training in various professional courses, including Computer Operator, Stenographer, Mushroom Producer, Radio Jockey, Data Entry Operator, Reflexology, and Gardener. &gt; **Essential Skill Training:** - Offers training in Braille orientation and mobility, computer typing, Hindi and English, and home management skills. &amp;gt; **Modern Learning Tools:** - Senior students receive smartphones and laptops to facilitate modern and convenient studies. &gt; **Comprehensive Support for Trainees:** - Free food and accommodation are provided. - Commuting allowance is given for participation in the program. - Uniforms are provided. - Library facilities are accessible. - Medical facilities are available. - Accessible teaching materials are supplied. - Assistive devices are provided. &lt;br&gt;



**Benefit Type:** In Kind






















**Contacts & Authorities:**
