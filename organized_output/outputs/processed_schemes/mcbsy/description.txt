
# Mukhyamantri Covid-19 Bal Seva Yojana


**Short Title:** MCBSY





**Launch Date:** 2021-05-25





**Tags:** Children Care, Educaction, Financial Support



The Madhya Pradesh government launched the Mukhyamantri Covid-19 Bal Seva Yojana under the Women and Child Development Department. Under this scheme, a pension on monthly basis will be provided to all those children whose parents or guardians have died due to coronavirus infection. This pension will be provided till the child attains the age of 21 years. Apart from this, education and ration facilities will also be provided. All those children whose parents or guardians have died between March 1, 2020, and July 31 will be able to avail the benefits of this scheme. Only the natives of the state can avail the benefit of this scheme.  




**Who is this for:** 
Individual; 
Category: Women and Child; 
Sector: Childcare



**Where it applies:** 
Madhya Pradesh; 
State/ UT









**Benefits / Assistance:** > ***Financial Assistance:*** - Each child beneficiary receives ₹5,000/- per month deposited in a joint account until they turn 18. - Guardian and child receive free monthly ration under National Food Security Act. > ***Education Assistance:*** - School Education: 1. Free education in government schools. 1. Fees paid for RTE provision private schools. 1. RTE reimbursement limit for other private schools. - Class 9 to 12: 1. Free education in government schools. 1. ₹10,000/- per year for private school students. 1. Additional to scholarships and other benefits. - Higher Education: 1. General Courses. 1. Full fee assistance in government/aided universities. 1. Up to ₹15,000/- fee reimbursement in private universities. - Technical Education: 1. Full fee assistance in government/aided colleges. 1. Up to ₹1,50,000/- per year or actual fee in private colleges (JEE Mains/separate entrance exam). 1. Up to ₹75,000/- per year or actual fee in private colleges (12th class exam). - Medical Education and AYUSH: 1. Full fee assistance in government/private colleges based on NEET score. 1. Doctors serve in rural areas for 2-5 years (bond required).



**Benefit Type:** Cash
