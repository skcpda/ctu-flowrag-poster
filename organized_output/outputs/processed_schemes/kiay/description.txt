
# Krishi Input Anudan Yojana


**Short Title:** KIAY









**Tags:** Agriculture, Subsidy, Crops Damage, Financial Assistance, Crop Insurance



“Krishi Input Anudan Yojana” launched by the Department of Agriculture, Bihar, is designed to offer agricultural input subsidies in areas affected by rising water levels in rivers such as the Ganga and Kosi. Assistance are provided for crop damage more than 33% caused by heavy rainfall and flooding. &lt;br&gt;




**Who is this for:** 
Individual; 
Category: Agriculture,Rural & Environment; 
Sector: Environmental grants



**Where it applies:** 
Bihar; 
State/ UT









**Benefits / Assistance:** &gt; **Subsidy Amounts per Hectare:** 1. Non-irrigated (year-dependent) crop areas: ₹8,500/-. 1. Irrigated crop areas: ₹17,000/-. 1. Perpetual/ perennial crops: ₹22,500/-. &gt; **Minimum Subsidy Amounts to Farmers:** 1. Non-irrigated (year-dependent) crop areas: ₹1,000/-. 1. Irrigated crop areas: ₹2,000/-. 1. Perpetual/ perennial crops: ₹2,500/-.



**Benefit Type:** Cash
