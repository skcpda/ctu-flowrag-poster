
# Safai Karmakar Prasuti Sahayata Yojana


**Short Title:** SKPSY





**Launch Date:** 2015-03-11





**Tags:** Heath, Financial Assistance, Maternity Assistance



Labor Department of Chhattisgarh State started this scheme &amp;quot;Safai Karmakar Prasuti Sahayata Yojana&amp;quot; for unorganized worker on 11 March 2015. The main objective of the scheme to empower unorganized worker by providing financial assistance for Maternity Assistance.




**Who is this for:** 
Individual; 
Category: Health & Wellness; 
Sector: Financial assistance



**Where it applies:** 
Chhattisgarh; 
State









**Benefits / Assistance:** - Female employees will receive a financial assistance of ₹4,200 in the third month of pregnancy and ₹2,800 in the eighth month of pregnancy as maternity benefit. - Male employees will receive a financial assistance of ₹3,000 as paternity leave after the birth of the child.



**Benefit Type:** Cash






















**Contacts & Authorities:** 

Nodal Department: Labour Department.
