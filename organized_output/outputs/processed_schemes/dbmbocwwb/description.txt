
# Death Benefit (MBOCWWB)


**Short Title:** DB(MBOCWWB)









**Tags:** Death, Building Worker, Construction Worker, Labour, Financial Assistance



The scheme “Death Benefit” is a social security scheme, implemented by the Meghalaya Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board (MBOCWWB), Labour Department, Government of Meghalaya. Under the scheme, financial assistance shall be provided to the nominees or dependents of registered construction workers in the event of their natural or accidental death during the course of employment.




**Who is this for:** 
Family; 
Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; 
Sector: Crisis/Disaster/Accident



**Where it applies:** 
Meghalaya; 
State/ UT









**Benefits / Assistance:** 1. Under the scheme, an amount of ₹2,00,000/- shall be given to the nominees or dependents of registered construction workers towards death benefit, in case of natural death. 1. If the death is due to an accident during the course of employment, the nominee or dependent of registered construction workers shall be given ₹3,00,000/- towards death benefit. 1. The amount of ₹2,00,000/- for Natural Death &amp;amp; ₹5,00,000/- for Accidental Death shall be given to the nominees or dependents of registered construction workers who are not covered under any insurance scheme. 1. There shall be no bar for the nominee to claim any other death or accidental death benefit under any insurance scheme covering the beneficiaries (i.e. PMJJBY/PMSBY/etc.).



**Benefit Type:** Cash
