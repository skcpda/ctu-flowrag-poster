
# Skill Development Training Manipur


**Short Title:** SDTM









**Tags:** Skill Development, Construction Worker, Training



The &quot;Skill Development Training&quot; scheme was implemented by the Manipur Building and Other Construction Workers&#39; Welfare Board. The scheme offers skill development training to registered building workers, their spouse, dependent siblings, and children. The training includes vocational and skill development courses from recognized government and private providers, aiming to enhance the employability of beneficiaries.




**Who is this for:** 
Individual; 
Category: Skills & Employment; 
Sector: Training and Skill Up-gradation



**Where it applies:** 
Manipur; 
State/ UT









**Benefits / Assistance:** - Financial assistance covering course training fees. - The financial assistance for boarding and lodging upto ₹150/- per day. - Stipends or compensation for loss of daily wages during the training period. - Training courses leading to certifications or degrees recognized by the government. - Upgradation of training infrastructure through grants for eligible training providers.



**Benefit Type:** Composite






















**Contacts & Authorities:** 

Nodal Department: Labour and Employment Department.
