
# Veer Chandra Singh Garhwali Paryatan Swarojgar Yojana


**Short Title:** VCSGPSYUK





**Launch Date:** 2002-06-01





**Tags:** Financial Assistance, Tourism, Loan, Self Employment, Tourist Attraction



Launched on June 1, 2002, the scheme &quot;Veer Chandra Singh Garhwali Paryatan Swarojgar Yojana&quot; by the Tourism Department, Government of Uttarakhand aims to provide maximum self-employment opportunities to the residents of Uttarakhand state and mainly the youth in the tourism sector, while developing tourism as a source of employment and revenue in Uttarakhand and establishing Uttarakhand as an international level tourist attraction on the world tourism map. Through this scheme, loans and grants are provided to the permanent residents of Uttarakhand state for tourism-related business establishments, including vehicle and non-vehicle items such as buses, taxis, hotels, fast food centers, and adventure activity equipment. The scheme is implemented by the Tourism Department, Government of Uttarakhand, in coordination with nationalised banks, rural banks, state cooperative banks, and regional rural banks. This self-employment scheme assists in the planned, coordinated and integrated development of tourism in the environmentally sensitive hill state while connecting with the social and economic conditions of the residents. > **Objectives:** 1. To make maximum use of the immense tourism potential of the Uttarakhand state. 1. To ensure the planned, coordinated and integrated development of tourism in the environmentally sensitive hill state. 1. To establish Uttarakhand as an international-level tourist attraction on the world tourism map. 1. To develop tourism as a source of employment and revenue in Uttarakhand. 1. To connect tourism development with the social and economic conditions of the residents. 1. To provide maximum self-employment opportunities in the tourism sector to residents and youth.




**Who is this for:** 
Individual; 
Category: Business & Entrepreneurship; 
Sector: Setting up / start-up / entrepreneurship



**Where it applies:** 
Uttarakhand; 
State/ UT









**Benefits / Assistance:** 1. Financial Benefits For Non-Vehicle Items: - Hilly Areas: Maximum 33% grant or maximum ₹33,00,000/- - Plain Areas: Maximum 25% grant or maximum ₹25,00,000/- 2. Financial Benefits For Vehicle Items: - Ordinary buses, taxis, maxis: Maximum 10% grant or maximum ₹25,00,000 in both hilly and plain areas - Push-back 2 and 26-seater to 28-seater bus/electric bus: Maximum 30% grant or maximum ₹42,20,000 - Push-back 2-seater and 50-seater electric bus/air-conditioned bus: Maximum 30% grant or maximum ₹42,20,000 3. Special Conditions for Electric/AC Buses: - Maximum 50 buses/electric buses allowed in any financial year - Only buses/electric buses meeting the prescribed criteria are eligible  > **Mode of Disbursement:** > Loans sanctioned through nationalized banks, rural banks, state cooperative banks, and regional rural banks > Interest rates as fixed by the loan bank as per rules > Frequency of Disbursement > One-time disbursement after loan sanction and approval > Expected Time of Disbursement > After selection and loan approval process completion > Validity > No specific validity period mentioned for the benefits



**Benefit Type:** Cash







**Exclusions / Not eligible:** 1. Bank or financial institution defaulters cannot avail benefits of this scheme.
