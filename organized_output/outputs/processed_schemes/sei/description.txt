
# Spurring Entrepreneurship Initiative


**Short Title:** SEI









**Tags:** Financial Assistance, Start Up Loan, Young Entrepreneurs, High Growth Entrepreneurship



**Spurring Entrepreneurship Initiative** is a scheme under the Mission Youth to provide financial assistance to youth of the Union Territory of Jammu &amp;amp; Kashmir for establishing their business units with a special focus on high-growth entrepreneurship. **Objectives:** 1. This scheme is based on the theme Youth Enterprise with Innovation (YouWiN)/ Champion for Innovation program for encouraging young entrepreneurs’ especially young women towards innovations in various enterprises. 1. Encourage innovation and job creation through creation of new businesses and expansion of existing businesses. 1. The scheme seeks to foster high-growth entrepreneurship through business plan competitions.




**Who is this for:** 
Individual; 
Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; 
Sector: Loan



**Where it applies:** 
Jammu and Kashmir; 
State









**Benefits / Assistance:** 1. Financial Assistance provided under this scheme shall be provided by J&amp;amp;K Bank as a start-up loan. 1. Assistance under the scheme shall be a minimum ₹2 Lakhs and a maximum of up to ₹20 Lakhs including the interest capitalized during the implementation period shall be provided as assistance. 1. There will be no margin money requirement to be paid by the applicant. 1. Selected applicants shall only have to repay the principal amount provided to them as assistance by Mission Youth. 1. Interest component subject to a maximum of ₹2 Lakh and subject to the condition that the applicant doesn’t make any default in repayment of the principal amount shall be paid by Mission Youth. 1. The amount of assistance provided by Mission Youth (MY) has to be compulsorily utilized for the establishment of a Startup only. 1. The bank shall extend the loan facility as per their normal assessment procedure.



**Benefit Type:** Cash







**Exclusions / Not eligible:** 1. Borrower should not be in default to any bank/financial institution. 1. The units which have already availed any incentive or subsidy under any scheme of the Government of India or the erstwhile State or UT Government shall not be eligible for assistance under the scheme.
















**Contacts & Authorities:** 
Implementing agency: Mission Youth.
Nodal Department: Department of Labour and Employment.
