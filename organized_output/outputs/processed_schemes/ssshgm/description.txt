
# Subsidy Scheme for Self-help Groups of Minorities


**Short Title:** SSSHGM









**Tags:** SHGs, Subsidy, Self-employment



The &quot;Subsidy Scheme for Self-help Groups of Minorities&quot; was launched by the Minorities Welfare Department, Government of Karnataka, and implemented by the Karnataka Minorities Development Corporation. The scheme provides a subsidy of 50% of the unit cost or a maximum of ₹2,00,000/- to the self-help groups of the minority community to engage in various types of self-employment activities and make them economically self-reliant, on the loans obtained from various financial institutions recognized by the Nationalized/Scheduled Bank/RBI. An SHG is to make fixed savings and provide internal lending to the members of the group as per requirement from the savings amount of the SHG.




**Who is this for:** 
Self Help Groups (SHGS); 
Category: Women and Child; 
Sector: Financial Assistance



**Where it applies:** 
Karnataka; 
State/ UT









**Benefits / Assistance:** - **Subsidy Amount:** 50% of the unit cost or a maximum of ₹2,00,000/- to the self-help groups.  **NOTE:** - The subsidy will be adjusted against the repayment of the loan taken by the SHG from recognized financial institutions. - SHGs focusing on good internal loan repayment will be given higher priority for the subsidy.



**Benefit Type:** Cash






















**Contacts & Authorities:** 
Implementing agency: Karnataka Minorities Development Corporation.
Nodal Department: Minorities Welfare Department.
