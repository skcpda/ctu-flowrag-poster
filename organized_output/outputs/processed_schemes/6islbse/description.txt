
# 6% Interest Subsidy on Loans taken through Banks up to a Limit of Rs.5 Lakh for Self-Employment


**Short Title:** 6ISLBSE









**Tags:** Loan, Subsidy, Employment, Scheduled Tribe, Tribal



The &quot;6% Interest Subsidy on Loans taken through Banks up to a Limit of Rs.5 Lakh for Self-Employment&quot; scheme was launched by the Tribal Development Department, Government of Gujarat. It aims to promote self-employment among scheduled tribe (ST) individuals by making business loans affordable. Under this scheme, ST beneficiaries availing loans for self-employment are provided a interest subsidy to reduce financial burden and encourage entrepreneurship. 




**Who is this for:** 
Individual; 
Category: Skills & Employment; 
Sector: Career information



**Where it applies:** 
Gujarat; 
State/ UT









**Benefits / Assistance:** - 6% interest subsidy on loans taken through banks. - Maximum loan amount eligible: ₹5,00,000/-. 



**Benefit Type:** Cash
