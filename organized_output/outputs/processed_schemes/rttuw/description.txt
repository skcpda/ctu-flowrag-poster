
# Relief to the Unregistered Workers


**Short Title:** RTTUW





**Launch Date:** 2011-11-21





**Tags:** Financial Assistance, Disabilitiy, Disease, Unregistered Worker



The &quot;Relief to the Unregistered Workers&quot; scheme was launched by the Telangana Building &amp; Other Construction Workers Welfare Board, LET&amp;F (Labour) Department Telangana. The scheme aims to provide financial assistance to the dependents of unregistered building and construction workers who die in accidents at construction sites and also offers compensation for unregistered workers who suffer permanent disabilities due to accidents. This initiative supports the welfare of construction workers and their families, even if the workers were not registered at the time of the incident.




**Who is this for:** 
Individual; 
Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; 
Sector: Financial assistance



**Where it applies:** 
Telangana; 
State/ UT









**Benefits / Assistance:** > **Compensation for Deceased Workers:** - Financial Assistance of ₹50,000/- to the dependents of unregistered workers who die in accidents at construction sites or related projects.  > **Relief for Permanent Disabilities:** - ₹20,000/- is provided for cases of permanent disability where the disability is 50% or above. - ₹10,000/- is provided for cases of permanent partial disability where the disability is below 50%.



**Benefit Type:** Cash






















**Contacts & Authorities:** 

Nodal Department: Labour, Employment Training and Factories Department.
