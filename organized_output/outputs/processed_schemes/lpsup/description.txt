
# Leprosy Pension Scheme


**Short Title:** LPSUP









**Tags:** Financial Assistance, Social Welfare, Empowerment, Disability



Leprosy Pension Scheme has been started by the Government of Uttar Pradesh and operated by the Department of Empowerment of Persons with Disabilities, Uttar Pradesh. The main objective of the scheme is to provide a grant-in-aid for the maintenance of persons with disabilities and leprosy, whose family income is not sufficient for them.




**Who is this for:** 
Individual; 
Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; 
Sector: Pension



**Where it applies:** 
Uttar Pradesh; 
State



**DBT Scheme:** Yes (Direct Benefit Transfer)







**Benefits / Assistance:** The rate of grant per beneficiary per month will be Rs. 3000/- who became disabled due to Leprosy.



**Benefit Type:** Cash






















**Contacts & Authorities:** 

Nodal Department: Empowerment of Persons With Disabilities Department, Uttar Pradesh.
