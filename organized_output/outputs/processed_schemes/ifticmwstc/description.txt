
#  Incentive For The Intra Caste Marriage within Scheduled Tribe Community


**Short Title:** IFTICMWSTC









**Tags:** Intra Caste, Marriage, Schedule Tribe, Incentive, ST



The scheme &quot;Incentive for Intra Caste Marriage within the Scheduled Tribe Community&quot; was launched by the Tribal Welfare Department, Government of Karnataka. The scheme provides an incentive of ₹2,00,000/- to couples who are both from the Scheduled Tribe (ST) community but belong to different sub-castes within the ST community. The marriage should have taken place on or after 1st April 2018. The couple must reside in Karnataka and apply for the scheme within one year of their marriage.




**Who is this for:** 
Individual; 
Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; 
Sector: Marriage



**Where it applies:** 
Karnataka; 
State/ UT









**Benefits / Assistance:** An incentive of ₹2,00,000/- is provided to the married couple.



**Benefit Type:** Cash






















**Contacts & Authorities:** 

Nodal Department: Tribal Welfare Department.
