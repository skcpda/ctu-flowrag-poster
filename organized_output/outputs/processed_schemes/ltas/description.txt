
# Labor Tool Assistance Scheme


**Short Title:** LTAS





**Launch Date:** 2018-01-01





**Tags:** Electrician, Plumber, Carpenter, Coolie, Painter



This scheme has been started by the Government of Chhattisgarh and operated by Labor Department. The benefit of the scheme will be given to the beneficiaries of trades like construction work like mason, electrician, plumber, carpenter, porter, painter, etc.




**Who is this for:** 
Individual; 
Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; 
Sector: Financial assistance



**Where it applies:** 
Chhattisgarh; 
State









**Benefits / Assistance:** - Tool kit



**Benefit Type:** In Kind






















**Contacts & Authorities:** 

Nodal Department: Labour Department.
