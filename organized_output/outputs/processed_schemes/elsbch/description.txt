
# Education Loan Scheme for Backward Classes Haryana


**Short Title:** ELSBCH









**Tags:** Pursing, Professional Degree, Technical Degree, Graduation



The scheme &quot;Education Loan Scheme for Backward Classes&quot; by the Haryana Backwards Classes and Economically Weaker Sections Kalyan Nigam, Government of Haryana, aims to provide education loans to members of Backward Classes for pursuing professional and technical education at the graduate and higher levels. Through this scheme, education loans are provided to eligible Backward Classes students to help them pursue professional and technical courses. The scheme is implemented by the Haryana Backward Classes and Economically Weaker Sections Kalyan Nigam. 




**Who is this for:** 
Individual; 
Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; 
Sector: Loan



**Where it applies:** 
Haryana; 
State/ UT









**Benefits / Assistance:** 1. Education loan for pursuing professional and technical education at the graduate and higher levels. 1. The maximum loan amount provided is ₹15,00,000/- for studies in India. 1. The maximum loan amount provided is ₹20,00,000/- for studies abroad. 1. The interest rate is 4% per annum. 1. For girl students, the interest rate is 3.5% per annum. 



**Benefit Type:** Cash






















**Contacts & Authorities:** 

Nodal Department: Welfare of Scheduled Caste & Backward Classes Department.
