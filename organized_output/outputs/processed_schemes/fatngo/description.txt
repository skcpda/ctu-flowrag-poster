
# Financial Assistance to Non Governmental Organisations


**Short Title:** FATNGO









**Tags:** NGO, Children, School, Person With Disability



The Department for the Welfare of Differently Abled Persons, Government of Tamil Nadu launched the scheme &quot;Financial Assistance to Non Governmental Organisations&quot;. Under this scheme, funds are sanctioned to schools for children with an intellectual disability run by NGOs towards feeding charges, rent, and construction/extension of buildings. 




**Who is this for:** 
Business Entity; 
Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; 
Sector: Shelter



**Where it applies:** 
Tamil Nadu; 
State/ UT









**Benefits / Assistance:** - Financial assistance is given to the NGO for feeding charges, rent, and construction/extension of building.



**Benefit Type:** In Kind






















**Contacts & Authorities:** 

Nodal Department: Welfare of Differently Abled Persons Department.
