
# Overseas Scholarships For Scheduled Tribes-Madhya Pradesh


**Short Title:** OSFST











Each year, the Tribal Welfare Department of the Madhya Pradesh Government provides 50 scholarships for foreign studies to scheduled Tribes students. With the scholarship, the students belonging to Scheduled Tribes will get quality higher education in recognized institutions from abroad universities. 




**Who is this for:** 
Individual; 
Category: Education & Learning; 
Sector: Scholarships and student finance



**Where it applies:** 
Madhya Pradesh; 
State









**Benefits / Assistance:** - Scholarship for Higher Education.



**Benefit Type:** Cash






















**Contacts & Authorities:** 

Nodal Department: Tribal Welfare and Scheduled Caste (SC) Department.
