
# Scheme of Developing High Quality Research (SHODH)


**Short Title:** SHODH









**Tags:** Stipend, Student, PhD, Research, Education



The &quot;Scheme of Developing High Quality Research (SHODH)&quot; was launched by the Education Department, Government of Gujarat. The scheme aims to encourage Ph.D. research scholars to pursue high-quality, regular, and full-time research in the state. > **Objectives of the Scheme:** - To provide financial assistance to maximum students in the state of Gujarat to encourage them to pursue high-quality research. - To encourage students studying in all branches of knowledge to pursue high-quality regular and full-time research. - To increase the capacity and scope of quality research in higher education institutions. - To create a global identity through industry and socially useful research. - To promote research to meet the current needs and to meet the modern requirements. - To also promote research that preserves India&#39;s rich cultural heritage and embraces new dimensions. 




**Who is this for:** 
Individual; 
Category: Education & Learning; 
Sector: Universities and higher education



**Where it applies:** 
Gujarat; 
State/ UT









**Benefits / Assistance:** > **Stipend:** - ₹15,000/- per month as stipend for 2 years. - ₹20,000/- per annum as contingency expense. - Total Assistance: ₹4,00,000/- per scholar at the end of 2 years. 



**Benefit Type:** Cash
