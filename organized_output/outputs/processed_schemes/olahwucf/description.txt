
# Old Age Pension To Handloom Weavers Under Coop. Fold


**Short Title:** OLAHWUCF









**Tags:** Pension, Handloom Weavers, Old Age



The “Old Age Pension To Handloom Weavers&amp;quot; scheme has been implemented by the Micro, Small &amp;amp; Medium Enterprise and Textiles Department, Government of West Bengal. The objective of the scheme is to provide monthly financial assistance in the form of pension to a weaver member of a handloom cooperative society. 




**Who is this for:** 
Individual; 
Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; 
Sector: Pension



**Where it applies:** 
West Bengal; 
State/ UT









**Benefits / Assistance:** - An amount of ₹1000/- per month.



**Benefit Type:** Cash






















**Contacts & Authorities:** 

Nodal Department: Micro, Small & Medium Enterprises and Textiles Department.
