
# Payment of Ex-Gratia Amount on Death of the UPNL Employee During Service


**Short Title:** PEGADUPNLEDS









**Tags:** Financial Assistance, Ex-gratia, Dependents, Death, UPNL



The scheme &amp;quot;Payment of Ex-Gratia Amount on Death of UPNL Employees During Service&amp;quot; is implemented by Uttarakhand Purv Sainik Kalyan Nigam Limited (UPNL) under the Sainik Welfare Department, Government of Uttarakhand. The scheme aims to provide financial assistance to the legal dependents of deceased UPNL employees in the event of the employee’s death during their service period. 




**Who is this for:** 
Family; 
Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; 
Sector: Financial assistance



**Where it applies:** 
Uttarakhand; 
State/ UT









**Benefits / Assistance:** A one-time ex-gratia payment of ₹1,00,000/- shall be made from the UPNL fund to the family/legal dependents of a UPNL employee in the event of the employee’s death during the service period. 



**Benefit Type:** Cash






















**Contacts & Authorities:** 
Implementing agency: Uttarakhand Purv Sainik Kalyan Nigam Limited.
Nodal Department: State Sainik Board.
