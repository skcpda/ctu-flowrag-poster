
# Mahila Nursery Yojana


**Short Title:** MNY









**Tags:** Financial Assistance, Women Group, Nursery, SHG, Plants



The &amp;quot;Mahila Nursery Yojana&amp;quot; scheme was launched by the Department of Forest, Government of Uttarakhand. The scheme aims to empower women&amp;#39;s groups through income generation by establishing plant nurseries. Under this scheme, high-quality, species-specific seeds and technical training are provided to self-help groups and local women to set up nurseries. Resources required for nursery operations are also funded via fixed grants. The plants grown in these nurseries are purchased by the Forest Department based on agreement.




**Who is this for:** 
Self Help Groups (SHGS); 
Category: Agriculture,Rural & Environment; 
Sector: Agricultural Inputs- seeds, fertilizer etc.



**Where it applies:** 
Uttarakhand; 
State/ UT









**Benefits / Assistance:** - High-quality species-wise seeds are provided to women’s groups. - Financial support in the form of a fixed grant for nursery equipment and resources. - Technical support and training for nursery management. - Purchase of grown plants by the Forest Department based on experience and quality. **Note:** The department disburses the grant in installments directly into the self-help group&#39;s account upon the purchase of resources required for establishing the nursery.



**Benefit Type:** Composite






















**Contacts & Authorities:** 

Nodal Department: Uttarakhand Forest Department.
