
# Dr. B. R. Ambedkar State Award To SC/ST/OBC/Minorities Students


**Short Title:** DBRASA





**Launch Date:** 2018-01-01





**Tags:** Dr. B.R. Ambedkar, State Award, Scheduled Castes (SC), Scheduled Tribes (ST), Other Backward Classes (OBC), Financial Assistance, Minorities



**Detailed** Department for the Welfare of SC/ST/OBC, Government of NCT of Delhi invites applications for Dr B.R. Ambedkar State Award to SC/ST/OBC/Minorities Students, Delhi 2021 from students pursuing graduation from recognized colleges/institutions. This scholarship aims to award the eligible meritorious students. Selected toppers in each discipline at graduation level will receive an award of INR 25,000.




**Who is this for:** 
Individual; 
Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; 
Sector: Financial assistance



**Where it applies:** 
Delhi; 
State









**Benefits / Assistance:** Benefits INR 25,000 to students who top among the SC/ST/OBC/Minorities in each discipline of the professional/technical degree course.



**Benefit Type:** Cash






















**Contacts & Authorities:** 

Nodal Department: Welfare of SC/ST/OBC/Minorities Department.
