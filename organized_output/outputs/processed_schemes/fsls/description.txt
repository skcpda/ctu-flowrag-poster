
# Foreign Study Loan Scheme (GUEEDC)


**Short Title:** FSLS









**Tags:** Unreserved Category, Higher Education Abroad, MBBS, Master, PG Diploma



The scheme provides **financial loan assistance** to students from the **unreserved category** to pursue **higher education abroad.** Loans up to **₹15 lakh** are granted at simple interest of **4% per annum**, with flexible repayment options. Security and repayment conditions depend on the loan amount. This scheme aims to support meritorious, economically weaker students for global education opportunities.




**Who is this for:** 
Individual; 
Category: Education & Learning; 
Sector: International education



**Where it applies:** 
Gujarat; 
State/ UT









**Benefits / Assistance:** **Loan Amount:** Up to ₹15.00 lakh **Interest Rate:** Simple interest at 4% per annum **Collateral:** **Up to ₹7.50 lakh:** Property valued at 1.5 times the loan amount **Above ₹7.50 lakh:** Property equal to the loan amount **Loan Repayment:** **Up to ₹5 lakh:** repay in 5 years after 1-year moratorium **Above ₹5 lakh**: repay in 6 years after 1-year moratorium  **Borrower can pre-pay without penalty**



**Benefit Type:** Cash






















**Contacts & Authorities:** 
Implementing agency: Gujarat Unreserved Educational And Economical Development Corporation.
Nodal Department: Social Justice and Empowerment Department.
