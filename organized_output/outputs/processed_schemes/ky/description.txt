
# Krishi Yantra


**Short Title:** KY









**Tags:** Farming, Farmer, Equipment, Development



The Krishi Yantra Scheme under the Agriculture Department of the Rajasthan government has decided to start the Agricultural Machinery Subsidy Scheme for the convenience of farmers. In this scheme, if the farmer purchases farming equipment from an authorized dealer, then the state government gives a subsidy to the farmers which is 40% of the total price. Up to 50% subsidy is given to government farmers.  > **Payment of grant** 1. Physical verification of the instrument will be done by the Agriculture Supervisor/Assistant Agriculture Officer. 1. Purchase bill will have to be presented at the time of verification. 1. The grant will be paid online into the farmer&#39;s bank account.
