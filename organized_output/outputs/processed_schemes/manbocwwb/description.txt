
# Medical Assistance (NBOCWWB)


**Short Title:** MANBOCWWB









**Tags:** Financial Assistance, Medical Treatment, Construction Worker



The &quot;Medical Assistance&quot; scheme launched by the Nagaland Building and Other Construction Workers&#39; Welfare Board provides financial assistance to registered beneficiaries and their family members for hospitalization. ₹1,000/- per day is provided for the first five days of hospitalization, followed by ₹500/- per day for up to 10 more days.




**Who is this for:** 
Individual; 
Category: Health & Wellness; 
Sector: Financial assistance



**Where it applies:** 
Nagaland; 
State/ UT









**Benefits / Assistance:** - ₹1,000/- per day for the first five days of hospitalization. - ₹500/- per day for the next 10 days (up to 15 days total).



**Benefit Type:** Cash






















**Contacts & Authorities:** 

Nodal Department: Labour and Employment Department, Nagaland.
