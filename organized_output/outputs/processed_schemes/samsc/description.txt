
# Scheme of Assistance for Management of Stray Cattle


**Short Title:** SAMSC









**Tags:** Agriculture, Animal Husbandry, Stray Cattle



The &amp;quot;Scheme of Assistance for Management of Stray Cattle&amp;quot; is designed to support Panjrapoles (animal shelters) registered under the Public Charity Act in their efforts to manage and maintain stray cattle. Each eligible shelter receives a one-time maintenance assistance for stray cattle to ensure proper care, control, and management of these animals.




**Who is this for:** 
Registered Societies, Government Organisation; 
Category: Agriculture,Rural & Environment; 
Sector: Animal husbandry



**Where it applies:** 
Gujarat; 
State/ UT
