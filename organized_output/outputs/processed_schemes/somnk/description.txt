
# Mukhyamantri Nahri Kendra


**Short Title:** MNK









**Tags:** Woman, Financial Assistance, Loan, Subsidy



The &quot;Mukhyamantri Nahri Kendra&quot; was launched by the Tribal Development Department, Government of Gujarat. The scheme aims to empower tribal women by providing them with livelihood opportunities through women-run centers. These centers promote the sale of tribal dishes and home-made products, thereby improving income and living standards. Under this scheme, tribal women-run groups receive a subsidy covering 50% of project costs and the remaining 50% as a loan at a concessional interest rate. 




**Who is this for:** 
Individual, Self Help Groups (SHGS); 
Category: Skills & Employment; 
Sector: Career information



**Where it applies:** 
Gujarat; 
State/ UT









**Benefits / Assistance:** > **Monetary Benefit** - 50% of the project cost or ₹5,00,000, whichever is less, provided as subsidy. - The remaining 50% of the project cost is provided as a loan. > ***Subsidy Disbursement*** - Given in two installments – 60% in the first installment and 40% in the second installment. > ***Interest Rate*** - Loan at 4% per annum. > ***Repayment*** - Loan (excluding subsidy) must be repaid in 20 equal installments over 5 years, including interest. - Option to repay earlier is allowed. > ***Beneficiary Contribution*** - No upfront contribution required from the beneficiary. 



**Benefit Type:** Cash






















**Contacts & Authorities:**
