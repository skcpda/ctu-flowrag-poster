
# Grant to Meritorious Wards of ESM in Sports: National / International Level (Sainik Welfare)


**Short Title:** GMWESNIL-SW









**Tags:** Sports, Student, Ex-Serviceman, Widow, Grant



&quot;Grant to Meritorious Wards of ESM in Sports: National / International Level&quot; is a Welfare Scheme by the Armed Forces Flag Day Fund of the Department of Sainik Welfare, Puducherry. Through this scheme, the meritorious wards of the ESM are provided grants for their achievements in sports at national and international levels. The application should be submitted offline within three months from the date of the achievement.




**Who is this for:** 

Category: Education & Learning; 
Sector: Education and training grants, fellowship, stipend



**Where it applies:** 
Puducherry; 
State/ UT









**Benefits / Assistance:** - Grant of ₹50,000/- for achievements in sports at the international level. - Grant of ₹30,000/- for achievements in sports at the national level. - The amount is credited to the beneficiary’s Bank Account through RTGS/ NEFT.



**Benefit Type:** Cash






















**Contacts & Authorities:** 
Implementing agency: Rajya Sainik Board, Puducherry.
Nodal Department: Sainik Welfare Department, Puducherry.
