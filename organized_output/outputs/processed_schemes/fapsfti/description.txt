
# Financial Assistance for Private Self-Financed Technical Institutions


**Short Title:** FAPSFTI









**Tags:** Financial Assistance, Technical, Institutions, Infrastructure



The &quot;Financial Assistance for Private Self-Financed Technical Institutions&quot; scheme by the Higher, Technical Education &amp; Skill Development Department, Government of Jharkhand, aims to enhance technical education by supporting private technical institutions. Under this scheme, financial assistance is provided to private engineering and polytechnic institutions for infrastructure development, modernization, and accreditation.




**Who is this for:** 
Business Entity; 
Category: Education & Learning; 
Sector: Universities and higher education



**Where it applies:** 
Jharkhand; 
State/ UT









**Benefits / Assistance:** 1. Grant of up to ₹2,00,00,000/- for new private engineering institutions. 1. Grant of up to ₹1,00,00,000/- for new private polytechnic institutions for infrastructure development. 1. Grant of up to ₹6,00,00,000/- for existing engineering institutions. 1. Grant of up to ₹3,00,00,000/- for existing polytechnic institutions for upgrading facilities.  **NOTE:** 1. Assistance is provided in three equal installments. 1. Accreditation-linked funding: the second installment requires two NBA-accredited courses, and the third installment requires four NBA-accredited courses. 1. At least 60% of seats are reserved for local students of Jharkhand.



**Benefit Type:** Cash
