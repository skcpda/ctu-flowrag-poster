
# Bihar Shatabdi Asangathit Karyakshetra Kamagaar Evan Shilpakar Samajik Suraksha Yojana: Medical Assistance for Incurable Diseases


**Short Title:** BSAKMAID









**Tags:** Social Security, Unorganised Worker, Craftsmen, Incurable Diseases, Medical Assistance



The “Bihar Shatabdi Asangathit Karyakshetra Kamagaar Evan Shilpakar Samajik Suraksha Yojana” launched by the Labour Resources Department, Bihar, aims to provide social security through various types of financial assistance to workers and artisans in the unorganized sector. &lt;br&gt;




**Who is this for:** 
Individual; 
Category: Health & Wellness; 
Sector: Financial assistance



**Where it applies:** 
Bihar; 
State/ UT









**Benefits / Assistance:** - **Financial Assistance:** ₹7,500/- to ₹30,000/- (for detailed view refer to Page no. 7 of the scheme [Guidelines](https://state.bihar.gov.in/labour/cache/40/14-May-21/SHOW_DOCS/Schemes-1.pdf)) &lt;br&gt; &lt;br&gt;



**Benefit Type:** Cash






















**Contacts & Authorities:** 

Nodal Department: Labour Resources Department.
