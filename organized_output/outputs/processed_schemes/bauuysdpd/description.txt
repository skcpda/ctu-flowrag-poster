
# Dr.Babasaheb Ambedkar Udyog Uday Yojana: Assistance to MSEs for Shed Developed by Private Developer


**Short Title:** BAUUYSDPD





**Launch Date:** 2020-08-07





**Tags:** Business, Entrepreneurship, MSMEs, Start Up, Scheduled Caste, Scheduled Tribe



The scheme &amp;quot;Dr.Babasaheb Ambedkar Udyog Uday Yojana&amp;quot; is an umbrella scheme launched by the Industries and Mines Department, Gujarat. It aims to empower the state&amp;#39;s dynamic MSME sector, which significantly contributes to employment, GDP, and exports. Effective from August 7th, 2020 to August 6th, 2025, this initiative supports SC/ST entrepreneurs and strengthens MSMEs, making them globally competitive. The component &amp;quot;Assistance to MSEs for Shed Developed by Private Developer&amp;quot; provides SC/ST entrepreneurs for acquiring industrial sheds in mini estates developed by private developers. It encourages industrial growth by offering support for land, building, and infrastructure costs.




**Who is this for:** 
Business Entity, Industries; 
Category: Business & Entrepreneurship; 
Sector: Setting up / start-up / entrepreneurship



**Where it applies:** 
Gujarat; 
State/ UT









**Benefits / Assistance:** - **Financial Assistance:** Private developers receive an additional 15% assistance on the total cost of the shed. - **Government Support:** SC/ST entrepreneurs need to pay only 30% of the shed price, after which possession is handed over, and developers can claim assistance. - **Subsidized Costs:** The government disburses 35% of the shed cost after the sale and the remaining amount after production begins.



**Benefit Type:** Cash







**Exclusions / Not eligible:** - Trading activity will not be eligible under the scheme.
















**Contacts & Authorities:** 

Nodal Department: Industries and Mines Department.
