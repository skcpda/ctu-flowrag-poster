
# Maternity Support Scheme (MPBOCWWB)


**Short Title:** MSSMPBOCWWB









**Tags:** Building Worker, Construction Workers, Labour, Maternity, Financial Assistance



The &quot;Maternity Support Scheme&quot; by the Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board, Labour Department, Jammu &amp; Kashmir, provides financial assistance of ₹16,000/- as maternity allowance, payable in two installments. Only registered construction workers or their wives are eligible to receive the benefits under this scheme.    




**Who is this for:** 
Individual; 
Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; 
Sector: Financial assistance



**Where it applies:** 
Madhya Pradesh; 
State/ UT









**Benefits / Assistance:** 1. **First Installment:** ₹4,000 for registration of pregnancy in the first trimester.1. **Second Installment:** ₹12,000 for: - Delivery in government or authorized non-government hospitals under the Ayushman Bharat &quot;Niramayam&quot; program for pregnancy and delivery-related high-risk packages. - Registration of newborns after institutional birth. - Early breastfeeding of babies. - BCG, OPV, and Hep B vaccination for the infant. **Total Amount: ₹16,000**



**Benefit Type:** Cash






















**Contacts & Authorities:** 

Nodal Department: Labour Department.
