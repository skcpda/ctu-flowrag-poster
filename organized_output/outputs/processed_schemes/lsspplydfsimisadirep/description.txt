
# Loktantra Senani Samman Pension (Police Line Yojana) for the democracy fighters of the state imprisoned under MISA/DIR during the emergency period (from 25.06.1975 to 21.03.1977)


**Short Title:** LSSPPLYDFSIMISADIREP





**Launch Date:** 2025-09-03





**Tags:** Pension, Police Line, Democracy Fighter, Emergency Period



The scheme &amp;quot;Loktantra Senani Samman Pension (Police Line Yojana)&amp;quot; is implemented by the Police Department under the Department of Home, Government of Uttarakhand. The scheme aims to provide financial assistance in the form of a monthly pension to democracy fighters of the state who were imprisoned under MISA/DIR during the Emergency period (25.06.1975 to 21.03.1977). 




**Who is this for:** 
Individual; 
Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; 
Sector: Pension



**Where it applies:** 
Uttarakhand; 
State/ UT









**Benefits / Assistance:** **Pension Amount:** ₹20,000/- per month 



**Benefit Type:** Cash






















**Contacts & Authorities:** 
Implementing agency: Police Department, Government Of Uttarakhand.
Nodal Department: Department of Home.
