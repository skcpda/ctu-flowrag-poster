
# Promoting to Farmer for Post Harvesting & Management (Value Addition)


**Short Title:** PFPHMVA









**Tags:** Agriculture, Farmer, Post Harvesting Technology



The scheme &amp;quot;Promoting to Farmer for Post Harvesting &amp;amp; Management (Value Addition)&amp;quot; introduced by the Agriculture, Farmers Welfare and Cooperation Department, Gujarat, aims to encourage all categories of farmers to establish crop value addition units that utilize post-harvesting technology and management strategies.




**Who is this for:** 
Individual, Joint Liability Groups (JLGS), Registered Societies, Self Help Groups (SHGS); 
Category: Agriculture,Rural & Environment; 
Sector: Financial assistance



**Where it applies:** 
Gujarat; 
State/ UT
