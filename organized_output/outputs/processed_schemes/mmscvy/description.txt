
# Mukhyamantri Samekit Chaur Vikas Yojana


**Short Title:** MMSCVY





**Launch Date:** 2023-07-05





**Tags:** Financial Assistance, Employment, Farmers, Fish Production



The &quot;Mukhyamantri Samekit Chaur Vikas Yojana&quot; was launched by the Animal and Fisheries Resources Department, Government of Bihar. under Seven Nishchay-2, aims to develop undeveloped and unutilized private land in Bihar for fish-based integrated aquaculture. With a budget of ₹2972.79 lakh, the scheme targets the sustainable development of 600 hectares of Chaur land  ***Objective:*** - Utilization of unutilized private land for fish farming. - Increase in fish production and productivity. - Groundwater level improvement, supporting the &quot;Jal-Jeevan-Hariyali&quot; campaign. - Additional employment opportunities for farmers. - Strengthening of the local economy.




**Who is this for:** 
Individual, Family, Business Entity; 
Category: Agriculture,Rural & Environment




**Where it applies:** 
Bihar; 
State/ UT









**Benefits / Assistance:** > **Beneficiary-based subsidy:** - 50% for other categories. - 70% for Most Backward Castes, Scheduled Castes, and Scheduled Tribes. - 40% Entrepreneur-based subsidy.



**Benefit Type:** Cash
