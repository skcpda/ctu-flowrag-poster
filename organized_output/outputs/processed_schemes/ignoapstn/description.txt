
# Indira Gandhi National Old Age Pension Scheme -Tamil Nadu


**Short Title:** IGNOAPSTN









**Tags:** Pension, Senior Citizen, BPL, Clothes, Meal



&#39;The Indira Gandhi National Widow Pension Scheme&#39; was launched by the Social Welfare and Women Empowerment Department, Government of Tamil Nadu. The scheme aims to provide a pension of ₹1,000/- to destitute people of 60 years and above belonging to households below the poverty line. The scheme also provides one free saree to females, one free dhoti to males during Pongal and Deepavali, and free rice in the Anganwadi Centres.




**Who is this for:** 
Individual; 
Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; 
Sector: Pension



**Where it applies:** 
Tamil Nadu; 
State/ UT









**Benefits / Assistance:** **Pension:** - ₹1,000/- per month. **Free Clothes during Pongal and Deepavali:** - One free dhoti to a male pensioner. - One free saree to a female pensioner. **Free Rice in Anganwadi Centres:** - 2kg/month to those who are taking nutritious meal. - 4kg/month to those who are not taking nutritious meal.



**Benefit Type:** Composite






















**Contacts & Authorities:** 

Nodal Department: Department of Social Welfare and Women Empowerment.
