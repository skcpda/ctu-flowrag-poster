
# Self-Employment Scheme for Unemployed Minority Communities


**Short Title:** SESUMC









**Tags:** Minority, Self Employment, Loan, Subsidy, Unemployed



The scheme “Self-Employment Scheme for Unemployed Minority Communities” was launched by the Minority Welfare Department, Government of Uttarakhand. The scheme aims to provide a grant of 25% of the loan amount to individuals belonging to minority communities who are permanent residents of Uttarakhand. The grant is available when a loan of ₹1 lakh to ₹10 lakh is taken for starting self-employment activities. 




**Who is this for:** 
Individual; 
Category: Skills & Employment; 
Sector: Loan



**Where it applies:** 
Uttarakhand; 
State/ UT









**Benefits / Assistance:** - Under this scheme, a grant is provided for self-employment. On taking a loan of ₹1 lakh to ₹10 lakh, a grant of 25% of the loan amount is given, with a minimum grant of ₹25,000/- and a maximum of ₹2,50,000/-. - The benefit of the scheme is available only on taking a loan, in which 60% of the project cost is provided as a loan, 25% as grant/subsidy by the department, and 15% as contribution by the applicant. - Interest on the loan will be charged according to the current prevailing rates of the bank. 



**Benefit Type:** Cash






















**Contacts & Authorities:**
