
# Sanctioning of Tricycles of Orthopedically Challenged


**Short Title:** SOTOOC









**Tags:** Social Welfare, Disable, RPwD Act



The A.P. Differently Abled &amp; Senior Citizens Assistance Corporation, Andhra Pradesh, under its Action Plan 2017-18, aims to provide tricycles to orthopedically challenged individuals to enhance their mobility and independence. The tricycles are distributed by the Assistant Directors, Department for the Welfare of Differently Abled &amp; Senior Citizens, and District Managers, A.P. Differently Abled &amp; Senior Citizens Assistance Corporation.




**Who is this for:** 
Individual; 
Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; 
Sector: Aids/Appliances



**Where it applies:** 
Andhra Pradesh; 
State/ UT









**Benefits / Assistance:** - Eligible beneficiaries receive a free tricycle to improve their mobility and independence. 



**Benefit Type:** In Kind






















**Contacts & Authorities:** 

Nodal Department: Dept of Social Welfare, WCD.
