
# Community Based Training Scheme


**Short Title:** CBTS









**Tags:** Skill Development, Employment, Unemployed, Minority Community



The &quot;Community Based Training Scheme&quot; was launched by the Minorities Welfare Department, Government of Karnataka, and implemented by the Karnataka Minorities Development Corporation. The scheme aims to provide training in Heavy Vehicle Driving, Short Hand, Security Services, Office Soft Skill, Retail Sales, Beauty Parlor Course (Male and Female) to the unemployed young men and women belonging to minority community for self employment or employment in MNC, BPOs and other activities.




**Who is this for:** 
Individual; 
Category: Skills & Employment; 
Sector: Training and Skill Up-gradation



**Where it applies:** 
Karnataka; 
State/ UT









**Benefits / Assistance:** - **The scheme provides training in Heavy Vehicle Driving, Short Hand, Security Services, Office Soft Skill, Retail Sales, and Beauty Parlor Course (Male and Female).**



**Benefit Type:** In Kind






















**Contacts & Authorities:** 
Implementing agency: Karnataka Minorities Development Corporation.
Nodal Department: Minorities Welfare Department.
