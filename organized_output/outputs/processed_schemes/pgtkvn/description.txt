
# Pashu Palan Yojana (GTKVN)


**Short Title:** PSGTKVN









**Tags:** Loan, Thakor Caste, Koli Caste, Animal Husbandry, Self-employment



The &amp;quot;Pashu Palan Yojana&amp;quot; is implemented by the Gujarat Thakor and Koli Vikas Nigam (GTKVN) under the Social Justice and Empowerment Department, Government of Gujarat. The scheme aims to strengthen the livelihoods of the Thakor and Koli communities by providing financial support for animal husbandry activities, such as dairy farming with cows and buffaloes. Under this scheme, loans are offered to purchase milch animals like cows and buffaloes, thereby promoting sustainable self-employment in agriculture-related sectors. 




**Who is this for:** 
Individual; 
Category: Agriculture,Rural & Environment; 
Sector: Animal husbandry



**Where it applies:** 
Gujarat; 
State/ UT









**Benefits / Assistance:** - The maximum loan limit under this scheme is up to ₹1,00,000/-. - The loan is provided for animal husbandry activities such as dairy farming, boosting rural self-employment. 



**Benefit Type:** Cash






















**Contacts & Authorities:** 
Implementing agency: Gujarat Thakor And Koli Vikas Nigam.
Nodal Department: Social Justice and Empowerment Department.
