
# Combined Merit Scholarship


**Short Title:** CMS-G









**Tags:** Student, Scholarship, Merit, Education, Degree, Academic



The scheme &quot;Combined Merit Scholarship&quot; by the Higher Education Department, Government of Assam, aims to provide financial assistance to students of Assam studying in first semester degree courses and master&#39;s degree courses in any recognized colleges or universities in the state and other states of the country, focusing on students with uniformly good academic records and at least 60% marks in last qualifying examinations. The scheme provides access to poor students of the state and helps them achieve the benefits of higher education through merit-based scholarship support for undergraduate and postgraduate studies. The applicant must be selected and admitted in degree or master&#39;s degree courses in any recognized universities or colleges and must be domiciled in Assam. The scheme is implemented by Higher Education Department. The applications for this scheme are accepted online. 




**Who is this for:** 
Individual; 
Category: Education & Learning; 
Sector: Scholarships and student finance



**Where it applies:** 
Assam; 
State/ UT









**Benefits / Assistance:** > **Financial Assistance** - For Degree Courses: ₹300/- per month. - For Master Degree Courses: ₹500/- per month. - The scheme provides 200 scholarships for degree students (three years duration) and 100 scholarships for master degree students (two years duration). **Selection:** Based on merit and budget allocation. **Coverage:** Students pursuing general degree and master degree courses in Arts, Science, and Commerce disciplines. > **Mode of Disbursement** **Mode:** Direct Benefit Transfer system. **Conditions:** Subject to selection by the selection committee and financial sanction approval. > **Conditions for Availment** - The applicant must maintain enrollment in the respective degree or master&#39;s degree course throughout the scholarship period. - Academic performance standards must be maintained as per institutional and scheme requirements. - Regular attendance and compliance with all institutional rules and regulations. - Bank account must remain active and updated for Direct Benefit Transfer disbursement. 



**Benefit Type:** Cash






















**Contacts & Authorities:** 

Nodal Department: Higher Education Department.
