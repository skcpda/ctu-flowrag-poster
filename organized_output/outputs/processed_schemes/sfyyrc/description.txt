
# State Food Scheme (Yellow Ration Card)


**Short Title:** SFSYRC









**Tags:** Ration Card, Food Distribution, Yellow Card, Rice



The scheme &amp;quot;State Food Scheme (Yellow Ration Card)&amp;quot; was launched by the Department of Food, Civil Supplies &amp;amp; Consumer Affairs, Government of Uttarakhand. The scheme aims to provide rice at subsidized rates to yellow ration card holders in the state. 




**Who is this for:** 
Individual; 
Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; 
Sector: Food Security / Public Distribution System



**Where it applies:** 
Uttarakhand; 
State/ UT









**Benefits / Assistance:** Under this scheme, each ration card holder receives 7.50 kg of rice per month at the rate of ₹11/- per kg. 



**Benefit Type:** In Kind






















**Contacts & Authorities:** 

Nodal Department: Department Of Food & Civil Supplies.
