
# Sero Positive Illness Scheme


**Short Title:** SPIS





**Launch Date:** 2009-01-01





**Tags:** Sero Disease, Children, Scholarship, Education



Launched in 2009, the scheme &amp;quot;Sero Positive Illness Scheme&amp;quot; by the Social Justice and Empowerment Department, Government of Gujarat aims to support the education of children affected by or born into families impacted by Sero Positive illness. Through this scheme, financial scholarships and support for school uniform, books, tuition fees, food, and hostel expenses are provided to destitute and orphaned children across education levels—from primary school to postgraduate studies. The scheme is implemented by the Director, Social Security Department, Gujarat State, Gandhinagar, and at the district level, by the District Social Security Officer or District Child Protection Officer.
