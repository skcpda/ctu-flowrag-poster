
# Aatmanirbhar Gujarat Scheme for assistance to MSMEs: Assistance for Patent Registration 


**Short Title:** AGMSMEASP





**Launch Date:** 2022-10-05





**Tags:** Business, Entrepreneurship, MSMEs, Patent Registration, Subsidy



The &amp;quot;Aatmanirbhar Gujarat Scheme for MSMEs&amp;quot; is an umbrella scheme launched by the Industries and Mines Department, Gujarat. It aims to empower the state&amp;#39;s dynamic MSME sector, which significantly contributes to employment, GDP, and exports. Effective from October 5, 2022, to October 4, 2027, this initiative supports growth, investments, and the vision of &amp;quot;Aatmanirbhar Gujarat&amp;quot; and &amp;quot;Aatmanirbhar Bharat.&amp;quot; The component &amp;quot;Assistance for Patent Registration&amp;quot;, provides financial support for individuals and legal entities to obtain patent registration for their developed products or processes. It covers a portion of the costs incurred for patent application, attorney fees, and associated expenses during the scheme&amp;#39;s operative period.




**Who is this for:** 
Business Entity, Industries, Individual; 
Category: Business & Entrepreneurship; 
Sector: Setting up / start-up / entrepreneurship



**Where it applies:** 
Gujarat; 
State/ UT









**Benefits / Assistance:** &gt; **Financial Support for Patent Registration:** - 75% of the cost incurred for patent applications. - Maximum assistance of ₹25,00,000/- per applicant/enterprise. &gt; **Eligible Expenditures:** - Fees for patent attorney, patent service center, and patent registration. - Expenses related to patent equipment development (excluding travel and hotel costs). &gt; **Cap on Attorney Fees:** - Domestic patent attorney fees are capped at ₹50,000/-. - International patent attorney fees are capped at ₹2,00,000/- per country. &gt; **Disbursement of Assistance:** - 50% of assistance disbursed after the publication/notification of the patent. - The remaining 50% will be disbursed after the issuance of the patent certificate.



**Benefit Type:** Cash






















**Contacts & Authorities:** 

Nodal Department: Industries and Mines Department.
