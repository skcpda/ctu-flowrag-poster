
# Marriage Assistance (O.B.O.C.W.W.B)


**Short Title:** MAOBOCWWB









**Tags:** Labour, Building Worker, Construction Worker, Marriage, Women



The “Marriage Assistance” scheme by the Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board, Labour Department, Odisha, is a welfare scheme for the registered workers of the board. Under this scheme, the Board shall provide marriage benefit to the unmarried female member on her marriage or marriage of two unmarried girl children who are of above 18 years of age subject to a minimum of one year as registered beneficiary. &lt;br&gt;




**Who is this for:** 
Individual; 
Category: Women and Child; 
Sector: Marriage



**Where it applies:** 
Odisha; 
State/ UT









**Benefits / Assistance:** - **Quantum of Assistance:** ₹50,000/-. > **Note:** - Assistance shall be given for a maximum of two children. - In case both spouses are registered beneficiaries, the benefit will be extended to one spouse only.



**Benefit Type:** Cash






















**Contacts & Authorities:** 

Nodal Department: Labour and Employees State Insurance Department.
