
# Free Travel in Uttarakhand Transport Corporation Buses to Widowed Daughters-in-Law of Freedom Fighters


**Short Title:** FTUKTCWDFF









**Tags:** Widow, Transport Facilitiy, Free Bus Travel, Freedom Fighter



The &amp;quot;Free Travel in Uttarakhand Transport Corporation Buses to Widowed Daughters-in-Law of Freedom Fighters&amp;quot; scheme is implemented by the Police Department under the Department of Home, Government of Uttarakhand. The scheme aims to provide free travel facilities in Uttarakhand Transport Corporation buses to the widowed daughters-in-law of freedom fighters. 




**Who is this for:** 
Individual; 
Category: Travel & Tourism




**Where it applies:** 
Uttarakhand; 
State/ UT









**Benefits / Assistance:** Under this scheme, widowed daughters-in-law of freedom fighters are entitled to free travel in Uttarakhand Transport Corporation (UTC) buses across the state. 



**Benefit Type:** In Kind






















**Contacts & Authorities:** 
Implementing agency: Police Department, Government Of Uttarakhand..
Nodal Department: Department of Home.
