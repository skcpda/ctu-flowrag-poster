
# Chief Minister Merit Award


**Short Title:** CMMA









**Tags:** Tribal Welfare, Students, Merit Award, Education, Scholarship



The &amp;quot;Chief Minister Merit Award&amp;quot; scheme launched on 7th May, 2012, by the Adi Dravidar and Tribal Welfare Department, Tamil Nadu, aims to support 1000 boys and 1000 girls belonging to the Adi Dravidar, Tribal, and Adi Dravidar converted to Christianity communities. These students are selected based on their marks in the plus 2 Public Examinations. Each selected student is awarded ₹3,000/- annually for five years, provided they continue their higher education. &lt;br&gt; &lt;br&gt;




**Who is this for:** 
Individual; 
Category: Education & Learning; 
Sector: Scholarships and student finance



**Where it applies:** 
Tamil Nadu; 
State/ UT









**Benefits / Assistance:** - **Financial Support:** ₹3,000/- per year for five consecutive years.



**Benefit Type:** Cash
