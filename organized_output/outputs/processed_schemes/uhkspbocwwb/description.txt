
# Urban Health Kiosk Scheme (P.B.O.C.W.W.B)


**Short Title:** UHKSPBOCWWB









**Tags:** Labour, Building Worker, Construction Worker, Silicosis Diseases, Health



The &amp;quot;Urban Health Kiosk Scheme&amp;#39;&amp;#39; by the BOCW Welfare Board, Department of Labour, Punjab, is a welfare scheme for the registered workers of the board. Under this Scheme, primary health facilities are provided to the construction workers &amp;amp; their families in urban health kiosks run by Punjab Health Society in slum areas.




**Who is this for:** 
Individual; 
Category: Health & Wellness; 
Sector: Emergency medical assistance



**Where it applies:** 
Punjab; 
State/ UT









**Benefits / Assistance:** - Health Facilities: Primary Health Facilities are provided to the construction workers &amp; their families in Urban Health Kiosks in slum areas.



**Benefit Type:** In Kind






















**Contacts & Authorities:** 

Nodal Department: Department of Labour.
