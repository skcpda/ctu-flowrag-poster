
# Bharati Scheme for Education Professional Education Course - Chartered Accountancy Final


**Short Title:** BSEPEC-CAF









**Tags:** Financial Assistance, Chartered Accountant, Brahmin, Student



Andhra Pradesh Brahmin Welfare Corporation (ABC) launched the &quot;Bharati Scheme for Education Professional Education Course - Chartered Accountancy Final&quot; for the academic year to encourage Brahmin students of Andhra Pradesh to continue their education. Under this scheme, eligible poor Brahmin student applicants will receive financial assistance for the academic year. This scheme applies to the Brahmin students of Andhra Pradesh covering all 13 districts.




**Who is this for:** 
Individual; 
Category: Education & Learning; 
Sector: Scholarships and student finance



**Where it applies:** 
Andhra Pradesh; 
State/ UT









**Benefits / Assistance:** - **Financial Assistance for the Professional Education Course (Chartered Accountancy Final): ₹20,000/-.**



**Benefit Type:** Cash
