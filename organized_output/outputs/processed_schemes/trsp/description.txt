
# Tiatr Research Scholarship Program


**Short Title:** TRSP









**Tags:** Student, Research, Scholarship, Culture, Education



The scheme &quot;Tiatr Research Scholarship Program&quot; was launched by the Tiatr Academy (TAG) under the Art, Culture and Museums Department, Government of Goa. The scheme aims to encourage young students to undertake research in different aspects of Tiatr, its song, and music. The scheme provides scholarships to college and university students for research related to Tiatr, which must be conducted in English and Konkani (Roman script only). It also includes the publication of the approved research work in book form by TAG, with 100 copies provided to the student concerned in addition to the scholarship amount. The applicant must submit a report of the research carried out duly signed by either the guide or the head of the institution. 




**Who is this for:** 
Individual; 
Category: Education & Learning; 
Sector: Scholarships and student finance



**Where it applies:** 
Goa; 
State/ UT









**Benefits / Assistance:** > ***Scholarship Amount*** The amount of each scholarship will be decided by Tiatr Academy of Goa (TAG) from time to time. > ***Publication of Research Work*** The research work of the students will be printed by Tiatr Academy of Goa (TAG) in book form after approval by the Recommendation Board. > ***Copies of Published Work*** Each student concerned will receive 100 copies of their printed research work in addition to the scholarship amount. > ***Number of Scholarships*** TAG will offer scholarships in three categories every year: ◦ PhD: 1 scholarship ◦ Post-Graduation: 3 scholarships ◦ Graduation: 6 scholarships _*Scholarships will be for every academic year._ _*A scholarship will be given only once in a particular category; however, the same applicant is free to apply for other categories in subsequent academic years._ 



**Benefit Type:** In Kind
