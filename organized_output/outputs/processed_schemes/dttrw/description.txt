
# Documentation of Tiatr through Research Work


**Short Title:** DTTRW









**Tags:** Research, Documentation, Book, Writer, Academia, Music



The scheme &quot;Documentation of Tiatr through Research Work&quot; was launched by the Tiatr Academy under the Art, Culture and Museums Department, Government of Goa. The scheme aims to encourage research work in the field of Tiatr and facilitate writers to take up research work in different aspects of Tiatr, its Song &amp; Music. The scheme provides publication of research work in book form with a minimum of 500 copies printed and remuneration as fixed by TAG as per the researcher&#39;s experience. The applicant must be a researcher interested in writing books on tiatr after doing thorough research and must submit three typed copies of the manuscript for approval. The scheme is implemented by the Tiatr Academy of Goa (TAG). The applications for this scheme are accepted offline. 




**Who is this for:** 
Individual; 
Category: Sports & Culture; 
Sector: Culture



**Where it applies:** 
Goa; 
State/ UT









**Benefits / Assistance:** > ***Publication Benefits*** - TAG will publish the research work in book form after thorough research on the subject. - The publication may be in English or Konkani (Roman Script). - TAG will print a minimum of 500 copies of the book. - 100 copies will be given to the writer. - The remaining copies will be used by TAG for various purposes. > ***Financial Benefits*** The author/researcher will be given remuneration as fixed by TAG as per his/her experience for the research work undertaken. > ***Editorial Support*** - The manuscript approved by the Recommendation Board shall be edited by a member of Editorial Board. - Editing will include checking for spelling and orthography. ***Conditions*** _*The manuscript received from the author/researcher will be considered for publication only after getting approval from the Recommendation Board appointed by TAG._ _*The author/researcher will have to provide TAG, 3 (three) typed copies of the manuscript._ _*If approved, the author/researcher will have to submit a soft copy of the manuscript on a Compact Disc/Digital Versatile Disc/pen drive._ 



**Benefit Type:** Cash
