
# Dr.Babasaheb Ambedkar Udyog Uday Yojana: Assistance for Interest Subsidy (Manufacturing Sector)


**Short Title:** BAUUYISMS





**Launch Date:** 2020-08-07





**Tags:** Business, Entrepreneurship, MSMEs, Start Up, Scheduled Caste, Scheduled Tribe



The scheme &amp;quot;Dr.Babasaheb Ambedkar Udyog Uday Yojana&amp;quot; is an umbrella scheme launched by the Industries and Mines Department, Gujarat. It aims to empower the state&amp;#39;s dynamic MSME sector, which significantly contributes to employment, GDP, and exports. Effective from August 7th, 2020 to August 6th, 2025, this initiative supports SC/ST entrepreneurs and strengthens MSMEs, making them globally competitive. The component &amp;quot;Assistance for Interest Subsidy (Manufacturing Sector)&amp;quot; provides financial relief to enterprises in the manufacturing sector by offering an interest subsidy on term loans. Additional benefits are available for women entrepreneurs, differently-abled entrepreneurs, startups, and young entrepreneurs below 35 years of age.




**Who is this for:** 
Business Entity, Industries; 
Category: Business & Entrepreneurship; 
Sector: Setting up / start-up / entrepreneurship



**Where it applies:** 
Gujarat; 
State/ UT









**Benefits / Assistance:** > **Financial Assistance:** **Category of Taluka** **Quantum of Incentive** [Category I](https://indextb.com/files/2020/11/a34fc914-6cdd-4fd6-8773-502017da4334_taluka%20category.pdf)8% on Term Loan with a maximum amount of ₹35,00,000/- per annum for a period of 7 years[Category II](https://indextb.com/files/2020/11/a34fc914-6cdd-4fd6-8773-502017da4334_taluka%20category.pdf)7% on Term Loan with a maximum amount of ₹30,00,000/- per annum for a period of 6 years [Category III](https://indextb.com/files/2020/11/a34fc914-6cdd-4fd6-8773-502017da4334_taluka%20category.pdf) &amp;amp;amp;amp; Municipal Corporation Areas6% on Term Loan with a maximum amount of ₹25,00,000/- per annum for a period of 5 years



**Benefit Type:** Cash







**Exclusions / Not eligible:** - Enterprises whose term loan is sanctioned after one year from the commencement of commercial production. - Enterprises defaulting on loan repayments as per RBI and bank guidelines. - Enterprises seeking reimbursement for penal interest or other bank charges. - Enterprises availing interest subsidy under any Central Government scheme where the total subsidy exceeds the total interest paid.
