
# Livelihood Opportunity Promotion Scheme (Disabled)


**Short Title:** LOPSD









**Tags:** Financial Assistance, Person With Disability, Self-employment, Loan, Margin Money, Shop



The &quot;Livelihood Opportunity Promotion Scheme (Disabled)&quot; scheme was launched by the Social Welfare Department, Government of Uttarakhand. It aims to empower persons with disabilities by providing financial assistance for sustainable self-employment and livelihood generation. Under this scheme, the beneficiaries are supported with a mix of grants, subsidized loans, and margin money for term loans, shop construction, and bank-linked enterprises.




**Who is this for:** 
Individual; 
Category: Banking,Financial Services and Insurance; 
Sector: Banking and money



**Where it applies:** 
Uttarakhand; 
State/ UT









**Benefits / Assistance:** **Term Loan Scheme:** - Project Cost: ₹75,000/- - Grant: 20% or ₹10,000/-, whichever is less - Term Loan: ₹65,000/- at 6% annual interest rate **Shop Construction Scheme:** - Project Cost: ₹50,000/- - Grant: 20% or ₹10,000/-, whichever is less - Margin Money Loan: ₹40,000/- at 4% annual interest rate, repayable in 60 monthly installments **Margin Money Loan Scheme for Bank Supported Loan:** - Project Cost: Up to ₹2,00,000/- - Grant: 20%, maximum of ₹10,000/- - Margin Money: 25% of the project cost at 4% annual interest rate, repayable in 36 monthly installments



**Benefit Type:** Cash






















**Contacts & Authorities:** 

Nodal Department: Department Of Social Welfare.
