
# Assistance for Area Expansion of Vegetable Crops through Hybrid Seed


**Short Title:** AAEVCTHS









**Tags:** Vegetable Farming, Hybrid Seed, Natural Farming, Area Expansion



The scheme &amp;quot;Assistance for Area Expansion of Vegetable Crops through Hybrid Seed&amp;quot; by the Agriculture and Co-operation Department, Government of Gujarat, aims to support vegetable farmers in enhancing their cultivation areas using hybrid seeds and to promote natural farming practices. The financial assistance provided under the scheme helps reduce input costs and encourages environmentally sustainable agricultural methods among the farming community. 




**Who is this for:** 
Individual; 
Category: Agriculture,Rural & Environment; 
Sector: Agricultural Inputs- seeds, fertilizer etc.



**Where it applies:** 
Gujarat; 
State/ UT









**Benefits / Assistance:** - **Subsidy Assistance for Area Expansion Using Hybrid Seeds:** 40% of unit cost or up to ₹20,000/- per hectare, whichever is less.- **Promotion of Natural Farming in Vegetable Crops:** Maximum Assistance: ₹20,000/- per hectare. 



**Benefit Type:** Cash






















**Contacts & Authorities:** 

Nodal Department: Agriculture, Farmers Welfare and Cooperation Department.
