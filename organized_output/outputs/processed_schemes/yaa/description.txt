
# Young Achievers Awards


**Short Title:** YAA









**Tags:** Artist, Youth, Award, Culture, Achievement



The scheme &quot;Young Achievers Awards&quot; was launched by the Tiatr Academy under the Art, Culture and Museums Department, Government of Goa. The scheme aims to recognize young talented artistes who have contributed and continue to work for the development of tiatr, its song and music, and have enriched Konkani Tiatr and Song through All India Radio, Doordarshan and other platforms at national and international levels. The scheme provides recognition through awards consisting of a memento, citation and cash burse, given to one male and one female artiste annually. The scheme also provides a special honour function held on 16th November on the occasion of the Birth Anniversary of Regina Fernandes, the First Lady of the Tiatr Stage. 




**Who is this for:** 
Individual; 
Category: Sports & Culture; 
Sector: Culture



**Where it applies:** 
Goa; 
State/ UT









**Benefits / Assistance:** **Award Components:** The Young Achievers Award consists of a memento, a Citation and a Cash Burse as fixed by Tiatr Academy of Goa. **Recognition Ceremony:** Selected artistes are honoured at a special function on 16th November on the occasion of the Birth Anniversary of Regina Fernandes, the First Lady of the Tiatr Stage. **Annual Awards:** The award is given to a male and a female artiste every year. _*The selected artistes must be compulsorily present in person to receive the award._ 



**Benefit Type:** Composite
