
# "Protected Cultivation" Component of "Establishment of New Gardens (Area Expansion)" Scheme


**Short Title:** PCCENGAES









**Tags:** Cultivation, Farmer, Subsidy, Garden, Horticulture



&quot;Protected Cultivation&quot; component of the &quot;Establishment of New Gardens (Area Expansion)&quot; scheme under the &quot;National Horticulture Mission (NHM)&quot;, a Centrally Sponsored Scheme by the Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers Welfare, Govt. of India. In Puducherry, this scheme is implemented by the Department of Agriculture and Farmer&#39;s Welfare, Union Territory of Puducherry. Through this scheme, the farmers are provided Poly House, Shade Net House (Tubular Structure), and Plastic Mulching at subsidised rates for practising Protected Cultivation.




**Who is this for:** 
Individual; 
Category: Agriculture,Rural & Environment; 
Sector: Agricultural Inputs- seeds, fertilizer etc.



**Where it applies:** 
Puducherry; 
State/ UT









**Benefits / Assistance:** - **Poly House:** Naturally Ventilated system (Tubular structure) – Subsidized Cost of ₹467.50/- per square meter.- **Shade Net House (Tubular Structure):** Subsidized Cost of ₹355/- per square meter. Maximum of 4000 square meters per beneficiary.- **Plastic Mulching:** ₹10,000/- per hectare as a subsidy (maximum of 2 hectares per beneficiary).



**Benefit Type:** Cash






















**Contacts & Authorities:** 

Nodal Department: Agriculture Department, Puducherry.
