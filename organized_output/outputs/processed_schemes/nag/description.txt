
# Nalanda Award


**Short Title:** NAG









**Tags:** Social Welfare, Nalanda Award



The &amp;quot;Nalanda Award&amp;quot; implemented by the Social Justice and Empowerment Department, Gujarat, aims to honor organizations that have significantly contributed to the upliftment of socially and educationally backward communities. This state-level recognition encourages continued efforts in the field of social welfare and highlights best practices that can be replicated across the state.




**Who is this for:** 
NGO, Government Organisation; 
Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; 
Sector: Financial assistance



**Where it applies:** 
Gujarat; 
State/ UT









**Benefits / Assistance:** - Financial assistance to the selected organization.



**Benefit Type:** In Kind






















**Contacts & Authorities:** 

Nodal Department: Social Justice and Empowerment Department.
