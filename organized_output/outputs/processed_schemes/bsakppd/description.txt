
# Bihar Shatabdi Asangathit Karyakshetra Kamagaar Evan Shilpakar Samajik Suraksha Yojana: Permanent Partial Disability


**Short Title:** BSAKPPD





**Launch Date:** 2011-04-01





**Tags:** Social Security, Unorganised Worker, Craftsmen, Permanent Partial Disability



The “Bihar Shatabdi Asangathit Karyakshetra Kamagaar Evan Shilpakar Samajik Suraksha Yojana” launched by the Labour Resources Department, Bihar, aims to provide social security through various types of financial assistance to workers and artisans in the unorganized sector.




**Who is this for:** 
Individual; 
Category: Health & Wellness; 
Sector: Crisis/Disaster/Accident



**Where it applies:** 
Bihar; 
State/ UT









**Benefits / Assistance:** - **Financial Assistance:** ₹37,500/-.



**Benefit Type:** Cash






















**Contacts & Authorities:** 

Nodal Department: Labour Resources Department.
