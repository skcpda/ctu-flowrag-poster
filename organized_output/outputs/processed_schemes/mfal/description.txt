
# Modular Functional Artificial Limbs


**Short Title:** MFAL









**Tags:** Orthopedically, Artificial Limbs, Differently-abled Person, Student, Self-employed, Employed



The &#39;Modular Functional Artificial Limbs&#39; scheme was launched by the Department for the Welfare of Differently Abled Persons, Government of Tamil Nadu. Under this scheme, modular functional artificial limbs are provided for orthopedically differently-abled students, employed or self-employed. The applications are accepted offline in the prescribed form available at the District Differently Abled Welfare Office.




**Who is this for:** 
Individual; 
Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; 
Sector: Aids/Appliances



**Where it applies:** 
Tamil Nadu; 
State/ UT









**Benefits / Assistance:** - **Free distribution of modular functional artificial limbs.**



**Benefit Type:** In Kind
