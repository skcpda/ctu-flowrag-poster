
# Shilpi Samridhi Yojana


**Short Title:** SSYH









**Tags:** Scheduled Caste, Loan, Subsidy



&amp;quot;Shilpi Samridhi Yojana&amp;quot; is an economic empowerment scheme by the Haryana Scheduled Castes Finance and Development Corporation, Government of Haryana. Through this scheme, loans/benefits are provided to the eligible families of the scheduled caste artisans to pursue various economic development activities for the betterment of their economic status. In order to be eligible, the artisan should be in the age group of 18 to 45 years. He/She should not be a defaulter of the Corporation/bank.




**Who is this for:** 
Individual; 
Category: Business & Entrepreneurship; 
Sector: Loan



**Where it applies:** 
Haryana; 
State/ UT









**Benefits / Assistance:** - Loan for various economic development schemes having unit cost up to ₹50,000/-. - Margin Money at 10% of the project cost. - Subsidy at 50% of the total project cost, subject to a maximum of ₹10,000/-.



**Benefit Type:** Cash






















**Contacts & Authorities:** 

Nodal Department: Welfare of Scheduled Caste & Backward Classes Department.
