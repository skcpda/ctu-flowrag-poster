
# Scheme to Provide Grant to the Institutions for Long Term Project in the Field of Art and Culture (Sarjansheel Goa)


**Short Title:** SPILTPFACSG









**Tags:** Grant, Project, Culture



&quot;Scheme to Provide Grant to the Institutions for Long Term Project in the Field of Art and Culture (Sarjansheel Goa)&quot; implemented by the by the Directorate of Art and Culture, Government of Goa, provides financial support to the institutions/ individuals involved in long-term projects in the field of Art &amp; Culture.  > **Objectives** 1. To create and develop a cultural environment in the State of Goa. 1. To provide financial support to the institutions/ individuals involved in long-term projects in the field of Art and culture. 1. To preserve and promote the cultural heritage of Goa.




**Who is this for:** 
Individual, University / Institution; 
Category: Sports & Culture; 
Sector: Culture



**Where it applies:** 
Goa; 
State









**Benefits / Assistance:** A grant to the extent of 80% of the total expenditure involved is subject to a maximum ceiling of ₹ 5,00,000 per annum.  > **Number of Beneficiaries** The Department/Government may restrict the number of institutions/individuals according to the availability of funds.



**Benefit Type:** Cash






















**Contacts & Authorities:** 

Nodal Department: Art, Culture and Museums Department.
