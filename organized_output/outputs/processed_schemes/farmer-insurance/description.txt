
# ₹ 5 Lakh Insurance Cover To Farmers


**Short Title:** LICF





**Launch Date:** 2018-08-15





**Tags:** LIC, Insurance Coverage, Financial, Security



Launched on 15 August 2018, the scheme &quot;₹5 Lakh Insurance Cover for Farmers&quot; is an Insurance Scheme by the Department of Agriculture, Government of Telangana. This scheme provides ₹5 lakh insurance coverage to all farmers aged between 18 and 60 years, ensuring financial security for their families in case of the farmer&#39;s death, irrespective of the cause. The nominee receives the compensation within 10 days of the farmer&#39;s death. The state government pays an annual premium of nearly ₹500 crore on behalf of around 50 lakh farmers to the Life Insurance Corporation of India (LIC), and farmers do not need to pay a single rupee. The scheme is being implemented by the Life Insurance Corporation of India (LIC).




**Who is this for:** 
Individual; 
Category: Agriculture,Rural & Environment; 
Sector: Financial assistance



**Where it applies:** 
Telangana; 
State









**Benefits / Assistance:** 1. **Financial Security:** Provides ₹5 lakh insurance coverage to farmers&#39; families in case of the farmer&#39;s death, offering substantial financial assistance. 1. **Inclusive Coverage:** Covers death due to any cause, including natural causes and accidents. 1. **Quick Disbursement:** Ensures compensation is paid to the nominee within 10 days of the farmer&#39;s death. 1. **Zero Cost to Farmers:** Farmers are not required to pay any premium, as the state government bears the entire cost on their behalf. 1. **Wide Beneficiary Base:** Approximately 50 lakh farmers benefit from this scheme annually.



**Benefit Type:** Cash
