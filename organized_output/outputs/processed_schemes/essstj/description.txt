
# AICTE e-Shodh Sindhu Scheme (eSS)


**Short Title:** ESSSTJ









**Tags:** Subscriptions Of E-Resources, Web Of Science, Scopus, ASME, ASCE, Bentham Science



Launched by the All India Council for Technical Education, AICTE e-Shodh Sindhu Scheme (eSS) provides subscriptions of e-Resources like Web of Science, Scopus, The American Society of Mechanical Engineers (ASME), American Society of Civil Engineers (ASCE) and Bentham Science to institutions. **Objective:** Facilitating access to e-Resource to the students, faculty and researchers, a new to augment the culture of research in the institution and overall quality of technical education.




**Who is this for:** 
University / Institution; 
Category: Education & Learning; 
Sector: Resources for schools



**Where it applies:** 

Central









**Benefits / Assistance:** **Benefits:** Subscriptions of e-Resources like Web of Science, Scopus, ASME, ASCE and Bentham Science to institutions.  **Duration of Subscriptions:** Initially for one calendar year, renewable for further year on the basis of usage by institute and fulfilments of other essential eligibility criteria.



**Benefit Type:** In Kind






















**Contacts & Authorities:** 

Nodal Department: Department Of Higher Education.
