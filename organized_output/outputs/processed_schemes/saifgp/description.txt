
# Scheme to Assist for Infrastructural Facilities for Gaushalas & Panjrapole


**Short Title:** SAIFGP









**Tags:** Agriculture, Animal Husbandry, Animal Welfare, Infrastructural Facilities



The “Scheme to Assist for Infrastructural Facilities for Gaushalas &amp;amp; Panjrapole” launched by the Department of Agriculture, Farmers Welfare &amp;amp; Co-operation, Government of Gujarat, aims to provide financial aid to registered gaushalas and panjrapoles for building essential infrastructure like cattle sheds, water facilities, and storage areas, ensuring better care for non-productive livestock.




**Who is this for:** 
Registered Societies; 
Category: Agriculture,Rural & Environment; 
Sector: Animal husbandry



**Where it applies:** 
Gujarat; 
State/ UT









**Benefits / Assistance:** - Financial assistance of up to 75% of the total cost or ₹4,00,000/-, whichever is lower, for each of the following: 1. Cattle Shed. 1. Compound Wall. 1. Grass Godown. 1. Water Bore (Electric Motor / Submersible Pump). 1. Overhead or Underground Water Tank. > **Note:** The benefit of this scheme will be available to an organization for a period of five years for different purpose-components.



**Benefit Type:** Cash






















**Contacts & Authorities:** 

Nodal Department: Agriculture, Farmers Welfare and Cooperation Department.
