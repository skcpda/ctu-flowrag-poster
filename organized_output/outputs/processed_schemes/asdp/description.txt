
# Accessibility Services to Disabled People


**Short Title:** ASDP









**Tags:** Disabled Person, Divyang, Employment Assistance, Visually Impaired, Low Vision, Job Facilitation



The scheme &amp;quot;Accessibility Services to Disabled People&amp;quot; is a central sector scheme that aims to support visually impaired and low vision individuals by registering them and facilitating suitable employment opportunities. Through this scheme, beneficiaries are helped in securing jobs in both the public and private sectors across various vacancies. The scheme is implemented by the National Institute for the Empowerment of Persons with Visual Disabilities, Dehradun. &lt;br&gt; 




**Who is this for:** 
Individual; 
Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; 
Sector: Citizen empowerment



**Where it applies:** 
Uttarakhand; 
State/ UT









**Benefits / Assistance:** - **Employment Registration:** Registers unemployed individuals who are visually impaired and have low vision. - **Job Placement Assistance:** Provides suitable employment opportunities in the public sector and private agencies.



**Benefit Type:** Cash






















**Contacts & Authorities:** 
Implementing agency: National Institute For The Empowerment Of Persons With Visual Disabilities, Dehradun..
Nodal Department: Department Of Social Welfare.
