
# Grant of Stipend to the Differently Abled Trainees of the Vocational Training Institutes


**Short Title:** GSDATVTI









**Tags:** Stipend, Trainee, Disability, Training, ITI



The scheme “Grant of Stipend to the Differently Abled Trainees of the Vocational Training Institutes&quot; was launched by the Department of Social Welfare, Government of Puducherry. Through this scheme, the differently abled persons undergoing any technical or professional training through Institutes are paid a fixed monthly stipend which will enable them to secure the training successfully in order to make their own earnings and become useful members of the society.




**Who is this for:** 
Individual; 
Category: Education & Learning; 
Sector: Apprenticeships and training



**Where it applies:** 
Puducherry; 
State/ UT









**Benefits / Assistance:** ₹1,000/- per month.



**Benefit Type:** Cash






















**Contacts & Authorities:** 

Nodal Department: Social Welfare Department, Puducherry.
