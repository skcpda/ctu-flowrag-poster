
# Pragati Scholarship (For 81 Girl Students Of Uttarakhand Origin (Diploma) And 50 for Graduation Level)


**Short Title:** PSGSDGL









**Tags:** Scholarship, Girls Education, Diploma, Higher Education Support



The scheme “Pragati Scholarship” aims to encourage higher education among girl students by offering financial assistance. It supports meritorious girl students of Uttarakhand pursuing diploma and graduate-level studies, providing ₹50,000/- per year to each eligible beneficiary. 




**Who is this for:** 
Individual; 
Category: Education & Learning; 
Sector: Scholarships and student finance



**Where it applies:** 
Uttarakhand; 
State/ UT









**Benefits / Assistance:** - **Financial Assistance:** ₹50,000/- per year. 



**Benefit Type:** Cash






















**Contacts & Authorities:** 

Nodal Department: Directorate of Technical Education .
