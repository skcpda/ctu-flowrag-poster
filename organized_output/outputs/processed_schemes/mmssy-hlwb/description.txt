
# Mukhya Mantri Sharmik Surksha Yojana- Haryana Labour Welfare Board


**Short Title:** MMSSY-HLWB









**Tags:** Financial Assistance, Worker, Death, Labour



The scheme “Mukhya Mantri Sharmik Surksha Yojana” is implemented by the Haryana Labour Welfare Board (HLWB), Labour Department, Government of Haryana. Under this scheme, financial assistance of ₹5,00,000/- will be provided to the widows/dependents of workers employed in industrial and commercial institutions in Haryana, in the event of the worker&amp;#39;s death while working at the workplace. The assistance is provided in the event of the death of both unregistered and registered workers with the Haryana Labour Welfare Board.




**Who is this for:** 
Family, Individual; 
Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; 
Sector: Crisis/Disaster/Accident



**Where it applies:** 
Haryana; 
State/ UT









**Benefits / Assistance:** 1. Under this scheme, financial assistance of ₹5,00,000/- will be provided to the widows/dependents of workers in the event of the worker&amp;#39;s death while working at the workplace.



**Benefit Type:** Cash
