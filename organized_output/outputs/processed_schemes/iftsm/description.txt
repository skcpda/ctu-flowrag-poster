
# Incentive For The Simple Marriage


**Short Title:** Iftsm









**Tags:** Marriage, Schedule Caste, Incentive, Social Welfare



The Simple Marriage scheme is a government initiative by the Social Welfare Department of Karnataka that offers incentives to couples belonging to the SC community who opt for a simple marriage ceremony. The scheme aims to promote the concept of simple marriages and provide financial assistance to eligible couples.  Under this scheme, the Social Welfare Department of Karnataka will provide incentives to Simple Married Couples belonging to the SC Community who have submitted their applications within 1 year of marriage. Only couples who got married on or after 11-08-2015 are eligible for the incentive, which is set at Rs. 50,000.00.  To be eligible for the scheme, the applicant must be a resident of Karnataka state, belong to the SC community, and have a family income from all sources that does not exceed Rs. 2 lakhs per annum. It is important to note that the scheme is applicable within 1 year of marriage

**Benefits / Assistance:** Financial Incentives: The scheme provides a financial incentive of Rs. 50,000.00 to eligible Simple Married Couples belonging to the SC community. Promotes Simple Marriages: The scheme aims to promote the concept of simple marriages, which can help reduce the financial burden on families and promote equality in society. Eligibility Criteria: The eligibility criteria for the scheme are straightforward, making it easier for eligible couples to apply for and receive the incentive. Encourages Timely Application: The incentive is provided only to those who have submitted applications within 1 year of their marriage, which encourages timely application and provides immediate financial assistance. Documentation Support: The scheme provides a clear list of required documents for application, which helps simplify the application process and provides transparency.



**Benefit Type:** Cash







**Exclusions / Not eligible:** Non-SC Community: The scheme is only applicable to Simple Married Couples belonging to the SC community. Couples belonging to other communities are not eligible for the incentive. Income Criteria: The scheme is applicable only to couples whose family income from all sources does not exceed Rs. 2 lakhs per annum. Couples with higher incomes are not eligible for the scheme. Time Limit: The scheme is applicable only within 1 year of the marriage. Couples who apply after this time limit are not eligible for the incentive. Incomplete Documentation: Couples who fail to provide all the required documents for the application are not eligible for the incentive. Marriage Date: Only couples who got married on or after 11-08-2015 are eligible for the incentive. Couples who got married before this date are not eligible for the scheme.
