
# District Central Cooperative Banks and through its Branches: For Maternity Loan through Self Help Groups


**Short Title:** DCCBFML









**Tags:** Maternity Loan, Self Help Groups, Women Empowerment, Health And Wellness, Pregnancy



The &amp;quot;District Central Cooperative Banks and through its Branches: For Maternity Loan through Self Help Groups&amp;quot; scheme launched by the Co-operation, Food and Consumer Protection Department, Tamil Nadu, offers financial support through Self Help Groups to pregnant women for maternity-related expenses. The loan is capped at ₹2,000/- with an interest rate of 11%, subject to changes. &lt;br&gt;




**Who is this for:** 
Individual; 
Category: Banking,Financial Services and Insurance; 
Sector: Loan



**Where it applies:** 
Tamil Nadu; 
State/ UT









**Benefits / Assistance:** - **Access to Loan:** ₹2,000/-. - **Interest Rate:** 11% (subject to change). &lt;br&gt;



**Benefit Type:** Cash






















**Contacts & Authorities:** 

Nodal Department: Co-operation, Food and Consumer Protection Department.
