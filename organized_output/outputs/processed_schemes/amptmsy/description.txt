
# Alpsankhyak Muslim Parityaqta/Talaqshuda Mahila Sahayata Yojana


**Short Title:** AMPTMSY





**Launch Date:** 2006-04-01





**Tags:** Minority, Muslim Mahila, Abandoned Woman, Divorced Woman, Financial Assistance



The scheme &amp;quot;Alpsankhyak Muslim Parityakta/Talaqshuda Mahila Sahayata Yojana&amp;quot; has been operated by the Minority Welfare Department, Government of Bihar, since the financial year 2006-07. The objective of this scheme is to strengthen the economic condition of minority Muslim abandoned/divorced women and make them self-dependent by providing financial assistance.




**Who is this for:** 
Individual; 
Category: Women and Child; 
Sector: Financial assistance



**Where it applies:** 
Bihar; 
State/ UT



**DBT Scheme:** Yes (Direct Benefit Transfer)







**Benefits / Assistance:** - Under this scheme, the state government provides financial assistance of ₹25,000/- to minority Muslim abandoned/divorced women to strengthen their economic condition and make them self-reliant. **Note:** The grant amount will be directly transferred to the beneficiary’s bank accounts through RTGS/DBT.



**Benefit Type:** Cash






















**Contacts & Authorities:** 
Implementing agency: Directorate Of Minority Welfare, Government Of Bihar.
Nodal Department: Minority Welfare Department.
