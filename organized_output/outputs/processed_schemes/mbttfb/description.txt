
# Maternity Benefit to the Female Beneficiary


**Short Title:** MBTTFB









**Tags:** Financial Assistance, Women, Construction Worker, Pregnant



The Manipur Building and Other Construction Workers&#39; Welfare Board (MBOCWWB) launched welfare scheme &quot;Maternity Benefit to the Female Beneficiary&quot;. The scheme provides financial assistance in cases of miscarriage or medical termination of pregnancy, up to two times. This benefit is aimed at ensuring health and financial support for female workers during such critical situations.




**Who is this for:** 
Individual; 
Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; 
Sector: Financial assistance



**Where it applies:** 
Manipur; 
State/ UT









**Benefits / Assistance:** - **Financial assistance of ₹6,000/- for miscarriage or medical termination.**



**Benefit Type:** Cash






















**Contacts & Authorities:** 

Nodal Department: Labour and Employment Department.
