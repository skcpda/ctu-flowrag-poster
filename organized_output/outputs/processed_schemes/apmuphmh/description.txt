
# Assistance in Packing Materials under Post Harvest Management of Horticultural Produce


**Short Title:** APMUPHMH









**Tags:** Horticulture, Post-harvest Management, Packing Material Subsidy, Farmer



The scheme &amp;quot;Assistance in Packing Materials under Post Harvest Management of Horticultural Produce&amp;quot; introduced by the Agriculture, Farmers Welfare and Cooperation Department, Gujarat, aims to support farmers by providing financial aid for packing materials used in post-harvest stages to maintain quality and reduce losses.




**Who is this for:** 
Individual; 
Category: Agriculture,Rural & Environment; 
Sector: Financial assistance



**Where it applies:** 
Gujarat; 
State/ UT









**Benefits / Assistance:** 1. **Unit Cost:** ₹10,000/ha. 1. 75% of the cost or ₹7,500/hectare, whichever is less. &lt;br&gt; &gt; **Note:** 1. Corrugated boxes, wooden boxes will be used as packing material. 1. Assistance will be available up to a maximum limit of 1 hectare per account. 1. Available on i-Khedut portal every year (once during the year),



**Benefit Type:** Cash






















**Contacts & Authorities:** 

Nodal Department: Agriculture, Farmers Welfare and Cooperation Department.
