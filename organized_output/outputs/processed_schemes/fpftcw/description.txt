
# Family Pension Scheme (UKBOCWWB)


**Short Title:** FPSUKBOCWWB









**Tags:** Labour, Family Pension, Construction Worker, Financial Assistance



The scheme “Family Pension Scheme” is implemented by the Uttarakhand Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board (UKBOCWWB) under the Labour Department, Government of Uttarakhand. The scheme aims to provide financial assistance to the spouse of a registered construction worker in the event of the worker’s death, if the deceased was a pensioner. 




**Who is this for:** 
Family; 
Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; 
Sector: Pension



**Where it applies:** 
Uttarakhand; 
State/ UT









**Benefits / Assistance:** - **Pension Amount:** ₹500/- per month or 50% of the pensioner&amp;#39;s pension, whichever is higher. 



**Benefit Type:** Cash






















**Contacts & Authorities:** 
Implementing agency: Uttarakhand Building And Other Construction Workers Welfare Board.
Nodal Department: Department Of Labour.
