
# Registered Construction Worker in case of his/her Natural Death (JKBOCWWB)


**Short Title:** RCWICOHNDJKBOCWWB









**Tags:** Worker, Construction, Labour, Financial Assistance, Death



Launched on 1st January 2018, the scheme &quot;Registered Construction Worker in Case of His/Her Natural Death&quot; is a Welfare Scheme by the Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board, Labour Department, Jammu &amp; Kashmir. Through this scheme, financial assistance of ₹2,00,000/- is provided to the nominee or dependents in the event of natural death of a registered BOC worker. The applications are accepted online as well as offline.




**Who is this for:** 
Individual; 
Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; 
Sector: Financial assistance



**Where it applies:** 
Jammu and Kashmir; 
State/ UT









**Benefits / Assistance:** ₹2,00,000/- is provided to the nominee/dependent of the deceased construction worker.



**Benefit Type:** Cash






















**Contacts & Authorities:** 

Nodal Department: Department of Labour and Employment.
