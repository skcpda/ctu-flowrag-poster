
# Scheme for Assistance to Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises (MSME): Assistance for Interest Subsidy (Service Sector)


**Short Title:** SAMSMEISSS





**Launch Date:** 2020-08-07





**Tags:** Business, Entrepreneurship, MSMEs, Start Up, Interest Subsidy



The &amp;quot;Scheme for Assistance to Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises (MSME)&amp;quot; is an umbrella scheme launched by the Industries and Mines Department, Gujarat. It aims to empower the state&amp;#39;s dynamic MSME sector, which significantly contributes to employment, GDP, and exports. Effective from August 7th, 2020 to August 6th, 2025, this initiative supports entrepreneurs and strengthens MSMEs, making them globally competitive. The component &amp;quot;Assistance for Interest Subsidy (Service Sector)&amp;quot; provides interest subsidies on term loans for new MSMEs in the service sector. The subsidy applies to new machinery, equipment, and furniture/fixtures (excluding land and buildings).




**Who is this for:** 
Business Entity, Industries; 
Category: Business & Entrepreneurship; 
Sector: Setting up / start-up / entrepreneurship



**Where it applies:** 
Gujarat; 
State/ UT









**Benefits / Assistance:** **Financial Assistance:** **Category of Taluka** **Quantum of Incentive** [Category I](https://imd.gujarat.gov.in/Document/I%20Branch_2297_02_Nov_2020_98.pdf)8% on Term Loan with a maximum amount of ₹35,00,000/- per annum for a period of 7 years[Category II](https://imd.gujarat.gov.in/Document/I%20Branch_2297_02_Nov_2020_98.pdf)7% on Term Loan with a maximum amount of ₹30,00,000/- per annum for a period of 6 years [Category III ](https://imd.gujarat.gov.in/Document/I%20Branch_2297_02_Nov_2020_98.pdf)&amp;amp;amp;amp; Municipal Corporation Areas6% on Term Loan with a maximum amount of ₹25,00,000/- per annum for a period of 5 years **Additional Subsidy:** 1% extra interest subsidy for registered startups in the service sector with Udyam/MSME Registration.



**Benefit Type:** Cash







**Exclusions / Not eligible:** - Loan sanctioned by Non-Banking Financial Institutes will not be eligible for incentives under this resolution.
