
# Mukhyamantri Alpsankhyak Chhatravas Yojana


**Short Title:** MMACY









**Tags:** Stipend, Student, Minority Community, Hostel



The &amp;quot;Mukhyamantri Alpsankhyak Chhatravas Yojana&amp;quot; is a scheme initiated by the Minority Welfare Department, Government of Bihar. Under this scheme, students belonging to minority communities who are studying in a recognized institution, school, college, university, or technical institution in their respective districts and residing in minority hostels are provided a monthly stipend of ₹1,000/-. Additionally, these students receive 9 kg of rice and 6 kg of wheat every month, while residing in hostels run by the Minority Welfare Department, Government of Bihar.




**Who is this for:** 
Individual; 
Category: Education & Learning; 
Sector: Scholarships and student finance



**Where it applies:** 
Bihar; 
State/ UT









**Benefits / Assistance:** Under this scheme, students from minority communities who are studying and residing in hostels run by the Minority Welfare Department, Government of Bihar, will receive the following benefits: - A monthly stipend of ₹1,000/-. - Provision of essential facilities such as cots, mattresses, bed sheets, tables, chairs for studying, as well as cooking utensils and kitchen facilities. - Monthly distribution of 9 kg of rice and 6 kg of wheat to each student.



**Benefit Type:** Composite






















**Contacts & Authorities:** 
Implementing agency: Minority Welfare Department, Government Of Bihar.
Nodal Department: Minority Welfare Department.
