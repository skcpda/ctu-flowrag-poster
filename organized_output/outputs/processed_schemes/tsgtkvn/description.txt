
# Vehicle Loan Scheme (GTKVN)


**Short Title:** VLSGTKVN









**Tags:** Loan, Self Employment, Auto Rickshaw, Vehicle, Transport



The &amp;quot;Vehicle Loan Scheme&amp;quot; is implemented by the Gujarat Thakor and Koli Vikas Nigam (GTKVN) under the Social Justice and Empowerment Department, Government of Gujarat. The scheme aims to promote self-employment among youth from the Thakor and Koli communities by providing financial assistance for the purchase of commercial vehicles. Under this scheme, beneficiaries can avail of a subsidized loan to purchase auto-rickshaws, loading rickshaws, or a Maruti Eeco van to start an income-generating transport business. 




**Who is this for:** 
Individual; 
Category: Transport & Infrastructure; 
Sector: Loan



**Where it applies:** 
Gujarat; 
State/ UT









**Benefits / Assistance:** Under this scheme, beneficiaries can avail of a loan of up to ₹4,00,000/- for the purchase of commercial vehicles such as auto-rickshaws, loading rickshaws, Maruti Eeco, etc. 



**Benefit Type:** Cash






















**Contacts & Authorities:** 
Implementing agency: Gujarat Thakor And Koli Vikas Nigam.
Nodal Department: Social Justice and Empowerment Department.
