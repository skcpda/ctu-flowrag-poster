
# Disability Pension (UKBOCWWB)


**Short Title:** DPUKBOCWWB









**Tags:** Labour, Pension, Construction Workers, Disability, Disabled Person



The “Disability Pension” scheme is implemented by the Uttarakhand Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board (UKBOCWWB) under the Labour Department, Government of Uttarakhand. The scheme aims to provide a monthly pension to registered construction workers who have become permanently disabled due to paralysis, leprosy, tuberculosis, accidents, etc. 




**Who is this for:** 
Individual; 
Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; 
Sector: Pension



**Where it applies:** 
Uttarakhand; 
State/ UT









**Benefits / Assistance:** - **Pension Amount:** ₹1,000/- per month - **One-time Ex-gratia Amount:** ₹40,000/- 



**Benefit Type:** Cash






















**Contacts & Authorities:** 
Implementing agency: Uttarakhand Building And Other Construction Workers Welfare Board.
Nodal Department: Department Of Labour.
