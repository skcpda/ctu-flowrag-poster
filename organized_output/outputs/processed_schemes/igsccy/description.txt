
# Indira Gandhi Shehri Credit Card Yojana


**Short Title:** IGSCCY





**Launch Date:** 2021-06-01



**Closing Date:** 2022-03-31



**Tags:** Empowerment, Unemployment, Financial Assistance, Covid



The scheme targets street vendors, people providing essential services in the informal sector such as hair dressers, rickshaw-pullers, potters, eaters, cobblers, mechanics, tailors, washermen, paint-painters, plumbing-electric repairmen, etc. and the unemployed. financially empowering the youth Provide and restore.




**Who is this for:** 
Individual; 
Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; 
Sector: Citizen empowerment



**Where it applies:** 
Rajasthan; 
State









**Benefits / Assistance:** 1. Providing interest-free loan facility up to Rs.50,000/- (fifty thousand). 1. To promote the development of small businesses in the informal sector. 1. To encourage self-employment. 1. To make available financial resources for day-to-day needs. 1. Mitigating the impact of COVID-19 on the informal business sector. 1. This scheme will pave the way for the development and promotion of the economy by helping 5 lakh beneficiaries working in the informal business sector of Rajasthan state.



**Benefit Type:** Composite
