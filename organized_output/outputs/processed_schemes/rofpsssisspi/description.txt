
# Reimbursement of Fees of ST Students Studying in Sainik School Private Institutions


**Short Title:** ROFPSSSISSPI









**Tags:** Scheduled Tribe, Sainik School, Reimbursement, Students



The scheme &quot;Reimbursement of Fees for ST Students Studying in Sainik Schools and Private Institutions,&quot; implemented by the Tribal Welfare and Scheduled Caste (SC) Department, Government of Madhya Pradesh, provides free arrangements for accommodation, school fees, and education for eligible students.




**Who is this for:** 
Individual; 
Category: Education & Learning; 
Sector: Scholarships and student finance



**Where it applies:** 
Madhya Pradesh; 
State/ UT









**Benefits / Assistance:** Free arrangement for accommodation, school fees and studies for the students.



**Benefit Type:** In Kind






















**Contacts & Authorities:** 

Nodal Department: Tribal Welfare and Scheduled Caste (SC) Department.
