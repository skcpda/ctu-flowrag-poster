
# Pradhan Mantri Matsya Sampada Yojana: Mini Fish Feed Mills of Production Capacity of 2 Ton /Day


**Short Title:** PMMSYMFFMPC2TD









**Tags:** Fish, Fish Farmer, Fish Production, Entrepreneur, Mini Fish Feed Mill



The “Mini Fish Feed Mills of Production Capacity of 2 Ton /Day” is a scheme component under the Pradhan Mantri Matsya Sampada Yojana (PMMSY), implemented by the Fisheries Department, Government of Haryana. This is a Centrally Sponsored Scheme, aims to strengthen inland aquaculture infrastructure by promoting the establishment of small-scale fish feed mills. Under this scheme, financial assistance, in form of subsidy, is provided to individuals for setting up mini feed mills, ensuring the local availability of quality fish feed at affordable prices for fishers and fish farmers. 




**Who is this for:** 
Business Entity; 
Category: Agriculture,Rural & Environment; 
Sector: Fishing and hunting



**Where it applies:** 
Haryana; 
State/ UT









**Benefits / Assistance:** **Tangible Benefits:** - Project Cost: ₹30,00,000/- per unit - Subsidy will be limited to 40% of the project cost for General Category of beneficiaries. - Subsidy will be limited to 60% of the project cost for Weaker sections/ SC/ST/Women and their co-operatives of beneficiaries. 



**Benefit Type:** Cash






















**Contacts & Authorities:** 

Nodal Department: Fisheries Department.
