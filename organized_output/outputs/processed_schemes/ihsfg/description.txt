
# Interest Help Scheme For Graduate Doctor-Lawyer-Technical Graduate


**Short Title:** IHSFG









**Tags:** Education, Loan, Subsidy



The scheme &amp;quot;Interest Help Scheme for Graduate Doctor-Lawyer-Technical Graduate&amp;quot; introduced by the Social Justice and Empowerment Department, Gujarat, provides financial assistance in the form of interest subsidy on loans for self-employment. Beneficiaries from the unreserved category who are doctors, lawyers, technical graduates, and want to open their own clinic, laboratory, radiology clinic or office will be eligible for interest subsidy on loans taken from the bank. The scheme is being implemented by the Gujarat Unreserved Education and Economical Development Corporation.




**Who is this for:** 
Individual; 
Category: Education & Learning; 
Sector: Universities and higher education



**Where it applies:** 
Gujarat; 
State/ UT









**Benefits / Assistance:** - **Quantum of Subsidy:** 5% interest subsidy on loans up to ₹10,00,000/- taken from a bank.



**Benefit Type:** Cash






















**Contacts & Authorities:** 
Implementing agency: Gujarat Unreserved Education And Economical Development Corporation.
Nodal Department: Social Justice and Empowerment Department.
