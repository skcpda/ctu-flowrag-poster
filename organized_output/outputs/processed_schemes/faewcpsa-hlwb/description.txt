
# Financial Assistance for Encouraging Worker's Children's Participation in Sports Activities- Haryana Labour Welfare Board


**Short Title:** FAEWCPSA-HLWB









**Tags:** Worker, Financial Assistance, Sports, Participation In Sports, Sports Competition



The scheme “Financial Assistance for Encouraging Worker&amp;#39;s Children&amp;#39;s Participation in Sports Activities” is implemented by the Haryana Labour Welfare Board (HLWB), Labour Department, Government of Haryana. Under this scheme, financial assistance is provided by the Haryana Labour Welfare Board to the children of the workers working in the industrial and commercial establishments of the state of Haryana for participating in sports competitions so that the children of the workers can also become good sportspersons.




**Who is this for:** 
Family; 
Category: Sports & Culture; 
Sector: Sports



**Where it applies:** 
Haryana; 
State/ UT
