
# Aatmanirbhar Gujarat Scheme for assistance to MSMEs: Assistance for Raising Capital through SME Exchange 


**Short Title:** AGMSMESMEE





**Launch Date:** 2022-10-05





**Tags:** Business, Entrepreneurship, Patent Registration, Subsidy, SME Exchange, Equity Capital



The &amp;quot;Aatmanirbhar Gujarat Scheme for MSMEs&amp;quot; is an umbrella scheme launched by the Industries and Mines Department, Gujarat. It aims to empower the state&amp;#39;s dynamic MSME sector, which significantly contributes to employment, GDP, and exports. Effective from October 5, 2022, to October 4, 2027, this initiative supports growth, investments, and the vision of &amp;quot;Aatmanirbhar Gujarat&amp;quot; and &amp;quot;Aatmanirbhar Bharat.&amp;quot; The component &amp;quot;Assistance for Raising Capital through SME Exchange&amp;quot; aims to help MSMEs raise equity capital by listing on SME Exchanges. It provides financial assistance to MSMEs for expenses incurred in this process, encouraging them to explore funding options through the capital markets. &lt;br&gt;




**Who is this for:** 
Business Entity, Industries; 
Category: Business & Entrepreneurship; 
Sector: Setting up / start-up / entrepreneurship



**Where it applies:** 
Gujarat; 
State/ UT









**Benefits / Assistance:** - **Financial Assistance:** 25% of the expenditure incurred in raising funds through the SME Exchange, with a maximum of ₹5,00,000/-.



**Benefit Type:** Cash






















**Contacts & Authorities:** 

Nodal Department: Industries and Mines Department.
