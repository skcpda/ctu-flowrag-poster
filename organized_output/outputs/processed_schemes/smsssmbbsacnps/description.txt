
# State Medical Scholarship (Scholarship Scheme for MBBS & Allied Courses, Nursing and Paramedical Students)


**Short Title:** SMS(SSMBBSACNPS)









**Tags:** Medical, Scholarship, MBBS, Nursing, Paramedical, Student



The “State Medical Scholarship” is a scholarship scheme, launched by the Department of Health &amp;amp; Family Welfare, Government of Nagaland. Under the scheme, financial assistance is provided to students who are pursuing MBBS &amp;amp; Allied Courses, Nursing and Paramedical courses.




**Who is this for:** 
Individual; 
Category: Education & Learning; 
Sector: Scholarships and student finance



**Where it applies:** 
Nagaland; 
State/ UT









**Benefits / Assistance:** - Under the scheme, financial assistance/scholarships will be provided to students according to the course/programme in which they are enrolled.



**Benefit Type:** Composite






















**Contacts & Authorities:** 
Implementing agency: Department Of Health & Family Welfare, Government Of Nagaland.
Nodal Department: Health and Family Welfare Department, Nagaland.
