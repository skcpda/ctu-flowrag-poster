
# Mukhyamantri Samajik Samrasta Antarjatiya Vivah Shagun Yojana


**Short Title:** MSSAVSY-HARYANA





**Launch Date:** 1983-04-01





**Tags:** Vivah, Shagun, Inter-caste Marriage, Scheduled Caste, Financial Assistance



The scheme “Mukhyamantri Samajik Samrasta Antarjatiya Vivah Shagun Yojana” is implemented by the Welfare of Scheduled Castes &amp;amp; Backward Classes Department, Government of Haryana to promote communal harmony by encouraging inter-caste marriages. The objective of the scheme is in diminishing caste consciousness among the people and to encourage inter-caste marriages. This scheme is effective from 01.04.1983. Under the scheme, financial assistance of ₹2,50,000/- is given to the couple when a Non-Scheduled Caste boy or girl marries a Scheduled Caste girl/boy. The amount is provided as an incentive to the married couple and deposited in the joint account of the couple for 3 years lock-in period. This is a 50:50 sharing basis scheme of the Government of India/State Government.




**Who is this for:** 
Individual; 
Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; 
Sector: Marriage



**Where it applies:** 
Haryana; 
State/ UT









**Benefits / Assistance:** 1. Under this scheme, financial assistance of ₹2,50,000/- is given to the couple when a Non-Scheduled Caste boy or girl marries a Scheduled Caste girl/boy. **Note:** Out of the total ₹2,50,000/-, ₹1,25,000/- will be directly transferred to the joint account of the couple immediately after the marriage. The remaining ₹1,25,000/- will be deposited in the couple&amp;#39;s joint fixed deposit account for a lock-in period of 3 years, without the facility for its pre-mature encashment.



**Benefit Type:** Cash
