
# District Central Cooperative Banks and through its Branches: For Working Women


**Short Title:** DCCBFWW









**Tags:** Working Women, Loan



The &amp;quot;District Central Cooperative Banks and through its Branches: For Working Women&amp;quot; scheme launched by the Co-operation, Food and Consumer Protection Department, Tamil Nadu, aims to provide financial support through a loan of up to ₹20,000/- at a nominal interest rate of 12%. The loans are facilitated by District Central Cooperative Banks and their branches, which ensure accessibility to women seeking financial assistance for professional and personal growth. &lt;br&gt;




**Who is this for:** 
Individual; 
Category: Banking,Financial Services and Insurance; 
Sector: Loan



**Where it applies:** 
Tamil Nadu; 
State/ UT









**Benefits / Assistance:** - **Access to Loan:** Up to ₹20,000/-. - **Interest Rate:** 12% (subject to change). &lt;br&gt;



**Benefit Type:** Cash






















**Contacts & Authorities:** 

Nodal Department: Co-operation, Food and Consumer Protection Department.
