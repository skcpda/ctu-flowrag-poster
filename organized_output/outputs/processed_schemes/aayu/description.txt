
# Atal Awas Yojana - Uttarakhand


**Short Title:** AAYU









**Tags:** Financial Assistance, BPL, Scheduled Caste, House, Atal, Homeless Family



The scheme &amp;#39;Atal Awas Yojana&amp;#39; is implemented by the Social Welfare Department, Government of Uttarakhand, to provide financial assistance for the construction of houses in both hilly and plain areas of the state. Under this scheme, financial assistance is provided to homeless Scheduled Caste families who are below the poverty line (BPL) or fall within a defined income limit for constructing a house. The beneficiaries should not own any house and should possess their own land within the state.




**Who is this for:** 
Family; 
Category: Housing & Shelter; 
Sector: Shelter, integrated support and assistance



**Where it applies:** 
Uttarakhand; 
State/ UT









**Benefits / Assistance:** **Financial Assistance** - ₹1,30,000/- for hilly areas - ₹1,20,000/- for plain areas > **Note:** The amount is disbursed in 3 installments to construct a house.



**Benefit Type:** Cash






















**Contacts & Authorities:** 

Nodal Department: Department Of Social Welfare.
