
# Gargi Puruskar Yojana


**Short Title:** GPY





**Launch Date:** 1998-01-01





**Tags:** Financial Assistance, Scholarship, Girl Education



Through the Gargi Puraskar Yojana, girls are given incentives from the government to move forward in the field of education. Girls of all classes can take advantage of the scheme. The amount of this scheme will be given every year on Basant Panchami. &lt;br&gt; The girl students who will get the benefit of the scheme in class 10th will be required to enrol in the school in classes 11th and 12th. And the government will give the amount to the beneficiary girl child through a check, so it is mandatory for the girl child to have a bank account. You can apply for the scheme online.




**Who is this for:** 
Individual; 
Category: Education & Learning; 
Sector: Scholarships and student finance



**Where it applies:** 
Rajasthan; 
State



**DBT Scheme:** Yes (Direct Benefit Transfer)







**Benefits / Assistance:** - Payable for 11 &amp; 12 class girl students: Rs. 3,000/- each year on the day of Basant Panchami.



**Benefit Type:** Cash






















**Contacts & Authorities:**
