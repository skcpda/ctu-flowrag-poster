
# Shop Construction/Shop Operation Scheme For Rehabilitation Of PwDs


**Short Title:** SCSFR





**Launch Date:** 2020-11-16





**Tags:** Financial Assistance, Business, Loan, Empowerment



The &amp;quot;Shop Construction/Shop Operation Scheme For Rehabilitation Of PwDs&amp;quot; started to support disabled people in the Uttar Pradesh State. Under this scheme, financial Assistance is provided to eligible disabled persons to start their own shop/business for Rehabilitation. **Site selection for Shop Construction/Buying Shop/Taking Shop on Rent:** - Urban Area - A place where there is a complete possibility of running a business. - Rural Area - A place where there is easy transportation and there is a possibility of running a business. **Process of Payment**: - Applications will be accepted as per rules based on the principle of first come and first serve and payment will be made in their bank account through e-payment. **Recovery of Loan** - The basic loan sanctioned for the construction of the shop will be recovered in thirty equal instalments at the rate of Rs. 500/- per quarterly instalment after one year of payment of the entire amount of loan and grant. - The basic loan sanctioned for the purchase of the shop will be recovered in thirty equal instalments at the rate of Rs. 500/- per quarterly instalment after three months of payment. - The basic loan sanctioned for the purchase of the Khokha/Gumti/Handcart will be recovered in thirty equal instalments at the rate of Rs. 250/- per quarterly instalment after three months of payment. - The interest amount will be recovered after the recovery of the basic loan sanctioned for the construction of the shop/ Khokha, Gumti, and Handcart. This recovery will be done in 24 equal monthly instalments. The beneficiary can also pay a lump sum of the entire amount of interest.




**Who is this for:** 
Individual; 
Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; 
Sector: Rehabilitation



**Where it applies:** 
Uttar Pradesh; 
State









**Benefits / Assistance:** 1. An amount of Rs. 20,000/- is allocated for the establishment of a shop. - Out of Rs. 20,000/-, Rs. 15,000/- is provided in the form of a loan at 4% interest and Rs. 5,000/- is provided as a grant. 1. An amount of Rs. 10,000/- is allocated for the operation of the shop/khokha/gumti/hand cart. - Out of Rs. 10,000/-, Rs. 7,500/- is provided in the form of a loan at 4% interest and Rs. 2,500/- is provided as a grant. 



**Benefit Type:** Cash
