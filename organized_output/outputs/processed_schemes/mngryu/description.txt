
# Mukhyamantri Nishulk Gas Refill Yojana


**Short Title:** MMNGRY









**Tags:** Gas Cylinder, Free Gas Refill, Antyodaya, Ration Card, Pink Card



The scheme &amp;quot;Mukhyamantri Nishulk Gas Refill Yojana&amp;quot; was launched by the Department of Food, Civil Supplies &amp;amp; Consumer Affairs, Government of Uttarakhand. The scheme aims to support poor families holding Antyodaya (pink) ration cards in the state by reducing their household fuel expenses. 




**Who is this for:** 
Family; 
Category: Social welfare & Empowerment




**Where it applies:** 
Uttarakhand; 
State/ UT



**DBT Scheme:** Yes (Direct Benefit Transfer)







**Benefits / Assistance:** Under this scheme, Antyodaya ration card (pink card) holders in the state are entitled to receive 03 free gas cylinder refills every year. 



**Benefit Type:** In Kind






















**Contacts & Authorities:** 

Nodal Department: Department Of Food & Civil Supplies.
