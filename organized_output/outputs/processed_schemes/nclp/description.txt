
# Nehru Child Literature Prize


**Short Title:** NCLP









**Tags:** Financial Assistance, Author, Publication, Books



The Arts and Culture Department of Puducherry launched the &quot;Nehru Child Literature Prize&quot; scheme. The scheme aims to provide financial assistance of ₹10,000/- with a certificate to writers/authors for the books published in Tamil on Children’s Literature. The applications will be accepted offline.




**Who is this for:** 
Individual; 
Category: Skills & Employment




**Where it applies:** 
Puducherry; 
State/ UT









**Benefits / Assistance:** - Financial Assistance of ₹10,000/-. with a certificate.



**Benefit Type:** Cash






















**Contacts & Authorities:** 

Nodal Department: Arts and Culture Department, Puducherry.
