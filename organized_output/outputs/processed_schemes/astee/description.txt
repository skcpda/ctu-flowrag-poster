
# Award Scheme to Encourage Entrepreneurs


**Short Title:** ASTEE









**Tags:** Award, Weavers, NGO, Handicrafts, Handloom



The scheme &amp;quot;Award Scheme to Encourage Entrepreneurs&amp;quot; is implemented by the Department of Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises, Government of Uttarakhand. The scheme aims to encourage and recognize the exceptional craftsmanship and weaving skills of registered artisans and weavers in the state. It provides awards at both the state and district levels to outstanding performers in the handicrafts and handloom (cotton and woolen textile) sectors. The scheme promotes traditional arts and incentivizes excellence in the handicraft and textile industries.




**Who is this for:** 
Individual, Business Entity, NGO, Registered Societies; 
Category: Business & Entrepreneurship




**Where it applies:** 
Uttarakhand; 
State/ UT
