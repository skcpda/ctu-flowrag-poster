
# Educational Assistance for Government Recognised Diploma Courses


**Short Title:** EAGRDC









**Tags:** Financial Assistance, Diploma, Construction Worker, Student, Scholarship



The Educational Assistance for Government Recognised Diploma Courses scheme was launched by the Maharashtra Building And Other Construction Workers Welfare Board (MBOCWW), Labour Department Maharashtra. The scheme aims to provide financial assistance to children of registered construction workers, providing ₹20,000/- per year for diploma courses and ₹25,000/- per year for postgraduate diploma courses.




**Who is this for:** 
Individual; 
Category: Education & Learning; 
Sector: Scholarships and student finance



**Where it applies:** 
Maharashtra; 
State/ UT









**Benefits / Assistance:** **Financial Assistance For:** - Government Recognized Diploma Courses- ₹20,000/- - Postgraduate Diploma Courses- ₹25,000/-  > **NOTE:** Benefit applies to a maximum of two children and to the wife of registered male workers.



**Benefit Type:** Cash
