# Scholarships to Students Studying in Central Government Institution like IIT, IIM, IIIT, NIT and Central Universities

**Short Title:** STSS

**Scheme For:** Family

**Tags:** Minority Welfare, Scholarship, Student Finance, Education

The scheme “Scholarships to Students Studying in Central Government Institution like IIT, IIM, IIIT, NIT and Central Universities&amp;quot; launched by the Backward Classes, Most Backward Classes and Minorities Welfare Department, Tamil Nadu, aims to support Tamil Nadu students from the Backward Classes (BC), Most Backward Classes (MBC), and Denotified Communities (DNC) who are pursuing undergraduate and postgraduate courses in institutions such as IITs, IIMs, IIITs, NITs, and Central Universities. Eligible students receive annual reimbursement of tuition fees, exam fees, and other compulsory fees.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Education & Learning; Sector: Scholarships and student finance

**Where it applies:** Tamil Nadu; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - **Financial Assistance:** Financial support for tuition, exam fees, and other compulsory fees up to ₹2,00,000/- annually. &lt;br&gt;

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**How to apply:** - The eligible and willing students are requested to fill out the application forms and submit the filled application to the institutions and send the filled application with the concurrence of the Principal of the Institution with all enclosures to the under-mentioned Directorate. &gt; **Commissioner of Backward Classes Welfare,** &gt; **Ezhilagam Annex Building,** &gt; **2nd floor, Chepauk, Chennai-5.** &gt; **Phone: 044-28551462** &gt; **E-mail: tngovtiitscholarship@gmail.com** &lt;br&gt; Download the Application form through the below-mentioned link: - [Fresh Application Form](https://bcmbcmw.tn.gov.in/forms/IIT_scholarship_Application_Fresh.pdf). - [Renewal Application Form](https://bcmbcmw.tn.gov.in/forms/IIT_scholarship_Application_Renewal.pdf). 
Mode: Offline.

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Backward Classes Most Backward Classes and Minorities Welfare Department.

**References / Annexures:** - **Guidelines**: https://bcmbcmw.tn.gov.in/welfschemes.htm#incentive_scheme - **Community List**: https://bcmbcmw.tn.gov.in/community_list.pdf

**Additional Information:**
Briefdescription: The scholarship provides financial assistance to students from Tamil Nadu's BC, MBC, and DNC communities pursuing UG/PG courses at premier Central Government institutions like IITs, IIMs, IIITs, NITs, and Central Universities.
