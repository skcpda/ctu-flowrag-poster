# Loan for the Purchase of Tools (GBOCWWB)

**Short Title:** LPT-GBOCWWB

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Tool, Loan, Labour, Worker, Financial Assistance

&quot;Loan for the Purchase of Tools (GBOCWWB)&quot; is a Welfare Scheme by the Goa Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board of the Department of Labour and Employment, Government of Goa. Through this scheme, the worker registered with the Board is provided with a loan of ₹5,000/- for the purchase of tools. The applications are accepted offline.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Machine & skill up-Gradation

**Where it applies:** Goa; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** Loan of ₹5,000/- for the purchase of tools.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**How to apply:** **Step 1:** The interested applicant should take print of the [prescribed format](https://labour.goa.gov.in/wp-content/uploads/2020/07/Scheme-7-Grant-For-The-Purchase-of-Tools.pdf) of the application form for the scheme from the Official Website of the Department of Labour and Employment, Goa. OR The interested applicant should visit the _Office of the Commissioner, Labour &amp; Employment, 2nd Floor, Sharma Shakti Bhavan, Patto Plaza, Panaji-Goa (0832-2437081/82/83, com-labo.goa@nic.in)_ and request a hard copy of the prescribed format of the application form from the concerned authority. **Step 2:** In the application form, fill in all the mandatory fields, paste the passport-sized photograph (signed across, if required), and attach copies of all the mandatory documents (self-attest, if required). **Step 3:** The interested applicant should submit the duly filled and signed application form and the documents to the concerned authority at the Office of the Commissioner, Labour &amp; Employment. **Step 4:** A receipt of the application will be assigned to the applicant by the concerned authority.
Mode: Offline.

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: Goa Building And Other Construction Workers Welfare Board; Nodal Department: Labour and Employment Department.
