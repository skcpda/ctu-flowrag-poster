# Sudama Shishyavritti Scheme (Class 11th and 12th)

**Short Title:** SSYMP

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Student, Sudama, Scholarship, BPL

The scheme &amp;quot;Sudama Shishyavritti Scheme (Class 11th and 12th)&amp;quot; was launched by the School Education Department, Government of Madhya Pradesh, to motivate students from lower economic backgrounds and support them economically. Under this scheme, scholarships are provided to Class 11 and 12 students in government schools who reside in hostels, have an annual family income of less than ₹1,00,000/-, and belong to the Below Poverty Line (BPL) category.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Education & Learning; Sector: Scholarships and student finance

**Where it applies:** Madhya Pradesh; State

**Benefits / Assistance:** Under this scheme, the girl student will get a scholarship of ₹5250/- and boy student will get a scholarship of ₹5000/- per year.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**How to apply:** - To avail of the benefits under this scheme, eligible students can contact the Principal of their respective government schools. Note: The student&#39;s profile is entered on the education portal [https://shikshaportal.mp.gov.in/](https://shikshaportal.mp.gov.in/) by the teacher of the educational institution as per the record/certificate provided by the student.
Mode: Offline.

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: School Education Department, Government Of Madhya Pradesh; Nodal Department: School Education Department.
