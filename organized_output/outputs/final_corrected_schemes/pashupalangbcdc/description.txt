# Pashupalan (GBCDC)

**Short Title:** PASHUPALANGBCDC

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Cow, Buffalo, Agriculture Activities, Loan

The scheme &quot;Pashupalan (GBCDC)&quot; by the Social Justice and Empowerment Department, owned by the Centre and the Gujarat Government, aims to provide financial assistance for agriculture-related activities. Through this scheme, a low-interest loan amount is provided to support Pashu Palaks (Animal Rearers) in undertaking animal husbandry activities such as purchasing cows and buffaloes. The scheme&#39;s core objective is to offer financial aid at low-interest rates, supporting eligible individuals from socially and educationally backwards classes. 

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Agriculture,Rural & Environment; Sector: Animal husbandry

**Where it applies:** Gujarat; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** 1. **Low-Interest Loan:** A loan is provided at an interest rate of 6 per cent per annum.1. **Maximum Loan Limit:** The maximum loan limit in this scheme is up to ₹1,00,000/-.1. **Unit Cost Coverage:** 85 per cent of the unit cost will be loaned. - Out of this 85 per cent, 85 per cent will be from the national corporation. - 10 per cent will be from the state government. - 5 per cent will be from the beneficiary&#39;s contribution.  > **Intended Use:** The loan is for agriculture-related activities, specifically animal husbandry (cow-buffalo). > **Repayment Terms:** This loan is to be repaid in 60 equal monthly instalments, including interest. 

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**How to apply:** **Step 1: Visit the Official Website:** Go to the [[Official Website](https://ggdconline.gujarat.gov.in/) Gujarat Backwards Caste Development Corporation.](https://gbcdconline.gujarat.gov.in/) **Step 2: Locate and Apply for Scheme:** Scroll down on the homepage, find the &quot;Pashupalan,&quot; and click on &quot;Apply Now&quot;. **Step 3:** **Access Application Form:** You will be directed to the [application form](https://gbcdconline.gujarat.gov.in/SchemePasupalan/AddEdit). **Step 4: Scheme Selection &amp; Loan Details:** Select &quot;Pashupalan&quot;, enter Unit Cost, and specify the Required Loan Amount. **Step 5: Applicant&#39;s Details:** Provide Aadhaar, Ration, Voter ID, full name (Last, First, Father/Husband), address, district, taluka, village, PIN, date of birth, age (21-45 as of 14/08/2023), caste, subcaste, phone, mobile, email, gender, and marital status. **Step 6: Income Details:** Enter income certificate number, issue date, issuing officer&#39;s name, total annual family income (up to ₹ 3,00,000/-), and parent/guardian&#39;s occupation. **Step 7: Disability Details (if applicable):** If &quot;Physically Handicapped,&quot; select &quot;Yes&quot; or &quot;No&quot;. If you selected &quot;Yes&quot;, provide the percentage (in %). **Step 8: Employment Details (if applicable):** Indicate if any family member works in a government or private job. If &quot;Yes,&quot; provide their Name, Designation, Place, and Salary details. **Step 9: Educational Qualification:** Select your Education Qualification: Uneducated, Educated, Graduation, Post Graduation, or Other Degree. **Step 10: Loan History:** If you have taken a past loan, select &quot;Yes&quot; or &quot;No&quot;. If you selected &quot;Yes&quot; provide loan details (date, amount, paid, remaining) and your account number. **Step 12: Bank Account Details:** Provide bank account number, bank name, branch name, name as per account, account type (Saving/Current), MICR code, and IFSC code. **Step 13: Submission:** Click the &quot;Submit&quot; button to complete your application. 
Mode: Online.

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Social Justice and Empowerment Department.
