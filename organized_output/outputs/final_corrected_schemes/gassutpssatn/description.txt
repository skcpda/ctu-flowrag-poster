# Grant of Annual Scholarship to the Students Belonging to UT of Puducherry Selected and Studying in the Sainik School, Amaravathi Nagar, Tamil Nadu

**Short Title:** GASSUTPSSATN

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Scholarship, Student, School, Education

The scheme &quot;Grant of Annual Scholarship to the Students Belonging to UT of Puducherry Selected and Studying in the Sainik School, Amaravathi Nagar, Tamil Nadu&quot; is a Scholarship Scheme by the Directorate of School Education, Union Territory of Puducherry. A scholarship of ₹10,000/- per annum per student is disbursed directly into the Aadhaar-seeded viable bank account by Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) through the Electronic Clearance System (ECS). In order to be eligible, the applicant should have studied from an educational institution located in the UT of Puducherry in Government or Private Schools recognised by the State/Central Governments.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Education & Learning; Sector: Scholarships and student finance

**Where it applies:** Puducherry; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** > **Scholarship** ₹10,000/- per annum per student. > **Mode of Disbursal** The amount is disbursed directly into the Aadhaar-seeded viable bank account by Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) through the Electronic Clearance System (ECS).

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**How to apply:** **Step 1:** The interested applicant should visit the Principal, Sainik School, Amaravathi Nagar, TamilNadu, and request the hard copy of the prescribed format of the application form for the concerned scheme. **Step 2:** In the application form, fill in all the mandatory fields, paste the passport-sized photograph (signed across, if required), and attach copies of all the mandatory documents (self-attest, if required). **Step 3:** The interested applicant should submit the duly filled and signed application form and the documents to the Principal. The Principal then sends the applications to the Directorate of School Education, Puducherry.
Mode: Offline.

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: School Education Department, Puducherry.
