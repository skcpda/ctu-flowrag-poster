# National Livestock Mission: Genetic Improvement of Pig breeds - Establishment of Pig Semen Collection and Processing Lab

**Short Title:** NLMGIPBEPSCPL

**Scheme For:** Infra

**Tags:** Pig Farm, Pig Semen, Processing Lab, Genetic Improvement, Boar

The &amp;quot;Establishment of Pig Semen Collection and Processing Lab&amp;quot; is a component under the Genetic Improvement of Pig Breeds activity of the Sub-Mission on Breed Development of Livestock and Poultry, part of the National Livestock Mission (NLM). This initiative is introduced by the Department of Animal Husbandry and Dairying, under the Ministry of Fisheries, Animal Husbandry and Dairying, Government of India. Under this scheme, a one-time grant-in-aid up to ₹150.00 lakh will be provided as Central share to the concerned state for construction of semen laboratory, maintaining breeding boar unit. ₹30 lakh will also be provided as onetime recurring expenditure for procurement of consumables, medicines, chemicals etc. for first time processing of semen. The scheme is implemented by the State Implementing Agencies of the State Animal Husbandry Department and State Animal Husbandry Departments. **Objective:** 1. Increase of per animal productivity through the propagation of high genetic merit male germplasm (boar semen) through artificial insemination technique. 1. Reduce inbreeding in pig populations. 1. Reduce ingress of sexually transmitted diseases in pigs.

**Who is this for:** Government Organisation, Business Entity; Category: Business & Entrepreneurship; Sector: Animal husbandry

**Where it applies:** Central

**Benefits / Assistance:** ***Assistance will be provided to establish semen processing lab at the government pig farm to produce high quality liquid boar semen for artificial insemination.*** 1. One-time grants-in-aid of up to ₹1,50,00,000/- (₹1.5 crore) are provided as the Central share to the concerned state for the construction of semen laboratories and maintaining a breeding boar unit. 1. ₹30,00,000 (₹30 lakh) is provided as a one-time recurring expenditure for the procurement of consumables, medicines, chemicals, and other necessary materials for the initial processing of semen. **Funding Pattern:** 1. For all states except the North Eastern Region (NER) and Himalayan states, the funding pattern is 60:40 (Central: State). 1. For NER and Himalayan states, the funding pattern is 90:10 (Central: State). &gt; **Mode of Disbursement:** - The funds are provided as a one-time grant to the concerned state government through its Animal Husbandry Department. &gt; **Frequency of Disbursement:** - One-time disbursement. &gt; **Validity:** - The financial assistance is granted for the establishment of the laboratory and the first-time procurement of necessary materials.

**Benefit Type:** Cash
