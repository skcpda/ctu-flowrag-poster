# Assistance to Co-operative Irrigation Societies (Trible Area)

**Short Title:** ATCISTA

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Irrigation, Co-operative Irrigation Societies, Farm Productivity, Tribal Area Farmers

The scheme &amp;quot;Assistance to Co-operative Irrigation Societies (Tribal Area)&amp;quot; by the Agriculture and Co-operation Department, Government of Gujarat, aims to increase farm productivity by offering financial support to irrigation co-operative societies formed by tribal area farmers. The scheme focuses on forming such societies and facilitating water distribution by setting up electric pumps and motors on wells and agar creeks. 

**Who is this for:** Registered Societies, Individual; Category: Agriculture,Rural & Environment; Sector: Land and water resources

**Where it applies:** Gujarat; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - **Monetary Support:** Financial help is provided for the installation of electric motors and pumps on wells and agar creeks to enhance irrigation facilities. 

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**How to apply:** **Step 1:** The interested applicant should prepare the application on plain paper. **Step 2:** In the application form, provide all the mandatory details, and attach copies of all the mandatory documents (self-attested, if required). **Step 3:** Submit the duly filled and signed application form along with the supporting documents to the District Registrar&#39;s Office. **Step 4:** Request a receipt or acknowledgement from the concerned authority to whom the application has been submitted. 
Mode: Offline.

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Agriculture, Farmers Welfare and Cooperation Department.

**References / Annexures:** - **Guidelines (Scheme No. 80)**: https://mariyojana.gujarat.gov.in/Schemeatoz.aspx

Briefdescription: The scheme "Assistance to Co-operative Irrigation Societies (Tribal Area)" aims to enhance agricultural productivity by financially supporting co-operative irrigation societies in tribal areas through the installation of water distribution infrastructure.
