# Livelihood Opportunity Promotion Scheme (Scheduled Caste)

**Short Title:** LOPSSC

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Financial Assistance, Loan, Scheduled Caste, Self-Employment

The Social Welfare Department, Government of Uttarakhand, launched the &quot;Livelihood Opportunity Promotion Scheme (Scheduled Caste)&quot; scheme. It aims to promote self-employment and income generation among Scheduled Caste individuals by supporting small businesses and livelihood projects. Under this scheme, financial assistance in the form of bank loans, margin money, and grants is provided to the beneficiaries for projects ranging from ₹50,000/- to ₹2,00,000/-.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Banking,Financial Services and Insurance; Sector: Banking and money

**Where it applies:** Uttarakhand; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** > **Financial Assistance:** - Project Cost Limit: ₹50,000/- to ₹2,00,000/- - Bank Loan: 60% of the project cost - Margin Money: 30% of the project cost (payable with applicable interest over 60 monthly installments) - Grant: Maximum of ₹10,000/- > **Beneficiary Contribution:** - No contribution for project cost up to ₹1,00,000/- - 10% contribution for project cost between ₹1,01,000/- and ₹2,00,000/-.  

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Exclusions / Not eligible:** - Beneficiaries already covered under similar livelihood support schemes (except for training components under Pradhan Mantri Anusuchit Jati Abhyuday Yojana) may not be eligible. 

**How to apply:** ***Application Process:*** **Step 1:** The interested applicant downloads the [application form](http://www.ukbvvn.org.in/Download/ApplicationFormFormatJeevika.pdf) from the official website. **Step 2:** In the application form, fill in all the mandatory fields and attach copies of all the mandatory documents (self-attested, if required). **Step 3:** Submit the duly filled and signed application form along with the documents to the office of the Vikas Khand (Block Development) Office. &amp;lt;br&amp;gt; ***Selection Process:*** **Step 1:** The application will be forwarded to the District Social Welfare Officer. **Step 2:** A District-Level Committee, headed by the District Social Welfare Officer, will review the proposals and select beneficiaries.
Mode: Offline.

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Department Of Social Welfare.
