# Individual loaning scheme: Haryana Women Development Corporation

**Short Title:** ILSHRWCD

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Women, Project, Subsidy, Loan

The scheme &quot;Individual Loaning Scheme&quot; by the Haryana Women Development Corporation, Government of Haryana, aims to provide financial assistance to women for economically viable projects. Through this scheme, subsidies and loans are provided to eligible women for projects costing. The scheme is implemented by the Haryana Women Development Corporation. 

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Women and Child; Sector: Loan

**Where it applies:** Haryana; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** **1. Financial Assistance:** For economically viable projects, the total cost of which should not exceed ₹1,50,000/-. **2. Subsidy:** - 25% (subject to a maximum of ₹10,000/-) for other categories. - 25% (subject to a maximum of ₹25,000/-) for Scheduled Caste (SC) Categories. - Earlier, the subsidy was only 10% (subject to a maximum of ₹5,000/-) for all categories. **3. Beneficiary Share:** 10% of the project cost. **4. Balance Loan Amount:** Provided by commercial/nationalised banks. 

**Benefit Type:** Cash
