# Interest Subsidy Scheme on Education Loan

**Short Title:** ISSOEL

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Education Loan, Higher Education, Interest Subsidy, Abroad Study

The scheme &amp;quot;Interest Subsidy Scheme on Education Loan&amp;quot; by the Education Department, Government of Gujarat, aims to provide complete financial relief to students through 100% interest subsidy on education loans for the entire moratorium period. This includes the study period and 1 additional year after course completion. 

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Education & Learning; Sector: Scholarships and student finance

**Where it applies:** Gujarat; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - **Interest Subsidy Assistance:** 100% interest subsidy on education loans up to ₹10,00,000/- for the moratorium period (course duration + 1 year). 

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**How to apply:** > **Registration Process:** **Step 1:** Visit [https://isel.guj.nic.in](https://isel.guj.nic.in/) and register using your mobile number and email ID. **Step 2:** Log in with OTP and change the password. **Step 3:** Enter name, Aadhaar number, Ration Card number, and enrollment details correctly. **Step 4:** Provide parent/guardian contact details and complete registration.  > **Application Process:** **Step 1:** Log in to [https://isel.guj.nic.in](https://isel.guj.nic.in/) and click “Apply.” **Step 2:** Enter course details and fill out the online form. **Step 3:** Upload all required documents in the specified format. **Step 4:** Visit the designated help center within 7 days for document verification and submit the application set and collect the acknowledgement receipt. (For students abroad – parent/guardian with Power of Attorney signs and submits documents).  > **Contact Us:** **Kcg E-Mail Id:** isel-kcg[at]gujgov[dot]edu[dot]in **Helpline No:** 9909039380 (10:30 AM to 6:00 PM) 
Mode: Online.

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Education Department.
