# Handicraft Mela Participation

**Short Title:** HMP

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Handicraft, Exhibition, Artisan, SHG, Embroidery, Pottery

The scheme &quot;Handicraft Mela Participation&quot; by the Industrial Extension Cottage (iNDEXT-C), Industries &amp; Mines Department, Government of Gujarat, aims to promote traditional handicrafts by providing artisans and organizations with platforms to showcase and sell their products at state and national-level fairs. The scheme facilitates participation in designated melas (fairs) by covering logistical support and stall allocations. Eligible applicants include individual artisans, self-help groups (SHGs), and registered handicraft organizations. The scheme is implemented by Indext-C (Industrial Extension Bureau, Gujarat), and applications are accepted offline through district offices or the Indext-C portal.

**Who is this for:** Individual, Self Help Groups (SHGS); Category: Business & Entrepreneurship; Sector: Exhibitions/Trade fairs/Buyer-seller meet, market development

**Where it applies:** Gujarat; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** **Stall Allocation:** Free or subsidized stalls at designated melas. **Promotional Support:** Marketing and branding assistance for participants. **Sales Platform:** Direct access to customers and bulk buyers. **Logistical Aid:** Basic infrastructure (tables, signage) provided at mela venues. **Financial Incentives:** Priority for artisans from SC/ST/SEBC/EWS categories.

**Benefit Type:** In Kind

**How to apply:** **Step 1:** The interested applicant should take a printout of the [prescribed format](https://craftofgujarat.gujarat.gov.in/Documents/SubDeal__2025-2-13_96.pdf) of the application form from the Indext-C website. **Step 2:** In the application form, fill in all the mandatory fields ( personal/organization details, artisan ID, and handicraft category, etc), and attach copies of all the mandatory documents (self-attest, if required). **Step 3:** Submit the duly filled and signed application form, along with the documents, either to your nearest [District Industries Office](https://www.cottage.gujarat.gov.in/Home-2140795B5D2D-282472282472-2824725B5D2D) or to the following address: **Indext-C Headquarters, Industrial Extension Cottage, Block No. 7, 1st Floor, Udyog Bhavan, Sector-11, Gandhinagar - 382 011.** The Indext-C will review the applications and notify the selected participants via SMS/email.
Mode: Offline.

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Industries and Mines Department.

**Additional Information:**
Briefdescription: The scheme enables artisans and handicraft organizations to participate in government-sponsored melas across Gujarat and other states, fostering market access and preserving traditional crafts. Benefits include subsidized stall fees, promotional support, sales opportunities for handmade products.
