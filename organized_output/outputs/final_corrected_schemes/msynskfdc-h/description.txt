# Mahila Samridhi Yojana under NSKFDC- Haryana

**Short Title:** MSYNSKFDC-H

**Scheme For:** Infra

**Tags:** Mahila, Samridhi, NSKFDC, HSFDC, Safai Karamcharis, Loan, Self-employment, Woman

The scheme &amp;quot;Mahila Samridhi Yojana under NSKFDC&amp;quot; is implemented by the Haryana Scheduled Castes Finance and Development Corporation (HSFDC) in collaboration with the National Safai Karamcharis Finance &amp;amp; Development Corporation (NSKFDC). The HSFDC is a Company registered under the Companies Act, 1956 on 02.01.1971. It is a wholly government-owned Corporation with a 51% share of the State Government and a 49% share of the Government of India. This scheme offers loans up to ₹1,00,000/- to women Safai Karamcharis or their dependents for economic development through self-employment initiatives, such as establishing cloth shops, dairies, tea shops, bangle shops, beauty parlours, papad making, basketry, tailoring shops, etc. The Corporation contributes margin money at 10% of the project cost.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Business & Entrepreneurship; Sector: Loan

**Where it applies:** Haryana; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** **Project Cost Ceiling:** - The Corporation (HSFDC), in collaboration with NSKFDC, provides loans of up to ₹1,00,000/- per beneficiary/unit for various economic development activities. **Margin Money:** - The Corporation provides margin money @ 10% of the project cost. **Subsidy:** - The corporation provides a subsidy @ 50% of the total project cost (Maximum amount of subsidy is ₹10,000/-). **Note:** The subsidy is provided only to the beneficiary living below the poverty line. &gt; **Interest criteria for the loan amount recoverable by HSFDC:** **Upto Amount(₹)****Interest % P.A.****Penal Interest % P.A.**10000044 **Interest Starts on****Penalty Start After****Principal Recovery Starts After****Installment Starts After****Installment Period**Same Day30 Days120 Days120 Days30 Days
