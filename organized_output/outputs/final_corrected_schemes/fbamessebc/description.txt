# Food Bill Assistant for Medical & Engineering Students of SEBC

**Short Title:** FBAMESSEBC

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Financial Assistance, Student, SEBC, Medical, Engineering

The &quot;Food Bill Assistant for Medical &amp; Engineering Students of SEBC&quot; scheme was launched by the Social Justice and Empowerment Department, Government of Gujarat. The scheme aims to provide financial relief for food expenses to SEBC category students pursuing higher education in the medical and engineering fields. Under this scheme, financial assistance per month for a maximum of 10 months is provided to eligible students. 

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Education & Learning; Sector: Scholarships and student finance

**Where it applies:** Gujarat; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - **Financial Assistance:** - ₹300/- per month (₹3,000/- annually) towards mess charges for most eligible SEBC students. - ₹700/- per month (₹7,000/- annually) towards mess charges for selected students (limited number of beneficiaries). - ₹1,500/- per month (₹15,000/- annually) for SEBC students studying in self-financed medical/engineering colleges and residing in hostels (with a fixed ceiling on the number of beneficiaries). - **Duration:** Maximum of 10 months in an academic year. 

**Benefit Type:** Cash

The profile will be updated after entering the verification Code (OTP). **Step 7:** Now under services, select ‘Scholarship Services’ and then click on ‘SC Scheme’. **Step 8:** Select the Financial Year for which you want to apply for and then click on ‘Change Financial Year’. **Step 9:** Select the scheme, read the interaction carefully, and then click on ‘Continue to Service’. **Step 10:** Fill in the application form completely and upload all the relevant documents. **Step 11:** Now, click on ‘Save as Draft’ and agree with the terms &amp;amp; conditions, and submit the application form by clicking on ‘Confirm &amp;amp; Final Submit’. &lt;br&gt; &gt; **Instructions****:** **Note 1:** Photo uploading for all applicants is mandatory. **Note 2:** Application will be considered complete only when all sections are completed. **Note 3:** Only one application per applicant will be acceptable for a scholarship. If any student submits more than one application either from a single institute or from multiple institutions his/her all applications will be rejected by the competent authority and no request from any side will be entertained. **Note 4:** In case of any wrong/misleading information provided in the application shall lead to rejection of the application by Department Authorities. **Note 5:** All required documents must be carefully scanned as per the instructions given. **Note 6:** The student has to fill his/her application carefully as there is no provision for institution change once the application is submitted successfully. **Note 7:** The unreadable uploaded documents will allow the competent authority to reject the student application. **Note 8:** Direct Individual submission of this application form will not be accepted in case of students studying inside the state. Only outside students have to fill out their form online and submit his/her application form to their native district officer.
