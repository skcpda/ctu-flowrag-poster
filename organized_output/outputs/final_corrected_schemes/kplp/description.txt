# Kamban Pugazh Literary Prize

**Short Title:** KPLP

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Artist, Financial Assistance, Prize, Publication, Book

The Arts and Culture Department, Puducherry launched the &amp;quot;Kamban Pugazh Literary Prize&amp;quot; scheme. The objective of this scheme is to provide prizes of ₹10,000/- and 10 awards to authors/writers in the field of novels, short stories, drama, poems &amp;amp; prose in Tamil. The applications are accepted offline in the prescribed form available at the Director of Art and Culture Department.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Sports & Culture; Sector: Financial assistance

**Where it applies:** Puducherry; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - Financial Assistance of ₹10,000/-. - No. of Awards: 10

**Benefit Type:** Composite

**How to apply:** **Step 1:** The interested applicant downloads the [application form](https://art.py.gov.in/sites/default/files/kamban202367.pdf) from the [official website](https://art.py.gov.in/application-kamban-award). **Step 2:** In the application form, fill in all the mandatory fields, paste the passport-sized photograph (signed across, if required), and attach copies of all the mandatory documents (self-attest, if required). **Step 3:** Submit the duly filled and signed application form along with the documents to the Director of Art and Culture Department, No.1, Roman Roland Road, Puducherry 605 001. **Step 4:** Request a receipt or acknowledgment from the Director of Art and Culture Department to whom the application has been submitted. Ensure that the receipt contains essential details such as the date and time of submission, and a unique identification number (if applicable). **NOTE:** An advertisement will be made in the first week of January the eligible writers to submit their last year&#39;s book by January 31.
Mode: Offline.

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Arts and Culture Department, Puducherry.
