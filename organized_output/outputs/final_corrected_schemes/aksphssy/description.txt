# Asangathit Karmakar Samachar-Patr Hawker Cycle Sahayata Yojana

**Short Title:** AKSPHSSY

**Launch Date:** 2012-09-10

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Free Cycle, Empowerment, Vehicle

Labor Department of Chhattisgarh State started this scheme &quot;Asangathit Karmakar Samachar-Patr Hawker Cycle Sahayata Yojana&quot; for unorganized worker on 10 September 2010. Under this scheme, One cycle will be provided free of cost to each unorganized beneficiary. The main objective of the scheme to empower the unorganized Samaachaar-Patr Hokar worker through provide them free cycle.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Transport & Infrastructure; Sector: Public transport and private vehicles

**Where it applies:** Chhattisgarh; State

**Benefits / Assistance:** > A free of cost cycle will be provided.

**Benefit Type:** In Kind

**How to apply:** **Unregistered Applicant :** 1. All the applicant have to visit the official [website](https://cglabour.nic.in) Chhattisgarh Labour Department. 1.
