# Old Age Pension Scheme - Punjab

**Short Title:** Oapsp

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Financial Assistance, Welfare, Pension

Punjab State Government started a pension for old age person of the state people. The main objective is to provide financial security to those sections of society who doesn&#39;t have any stable source of income. its a DBT scheme so pension amount will directly transferred in the bank account of beneficiary. 

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Pension

**Where it applies:** Punjab; State

**DBT Scheme:** Yes (Direct Benefit Transfer)

**Benefits / Assistance:** Amount : ₹ 1,500/- per month

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**How to apply:** **Step-1:** Applicants have to visit the [official website.](https://connect.punjab.gov.in/service/citizenservice/108) **Step-2**: Click on the &quot;Click to apply services&quot;. **Step-3**: Now login on Digital Punjab portal with Email/Phone number and password. If does have any account then register [here](https://ssocitizen.psegs.in/register) **Step-4:** Fill out the application form. **Step-5**: Attach the required documents **Step-6**: Submit.
Mode: Online.

**Additional Application Processes:** **Process 2:** **Step-1:** Applicants have to visit the official website and download the [application form.](https://punjab.gov.in/wp-content/uploads/2023/08/OldAgePension-1_merged.pdf) **Step-2**: Beneficiary can get the Offline Application Form of Punjab Old Age Pension Scheme free of cost from any of the following Offices or Centre :- 1. Office of District Social Security Officer. 1. Office of Child Development Project Officer. 1. Panchayat and B.D.P.O Office. 1. SDM Office. 1. Anganwadi Centre. 1. Sewa Kendra **Step-3**: Fill out the application form. **Step-4**: Attach required documents. **Step-5**: Submit the Application Form of Punjab Old Age Pension Scheme along with all the documents in the same office or centre from where it was collected.

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Department of Social Security and Development of Women and Children.
