# Sanction of Motorized Three Wheeler to Orthopedically Challenged

**Short Title:** SMTWOC

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Empowerment, Person With Disability, Self-employed, Student

The A.P. Differently Abled &amp; Senior Citizens Assistance Corporation, Andhra Pradesh launched scheme &quot;Sanction of Motorized Three Wheeler to Orthopedically Challenged&quot; under its Action Plan 2017-18, aims to provide Motorized Three Wheelers to orthopedically challenged persons. The Motorized Three Wheelers are distributed by the Assistant Directors, Department for the Welfare of Differently Abled &amp; Senior Citizens, and District Managers, A.P. Differently Abled &amp; Senior Citizens Assistance Corporation.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Aids/Appliances

**Where it applies:** Andhra Pradesh; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - Eligible persons with disabilities are provided with a motorized three-wheeler (customized/adapted for their use) to enhance their independence, mobility, and access to education or livelihood opportunities. 

**Benefit Type:** In Kind

**How to apply:** **Step 01:** Visit the official website: [https://apdascac.ap.gov.in](https://apdascac.ap.gov.in) **Step 02:** On the homepage, click on &quot;[APDASCAC Schemes](https://apdascac.ap.gov.in/APDASCAC-Schemes/)&quot;. **Step 03:** Select the &quot;SANCTION OF ADULT STICKS&quot; option. **Step 04:** Click on &quot;Apply Now&quot; to begin the application process. **Step 05:** Fill in the required details and submit your application. > **Note:** Online applications will be allowed when the scheme is permitted by an advertisement in newspaper. 
Mode: Online.

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Dept of Social Welfare, WCD.
