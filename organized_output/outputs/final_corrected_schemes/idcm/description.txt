# Incentive For The Devadasi Children’s Marriage

**Short Title:** Idcm

**Launch Date:** 1989-01-01

**Scheme For:** Family

**Tags:** Devadasi, Marriage, Incentive, SC

Under this scheme, the Social Welfare Department, Government of Karnataka will give incentives to the Devadasi&#39;s Children&#39;s Married Couple. To avail this scheme, both bride and groom should belong to SC Community. The incentive will be provided only to those who have submitted applications within 1.5 years (18 months) of marriage. The couple who are married on or after 01-04-2018 are eligible for a revised rate of incentive (i.e. 3.00 lakh for men, 5.00 lakh for women) The annual family income of the applicant should be below Rs.5,00,000/-  The following are the eligibility criteria for this scheme - Applicant should be a resident of Karnataka state. - Applicant should belong to the SC community. - Family income from all sources should be within Rs. 5 lakhs per annum. - The scheme is applicable within 18 months of marriage. The following documents are required while applying for this scheme - Aadhaar - RD Number of Caste Certificate - RD Number of Income Certificate - Copy of the Bank Passbook - Marriage Registration Certificate - Marriage Photo - Mobile Number - Devadasi Registration Certificate
