# Khule Jalsroto Mei Pen Aadharit Matsya Paalan ki Yojana

**Short Title:** KJMPAMPKY

**Launch Date:** 2022-07-22

**Scheme For:** Infra

**Tags:** Financial Assistance, Productivity, Fish Farming, Group

The &quot;Khule Jalsroto Mei Pen Aadharit Matsya Paalan ki Yojana&quot; was launched by the Animal and Fisheries Resources Department, Government of Bihar. The scheme aims to increase fish productivity and production through semi-intensive/intensive fisheries of large-scale available government wetland water sources (Man, Chaur, and Lake, etc.), and strengthen aquaculture through species diversification. It seeks to increase the income of farmers/fishermen and provide new employment opportunities.       

**Who is this for:** Joint Liability Groups (JLGS); Category: Agriculture,Rural & Environment; Sector: Land and water resources

**Where it applies:** Bihar; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - **A 75% subsidy is provided on the fixed unit expenditure of ₹10,50,000/- per pen unit.**

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**How to apply:** > ***Registration Process:*** **Step-01:** The applicant visits the [official website](https://fisheries.bihar.gov.in/Default.aspx) and clicks the &quot;Applications are being invited for all the schemes of the financial year (2024-25), [click here](https://fisheries.bihar.gov.in/Accordion.aspx) to apply&quot;. **Step-02:** Select the &quot;[Registration for Fisheries Schemes](https://misfisheries.bihar.gov.in/RegisterFisheries.aspx)&quot; **Step-03:** Select the category: Individual and complete all basic details of the applicant for registration, including category, caste, applicant name, date of birth, address, bank details, etc. **Step-04:** Enter a valid 10-digit mobile number and click to send OTP. **Step-05:** An OTP will be sent to the provided mobile number. **Step-06:** Enter the OTP; after verification of the OTP, you will receive a Registration Number and Password. **Step-07:** Click on the &quot;Login&quot; and enter the Registration Number and Password.  > ***Application Form Filling Process:*** **Step-01:** The applicant visits the [official website](https://fisheries.bihar.gov.in/Default.aspx) and clicks the &quot;Applications are being invited for all the schemes of the financial year (2024-25), [click here](https://fisheries.bihar.gov.in/Accordion.aspx) to apply&quot;. **Step-02:** Click &quot;If your already register, [login here](https://misfisheries.bihar.gov.in/login.aspx)&quot;. **Step-03**: Select &quot;Application form&quot; under the &quot;scheme name&quot; option on the top menu. **Step-04:** Applicant need to provide the following details : - Details of RFF group - Permanent address - Details of members of Fishermen Cooperative Society and group - Bank account details - Land details - Financial availability of the scheme **Step-05**: Upload the required documents - Training Certificate - Land Lease Receipt - PAN/RFF Group Statement - Photocopy of Photo ID (Aadhaar/Photo ID Card recognized by Govt. of India) - Original copy of Consent Letter (in prescribed format) - Self attested photocopy of Bank Passbook or Cheque (with Bank Name, Branch Name, Account No., IFSC Code) **Step-06:** Click on the submit button and a receipt will be generated.
Mode: Online.
