# Mukhyamantri Baristha Bunakar and Karigara Sahayata Yojana

**Short Title:** MBBKSY

**Launch Date:** 2024-02-01

**Closing Date:** 2027-03-31

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Senior Artisans And Weavers, Baristha Bunakar, Karigara Sahayata, Financial Assistance

The &amp;quot;**Mukhyamantri Baristha Bunakar and Karigara Sahayata Yojana**&amp;quot; is a welfare scheme initiated by the Handlooms, Textiles and Handicrafts Department, Government of Odisha to provide financial assistance to elderly individuals involved in weaving and craft work, who may face economic difficulties. **Objective:-** 1. To provide social security to the baristha bunakar and karigar of the state &amp;amp; to ensure them a decent level of living. 1. To enable him/her free from additional financial burden due to illness, injury, need for care, unemployment &amp;amp; other unforeseen expenses, etc. 1. To take care of old age and other consequences resulting therefrom. **Note:** The Directorate of Textiles, Odisha and Directorate of Handicrafts, Odisha shall be the implementing agency of this scheme.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Financial assistance

**Where it applies:** Odisha; State

**Benefits / Assistance:** The financial assistance for weavers &amp;amp; ancillary workers /handicraft artisan shall be as follows: **S.No****Category****Age of the Beneficiary****Quantum of Assistance (in Rs.)**1FemaleMore than 40 years to less than 80 years20002MaleMore than 50 years to less than 80 years20003Both Female &amp;amp;amp;amp; Male80 years or more2500

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Exclusions / Not eligible:** The Financial Assistance granted to the beneficiary shall be cancelled on any of the following grounds: 1. On his/her death (or) 1. On the satisfaction of Director of Textile, Odisha/ Director of Handicrafts, Odisha that information furnished to the District Level Selection Committee was either erroneous, false or inconclusive (or) 1. On his/her ceasing to have the eligibilities referred in the guidelines.

**How to apply:** 1. Application in the prescribed format [**(Annexure-l)**](https://handloom.odisha.gov.in/sites/default/files/2024-04/Mukhyamantri%20Baristha%20Bunakar%20o%20Karigara%20Sahayata%20Yojana%20%28MBBKSY%29.pdf) along with required documents shall be submitted to The Director of Textiles &amp;amp; Handlooms and the Director of Handicrafts 1. After required scrutiny the qualified applications shall be placed before the District Level Selection Committee on quarterly basis. Beneficiary once enrolled and received assistance under this scheme, need not apply again.
Mode: Offline.

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: The Directorate Of Textiles, Odisha And Directorate Of Handicrafts; Nodal Department: Textiles and Handloom Department.
