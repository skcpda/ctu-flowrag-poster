# Post Metric Scholarship For OBC Students

**Short Title:** PMS

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Financial Assistance, Higher Education, Student Finance

Madhya Pradesh State government started a scholarship for student who belong to the OBC category. Now the students of the state will not be deprived of education due to financial constraints, because the Madhya Pradesh government will provide scholarships to the students. Through the scholarship, the state&amp;#39;s literacy rate will also increase and also will encourage the student to higher education.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Education & Learning

**Where it applies:** Madhya Pradesh; State

**Benefits / Assistance:** - The benefit of the scholarship depends on the annual family income of the students, institution, course, course subscriber, fee capping, etc. - All students whose annual family income is up to Rs 3,00,000, those students will be eligible for a 100% scholarship.

**Benefit Type:** In Kind

**How to apply:** 1. Applicants have to visit the [official website. ](http://www.scholarshipportal.mp.nic.in/Index.aspx)1. Applicants need to register himself/herself. 1. Fill out the required details. 1. Now login with credentials and fill out the application form. 1. And submit.
Mode: Online.

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: School Education Department.

**References / Annexures:** - **Guidelines**: http://www.scholarshipportal.mp.nic.in/Public/OtherSchemes/GB.aspx?ID=MTM=

**Additional Information:**
Briefdescription: This scheme is to provide financial assistance to the Other Backward Classes students studying at the post-matriculation stage to enable students to complete their education.
