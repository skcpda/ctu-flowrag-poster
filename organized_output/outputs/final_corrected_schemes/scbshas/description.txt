# Scheduled Caste Boys/Girls Housing Assistance Scheme

**Short Title:** SCBSHAS

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Scheduled Caste, Housing, Student, College

The scheme &quot;Scheduled Caste Boys/Girls Housing Assistance Scheme&quot; by the Tribal Welfare and Scheduled Caste (SC) Department, Government of Madhya Pradesh, provides financial support for residential facilities to Scheduled Caste students who are studying in colleges outside their native village/city, enabling them to continue their college education.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Housing & Shelter; Sector: Hostel service

**Where it applies:** Madhya Pradesh; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - ₹2,000/- per month, if the student is residing in a Divisional headquarters (Bhopal, Indore, Gwalior, Jabalpur, Ujjain). - ₹1,250/- per month, if the student is residing in Other District Headquarters. - ₹1,000/- per month, if the student is residing in a Tehsil/Development Block Headquarters.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**How to apply:** **Registration** **Step 1:** The applicant should visit the Madhya Pradesh Tribal Affairs &amp; Scheduled Caste Welfare Automation System [MPTAAS portal.](https://www.tribal.mp.gov.in/mptaas) **Step 2:** Click on &quot;New Beneficiary Profile [Registration](https://www.tribal.mp.gov.in/PMS/Registration/BeneficiaryRegistration/PersonalDetail).&quot; **Step 3:** Fill in all the required details. Fields marked with * are mandatory. Complete the form by entering all necessary information. **Step 4:** Upload all required documents, such as ID proof and other relevant certificates. **Step 5:** After completing the form and uploading the documents, click &quot;Submit&quot;. **Step 6:** After successful payment and submission, you will receive a registration number. **Application** **Step 1:** The applicant should visit the Madhya Pradesh Tribal Affairs &amp; Scheduled Caste Welfare Automation System [MPTAAS portal.](https://www.tribal.mp.gov.in/mptaas) **Step 2:** Enter your user ID, password, and captcha code, then click &quot;Login.&quot; **Step 3:** Choose the scheme you wish to apply for to avail the benefits. **Step 4:** Enter all required information and upload the necessary documents as prompted. **Step 5:** Finally, click on &quot;Submit Application&quot; to complete the process.
Mode: Online.
