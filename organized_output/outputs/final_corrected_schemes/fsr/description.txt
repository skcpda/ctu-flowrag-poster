# Fawara Sanyantra

**Short Title:** FSR

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Farming, Agriculture, Development, Fountain Plant

The Fawara Sanyantra under the Agriculture Department of the Rajasthan government will give 70% to 75% subsidy to the farmers for installing sprinkler plants. Through this farmers can irrigate more land by getting sprinkler subsidy. The subsidy given by the state government to the farmers will be sent directly to their bank accounts through DBT. To avail of the benefit of the Rajasthan Fawara Sanyantra Subsidy Yojana, a farmer should have land up to 0.2 hectares. So that the fields can be irrigated by installing sprinkler plants. The income of farmers will improve through this scheme. Apart from this, efficient utilization and production will also increase. Besides, 50% to 55% of water can be saved by irrigating the fields through sprinklers.  > **Important points** - If the plant purchase bill for the fountain plant is of the same financial year in which the farmer has applied, the grant will be payable to the eligible farmers. - If the sprinkler plant is found to be correct as per the criteria prescribed in the physical verification, the grant amount will be transferred directly to the farmer&#39;s account.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Agriculture,Rural & Environment

**Where it applies:** Rajasthan; State/ UT

**DBT Scheme:** Yes (Direct Benefit Transfer)

**Benefits / Assistance:** 1. The grant amount is 70% of the unit cost for general farmers. 1. The grant amount is 75% of the unit cost for small and marginal farmers, Scheduled Caste/Tribe farmers, and women farmers. 1. The grant is payable up to a maximum area of 5 hectares.

**Benefit Type:** Cash
