# Competition of Zomnivoilo Khell

**Short Title:** CZK

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Culture, Artist, Folk, Competition, Stage

The scheme &quot;Competition of Zomnivoilo Khell&quot; was launched by the Tiatr Academy under the Art, Culture and Museums Department, Government of Goa. The scheme aims to preserve and promote the traditional folk theatre of Goa called &#39;The Khell&#39;, also known as &#39;Fell&#39; or &#39;Zomnivoilo Khell&#39;, by organizing annual competitions in different parts of Goa to maintain this cultural heritage for future generations. The scheme provides financial assistance towards production costs for participating troupes and creates a platform for showcasing traditional Khell performances through structured competition format. The applicant must be an institution or individual with a Khell that has been staged during the current carnival season and must comply with the traditional format requirements including 8 to 10 cantos with specific duration limits. The scheme is implemented by the Tiatr Academy of Goa. The applications for this scheme are accepted offline through prescribed application forms. 

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Sports & Culture; Sector: Culture

**Where it applies:** Goa; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - Financial assistance towards production costs as fixed by Tiatr Academy of Goa from time to time. - Mementos awarded to first three winning positions (no cash prizes). - Video recording of all performances for preservation in Tiatr Academy of Goa library. - Complimentary Digital Versatile Disc copy of the performance provided to the director. - Platform for showcasing traditional Khell performances to wider audiences. 

**Benefit Type:** Composite

**How to apply:** **Step 1:** The interested applicant should take a printout of the [prescribed format](https://www.goa.gov.in/wp-content/uploads/2021/06/Citizen-Charter-TAG.pdf) of the application form. **Step 2:** In the application form, fill in all the mandatory fields, attach copies of all the mandatory documents (self-attest, if required). Sign the application form and mention the date. **Step 3:** Submit the duly filled and signed application form along with the documents to - ***Tiatr Academy of Goa, AS-1 / AS-4, Block A, 2nd Floor, Campal Trade Centre, Opposite Kala Academy, Campal, Panaji, Goa, India – 403001*** _*Applicants are advised to save a copy of the submitted application form and any acknowledgment._ ##### ***Selection Process*** If more than 12 applications are received, attend the meeting of all applicants where selection of 9 remaining troupes (after 3 direct entries from previous winners) will be done through lottery system. **Step 1:****** ***Await Communication*** Wait for communication regarding the date, time, and place of staging of the Khell performances which will be provided well in advance to all selected participating groups. **Step 2:****** ***Attend Mandatory Discussion*** Participate in the compulsory discussion session on positive and negative aspects of performances at the Conference Hall of Tiatr Academy of Goa, Block A, 2nd Floor, Campal Trade Centre, Campal, Panjim on the appointed date and time. **Step 3:****** ***Performance and Payment*** Perform the Khell as scheduled and receive the financial assistance cheque issued in the name of the applicant as mentioned in Serial Number 1 of the application form. 
Mode: Offline.
