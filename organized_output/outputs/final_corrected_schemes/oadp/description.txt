# Old Age And Destitute Pension

**Short Title:** Oadp

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Pudecherry, Widows, Unmarried, Transgender, Deserted Women

The Government of Puducherry introduced Old Age And Destitute Pension Scheme to extend a helping hand by way of monthly financial assistance to Old Age Persons/Widows/ Deserted Women/ Unmarried Women and Enuches so that they could defray some petty personal expenses without depending on others. > Amount of assistance - ₹1500/- per month for Old Age Persons, Destitute women, Widows, and Transgender with age less than 60 years. - ₹2000/- per month for Old Age Persons, Destitute women, Widows, and Transgender with age between 60 years and 79 years. - ₹3000/- per month for Old Age Persons, Destitute women, Widows, and Transgender with age 80 years and above.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment

**Where it applies:** Puducherry; State

**Benefits / Assistance:** - ₹1500/- per month for Old Age Persons, Destitute women, Widows, and Transgender with age less than 60 years. - ₹2000/- per month for Old Age Persons, Destitute women, Widows, and Transgender with age between 60 years and 79 years. - ₹3000/- per month for Old Age Persons, Destitute women, Widows, and Transgender with age 80 years and above.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**How to apply:** > Whom to Apply - Puducherry: The Deputy Director (Social Defence), Old Age Pension Section, Department of Women and Child Development, Puducherry.- Karaikal: The Child Development Project Officer, Karaikal.- Mahe/Yanam: The Welfare Officer, Mahe/ Yanam. > Processing Time - The selection is periodical and depends on the vacancies and on the availability of funds. > In case of delay whom to contact? - Puducherry: The Director, Department of Women and Child Development, Sithankudi, Puducherry.- Karaikal: The Collector, Karaikal.- Mahe/Yanam: The Regional Administrator, Mahe/Yanam.
Mode: Offline.

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Women and Child Development Department, Puducherry.
