# Vadh Se Bachaye Govansh Ko Sahayata Yojana

**Short Title:** VSBGKS

**Scheme For:** Infra

**Tags:** Animal Shelter, Cattle Welfare Scheme, Cow Protection, Animal Husbandry

The &amp;quot;Vadh Se Bachaye Govansh Ko Sahayata Yojana&amp;quot; scheme, by the Animal Husbandry Department, Rajasthan, aims to ensure the welfare of cattle saved from illegal slaughter or smuggling. Registered cow shelters (gaushalas), municipal bodies, Panchayati Raj institutions, or other approved organizations are entrusted with the custody of such cattle. Financial assistance is provided for their fodder and care for the duration of custody or up to one year, whichever is shorter.

**Who is this for:** Government Organisation, Joint Liability Groups (JLGS), Registered Societies, NGO; Category: Housing & Shelter; Sector: Shelter, integrated support and assistance

**Where it applies:** Rajasthan; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - Financial assistance of ₹40/day for large cattle and ₹20/day for small cattle. - Support is provided for a maximum of one (1) year or the custody period, whichever is shorter.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**How to apply:** **Step 1:** Collect the application form from the office of the District Joint Director, Department of Animal Husbandry. **Step 2:** Fill in the details and attach the required documents (FIR copy and approval). **Step 3:** Submit the application to the office of the District Joint Director, Department of Animal Husbandry. **Step 4:** The application will be reviewed and approved by the District-level Gopalan Committee. **Step 5:** Upon approval, funds will be disbursed to the organization. &gt; **Scheme Contact Details:** &gt; Nodal Officer Name &amp;amp; Designation: Dr Kaushal Kumar Verma; Joint Director &gt; Phone Number: 9414207430 &gt; Email: dir.dgs@rajasthan.gov.in
