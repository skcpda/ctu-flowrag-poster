# Bharati Scheme for Education - Intermediate or Equivalent

**Short Title:** BSE-IE

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Financial Assistance, Student, Polytechnic, ITI

The &quot;Bharati Scheme for Education - Intermediate or Equivalent (ITI, Polytechnic, D.Ed, TTC, D.Pharmacy, etc.)&quot; introduced by the Andhra Pradesh Brahmin Welfare Corporation aims to encourage Brahmin students of Andhra Pradesh to continue their education. Under this scheme, eligible poor Brahmin student applicants will receive financial assistance for the academic year. This scheme applies to the Brahmin students of Andhra Pradesh, covering all 13 districts. The Andhra Pradesh Brahmin Welfare Corporation implements the scheme. 

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Education & Learning; Sector: Financial Assistance

**Where it applies:** Andhra Pradesh; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** 1. Financial Assistance: ₹10,000/-. 1. Intended Use: To encourage Brahmin students to continue their education.  > **Mode of Disbursement:** The amount of Financial Assistance granted will be credited through online to the SB Accounts of the students. 

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Exclusions / Not eligible:** The applicant should not have availed himself/herself any amount under any other Government scheme for the same purpose. 

**How to apply:** > **Registration** **Step-01:** Applicants have to visit the [official website](https://www.andhrabrahmin.ap.gov.in/) and click on [Scheme](https://www.andhrabrahmin.ap.gov.in/schemes/schemes.aspx#). **Step-02:** Click on &quot;Registration&quot; under the concerned scheme name. **Step-03:** Fill out the application form with required details like: - Personal Details: Name, Residence address, Aadhaar no, Ration card no, Bank detail etc - Upload: Photo, Signature and Certificate Details - Documents: Birth Certificate, Residential Certificate, Aadhaar Card, Caste Certificate, Bank Passbook etc - Finalize and &quot;Submit&quot; the application. **Step-04:** Receive the reference number after the submission.  > **Check Application Status:** **Step-01:** Click on the “Services” link on the menu and select &quot;[Know your status](https://www.andhrabrahmin.ap.gov.in/eSchemes/know_stat.aspx)&quot;. **Step-02:** Enter the Reference or Mobile or Aadhaar Card Number to get the application status. **Step-03:** Select the &quot;Financial Year&quot; and Fetch the details.  > **Download the Acknowledgement slip:** **Step-01:** Applicants have to visit the [official website](https://www.andhrabrahmin.ap.gov.in/) and click on [Scheme](https://www.andhrabrahmin.ap.gov.in/schemes/schemes.aspx#). **Step-02:** Click on the &quot;Acknowledgement Slip&quot; under the concerned scheme. **Step-03:** Enter the Aadhaar Card Number and select the &quot;Scheme&quot;. **Step-04:** Click on the &quot;Submit&quot;.
Mode: Online.

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Andhra Pradesh Brahmin Welfare Corporation.
