# Personal Accident Assistance Scheme

**Short Title:** PAAS

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Agricultural Laborers, Farmers, Mandi Workers, Agricultural Accidents, Compensation

Under the Uttarakhand State Mandi Act, the Personal Accident Assistance Scheme provides compensation in cases of injury or death caused by accidents occurring during agricultural activities or the use of farm-related equipment. It covers farmers, agricultural laborers, and mandi workers operating in regulated mandi areas. > **Covered activities include:** - Use of electric agricultural equipment - Digging/deepening of wells and ponds for irrigation - Use of tractors in agriculture - Threshing and harvesting - Any accident while performing farming tasks Compensation is disbursed by Mandi Parishads (Market Committees) under the supervision of the Mandi Board. 

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Agriculture,Rural & Environment; Sector: Financial assistance

**Where it applies:** Uttarakhand; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - Compensation for death or physical injury due to agricultural accidents. - Coverage includes regulated mandi area farmers, agri-laborers, and mandi workers. 

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Exclusions / Not eligible:** - Accidents not related to agricultural activities - Incidents occurring outside the jurisdiction of the Mandi Samiti.

**How to apply:** 1. The accident must be reported to the Mandi Committee/authority within 45 days. 1. The committee will initiate an investigation within 15 days of receiving the intimation. 1. In case of death, testimonies from two relatives or an authority must be attached. 1. The claim form must be verified by the Gram Pradhan or a Panchayat member.  > **Submit the following:** 1. Filled claim form 1. Signature/thumb/finger/body part impression (as applicable) 1. Medical or death certificate 1. Accident report from the Mandi Committee - The Mandi Secretary will forward the complete file for approval. - After verification, the Chairperson/Administrator sends it for final approval. - If approved, a demand draft is issued to the beneficiary via the Mandi Secretary. - Approval generally takes 1 month (may be extended under special conditions). 
Mode: Offline.

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Agriculture Department.
