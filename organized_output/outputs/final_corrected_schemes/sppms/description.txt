# Sweekruti: Pre & Post Matric Scholarship

**Short Title:** SPPMS

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Scholarship, Sweekruti, Student, Transgender, Post Matric, Pre Matric

The scheme &amp;quot;Pre &amp;amp; Post Matric Scholarship&amp;quot; is a component of the “Sweekruti” scheme, implemented by the Social Security and Empowerment of Persons with Disabilities (SSEPD) Department, Government of Odisha. The objective of the component is to provide financial assistance to Transgender students studying at post matriculation or post-secondary stage to enable them to complete their education. These scholarships are available to transgender students of Odisha for studies in India only and are awarded where the applicant is studying.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Education & Learning; Sector: Scholarships and student finance

**Where it applies:** Odisha; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:**  **Category****Rate/ Per Month**Pre-Matric (From Std. V to X) ₹1,000/-Post-Matric (From Std. XI-XII)₹1,500/-General Graduate/ Post Graduate Courses₹2,000/-Professional Degrees/ Diplomas/ Certificates₹2,500/-Open University/ Non-formal Courses₹3,000/-

**Benefit Type:** Cash
