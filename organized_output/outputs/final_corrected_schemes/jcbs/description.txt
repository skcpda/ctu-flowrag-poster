# J.C. Bose Fellowship

**Short Title:** JCBS

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Fellowship, Science, Research, Scientist, Bose

The “J.C. Bose fellowship” is awarded to active scientists in recognition of their outstanding performance. The fellowship is scientist-specific and very selective. The scientist should be in service at the time of nomination to this fellowship. The nominee should be an Indian national working in institutions in India. &gt; **Duration of Fellowship:** - The duration of the fellowship will be initially for five years. In case, the fellow superannuates during the term of the fellowship, the fellowship can be continued if a host institution is willing to host the fellowship - The tenure of the fellowship may be extended for the subsequent term of 5 years based on a rigorous assessment of research performance during the tenure of the fellowship. A minimum of 25% of the applicants seeking an extension will not be granted the extension.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Education & Learning; Sector: Education and training grants, fellowship, stipend

**Where it applies:** Central

**Benefits / Assistance:** 1. The fellowship amount is ₹25,000/- per month in addition to regular income. 1. Research grant of ₹15 lakhs per annum. 1. Overhead of ₹1.00 lakh per annum to the host institute.

**Benefit Type:** Composite
