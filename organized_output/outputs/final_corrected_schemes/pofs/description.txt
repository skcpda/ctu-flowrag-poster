# Production of Foundation Seeds

**Short Title:** POFS

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Agriculture, Farmer, Seed Production, Foundation Seeds

The scheme “Production of Foundation Seeds” by the Agriculture-Farmers Welfare Department, Tamil Nadu, is designed to support farmers in the production of foundation seeds, particularly for pulses. Eligible farmers receive a subsidy. Special benefits are provided for SC/ST farmers (24%) and farm women (20%).  

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Agriculture,Rural & Environment; Sector: Financial assistance

**Where it applies:** Tamil Nadu; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - **Financial Assistance:** ₹1,000/- per quintal.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**How to apply:** **Step 1:** Collect the application form from the nearest Agricultural Office. **Step 2:** Fill out the application form with the required details and attach mandatory documents (self-attested, if required). **Step 3:** Submit the form to the concerned office: &gt; **Village Level:** Submit the application to the Assistant Agricultural Officer. &gt; **Block Level:** Submit to the Agricultural Officer or Deputy Agricultural Officer. &gt; **District Level:** Submit to the Joint Director of Agriculture.
Mode: Offline.

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Agriculture Department.

**References / Annexures:** - **Guidelines**: https://www.tn.gov.in/scheme/data_view/6891

**Additional Information:**
Briefdescription: The "Production of Foundation Seeds" scheme, provides subsidies to farmers cultivating seed farms for pulses, offering specific benefits to SC/ST farmers and farm women.
