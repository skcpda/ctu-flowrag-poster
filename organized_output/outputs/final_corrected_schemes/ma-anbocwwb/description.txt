# Marriage Assistance (ANBOCWWB)

**Short Title:** MA-ANBOCWWB

**Scheme For:** Family

**Tags:** Marriage, Financial Assistance, Building Worker, Construction Worker, Labour

The scheme “Marriage Assistance” was started by the Andaman &amp;amp; Nicobar Islands Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board (ANBOCWWB), Department of Labour, Employment &amp;amp; Training, Andaman &amp;amp; Nicobar Administration. Under this scheme, registered construction workers will receive a financial allowance to cover either their own marriage expenses or those of their two dependent children.

**Who is this for:** Family, Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Marriage

**Where it applies:** Andaman and Nicobar Islands; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** 1. Under this scheme, registered construction workers will receive financial assistance of ₹20,000/- to cover either their own marriage expenses or those of their two dependent children.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**How to apply:** &gt; **Portal Registration Process:** **Step 01:** Visit online Portal: [https://serviceonline.gov.in/login.do](https://serviceonline.gov.in/login.do) **Step 02:** On the home page, click on ‘Register’ and fill in all the mandatory details. **Step 03:** Click on the ‘Submit’ button and validate your Email ID &amp;amp; Mobile Number using OTP received. **Step 04:** After successful validation, the applicant will be registered. &gt; **Registration Process of Construction Workers under A&amp;amp;N Islands Building &amp;amp; Other Construction Workers Welfare Board:** **Step 01:** Applicant can [login](https://serviceonline.gov.in/loginWindow.do) through their email ID that is used while registering **Step 02:** Enter Password &amp;amp; Captcha **Step 03:** Click on ‘Login’ **Step 04:** After successful login, click on ‘Apply for Services’ and then ‘View all Available Services’ **Step 05:** Now, search for the “Registration of Construction Workers in AN BOCWWB” and then click to proceed registration process. **Step 06:** Fill in all the mandatory details and upload all the relevant documents. **Step 07:** Preview the form and click on ‘Submit’. **Step 08:** After successful registration, the applicant will receive a registration ID. &gt; **Application Process for Marriage Financial Assistance:** **Step 01:** Now, to avail the benefits of the scheme, the applicants can [login](https://serviceonline.gov.in/loginWindow.do) through their email ID that is used while registering **Step 02:** Enter Password &amp;amp; Captcha **Step 03:** Click on ‘Login’ **Step 04:** After successful login, click on ‘Apply for Services’ and then ‘View all Available Services’ **Step 05:** Now, search for the ‘Marriage Financial Assistance’ and click on the scheme to proceed to fill out the application form. **Step 06:** Fill in all the mandatory details and upload all the mandatory documents. **Step 07:** Preview the application form and click on ‘Submit’. &gt; **Application Tracking:** **Step 01:** To track the application status, the applicant can visit the online Portal and [login](https://serviceonline.gov.in/loginWindow.do) through their ‘User ID’ &amp;amp; ‘Password’. **Step 02:** On the home page, click on “View Application Status’ and then click on ‘Track Application Status’. **Step 03:** Enter the Application Reference Number and click on ‘Get Data’ to know the status of your application.
Mode: Online.

**Additional Application Processes:** **Process 2:** The applicant may submit the application form himself/herself or through the contractor to the concerned ANBOCWWB Office along with all the relevant documents. - [Application Form](https://labour.and.nic.in/webPages/forms/welfareboard.pdf) for Registration as a Beneficiary - [Application Form](https://labour.and.nic.in/webPages/forms/Marr.pdf) for Marriage Assistance
