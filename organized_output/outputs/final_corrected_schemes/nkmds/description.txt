# Nirmaan Kamgar Mrtyu Va Divyangta Sahayta Yojana 

**Short Title:** NKMDS

**Launch Date:** 2022-08-04

**Scheme For:** Family

**Tags:** Financial Assistance, Social Welfare, Health & Wellness

The Nirman Kamgar Mrtyu Va Divyangta Sahayata Yojana Uttar Pradesh is a crucial scheme launched by the Uttar Pradesh government for the welfare of registered construction workers in the state. It provides financial assistance to the families of construction workers in case of death or disability due to accident or illness.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Health & Wellness; Sector: Financial assistance

**Where it applies:** Uttar Pradesh; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** > **Death Benefits for register construction worker who die due to an accident:** - Total amount will be ₹5,25,000/-. 1. To dependents of a registered construction worker who dies due to an accident : ₹5,00,000/- as approximately ₹ 9,395/- per month for 5 years (as per interest rate). 1. For funeral expenses: ₹25,000/- lump sum.  > **Death Benefits for register construction worker who die due to natural causes:** - Total amount will be ₹2,25,000/-. 1. To dependents of a registered construction worker who dies due to an accident : ₹2,00,000/- as approximately ₹ 8,736/- per month for 2 years (as per interest rate). 1. For funeral expenses: ₹25,000/- lump sum.  > **Death Benefits for register construction worker who die due at the work place:** - Total amount: ₹1,00,000/- lump sum.  > **Disability Benefits for register construction worker who suffer 100% permanent disability due to an accident or illness** - Total amount will be ₹4,00,000/-. 1. Beneficiary will get approximately ₹ 9,172/- per month for 4 years (as per interest rate).  > **Disability Benefits for registered construction workers with permanent disabilities between 50% and 99% due to accidents or illness.** - Total amount will be ₹3,00,000/-. 1. Beneficiary will get approximately ₹ 8,953/- per month for 3 years (as per interest rate).  > **Disability Benefits for registered construction workers with permanent disabilities between 26% and 49% due to accidents or illness.** - Total amount will be ₹2,00,000/-. 1. Beneficiary will get approximately ₹ 8,736/- per month for 2 years (as per interest rate).

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**How to apply:** > **How to get register** 1. Applicant must visit the [official web portal.](https://www.uplmis.in/Guest/frm_createlogin_forlabs.aspx)1. Provide the required details - Enter Aadhaar Number. - Choose Circle. - Choose District. - Mobile Number. 1. Submit the registration request.  > **How to submit the application** 1. Applicant must visit the official [web portal](https://www.uplmis.in/Guest/frm_CreateLogin.aspx?type=2).1. Provide the required details - Choose Circle. - Choose Scheme name. - Enter Registered Aadhaar Number. - Enter Registered Mobile Number. 1. Click on &quot;Open Application form&quot;. 1. Provide the required details in application form. - Applicant/Worker details - Child details - Bank Account Details - Submit the required documents 1. Submit the application request.
Mode: Online.

**Additional Application Processes:** **Process 2:** > **How to get register and apply for the scheme** 1. Applicant must visit the below offices - Nearest labor office. - Tehsildar of the concerned tehsil. - Block Development Officer of the concerned development block. 1. Get the application form and fill out the completely. 1. Attach required documents in the prescribed format. 1. Submit the application form in the same office.

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Labour Department, Uttar Pradesh.
