# Pashu Palan Yojana (GSKVN)

**Short Title:** PPYGSKVN

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Loan, Animal Husbandry, Sanitation Worker, Self-employment, Scavenger

The scheme &amp;#39;Pashu Palan Yojana&amp;#39; is implemented by the Gujarat Safai Kamdar Vikas Nigam (GSKVN) under the Social Justice and Empowerment Department, Government of Gujarat. The scheme aims to provide loans to sanitation workers and their dependents to promote self-employment through animal husbandry and related activities. 

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Agriculture,Rural & Environment; Sector: Animal husbandry

**Where it applies:** Gujarat; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - Loan up to ₹1,00,000/- is provided at an interest rate of 6% to sanitation workers or their dependents for initiating animal husbandry as a business or occupation. 

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**How to apply:** > **Registration Process:** **Step 1:** Visit the Social Justice &amp; Empowerment Department website: [https://esamajkalyan.gujarat.gov.in](https://esamajkalyan.gujarat.gov.in/index.aspx) **Step 2:** Click on “Please Register Here” on the homepage. **Step 3:** Fill in your personal details as per Aadhaar. **Step 4**: Create a password and submit the form. **Step 5:** Verify details and click “Confirm”. **Step 6:** You will receive your User ID &amp; Password via SMS/email. > **Login Process:** **Step 1:** Go to the portal homepage. **Step 2:** Enter your User ID, Password, and Captcha. **Step 3**: Click “Login” to access your dashboard. > **Application Process:** After login, complete your User Profile (first-time only). **Step 1:** Homepage will show eligible schemes – click on the one you want. **Step 2:** Fill out the scheme application form. **Step 3:** Upload the required documents. **Step 4:** Review all entries, agree to terms, and click “Save Application”. **Step 5:** Note your Application Number for tracking. > **Track Application:** **Step 1:** Click on “[View Application Status](https://esamajkalyan.gujarat.gov.in/ViewApplicationStatus.aspx)”. **Step 2:** Enter your Application Number and Date of Birth. **Step 3:** Click “View Status” to check progress. 
Mode: Online.

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: Gujarat Safai Kamdar Vikas Nigam; Nodal Department: Social Justice and Empowerment Department.
