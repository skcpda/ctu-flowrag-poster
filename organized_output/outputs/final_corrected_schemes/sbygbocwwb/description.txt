# Shramik Basera Yojana (GBOCWWB)

**Short Title:** SBY(GBOCWWB)

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Building Worker, Construction Worker, Labour, Basera, Accommodation Facility

The “Shramik Basera Yojana” is implemented by the Gujarat Building and Other Construction Worker’s Welfare Board (GBOCWWB), Labour, Skill Development &amp;amp; Employment Department, Government of Gujarat. Under the scheme, accommodation facilities will be provided to registered construction workers in temporary prefabricated housing with basic amenities that can be dismantled.

**Who is this for:** Family, Individual; Category: Housing & Shelter; Sector: Short home Stay

**Where it applies:** Gujarat; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** 1. Under this scheme, accommodation facilities will be provided to the laborers/workers at the rate of ₹5/- per day.

**Benefit Type:** In Kind

**How to apply:** &gt; **Application Process for ‘E-Nirman Card’ under the GBOCWWB:** **Step 01:** The applicant may visit the e-Nirman Portal: [https://enirmanbocw.gujarat.gov.in/](https://enirmanbocw.gujarat.gov.in/) **Step 02:** On the home page, under the tab ‘Login to Portal’, click on ‘[Please Register Here](Registration: https://enirmanbocw.gujarat.gov.in/Registration.aspx)’. **Step 03:** Enter all the mandatory information and then click on ‘Register’. **Step 04**: After successful registration, the applicants can login through their User ID and Password. **Step 05:** Now, update your profile by entering all the necessary details as required and click on ‘Update’. **Step 06:** Select the ‘Construction Works Form’ and fill out all the mandatory information in the form. **Step 07:** Upload all the relevant documents, agree with the terms &amp;amp; conditions, and submit the form. **Step 08:** Once the application has been submitted, an application Number is generated which can be kept for future reference. &gt; **Process to track Application Status:** **Step 01:** The applicant may visit the e-Nirman Portal: [https://enirmanbocw.gujarat.gov.in/](https://enirmanbocw.gujarat.gov.in/) **Step 02:** On the home page, click on ‘View Citizen Application Status’. **Step 03:** Enter the Application Number and Date of Birth. **Step 04:** Now click on ‘View Status’. &gt; **Application Process for Scheme Benefit:** - Prefabricated temporary accommodation for the laborers/workers will be constructed near their workplace/Kadianaka. - The registered construction workers can avail the benefits by visiting there.
Mode: Offline.
