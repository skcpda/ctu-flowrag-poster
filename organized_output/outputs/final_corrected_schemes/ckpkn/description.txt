# Contract Khel Prashikshakon Ki Niyukti

**Short Title:** CKPKN

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Sports, Employment, Coach Appointment, Sports Trainer

The scheme “Contract Khel Prashikshakon Ki Niyukti” implemented by the Department Of Sports, Uttarakhand, aims to appoint contract-based sports coaches to conduct local training camps for various sports across the state, benefiting approximately 250 coaches annually with employment. Coaches aged 21-70 years, with relevant qualifications, can apply, and permanent residency is not mandatory. 

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Sports & Culture

**Where it applies:** Uttarakhand; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - **Employment Opportunity:** Provides annual employment to approximately 250 sports coaches on a contract basis. - **Promotion of Sports:** Facilitates the organization of local training camps for various sports, contributing to sports development. - **Skill Utilization:** Offers a platform for qualified individuals with sports expertise to contribute to athlete development.

**Benefit Type:** In Kind

**How to apply:** - **Advertisement:** An advertisement for the Yojana is likely issued annually around March, typically through daily newspapers. - **Application Submission:** Interested candidates should apply based on the guidelines and deadlines mentioned in the advertisement. - **Selection:** Applications are submitted to the District Sports Officers, who evaluate and forward eligible candidates. - **Appointment:** Coaches are selected on a contractual basis to conduct training camps.
Mode: Offline.

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Department Of Sports.

**References / Annexures:** - **Guidelines (Page No. 79)**: https://uk.gov.in/department92/library_file/file-30-01-2025-04-25-16.pdf

**Additional Information:**
Briefdescription: The scheme facilitates the annual appointment of contract-based sports trainers for conducting various local training camps across Uttarakhand, thereby promoting employment opportunities and strengthening grassroots sports infrastructure.
