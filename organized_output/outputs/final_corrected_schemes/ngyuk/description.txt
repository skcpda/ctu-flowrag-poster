# Nishulk Ganavesh Yojana

**Short Title:** NGYUK

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Student, Scheduled Castes, Scheduled Caste, Scheduled Tribe, Girl Student, BPL

The “Nishulk Ganavesh Yojana” is a scheme under the Samagra Shiksha Programme, implemented by the Secondary Education Department, Government of Uttarakhand. The scheme aims to provide free uniforms to all girl students studying in Classes 1 to 8 of government and government-aided schools, as well as to all boys belonging to Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes, and boys from the General category who fall under the Below Poverty Line category. 

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Education & Learning; Sector: Resources for schools

**Where it applies:** Uttarakhand; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** Under this scheme, two sets of uniforms per student are provided through DBT/SMC to students studying in classes 1 to 8 of government and government-aided schools, covering all girls, all boys belonging to Scheduled Caste and Scheduled Tribe categories, and boys from the General category belonging to BPL families. 

**Benefit Type:** In Kind

**How to apply:** There is no selection process; all eligible students from Classes 1 to 8 are provided free uniforms or the corresponding financial assistance. 
Mode: Offline.

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: Secondary Education Department, Government Of Uttarakhand; Nodal Department: Department Of Education.
