# Aatmanirbhar Gujarat Scheme for assistance to MSMEs: Net SGST reimbursement to Micro, Small and Medium Manufacturing Enterprise (MSMEs):

**Short Title:** AGMSMECSGST

**Launch Date:** 2022-10-05

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Business, Entrepreneurship, MSMEs, Interest Subsidy

The &amp;quot;Aatmanirbhar Gujarat Scheme for MSMEs&amp;quot; is an umbrella scheme launched by the Industries and Mines Department, Gujarat. It aims to empower the state&amp;#39;s dynamic MSME sector, which significantly contributes to employment, GDP, and exports. Effective from October 5, 2022, to October 4, 2027, this initiative supports growth, investments, and the vision of &amp;quot;Aatmanirbhar Gujarat&amp;quot; and &amp;quot;Aatmanirbhar Bharat.&amp;quot; The component &amp;quot;Net SGST reimbursement to Micro, Small and Medium Manufacturing Enterprise (MSMEs)&amp;quot; provides reimbursement of net SGST based on the taluka category of the project.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Business & Entrepreneurship; Sector: Setting up / start-up / entrepreneurship

**Where it applies:** Gujarat; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** **Category of Taluka** **Quantum of Incentive** Category 1100% of net SGST for 10 years upto 7.5% of eFCl p.a. Category 2 90% of net SGST for 10 years upto 6.5% of eFCl p.a. Category 3 &amp;amp;amp;amp; Municipal Corporation Areas80% of net SGST for 10 years upto 5% of eFCI p.a.  > **Note:** The period of 10 years shall be from the date of commencement of commercial production.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Exclusions / Not eligible:** - Enterprises that have already availed incentives for the same GFCI under any state government scheme are ineligible unless specifically allowed. - Enterprises engaging in resale of goods are ineligible. - Enterprises showing inter-State sales as intra-State sales to claim higher incentives are disqualified and liable for repayment with interest. - Failure to submit certified details of reimbursement claims will render the enterprise ineligible. &lt;br&gt; &lt;br&gt; &lt;br&gt; &lt;br&gt; &lt;br&gt; &lt;br&gt;

**How to apply:** > **Registration Process:** **Step 1:** Visit the [Investor Facilitation Portal (IFP)](https://ifp.gujarat.gov.in/DIGIGOV/) portal. Click on &quot;New Investor Registration&quot; to access the registration form. **Step 2:** Fill in the Registration Form. Click on &quot;Submit&quot;. **Step 3:** Verify the E-mail ID using the link sent on your registered E-mail ID. **Step 4:** After verification, you will be registered successfully.  > **Post-Registration Process:** **Step 1:** Visit the [Investor Facilitation Portal (IFP)](https://ifp.gujarat.gov.in/DIGIGOV/) portal. Click on &quot;Login&quot;. **Step 2:** Enter the email address and password you used during registration. **Step 3:** Click the &quot;Login&quot; button to access your account. **Step 4:** Now fill in the required details and mandatory documents about your business to complete the application process.  > **Application for Provisional/Final Eligibility Certificate for Net SGST:** After commencement of commercial production, the eligible enterprise, shall submit an application for provisional eligibility certificate within 1 year from the date of commencement of commercial production or within one year from the date of issue of this G.R., whichever is later. > **Procedure for claim of assistance for Net SGST:** The eligible enterprise shall submit the claim application for assistance, at the interval of every 3 months, to Sanctioning &amp; disbursing authority. > **Helpdesk:** [Contact here.](https://ifp.gujarat.gov.in/DIGIGOV/IFP-pages/contact.jsp?pagedisp=static)
Mode: Online.

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Industries and Mines Department.
