# Mrityu Ki Dasha Me Antyeshti Evam Anugrah Sahayata Yojana

**Short Title:** MAEAS

**Launch Date:** 2017-01-01

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Construction Worker, Financial Assistance, Labour

The &quot;Mrityu ki Dasha Mein Antyeshti Evam Anugrah Sahayata Yojana&quot; scheme, implemented by the Madhya Pradesh Labor Department since 2004. The objective of the scheme is to provide financial assistance to the dependents of registered construction workers in Madhya Pradesh in the case of their death. The scheme has the following financial assistance aims: - Alleviate the immediate financial burden on the family caused by funeral expenses. - Offer some financial support during a difficult time after the loss of a breadwinner. - Additionally, the scheme offers ex-gratia payments in case of permanent or partial disability due to an accident, further aiming to provide some financial security to registered workers who may face challenges due to an injury sustained while working. 

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Financial assistance

**Where it applies:** Madhya Pradesh; State/ UT

**DBT Scheme:** Yes (Direct Benefit Transfer)

**Benefits / Assistance:** 1. Funeral assistance- ₹6,000/- 1. Ex-gratia payment- - In case of normal death : ₹2,00,000/- - In case of death due to accident : ₹4,00,000/- - In case of permanent disability due to accident : ₹2,00,000/- - In case of partial permanent disability due to accident : ₹1,00,000/-

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**How to apply:** 1. The applicant has to submit the application form in the prescribed format and within the time limit to the office of the designated officer. - Rural Area - Chief Executive Officer, Janpad Panchayat - Urban Area - Commissioner, Municipal Corporation/Chief Municipal Officer, Municipality/Municipal Council 1. Apply duration - Within 06 months from the death of the construction worker.
Mode: Offline.

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Labour Department.
