# Scholarship for Self Finance Colleges Students of NT/DNT

**Short Title:** SFSFCS

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Higher Education, Scholarship, Nomadic And Denotified Tribe

The scheme &amp;quot;Scholarship for Self Finance Colleges Students of NT/DNT&amp;quot; by the Social Justice and Empowerment Department, Government of Gujarat, aims to support students belonging to Nomadic Tribes and De-notified Tribes (NT/DNT) categories in accessing quality education in self-financed colleges by reducing the financial burden. Through this scheme, financial assistance of up to ₹50,000/- is provided to cover tuition fees, registration charges, education fees, and other scholarship-related expenses for students enrolled in self-financed colleges. The scheme is implemented by the Social Justice and Empowerment Department, Government of Gujarat, and seeks to empower NT/DNT students with improved access to higher education opportunities. 

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Education & Learning; Sector: Universities and higher education

**Where it applies:** Gujarat; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - **Financial Assistance:** Up to ₹50,000/- for tuition fee, registration fee, education fee, and other scholarship-related charges.

**Benefit Type:** Cash
