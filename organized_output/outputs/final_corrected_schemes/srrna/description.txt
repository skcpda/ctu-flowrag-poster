# State Reward to the Recipient of National Award

**Short Title:** SRRNA

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Award, Culture, Artist, Literature, Music, Dance

The scheme &quot;State Reward to the Recipient of National Award&quot; has been designed by the Directorate of Art and Culture, Government of Goa as a token of appreciation in order to support and promote the local artists, who have received recognition at the National and International levels.  > **Objectives** 1. To give State recognition to the local artists receiving the National Award. 1. To appreciate local talents and promote local artists. 1. To give financial support to local artists.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Sports & Culture; Sector: Culture

**Where it applies:** Goa; State

**Benefits / Assistance:** A memento, a certificate of appreciation and a financial purse to the extent of ₹ 1,00,000/- per individual.

**Benefit Type:** Composite

**Exclusions / Not eligible:** 1. No award shall be given posthumously. 1. The Sahitya Akademi Award for translation will not be considered for this scheme.

**How to apply:** **Step 1:** The interested applicant should visit the Directorate Of Art &amp; Culture, Government of Goa, 5th Floor, Shram Shakti Bhavan, Panaji, Patto, Panaji, Goa - 403001 **Step 2:** Take a print of the proforma of the application form of the respective scheme, or request a hard copy from the concerned authority. **Step 3:** In the application form, fill in all the mandatory fields, paste the passport-sized photograph (signed across), and attach copies of all the mandatory documents (self-attest if required). **Step 4:** Submit the duly filled and signed application form along with the documents to the Director or to the ACO-III, Directorate of Art &amp; Culture, Phone: 0832-2404606, Email: aco6-dac.goa@nic.in
Mode: Offline.

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Art, Culture and Museums Department.
