# Encouragement Scheme for Taking Admission in Science and Contemporary Subjects

**Short Title:** ESTASCS

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Science Stream, Contemporary Subject, Student, Scholarship, Scheduled Tribe, Bachelor Of Science

The &amp;quot;Encouragement Scheme for Taking Admission in Science and Contemporary Subjects&amp;quot; scheme, launched by the Tribal Affairs Department, Government of Madhya Pradesh, aims to encourage student for taking admission in Science and Contemporary subjects. This scheme provides scholarships to Scheduled Tribe students who have passed Class 10 and enrolled in Class 11 Science stream, and to those who have passed Class 12 and enrolled in a B.Sc. course in Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics, or Biology.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Education & Learning; Sector: Scholarships and student finance

**Where it applies:** Madhya Pradesh; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** Under this scheme, scholarships will be provided to Scheduled Tribe students at the following rates: - **Students who passed Class 10th and are enrolled in Class 11th in the Science stream:** ₹2,000/-- **Students who passed Class 12th and are enrolled in a B.Sc. course in Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics, or Biology:** ₹3,000/-

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**How to apply:** &gt; **Registration Process:** **Step 1:** Go to the [official website](https://www.tribal.mp.gov.in/) of the Tribal Welfare Department of Madhya Pradesh. **Step 2:** On the home page, click on ‘[MPTAASC](https://www.tribal.mp.gov.in/MPTAAS)’. **Step 3:** Click on &amp;quot;[New Beneficiary Profile Registration](https://www.tribal.mp.gov.in/PMS/Registration/BeneficiaryRegistration/PersonalDetail)&amp;quot; under the ‘Login’ section. **Step 4:** Complete all mandatory fields marked with * and upload the required documents. **Step 5:** Review your details and click &amp;quot;Submit.&amp;quot; **Step 6:** After submission, check your email and SMS for your user ID and password. &gt; **Application Process:** **Step 1:** Revisit the [official website](https://www.tribal.mp.gov.in/) of the Tribal Welfare Department of Madhya Pradesh. **Step 2:** On the home page, click on ‘[MPTAASC](https://www.tribal.mp.gov.in/MPTAAS)’. **Step 3:** Enter your user ID, password, and captcha code, then click &amp;quot;Login.&amp;quot; **Step 4:** On the dashboard, click &amp;quot;PMS&amp;quot; and then select &amp;quot;Apply for Scheme.&amp;quot; **Step 5:** Fill out the online application form, upload the necessary documents, and submit it. **Step 6:** After successful submission, print a copy of the application for your records. **Note 1:** It is mandatory for the applicant to register the beneficiary profile. **Note 2:** Current status information is sent to the registered email and SMS. **Note 3:** The process of approval, acceptance, and payment under the scheme is done online through the [MPTAAS portal.](https://www.tribal.mp.gov.in/MPTAAS)
Mode: Online.

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: Tribal Affairs Department, Government Of Madhya Pradesh; Nodal Department: Tribal Welfare and Scheduled Caste (SC) Department.
