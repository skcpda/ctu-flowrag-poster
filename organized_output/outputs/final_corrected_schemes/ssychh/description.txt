# Sukhad Sahara Yojana

**Short Title:** SSYCHH

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Widow, Deserted Woman, BPL, Financial Assistance

The &amp;quot;Sukhad Sahara Yojana&amp;quot; was launched by the Social Welfare Department, Government of Chhattisgarh. Under this scheme, financial assistance are provided to the widows and deserted women who are living below poverty line (BPL) to support them in living a respectful life.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Pension

**Where it applies:** Chhattisgarh; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - Under this scheme, eligible beneficiaries will receive financial assistance of ₹500/- per month. **Note:** The amount of assistance will be directly transferred to the applicant&amp;#39;s bank account.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**How to apply:** &gt; **In Rural Areas:** **Step 1:** The applicant, who fulfills the eligibility criteria, may visit the office of the Gram Panchayat and collect the [application form](https://sw.cg.gov.in/sites/default/files/pensionscheme.pdf), which is available free of cost. **Step 2:** Fill out the application form and submit it to the Gram Panchayat. You will receive an acknowledgment receipt from the Gram Panchayat. **Step 3:** The Gram Panchayat will review and recommend the application and then forward it to the District Panchayat within 07 days. &gt; **In Urban Areas:** **Step 1:** Eligible applicants should visit the office of the concerned Municipal Corporation, Municipality, Nagar Panchayat, or Panchayat Raj institution to collect the [application form](https://sw.cg.gov.in/sites/default/files/pensionscheme.pdf), which is available free of cost. **Step 2:** Fill out the application form and submit it to the respective office (Municipality/Municipal Corporation/Nagar Panchayat). You will receive an acknowledgment receipt from the office. **Note 1:** The concerned urban body/District Panchayat has the authority to approve or reject the applications. **Note 2:** If a printed application form is not available, you may submit your application on plain paper.
Mode: Offline.

**Additional Application Processes:** **Process 2:** &gt; **Registration Process:** **Step 1:** Go to the [eDistrict Chhattisgarh Portal](https://edistrict.cgstate.gov.in/). **Step 2:** Click on &amp;quot;[Citizen Login](https://edistrict.cgstate.gov.in/PACE/newCitizenLogin.do?OWASP_CSRFTOKEN=Z3AL-OEG6-GG3T-CYAI-8QD5-LOA1-TG5O-C4MJ).&amp;quot; **Step 3:** If you are a new user, select &amp;quot;[New Registration](https://edistrict.cgstate.gov.in/PACE/CitizenLogin.do?OWASP_CSRFTOKEN=Q3NL-KVXM-3K8D-QBY3-1BCW-175P-MVSM-6BNX).&amp;quot; **Step 4:** Enter your registration details and click &amp;quot;Submit.&amp;quot; **Step 5:** Your Registration Summary will be displayed, and you will receive your password on your registered mobile number. &gt; **Application Process:** **Step 1:** Go to the [eDistrict Chhattisgarh Portal](https://edistrict.cgstate.gov.in/). **Step 2:** Click on &amp;#39;Login&amp;#39; or &amp;quot;[Citizen Login](https://edistrict.cgstate.gov.in/PACE/newCitizenLogin.do?OWASP_CSRFTOKEN=Z3AL-OEG6-GG3T-CYAI-8QD5-LOA1-TG5O-C4MJ)&amp;quot;. **Step 3:** Enter your username and password, and then click &amp;quot;Login&amp;quot;. **Step 4:** After logging in, click on &amp;quot;My Service&amp;quot; and search for the scheme to apply for. **Step 5:** Click on ‘Apply Online’ and fill in all required details, upload important documents, and click ‘Submit.’
