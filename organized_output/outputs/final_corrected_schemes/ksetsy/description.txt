# Kotwar Cycle Evam Torch Sahayata Yojana

**Short Title:** KSETSY

**Launch Date:** 2013-09-13

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Financial Assistance, Vehicles, Empowerment

Labor Department of Chhattisgarh State started this scheme &amp;quot;Kotwar Cycle Evam Torch Sahayata Yojana&amp;quot; for unorganized worker on 10 September 2013. The main objective of the scheme to empower unorganized worker by providing financial assistance of Cycle or Torch .

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Financial assistance

**Where it applies:** Chhattisgarh; State

**Benefits / Assistance:** > 1. Financial Assistance of ₹ 3,000/- per person for bicycle or the rate fixed by CSIDC for bicycle which will be lower > 2. Financial Assistance of ₹. 750/- per person for torch.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Exclusions / Not eligible:** > Applicant should not be receiving the benefit of the scheme from any other parallel scheme of the state government.
