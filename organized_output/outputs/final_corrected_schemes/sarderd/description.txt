# Scheme for assistance for Research & Development Activities: Assistance to Establish Research & Development/Product Development Centre

**Short Title:** SARDERD

**Launch Date:** 2020-08-07

**Scheme For:** Infra

**Tags:** Research And Development, Product Development Centre, Industrial Research

The &amp;quot;Scheme for Assistance for Research &amp;amp; Development Activities&amp;quot; is an umbrella scheme launched by the Industries and Mines Department, Gujarat under the Gujarat Industrial Policy. Effective from August 7th, 2020 to August 6th, 2025, this initiative promotes sustainable industrial growth by supporting industries in adopting eco-friendly practices and ensuring compliance with environmental norms. The component &amp;quot;Assistance to Establish Research &amp;amp; Development/Product Development Centre&amp;quot; provides financial support to eligible institutions for establishing R&amp;amp;D or product development centers. It aims to promote innovation, technological advancements, and product design improvements.

**Who is this for:** Industries, Business Entity, University / Institution, Registered Societies, Trust, Individual; Category: Business & Entrepreneurship; Sector: Research & Development

**Where it applies:** Gujarat; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - **Financial Assistance:** Up to 30% of the investment in machinery, equipment, hardware, software, and related assets (excluding land and building costs) subject to a maximum up to ₹5,00,00,000/- for one or more activities. 

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**How to apply:** **Step-1:** The R&amp;amp;D institution must apply to the Industries Commissionerate in the [prescribed format](https://ic.gujarat.gov.in/documents/commondoc/2023/2.%20CLAIM%20APPLICATION%20FORM%20SCHEME%201.pdf), along with all necessary [documents](https://ic.gujarat.gov.in/documents/commondoc/2023/1.%20CLAIM%20CHECKLIST%20SCHEME%201.pdf). **Step-2:** The office of the Industries Commissioner will scrutinize the proposals to ensure compliance with the scheme&amp;#39;s eligibility and guidelines. **Step-3:** The proposals will be presented to the State Level Empowered Committee (SLEC) for review and approval. **Step-4:** Upon approval by SLEC, the reimbursement of the sanctioned assistance will be processed, and the claim will be disbursed by the office of the Industries Commissioner. &lt;br&gt; &lt;br&gt; &lt;br&gt; &lt;br&gt; &lt;br&gt; &lt;br&gt; &lt;br&gt;
Mode: Offline.

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Industries and Mines Department.
