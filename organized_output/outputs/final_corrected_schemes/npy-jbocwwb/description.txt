# Nishaktata Pension Yojana (JBOCWWB)

**Short Title:** NPY-JBOCWWB

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Pension, Disability, Construction Worker, Social Welfare

&quot;Nishaktata Pension Yojana (JBOCWWB)&quot; is a scheme by the Department of Labour, Employment, Training &amp; Skill Development Govt of Jharkhand. This is a 100% State Sponsored Scheme. Only the residents/domicile of the state of Jharkhand can apply to this scheme. Applications for this scheme are accepted online through the official website of the department. The scheme provides a pension of ₹ 1,000/- per month to the registered beneficiary who is permanently disabled due to paralysis/leprosy/tuberculosis/accident.

**Who is this for:** Category: Banking,Financial Services and Insurance; Sector: Pension

**Where it applies:** Jharkhand; State
