# Marketing Assistance Scheme 

**Short Title:** MAS

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Financial Assistance, Marketing, Artisans, Leather

The &quot;Marketing Assistance Scheme&quot; was implemented by Karnataka Leather Industries Development Corporation Ltd, Govt of Karnataka. The scheme aims to empower SC leather artisans by enabling them to market their products through Lidkar Leather Emporia under the Lidkar brand. It helps artisans avoid exploitation by private dealers and provides regular procurement of their products based on demand.  **Objective:** Regular procurement of products (once every 1-2 months) based on showroom demand. Branding and marketing through Lidkar Leather Emporia.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Skills & Employment; Sector: Setting up / start-up / entrepreneurship

**Where it applies:** Karnataka; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - **Financial assistance of ₹50,000/- to ₹1,00,000/- worth of procurement from each artisan.**

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**How to apply:** > **Registration Process:** **Step-01:** Visit the official [Seva Sindhu Portal](https://sevasindhu.karnataka.gov.in/Sevasindhu/English). **Step-02:** Choose &quot;New Users Register Here&quot; and fill in your Aadhaar Number and the Captcha code. **Step-03:** An OTP will be sent to your Aadhaar-linked mobile number. Enter the OTP to continue. **Step-04:** Once your DigiLocker account is created, click &quot;Allow&quot; to proceed. **Step-05:** Fill out your email ID, mobile number, create a password, and enter the captcha, then click &quot;Submit&quot;. **Step-06:** You will receive OTPs on both your email and mobile number. Enter both OTPs and select &quot;Validate&quot;. **Step-07:** Once validated, a confirmation message will indicate your registration is successful.  > **Applying for a Scheme:** **Step-01:** Go to [Seva Sindhu Portal](https://sevasindhu.karnataka.gov.in/Sevasindhu/English) and select &quot;Department &amp; Services&quot;. **Step-02:** Select department &quot;Social Welfare Department&quot; and click on the scheme &quot;Marketing Assistance Scheme&quot; **Step-03:** Click &quot;Apply Online&quot; and enter your registered email ID/mobile number and the OTP. **Step-04:** Select the scheme and provide your personal, address, and banking details as required. **Step-05:** Once submitted, you will receive an Application ID. You can use this ID to track your application status on the portal.
Mode: Online.

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: Karnataka Leather Industries Development Corporation Ltd; Nodal Department: Social Welfare Department.
