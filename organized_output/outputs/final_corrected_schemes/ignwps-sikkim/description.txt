# Indira Gandhi National Widow Pension Scheme - Sikkim

**Short Title:** IGNWPS-SIKKIM

**Launch Date:** 2014-01-01

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Widow, Pension, Women, Below Poverty Line, Direct Benefit Transfer

Launched in the year 2014, the scheme “Indira Gandhi National Widow Pension Scheme (IGNWPS)” is a Centrally Sponsored Scheme by the Department of Women &amp;amp; Child Welfare, Govt. of Sikkim. Under this scheme, financial support is provided to the poor widows of the state of Sikkim. A widow whose household is below the poverty line is eligible to get the benefits of the pension scheme. The beneficiary receives a monthly pension of ₹ 2000/- and the pension amount consists of the Central and State governments&amp;#39; share.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Banking,Financial Services and Insurance; Sector: Pension

**Where it applies:** Sikkim; State

**DBT Scheme:** Yes (Direct Benefit Transfer)
