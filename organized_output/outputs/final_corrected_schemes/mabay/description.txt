# Mukhyamantri Aawasiye Bhu-Adhikaar Yojana

**Short Title:** MABAY

**Launch Date:** 2021-10-28

**Closing Date:** 2023-05-31

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Housing, Shelter, Land, Plots

Each family is entitled to live a dignified life with access to essential necessities. Those who are eligible for the housing schemes initiated by either the Central or State government can truly reap the benefits once they obtain a housing plot. Once the residential plot is secured, assistance can be availed for housing loans through government schemes and banks. In light of this, the state government is launching the &amp;quot;Mukhyamantri Awasiya Bhu-Adhikar Yojana,&amp;quot; aimed at granting eligible families residential plots on the &amp;#39;populated areas&amp;#39; within each gram panchayat region in rural areas.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Housing & Shelter; Sector: Housing

**Where it applies:** Madhya Pradesh; State

**Benefits / Assistance:** - Plot will be provided to all those families who do not have their own house.

**Benefit Type:** In Kind

**How to apply:** 1. Applicants have to visit [official website.](https://saara.mp.gov.in/)1. Click on &quot;Apply&quot; under the scheme name. 1. On the [new page](https://saara.mp.gov.in/saaraweb/BhuSwamiAdhikar/ruralplot.html), click on the &quot;Avedan&quot;. 1. Select the &quot;New Application&quot; option. 1. Now fill out the application form and provide the required details. 1. Submit
