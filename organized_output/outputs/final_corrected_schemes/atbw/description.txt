# Award of ₹20,000/- to Best Writers

**Short Title:** ATBW

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Tribal Welfare, Writers, Cash Award, Unemployment

The “Award of ₹20,000/- to Best Writers” scheme by the Adi Dravidar and Tribal Welfare Department, Tamil Nadu, aimed at recognizing and promoting literary talent within the Adi Dravidar, Tribal, and Adi Dravidar Christian-converted communities. A total of 10 writers from these communities will be selected to receive a cash prize. Additionally, one writer from any other community will also be chosen. &lt;br&gt;

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Education & Learning; Sector: Education and training grants, fellowship, stipend

**Where it applies:** Tamil Nadu; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - **Cash Prize:** ₹20,000/- to each of the selected writers (10 from Adi Dravidar/Tribal communities and one from other communities). - **Recognition:** Selected writers receive state recognition for their literary contributions. 

**Benefit Type:** Composite

**How to apply:** **Step 1:** The scheme will be advertised in newspapers during April/May. **Step 2:** Interested candidates must submit their literary works along with the required documents to the designated authority as mentioned in the advertisement. &lt;br&gt;
Mode: Offline.

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Adi Dravidar and Tribal Welfare Department.

**References / Annexures:** - **Guidelines**: https://www.tn.gov.in/scheme/data_view/83092

**Additional Information:**
Briefdescription: The scheme provides a cash award of ₹20,000/- to the top 10 writers from Adi Dravidar, Tribal, or Adi Dravidar communities converted to Christianity, with one additional spot for a writer from other communities.
