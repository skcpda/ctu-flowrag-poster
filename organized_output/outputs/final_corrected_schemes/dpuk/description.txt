# Drone Policy

**Short Title:** DPUK

**Scheme For:** #N/A

**Tags:** Innovation, Drone Training, Skill Development

The scheme &amp;quot;Drone Policy&amp;quot; by the Information Technology Development Agency, Government of Uttarakhand, aims to promote large-scale deployment of drones for governance and public services, especially in remote regions of the state. Through this scheme, support is extended for innovation in drone technology, drone system design and manufacturing, and for developing a drone service-based ecosystem. The scheme also aims to enhance the use of drones in industries, administration, and education, and to increase the reach of the state through drone-based applications.

**Who is this for:** Individual, Industries, Registered Societies; Category: Skills & Employment; Sector: Training and Skill Up-gradation

**Where it applies:** Uttarakhand; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - **Institutional Support:** Guidance and coordination from ITDA (Information Technology Development Agency) for participation and implementation. - **Training &amp;amp; Capacity Building:** Skill development in drone manufacturing, system design, and drone-based services. - **Opportunities for Startups:** Facilitation for Uttarakhand-based drone startups and innovators. - **Academic Participation:** Inclusion of students and institutions like universities and polytechnics in drone ecosystem growth.

**Benefit Type:** In Kind

**How to apply:** **Step 1:** The applicant must approach an institution affiliated with the Information Technology Development Agency, Uttarakhand (e.g., ITDA). **Step 2:** The applicant must submit the application form along with required documents either offline at the ITDA office or via the notified online portal (if applicable). **Step 3:** For guidance on application procedures and selection criteria, the applicant can directly contact the ITDA office. **Step 4:** Upon review and approval, eligible candidates will be notified about further steps, such as training schedules, collaboration opportunities, or program participation. &gt; **Information Technology Development Agency,** &gt; **ITDA Dehradun** &gt; **Address: IT Bhawan Plot No. IT-07, Sahastradhara Rd, IT Park, Dehradun, Uttarakhand 248013** &gt; **Phone: 0135-2608330**
