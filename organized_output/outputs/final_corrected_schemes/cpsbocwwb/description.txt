# Cash Prizes Scheme (BBOCWWB)

**Short Title:** CPSBOCWWB

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Construction, Building, Labour, Financial Assistance, Worker, Student, Exam

&quot;Cash Prizes Scheme&quot; was launched by the Bihar Building &amp; Other Construction Workers Welfare Board (BBOCWWB), Labour Resources Department, Government of Bihar. Students securing 60% or more in the 10th and 12th exams receive cash prizes of up to ₹25,000/- per year. Upto two children of a registered construction worker are eligible.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment

**Where it applies:** Bihar; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - On securing 80% or more in the 10th or 12th examination: ₹25,000/- per year. - On securing 70% to 79.99% in the 10th or 12th examination: ₹15,000/- per year. - On securing 60% to 69.99% in the 10th or 12th examination: ₹10,000/- per year.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

Enter all required details, ensuring that all fields marked with an asterisk (*) are filled in correctly. **Step 5:** Click on &amp;amp;amp;amp;amp;amp;quot;Submit&amp;amp;amp;amp;amp;amp;quot; and proceed to pay the application fee online. **Step 6:** You will receive your construction worker registration number upon successful registration. &amp;amp;amp;amp;amp;gt; **Apply for Scheme Benefits:** **Step 1:** Visit the [Official Website](https://bocwscheme.bihar.gov.in/home) of the Bihar Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board. **Step 2:** Click on &amp;amp;amp;amp;amp;amp;quot;Login&amp;amp;amp;amp;amp;amp;quot; and select &amp;amp;amp;amp;amp;amp;quot;Labour&amp;amp;amp;amp;amp;amp;quot;. **Step 3:** Enter your registration number &amp;amp;amp;amp;amp;amp;amp; year of Birth, click &amp;amp;amp;amp;amp;amp;quot;Show&amp;amp;amp;amp;amp;amp;quot; to view your details. **Step 4:** Select the scheme you wish to apply for to avail the benefits. **Step 5:** Enter all required information and upload the necessary documents. **Step 6:** Click on &amp;amp;amp;amp;amp;amp;quot;Submit Application&amp;amp;amp;amp;amp;amp;quot; to complete the process. &amp;amp;amp;amp;amp;gt; **Application Tracking:** Applicant can track their application status online through the [official website](https://bocwscheme.bihar.gov.in/home) of the Bihar Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board.
