# Assistance Scheme for Handloom Spinning-Weaving Women Workers

**Short Title:** ASHSWWW

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Financial Assistance, Woman, Artisans, Handloom, Spinning, Weaving

The scheme &amp;quot;Assistance Scheme for Handloom Spinning-Weaving Women Workers&amp;quot; was launched by the Department of Industries, Government of Uttarakhand. The scheme aims to provide financial support to traditional women workers whose primary occupation is handloom weaving, spinning, or related weaving activities. Under this scheme, eligible women workers are provided with handloom equipment, including looms and related equipment, worth up to ₹25,000/-. Of this amount, 90% is subsidized by the department, while the remaining 10% is contributed by the beneficiary. Priority is given to women workers who have experience in the handloom sector but are unable to continue their work due to the unavailability of looms. To be eligible, women workers must be registered with either the Government of India or the State Government.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Skills & Employment; Sector: Financial Assistance

**Where it applies:** Uttarakhand; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** **Financial Assistance** - Financial assistance of up to ₹25,000/- per woman beneficiary. - 90% of the cost is covered by the department. - The remaining 10% is borne by the applicant. **Intended Use** - Purchase and installation of handloom / pantloom / frameloom and other related tools and equipment. **Mode of Disbursement** - The sanctioned amount (90%) is directly transferred to the beneficiary’s bank account after selection and approval.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**How to apply:** > **Application Process:** **Step 1:** The interested applicant should visit (during office hours) the General Manager, District Industries Centre (DIC) and request a hard copy of the prescribed format of the application form from the staff exclusively entrusted to issue and collect filled-in applications. **OR** The interested applicant downloads the application form from the [official website](https://doi.uk.gov.in/mysite/home). **Step 2:** In the application form, fill in all the mandatory fields and attach copies of all the mandatory documents (self-attested, if required). **Step 3:** Submit the duly filled and signed application form along with the documents to the General Manager, District Industries Centre. &gt; **Post-application Process:** **Step 1:** The applications will be verified by a committee headed by the General Manager, DIC. **Step 2:** Eligible applications are selected based on criteria and forwarded to the Directorate of Industries for final approval.
Mode: Offline.

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: District Industries Centre Uttarakhand; Nodal Department: Department Of The Industrial Development.
