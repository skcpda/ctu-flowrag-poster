# Unemployment Allowance

**Short Title:** UEA

**Launch Date:** 2023-04-01

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Financial Assistance, Berozgari Bhatta, Unemployment

The main objective of the Chhattisgarh Berozgari Bhatta Yojana is to provide financial assistance to the unemployed youth of the state till they get employment. &lt;br&gt;

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Education & Learning; Sector: Education and training grants, fellowship, stipend

**Where it applies:** Chhattisgarh; State
