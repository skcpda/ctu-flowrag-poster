# Bhargava Matching Scheme

**Short Title:** BMS

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Financial Assistance, Organization, Skill Development, Brahmin Community

The &quot;Bhargava Matching Scheme&quot; was launched by the Andhra Pradesh Brahmin Welfare Corporation. The scheme aims to catalyse, energize and strengthen the efforts of Individuals or groups of individuals or an association or an organization representing Brahmin Community. The funds will be utilized for creating permanent Assets viz. Andhra Kesari Bhavan (Community Hall), Viswanadha Aarama Kshetram, Brahmin Old Age Homes, or any socially relevant activity for the community to meet the needs of beneficiaries in the areas of Education, Coaching, Skill Development, Entrepreneurship, Healthcare, Food and Shelter, etc., within the framework and policies of ABC Ltd.

**Who is this for:** Business Entity; Category: Business & Entrepreneurship; Sector: Financial assistance

**Where it applies:** Andhra Pradesh; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - **Financial Support:** Provides matching funds to double the community&#39;s financial contributions.- **Community Development:** Enhances the overall development and strengthening of the Brahmin community by creating essential facilities.- **Encourages Philanthropy:** Fosters a culture of giving within the Brahmin community.- **Permanent Assets Creation:** Supports the construction of long-term infrastructure like community halls and old age homes.- **Educational and Skill Development:** Funds projects in education and skill development, improving employability.- **Entrepreneurship Support:** Promotes economic self-reliance and innovation within the community.- **Healthcare and Welfare:** Improves overall well-being by funding healthcare, food, and shelter projects.

**Benefit Type:** Composite

**How to apply:** > **Registration and apply** **Step 01:** Applicants have to visit the official website(https://www.andhrabrahmin.ap.gov.in/schemes/schemes.aspx#) and click on &quot;Scheme&quot; **Step 02:** Click on &quot;Registration&quot; After that application form will open **Step 03:** Fill out the required details: - Personal Details: Name, Residence address, Aadhaar no, Ration card no, Bank detail etc - Upload: Photo, Signature and Certificate Details - Documents: Birth Certificate, Residential Certificate, Aadhar Card, Caste Certificate, Bank Passbook etc - Finalize and &quot;Submit&quot; the application. **Step-4:** After submission receive the reference number.  > **Check Application Status:** **Step 01:** Click on the “Services” link on the menu and select &quot;Know your status&quot;. **Step 02:** Enter the Reference or Mobile or Aadhaar Card Number to get the application status. **Step 03:** Select the &quot;Financial Year&quot; and Fetch the details.  > **Download the Acknowledgement slip:** **Step 01:** Click on the &quot;Acknowledgement Slip&quot; **Step 02:** Enter the Aadhaar Card Number and select the &quot;Scheme&quot; **Step 03:** Click on the &quot;Submit&quot;
Mode: Online.
