# Replanting & Rejuvenation of Coconut Garden" by the Coconut Development Board

**Short Title:** RRCGCDB

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Plant, Garden, Coconut, Seed, Financial Assistance, Farmer

The scheme “Replanting &amp; Rejuvenation of Coconut Garden (R&amp;R)&quot; by the Coconut Development Board was launched by the Department of Agriculture and Farmer&#39;s Welfare, Union Territory of Puducherry. Through this scheme, the coconut farmers are provided financial assistance through DBT (Direct Benefit Transfer) mode for the cutting &amp; removal, replanting, and for integrated management practices.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Agriculture,Rural & Environment; Sector: Wildlife and biodiversity

**Where it applies:** Puducherry; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - **Cutting &amp; Removal** @ ₹ 1,000/‐ per palm subject to a maximum of ₹ 32,000/ per hectare. - **Assistance for Replanting** @ ₹ 40 per seedling subject to a maximum of ₹ 4,000.- **Integrated Management Practices** @ ₹ 17,500/‐ per hectare in two annual installments, for upto 4 hectares per farmer.- Seasonality: Annually.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**How to apply:** **Step 1:** The interested applicant should visit (during office hours) the [Uzhavar Udhaviyagam (Farmers&#39; Help Centres) or the Office of the Additional Director of Agriculture (Horticulture)](https://agri.py.gov.in/emailcontact.html) and request a hard copy of the [prescribed format](https://agri.py.gov.in/sites/default/files/pdf/Horti_Application_Coconut.pdf) of the application form from the Agricultural Officer. **Step 2:** In the application form, fill in all the mandatory fields, paste the passport-sized photograph (signed across, if required), and attach copies of all the mandatory documents (self-attest, if required). **Step 3:** Submit the duly filled and signed application form and the documents to the concerned authority. **Step 4:** On receipt of the application, necessary entries will be made in the application receipt register and a unique number is assigned to the applicant for identifying each applicant which shall be used for all further references and also as acknowledgement for the receipt of the application.
Mode: Offline.

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Agriculture Department, Puducherry.
