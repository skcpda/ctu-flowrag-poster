# Large Sized Multi-Purpose Coop. Society: Medium Term Loan to Tribal Members

**Short Title:** LSMPCLTT

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Interest Free Loan, Cooperative Society, Medium Term Loan, Tribal Welfare

The &amp;quot;Large Sized Multi-Purpose Coop. Society: Medium Term Loan to Tribal Members&amp;quot; scheme launched by the Co-operation, Food and Consumer Protection Department, Tamil Nadu, aims to provide a medium-term loan facility to tribal members through the Large Sized Multi Purpose Cooperative Society. The maximum loan amount is ₹60,000/- with no interest, making it a cost-effective financing option for the community. &lt;br&gt;

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Banking,Financial Services and Insurance; Sector: Loan

**Where it applies:** Tamil Nadu; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - **Access to Loan:** Loan up to ₹60,000/-. - **Interest Rate:** No interest.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**How to apply:** **Step 1:** The interested applicant should collect the application form by contacting the Special Officer of the Large Sized Multi Purpose Cooperative Society. **Step 2:** In the application form, fill in all the mandatory fields, paste the passport-sized photograph (signed across, if required), and attach copies of all the mandatory documents (self-attest, if required). **Step 3:** Submit the duly filled and signed application form along with the documents to the concerned authority.. **Step 4:** Request a receipt or acknowledgment from the concerned authority to whom the application has been submitted. Ensure that the receipt contains essential details such as the date and time of submission, a unique identification number (if applicable). &lt;br&gt; &gt; **Region-wise Address and Contact Information of Large Sized Multipurpose Cooperative Societies:** [https://www.rcs.tn.gov.in/mpd_cooperative_4.php](https://www.rcs.tn.gov.in/mpd_cooperative_4.php)
Mode: Offline.

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Co-operation, Food and Consumer Protection Department.

**References / Annexures:** - **Guidelines**: https://www.tn.gov.in/scheme/data_view/3611

**Additional Information:**
Briefdescription: The scheme offers medium-term loans of up to ₹60,000/- without interest to tribal members, enabling them to fulfill various financial needs.
