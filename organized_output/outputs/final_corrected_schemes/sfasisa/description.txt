# Scheme for assistance for Start Ups/ Innovation: Sustenance Allowance

**Short Title:** SFASISA

**Launch Date:** 2020-08-07

**Scheme For:** Infra

**Tags:** Start Up, Innovation, Entrepreneurship, Sustenance Allowance

The &amp;quot;Scheme for Assistance for Start Ups/ Innovation&amp;quot; is an umbrella scheme launched by the Industries and Mines Department, Gujarat under the Gujarat Industrial Policy. Effective from August 7th, 2020 to August 6th, 2025, it aims to drive economic growth by generating jobs, fostering competition, and introducing next-gen solutions. The component &amp;quot;Sustenance Allowance&amp;quot;, provides financial relief to developers who have paid Stamp Duty on the purchase of land required for a new unit/project. The scheme is aimed at promoting new developments by reducing the financial burden on eligible projects during their establishment phase.

**Who is this for:** Business Entity, Industries; Category: Business & Entrepreneurship; Sector: Setting up / start-up / entrepreneurship

**Where it applies:** Gujarat; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - **Financial Assistance:** ₹20,000/- per month for one year based on eligibility. - **Encouragement for Women Entrepreneurs:** ₹25,000/- per month for startups with a woman co-founder.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Exclusions / Not eligible:** - Entities formed by splitting or restructuring an existing business are not considered startups under this scheme. - Startups availing benefits under other startup schemes of the Government of Gujarat are ineligible.
