# Organizing Block Demonstration

**Short Title:** OBD

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Agriculture, Farmer, Block Demonstration, Subsidy

The “Organizing Block Demonstration” scheme by the Agriculture-Farmers Welfare Department, Tamil Nadu, provides subsidy to promote best agricultural practices to farmers who cultivate seed farms with Pulses crops. The subsidy covers 50% of the input costs or ₹2,000/- per demonstration, whichever is less.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Agriculture,Rural & Environment; Sector: Financial assistance

**Where it applies:** Tamil Nadu; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** **Financial Assistance:** A subsidy of 50% on the cost of inputs required to layout the demonstration of 5 hectare each or ₹2,000/- whichever is less.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**How to apply:** **Step 1:** Collect the application form from the nearest Agricultural Office. **Step 2:** Fill out the application form with the required details and attach mandatory documents (self-attested, if required). **Step 3:** Submit the form to the concerned office: &gt; **Village Level:** Submit the application to the Assistant Agricultural Officer. &gt; **Block Level:** Submit to the Agricultural Officer or Deputy Agricultural Officer. &gt; **District Level:** Submit to the Joint Director of Agriculture.
Mode: Offline.

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Agriculture Department.

**References / Annexures:** - **Guidelines**: https://www.tn.gov.in/scheme/data_view/6902

**Additional Information:**
Briefdescription: The “Organizing Block Demonstration” scheme provides a 50% subsidy or ₹2,000/- (whichever is less) on input costs for demonstrating the cultivation of Pulses crops over 5 hectares.
