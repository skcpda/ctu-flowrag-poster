# Adi Dravidar and Tribal Welfare Department: Scholarship-Public Examination Fee for 10th and 12th Std.

**Short Title:** ADTWSPE

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Tribal Welfare, Adi Dravidar, Examination Fee, Scholarship, Education

The scheme “Adi Dravidar and Tribal Welfare Department: Scholarship-Public Examination Fee for 10th and 12th Std.” by the Adi Dravidar and Tribal Welfare Department, Tamil Nadu, is designed to support students from the Adi Dravidar, Tribal, and Adi Dravidar Converted to Christianity communities. Under this scheme, the government reimburses the public examination fees for 10th and 12th-standard students directly to the Directorate of Government Examinations. There is no income limit for eligibility under this scheme.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Education & Learning; Sector: Scholarships and student finance

**Where it applies:** Tamil Nadu; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - **Reimbursement:** Full reimbursement on Public Examination fee for 10th and 12th Std. students. &lt;br&gt;

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**How to apply:** - Students/ Guardians should contact the Headmaster of the School with all the required documents as directed by the authority. &lt;br&gt;
Mode: Offline.

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Adi Dravidar and Tribal Welfare Department.

**References / Annexures:** - **Guidelines**: https://www.tn.gov.in/scheme/data_view/83085

**Additional Information:**
Briefdescription: The scheme provides reimbursement of public examination fees for 10th and 12th standard students from the Adi Dravidar, Tribal, and Adi Dravidar Converted to Christianity communities, with no income limit.
