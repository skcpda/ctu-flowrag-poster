# Kachara Binane Vaale Hetu Suraksha Upakaran Sahaayata Yojana

**Short Title:** KBVHSUSY

**Launch Date:** 2017-06-19

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Equipment, Empowerment, Gloves, Shoes, Rain Coat

Labor Department of Chhattisgarh State started this scheme &amp;quot;Kachara Beenane Vaale Hetu Suraksha Upakaran Sahaayata Yojana&amp;quot; for unorganized worker on 19 June 2017. The main objective of the scheme to empower unorganized worker by providing safety equipment.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Skills & Employment

**Where it applies:** Chhattisgarh; State

**Benefits / Assistance:** - Gloves, Shoes and Rain Coat (OR) - A cash assistance in the amount determined by the CSID.

**Benefit Type:** Composite

**How to apply:** **Unregistered Applicant :** 1. All the applicant have to visit the official [website](https://cglabour.nic.in) Chhattisgarh Labour Department. 1. On the home page click on &quot;[Apply](https://cglabour.nic.in/Departments.aspx?id=4)&quot; under &quot;Chhattisgarh Unorganized Workers State Social Security Board&quot;.1. Enter the following details 1. Select Group Name : &quot;Asangathit Marmakaar Mandal&quot; 1. Select Service : &quot;Asangathit Shramik Panjikaran&quot; 1. What do you want to do : &quot;Aavedan&quot; 1. Click next. 1. Check the eligibility criteria for registration in Chhattisgarh Unorganized Workers State Social Security Board and click next. 1. Fill out the online &quot;Registration form&quot;. 1. Submit.  **Already Registered Applicant :** 1. All the applicant have to visit the official [website](https://cglabour.nic.in) Chhattisgarh Labour Department. 1. On the home page click on &quot;[Apply](https://cglabour.nic.in/Departments.aspx?id=4)&quot; under &quot;Chhattisgarh Unorganized Workers State Social Security Board&quot;.1. Enter the following details 1. Select Group Name : &quot;Asangathit Marmakaar Mandal&quot; 1. Select Service : &quot;Yojana&quot; 1. What do you want to do : &quot;Aavedan&quot; 1. Click next. 1. Select your district name and provide old/new registration number and click next. 1. Select the scheme name. 1. Fill out the online with required details. 1. Submit.
Mode: Online.
