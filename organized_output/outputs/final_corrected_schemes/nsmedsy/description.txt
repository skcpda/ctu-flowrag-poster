# Nirmaan Shramik Mrtyu Evan Divyaang Sahaayata Yojana

**Short Title:** NSMEDSY

**Launch Date:** 2020-06-09

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Financial Assistance, Disability Benefits, Death Benefits

In 2020, Chhattisgarh State Government started the &quot;Nirmaan Shramik Mrtyu Evan Divyaang Sahaayata Yojana&quot; fBuilding and other construction workers. With this scheme, family will able to get the financials benefits after the death or disability of the construction worker.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Health & Wellness; Sector: Financial assistance

**Where it applies:** Chhattisgarh; State

**Benefits / Assistance:**  SituationFinancial HelpOn Normal Death₹ 1,00,000/-Death on work location₹ 5,00,000/-Permanent disability on work location₹ 2,50,000/-

**Benefit Type:** Cash
