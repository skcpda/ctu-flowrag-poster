# Scheme for Fruit and Vegetable Preservation and Training Program in Horticulture Department HRT 5

**Short Title:** SFVPTPHDF

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Horticultural Training, Fruit Preservation, Women Empowerment, Kitchen Gardening, Stipend

The &amp;quot;Scheme for Fruit and Vegetable Preservation and Training Program in Horticulture Department HRT 5&amp;quot; by the Agriculture and Co-operation Department, Government of Gujarat, aims to empower women and urban citizens by offering hands-on training in value addition, preservation, canning, and kitchen-gardening of fruits and vegetables, along with promoting self-sustenance and nutritional well-being. The scheme includes financial support of ₹500/- per trainee per day- ₹250/- as a direct stipend and the remaining ₹250/- towards training material, expert honorarium, and other resources. 

**Who is this for:** Family; Category: Agriculture,Rural & Environment; Sector: Financial assistance

**Where it applies:** Gujarat; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - **Stipend:** ₹500/- per trainee per day sanctioned as training expenditure. Out of this, ₹250/- is provided as a direct stipend to the trainee, and ₹250/- is utilized for material cost, training literature kit, and honorarium to resource persons. - **Skill Development Training:** Training duration ranges from 2 to 5 days. Modules include fruit and vegetable preservation, canning, kitchen gardening, and value addition. &lt;br&gt; 

**Benefit Type:** Composite
