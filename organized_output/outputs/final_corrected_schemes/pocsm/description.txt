# Production of Certified Seeds of Maize

**Short Title:** POCSM

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Agriculture, Farmer, Certified Seeds, Production Of Seeds, Maize

The scheme “Production of Certified Seeds of Maize” by the Agriculture-Farmers Welfare Department, Tamil Nadu, is designed to support maize farmers in specific districts of Tamil Nadu. By offering a subsidy of ₹1,000/- per quintal, the scheme encourages farmers to produce certified maize seeds, ensuring a steady supply of high-quality seeds. &lt;br&gt; &lt;br&gt;

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Agriculture,Rural & Environment; Sector: Financial assistance

**Where it applies:** Tamil Nadu; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - **Financial Assistance:** ₹,1000/- per quintal. 

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**How to apply:** **Step 1:** Collect the application form from the nearest Agricultural Office. **Step 2:** Fill out the application form with the required details and attach mandatory documents (self-attested, if required). **Step 3:** Submit the form to the concerned office: &gt; **Village Level:** Submit the application to the Assistant Agricultural Officer. &gt; **Block Level:** Submit to the Agricultural Officer or Deputy Agricultural Officer. &gt; **District Level:** Submit to the Joint Director of Agriculture.
Mode: Offline.

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Agriculture Department.

**References / Annexures:** - **Guidelines**: https://www.tn.gov.in/scheme/data_view/6852

**Additional Information:**
Briefdescription: The "Production of Certified Seeds of Maize" scheme, aims to promote the production of certified maize seeds by providing subsidy to farmers in select districts of Tamil Nadu.
