# Popular Tiatr Competition

**Short Title:** PTC-G

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Artist, Culture, Competition, Award, Acting, Singing

The scheme &quot;Popular Tiatr Competition&quot; was launched by the Tiatr Academy under the Art, Culture and Museums Department, Government of Goa. The scheme aims to bring about all round development of Popular Tiatr by organizing annual competitions in different parts of Goa, preserving and promoting the traditional format of tiatr performances. The scheme provides cash prizes ranging from ₹2,000/- to ₹2,00,000/- for various categories including performance, direction, acting, singing, and technical aspects, along with mementos and certificates for winners. The applicant must have completed at least 5 performances including Kantaram during the period from 1st April 2018 to 31st December 2018 and must perform in specified auditoriums across Goa. The scheme is implemented by Tiatr Academy of Goa. The applications for this scheme are accepted offline through submission of application forms along with required documents. 

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Sports & Culture; Sector: Culture

**Where it applies:** Goa; State/ UT

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Exclusions / Not eligible:** - The applicant cannot include Kantaram presented in the Kala Academy Tiatr Competitions in the Popular Tiatr Competition, unless a tiatr from the Kala Academy&#39;s Competition is admitted in the Popular Tiatr Competition. - The applicant cannot participate artists including technicians and backstage artists in more than one group and one Tiatr, however musicians and stage setting personnel can participate in more than 2 Tiatrs. 

**How to apply:** ***Applications will be invited by releasing press notes and advertisements in the local dailies. Applicants should regularly monitor these announcements for application opening dates.*** **Step 1:** The interested applicant should take a printout of the [prescribed format](https://www.goa.gov.in/wp-content/uploads/2021/06/Citizen-Charter-TAG.pdf) of the application form. **Step 2:** In the application form, fill in all the mandatory fields, attach copies of all the mandatory documents (self-attest, if required). Sign the application form and mention the date. **Step 3:** Submit the duly filled and signed application form along with the documents to - ***Tiatr Academy of Goa, AS-1 / AS-4, Block A, 2nd Floor, Campal Trade Centre, Opposite Kala Academy, Campal, Panaji, Goa, India – 403001*** _*Applicants are advised to save a copy of the submitted application form and any acknowledgment._  ##### ***Post-Application Processes*** **Step 1:****** ***Await Scheduling Communication*** After successful submission, wait for communication from organizers regarding the date, time and venue allocation for the competition performance. The judgment will be conducted from 5th January 2019 to 5th February 2019 at respective auditoriums. **Step 2:****** ***Confirm Performance Schedule*** Once the performance schedule is communicated, confirm participation and ensure all logistical arrangements including arranging 6 passes for judges and organizers at the performance venue. **Step 3:****** ***Stage the Performance*** Present the tiatr performance strictly as per the traditional format at the assigned auditorium during the competition period. Ensure compliance with all technical requirements including live music band with minimum 5 specified instruments. _*The judgment will be conducted in January-February at respective auditoriums._ _*The decision of the panel of judges will be final and binding, and no correspondence regarding results will be entertained._
