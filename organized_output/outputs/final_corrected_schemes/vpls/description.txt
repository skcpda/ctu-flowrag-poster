# Vrutti Protsaha Loan Scheme

**Short Title:** VPLS

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Loan, Self-employment, Subsidy, Minority

The &quot;Vrutti Protsaha Loan Scheme&quot; was launched by the Minorities Welfare Department, Government of Karnataka, and implemented by the Karnataka Minorities Development Corporation. The scheme aims to promote self-employment and economic activities among minority communities and provides financial assistance to eligible individuals from minority communities to start or expand their economic activities, thereby fostering economic empowerment and self-sufficiency.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Skills & Employment; Sector: Citizen empowerment

**Where it applies:** Karnataka; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** Financial Assistance provided totals ₹1,00,000/- in two parts: - 50% Loan Amount - 50% Subsidy Amount

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**How to apply:** **Step-01:** Applicant need to visits the Karnataka Minorities Development Corporation [official website](https://kmdconline.karnataka.gov.in/Portal/home). **Step-02:** Click &quot;[Apply Online](https://kmdconline.karnataka.gov.in/Portal/login)&quot; and Enter &quot;Mobile Number&quot; and &quot;Submit&quot;. **Step-03:** Enter &quot;Aadhaar Number&quot; Fill in the &quot;Captcha&quot; and Select &quot;Next&quot;. **Step-04:** Applicant will get OTP in his/her Aadhaar Linked Mobile Number. **Step-05:** Enter &quot;OTP&quot; and select &quot;Continue&quot;, After that Allow the official notice. **Step-06:** After verifying the Aadhaar again fill in the &quot;OTP&quot; and Submit. **Step-07:** Select the scheme &quot;Sikhligar’s Community Development Scheme&quot; and fill in the basic details father&#39;s name, gender, qualification detail **Step-08:** Select &quot;Next&quot; Fill in the address detail and again select &quot;Next&quot;. **Step-09:** Upload the required document passport-size photo, educational document, income certificate, etc. **Step-10:** Click &quot;Proceed&quot; and Select &quot;Submit Application&quot; and get an Application ID.
Mode: Online.

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Minorities Welfare Department.
