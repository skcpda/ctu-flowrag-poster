# Samagr Samajik Suraksha Pension Yojana

**Short Title:** SSSPY

**Launch Date:** 1981-01-01

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Financial Assistance, Empowerment, Social Welfare

In Madhya Pradesh, &amp;quot;Samagra Samajik Suraksha Pension Yojana&amp;quot; was started for poor, disabled and destitute citizens. This scheme has been started by the Chief Minister of Madhya Pradesh. In which those people of the state who are destitute such as – old people, handicapped, disabled and poor class people have been kept under the scheme. In which all social security has been provided from women to children. The objective of Social Security Pension Scheme Madhya Pradesh is to provide financial assistance to destitute people so that they do not need any other person to meet their needs.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Pension

**Where it applies:** Madhya Pradesh; State

**Benefits / Assistance:** Pension amount: Rs. 600/- per momth.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**How to apply:** 1. Applicants have to visit the Gram Panchayat / Janpad Panchayat in rural areas and Municipal Corporation / Municipality / City Council in urban areas. 1. Get the [application form ](https://socialjustice.mp.gov.in/uploads/schemes/ssp_sanctioned_ordere_form_1.pdf)and fill it with the required information.1. Attach the necessary document. 1. Get the acknowledgement from the concerned office.   
Mode: Offline.

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Department of Social Justice and Empowerment of Persons with Disabilities.
