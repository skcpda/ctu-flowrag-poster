# Scheme for Financial Assistance to Integrated Logistics Facilities: Exemption of Electricity Duty

**Short Title:** SFAILFEED

**Launch Date:** 2021-08-02

**Closing Date:** 2026-08-01

**Scheme For:** Infra

**Tags:** Logistics Facilities, Electricity Duty

The &amp;quot;Scheme for Financial Assistance to Integrated Logistics Facilities&amp;quot; is an umbrella scheme launched by the Industries and Mines Department, Gujarat under the Gujarat Integrated Logistics and Logistics Park Policy. Effective from August 2nd, 2021 to August 1st, 2026, this initiative aims to provide financial assistance to the private developer for developing Logistics Facilities and Logistics Park with all required infrastructure and logistic services. The component &amp;quot;Exemption of Electricity Duty&amp;quot; is aimed at promoting industrial growth and economic development. Under this scheme, newly established units or projects can avail of electricity duty exemptions after the commencement of their operations.

**Who is this for:** Business Entity, Industries; Category: Business & Entrepreneurship; Sector: Setting up / start-up / entrepreneurship

**Where it applies:** Gujarat; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** Financial Assistance: Unit/Project is eligible for exemption of Electricity Duty from the date of Commencement of Operation.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Exclusions / Not eligible:** - If any Unit/Project is availing incentive under any other State Government scheme, it will not be eligible under this scheme.

Click on &amp;quot;New Investor Registration&amp;quot; to access the registration form. **Step-2:** Fill in the Registration Form. Click on &amp;quot;Submit&amp;quot;. **Step-3:** Verify the E-mail ID using the link sent on your registered E-mail ID. **Step-4:** After verification, you will be registered successfully. &lt;br&gt; &gt; **Post-Registration Process:** **Step-1:** Visit the [Investor Facilitation Portal (IFP)](https://ifp.gujarat.gov.in/DIGIGOV/) portal. Click on &amp;quot;Login&amp;quot;. **Step-2:** Enter the email address and password you used during registration. **Step-3:** Click the &amp;quot;Login&amp;quot; button to access your account. **Step-4:** Now fill in the required details and mandatory documents about your business to complete the application process. &lt;br&gt; &gt; **Helpdesk****:** [Contact Here.](https://ifp.gujarat.gov.in/DIGIGOV/IFP-pages/contact.jsp?pagedisp=static) &lt;br&gt;
Mode: Online.

**Additional Application Processes:** **Process 2:** &gt; **Registration Procedure:** **Step 1:** Submit the application in the [prescribed form](https://ic.gujarat.gov.in/documents/commondoc/2021/Application_Form_for_Registration.pdf) along with the [required documents](https://ic.gujarat.gov.in/documents/commondoc/2021/Checklist_for_Registration.pdf) to the [Industries Commissionerate](https://ic.gujarat.gov.in/ic-contact.aspx). **Step 2:** The Industries Commissionerate will review and request missing details, if any, within 15 days. **Step 3:** The application will then be forwarded to the GM of the District Industries Centre (DIC) for a site visit and inspection report. **Step 4:** Based on the report, the Industries Commissionerate may issue a Registration Certificate. &gt; **Sanction Procedure:** **Step 1:** After registration and approval of the project layout plan, submit the application in the [prescribed format](https://ic.gujarat.gov.in/documents/commondoc/2023/Application%20Form%20for%20Sanction.pdf) for sanction with a detailed project report and [required documents](https://ic.gujarat.gov.in/documents/commondoc/2023/Checklist%20for%20Sanction.pdf) to the [Industries Commissionerate](https://ic.gujarat.gov.in/ic-contact.aspx). **Step 2:** The Industries Commissionerate will scrutinize and request missing details within 15 days. **Step 3:** Applicants must present their project to a Screening Committee chaired by the Industries Commissioner. **Step 4:** After addressing committee remarks, the application is reviewed by the Sanctioning Authority for approval based on scheme guidelines.
