# Jharkhand Sportsperson Pension Scheme

**Short Title:** JSPS

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Financial Assistance, Sportsperson, Pension

The “Jharkhand Sportsperson Pension Scheme” was launched by the Department of Sports &amp; Youth Affairs, Government of Jharkhand. aims to provide financial support to retired athletes for their lifelong contribution to sports. Athletes, including Arjuna, Dronacharya, and Dhyan Chand awardees, Olympic participants, and Commonwealth or Asian Games medalists, receive a monthly pension.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Sports & Culture; Sector: Sports

**Where it applies:** Jharkhand; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - Athletes receive a lifetime monthly pension of ₹10,000/-. - Dependents of deceased athletes (spouse and minor children) receive ₹5,000/- monthly.  **NOTE:** Pension payments are directly transferred to the beneficiary&#39;s bank account.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**How to apply:** **Step-1:** The interested applicant downloads the [application form](https://sports.jharkhand.gov.in/notification_docs/f63f2766Notification%20Regarding%20Khilari%20Pension%20Niyamawali.pdf) from the official website. **Step-2:** In the application form, fill in all the mandatory fields, and attach copies of all the mandatory documents (self-attest, if required). **Step-3:** Submit the duly filled and signed application form along with the documents to the District Sports Officer. **Step-4:** Request a receipt or acknowledgment from the District Sports Officer to whom the application has been submitted. Ensure that the receipt contains essential details such as the date and time of submission, and a unique identification number (if applicable).  ***Processing of the Applications by the Concerned Authorities:*** - The DSO verifies the application and forwards it with recommendations to the Directorate of Sports and Youth Affairs. - Approval is granted by the committee formed under Jharkhand Sports Policy 2022. - Upon approval, the pension amount is transferred to the beneficiary’s bank account. 
Mode: Offline.

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Department of Sports & Youth Affairs.
