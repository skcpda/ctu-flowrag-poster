# Mobile Food Court Van (DAADC)

**Short Title:** MFCV

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Loan, Self-employment, Scheduled Caste, Antyodaya, Food Court Van

The &amp;quot;Mobile Food Court Van&amp;quot; scheme is implemented by the Dr. Ambedkar Antyodaya Development Corporation (DAADC) under the Social Justice and Empowerment Department, Government of Gujarat. The scheme aims to generate self-employment opportunities in the food and beverage sector by providing loan assistance to unemployed scheduled caste individuals belonging to Antyodaya families. Under this scheme, loan is provided to the eligible beneficiaries at a concessional interest rate. 

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Banking,Financial Services and Insurance; Sector: Loan

**Where it applies:** Gujarat; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** **Loan Amount:** Up to ₹6,00,000/- **Interest Rate:** 6% per annum **Contribution:** - State Government Contribution: 95 % - Beneficiary Contribution: 5% **Possible Installment Amount:** - ₹11,020/- per month (for 5 years) - ₹ 9,447/- per month (for 6 years) - ₹ 8,327/- per month (for 7 years) 

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**How to apply:** **Step 1:** Visit the [official website](https://daadconline.gujarat.gov.in/) of the Dr. Ambedkar Antyodaya Vikas Nigam (SC). **Step 2:** On the top menu bar, click on &quot;Apply Now&quot;. **Step 3:** Click on the loan scheme name. **Step 4:** Select the loan category and fill in the loan amount. **Step 5:** Fill out the Application Form, enter the following details: - Personal Information: Name, gender, date of birth, caste, etc. - Address Information: Permanent and correspondence address - Income Details: Monthly family income and source - Loan Requirement: Amount needed, proposed use (e.g., business, self-employment) - Bank Account Details: For loan disbursement **Step 6:** Upload scanned copies of the Caste certificate, Income certificate, Aadhaar card, Passport-size photo, Bank passbook (first page), and Residence proof. **Step 7:** Double-check all details. Click on “Preview” to verify. If all details are correct, click “Submit”. **Step 8:** The applicant will receive an Application ID/Reference Number. Save or print the acknowledgement for future tracking. 
Mode: Online.

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: Dr. Ambedkar Antyodaya Development Corporation; Nodal Department: Social Justice and Empowerment Department.
