# Housing Subsidy Scheme (GBOCWWB)

**Short Title:** HSS(GBOCWWB)

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Building Worker, Construction Worker, Labour, House, Financial Assistance, Loan

The “Housing Subsidy Scheme” is implemented by the Gujarat Building and Other Construction Worker’s Welfare Board (GBOCWWB), Labour, Skill Development &amp;amp; Employment Department, Government of Gujarat. The main objective of this scheme is to provide financial assistance to construction workers registered with the Gujarat Building and Other Construction Worker’s Welfare Board to build their own houses. In order to provide financial relief to the construction workers by giving subsidies for the houses purchased by them and to provide social stability to the construction workers, the &amp;quot;Housing Subsidy&amp;quot; schemewasn started in the year 2021-22. It is planned to provide financial relief and social stability to construction workers through providing subsidies.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Housing & Shelter; Sector: Loan

**Where it applies:** Gujarat; State/ UT

**DBT Scheme:** Yes (Direct Benefit Transfer)

**Benefits / Assistance:** 1. Under the scheme, the assistance of ₹1,50,000/- will be provided to registered construction workers for a duration of 5 years, with ₹30,000/- disbursed annually subject to the terms of assistance. 1. The construction workers who have taken a loan up to a maximum of ₹15 lakhs to buy their house will be provided ₹30,000/- per year for the five years as per plan.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

conditions, and submit the form. **Step 08:** Once the application has been submitted, an application Number is generated which can be kept for future reference. &gt; **Process to track Application Status:** **Step 01:** The applicant may visit the e-Nirman Portal: [https://enirmanbocw.gujarat.gov.in/](https://enirmanbocw.gujarat.gov.in/) **Step 02:** On the home page, click on ‘View Citizen Application Status’. **Step 03:** Enter the Application Number and Date of Birth. **Step 04:** Now click on ‘View Status’. &gt; **Application Process for Scheme Benefit:** **Step 01:** The applicant may visit the Sanman Portal: [https://sanman.gujarat.gov.in/](https://sanman.gujarat.gov.in/) **Step 02:** On the home page, under the tab ‘Citizen Login’, click on ‘[Please Register Here](https://sanman.gujarat.gov.in/Home/Registration)’. **Step 03:** Enter your Aadhaar Card Number, select user type, and then enter your E-Nirman Card Number. **Step 04:** Now, click on ‘Fetch’ &amp;amp; submit the details. **Step 05:** After successful registration, the applicants can login through their User ID and Password. **Step 06:** Now, select the scheme and read the instructions carefully for the selected scheme. **Step 07:** Fill out the application form and upload all the relevant documents. **Step 08:** Agree with the Rules &amp;amp; Mode: Online.

**Additional Application Processes:** **Process 2:** &gt; **Application Process:** **Step 01:** The applicant may visit the district office of the BOCW Board and collect the application form. **Step 02:** Fill out the form completely and attach all the relevant documents. **Step 03:** Submit the filled application form to the District Project Manager. &gt; **Post-Application Process:** **Step 01:** The application form is submitted by the construction worker, followed by the application form by the District Project Manager will verify and approve. **Step 02:** After that, the application will be verified and approved by the district inspector and sent to the head office. **Step 03:** The State Project Manager of the Head Office will verify the application and send it to the Government Labour Officer (GLO). **Step 04:** After that by verifying the application by the Government Labour Officer of the Head Office and then by the Member Secretary will be approved. **Step 05:** After approval of the application, the Accounts Officer will credit the assistance through DBT (Direct Benefit Transfer) to the loan account of the construction worker.
