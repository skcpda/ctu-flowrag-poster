# Distribution of Horticulture Kit to Urban and Rural Areas

**Short Title:** DHKURA

**Tags:** Horticulture, Agriculture, Farmer, Subsidy, DBT

The scheme “Distribution of Horticulture Kit to Urban and Rural Areas&quot; was launched by the Department of Agriculture and Farmer&#39;s Welfare, Union Territory of Puducherry. Through this scheme, all the horticulture growers in rural &amp; urban areas are provided with a horticulture kit worth ₹ 3,000 per kit to urban and rural areas, during Aadipattam/Thaipattam, through the Additional Director of Agriculture (Horticulture).

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Agriculture,Rural & Environment; Sector: Agricultural Inputs- seeds, fertilizer etc.

**Where it applies:** Puducherry; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** 75% subsidy, i.e. distribution of horticulture kit worth ₹ 3,000 per kit to urban and rural areas (homestead/backyards), through the Additional Director of Agriculture (Horticulture), during Aadipattam/Thaipattam seasonality.

**Benefit Type:** In Kind

**How to apply:** **Step 1:** The interested applicant should visit (during office hours) the [Uzhavar Udhaviyagam (Farmers&#39; Help Centres) or the Office of the Additional Director of Agriculture (Horticulture)](https://agri.py.gov.in/emailcontact.html) and request the hard copy of the prescribed format of the application form from the Agricultural Officer. **Step 2:** In the application form, fill in all the mandatory fields, paste the passport-sized photograph (signed across, if required), and attach copies of all the mandatory documents (self-attest, if required). **Step 3:** Submit the duly filled and signed application form and the documents to the concerned authority. **Step 4:** On receipt of the application, necessary entries will be made in the application receipt register and a unique number is assigned to the applicant for identifying each applicant which shall be used for all further references and also as acknowledgement for the receipt of the application.
Mode: Offline.

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Agriculture Department, Puducherry.
