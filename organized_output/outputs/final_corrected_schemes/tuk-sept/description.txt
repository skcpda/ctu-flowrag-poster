# Transgender Utthan Kosh - Self Employment Programme for Transgender

**Short Title:** TUK-SEPT

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Self-employment, Business, Transgender, Financial Assistance, Loan

The scheme &amp;quot;Transgender Utthan Kosh - Self Employment Programme for Transgender&amp;quot; was launched by the Social Justice and Empowerment Department, Government of Rajasthan, with the aim of providing financial support for self-employment or business establishment to transgender individuals, promoting their economic independence. Under the scheme, transgender persons aged between 21 and 50 years are eligible to take a loan from any nationalized or cooperative bank to set up their self-employment or business. The loan amount may be revised by the state government from time to time. The benefit of the scheme is available to a transgender person only once.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Business & Entrepreneurship; Sector: Setting up / start-up / entrepreneurship

**Where it applies:** Rajasthan; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - **Financial Assistance:** 25% of the total business cost or ₹50,000/- (whichever is lower) for self-employment/business establishment.- **Loan Support:** The beneficiary must secure a loan from a nationalized/cooperative bank to avail of the grant.- **One-Time Benefit:** The assistance is available only once per individual. **Note:** The grant is disbursed only after the beneficiary starts the business and gets it verified by the lending agency.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**How to apply:** Step 1: The interested applicant should visit (during office hours) the Social Justice and Empowerment Office and request a hard copy of the prescribed format of the application form from the staff exclusively entrusted to issue and collect filled-in application. Step 2: In the application form, fill in all the mandatory fields and attach copies of all the mandatory documents (self-attest, if required). Step 3: Submit the duly filled and signed application form along with the documents to the Social Justice and Empowerment Office. Step 4: Request a receipt or acknowledgment from the Social Justice and Empowerment Office to whom the application has been submitted. **Note 1:** After a transgender person takes a loan from any nationalized / cooperative bank, on the basis of loan approval, the District Magistrate will deposit the grant amount in the loan account of the transgender person. **Note 2:** The applicant will receive the said amount only on the condition that he starts the related business and gets its physical verification done by the related lending agency.
Mode: Offline.

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Social Justice and Empowerment Department.
