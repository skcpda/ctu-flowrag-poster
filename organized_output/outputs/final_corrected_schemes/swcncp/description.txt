# Scheme For Welfare Of Children In Need Of Care And Protection

**Short Title:** SWCNCP

**Scheme For:** Infra

**Tags:** Shelter, Orphan, Children, Destitute, Protection

&quot;Scheme for Welfare of Children in Need of Care and Protection&quot; is a Social Welfare Scheme by the Directorate of Women &amp; Child Development, Govt. of Goa. The objective of this scheme is to assist the Voluntary Organizations that cater to the needs of destitute and orphans up to the age of 18 years. To apply, the Voluntary Organizations should approach the Directorate of Women &amp; Child Development, Panaji, Goa.

**Who is this for:** NGO, Registered Societies, Trust; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Shelter

**Where it applies:** Goa; State

**Benefits / Assistance:** > **Financial Assistance (Grants)** 1. ₹ 400/- per child per month, if the children are housed on their own premises. 1. ₹ 500/- per child per month, if the children are housed in rented premises.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**How to apply:** **Step 1:** Visit the Directorate of Women &amp; Child Development, Panaji-Goa, and request a hard copy of the proforma of the application form for the scheme from the Concerned Authority. **Step 2:** In the application form, fill in all the mandatory fields, paste the passport-sized photograph (signed across), and attach copies of all the mandatory documents (self-attest if required). **Step 3:** Submit the duly filled and signed application form along with the documents to the Concerned Authority. **Step 4:** Acquire the receipt/acknowledgement of the successful submission of the application.
Mode: Offline.

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Women and Child Development Department.
