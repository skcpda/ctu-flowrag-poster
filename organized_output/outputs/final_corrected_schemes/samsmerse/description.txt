# Scheme for Assistance to Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises (MSME): Rehabilitation of Sick Enterprises 

**Short Title:** SAMSMERSE

**Launch Date:** 2020-08-07

**Closing Date:** 2025-08-06

**Scheme For:** Infra

**Tags:** Business, Entrepreneurship, MSMEs, Start Up

The scheme &amp;quot;Scheme for Assistance to Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises (MSME)&amp;quot; is an umbrella scheme launched by the Industries and Mines Department, Gujarat. It aims to empower the state&amp;#39;s dynamic MSME sector, which significantly contributes to employment, GDP, and exports. Effective from August 7th, 2020 to August 6th, 2025, this initiative supports entrepreneurs and strengthens MSMEs, making them globally competitive. The component &amp;quot;Rehabilitation of Sick Enterprises&amp;quot; aims to support MSME sick industrial enterprises that meet the RBI&amp;#39;s criteria for sickness.

**Who is this for:** Business Entity, Industries; Category: Business & Entrepreneurship; Sector: Setting up / start-up / entrepreneurship

**Where it applies:** Gujarat; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - **Financial Reimbursement:** 50% of the cost of preparing the Draft Rehabilitation Scheme is reimbursed, up to a maximum of ₹1,00,000/-.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**How to apply:** > **Registration Process:** **Step-1:** Visit the [Investor Facilitation Portal (IFP)](https://ifp.gujarat.gov.in/DIGIGOV/) portal. Click on &quot;New Investor Registration&quot; to access the registration form. **Step-2:** Fill in the Registration Form. Click on &quot;Submit&quot;. **Step-3:** Verify the E-mail ID using the link sent on your registered E-mail ID. **Step-4:** After verification, you will be registered successfully.  > **Post-Registration Process:** **Step-1:** Visit the [Investor Facilitation Portal (IFP)](https://ifp.gujarat.gov.in/DIGIGOV/) portal. Click on &quot;Login&quot;. **Step-2:** Enter the email address and password you used during registration. **Step-3:** Click the &quot;Login&quot; button to access your account. **Step-4:** Now fill in the required details and mandatory documents about your business to complete the application process.  > **Helpdesk:** [**Contact Here.**](https://ifp.gujarat.gov.in/DIGIGOV/IFP-pages/contact.jsp?pagedisp=static)
Mode: Online.

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Industries and Mines Department.
