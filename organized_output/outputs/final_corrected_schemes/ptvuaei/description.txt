# Production of Tiatr in Villages/Urban Areas/Educational Institutions

**Short Title:** PTVUAEI

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Artist, Culture, Program, Village, Singing, Acting

The scheme &quot;Production of Tiatr in Villages/Urban Areas/Educational Institutions&quot; by the Tiatr Academy of Goa under the Art, Culture and Museums Department, Government of Goa, aims to explore hidden singing and acting talents among the youths in villages and provide encouragement to develop such talents by promoting tiatr activities among villagers, addressing the need to nurture local artistic capabilities in rural and urban areas including educational institutions. The scheme provides financial and technical support for various aspects including direction, live band, outside artistes, stage setting, background music, costumes, make-up, and light effects, along with technical guidance through directors from Tiatr Academy of Goa&#39;s Panel of Directors. 

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Sports & Culture; Sector: Culture

**Where it applies:** Goa; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - **Financial Support:** Tiatr Academy of Goa will provide financial support to the extent as fixed by Tiatr Academy of Goa towards various aspects: Direction, Live Band (with at least 2 blowing instruments), Outside Artistes, Stage Setting, Background Music, Costumes, Make-up, Light Effects.- **Technical Guidance:** Provision of director from Tiatr Academy of Goa&#39;s Panel of Directors who will direct village talents and produce the tiatr.- **Director Expenses:** The expenditure incurred in engaging the director will be borne by Tiatr Academy of Goa.- **Marketing Materials:** Provision of one banner which should be compulsorily displayed on one of the two front side wings of the stage.- **Documentation:** Tiatr Academy of Goa may video film the performance at Kala Academy.- **Observer Support:** Tiatr Academy of Goa may depute an observer from its Panel of Observers to see whether the performance has been made as per the norms of the scheme.- **Payment Method:** Payment will be made directly to the service providers.- **Sponsorship Policy:** Sponsorship from businessmen, firms etc. may be taken.  > ***Conditions*** _*The performance of the tiatr should be staged in the village/urban area of production._ _*The tiatr should be shown to the people free without charging any tickets._ _*Submit statement of expenditure enclosing all original bills (stamped receipt if the amount exceeds ₹5,000/-)._ _*Provide 20 invitation cards/entry passes to Tiatr Academy of Goa well in advance from the middle of rows A, B, or C._ _*Return banner to Tiatr Academy of Goa after the show is staged._ _*Payment will be made only after the receipt of the observer&#39;s certificate._ 

**Benefit Type:** Composite

**How to apply:** **Step 1:** The interested applicant should take a printout of the [prescribed format](https://www.goa.gov.in/wp-content/uploads/2021/06/Citizen-Charter-TAG.pdf) of the application form. **Step 2:** In the application form, fill in all the mandatory fields, attach copies of all the mandatory documents (self-attest, if required). Sign the application form and mention the date. **Step 3:** Submit the duly filled and signed application form along with the documents to - ***Tiatr Academy of Goa, AS-1 / AS-4, Block A, 2nd Floor, Campal Trade Centre, Opposite Kala Academy, Campal, Panaji, Goa, India – 403001*** _*Applicants are advised to save a copy of the submitted application form and any acknowledgment._ _*Applications are considered on a &quot;First come First Serve&quot; basis, subject to the availability of funds._ _*The application should reach Tiatr Academy of Goa 30 days in advance of the date of staging the tiatr._ _*The cheque will be issued in the name appearing at Serial Number 1 of the application form (name of individual/registered association/dramatic troupes/educational institution)._  ##### ***Post-Application Processes*** **Step 1:****** ***Schedule Coordination &amp; Chief Guest Arrangement*** The applicant must communicate the schedule of practices to Tiatr Academy of Goa well in advance. Discuss with Tiatr Academy of Goa and decide regarding the Chief Guest/Guest of Honour at the performance well in advance. **Step 2:****** ***Collect Banner &amp; Provide Invitation Cards*** The applicant must collect the banner from the office of Tiatr Academy of Goa and arrange for its compulsory display on one of the two front side wings of the stage during the performance. The applicant must provide 20 invitation cards/entry passes to Tiatr Academy of Goa well in advance, ensuring these seats are from the middle of the rows A, B, or C for proper viewing of the performance. **Step 3:****** ***Stage Performance*** The applicant must stage the tiatr in the village/urban area of production ensuring all conditions are met including free admission for public, proper acknowledgment of Tiatr Academy of Goa collaboration in all advertisements and media, and maintaining the approved script and format. **Step 4:****** ***Observer Coordination, Documentation and Reporting*** The applicant must cooperate with the observer deputed by Tiatr Academy of Goa from its Panel of Observers who will certify that the performance was in accordance to Tiatr Academy of Goa&#39;s norms, as payment will be made only after receipt of the observer&#39;s certificate. The applicant must document the performance through photographs and submit 5 copies to Tiatr Academy of Goa for records, ensuring two photographs show the Tiatr Academy of Goa banner during performance. **Step 5:****** ***Submit Financial Documentation &amp; Return Materials*** The applicant must prepare and submit a detailed statement of expenditure with all original bills (stamped receipts if amount exceeds ₹5,000/-) to Tiatr Academy of Goa for direct payment to service providers. The applicant must return the Tiatr Academy of Goa banner to the office after the show is staged to complete the programme requirements and maintain the academy&#39;s inventory. 
Mode: Offline.
