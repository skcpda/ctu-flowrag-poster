# Self Employment Scheme

**Short Title:** SES

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Self Employment, Minorities, Empowerment

**Detailed** - Under this scheme, Subsidies will be provided to the religious minority communities with the help of Nationalized / Scheduled banks to start or improve a small-scale handicraft industry, service sector, and agro-based activities. - People belonging to State Religious Minority Communities are covered under this scheme. (State Religious Minority Communities consist of People belonging to Muslim, Christian, Sikh, Buddhist, and Jain Communities). - Subsidy will be 33% of the unit cost or a maximum of Rs. 1.00 Lakh.  **The beneficiaries will be selected by the below Selection Panel.** 1. MLA of the concerned Assembly Constituency – Chairman 1. Member of the Legislative Council who is a permanent resident in that Taluk - Vice-Chairman 1. Concerned Taluk Tahsildar – Member 1. Executive Officer of concerned Taluk Panchayat-Member 1. The District Lead Bank Manager – Member 1. District Joint Director, Commerce, and Industry – Member 1. Concerned District’s Deputy Director, Women and Child Welfare Department – Member 1. Concerned District Manager, Karnataka Minority Development Corporation- Member Secretary. 

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Financial assistance

**Where it applies:** Karnataka; State
