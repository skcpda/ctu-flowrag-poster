# Soil Testing Laboratory

**Short Title:** STL

**Launch Date:** 2013-04-01

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Farmers, Agriculture, Soil Health

**Description** - Soil Testing Lab - For analysing soil samples preceding the application of fertilizer in the correct dosage in farmers&#39; field. **Objectives** - The analysis is free of cost of soil samples preceding application of fertilizers/ liming etc. for farmers along with distribution of Soil Health Card (SHC). **Mode of Selection of Beneficiary(s)** - Bonafide farmers have sufficient available cultivable land on a first come first served basis.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Agriculture,Rural & Environment; Sector: Soil health

**Where it applies:** Meghalaya; State
