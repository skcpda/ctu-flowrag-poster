# Plant Protection including Integrated Pest Management Scheme

**Short Title:** IPM

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Plant Protection, Pest Management, Agriculture, Farmer, Bio-pesticide, IPM, Organic Crop

The “Plant Protection including Integrated Pest Management (IPM) Scheme” is a State Plan Scheme, which is implemented by the Department of Agriculture &amp;amp; Farmers’ Welfare, Government of Meghalaya. The objective of the scheme is to mobilize and support farmers to use bio-pesticides and bio- agents for plant health management through the distribution of bio-pesticides and other IPM tools and through the sale of Sprayers and Plant Protection Equipment to encourage organic crop production.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Agriculture,Rural & Environment; Sector: Agricultural Inputs- seeds, fertilizer etc.

**Where it applies:** Meghalaya; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** > **Pattern of Assistance:** - Distribution of bio-pesticide and other IPM tools to farmers. - Sale of Sprayers and Plant Protection Equipment. > **Type of Assistance and Entitlement (If any):** 1. Free distribution of Bio-pesticides and other IPM tools. 1. 50% subsidy sale on Plant Protection Equipment.

**Benefit Type:** Composite

**How to apply:** &gt; **Application Process:** **Step 01:** The eligible bonafide farmers can apply/write an application on plain paper. **Step 02:** Along with the application, attach copies of all the mandatory documents (self-attest, if required). **Step 03:** Submit the duly filled and signed application along with the relevant documents at the nearest office of the Agriculture Development Officer of a CD Block/Agriculture Circle. &gt; **Mode of Selection of Beneficiary(s):** The bonafide farmers having sufficient available cultivable land shall be selected on a first come first served basis. &gt; **Whom to Contact:** 1. District Agriculture Officers of the concerned districts. 1. Sub-Divisional Agricultural Officers of the concerned sub-divisions. 1. District Training Officers, Tura/Shillong. 1. Agricultural Development Officers of the concerned Community and Rural Development Blocks.
Mode: Offline.

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: Department Of Agriculture & Farmers’ Welfare, Government Of Meghalaya; Nodal Department: Agriculture Department.
