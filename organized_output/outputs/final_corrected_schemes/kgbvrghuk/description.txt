# Kasturba Gandhi Residential Girls’ Hostel

**Short Title:** KGRGH

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Hostel, Girl Student, Upper Primary Level, Secondary Level, Kasturba Gandhi

The scheme “Kasturba Gandhi Residential Girls’ Hostel” is an initiative under the Samagra Shiksha Programme, implemented by the Secondary Education Department, Government of Uttarakhand. The scheme aims to encourage girls’ participation in education at the upper primary and secondary levels and to provide quality education to girls from disadvantaged groups studying in Classes 6 to 12. 

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Housing & Shelter

**Where it applies:** Uttarakhand; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** Girls from various disadvantaged groups such as socially disadvantaged classes (Scheduled Castes, Scheduled Tribes, Minorities, and Backward Classes), economically weaker sections, and girls with special needs are identified and enrolled in the hostels at the upper primary and secondary levels (Classes 6 to 12). 

**Benefit Type:** In Kind

**How to apply:** Before the commencement of the academic session, applications for vacant seats are invited through wide publicity. **Documents/Certificates required for admission:** 1. School leaving certificate / certificate of never having attended school / affidavit / declaration. 1. BPL certificate / ration card. 1. Income certificate / caste certificate. 1. Certificate stating that the distance between the school and the residence is more than 3 km. 
Mode: Offline.

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: Secondary Education Department, Government Of Uttarakhand; Nodal Department: Department Of Education.
