# Dependent Children Pension Scheme - Punjab

**Short Title:** Dcps

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Financial Assistance, Social Welfare, Children

The main objective behind launching Dependent Children Pension Scheme is to provide financial security cover to those childrens whose income is not stable due to non availability of his/ her father/ parents. This Scheme is implemented by Social Security and Women &amp; Child Development Department of Punjab Government. Eligible Beneficiary will get Monthly Pension direct to their bank account.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Pension

**Where it applies:** Punjab; State

**DBT Scheme:** Yes (Direct Benefit Transfer)

**Benefits / Assistance:** Amount : ₹ 1,500/- per month

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**How to apply:** **Step-1:** Applicants have to visit the [official website](https://connect.punjab.gov.in/service/citizenservice/116). **Step-2**: Click on the &quot;Click to apply services&quot;. **Step-3**: Now login on Digital Punjab portal with Email/Phone number and password. If does have any account then register [here](https://ssocitizen.psegs.in/register) **Step-4:** Fill out the application form. **Step-5**: Attach the required documents **Step-6**: Submit.
Mode: Online.

**Additional Application Processes:** **Process 2:** **Step-1**: Applicants have to visit the official website and download the [application form](https://punjab.gov.in/wp-content/uploads/2023/08/Dependent-Children-Pension.pdf). **Step-2**: Fill out the application form. **Step-3**: Attach required documents. **Step-4**: Submit the Application Form along with all the documents in the same office or centre from where it was collected.

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Department of Social Security and Development of Women and Children.
