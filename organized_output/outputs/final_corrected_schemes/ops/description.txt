# Old-Age Pension Scheme

**Short Title:** OPS

**Launch Date:** 1971-11-01

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Social Security, Financial Assistance, DBT, Retirement Pension

The Government of Himachal Pradesh, Department of Social Justice &amp; Empowerment has launched Old Age Pension Scheme with the aim to provide social security to destitute elderly people of the state. In this scheme, a financial assistance of ₹ 850/- per month to pensioners aged between 60 years to 69 years and ₹ 1500/- per month to pensioners aged 70 years and above is provided.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment

**Where it applies:** Himachal Pradesh; State

**DBT Scheme:** Yes (Direct Benefit Transfer)

**Benefits / Assistance:** 1. ₹ 850/- per month to pensioners aged between 60 years to 69 years. 1. ₹ 1500/- per month to pensioners aged 70 years and above.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**How to apply:** **Step 1:** Download the [Social Security Pension Application Form](http://esomsa.hp.gov.in/sites/default/files/scan0001-44250128_0.pdf). **Step 2:** Fill in all the mandatory fields, paste the recent passport-sized photograph (signed across), and attach copies of all the mandatory documents (self-attested). **Step 3:** Submit the duly filled and signed application form along with the documents to the concerned authority at: - Office of Panchayat - Tehsil Welfare Officer - District Welfare Officer.
Mode: Offline.

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Department of Social Justice and Empowerment.

Briefdescription: The Himachal Pradesh government offers an Old Age pension scheme to support elderly citizens who have no means of livelihood. The scheme offers financial assistance to eligible citizens based on their age and income level.
