# Award of Scholarship to the Cadets Belonging to UT of Puducherry and Studying in Rashtriya Indian Military College, Dehradun

**Short Title:** ASCBUTPSRIMCD

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Cadet, Military, Scholarship, Education

The scheme &quot;Award of Scholarship to the Cadets Belonging to UT of Puducherry and Studying in Rashtriya Indian Military College, Dehradun&quot; is a Scholarship Scheme by the Directorate of School Education, Union Territory of Puducherry. The objective of this scheme is to extend scholarships worth of ₹20,000/- per annum to the cadets belonging to the Union Territory of Puducherry who come from economically weak backgrounds and have been selected for admission into the Rashtriya Indian Military College, Dehradun.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Education & Learning; Sector: Scholarships and student finance

**Where it applies:** Puducherry; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** > **Scholarship** ₹20,000/- per annum per cadet. > **Mode of Disbursal** The amount is disbursed directly into the Aadhaar-seeded viable bank account by Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) through the Electronic Clearance System (ECS).

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**How to apply:** **Step 1:** The interested applicant should visit the Principal, Rashtriya Indian Military College, Dehradun, and request a hard copy of the prescribed format of the application form for the concerned scheme. **Step 2:** In the application form, fill in all the mandatory fields, paste the passport-sized photograph (signed across, if required), and attach copies of all the mandatory documents (self-attest, if required). **Step 3:** The interested applicant should submit the duly filled and signed application form and the documents to the Principal. The Principal then sends the applications to the Directorate of School Education, Puducherry.
Mode: Offline.

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: School Education Department, Puducherry.
