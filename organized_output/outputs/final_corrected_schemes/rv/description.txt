# Resham Vriksharopan

**Short Title:** RV

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Financial Assistance, Plantation, Farmer, Clusters, Silk

The “Resham Vriksharopan” scheme was launched by the Sericulture Department, Government of Uttarakhand. Under this scheme, selected farmers organized into clusters are provided a financial grant along with a free supply of mulberry saplings for plantation. 

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Agriculture,Rural & Environment; Sector: Environmental grants

**Where it applies:** Uttarakhand; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - Selected farmers in the cluster are provided with a grant assistance of ₹17,244 for mulberry plantation. - 2-Mulberry trees are provided free of charge by the department. 

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**How to apply:** - Representatives of cooperative societies associated with the block centers / silk self-help groups and center / range in-charges select interested beneficiaries through awareness programs / meetings held in the area. - Upon receiving a proposal for a cluster in a specific area, an expert committee conducts a field inspection to determine whether the area is suitable for silk plantation/activities. - Following the recommendation of the field inspection, mulberry plantation and other activities are carried out with the technical assistance of departmental personnel, in accordance with the guidelines of the action plan. - To prepare the P.I.S. of the selected beneficiaries, voter ID / ration card, bank account details, and land-related documents (photographs) for the area where they wish to plant trees are obtained. - Subsequently, under the supervision of departmental personnel, planting and other activities are completed, and the grant amount is provided to farmers in accordance with the completed works. 
Mode: Offline.

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Department Of Sericulture.
