# Rajasthan Chief Minister's Relief Fund - Medical Help

**Short Title:** RCMRF-MH

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Medical Help, Non BPL, Relief Fund, Rajasthan Scheme, Hospital

Rajasthan Chief Minister’s Relief Fund – medical help scheme launched on April 1999 under Chief Minister’s Relief Fund in Rajasthan. Dependents of untimely dead due to famine, floods, excessive rains, fires and accidental accidents help.  For treatment information - Assistance is accepted in recognized private hospitals from the major Government/ Chief Minister’s Relief Fund of the state. 40% maximum amount of rs1.50 lakh in government hospitals and 30% maximum amount of Rs 90,000 in recognized private hospitals, whichever is less, is accepted and sent to the concerned hospital.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Health & Wellness

**Where it applies:** Rajasthan; State
