# Viswakarma Pension Scheme

**Short Title:** VPS

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Pension, Viswakarma Community, Carpenters, Wood Worker, Goldsmiths, Iron Worker, Stone Worker, Moosari

The Scheme “Viswakarma Pension” was launched by the Backward Classes Development Department, Government of Kerala to give a pension to traditional workers of Viswakarma Communities (Carpenters (wood, iron, stone), Goldsmiths, Moosari) who have completed 60 years of age in the Kerala State.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Pension

**Where it applies:** Kerala; State

**Benefits / Assistance:** 1. The monthly pension amount of ₹1100/- will be given to the persons belonging to the traditional Viswakarma Communities.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**How to apply:** **Step 01:** The applicant should visit the [Official Website](https://bcdd.kerala.gov.in/en/schemes/development-schemes/viswakarma-pension/) of the Backward Classes Development Department, Government of Kerala, and download the [application form](https://bcdd.kerala.gov.in/wp-content/uploads/2021/07/Viswakarma-Pension-Application-Form-2.pdf). **Step 02:** Fill out the application form completely and send it along with all the relevant documents to the following addresses: Director, Backward Classes Development Department Ayyankali Bhavan, 4th Floor, Kanakanagar Vellayambalam, KawdiarP.O, Thiruvananthapuram- 695003, Kerala
Mode: Offline.

**Additional Application Processes:** **Process 2:** **Step 01:** The applicant should visit the [official website](http://www.eep.bcdd.kerala.gov.in/) of the Backward Classes Development Department, Government of Kerala. **Step 02:** If not registered, click on ‘Registration’ and fill in all the mandatory details. **Step 03:** After successful registration, click on the ‘[Login](http://www.eep.bcdd.kerala.gov.in/index.php/user/login)’ button &amp;amp; select the scheme name “Viswakarma Pension”. **Step 04:** Click on the ‘Apply’ button &amp;amp; fill in all the mandatory details in the application form. **Step 05:** Upload all the relevant documents and submit the application form.

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: Backward Classes Development Department, Government Of Kerala; Nodal Department: Backward Classes Development Department.
