# Integrated Agriculture Training Centre Scheme

**Short Title:** IATC

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Integrated Agriculture, Training Centre, Farmer, Skill, Training

The “Integrated Agriculture Training Centre (IATC) Scheme” was launched by the Department of Agriculture &amp;amp; Farmers’ Welfare, Government of Meghalaya to provide training for enhancing the skills of farmers besides imparting updated agricultural technologies to departmental functionaries. Awareness Programmes/ trainings conducted periodically.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Agriculture,Rural & Environment

**Where it applies:** Meghalaya; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** > **Pattern of Assistance:** - Conduct trainings and awareness programmes.

**Benefit Type:** In Kind

**How to apply:** &gt; **Application Process:** To avail of the benefits of the scheme, the bonafide farmers can approach the nearest Agriculture Development Officer of a Community Development Block/Agriculture Circle. &gt; **Mode of Selection of Beneficiary(s):** The bonafide farmers shall be selected on a first come first serve basis. &gt; **Whom to Contact:** 1. District Agriculture Officers of the concerned districts 1. Sub-Divisional Agricultural Officers of the concerned sub-divisions 1. District Training Officers, Tura/Shillong 1. Agricultural Development Officers of the concerned Community and Rural Development Blocks
Mode: Offline.

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: Department Of Agriculture & Farmers’ Welfare, Government Of Meghalaya; Nodal Department: Agriculture Department.
