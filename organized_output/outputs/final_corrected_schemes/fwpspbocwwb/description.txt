# Family/ Widow Pension Scheme (P.B.O.C.W.W.B)

**Short Title:** FWPSPBOCWWB

**Scheme For:** Family

**Tags:** Labour, Building Worker, Construction Worker, Family Pension, Widow Pension

The &amp;quot;Family/ Widow Pension Scheme&amp;#39;&amp;#39; by the BOCW Welfare Board, Department of Labour, Punjab, is a welfare scheme for the registered workers of the board. Under this scheme, the Board will provide financial assistance in the form of pension to the spouse of the registered construction worker in the event of his/ her death. &lt;br&gt;

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Pension

**Where it applies:** Punjab; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** **Financial Assistance:** ₹1,000/- as family pension until remarriage. Note: - If both spouses are Board beneficiaries, the surviving spouse does not receive family pension. - But if the husband/wife, either remarried or he/she gets an old age pension from the Government then they shall not be eligible for family pension. - If both the husband and wife are the beneficiaries of the Board and both are getting pension then in the event of the death of one of them, the other one shall not be eligible for family pension. - If only one spouse receives a pension, then on the eve of death of the pensioner, the surviving spouse receives family pension until they become eligible for their own pension from the Board, at which point the family pension stops. 

**Benefit Type:** Cash
