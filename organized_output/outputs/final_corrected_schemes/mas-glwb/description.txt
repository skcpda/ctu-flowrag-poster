# Marriage Assistance Scheme- Gujarat Labour Welfare Board

**Short Title:** MAS-GLWB

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Marriage, Industrial Worker, Labour, Organized Worker, Financial Assistance

The “Marriage Assistance Scheme” was launched by the Gujarat Labour Welfare Board, Labour, Skill Development &amp;amp; Employment Department, Government of Gujarat. Under the scheme, financial assistance is provided to female workers who are working in a factory/organization in the state of Gujarat after their marriage.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Marriage

**Where it applies:** Gujarat; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** 1. Under the scheme, female workers will be entitled to receive ₹11,000/- after their marriage.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**How to apply:** > **Application Process for Scheme Benefit:** **Step 01:** The applicant may visit the Sanman Portal: [https://sanman.gujarat.gov.in/](https://sanman.gujarat.gov.in/) **Step 02:** On the home page, under the tab ‘Citizen Login’, click on ‘[Please Register Here](https://sanman.gujarat.gov.in/Home/Registration)’. **Step 03:** Enter your Aadhaar Card Number, select user type, and then enter your Labour Welfare Fund Account Number. **Step 04:** Now, click on ‘Fetch’ &amp;amp; verify the details. **Step 05:** Enter user details and Password. **Step 06**: After successful registration, the applicants can login through their User ID and Password. **Step 07:** Now, select the scheme and read the instructions carefully for the selected scheme. **Step 08:** Fill out the application form and upload all the relevant documents. **Step 09:** Agree with the Rules &amp;amp; Regulations and submit the application form. A confirmation email with the application Number will be sent on the registered email ID.
Mode: Online.

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: Gujarat Labour Welfare Board; Nodal Department: Labour and Employment Department.
