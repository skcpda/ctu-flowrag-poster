# Swanath Scholarship Scheme

**Short Title:** SSSUK

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Scholarship, Technical Education, Diploma Scholarship, Orphan Student

The scheme &amp;quot;Swanath Scholarship Scheme&amp;quot; aims to provide financial support to diploma and graduate-level orphans / whose parents or either parent has died due to COVID-19/ dependent of martyr in any military and central armed force. The scheme provides ₹50,000/- annually to each eligible student. 

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Education & Learning; Sector: Scholarships and student finance

**Where it applies:** Uttarakhand; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - **Financial Assistance:** ₹50,000/- per year. 

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**How to apply:** - Scholarship schemes conducted by the All India Council for Technical Education (AICTE) follow the below-mentioned procedure: Applications under the AICTE scheme are invited through the NSP portal between the months of September to December. Scholarships are granted by AICTE on the basis of merit. - Documents required for application – High School and Intermediate mark sheets and certificates, and income certificate. - For Swanath Scheme – Death certificate of the applicant’s father/mother is required. - Students must first apply online through their respective institutions, which is then verified by the concerned principal. After that, the online application verified by the State Level Officer (S.L.O.) is forwarded to AICTE, New Delhi (AICTE). 
Mode: Online.

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Directorate of Technical Education .
