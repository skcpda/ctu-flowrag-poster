# Small Business Scheme (Gujarat Gopalak Vikas Nigam)

**Short Title:** SBSGGVN

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Grocery Stores, Dairy Parlors, Animal Feed Sales Centers, Mobile Repair Shop, Computer Repair Shop

The scheme &quot;Small Business Scheme&quot; by the Social Justice &amp; Empowerment Department, Government of Gujarat, aims to support small businesses. Through this scheme, individual loans at an annual interest rate are provided to individuals from the SEBC Rabari and Bharwad communities for establishing businesses such as grocery shops, dairy parlours, animal food sales centres, and mobile-computer repair shops. Gujarat Gopalak Vikas Nigam implements the scheme. 

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Banking,Financial Services and Insurance; Sector: Loan

**Where it applies:** Gujarat; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** 1. The maximum loan limit is ₹2,00,000/- per individual. 1. The interest rate is 6% per annum. 1. The loan amount disbursed is 95% of the total project cost. 1. The beneficiary Contribution is 5% of the total project cost. 

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**How to apply:** **Step 1: Register on the Official Portal** - Go to the [Official Website ](https://ggdconline.gujarat.gov.in/)of Gujarat Gopalak Development Corporation.- Scroll down on the homepage, find the &quot;Small Business Scheme,&quot; and click on &quot;Apply Now&quot;. - You will be directed to the [application form](https://gbcdconline.gujarat.gov.in/SchemeSmallBusiness/AddEdit). **Step 2: Access and Fill Out the Application Form** - Select &quot;Small Business Scheme&quot;, enter Unit Cost, and specify the Required Loan Amount. - Provide Aadhaar, Ration, Voter ID, full name (Last, First, Father/Husband), address, district, taluka, village, PIN, date of birth, age (21-45 as of 14/08/2023), caste, subcaste, phone, mobile, email, gender, and marital status. - Enter income certificate number, issue date, issuing officer&#39;s name, total annual family income (up to ₹ 3,00,000/-), and parent/guardian&#39;s occupation. - If &quot;Physically Handicapped,&quot; select &quot;Yes&quot; or &quot;No&quot;. If you selected &quot;Yes&quot;, provide the percentage (in %). - Indicate if any family member works in a government or private job. If &quot;Yes,&quot; provide their Name, Designation, Place, and Salary details. - Select your Education Qualification: Uneducated, Educated, Graduation, Post Graduation, or Other Degree. - If you have taken a past loan, select &quot;Yes&quot; or &quot;No&quot;. If you selected &quot;Yes&quot;, provide loan details (date, amount, paid, remaining) and your account number. - Provide bank account number, bank name, branch name, name as per account, account type (Saving/Current), MICR code, and IFSC code. **Step 3: Review and Final Submission** - Carefully review each module. Use the preview option to examine the application before final submission. Fill out the provided &quot;CAPTCHA&quot; and Click on “Submit”.  **Track and Review Status** - Applicant will receive an Application ID/Reference Number. Save or print the acknowledgement for future tracking. - Go to the [Official Website](https://ggdconline.gujarat.gov.in/) of Gujarat Gopalak Development Corporation.- Scroll down and locate the Know App Status. Click on &quot;Know App Status&quot;. - Select the Scheme name, Enter App No., and Birth Date. Fill out the Captcha and click on Submit. 
Mode: Online.
