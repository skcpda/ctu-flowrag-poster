# Keetpalan Kaksh

**Short Title:** KK

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Financial Assistance, Farmers, Rearing House, Silkworm

The “Keetpalan Kaksh&quot; scheme was launched by the Sericulture Department, Government of Uttarakhand. The scheme aims to support sericulture farmers in constructing rearing houses to improve silkworm rearing facilities. 

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Agriculture,Rural & Environment; Sector: Financial assistance

**Where it applies:** Uttarakhand; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - Financial assistance of ₹1,00,000 (80:10:10) is provided for the insect breeding facility. - The farmer will bear ₹10,000 while completing the foundation work of the insect breeding facility. - Financial assistance of ₹90,000 under the state assistance fund is provided in two equal installments. 

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**How to apply:** - Under the Silk Comprehensive Plan, farmers are selected in a cluster mode, for which a ground inspection is conducted by the departmental committee and the plan is implemented based on the suitability of the area, in which documents mentioned earlier are collected to prepare the farmer&#39;s P.I.S. In the construction of the insect rearing room, the farmer lays the foundation of the insect rearing room using his own funds. - Based on the progress of the construction work and the guidelines of the plans, the state assistance amount is paid into the farmer&#39;s account upon the recommendation of the cluster in-charge. 
Mode: Offline.

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Department Of Sericulture.

**References / Annexures:** - **Guidelines (Page No. 244)**: https://uk.gov.in/department92/library_file/file-04-12-2023-06-02-23.pdf

Briefdescription: The scheme aims to provide ₹1,00,000 assistance (80:10:10 pattern) for farmers to construct rearing houses, ensuring better infrastructure for silkworm farming.
