# “RAKSHAK PURASKARS’’ Award

**Short Title:** RPA

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Award, Crime, Women, Girl, Violence, Harrassment

The Directorate of Women and Child Development, Government of Goa, has instituted the “RAKSHAK PURASKARS” Award. The awards are being conferred from the year 2015 onwards and presented on Independence Day i.e. 15th August. The awards are conferred to two persons every year. The awards are conferred to the individuals who have shown exceptional courage, especially in adverse circumstances in coming to aid, protecting or helping beyond the call of duty and assisting in dealing with the victims of any type of violence, attack or harassment against women and girls at home, in the workplace and everywhere in the public domain and has shown their respect and dignity to women in society within the State of Goa. The Award aims to spread the message to encourage and motivate all to bestow respect and dignity to women in society in the State of Goa.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Reporting crimes and getting compensation

**Where it applies:** Goa; State

**Benefits / Assistance:** The recipients of the awarded are given a scroll/citation and cash prize of ₹ 20,000/- each.

**Benefit Type:** Composite

**How to apply:** **Step 1:** In the [prescribed format](https://www.dwcd.goa.gov.in/wp-content/uploads/2020/05/RAKSHAK-PURASKAR-PRESS-NOTE-AND-APPLICATION-FORM.pdf) of the application form, fill in all the mandatory fields, paste the passport-sized photograph (signed across), and attach copies of all the mandatory documents (self-attest if required). **Step 2:** Visit the Office of the Directorate of Women &amp; Child Development (Old Education Building, 2nd Floor, 18th June Road, Panaji Goa 403001), and submit the duly filled and signed application form along with the copies of the required documents to the Concerned Officer. **Step 3:** Acquire the receipt/acknowledgement of the successful submission of the application.  > **Helpdesk** For any clarifications regarding the application, the applicants may contact the Directorate on Phone No. **2235308/2426112** during office hours.
Mode: Offline.

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Women and Child Development Department.
