# Jharkhand State Food Security Scheme

**Short Title:** JSFSS

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Subsidized Rate, JSFSS, Food Grain

The &quot;Jharkhand State Food Security Scheme (JSFSS)&quot; by the Department of Food, Public Distribution and Consumer Affairs, Government of Jharkhand, aims to provide subsidized/free food grains to eligible beneficiaries. Through this scheme, food grain, primarily rice, is provided at a subsidized rate or free of cost to individuals holding a Green Card. The scheme is implemented by the Directorate of Food and Consumer Affairs, Ranchi.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Food Security / Public Distribution System

**Where it applies:** Jharkhand; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** 1. Subsidized/free rice is provided to Green Card holders beneficiaries under JSFSS. 1. Benefits are delivered directly through the Directorate of Food and Consumer Affairs.  **NOTE****:** Priority is given to individuals from economically weaker sections.

**Benefit Type:** In Kind

**How to apply:** 1. Visit the nearest ration shop or designated enrollment center with the required documents. 1. If an Aadhaar is not available, apply for Aadhaar enrollment at an authorized center. 1. Submit the Aadhaar number or Aadhaar Enrolment Identification Slip during registration. 1. Verify the eligibility and documents with the designated officer. 1. Collect the food grains from the distribution center as per the monthly schedule.
Mode: Offline.

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: Directorate Of Food And Consumer Affairs, Ranchi; Nodal Department: Food, Public Distribution and Consumer affairs Department.
