# Economic Help To Sahariya Tribal Girl Students (Class 11 And 12)

**Short Title:** EHSTGS

**Launch Date:** 2001-01-01

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Financial Assistance, Education, Girl Empowerment

To promote girl education by motivating Saharia tribe girl students of Baran district to study in higher education and provide financial assistance to Sahariya tribe girl students for encouragement for higher secondary education. Those who are studying regularly in government schools in higher secondary classes (11th and 12th) and who have not got accommodation in government hostels. &lt;br&gt; &lt;br&gt;

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Education & Learning; Sector: Scholarships and student finance

**Where it applies:** Rajasthan; State

**Benefits / Assistance:** The student will be paid ₹3,500/- for a total of 10 months at the rate of Rs. 350/- per month.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**How to apply:** 1. Applicants have to visit the official [web portal](https://tad.rajasthan.gov.in/Content/Hindi/index.aspx).1. On the Home page, click on &quot;Online Portal For Tad Educational Incentive Schemes&quot;. 1. Now click on “click here to apply online”. 1. If applicants are existing SSO users, log in with their SSO user and password. and If applicants don’t have SSO Id then create one by clicking “Registration”. Applicants can create SSO IDs by using Janaadhar &amp; Google 1. After completing the SSO profile click on the “SCHOLARSHIP (CE, TAD, MINORITY)” App. 1. Select the “STUDENT” button. 1. Select Appropriate Scheme and click on the “Next” button 1. Fill out all required academic information and click on the “Submit” button
Mode: Online.

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Tribal Area Development Department.
