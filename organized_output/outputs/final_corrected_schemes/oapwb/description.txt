# Old Age Pension

**Short Title:** OAPWB

**Launch Date:** 2010-01-01

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Financial Assistance, Social Welfare, Pension, Old Age, Senior Citizens

In 2010, the West Bengal state government, through the Women and Child Development and Social Welfare Department, launched a compassionate initiative called the &amp;quot;Old Age Pension&amp;quot; scheme. The West Bengal Disability Pension Scheme provides financial assistance to persons who are above 60 years and unable to work and earn a living.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Pension

**Where it applies:** West Bengal; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - ₹1,000/- per month.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Exclusions / Not eligible:** &gt; **Professional Beggars and Mendicants:** - Professional beggars and mendicants of urban areas (cities and towns) are not eligible for pension under this scheme. &gt; **Recipients of Other Pensions:** - Persons receiving “Disability Pension”, “Widow Pension”, “Farmers’ Pension”, “Political Sufferers”, or any other pension of any State Government or the Central Government shall not be eligible for pension under this scheme.

**How to apply:** &gt; **Application Form(**[**Form-P)**](https://wbswpension.gov.in/readwrite/miscellaneous/form-P.pdf) **for Pension may be obtained free of cost from following offices namely****:** 1. Office of the Block Development Officer or the Executive Officer of the concerned Panchayat Samity in case of the applicant resides in the rural areas under the said Panchayat Samity. 1. Office of the Sub-Divisional Officer in case of the applicant resides in Municipal / Notified areas outside the areas of Kolkata Municipal Corporation. 1. Office of the Controller of Vagrancy, West Bengal situated at Purta Bhawan, Bidhan Nagar, Kolkata-700091, in case of the applicant resides within the areas under Kolkata Municipal Corporation. &gt; ***Application Form for Pension shall be addressed to******:-*** 1. The District Magistrate of the concerned District and shall be submitted to the Block Development Officer or the Executive Officer of the Panchayat Samity in case of the applicant residing in the rural areas under a Panchayat Samity. 1. The District magistrate of the concerned District and Shall be submitted to the Sub-Divisional Officer in case of the applicant residing in Municipal / Notified areas outside the areas of Kolkata Municipal Corporation. 1. The Controller of Vagrancy, West Bengal and shall be submitted to the Controller Vagrancy West Bengal in case of the applicant residing within the areas under Kolkata Municipal Corporation. &gt; **District Nodal Officers:** For any query related to the scheme concerned [District Nodal Officers](https://wbswpension.gov.in/pages/wcd_key_officials.php) can be contacted.
Mode: Offline.
