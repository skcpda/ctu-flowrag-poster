# Theka Shramik, Gharelu Mahila Kamgar Evan Hamaal Shramik Ke Bachcho Hetu Chhaatravrtti Yojana

**Short Title:** TSHMCY

**Launch Date:** 2015-03-15

**Scheme For:** Family

**Tags:** Education, Scholarship

Labor Department of Chhattisgarh State started this scheme &amp;amp;quot;Theka Shramik, Gharelu Mahila Kamgar Evan Hamaal Shramik Ke Bachcho Hetu Chhaatravrtti Yojana&amp;amp;quot; for unorganized worker on 11 March 2015. The main objective of the scheme to provides financial assistance to the children of sanitation workers in India for higher education.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Education & Learning

**Where it applies:** Chhattisgarh; State

**Benefits / Assistance:** - Financial assistance provided according to the below table ClassesBoy Students (Annual Scholarship amount)Girl Students (Annual Scholarship amount)Class I to V₹500/-₹750/-Class VI to VIII₹750/-₹1,000/-Class IX to XII₹1,000/-₹1,500/-Graduation ( BA, BSe, BCom)₹1,500/-₹2,000/-Post Graduation (MA , MSe, M.Com, Diploma)₹2,500/-₹3,000/-Pursuing graduate level professional course₹3,000/-₹4,000/-Studying for professional level, doing PhD or research work₹4,000/-₹5,000/-

**Benefit Type:** Cash
