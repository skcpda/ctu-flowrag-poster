# Financial Assistance For The Marriage Of Daughters Of Poor Widows And Orphan Girls

**Short Title:** FAMDPWOG

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Financial Asisstance, Marriage, Widow, Orphan, Girl Child

The scheme of &quot;Financial Assistance for the Marriage of Daughters of Poor Widows and Orphan Girls&quot; is the welfare scheme by the Government of National Capital Territory of Delhi to provide financial assistance to poor widows for performing the marriage of their daughters (up to two daughters) and to provide financial assistance to the guardians including Homes/Institutions or foster parents of an orphan girl or an orphan girl for her marriage.  **Note:** This scheme was been transferred from the Department for the Welfare of SC/ST/OBC/Min. to Social Welfare Department from the financial year 2006-07.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Financial assistance

**Where it applies:** Delhi; State

**Benefits / Assistance:** The quantum of Assistance is one-time ₹ 30,000/- up to two daughters only.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Exclusions / Not eligible:** NA

**How to apply:** The application is to be made online on the e-District portal (Delhi) Link: [https://edistrict.delhigovt.nic.in/](https://edistrict.delhigovt.nic.in/)  > **Steps to register on the e-district portal of GNCTD -** **Step 1:** Log on to https://edistrict.delhigovt.nic.in/ **Step 2:** Under Citizen Corner click on &quot;New User&quot; **Step 3:** Click on select the document – Aadhar or Voter ID **Step 4:** Enter your Aadhar Card Number/Voter ID Card Number **Step 5:** Type the Captcha in the box shown. The &quot;Citizen Registration Form&quot; will Open. **Step 6:** Fill up all fields including details of the Present Residential Address **Step 7:** Enter Captcha, and Click on Continue. **Step 8:** Login ID and Password will be received on the given mobile number/e-mail address. **Step 9:** Now Registration on the e-district portal is complete  > **Steps to register for the scheme -** **Step 1:** Click on Registered User Login. Enter the given login ID and Password. Enter Captcha and Click on Login. **Step 2:** Go to Main Page (Main Page) will open click on apply online. Drop Box applies for services will open – click on the link. **Step 3:** A list of Departments providing online services shall open – Select the Department of Women &amp; Child Development. **Step 4:** Three Financial Assistance Schemes will be shown. Choose the relevant scheme and Click on Apply. **Step 5:** Check the BASIC/PERSONAL DETAILS FORM and click on Continue. The form will open fill up all required fields &amp; upload all required documents as per the requirement of the scheme selected. **Step 6:** Then click next and upload the photograph. Then click on finish. One OTP will be received on the registered mobile number. Enter OTP and Submit. **Step 7:** Acknowledgment of successful submission shall be generated.
Mode: Online.

**Additional Application Processes:** **Process 2:** - The application can also be submitted offline at the concerned District Office for the time being. The application form is available at http://www.wcddel.in &lt;[downloads](https://edistrict.delhigovt.nic.in/in/en/Public/ApplicationForm.html)&gt; and at all District Offices, DWCD.
