# Kanya Sumangala Yojana

**Short Title:** KSY

**Launch Date:** 2019-04-01

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Financial Assistance, Girl Welfare, Child Empowerment, Education

Women empowerment is the commitment of the state government. In view of the above, it has been decided by the state government to implement the &amp;quot;Kanya Sumangala Yojana&amp;quot;. The main objective of Kanya Sumangala Yojana is to end female feticide, establish an equal gender ratio, stop the malpractice of child marriage, and improve the health of girls. And to encourage education, help in making girls self-reliant, to develop positive thinking in the society towards the birth of a girl child.&amp;quot;

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Financial assistance

**Where it applies:** Uttar Pradesh; State

**Benefits / Assistance:** Kanya Sumangala Yojana will be applicable in six categories. The categories of beneficiary classification and distribution of funds for them are determined as follows:- First PhaseAt the birth of a girl child.Rs. 2,000/- lump sumSecond PhaseAfter complete vaccination of the girl child up to one year. Rs. 1,000/- lump sumThird PhaseAfter the admission of girl child in first classRs. 2,000/- lump sumFourth PhaseAfter the admission of girl child in class VIRs. 2,000/- lump sumFifth PhaseAfter the admission of girl child in class IXRs. 3,000/- lump sumSixth PhaseSuch girls who have passed class 12th and have taken admission in graduation or diploma courses of 02 years or more duration.Rs. 5,000/- lump sum

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**How to apply:** 1. First of all, the Applicants have to go to the [official website](https://mksy.up.gov.in/women_welfare/index.php) of MKSY of the Women and Child Development Department. 1. On the home page, click on [Citizen Service Portal](https://mksy.up.gov.in/women_welfare/citizen/guest_login.php) option. 1. After clicking on the option, a new page will open. 1. On this page, applicants will see the option of consent. There will be a tick mark on &#39;I agree&#39; and click on &#39;Continue&#39;. After clicking on it, the next page will open on which applicants will get the registration form. 1. All the information asked in the registration form like name, Aadhaar number, mobile number and OTP will have to be verified by entering. After verification, registration will be done. 1. User ID will be received on a mobile phone after successful registration. 1. After login, applicants will see the registration form of the girl. 1. Upload the required documents. 1. Submit.
Mode: Online.

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Department of Women & Child Development.
