# Mukhyamantri Balika Protsahan Yojana (Matriculation)

**Short Title:** MBPYM

**Tags:** Education, Girl, Incentives, Scholarship

&amp;quot;Mukhyamantri Balika Protsahan Yojana&amp;quot;, launched by the Education Department, Bihar, aims to promote education among girls and encourage academic excellence. The scheme rewards girls who secure a first division in their matriculation examination with a one-time financial incentive of ₹10,000/-.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Education & Learning; Sector: Scholarships and student finance

**Where it applies:** Bihar; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - **Financial Incentive:** ₹10,000/-.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**How to apply:** > **Registration Process:** **Step 1:** Visit the Registration [page](https://medhasoft.bih.nic.in/medhamat2022/(S(isowffkahr0cb41idfmeymls))/Default.aspx). **Step 2:** Scroll down below and click on &quot;Apply For Matric 2022 Scholarship Only&quot;. **Step 3:** Read the instructions and click on the check-boxes. Click on &quot;Continue&quot;. **Step 4:** Enter all the mandatory details and create a new password. Validate the Captcha code. **Step 5:** Click on “Submit” to generate the login ID and Password. > **Application Process:** **Step 1:** Visit the Login [page](https://medhasoft.bih.nic.in/medhamat2022/(S(isowffkahr0cb41idfmeymls))/Login.aspx). **Step 2:** Enter all the mandatory details and upload all the required documents. **Step 3:** Recheck all entries before final submission, no modifications can be made in the application after final submission. **Step 4:** Click on “Submit” and take a printout of a copy of the submitted application. > **Check application Details:** Click [Here](https://medhasoft.bih.nic.in/medhamat2022/(S(isowffkahr0cb41idfmeymls))/StudentStatus.aspx). > **Payment Done Information:** Click [Here](https://medhasoft.bih.nic.in/medhamat2022/(S(isowffkahr0cb41idfmeymls))/PaymentDoneList.aspx).  > ***Helpdesk:*** For technical problems: Click [Here](https://medhasoft.bih.nic.in/medhamat2022/(S(isowffkahr0cb41idfmeymls))/Helpdesk.aspx).
Mode: Offline.
