# Refund of Subscriptions for the Construction Workers

**Short Title:** ROSFTCW

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Labour, Construction Worker, Subscriptions Refund

The “Refund of Subscriptions for the Construction Workers” scheme by the Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board, Labour Department, West Bengal, is a welfare scheme for the registered workers of the board. Under this scheme, the entire subscription deposited by the beneficiary will be refunded with 8% interest to the subscriber on maturity at the age of 60 years or unwillingness to continue as a beneficiary under the Board after 3 years or to the nominee (legal heir) upon the death of the subscriber. 

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment

**Where it applies:** West Bengal; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - **Quantum of Assistance:** Subscription deposited by the beneficiary will be refunded with 8% interest to the subscriber.

**Benefit Type:** Cash
