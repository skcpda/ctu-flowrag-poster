# Integrated Pest Management Demonstration Cum Training

**Short Title:** IPMDCT

**Scheme For:** Infra

**Tags:** Agriculture, Farmer, Integrated Pest Management, Training

The “Integrated Pest Management Demonstration Cum Training” scheme by the Agriculture-Farmers Welfare Department, Tamil Nadu, supports pest management practices through demonstrations and training in Farmer Field Schools. Financial assistance is granted for each 40-hectare demonstration and training cluster. The scheme aims to enhance pest control knowledge among farmers, helping them manage pests effectively while reducing reliance on chemical pesticides.

**Who is this for:** Individual, University / Institution; Category: Agriculture,Rural & Environment; Sector: Financial assistance

**Where it applies:** Tamil Nadu; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - **For Farmer Field Schools:** Financial Assistance: Grants of ₹17,000/- is allowed towards Demonstration of 40 Ha. each and Training per Farmers Field School through cluster approach. - **For Farmers:** Training &amp;amp; Demonstration: Hands-on training in pest management through Farmer Field Schools. 

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**How to apply:** **Step 1:** Collect the application form from the nearest Agricultural Office. **Step 2:** Fill out the application form with the required details and attach mandatory documents (self-attested, if required). **Step 3:** Submit the form to the concerned office: &gt; **Village Level:** Submit the application to the Assistant Agricultural Officer. &gt; **Block Level:** Submit to the Agricultural Officer or Deputy Agricultural Officer. &gt; **District Level:** Submit to the Joint Director of Agriculture.
Mode: Offline.

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Agriculture Department.

**References / Annexures:** - **Guidelines**: https://www.tn.gov.in/scheme/data_view/6848

**Additional Information:**
Briefdescription: The “Integrated Pest Management Demonstration Cum Training” scheme provides financial assistance for 30 farmers to conduct pest management demonstrations and training using a cluster approach in Farmer Field Schools.
