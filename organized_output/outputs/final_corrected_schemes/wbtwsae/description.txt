# West Bengal Transport Workers’ Social Security Scheme: Assistance for Education of Children

**Short Title:** WBTWSAE

**Scheme For:** Family

**Tags:** Social Security, Transport Worker, Engineering And Medical, Education, Scholarship, Higher Secondary, Graduation, Post-Graduation

The “West Bengal Transport Workers’ Social Security Scheme: Assistance for Education of Children” by the Labour Department, West Bengal, offers one-time financial aid to beneficiaries to ease the cost of their children&amp;#39;s (maximum two) education. The scheme is structured to provide assistance based on the level of education. &lt;br&gt;

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Education & Learning; Sector: Scholarships and student finance

**Where it applies:** West Bengal; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - **Higher Secondary/ Diploma:** ₹4,000/-. - **Graduation/ Diploma:** ₹6,000/-. - **Post-Graduation:** ₹10,000/-. - **Engineering/ Medical:** ₹30,000/-. &lt;br&gt;

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**How to apply:** **Step 1:** Visit the “[eDistrict 2.0](https://edistrict.wb.gov.in/portal/home)” portal. Click on Log In/Sign Up. **Step 2:** In the User Login window, scroll down to the bottom and click on “Register”. **Step 3:** Fill in all the details carefully, enter a username, and click on “Register”. **Step 4:** Upon entering the One Time Password received on your mobile, you will be successfully registered. **Step 5:** Now in the User Login window, enter your Username or email and captcha and click on “Sign In”. **Step 6:** Enter the received O.T.P and click on “Submit”. **Step 7:** Upon successful Login, You will be redirected to the “Dashboard”. From the right side menu apply for the concerned scheme.
Mode: Online.

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Labour Department.

**References / Annexures:** - **Guideline 1**: https://employmentbankwb.gov.in/social_security_scheme.php#ans8 - **Guideline 2**: https://wblc.gov.in/sites/default/files/ckfinder/userfiles/images/WBTWSSS_at_a_glance.pdf

**Additional Information:**
Briefdescription: This scheme provides financial support to beneficiaries for their children's (maximum two) education at various levels, including higher secondary, graduation, post-graduation, and professional courses like engineering and medical studies.
