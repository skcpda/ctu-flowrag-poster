# Incentive Scheme for Marrying a Disabled Young Man/Woman

**Short Title:** ISMDYMW

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Marriage, Divyang, Disabled Person, PwD

The scheme &amp;quot;Incentive Scheme for Marrying a Disabled Young Man/Woman&amp;quot; is implemented by the Social Welfare Department, Government of Uttarakhand. The scheme aims to provide financial assistance to individuals with 40% or more disability when they marry a non-disabled person, or when both partners have disabilities. 

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Marriage

**Where it applies:** Uttarakhand; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** On marriage, the couple is given ₹25,000/- as incentive. 

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**How to apply:** - At present, applications for this scheme are submitted offline. The application form can be obtained from the office of the Assistant Social Welfare Officer or downloaded from the departmental website ([https://socialwelfare.uk.gov.in](https://socialwelfare.uk.gov.in)). The duly filled application form, in the prescribed format, must be submitted to the office of the Assistant Social Welfare Officer.&amp;quot; - The following documents must be attached with the application form: Aadhaar card of the couple, Disability Certificate, Copy of U.D.I. card, Permanent residence certificate / certificate confirming residence in the state for at least 5 years, Copy of bank account statement, Copy of marriage registration certificate, Affidavit (stating that the applicant is not an income tax payer, not involved in any criminal activities, and does not have any living spouse from before) - The applications will be verified by the Assistant Social Welfare Officer and forwarded to the District Social Welfare Officer with recommendations based on eligibility. At the district level, the District Committee will review the application, and upon approval, the incentive amount will be provided to the applicant as per rules. 
Mode: Offline.

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Department Of Social Welfare.

**References / Annexures:** - **Guidelines (Page No. 18)**: https://uk.gov.in/department92/library_file/file-04-12-2023-06-02-23.pdf

**Additional Information:**
Briefdescription: The scheme aims to provide financial assistance of ₹25,000/- to couples on marriage, where at least one partner has 40% or more disability and marries a non-disabled person, or where both partners have disabilities.
