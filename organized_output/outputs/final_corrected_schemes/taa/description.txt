# Technology Acquisition Assistance

**Short Title:** TAA

**Launch Date:** 2024-04-12

**Closing Date:** 2029-03-31

**Scheme For:** Infra

**Tags:** Reimbursement, Technology Acquisition, MSME, Micro Enterprise, Small Enterprise, Software

The &amp;quot;Technology Acquisition Assistance&amp;quot; scheme has been introduced under the Rajasthan MSME Policy 2024 by the Department of Industries and Commerce, Government of Rajasthan. The scheme aims to support micro and small enterprises (MSEs) in the state in acquiring advanced technology or software related to their production/manufacturing processes. This assistance encourages efficiency, modernization, and competitiveness through measurable performance improvements. The scheme will remain in force until 31st March 2029. ***Scheme Implementation*** This scheme component will be implemented through the concerned District Industries &amp; Commerce Centre (DICC) under the aegis of the Department of Industries &amp; Commerce. The Office of the Commissioner, Industries &amp; Commerce will be the nodal agency for implementation and supervision at the state level.

**Who is this for:** Business Entity; Category: Business & Entrepreneurship; Sector: Sales of goods, services & data protection

**Where it applies:** Rajasthan; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** **One-time Reimbursement Assistance:** - Up to 50% of the cost incurred for technology/software acquisition. - Maximum assistance limit: ₹5,00,000/- per unit. **MSEs may acquire technology/software from premier national institutes like:** - Indian Institute of Science (IISc) - Indian Institutes of Technology (IITs) - National Institutes of Technology (NITs) - National Institutes of Design (NIDs) - Council of Scientific and Industrial Research (CSIR), OR - Other institutions set up by Government of India/Government of Rajasthan.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**How to apply:** ***Application process for the reimbursement of the cost of acquisition of technology/software*** **Step 1:** The interested applicant downloads the application form ([Annexure 3.1](https://jankalyanfile.rajasthan.gov.in//Content/UploadFolder/OrderEntry/ICOM/2025/Guidelines/O_030325_26f50adb-f7b4-4eb5-b94d-1e5e45451a85.pdf)) from the official website. **Step 2:** In the application form, fill in all the mandatory fields, and attach copies of all the mandatory documents (self-attest, if required). **Step 3:** Submit the duly filled and signed application form along with the documents to the respective General Manager, District Industries &amp; Commerce Centre (DICC). ***Resubmit the Application:*** **Step 1:** The applicant enterprise would be required to submit the application after the acquisition and deployment of the technology from the Technology Institution. **Step 2:** The State Government would reserve the right to approve/partially approve or reject any of the application. ***Post Application Process*** **Step 1:** Evaluation of the application by GM, DICC, including the Chartered Engineer&#39;s certificate. **Step 2:** Approval &amp; sanction by the District Level Task Force Committee (DLTFC). **Step 3:** Upon approval, an approval letter will be issued. **Step 4:** Disbursement of funds to the applicant’s registered bank account.
Mode: Offline.

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: District Industries & Commerce Centre; Nodal Department: Industry Department.
