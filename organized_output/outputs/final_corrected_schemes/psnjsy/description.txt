# Panchayat Samiti Nandishala Jan Sahbhagita Yojana

**Short Title:** PSNJSY

**Scheme For:** Infra

**Tags:** Animal Shelter, Animal Husbandry

The &amp;quot;Panchayat Samiti Nandishala Jan Sahbhagita Yojana&amp;quot;, launched in 2021-22, by the Animal Husbandry Department, Rajasthan, aims to support the construction of permanent infrastructure for the shelter of stray male cattle in each Panchayat Samiti. The scheme provides financial assistance for Nandishala.

**Who is this for:** Government Organisation, Joint Liability Groups (JLGS), Registered Societies, NGO; Category: Housing & Shelter; Sector: Shelter, integrated support and assistance

**Where it applies:** Rajasthan; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - One-time assistance of 90% of the total construction cost.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**How to apply:** **Step 1:** Obtain the application form from the Office of the District Joint Director, Animal Husbandry Department. **Step 2:** Review the tender notices and apply online as per the guidelines. **Step 3:** Submit the application form along with the required documents. **Step 4:** Await verification and approval from the concerned authority. &lt;br&gt; &gt; **Scheme Contact Details:** &gt; Nodal Officer Name &amp;amp; Designation: Dr. Anil Kaushik, Joint Director &gt; Phone Number: 9829332050 &gt; Email: dir.dgs@rajasthan.gov.in
Mode: Offline.

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Animal Husbandry Department.
