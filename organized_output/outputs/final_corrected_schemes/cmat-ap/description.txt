# Chief Ministergi Artistesinggi Tengbang (CMAT) – Artiste’s Pension

**Short Title:** CMAT-AP

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Artist, Pension, Financial Assistance, Manipur, Culture

The scheme &quot;Chief Ministergi Artistesinggi Tengbang (CMAT) – Artiste’s Pension&quot; by the Dept. of Art and Culture, Govt. of Manipur provides a monthly pension of up to ₹ 4,000/- to the aged reputed artists who have contributed to making the tradition, culture, and art forms of Manipur more vibrant by working in various fields of arts such as Theatre (traditional/modern) / Shumang Leela / Nupi Leela / Mime/ Classical Dance / Traditional Dance / Folk Dance / Tribal Dance, etc.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Sports & Culture; Sector: Culture

**Where it applies:** Manipur; State
