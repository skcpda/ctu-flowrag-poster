# Post Matric College Scholarship (Scheduled Caste)

**Short Title:** PMCSSC

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** College, Scholarship, Students, Scheduled Caste

Launched in 1976-77, the &quot;Post Matric College Scholarship&quot; is a scheme by the Scheduled Caste Welfare Department, Government of Madhya Pradesh. The scheme provides financial assistance to encourage Scheduled Caste students to pursue higher education. One-third of the subsistence allowance is payable to students residing in departmental hostels who are provided free lodging and boarding facilities.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Education & Learning; Sector: Scholarships and student finance

**Where it applies:** Madhya Pradesh; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** Post-matric students are provided with scholarships.

**Benefit Type:** In Kind

**Exclusions / Not eligible:** 1. Non-Govt Institutions: Students whose parents&#39; income exceeds ₹2.50 lakh are not eligible for the subsistence allowance. 1. Govt Institutions: Students with an income above ₹2.50 lakh are not eligible for the subsistence allowance but are eligible for full fee reimbursement. 1. Sambal Yojana Families: Students from Sambal Yojana families do not have to pay fees at government schools and are not eligible for fee reimbursement.

**How to apply:** **Registration Process:** **Step1:** Go to the official [State Scholarship Portal 2.0](https://scholarshipportal.mp.nic.in/Index.aspx) for Madhya Pradesh. **Step2:** Scroll down the page and find the &quot;Student Corner&quot; section on the left side of the screen. **Step3:** Under the Student Corner, click on the “Student Registration” option. **Step4:** Complete the online registration form by filling in all mandatory fields and uploading the required documents in the specified format and size. **Step5:** Carefully review the details entered and the documents uploaded. Once you have verified everything, click on the &quot;Submit&quot; button. **Step6:** After submitting, you will receive your registration number on the registered email address or mobile number.  **Application Process:** **Step1:** Go to the [State Scholarship Portal 2.0](https://scholarshipportal.mp.nic.in/Index.aspx) for Madhya Pradesh. **Step2:** Scroll down to the &quot;Student Corner&quot; section on the left and click on &quot;Student Login.&quot; Enter your User ID and Password to log in. **Step3:** After logging in, a dashboard will appear in front of you. From your dashboard, select the scheme you wish to apply for and click on it. **Step4:** Complete the application form by filling in all required fields and uploading necessary documents in the specified format and size. **Step5:** Double-check the information and documents provided, then click on the &quot;Submit&quot; button to submit your application.
Mode: Online.
