# Pension Scheme (BBOCWWB)

**Short Title:** PSBBOCWWB

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Pension, Construction Worker, Building Worker, Labour, Unorganized Worker

The &amp;#39;Pension Scheme&amp;#39; is implemented by the Bihar Building &amp;amp; Other Construction Workers Welfare Board (BBOCWWB), Labour Resources Department, Government of Bihar. Under this scheme, registered construction workers who have completed a minimum of five years of membership and have attained the age of 60 are eligible for a pension of ₹1,000/- per month. This benefit is provided only if the worker has not received a pension under any other social security scheme.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Pension

**Where it applies:** Bihar; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** Under this scheme, registered construction workers receive a monthly pension of ₹1,000/- after attaining the age of 60 years.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**How to apply:** &amp;gt; **Registration of a Construction Worker under BBOCWWB:** **Step 1:** Visit the [Official Website](https://bocwscheme.bihar.gov.in/home) of the Bihar Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board. **Step 2:** On the homepage, click on &amp;amp;quot;[Labour Registration](https://bocwscheme.bihar.gov.in/worker_reg)&amp;amp;quot;. **Step 3:** Enter your Aadhar card number and name, then click &amp;amp;quot;Authenticate&amp;amp;quot;. **Step 4:** A dashboard will appear. Enter all required details, ensuring that all fields marked with an asterisk (*) are filled in correctly. **Step 5:** Click on &amp;amp;quot;Submit&amp;amp;quot; and proceed to pay the application fee online. **Step 6:** You will receive your construction worker registration number upon successful registration. &amp;gt; **Apply for Scheme Benefits:** **Step 1:** Visit the [Official Website](https://bocwscheme.bihar.gov.in/home) of the Bihar Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board. **Step 2:** Click on &amp;amp;quot;Login&amp;amp;quot; and select &amp;amp;quot;Labour&amp;amp;quot;. **Step 3:** Enter your registration number &amp;amp;amp; year of Birth, click &amp;amp;quot;Show&amp;amp;quot; to view your details. **Step 4:** Select the scheme you wish to apply for to avail the benefits. **Step 5:** Enter all required information and upload the necessary documents. **Step 6:** Click on &amp;amp;quot;Submit Application&amp;amp;quot; to complete the process. &amp;gt; **Application Tracking:** Applicant can track their application status online through the [official website](https://bocwscheme.bihar.gov.in/home) of the Bihar Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board. Relevant updates will be sent to the registered mobile number. 
Mode: Online.

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: Bihar Building & Other Construction Workers Welfare Board; Nodal Department: Labour Resources Department.
