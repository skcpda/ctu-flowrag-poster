# Intensive Fisheries Development Programme - Subsidy on Commercial Four-Wheeler Vehicles

**Short Title:** IFDPSCFWV

**Tags:** Subsidy, Four-wheeler, Fishermen, Fish Transportation, Commercial Vehicle

The &amp;quot;Subsidy on Commercial Four-Wheeler Vehicles&amp;quot; is a scheme component under the Intensive Fisheries Development Programme, implemented by the Fisheries Department, Government of Haryana. The scheme aims to strengthen the fisheries value chain by providing transportation support to fish farmers. Under this scheme, eligible fish farmers receive a subsidy of 25% of the actual cost on the purchase of a commercial four-wheeler vehicle used for fisheries-related activities.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Agriculture,Rural & Environment; Sector: Fishing and hunting

**Where it applies:** Haryana; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - Subsidy under the scheme is provided at the rate of 25% of the actual cost of the four-wheeler (₹9 lakh) or ₹2.25 lakh, whichever is lower. 

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Exclusions / Not eligible:** 1. The applicant should not be employed in any government or semi-government institution/organization.
