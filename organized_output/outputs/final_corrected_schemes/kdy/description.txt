# Kailash Darshan Yatra

**Short Title:** KDY

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Travel, Tourism, Pilgrim, Culture, Heritage

Launched in 2001, the scheme &quot;Kailash Darshan Yatra&quot; by the Gujarat Holy Pilgrimage Development Board, Tourism Department, Government of Gujarat, aims to provide financial assistance to pilgrims from Gujarat undertaking the sacred Kailash Mansarovar pilgrimage. The scheme offers one-time financial assistance to selected applicants who complete the yatra. To be eligible, the applicant must be a selected pilgrim from Gujarat and submit proof of yatra completion within 90 days. Applications for this scheme are accepted offline.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Sports & Culture; Sector: Culture

**Where it applies:** Gujarat; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** One-time financial assistance is provided to the pilgrim for undertaking the Kailash Darshan Yatra. **Validity:** Once in a lifetime per pilgrim. _*The scheme guidelines do not specify the amount of the benefit; applicants should verify with the authorities._

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**How to apply:** **Step 1:** The interested applicant should take a printout of the [prescribed format](https://yatradham.gujarat.gov.in/Documents/SubDeals_2018-9-27_365.pdf) of the application form. **Step 2:** In the application form, fill in all the mandatory fields (details of the authorized travel agent/organizer, details of the pilgrims), and attach copies of all the mandatory documents (self-attested, if required). **Step 3:** Submit the duly filled and signed application form along with the documents, within the prescribed period (if any), to **&quot;Gujarat Holy Pilgrimage Development Board, Old Secretariat, Gandhinagar&quot;** within 90 days of yatra completion. _*Retain a copy of the submitted application for future reference._  **Verification &amp; Disbursement** The board processes applications, and financial assistance is credited to the beneficiary’s account.
Mode: Offline.

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Tourism Department.
