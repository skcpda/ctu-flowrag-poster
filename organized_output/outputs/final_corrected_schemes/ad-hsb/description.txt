# Assistance on Death- Haryana Silicosis Board

**Short Title:** AD-HSB

**Scheme For:** Family

**Tags:** Death, Silicosis, Financial Assistance, Worker

The &amp;quot;Assistance on Death&amp;quot; scheme, implemented by the Haryana Labour Welfare Board, Government of Haryana, provides financial assistance of ₹1,00,000/- in the event of the death of a Silicosis victim. If the deceased was married, the assistance will be payable to the widow or widower. If the deceased was unmarried, the amount will be given to their nominee.

**Who is this for:** Family; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Financial assistance

**Where it applies:** Haryana; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** 1. Under this scheme, in the event of the death of a Silicosis victim, financial assistance of ₹1,00,000/- will be provided. **Note 1:** If the deceased was married, the assistance will be payable to the widow or widower. **Note 2:** If the deceased was unmarried, the financial assistance will be given to the nominee.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**How to apply:** **Step 01:** The applicant may visit the nearest Labour Office and collect the application form. The Application Performa ([Annexure VIII](https://hrylabour.gov.in/staticdocs/labourpolicies/Haryana%20Silicosis%20Rehabilitation%20Policy%20(English).pdf)) may also be downloaded from the Official Website of the Labour Department Haryana. **Step 02:** Fill out the application form with all required details, ensuring accuracy and completeness. **Step 03:** Attach all necessary documents, including medical certificates, proof of identity, and the certificate of being the widow/widower or nominee, if applicable, etc. **Step 04:** Submit the completely filled application form along with supporting documents to the Assistant Director, Industrial Health of your concerned jurisdiction. **Step 05:** The Assistant Director will review the application and then forward it to the Office of the Labour Commissioner-cum-Welfare Commissioner, Haryana. **Step 06:** Await notification from the Labour Department regarding the approval or any further requirements for your application. **Step 07:** Upon approval, the financial assistance will be processed and provided to the applicant as per the scheme&amp;#39;s guidelines.
Mode: Offline.

**Additional Application Processes:** **Process 2:** To avail of the benefits of the scheme, the eligible applicant may visit the nearest Antyodaya Kendra and fill up the form.

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: Haryana Labour Welfare Board, Government Of Haryana; Nodal Department: Labour Department.
