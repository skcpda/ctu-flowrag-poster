# Remembering Tiatr Artistes of Yesteryears - Organisation of Special Dedicated Programmes

**Short Title:** RTAY-OSDP

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Artist, Culture, Program, Show, Auditorium

The scheme &quot;Remembering Tiatr Artistes of Yesteryears - Organisation of Special Dedicated Programmes&quot; was launched by the Tiatr Academy under the Art, Culture and Museums Department, Government of Goa. The scheme aims to make the younger generation aware of the contribution and sacrifices of great tiatr personalities of the past who are no more with us, addressing the problem that today&#39;s younger generation does not know much about these pioneers whose contribution to Goan tiatr is immense. The scheme provides collaboration with troupes/institutions/individuals for producing special dedicated programmes on the lives of particular artistes and financial assistance with auditorium/hall facilities for staging programmes. The applicant must be a troupe/institution/individual willing to produce special programmes dedicated to particular artistes and must obtain No Objection Certificate from the nearest family members of the late artiste. The scheme is implemented by Tiatr Academy of Goa and applications for this scheme are accepted offline. 

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Sports & Culture; Sector: Culture

**Where it applies:** Goa; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** **Financial Assistance:** Tiatr Academy of Goa will provide financial assistance as fixed by Tiatr Academy of Goa for each show. **Infrastructure Support:** Provision of auditorium/hall to stage the programme. **Village Performances:** Financial assistance as fixed by Tiatr Academy of Goa for erecting temporary pandal, providing stage, sound, etc. for village performances. **Additional Shows:** Option for additional 2 shows in villages at an amount as fixed by Tiatr Academy of Goa plus additional amount as fixed by Tiatr Academy of Goa for Pandal/Hall/Auditorium including Stage, Sound, etc. **Documentation:** Tiatr Academy of Goa will document the programme and preserve it at Tiatr Academy of Goa&#39;s library. **Marketing Materials:** Provision of banner for tiatr performances and logo for musical shows. **Number of Shows:** Maximum of 4 shows at different places including 1 show at the area of residence. _* Tiatr Academy of Goa has no objection if sponsorship is taken by the producer from other sources._  > ***Conditions*** _*All shows must be shown to the public free without charging any tickets._ _*Programme duration should be at least 2 hours for musical show and 3 hours for tiatr._ _*Display Tiatr Academy of Goa banner compulsorily on front side wing of stage for tiatr or minimum size of 6ft. x 10ft. backdrop with Tiatr Academy of Goa logo for musical show._ _*Return banner to Tiatr Academy of Goa after all shows are staged._ 

**Benefit Type:** Composite

**How to apply:** **Step 1:** The interested applicant should take a printout of the [prescribed format](https://www.goa.gov.in/wp-content/uploads/2021/06/Citizen-Charter-TAG.pdf) of the application form. Alternatively, the applicant may visit the office of Tiatr Academy of Goa to collect the form. **Step 2:** In the application form, fill in all the mandatory fields, attach copies of all the mandatory documents (self-attest, if required). Sign the application form and mention the date. **Step 3:** Submit the duly filled and signed application form along with the documents to - ***Tiatr Academy of Goa, AS-1 / AS-4, Block A, 2nd Floor, Campal Trade Centre, Opposite Kala Academy, Campal, Panaji, Goa, India – 403001*** _*Applicants are advised to save a copy of the submitted application form and any acknowledgment._ _*Applications are considered on a &quot;First come First Serve&quot; basis, subject to the availability of funds._ _*Tiatr Academy of Goa reserves the right to make any changes in the rules._ _*If adverse remarks are proved, Tiatr Academy of Goa reserves the right to disqualify the performance and refrain from making payment or recall the payment made._  ##### **Post Application Processes** **Step 1:****** ***Coordinate with Tiatr Academy of Goa*** The applicant must discuss and decide about the Chief Guest/Guest of Honour well in advance with Tiatr Academy of Goa, as the President or Vice President of Tiatr Academy of Goa will either be the Chief Guest/Guest of Honour at the function. **Step 2:** ***Collect Banner and Materials*** The applicant must collect the banner from the office of Tiatr Academy of Goa before the performances (for tiatr) or arrange for backdrop with Tiatr Academy of Goa logo (for musical shows) and ensure proper display during all shows. **Step 3:** ***Stage Performances*** The applicant must stage the approved programme ensuring all conditions are met including free admission for public, proper acknowledgment of Tiatr Academy of Goa collaboration in all advertisements and media, and maintaining the specified duration for each type of performance. **Step 4:** ***Documentation and Reporting*** The applicant must document the programme through photographs and submit 5 copies to Tiatr Academy of Goa for records, ensuring two photographs show the Tiatr Academy of Goa banner during performance. **Step 5:** ***Submit Financial Documentation*** The applicant must prepare and submit a detailed statement of expenditure with all original bills (stamped receipts if amount exceeds ₹5,000/-) to Tiatr Academy of Goa for reimbursement of approved expenses. **Step 6:** ***Return Materials*** The applicant must return the Tiatr Academy of Goa banner to the office after all shows are completed to maintain the academy&#39;s inventory and complete the programme cycle.  ##### ***Application Deadline*** Applications must reach the office of Tiatr Academy of Goa at least 30 days in advance along with typed script/lyrics. 
Mode: Offline.
