# Integrated Scholarship Scheme

**Short Title:** ISSMP

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Scholarship, Student, Higher Education, Sports, Training

The &amp;quot;Integrated Scholarship Scheme,&amp;quot; implemented by the Higher Education Department, Government of Madhya Pradesh, aims to provide financial assistance to meritorious students who are deprived of other scholarships to pursue higher education and training in various fields. The scheme offers scholarships for PhD, M.Phil., Postgraduate (qualification-based and qualification cum instrument-based), and sports, as well as merit-based scholarships for graduates, ranging from ₹300/- to ₹600/- per month. Eligible students must be natives of Madhya Pradesh, studying in a government college, with a family income not exceeding ₹54,000/- per annum from all sources.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Education & Learning; Sector: Scholarships and student finance

**Where it applies:** Madhya Pradesh; State/ UT

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**How to apply:** **Advertisement Issuanc**e: The [Directorate of Higher Education](https://highereducation.mp.gov.in/) issues advertisements inviting applications for the scholarship. **Application Review and Selection:** Applications received are evaluated and applicants are selected based on the prescribed quota limit. **Approval and Disbursement:** Upon approval from the Commissioner of Higher Education, the scholarship amount is deposited directly into the bank account of the selected applicant. **Note:** Selection of new applicants is done on the basis of merit process.
Mode: Offline.

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: Higher Education Department, Government Of Madhya Pradesh; Nodal Department: Higher Education Department.
