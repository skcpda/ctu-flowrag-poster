# One Time Grant For Media Personality

**Short Title:** OTGMP

**Scheme For:** Infra

**Tags:** Journalist, Financial Assistance, Social Welfare

&amp;quot;One Time Grant for Media Personality&amp;quot; is a Financial Assistance Scheme by the Information and Public Relations Department, Govt. of Assam. Through this scheme, the govt. provides a one-time grant to twenty journalists @ ₹ 50,000/- each every year so that they can engage in their varied performances in a more productive and efficient manner. The assistance is intended to support journalists in their pursuit of outstanding achievements and also to support them in their time of need. The objectives of this scheme is to uphold the dignity of media persons and to inspire and motivate the media persons to excel in the field of journalism.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Financial assistance

**Where it applies:** Assam; State
