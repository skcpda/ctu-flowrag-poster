# Bihar Shatabdi Asangathit Karyakshetra Kamagaar Evan Shilpakar Samajik Suraksha Yojana: Permanent Partial Disability

**Short Title:** BSAKPPD

**Launch Date:** 2011-04-01

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Social Security, Unorganised Worker, Craftsmen, Permanent Partial Disability

The “Bihar Shatabdi Asangathit Karyakshetra Kamagaar Evan Shilpakar Samajik Suraksha Yojana” launched by the Labour Resources Department, Bihar, aims to provide social security through various types of financial assistance to workers and artisans in the unorganized sector.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Health & Wellness; Sector: Crisis/Disaster/Accident

**Where it applies:** Bihar; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - **Financial Assistance:** ₹37,500/-.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**How to apply:** **Step 1:** Visit the concerned office of the District Officer or Committee. **Step 2:** Collect the application form; fill in and attach all the mandatory documents (attested, if required). **Step 3:** Submit the complete application to the concerned District Officer or Labour Welfare Committee at the address below. &gt; **Office Address:** &gt; Labour Resources Department, Government of Bihar &gt; Bihar State Labour Welfare Committee &gt; Niyojan Bhawan, Patna- 800 007 &gt; Tel: 062-2520 053 &gt; Email-edbslws2017@gmail.com
Mode: Offline.

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Labour Resources Department.

**Additional Information:**
Briefdescription: “Permanent Partial Disability” under the “Bihar Shatabdi Asangathit Karyakshetra Kamagaar Evan Shilpakar Samajik Suraksha Yojana” launched by the Labour Resources Dept, Bihar, provides financial assistance of ₹37,500/- to the families of workers or craftsmen in case of permanent partial disablement.
