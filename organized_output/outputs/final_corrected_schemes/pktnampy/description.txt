# Pathari Kshetr Talab Nirman Aadharit Matsya Paalan ki Yojana

**Short Title:** PKTNAMPY

**Launch Date:** 2023-07-04

**Scheme For:** Infra

**Tags:** Fish Farmer, Financial Assistance, Ponds, Opportunities

The &quot;Pathari Kshetr Talab Nirman Aadharit Matsya Paalan ki Yojana&quot; was launched by the Animal and Fisheries Resources Department, Government of Bihar. The scheme aims to promote fisheries by constructing ponds and setting up related supporting units in plateau-dominated districts. The initiative is designed to create new employment opportunities and provide a means of livelihood for farmers.

**Who is this for:** Individual, Family; Category: Agriculture,Rural & Environment; Sector: Land and water resources

**Where it applies:** Bihar; State/ UT

**DBT Scheme:** Yes (Direct Benefit Transfer)

**Benefits / Assistance:** - **Maximum Subsidy per Beneficiary:** 80% of ₹16.70 lakh per acre.- **Subsidy Installments:** 60% after pond construction, 40% after installation of related units and purchase of inputs.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**How to apply:** > ***Registration Process:*** **Step-01:** The applicant visits the [official website](https://fisheries.bihar.gov.in/Default.aspx) and clicks the &quot;Applications are being invited for all the schemes of the financial year (2024-25), [click here](https://fisheries.bihar.gov.in/Accordion.aspx) to apply&quot;. **Step-02:** Select the &quot;[Registration for Fisheries Schemes](https://misfisheries.bihar.gov.in/RegisterFisheries.aspx)&quot; **Step-03:** Select the category: Individual and complete all basic details of the applicant for registration, including category, caste, applicant name, date of birth, address, bank details, etc. **Step-04:** Enter a valid 10-digit mobile number and click to send OTP. **Step-05:** An OTP will be sent to the provided mobile number. **Step-06:** Enter the OTP; after verification of the OTP, you will receive a Registration Number and Password. **Step-07:** Click on the &quot;Login&quot; and enter the Registration Number and Password.  > ***Application Form Filling Process:*** **Step-01:** The applicant visits the [official website](https://fisheries.bihar.gov.in/Default.aspx) and clicks the &quot;Applications are being invited for all the schemes of the financial year (2024-25), [click here](https://fisheries.bihar.gov.in/Accordion.aspx) to apply&quot;. **Step-02:** Click &quot;If your already register, [login here](https://misfisheries.bihar.gov.in/login.aspx)&quot;. **Step-03**: Select &quot;Scheme name&quot; under the &quot;Matsya Yojana&quot; option on the top menu. **Step-04:** Applicant need to provide the following details : - Details of individual (applicant/group leader) - Permanent address - Bank account details - Land/pond details **Step-05**: Upload the required documents. **Step-06:** Click on the submit button as well print the application form and submit it to the District Fisheries Office along with attachments.  Note: Download the [Affidavit form](https://misfisheries.bihar.gov.in/Docs/Specialpackagesapat.pdf) and [Consent form](https://misfisheries.bihar.gov.in/Docs/specialpackagesahmati.pdf) also attached with the application.
Mode: Online.
