# Grant-in-Aid Scheme To Provide Financial Assistance To The Mahila Mandals/ Women’s Self-help Groups (SWAWLAMBHAN)

**Short Title:** GIASPFAMMWSHG

**Scheme For:** Infra

**Tags:** Self Help Group, SHG, Employment, Training

The scheme &quot;Grant-in-Aid Scheme to Provide Financial Assistance to the Mahila Mandals/ Women’s Self-help Groups (SWAWLAMBHAN)&quot; by the Directorate of Women And Child Development, Govt. of Goa, provides financial assistance to Mahila Mandals/Self-Help Groups for training/orientation for members of the Mahila Mandal/ Self Help Groups for generating their own activity for self-employment. This Scheme is applicable to all the Mahila Mandals/Women&#39;s Self-Help Groups duly registered before the competent authority within the territory of Goa.

**Who is this for:** Joint Liability Groups (JLGS), Self Help Groups (SHGS); Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Financial assistance

**Where it applies:** Goa; State

**Benefits / Assistance:** **Financial Assistance:** ₹ 20,000/- sanctioned as annual grants. **Purpose:** For successful functioning in the state of Goa.  **Financial Assistance:** ₹ 5,000/-. **Purpose:** For undertaking any gainful self-employment activity training orientation in the [activities](https://goaprintingpress.gov.in/downloads/2122/2122-39-SI-OG-0.pdf).  > **For the Master Trainers** 1. The Directorate may provide resource persons/ Master Trainers/Instructors for each course of activity through CDPOs. 1. A fixed **honorarium of ₹ 5,000/-** will be paid per course..1. Actual **Travel Allowance (TA)** incurred shall be paid if travelling in the Taluka outside his/her jurisdiction.

**Benefit Type:** Composite

**How to apply:** > **For the Mahila Mandal/ Self Help Groups** The application in the [prescribed format](https://goaprintingpress.gov.in/downloads/2122/2122-39-SI-OG-0.pdf), along with copies of the required documents shall be submitted through the [CDPO of the concerned block](https://www.goa.gov.in/wp-content/uploads/2022/08/DWCD-Citizen-Charter-1.pdf), Mukhya Sevika at the village level of the respective block, at the cost of ₹ 100/-.  > **For the Master Trainers/Instructors** The application in the [prescribed format](https://goaprintingpress.gov.in/downloads/2122/2122-39-SI-OG-0.pdf), along with copies of the required documents shall be submitted through the [CDPO of the concerned block](https://www.goa.gov.in/wp-content/uploads/2022/08/DWCD-Citizen-Charter-1.pdf), Mukhya Sevika at the village level of the respective block. 
Mode: Offline.

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Women and Child Development Department.
