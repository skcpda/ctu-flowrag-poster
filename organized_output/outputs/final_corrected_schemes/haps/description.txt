# Hiv / Aids Pension Scheme

**Short Title:** Haps

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Financial Assistance, HIV / AIDS, Pension, Medical Expenses, Petty Personal Expenses

The Government of Puducherry introduced the HIV / AIDS Pension Scheme to extend a helping hand through monthly financial assistance to HIV/AIDS Affected People so that they could defray some petty personal/ medical expenses without depending on others.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Financial assistance

**Where it applies:** Puducherry; State

**Benefits / Assistance:** 1. ₹ 1500/- per month with age less than 60 years. 1. ₹ 2000/- per month with age between 60 years and 79 years. 1. ₹ 3000/- per month with age 80 years and above.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**How to apply:** > Whom to Apply - Puducherry: The Deputy Director (Social Defence), Old Age Pension Section, Department of Women and Child Development, Puducherry.- Karaikal: The Child Development Project Officer, Karaikal.- Mahe/Yanam: The Welfare Officer, Mahe/ Yanam. > Processing Time - The selection is periodical and depends on the vacancies and on the availability of funds. > In case of delay whom to contact? - Puducherry: The Director, Department of Women and Child Development, Sithankudi, Puducherry.- Karaikal: The Collector, Karaikal.- Mahe/Yanam: The Regional Administrator, Mahe/Yanam.
Mode: Offline.

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Women and Child Development Department, Puducherry.
