# Rajya Samekit Sahkari Vikas Yojna Evam Ganga Gaaye Mahila Dairy Yojana

**Short Title:** RSSVYEGGMDY

**Scheme For:** Infra

**Tags:** Financial Assistance, Dairy Farmers, Cattle Shed, Cooperative Societies, Scheduled Caste, Scheduled Tribe

The “Rajya Samekit Sahkari Vikas Yojna Evam Ganga Gaaye Mahila Dairy Yojana” scheme was launched by the Dairy Development Department, Government of Uttarakhand. The scheme aims to support dairy farmers and cooperative members in purchasing high-yielding cows and buffaloes by providing subsidies and loans. 

**Who is this for:** Individual, Registered Societies; Category: Agriculture,Rural & Environment; Sector: Animal husbandry

**Where it applies:** Uttarakhand; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** > **Members of dairy cooperative societies are provided the following benefits to purchase cows/buffaloes:** **For members of the general category** - A grant of 50% and a loan of 40% is approved through the district cooperative bank for the establishment of a unit of 02, 03, and 05 dairy animals (including animal purchase, insurance, transportation expenses, cattle shed, milk van, etc.), and the remaining 10% is borne by the beneficiary. - The dairy department will provide a grant of ₹80,000 for 2 animals, ₹1,23,250 for 3 animals, and ₹2,03,625 for 5 animals. **For Scheduled Caste, Scheduled Tribe and women members (of any caste)** - For the establishment of units for 02, 03 and 05 dairy animals (including animal care, insurance, transportation costs, cattle shed, milk vans, etc.), a 75% grant and a 25% loan are approved through the district cooperative bank. - The dairy department will provide funds of ₹1,20,000/- for 2 animals, ₹1,84,875/- for 3 animals, and ₹3,05,438/- for 5 animals. - A minimum of 2 animals must be purchased, and taking a loan is mandatory to receive the subsidy. 

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**How to apply:** - The applicant must be a member of the committee, a member above 18 years of age in the relevant village panchayat / rural area. Membership can be obtained by depositing a membership fee of ₹25 to the nearest committee secretary and obtaining a receipt; it is not mandatory to be a livestock keeper. - The applicant will receive the application form from the dairy cooperative society or the nearest district cooperative bank. - Along with the application form, the applicant must provide an Aadhaar card, bank account details, caste certificate, and if necessary, the cooperative society membership number, movable/immovable asset or land certificate. If these documents are not available, two sureties who are members of the milk cooperative will need to provide a guarantee. If a loan has been taken from any institution, its details must be submitted alongside how many animals are to be acquired, to the milk cooperative society. After that, the secretary of the committee certifies. - The manager / chief manager of the milk producer cooperative association and the assistant director of dairy conduct an inspection and provide recommendations. - After the recommendation, the application form is submitted to the bank, which checks all documents and grants the loan based on the beneficiary&#39;s eligibility, and the loan amount is distributed into the beneficiary&#39;s account. - After the receipt of the financial grant, the dairy cooperative society / dairy department’s committee informs the concerned beneficiary, and along with the committee, they go to purchase animals from the listed dairy farms outside the state. - They select and purchase the animals, conduct health checks, and deliver them to the livestock owner&#39;s home. For this, the livestock owner does not have to pay any amount to the departmental personnel. If any beneficiary does not like the animals from the listed firms, then in that case, the beneficiary will inform the concerned district manager in writing, and after receiving permission, animals will be provided along with the committee from outside the state.
