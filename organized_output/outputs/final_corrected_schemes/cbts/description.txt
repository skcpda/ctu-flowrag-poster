# Community Based Training Scheme

**Short Title:** CBTS

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Skill Development, Employment, Unemployed, Minority Community

The &quot;Community Based Training Scheme&quot; was launched by the Minorities Welfare Department, Government of Karnataka, and implemented by the Karnataka Minorities Development Corporation. The scheme aims to provide training in Heavy Vehicle Driving, Short Hand, Security Services, Office Soft Skill, Retail Sales, Beauty Parlor Course (Male and Female) to the unemployed young men and women belonging to minority community for self employment or employment in MNC, BPOs and other activities.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Skills & Employment; Sector: Training and Skill Up-gradation

**Where it applies:** Karnataka; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - **The scheme provides training in Heavy Vehicle Driving, Short Hand, Security Services, Office Soft Skill, Retail Sales, and Beauty Parlor Course (Male and Female).**

**Benefit Type:** In Kind

**How to apply:** Step-01: Applicant need to visits the Karnataka Minorities Development Corporation [official website](https://kmdconline.karnataka.gov.in/Portal/home). Step-02: Click &quot;Apply Online&quot; and Enter &quot;Mobile Number&quot; and &quot;Submit&quot;. Step-03: Enter &quot;Aadhaar Number&quot; Fill in the &quot;Captcha&quot; and Select &quot;Next&quot;. Step-04: Applicant will get OTP in his/her Aadhaar Linked Mobile Number. Step-05: Enter &quot;OTP&quot; and select &quot;Continue&quot;, After that Allow the official notice. Step-06: After verifying the Aadhaar again fill in the &quot;OTP&quot; and Submit. Step-07: Select the scheme &quot;Sikhligar’s Community Development Scheme&quot; and fill in the basic details father&#39;s name, gender, qualification detail Step-08: Select &quot;Next&quot; Fill in the address detail and again select &quot;Next&quot;. Step-09: Upload the required document passport-size photo, educational document, income certificate, etc. Step-10: Click &quot;Proceed&quot; and Select &quot;Submit Application&quot; and get an Application ID.
Mode: Online.

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: Karnataka Minorities Development Corporation; Nodal Department: Minorities Welfare Department.
