# Self-Employment Scheme: Vahan Loan Sahay Yojana

**Short Title:** SESVLSY

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Business, Self Employment, Vehicle Loan

&amp;quot;Vahan Loan Sahay Yojana&amp;quot; is a component under the &amp;quot;Self-Employment Scheme&amp;quot; introduced by the Social Justice and Empowerment Department, Gujarat. Under this scheme, self-employed individuals can avail of financial assistance as on-road unit cost for purchasing vehicles such as rickshaws, loading rickshaws, Maruti cars, jeep taxis, etc. The scheme is being implemented by the Gujarat Unreserved Education and Economical Development Corporation.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Banking,Financial Services and Insurance; Sector: Loan

**Where it applies:** Gujarat; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** **Financial Assistance:** On-road unit cost for vehicles like rickshaws, loading rickshaws, Maruti, jeep-taxi, etc. 

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**How to apply:** > **Registration Process:** **Step 1:** Go to the Corporation&#39;s [official website](https://esamajkalyan.gujarat.gov.in/) and navigate to the &quot;Gujarat Unreserved Education and Economical Development Corporation&quot; section under the Corporation menu. **Step 2:**Click on &quot;Vahan Loan Sahay Yojana&quot; under &quot;Self-Employment Scheme&quot; and then Apply Now. **Step 3:** Click on &quot;New User? Please Register Here&quot;. **Step 4:** Enter the required details and click Register.  > **Application Process:** **Step 1:** Enter your User ID, Password, and Captcha Code, then click &quot;Login&quot;. **Step 2:** Read the scheme details carefully and click OK. **Step 3:** If your permanent address is correct, confirm it. Otherwise, enter your correct address. **Step 4:** Click Save &amp; Next to proceed. Upload the relevant documents. **Step 5:** If you need to make changes, click &quot;No, Cancel&quot;. If all details are correct, click &quot;Yes, Submit&quot;. **Step 6:** Click Save Application. Note down your Application Number for future reference. You can also take a printout by clicking Print Application.  > **Check Application Status****:** [Click here](https://esamajkalyan.gujarat.gov.in/ViewApplicationStatus.aspx). > **Post Online Application Procedure:****** After submitting the online application, the applicant must take a printout of the application, sign it, and submit it along with all uploaded documents (certificates, mark sheets, etc.) to the concerned Deputy Director (Non-Reserved Category) or the District Social Welfare Officer through courier, post, or by visiting in person. > **Contact Us:** [Click here](https://gueedc.gujarat.gov.in/contact-us.html). 
Mode: Online.

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: Gujarat Unreserved Education And Economical Development Corporation; Nodal Department: Social Justice and Empowerment Department.
