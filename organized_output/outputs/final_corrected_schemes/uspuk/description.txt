# Uttarakhand Startup Policy

**Short Title:** USPUK

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Incubation Center, Entrepreneurship, Startups, Women Entrepreneurs, Student Entrepreneurs, Business

The &amp;quot;Uttarakhand Startup Policy&amp;quot; is implemented by the Department of Industries, Government of Uttarakhand, aims to support the growth of startup ecosystem in Uttarakhand. Through this scheme, comprehensive benefits including monthly allowance, one-time seed funding, patent assistance, need-based assistance, and various financial provisions as per the Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises (MSME) Policy are provided to entrepreneurial startups and specifically enhanced support to women, students, Scheduled Caste (SC), Scheduled Tribe (ST), Persons with Disabilities (PWD), Transgender, or grassroots innovation-based startups. **Objectives:** The objective of this policy is to create the culture of innovation and entrepreneurship by establishing a robust startup ecosystem in the state. The growth of the startup ecosystem will be fueled with the establishment of world-class institutional infrastructure, incubation and mentoring network, enabling access to funds, providing market access, public procurement, business friendly and regulatory environment along with promotion of employment generation and wealth creation opportunities across the state. **Goals:** - Stimulate the growth of 1000 startups including technology driven enterprises in next 5 years - Encourage the growth of startup enterprises working on innovative technologies, processes or procedures, eco-friendly technologies, social impact or sustainability having focus on solving complex problems and stimulating the growth of local economy through employment creation and wealth generations - Establish state of the art infrastructure and facilities for innovators, startups, aspiring students entrepreneurs, women entrepreneurs and other ecosystem stakeholders across the state - Establish 30 new incubation centers across the state with at least 1 incubation center in each district - Establish a mechanism to enable access to funds, key infrastructure and other key resources that are important for the growth of startup enterprises in the state. - Enable collaborations between industry and academia to promote entrepreneurship, innovations and its commercialization and the growth of technology enterprises - Develop an environment conducive to the creation, growth or transformation of startups enterprises in to a highly successful enterprise. **Validity of the Policy:** This Policy is valid for a period of 5 years from the date of its notification or until new Policy is formulated.

**Who is this for:** Business Entity; Category: Business & Entrepreneurship; Sector: Setting up / start-up / entrepreneurship

**Where it applies:** Uttarakhand; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** **1. Monthly Allowance:** - ₹15,000/- per month to entrepreneurial startups for one year. - ₹20,000/- per month to women entrepreneurs, person from Scheduled Caste, Scheduled Tribes, Differently Abled, Transgender, startups which is working towards grassroot innovations or innovations with rural impact or in any startup working on disruptive technologies, for one year. **Note:** The monthly allowances shall be a one-time assistance given to startups at the ideation stage for a maximum period of twelve (12) months. **2. One-Time Seed Funding:** - Up to ₹10,00,000/- to recognized startup, which is at the ideation stage or prototyping or MVP stage. - Up to ₹12,50,000/- for women entrepreneurs, scheduled caste, scheduled tribes, differently abled, transgender or a startups that is working towards grassroot innovations or innovations with rural impact, - Up to ₹12,50,000/- to startups working on disruptive technologies. **3. Patent and International Patent Assistance:** - Reimbursement assistance of ₹1,00,000/- per Indian patent. - Reimbursement assistance of up to ₹5,00,000/- for international patents. - Reimbursement assistance of ₹10,000/- (per trademark) for filing trademark application. **Note:** Reimbursement of expenses shall be made in two stages - 75 % after filing of patent application and - 25 % at the time of grant of patent. **4. Financial Provisions from Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises (MSME) Policy:** - Special Capital Subsidy. - Interest Subsidy. - Stamp Duty Exemption. - State Goods and Services Tax (SGST) Reimbursement Assistance. - Pre-Incubation and Incubation Support: - One-time free assistance for pre-incubation support. - One-time free assistance for incubation support. - Capital subsidy up to ₹1,00,00,000/- for setting up new incubation centres. - Capital subsidy up to ₹50,00,000/- for expansion of existing incubation centres. - Startups recognised by the Startup Council and operated by Scheduled Caste (SC), Scheduled Tribes (ST), Women, or Physically Challenged individuals will be provided incubation space at a discount of 25% of the notified rates at recognised Incubators. **Venture Fund Provision:** - Provision of ₹200,00,00,000/- for setting up a venture fund.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

This button is typically found in the top right corner of the navigation bar. Clicking it will direct you to the [registration page](https://startuputtarakhand.uk.gov.in/signup). **Step 3:** Fill in Basic Registration Details: - **Select from list:** From the provided dropdown menu, choose the type of entity you are registering as: Startup, Nodal Agency, Incubator.- **Enter your complete details:** Name, Email Address, Mobile Number( A 6-digit verification code will be sent to this number, and providing it is mandatory for successful submission) and Address.- **Create Login Credentials:** Username and Password. Confirm password: Re-enter your chosen password to confirm it matches.- Select your gender from the dropdown. - **Agree to Terms and Conditions:** Read the &quot;terms and conditions&quot; carefully. Check the box labelled. You must agree to proceed.- **Complete Registration:** Click the &quot;Sign up&quot; button to submit your registration details and create your account. > **Application Process:** **Step 1:** Visit the [Official Portal](https://startuputtarakhand.uk.gov.in/) of the Startup Uttarakhand. **Step 2:** On the homepage, locate and click the &quot;Sign in&quot; button. Use your newly created username and password to log in. You will be taken to your dashboard. **Step 3:** On your dashboard, look for the section titled &quot;Application apply for recognition/incentives 2023&quot;. **Step 4:** Click on the button labelled &quot;RECOGNITION&quot; or &quot;Apply for Recognition - 2023&quot;. This action will lead you to the &quot;STARTUP - Recognition Form,&quot; specifically the first step: &quot;Pre-eligibility criteria / Entity Details.&quot; **Step 5:** **Filling Pre-Eligibility Criteria / Entity Details** On this page, you will provide fundamental information about your entity. - Enter Name of Entity, Nature of Legal Entity(Private Limited Company, LLP, Registered Partnership Firm), Incorporation Date( entity was incorporated or registered), GST Number, State/UT of Registered Office, Total No. of Employees (Inclusive of Founder/Co-founder/Directors/Partners), Total No. of Employees Domiciled in Uttarakhand (Inclusive of Founder/Co-founder/Directors/Partners). - **** Select &quot;Yes&quot; or &quot;No&quot; for Certification for 70% Domiciled Workforce, Company Exceeded Turnover of ₹100,00,00,000/-, Entity Not Extension of Family Business/Reconstruction, Is your startup recognised by DPIIT? and DPIIT Certificate Number**:** If you selected &quot;Yes&quot; for DPIIT recognition, enter your DPIIT certificate number in this field.- Enter your**** PAN Number.- If your startup is associated with an incubator, select its name from the dropdown list. - Provide the official website link for your startup, if available. Address of Registered Office, Confirm &quot;Uttarakhand&quot; as the state for your registered office, Select the district of your registered office from the dropdown and Enter the pincode of your registered office. **Step 6:** Click the &quot;Save &amp; Next&quot; button at the bottom right of the page to save your entries and move to the next step. **Step 7: Authorised Representative &amp; Key Personnel:** - This section requires details of the authorised person and the founders/directors/partners. - **Authorised Representative Details:** Enter the full name, select the gender, enter the mobile number, email address, and Aadhar number. - **Details of Founders / Co-founders / Directors / Partners:** Click the &quot;Add More&quot; button to add entries for each individual. For each person, provide: Enter name, for designation select the role (e.g., Founder, Co-founder, Director, Partner) from the dropdown, Mobile number, Email address, and Percentage of Equity Holding: The percentage of equity they hold in the company, and select the Gender. Select SC/ST/PwD/Transgender certificate holder if the applicant holds such a certificate.- **Other Details:** Select the primary business activity, the industry sector your startup operates in, from the dropdown. If Other, specify: If you selected &quot;Other&quot; for Activity or Sector, provide a specific description here. Clearly state the name of the main product, service, or trade that your startup offers. Write a concise brief description of your startup&#39;s core concept or business idea. Select the current development stage of your startup from the dropdown (e.g., Idea Stage, Prototype, Minimum Viable Product (MVP), Revenue Generating). Select &quot;Yes&quot; or &quot;No&quot; if you have received funding from investors previously.- **Detail of Funding Received (Conditional):** If you selected &quot;Yes&quot; for having received funding previously, click the &quot;Add More&quot; button. For each instance of funding, provide the source of the funding (e.g., Angel Investor, Venture Capital, Grant), the monetary amount received, and the date the funding was received.- **Update Progress:** Click the &quot;Update&quot; button at the bottom right of the page to save the entered information and continue. This button will typically serve as the &quot;Save &amp; Next&quot; to move to the documents section. **Step 8: Upload the Documents:** - On this page, you will upload all the necessary supporting documents. For each document listed, you will need to perform the following actions: Required Documents: Incorporation Certificate, GST Certificate, DPIIT Certificate, PAN Card Copy (If student entrepreneur, upload personal PAN card), Aadhar Card Copy, Shareholding Certificate (For founders/co-founders/partners/directors), Attach a file to show details of funding received, MoA/Partnership Deed, Attach Business Plan, Pitch Deck, Financial Projections, Other awards/recognitions/relevant documents, CA Certified Document to show that the legal entity has not had a turnover of over ₹100,00,00,00,000/- since the date of incorporation (This can be a Chartered Accountant (CA) certified document, a CA certified Balance Sheet since the date of incorporation, or a GST return certified by a CA). **Step 9:** Once all required documents are uploaded and any optional remarks are added, click the &quot;Submit &amp; Proceed&quot; button at the bottom right of the page to move to the final declaration step. **Step 10:** Carefully read the entire declaration statement presented. Click the final &quot;Submit&quot; or &quot;Confirm&quot; button.
