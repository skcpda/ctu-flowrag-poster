# Uniform Assistance for Notified Tribes and Denotified Tribes Castes students Studying in Class 1st to 8th

**Short Title:** UANDTSOE

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Uniform Assistance, Education, Notified Tribes And Denotified Tribes

The scheme &amp;quot;Uniform Assistance for Notified Tribes and Denotified Tribes Castes students Studying in Class 1st to 8th&amp;quot; by the Social Justice and Empowerment Department, Government of Gujarat, aims to support students from Notified Tribes and Denotified Tribes Castes by reducing the burden of education-related expenses and promoting school attendance. The scheme ensures that eligible students from Classes 1st to 8th studying in government and aided schools receive ₹900/- for three pairs of school uniforms annually, enhancing their dignity and inclusion in the school environment.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Education & Learning; Sector: Resources for schools

**Where it applies:** Gujarat; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - **Financial Assistance:** ₹900/- is provided to each eligible student for the purchase of three pairs of school uniforms.

**Benefit Type:** Cash
