# Fellowship Scholarship Scheme

**Short Title:** FSSG

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Scholarship, Fellowship, Higher Education, Student

The &amp;quot;Fellowship Scholarship Scheme&amp;quot; by the Education Department, Government of Gujarat, aims to provide financial assistance to meritorious students who secure 70% or more marks in Class 10th and are pursuing higher secondary or higher education. The primary objective is to encourage education among the Scheduled Caste (SC), Scheduled Tribe (ST), Physically Backward Classes, and other eligible categories by providing monetary support based on their merit in Class 11th and subsequent studies. 

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Education & Learning; Sector: Scholarships and student finance

**Where it applies:** Gujarat; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - **Annual Scholarship:** Financial assistance is provided every year to eligible students. 

**Benefit Type:** Cash
