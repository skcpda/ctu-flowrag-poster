# Thayi Magu Sahaya Hastha (K.B.O.C.W.W.B)

**Short Title:** TMSHKBOCWWB

**Scheme For:** Family

**Tags:** Labour, Building Worker, Construction Worker, Pre-school, Education, Child, Women

The &amp;quot;Thayi Magu Sahaya Hastha&amp;#39;&amp;#39; by the BOCW Welfare Board, Department of Labour, Karnataka, is a welfare scheme for the registered workers of the board. Under this Scheme, financial support shall be provided to registered women construction workers for pre-school education and nutritional support of their child for up to 3 years after delivery. Assistance can be availed for up to 2 children. &lt;br&gt;

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Education & Learning; Sector: Early childhood

**Where it applies:** Karnataka; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - **Financial Assistance:** ₹6,000/- (at the rate of ₹500/- per month).

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**How to apply:** &gt; ***Registration Process as a Building/ Construction Worker:*** **Step 1:** Visit the official [Website ](https://kbocwwb.karnataka.gov.in/login)of Karnataka Building &amp;amp; Other Construction Workers Welfare Board (KBOCWWB) and click on “Register”. **Step 2:** Select “Register as New Construction Worker” then enter your mobile number as per the Aadhaar Registered and verify with the received OTP. **Step 3:** From the dashboard, click on “Registration”. **Step 4:** Fill in all the mandatory details and upload the required documents. Click on “Final Submit” to complete the registration process. &gt; **Application Process of the Welfare Scheme:** **Step 1:** Visit the official [Website ](https://kbocwwb.karnataka.gov.in/login)of Karnataka Building &amp;amp; Other Construction Workers Welfare Board (KBOCWWB) and click on “Login”. ***Step 2:*** Enter your registered mobile number and the received OTP. **Step 3:** From the dashboard, click on “Schemes” (After the user Registration is approved, the user can access the schemes page). **Step 4:** Select the concerned scheme. Fill in all the mandatory details and upload the required documents. **Step 5:** Review the details and check the declaration box. Now click on “Submit” to complete the application process. &gt; **Renewal Process:** **Step 1:** Visit the official [Website ](https://kbocwwb.karnataka.gov.in/login)of Karnataka Building &amp;amp; Other Construction Workers Welfare Board (KBOCWWB) and click on “Login”. Step 2: Enter your registered mobile number and the received OTP. **Step 3:** From the dashboard, click on “Renewal”. Fill in mandatory details and upload the required documents. **Step 4:** Review the details and click on “Submit” to complete the renewal process. &gt; **Note:** - **Renewal:** Every construction worker must renew their registration with the Karnataka Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board every 3 years. If not renewed within this period, a 1-year grace period is granted. After that, the registration becomes invalid. - **Timeline:** - Application can be submitted within 6 months of delivery. - Application can be submitted every year (up to 3 years). - Child living Affidavit shall be submitted for second and third year. &gt; **Helpdesk:** Karnataka Building And Other Construction Workers Welfare Board, **Address:** Karmika Bhavan, Dairy Circle, Bannerghatta Road, Bangalore-29. **Office No:** 080-29753078 **E-mail:** labour.commissioner42@gmail.com [List of District Office.](https://karbwwb.karnataka.gov.in/7/district-office/en) [List of Taluk Offices.](https://karmikaspandana.karnataka.gov.in/page/Contact+Us/Labour+Inspector/en)
