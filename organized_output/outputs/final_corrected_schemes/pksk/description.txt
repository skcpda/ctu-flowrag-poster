# Padhuke Kuteera Scheme Karnataka

**Short Title:** PKSK

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Financial Assistance, Artisans, Self-employment, Scheduled Caste

The &quot;Paduke Kuteera Scheme&quot; was implemented by Karnataka Leather Industries Development Corporation Ltd, Department of Social Welfare, Govt of Karnataka. The scheme aims to provide a steel-made kiosk (Paduke Kuteera) and a tool kit free of cost to roadside cobblers engaged in repair work or footwear production. The scheme promotes self-employment by offering proper infrastructure and resources to cobblers.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Business & Entrepreneurship; Sector: Setting up / start-up / entrepreneurship

**Where it applies:** Karnataka; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - **Free Paduke Kuteera made of steel material with signage board display arrangement.** - **Free Tool Kit.**  **NOTE:** Unit cost for each Kuteera is ₹1,25,000/-.

**Benefit Type:** In Kind

**How to apply:** > **Registration Process:** **Step-01:** Visit the official [Seva Sindhu Portal](https://sevasindhu.karnataka.gov.in/Sevasindhu/English). **Step-02:** Choose &quot;New Users Register Here&quot; and fill in your Aadhaar Number and the Captcha code. **Step-03:** An OTP will be sent to your Aadhaar-linked mobile number. Enter the OTP to continue. **Step-04:** Once your DigiLocker account is created, click &quot;Allow&quot; to proceed. **Step-05:** Fill out your email ID, mobile number, create a password, and enter the captcha, then click &quot;Submit&quot;. **Step-06:** You will receive OTPs on both your email and mobile number. Enter both OTPs and select &quot;Validate&quot;. **Step-07:** Once validated, a confirmation message will indicate your registration is successful.  > **Applying for a Scheme:** **Step-01:** Go to [Seva Sindhu Portal](https://sevasindhu.karnataka.gov.in/Sevasindhu/English) and select &quot;Department &amp; Services&quot;. **Step-02:** Select department &quot;Social Welfare Department&quot; and click on the scheme &quot;Padhuke Kuteera Scheme&quot; **Step-03:** Click &quot;Apply Online&quot; and enter your registered email ID/mobile number and the OTP. **Step-04:** Select the scheme and provide your personal, address, and banking details as required. **Step-05:** Once submitted, you will receive an Application ID. You can use this ID to track your application status on the portal.
Mode: Online.

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: Karnataka Leather Industries Development Corporation Ltd; Nodal Department: Social Welfare Department.

**References / Annexures:** - **Guidelines**: https://schemes.lidkar.com/schemes/self-employment-programs/

**Additional Information:**
Briefdescription: The "Paduke Kuteera Scheme" provides roadside cobblers engaged in repair work or footwear production with a steel-made kiosk (Paduke Kuteera) and a tool kit free of cost. The unit cost of each Kuteera is ₹1,25,000, and the scheme includes a signage board display arrangement.
