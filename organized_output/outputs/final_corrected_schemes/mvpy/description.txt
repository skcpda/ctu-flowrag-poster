# Mukhyamantri Vidyarthi Pratibha Yojna

**Short Title:** MVPY

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Financial Assistance, Student Finance, Social Welfare, Education

The objective of the scheme is to provide scholarships to the students studying in class 9th to 12th for enabling them to bear the expenses required for their studies,SC/ST/OBC students of class IX to XII of all Government/Aided/Recognized public schools affiliated to Directorate of Education/Kendriya Vidyalaya Sangathan/National Open School/Schools affiliated to NDMC/Delhi Cantonment Board, East Delhi Municipal Corporation, North Delhi Municipal Corporation and South Delhi Municipal Corporation

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Pension

**Where it applies:** Delhi; State

**Benefits / Assistance:** Amount of Assistance: 1. IX to X Class : Rs. 5,000/- Per annum 1. XI to XII Class : Rs.10,000/- Per annum.

**Benefit Type:** In Kind

**How to apply:** - Applicants have to visit [https://edistrict.delhigovt.nic.in/](https://edistrict.delhigovt.nic.in/) 1. Under Citizen Corner click on New User 1. Click on select the document – Aadhar or Voter ID 1. Enter your Aadhar Card Number/Voter ID Card Number 1. Type the Captcha in the box shown - Citizen Registration Form will Open for registration 1. Fill up all fields including details of the Present Residential Address 1. Enter Captcha, and Click on Continue. 1. Login ID and Password will be received on the given mobile number/e-mail address. 1. Now Registration on the e-district portal is complete - Registration for Financial Assistance Services 1. Click on Registered User Login 1. Enter the given login ID and Password 1. Enter Captcha and Click on Login 1. Go to Main Page (Main Page) will open click on apply online. 1. Drop Box apply for services will open – click 1. A list of Departments providing online services shall open – 1. Select Department of Social Welfare. 1. Click on the scheme name to Apply. 1. Check the BASIC/PERSONAL DETAILS FORM and click on Continue. 1. The form will open fill up all required fields &amp; upload all required documents as per the requirement of the scheme selected. 1. Then click next and upload a photograph 1. Then click on finish. 1. One OTP will be received on the registered mobile number. 1. Enter OTP and Submit. 1. Acknowledgement of successful submission shall be generated.
Mode: Online.

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Social Welfare Department.
