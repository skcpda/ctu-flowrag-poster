# Establishment of Goat Unit (10 +1)

**Short Title:** EOGU

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Animal Husbandry, Goat Breeding, Goat Unit

The &amp;quot;Establishment of Goat Unit (10+1)&amp;quot; scheme by the Agriculture, Farmers Welfare and Cooperation Department, Gujarat, aims to promote the social and economic upliftment of goat breeders by encouraging goat breeding and genetic improvement through pure breeding. The scheme provides financial assistance to Scheduled Caste (SC), Scheduled Tribe (ST) women, and General category individuals, with priority given to widowed and abandoned women from Scheduled Tribes.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Agriculture,Rural & Environment; Sector: Animal husbandry

**Where it applies:** Gujarat; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:**  **Category****Standard of Assistance****Minimum Time Limit(In Year) for Reclaiming Benefits**Scheduled Tribe women beneficiaries50% Subsidy on Unit Cost or Maximum ₹45,000/-once in a lifetimeScheduled Caste beneficiaries50% Subsidy on Unit Cost or Maximum ₹45,000/- once in a lifetimeGeneral Category beneficiaries50% Subsidy on Unit Cost or Maximum ₹45,000/-once in a lifetime

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**How to apply:** **Step 1:** The applicant may visit and apply online at I-Khedut Portal: [https://ikhedut.gujarat.gov.in/](https://ikhedut.gujarat.gov.in/) **Step 2:** On the home page, click on ‘Schemes’ and then click on ‘Animal Husbandry Schemes’. **Step 3:** Now select the scheme, click on the ‘Apply’ button and an application page will open. **Step 4:** Click on &amp;quot;Apply New&amp;quot; button and submit a new application. **Step 5:** Click on the &amp;quot;Update Application&amp;quot; button to add corrections to the application. **Step 6:** Once the application is done, confirm it. **Step 7:** Take a print out of the confirmed application and submit Related Documents through to concern Taluka office. **Step 8:** Applicant can also check their Application Status/View/Print/Upload Application details from the below link: [https://ikhedut.gujarat.gov.in/public/frm_Applicant_Corner.aspx](https://ikhedut.gujarat.gov.in/public/frm_Applicant_Corner.aspx) &gt; **Contact Us:** [Click here](https://ikhedut.gujarat.gov.in/Public/frm_Public_Contacts.aspx).
Mode: Online.

**Additional Application Processes:** **Process 2:** **Step 1:** Visit Gram Panchayat or the nearest Government Veterinary Dispensary to collect the application form. **Step 2:** Carefully fill in all the required details, including personal information, category details, and bank details. **Step 3:** Attach photocopies of all the necessary documents (self-attested, if required) and submit the application form.
