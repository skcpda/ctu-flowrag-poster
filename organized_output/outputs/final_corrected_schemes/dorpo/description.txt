# Distribution of Rhizobium Packets-Oilseeds

**Short Title:** DORPO

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Agriculture, Farmer, Rhizobium, Oilseeds, Subsidy

The “Distribution of Rhizobium Packets-Oilseeds” scheme by the Agriculture-Farmers Welfare Department, Tamil Nadu, provides a subsidy to farmers on the cost of Rhizobium. It targets farmers who produce and supply Foundation and Certified Class seeds to the Department of Agriculture.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Agriculture,Rural & Environment; Sector: Agricultural Inputs- seeds, fertilizer etc.

**Where it applies:** Tamil Nadu; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - **Quantum of Assistance:** 50% on the cost of Rhizobium or ₹100/- per ha. whichever is less. 

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**How to apply:** **Step 1:** Collect the application form from the nearest Agricultural Office. **Step 2:** Fill out the application form with the required details and attach mandatory documents (self-attested, if required). **Step 3:** Submit the form to the concerned office: &gt; **Village Level:** Submit the application to the Assistant Agricultural Officer. &gt; **Block Level:** Submit to the Agricultural Officer or Deputy Agricultural Officer. &gt; **District Level:** Submit to the Joint Director of Agriculture.
Mode: Offline.

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Agriculture Department.

**References / Annexures:** - **Guidelines**: https://www.tn.gov.in/scheme/data_view/7616

**Additional Information:**
Briefdescription: The “Distribution of Rhizobium Packets-Oilseeds” scheme provides a subsidy on the cost of Rhizobium, to eligible farmers who produce and supply Foundation and Certified Class seeds.
