# Post-Matric Scholarship for Backward Classes- Uttarakhand

**Short Title:** PMTSBCUK

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Post Matric Scholarship, Backward Classes, Education, Student

The scheme &quot;Pre-Matric Scholarship for Backward Classes- Uttarakhand&quot; by the Department of Social Welfare, Government of Uttarakhand, aims to provide financial assistance to students belonging to the Backward Classes category in Uttarakhand for pursuing education after Class 10th. 

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Education & Learning; Sector: Scholarships and student finance

**Where it applies:** Uttarakhand; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - **For Hostellers:** ₹750/- per month.- **For Day Scholars:** ₹350/- per month. 

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**How to apply:** - Students can apply online for scholarship by visiting the NSP Portal of Government of India ([https://scholarships.gov.in/](https://scholarships.gov.in/)). - The following documents will be attached with the application: Aadhaar card of the applicant, caste certificate, mobile number, certified photo and CBS bank account which is Aadhaar linked/seeded, previous class passed mark sheet, self-declaration certificate that the applicant is not taking benefit of any other scholarship scheme of the government. - The student sends the application to his educational institution with his own ID; the concerned educational institution forwards the received online application form online to the District Social Welfare Officer. - The District Social Welfare Officer examines the received application form and gets the complete application forms physically verified. Incomplete application forms, which can be corrected, are temporarily defected and those which cannot be corrected are permanently rejected. The amount is sent directly to the account of the eligible applicants as per the availability of budget. 
Mode: Online.

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Department Of Social Welfare.
