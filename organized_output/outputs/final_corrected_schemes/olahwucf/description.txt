# Old Age Pension To Handloom Weavers Under Coop. Fold

**Short Title:** OLAHWUCF

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Pension, Handloom Weavers, Old Age

The “Old Age Pension To Handloom Weavers&amp;quot; scheme has been implemented by the Micro, Small &amp;amp; Medium Enterprise and Textiles Department, Government of West Bengal. The objective of the scheme is to provide monthly financial assistance in the form of pension to a weaver member of a handloom cooperative society. 

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Pension

**Where it applies:** West Bengal; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - An amount of ₹1000/- per month.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**How to apply:** **Step 1:** Application may be obtained free of cost from the office of the Handloom Development officer of the area where the application resides. **Step 2:** Application for Pension shall be addressed to the Handloom Development Officer with all the required documents. > **Area wise address the office of the Handloom Development officer:** [Click here](https://westbengalhandloom.org/off_addrs#:~:text=Handloom%20Development%20Office%2C,-Contai%2C&text=Shreerupa%20Road%2C%20Floor%2D%20II%2C,Pin%2D%20721%20433.).
Mode: Offline.

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Micro, Small & Medium Enterprises and Textiles Department.
