# Asangathit Karmakaar Silai Machine Sahayata Yojana

**Short Title:** AKSMSY

**Launch Date:** 2012-04-28

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Sewing Machine, Empowerment, Business

The Asangathit Karmakaar Silai Machine Sahayata Yojana (Unorganized Workers Sewing Machine Assistance Scheme) is a government scheme in Chhattisgarh State that provides sewing machines. The objective of this scheme is to promote unorganized workers in their businesses and make them self-reliant.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Business & Entrepreneurship; Sector: Machine & skill up-Gradation

**Where it applies:** Chhattisgarh; State

**Benefits / Assistance:** Under the scheme, sewing machines will be supplied every year to the registered unorganized women workers of the state.

**Benefit Type:** In Kind
