# "Fishing Ban Period Assistance" under "Welfare and Relief for Fishermen During Lean Seasons and Natural Calamities Scheme"

**Short Title:** WRFLSNCS-FBPS

**Scheme For:** Family

**Tags:** Welfare, Relief, Clothes, Fisheries, Fisherman

The scheme &quot;Fishing Ban Period Assistance&quot; is a Sub-Component under the scheme &quot;Welfare and Relief for Fishermen During Lean Seasons and Natural Calamities Scheme&quot;. The scheme is extended to all the regions of the Union territory of Puducherry. The scheme is introduced with the objective to assist fishermen families who are deprived of income during natural calamities and fishing ban periods, and to conserve the fishery wealth in sea during breeding season.

**Who is this for:** Individual, Family; Category: Agriculture,Rural & Environment; Sector: Fishing and hunting

**Where it applies:** Puducherry; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** 100 kgs. of rice and a cash dole at ₹ 750/- per fishermen&#39;s family during the period of ban on fishing during the year.

**Benefit Type:** Composite

**How to apply:** **Step 1:** The interested applicant should visit the office of the [concerned authority](https://fisheries.py.gov.in/sub-offices) i.e. Pondicherry State Fishermen Co-operative Federation Limited, Puducherry/The Karaikal Fishermen Co-operative Marketing Union, Karaikal/The Assistant Director of Fisheries and Fishermen Welfare, Mahe/The Assistant Director of Fisheries and Fishermen Welfare, Yanam of the respective regions. **Step 1:** The interested applicant should request the hard copy of the [prescribed format](https://fisheries.py.gov.in/sites/default/files/ban-relief-application003.pdf) of the application form from the concerned authority. **Step 2:** In the application form, fill in all the mandatory fields, paste the passport-sized photograph (signed across, if required), and attach copies of all the mandatory documents (self-attest, if required). **Step 3:** Submit the duly filled and signed application form along with the documents to the concerned authority. **Step 4:** Request a receipt or acknowledgment from the concerned authority to whom the application has been submitted. Ensure that the receipt contains essential details such as the date and time of submission, and a unique identification number (if applicable).
Mode: Offline.

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Fisheries and Fishermen Welfare Department, Puducherry.
