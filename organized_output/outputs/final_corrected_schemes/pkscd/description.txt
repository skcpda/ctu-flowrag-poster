# Promotion of Konkani Songs with Choreography & Dancing

**Short Title:** PKSCD

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Artist, Youth, Choreography, Dance, Culture, Competition

The scheme &quot;Promotion of Konkani Songs with Choreography &amp; Dancing&quot; was launched by the Tiatr Academy of Goa, Government of Goa. The scheme aims to promote Konkani songs by presenting them with choreography and dancing, attracting youth towards Konkani &#39;kantar&#39; and improving the presentation quality of Konkani cultural performances. The scheme provides cash prizes ranging from ₹2,000/- to ₹7,000/- for winners along with mementos and certificates, and traveling allowances between ₹1,000/- to ₹2,000/- based on distance traveled by participating groups. The applicant must form a group of 6 to 10 participants and submit a recording of one selected Konkani song at the time of application. The scheme is implemented by Tiatr Academy of Goa and applications for this scheme are accepted offline. 

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Sports & Culture; Sector: Culture

**Where it applies:** Goa; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** ##### ***Cash Prizes for Performance*** - First Prize: ₹7,000/- plus memento and certificate. - Second Prize: ₹5,000/- plus memento and certificate. - Third Prize: ₹3,000/- plus memento and certificate. - Special Prize for Best Choreographer: ₹2,000/- plus memento and certificate. ##### ***Traveling Allowance*** - Groups traveling up to 10 kilometers radius: ₹1,000/-. - Groups traveling within 10-20 kilometers radius: ₹1,500/-. - Groups traveling 20 kilometers or more: ₹2,000/-. 

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**How to apply:** **Step 1:** The interested applicant should take a printout of the [prescribed format](https://www.goa.gov.in/wp-content/uploads/2021/06/Citizen-Charter-TAG.pdf) of the application form. **Step 2:** In the application form, fill in all the mandatory fields, attach copies of all the mandatory documents (self-attest, if required). Sign the application form and mention the date. **Step 3:** Submit the duly filled and signed application form along with the documents to - ***Tiatr Academy of Goa, AS-1 / AS-4, Block A, 2nd Floor, Campal Trade Centre, Opposite Kala Academy, Campal, Panaji, Goa, India – 403001*** _*Applicants are advised to save a copy of the submitted application form and any acknowledgment._ _*The application must be submitted to the office of the Tiatr Academy of Goa at least 30 days in advance of the proposed programme date._ _*Applications are considered on a &quot;First come First Serve&quot; basis, subject to the availability of funds._ > ***Post-Application Process*** **Step 1:** Competition Details Wait for Tiatr Academy of Goa will communicate the venue, date, and other competition details to participating groups. **Step 2:** Participate in Competition Attend the competition at the designated venue and perform the registered Konkani song with choreography and dancing. 
Mode: Offline.

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: Tiatr Academy Of Goa; Nodal Department: Art, Culture and Museums Department.
