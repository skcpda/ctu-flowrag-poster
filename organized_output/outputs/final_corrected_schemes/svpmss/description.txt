# Swami Vivekananda Post-Matric Scholarship Scheme (Class 11th & 12th)

**Short Title:** SVPMSS

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Student, Scholarship, Swami Vivekananda, Post-Matric, BPL

The scheme &amp;quot;Swami Vivekananda Post-Matric Scholarship Scheme&amp;quot; was launched by the School Education Department, Government of Madhya Pradesh, to motivate students from the lower economic backgrounds and support them economically. Under this scheme, scholarships are provided to students studying in Class 11 and Class 12 in government schools, whose annual family income is less than ₹1,00,000/- and who belong to the Below Poverty Line (BPL) category.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Education & Learning; Sector: Scholarships and student finance

**Where it applies:** Madhya Pradesh; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - Under this scheme, the girl student will get a scholarship of ₹550/- per year and boy student will get a scholarship of ₹500/- per year. **Note:** The scholarship is sent directly to the student&amp;#39;s bank account.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**How to apply:** To avail of the benefits under this scheme, eligible students can contact the Principal of their respective government schools/institutions. **Note:** The student&amp;#39;s profile is entered on the education portal [https://shikshaportal.mp.gov.in/](https://shikshaportal.mp.gov.in/) by the teacher of the educational institution as per the record/certificate provided by the student.
Mode: Offline.

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: School Education Department, Government Of Madhya Pradesh; Nodal Department: School Education Department.
