# Madho Singh Bhandari Cooperative Collective Farming Scheme

**Short Title:** MSBCCFS

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Cooperative Society, Committee Members, Farmers, Production, Store

The scheme &quot;Madho Singh Bhandari Cooperative Collective Farming Scheme&quot; by the Cooperative Department, Government of Uttarakhand, aims to uplift the living standards of cooperative society and committee members by enabling large-scale production, storage, and release of finished products at remunerative prices and providing them fair prices for their products. Through this scheme, the establishment of a collective cooperative integrated farming model, conversion of barren and vacant land into cultivable land, increased production through collective farming clusters, promotion of reverse migration, development of M.PAX as a business entity, creation of livelihood opportunities, promotion of diverse crop production, promotion of agri-tourism, and employment generation are provided to the cooperative society and committee members and local villagers.

**Who is this for:** Registered Societies; Category: Agriculture,Rural & Environment; Sector: Agricultural Inputs- seeds, fertilizer etc.

**Where it applies:** Uttarakhand; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** 1. Establishment of a collective cooperative integrated farming model that can be replicated in every block of Uttarakhand. 1. Conversion of barren and vacant land into cultivable land, expanding agricultural areas and increasing potential yield. 1. To increase production by developing collective farming clusters through cooperative societies and to provide a proper market for the crops produced. 1. Initiatives to promote reverse migration within the state, encouraging people to return to their native places for livelihood. 1. To the development of M.PAX as a business entity, likely strengthening the cooperative framework for economic activities. 1. To create livelihood opportunities for local villagers through commercial farming. 1. To promote the production of vegetables, coarse cereals, pulses, fruits, mushrooms, herbs, fodder crops, etc., through improved agricultural technologies and modern farming practices. 1. Promotion of agri-tourism under the C.C.F. (Collective Cooperative Farming) model, diversifying income sources and promoting rural tourism. 1. Promotion of other agriculture-related activities in the surrounding area, like agriculture, tourism, dairy, etc. 1. Employment generation through different activities during project implementation.

**Benefit Type:** In Kind

**How to apply:** - Contact Cooperative Society: Approach your local Multipurpose Cooperative Society or District Cooperative Office. - Submit Proposal: The society submits a proposal for collective farming cluster development to the Cooperative Department. - Approval &amp; Formation: Upon approval, cooperative members collaborate to convert land, adopt modern techniques, and begin collective farming.
Mode: Offline.

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Co-Operative Department.
