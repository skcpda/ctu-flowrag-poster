# Saraswati Cycle Yojana

**Short Title:** SCY

**Launch Date:** 2004-01-01

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Transportation Facilities, Student, Girl Education

**Objective** - Providing transportation facilities to the girl students of class 9th studying in government schools and aided non-government schools - Encouraging girl child education. - To reduce the school dropout rate under this scheme, the state government has decided to give cycles to girls. - Girls will be able to go to school if the school is away from them by getting cycles. 

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Education & Learning

**Where it applies:** Chhattisgarh; State

**Benefits / Assistance:** - Free cycles are being distributed to girl students under the scheme.

**Benefit Type:** In Kind

**How to apply:** 1. The applicant should first contact the headmaster of the concerned school or institution. 1. Applicants can also approach the District Education Officer (DEO) to apply for this scheme. 1. After this, the applicant will have to get the application form of the Chhattisgarh Saraswati Cycle Yojana. 1. Now fill in all the information asked in the application form completely. 1. Attach all the necessary documents with the application form. 1. The application form has to be submitted. 1. The application form will be verified by the concerned officer. 1. The applicant will be informed after the approval of the application form. 1. Beneficiary girls are selected by the principal on the basis of caste certificate and B.P.L. Selection of girls of the class related to K.B.P.L. done on card basis.
Mode: Offline.

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: School Education Department.
