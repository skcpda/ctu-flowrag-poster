# Scholarship Scheme For Disabled Students To Study Abroad For Higher Education

**Short Title:** SSDS

**Launch Date:** 2008-08-12

**Scheme For:** Individual

Scholarships are to be provided to twelve students every year by the Department of Social Justice proposed. So scholarship is provided to 2-2 orthopedically disabled students, 2-2 hearing impaired students and 2-2 visually impaired students of postgraduate level courses in specific fields abroad for a research degree (P. .HD) and to provide financial assistance to participate in research programs after the research degree. &lt;br&gt; While, the students of the Department of Social Justice, who are benefited, will be able to obtain higher education abroad and get special academic achievements, on the other hand, other students of the Department of Social Justice will also be attracted by their achievements and move towards higher education.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Education & Learning; Sector: Scholarships and student finance

**Where it applies:** Madhya Pradesh; State

**Benefits / Assistance:** - Scholarship for Higher Education in abroad

**Benefit Type:** In Kind

**How to apply:** 1. Applicants have to visit the [official web site](https://socialjustice.mp.gov.in) and download the [application form](https://socialjustice.mp.gov.in/uploads/schemes/Order__38.pdf).1. Fill out the application with the necessary information. 1. Attach the required documents. 1. Application in the prescribed form will have to be submitted to the District Office of Social Justice and Disabled Welfare
Mode: Online.

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Social Welfare Department.

**References / Annexures:** - **Guidelines**: https://socialjustice.mp.gov.in/uploads/schemes/Order__37.pdf

**Additional Information:**
Briefdescription: In 2008, Madhya Pradesh State Government started the "Nihshakt Vidyarthiyon Ko Uchch Shiksha Hetu Videsh Adhyayan Hetu Chhaatravrtti Yojana" for disabled students to study abroad for higher education.
