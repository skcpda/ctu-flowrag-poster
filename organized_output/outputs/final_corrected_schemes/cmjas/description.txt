# CM's Jibon Anuprerana Scheme

**Short Title:** CMJAS

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Research, Scholar, PhD, Financial Assistance, PwD, Disability

The scheme &quot;CM&#39;s Jibon Anuprerana Scheme&quot; by the Skill, Employment &amp; Entrepreneurship Department, Government of Assam aims to provide one-time financial assistance to eligible full-time research scholars and full-time Divyang (differently-abled) research scholars from public institutions in Assam, thereby promoting academic excellence and innovation in Assam. The scheme provides financial assistance of ₹25,000/- for research scholars and ₹40,000/- for Divyang research scholars as a one-time benefit during their scholastic journey. To be eligible, the applicant must be a permanent resident of Assam and must be admitted to PhD program in 2025 at Central or State Universities in Assam. The scheme is implemented by the Directorate of Employment and Craftsmen Training through the Directorate of Investment and Development of Skill Portal. The applications for this scheme are accepted online. 

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Skills & Employment; Sector: Training and Skill Up-gradation

**Where it applies:** Assam; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** **For Research Scholars:** One-time Financial Assistance of ₹25,000/-. **For Divyang Research Scholars:** One-time Financial Assistance of ₹40,000/-. **Mode of Disbursement:** Direct Benefit Transfer to eligible scholars. 

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**How to apply:** **Step 1:** **Access the Directorate of Investment and Development of Skill Portal** Visit the [official portal ](https://dids.assam.gov.in/homeUser)of the Directorate of Investment and Development of Skills (DIDS). Navigate to the CM&#39;s Jibon Anuprerana Scheme section. **Step 2:** **Submit Application Online** Fill in all required personal details including name, contact details, and identity proof information. Enter institution details and enrollment number. Upload proof of PhD admission. Submit self-declaration and exclusion compliance documents. Upload all required supporting documents in the specified format. **Step 3:** **Institutional Verification** The concerned institution&#39;s designated verifier (Registrar, Principal, or Vice Chancellor) will access the Visit the [official portal ](https://dids.assam.gov.in/homeUser)of the Directorate of Investment and Development of Skills (DIDS). Institution will verify applicant information including name, proof of identity, contact details, institution details, enrollment number, proof of PhD admission, and self-declaration compliance. Institution will mark verification status as either Accept (verified and eligible) or Reject (ineligible or non-compliant) with specific remarks. Institution will forward verified applications to Directorate of Employment and Craftsmen Training within 7 working days. **Step 4:** **Scrutiny by Directorate of Employment and Craftsmen Training** Directorate of Employment and Craftsmen Training will conduct scrutiny of verified applications. Bulk forwarding of eligible applications for final processing. **Step 5:** **Preparation of Final Beneficiary List** Final list of eligible beneficiaries will be prepared after complete scrutiny. **Step 6:** **Direct Benefit Transfer Disbursement** Eligible scholars will receive financial assistance through Direct Benefit Transfer to their bank accounts.  **Help &amp; Support / Grievance Redressal** Email: infochiefministersscheme@gmail.com; Phone: 8099897266 **Timelines** - Verification by Institution: Within 5 working days of application submission - Final Forwarding by Institution: Within 7 working days 
Mode: Online.

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Skill, Employment & Entrepreneurship Department.
