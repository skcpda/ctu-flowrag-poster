# Training for Horticulture Skill Development for Self-Employment at Centre of Excellence

**Short Title:** THSDSE

**Tags:** Horticulture Training, Skill Development, Self-Employment, Student

The scheme &amp;quot;Training for Horticulture Skill Development for Self-Employment at Centre of Excellence&amp;quot; introduced by the Agriculture, Farmers Welfare and Cooperation Department, Gujarat, aims to offer structured training programs ranging from 10 to 30 days, focusing on horticultural skills to enable self-employment. The program is tailored for individuals across all social categories, with subsidized training costs. Beneficiaries gain access to a complete learning experience, including expert sessions, training literature, educational tours, and practical kits.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Skills & Employment; Sector: Training and Skill Up-gradation

**Where it applies:** Gujarat; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - **Stipend:** ₹250/- per trainee.- **Other Facilities:** Dining facility, training literature kit, experiential education tour, trainee kit, etc. > **Duration of the Training:** - Minimum: 10 days - Maximum: 30 days

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**How to apply:** **Step 1:** Obtain a plain paper application form from the District Office of the Deputy Director of Horticulture. **Step 2:** Fill in the application form with accurate details. Attach the required documents. **Step 3:** Submit the form to the District Office of the Deputy Director of Horticulture.
Mode: Offline.

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Agriculture, Farmers Welfare and Cooperation Department.

**References / Annexures:** - **Guidelines (Scheme No. 560)**: https://mariyojana.gujarat.gov.in/MoreDetails.aspx - **User Manual (Applicant Registration)**: https://ikhedut.gujarat.gov.in/site/iKhedut%202.0%20User%20Manual%20PPT_Gujarati.pdf

**Additional Information:**
Briefdescription: The scheme "Training for Horticulture Skill Development for Self-Employment at Centre of Excellence " is aimed at promoting self-employment opportunities across all communities in Gujarat through practical and theoretical learning at the Centre of Excellence.
