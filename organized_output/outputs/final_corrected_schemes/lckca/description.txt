# Lifetime Contribution to Konkani Cinema Award

**Short Title:** LCKCA

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Artist, Cinema, Award, Culture, Heritage

The scheme &quot;Lifetime Contribution to Konkani Cinema Award&quot; was launched by the Tiatr Academy under the Art, Culture and Museums Department, Government of Goa. The scheme aims to recognize and honor artistes including tiatr artistes who have contributed immensely for the development of Konkani Cinema in the past, addressing the need to acknowledge their significant contributions to this cultural art form. The scheme provides a prestigious annual award consisting of a shawl, memento, citation, and cash purse of ₹50,000/-, with recognition given at a special function held on 24th April every year on the anniversary of the release of the first Konkani film &#39;Mogacho Anvddo&#39; by late Al-Jerry Braganza, &#39;The Father of Konkani Cinema&#39;. 

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Sports & Culture; Sector: Culture

**Where it applies:** Goa; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** **Cash Award:** ₹50,000/- as fixed by Tiatr Academy of Goa. **Honorary Items:** Shawl, memento, and citation presented to the awardee. **Recognition Ceremony:** Special function held on 24th April every year on the anniversary of the first Konkani film. _*The awardee must be present in person to receive the award at the ceremony._ _*The award ceremony attendance is mandatory - no proxy or representative acceptance is allowed._ _*Once received, the award cannot be conferred again in the recipient&#39;s lifetime across any category._ 

**Benefit Type:** Composite

**Exclusions / Not eligible:** The applicant cannot receive the award more than once in their lifetime across any category (Tiatr, Khell, Khell-Tiatr, or Konkani Cinema). 

**How to apply:** **Step 1:** Recommendations are invited from individuals and institutions for deserving candidates. **Step 2:** Advertisements are published in local newspapers seeking nominations. **Step 3:** The Executive Board of Tiatr Academy of Goa reviews all recommendations and may also select deserving artistes from outside the recommended list. **Step 4:** Selection is made based on the specified criteria including years of service, excellence in their field, awards received, innovations made, and published works. **Step 5:** The selected artiste is informed and must confirm their availability to attend the award ceremony in person. 
Mode: Offline.

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Art, Culture and Museums Department.
