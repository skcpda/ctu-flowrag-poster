# Craftsman Training Scheme - Maharashtra

**Short Title:** CTS-MAHARASHTRA

**Launch Date:** 2023-09-13

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Stipend, Craftsman, Training, Skill Development

The &quot;Craftsman Training Scheme&quot; was launched by the Skill Development, Employment and Entrepreneurship Department, Government of Maharashtra. This stipend aims to support the trainees financially and encourage them to pursue and complete their vocational training, thereby contributing to skilled manpower development in the state.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Skills & Employment; Sector: Training and Skill Up-gradation

**Where it applies:** Maharashtra; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - **Stipend ₹500/- per month.**

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**How to apply:** > ***MAHA DBT Registration Process:*** **Step 01:** Visit the [official website](https://mahadbt.maharashtra.gov.in/home/flow). **Step 02:** Click &quot;New Applicant Registration&quot; and fill in the Applicant Name, Username, Confirm Password, Email ID, and Mobile Number. **Step 03:** Fill in the Captcha, OTP and Click to &quot;Register&quot;  > ***Login and Apply*** **Step 01:** Click &quot;Applicant Login&quot; and Enter your registered username, password **Step 02:** Enter the security text shown in the image and click on the &quot;Login Here&quot; button for login **Step 03:** Create Your Profile Personal Information, Address Information, Current Course, Current Course, etc. **Step 04:** View Suggested Eligible Schemes and Choose Scheme **Step 05:** Apply for the scheme and Upload the relevant document. **Step 06:** After that, you get the Application ID.  **Note:** - The applicant having an Aadhaar-seeded savings bank account in a nationalized bank is mandatory. - Trainee registration on the MAHA-DBT portal is compulsory. 
Mode: Online.

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Skill Development and Entrepreneurship Department.
