# Keetpalan Upkaran

**Short Title:** KU

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Financial Assistance, Farmer, Equipment, Silkworm, Rearing

The “Keetpalan Upkaran” scheme by the Sericulture Department, Government of Uttarakhand. The scheme aims to provide financial assistance to farmers for procuring essential equipment required for silkworm rearing. 

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Agriculture,Rural & Environment; Sector: Financial assistance

**Where it applies:** Uttarakhand; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - An assistance of ₹40,000 (80:10:10) is provided to selected beneficiaries for purchasing necessary equipment for insect farming. - Out of this amount, 10% is to be spent by the beneficiary themselves to establish a leaf chamber, while other insect farming materials are provided by the department. 

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**How to apply:** - Interested beneficiaries are selected through promotional meetings/trainings organized by representatives of cooperative societies associated with the block centers / sericulture self-help groups and center/range in the field. - Upon receiving a cluster proposal for a specific area, a specialist committee conducts a ground inspection to determine whether the area is suitable for mulberry plantation/activities. - Following the recommendation of the ground inspection, the technical support of departmental personnel is provided for mulberry planting and other work plans in accordance with the guidelines. - To prepare the P.I.S. for the selected beneficiaries, voter ID/ration card, bank account details, and land-related documents of the area where they wish to plant trees are collected. - The establishment of the leaf chamber is done by the farmers, and other pest rearing materials are provided by the department. Under the Silk Samagra scheme, there is a provision for training farmers, in which all farmers selected in the cluster are trained in tree planting/pest rearing, etc. Those beneficiaries who are not selected under the scheme are given training as mentioned above.
