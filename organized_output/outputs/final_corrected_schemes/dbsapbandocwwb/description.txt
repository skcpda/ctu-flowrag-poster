# Death Benefit Scheme (APB&OCWWB)

**Short Title:** DBSAPB&OCWWB

**Scheme For:** Family

**Tags:** Construction Worker, Death, Financial Assistance, Labour

The scheme “Death Benefit Scheme” was started by the Department of Labour and Employment, Govt. of Arunachal Pradesh for registered workers of the Arunachal Pradesh Building &amp; Other Construction Workers Welfare Board (APB&amp;OCWWB). The state government has proposed and implemented this scheme through the APB&amp;OCWWB for those families who suffer due to economic loss caused by the demise of the bread earner of the house and have registered their names in the Labour Welfare Board. **Objective:** - To mitigate such economic loss, the govt. has introduced the death benefit scheme for normal death and death due to accidents. 

**Who is this for:** Family; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Crisis/Disaster/Accident

**Where it applies:** Arunachal Pradesh; State
