# Chief Minister’s Paryatan Siksha Yojana

**Short Title:** CMPSY

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Tourism, Professional, Training, Diploma Course, Sponsorship, Scheduled Tribe

The scheme “Chief Minister’s Paryatan Siksha Yojana” was launched by the Government of Arunachal Pradesh to promote tourism and develop the industry by means of trained individuals. With the increasing demand for skilled manpower in the field of Tourism and hospitality industry, it is imperative to encourage local youths to enroll in such courses. Therefore the Govt. of Arunachal Pradesh is pleased to sponsor students of the state for taking admission in different courses such as Hospitality and Catering, Food Production, Food &amp;amp; Beverage Service, Front Office, Housekeeping, Bakery &amp;amp; Confectionaries, etc. The need for trained professionals was felt for the growth of the Tourism industry in the state. Thus, this scheme was launched to support the training of individuals in the field of tourism.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Travel & Tourism; Sector: Education and training grants, fellowship, stipend

**Where it applies:** Arunachal Pradesh; State

**Benefits / Assistance:** 1. Sponsorship to study and take up courses in the field of tourism. 1. The local youths may enroll in courses such as B.Sc. (Hospitality and Catering) and Diploma/PGD courses (Food Production, Food &amp;amp; Beverage Service, Front Office, Housekeeping, Bakery &amp;amp; Confectionaries). 1. 8 students of the State will be sponsored to take up B. Sc. (H&amp;amp;C) for 3 years degree course in a prestigious Institute recognized by the Ministry of Tourism, Government of India. 1. 25 students of the State will be sponsored to take up a Diploma course for one and half years in five demand-driven categories being offered by the Food Craft Institute, Nagaon, Assam which is affiliated with National Council for Hotel Management and Catering Technology.

**Benefit Type:** In Kind

**How to apply:** **Step 01:** For taking benefit of this scheme, the applicant needs to visit the nearest District Tourism Office, Arunachal Pradesh. **Step 02:** After the published advertisement, the interested candidates may submit their applications in plain paper or a prescribed format with relevant documents at the Directorate of Tourism, Arunachal Pradesh. **Step 03:** After successful verification, the students will be selected as per the merit list drawn on the basis of the percentage of marks obtained under the criteria. **Step 04:** Further, the applicant will be interviewed and assessed by the District administration (District Tourism Officer) before he/she receives the benefit. 
Mode: Offline.

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: Department Of Tourism, Arunachal Pradesh; Nodal Department: Tourism Department.
