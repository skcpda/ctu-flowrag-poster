# Nari Niketans

**Short Title:** NN

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Shelter, Widow, Destitute, Rehabilitation, Social Welfare

The &quot;[Nari Niketans](https://jksocialwelfare.nic.in/welfareIns/NariNiketans.pdf)&quot; were established in 1976 by the Social Welfare Department, Govt. of Jammu and Kashmir wherein deserted /destitute ladies/ widows having no means of livelihood are given admissions. There are seven Nari Niketan in Jammu &amp; Kashmir, i.e. in Jammu, Udhampur, Kathua, Doda, Rajouri, and two Nari Niketans in Poonch, with a total intake capacity of 280. The inmate shall remain in the institution till they are rehabilitated/get married/covered under any schemes for employment and self-employment. The beneficiary shall approach the concerned District Social Welfare Officer (DSWO) for submission of the application. This is a 100% State Sponsored Scheme, and only the permanent residents of the state of Jammu &amp; Kashmir can apply for this scheme.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Housing & Shelter; Sector: Shelter, integrated support and assistance
