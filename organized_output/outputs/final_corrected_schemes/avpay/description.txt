# Antarjatiye Vivah Protsahan Anudan Yojana

**Short Title:** AVPAY

**Scheme For:** Family

**Tags:** Antarjatiye, Vivah, Inter-caste Marriage, Financial Assistance

The &amp;quot;Antarjatiye Vivah Protsahan Anudan Yojana&amp;quot; is a sub-scheme of the &amp;quot;Mukhyamantri Vivah Protsahan Anudan Yojana&amp;quot;, initiated by the Social Welfare Department, Government of Bihar. The objective of this scheme is to end the caste system in the state and encourage inter-caste marriages by providing financial assistance to couples. This scheme is fully funded by the Government of Bihar.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Marriage

**Where it applies:** Bihar; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - Under this scheme, eligible beneficiaries will receive a one-time grant of ₹1,00,000/-. **Note:** The grant will be provided to the bride.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**How to apply:** - To avail of the benefits of this scheme, individuals who meet the eligibility criteria should submit their applications in the prescribed form to the Assistant Director, District Social Security Office. **Note:** After approval and verification, the details of the beneficiaries will be transmitted on the e-Suvidha portal for payment by the Assistant Director, District Social Security Office. &gt; **Grievance Redressal &amp;amp; Escalation Matrix:** - The complaint can be lodged in the Block Development Officer, Sub-divisional Office, Assistant Director, District Social Security Cell/District Child Protection Unit, Director, Social Security Office and the office of Additional Chief Secretary/Principal Secretary/Secretary, Social Welfare Department. The office of Public Grievance Redressal Officer is functioning at the sub-divisional level and the office of District Public Grievance Redressal Officer is functioning at the district level.
Mode: Offline.

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: Social Welfare Department, Government Of Bihar; Nodal Department: Social Welfare Department.
