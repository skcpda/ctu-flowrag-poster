# Production of Audio Albums of songs on Patriotism & National Integration

**Short Title:** PAASPNI

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Culture, Artist, Album, Song, Music

The scheme &quot;Production of Audio Albums of songs on Patriotism &amp; National Integration&quot; was launched by the Tiatr Academy of Goa under the Art, Culture and Museums Department, Government of Goa. The scheme aims to strengthen the values of Patriotism &amp; National Integration among members of Tiatr fraternity, addressing the problem that audio albums of Konkani songs of Patriotism &amp; National Integration are rarely produced in Goa despite numerous audio albums of songs (Kantaram) in Konkani being produced. The scheme provides financial support as decided by Tiatr Academy of Goa for the production of audio albums containing at least 10 songs of Patriotism &amp; National Integration and distribution of 300 copies to schools and groups participating in various competitions. The applicant must be a tiatr artiste (commercial or non-commercial). The scheme is implemented by Tiatr Academy of Goa and applications for this scheme are accepted offline. 

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Sports & Culture; Sector: Culture

**Where it applies:** Goa; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** ##### ***Financial Support*** Tiatr Academy of Goa will give financial support as decided by Tiatr Academy of Goa for the production of the Album. ##### ***Distribution Channel*** Tiatr Academy of Goa will distribute 300 copies to schools &amp; groups which participate in its various competitions and may also offer them to other schools, groups, individuals, etc. who are interested in promoting Patriotism &amp; National Integration. ##### ***Commercial Rights*** The applicant is free to sell the remaining Compact Discs. ##### ***Sponsorship Permission*** The applicant may take sponsorship to meet the cost of production of Album. ##### ***Quality Assurance*** Lyrics will be scrutinized by the Recommendation Committee appointed by Tiatr Academy of Goa with advisory support to improve quality.  **Conditions** _*The applicant must produce the Audio album strictly following the specified norms._ _*Melody and musical arrangement must be appropriate to the content of the song._ _*Lyrics must be approved by the Recommendation Committee._ _*Official letter of acceptance must be obtained from Tiatr Academy of Goa before production._ 

**Benefit Type:** Composite

**Exclusions / Not eligible:**  

**How to apply:** **Step 1:** The interested applicant should take a printout of the [prescribed format](https://www.goa.gov.in/wp-content/uploads/2021/06/Citizen-Charter-TAG.pdf) of the application form. **Step 2:** In the application form, fill in all the mandatory fields, attach copies of all the mandatory documents (self-attest, if required). Sign the application form and mention the date. **Step 3:** Submit the duly filled and signed application form along with the documents to - ***Tiatr Academy of Goa, AS-1 / AS-4, Block A, 2nd Floor, Campal Trade Centre, Opposite Kala Academy, Campal, Panaji, Goa, India – 403001*** _*Applicants are advised to save a copy of the submitted application form and any acknowledgment._ _*The application must be submitted to the office of the Tiatr Academy of Goa at least 30 days in advance of the proposed programme date._ _*Applications are considered on a &quot;First come First Serve&quot; basis, subject to the availability of funds._  ##### ***Post-Application Processes*** **Step 1:****** ***Lyrics Scrutiny &amp; Committee Feedback*** - The applicant must wait for the Recommendation Committee appointed by Tiatr Academy of Goa to scrutinize the submitted lyrics, during which the committee may advise improvements to enhance the quality of lyrics and ensure alignment with patriotism and national integration themes. - The applicant must incorporate any suggestions or improvements recommended by the Recommendation Committee to enhance the quality of lyrics, ensuring the content meets the required standards for patriotism and national integration themes. **Step 2:****** ***Official Approval &amp; Acceptance Letter*** - The applicant must wait for the final decision from Tiatr Academy of Goa, as the academy reserves the right to disapprove the lyrics based on the opinion given by the Recommendation Committee, and will issue an official letter of acceptance if the lyrics are approved. - The applicant must obtain the official letter of acceptance from Tiatr Academy of Goa confirming approval of the lyrics before proceeding with the production of the audio album, as this letter is mandatory for proceeding to the production phase. **Step 3:****** ***Album Production &amp; Financial Support*** - The applicant must plan the production of the audio album strictly following the approved norms including minimum 10 songs, ensuring 50% are group and children&#39;s songs, and that melody and musical arrangement are appropriate to the content of each song. - The applicant must coordinate with Tiatr Academy of Goa to receive the financial support as decided by the academy for the production of the album, and may also arrange additional sponsorship to meet the complete cost of production if needed. **Step 4:****** ***Produce Audio Album &amp; Submit Required Copies*** - The applicant must produce the audio album following all specified norms and quality standards, ensuring professional recording quality and appropriate musical arrangements that complement the patriotic and national integration themes of the songs. - The applicant must provide 300 copies of the completed audio albums to Tiatr Academy of Goa as per the scheme requirements, which will be used by the academy for distribution to schools and groups participating in various competitions. **Step 5:****** ***Complete Documentation*** - The applicant must ensure all production documentation is complete and maintain records of the album production process, quality standards met, and compliance with all scheme norms for future reference and verification. 
Mode: Offline.
