# Scheme to Provide Assistance to Sate Market Committees as well as Taluka-District Co-operative Purchase and Sale Union to Construct Large Size Warehouse Godowns

**Short Title:** SPSMCT

**Scheme For:** Infra

**Tags:** Agriculture, Market Committee, Warehouse Godowns

The &amp;quot;Scheme to Provide Assistance to Sate Market Committees as well as Taluka-District Co-operative Purchase and Sale Union to Construct Large Size Warehouse Godowns&amp;quot; introduced by the Agriculture, Farmers Welfare and Cooperation Department, Gujarat, aims to address the storage needs of farmers through providing financial assistance for warehouse godowns construction within market committees. This will allow farmers to store their produce during the harvest season when prices are low and sell it later when market prices improve.

**Who is this for:** Government Organisation; Category: Agriculture,Rural & Environment; Sector: Financial assistance

**Where it applies:** Gujarat; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:**  **Component****Support Standard**Large size warehouse godowns up to 5000 MT in market committeesAll State Market Committees, each without considering the class standards will be paid ₹6,500/- per metric ton up to 50% of the project cost will be available for godowns up to 5000 mt 

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**How to apply:** **Step 1:** The market committee must identify land for constructing the warehouse. **Step 2:** Engineers from the approved panel will prepare maps and estimates based on the uniform design set by the Director of Agricultural Marketing and Rural Finance, Gujarat. **Step 3:** The Managing Director, Gujarat Agricultural Marketing Board, will oversee implementation. **Step 4:** The beneficiary must hire government-approved contractors of class &amp;quot;AA&amp;quot; to &amp;quot;D&amp;quot; for construction. **Step 5:** The construction work must be carried out through the e-tendering process.
Mode: Offline.

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Agriculture, Farmers Welfare and Cooperation Department.
