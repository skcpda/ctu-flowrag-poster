# Alpsankhyak Muslim Parityaqta/Talaqshuda Mahila Sahayata Yojana

**Short Title:** AMPTMSY

**Launch Date:** 2006-04-01

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Minority, Muslim Mahila, Abandoned Woman, Divorced Woman, Financial Assistance

The scheme &amp;quot;Alpsankhyak Muslim Parityakta/Talaqshuda Mahila Sahayata Yojana&amp;quot; has been operated by the Minority Welfare Department, Government of Bihar, since the financial year 2006-07. The objective of this scheme is to strengthen the economic condition of minority Muslim abandoned/divorced women and make them self-dependent by providing financial assistance.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Women and Child; Sector: Financial assistance

**Where it applies:** Bihar; State/ UT

**DBT Scheme:** Yes (Direct Benefit Transfer)

**Benefits / Assistance:** - Under this scheme, the state government provides financial assistance of ₹25,000/- to minority Muslim abandoned/divorced women to strengthen their economic condition and make them self-reliant. **Note:** The grant amount will be directly transferred to the beneficiary’s bank accounts through RTGS/DBT.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**How to apply:** **Step 1:** To apply for benefits under this scheme, applicants should visit the office of the District Minority Welfare Officer in their respective district to collect the application form. The [application form ](https://state.bihar.gov.in/cache/42/Old%20Notice%20Board/Schemes%20for%20Minorities/pratiyakta_yojna.pdf)can also be downloaded from the [department&amp;#39;s website](https://state.bihar.gov.in/minoritywelfare/CitizenHome.html). **Step 2:** Fill out the application form and attach all required documents. **Step 3:** Submit the completed application form, along with all attachments, to the District Minority Welfare Office of your district. **Note 1:** For applications and more information, please contact the office of the District Minority Welfare Officer located at the District Headquarters. **Note 2:** Advertisements for the application of this scheme will be published in newspapers by the Directorate of Minority Welfare. **Note 3:** The District Minority Welfare Officer will ensure the scrutiny of applications and attachments as per the prescribed procedure. **Note 4:** The names and addresses of beneficiaries will be published on the departmental website and in a daily Urdu/Hindi newspaper. Beneficiaries will also be informed via SMS.
Mode: Offline.

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: Directorate Of Minority Welfare, Government Of Bihar; Nodal Department: Minority Welfare Department.
