# Operation of River Rafting / Kayaking Activities

**Short Title:** ORRKAUK

**Scheme For:** Infra

**Tags:** Tourism Business, River Rafting, License, Kayaking Activities, Adventure

The scheme &amp;quot;Operation of River Rafting / Kayaking Activities&amp;quot;, implemented by the Uttarakhand Tourism Development Board under the Tourism Department, Government of Uttarakhand, aims to promote adventure tourism by regulating and authorizing rafting and kayaking operations in the state. Under this scheme, rafting companies are granted permission to operate, and licenses are issued to qualified river guides. This initiative not only generates revenue for the state but also provides significant economic opportunities for individuals and businesses involved in the tourism sector.

**Who is this for:** Business Entity; Category: Travel & Tourism

**Where it applies:** Uttarakhand; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - Authorized rafting companies are permitted to operate, ensuring safety and quality standards in adventure tourism. - Licenses are issued to river guides, creating job opportunities for skilled individuals. - Local entrepreneurs and businesses associated with the tourism sector benefit financially from increased tourist activity.

**Benefit Type:** In Kind

**How to apply:** **Step 1:** The applicant must submit the application along with all required documents to the Tourism Department office or the CEO of the Uttarakhand Tourism Development Board. **Step 2:** The Department scrutinizes the submitted applications and forwards them to the Technical Committee for further evaluation. **Step 3:** Following the inspection and review by the Technical Committee, the Uttarakhand Tourism Development Board communicates the decision regarding the grant of permission to all applicants along with reasons where applicable within a maximum of 45 days. > **Note:** Applications for river rafting/kayaking activities in the Ganga River can be submitted from 1st July to 31st July each year. For other rivers, applications are accepted throughout the year.
Mode: Offline.

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: Uttarakhand Tourism Development Board; Nodal Department: Tourism Department.
