# Subsidy on Rehri alongwith (Stove, Gas Chulha, Utensil etc.) for the Fish/Fish Product Sale

**Short Title:** REHRIHR

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Fish, Fish Products, Subsidy, Handcart, Scheduled Caste, Fish Farmer

The scheme “Subsidy on Rehri along with Stove, Gas Chulha, Utensils, etc. for Sale of Fish/Fish Products” is implemented by the Fisheries Department, Government of Haryana. Under this scheme, financial assistance is provided to Scheduled Caste fish farmers for the purchase of a Rehri (handcart) equipped with stove, gas chulha, utensils, and other essentials for selling fish and fish products. A 60% subsidy is provided on the total project cost of ₹60,000/- per beneficiary.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment

**Where it applies:** Haryana; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - Under this scheme, financial assistance will be provided for the purchase of a Rehri (Handcart) along with essential items such as stove, gas chulha, utensils, etc., for selling fish or fish products. - A subsidy of 60% on the total project cost of ₹60,000/- will be granted, amounting to ₹36,000/- per beneficiary.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Exclusions / Not eligible:** 1. The applicant should not be a Government/Semi-Government employee.
