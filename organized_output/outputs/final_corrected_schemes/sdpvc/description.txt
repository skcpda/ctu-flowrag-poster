# Sanctioning of Daisy Players to Visually Challenged

**Short Title:** SDPVC

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Daisy Players, Person With Disabilities, Student

The Department for the Welfare of Differently Abled &amp; Senior Citizens launched the &quot;Sanctioning of Daisy Players to Visually Challenged&quot; scheme to distribute free daisy players to visually challenged students pursuing 9th Class to Degree as per the Action Plan 2017-18. The Assistant Directors and District Managers are instructed to follow these guidelines to ensure proper distribution. The application is accepted online for this scheme.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Aids/Appliances

**Where it applies:** Andhra Pradesh; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** Eligible beneficiaries of this scheme will be provided with a DAISY Player to facilitate accessible learning and information access. 

**Benefit Type:** In Kind

**How to apply:** **Step 01:** Visit the official website: [https://apdascac.ap.gov.in](https://apdascac.ap.gov.in) **Step 02:** On the homepage, click on &quot;[APDASCAC Schemes](https://apdascac.ap.gov.in/APDASCAC-Schemes/)&quot;. **Step 03:** Select the &quot;Sanction of DAISY Players&quot; option. **Step 04:** Click on &quot;[Apply Now](https://apdascac.ap.gov.in/Schemes/daisy-players/)&quot; to begin the application process. **Step 05:** Fill in the required details and submit your application. 
Mode: Online.

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Dept of Social Welfare, WCD.
