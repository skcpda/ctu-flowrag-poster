# Providing Latest Technology to Group of Pottery Artisans (Energy Efficient Kiln)

**Short Title:** PLTGPA-EEK

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Technology, Artisan, Pottery, Energy, Equipment, Kiln

The scheme &quot;Providing Latest Technology to Group of Pottery Artisans (Energy Efficient Kiln)&quot; by the Gujarat Matikam Kalakari &amp; Rural Technology Institute, Industries &amp; Mines Department, Government of Gujarat, aims to provide latest technology and equipment to pottery artisans for better productivity, improved quality and reduce health risks. The scheme provides 100% financial assistance on development of the kiln with unit cost of ₹83,000/- and energy-efficient furnaces to groups of registered clay artisans and potters. The applicant must be directly involved in the pottery business and be in the age group of 18 to 58 years, with at least 800 to 1000 square feet of open space available for pottery business. The scheme is implemented by the relevant Gujarat State Government Department and applications are accepted through prescribed application forms with required details and certificates. 

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Business & Entrepreneurship; Sector: Setting up / start-up / entrepreneurship

**Where it applies:** Gujarat; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** **Financial Assistance:** 100% financial assistance is provided on development of the kiln with unit cost of ₹83,000/-. **Technology Provision:** Energy-efficient furnaces are provided to improve productivity and quality. **Health Benefits:** Reduced health risks for pottery artisans through improved technology.  > **Mode and Frequency of Disbursement** - The assistance will be given only in the form of an energy saving furnace. - No assistance related to the kiln will be paid in cash.  > **Conditions for Availment/Continuation** - The energy-saving furnace must be used only for pottery business. - Beneficiaries must send a report about the utility of the furnace to the organization every 3 months after the furnace is ready. - Cost of maintenance and repair of the furnace must be borne by the beneficiaries at their own expense after construction and installation. - Misuse of the furnace will result in legal action by the institute. 

**Benefit Type:** Cash

If any information is incorrect, click &quot;Cancel&quot;, make the necessary corrections, and then click &quot;Register&quot; again. **Step 4:** Upon successful registration, the User ID and Password will be sent to your registered mobile number via SMS. Then click &quot;Login&quot;. **Step 2:** Upon first login, the user must complete the &quot;User Profile&quot; by providing personal and organisational details.
