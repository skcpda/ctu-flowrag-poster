# Compensation Amount Towards Cattle Injury Caused by Forest Animals

**Short Title:** CATCICBFA

**Launch Date:** 2014-02-06

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Animal, Forest, Financial Assistance, Death, Compensation, Injury, Cattle

Launched on 6th February 2024, the scheme &quot;Compensation Amount towards Cattle Injury Caused by Forest Animals&quot; by the Forest Department, Government of Madhya Pradesh, provides compensation in case of death of cattle due to attacks by wild animals. The cattle owner will receive a relief amount. The nearest forest officer must be informed in writing or verbally within 48 hours of the incident, and the dead cattle should not be removed from the place of death.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Agriculture,Rural & Environment; Sector: Animal husbandry

**Where it applies:** Madhya Pradesh; State/ UT

**DBT Scheme:** Yes (Direct Benefit Transfer)

**Benefits / Assistance:** - ₹1,000/- as immediate assistance for wild animal injury. - ₹30,000/- for medical expenses (normal injury). - ₹1,00,000/- for permanent disability and full treatment cost.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**How to apply:** The [application ](https://mpedistrict.gov.in/MPL/getphoto.aspx?ViewCircular=r5lUDQ5ydUg=)should be submitted to the nearest forest officer within 48 hours of the incident, and the dead cattle should not be removed from the place of death. **First Appeal** - Can be filed with the concerned Divisional Forest Officer, Deputy Director, or Assistant Director of the protected area. - Must be submitted within 30 days from the date of non-acceptance of the application or after the stipulated time limit expires. - The appellate officer will resolve the appeal within 30 working days. **Second Appeal** - Can be filed with the Director of Forest Conservation/Protected Area. - Must be submitted within 60 days from the order of the first appellate officer.
Mode: Offline.

Enter the code and complete the registration. **Application** **Step 1:** Visit the Official Website of &quot;Madhya Pradesh Lok Sewa Guarantee Portal&quot;. **Step 2:** Enter your User ID, Password, and Captcha Code, and click &quot;Login.&quot; **Step 3:** Click on &quot;Apply Online Service&quot; and select the scheme you intend to apply for. **Step 4:** In the scheme&#39;s online application form, fill in all the mandatory fields and upload all the mandatory documents in the specified format and size (fields marked with * are mandatory), and click &quot;Submit&quot;.
