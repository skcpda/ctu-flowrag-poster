# Transgender Utthan Kosh - Pre Matric Scholarship for Studying Transgender

**Short Title:** TUKPMSST

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Scholarship, Transgender, Pre-Matric, Student

The &amp;quot;Transgender Utthan Kosh - Pre Matric Scholarship for Studying Transgender&amp;quot; scheme was introduced by the Social Justice and Empowerment Department, Government of Rajasthan. It aims to support transgender students by providing scholarships to encourage their education and social upliftment. Under this scheme, transgender students receive a monthly scholarship to cover educational expenses from primary to pre-matric levels.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Education & Learning; Sector: Scholarships and student finance

**Where it applies:** Rajasthan; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** ***Pre-Matric Scholarship:*** - ₹225/- per month per student for 10 months. **NOTE:** The scholarship amount is directly transferred to the beneficiary&#39;s bank account.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**How to apply:** ***Application Submission:*** - The applicant/parents/guardians must fill out the scholarship application form and attach the required documents. - Submit the complete application form to their school principal. ***Institutional Verification:*** - The school principal verifies the application and forwards it to the Social Justice and Empowerment Department&#39;s district office. ***Approval Process:*** - The district office verifies the documents and approves/rejects the application within one month. ***Disbursement:*** - Upon approval, the scholarship amount is transferred to the applicant&#39;s bank account.
Mode: Offline.

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Social Justice and Empowerment Department.

**References / Annexures:** - **Guidelines**: https://sje.rajasthan.gov.in/siteadmin/Uploads/202110051259226370.pdf
