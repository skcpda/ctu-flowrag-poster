# Old Age Pension Scheme - Uttarakhand

**Short Title:** OAPS

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Old Age, Pension, Senior Citizen, BPL

The &amp;amp;quot;Old Age Pension Scheme&amp;amp;quot; was launched by the Department of Social Welfare, Government of Uttarakhand. The scheme aims to help elderly persons meet their basic needs and improve their quality of life. Under this scheme, a monthly pension is provided to senior citizens (both husband and wife) in the state who are 60 years of age or above. 

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Pension

**Where it applies:** Uttarakhand; State

**Benefits / Assistance:** A pension of ₹1,500/- per month is provided to eligible beneficiaries. 

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**How to apply:** The applicant can apply online either by themselves or through a Common Service Center (CSC) via the Department’s Pension Portal ([https://ssp.uk.gov.in](https://ssp.uk.gov.in)), the [UMANG](https://web.umang.gov.in) Mobile App, or the Apuni Sarkar Portal ([https://eservices.uk.gov.in](https://eservices.uk.gov.in)). **The following documents are mandatory at the time of applying:** Applicant’s mobile number, Passport-size photograph (attested by Gram Pradhan / V.P.D.O. / certified by Councillor), Photocopy of Aadhaar card and Voter ID card, CBS bank account details (linked/seeded with Aadhaar), Valid family income certificate or BPL certificate, Copy of family register (for rural areas only), Copy of the resolution passed in the open meeting of Gram Panchayat (for rural areas) / certified by Councillor (for urban areas) **All the above documents must be issued from the district where the application is being submitted.** &gt; For verification of the online application form in rural areas, the final decision is taken by the District Social Welfare Officer after recommendations from the Gram Panchayat Development Officer, Assistant Social Welfare Officer, and Block Development Officer, respectively. In urban areas, verification and recommendation are done by the Assistant Social Welfare Officer and Sub-District Magistrate. If the application is found correct, the District Social Welfare Officer approves it, after which the pension payment process is initiated. **The pension is credited to the applicant’s bank account one month after the approval of the application.** 
Mode: Online.

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Department Of Social Welfare.

**Additional Information:**
Briefdescription: Under this scheme, a monthly pension of ₹1,500/- is provided to senior citizens (both husband and wife) in the state who are 60 years of age or above and whose family’s monthly income is up to ₹4,000/- or who belong to the BPL category.
