# Scheme for Campaign, Seminar and Sports under Disability Sector

**Short Title:** SFCSSUDS

**Scheme For:** Infra

**Tags:** Person With Disabilities, PWD, Campaign, Seminar, Sports, Organizations

The &amp;quot;Scheme for Campaign, Seminar and Sports under Disability Sector&amp;quot; is a social welfare scheme, implemented by the Social Security and Empowerment of Persons with Disabilities (SSEPD) Department, Government of Odisha. This scheme promotes sports, cultural activities, and various events to highlight the rights and needs of PwDs, encouraging volunteer action and ensuring effective implementation of legal provisions and welfare schemes through extending financial support to the reputed organizations or an individual person with disabilities working for welfare of Persons with Disabilities. &lt;br&gt;

**Who is this for:** Individual, NGO; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Financial assistance

**Where it applies:** Odisha; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - **State-Level Events:** Financial assistance limited to ₹1,00,000/- per event. - **District-Level Events:** Financial assistance limited to ₹50,000/- per event. 

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**How to apply:** **Step 1:** Submit a proposal to the concerned District Social Security Officer (DSSO) at least one month before the event. **Step 2:** The proposal should include details of the event and the financial assistance required. **Step 3:** Ensure you receive a submission receipt for future reference (if provided). &lt;br&gt; &gt; **Contact Us** &gt; **Address: SSEPD Department, Red Building, Lokseva Bhawan, Bhubaneswar - 751001** &gt; **E-mail: ssepdsec.od@nic.in** &gt; **Tel No: 0674- 2392803** &gt; **List of District Social Security Officers**
Mode: Offline.

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Social Security and Empowerment of Persons with Disabilities Department.

**Additional Information:**
Briefdescription: The scheme aims to extend financial support for National & International Events organized by reputed organizations or an individual person with disabilities working for welfare of Persons with Disabilities.
