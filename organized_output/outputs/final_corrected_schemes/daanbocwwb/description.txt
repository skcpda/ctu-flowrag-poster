# Disability Assistance (ANBOCWWB)

**Short Title:** DA(ANBOCWWB)

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Disability, PwD, Construction Worker, Building Worker, Labour, Financial Assistance

The scheme “Disability Assistance” was started by the Andaman &amp;amp; Nicobar Islands Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board (ANBOCWWB), Department of Labour, Employment &amp;amp; Training, Andaman &amp;amp; Nicobar Administration. Under this scheme, the Secretary of the Board may sanction an amount not exceeding 25% of the total amount of compensation due, as calculated under the Employees Compensation Act 1923 on the production of a certificate from competent medical authorities in respect of the percentage of disability along with application for grant of assistance along with last pay slip &amp;amp; age proof. The ex-facto approval from the Board shall be obtained for the same.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Crisis/Disaster/Accident

**Where it applies:** Andaman and Nicobar Islands; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** 1. Under this scheme, the Board may sanction an amount not exceeding 25% of the total amount of compensation due, as calculated under the Employees Compensation Act 1923 on the production of a certificate from competent medical authorities in respect of the percentage of disability.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Application Process for Disability Financial Assistance:** **Step 01:** Now, to avail the benefits of the scheme, the applicants can [login](https://serviceonline.gov.in/loginWindow.do) through their email ID that is used while registering **Step 02:** Enter Password &amp;amp; **Additional Information:**
Briefdescription: The scheme provides financial assistance to registered construction workers who become disabled due to an accident during the course of their employment on the production of a certificate from competent medical authorities in respect of the percentage of disability.
