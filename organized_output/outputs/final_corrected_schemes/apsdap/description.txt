# Assistance For Purchase Of Spectacles By A Differently Abled Person

**Short Title:** Apsdap

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Differently Abled Persons, Spectacles, Tamil Nadu

The &quot;Assistance for Purchase of Spectacles by A Differently Abled Persons&quot; scheme in Tamil Nadu is a government initiative that aims to provide financial assistance to differently-abled individuals. A sum of Rs.500/- (Rupees Five hundred only) is paid as assistance towards the reimbursement of the cost of spectacles for differently-abled persons.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Financial assistance

**Where it applies:** Tamil Nadu; State

**Benefits / Assistance:** A sum of Rs.500/- (Rupees Five hundred only) is paid as an assistance towards the reimbursement of the cost of spectacles for differently-abled persons.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**How to apply:** Step 1: Visit the official website of the[ Chennai district](https://chennai.nic.in/ta/differently-abled-welfare-schemes/). Step 2: Click on the Departments. Select Differently Abled Welfare, after selecting click on Differently Abled Welfare Schemes. Step 3: You will land on the [List of Scheme page](https://chennai.nic.in/ta/differently-abled-welfare-schemes/) of Differently Abled Welfare Schemes. Step 4: Scroll down to the section &quot;L. Tamilnadu differently abled welfare board schemes&quot;. Step 5: Download the [application form](https://cdn.s3waas.gov.in/s313f3cf8c531952d72e5847c4183e6910/uploads/2021/06/2021060112.pdf) of the &quot;[S.No. 7 Reimbursement for purchase of spectacles for visually impaired persons Rs. 500](https://cdn.s3waas.gov.in/s313f3cf8c531952d72e5847c4183e6910/uploads/2021/06/2021060141-1.pdf)&quot;.  Note: The application is to be submitted District Differently Abled Welfare Officer.
Mode: Offline.

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Welfare of Differently Abled Persons Department.
