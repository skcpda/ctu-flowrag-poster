# Assistance Program for Planting in Floriculture

**Short Title:** APFPF

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Floriculture, Flower Farming, Subsidy

The scheme &amp;quot;Assistance Program for Planting in Floriculture&amp;quot; introduced by the Agriculture, Farmers Welfare and Cooperation Department, Gujarat, aims to encourage floriculture in Gujarat by offering financial support to farmers cultivating various types of flowers. The assistance amount varies depending on the type of flower and whether the farmer is classified as a small/marginal or other category.

**Who is this for:** Individual, Business Entity, Industries; Category: Agriculture,Rural & Environment; Sector: Financial assistance

**Where it applies:** Gujarat; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** > **Loose Flowers:** - ₹16,000 per hectare or 40% of cost for small/ marginal farmers. - ₹10,000 per hectare or 25% of cost for other farmers.  > **Stem (Cut) Flowers:** - ₹40,000 per hectare or 40% of cost for small/ marginal farmers. - ₹25,000 per hectare or 25% of cost for other farmers.  > **Tuberous Flowers:** - ₹60,000/- per hectare or 40% of cost for small/ marginal farmers. - ₹37,500/- per hectare or 25% of cost for other farmers.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**Additional Information:**
Briefdescription: The scheme offers financial assistance to farmers in Gujarat for the cultivation of floriculture crops, including loose, cut, and tuberous flowers, based on crop type and farmer category.
