#  Safai Karmakaar Ke Putr/Putri Hetu Vishesh Coaching Yojana

**Short Title:** SKHVC

**Launch Date:** 2015-03-15

**Scheme For:** Family

**Tags:** Education, Coaching

Labor Department of Chhattisgarh State started this scheme &amp;amp;quot;Safai Karmakaar Ke Putr/Putri Hetu Vishesh Coaching Yojana&amp;amp;quot; for unorganized worker on 11 March 2015. The main objective of the scheme to provide financial assistance for free coaching.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Education & Learning; Sector: Coaching

**Where it applies:** Chhattisgarh; State

**Benefits / Assistance:** > The fee for special coaching for admission in engineering, medical, CA and MBA or other professional education to the son/daughter (two children) studying in class 10th and above will be paid by the board.

**Benefit Type:** In Kind

**How to apply:** **Unregistered Applicant :** 1. All the applicant have to visit the official [website](https://cglabour.nic.in) Chhattisgarh Labour Department. 1. On the home page click on &quot;[Apply](https://cglabour.nic.in/Departments.aspx?id=4)&quot; under &quot;Chhattisgarh Unorganized Workers State Social Security Board&quot;.1. Enter the following details 1. Select Group Name : &quot;Asangathit Marmakaar Mandal&quot; 1. Select Service : &quot;Asangathit Shramik Panjikaran&quot; 1. What do you want to do : &quot;Aavedan&quot; 1. Click next. 1. Check the eligibility criteria for registration in Chhattisgarh Unorganized Workers State Social Security Board and click next. 1. Fill out the online &quot;Registration form&quot;. 1. Submit.  **Already Registered Applicant :** 1.
