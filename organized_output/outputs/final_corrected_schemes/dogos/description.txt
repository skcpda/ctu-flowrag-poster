# Distribution of Gypsum-Oil Seeds

**Short Title:** DOGOS

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Agriculture, Farmer, Seed Distribution, Gypsum, Oil Seeds, Subsidy

The “Distribution of Gypsum-Oil Seeds” scheme by the Agriculture-Farmers Welfare Department, Tamil Nadu, provides financial assistance to farmers by subsidizing 50% of the cost of gypsum material and transportation costs. The subsidy is available to farmers who produce and supply Foundation and Certified Class seeds to the Department of Agriculture.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Agriculture,Rural & Environment; Sector: Agricultural Inputs- seeds, fertilizer etc.

**Where it applies:** Tamil Nadu; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** **Financial Assistance:** 50% of the cost of material plus transport limited to ₹750/- per ha whichever is less.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**How to apply:** **Step 1:** Collect the application form from the nearest Agricultural Office. **Step 2:** Fill out the application form with the required details and attach mandatory documents (self-attested, if required). **Step 3:** Submit the form to the concerned office: &gt; **Village Level:** Submit the application to the Assistant Agricultural Officer. &gt; **Block Level:** Submit to the Agricultural Officer or Deputy Agricultural Officer. &gt; **District Level:** Submit to the Joint Director of Agriculture. &lt;br&gt;
Mode: Offline.

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Agriculture Department.

**References / Annexures:** - **Guidelines**: https://www.tn.gov.in/scheme/data_view/7618
