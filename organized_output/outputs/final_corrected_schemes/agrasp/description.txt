# AGR-61 Agro Service Providers for the Purpose of Making Farming More Profitable With the Help of Farm Mechanization

**Short Title:** AGRASP

**Scheme For:** Infra

**Tags:** Agriculture, Agro Service Providers, Farm Mechanization

The scheme &amp;quot;AGR-61 Agro Service Providers for the Purpose of Making Farming More Profitable With the Help of Farm Mechanization&amp;quot; introduced by the Agriculture, Farmers Welfare and Cooperation Department, Gujarat, aims to help farmers by providing financial support for farm mechanization through Agro Service Providers, making agriculture more productive and cost-effective.

**Who is this for:** Individual, Joint Liability Groups (JLGS), Self Help Groups (SHGS), Registered Societies; Category: Agriculture,Rural & Environment; Sector: Mechanization- solar power, farming systems

**Where it applies:** Gujarat; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:**  **Name of the Component****Standard of Assistance (General areas)****Standard of Assistance (Tribal areas)**Agro Service Provider UnitUp to 75% or limited to ₹7,50,000/- (40% in the 1st year, 10% in the 2nd and 3rd years, and 7.5% in the 4th and 5th years)Up to 85% or limited to ₹8,50,000/- (50% in the 1st year, 10% in the 2nd and 3rd years, and 7.5% in the 4th and 5th years)> **Note:** - Assistance is available once in a lifetime for eligible applicants.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**How to apply:** **Step 1:** The applicant may visit and apply online at I-Khedut Portal: [https://ikhedut.gujarat.gov.in/](https://ikhedut.gujarat.gov.in/) **Step 2:** On the home page, click on ‘Schemes’ and then click on ‘Agricultural Schemes’. **Step 3:** Now select the scheme, click on the ‘Apply’ button and an application page will open. **Step 4:** Click on &amp;quot;Apply New&amp;quot; button and submit a new application. **Step 5:** Click on the &amp;quot;Update Application&amp;quot; button to add corrections to the application. **Step 6:** Once the application is done, confirm it. **Step 7:** Take a print out of the confirmed application. **Step 8:** Applicant can also check their Application Status/View/Print/Upload Application details from the below link: [https://ikhedut.gujarat.gov.in/public/frm_Applicant_Corner.aspx](https://ikhedut.gujarat.gov.in/public/frm_Applicant_Corner.aspx) &gt; **Contact Us:** [Click here](https://ikhedut.gujarat.gov.in/Public/frm_Public_Contacts.aspx).
