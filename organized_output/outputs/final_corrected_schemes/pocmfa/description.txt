# Pandit Omkarnath Classical Music Festival and Awards

**Short Title:** POCMFA

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Classical Music Recognition, Indian Music Awards, Cultural Award

The scheme &amp;quot;Pandit Omkarnath Classical Music Festival and Awards&amp;quot; by the Sports, Youth and Cultural Activities Department, Government of Gujarat, aims to preserve, promote, and celebrate the Indian classical music heritage by recognizing and awarding distinguished male artists who have significantly contributed to this field. The award is conferred annually during the Pt. Omkarnath Shastri Sangeet Mohotsav, where artists are selected by an expert committee based on their lifelong service, adherence to the guru-shishya tradition, and national or international recognition in classical music. 

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Sports & Culture; Sector: Culture

**Where it applies:** Gujarat; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - **Financial Assistance and Recognition:** Eligible male artists receive a prestigious award that acknowledges their lifelong dedication to Indian classical music.  

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**How to apply:** **Step 1:** The interested applicant should request a hard copy of the prescribed format of the application form Sangeet Natak Academy. **Step 2:** In the application form, provide all the mandatory details, and attach copies of all the mandatory documents (self-attested, if required). **Step 3:** Submit the duly filled and signed application form along with the supporting documents to the Sangeet Natak Academy. **Step 4:** Request a receipt or acknowledgement from the concerned authority to whom the application has been submitted. 
Mode: Online.

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Sports, Youth and Cultural Activities Department.

**References / Annexures:** - **Guidelines (Scheme No. 410)**: https://mariyojana.gujarat.gov.in/Schemeatoz.aspx
