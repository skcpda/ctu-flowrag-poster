# Assistance to Scheduled Castes for Fisheries: Boat Net Gujarat

**Short Title:** ASCFBNGUJ

**Scheme For:** Infra

**Tags:** Fish Production, Fish Farming, Fishermen, Fish Farmer, Boat Net

The scheme &quot;Assistance to Scheduled Castes for Fisheries&quot;, with the component of Boat Net, introduced by the Agriculture, Farmers Welfare and Co-operation Department, Government of Gujarat, aims to increase fish production by rearing fish in reservoirs and providing employment to Scheduled Caste (SC) individuals involved in inland fisheries. Through this scheme, assistance is provided for boat nets, which are provided to Scheduled Caste fishermen and fish farmers. The scheme is implemented by the Commissioner of Fisheries, Government of Gujarat. **Objectives:** - To increase fish production by rearing fish in reservoirs and provide employment to scheduled caste (SC) people involved in inland fisheries activities. - To boost fish farming or fishing activities in freshwater bodies by scheduled caste (SC) fish farmers and fishermen. - Fishing in reservoirs, aquaculture in ponds, and catch from rivers increase fish production and boost inland fisheries through scheduled caste (SC) fish farmers and fishermen. - The main objective is to provide employment opportunities to Scheduled Caste fishermen in the state and increase fish production. 

**Who is this for:** Business Entity, Individual, Self Help Groups (SHGS), Registered Societies; Category: Agriculture,Rural & Environment; Sector: Fishing and hunting

**Where it applies:** Gujarat; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** 1. **Tin Boat-Net Unit:** 50% of unit cost or ₹7,500/- (Boat: ₹5,000/-; Net: ₹2,500/-), whichever is less.1. **F.R.P. (Fibre-Reinforced Plastic) Boat-Net Unit:** 50% of unit cost or ₹15,000/- (Boat: ₹12,500/-; Net: ₹2,500/-), whichever is less. > **Conditions/Aspects/Criteria Associated:****** Benefit amount is calculated based on 50% of the unit cost or the fixed maximum amount mentioned above, whichever is less. > **Minimum Time Limit (in Years) for Reclaiming Benefits:** 6 Years 

**Benefit Type:** Cash
