# Self-Employment Scheme: Transport/ Logistics/ Travels/ Food Court Wyaaj Sahaay

**Short Title:** SESTLTF

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Business, Entrepreneurship, Self Employment, Transport, Logistics, Food Courts

&amp;quot;Transport/ Logistics/ Travels/ Food Court Wyaaj Sahaay&amp;quot; is a component under the &amp;quot;Self-Employment Scheme&amp;quot; introduced by the Social Justice and Empowerment Department, Gujarat. Under this scheme, eligible beneficiaries can avail of a 5% interest subsidy on a bank loan of ₹6.00 lakh for purchasing vehicles and setting up the required infrastructure for businesses like transport, logistics, travelers, food courts, etc.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Banking,Financial Services and Insurance; Sector: Loan

**Where it applies:** Gujarat; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - **Interest Subsidy:** A 5% subsidy on the interest for a bank loan of ₹6,00,000/-. 

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**How to apply:** > **Registration Process:** **Step 1:** Go to the Corporation&#39;s [official website](https://esamajkalyan.gujarat.gov.in/) and navigate to the &quot;Gujarat Unreserved Education and Economical Development Corporation&quot; section under the Corporation menu. **Step 2:**Click on &quot;Transport/ Logistics/ Travels/ Food Court Wyaaj Sahaay&quot; under &quot;Self-Employment Scheme&quot; and then Apply Now. **Step 3:** Click on &quot;New User? Please Register Here&quot;. **Step 4:** Enter the required details and click Register.  > **Application Process:** **Step 1:** Enter your User ID, Password, and Captcha Code, then click &quot;Login&quot;. **Step 2:** Read the scheme details carefully and click OK. **Step 3:** If your permanent address is correct, confirm it. Otherwise, enter your correct address. **Step 4:** Click Save &amp; Next to proceed. Upload the relevant documents. **Step 5:** If you need to make changes, click &quot;No, Cancel&quot;. If all details are correct, click &quot;Yes, Submit&quot;. **Step 6:** Click Save Application. Note down your Application Number for future reference. You can also take a printout by clicking Print Application. > **Check Application Status:** [Click here](https://esamajkalyan.gujarat.gov.in/ViewApplicationStatus.aspx). > **Post Online Application Procedure:** After submitting the online application, the applicant must take a printout of the application, sign it, and submit it along with all uploaded documents (certificates, mark sheets, etc.) to the concerned Deputy Director (Non-Reserved Category) or the District Social Welfare Officer through courier, post, or by visiting in person. > **Contact Us:** [Click here](https://gueedc.gujarat.gov.in/contact-us.html). 
Mode: Online.
