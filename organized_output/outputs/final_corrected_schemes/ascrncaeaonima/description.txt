# Award of Scholarship to the Children who are Recipients of National Child Award for Exceptional Achievements and Other National / International Merit Awards

**Short Title:** ASCRNCAEAONIMA

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Scholarship, Award, Merit, School, Student

The scheme &quot;Award of Scholarship to the Children who are Recipients of National Child Award for Exceptional Achievements and Other National / International Merit Awards&quot; is a Scholarship Scheme by the Directorate of School Education, Union Territory of Puducherry. The objectives of this scheme are to recognize the talents of children who are recipients of the National Child Award for Exceptional Achievements and other National / International Merit Awards and to recognize the talents of other children who are pursuing outstanding achievements in different fields of Human Resource Development.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Education & Learning; Sector: Scholarships and student finance

**Where it applies:** Puducherry; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** > **Scholarship** ₹6,000/- per annum per student. > **Mode of Disbursal** The amount is disbursed directly into the Aadhaar-seeded viable bank account by Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) through the Electronic Clearance System (ECS).

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**How to apply:** **Step 1:** The interested applicant should visit the Head of the Institution (HoI) of his/her respective educational institution, and request the hard copy of the prescribed format of the application form for the concerned scheme. **Step 2:** In the application form, fill in all the mandatory fields, paste the passport-sized photograph (signed across, if required), and attach copies of all the mandatory documents (self-attest, if required). **Step 3:** The interested applicant should submit the duly filled and signed application form and the documents to the HoI. The HoI then sends the applications to the Directorate of School Education, Puducherry.
Mode: Offline.

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Labour Department, Puducherry.
