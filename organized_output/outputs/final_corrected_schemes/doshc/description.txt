# Distribution of Soil Health Card

**Short Title:** DOSHC

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Agriculture, Farmer, Soil Health Card

Under the “Distribution of Soil Health Card” scheme, by the Agriculture-Farmers Welfare Department, Tamil Nadu, Soil health cards will be distributed to all the farmers who have offered the soil sample for testing by paying a prescribed fee of ₹5/- each for Macro and Micronutrient testing. &lt;br&gt;

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Agriculture,Rural & Environment; Sector: Soil health

**Where it applies:** Tamil Nadu; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - Soil Health Cards to farmers. 

**Benefit Type:** In Kind

**How to apply:** **Step 1:** Collect the application form from the nearest Agricultural Office. **Step 2:** Fill out the application form with the required details and attach mandatory documents (self-attested, if required). **Step 3:** Submit the form to the concerned office: &gt; **Village Level:** Submit the application to the Assistant Agricultural Officer. &gt; **Block Level:** Submit to the Agricultural Officer or Deputy Agricultural Officer. &gt; **District Level:** Submit to the Joint Director of Agriculture.
Mode: Offline.

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Agriculture Department.

**References / Annexures:** - **Guidelines**: https://www.tn.gov.in/scheme/data_view/6849
