# PMMSY: Inputs for Saline /Alkaline Water Aquaculture - Haryana

**Short Title:** IFSAWA

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Fish Farmers, Aquaculture, Entrepreneurs, Initial Crop

The Pradhan Mantri Matsya Sampada Yojana: Inputs for Saline /Alkaline Water Aquaculture, introduced by the Ministry Of Fisheries, Animal Husbandry &amp; Dairying, Government of India, aims to provide input cost subsidy for saline/alkaline water aquaculture in newly created water bodies. This centrally sponsored scheme is jointly funded 50% by the Central Government and 50% by the State Government. It supports input costs for the initial crop in new ponds/tanks, encouraging fish and shrimp culture in Haryana. 

**Who is this for:** Business Entity, Individual; Category: Agriculture,Rural & Environment; Sector: Fishing and hunting

**Where it applies:** Haryana; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - The project cost is ₹6,00,000/- per hectare. > **Subsidy Distribution:** 1. 40% of the project cost for General Category beneficiaries 1. 60% of the project cost for SC/Women beneficiaries

**Benefit Type:** Composite
