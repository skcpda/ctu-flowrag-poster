# State Rice Mission Scheme

**Short Title:** SRMSMEGHALAYA

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Rice, Farmer, Agriculture, Subsidy, Training

The “State Rice Mission Scheme” is a State Plan Scheme, which is implemented by the Department of Agriculture &amp;amp; Farmers’ Welfare, Government of Meghalaya. The objective of the scheme is to increase productivity and production of rice through improved technologies and extension methods and, to supply the quality/certified seeds/organic fertilizers at 50% subsidy.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Agriculture,Rural & Environment; Sector: Agricultural Inputs- seeds, fertilizer etc.

**Where it applies:** Meghalaya; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** > **Pattern of Assistance:** - Training, Awareness program, extension, Technology support, Subsidy on inputs. > **Type of Assistance and Entitlement (If any):** 1. 50 % Subsidy for quality paddy seeds to farmers. 1. Minor irrigation (Check dam, shallow tube well, etc.) 1. 50 % sale of Power Tillers SRI Tools for demonstration. 1. Trainings

**Benefit Type:** Composite

**How to apply:** &gt; **Application Process:** **Step 01:** The eligible bonafide farmer can apply/write an application on plain paper. **Step 02:** Along with the application, attach copies of all the mandatory documents (self-attest, if required). **Step 03:** Submit the duly filled and signed application along with the relevant documents at the nearest office of the Agriculture Development Officer of a CD Block/ Agriculture Circle. &gt; **Mode of Selection of Beneficiary(s):** The bonafide farmers having sufficient available cultivable land shall be selected on a first come first served basis. &gt; **Whom to Contact:** 1. District Agriculture Officers of the concerned districts. 1. Sub-divisional Officers of the concerned sub-divisions. 1. District Training Officers, Tura/Shillong. 1. Agriculture Development Officers of the concerned Community and Rural Development Blocks.
Mode: Offline.

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: Department Of Agriculture & Farmers’ Welfare, Government Of Meghalaya; Nodal Department: Agriculture Department.
