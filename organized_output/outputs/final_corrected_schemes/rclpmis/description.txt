# Rehabilitation of Cured Leprosy Persons: Marriage Incentives & Support

**Short Title:** RCLPMIS

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Leprosy Persons, Rehabilitation, Marriage, Daughter, Girl, Women

The scheme &amp;quot;Marriage Incentives &amp;amp; Support&amp;quot; is a component of the “Rehabilitation of Cured Leprosy Persons” scheme, implemented by the Social Security and Empowerment of Persons with Disabilities (SSEPD) Department, Government of Odisha. The scheme aims to provide financial assistance to the Cured Leprosy Persons who face social and economic challenges in arranging marriages for their daughters.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Marriage

**Where it applies:** Odisha; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** **Financial Support:** A one-time grant of up to ₹50,000/- for the marriage of daughters of Cured Leprosy Persons. 

**Benefit Type:** Cash
