# RIMC Scholarship (Military Scholarship)

**Short Title:** RIMCSMS

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Scholarship, Student, Rashtriya Indian Military College, Military

The “RIMC Scholarship (Military Scholarship)” scheme is implemented by the Secondary Education Department under the Department of Education, Government of Uttarakhand. The scheme aims to provide financial assistance to students from Uttarakhand who are pursuing their education at the Rashtriya Indian Military College (RIMC). 

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Education & Learning; Sector: Scholarships and student finance

**Where it applies:** Uttarakhand; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** The scholarship is payable to students at the rate of ₹1,000/- per month. 

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**How to apply:** - After securing admission in RIMC, the student submits a request letter for the scholarship, along with their Aadhaar card, permanent residence certificate, and Aadhaar-linked bank account certificate, to the college’s administrative office. - RIMC then forwards the proposals of all eligible students from Uttarakhand to the Secondary Education Department. Based on the proposals received, the department provides funds to the college, which in turn disburses ₹1,000/- per month to the students for the duration of their studies. 
Mode: Offline.

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: Secondary Education Department, Government Of Uttarakhand; Nodal Department: Department Of Education.
