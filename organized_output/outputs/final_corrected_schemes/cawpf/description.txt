# Cash Award to Women Players of the State

**Short Title:** CAWPF

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Sports, Women Athletes, Cash Prize

The scheme &amp;quot;Cash Award to Women Players of the State&amp;quot; by the Sports, Youth and Cultural Activities Department, Government of Gujarat, aims to promote participation of women in sports and recognize their achievements by awarding them cash incentives for outstanding performance in state-level and national-level competitions. Female athletes securing the first, second, or third position in individual or team sports at the state level, or representing Gujarat at national competitions, receive annual scholarships based on their rank and category. The scheme is implemented by the Sports Authority of Gujarat. 

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Sports & Culture; Sector: Sports

**Where it applies:** Gujarat; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - **First Position (Individual &amp;amp; Team Sports at State Level/ National Representation):** ₹4,800/- per year - **Second Position (Individual &amp;amp; Team Sports at State Level):** ₹3,600/- per year - **Third Position (Individual Sports at State Level):** ₹2,400/- per year 

**Benefit Type:** Cash
