# Strengthening Skills, Arts and Employment through ITIs

**Short Title:** SKAAETI

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Student, Employment, Nomadic, Training, Skill, Arts

The &quot;Strengthening Skills, Arts, and Employment through ITIs&quot; initiative by the Department of De-notified, Nomadic, and Semi-Nomadic Welfare, Government of Madhya Pradesh, aims to bridge traditional knowledge and art with modern technology. Communities under this department have historically relied on traditional knowledge and art for their livelihood. To connect these skills with modern technology, short-term, employment-oriented training has been provided through ITIs. The target for the financial year 2022-23 is to train 180 students, with a goal of benefiting 250 students in the coming year. After completing the training, there is a provision for employment or self-employment under the self-employment scheme.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Skills & Employment; Sector: Training and Skill Up-gradation

**Where it applies:** Madhya Pradesh; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** The scheme provides employment-oriented training through ITIs, aiming to enhance traditional skills and offer employment or self-employment opportunities.

**Benefit Type:** In Kind
