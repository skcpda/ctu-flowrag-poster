# Acharya Vidyasagar Gau Samvardhan Yojana

**Short Title:** AVGSY

**Scheme For:** Infra

**Tags:** Animal Husbandary, Dairy, Milk Production, Cow, Veterinary

This scheme is for all categories of beneficiaries. The beneficiary must have a minimum of 1 acre of agricultural land for 5 animals and with the increase in the number of animals, the minimum agricultural land will be determined by increasing it proportionately. Priority to implementation of milk route.  Plan Unit Cost - The animal owner can get the plan approved for minimum 5 or more animals and the maximum limit amount of the project is Rs. 10.00 lakhs. - 75 percent of the project cost will have to be received through bank loan and the balance amount will have to be arranged in the form of margin money assistance and the beneficiary&#39;s own contribution. - Reimbursement of interest on 75 per cent of the unit cost or 5 per cent per annum (maximum Rs. 25,000 per annum) by the beneficiary on the loan availed from the bank, whichever is less, will be done by the department for 7 years. Interest on the remaining interest rate above 5 percent will have to be reimbursed by the beneficiary himself. Margin money support - 25% of the project cost for general category subject to a maximum of Rs. 1.50 lakh 33 percent of the project cost for SC/ST, maximum Rs. 2.00 lakhs. 

**Who is this for:** All; Category: Agriculture,Rural & Environment; Sector: Animal husbandry

**Where it applies:** Madhya Pradesh; State

**DBT Scheme:** Yes (Direct Benefit Transfer)
