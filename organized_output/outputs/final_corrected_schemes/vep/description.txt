# Vocational Education Programme

**Short Title:** VEP

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Vocational Education, Skill Development, Training, Student, Government Secondary Schools

The “Vocational Education Programme” is a scheme under the Samagra Shiksha Programme, implemented by the Secondary Education Department, Government of Uttarakhand. The scheme aims to impart vocational education alongside academic learning to students of classes 9 to 12 in selected government secondary schools. 

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Education & Learning; Sector: Voctional education

**Where it applies:** Uttarakhand; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** Under the programme, 08 sectors approved by the Government of India are being implemented under NSQF to provide vocational education alongside academic education for students of classes 9 to 12 in selected schools, where they receive both vocational education and training. 

**Benefit Type:** In Kind

**How to apply:** - All students studying in classes 9 to 12 in government secondary schools where vocational education is conducted can take admission in the sectors operated under this programme. - If a student from another school wishes to study the vocational education curriculum, they must take admission in a school where the vocational education curriculum is being conducted. - Students already studying in such schools can choose vocational education as one of their subjects like other subjects. For this, no separate application is required. - There is also no need to submit any documents for the selection of vocational education subject. 
Mode: Offline.

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: Secondary Education Department, Government Of Uttarakhand; Nodal Department: Department Of Education.
