# Shelter Home For Women (SHW)

**Short Title:** SHW

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Women, Shelter, Counselling, Skill, Training

The scheme &quot;Shelter Home for Women (SHW)&quot; is a Social Welfare Scheme by the Directorate of Women &amp; Child Development, Govt. of Goa. The scheme provides temporary shelter and support to women who have no family or social support systems to rely on and rehabilitate the women socially and economically by the provision of skill training, and counselling. The shelter Homes are given Financial Assistance in the form of grants consisting of salary components and maintenance expenditures.

**Who is this for:** Government Organisation, NGO, Trust; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Shelter

**Where it applies:** Goa; State

**Benefits / Assistance:** The Shelter Homes are given Financial Assistance in the form of Grants consisting of Salary Components and Maintenance Expenditures.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**How to apply:** **Step 1:** Visit the Directorate of Women &amp; Child Development, Panaji-Goa, and request a hard copy of the proforma of the application form for the scheme from the Concerned Authority. **Step 2:** In the application form, fill in all the mandatory fields, paste the passport-sized photograph (signed across), and attach copies of all the mandatory documents (self-attest if required). **Step 3:** Submit the duly filled and signed application form along with the documents to the Concerned Authority. **Step 4:** Acquire the receipt/acknowledgement of the successful submission of the application.
Mode: Offline.

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Women and Child Development Department.
