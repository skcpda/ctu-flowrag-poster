# Grant for Construction of Sports Infrastructure

**Short Title:** GFCOSI

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Sports Infrastructure, Employment Generation, Youth Welfare

The scheme &amp;quot;Grant for Construction of Sports Infrastructure&amp;quot; by the Department of Sports, Government of Uttarakhand, aims to promote the development of sports infrastructure through financial support. Through this scheme, a grant of up to ₹50,00,000/- or 25% of the total project cost is provided for the construction of sports facilities such as badminton, table tennis, squash, indoor cricket, basketball, volleyball, football, hockey, and swimming. These facilities may also generate employment through operations. &lt;br&gt; 

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Sports & Culture; Sector: Sports

**Where it applies:** Uttarakhand; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - **Financial Support for Construction:** Provides a significant grant, covering 25% of the total cost or up to ₹50,00,000/-, for building private sports facilities.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**How to apply:** **Step 1:** The interested applicant should visit the Sports Directorate and request a hard copy of the prescribed format of the application form. **Step 2:** In the application form, provide all the mandatory details, and attach copies of all the mandatory documents (self-attested, if required). **Step 3:** Submit the duly filled and signed application form along with the supporting documents to the Sports Directorate. **Step 4:** Request a receipt or acknowledgement from the concerned authority to whom the application has been submitted. **Note:** The application can be submitted at any time as per the convenience of the applicant.
Mode: Offline.

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Department Of Sports.
