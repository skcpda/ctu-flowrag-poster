# Mukhyamantri Yuva Karya Prashikshan Yojana

**Short Title:** MMYKPY

**Scheme For:** Infra

**Tags:** Vocational Training, Specially-Abled Person, Student, Stipend

The Mukhyamantri Yuva Karya Prashikshan Yojana scheme was launched by the Department of Skill, Employment, Entrepreneurship and Innovation to enhance candidates&#39; employability through practical training with entrepreneurs. The scheme budget of ₹5,500 crore, and the training programs for approximately 10 lakh candidates per year.  ***Training Duration:*** Job Training will be for 6 months. 

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Skills & Employment; Sector: Training and Skill Up-gradation

**Where it applies:** Maharashtra; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** **Stipend:** 12th Pass - 6,000/- ITI / Diploma - 8,000/- Degree / Post Graduation - 10,000/-  **Note:** Stipends are transferred directly to trainees&#39; bank accounts linked with Aadhaar. 

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**How to apply:** ***Registration Process for Candidate:*** - Go to the official [CMYKPY website](https://cmykpy.mahaswayam.gov.in/Index.aspx) for job seeker registration and click on “**Intern Login**”.- Use your Aadhaar number as the username for your account. - Fill in your full name, date of birth, gender, email, and mobile number. - Create a secure password and confirm it. - After completing all required fields, submit the registration form to finish the process.  ***Log In Process:*** - Fill Your Aadhar Number as a User Name and Password. - Complete your profile details, and fill in the personal, address, qualification, and bank details. - Upload the photo, qualification document, birth certificate, etc. - Click on “Submit” and apply for the job.
Mode: Online.

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Skill Development and Entrepreneurship Department.
