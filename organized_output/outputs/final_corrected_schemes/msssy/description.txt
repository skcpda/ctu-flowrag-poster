# Mukhyamantri Shramik Siyaan Sahayata Yojana

**Short Title:** MSSSY

**Launch Date:** 2022-05-12

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Social Welfare, Financial Assistance

Labor Department of Chhattisgarh State started this scheme &amp;amp;quot;Mukhyamantri Shramik Siyaan Sahayata Yojana&amp;amp;quot; for Construction Workers on 12 May 2022. Upon reaching 60 years of age, construction workers&amp;amp;#39; membership automatically terminates. Construction workers who are about to retire from Board membership under the aforementioned provision will be provided with financial assistance to enhance their quality of life prior to retirement. &amp;lt;br&amp;gt;

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Financial assistance

**Where it applies:** Chhattisgarh; State

**DBT Scheme:** Yes (Direct Benefit Transfer)

**Benefits / Assistance:** A lump sum assistance amount of ₹ 20,000/- will be given to the eligible beneficiaries.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**How to apply:** 1. All the applicant have to visit the official [website](https://cglabour.nic.in) Chhattisgarh Labour Department. 1. On the home page click on &quot;[Apply](https://cglabour.nic.in/Departments.aspx?id=4)&quot; under &quot;Chhattisgarh Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board&quot;.1. Enter the following details 1. Select Group Name : &quot;Bhavan Evan Koi Sannirmaan&quot; 1. Select Service : &quot;Yojana&quot; 1. What do you want to do : &quot;Aavedan&quot; 1. Click next. 1. Select your district name and provide old/new registration number and click next. 1. Select the scheme name. 1. Fill out the online with required details. 1. Submit.
Mode: Online.
