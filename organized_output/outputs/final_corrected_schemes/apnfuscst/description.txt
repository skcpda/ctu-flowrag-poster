# Assistance to Farmers in Purchasing Nano Fertilizers Under the Agricultural Assistance Program for Farmers Other than Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes

**Short Title:** APNFUSCST

**Tags:** Agriculture, Nano Fertilizers, Farmer

The scheme &amp;quot;Assistance to Farmers in Purchasing Nano Fertilizers Under the Agricultural Assistance Program for Farmers Other than Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes&amp;quot; introduced by the Agriculture, Farmers Welfare and Cooperation Department, Gujarat, aims to provide financial aid to non-SC/ST farmers in Gujarat to help them purchase Nano Fertilizers. By encouraging the use of efficient and innovative agricultural inputs, the scheme strives to boost farm productivity, improve crop yields, and raise the overall economic well-being of farmers.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Agriculture,Rural & Environment; Sector: Agricultural Inputs- seeds, fertilizer etc.

**Where it applies:** Gujarat; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - Up to 50% of the purchased price per hectare or ₹750/- (within a maximum limit of 4 hectares per account.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**How to apply:** **Step 1:** The applicant may visit and apply online at I-Khedut Portal: [https://ikhedut.gujarat.gov.in/](https://ikhedut.gujarat.gov.in/) **Step 2:** On the home page, click on ‘Schemes’ and then click on ‘Agricultural Schemes’. **Step 3:** Now select the scheme, click on the ‘Apply’ button and an application page will open. **Step 4:** Click on &amp;quot;Apply New&amp;quot; button and submit a new application. **Step 5:** Click on the &amp;quot;Update Application&amp;quot; button to add corrections to the application. **Step 6:** Once the application is done, confirm it. **Step 7:** Take a print out of the confirmed application. **Step 8:** Applicant can also check their Application Status/View/Print/Upload Application details from the below link: [https://ikhedut.gujarat.gov.in/public/frm_Applicant_Corner.aspx](https://ikhedut.gujarat.gov.in/public/frm_Applicant_Corner.aspx) &gt; **Contact Us:** [Click here](https://ikhedut.gujarat.gov.in/Public/frm_Public_Contacts.aspx).
Mode: Online.

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Agriculture, Farmers Welfare and Cooperation Department.
