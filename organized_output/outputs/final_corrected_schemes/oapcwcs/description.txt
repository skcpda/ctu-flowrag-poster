# Old Age Pension Scheme - On Completion of 60 Years (UKBOCWWB)

**Short Title:** OAPSCYUKBOCWWB

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Labour, Old Age, Construction Worker, Senior Citizen, Pension

The scheme “Old Age Pension Scheme” is implemented by the Uttarakhand Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board (UKBOCWWB) under the Labour Department, Government of Uttarakhand. The scheme aims to provide a monthly pension to registered construction workers who have attained the age of 60 years. 

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Pension

**Where it applies:** Uttarakhand; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - **Pension Amount:** ₹1,000/- per month 

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**How to apply:** - The online application can be submitted through Shramik Suvidha Kendras. - During the application process, the applicant is required to provide the Shramik Card, Aadhaar Card, Bank Passbook, a Self-Declaration Certificate, and an affidavit stating that no benefit from any other scheme is being availed. - If the documents are found correct during departmental investigation/verification, the pension is sanctioned and disbursed on a half-yearly basis. - After every six months, the worker must submit a life certificate to continue receiving the pension. 
Mode: Online - via CSC.

**Contacts & Authorities:** Implementing agency: Uttarakhand Building And Other Construction Workers Welfare Board; Nodal Department: Department Of Labour.

**References / Annexures:** - **Guidelines (Page No. 56)**: https://uk.gov.in/department92/library_file/file-04-12-2023-06-02-23.pdf - **Official Website**: https://ukbocw.uk.gov.in/

**Additional Information:**
Briefdescription: The scheme aims to provide a monthly pension of ₹1,000/- to construction workers registered with the Uttarakhand Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board who have attained the age of 60 years.
