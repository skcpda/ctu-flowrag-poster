# Dr.Babasaheb Ambedkar Udyog Uday Yojana: Assistance to Special Entrepreneurship Degree Approach Training with 360 Promoters/ with Mentoring Support and Sustenance Allowance for Entrepreneurs

**Short Title:** BAUUYSEAT

**Launch Date:** 2020-08-07

**Scheme For:** Infra

**Tags:** Business, Entrepreneurship, MSMEs, Start Up, Entrepreneurship Training, Scheduled Caste, Scheduled Tribe

The scheme &amp;quot;Dr.Babasaheb Ambedkar Udyog Uday Yojana&amp;quot; is an umbrella scheme launched by the Industries and Mines Department, Gujarat. It aims to empower the state&amp;#39;s dynamic MSME sector, which significantly contributes to employment, GDP, and exports. Effective from August 7th, 2020 to August 6th, 2025, this initiative supports SC/ST entrepreneurs and strengthens MSMEs, making them globally competitive. The component &amp;quot;Assistance to Special Entrepreneurship Degree Approach Training with 360 Promoters/ with Mentoring Support and Sustenance Allowance for Entrepreneurs&amp;quot; provides 360-degree training, mentoring support, and sustenance allowance to help first-generation entrepreneurs establish their businesses. It is implemented by the Center for Entrepreneurship Development (CED), a premier government organization engaged in entrepreneurship and skill development.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Business & Entrepreneurship; Sector: Setting up / start-up / entrepreneurship

**Where it applies:** Gujarat; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** > **Special Entrepreneurship Training:** 1. 100% coverage of training fees, up to ₹10,000/- per month per person. 1. Training duration between 6 months to 1 year. 1. Covers business management, finance, marketing, logistics, soft skills, and best practices. > **Mentorship Support:** 1. Entrepreneurs receive expert guidance from industrial mentors. 1. 100% coverage of mentorship fees, up to ₹5,000/- per month per person for 3 months. 1. Helps in understanding business operations, strategies, and management. > **Sustenance Allowance:** 1. ₹5,000/- per month for the duration of training to support personal expenses.

**Benefit Type:** Composite
