# Tiatr Script Writing Competition

**Short Title:** TSWC

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Culture, Artist, Writer, Script, Competition

The scheme &quot;Tiatr Script Writing Competition&quot; by the Tiatr Academy of Goa aims to encourage new generation of Tiatr playwrights to script better quality of Tiatrs and enrich the Konkani language through annual script writing competitions. The scheme provides cash prizes up to ₹15,000/- for winning scripts and opportunities for publication with honorarium payments, along with certificates for recognition of literary excellence in traditional Konkani theatre. The applicant must write original scripts in traditional tiatr format with minimum 6-7 podd&#39;de and at least 4 Cantos, and must submit scripts that have not been published or performed earlier. The scheme is implemented by Tiatr Academy of Goa and applications are accepted through offline submission of typed copies with duly filled application forms. 

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Sports & Culture; Sector: Culture

**Where it applies:** Goa; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** ##### ***Cash Prizes*** - 1st Prize: ₹15,000/- - 2nd Prize: ₹10,000/- - 3rd Prize: ₹5,000/- ##### ***Additional Benefits*** - **Certificates:** Provided to first three prize winners.- **Publication Opportunity:** Prize winning scripts can be printed in book form at Tiatr Academy of Goa&#39;s cost (subject to No Objection Certificate from script writer).- **Honorarium:** ₹5,000/- one-time payment for published scripts (in addition to prize money).- **Complimentary Copies:** 100 copies of the published book provided to script writer. ##### ***Conditions*** - Scripts must comply with content guidelines - no adverse remarks against any Governments/Individuals/Institutions or anything against caste, creed and religion - For publication benefits, script writers must provide No Objection Certificate to Tiatr Academy of Goa. 

**Benefit Type:** Composite

**How to apply:** **Step 1:** The interested applicant should take a printout of the [prescribed format](https://www.goa.gov.in/wp-content/uploads/2021/06/Citizen-Charter-TAG.pdf) of the application form. **Step 2:** In the application form, fill in all the mandatory fields. Attach copies of all the mandatory documents (self-attest, if required). Sign the application form and mention the date. **Step 3:** Submit the duly filled and signed application form along with the documents to - ***Tiatr Academy of Goa, AS-1 / AS-4, Block A, 2nd Floor, Campal Trade Centre, Opposite Kala Academy, Campal, Panaji, Goa, India – 403001*** _*Applicants are advised to save a copy of the submitted application form and any acknowledgment_ ##### ***Post-Application Processes*** **Step 1:****** ***Evaluation Process*** The submitted scripts will be evaluated by a panel of judges appointed by Tiatr Academy of Goa, whose decision will be final and binding. The judging process will assess scripts based on quality, format compliance, and adherence to specified themes. **Step 2:** **Result Declaration and Prize Distribution** Winners will be announced after the judging process is completed, and the first three best scripts will receive cash prizes and certificates as specified. Prize winners will be contacted for any additional procedures related to publication opportunities. 
Mode: Offline.

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Art, Culture and Museums Department.
