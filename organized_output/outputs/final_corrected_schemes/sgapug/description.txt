# Scheme for Granting Assistance to Processing Units in APMC (General Area)

**Short Title:** SGAPUG

**Scheme For:** Infra

**Tags:** Agro Processing, Processing Units, Agricultural Marketing, Food Industry

The scheme &amp;quot;Scheme for Granting Assistance to Processing Units in APMC (General Area)&amp;quot; by the Agriculture, Farmers Welfare and Cooperation Department, Government of Gujarat, aims to encourage establishment of agro and food processing units in the general areas of Agricultural Produce Market Committees (APMCs) to increase farmers’ income, reduce post-harvest losses, generate employment opportunities, and improve supply chain efficiencies. The scheme provides financial assistance in the form of capital subsidy to co-operatives institutions that set up processing units. 

**Who is this for:** Industries; Category: Agriculture,Rural & Environment; Sector: Financial assistance

**Where it applies:** Gujarat; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** - **Variable Grant Amount:** The grant percentage is tailored to the needs of different market committees, offering 25% for &#39;A&#39; and &#39;B&#39; class committees and 50% for &#39;C&#39; and &#39;D&#39; class committees.- **Maximum Assistance:** The maximum financial assistance provided under the scheme is capped at ₹1,50,00,000/-, ensuring substantial support for eligible projects. 

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**How to apply:** **Step 1:** The interested applicant should prepare the application on plain paper. **Step 2:** In the application form, provide all the mandatory details, and attach copies of all the mandatory documents (self-attested, if required). **Step 3:** Submit the duly filled and signed application form along with the supporting documents to the District Registrar&#39;s Office. **Step 4:** Request a receipt or acknowledgement from the concerned authority to whom the application has been submitted. 
Mode: Offline.

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Agriculture, Farmers Welfare and Cooperation Department.

**References / Annexures:** - **Guidelines (Scheme No. 525)**: https://mariyojana.gujarat.gov.in/Schemeatoz.aspx

**Additional Information:**
Briefdescription: The scheme aims to promote agro-processing and value addition by providing capital investment assistance to private entrepreneurs operating in regulated APMC areas (general zones).
