# Workers In Case Of Permanent Disablement-JKBOCWWB

**Short Title:** WICOPDJKBOCWWB

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Building Worker, Construction Workers, Labour, Permanent Disablement, Financial Assistance

The &quot;Workers In Case of Partial Permanent Disablement&quot; scheme by the Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board, Labour Department, Jammu &amp; Kashmir, under the MUHAFIZ Scheme (PMJJBY: Pradhan Mantri Jeevan Jyoti Bima Yojana, PMSBY: Pradhan Mantri Suraksha Bima Yojana) from 01.01.2018, provides ₹2,00,000 to registered BOC workers in the case of permanent disablement.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Health & Wellness; Sector: Financial assistance

**Where it applies:** Jammu and Kashmir; State/ UT

**Benefits / Assistance:** > Under the scheme, ₹2,00,000 is provided to registered BOC workers in case of partial permanent disablement.

**Benefit Type:** Cash

**How to apply:** **Registration of a Construction Worker under JKBOCWWB:** Step 1: Go to the J&amp;K Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board (JKBOCWWB) [official website.](https://www.jkbocw.gov.in/Index.aspx) Step 2: On the homepage, find and click the &quot;[Labour Registration&quot;](https://portal.jkbocw.gov.in/MemberRegistration.aspx) link. Step 3:If you&#39;re a new user, click on &quot;Apply for New Registration.&quot; Step 4: Complete the registration form with your details, submit the form, and send the OTP to your registered mobile number. Enter the OTP and click &quot;Verify OTP &amp; Submit.&quot; Step 5: After the OTP is verified, a confirmation message with your Login ID and password will be sent to your registered mobile number. Step 6:Use the Login ID, password, and captcha to log in (remember, the username and password are case-sensitive). filled are case sensitive)Read all the instruction carefully and Click to continue Step 7: Once logged in, choose the &quot;Construction Worker’s Registration&quot; option and click on &quot;Registration Form.&quot; Step 8: A dashboard will appear. Complete the form by entering all required details (marked with *). Step 9: Upload all the important documents, such as ID proof and other relevant certificates. Step 10: Once the form is completed and documents uploaded, click &quot;Submit&quot; and pay the application fee online. You will receive a registration number after successful payment and submission.   **Steps to Apply for Scheme Benefits:** Step1: Go to the [official website ](https://www.jkbocw.gov.in/Index.aspx)of the Jammu &amp; Kashmir Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board. Step2: Click on [&quot;Login,&quot;](https://portal.jkbocw.gov.in/LoginGuest.aspx) enter your details, and sign in to your account. Step3: After logging in, click on &quot;Scheme Registration&quot; and select &quot;Apply for Scheme.&quot; Step4: Choose the scheme you wish to apply for in order to avail the benefits. Step5: Enter all required information and upload the necessary documents as prompted. Step6: Finally, click on &quot;Submit Application&quot; to complete the process.   **Application Tracking:** Applicant can track their application status online through the [official website](https://www.jkbocw.gov.in/Index.aspx) of the Jammu &amp; Kashmir Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board. Relevant updates will be sent to the registered mobile number.
