# Chief Minister Divyangjan Empowerment Scheme (Sambal)

**Short Title:** CMDES

**Launch Date:** 2016-01-01

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Tags:** Financial Assistance, Empowerment, Benefits, Disability

The main objective of this scheme is to provide quick benefits for the welfare of the disabled to all the schemes of the disability sector run by the state government. To increase awareness towards the disabled, to ensure their rights while empowering them physically, socially, educationally and economically. For the operation of Sambal, a provision of 100 percent amount is made by the state government. Similarly, for the participation of the state in the schemes of the National Trust, the necessary supplementary grant amount is given by the state government under this scheme to the beneficiaries.

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Social welfare & Empowerment; Sector: Financial assistance

**Where it applies:** Bihar; State

**Benefits / Assistance:** 1. A loan of up to Rs.2.00 lakh will be given for self-employment. 1. The scholarship will be given for teaching in special schools, the rate of which will be less than the rate given to non-disabled people. 1. In very special cases, the rate of grant and state grant in schemes of National Trust will be payable according to the needs of the scheme and the specific beneficiary.

**Benefit Type:** Composite

**How to apply:** 1. Visit the official [website](https://online.bih.nic.in/). 1. Registration is mandatory for e-filling up the [form.](https://online.bih.nic.in/SWF/SWFTC/Register.aspx) 1. Use login credentials to log in. 1. Only eligible candidates as per guidelines/advertisement should fill up the form with Personal Details. 1. Upload Photo &amp;amp; Signature. 1. Upload Documents. 1. Finalise and submit application.
Mode: Online.

**Contacts & Authorities:** Nodal Department: Social Welfare Department.
