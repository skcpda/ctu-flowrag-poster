# Financial Assistance For Persons With Special Needs

**Short Title:** FAFPWSN

**Scheme For:** Individual

**Details** Delhi Social Welfare Department is implementing a disability pension which is fusion of State as well Central Government contribution. The scheme is known as Subsistence Allowance/Financial Allowance to person with special need. The grant is Rs 2500/- per month. These are the below mention disability consider under the scheme. 1. Blindness 1. Low vision 1. Leprosy-cured person 1. Deaf 1. Locomotor Disability 1. Cerebral Palsy 1. Autism Spectrum Disorder 1. Intellectual Disability (Mental Retardation) 1. Mental Illness 1. Dwarfism 1. Muscular Dystrophy 1. Acid Attack Victims 1. Hard of hearing 1. Speech and language disability 1. Specific Learning Disabilities 1. Multiple Sclerosis 1. Parkinson’s Disease 1. Hemophilia 1. Thalassemia 1. Sickle Cell Disease 1. Multiple Disabilities &lt;br&gt;

**Who is this for:** Individual; Category: Health & Wellness; Sector: Financial assistance

**Where it applies:** Delhi; State
